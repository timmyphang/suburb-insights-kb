1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**  
St Albans is a working-class, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,205, well below the Victorian median of $1,759 [citation:2021 St Albans (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22330). The median age is 36, and the population is 38,042, with a mix of families and singles. The suburb has a high proportion of blue-collar workers and a strong multicultural community.

3) **Working-parent support**  
No on-site Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is mentioned for St Albans North Primary School in the available sources. Nearby OSHC options include OSHClub at St Albans Primary School (39 West Esplanade, operating before and after school) and Big Childcare at St Albans Meadows PS (2A Laurel St, operating 6:45am–8:45am and after school) [citation:OSHC in St Albans, VIC 3021 (Before & After School Care) - Toddle](https://toddle.com.au/oshc/vic/st-albans).

4) **School culture**  
St Albans North Primary School offers a **Breakfast Club** each morning, a **Gardening Club** (with a Kitchen Garden lunchtime program), and an **End of Year Whole School Concert** with a different theme each year [citation:Breakfast Club | St Albans North PS](https://www.stalbansnth.vic.edu.au/breakfast-club) [citation:Gardening Club | St Albans North PS](https://www.stalbansnth.vic.edu.au/gardening-club) [citation:School Concert | St Albans North PS](https://www.stalbansnth.vic.edu.au/copy-2-of-camp-program). The school also runs a Camp Program and celebrates Harmony Day.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**  
St Albans has a vibrant, multicultural shopping scene with many specialty grocers. **Pahal Grocers** (282 Main Road East) offers South Asian, Sri Lankan, Pakistani, and Indian groceries [citation:Pahal Grocers: South Asian Flavours and Everyday Essentials in St Albans - St Albans Town Centre](https://www.stalbanstowncentre.com.au/news-stories/pahal-grocers). **KP Asian Grocery** (12 Alfrieda Street) stocks Korean, Japanese, Vietnamese, Thai, and Malaysian products [citation:KP Asian Grocery - St Albans Town Centre](https://www.stalbanstowncentre.com.au/directory/kp-asian-grocery). **KFL Supermarket** (330 Main Road East) is a well-known Asian food specialist [citation:St Albans KFL | The Asian Food Specialist](https://www.kflsupermarkets.com.au/st-albans-vic).

6) **Weeknight dining**  
Family-friendly takeaway and dining options include **Phi Phi Vietnamese & Chinese Restaurant** (on Main Road East) for Vietnamese and Chinese dishes [citation:THE 10 BEST Restaurants in St Albans](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g4358126-St_Albans_Brimbank_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). **Pizza Del Mondo** offers popular pizza for families [citation:THE 10 BEST Restaurants in St Albans](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g4358126-St_Albans_Brimbank_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). **Shahi Darbaar** (10A East Esplanade) serves Indian cuisine with takeaway available [citation:St Albans 3021 Food Delivery and Takeaway VIC | ozfoodhunter.com.au](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/st-albans-3021).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**  
**Innate Tutoring** (online and in-person) provides VCE tutoring in English, Math, Chemistry, and Biology for St Albans students [citation:Tutoring St Albans | VCE Tutors, English, Online Biology & Chemistry Help](https://www.innatetutoring.com.au/st-albans-tutoring/). **Success Tutoring St Albans** offers English and Math tutoring for primary and secondary students [citation:St Albans – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-st-albans).

8) **Commute**  
St Albans Station is on the **Sunbury line** (Metro Trains). The average train journey from St Albans Station to Flinders Street Station is **31 minutes**, with trains running every 15 minutes [citation:Train St Albans Station to Flinders Street Station from $4 | Tickets & Timetables | Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/St-Albans-Station/Flinders-Street-Station). Driving takes about 22 minutes.
