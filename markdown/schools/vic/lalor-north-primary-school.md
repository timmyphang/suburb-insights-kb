1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Lalor is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a mix of blue-collar and white-collar workers. The median weekly household income is approximately $1,374 (based on a median monthly income of $5,956), and the median age is 38 [citation:2021 Lalor - East QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/209041529) [citation:Lalor property market](https://www.htag.com.au/vic/vic197-whittlesea-city/lalor-vic-3075/). The suburb has a high proportion of families, with an average household size of 2.8 people, and 49.2% of residents are married [citation:Lalor property market](https://www.htag.com.au/vic/vic197-whittlesea-city/lalor-vic-3075/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The exact percentage for the most common overseas country of birth is not available in the provided sources. However, the school community is highly multicultural, with the school celebrating bilingual concerts in Macedonian and Greek, indicating significant communities from those backgrounds [citation:The ARTS - Lalor North Primary School](https://www.lalornorthps.vic.edu.au/the-arts).

3) **Working-parent support**: Lalor North Primary School partners with TheirCare to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC). Before School Care runs from 6:30 AM to 8:30 AM, and After School Care runs from 3:30 PM to 6:00 PM, with Pupil Free Day care from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM [citation:Outside School Hours Care - Lalor North Primary School](https://www.lalornorthps.vic.edu.au/outside-school-hours-care). The service also offers school holiday programs and is eligible for the Child Care Subsidy.

4) **School culture**: Lalor North Primary School offers a range of named extracurricular activities. These include a **school choir** with weekly practices and community performances, a **STOMP Dance Program** for Grades 5 and 6 focusing on movement and rhythm, and a **Breakfast Club** every Thursday morning from 8:15 AM providing free healthy breakfast [citation:The ARTS - Lalor North Primary School](https://www.lalornorthps.vic.edu.au/the-arts) [citation:Breakfast Club - Lalor North Primary School](https://www.lalornorthps.vic.edu.au/breakfast-club). Students also participate in district sport competitions [citation:Curriculum Framework Policy](https://www.lalornorthps.vic.edu.au/uploads/165/76/Curriculum-Framework-Policy-june-2025.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Lalor has a vibrant, family-friendly atmosphere with several speciality grocery stores catering to diverse cultures. At the Lalor Shopping Centre and along Station Street, you can find **Thien Lien Asian Grocery** (29 May Road), **Gran Monte Supermarket** (296 Station Street), and **Afghan Supermarket** (322-324 Station Street), alongside a Coles supermarket [citation:Lalor Shopping Centre](https://lalorshoppingcentre.au/store/thien-lien-asian-grocery/). There are also many fruit and vegetable shops and halal butchers.

6) **Weeknight dining**: For casual family-friendly dining, Lalor offers several takeaway options. **Nacools Pizza** serves Italian and pizza cuisine, **Ginos Pizza - Lalor** also offers Italian and pizza, and **Fat Wraps and Wings** provides chicken and chips [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Lalor, VIC 3075](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/lalor-vic-3075). These venues are available for delivery or takeaway via platforms like TuckerFox.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Families can access tutoring at **Brainworks Lalor**, which offers one-to-one maths and English tutoring for Years 3–9, as well as selective school entry preparation, located at the Lalor Library Complex (2A May Road) [citation:Brainworks Lalor](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-lalor). Another option is the **Lalor Neighbourhood House After School Tutoring** program at 47A French Street, providing affordable one-to-one or small-group sessions for primary and secondary students in literacy, numeracy, and other subjects [citation:After School Tutoring - Lalor Neighbourhood House](https://lalornh.org.au/youth-and-childrens-programs/after-school-tutoring/).

8) **Commute**: Lalor is served by **Lalor Station** on the **Mernda line**. The train journey from Lalor Station to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD takes approximately **37 minutes**, with trains running every 15–20 minutes [citation:Lalor to Flinders Street Trains](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Lalor-Station/Flinders-Street-Station) [citation:Lalor Station - Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/lalor/). Direct services operate daily.
