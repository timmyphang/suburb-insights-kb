1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Mordialloc is a middle-to-upper-income, largely white-collar suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,037, and the median age is 40 [citation:2021 Mordialloc, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21760). Families account for a significant share: 44.9% of adults are married, and the average household size is 2.4 people [citation:Mordialloc, VIC 3195 Property Market and House Prices 2026](https://www.htag.com.au/vic/vic226-kingston-city/mordialloc-vic-3195/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Mordialloc is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 63.5% of residents born in Australia [citation:2021 Mordialloc, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21760). Among overseas-born residents, the most common country of birth is not specified in the available source.

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) at Mordialloc Beach Primary School is provided by TeamKids (formerly Camp Australia) [citation:TeamKids - Mordialloc Beach Primary | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/camp-australia-mordialloc-beach-primary-school-oshc). During school term, before-school care runs 6:45–8:45 am and after-school care runs 3:30–6:15 pm, Monday to Friday; holiday care runs 6:45 am–6:15 pm [citation:TeamKids - Mordialloc Beach Primary | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/camp-australia-mordialloc-beach-primary-school-oshc).

4) **School culture:** Named extracurriculars at Mordialloc Beach Primary School include the Colour Fun Run [citation:Mordialloc Beach Primary School](https://www.mordiallocbeachps.vic.edu.au/), TeamKids Mother's Day and Special Friends Morning [citation:Mordialloc Beach Primary School](https://www.mordiallocbeachps.vic.edu.au/), and Pre-School Club sessions [citation:Mordialloc Beach Primary School](https://www.mordiallocbeachps.vic.edu.au/). The school also offers camps and excursions as part of its curriculum framework [citation:1 CAMPS AND EXCURSIONS Help for non-English speakers](https://www.mordiallocbeachps.vic.edu.au/source/School%20Policies/Camps-and-Excursions-Policy%20June%202023.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Mordialloc has a coastal, family-friendly vibe with multiple major supermarkets including Woolworths (600 Main Street, open 8 am–10 pm weekdays) and ALDI (542 Main Street) [citation:Supermarkets in Mordialloc, Victoria](https://mordialloc-vic.aussiestoresonline.com/supermarket/). For specialty items, residents can visit Four Cousins (continental/upmarket grocer at 383 Nepean Highway, Parkdale) and IGA Friendly Grocers (81 Warren Road) [citation:Supermarkets in Mordialloc, VIC 3195](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/mordialloc-vic-3195).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly takeaway and dining options in Mordialloc include Tommy Ruff Fish Bar (American/seafood, takeaway) [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Mordialloc - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g1937587-zfp10601-Mordialloc_Kingston_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html), Manow Thai Restaurant (Thai cuisine) [citation:Restaurants in Mordialloc,VIC With Takeaway | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/mordialloc/takeaway-category), and Sun Wah Restaurant (Chinese/Asian) [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Mordialloc - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g1937587-zfp10601-Mordialloc_Kingston_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Kip McGrath Mordialloc (131 Parkers Road, Parkdale) offers individualised English and maths tutoring from prep to Year 10, with 80-minute sessions combining one-on-one attention and computer-based activities [citation:KMEC Mordialloc Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/mordialloc). Success Tutoring Mordialloc provides personalised English and math programs mapped to the Australian Curriculum [citation:Tutoring Mordialloc | Primary & High School Tutors](https://www.successtutoring.com/tutoring-melbourne/mordialloc).

8) **Commute:** Mordialloc is on the Frankston train line. The journey from Mordialloc Station to Flinders Street Station takes approximately 53 minutes, with trains running every 10–15 minutes [citation:Mordialloc to Flinders Street Station - 4 ways to travel via train](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Mordialloc/Flinders-Street-Station).
