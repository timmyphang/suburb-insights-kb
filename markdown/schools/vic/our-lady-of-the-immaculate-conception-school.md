1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Sunshine is a working-class to middle-income suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,566 and a median age of 35, indicating a mix of young families and singles [citation:2021 Sunshine (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22395). The suburb has a population density of 1,240 persons per square km and is part of the City of Brimbank, with a notable proportion of blue-collar and service industry workers [citation:About the profile areas | Sunshine | profile.id](https://profile.id.com.au/brimbank/about?WebID=240).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas country of birth for Sunshine residents is not explicitly listed in the available sources, but the suburb is known for its multicultural population with significant Vietnamese, Indian, and other Asian communities. Australian-born residents make up a substantial portion, though the exact percentage is not provided in the sourced data [citation:2021 Sunshine (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22395).

3) **Working-parent support:** Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by Extend, operating after school from 3:05 pm to 6:05 pm at 32 Station Place, Sunshine [citation:Our Lady of Immaculate Conception - Extend Before and After School Care](https://www.extend.com.au/school/our-lady-of-immaculate-conception/). Before school care hours are not listed in the sourced information.

4) **School culture:** The school offers several named extracurriculars including **Choir** (which leads singing during mass), **STEM Club**, and **Interschool Sport** for Years 5 and 6 (sports such as cricket, basketball, football, netball, and soccer) [citation:Search Through Site](https://www.olsunshine.catholic.edu.au/search/) [citation:Interschool Sport - Our Lady Immaculate Conception](https://www.olsunshine.catholic.edu.au/page/13/Interschool-Sport). These activities complement a strong focus on community and wellbeing.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Sunshine has a lively, family-friendly atmosphere with a range of ethnic grocers. **Tai Phat Asian Grocery** (286b Hampshire Rd) offers fresh Asian fruits, vegetables, and groceries, while **Sunshine Supermarket (Thuan Hung)** provides authentic Asian products including fresh meat from its in‑house butcher [citation:Tai Phat Asian Grocery - Sunshine Business Association](https://www.sunshinebusiness.com.au/business_listing/tai-phat-asian-grocery/) [citation:Sunshine Supermarket (Thuan Hung) - Sunshine Business Association](https://www.sunshinebusiness.com.au/business_listing/sunshine-supermarket/). Major supermarkets such as Coles and Woolworths are also accessible.

6) **Weeknight dining:** Specific casual family-friendly dine‑in or takeaway restaurants in Sunshine are not named in the provided sources. However, the suburb hosts a variety of takeaway options including pizza, fish and chips, and Asian cuisine, as indicated by local business directories [citation:10 BEST Takeaway Food in Sunshine Coast QLD | Localsearch](https://www.localsearch.com.au/find/takeaway-food/sunshine-coast-qld) — note this source is for Sunshine Coast QLD, not VIC; no V‑specific restaurant names were found.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Two named tutoring centres near Sunshine are **ABC to VCE** (with a learning centre in Sunshine offering primary and secondary coaching) and **Emu Education** (providing VCE Maths, English, Chemistry, Biology, and Physics tutoring) [citation:Tutor Sunshine | Secondary High School & SEAL Coaching Sunshine](https://www.abctovce.com.au/tutors-sunshine/) [citation:Tutor Sunshine | VCE, Primary and High School Tutoring | Emu Education](https://www.emueducation.com.au/tutor-sunshine/).

8) **Commute:** Sunshine is served by **Sunshine Station** on the **Sunbury line** (also used by Werribee and Williamstown lines). The train journey from Sunshine Station to Flinders Street Station takes approximately **24 minutes**, with services running every 20 minutes [citation:Sunshine Station to Flinders Street Station - Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Sunshine-Station/Flinders-Street-Station) [citation:Sunshine | Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/sunshine/).
