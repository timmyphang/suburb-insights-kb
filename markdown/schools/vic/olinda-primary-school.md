1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Olinda is a predominantly middle-to-upper-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,995 [citation:2021 Olinda (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22009). The median age is 45, notably older than the Victorian median of 38, reflecting a mature resident base [citation:2021 Olinda (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22009). The suburb has 494 families and an average of 1.8 children per family with children, indicating a strong family presence [citation:2021 Olinda (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22009). Detailed occupation breakdowns (white-collar vs blue-collar) were not found in the available sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Olinda is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 51.7% of residents having both parents born in Australia and 24.5% having both parents born overseas [citation:2016 Olinda (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21997). The 2021 QuickStats country-of-birth detail was not available in the provided context, but the suburb shows a strong Australian-born majority. The most common overseas country of birth is not named in the available snippets.

3) **Working-parent support:** Olinda Primary School directs families to Council Child Care for before and after school supervision, stating it can "provide information about Council Child Care before and after school" [citation:Out of School Hours Care](https://www.olindaps.vic.edu.au/content/out-school-hours-care). The specific provider name and exact hours of operation are not listed on the school’s website; contact the school directly at olinda.ps@edumail.vic.gov.au for details [citation:Out of School Hours Care](https://www.olindaps.vic.edu.au/content/out-school-hours-care). No on-site OSHC provider name was found in the provided sources.

4) **School culture:** Olinda Primary School offers a weekly lunchtime singing group that builds social and performance confidence [citation:Music](https://www.olindaps.vic.edu.au/content/music). The school also provides instrumental classes in piano and violin, taught by a local community member [citation:Music](https://www.olindaps.vic.edu.au/content/music). Additionally, a group of senior children attend "Woorabinda Camp" for outdoor activities, as noted in school news [citation:News](https://www.olindaps.vic.edu.au/news?page=2).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Olinda has a village feel with local amenities including The Organic Fanatic at 234 Ridge Road for health-focused groceries [citation:35 BEST local Supermarkets & Grocery Stores in Olinda, VIC](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/olinda-vic-3788). There are no specialty Asian or ethnic supermarkets in Olinda proper, but nearby Montrose IGA (916 Mount Dandenong Road, Montrose) and Woolworths Belgrave (1629 Burwood Highway, Belgrave) serve general grocery needs [citation:35 BEST local Supermarkets & Grocery Stores in Olinda, VIC](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/olinda-vic-3788). Haodi Asian Supermarket (Unit 2, 262a Dorset Road, Boronia) is an option for Asian groceries [citation:35 BEST local Supermarkets & Grocery Stores in Olinda, VIC](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/olinda-vic-3788).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For family-friendly takeaway and dining, Olinda Pizza & Take Away (1514 Mount Dandenong Tourist Rd) offers pizza and fast food with dinner service [citation:OLINDA PIZZA & TAKE AWAY](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurant_Review-g552232-d4774249-Reviews-Olinda_Pizza_Take_Away-Olinda_Victoria.html). Indian by Nature provides Indian cuisine and is open for dinner, described as a cosy option [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Olinda](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g552232-zfp10601-Olinda_Victoria.html). Dudley's of Olinda offers woodfired pizza and European fare with a kids menu and takeaway [citation:Dudleys of Olinda](https://www.dudleysolinda.com.au/).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** The Ranges Learning Centre (located in Olinda) provides evidence-based intervention in literacy, numeracy, and writing, with programs including MSL (Multi-Sensory Structured Language) intervention and Maths Intervention [citation:The Ranges Learning Centre](https://www.therangeslc.com.au/). Success Tutoring also offers personalised academic support in Olinda, with 24/7 access to resources [citation:Olinda – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-olinda).

8) **Commute:** Olinda is not served by a train station. The nearest train station is Belgrave, which is on the Belgrave Line, approximately 8 km away by car. From Belgrave Station, the train journey to Flinders Street Station takes about 54 minutes [citation:Flinders Street Station to Olinda](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Olinda-VIC-Australia). For Olinda itself, residents typically drive to Belgrave or catch a bus (route 694 from Belgrave to Olinda), with total travel time to the Melbourne CBD around 1 hour 27 minutes by car and train [citation:Flinders Street Station to Olinda](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Olinda-VIC-Australia).
