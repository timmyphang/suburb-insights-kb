The median weekly household income is $1,766, slightly above the Victorian median, and the median age is 42, reflecting an older demographic than the state average [citation:2021 Bonbeach QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20278). The suburb has a relatively high proportion of couples with children, and the workforce is a mix of white‑collar and blue‑collar occupations, though specific occupational breakdown is not available in the provided sources [citation:2021 Bonbeach QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20278).

3) **Working‑parent support**: Bonbeach Primary School partners with **TheirCare** to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC). The program runs from **7:00am to 8:45am** before school and **3:30pm to 6:00pm** after school on school days, and from **7:00am to 6:00pm** on pupil‑free days [citation:TheirCare](https://www.bonbeachps.vic.edu.au/page/236/TheirCare). This service is available to all children at the school and preschool.

4) **School culture**: Bonbeach Primary School offers a range of named extracurricular activities. These include **weekly interschool sport**, **HPV (Human Powered Vehicle) racing**, and the **Premier’s Reading Challenge** [citation:Senior School](https://www.bonbeachps.vic.edu.au/page/189/Senior-School). Students can also participate in **gymnastics**, **aerobics**, and the **Maths Olympiad** [citation:Senior School](https://www.bonbeachps.vic.edu.au/page/189/Senior-School). The school choir performs at community events such as the Easter Movie Night [citation:Happy Easter! Movie Night](https://www.bonbeachps.vic.edu.au/uploaded_files/media/newsletter_04.04.19.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Bonbeach is a coastal suburb with a relaxed, family‑friendly atmosphere, featuring parks, beaches, and local shops along Nepean Highway. For specialty groceries, **Baan Chum** is an Asian grocer serving Chelsea, Bonbeach, and Edithvale, offering a wide range of Asian ingredients and cooking advice [citation:Baan Chum](https://www.baanchum.com.au/about-us/). There are also several supermarkets and independent grocery stores in the area, though specific names beyond Baan Chum were not listed in the provided sources.

6) **Weeknight dining**: Casual family‑friendly takeaway options in Bonbeach include **Sushi Brazilia** (Japanese/Brazilian fusion), **Gabby’s Pizza** (pizza), and **That Parma Place** (chicken parma and pub‑style meals) [citation:Uber Eats Bonbeach](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/bonbeach-melbourne-vic). Additionally, **The Indian Hut** offers Indian cuisine with takeaway and delivery, located near Beachside Apartments Bonbeach [citation:Restaurants Near Beachside Apartments Bonbeach – OpenTable](https://www.opentable.com.au/landmark/restaurants-near-beachside-apartments-bonbeach).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Tutoring options in or near Bonbeach include **Tutero**, which provides 1‑on‑1 online tutoring for Prep to Year 12 students, matching them with top‑2% tutors [citation:Tutero](https://www.tutero.com/au/online-tutoring/locations/bonbeach-vic). **Tutor2you** offers in‑home tutoring in Bonbeach for subjects such as Maths, English, and Science, with all tutors holding valid working‑with‑children checks [citation:Tutor2you](https://tutor2you.com.au/our-tutors/suburb-3196-Bonbeach/).

8) **Commute**: Bonbeach is served by the **Frankston line** with trains stopping at **Bonbeach Station**. The journey to **Flinders Street Station** in the Melbourne CBD takes approximately **54 minutes**, with trains running every 15 minutes [citation:Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Bonbeach-Station). Driving time is about 31 minutes via Nepean Highway [citation:Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Bonbeach-Station).
