1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Ashburton is an affluent, white-collar suburb with a strong family focus. According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income is $2,743, and the median age is 41 [citation:ABS 2021 QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/207011146). Families make up a significant proportion of the population [citation:CompoundInvest Suburb Profile](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/ashburton/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The suburb is predominantly Australian-born. In the 2021 Census, 75.9% of residents were born in Australia. The most common overseas country of birth is not specified in the available data [citation:ABS 2021 QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/207011146).

3) **Working-parent support:** Ashburton Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by TeamKids. Specific hours of operation are not detailed in the available sources, but the school lists 'TeamKids Before & After Care, Vacation Care' as a service [citation:Ashburton PS Prep FAQ](https://www.ashburtonps.vic.edu.au/enrolments/frequently-asked-questions-faq).

4) **School culture:** The school supplements its curriculum with several named extracurricular activities. These include a lunchtime "soccer" club, a "chess club," and "yoga" [citation:Supplementing the Curriculum](https://ashburtonps.vic.edu.au/student-learning/supplementing-the-curriculum). The school also has an "Ashy Out Loud Junior" performance group for Prep to Year 2 students [citation:Prin189](https://www.ashburtonps.vic.edu.au/news/prin189).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Ashburton has a leafy, family-friendly atmosphere with parks like Ashburton Park and Warner Reserve [citation:CompoundInvest Suburb Profile](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/ashburton/). For groceries, Woolworths and IGA Everyday are located in Ashburton, and MKS Spices'n Things on Warrigal Road offers a more specialised selection of spices and groceries [citation:Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/ashburton-vic-3147/supermarkets-grocery-stores).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Ashburton has several casual dining options for families. Ashy's Afghan Restaurant offers Afghan cuisine [citation:AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/ashburton/takeaway-category). Tong Lok Chinese Restaurant and Sweet Tamarind provide Chinese and Thai options, respectively, all located in Ashburton [citation:AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/ashburton/takeaway-category).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Learningland 4 Kids, located at 44 Taylor Street in Ashburton, provides primary school tutoring in literacy and numeracy, along with school readiness programs [citation:Learningland 4 Kids](https://learningland4kids.com.au/). The Kumon Ashburton Education Centre offers its English and mathematics tutoring programs [citation:Kumon Ashburton](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/Kumon-Ashburton-VIC).

8) **Commute:** Ashburton is served by the Alamein train line. The local station is Ashburton Station. A direct train from Ashburton Station to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD takes approximately 22 minutes [citation:Alamein line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alamein_line) [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Ashburton-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
