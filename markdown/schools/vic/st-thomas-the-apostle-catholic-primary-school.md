Below is a concise, family-facing eight‑point briefing for St Thomas the Apostle Catholic Primary School (Cranbourne East). I used one search per data point and opened a page only when the snippet lacked the needed fact. Inline citations follow each claim; full source list is at the end.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile
- Cranbourne East is a young, family‑oriented, middle‑income suburb (median age 31; average household size ~3.3) with many couples with children rather than singles. Median weekly household income is about $1,900–$1,930 (roughly middle-income for Melbourne suburbs) [citation:2021 Cranbourne East, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20663).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- The top overseas birthplace is India (the largest overseas-born group in Cranbourne East — reported in 2021 Census profiles) [citation:2021 Cranbourne East, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20663).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- St Thomas runs an on‑site Village OSHC offering before‑school, after‑school and school‑holiday programs (vacation care) at the school site (VillageOSHC provider) [citation:Outside School Hours Care - Village - St Thomas the Apostle](https://cranbourneeast.catholic.edu.au/oshc-village) [citation:St Thomas the Apostle — VillageOSHC | StartingBlocks](https://www.startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01HGCR52RAZ8X9E6HXJ49R62T2/St-Thomas-the-Apostle-Catholic-Primary-School-Cranbourne-East-VillageOSHC).

4) School culture — extracurriculars
- Specialist/extra activities include Languages (Auslan from F–6), The Arts (music/visual & media arts programs) and Health & Physical Education with school sport/interschool sports opportunities [citation:Subjects and Specialist Subjects - St Thomas the Apostle Catholic Primary School Cranbourne East](https://cranbourneeast.catholic.edu.au/specialist-areas) [citation:Proposed School Fees Schedule (notes interschool sports)](https://cranbourneeast.catholic.edu.au/_d/2134).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries
- Cranbourne East feels like a growing family suburb with lots of young children and community facilities; local shopping hubs serve day‑to‑day family needs [citation:2021 Cranbourne East, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20663). Local supermarkets include a Woolworths (Hunt Club / Linsell Blvd) and an ALDI; there are also small specialty Asian/Filipino grocers listed for the area (e.g., Kuya JJ Asian Grocery) [citation:Woolworths Cranbourne East (Hunt Club) listing](https://cranbourne-east-vic.aussiestoresonline.com/woolworths-cranbourne-east-hunt-club/) [citation:Kuya JJ Asian Grocery listing](https://australia.chamberofcommerce.com/business-directory/victoria/cranbourne-east/asian-grocery-store/3786944-kuya-jj-asian-grocery).

6) Weeknight dining (family‑friendly takeaways)
- Hunt Club Pizza & Pasta (Hunt Club Village) — pizza/pasta takeaway; plus the Hunt Club Village shopping hub offers multiple casual takeaway options for families [citation:Hunt Club Pizza & Pasta Cranbourne East](https://huntclubpizza.com.au/) [citation:Hunt Club Village listing (Hunt Club, Linsell Blvd)](https://www.mapquest.com/au/victoria/hunt-club-village-794776981).

7) Tuition and enrichment
- Local tutoring/enrichment options include STEM Academic College (Cranbourne tuition centre) and private/local tutoring services such as Alchemy Tuition (and many individual tutors listed on Superprof) — useful for extra maths/English support or selective‑entry prep if required [citation:Tuition Centre Cranbourne | STEM Academic College](https://stemacademiccollege.com.au/) [citation:Alchemy Tuition - Tutoring Cranbourne page](https://alchemytuition.com.au/tutoring-cranbourne/).

8) Commute
- Cranbourne East currently does not have an operational train station (a Cranbourne East station is proposed/planned but not built). The typical local route is a bus to Cranbourne station, then Cranbourne line train to Flinders Street — the Cranbourne→Flinders Street train leg is about 56–60 minutes on typical timetables; add a short (e.g., 10–20 minute) bus/drive to Cranbourne station for a door‑to‑door estimate ~70–80 minutes [citation:Cranbourne East precinct / future station (City of Casey)](https://www.casey.vic.gov.au/cranbourne-east) [citation:Cranbourne Line - Transport Victoria timetable](https://transport.vic.gov.au/route/timetable/4/cranbourne-line) [citation:Cranbourne to Melbourne CBD typical time (Rome2rio)](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Cranbourne/Melbourne-CBD).
