Greenhills Primary runs an accredited OSHC program operated by TeamKids: Before School Care 6:45–8:45am, After School Care 3:30–6:30pm, plus Vacation Care during school holidays (families register via TeamKids) [citation:Out of School Hours - greenhillsps.vic.edu.au](https://www.greenhillsps.vic.edu.au/oshcare).

Notable extracurriculars: school choirs (Grade 3, Grade 4 and auditioned Recital Choir), Marimba & Calypso percussion groups, and private/small‑group instrumental lessons (guitar, flute, piano, violin, clarinet, saxophone) [citation:Specialist Subjects - ghps](https://www.greenhillsps.vic.edu.au/specialist-subjects).
