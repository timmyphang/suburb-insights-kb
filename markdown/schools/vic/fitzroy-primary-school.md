1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Fitzroy is an inner-city suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,194 and a median age of 35, indicating a relatively affluent, young professional population [citation:2021 Fitzroy QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/206071142). The area has a mix of singles and families, with 2,273 families and an average of 0.3 children per household, reflecting a high proportion of childless households [citation:2021 Fitzroy QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20924). The suburb serves as a regional employment centre, hosting 1.5 jobs per resident [citation:Fitzroy – SA2 AreaSearch](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/fitzroy).

4) **School culture**: Fitzroy Primary School offers a **French Bilingual Program** as its signature immersion curriculum [citation:Welcome to Fitzroy Primary School](https://www.fitzroyprimaryschool.vic.edu.au/). The school also runs **La Fête Fantastique**, an annual French festival celebrating language and culture [citation:Welcome to Fitzroy Primary School](https://www.fitzroyprimaryschool.vic.edu.au/). In physical education, students participate in **inter-school sports competitions** and house events, fostering teamwork and school pride [citation:Physical Education Fitzroy Primary](https://www.fitzroyprimaryschool.vic.edu.au/index.php/our-learning/physical-education/).

6) **Weeknight dining**: **Fitz Curry Cafe** (44 Johnston St, Fitzroy) offers authentic Indian cuisine with takeaway and dine-in options [citation:Fitz Curry Cafe](https://fitzcurrycafe.com.au/). **Bontempo** (416-418 Brunswick St, Fitzroy) serves wood-fired pizza and pasta in a casual Italian setting, with takeaway available [citation:Bontempo](https://www.localsearch.com.au/find/takeaway-food/fitzroy-vic-3065). **Guzman y Gomez** (229 Smith St, Fitzroy) provides fast, family-friendly Mexican food with takeaway and delivery [citation:Guzman y Gomez Fitzroy](https://www.localsearch.com.au/find/takeaway-food/fitzroy-vic-3065).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: **Success Tutoring Fitzroy** offers personalised primary and high school tutoring with customised learning plans [citation:Success Tutoring Fitzroy](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-fitzroy). **Apex Tuition Australia** provides in-person and online tutoring across Fitzroy for VCE, IB, and high school subjects, including support for students at Fitzroy Primary School [citation:Apex Tuition Fitzroy](https://www.apextuitionaustralia.com/locations/fitzroy).

8) **Commute**: Fitzroy has no train station; the best public transport option is **tram route 11** from Flinders Street Station to Brunswick Street, taking approximately 18 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio Fitzroy to Flinders](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Fitzroy/Flinders-Street-Station). The tram runs frequently, with services every 15 minutes during peak times [citation:Rome2Rio Flinders to Fitzroy](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Fitzroy). This provides a convenient, direct connection to Melbourne’s CBD.
