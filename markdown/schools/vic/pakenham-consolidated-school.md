1) Suburb socio‑economic profile
- Younger, family‑heavy growth suburb: median age 33 (younger than Victoria’s 38) and a high share of children (0–14 ≈24% of population), indicating many young families. Pakenham is broadly middle‑income / growth‑area with mixed white‑ and blue‑collar employment (family‑oriented outer‑suburban profile) [citation:2021 Pakenham, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22027).

2) Most common overseas country of birth
- Majority Australian‑born (≈67%); the largest overseas birthplace is India (about 6% of residents) — India is the clear top overseas country [citation:Pakenham, VIC — Suburb Demographics & Profile | Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/pakenham) and supported by ABS QuickStats country‑of‑birth tables [citation:2021 Pakenham, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22027).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- On‑site OSHC available: Pakenham Consolidated runs "PCS LEAP" (Learn, Explore & Play) offering before‑school, after‑school and vacation care; it is an approved CCS‑eligible centre managed through the school community [citation:PCS LEAP - pakconps.vic.edu.au](https://www.pakconps.vic.edu.au/page/176/OSHC) (OSHC parent info PDF also available on the school site) [citation:Out of School Hours Care (PDF) - Pakenham Consolidated School](https://www.pakconps.vic.edu.au/uploaded_files/media/out_of_school_hours_care_info_for_parents.pdf).

4) School culture — extracurriculars (2–3 specific items)
- Specialist program: weekly specialist classes including PE, Visual Arts, Performing Arts, STEM and Cultural Studies [citation:Specialists - Pakenham Consolidated School](https://www.pakconps.vic.edu.au/page/185/Specialists).  
- Performing arts / school concerts and School Sports (School Sport Victoria affiliation) plus Year 5–6 electives (fitness, drama) and formal student leadership (Arts Captains, House Captains) [citation:2023/2024 Parent Contributions (Extra‑Curricular) - pakconps.vic.edu.au](https://www.pakconps.vic.edu.au/source/2023/2024_Parent_Contributions_Year_5-6.pdf) and [citation:Student Leadership - Pakenham Consolidated School](https://www.pakconps.vic.edu.au/page/177/Student-Leadership).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
