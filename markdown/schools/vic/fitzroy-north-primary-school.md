1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Fitzroy North is an affluent, white-collar inner suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,300 (2021 Census) [citation:ABS 2021 QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20925). The median age is 36, and the suburb has a high proportion of professionals and managers, with 36.5% of households earning $3,000 or more per week [citation:Household income profile.id](https://profile.id.com.au/yarra/household-income?WebID=170). Families are common—there are 2,956 families in the suburb—but the area also has many singles, reflected in an average household size of just 2.2 people [citation:ABS 2021 QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20925).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The suburb is predominantly Australian-born, with 69.4% of residents born in Australia (2021 Census) [citation:ABS 2021 QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20925). Among overseas-born residents, the most common country of birth is England (4.6%), followed by New Zealand (2.5%) and China (2.0%) [citation:ABS 2021 QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20925).

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) at Fitzroy North Primary School is delivered by TheirCare Pty Ltd, operating before school from 7:00 AM to 9:00 AM and after school from 3:30 PM to 6:00 PM on school days [citation:ACECQA Service Register](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/fitzroy-north-primary-school-oshc). Vacation care is also offered from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM Monday to Friday during holiday periods [citation:ACECQA Service Register](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/fitzroy-north-primary-school-oshc).

4) **School culture:** Fitzroy North Primary School offers a range of named extracurriculars including a **Grade 5 and Grade 6 rock band** (audition-based), a **Friday morning concert band**, and a **before-school Running Club** for grades 2–6 held at Brunswick Street Oval [citation:FNPS Music](https://fitzroynthps.vic.edu.au/music) [citation:FNPS Physical Education](https://www.fitzroynthps.vic.edu.au/physical-education). The school also has private and small-group woodwind and brass lessons through ABC Music, and students in grade 6 use Ableton Live for music production [citation:FNPS Music](https://fitzroynthps.vic.edu.au/music).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Fitzroy North has a village feel with abundant family amenities, including Edinburgh Gardens, the Fitzroy North Bowling Club, and the Brunswick Street Oval. For groceries, the standout is **Piedimonte's Supermarket** at 37-49A Best Street, a gourmet independent supermarket with an extensive deli and liquor section [citation:Piedimonte's Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/piedimontes-supermarket-and-liquor-north-fitzroy-fitzroy-north-3). Additional options include **Tales of India** for Indian grocery delivery to the area and **Tan Phat Asian Grocery** on Victoria Street in nearby Richmond [citation:Tales of India](https://talesofindia.com.au/pages/indian-grocery-store-fitzroy-3065) [citation:Yellow Pages Fitzroy North](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/fitzroy-north-vic-3068/supermarkets-grocery-stores).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family-friendly takeaway or dining, **Piccolino Woodfired Pizza & Homemade Pasta** on 148 Scotchmer Street serves authentic Italian pizzas and pasta [citation:Localsearch Fitzroy North](https://www.localsearch.com.au/find/takeaway-food/fitzroy-north-vic-3068). **Cookshop Rotisserie** on 392 Queens Parade offers healthy rotisserie chicken and salads, perfect for quick takeaway [citation:Localsearch Fitzroy North](https://www.localsearch.com.au/find/takeaway-food/fitzroy-north-vic-3068). For Indian food, **Singh's Indian Take Away Food** at 43 Nicholson Street in nearby Brunswick East provides reliable curries and vegetarian options [citation:Localsearch Fitzroy North](https://www.localsearch.com.au/find/takeaway-food/fitzroy-north-vic-3068).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Edu-Kingdom** offers in-person maths and English tutoring for Years 1 to 12 at its Fitzroy North centre, with free assessments and structured programs [citation:Edu-Kingdom Maths Tutor Fitzroy North](https://edukingdom.com.au/cm-maths-tutor-fitzroy-north/). Nearby, **Kumon Brunswick Education Centre** provides individualised mathematics, English, and pencil skills programmes for children [citation:EarlyBloom Learning Support](https://www.earlybloom.com.au/tutoring-in-fitzroy-north).

8) **Commute:** The nearest train station is **Rushall Station** on the **Mernda line**. Travel from Rushall Station to Flinders Street Station takes approximately **14 minutes**, with trains running every 20 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio Fitzroy North to Flinders](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Fitzroy-North/Flinders-Street-Station). Alternatively, the **Line 250 bus** from Porter Street/Holden Street to the CBD takes about 20–25 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio Flinders to Fitzroy North](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Fitzroy-North).
