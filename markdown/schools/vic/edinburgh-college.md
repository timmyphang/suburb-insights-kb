Quick note: Edinburgh College is an Early Learning–Year 12 independent college on one Lilydale campus (so it includes primary years but is not only a primary school) [citation:Edinburgh College homepage](https://edinburghcollege.vic.edu.au/).

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
Lilydale is a middle‑income outer‑east suburb with a median age of 40 and median weekly household income around $1,764 (2021 Census). The area has a mix of family households and a sizable proportion of married couples, reflecting a family‑oriented, predominantly white‑collar/mixed workforce profile for an outer suburban area [citation:2021 Lilydale QuickStats (ABS)](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21504).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
Lilydale is predominantly Australian‑born (about 79–80%); the most common overseas birthplace is England (≈5.0%) in 2021 — otherwise there is no large single non‑Australian community dominating the suburb [citation:BehindTheFence Lilydale profile citing 2021 Census](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/lilydale).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
Edinburgh College runs an on‑campus OSHC program: morning care 7:00–8:45am, afternoon care 3:30–5:30pm, plus vacation care when the Early Learning Centre is open — based in the Primary Multipurpose Hall [citation:Edinburgh College OSHC page](https://edinburgh.vic.edu.au/school-services/oshc).

4) School culture — extracurriculars (2–3 items)
- Instrumental/band program (band program from Year 5 with wind instruments; concerts, eisteddfods and tours). [citation:Edinburgh College Music](https://edinburgh.vic.edu.au/learning/secondary-school/music)  
- Basketball Academy / sports academy (school runs a Basketball Academy and broader sporting programs). [citation:Edinburgh College Basketball Academy](https://edinburgh.vic.edu.au/basketball-academy)

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
Lilydale is a semi‑rural, outer‑eastern town centre with family amenities, parks and a sub‑regional shopping centre serving local needs. Major grocery options are at Lilydale Marketplace (anchored by Woolworths, ALDI and Big W) and a mix of specialty shops in the centre; there are no large dedicated multicultural supermarket chains inside Lilydale’s core shopping centre [citation:Lilydale Marketplace stores / centre info](https://www.lilydalemarketplace.com.au/stores).

6) Weeknight dining (2–3 casual, family‑friendly options)
Common family takeaway/dine‑in choices include La Porchetta (Italian / pizza), several Thai places (e.g., Aurora Thai / enTHAIced) and local Indian spots such as Savera / Royal Time — a typical mix of pizza, Thai and Indian takeaway/dining options in Lilydale’s main strip and shopping centre [citation:La Porchetta Lilydale; Lilydale takeaway listings (TripAdvisor / AGFG)](https://laporchetta.com.au/lilydale/)(https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g552201-zfp10601-Lilydale_Yarra_Valley_Victoria.html).

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 centres)
Local tutoring options include Lilydale Tuition Centre (maths & English tuition for primary/secondary students) and access to larger providers or online programs (e.g., Cluey Learning / Kumon networks servicing the area) — local centres advertise one‑to‑one and small‑group academic support [citation:Lilydale Tuition Centre; Cluey Learning Lilydale page](https://lilydaletuition.com/tutoring-lilydale/)(https://clueylearning.com.au/lts/tutoring-lilydale/).

8) Commute — train time to Melbourne CBD
There is a direct train service from Lilydale station to Flinders Street (Lilydale line). Typical scheduled journey time is about 55–60 minutes to Flinders Street (approx. 58 minutes is commonly cited), depending on the service/timetable [citation:Rome2rio Lilydale→Flinders Street; Metro/Transport Victoria Lilydale line timetable info](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Lilydale-Station/Flinders-Street-Station)(https://transport.vic.gov.au/route/timetable/9/lilydale-line).
