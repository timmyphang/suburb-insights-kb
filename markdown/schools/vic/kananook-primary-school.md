1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Seaford is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $1,500, and the median age is 40 years [citation:2021 Seaford (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/214011375). The suburb has a mix of families, with 4,580 families and an average of 0.6 children per household [citation:2021 Seaford (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/214011375). Detailed white-collar vs. blue-collar breakdown was not found in available sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Seaford is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 83.0% of residents born in Australia [citation:2021 Seaford (SA), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL41315). Among overseas-born residents, the most common country of birth is England, at 15.1% of the population [citation:2021 Seaford (SA), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL41315).

3) **Working-parent support:** Kananook Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by TheirCare [citation:Before & After School Care](https://www.kps.vic.edu.au/page/203/Before-&-After-School-Care-). The program includes before school, after school, and holiday care, though specific session hours are not detailed in available sources [citation:Before & After School Care](https://www.kps.vic.edu.au/page/203/Before-&-After-School-Care-). Families may be eligible for up to 90% off through the Child Care Subsidy [citation:Kananook Primary School Seaford](https://www.kps.vic.edu.au/).

4) **School culture:** Kananook Primary School offers a range of named extracurricular programs. These include a **Beach School for Preps** as a unique outdoor learning start, a **Breakfast Club** run by the Student Representative Council, and **The Music Bus** for private on-site music tuition [citation:School Programs](https://www.kps.vic.edu.au/page/184/School-Programs). The school also runs lunchtime clubs and activities such as four square areas, tennis during summer terms, and a **Let’s Play Program** led by senior students [citation:Lunchtime Clubs](https://www.kps.vic.edu.au/page/345/Lunchtime-Clubs).

Additional supermarkets include **Woolworths Seaford** [citation:Seaford, VIC | Woolworths](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/storelocator/vic-seaford-3176).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Several casual family-friendly dining options are available in Seaford. **Seaford Thai Takeaway** offers Thai and Asian cuisine at 3a/366 Frankston-Dandenong Rd, with takeaway and delivery [citation:Home » Seaford Thai Takeaway](https://seafordthai.com/). **Thai Beach Cafe** provides Thai food with dine-in, pickup, and delivery options [citation:Thai Beach Cafe - Food delivery - Seaford - Order online](https://www.thaibeachseaford.com.au/). **Udon Thai Takeaway** is another Thai option with positive reviews [citation:UDON THAI TAKEAWAY, Seaford - Menu, Prices & Restaurant Reviews - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurant_Review-g2642471-d14106425-Reviews-Udon_Thai_Takeaway-Seaford_Victoria.html).

Trains depart from **Seaford Station** to **Flinders Street Station** every 15 minutes [citation:Seaford to Flinders Street Station - 3 ways to travel via train ...](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Seaford-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
