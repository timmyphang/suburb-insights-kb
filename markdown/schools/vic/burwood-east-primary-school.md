Here’s a concise, family-facing eight-point briefing for Burwood East Primary School / Burwood East suburb. I followed a snippet-first, one-search-per-data-point approach and cite each source inline.

1) Suburb socio-economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Burwood East is a middle‑to‑upper middle‑income, established family suburb with a median age of 41 and median weekly household income about $1,610. [citation:2021 Burwood East QuickStats (SA2)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/207031166)  
- Household structure shows a high share of married adults and many family households rather than a singles‑oriented demographic. [citation:2021 Burwood East QuickStats (SA2)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/207031166)

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- China is the most common overseas country of birth noted for the area (China was the top overseas birthplace in earlier QuickStats and remains the largest non‑English speaking birthplace in the local council area). [citation:2016 Burwood East QuickStats (top responses)](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20425) [citation:Burwood council birthplace profile (profile.id)](https://profile.id.com.au/burwood/birthplace)

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- Burwood East Primary has before‑school, after‑school and vacation care run on site by TeamKids (OSHC + school holiday program). [citation:BEPS OSHC / TeamKids page](https://www.burwoodeastps.vic.edu.au/page/35/Before-and-After-School-Care/School-Holiday-Program---Team-Kids) [citation:TeamKids venue listing](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/burwood-east-primary-oshc-6/)

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars (specific)
- Choir; School Band / instrumental tuition (including ukulele and individual music tuition). [citation:BEPS Performing Arts / Clubs](https://www.burwoodeastps.vic.edu.au/page/19/Performing-Arts)  
- Robotics Club and Junior Mathematics Club / Maths Olympiad (plus Sports Academy and running clubs). [citation:BEPS Extra Curricular Activities](https://www.burwoodeastps.vic.edu.au/page/25/Extra-Curricular-Activities)

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Burwood East is a family‑oriented suburb with two nearby retail hubs: Burwood Brickworks (large sustainable regional centre with Woolworths, cinemas and a community dining precinct) and Burwood One (local centre with Coles and specialty shops). [citation:Burwood Brickworks centre info](https://www.frasersproperty.com.au/portfolio/retail/vic/burwood-brickworks-shopping-centre) [citation:Burwood One centre site](https://www.burwoodone.com.au/)  
- For specialty/ethnic groceries there is a Burwood Highway Asian Grocery (Burwood Heights Shopping Centre) serving Chinese, Korean, Japanese and Southeast Asian products. [citation:Burwood Highway Asian Grocery listing](https://www.burwoodheightsshoppingcentre.com.au/stores/burwoodhighwayasiangrocery/)

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 family takeaway / casual options (call out cuisines)
- Lucky Dumpling Kitchen — casual Chinese/dumplings (Burwood Brickworks precinct). [citation:Lucky Dumpling Kitchen (Brickworks listing)](https://home-co.com.au/brickworks/lucky-dumpling-kitchen)  
- Little Monkey Hot Pot — hot‑pot / modern Chinese, family friendly (Brickworks). [citation:Little Monkey Hot Pot (Brickworks listing)](https://home-co.com.au/brickworks/little-monkey-hot-pot)  
- Toro Sushi — Japanese sushi/takeaway (local Brickworks/nearby outlets). [citation:Toro Sushi site/listing](https://thetorosushi.com/)

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 local centres)
- Kumon Burwood East (English & maths after‑school tuition centre). [citation:Kumon Burwood East listing](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-BURWOOD-EAST-VIC)  
- Brainworks / Math Minds — local one‑to‑one and specialist maths tutoring options serving Burwood East. [citation:Brainworks Burwood East listing](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-burwood-east) [citation:Math Minds Burwood East page](https://mathminds.com.au/maths-tutor-burwood-east/)

8) Commute — train / tram / bus to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)
- Burwood East does not have a suburban heavy‑rail station; the practical public option is the Route 75 tram (and buses) into the city. Typical public‑transit travel from Burwood East to Flinders Street via tram/bus is about 45–60 minutes depending on transfers and exact start point (local travel guides and trip planners show ~50–60 minutes as typical). [citation:Rome2rio Burwood East → Flinders St travel options & times](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Burwood-East/Flinders-Street-Station) [citation:Local Burwood East transport guide (Melbz)](https://www.melbz.com.au/burwood-east/transport-guide/)
