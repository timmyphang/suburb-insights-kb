1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Oakleigh East is a middle-income suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,951 and a median age of 35, indicating a relatively young population [citation:ABS 2021 QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22001). The area has 1,794 families and an average of 1.6 children per family, suggesting a mix of young families and singles. The suburb is predominantly white-collar, with many residents working in professional and managerial roles, though specific occupation data is not available in the provided sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The most common overseas country of birth for Oakleigh East residents is not explicitly listed in the 2021 Census QuickStats provided. However, over 64% of residents had both parents born overseas, indicating a highly multicultural population [citation:ABS 2016 QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21989). The dominant overseas-born group is likely from India or China, common in the City of Monash, but exact percentages are missing from the available context.

3) **Working-parent support**: Christ Our Holy Redeemer School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) through a dedicated service named "Christ Our Holy Redeemer OSHC" [citation:CareforKids](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/n31e4/christ-our-holy-redeemer-oshc-oakleigh-east-3166). The service provides after-school care for children aged 5–12 years, Monday to Friday, but specific operating hours are not listed in the available sources. Contact the school directly for exact times.

4) **School culture**: Christ Our Holy Redeemer School offers a range of named extracurricular activities, including Cube Club, Movie Creator Club, and Art Club, as detailed in the 2024 Annual Report [citation:2024 Annual Report](https://www.cohroakeast.catholic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Annual-Report-to-the-Community-2024.pdf). The school also partners with ABC Music to provide small-group and individual instrument lessons [citation:2023 School Update](https://www.cohroakeast.catholic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/2023.pdf). These programs reflect a focus on creativity and student wellbeing.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Oakleigh East has a family-friendly vibe with several grocery options. The IGA Friendly Grocers at 188-190 Huntingdale Road serves everyday needs [citation:Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/oakleigh-vic-3166). For specialty groceries, Hanaromart at Oakleigh Central offers Korean-Asian products, and Bengal Traders at 321 Huntingdale Road provides Halal and Indian groceries [citation:Oakleigh Central](https://www.oakleighcentral.com.au/stores/hanaromart) [citation:Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/oakleigh-vic-3166). Additional Asian grocery stores are also available in the area.

6) **Weeknight dining**: For casual family-friendly dining, Teerak Thai on Huntingdale Road serves homemade Thai street food with eat-in and takeaway options [citation:Teerak Thai Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/teerakthaioakleigh/). Nice Gourmet Pizza in nearby Oakleigh offers consistently good pizza and is popular for takeaway [citation:TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g580518-Oakleigh_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). Many other cuisines are available via delivery services like Uber Eats.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Success Tutoring in Oakleigh East provides personalised tutoring for primary and secondary students, focusing on literacy and numeracy [citation:Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-oakleigh-east). Tuition Zone also offers structured lessons from Prep to Year 12, with programs aligned to the Victorian Curriculum and a dedicated VCE program [citation:Tuition Zone](https://tuitionzone.com.au/locations/tutoring-oakleigh-east). Both centres are located in or near Oakleigh East.

8) **Commute**: Oakleigh East is served by Oakleigh Station on the Cranbourne/Pakenham line. The train journey from Oakleigh Station to Flinders Street Station takes approximately 22–27 minutes, with trains running every 10 minutes during peak times [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Oakleigh/Flinders-Street-Station) [citation:Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakleigh_railway_station). The station is about 15 kilometres from the CBD.
