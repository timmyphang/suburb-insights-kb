The median age is 36, younger than the state median of 38 [citation:2021 Oak Park, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21998). The suburb has a family-oriented mix, with 1,851 families and an average of 1.7 children per family with children, and 46.63% of residents are married [citation:2021 Oak Park, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21998) [citation:Oak Park, VIC 3046 Property Market and House Prices 2026](https://www.htag.com.au/vic/vic131-moreland-city/oak-park-vic-3046/).
