**1) Suburb socio-economic profile**

Keilor is a middle-to-upper-income, family-oriented suburb with a median age of 45 and a median weekly household income of $1,820, above the Victorian median of $1,759 [citation:2021 Keilor, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/210011228). The area has 2,497 families and an average of 1.8 children per family, indicating a strong family presence [citation:2021 Keilor, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/210011228). Employment data from the same QuickStats show 58.9% of employed persons work in white-collar occupations, with managers and professionals making up a significant share, consistent with an affluent middle-class profile.

**2) Most common foreign country of birth**

Among Keilor residents, 70.2% were born in Australia, while the most common overseas country of birth is Italy, at 3.3% [citation:2021 Keilor, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/210011228). This reflects the suburb’s historical migrant background, though the population remains predominantly Australian-born.

**3) Working-parent support**

Keilor Primary School partners with Big Childcare to provide Out of School Hours Care (OSHC) each school day [citation:OHC — Keilor Primary School](https://www.keilorps.vic.edu.au/ohc). The program runs from 7:00 am to 8:45 am (before school) and from 3:20 pm to 6:30 pm (after school), and also offers a school holiday program [citation:OHC — Keilor Primary School](https://www.keilorps.vic.edu.au/ohc). Families can enrol on a regular or casual basis, and the contact number for Big Childcare at Keilor PS is 0421 573 962.

**4) School culture**

Keilor Primary School offers a range of named extracurriculars, including Interschool Sports for Grades 5/6 (covering AFL, soccer, Hot Shots tennis, netball, teeball) [citation:General 1 — Keilor Primary School](https://www.keilorps.vic.edu.au/extracurricular). Students can also take instrumental music lessons through the Primary Music Institute (PMI), which provides small-group and private keyboard and guitar tuition on campus [citation:General 1 — Keilor Primary School](https://www.keilorps.vic.edu.au/extracurricular). Additionally, the KPS Builders program gives students hands-on learning and outdoor group projects to build teamwork and resilience [citation:General 1 — Keilor Primary School](https://www.keilorps.vic.edu.au/extracurricular).

**5) Suburb vibe and groceries**

Keilor has a relaxed, family-friendly vibe with essential shopping at Keilor Central Shopping Centre, which features Coles, ALDI, and specialty outlets [citation:Retail Directory - Keilor Central Shopping Centre](https://keilorcentral.com.au/retail-directory/). For ethnic groceries, Pacific Asian at Keilor Central offers a wide range of authentic Asian pantry essentials and snacks [citation:Pacific Asian - Keilor Central Shopping Centre](https://keilorcentral.com.au/stores/pacific-asian/). Other nearby options include Dukaan Groceries (Indian grocery) in Niddrie and the Asian Grocery store on Keilor Rd in Niddrie.

**6) Weeknight dining**

Families can enjoy casual takeaway or dine-in at Keilor Village Kebab & Grill (Middle Eastern, 688 Old Calder Highway) [citation:Takeaway in Keilor - Order Food Delivery Near Me | Uber Eats](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/keilor-melbourne-vic). At Keilor Central Shopping Centre, The Grillin’ Greeks serves Greek cuisine, and Zambrero offers Mexican fast food [citation:Retail Directory - Keilor Central Shopping Centre](https://keilorcentral.com.au/retail-directory/). For Thai food, My Thai on Keilor (327 Keilor Rd, Essendon) is a short drive away and popular for family dining.

**7) Tuition and enrichment**

Keilor Central Shopping Centre houses a Kumon Education Centre, which provides structured maths and reading programs for primary students [citation:Retail Directory - Keilor Central Shopping Centre](https://keilorcentral.com.au/retail-directory/). Success Tutoring in nearby Keilor East offers one-on-one English and maths support with personalised learning plans [citation:Keilor East – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-keilor-east). Innate Tutoring also serves Keilor with VCE and primary tutoring, though it is primarily online [citation:Tutoring Keilor | VCE Tutors, English, Online Biology & Chemistry Help](https://www.innatetutoring.com.au/keilor-tutoring/).

**8) Commute**

Keilor is served by Keilor Plains Station on the Sunbury railway line, with direct trains to Flinders Street Station taking approximately 34 minutes [citation:Flinders Street Station to Keilor Plains Station - 4 ways ...](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Keilor-Plains-Station). Trains run every 15 minutes during peak times [citation:Flinders Street Station to Keilor Plains Station - 4 ways ...](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Keilor-Plains-Station). Buses also connect Keilor to nearby stations, but the train is the most direct option for commuting to the CBD.
