3) **Working-parent support:** Pembroke Primary School runs its own Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) program, operated by the school council [citation:Pembroke Primary School OSHC | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/pembroke-primary-school-oshc). Before-school care runs from 7:00 AM to 8:45 AM, and after-school care runs from 3:30 PM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday [citation:Pembroke Primary School OSHC | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/pembroke-primary-school-oshc). Note that vacation care is not listed for this service.

4) **School culture:** Pembroke Primary School offers a variety of named extracurricular activities including interschool sport, a choir, a Student Representative Council, and a school production [citation:Principal's Message | Pembroke Primary](https://www.pemprim.vic.edu.au/principal-s-message). The school also runs a weekly gardening program and lunchtime clubs [citation:Principal's Message | Pembroke Primary](https://www.pemprim.vic.edu.au/principal-s-message). Their Sporting Schools program features term-based activities such as swimming, table tennis, and volleyball.
