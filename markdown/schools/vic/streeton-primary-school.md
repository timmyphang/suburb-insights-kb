1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Yallambie is an affluent, family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,320, well above the national median, and the median age is 35, indicating a younger population [citation:2021 Yallambie, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22892). With a high proportion of families (average household size of 2.8) and a majority of couples with children, the suburb skews white-collar, professional, and family-centric [citation:YALLAMBIE - Melbourne Suburb Data Analysis](https://www.auguide.com.au/en/suburbs/vic/yallambie/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The suburb is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 89.8% of residents born in Australia [citation:Yallambie VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/yallambie-vic). Among overseas-born residents, the most common country of birth is England at 3.2%, followed by India at 2.5% and China at 2.0% [citation:Yallambie VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/yallambie-vic).

3) **Working-parent support:** Streeton Primary School’s OSHC is provided by TheirCare Pty Ltd (trading as TheirCare) at the school address of 209 Yallambie Rd [citation:Camp Australia - Streeton Primary School OSHC](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/camp-australia-streeton-primary-school-oshc). During school term, before-school care runs from 6:45 AM to 8:45 AM and after-school care from 3:30 PM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday; vacation care operates 6:45 AM to 6:00 PM on the same days [citation:Camp Australia - Streeton Primary School OSHC](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/camp-australia-streeton-primary-school-oshc).

4) **School culture:** Streeton Primary School offers a Year 5 & 6 Watsonia District Inter-school Sport competition each Friday, with summer sports including Cricket, Bat Tennis, Basketball, and Rounders, and winter sports including AFL, Soccer, Netball, and Softball [citation:Sport — Streeton Primary School](https://www.streetonps.vic.edu.au/sports). The school also holds a whole-school concert every two years involving all students Prep through Grade 6 [citation:School Production | Streeton Primary School](https://www.streetonps.vic.edu.au/school-production). Additionally, the school has an outdoor learning area with a Farm and Pond used for hands-on science and environmental programs [citation:Outdoor Learning — Streeton Primary School](https://www.streetonps.vic.edu.au/outdoorlearning).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Yallambie has a quiet suburban, family-friendly feel with local amenities including a small shopping precinct. The key local grocer is Yallambie Foodmart Liquor Asian Groceries Supermarket at 305 Yallambie Rd, which stocks a wide range of Asian groceries and liquor [citation:Yallambie Foodmart Liquor Asian Groceries Supermarket](https://www.mapquest.com/au/victoria/yallambie-foodmart-liquor-asian-groceries-supermarket-794379492). For more extensive fresh-food shopping, the Lower Plenty shopping strip is nearby, featuring a large-scale supermarket complex [citation:Yallambie Food Mart/Asian Groceries/Dollar Shop Supermarket – Yallambie](https://yallambie.wordpress.com/tag/yallambie-food-mart-asian-groceries-dollar-shop-supermarket/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual takeaway or dine-in, Yallambie Food Mart & Pizza at 305 Yallambie Rd offers pizza and other takeaway items [citation:YALLAMBIE FOOD MART & PIZZA - Updated May 2026](https://www.yelp.com/biz/yallambie-food-mart-and-pizza-yallambie). In the nearby suburb of Allambie Heights, The Allambie Pizza Shop (141 Allambie Road) serves hand-crafted pizzas with dine-in, takeaway, and home-delivery options [citation:The Allambie Pizza Shop](https://www.theallambiepizzashop.com.au/). Piyada Thai Restaurant at 72 Aberdeen Road in Macleod offers authentic Thai cuisine and is a short drive from Yallambie [citation:30 Best Local Restaurants in Yallambie, VIC 3085](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/yallambie-vic-3085/restaurants).
