1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Dandenong is a working-class, culturally diverse suburb with a SEIFA score of about 870, placing it in the bottom 10% of Victorian suburbs for socio-economic disadvantage [citation:Dandenong Suburb Profile 2026 | Demographics & Lifestyle](https://www.collings.com.au/dandenong-suburb-profile-2026-demographics-schools-transport-and-lifestyle/). Median weekly household income is around $1,050 (significantly below the Greater Melbourne median of $1,750), and 43% of dwellings are renter-occupied [citation:Dandenong Suburb Profile 2026 | Demographics & Lifestyle](https://www.collings.com.au/dandenong-suburb-profile-2026-demographics-schools-transport-and-lifestyle/). Household composition leans toward larger family groups, with an average household size of 3.0 persons [citation:Dandenong Suburb Profile 2026 | Demographics & Lifestyle](https://www.collings.com.au/dandenong-suburb-profile-2026-demographics-schools-transport-and-lifestyle/).
