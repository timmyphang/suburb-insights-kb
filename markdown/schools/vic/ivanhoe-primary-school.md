Here’s a concise, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Ivanhoe Primary School (Ivanhoe, VIC). Each item is snippet‑first with a primary citation.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Ivanhoe is an upper‑middle / relatively affluent, mostly white‑collar suburb — median weekly household income $2,232 (2021), with median age 40 (above state median age) [citation:2021 Ivanhoe (Vic.) QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21246).

2) Most common overseas country of birth  
About 69.9% of Ivanhoe residents were born in Australia (2021); the largest overseas birthplace is China (~4.3%) [citation:2021 Ivanhoe (Vic.) QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21246).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
Ivanhoe Primary runs its own OSHC: Before‑School Care 7:00–8:45 and After‑School Care 3:30–6:00; service approved for ~150 places (places in high demand) [citation:Out Of School Hours Care — Ivanhoe Primary School](https://www.ivanhoeps.vic.edu.au/out-of-school-hours-care). The service is listed on the government StartingBlocks register (rated “Exceeding NQS”) [citation:StartingBlocks Ivanhoe OSHC listing](https://www.startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01HGCPRV63SV3HSW4CQDEK4EWE/Ivanhoe-Primary-Out-of-School-Hours-Care-Program).

4) School culture — key extracurriculars  
Notable offerings: Strings & Choir (music program), Chess Club (before school), and a formal camping program (Year 4 camp; Year 5 Sovereign Hill; Year 6 Canberra tour) [citation:Extra‑Curricular — Ivanhoe Primary School](https://www.ivanhoeps.vic.edu.au/extra-curricular).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (2 sentences)  
Ivanhoe is a leafy, family‑oriented suburb with local shopping strips, cafes and parks that support an active family lifestyle. Local specialty grocers include small Italian providores/deliveries (The Italian Corner, Ivanhoe Pasta Deli) and an Asian grocery (Journey to the East Asian Mart); there is also a FoodWorks supermarket nearby for everyday shopping [citation:The Italian Corner — Ivanhoe](https://theitaliancornerivanhoe.com.au/), [citation:Ivanhoe Pasta Deli (Ivanhoe Plaza listing)](https://ivanhoeplaza.com.au/store/ivanhoe-pasta-deli/), [citation:Journey to the East Asian Mart (Shop in Ivanhoe)](https://shopinivanhoe.com.au/traders/journey-east-asian-mart/), [citation:FoodWorks Ivanhoe East](https://ivanhoeeast.foodworks.com.au/).

6) Weeknight dining — family‑friendly takeaway / casual spots  
Examples: Nando’s (flame‑grilled chicken) and Crust Pizza (local pizza takeaway), plus multiple local pizza/Italian and Thai options listed on local restaurant directories (e.g., Little Naples Pizzeria, Thai Lantern) [citation:Nando's Ivanhoe (listing)](https://www.whereis.com/ivanhoe-vic/wip/nandos-australia-pty-ltd-580904081), [citation:Crust Pizza Ivanhoe](https://www.crust.com.au/stores/vic/ivanhoe/), [citation:AGFG Ivanhoe restaurants directory](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/ivanhoe).

7) Tuition and enrichment near Ivanhoe  
Local tuition options include Kumon Ivanhoe (maths/English learning centre) and private tutors/centres such as Brainworks (one‑to‑one tutoring) — both advertise services in the Ivanhoe area [citation:Kumon Ivanhoe centre listing](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-IVANHOE-VIC), [citation:Brainworks Ivanhoe tutoring](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-ivanhoe).

8) Commute — train travel time to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)  
Ivanhoe has a train station on the Hurstbridge line with direct services to Flinders Street; typical travel time is about 20–25 minutes (fastest ~19 minutes, average around 22 minutes) [citation:Ivanhoe station — Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/ivanhoe/), [citation:Rome2rio Ivanhoe → Flinders Street (example travel times)](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Ivanhoe/Flinders-Street-Station).
