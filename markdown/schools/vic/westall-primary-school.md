### 1) Suburb socio-economic profile
Clayton South is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,630 and a median age of 34 [citation:ABS 2021 QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20570). Of the 13,381 residents, 3,500 are families, and the average household size is 2.7 people, indicating a mix of families and younger singles [citation:ABS 2021 QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20570). Occupations are not specified in the available data, but the income level suggests a blend of white-collar and blue-collar workers.

### 5) Suburb vibe and groceries
Clayton South has a diverse, multicultural character with several specialty grocers. For Indian groceries, visit **Sarawan Spices** at 335 Clayton Rd, Clayton, or **MK Elite Groceries and Convenience** at Shop 1/3 Orchid St, Clayton South [citation:Hello Clayton](https://helloclayton.com.au/category/shop/groceries/) [citation:Singh](https://singh.com.au/location/clayton_vic/category/indian-grocery-shops/). For Korean and Asian groceries, **Top Mart Korean Grocery** and **Jireh Mart Asian Grocery** are located on Clayton Road [citation:Hello Clayton](https://helloclayton.com.au/category/shop/groceries/). Mainstream supermarkets include **Coles Clayton** on Clayton Road [citation:Hello Clayton](https://helloclayton.com.au/category/shop/groceries/).

### 6) Weeknight dining
For casual family-friendly dining with takeaway options in Clayton South: **Lala’s Kitchen** (536 Clayton Road) serves authentic Indian cuisine with dine-in and takeaway [citation:Local Business Guide](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/victoria/clayton-south/food-and-dining/restaurants/). **Everest Eats** (1358 Centre Rd, Clayton South) offers Nepalese takeaway and delivery [citation:OzFoodHunter](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/clayton-south-3169). In nearby Clayton, **Dosa Hut** (309 Clayton Road) provides Indian takeaway and dine-in [citation:Dosa Hut](https://www.dosahut.net.au/indian-restaurant-clayton/).

### 7) Tuition and enrichment
**NQT Education** runs tutoring classes on Sunday mornings at Westall Primary School itself, covering Primary & Secondary Maths and English, as well as selective school preparation [citation:NQT Clayton South](https://www.nqt.com.au/centres/clayton-south). **Success Tutoring Clayton South** offers personalised learning support for primary and secondary students, with 24/7 access to resources [citation:Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-clayton-south). For high school maths, **Strive Academy** in Clayton provides small-group VCE Maths Methods tutoring [citation:Strive Academy](https://www.striveacademytutoring.com.au/clayton-tutors).

### 8) Commute
Clayton South is served by **Westall Station** on the **Pakenham/Cranbourne line**. Travel time to Flinders Street Station is approximately 25–30 minutes by train (typical weekday schedule). For specific timetables, consult Metro Trains Melbourne [citation:Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/timetables/).
