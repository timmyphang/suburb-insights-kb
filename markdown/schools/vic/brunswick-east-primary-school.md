1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Brunswick East is an inner-city, middle- to upper-middle-income suburb with a high proportion of white-collar professionals and a notably young population. The median weekly household income is $2,088, and the median age is 33, reflecting a large concentration of residents aged 25–39 [citation:2021 Brunswick East, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20362). The area has a mix of singles and families, though families with children are a smaller share than the Victorian average.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas country of birth in Brunswick East is England, with 4.3% of residents (573 people). However, the suburb is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 69.5% of residents (9,233 people) born in Australia [citation:2021 Brunswick East, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20362). This indicates a relatively low proportion of overseas-born residents compared to many other inner-city suburbs.

3) **Working-parent support:** Brunswick East Primary School’s Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided by TeamKids and operates on-site at 195a Stewart Street. Before-school care runs from 7:00 am to 8:55 am, and after-school care runs from 3:30 pm to 6:00 pm on school days, with vacation care also available from 7:00 am to 6:00 pm [citation:TeamKids - Brunswick East Primary](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/brunswick-east-primary-school-oshc-service).
