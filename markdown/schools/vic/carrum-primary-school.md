1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Carrum is a middle-income suburb with a mix of families and singles. According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income was $1,732, compared to $1,759 for Greater Melbourne [citation:2021 Carrum, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20507). The median age was 40 years, and households had an average of 0.6 children per household, indicating a mix of family and non-family households [citation:2021 Carrum, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20507). Specific white-collar/blue-collar breakdown was not found in the available sources, but the suburb is primarily residential with a coastal, family-friendly feel.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Carrum is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 76.3% of residents born in Australia according to the 2021 Census [citation:2021 Carrum, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20507). The most common overseas country of birth was England at 4.0%, followed by New Zealand at 1.7% and India at 0.7% [citation:2021 Carrum, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20507).
