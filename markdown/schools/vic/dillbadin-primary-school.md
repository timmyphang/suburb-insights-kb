1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Boronia is a middle-income suburb with a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers. The median weekly household income is $1,619, and the median age is 39 [citation:2021 Boronia, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20304). The suburb has 6,522 families, with an average of 0.7 children per household, indicating a mix of families and singles [citation:2021 Boronia, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20304).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Boronia is predominantly Australian-born, with 66.9% of residents born in Australia [citation:2021 Boronia, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/211011446).
