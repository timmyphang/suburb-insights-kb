1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Frankston City has a median weekly household income of $1,653 and a median age of 39, according to the 2021 Census [citation:2021 Frankston, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/LGA22170). The area has a mix of socio-economic levels; Frankston Central and Frankston North have relatively low SEIFA scores (indicating greater disadvantage), while suburbs like Sandhurst have the least disadvantage in the LGA [citation:SEIFA by profile area | Frankston City](https://profile.id.com.au/frankston/seifa-disadvantage-small-area). Overall, the workforce is a mix of white-collar and blue-collar occupations, with a strong family presence—37,590 families were counted in 2021 [citation:2021 Frankston, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/LGA22170).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: Frankston is predominantly Australian-born. In the 2021 Census, 76.2% of residents were born in Australia, which is higher than the national average of 66.9% [citation:2021 Frankston, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/LGA22170). The most common overseas country of birth is England, at 4.8% of the population, followed by New Zealand (1.8%) and India (1.2%) [citation:2021 Frankston, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/LGA22170).

3) **Working-parent support**: Karingal Primary School partners with Camp Australia to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) [citation:Out of School Hours Care](https://www.karingalps.vic.edu.au/page/16/Out-of-School-Hours-Care). The program runs Monday to Friday from 6:30am to 9:00am for before-school care and 3:15pm to 6:30pm for after-school care [citation:Before & After School Care](https://www.frankstoneastps.vic.edu.au/page/20/Before-&-After-School-Care). Camp Australia also offers a holiday program called Rocketeers during school breaks at the school [citation:Out of School Hours Care](https://www.karingalps.vic.edu.au/page/16/Out-of-School-Hours-Care).

4) **School culture**: Karingal Primary School offers a Glee Club called “The Parrwangs” for students in grades 3–6, which performs at school events and the Mornington Peninsula Choral Festival [citation:Performing Arts](https://www.karingalps.vic.edu.au/page/13/Performing-Arts). The school runs a Breakfast Club supported by volunteers to help students start the day ready to learn [citation:Wellbeing at Karingal Primary School](https://www.karingalps.vic.edu.au/page/124/Wellbeing-at-Karingal-Primary-School). Individual private lessons in guitar and keyboards are available through the Primary Music Institute [citation:Performing Arts](https://www.karingalps.vic.edu.au/page/13/Performing-Arts).
