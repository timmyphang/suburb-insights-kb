1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Boronia is a middle-income suburb with a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers. The median weekly household income is $1,619, and the median age is 39 [citation:2021 Boronia, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics]. Families make up a significant portion, with 6,522 families and an average of 1.7 children per family for those with children [citation:2021 Boronia, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics].

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: Boronia is predominantly Australian-born, with 69.8% of residents born in Australia [citation:2021 Boronia, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics]. The most common overseas country of birth is England, at 4.0% [citation:2021 Boronia, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics].

3) **Working-parent support**: Knox Central Primary School runs its own Out of School Hours Care (OSHC) program, led by coordinator Jigna [citation:Programs](https://www.knoxcentral.vic.edu.au/page/205/Programs). The service operates before school from 7:00 AM to 8:45 AM and after school from 3:30 PM to 6:00 PM on school term days [citation:Knox Central Primary School OSHC | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/knox-central-primary-school-oshc).

4) **School culture**: Knox Central Primary School offers several named extracurriculars. The **Breakfast Club** runs every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday from 8:30 AM to 8:50 AM in the staffroom [citation:Programs](https://www.knoxcentral.vic.edu.au/page/205/Programs). The school also runs the **Phoenix Soar** program, a four-week mental health and wellbeing initiative for Years 5 and 6 delivered with the South East Melbourne Phoenix [citation:Programs](https://www.knoxcentral.vic.edu.au/page/205/Programs). Additionally, **Interschool Sports**, **Athletics Carnival**, and **Cross-Country** are regular activities [citation:Programs](https://www.knoxcentral.vic.edu.au/page/205/Programs).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Boronia has a suburban, family-friendly feel with several specialty grocery stores. For Asian groceries, **Masagana Fil-Asian Grocery** is located at 12/159 Boronia Road [citation:Masagana Fil-Asian Grocery | Boronia VIC | Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/p/Masagana-Fil-Asian-Grocery-100057629102414/), and **Divya Indian Groceries** is at 104 Coleman Road [citation:Indian Grocery Shops in Boronia, VIC](https://singh.com.au/indian-grocery-shops-in-boronia-vic/). **Haodi Asian Supermarket** operates on Dorset Road [citation:Haodi Asian Supermarket | Dorset Road, Boronia, VIC | White Pages®](https://www.whitepages.com.au/haodi-asian-supermarket-11798302/boronia-vic-11798298B).
