Below is a concise, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Berwick Chase Primary School (Berwick, VIC). Each item is a short fact/snippet with a primary citation.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Berwick is a broadly family‑oriented, middle‑to‑upper‑middle income suburb — median weekly household income ≈ $2,113 (2021), larger average household size (≈2.9) and a high share of married households (54%). This points to many established families rather than a singles‑heavy area [citation:2021 Berwick QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20224).

2) Most common overseas birthplace (short)  
Berwick is predominantly Australian‑born (majority in SA2s: ~56–68%); where there is a visible overseas‑born population, Southern‑Asian origins (e.g., people from South Asia) are the largest overseas group in southern parts of Berwick, while the UK is more prominent in Berwick‑North. (City of Casey 2021 SA2 breakdowns show Australia top, then Southern Asia or United Kingdom depending on the SA2) [citation:City of Casey cultural‑diversity CSV (derived from 2021 Census)](https://data.casey.vic.gov.au/api/datasets/1.0/cultural-diversity-country-of-birth-persons-od/attachments/cen21_coc_cultural_diversity_country_of_birth_persons_sa2_csv/).
