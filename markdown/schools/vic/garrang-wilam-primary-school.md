Below is a short, family-facing eight‑point briefing for Garrang Wilam Primary School (Truganina). I used official ABS suburb profiles and the school’s pages for school-specific items; inline citations point to the source for each fact.

1) Suburb socio-economic profile (2–3 sentences)  
- Truganina is a fast-growing, young, family‑dominated suburb (median age 30; high shares of 0–4 and 5–9 year olds) with many married couples — signalling lots of young families rather than singles. Median household measures and the age/household structure indicate a mixed to middle‑income growth suburb with strong family orientation [citation:2021 Truganina QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22582).

2) Most common foreign country of birth  
- India is the most common overseas birthplace for Truganina residents (reported as the top overseas country of birth in 2021; several local profiles cite a large India-born share) [citation:2021 Truganina QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22582) [citation:Truganina VIC Suburb Profile — Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/truganina).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
- Garrang Wilam Primary runs an on‑site OSHC program coordinated by TeamKids: Before School Care 6:45–8:40am, After School Care 3:00–6:15pm, plus Holiday and Curriculum‑day care options [citation:Out of School Hours Care – Garrang Wilam Primary School](https://garrangwilamps.vic.edu.au/parents/out-of-school-hours-care/) [citation:TeamKids - Garrang Wilam Primary (venue)](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/garrang-wilam-primary-truganina-oshc/).

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars (specific)  
- Regular interschool sport (Gala Days / district pathways — cricket, AFL, netball, soccer, etc.).  
- A growing, curriculum‑aligned music program (two‑year cycle).  
- Performing‑arts / PE facilities used for school performances and activities (school lists STEM/food‑tech and performing arts among specialist areas) [citation:PE – Garrang Wilam Primary School](https://garrangwilamps.vic.edu.au/learning/pe/) [citation:Music – Garrang Wilam Primary School](https://garrangwilamps.vic.edu.au/learning/music/) [citation:Our Learning Spaces – Garrang Wilam Primary School](https://garrangwilamps.vic.edu.au/about-us/our-learning-spaces/).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)  
- Truganina feels like a newly developed, family‑focused outer‑metro suburb with playgrounds, new estates and local shopping hubs serving young households (ABS shows a high share of young children and married households).  
- Main grocery options include Coles (Truganina) and local grocers such as Abu Rashed’s (Friendly Grocer) at the local shopping precincts [citation:2021 Truganina QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22582) [citation:Coles Truganina](https://www.coles.com.au/find-stores/coles/vic/truganina-7685) [citation:Abu Rashed's Truganina | Friendly Grocer](https://friendlygrocer.com.au/abu-rasheds-truganina).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 casual, family‑friendly options (call out cuisines)

- Kasba Indian (Truganina) — Indian takeaway/dine‑in.  
- Wok Hunter / various Asian takeaways (Chinese/Asian fusion) and local pizza outlets (Pizza Planet) for quick family meals [citation:Truganina - Stella Pizza](https://www.stellapizza.com.au/store/truganina) [citation:Kasba Indian Restaurant | Indian Takeaway in Truganina](https://kasbaindianonline.com.au/) [citation:Order Wok Hunter (Truganina) | Uber Eats listing](https://www.ubereats.com/au/store/wok-hunter-truganina/yp3yWZXTTNqkKiPcwD3B9Q).

7) Tuition and enrichment nearby  
- Kumon Truganina (maths & English tuition centre).  
- Local private tutors/centres serving Truganina such as Success Tutoring and Brainworks (one‑to‑one/tailored tuition options) [citation:Search for a Kumon Centre | Kumon Australia](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/centre/kumon-truganina) [citation:Truganina Tutoring – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/truganina-tutoring) [citation:Brainworks Truganina tutoring](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-truganina-south).

8) Commute — train travel time to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)  
- Truganina does not currently have an operational suburban train station; the nearest ready rail option is Williams Landing (~3–4 km from central Truganina) or Laverton/Hoppers Crossing on the Werribee line. From Williams Landing to Flinders Street by Metro train is typically about 33–36 minutes (direct Werribee‑line services) — families commonly connect to those stations by local bus or car for CBD train trips [citation:Truganina - Wikipedia (nearest station Williams Landing)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truganina) [citation:Werribee Line timetable / Rome2rio estimates](https://transport.vic.gov.au/route/timetable/16/werribee-line) [citation:Williams Landing to Flinders Street - Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Williams-Landing/Flinders-Street-Station).
