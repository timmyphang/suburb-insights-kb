1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Wandin North is a middle-to-upper-income, family-oriented suburb with a high rate of home ownership. The median weekly household income is $1,969 (approx. $102,388/year), and the median age is 39, slightly older than the national average [citation:GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/wandin-north-vic). The suburb has a very high proportion of families living in separate houses, with 58.2% of dwellings owned with a mortgage and 41.8% owned outright [citation:ABS QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22673). Details on the white-collar vs blue-collar split are not available in the provided sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Wandin North is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 95.1% of residents born in Australia [citation:GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/wandin-north-vic). The most common overseas birthplace is England, at 4.3% of the population [citation:GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/wandin-north-vic). No other country of birth exceeds 1% according to the 2021 Census data.

3) **Working-parent support:** Wandin Yallock Primary School runs its own Out of School Hours Care (OSHC) program on site, operated by the school council [citation:Wandin Yallock PS OSHC page](https://wandinyallockps.vic.edu.au/after-school-care). The hours are 7:00am to 8:45am for before-school care and 3:30pm to 6:00pm for after-school care [citation:Wandin Yallock PS OSHC page](https://wandinyallockps.vic.edu.au/after-school-care). The service also offers vacation care during school holidays, operating from 7:00am to 6:00pm [citation:Wandin Yallock PS OSHC page](https://wandinyallockps.vic.edu.au/after-school-care).

4) **School culture:** Wandin Yallock Primary School offers several named extracurricular programs that support student engagement and wellbeing.
