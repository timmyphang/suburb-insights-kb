1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Seaford is a middle-income suburb with a mix of families and older residents. The median weekly household income is $1,500, and the median age is 40, which is slightly older than the national median of 38 [citation:2021 Seaford (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/214011375). The suburb has a diverse occupational mix; specific white-collar vs blue-collar breakdown is not found in the available sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Seaford is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 83.0% of residents born in Australia [citation:2021 Seaford (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/214011375). Among overseas-born residents, the most common country of birth is England, but its exact percentage share is not found in the available sources for the Victorian suburb.

3) **Working-parent support:** Belvedere Park Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by TeamKids [citation:Belvedere Park Primary - Seaford | TeamKids](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/belvedere-park-primary-oshc-6/). Before-school care runs from 6:30 am to 8:45 am, and after-school care runs from 3:15 pm to 6:30 pm [citation:Belvedere Park Primary - Seaford | TeamKids](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/belvedere-park-primary-oshc-6/).
