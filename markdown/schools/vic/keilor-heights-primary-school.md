1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Keilor East is a middle-to-upper-middle-income, family-oriented suburb. According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income is $1,913, and the median age is 43, which is older than the Victorian median of 38 [citation:ABS QuickStats 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/210011534). The suburb has a high proportion of families, with 4,321 families among 15,073 people, and 80.5% of dwellings are standalone houses [citation:AreaSearch](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/keilor_east).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The suburb is predominantly Australian-born. In the 2016 Census, 59.8% of people spoke only English at home, with the most common overseas country of birth being Italy at 6.7%, followed by Vietnam at 2.5% [citation:ABS QuickStats 2016](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21308). For the 2021 Census, detailed country-of-birth percentages were not available in the provided source, but Italian and Greek heritage remain strong in the area.

3) **Working-parent support:** Keilor Heights Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by **Big Childcare**, operating at the school site on Ronald Grove [citation:CareforKids](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/n7dg2/big-childcare-keilor-heights-primary-school-oshc-keilor-east-3033). The service provides Before School Care and After School Care, with specific hours listed as morning sessions and afternoon sessions from 3:25 PM to 6:30 PM (Monday to Friday) [citation:KindiCare](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3033/keilor-east/big-childcare-keilor-heights-primary-school-oshc). Note that vacation care may also be available through the provider.

4) **School culture:** Keilor Heights Primary School offers several named extracurricular activities. These include a **School Choir** that rehearses weekly during lunchtimes and performs both contemporary and Italian songs [citation:Clubs page](https://kheights.vic.edu.au/learning/clubs/); **Instrumental Music Tutoring** through the Primary Music Institute (PMI) for keyboard, guitar, and other instruments [citation:Instrumental Music page](https://kheights.vic.edu.au/learning/instrumental-music/); and **Inter-School Sports** for senior students, complemented by House Athletics and Swimming programs [citation:School Profile](https://kheights.vic.edu.au/our-school/school-profile/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Keilor East has a quiet residential feel with excellent local amenities. The **Keilor Central Shopping Centre** on Milleara Road offers a full-size **Coles** supermarket, an **ALDI**, and a range of specialty stores [citation:Keilor Central Retail Directory](https://keilorcentral.com.au/retail-directory/). For ethnic groceries, the centre houses **Pacific Asian**, which stocks a wide variety of Asian pantry staples and snacks [citation:Pacific Asian](https://keilorcentral.com.au/stores/pacific-asian/), and nearby in Niddrie there is **Dukaan Groceries** for Indian groceries and **Asian Grocery** at 439 Keilor Road [citation:Dukaan](https://singh.com.au/dukaan-groceries-indian-grocery-store-niddrie-melbourne/) [citation:Asian Grocery](https://au.showmelocal.com/profile.aspx?bid=17785243).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Families can choose from several casual takeaway options. **Dinah Pde Pizzeria** on Dinah Parade (Italian/Pizza) is a local favourite for delivery and takeaway [citation:TuckerFox](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/keilor-east-vic-3033). **8 Asian Food Dumpling**, also in Keilor East, offers Chinese dumplings and Asian dishes [citation:TuckerFox](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/keilor-east-vic-3033). For a quick meal, **Burger Road** at Keilor Central serves burgers and fries [citation:Keilor Central Retail Directory](https://keilorcentral.com.au/retail-directory/).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Several tutoring services operate in or near Keilor East. **Success Tutoring** offers personalised tuition in Maths and English for primary and secondary students, with resources available 24/7 [citation:Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-keilor-east). **Ezy Math Tutoring** provides in-home and online Maths and English tutoring across Keilor East, with tutors including VCE high achievers and experienced educators [citation:Ezy Math Tutoring](https://www.ezymathtutoring.com.au/tutors/keilor-east-3033).

8) **Commute:** Keilor East is not directly served by a train station. The nearest station is **Keilor Plains** on the **Sunbury line**, located approximately 3-4 km from the suburb’s centre [citation:Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/keilor-plains/). The train journey from Keilor Plains to Flinders Street Station takes roughly **30–35 minutes** depending on the service. Local bus routes (such as the **465** or **476**) connect Keilor East to Keilor Plains station or to Essendon station for additional train options [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Keilor-East).
