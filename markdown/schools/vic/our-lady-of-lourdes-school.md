1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Prahran is an affluent, white-collar suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,121, well above the Victorian median [citation:2021 Prahran, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22118). The median age is 34, reflecting a youthful population, and the area has a high proportion of singles, with an average of only 1.8 people per household and 0.3 children per household [citation:2021 Prahran, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22118). Families with children make up a smaller share of the population compared to the broader Melbourne average.

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) at Our Lady of Lourdes School is managed by MACSEYE, a subsidiary of Melbourne Archdiocese Catholic Schools [citation:Our Community | Our Lady of Lourdes Catholic Primary School Prahran](https://www.ololprimary.com.au/ourcommunity). Specific hours of operation are not detailed in the available sources, but the school also previously lists Extend as a provider, though the current provider appears to be MACSEYE [citation:Our Lady of Lourdes Catholic Primary School Prahran East Website](https://ololprahan.schoolzineplus.com/before-and-after-care).

4) **School culture:** Our Lady of Lourdes School offers a **Chess Club** and an **Instrumental Music Program** (including guitar, piano, and violin lessons) as named extracurricular activities [citation:Our Community | Our Lady of Lourdes Catholic Primary School Prahran](https://www.ololprimary.com.au/ourcommunity). The school also participates in the **Premier's Reading Challenge** and has a **School Choir**. These programs are drawn from the school's community page.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Prahran has a vibrant, market-driven vibe centred on the historic Prahran Market, which features specialty grocers such as **Lee’s Asian Grocery** (known for fresh Asian ingredients and homemade dumplings) [citation:Lee’s Asian Grocery | Prahran Market](https://www.prahranmarket.com.au/trader/lees-asian-grocery/). Other specialty options include **In the Pantry**, an Asian grocery on Commercial Road, and **Prahran Convenience**, a provisions shop offering Indian, Mexican, and Chinese food items [citation:In The Pantry | Prahran VIC | Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/inthepantry/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly casual dining options in Prahran include **Hu Tong Dumpling Bar** (Chinese, with takeaway) and **Lemnos Tavern** (Greek/Mediterranean) [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Prahran - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g261664-zfp10601-Prahran_Stonnington_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). **David's Restaurant** is another popular Chinese option known for its dumplings, suitable for takeaway or dining in [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Prahran - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g261664-zfp10601-Prahran_Stonnington_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).
