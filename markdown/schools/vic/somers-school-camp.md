1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Somers is an affluent, older suburb with a median age of 53 and a median weekly household income of $1,906 [citation:2021 Somers, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22303). The workforce profile is predominantly white-collar, and the area has a family-vs-singles mix that leans toward older couples and empty-nesters, with 518 families among 1,857 residents [citation:2021 Somers, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22303). The median age is significantly higher than the Victorian median of 38, indicating a mature demographic [citation:2021 Somers, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22303).

3) **Working-parent support:** Somers School Camp itself is a specialist camp for Year 5 and 6 students and does not operate an OSHC program [citation:Somers School Camp](https://www.somersschoolcamp.org/). However, Somers Primary School, located at 87 Camp Hill Road, offers a Before and After School Care program from 7:00am until 8:45am and 3:30pm until 6:00pm, operated by the school itself [citation:OSHC | Somers Primary School](https://somersps.vic.edu.au/oshc/). The provider is the school, not an external company, according to the school's website.

4) **School culture:** Somers School Camp is a Department of Education and Early Childhood Development (DEECD) school that offers Year 5 and 6 students a range of outdoor and environmental activities [citation:Somers School Camp](https://www.somersschoolcamp.org/). Specific named extracurriculars were not listed in the available sources, but the core program centers on residential outdoor education [citation:Somers School Camp](https://www.somersschoolcamp.org/). For families seeking a broader extracurricular range, the nearby Somers Primary School would offer a different set of activities.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Somers has a quiet, coastal-village vibe with limited commercial amenities. The nearest major supermarkets are in Hastings (about 5 km away), including Woolworths at 11-23 Victoria Street and Coles on Victoria Street [citation:15 BEST local Supermarkets & Grocery Stores in Somers, VIC | Yellow Pages®](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/somers-vic-3927). Specialty or ethnic supermarkets are not present in Somers itself; the closest Asian grocery options are in the wider Mornington Peninsula area, not detailed in available sources.

6) **Weeknight dining:** Somers Pizza and Family Restaurant offers pizza, pasta, and family-style Italian-American dishes for takeaway and dine-in [citation:Menu - Somers Pizza and Family Restaurant](https://somerspizza.com/menu/). The Somers General serves casual takeaway food, drinks, and MUBBLE ice cream, and also operates as a general store [citation:The Somers General - Somers, AU-VIC | OpenTable](https://www.opentable.com.au/r/the-somers-general-somers). 8) **Commute:** Somers does not have a train station. The nearest station is Hastings, approximately 5 km away, served by the Stony Point line on the Metro network [citation:Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/timetables/). From Hastings, travel by train to Flinders Street Station via Frankston takes approximately 1 hour and 15 minutes, depending on connections [citation:Somers to Flinders Street Station - 5 ways to travel via train, bus, and taxi](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Somers-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
