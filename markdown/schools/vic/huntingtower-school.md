Quick note: Huntingtower is a Prep–12 independent school (not only a primary school); I’ll treat its Prep/junior offering as the focus for this family briefing and note the school’s wider Prep–12 status. [citation:Huntingtower - Home](https://www.huntingtower.vic.edu.au/)

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
Mount Waverley is a settled, family‑oriented suburb with many married/established households and a median age of 40. Median weekly household income is relatively high at about $2,066, consistent with a middle‑to‑upper middle income, family-focused area. [citation:2021 Mount Waverley, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21816)

2) Most common overseas country of birth
About half the suburb is Australian‑born (~49%); the largest overseas birthplace is China (~13%). If you’re looking for visible cultural communities, Chinese‑born residents are the single largest overseas birthgroup in Mount Waverley. [citation:SBS Australian Census Explorer](https://www.sbs.com.au/census-explorer-2021/index.html?lang=en&placeType=sal&places=mount-waverley&topic=cultural-diversity)

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
Huntingtower runs an Aftercare/ASC program (Before & After‑School Care) — there is an ASC Parent Handbook describing the school’s Aftercare services for families. (So yes: on‑site after‑school care is provided.) [citation:ASC Parent Handbook (Huntingtower Aftercare)](https://cdn.huntingtower.vic.edu.au/htdocuments/handbooks/ASC-Parent-Handbook.pdf)

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars
Notable co‑curriculars include a strong music program (concert & jazz bands, orchestras, choirs and instrumental ensembles), drama/performing arts and a comprehensive outdoor education program; the school also lists debating and dance among activities. [citation:Huntingtower - Co-curricular (Music Program)](https://www.huntingtower.vic.edu.au/index.php/music-program) [citation:International School Guide - Huntingtower School](https://internationalschoolguide.com/australia/huntingtower_school_mount_waverley.htm)

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
Mount Waverley feels suburban and family‑friendly with local shopping along Stephensons Road and small neighbourhood centres (parks, schools and playgrounds common). Local specialty/ethnic grocers include TK Asian Supermarket and Lion City Asian Grocery, plus mainstream options like Ritchies and Coles for everyday groceries. [citation:Mount Waverley Village Shopping Centre - Directory](https://mountwaverley3149.com.au/directory/) [citation:TK Asian Supermarket - Mount Waverley Village Shopping Centre](https://mountwaverley3149.com.au/stores/tk-asian-supermarket/) [citation:Mt Waverley Supermarket (Ritchies)](https://mt-waverley.shop.ritchies.com.au/)

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 family‑friendly options
Easy family takeaway/dine options on Stephensons Road include Mango Tree (Thai), Pinewood / Sugami Noodle & Sushi / other Asian takeaway venues, and Smoky Hen (charcoal/K‑style chicken) — a mix of Thai, Asian noodles/sushi and popular chicken/BBQ takeaway. 8) Commute — train travel time to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)
Mount Waverley is served by the Glen Waverley line; typical train journey time from Mount Waverley station to Flinders Street is about 27–32 minutes (roughly 30 minutes as a practical estimate, depending on time of day and service).
