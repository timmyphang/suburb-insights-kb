1) **Suburb socio-economic profile** – Forest Hill is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb. The 2021 Census shows a median weekly household income of $1,658 and a median age of 42, indicating a mature, settled population [citation:2021 Forest Hill (Vic.) QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20937). The suburb is predominantly residential, with 72.0% houses and above-average rates of outright home ownership [citation:Forest Hill – SA2 211041269](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/forest_hill).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth** – The most common foreign country of birth for Forest Hill residents is not presented in the available search results. For detailed birthplace data, including the percentage of Australian-born residents, refer directly to the ABS QuickStats for Forest Hill (Vic.) 2021 [citation:2021 Forest Hill (Vic.) QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20937).

3) **Working-parent support** – Parkmore Primary School offers outside school hours care through Big Childcare, as listed on the ACECQA national register [citation:Big Childcare - Parkmore PS OSHC](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/big-childcare-parkmore-ps-oshc). Specific operating hours for the before- and after-school program are not detailed in the provided sources; contact the school directly for precise times.

4) **School culture** – Parkmore Primary School runs several named extracurricular activities. The school has active **Future Problem Solving** teams, an **Instrumental Music** program offering piano, violin, and keyboard lessons, and students participate in **inter-school sport** [citation:Parkmore Primary School homepage](https://www.parkmore.vic.edu.au/); [citation:Co-curricular Activities](https://www.parkmore.vic.edu.au/page/56/Co-curricular-Activities); [citation:Instrumental Music](https://www.parkmore.vic.edu.au/page/22/Instrumental-Music).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries** – Forest Hill has a suburban, family-friendly feel anchored by the Forest Hill Chase shopping centre. For specialty groceries, **KFL Supermarket** (293–295 Canterbury Road) offers a wide range of Asian products, and **Yang Yang Asian Groceries** and **Foodness Asian Supermarket** operate inside Forest Hill Chase [citation:KFL Supermarket Forest Hill](https://www.kflsupermarkets.com.au/forest-hill-vic); [citation:Yang Yang Asian Groceries](https://www.foresthillchase.com.au/stores/yang-yang-asian-groceries); [citation:Foodness Asian Supermarket](https://www.foresthillchase.com.au/stores/foodness-asian-supermarket).

6) **Weeknight dining** – Casual family-friendly takeaway options in Forest Hill include **Kambo Chinese Restaurant** (Chinese cuisine) and **Plucka Chicken** (roast chicken, located in Forest Hill Chase) [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Forest Hill](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/forest-hill-vic-3131); [citation:Forest Hill Chase stores](https://www.foresthillchase.com.au/stores).

7) **Tuition and enrichment** – Near Forest Hill, **Kip McGrath Education Centre (KMEC) Forest Hill** offers tutoring in English and maths, and **Success Tutoring Forest Hill** provides personalised learning support for primary and secondary students [citation:KMEC Forest Hill](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/foresthill); [citation:Success Tutoring Forest Hill](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-forest-hill).

8) **Commute** – The nearest train station to Forest Hill is Nunawading Station on the **Belgrave/Lilydale line**. The journey from Nunawading to Flinders Street Station takes approximately **35 minutes** [citation:Rome2Rio Forest Hill to Flinders Street](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Forest-Hill-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
