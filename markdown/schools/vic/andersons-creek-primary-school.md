1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Warrandyte is an affluent suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,742, significantly higher than the Victorian median [citation:2021 Warrandyte, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22702). The suburb has a median age of 45, indicating an older demographic, and families are well-represented with an average of 1.9 children per family [citation:2021 Warrandyte, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22702). The area is predominantly white-collar and residentially settled, with 70.7% of dwellings being separate houses and a low population density [citation:2021 Warrandyte, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22702).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Warrandyte is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 76.8% of residents born in Australia [citation:2021 Warrandyte, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22702). For those born overseas, the most common country of birth is England, at 5.7% [citation:2021 Warrandyte, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22702).

3) **Working-parent support:** Andersons Creek Primary School partners with TheirCare to provide Outside School Hours Care [citation:Outside School Hours Care | Andersons Creek Primary School](https://www.andersonscreekps.vic.edu.au/community/outside-school-hours-care/). Before school care operates from 7:00–8:45 am, and after school care runs 3:30–6:30 pm, including pupil-free days and school holidays [citation:Outside School Hours Care | Andersons Creek Primary School](https://www.andersonscreekps.vic.edu.au/community/outside-school-hours-care/).

4) **School culture:** Andersons Creek Primary School offers a variety of named extracurricular activities. These include **Kelly Sports** (multi-sport sessions for Prep–Grade 4), **Digimaker** (coding program for Grades 2–6), and **Wonnies Music** (individual or group music lessons for all grades) [citation:Extra-Curricular Activities This Term | Andersons Creek Primary School](https://www.andersonscreekps.vic.edu.au/news/2026/01/extra-curricular-activities-this-term/). Grade 5 and 6 students also participate in **interschool sports** such as football, soccer, and netball [citation:Extracurricular activities | Andersons Creek Primary School](https://www.andersonscreekps.vic.edu.au/learning/extracurricular-activities/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Warrandyte has a relaxed, semi-rural feel with bushland and river amenities, including the Yarra River and Warrandyte Riverside Market. For groceries, families can access **Wanlong Oriental Supermarket** (an Asian grocery in nearby Ringwood) [citation:Wanlong Oriental – Get your fresh food](https://wanlongoriental.com.au/). There is also an online Indian grocery **Tales of India** that delivers to Warrandyte [citation:Indian Grocery Store Warrandyte | Free Delivery | Tales Of India](https://talesofindia.com.au/pages/indian-grocery-store-warrandyte-3113).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly takeaway options in Warrandyte include **Indian Affair Restaurant** at 35 Drysdale Road, offering Indian cuisine [citation:Warrandyte 3113 Food Delivery and Takeaway](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/warrandyte-3113). Another option is **Mooroolbark Pizza & Pasta** (Italian, 9.8 km away) [citation:Warrandyte 3113 Food Delivery and Takeaway](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/warrandyte-3113). Additionally, **Fantastic Kebab and Pizza** (Mooroolbark) delivers to Warrandyte for a more casual meal [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Warrandyte, VIC 3113](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/warrandyte-vic-3113).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Success Tutoring** offers in-centre and online tutoring for primary and high school students in Warrandyte [citation:Warrandyte – Success Tutoring](https://www.successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-Warrandyte). **Cloud Tuition** provides personalised online tutoring for Years 2–12 in English, Maths, and Science, with flexible scheduling [citation:Tutoring Warrandyte | Online Tutors for Years 2-12](https://www.cloudtuition.com.au/locations/tutoring-vic/warrandyte).

8) **Commute:** Warrandyte does not have a train station; the nearest train service is from **Ringwood Station** on the **Belgrave/Lilydale line**. Travel time from Ringwood to Flinders Street Station is approximately 40–50 minutes. Alternatively, the **SmartBus 906** route runs from Warrandyte to the city, taking about 1 hour 3 minutes to reach Flinders Street Station [citation:Flinders Street Station to Warrandyte](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Warrandyte).
