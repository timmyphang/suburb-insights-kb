1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Mixed inner‑urban, younger suburb with median age 35 and a population ~11,729. Median weekly household income $1,989 and median weekly rent $351 (2021 Census) — a middle‑income, gentrifying area with both families and many young adults/singles (47.9% never married) [citation:2021 West Footscray QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22756).  
2) Most common foreign country of birth
- Most common overseas birthplace is India (ABS‑derived profiles show India as the top overseas country; Australia remains the majority birthplace). See 2021 Census quickstats/community profile summary [citation:2021 West Footscray QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22756) [citation:West Footscray suburb profile (GDP.com.au)](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/west-footscray-vic).  
3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- On‑site Before & After School Care and vacation/holiday programs are provided on campus by TheirCare (Footscray West PS TheirCare at 17 Argyle St). Holiday hours listed ~07:00–18:15; contact TheirCare for bookings (theircare.com.au / phone listed on service pages) [citation:Out-of-School-Hours-Care (fwps.vic.edu.au)](https://www.fwps.vic.edu.au/page/224/Out-of-School-Hours-Care) [citation:TheirCare program page](https://theircare.com.au/holiday-programs/footscray-west-ps-theircare/).  
4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars (specific)
- Musical Futures / school music program (grade 1–6) and a user‑pays SoundGarage instrumental program (keyboards, drums, guitar, singing).  
- Sports teams, lunchtime clubs (e.g., chess), Environment Team and camps for Grades 3–6 (Phillip Island, Yarra Valley, etc.) [citation:Extra‑Curricular (fwps.vic.edu.au)](https://www.fwps.vic.edu.au/page/235/Extra-Curricular) [citation:SoundGarage (fwps.vic.edu.au)](https://www.fwps.vic.edu.au/page/271/SoundGarage).  
5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- West Footscray is inner‑urban and multicultural with easy access to fresh produce and market stalls (Footscray Market) and pockets of family‑friendly parks and cafés. Ethnic/specialty grocers include Asian/Vietnamese supermarkets (e.g., KFL Supermarket on Barkly St) and local Vietnamese grocers (e.g., Vn Grocery) plus many Footscray Market traders [citation:Footscray Market](https://www.footscraymkt.com.au/) [citation:KFL Supermarkets — Footscray](https://www.kflsupermarkets.com.au/footscray-vic) [citation:Vn Grocery listing](https://australia.chamberofcommerce.com/business-directory/victoria/west-footscray/grocery-store/3538652-vn-grocery).

- Jaathara at Barkly — Indian/South‑Indian takeaway/dine‑in on Barkly St [citation:Jaathara at Barkly (restaurant listing)](https://restaurantguru.com/Jaathara-at-barkly-West-Footscray).
