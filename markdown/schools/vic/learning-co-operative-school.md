1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Cottles Bridge is an affluent suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,170, considerably higher than the Victorian median [citation:2021 Cottles Bridge, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20654). The median age is 52, well above the state median of 38, indicating an older population [citation:2021 Cottles Bridge, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20654). With 180 families and an average of 2.7 people per household, the area leans towards established families and older couples rather than young singles [citation:2021 Cottles Bridge, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20654).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The suburb is overwhelmingly Australian-born; according to 2016 data, 62.1% of people had both parents born in Australia [citation:2016 Cottles Bridge, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20650). Specific country-of-birth percentages were not found in the available 2021 QuickStats snippets, but the dominant foreign-born group is not identified in the provided sources.

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is not offered on-site at Learning Co-operative School. Nearby options include Hurstbridge PS TheirCare (Main Road, Hurstbridge) and Camp Australia - Wattle Glen Primary School OSHC (16 Reynolds Rd, Wattle Glen) [citation:OSHC in Cottles Bridge](https://toddle.com.au/oshc/vic/cottles-bridge). Operating hours for these services are not specified in the available snippets.

4) **School culture:** Learning Co-operative School offers several named extracurriculars including an annual "Co-op concert" (a drama and music extravaganza) and a Friday morning playgroup from 9:30-11am [citation:Our Teachers | Learning Co-op Primary School](https://www.learningcooperative.vic.edu.au/our-teachers) [citation:Welcome | Learning Co-operative Primary School](https://www.learningcooperative.vic.edu.au/). Parents also lead activities such as art, music, sport, woodwork, and cooking during their "time on" [citation:FAQs | Learning Co-op Primary School](https://www.learningcooperative.vic.edu.au/faqs).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Cottles Bridge is a rural bushland suburb with limited commercial amenities. For groceries, residents can visit the nearby Hurstbridge Village, which includes The Bridge Store (a milk bar open 365 days), Hurstbridge Deli & Larder (offering local cheeses, meats, and fresh pasta), and Nature's Harvest (a health food store) [citation:Groceries | Hurstbridge Village](https://www.hurstbridgevillage.com.au/groceries). No specialty ethnic supermarkets are noted in the available sources for Cottles Bridge itself.

6) **Weeknight dining:** Cottles Bridge has very limited dining options in the suburb itself, but nearby Hurstbridge offers casual family-friendly choices. These include Henry Hursts Pizza & Pasta (Italian), Hursty fish and chips (seafood), and Hurstbridge Pizza and Pasta (pizza) [citation:The Best Cottles Bridge,VIC Restaurants](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/cottles-bridge).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** No dedicated tutoring or enrichment centres are listed for Cottles Bridge in the available sources. Families can access online tutoring platforms like Learnmate, which connects local tutors in subjects including English, Maths, and Science [citation:Looking for tutors in Cottles Bridge?](https://learnmate.com.au/cmloc-cottles-bridge-vic-3099/). 8) **Commute:** Cottles Bridge does not have a train station. The nearest station is Hurstbridge, which is the terminus of the Hurstbridge line on the Melbourne Metro network. The train journey from Hurstbridge station to Flinders Street station typically takes approximately 55-60 minutes [citation:Timetable Search | Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/timetables/).
