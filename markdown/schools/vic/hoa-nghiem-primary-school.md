1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Springvale South is a working-class to middle-income, predominantly suburban area with a median weekly household income of $1,505 and a median age of 38 [citation:2021 Springvale South, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/212041318). The suburb has a high proportion of houses (85.8% of dwellings) and above-average rates of outright home ownership, but also exhibits indicators of disadvantage [citation:Springvale South – SA2 212041318 | AreaSearch](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/springvale_south); [citation:Report Addressing concentrations of disadvantage Springvale case study report](https://www.ahuri.edu.au/sites/default/files/migration/documents/AHURI_Research_Paper_Addressing-concentrations-of-disadvantage-Springvale-case-study-report.pdf). The area is family-oriented, with an average of 0.9 children per household and 3.2 people per household [citation:2021 Springvale South, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/212041318).
