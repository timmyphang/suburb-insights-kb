The workforce mix is not explicitly stated in the available data, but the above-average income and family structure suggest a predominantly white-collar, professional resident base.

4) **School culture**: Holy Trinity runs several named extracurricular programs. The **Friendship Groups** program is a vertical curriculum activity where Prep to Year 6 students meet every second Friday to build cross-age connections [citation:Friendship Groups - Holy Trinity Primary School](https://www.htws.catholic.edu.au/student-wellbeing/friendship-groups/). The school also offers a **Buddies Program** and **Lunchtime Clubs** as part of its student wellbeing and enrichment initiatives [citation:Curriculum | Holy Trinity Primary School](https://htws.catholic.edu.au/curriculum/). Additionally, the **Physical Education** program is linked to the school’s TORCH Values (Tolerance, Optimism, Respect, Confidence, Honesty) [citation:Physical Education - Holy Trinity Primary School](https://www.htws.catholic.edu.au/learning-teaching/physical-education/).
