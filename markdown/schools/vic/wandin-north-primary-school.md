1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Wandin North is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $1,969, and the median personal income is $803 per week [citation:2021 Wandin North, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22673). The median age is 39, and the average household size is 2.8 people, indicating a strong family presence [citation:Wandin North VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/wandin-north-vic). Occupation breakdown (white-collar vs blue-collar) was not found in available sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Wandin North is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 95.1% of residents born in Australia [citation:Wandin North VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/wandin-north-vic). Among overseas-born residents, the most common country of birth is England at 4.3% [citation:Wandin North VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/wandin-north-vic).

3) **Working-parent support:** Wandin North Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by TheirCare [citation:Before & After Care | Wandin North Primary School](https://www.wandinnorthps.vic.edu.au/before-and-after-care/). The service operates Monday to Friday from 6:30–9:00 am and 3:30–6:00 pm [citation:TheirCare OSHC - Wandin North Primary School](https://www.wandinnorthps.vic.edu.au/theircare-oshc/).
