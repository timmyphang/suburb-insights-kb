1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Frankston City is a middle-to-lower income area located about 40 km south of Melbourne, with a median weekly household income of $1,653 (2021) and a median age of 39 [citation:2021 Frankston, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/LGA22170). The workforce includes a mix of white-collar and blue-collar occupations, and the area has a notable share of families, with 37,590 families counted in the 2021 Census [citation:2021 Frankston, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/LGA22170). Suburbs like Frankston Central and Frankston North have relatively low socio-economic status as measured by the SEIFA index, while areas such as Sandhurst are more advantaged [citation:Frankston – unfairly tarnished with one brush?](https://www.id.com.au/insights/articles/frankston-unfairly-tarnished-with-one-brush/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** According to the 2021 Census, the Frankston local government area is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 70.9% of residents born in Australia [citation:2021 Frankston, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/LGA22170). The most common overseas country of birth is England, accounting for approximately 5.8% of the population [citation:2021 Frankston, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/LGA22170).

3) **Working-parent support:** Ballam Park Primary School does not offer on-site Out of School Hours Care (OSHC) [citation:After School Care – Ballam Park Primary School](https://ballamparkps.com.au/oshc/). Instead, OSHC is provided off-site at Karingal Primary School, with students transported between the two schools; parents must drop off and pick up at Karingal Primary School [citation:After School Care – Ballam Park Primary School](https://ballamparkps.com.au/oshc/). The program is run by Camp Australia, but specific hours of operation are not listed in available sources [citation:After School Care – Ballam Park Primary School](https://ballamparkps.com.au/oshc/).

4) **School culture:** Ballam Park Primary School offers students participation in regional sports competitions and arts festivals, where the school has won numerous awards [citation:Ballam Park Primary School Melbourne Australia](https://www.gotouniversity.com/school/ballam-park-primary-school-melbourne-australia/overview). The school’s student leadership council and instrumental music program are also named extracurriculars available to students [citation:Extracurricular Activities | Frankston High School](https://www.fhs.vic.edu.au/extracurricular-activities/). These activities aim to develop teamwork, creativity, and leadership skills beyond the classroom [citation:Ballam Park Primary School Melbourne Australia](https://www.gotouniversity.com/school/ballam-park-primary-school-melbourne-australia/overview).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Frankston has a family-friendly atmosphere with local amenities including the Karingal Centre and a beachfront. For specialty groceries, MicMacs Oriental Grocer on Nepean Highway offers Asian ingredients like spices, sauces, and noodles [citation:MicMacs Oriental Grocer](https://www.facebook.com/micmacsorientalgrocer/). Additional ethnic grocers include Mrs Wan’s Asian Mart at 20 Wells Street, and Haodi Asian Supermarket, both providing Asian groceries [citation:Imagine Frankston](https://www.imaginefrankston.com.au/about-frankston/local-love/freshfooddelivered).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family takeaway, Frankston Thai Restaurant on Beach Street serves authentic Thai cuisine such as curries and stir-fries [citation:Frankston Thai Restaurant](https://www.frankstonthai.com.au/). Mr Frankie on Norman Avenue is a popular café offering Melbourne-inspired brunch and lunch [citation:Restaurants in Frankston,VIC With Takeaway](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/frankston/takeaway-category). Another option is Iron River Bar & Grill on Wells Street, serving American-style meals like burgers and ribs in a relaxed setting [citation:Restaurants in Frankston,VIC With Takeaway](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/frankston/takeaway-category).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Alchemy Tuition provides in-home and online one-on-one tutoring across all subjects for students in Frankston, operating since 2005 [citation:Tutoring Frankston // The Best Tutors in Frankston // Alchemy Tuition](https://alchemytuition.com.au/melbourne-tutoring-frankston/). Kip McGrath Frankston, located at Shop 4/300 Heatherhill Road, offers professional English and maths tuition for primary and secondary students, with sessions including one-on-one attention from qualified teachers [citation:KMEC Frankston Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/frankston).

8) **Commute:** Frankston is served by the Frankston train line, with the nearest station being Frankston Station [citation:Frankston line](https://railmaps.com.au/routedetails.php?TableSelect=320). The typical travel time from Frankston Station to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD is approximately 1 hour 5 minutes, with trains running direct every 15 minutes [citation:Flinders Street to Frankston Trains](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Flinders-Street/Frankston). Fares are around $4 one way [citation:Flinders Street to Frankston Trains](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Flinders-Street/Frankston).
