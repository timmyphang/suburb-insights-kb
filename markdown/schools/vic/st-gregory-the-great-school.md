1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Doncaster is an affluent, predominantly white-collar suburb with a strong family orientation. The median weekly household income in the broader Doncaster area is $1,934 (Doncaster East – North) [citation:2021 Doncaster East - North, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/207021424), and the median age is 40–43 years [citation:2021 Doncaster, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20771). The suburb has a high proportion of professionals and managers, reflecting its position as a well-established middle-to-upper-income community [citation:2021 Doncaster East - North, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/207021424).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** In Doncaster, the most common overseas country of birth is China, accounting for approximately 8–10% of residents (data varies slightly by SA2). Australian-born residents make up around 55–60% of the population [citation:2021 Doncaster, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20771).

3) **Working-parent support:** St Gregory the Great School partners with Camp Australia to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) [citation:Before & After Care | St Gregory the Great Catholic Primary School](https://sgdoncaster2025v3.digitalschoolsbuilds.com/before-and-after-care/). The morning program runs from 6:30am–8:30am (based on Camp Australia’s typical schedule) and the afternoon program runs from 3:30pm–6:00pm (Monday–Friday) [citation:Camp Australia - St Gregory the Great School OSHC - Doncaster 3108](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3108/doncaster/camp-australia-st-gregory-the-great-school-oshc). Care is also available on pupil-free days and during school holidays.

4) **School culture:** St Gregory the Great offers several named extracurricular activities, including the **School Choir**, **School Band**, and **Mini Vinnies** (for Years 5–6) [citation:Extra Curricular Activities - St Gregory the Great Catholic Primary School](https://sgdoncaster.catholic.edu.au/extra-curricular-activities). Students can also join onsite **chess tuition** (Years 3–4), **Altar Servers** (from Year 3), and **Lunchtime Craft Club** [citation:Extra Curricular Activities - St Gregory the Great Catholic Primary School](https://sgdoncaster.catholic.edu.au/extra-curricular-activities).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Doncaster is a family-friendly suburb centred around Westfield Doncaster shopping centre, which includes **Colonial Fresh Markets** for fresh produce and daily baked bread [citation:Colonial Fresh Markets at Westfield Doncaster](https://www.westfield.com.au/doncaster/store/4lVH1h3Pdm2MsAU6Kskwoi/colonial-fresh-markets). For Asian groceries, families can visit **KFL Convenience Store** (1029 Doncaster Road, Doncaster East) or **Shining Asia** inside Westfield Doncaster [citation:KFL Supermarket - Doncaster East](https://www.kflsupermarkets.com.au/doncaster-east-vic); [citation:Shining Asia at Westfield Doncaster](https://www.westfield.com.au/doncaster/store/4UVzopNU92QW2e0iMQy8SM/shining-asia).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family-friendly meals, **Little Bangkok** (Shop 2007, Westfield Doncaster) serves Thai street food and offers takeaway [citation:Little Bangkok Doncaster](https://www.littlebangkok.com.au/doncaster/). **BBQ-K** (Doncaster East) offers Korean BBQ with dine-in and takeaway options [citation:The 10 Best Restaurants Near Me in Doncaster East | OpenTable](https://www.opentable.com.au/nearby/restaurants-near-me-doncaster-east-australia). **Pizza** from **Smokin Joes** (Doncaster East) is also popular for delivery [citation:Takeaway in Doncaster - Order Food Delivery Near Me | Uber Eats](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/doncaster-melbourne-vic).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Brainworks Doncaster** (123 Church Road, Doncaster) offers one-to-one tutoring in Maths, English, and selective-school exam preparation (Years 3–9) [citation:Brainworks Doncaster. One to One Tutoring For Your Child](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-doncaster). **Kip McGrath Doncaster** (251 Blackburn Road, Doncaster East) provides individualised programs in Reading, English, and Mathematics for Prep to VCE students, with sessions Monday–Friday [citation:KMEC Doncaster Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/doncaster).

8) **Commute:** Doncaster does not have a train station. The primary public transport option is the **Route 907** bus (SmartBus), which runs from Doncaster Shopping Centre to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street) in approximately 30–40 minutes during peak times. Alternatively, the **Route 906** bus also provides service to the city, with a typical travel time of about 35 minutes.
