St Albans is lower‑to‑middle income compared with Victoria: median weekly household income $1,205 (Victoria median ~$1,759), and a median age of 36 — a relatively young, family‑oriented suburb with many households and higher rental tenancy than the state average [citation:2021 St Albans QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22330) [citation:2021 Victoria QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/2).
