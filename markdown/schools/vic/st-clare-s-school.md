1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Thomastown is a working-class suburb with a mix of blue-collar and white-collar workers. The median weekly household income is $1,225, which is below the national average [citation:2021 Thomastown, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22504). The median age is 39 years, and 63.0% of dwellings are owned outright, indicating a stable, family-oriented population [citation:Thomastown VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/thomastown-vic).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas birthplace in Thomastown is India, accounting for 11.8% of residents [citation:Thomastown VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/thomastown-vic). The suburb is predominantly Australian-born, with 73.9% of residents born in Australia [citation:Thomastown VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/thomastown-vic).
