1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Coburg is a middle-income, culturally diverse suburb. The median weekly household income in the Coburg-West area (the most relevant SA2 containing Coburg Primary School) is $2,186[citation:2021 Coburg - West, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/206011498). The median age is 37, and the area has a balanced mix of families and singles, with 3,688 families and 5,711 dwellings[citation:2021 Coburg - West, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/206011498). The workforce is mixed white-collar and blue-collar, though specific occupational breakdown is not available in the provided snippets.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas country of birth in Coburg (Coburg-West SA2) is not explicitly stated in the provided 2021 QuickStats data. However, the same QuickStats show that 28.8% of residents were born overseas (top responses include Greece and Italy in previous censuses, but specific 2021 percentages are not given). Australian-born residents make up approximately 67.8% of the population[citation:2021 Coburg - West, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/206011498).

3) **Working-parent support:** Coburg Primary School’s Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided by OSHClub. The service runs before school as "Rise then Shine" from 7:00 AM until bell time, and after school as "Stay and Play" from bell time until 6:30 PM[citation:Coburg Primary School | OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/coburg-primary-school/). Holiday programs ("Holiday HQ") are also available from 7:00 AM to 6:30 PM during school breaks[citation:Coburg Primary School | OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/coburg-primary-school/).

4) **School culture:** Coburg Primary School offers several named extracurricular programs. The **Instrumental Music Program (IMP)** is run in partnership with Junior Rockers Music, providing lessons for Prep to Year 6 students[citation:Instrumental Music Program](https://www.coburgps.vic.edu.au/page/18/Instrumental-Music-Program). The school also runs a **Breakfast Club** each week and a **Kitchen Garden Program** for Years 3/4 students[citation:2023 Annual Report to the School Community](https://www.coburgps.vic.edu.au/source/2025/CPS_Annual_Report_2023.pdf)[citation:A Message from the Principal](https://www.coburgps.vic.edu.au/page/4/A-Message-from-the-Principal). Additionally, there are lunchtime rock bands and a Year 5/6 Elective Program[citation:A Message from the Principal](https://www.coburgps.vic.edu.au/page/4/A-Message-from-the-Principal).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Coburg has a lively, multicultural atmosphere centred on Sydney Road, with a strong family-friendly community. For groceries, **KFL Supermarket** at 376 Sydney Road is a large Asian grocery store specialising in authentic Asian products[citation:Coburg KFL | The Asian Food Specialist](https://www.kflsupermarkets.com.au/coburg-vic). For Indian groceries, **Namaste Indian Groceries** in Central Coburg stocks spices and staples[citation:Namaste Indian Groceries - Central Coburg](https://centralcoburg.com.au/trader/namaste-indian-groceries/). Other international options include Dhaka Supreme Asian Grocer for South Asian items[citation:Dhaka Supreme Asian Grocer and Gift Shop Coburg, Melbourne](https://singh.com.au/dhaka-supreme-asian-grocer-and-gift-shop-melbourne/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family-friendly meals, **Simple Thai Food** on Sydney Road offers halal Thai dishes with fast service (TripAdvisor 4.5 stars)[citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Coburg - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g1816502-zfp10601-Coburg_Moreland_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). **Pizza on Bell** at 242 Bell Street is a popular takeaway spot for pizza, chicken, and Australian fare[citation:Coburg 3058 Food Delivery and Takeaway VIC](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/coburg-3058). For Indian cuisine, **Classic Curry Restaurant** (delivery only from Melbourne CBD) is another option, though in-Coburg Indian restaurants like Desi Cuisine are available via delivery[citation:Indian Delivery & Takeaway Online in Brunswick and Coburg](https://www.menulog.com.au/takeaway/brunswick-coburg-vic/indian).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **NumberWorks'nWords Coburg** at 11 Munro Street provides expert maths and English tutoring for children aged 5–16, with tailored plans and free assessments[citation:Tutoring Coburg | NumberWorks'nWords](https://numberworksnwords.com/au/our-locations/australia/coburg). **Alchemy Tuition** offers in-home and online tutoring in Coburg, having served the area since 2005 for primary and high school students[citation:Tutoring Coburg // The Best Tutors in Coburg // Alchemy Tuition](https://alchemytuition.com.au/melbourne-tutoring-coburg/).

8) **Commute:** Coburg is serviced by **Coburg Railway Station** on the **Upfield** train line. A direct train runs every 30 minutes and takes approximately **25 minutes** to reach Flinders Street Station[citation:Coburg (Station) to Flinders Street Station - 4 ways to travel](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Coburg-Station-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
