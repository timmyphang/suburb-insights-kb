1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Balwyn North is an affluent, family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,346, and the median age is 42, which is older than the national median of 38 [citation:QuickStats 2021 Balwyn North](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20124). With 87.7% of residents having completed Year 12 or equivalent and a high proportion of white-collar professionals (not broken out in source), the suburb is strongly middle-to-upper class [citation:Balwyn North VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/balwyn-north-vic).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas country of birth is China, at 16.5% of the population. Australia-born residents make up 70.2% of the suburb, so the majority are Australian-born, with China the largest migrant group [citation:Balwyn North VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/balwyn-north-vic).

3) **Working-parent support:** St Bede's School offers Out of School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by Camp Australia. Before-school care runs from 7:00 am to 8:40 am, and after-school care from 3:15 pm to 6:00 pm, Monday to Friday during school term [citation:St Bede's School OSHC Balwyn North | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/st-bedes-school-oshc-balwyn-north). Vacation care is not listed on the ACECQA register [citation:St Bede's School OSHC Balwyn North | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/st-bedes-school-oshc-balwyn-north).

4) **School culture:** St Bede's offers a variety of named extracurricular activities including a Mini Vinnies group (social justice), French language club, and participation in the STEM MAD showcase [citation:OUR LEARNING | St Bede's Balwyn](https://www.sbedesbn.catholic.edu.au/ourlearning). Students can also join the Sacramental Program (Reconciliation, Eucharist, Confirmation) and a Transition Program [citation:OUR LEARNING | St Bede's Balwyn](https://www.sbedesbn.catholic.edu.au/ourlearning).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Balwyn North has a quiet, leafy residential feel with local family amenities including Balwyn North Village and Greythorn Park. For specialty groceries, Balwyn Asian Supermarket (76 Doncaster Road) offers a wide range of Asian products, spices, and fresh produce [citation:Balwyn Asian Supermarket - The Village Balwyn North](https://balwynnorthvillage.com.au/directory/balwyn-asian-supermarket/). Hans Asian Mart (on Doncaster Road) is another local Asian grocer [citation:Hans Asian Mart | Boroondara (C) VIC](https://www.facebook.com/HansAsianMart/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly options in Balwyn North include Pizza on Belmore (Italian/pizza, 258 Belmore Road) [citation:Pizza On Belmore](https://pizzaonbelmore.com.au/) and Olive Lane Cafe (Greek/Mediterranean, 69 Doncaster Road) [citation:Takeaway in Balwyn North - Uber Eats](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/balwyn-north-melbourne-vic). Nan Yang Express Balwyn North (Malaysian) is also available for takeaway [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Balwyn North](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/balwyn-north-vic-3104).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** ACE Learning Centre (Suite 6, 70 Doncaster Road, Balwyn North) offers small-group tutoring with a maximum of 4 students per tutor [citation:Tutoring Centre in Balwyn, Melbourne | ACE Learning Centre](https://acelearningcentre.com.au/centre-tutoring/). Brainworks Balwyn (Balwyn Community Centre, 412 Whitehorse Road, Surrey Hills) provides one-on-one tutoring in Maths, English, and Science, and also offers selective school exam preparation [citation:Balwyn Maths & English Tutoring - Brainworks](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-balwyn).

8) **Commute:** Balwyn North does not have a train station. The nearest public transport to the CBD is the Route 48 tram, which runs from Melbourne Town Hall (Collins Street) to Balwyn Road/Doncaster Road, with a travel time of approximately 42 minutes to Flinders Street Station [citation:Flinders Street Station to Balwyn North - Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Balwyn-North). Alternatively, the Route 207 bus from Little Collins Street/Queen Street to Sylvander Street/Doncaster Road takes about 52 minutes [citation:Flinders Street Station to Balwyn North - Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Balwyn-North).
