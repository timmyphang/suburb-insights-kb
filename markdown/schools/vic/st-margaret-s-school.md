### 1) Suburb socio-economic profile
East Geelong is a middle-income, predominantly white-collar suburb with a mix of families and singles. According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income is $1,782, and the median age is 40 [citation:2021 East Geelong, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20829). The population density is 1,188 persons per square km (2025 estimate) [citation:About the profile areas | City of Greater Geelong | Community profile](https://profile.id.com.au/geelong/about?WebID=190). Detailed occupation breakdown (white-collar vs blue-collar) was not found in the available sources.

### 2) Most common foreign country of birth
The most common foreign country of birth for East Geelong residents was not explicitly stated in the available QuickStats page for the suburb. However, the 2021 Census data indicates that East Geelong is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 3,852 residents (96.0%) identifying as non-Indigenous and only 43 (1.1%) as Aboriginal and/or Torres Strait Islander [citation:2021 East Geelong, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20829). The actual Australian-born percentage was not directly reported in the snippet provided.

### 3) Working-parent support
Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) at St Margaret's School is provided by Kelly Club St Margaret's Primary School [citation:Kelly Club St Margaret's Primary School | Out-of-school Care](https://www.kellyclub.com.au/stmargarets). The service offers before-school and after-school care for students of St Margaret's Primary School, as well as holiday programs. Specific opening/closing hours were not listed in the available sources; contact is available at 0422 775 712 or stmargarets@kellyclub.com.au [citation:Kelly Club St Margaret's Primary School | Out-of-school Care](https://www.kellyclub.com.au/stmargarets).

### 4) School culture
St Margaret's School offers several named extracurriculars. The school participates in inter-school sports, including a visit to GMHBA Stadium for house captains [citation:Term Four, Week 8 November 25th, 2024 #36 From Susan’s Desk](https://www.smgeelongeast.catholic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/newsletter_2024_4_08.pdf). Students engage in a music and performance program covering singing, playing instruments, and dancing, with a focus on traditional singing games and great art music [citation:Learning and Teaching – St. Margaret’s Primary School](https://www.smgeelongeast.catholic.edu.au/learning-and-teaching/). The school also runs a P&F disco and sports activities facilitated by KABOOM! [citation:Term Four, Week 6 November 11th, 2024 #34 From Susan’s Desk](https://www.smgeelongeast.catholic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/newsletter_2024_4_06.pdf).

### 5) Suburb vibe and groceries
East Geelong has a relaxed, family-friendly vibe with local amenities like parks and easy access to Corio Bay. For groceries, East Fruit Market in East Geelong is noted for special ingredients [citation:r/Geelong on Reddit: Best places for special produce?](https://www.reddit.com/r/Geelong/comments/11vm7vo/best_places_for_special_produce/). Nearby, in Geelong CBD, Wanlong Oriental is the largest Asian supermarket in the region, stocking Chinese, Indian, Malaysian, and other Asian items [citation:Wanlong Oriental – Get your fresh food](https://wanlongoriental.com.au/). Chung Vinh Phat Asian Groceries, a family-owned emporium in Geelong, also offers a wide range of Asian products [citation:10 BEST Supermarkets & Grocery Stores in Geelong VIC | Localsearch](https://www.localsearch.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/geelong-vic).

### 6) Weeknight dining
Family-friendly dining options in East Geelong include Charles St Pizza & Pasta, offering Italian cuisine with a loyal local following [citation:Restaurants in East Geelong,VIC With Child Friendly | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/geelong-bellarine-peninsula/east-geelong/childfriendly-category). YangJi Dumpling serves Chinese cuisine and is available for takeaway [citation:Restaurants in East Geelong,VIC With Takeaway | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/geelong-bellarine-peninsula/east-geelong/takeaway-category). Family Pho Geelong offers Vietnamese dishes and is listed as a child-friendly option [citation:Restaurants in East Geelong,VIC With Child Friendly | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/geelong-bellarine-peninsula/east-geelong/childfriendly-category).

### 7) Tuition and enrichment
Nearby tutoring centres include Kip McGrath Geelong, located at 2/396 Latrobe Terrace, Newtown (close to East Geelong), offering English and maths tutoring for K-12 students with a free assessment [citation:KMEC Geelong Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/geelong). NextStepAcademy provides hand-picked one-on-one tutoring in-home or online across Geelong, with no contracts [citation:Tutoring Geelong | NextStepAcademy](https://nextstepacademy.com.au/tutoring-geelong/).

### 8) Commute
East Geelong is served by Geelong Railway Station on the V/Line Geelong line. The train journey from Geelong Station to Flinders Street Station takes approximately 1 hour and 16 minutes on average, with services running every 20 minutes [citation:Geelong to Flinders Street Station - 5 ways to travel via ...](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Geelong/Flinders-Street-Station). No tram or bus route is needed for this commute, as it is a direct train service.
