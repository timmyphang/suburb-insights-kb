Below is a short, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Mooroolbark East Primary School (Mooroolbark VIC). I used a single targeted search per data point and cite the source after each fact.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Mooroolbark is a predominantly middle‑income, family‑oriented suburb (median age 37; large share of married couples and children) with a mix of white‑ and blue‑collar employment patterns. [citation:2021 Mooroolbark QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/211051281)

2) Most common overseas country of birth  
About three‑quarters of residents are Australian‑born (≈75–78%); the most common overseas birthplace is England (small single‑digit percent share). [citation:2021 Mooroolbark QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/211051281)

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
Mooroolbark East offers on‑site Before & After School Care and a Holiday program run by Camp Australia (Before 7:00–8:45; After 3:30–6:00; Holiday program 7:00–6:15 as listed). [citation:OSHC – Mooroolbark East Primary School](https://meps.vic.edu.au/oshc)

4) School culture — extracurriculars (2–3 specific items)  
Senior Choir (Years 3–6), an extension rock band, and regular interschool/school sporting programs including Swimming, Cross‑Country and Interschool Sport. [citation:Music – Mooroolbark East Primary School](https://meps.vic.edu.au/curriculum/specialists/music) [citation:Physical Education – Mooroolbark East Primary School](https://meps.vic.edu.au/curriculum/specialists/physical-education)

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)  
Mooroolbark has a suburban, family‑focused vibe with shopping strips, local parks and community facilities close to the station and main strip. Major supermarkets include Coles and a SUPA IGA, and there are local specialty/ethnic grocers such as Moy Asian Grocery and small greengrocers for fresh produce. [citation:Coles Mooroolbark](https://www.coles.com.au/find-stores/coles/vic/mooroolbark-570) [citation:Moy Asian Grocery — Mooroolbark](https://mooroolbark-vic.aussiestoresonline.com/moy-asian-grocery/)

6) Weeknight dining (2–3 family‑friendly options)  
Mooroolbark Pizza (pizza/takeaway), Mooroolbark Fish & Chips (classic fish & chips/souvlaki takeaway), and Meetang Thai Kitchen (Thai takeaway/dine‑in) are casual family‑friendly picks in town. [citation:Mooroolbark Pizza - Order Online](https://www.mooroolbarkpizza.com.au/) [citation:Mooroolbark Fish & Chips (Uber Eats)](https://www.ubereats.com/au/store/mooroolbark-fish-%26-chips/hhBCTabrRA-hh0NjToni5w) [citation:Meetang Thai Kitchen - Mooroolbark](https://meetangthai.com.au/mooroolbark/)

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 local providers)  
Kumon Mooroolbark (Kumon Maths & English centre) and local private/home tutoring services (e.g., Success Tutoring offering in‑person/online support) operate in/near Mooroolbark. [citation:Kumon Mooroolbark Education Centre](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-MOOROOLBARK-VIC) [citation:Tutoring Mooroolbark | Success Tutoring](https://www.successtutoring.com/tutoring-mooroolbark/)

8) Commute — train time to CBD (Flinders Street)  
Mooroolbark is on the Lilydale line; typical direct train travel to Flinders Street is about 45–55 minutes (average ≈50–52 minutes depending on stopping pattern and time of day).
