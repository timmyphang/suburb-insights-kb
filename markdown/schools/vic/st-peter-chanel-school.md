St Peter Chanel Primary — Deer Park (short family briefing)

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Deer Park is a mixed, largely working‑to‑middle‑income, family‑oriented suburb with a median age of 35. Median weekly household income is about $1,456 (2021 Census) — below some inner‑Melbourne suburbs but typical for outer‑west family suburbs [citation:2021 Deer Park QuickStats (SAL20729)](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20729).

2) Most common overseas birthplace  
About 42% of Deer Park residents were born in Australia; the single largest overseas birthplace is Vietnam (≈10% of residents) per 2021 Census‑derived suburb summaries [citation:Deer Park VIC Suburb Profile — Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/deer-park).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
Yes — St Peter Chanel runs before‑ and after‑school care on site (Before School and After School sessions); the service is listed on government child‑care registers (CCS‑approved) with typical session hours in the early morning and until about 6pm [citation:St Peter Chanel OSHC, Deer Park | StartingBlocks](https://www.startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01KXEF5PHME1NHF098TTREJRGN/St-Peter-Chanel-OSHC-Deer-Park).

4) School culture — extracurriculars  
Noted extracurriculars include school sport teams (soccer, netball and interschool athletics), performing arts/choir and music programs (school performing arts opportunities and a children’s choir) [citation:St Peter Chanel — AroundSchools](https://www.aroundschools.com.au/schools/st-peter-chanel-catholic-primary-school-deer-park-deer-park-vic/) [citation:St Peter Chanel School (MACS profile)](https://www.macs.vic.edu.au/SchoolInformation.aspx?School_ENumber=E1188).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries  
Deer Park is a busy outer‑west strip suburb (Ballarat Rd) with family amenities (parks, a family bistro/club and local shops) and a strong multicultural trading strip. Local grocery options include mainstream supermarkets and specialty/ethnic grocers such as FoodWorks/Deer Park Food‑way and Asian/Indian grocers (e.g. A Dat Asian Grocery, Real Spice) close to the school precinct [citation:FoodWorks Deer Park](https://deerpark.foodworks.com.au/) [citation:A Dat Asian Grocery listing](https://australia.chamberofcommerce.com/business-directory/victoria/deer-park/asian-grocery-store/3359902-a-dat-asian-grocery) [citation:Real Spice Deer Park](https://singh.com.au/real-spice-deer-park-melbourne/).

6) Weeknight dining (casual / family‑friendly)  
Good quick options on Ballarat Rd include Deer Park Club bistro (family venue with kid’s play area), Eat Viet / Western Pho (Vietnamese takeaway/dine‑in) and local fish & chips or halal burger takeaways (BRNX) — a mix of Vietnamese and halal/fast‑casual choices [citation:DEER PARK CLUB](https://www.deerparkclub.com.au/) [citation:Eat Viet (Western Pho)](https://www.eatviet.com.au/) [citation:BRNX — Deer Park location](https://brnx.com.au/).

7) Tuition and enrichment nearby  
Local tutoring/enrichment options include a Kumon centre serving Deer Park and private maths tutors/tuition services (e.g. Cloud Tuition listings for Deer Park); these are the typical academic supports families use locally [citation:Kumon Deer Park listing](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-DEER-PARK-VIC) [citation:Cloud Tuition — Maths Tutors Deer Park](https://www.cloudtuition.com.au/locations/maths-tutors-deer-park).

8) Commute to CBD (public transport)  
Deer Park has a train station on the Sunbury/metro corridor; typical train time to Flinders Street is roughly 25–30 minutes (service times vary by stopping pattern and time of day) [citation:Sunbury line - Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/lines/sunbury/) [citation:Flinders Street to Deer Park - Rome2rio (travel time estimate)](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Flinders-Street-Station/Deer-Park-Station).
