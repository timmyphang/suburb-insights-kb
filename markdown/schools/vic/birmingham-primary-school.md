1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 lines)
- Mount Evelyn is a middle‑income, family‑oriented outer‑suburb (median age 38; many married households and a sizeable cohort of children) with higher-than-typical household incomes for the area (median weekly household income $2,045). [citation:2021 Mount Evelyn QuickStats (ABS)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21795)

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- The area is overwhelmingly Australian‑born; the most common overseas birthplace recorded is England (small single‑digit share of residents). [citation:2021 Mount Evelyn QuickStats (ABS)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21795)

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- Birmingham Primary partners with TheirCare for on‑site Outside School Hours Care: before school 7:00–8:45, after school 3:30–6:00, and vacation care 7:00–18:00 (book via TheirCare). [citation:TheirCare - Birmingham Primary School](https://birminghamps.vic.edu.au/theircare/)

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars
- Regular specialist and extracurricular programs include Music (performance/composition), Italian language classes, and a strong STEM program (plus weekly sport sessions for Years 3–6). [citation:Specialists - Birmingham Primary School](https://birminghamps.vic.edu.au/specialists/)

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Mount Evelyn feels like a small, leafy, family town with cafes on the rail‑trail, local parks/playgrounds and a main shopping strip that services families. The main supermarkets are Mt Evelyn IGA / Woolworths in the village and local specialty grocers such as Organic Empire for organic/health foods. [citation:Mt Evelyn IGA Supermarket (IGA)](https://www.iga.com.au/stores/mt-evelyn-iga-supermarket/) [citation:The Trail Cafe (family playground)](https://www.thetrailcafe.com/)
