**1) Suburb socio-economic profile**
Carlton North is an affluent, largely white-collar inner suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,400, well above the Victorian median, and the median age is 33, indicating a concentration of younger professionals and families [citation:2021 Carlton North, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20496). The suburb has a relatively low average household size (2.3 people) and 1,462 families, with an average of 1.6 children per family for those with children [citation:2021 Carlton North, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20496).

**8) Commute**
Carlton North does not have a train station. The most convenient public transport is tram route 96, which runs along Nicholson Street and connects to Flinders Street station in the CBD [citation:Carlton public transport - Travel Victoria: accommodation & visitor guide](https://www.travelvictoria.com.au/carlton/transport/). The tram journey to Flinders Street typically takes around 20–25 minutes.
