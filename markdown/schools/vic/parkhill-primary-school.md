1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Ashwood is a middle-to-upper-middle-income suburb with a strong family presence. The median weekly household income is $1,909, and the median age is 39 [citation:2021 Ashwood Census QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20078). The suburb has seen gentrification and an influx of young families and professionals attracted to its proximity to the city [citation:Ashwood Suburb Profile - CompoundInvest](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/ashwood/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Specific country of birth percentages for Ashwood are not available in the provided search results. However, the suburb is home to a significant number of overseas-born residents, reflecting its multicultural character.

3) **Working-parent support:** Parkhill Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by **TheirCare Pty Ltd**, operating as **Parkhill PS TheirCare**. Before School Care runs from **6:45 AM to 8:45 AM** and After School Care from **3:30 PM to 6:00 PM** on school days; vacation care is not offered [citation:ACECQA - Parkhill PS TheirCare](https://acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/parkhill-primary-school-oshclub).

4) **School culture:** Parkhill Primary School runs a strong performing arts program, including the **NEVR Youth Choir** (a 70-member strong choir that rehearses before school on Fridays and performs at venues like Hamer Hall) [citation:Our Performing Arts Extension Programs – Parkhill Primary School](https://parkhillps.vic.edu.au/our-performing-arts-programs/). The school also hosts an annual **Twilight Sports** evening with family-friendly activities and sprint races [citation:TWILIGHT SPORTS – Parkhill Primary School](https://parkhillps.vic.edu.au/twilight-sports/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Ashwood offers a family-friendly atmosphere with parks like Ashwood Reserve and Gardiners Creek Trail, plus a thriving café culture [citation:Ashwood Suburb Profile - CompoundInvest](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/ashwood/). For specialty groceries, **Original Indian Spices** (723 Warrigal Rd, Chadstone) stocks Indian/Pakistani/Sri Lankan products, and **Burwood Highway Asian Grocery** delivers Chinese, Korean, Japanese and other Asian groceries to the area [citation:Indian Grocery Stores Ashwood, VIC](https://singh.com.au/indian-grocery-stores-ashwood-vic/); [citation:Burwood Highway Asian Grocery: Home](https://groceryasia.com.au/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly casual dining options in Ashwood include **Banger Burger & Flake Eatery** (burgers), **Dough Pizzeria** (pizza), and **Ashwood Thai Cafe** (Thai cuisine) [citation:Ashwood Restaurants - AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/ashwood).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Local tutoring options include **Success Tutoring Ashwood**, which offers personalised learning support for primary and secondary students, and **Alchemy Tuition**, providing in-home and online tutoring in Ashwood since 2005 [citation:Ashwood – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-ashwood); [citation:Tutoring Ashwood // Alchemy Tuition](https://alchemytuition.com.au/melbourne-tutoring-ashwood/).

8) **Commute:** Ashwood is served by the **Glen Waverley line** via **Jordanville Station**. The train journey to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD takes approximately **29 minutes** [citation:Ashwood to Flinders Street Station - Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Ashwood/Flinders-Street-Station).
