1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Brighton is a highly affluent suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,710, and the median age is 48, indicating an older, established population [citation:2021 Brighton (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20337). Of households, 43.6% earn $3,000 or more per week, far exceeding the national average, and the suburb has a high proportion of professionals and managers, reflecting a white-collar workforce [citation:Household income | City of Bayside | Community profile](https://profile.id.com.au/bayside/household-income?WebID=120). The area has 6,406 families, with 0.7 children per household, suggesting a mix of families and older couples/singles [citation:2021 Brighton (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20337).

3) **Working-parent support:** Elsternwick Primary School’s Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided by TeamKids [citation:Elsternwick Primary | TeamKids](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/elsternwick-primary-oshc-6/). Specific operating hours for the program are not detailed in the available snippets, but TeamKids typically offers before-school care from 7:00am and after-school care until 6:00pm, plus vacation care [citation:Outside school hours care (OSHC)](https://australist-p.schools.nsw.gov.au/about-us/oosh). Contact the school or TeamKids directly for exact times and fees.

4) **School culture:** Elsternwick Primary School offers several named extracurriculars. The EPS Rock Band is open to Year 5/6 instrumentalists and vocalists, rehearsing weekly [citation:Extracurricular Activities - Elsternwick Primary School](https://elsternwickps.vic.edu.au/beyond-the-classroom/). The Choir is available for Year 3-6 students with weekly rehearsals, and Drama Club is held fortnightly for all students, led by Year 6 Performing Arts Captains [citation:Extracurricular Activities - Elsternwick Primary School](https://elsternwickps.vic.edu.au/beyond-the-classroom/). Students also participate in inter-school sports through the Balaclava District Primary Schools’ Sports Association [citation:Extracurricular Activities - Elsternwick Primary School](https://elsternwickps.vic.edu.au/beyond-the-classroom/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Brighton has a vibrant coastal village feel with boutique shopping along Church Street and Bay Street. For specialty groceries, Brighton Asian Grocery (341 Bay Street) offers Asian ingredients and is open weekdays 9am-7:30pm and weekends 10am-7pm [citation:Brighton Asian Grocery (@brighton.asiangrocery)](https://www.instagram.com/brighton.asiangrocery/?hl=en). Fresh Connection, on Were Street, is a local grocer supplying fresh produce, meat, and gourmet items from Australian farmers, with online delivery available [citation:Brighton Grocery Store — Fresh Connection](https://www.freshconnection.com.au/pages/brighton-grocery-store). There are also standard supermarkets like Woolworths Brighton.

6) **Weeknight dining:** Siriphen Thai Restaurant (on Bay Street) offers dine-in and takeaway Thai cuisine, open seven days a week [citation:Siriphen Thai Restaurant - Order Online](https://www.siriphenthai.com.au/). Indian Palace Restaurant is a well-regarded Indian option with takeaway service in Brighton [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Brighton - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g954016-zfp10601-Brighton_Bayside_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). Brighton Seafoods is a casual fish and chip shop on Bay Street, popular for family takeaway meals [citation:Restaurants in Brighton,VIC With Takeaway | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/brighton/takeaway-category).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Bayside Academics, based in Brighton, offers 1-on-1 tutoring for Prep to Year 12 across all subjects, with VCAA-aligned curriculum support [citation:Bayside Academics - Premium Tutoring in Brighton, VIC](https://baysideacademics.com.au/). Team Tutor Melbourne, located in nearby Moorabbin, provides flexible tutoring for primary through VCE levels, including subjects like Maths, English, and Chemistry, with options for home visits [citation:Team Tutor | Brighton Secondary College](https://brightonsc.vic.edu.au/project/team-tutor/).

8) **Commute:** Brighton is served by the Sandringham train line. The nearest station to Elsternwick Primary School is North Brighton Station, with trains to Flinders Street Station taking approximately 21 minutes [citation:North Brighton to Flinders Street Station - 6 ways to travel](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/North-Brighton-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). Trains run frequently, about every 20 minutes during peak times, and the one-way fare is around $4 [citation:Flinders Street Station to Brighton - 4 ways to travel](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Brighton-VIC-Australia).
