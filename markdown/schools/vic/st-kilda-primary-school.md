Here’s a compact, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for St Kilda Primary School (St Kilda, VIC). I followed ABS QuickStats and local sources; if a premise needed substituting I noted it (no train station → tram).

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Inner‑urban, seaside suburb with many young adults and high single/de‑facto households; median age 36 and a high share “never married” (65.8%). Median weekly household income $1,779 (2021). [citation:2021 St Kilda (Vic.) QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22343)

2) Most common overseas country of birth
- Majority Australian‑born (about 80%); the most common overseas birthplace is England (~9%). [citation:2021 St Kilda (Vic.) QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22343)

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)

[citation:St Kilda PS TheirCare (StartingBlocks listing)](https://www.startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01HGCPS4NDAWW0VGB0VCTWBH2Z/St-Kilda-PS-TheirCare)

8) Commute (train/tram)
- There is no suburban heavy‑rail “St Kilda” train station in regular Metro service; tram is the realistic public option. Tram route 96 (Acland/Fitzroy St area → city) provides a direct light‑rail/tram link to the CBD; typical travel time to Flinders Street is around 15–25 minutes depending on exact stop and traffic — allow ~20 minutes off‑peak.
