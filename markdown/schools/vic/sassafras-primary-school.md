1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Sassafras (Vic.) is an affluent, family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,147 [citation:QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22239). The median age is 47, and 64.0% of families are couples with children [citation:QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22239). Occupation data is not found in available sources, but the high income and family structure suggest a white-collar, professional demographic.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The suburb is overwhelmingly Australian-born. In the 2021 Census, 84.2% of residents were born in Australia [citation:QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22239). The most common overseas country of birth was England, at 3.6% [citation:QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22239).

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is available at Sassafras Primary School. The provider is TheirCare (indicated on the school's OSHC page) [citation:OSHC - Sassafras Primary School](http://www.sassafrasps.vic.edu.au/oshc.html). The program operates from 7:00am to 8:45am for before-school care and from 3:30pm to 6:00pm for after-school care [citation:Outside Hours School Care - Sassafras Primary School](https://sassafrasps.vic.edu.au/outside-hours-school-care).

4) **School culture:** Sassafras Primary School offers several named extracurricular activities. Students can participate in the school choir, which performs at the Dandenong Ranges RSL Anzac Day commemorations and CFA Carols on the Mountain [citation:Curriculum & Subjects - Sassafras Primary School](https://sassafrasps.vic.edu.au/curriculum-and-subjects). The school also produces a biannual school production at the Burrinja Cultural Centre, with past performances including *Wizard of Oz* [citation:Curriculum & Subjects - Sassafras Primary School](https://sassafrasps.vic.edu.au/curriculum-and-subjects).
