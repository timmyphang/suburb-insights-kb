Below is a short, family-facing briefing for Northern Bay P‑12 College (Corio). I used ABS QuickStats and local sources; each fact has a source citation. I kept items short and numeric where possible.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 lines)
- Corio is lower‑to‑middle income with a relatively young population (median age 35). Median weekly household income ≈ $1,152. [citation:2021 Corio QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20640)

[citation:BehindTheFence Corio profile](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/corio) [citation:2021 Corio QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20640)

8) Commute — train time to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)
- Corio has a V/Line station on the Geelong line. [citation:Rome2Rio — Corio to Melbourne train times](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Corio/Melbourne) [citation:Transit App (Geelong–Melbourne V/Line info)](https://transitapp.com/en/region/melbourne/v-line/commuter-rail-geelong-melbourne)
