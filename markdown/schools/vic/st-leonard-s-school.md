1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Glen Waverley is an affluent, white-collar suburb with a strong family focus. The median weekly household income is $1,918, and the median age is 40 [citation:2021 Glen Waverley, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21013). 78.3% of households are families, and only 35% of residents speak only English at home, reflecting the area's high diversity [citation:Who Lives in Glen Waverley? | Microburbs Research](https://www.microburbs.com.au/People-Lifestyle/Glen-Waverley). Employment is predominantly white-collar, with stated median weekly income slightly above the national average [citation:GLEN WAVERLEY suburb profile](https://landchecker.com.au/suburb/glen-waverley-vic-3150/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas country of birth in Glen Waverley is China (excluding Taiwan and SARs). According to the 2021 ABS QuickStats for the suburb, China-born residents account for 14.4% of the population [citation:2021 Glen Waverley, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21013). Additionally, 65.0% of residents were born in Australia [citation:2021 Glen Waverley, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21013).

3) **Working-parent support:** St Leonard's School offers an Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) program operated by Camp Australia on the school premises [citation:Out of Hours Care - St Leonards Catholic Primary School](https://www.leonardsgwav.catholic.edu.au/school-community/out-of-hours-care/). Before-school care runs from 7:00 AM to 8:35 AM, and after-school care operates from 3:15 PM to 6:15 PM, Monday to Friday during school terms [citation:St Leonard's OSHC, Glen Waverley | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/st-leonards-oshc-glen-waverley). The service holds a 'Meeting NQS' rating from ACECQA as of May 2024 [citation:St Leonard's OSHC, Glen Waverley | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/st-leonards-oshc-glen-waverley).

4) **School culture:** St Leonard's offers a range of named extracurricular activities. The school runs a **School Choir** for students in Years 1-6, which performs weekly at school and community events [citation:Other Learning Opportunities - St Leonards Catholic Primary School](https://www.leonardsgwav.catholic.edu.au/learning-teaching/other-learning-opportunities/). Students can also join the **Chess Club**, which includes a major internal tournament and selected students representing the school at Victorian Chess Titles [citation:Other Learning Opportunities - St Leonards Catholic Primary School](https://www.leonardsgwav.catholic.edu.au/learning-teaching/other-learning-opportunities/). A **Seasons Program** is available to support children experiencing grief or loss [citation:Other Learning Opportunities - St Leonards Catholic Primary School](https://www.leonardsgwav.catholic.edu.au/learning-teaching/other-learning-opportunities/). The school also hosts a biennial **Performing Arts Musical Concert** and **Art Show** [citation:Specialist Subjects & Intervention Support](https://www.leonardsgwav.catholic.edu.au/learning-teaching/specialist-subjects-intervention-support/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Glen Waverley is a vibrant, family-friendly suburb anchored by **The Glen** shopping centre, which houses major supermarkets like **Coles**, **Woolworths**, and **ALDI**, along with specialty providers such as **Colonial Fresh Markets** [citation:Supermarkets & Fresh Food - The Glen](https://www.theglen.com.au/shopping/supermarkets-fresh-food). For ethnic groceries, **The Glen Asian Grocery** offers a large selection of fresh Asian fruits, vegetables, and condiments [citation:The Glen Asian Grocery - The Glen](https://www.theglen.com.au/stores/the-glen-asian-grocery). **Astee Asian Groceries & Butcher** on Railway Parade is another well-stocked specialty option [citation:ASTEE ASIAN GROCERIES & BUTCHER - Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/astee-asian-groceries-and-butcher-glen-waverley).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family-friendly dining, **Thai Garden Restaurant & Takeaway** offers authentic Thai food on High Street Road [citation:Thai Garden Restaurant & Takeaway - Order Online](https://thaigardenglenwaverley.com.au/). **Scusa Mi Italiano** on Springvale Road serves Italian cuisine in a lively setting [citation:Restaurants in Glen Waverley,VIC With Takeaway | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/glen-waverley/takeaway-category). **Meze Restaurant & Bar** on Springvale Road provides a modern take on Middle Eastern and Indian classics [citation:Restaurants in Glen Waverley,VIC With Takeaway | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/glen-waverley/takeaway-category).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **NumberWorks'nWords** at 191 Coleman Parade offers specialised tutoring in maths and English for children aged 5-16 [citation:Tutoring in Glen Waverley | NumberWorks'nWords](https://numberworksnwords.com/au/our-locations/australia/glen-waverley). **BT Education** has a campus on the Dining Terrace at The Glen Shopping Centre, providing enrichment and scholarship preparation for Years 3-10, plus VCE and IB subjects [citation:GLEN WAVERLEY | Bteducation](https://bt-education.com/glen-waverley/).

8) **Commute:** St Leonard's School is located near Glen Waverley Station, the terminus of the **Glen Waverley line**. The train travel time from Glen Waverley Station to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD is approximately **35 minutes** [citation:Metro Glen Waverley Line - Transit.app](https://transitapp.com/en/region/melbourne/metro/commuter-rail-glen-waverley). Trains run frequently, approximately every 20 minutes during peak times [citation:Glen Waverley to Flinders Street Station - Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Glen-Waverley/Flinders-Street-Station).
