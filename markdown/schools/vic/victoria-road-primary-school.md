The area has 4,889 families and an average of 1.8 children per family (for families with children), indicating a strong family presence. The suburb is predominantly residential with a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers, though specific occupational data was not provided in the available sources.

For grocery shopping, there is a **Coles Lilydale Village** supermarket, and for Asian/ethnic groceries, families can visit **Haodi Asian Supermarket** (Boronia) or **Moy Asian Grocery** (Mooroolbark), both a short drive away [citation:TOP 10 BEST Asian Supermarket near Lilydale Victoria 3140](https://www.yelp.com.au/search?find_desc=asian+supermarket&find_loc=Lilydale+Victoria+3140). Other convenient picks include **La Porchetta** for Italian and **Yarra Valley Fish & Chips** for classic takeaway [citation:THE BEST 10 Take Away Food in Lilydale, Victoria](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/victoria/lilydale/food-and-dining/take-away-food/).
