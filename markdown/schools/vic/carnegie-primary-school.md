1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Carnegie is middle‑income with many young professionals and a strong 25–39 age cohort (median age 36); household incomes sit above many Melbourne middle‑ring suburbs (median weekly household income reported ~$1,878) [citation:2021 Carnegie, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20498). The area mixes white‑collar professionals and singles/young families rather than heavy industry — a lively retail strip and commuter catchment dominate local life [citation:Carnegie VIC Suburb Profile — Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/carnegie).

2) Most common overseas country of birth  
India is the most common overseas birthplace in Carnegie (about 9.2% of residents); just over half of residents were born in Australia (≈53.7%) [citation:Carnegie VIC Suburb Profile — Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/carnegie).
