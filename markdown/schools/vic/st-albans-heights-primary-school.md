1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** St Albans is a working-class, blue-collar suburb with a mix of families and singles. The median weekly household income is $1,205, significantly below the Victorian median, and the median age is 36 [citation:2021 St Albans (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22330). The population includes a high proportion of residents employed in manufacturing and trades.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas country of birth in St Albans is Vietnam, with 16.1% of residents born there [citation:2021 St Albans (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22330). The suburb is highly multicultural, with only 28.1% of residents born in Australia.

3) **Working-parent support:** St Albans Heights Primary School provides Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) through CommunityOSH [citation:St Albans Heights Primary School | communityosh](https://www.commosh.edu.au/stalbansheightsps). Before School Care runs from 7:00 am to 9:00 am, and After School Care runs from 3:15 pm to 6:00 pm. The service is located at the school, 1 Norwich St, St Albans.

4) **School culture:** The school offers the **Stephanie Alexander Kitchen Garden Program** for all Grade 3/4 students, where children grow, harvest, and prepare food from the school’s garden [citation:Additional School Programs](https://mail.sahps.vic.edu.au/additionalschool.html). The **Song Room** program provides music and singing sessions with a teaching artist for students in Years 1/2 and 5/6, culminating in a professionally recorded album [citation:The Song Room](https://mail.sahps.vic.edu.au/thesongroom.html). The **Play & Say** program supports Foundation to Grade 2 students with language-based inquiry learning [citation:Additional School Programs](https://mail.sahps.vic.edu.au/additionalschool.html).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** St Albans has a vibrant, multicultural town centre with many Asian and ethnic grocers. **Pahal Grocers** (282 Main Road East) specialises in South Asian, Sri Lankan, and Indian groceries, while **KP Asian Grocery** (12 Alfrieda Street) offers Korean, Japanese, and Vietnamese products [citation:Pahal Grocers](https://www.stalbanstowncentre.com.au/news-stories/pahal-grocers) [citation:KP Asian Grocery](https://www.stalbanstowncentre.com.au/directory/kp-asian-grocery). **KFL Supermarket** (330 Main Road East) is a large Asian supermarket chain popular for fresh produce and pantry staples [citation:St Albans KFL](https://www.kflsupermarkets.com.au/st-albans-vic).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Families can enjoy casual meals at **Phi Phi Vietnamese & Chinese Restaurant**, a popular spot on Main Road East for affordable pho and stir-fries [citation:THE 10 BEST Restaurants in St Albans](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g4358126-St_Albans_Brimbank_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). **Pho Kim Long** is a well-reviewed Vietnamese restaurant serving hearty noodle soups [citation:THE 10 BEST Restaurants in St Albans](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g4358126-St_Albans_Brimbank_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). **Lasang Pinoy** offers Filipino takeaway and dine-in options, including popular dishes like crispy pata [citation:THE 10 BEST Restaurants in St Albans](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g4358126-St_Albans_Brimbank_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Innate Tutoring** (serving St Albans) provides personalised VCE tutoring in subjects like English, Maths, Biology, and Chemistry, with both online and in-person options [citation:Tutoring St Albans | VCE Tutors](https://www.innatetutoring.com.au/st-albans-tutoring/). **Success Tutoring St Albans** offers K-12 tutoring with customised learning plans and flexible session bookings at local study hubs [citation:St Albans – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-st-albans).

8) **Commute:** St Albans Station is on the **Sunbury train line**, with direct services to Flinders Street Station. The average train journey from St Albans Station to Flinders Street Station takes approximately **31 minutes**, with trains running every 15 minutes [citation:Train St Albans Station to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/St-Albans-Station/Flinders-Street-Station).
