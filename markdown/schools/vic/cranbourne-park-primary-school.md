1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Cranbourne is a middle-to-working-class suburb with a strong family orientation. According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income is $1,477, and the median age is 35, with children aged 0–14 making up over 20% of the population [citation:2021 Cranbourne QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20662). The suburb has a mix of blue-collar and white-collar workers, and the overall population density is 1,366 persons per square km [citation:About the profile areas | City of Casey](https://profile.id.com.au/casey/about?WebID=160).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The most common overseas country of birth for Cranbourne residents is not specified in the available data; however, the suburb is culturally diverse. In the 2021 Census, 68.2% of residents were born in Australia, though a detailed breakdown of specific overseas birthplaces is not provided in the search results [citation:2021 Cranbourne QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20662). For a more precise figure, please consult the full Community Profile tables on the ABS website.

3) **Working-parent support**: Cranbourne Park Primary School operates its own Out of School Hours Care (OSHC) program on-site. Before School Care (Breakfast Club) runs from 6:45am to 8:30am, and After School Care runs from 3:15pm to 6:00pm, including pupil-free days and vacation care [citation:Out of School Hours Care](https://www.cranbourne-ps.vic.edu.au/page/409/Out-of-School-Hours-Care). The program is school-operated, meaning it is affordable and staffed by familiar educators.

4) **School culture**: Cranbourne Park Primary School offers several named extracurricular activities, including a **Colour Run** fundraising event, **Junior Tabloid Sports** (a multi-sport carnival), and a **Harmony Day** whole-school incursion [citation:Front page events](http://www.cranbournepark.vic.edu.au/articles/9). These events foster community spirit and physical activity, complementing the school’s focus on wellbeing and inclusion.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Cranbourne has a family-friendly feel with several shopping centres, including Cranbourne Park Shopping Centre and Parkmore Shopping Centre. For specialty groceries, families can shop at **Wirsa Indian Grocery** (Indian and Asian foods) [citation:Indian flavours at Our Cranbourne Grocery Store](https://wirsa.com.au/indian-grocery-store-cranbourne/), **Mrs Wans Asian Mart** (Malaysian, Chinese, Vietnamese, and more) [citation:Mrs Wans Asian Mart](https://mrswans.com.au/), and **All Asia Grocery** at Parkmore Shopping Centre [citation:All Asia Grocery Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/allasiagrocery/). These stores offer a wide range of authentic ingredients.

6) **Weeknight dining**: For casual family-friendly takeaway, **Thai Cottage** (Thai cuisine) is a popular choice with good reviews for its red curry and pad Thai [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g552156-zfp10601-Cranbourne_City_of_Casey_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). **Ajanta Indian Restaurant** offers reliable Indian dishes, and **Dumpling De' Chef** (Chinese) is loved for its dumplings and satay skewers [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g552156-zfp10601-Cranbourne_City_of_Casey_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). Another option is **Don Vittorio's Pizzeria** for pizza takeaway [citation:Restaurants in Cranbourne with Takeaway - AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/cranbourne/takeaway-category).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: **Stem Academic College** in Cranbourne provides tutoring from Year 1 to VCE, covering English, maths, science, and selective school exam preparation [citation:Tuition Centre Cranbourne - Stem Academic College](https://stemacademiccollege.com.au/). **Edu Kingdom College Cranbourne West** offers regular tuition, scholarship preparation, and selective school test preparation for Years 1–12 [citation:Tutoring Centre Cranbourne](https://www.tutoringcentre.net/). Both centres are local and well-regarded.

8) **Commute**: Cranbourne is served by the **Cranbourne Line** on the Metro Trains network. The nearest station is **Cranbourne Station**, and the typical train travel time to **Flinders Street Station** in the Melbourne CBD is approximately **1 hour 6 minutes** [citation:Train Cranbourne Station to Flinders Street Station - Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Cranbourne-Station/Flinders-Street-Station). Trains run every 30 minutes during peak times [citation:Flinders Street Station to Cranbourne Station - Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Cranbourne-Station).
