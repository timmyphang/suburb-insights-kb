Here’s a compact, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Coatesville Primary School (Bentleigh East). I used ABS 2021 QuickStats and local sources where possible; each fact has a short citation.

1) Suburb socio-economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Bentleigh East is a mostly established, middle-to-upper‑middle suburb with a median age of 40 and a strong family presence (high share of married households). Median weekly household income is about $2,215 — above the Victoria median — which points to a relatively comfortable, mostly white‑collar residential profile. [citation:2021 Bentleigh East QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20215) [citation:2021 Victoria QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/2)

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- The largest overseas birthplace after Australia in Bentleigh East is China (reported as the top overseas birthplace in local 2021 summaries; third‑party suburb profiles list China ≈5–6% of residents). 3) Working‑parent support — OSHC
- Coatesville Primary runs an on‑site Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) operated by TeamKids: before school, after school and vacation‑care programs are available (TeamKids registration required). [citation:TeamKids — Coatesville Primary OSHC](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/coatesville-primary-oshc-6/) [citation:Coatesville Primary OSHC page](https://www.coatesps.vic.edu.au/page/179/Outside-Hours-School-Care-%28OSHC%29---TeamKids)

4) School culture — 2–3 specific extracurriculars
- After‑school sport programs via SoccerWise / SportWise (multi‑sport sessions, Prep–6). [citation:Extra‑Curricular Activities — Coatesville PS](https://www.coatesps.vic.edu.au/page/206/Extra-Curricular-Activities)
- Music program with classroom music, instrumental lessons (drums, guitar, keyboard) and junior/senior choir; early‑years ukulele/recorder activities are also part of the program. [citation:Music — Coatesville PS](https://www.coatesps.vic.edu.au/page/173/Music)

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Bentleigh East feels family‑oriented with parks, local primary schools and neighbourhood shopping strips — a settled middle‑income suburb popular with families. Key local grocery options include a Ritchies Bentleigh East supermarket and Friendly Grocer / Mackie Road Supermarket, plus smaller premium greengrocers (Monet’s Grocer) and nearby Asian grocers on the Centre Rd / Glen Huntly Rd corridor. [citation:2021 Bentleigh East QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20215) [citation:Ritchies Bentleigh East](https://bentleigh-east.shop.ritchies.com.au/) [citation:Friendly Grocer Bentleigh East](https://friendlygrocer.com.au/friendly-grocer-bentleigh-east) [citation:Monet's Grocer](https://www.monetsgrocer.com.au/)

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 casual family options & cuisines
- Bentleigh Fish & Chips / Salt Water Fish & Chips — classic fish & chips takeaway for families. [citation:Bentleigh Fish and Chips menu](https://bentleighfishandchips.com.au/menu) [citation:Salt Water Fish & Chips listing](https://restaurantguru.com/Salt-Water-Fish-and-Chips-Bentleigh-East)
- Little Thai Cafe — local, family‑friendly Thai takeaway/dine‑in for curries and noodle dishes. [citation:Little Thai Cafe menu](https://little-thai-cafe.menustic.net/menu)

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 local options)
- Mathnasium (Bentleigh East) — local maths tuition centre offering programs for school students. [citation:Mathnasium Bentleigh East](https://www.mathnasium.com/au/maths-centres/bentleigheast)
- Kumon Bentleigh East — maths and English Kumon centre nearby. [citation:Kumon centre listing](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-BENTLEIGH-EAST-VIC)

8) Commute — train time to CBD
- Bentleigh East does not have its own train station; the nearest station is Bentleigh (Frankston line). Typical train time from Bentleigh Station to Flinders Street / CBD is about 25–30 minutes depending on service and stops (roughly ~28 minutes on common journey estimates). [citation:Bentleigh station — Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/bentleigh/) [citation:Bentleigh East to Flinders Street travel estimate (Rome2rio)](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Bentleigh-East/Flinders-Street-Station)
