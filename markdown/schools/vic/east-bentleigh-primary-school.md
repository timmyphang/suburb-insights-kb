1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Bentleigh East is a middle-to-upper-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,215 (2021 Census) and a median age of 40 [citation:2021 Bentleigh East, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20215). The suburb has 8,496 families and a population of 30,159, with many drawn by its family-friendly atmosphere and strong school zones [citation:Bentleigh East, VIC 3165 Suburb Profile | Buyers Agency](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/bentleigh-east/). While a detailed white/blue-collar breakdown is not in available sources, the income and housing data suggest a mix of professionals and tradespeople undergoing steady gentrification.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The leading overseas country of birth for Bentleigh East residents was not specified in the provided census extracts. In 2016, 36.0% of people had both parents born in Australia and 46.7% had both parents born overseas, but the most common individual country is not listed in the available 2021 QuickStats data [citation:2016 Bentleigh East, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20214). For the most recent figures, refer directly to the ABS 2021 QuickStats page.

3) **Working-parent support:** East Bentleigh Primary School’s Outside School Hours Care is provided by TheirCare Pty Ltd. Before-school care operates Monday–Friday from 7:00 to 8:45 am, after-school care from 3:30 to 6:00 pm, and vacation care from 7:00 am to 6:00 pm [citation:East Bentleigh PS TheirCare | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/camp-australia-east-bentleigh-primary-school-oshc). The service is approved for 60 children and holds a Meeting National Quality Standard rating.

4) **School culture:** East Bentleigh Primary School offers a String Music program with individual lessons, orchestras, and ensembles, compulsory for Steiner Stream students in Years 3–6 [citation:String Music](https://www.eastbentleighps.vic.edu.au/page/16/String-Music). The school participates in Interschool Sports including swimming, cross-country, athletics, and Friday morning summer/winter sports [citation:Interschool Sports](https://www.eastbentleighps.vic.edu.au/page/19/Interschool-Sports). A Choir also performs at festivals and in the community [citation:Growing Together](https://www.eastbentleighps.vic.edu.au/page/7/Growing-Together).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Bentleigh East has a family-friendly feel with several reserves such as Centenary Park and Duncan Mackinnon Reserve for outdoor activities [citation:Bentleigh East, VIC 3165 Suburb Profile | Buyers Agency](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/bentleigh-east/). Grocery shopping is served by multiple supermarkets including Ritchies Supermarket (10–16 Heather Street), IGA, and Coles [citation:TOP 10 BEST Grocery Store near Bentleigh East](https://www.yelp.com/search?find_desc=Grocery+Store&find_loc=Bentleigh+East+Victoria+3165). For Asian and Indian specialities, locals can visit Chen Oriental Grocery on Centre Road in Bentleigh or Raj Kamal Enterprise in nearby Moorabbin [citation:Chen Oriental Grocery](https://www.whitepages.com.au/chen-oriental-grocery-11670965/bentleigh-vic-11670963B); [citation:Indian Grocery Stores Bentleigh](https://singh.com.au/indian-grocery-stores-bentleigh-vic/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Specific family-friendly restaurants physically located in Bentleigh East were not named in the available search results. Many takeaway and delivery options are accessible via Uber Eats from neighbouring suburbs, including Royal Stacks (burgers) in Moorabbin and Guzman y Gomez (Mexican) in Malvern East [citation:Takeaway in Bentleigh East - Uber Eats](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/bentleigh-east-melbourne-vic). For a broader list of kid-friendly eateries in the area, check OpenTable’s Bentleigh East guide.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Success Tutoring Bentleigh East offers personalised in-centre and online programs for primary and secondary students [citation:Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-bentleigh-east). Kumon Australia has a centre at 142–144 Drummond Street, Oakleigh, providing maths and English enrichment [citation:Yellow Pages Tuition Tutoring](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/tuition-tutoring/bentleigh-east-vic-3165). Cloud Tuition also services Bentleigh East with online tutoring for Years 2–12 [citation:Cloud Tuition](https://www.cloudtuition.com.au/locations/tutoring-vic/bentleigh-east).

8) **Commute:** The nearest train station is Bentleigh on the Frankston line, about 2 km from much of Bentleigh East. The train journey from Bentleigh Station to Flinders Street Station takes approximately 27–36 minutes, with services running every 15 minutes during peak times [citation:Train Bentleigh to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Bentleigh/Flinders-Street-Station). Bus route 703 also connects the area to Middle Brighton Station and the city.
