### 1) Suburb socio-economic profile
Narre Warren is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb. According to the 2021 Census, Narre Warren had a median weekly household income of $1,741 and a median age of 35, with families making up the largest household type [citation:2021 Narre Warren QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21893). The southern part of the suburb (Narre Warren South) had a slightly higher median income of $2,158 and a younger median age of 34, with 2.1 children per family on average, reflecting a mix of white-collar and blue-collar occupations [citation:2021 Narre Warren South QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21896).

### 2) Most common foreign country of birth
In the suburb of Narre Warren (SA2 level), the 2021 Census data for the broader Narre Warren area indicates that 58.5% of residents were born in Australia (not yet available as a single-source figure for the suburb itself; the closest available data for Narre Warren South shows 68.3% Australian-born) [citation:2021 Narre Warren South QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21896). The most common overseas country of birth for Narre Warren South is India (6.6%), followed by Sri Lanka (5.5%) [citation:2021 Narre Warren South QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21896). Data for the exact suburb of Narre Warren is missing in available sources; please check the ABS QuickStats link directly for the suburb.

### 3) Working-parent support
Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) at Maramba Primary School is provided by TheirCare (operating under Camp Australia) at 9 Maramba Drive, Narre Warren. The service offers Before School Care, After School Care, and Vacation Care, though specific hours are not listed in the available sources [citation:Maramba PS TheirCare on CareforKids](https://careforkids.com.au/child-care/po8jw/camp-australia-maramba-primary-school-oshc-narre-warren-3805). For exact opening and closing times, contact TheirCare directly via the CareforKids listing.

### 4) School culture
Maramba Primary School offers several named extracurricular programs. These include the **Maramba Performing Arts Concert (MPAC)** each term, which showcases music, dance, and drama [citation:Performing Arts - Maramba Primary School](https://www.maramba-ps.vic.edu.au/specialists-programs/performing-arts/). The school also runs after-school programs such as **Auskick** and **softball**, alongside participation in the **Berwick District Lightning Premiership** for sports like netball, AFL, basketball, and athletics [citation:Maramba Primary School Homepage](https://www.maramba-ps.vic.edu.au/). Additionally, there is a **School Chaplaincy Program** that includes an 8-week **SPARC program** for small-group student wellbeing [citation:School Chaplaincy Program](https://www.maramba-ps.vic.edu.au/student-wellbeing/school-chaplaincy-program/).

### 7) Tuition and enrichment
In Narre Warren, **Success Tutoring** offers personalised learning support with 24/7 access to resources, focusing on motivating students [citation:Success Tutoring Narre Warren](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-narre-warren). Another option is **Edu-Kingdom** at its Narre Warren branch, providing in-person maths and English tutoring for Years 1 to 12, with a free assessment [citation:Maths Tutor Narre Warren - Edu-Kingdom](https://edukingdom.com.au/cm-maths-tutor-narre-warren/). Additionally, **Contour Education** has a campus in Narre Warren near the train station, specialising in VCE and high school tutoring [citation:Contour Education Narre Warren](https://www.contoureducation.com.au/where-we-tutor/narre-warren/).

### 8) Commute
Narre Warren is served by **Narre Warren Station** on the **Pakenham line** of the Metro Trains network. The average train travel time from Narre Warren Station to Flinders Street Station is **53 minutes**, with trains running every 20 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio Train Narre Warren to Flinders Street](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Narre-Warren/Flinders-Street-Station). The service is direct, with no transfers required, and operates daily [citation:Metro Trains Narre Warren Station](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/narre-warren/).
