1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Kensington is a relatively affluent, white-collar, inner-city suburb with a young professional profile. The median weekly household income is $2,216, and the median age is 35 [citation:2021 Kensington (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21327). The area has a high proportion of singles and couples without children, with an average household size of just 2.1 people [citation:2021 Kensington (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21327).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Kensington is predominantly Australian-born, with 86.8% of residents born in Australia [citation:Kensington VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/kensington-vic). The most common overseas country of birth is England, accounting for 4.7% of the population [citation:Kensington VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/kensington-vic).

3) **Working-parent support:** Holy Rosary School’s Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided by TeamKids [citation:Outside School Hours Care – Holy Rosary School](https://www.hrkensington.catholic.edu.au/school-community/outside-school-hours-care/). Before School Care runs from 6:30 am to 8:45 am, and After School Care runs from 3:20 pm to 6:30 pm [citation:Holy Rosary School - Kensington | TeamKids](https://teamkids.com.au/venues/holy-rosary-school-oshc/).

4) **School culture:** Holy Rosary offers several named extracurriculars including the **Concert Band**, **Senior Choir**, and **5/6 Rock Band** [citation:Specialist Programs – Holy Rosary School](https://www.hrkensington.catholic.edu.au/learning-teaching/education-and-learning/). Students in Years 5 and 6 can also participate in **Summer and Winter Interschool Sport** [citation:Additional Learning Opportunities – Holy Rosary School](https://www.hrkensington.catholic.edu.au/learning-teaching/additional-learning-opportunities/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Kensington has a village feel with a mix of cafes, parks, and historic housing, popular with young families and professionals. For groceries, the suburb has a **Woolworths** on Bellair Street and a **Kensington IGA** on Canterbury Road [citation:28 BEST local Supermarkets & Grocery Stores in Kensington, VIC | Yellow Pages®](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/kensington-vic-3031). Specialty options include the **Boccaccio Cellars & Foodstore** for Italian and gourmet goods, also on Bellair Street.

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family takeaway, **Pimm Thai Cafe** on Bellair Street serves Thai cuisine [citation:Pimm Thai Cafe Kensington - Takeaway food - Kensington - Order online](https://www.pimmthaicafe.com.au/). Another popular option is **D’Lish Fish** on Epsom Road for fish and chips. For pizza, **Napoli Pizza** on Bellair Street is a local favourite.
