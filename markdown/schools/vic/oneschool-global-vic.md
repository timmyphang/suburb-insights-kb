Below is a concise, snippet-first eight‑point briefing for families considering OneSchool Global Vic — Melton (Melton West). Each item gives the requested fact(s) with source citations.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Melton West has a median weekly household income of $1,450 (lower than Victoria’s $1,759), a median age of 36 and a high share of families/children — overall a lower‑to‑middle income, family‑oriented suburb rather than an affluent area [citation:2021 Melton West, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21644).  
- Local council profiles also present Melton West as a growth‑area suburb used by families and service planning (SEIFA/socio‑economic indicators show relative disadvantage versus the state average) [citation:Draft | Community Profile - Melton West (City of Melton)](https://www.melton.vic.gov.au/files/assets/public/v/1/council/about-the-city/demographics/community-profiles/community-profile-2023-infographics/community-profile-melton-west.pdf).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- About 26.5% of Melton West residents were born overseas; the largest single overseas birthplace shown in local council infographics is the United Kingdom (UK) at ~3.4% — so the UK is the suburb’s most common foreign country of birth (no single large overseas community dominates) [citation:Draft | Community Profile - Melton West (City of Melton)](https://www.melton.vic.gov.au/files/assets/public/v/1/council/about-the-city/demographics/community-profiles/community-profile-2023-infographics/community-profile-melton-west.pdf).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Melton West / High Street precinct is family‑focused with a major shopping strip and Woodgrove/High Street retail — convenient for families (supermarkets, takeaways, parks and community services are concentrated on High Street) [citation:Draft | Community Profile - Melton West (City of Melton)](https://www.melton.vic.gov.au/files/assets/public/v/1/council/about-the-city/demographics/community-profiles/community-profile-melton-west.pdf).  
- Local grocery options include mainstream supermarkets (Woolworths on High Street; IGA in the High Street precinct) and ethnic/specialty shops such as MGS High Street Indian Wholesale and Melton International Grocery nearby [citation:Woolworths Supermarket location in Melton West (High St)](https://woolworths.supermarketlocationmaps.com/en/location/21907-woolies-533-555-high-street) [citation:MGS High Street Indian Wholesale Supermarket (listing)](https://www.everythingindian.com.au/mgs-high-street-indian-wholesale-supermarket) [citation:Melton International Grocery (local listing)](https://www.dlook.com.au/business/supermarket-in-melton-west-vic-3337/melton-international-grocery).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 casual family‑friendly takeaway/dining options

- La Porchetta (Woodgrove / High Street area) — family pizza/Italian dining and takeaway [citation:La Porchetta Melton (Woodgrove)](https://laporchetta.com.au/melton/).

8) Commute — train travel time to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)
- Melton West does not have its own train station; the nearest heavy‑rail station is Melton railway station (served by V/Line regional services). I’m substituting Melton station for “Melton West station.” [citation:Public Transport - Melton City Council / Melton Railway Station (Transport Victoria)](https://www.melton.vic.gov.au/Services/Building-Planning-Transport/Roads-and-traffic/Public-Transport) [citation:Melton Railway Station - Transport Victoria (stop page)](https://transport.vic.gov.au/stop/1121/melton-station/3/regional-train).  
- Typical V/Line train journey from Melton to Melbourne CBD (Southern Cross) is about 40–50 minutes depending on service; you would usually arrive at Southern Cross and then change/travel a few minutes to Flinders Street (or walk/transfer via the free city loop) [citation:V/Line — Timetables / plan trip (V/Line)](https://www.vline.com.au/Timetables/Train-coach-timetables?loc=Melbourne%2C+Southern+Cross) [citation:Melton to Southern Cross — Rome2rio summary of options (typical durations)](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Melton/Southern-Cross-Station-VIC-Australia).
