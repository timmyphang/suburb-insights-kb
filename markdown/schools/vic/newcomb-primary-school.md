1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Newcomb is a working-to-middle-class suburb of Geelong with a mix of families and older residents. According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income is $1,148, below the Victorian median of $1,759, and the median age is 40 [citation:2021 Newcomb, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21925). Housing is predominantly detached mid-20th-century dwellings, with a growing number of first-home buyers [citation:NEWCOMB VIC, AU SUBURB PROFILE - FREE REPORT - Smart Property Investment](https://www.smartpropertyinvestment.com.au/data/vic/3219/newcomb).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Newcomb’s population is overwhelmingly Australian-born. According to the 2021 Census, 84.4% of residents were born in Australia [citation:2021 Newcomb, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21925). The most common overseas country of birth is England at 2.4% [citation:2021 Newcomb, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21925).

3) **Working-parent support:** Newcomb Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care through Kelly Club, operating on the school site. Before-school care runs from 7:00 am to 8:30 am, and after-school care runs from 3:15 pm to 6:00 pm on school days [citation:Kelly Club - Before and After School Care](https://www.newcombps.vic.edu.au/page/105/Kelly-Club---Before-and-After-School-Care). The service is a CCS-approved provider, so eligible families can receive a Child Care Subsidy [citation:Kelly Club - Before and After School Care](https://www.newcombps.vic.edu.au/page/105/Kelly-Club---Before-and-After-School-Care).

4) **School culture:** Newcomb Primary School runs the **Hands On Learning** program in partnership with Save the Children, a practical program focused on student wellbeing and engagement through hands-on projects [citation:Newcomb Primary School](https://www.newcombps.vic.edu.au/). The school has achieved **Gold status in School Wide Positive Behaviour Support (SWPBS)** for two consecutive years (2023 and 2024), emphasising respect, responsibility, and resilience [citation:Newcomb Primary School](https://www.newcombps.vic.edu.au/). Kelly Club’s OSHC program also provides cooking, arts and crafts, and sports and games activities [citation:Kelly Club - Before and After School Care](https://www.newcombps.vic.edu.au/page/105/Kelly-Club---Before-and-After-School-Care).
