1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Wattle Glen is an affluent, family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,425, well above the national median [citation:2021 Wattle Glen QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22727). The median age is 42, and there are 540 families among 1,911 residents, indicating a mature, family-focussed population [citation:2021 Wattle Glen QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22727). Housing is almost entirely separate houses (95.0%), typical of an established middle-to-upper-income area [citation:AreaSearch](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/wattle_glen_diamond_creek).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Wattle Glen is overwhelmingly Australian-born. In 2021, 63.2% of people had both parents born in Australia, and 19.0% had both parents born overseas [citation:2016 Wattle Glen QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22714). The most common overseas country of birth for the broader Wattle Glen – Diamond Creek SA2 is not specified in available sources, but the area is predominantly Anglo-Australian.

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided at Wattle Glen Primary School by Camp Australia. The program operates before school from 7:00am to 8:00am and after school until approximately 6:00pm (exact closing time not specified in sources) [citation:Camp Australia OSHC](https://www.wattlegps.vic.edu.au/camp-australia-ohsc)[citation:KindiCare](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3096/wattle-glen/camp-australia-wattle-glen-primary-school-oshc). Pupil-free day and school holiday programs are also available through Camp Australia [citation:Camp Australia School Page](https://campaustralia.com.au/school/?accessCode=F47D).

4) **School culture:** Wattle Glen Primary School offers a Kitchen Garden program, where students grow and cook fresh produce [citation:Wattle Glen Primary School website](https://www.wattlegps.vic.edu.au/). The school also provides instrumental music lessons (e.g., guitar, keyboard) and participates in inter-school sport competitions (specific sport names not listed in provided sources) [citation:Wattle Glen Primary School website](https://www.wattlegps.vic.edu.au/). Additionally, a Breakfast Club runs weekly to support student wellbeing [citation:Wattle Glen Primary School website](https://www.wattlegps.vic.edu.au/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Wattle Glen is a leafy, quiet suburb with a strong community feel, centred on the Diamond Creek corridor. For groceries, residents shop at the Diamond Creek Shopping Centre (2 km away), which houses Coles and Woolworths [citation:Diamond Creek Shopping Centre](https://www.diamondcreekshopping.com.au/). Specialty options include The Glen Asian Grocery (in Glen Waverley, not local) and nearby IGA in Diamond Creek [citation:Uber Eats Wattle Glen](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/wattle-glen-melbourne-vic).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Casual family-friendly dining options are found in neighbouring Diamond Creek. Try Ogawa Japanese Cafe (Japanese, sushi) at Shop 7/75 Main Hurstbridge Rd [citation:Uber Eats Wattle Glen](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/wattle-glen-melbourne-vic). For pub-style meals, the Diamond Creek Hotel offers bistro dining, and Ferguson Plarre's Bakehouse provides pies, cakes, and takeaway at 67 Main Hurstbridge Rd [citation:Uber Eats Wattle Glen](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/wattle-glen-melbourne-vic).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Specific tutoring centres in Wattle Glen are not found in available sources. However, families access Ezy Math Tutoring, which offers in-home English and maths tutors in Wattle Glen [citation:Ezy English Tutoring](https://www.ezymathtutoring.com.au/tutors/wattle-glen-3096/learn-english). For enrichment, BT Education is located in Glen Waverley (not local) and offers Year 3–4 enrichment programs and scholarship preparation [citation:BT Education Glen Waverley](https://bt-education.com/glen-waverley/).

8) **Commute:** Wattle Glen has its own station on the Hurstbridge line, operated by Metro Trains Melbourne. The typical train travel time from Wattle Glen Station to Flinders Street Station is approximately 54–67 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio Train](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Flinders-Street-Station/Wattle-Glen-Station)[citation:Metro Trains Wattle Glen](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/wattle-glen/). Trains run every 30 minutes, with direct service to the CBD [citation:Rome2Rio Train](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Flinders-Street-Station/Wattle-Glen-Station).
