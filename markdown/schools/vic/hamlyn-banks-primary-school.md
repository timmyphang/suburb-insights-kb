1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Hamlyn Heights is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb. The 2021 Census shows a median weekly household income of $1,643 (slightly below the national median of $1,746) and a median age of 38, with 1,730 families among 6,518 residents [citation:2021 Hamlyn Heights QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21130). The suburb has a mix of families and older residents, with a strong proportion of households with children, reflected in the 0.6 children per household average.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Hamlyn Heights is overwhelmingly Australian-born. In the 2016 Census, 59.6% of residents had both parents born in Australia, and 23.0% had both parents born overseas [citation:2016 Hamlyn Heights QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21122). The specific dominant overseas country of birth is not available in the provided sources.

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) at Hamlyn Banks Primary School is provided by **Kelly Club Hamlyn Banks Primary School**, offering before-school, after-school, and holiday program care [citation:Kelly Club Hamlyn Banks](https://kellyclub.com.au/hamlyn-banks). The provider is registered for Child Care Subsidy and can be contacted at 0467 770 079 or hamlynbanks@kellyclub.com.au [citation:Startingblocks Kelly Club OSHC](https://startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01JJ24GRQPXW4YWAGC40JYABB8/Kelly-Club-OSHC-Hamlyn-Banks-Primary-School). Specific opening and closing hours are not listed in the available sources.

4) **School culture:** Hamlyn Banks Primary School offers several named extracurricular activities, including **School Band** (for Years 3–6, rehearsing Thursday afternoons) and **Choir** (performing at events such as the Christmas choir at Market Square Geelong) [citation:Our School](https://hbps.vic.edu.au/our-school/)[citation:Performing Arts News](https://www.hbps.vic.edu.au/uploaded_files/media/performing_arts_news_term_1_bulletin_2015.pdf). The school also runs **Kelly Sports** after-school programs and **Bike Education** for Year 5/6 students [citation:Kelly Sports](https://www.hbps.vic.edu.au/page/201/Kelly-Sports---Programs)[citation:Student Wellbeing Policy](https://www.hbps.vic.edu.au/uploaded_files/media/student_wellbeing_and_engagement_policy_hbps__mar_2020_1.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Hamlyn Heights has a strong community feel centred on the **Vallis IGA + Liquor** supermarket at 113–119 Vines Road, a family-run store with a fresh deli, bakery, and produce section [citation:Vallis IGA Hamlyn Heights](https://fortemagazine.com.au/vallis-iga-hamlyn-heights-a-community-supermarket-built-on-quality-and-connection/). No specific ethnic or specialty grocery store in Hamlyn Heights was identified in the available sources.

6) **Weeknight dining:** Casual family-friendly dining options in Hamlyn Heights include **Bumble Bee Eatery** (a café/diner) and **Tulip** (a restaurant, likely Asian cuisine), both mentioned in local reviews [citation:TripAdvisor Bumble Bee Eatery](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/RestaurantsNear-g26689900-d26687085-Bumble_Bee_Eatery-Hamlyn_Heights_City_of_Greater_Geelong_Victoria.html). Additional takeaway options such as pizza are available, but specific names beyond these two were not found in the provided sources.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** In the Hamlyn Heights area, **Kumon Hamlyn Heights Education Centre** offers after-school tutoring in maths and English [citation:Kumon Hamlyn Heights](https://childcare-australia.nears.me/business/australia/victoria/hamlyn-heights/after-school-program/kumon-hamlyn-heights-education-centre-oshc-in-hamlyn-heights/). Additionally, **Ezy Math Tutoring** provides in-home maths tutoring for primary and secondary students in the suburb [citation:Ezy Math Tutoring Hamlyn Heights](https://www.ezymathtutoring.com.au/tutors/hamlyn-heights-3215).

8) **Commute:** The nearest train station is **North Geelong** on the **Geelong V/Line** line. Travel time from North Geelong to Melbourne's Southern Cross Station is approximately 60–70 minutes; from there, Flinders Street Station is a short walk or tram ride away [citation:Smart Property Investment Hamlyn Heights](https://www.smartpropertyinvestment.com.au/data/vic/3215/hamlyn-heights)[citation:V/Line Timetables](https://www.vline.com.au/Timetables/Train-coach-timetables).
