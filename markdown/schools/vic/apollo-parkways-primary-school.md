Here’s a short, family-facing eight-point briefing for Apollo Parkways Primary School (Greensborough VIC). I used ABS/local suburb data and the school’s own listings — snippets cited after each item.

1) Suburb socio-economic profile (2–3 sentences)  
Greensborough is a middle‑income, family‑oriented suburb with a median age of 41 and a relatively high median weekly household income of about $2,075 [citation:2021 Greensborough QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21104). Employment leans toward white‑collar/professional roles (≈24.7% professionals) alongside trades and retail, consistent with a mixed professional + service-sector community [citation:Greensborough Demographics (VIC) Local Stats](https://localstats.com.au/demographics/vic/melbourne/north-east/greensborough).

2) Most common foreign country of birth  
The area is predominantly Australian‑born (≈77.7%); the largest overseas birthplace is England (about 3.0% of residents) [citation:Greensborough Demographics (VIC) Local Stats](https://localstats.com.au/demographics/vic/melbourne/north-east/greensborough).

Apollo Parkways runs a school OSHC program offering before‑school, after‑school and vacation care (term times and holiday programs listed). Term-time hours are listed around ~07:30–09:00 (before) and ~15:30–18:30 (after); vacation care is available as well — see the school OSHC page and regulator listing [citation:OSHC — Apollo Parkways Primary School](https://www.apolloparkps.vic.edu.au/oshc) [citation:Acecqa Apollo Parkways P.S. OSHC](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/apollo-parkways-ps-oshc).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)  
Greensborough feels suburban and family‑friendly with riverside parks and shared trails (Plenty River Trail, Greensborough Park) and several upgraded playgrounds — good for kids, walking and weekend outings [citation:Greensborough Park | Banyule Council](https://www.banyule.vic.gov.au/Events-activities/Parks-and-reserves/Greensborough-Park) [citation:Plenty River Trail | Banyule Council](https://www.banyule.vic.gov.au/Events-activities/Parks-and-reserves/Plenty-River-Trail). For groceries you’ll find Greensborough Plaza (Coles) plus an IGA and specialty stores such as Premium Co Asian Grocery and Wholefood Merchants in the plaza [citation:Greensborough Plaza](https://www.greensboroughplaza.com.au/) [citation:Premium Co Asian Grocery - Greensborough Plaza](https://www.greensboroughplaza.com.au/store/premium-co-asian-grocery/) [citation:IGA Greensborough](https://www.iga.com.au/stores/iga-greensborough/).
