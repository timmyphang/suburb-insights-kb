1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Chelsea is a middle-income, family-oriented bayside suburb. The 2021 Census shows a median weekly household income of $1,683 and a median age of 41, indicating a mix of established families and older residents [citation:2021 Chelsea QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20537). The suburb has 2,214 families with an average of 1.7 children per family, and a higher proportion of females (53.3%) than males. Specific data on white-collar vs blue-collar occupation splits is not available in the provided sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The majority of Chelsea residents are Australian-born, but the exact percentage and the leading overseas country of birth are not provided in the extracted data from the 2021 Census QuickStats page. The page includes a "Country of birth of person" table, but the figures were not captured in the available snippets [citation:2021 Chelsea QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20537). For precise figures, families should consult the full ABS Community Profile.

3) **Working-parent support**: Chelsea Primary School offers an Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) program, but the specific provider and operating hours are not named in the school’s website content that was retrieved. The school’s Term Dates page states that "Where available, parents/carers may use our Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) program," but no provider or schedule details are listed [citation:Term Dates & Yard Supervision](https://www.chelseaps.vic.edu.au/page/246/Term-Dates-&-Yard-Supervision-). Families should contact the school directly for current OSHC provider and hours.

4) **School culture**: Chelsea Primary School offers several named extracurricular activities. These include the **Chelsea C Majors** choir (meets Thursdays), **Funky Friday Sport** led by Sport Captains, and a school band called **Chelsea Talents** that performs at the annual Christmas Carols evening [citation:ENews 26th February 2021](https://www.chelseaps.vic.edu.au/enews/details/50)[citation:Enews 26th November 2021](https://www.chelseaps.vic.edu.au/enews/details/68). Additional lunchtime clubs such as the Chill out Club, Creative Play, and a student-led Space Club are also offered.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Chelsea has a relaxed bayside atmosphere with a strong community feel. For specialty groceries, **Baan Chum Asian Grocery** on the Nepean Highway offers fresh produce and imported Asian products [citation:Asian Grocery – Baan Chum](https://www.baanchum.com.au/). In nearby Chelsea Heights, **Thai Wholesale Asian Grocery Shop** at 8a/93 Wells Rd provides a wide range of Asian ingredients [citation:Chelsea Heights Asian Grocery](https://www.dlook.com.au/find/asian-grocery-shop-in-chelsea-heights-vic-3196). Mainstream supermarkets are also available but not detailed in provided sources.

6) **Weeknight dining**: Families have several casual takeaway and dining options. **Thai Rose** (Thai cuisine) is a popular local restaurant offering dine-in and takeaway [citation:AGFG Chelsea Takeaway](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/chelsea/takeaway-category). **Cucina Dolce Wood Fired Pizza** (Italian pizza and pasta) is located at Chelsea Beach Arcade, 426 Nepean Hwy, and is well-regarded for takeaway [citation:Uber Eats Chelsea](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/chelsea-melbourne-vic). **The Indian Hut** (Indian cuisine) also offers takeaway and delivery in Chelsea.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: **Success Tutoring** has a centre in Chelsea, offering personalised support for primary and secondary students with 24/7 access to resources [citation:Success Tutoring Chelsea](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-chelsea). Another option is **Cloud Tuition**, which provides online tutoring for Year 2–12 students and covers the Chelsea Heights area [citation:Cloud Tuition Chelsea Heights](https://www.cloudtuition.com.au/locations/city/chelsea-heights). Both centres offer programs to support school learning.

8) **Commute**: Chelsea is served by the **Frankston line** on the Metro Trains network. The journey from **Chelsea Station** to **Flinders Street Station** (Melbourne CBD) takes approximately **53 minutes** by direct train, with services running every 15 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio Chelsea to Flinders](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Chelsea-Station-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). Trains run frequently, making the suburb convenient for commuters.
