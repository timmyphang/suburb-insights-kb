3) **Working-parent support:** Coburg West Primary School operates its own Out of School Hours Care (OSHC) program on-site. Before-school care runs from 7:15am to 9:00am, and after-school care runs from 3:30pm to 6:00pm [citation:Outside of School Hours Care](https://www.cobw.vic.edu.au/page/170/Outside-of-School-Hours-Care). The program is coordinated by Mrs Gina Blease and can be contacted at oshc.coburg.west.ps@edumail.vic.gov.au [citation:Outside of School Hours Care](https://www.cobw.vic.edu.au/page/170/Outside-of-School-Hours-Care).

4) **School culture:** Coburg West Primary School offers several named extracurricular programs, including a **Choir** (junior and senior), a **Drum Band**, and a **Rock Band** [citation:Specialist Programs](https://www.cobw.vic.edu.au/page/185/Specialist-Programs). These groups rehearse weekly and perform at school assemblies and community events [citation:Specialist Programs](https://www.cobw.vic.edu.au/page/185/Specialist-Programs).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Coburg has a vibrant, multicultural atmosphere with a strong community feel. For specialty groceries, families can visit **KFL Supermarket** (376 Sydney Road), a large Asian grocery store open Monday–Saturday 8:30am–6:00pm and Sunday 9:00am–6:00pm [citation:Coburg KFL](https://www.kflsupermarkets.com.au/coburg-vic). Other options include **Namaste Indian Groceries** and **Dhaka Supreme Asian Grocer and Gift Shop**, both located in central Coburg.

6) **Weeknight dining:** Families can enjoy takeaway or dine-in at several casual restaurants in Coburg. **Bluestone American BBQ** (American barbecue, with takeout) is a family-friendly option [citation:Best Takeaway Restaurants in Coburg - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g1816502-zfp10601-Coburg_Moreland_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). **Cornerstone Pizzeria** (Italian/Pizza) and **Antalya Restaurant** (Mediterranean/Turkish) also offer takeout and are well-rated for families [citation:Best Takeaway Restaurants in Coburg - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g1816502-zfp10601-Coburg_Moreland_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).
