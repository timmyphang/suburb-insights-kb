1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Northcote is a gentrified inner-northern suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,287 and a median age of 37, indicating a relatively affluent, white-collar demographic [citation:2021 Northcote, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21971). The suburb has undergone significant gentrification since the 1990s, with a high proportion of professional residents and a mix of families and singles [citation:Northcote, Victoria - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northcote,_Victoria). Families with children are common, with 6,257 families recorded in 2021, and an average of 0.6 children per household [citation:2021 Northcote, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21971).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Northcote offers a vibrant, family-friendly atmosphere with High Street's shops, live music venues, and parks like All Nations Park. For groceries, Epoch Supermarket at Northcote Plaza stocks a wide range of international foods, including Italian, Indian, and Asian products [citation:Epoch Supermarket](https://northcoteplaza.com/epoch-supermarket/). Tales of India provides Indian grocery delivery, and IGA on High Street is a convenient local option [citation:Indian Grocery Store Northcote](https://talesofindia.com.au/pages/indian-grocery-store-northcote-3070/).
