1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Rowville is a middle-to-upper-middle-income suburb with a strong family orientation. The median weekly household income is $2,205, and the median age is 41 [citation:2021 Rowville QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22207). The population is 33,571, with a high proportion of families—there are 9,779 families and an average of 2.9 people per household [citation:2021 Rowville QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22207). The suburb is predominantly white-collar, with car-based commuting the norm (73.1% drove to work in 2016) [citation:2016 Rowville QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22196).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Rowville is largely Australian-born; in 2021, 68.7% of residents were born in Australia [citation:2021 Rowville QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22207). The most common overseas country of birth is India, at 4.2% [citation:2021 Rowville QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22207).

3) **Working-parent support:** St Simon’s School offers Out of School Hours Care (OSHC) on-site at 2 Taylors Lane, Rowville, provided by Beyond Educare Pty Ltd [citation:St Simons OSHC childcareDB](http://www.childcaredb.com.au/service/3830/st_simons_oshc). The service includes Before School Care, After School Care, and Vacation Care, though specific operating hours are not listed in available sources [citation:St Simons OSHC CareforKids](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/nq4od/st-simons-oshc-rowville-3178). For exact hours, contact the school directly.

4) **School culture:** St Simon’s School promotes a welcoming Catholic faith-based community with a focus on student wellbeing and growth mindset [citation:St Simon the Apostle website](https://www.ssrowville.catholic.edu.au/). Named extracurriculars include the school's **Physical Education program** (which promotes lifelong activity) and **Student Wellbeing initiatives** (including mindfulness and gratitude practices) [citation:Physical Education](https://www.ssrowville.catholic.edu.au/school-curriculum-1/physical-education) [citation:Student Wellbeing](https://www.ssrowville.catholic.edu.au/school-curriculum-1/student-wellbeing). The school also runs **whole-school and year-level events** to create happy memories [citation:St Simon the Apostle website](https://www.ssrowville.catholic.edu.au/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Rowville is a family-oriented suburb with a village feel, featuring parks, schools, and local shopping centres. For grocery shopping, **Ritchies Supermarket** at Shop 18, 1100 Wellington Road offers Asian and Indian food sections [citation:Rowville Supermarket](https://rowville.shop.ritchies.com.au/). Other specialty grocers include **Spice World** at 2/1221 Stud Road (Asian grocery) and **CQV Online Indian Grocery Store** [citation:Asian grocery store Rowville](https://www.australianplanet.com/rowville/asian+grocery+store) [citation:CQV Online Indian Grocery](https://singh.com.au/cqv-online-indian-grocery-store-rowville-melbourne/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For family-friendly dining in Rowville, **Eating House** (2 Taylors Lane) offers a modern Australian menu with pizza, pasta, and a 'Kids Eat Free' offer on Wednesdays and Thursdays from 5pm [citation:Eating House](https://eatinghouse.com.au/). **Tosaria** at 60 Henderson Road serves modern Asian cuisine, with weekly specials such as Steak Night on Wednesdays and Parma Night on Thursdays [citation:Tosaria](https://www.tosaria.com.au/). For Indian takeaway, **Dosa Hut** is available near Rowville [citation:Dosa Hut Rowville](https://www.dosahut.net.au/indian-restaurant-rowville/).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **DW Tuition** at its Rowville centre offers one-to-one tutoring in Maths, English, and VCE subjects for primary, secondary, and VCE students, with in-person and online options [citation:DW Tuition](https://www.dwtuition.com.au/). **KIS Academics** provides English and Chemistry tutoring for Year 11–12 students in Rowville [citation:KIS Academics English](https://kisacademics.com/w/tutoring-locations/rowville-english-tutoring) [citation:KIS Academics Chemistry](https://kisacademics.com/w/tutoring-locations/rowville-chemistry-tutoring).

8) **Commute:** Rowville has no train station; the nearest railway is on the **Belgrave/Lilydale line** at stations like Bayswater or Boronia. For a direct route, commuters typically take the **bus route 754** (Rowville to Bayswater station) or **route 664** (Rowville to Knox City and Chirnside Park), then connect to the train [citation:Timetable Search](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/timetables/). Driving to Melbourne CBD takes about 24 minutes, while public transport via train from Bayswater station (after a bus connection) takes approximately 50–60 minutes to Flinders Street [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Rowville/Flinders-Street-Station).
