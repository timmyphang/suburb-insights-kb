Quick note: Boronia K‑12 College is a combined Early Learning–Year 12 public college (not a standalone primary school); this briefing treats the school’s junior/primary offering within that K–12 campus [citation:Home - Boronia K-12 College](https://www.boroniak-12.vic.edu.au/).

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Boronia is middle‑income with a median household weekly income of about $1,609 (slightly below the Victoria median). Median age 39. [citation:2021 Boronia, Census All persons QuickStats - Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/211011446)

2) Most common overseas country of birth
- Most residents are Australian‑born (around 69%); the largest overseas birthplace is England (~3.9% of residents). [citation:2021 Boronia, Census All persons QuickStats - Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/211011446) [citation:Boronia VIC Suburb Profile — Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/boronia)

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- Boronia K‑12 College offers on‑site Outside School Hours Care (before & after school + holiday programs) delivered by Camp Australia; families must register via Camp Australia. (Before care ~07:00–08:45; after‑school care ~13:30–18:00 per provider listing.) [citation:Out of School Hours Care (OSHC) - Boronia K-12 College](https://www.boroniak-12.vic.edu.au/early-learning-centre/out-of-school-hours-care-oshc/) [citation:Camp Australia - Boronia K-12 College](https://campaustralia.com.au/school/?accessCode=BEFD)

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Boronia is a family‑oriented middle‑ring suburb with a shopping strip on Boronia Road, local parks and community facilities serving families. Major supermarkets (Coles, Woolworths) are present and there are small specialty grocers including an Asian/Fil‑Asian grocer (Masagana) and local fruit & veg shops.
