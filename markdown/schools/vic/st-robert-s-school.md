1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Newtown, VIC is an affluent, family-oriented suburb. According to 2021 Census data, the median weekly household income is $2,092, and the median age is 40, indicating a mature, established population [citation:2021 Newtown (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/203021046). The suburb is predominantly white-collar, with a high proportion of professionals and managers, and has a strong family mix, as reflected in the average of 1.9 children per family [citation:2021 Newtown (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/203021046).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The data for Newtown, VIC shows that the suburb is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 81.2% of residents born in Australia [citation:2021 Newtown (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/203021046). No single overseas country of birth dominates, but the most common foreign-born groups come from England (3.4%) and India (1.2%) [citation:2021 Newtown (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/203021046).

3) **Working-parent support:** St Robert's School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by MACSEYE, the Melbourne Archdiocese Catholic Schools Early Years Education provider [citation:BEFORE & AFTER SCHOOL CARE | St Robert's Newtown](https://www.srnewtown.catholic.edu.au/before-and-after-school-care). The program runs Monday to Friday during term time, with before-school care from 7:00am to 8:50am and after-school care from 3:20pm to 6:00pm [citation:BEFORE & AFTER SCHOOL CARE | St Robert's Newtown](https://www.srnewtown.catholic.edu.au/before-and-after-school-care).

4) **School culture:** St Robert's School offers a range of extracurricular activities, including a school music program for Foundation to Year Four students that incorporates Drama and Dance [citation:EDUCATION | St Robert's Newtown](https://www.srnewtown.catholic.edu.au/education). Specific named activities include Numbots (for Foundation to Year 2) and Times Tables Rockstars (for Years 3-6), which are used to build numeracy fluency [citation:EDUCATION | St Robert's Newtown](https://www.srnewtown.catholic.edu.au/education). 8) **Commute:** Newtown, VIC is located near Geelong, approximately 75 km from Melbourne CBD. There is no train station in Newtown itself, but the nearest station is South Geelong Station on the Geelong line, which is a short drive or bus ride away. The train journey from South Geelong to Flinders Street Station typically takes about 60 to 75 minutes, depending on the service, via V/Line trains [citation:V/Line - Regional public transport for Victoria - Train & coach timetables](https://www.vline.com.au/Timetables/Train-coach-timetables).
