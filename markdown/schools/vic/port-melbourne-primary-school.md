Here’s a concise, family-facing eight‑point briefing for Port Melbourne Primary School (Port Melbourne, VIC). I used ABS QuickStats for demographic items and the school/provider sites for school details — single primary source cited for each point.

1) Suburb socio-economic profile  
Port Melbourne is an inner‑city, gentrified bayside suburb with a mix of modern apartments and restored workers’ cottages; median age 42 and a large cohort aged 25–44. Household/family mix shows both couples and a high share of never‑married adults (42.2%), indicating a strong singles/young‑adult presence alongside families [citation:2021 Port Melbourne QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22107).

2) Most common foreign country of birth  
The most common overseas birthplace after Australia is England — roughly about 5% of residents (Australia-born is the clear majority) [citation:2021 Port Melbourne QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22107).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
Port Melbourne Primary runs before/after‑school care at the school through TeamKids (before school 7:00–8:45am; after school 3:30–6:15pm) and offers holiday programs — families register via TeamKids [citation:Before and After School Care - Port Melbourne Primary School](https://portmelb.vic.edu.au/enrolment/before-and-after-school-care/).

4) School culture / extracurriculars  
The school advertises regular extracurricular choices including team sports (tennis, basketball, football, soccer), music (private/group piano, guitar, drums) and choir, plus clubs such as chess, gardening/sustainability and a “Young Engineers” program [citation:Learning beyond the classroom - Port Melbourne Primary School](https://portmelb.vic.edu.au/learning/learning-beyond-the-classroom/).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (2 sentences)  
Port Melbourne is a bayside, walkable suburb with foreshore/beach access, parks and a compact shopping strip — family‑friendly for beach walks and playground time. Local grocery options include a Coles supermarket and an IGA (Beacon Cove / Beach St) for everyday shopping; smaller local grocers/foodstores serve the Beacon Cove area [citation:Port Melbourne - Superb Suburb](https://superbsuburb.com.au/victoria/port-melbourne-3207/) [citation:Coles Port Melbourne](https://www.coles.com.au/find-stores/coles/vic/port-melbourne-646) [citation:IGA X-press Beacon Cove - Place Ai](https://placeai.com/local/businesses/iga-x-press-beacon-cove-port-melbourne-vic-3207-au).

6) Weeknight dining (casual, family‑friendly takeaway/dining)  
Family‑friendly takeaway favourites on Beach/Bay Street include D’lish Fish (fish & chips) and Port Melbourne Chippery; Bay Beach Pizza & Pasta is a local casual option for pizza/pasta — good for easy weeknight meals [citation:D'lish Fish](https://dlishfish.com/) [citation:Port Melbourne Chippery](https://portmelbournechippery.com.au/) [citation:Bay Beach Pizza and Pasta Restaurant — Port Melbourne](https://www.baybeachpizzaandpasta.com.au/main).

7) Tuition and enrichment near the school  
Local options include Kumon Port Melbourne (maths & English tuition) and small-group tutoring/enrichment centres serving the Bayside area (e.g., Launch Learning Centre / nearby Brighton tutors) — Kumon has a Port Melbourne centre listing/address and Launch Learning offers small‑group primary programmes [citation:Kumon Port Melbourne Education Centre](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-PORT-MELBOURNE-VIC) [citation:Launch Learning Centre - Small Group Tutoring & School Readiness](https://www.launchlearningcentre.com/).

8) Commute (train / tram)  
There is no heavy‑rail (train) station in Port Melbourne; the realistic public‑transport option is tram route 109 (Port Melbourne ⇄ Collins/Collins St), which typically takes about 20–25 minutes to reach the CBD (Flinders Street area) depending on traffic and stop location [citation:Melbourne tram route 109 - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melbourne_tram_route_109) [citation:Port Melbourne to Flinders Street Station - Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Port-Melbourne/Flinders-Street-Station).
