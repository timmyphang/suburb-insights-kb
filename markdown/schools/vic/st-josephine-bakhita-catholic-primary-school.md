1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Clyde North is a rapidly growing, middle-to-upper-middle-income family suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,163, well above the Victorian median, and the median age is just 30, reflecting a very young population [citation:2021 Clyde North, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20582). Over 40% of residents are children (0–14 years) or young adults (25–39), indicating a strong family-oriented demographic [citation:2021 Clyde North, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20582). The suburb is predominantly white-collar, with many professionals and managers, and has a low unemployment rate.
