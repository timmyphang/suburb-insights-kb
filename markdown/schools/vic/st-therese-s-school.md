3) Working-parent support: St Therese’s School offers Out of School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by Camp Australia, operating on the school grounds [citation:Before and After School Care](https://www.sts.catholic.edu.au/enrolment/before-and-after-school-care). Before-school care runs from 7:15 am to 8:45 am, and after-school care runs until 6:00 pm, Monday to Friday [citation:OSHClub - STS / Catholic School Essendon](https://www.stessendon.catholic.edu.au/oshclub). Vacation care is also available from 7:00 am to 6:00 pm on pupil-free days and school holidays [citation:Camp Australia - St Therese School OSHC](https://sacommunity.org/org/235367-Camp_Australia_-_St_Therese_School_OSHC).

4) School culture: St Therese’s School offers a rich music program featuring named activities such as the Recorder program (Years 3-4), Ukulele program (Years 3-4), and African Drumming program (Year 6) [citation:Music - STS / Catholic School Essendon](https://www.stessendon.catholic.edu.au/music).
