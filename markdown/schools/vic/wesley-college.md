1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**  
Melbourne (postcode 3000) is an affluent inner‑city area with a median weekly household income of $1,678 and a median age of 30 – well above the national average [citation:2021 Melbourne, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/LGA24600). The workforce is predominantly white‑collar (professionals, managers), and the population includes a mix of singles (average 1.8 people per household) and families, with 30,685 families and an average of 1.5 children per family with children [citation:2021 Melbourne, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/LGA24600). Overall, it ranks in the top quartile of socio‑economic advantage in Australia.

3) **Working‑parent support: Outside School Hours Care (OSHC)**  
Wesley College’s St Kilda Road campus offers Outside School Hours Care through **Extend**, providing before‑school care (7:00‑9:00 am) and after‑school care (2:20‑6:00 pm) for enrolled students from Pre‑K to Year 7 [citation:Vacancies, Enrol now at Wesley College (St Kilda) OSHC - Extend](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/kd2zj/wesley-college-st-kilda-oshc-extend-melbourne-3004) [citation:Wesley College, St Kilda Road Campus - Extend Before and After School Care](https://www.extend.com.au/school/wesley-college-st-kilda-road-campus/). The service is licensed for 60 children and is also available on pupil‑free days and during school holidays.

4) **School culture: extracurriculars**  
Wesley College has a strong performing arts program. Its music department alone includes 21 chamber ensembles, 23 bands, 10 orchestras, and 16 choirs, and offers private tuition on 21 instruments [citation:Music | School life | Wesley College](https://www.wesleycollege.edu.au/school-life/music). Beyond music, the college offers a wide range of sports (e.g., swimming) and drama productions, with students also participating in incursions featuring politicians, thought leaders and sports stars [citation:Extra-Curricular activities at Wesley College Melbourne, Australia](https://www.doris.school/schools/australia/wesley-college-melbourne/extra-curricular) [citation:Wesley website - Beyond the classroom](https://www.wesleycollege.edu.au/news-events-and-publications/lion-magazine/lion-december-2023/Beyond-the-classroom).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**  
Melbourne’s inner city is a vibrant, walkable hub with parks, playgrounds, and cultural venues ideal for families. For specialty groceries, there are numerous Asian supermarkets such as **Golden Dragon Asian Grocery** at South Melbourne Market and **Tang Food Emporium** in the CBD, alongside Indian and other ethnic grocers [citation:TOP 10 BEST Asian Grocery Stores in Melbourne Victoria, Australia - Updated 2026 - Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/search?find_desc=Asian+Grocery+Stores&find_loc=Melbourne+Victoria) [citation:Tang Food Emporium | Asian grocery store in melbourne](https://www.tangfoodemporium.com.au/). Major supermarkets (Woolworths, Coles) are also easily accessible.

8) **Commute**  
Wesley College’s St Kilda Road campus is centrally located, approximately a 15‑minute walk from **Flinders Street Station** or a 5‑minute tram ride. There is no dedicated train station within the suburb itself; the nearest rail access is via Flinders Street or Melbourne Central stations. The estimated travel time from Flinders Street to the Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street is already in the CBD) is effectively zero – the station is the heart of the CBD. For those commuting from other suburbs, trams and buses serve the school directly.
