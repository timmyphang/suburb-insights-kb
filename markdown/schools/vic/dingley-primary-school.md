1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Dingley Village is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median age of 45, notably older than the Victorian median of 38 [citation:2021 Dingley Village, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/212041313). The median weekly household income is $1,981 [citation:2021 Dingley Village, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/212041313). The suburb features predominantly low-density housing with above-average rates of outright home ownership, and 80.5% of dwellings are houses [citation:Dingley Village – SA2 212041313 | AreaSearch](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/dingley_village).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** In the 2021 Census, 46.9% of Dingley Village residents had both parents born in Australia, while 36.2% had both parents born overseas [citation:2016 Census QuickStats: Dingley Village](https://quickstats.censusdata.abs.gov.au/census_services/getproduct/census/2016/quickstat/SSC20752). The most common overseas country of birth is not explicitly stated in the provided search snippets for the 2021 data.

3) **Working-parent support:** Dingley Primary School partners with **Camp Australia** to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) [citation:Outside School Hours Care - Camp Australia Dingley](https://www.dingleyps.vic.edu.au/page/8/Camp-Australia-Dingley). The specific operating hours for before and after school care are not listed in the available sources. Families can register and find more details via the Camp Australia school portal [citation:Dingley Primary School | Camp Australia](https://campaustralia.com.au/school/?accessCode=7F33).

4) **School culture:** Dingley Primary School offers a **Breakfast Club** program, operating each school day from 8:30am to 8:50am [citation:Breakfast Club](https://www.dingleyps.vic.edu.au/page/26/Breakfast-Club). The school also provides a broad enrichment program that includes **instrumental music** lessons and participation in **interschool sports** competitions [citation:Dingley Primary School Student Wellbeing and Engagement Policy](https://www.dingleyps.vic.edu.au/source/Student%20Wellbeing%20and%20Engagement%20Policy%202024%20(1).pdf). Additionally, students can take part in district events such as **public speaking and debating** [citation:Dingley Primary School Curriculum Framework Policy](https://www.dingleyps.vic.edu.au/source/Build/Curriculum%20Framework%20Policy%202021.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Dingley Village is a family-oriented community with local amenities including the **Dingley Village Shopping Centre** [citation:Home - Dingley Village Shopping Centre](https://dingleyvillageshoppingcentre.com.au/). Major grocery options include **Woolworths** at 89 Centre Dandenong Road and **Coles** at 1 Douglas Street in nearby Noble Park [citation:30 Best Local Supermarkets Grocery Stores in Dingley Village](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/dingley-village-vic-3172). For specialty groceries, **India at Home** offers Indian grocery delivery to Dingley Village, and nearby Springvale has numerous Asian supermarkets like **Nan Yang Supermarket** on Springvale Road [citation:Online Grocery Store, Melbourne & Surround Suburbs - India At Home](https://www.indiaathome.com.au/pages/useful-links).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Families can enjoy **Padre Pizzeria** for casual pizza, **The Bangkok Eatery** for Thai cuisine, and **Dingley Pizza and Pasta** for reliable pizza and pasta options [citation:80 Best Casual Restaurants in Dingley Village | OpenTable](https://www.opentable.com.au/features/best-casual-restaurants-melbourne-dingley-village) [citation:Dingley Pizza and Pasta - Wheree](https://dingley-pizza-and-pasta.wheree.com/). The **Dingley Hotel** is also a popular family-friendly pub for dining and live sports [citation:The 10 Best Restaurants Near Me in Dingley Village | OpenTable](https://www.opentable.com.au/nearby/restaurants-near-me-dingley-village-australia).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Families can access **Strive Academy**, which offers in-person small-group maths tutoring (Year 7–VCE) in Dingley Village, taught by the co-founders [citation:Tutors Dingley Village | VCE Maths & English Tutoring Services - Strive Academy](https://striveacademytutoring.com.au/dingley-village-tutors). **Tuition Zone** provides personalised tutoring from Prep to Year 12, aligned with the Victorian Curriculum, with centres in Mount Waverley, Keysborough, and Cranbourne (and online options) [citation:Tutoring Dingley Village | Prep to Year 12 | Tuition Zone](https://tuitionzone.com.au/locations/tutoring-dingley-village).

8) **Commute:** Dingley Village does not have its own train station. The most efficient public transport route is to take the **Bus 811** from Howard Rd/Centre Dandenong Rd to **Springvale Station**, then catch the **Cranbourne/Pakenham line** train to Flinders Street Station, taking approximately **57 minutes** total [citation:Dingley Village to Flinders Street Station - 5 ways to travel](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Dingley-Village/Flinders-Street-Station). Driving to the CBD takes about **24 minutes** [citation:Dingley Village to Flinders Street Station - 5 ways to travel](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Dingley-Village/Flinders-Street-Station).
