Below is a compact, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Mount View Primary School (Glen Waverley). I used one search per data point and opened pages only when needed.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)  
Glen Waverley is a relatively affluent, family‑oriented middle‑to‑upper income suburb with a higher median household income and high rates of home ownership and marriage compared with Greater Melbourne. Median age is about 40 and many households are family households rather than single‑person households [citation:2021 Glen Waverley QuickStats (ABS)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21013).

2) Most common overseas birthplace (single country and share if clear)  
China is the largest overseas birthplace group reported for Glen Waverley (around 17–18% of residents as per 2021 suburb summaries) — there is a large Chinese‑born community alongside Australian‑born residents [citation:2021 Glen Waverley QuickStats (ABS)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21013) [citation:Glen Waverley VIC Suburb Profile — Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/glen-waverley).

3) Working‑parent support — OSHC availability at this school  
Mount View Primary runs on‑site before & after school care (OSHC) through TheirCare (TheirCare lists Mount View PS; school OSHC page confirms hours and enrolment info). TheirCare offers Before School, After School and Vacation care at the school site [citation:After School Hours Care — Mount View PS](https://www.mountviewps.vic.edu.au/page/33/After-School-Hours-Care) [citation:Mount View PS TheirCare (TheirCare)](https://theircare.com.au/holiday-programs/mount-view-ps-theircare/).

4) School culture — 2–3 named extracurriculars  
Mount View PS runs a high‑performing interschool chess program (state and national winners), choir (Junior & Senior choirs), dance clubs (Junior & Senior) and an instrumental music program via external providers [citation:Extra Curricular Activities — Mount View PS](https://www.mountviewps.vic.edu.au/page/19/Extra-Curricular-Activities) [citation:Performing Arts — Mount View PS](https://www.mountviewps.vic.edu.au/page/22/Performing-Arts).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)  
Glen Waverley’s town centre (Kingsway and The Glen) is a busy, family‑friendly retail and dining precinct with parks, library branch and sports reserves nearby (Central Reserve and Monash playgrounds). For groceries there are major supermarkets at The Glen plus Asian specialty grocers such as The Glen Asian Grocery and several independent Asian grocers along Kingsway [citation:The Glen Shopping Centre](https://www.theglen.com.au/) [citation:Glen Waverley Library — Monash Public Library Service](https://www.monlib.vic.gov.au/Branches/Glen-Waverley-Library) [citation:The Glen Asian Grocery](https://www.theglen.com.au/stores/the-glen-asian-grocery).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 family‑friendly takeaway/dining options and standout cuisines  
Kingsway offers many casual, family‑friendly options — examples include Laksa King (Malaysian laksa and Malaysian‑Chinese dishes), multiple Korean restaurants (casual BBQ and takeaway), and Hong Kong–style dim sum/dumpling outlets for affordable family meals [citation:Laksa King — OpenTable](https://www.opentable.com.au/r/laksa-king-glen-waverley) [citation:The 12 Best Glen Waverley Korean Restaurants | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/glen-waverley/korean-cuisine) [citation:Hong Kong Dim Sum — Asian Inspirations](https://asianinspirations.com.au/restaurants/hong-kong-dim-sum/).

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 local options)  
Local tutoring/enrichment providers include a Kumon centre in Glen Waverley (English & maths programs) and Mathnasium (maths tuition centre) — both serve primary‑school students in the area [citation:Kumon Glen Waverley](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-GLEN-WAVERLEY-VIC) [citation:Mathnasium Glen Waverley](https://www.mathnasium.com/au/maths-centres/glenwaverley).
