1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Hampton Park is a working-class suburb, with a median weekly household income of $1,538 (2021 Census) [citation:ABS QuickStats 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21133). Families make up 80% of households and the median age is 33, indicating a young, family-oriented population [citation:Propradar](https://propradar.com.au/suburbs/hampton-park-vic-3976). The suburb ranks in the bottom national quintile on the ABS socio-economic advantage index (decile 2), confirming its working-class profile [citation:DA Leads Australia](https://daleads.com.au/suburbs/hampton-park-vic-3976/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Over half of Hampton Park residents (56.2%) were born overseas, well above the national average [citation:DA Leads Australia](https://daleads.com.au/suburbs/hampton-park-vic-3976/). The most common overseas country of birth is Sri Lanka (Sinhalese speakers make up 3.8% of the population), followed by Afghanistan (Dari 3.7%, Hazaraghi 3.1%) [citation:ABS 2016 QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21125). Australian-born residents account for 43.8% of the population [citation:DA Leads Australia](https://daleads.com.au/suburbs/hampton-park-vic-3976/).

3) **Working-parent support:** Coral Park Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) through OSHClub, operating from 7:00am until bell time (Rise then Shine program) and from bell time until 6:00pm (Stay and Play program) [citation:OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/coral-park-primary-school/). Holiday programs (Holiday HQ) are also available from 7:30am to 6:00pm during school breaks [citation:OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/coral-park-primary-school/). Families can book early bird, flexible, or last-minute slots, with fees after Child Care Subsidy starting at $1.96 for before-school care [citation:OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/coral-park-primary-school/).

4) **School culture:** Coral Park Primary School has a dedicated music program where students learn guitar, keyboard, drums, and voice, and can join the School Band which performs at fortnightly assemblies [citation:Music - Coral Park PS](https://www.coralparkps.vic.edu.au/learning/music/) [citation:Specialists - Coral Park PS](https://www.coralparkps.vic.edu.au/page/223/Specialists). The school also offers a Guitar Club, where students progress through levels and can graduate to the school band [citation:Front - Coral Park PS](https://www.coralparkps.vic.edu.au/articles/107). Students participate in Whole School Assemblies, where they introduce segments and showcase classroom highlights [citation:Assembly - Coral Park PS](https://www.coralparkps.vic.edu.au/beyond-the-classroom/assembly/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Hampton Park is a car-dependent suburb with a neighbourhood shopping centre, the Hampton Park Shopping Square, which features fresh food stores and multicultural grocers [citation:Hampton Park Shopping Square](https://hamptonparkshoppingsquare.com.au/hampton-park-shops/). Specialty supermarkets include Melly's Asian Groceries (Pan-Asian), Indah Asian Market (herbs, spices, frozen goods), Grover Groceries (full Indian grocery), and Pacific Mart Indian, Pacific & Asian Grocery Store [citation:Mellys Asian Groceries](https://hamptonparkshoppingsquare.com.au/stores/mellys-asian-groceries/) [citation:Indah Asian Market](https://hamptonparkshoppingsquare.com.au/stores/indah-asian-market/) [citation:Grover Groceries](https://grovergroceries.com.au/) [citation:Pacific Mart](https://singh.com.au/pacific-mart-indian-pacific-asian-grocery-store-hampton-park-melbourne/). The area is predominantly detached houses (92.9%), reflecting a classic suburban layout [citation:DA Leads Australia](https://daleads.com.au/suburbs/hampton-park-vic-3976/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family dining, Hampton Park Thai Halal Restaurant serves authentic Thai cuisine with takeaway availability [citation:Hampton Park Thai Halal Restaurant](https://hpthai.com.au/). Banana Leaf Restaurant offers Sri Lankan cuisine [citation:AGFG Restaurants](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/hampton-park/takeaway-category), while Freshpoint Kebab provides Turkish takeaway [citation:AGFG Restaurants](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/hampton-park/takeaway-category). PizzaCorp and Fairway Fish & Chips are also popular takeaway options in the suburb [citation:AGFG Restaurants](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/hampton-park/takeaway-category).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Lynn’s Learning Hampton Park offers Maths and English tutoring (Foundation to Year 10) plus Selective Entry & Scholarship preparation (Years 3–9) at the Hampton Park Uniting Place, with sessions from 4pm on Wednesdays [citation:Lynn’s Learning](https://lynnslearning.com.au/locations/victoria-locations/lynns-learning-hampton-park-maths-english-tuition-centre/). Tuition Zone provides tutoring from Prep to Year 12 across Maths, English, Science, and VCE subjects, with a centre in nearby Cranbourne and also online [citation:Tuition Zone](https://tuitionzone.com.au/locations/tutoring-hampton-park).

8) **Commute:** Hampton Park does not have its own train station. The nearest station is **Hallam Station** on the **Pakenham line**, approximately 5 km away. A train from Hallam Station to Flinders Street Station takes about 50–55 minutes [citation:TuckerFox](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/hampton-park). Bus route 828 (Hampton Park Shopping Square to Dandenong Station) connects to the Cranbourne/Pakenham line for an alternative commute.
