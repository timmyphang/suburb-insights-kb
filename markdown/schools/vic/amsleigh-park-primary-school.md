1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Oakleigh East is a middle-to-upper-middle-income, family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $1,951, above the national average, and the median age is 35, slightly younger than the national median of 38 [citation:2021 Oakleigh East QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22001). There are 1,794 families in the suburb, with an average of 1.6 children per family for those with children, indicating a strong family presence [citation:2021 Oakleigh East QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22001).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: Specific data on the most common overseas country of birth for Oakleigh East is not detailed in the available sources. However, 2021 Census data shows that 64.0% of residents had both parents born overseas, while only 22.1% had both parents born in Australia, highlighting a very diverse population [citation:2016 Oakleigh East QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21989). The dominant overseas country of birth is not specified in the provided search results.

3) **Working-parent support**: Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) at Amsleigh Park Primary School is provided by TeamKids, offering Before and After Care programs as well as Vacation Care [citation:Parents | Amsleigh Park Primary School](https://amsleigh.vic.edu.au/parents/). Specific operating hours are not listed in the available sources, but the service runs year-round to support working families [citation:Parents | Amsleigh Park Primary School](https://amsleigh.vic.edu.au/parents/).

4) **School culture**: Amsleigh Park Primary School offers a range of named extracurricular activities. The school choir is called **Leigh Club** [citation:About Us | Amsleigh Park Primary School](https://amsleigh.vic.edu.au/about-us/). The school also has competitive **aerobics teams** including *Amsleigh Spirit* and *Intensity* [citation:About Us | Amsleigh Park Primary School](https://amsleigh.vic.edu.au/about-us/). Additionally, the **Enviro-Stars** program engages students in environmental projects [citation:About Us | Amsleigh Park Primary School](https://amsleigh.vic.edu.au/about-us/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Oakleigh East is a quiet, family-friendly suburb with convenient access to grocery shopping. Key supermarkets include **IGA Friendly Grocers** at 188-190 Huntingdale Road [citation:35 BEST local Supermarkets & Grocery Stores in Oakleigh, VIC](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/oakleigh-vic-3166) and **Hanaromart**, a Korean-Asian supermarket located at Oakleigh Central [citation:Hanaromart - Oakleigh Central](https://www.oakleighcentral.com.au/stores/hanaromart). For Indian and Pakistani groceries, **Sarawan Spices** is a nearby option on Warrigal Road, Oakleigh South [citation:THE BEST 10 Supermarkets and Grocery Stores in Oakleigh East, Victoria](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/victoria/oakleigh-east/shopping/supermarkets-and-grocery-stores/). The Oakleigh Central shopping centre also provides a Woolworths and other retailers.

6) **Weeknight dining**: Families have several casual takeaway and dining options in Oakleigh East. **Teerak Thai** offers Thai-style homemade street food for eat-in or takeaway [citation:Teerak Thai | Oakleigh East VIC](https://www.facebook.com/teerakthaioakleigh/). **Thanadee Thai Restaurant** provides authentic Thai takeaway in nearby Oakleigh [citation:Thanadee Thai Restaurant](https://thanadeethai.com.au/). For pizza and other options, many restaurants are available via Uber Eats and Menulog in the area [citation:Takeaway in Oakleigh East](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/oakleigh-east-melbourne-vic).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Named tutoring centres in or near Oakleigh East include **Success Tutoring Oakleigh East**, which offers personalised academic support for primary and secondary students [citation:Oakleigh East – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-oakleigh-east). Another option is **Tuition Zone Oakleigh East**, providing structured lessons from Prep to Year 12 aligned with the Victorian Curriculum [citation:Tutoring Oakleigh East | Tuition Zone](https://tuitionzone.com.au/locations/tutoring-oakleigh-east). Additionally, **Kumon Oakleigh Education Centre** offers English and mathematics programmes for skill mastery [citation:Kumon Oakleigh Education Centre](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/Kumon-Oakleigh-VIC).

8) **Commute**: Oakleigh East is served by **Oakleigh Station** on the **Cranbourne/Pakenham line**. The train journey from Oakleigh Station to Flinders Street Station (CBD) takes approximately 22–27 minutes [citation:Oakleigh to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Oakleigh/Flinders-Street-Station) [citation:Oakleigh railway station - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakleigh_railway_station). Trains run frequently, about every 10–15 minutes, making for an easy commute.
