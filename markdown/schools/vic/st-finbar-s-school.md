1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Brighton East is an affluent, family-oriented suburb. According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income was $2,720, and the median age was 44, indicating a mature population with many established families [citation:2021 Brighton East QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/208011170). The suburb is predominantly white-collar, with a high proportion of professionals living in detached homes.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas country of birth for Brighton East is not explicitly stated in the available Census data. However, the 2016 Census reported that 75.7% of residents spoke only English at home, with Mandarin and Greek each spoken by 2.9% of the population, suggesting significant communities from China and Greece [citation:2016 Brighton East QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20336). More recent 2021 data for this suburb is not available in the provided sources.

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) at St Finbar’s is provided by TeamKids, operating from a dedicated room in the school hall [citation:Outside School Hours Care (OSHC)](https://www.sfbrightoneast.catholic.edu.au/page/199/Outside-School-Hours-Care-(OSHC)). Specific operating hours are not listed in the available school materials, but TeamKids typically offers before-school and after-school care for working families.

4) **School culture:** St Finbar’s offers a range of named extracurriculars, including a **Choir** (open to Years 3–6, rehearses weekly), a **Band Club** (Thursdays for students learning instruments), and a **Chess Club** (Wednesday snack time) [citation:Lunch Time School Clubs](https://www.sfbrightoneast.catholic.edu.au/page/211/Lunch-Time-School-Clubs). Additionally, external providers run **Drama Time** (Lara Wiser) and **Tennis** (Ray Kent) on site [citation:Extra Curricular Activities](https://www.sfbrightoneast.catholic.edu.au/page/183/Clubs).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Brighton East is a leafy bayside suburb with parks, playgrounds, and easy beach access, making it highly family-friendly. For ethnic groceries, **Brighton Asian Grocery** (341 Bay Street, Brighton) stocks Chinese, Japanese, and Korean ingredients, while **PAMIR Grocery** (Brighton) offers Indian, Middle Eastern, and organic produce [citation:Brighton Asian Grocery](https://www.instagram.com/brighton.asiangrocery/) [citation:PAMIR Grocery Brighton](https://www.facebook.com/pamirgrocery/). **Fresh Connection** also delivers groceries locally [citation:Brighton Grocery Store — Fresh Connection](https://www.freshconnection.com.au/pages/brighton-grocery-store).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family-friendly takeaway, **Baan Phra Ya Thai** (Bay Street, Brighton) serves authentic Thai cuisine for dine-in or takeaway [citation:Baan Phra Ya Thai Brighton](https://www.baanphrayabrighton.com.au/). **Zanini Pizzeria & Cucina Italiana** (Elwood, near Brighton East) offers pizza and pasta with takeaway options [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Brighton](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g954016-zfp10601-Brighton_Bayside_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). **Brighton Seafoods** is a local favourite for fish and chips [citation:Restaurants in Brighton,VIC With Takeaway](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/brighton/takeaway-category).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Fruition Tuition** (15-17 Scanlan St, Bentleigh East) offers one-on-one tutoring in maths and English for primary and secondary students [citation:Private Tutors Brighton East - Fruition Tuition](https://www.fruition.com.au/tutoring-brighton-east/). **KIS Academics** provides online maths tutoring for Years 5–10, with tutors available in the Brighton East area [citation:Best Year 5-10 Maths Tutors in Brighton east](https://kisacademics.com/w/tutoring-locations/brighton-east-maths-year-5-10-tutoring).

8) **Commute:** Brighton East is served by the **Sandringham line** via **North Brighton Station**. The train ride from North Brighton to Flinders Street Station takes approximately **21 minutes** [citation:North Brighton to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/North-Brighton-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). Trains run frequently throughout the day.
