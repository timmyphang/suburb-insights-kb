1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Crib Point is a working-to-middle-income suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,447, below the national median [citation:2021 Crib Point QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20674). The median age is 39 years, and families make up a significant portion of households (919 families from 1,402 dwellings) [citation:2021 Crib Point QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20674). The suburb has a strong family-oriented feel, with over 20% of the population aged under 15 years [citation:2021 Crib Point QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20674).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The population is overwhelmingly Australian-born. According to the 2021 Census, 78.7% of people in Crib Point were born in Australia, with no single overseas country of birth exceeding 3% [citation:2021 Crib Point QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20674). Specific foreign-born percentages for individual countries are not provided in the available sources.

3) **Working-parent support:** Crib Point Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by **Camp Australia**, operating from the school grounds at 70 Milne Street [citation:Camp Australia - Crib Point Primary School](https://campaustralia.com.au/school/?accessCode=1E5C). The service runs **3:15 pm to 6:15 pm** on weekdays [citation:Camp Australia - Crib Point Primary School OSHC on Kindicare](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3919/crib-point/camp-australia-crib-point-primary-school-oshc). Morning care hours are not listed in the available sources.

4) **School culture:** Crib Point Primary School offers a **weekly singing club** for students in Years 2 to 6, and students can participate in the **State Schools Spectacular** performance [citation:Visual & Performing Arts | Crib Point Primary School](https://cpps.vic.edu.au/learning-teaching/visual-performing-arts/). The school also provides **guitar tuition**, with students performing in the community [citation:Visual & Performing Arts | Crib Point Primary School](https://cpps.vic.edu.au/learning-teaching/visual-performing-arts/). Additionally, the school has a **Year 6 Buddy Program** connecting older students with Foundation children through shared activities [citation:Leadership | Crib Point Primary School](https://cpps.vic.edu.au/wellbeing/leadership/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Crib Point has a semi-rural, coastal atmosphere within the Mornington Peninsula, centered around local community and outdoor activities. For grocery shopping, residents can visit the **Crib Point General Store** on Stony Point Road, or drive 5 km to Hastings for large supermarkets like **Woolworths Hastings** and **Coles Hastings**. Specialty ethnic grocers are not listed in the available sources for Crib Point itself; the nearest Asian grocery appears to be further afield in Frankston (about 20 km away).

8) **Commute:** Crib Point is served by the **Stony Point line** on the Metro Trains network, with trains departing from **Crib Point station** (also known as Morradoo station) [citation:Crib Point | Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/crib-point/). Travel to Flinders Street Station requires a train change at Frankston, with a total journey time of approximately **1 hour 45 minutes to 2 hours 17 minutes** [citation:Crib Point to Flinders Street Station on Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Crib-Point/Flinders-Street-Station). Buses (Route 782) also run to Frankston station but take longer.
