Short family briefing — Laburnum Primary School (Blackburn VIC). Eight quick items (one‑search per datapoint). Sources listed below.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
- Middle‑income / relatively advantaged, established family area: median age 40 (vs Victoria 38) and a higher share married (52% aged 15+) — points to many settled households and families rather than a young singles precinct [citation:2021 Blackburn QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/207031161).  

2) Most common overseas birthplace (if any)  
- Majority Australian‑born; no single large overseas community dominates. ABS shows a high Australia‑born share for Blackburn (QuickStats place summary for Blackburn) — the suburb is not overwhelmingly defined by one other country of birth in the top lines [citation:2021 Blackburn QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/207031161).  

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
- Yes — before/after school care on site (Before 07:00–08:45; After 15:30–18:00) run via the school’s OSH provider (OSHClub) and the school lists TeamKids/approved services for Vacation/Before/After Care [citation:Out of School Hours Care - Laburnum Primary School](https://www.laburnumps.vic.edu.au/page/227/Out-of-School-Hours-Care) [citation:TeamKids - Laburnum Primary | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/teamkids-laburnum-primary).  

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars (specific)  
- Performing arts program (music, dance, drama) and school choir/dance groups [citation:Music - Performing Arts](https://www.laburnumps.vic.edu.au/page/254/Music).  
- Instrumental tuition (violin, viola, guitar, piano, percussion, euphonium, drums listed) and a school instrumental expression‑of‑interest program [citation:Expression of Interest for Instrumental Lessons](https://www.laburnumps.vic.edu.au/page/857).  
- Chess Club and Joggers Club (lunchtime/house activities) plus Culture Club and Junior School Council [citation:Extra‑Curricular Programs & Activities](https://www.laburnumps.vic.edu.au/page/259/Extra-Curricular-Programs-%26-Activities).  

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (2 sentences + named specialty grocers)  
- Blackburn is a leafy, family‑oriented suburb with local shopping along Whitehorse Rd and community amenities (cafés, parks and schools) close by [citation:2021 Blackburn QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/207031161).  
- Notable specialty/ethnic grocers in Blackburn include Lin’s Asian Grocery (Blackburn Square) and Ashok’s Bombay Supermarket / Satnam Indian Grocery on Whitehorse Rd for South Asian ingredients [citation:Lin's Asian Grocery - Blackburn (Blackburn Square)](https://www.blackburnsquare.com.au/store/lins-asian-grocery/) [citation:Ashok's Bombay Supermarket listing](https://australia.chamberofcommerce.com/business-directory/victoria/blackburn/indian-grocery-store/3608073-ashok-s-bombay-supermarket).  

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 casual family options + cuisines  
- Cuto Kids Café — family‑friendly indoor play + café (good for younger kids).  
- The Groove Train (diner / casual meals) and Blackburn Eatery (modern‑Australian / takeaway options); Blackburn Hotel offers pub meals/takeaway — all close to Whitehorse Rd shopping strip [citation:Cuto Kids Cafe Blackburn](https://www.cuto.com.au/pages/blackburn) [citation:The Groove Train Blackburn listing](https://restaurant.reservemytable.com.au/reservation/The%20Groove%20Train%20Blackburn?rts=1f46465c5d) [citation:Blackburn Eatery — Contact / Order](https://blackburneatery.com.au/contact-us/).  

7) Tuition / enrichment nearby (1–2 centres)  
- A4 Maths Learning Centre (local tutoring in Blackburn) and multiple private tutors listed via Ezy Math Tutoring / local directories; nearby franchise options (e.g., Kumon in neighbouring suburbs / Forest Hill) are also commonly used by families [citation:A4 Maths Learning Centre listing](https://www.dlook.com.au/business/tutor-in-blackburn-vic-3130/a4-maths-learning-centre) [citation:Ezy Math Tutoring - Blackburn tutors list](https://www.ezymathtutoring.com.au/tutors/blackburn-3130).  

8) Commute — estimated train time to CBD  
- Blackburn has a train station (Lilydale/Belgrave lines). Typical train travel time to Flinders Street Station is about 30–35 minutes (commonly shown ≈32 minutes) depending on service/timetable [citation:Blackburn to Flinders Street Station trains - Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Blackburn-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station) [citation:Lilydale Line - Transport Victoria](https://transport.vic.gov.au/route/timetable/9/lilydale-line).
