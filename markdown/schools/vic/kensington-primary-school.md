Here’s a concise, family-facing eight‑point briefing for Kensington Primary School (Kensington, VIC). Each point is a short fact/snippet with the primary source cited.

1) Suburb socio-economic profile  
Kensington is a relatively young, inner‑urban, gentrifying suburb with many renters and a strong mix of single-person households and families — more singles/never‑married adults than the state average and a median age of 35. It reads as middle‑income/inner‑city professional rather than traditionally working‑class or wealthy suburbia. [citation:2021 Kensington (Vic.), Census QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21327) [citation:Kensington Residents' Association census snapshot](https://www.kra.org.au/kensington-a-demographic-snapshot-from-the-2021-census/)

2) Most common foreign country of birth  
Most residents are Australia‑born (majority). The largest overseas birthplace after Australia is India (≈5.1% reported in the local summary). [citation:Kensington Residents' Association census snapshot](https://www.kra.org.au/kensington-a-demographic-snapshot-from-the-2021-census/) [citation:2021 Kensington (Vic.), Census QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21327)

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC) at the school  
Yes — Kensington Primary runs an on‑site Before, After and Vacation Care program operated by TeamKids (families must register/book; TeamKids lists the Kensington Primary venue). [citation:OSHC Program - Kensington Primary School](https://www.kensingtonps.vic.edu.au/page/198/OSHC-Program) [citation:TeamKids - Kensington Primary venue](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/kensington-primary-oshc/)

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars offered  
- Instrumental music lessons (piano, guitar, violin, cello, drums, woodwind, voice) via Melbourne Music Tuition.  
- Performing Arts / Spanish specialist and regular performing ensembles/concerts.  
- Interschool sport (athletics, cross country and representative sports). [citation:Music Lessons - Kensington PS](https://www.kensingtonps.vic.edu.au/page/265/) [citation:Specialist Learning Program - Kensington PS](https://www.kensingtonps.vic.edu.au/page/268/Specialist-Learning-Program)

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (2 sentences)  
Kensington feels like an inner‑city village: compact streets, strong active‑transport use and a mix of young professionals and families with local shopping along Racecourse/Macaulay Rd. For groceries there are specialty/deli options and local supermarkets (Gertrude Grocer has a Kensington store; FoodWorks/independent delis operate on Macaulay/Racecourse Road). [citation:Kensington Residents' Association census snapshot](https://www.kra.org.au/kensington-a-demographic-snapshot-from-the-2021-census/) [citation:Gertrude Grocer Kensington](https://www.gertrudegrocer.com/) [citation:FoodWorks Kensington listing](https://kensingtonliquor.foodworks.com.au/)

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 casual family‑friendly options & cuisines  
- Crisp Pizza (pizza/takeaway on Racecourse Road).  
- Zamzam Kitchen (Somali/African halal fare on Racecourse Road).  
- Pimm Thai Cafe (Thai takeaway in Kensington). These are convenient, casual weeknight choices near the school. [citation:Crisp Pizza (Kensington)](https://crisppizza.com.au/) [citation:Zamzam Kitchen listing](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurant/zamzam-kitchen-120614) [citation:Pimm Thai Cafe](https://www.pimmthaicafe.com.au/)

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 local providers)  
Local tutoring/enrichment options include Cloud Tuition (Kensington tutoring/online lessons) and Innate/independent private tutors advertising services in Kensington (in‑person and online options). [citation:Cloud Tuition - Kensington](https://www.cloudtuition.com.au/locations/tutoring-vic/kensington-vic) [citation:Innate Tutoring Kensington listing](https://www.innatetutoring.com.au/kensington-tutoring/)

8) Commute — train time to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)  
Kensington has a local Metro train station. Typical direct train travel time to Flinders Street is about 12–14 minutes (most sources show roughly 13 minutes as an average). [citation:Kensington | Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/kensington/) [citation:Kensington Station to Flinders Street trains (Rome2rio)](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Kensington-Station-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station)
