1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Kalkallo is a rapidly growing, middle-income suburb with a strong family focus. The median weekly household income is $2,047, and the median age is 30, reflecting a very young population [citation:2021 Kalkallo, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21284). The area is dominated by couples with children living in separate houses, and over 78% of the land is zoned for urban growth [citation:KALKALLO suburb profile](https://landchecker.com.au/suburb/kalkallo-vic-3064/). Employment type data is not available in the provided sources.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Several tutoring options are available in and near Kalkallo. Brainworks offers one-to-one maths and English tutoring at the Kalkallo Community Centre (33 Toyon Rd) for students from Year 1 to Year 10, with specialist programs for selective school entry [citation:Kalkallo Maths & English Tutoring - Melbourne - Brainworks](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-kalkallo). 8) **Commute:** There is no direct train station in Kalkallo. The closest V/Line station is Donnybrook Station on the Seymour line, approximately a 5-minute drive away. From there, the train journey to Southern Cross Station takes around 40 minutes, with a total travel time to Flinders Street Station of about 1 hour and 40 minutes [citation:Flinders Street Station to Kalkallo - 5 ways to travel via train](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Kalkallo).
