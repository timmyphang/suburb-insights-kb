3) **Working-parent support**: Preshil offers on-site Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by the school itself. The ACECQA registration confirms before-school and after-school care are available, operating Monday to Friday from 8:00 am to 6:00 pm during school term, with no vacation care [citation:Preshil - The Margaret Lyttle Memorial School | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/preshil-margaret-lyttle-memorial-school). A separate source notes that the after-school program runs from 3:30 pm to 6:00 pm [citation:"Preshil" The Margaret Lyttle Memorial School - OSHC, Kew | The Australian Child Care Index](https://www.echildcare.com.au/kew-oosh-2/).

4) **School culture**: Preshil offers a range of named extracurricular activities, including the **Jazz Band**, **Choir**, and a **String Quartet** as part of its instrumental music program [citation:IN THE SPOTLIGHT: Instrumental Music | Preshil](https://preshil.vic.edu.au/in-the-spotlight-music/). Additionally, the school runs an **After School Sports** program on Wednesdays and Thursdays for Years 5–12, where students explore various sports [citation:After School Sports](https://preshil.vic.edu.au/preshil-weekly-term-3-week-6/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Kew has a family-friendly, leafy character with several major supermarkets. Key grocery options include **Woolworths Kew** at 17 Walpole Street, **Q Asian Store** at 229 High Street for Asian groceries, and **Leo’s Fine Food & Wine** at 26 Princess Street for specialty items [citation:THE BEST 10 Supermarkets and Grocery Stores in Kew, Victoria](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/victoria/kew/shopping/supermarkets-and-grocery-stores/).
