1) Suburb socio-economic profile (2–3 sentences)  
Frankston has a median age of 39 and a mixed household profile; marriage rates are lower and “never married” shares higher than Victoria’s average, suggesting a sizable single/adult population alongside families [citation:2021 Frankston QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20947). The wider Frankston SA3 shows a median weekly household income around $1,653 (middle‑income range), so Frankston is best described as middle‑income with a mix of white‑ and blue‑collar employment and both families and singles present [citation:2021 Frankston QuickStats (SA3)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/21401).

2) Most common foreign country of birth  
The most common overseas birthplace for Frankston residents is England (UK); England is the largest single overseas country of birth in Frankston (top overseas share ~5% of residents) — the area is nevertheless majority Australian‑born [citation:Frankston suburb QuickStats / local summaries](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20947).

3) Working‑parent support — OSHC availability at Overport Primary  
Yes — Overport offers on‑site before‑ and after‑school care operated by Extend. Program hours listed: Before school 7:00–9:00 and After school 3:30–6:00; holiday care is also advertised via Extend [citation:Overport PS Out of Hours Care](https://www.overport-ps.vic.edu.au/out-of-hours-care.html) [citation:StartingBlocks — Overport OSHC (Extend)](https://www.startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01HGCPT8BQRFJ8JJGC4ARJD234/Overport-Primary-School-OSHC-Extend).

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars offered  
- Annual Years 4–6 school production (performed at Frankston Arts Centre) and a yearly Christmas concert [citation:Overport Performing & Visual Arts](https://www.overport-ps.vic.edu.au/performing-and-visual-arts.html).  
- Parent‑funded private instrumental lessons (guitar, drums, brass and woodwind) and weekly whole‑school music lessons [citation:Overport Performing & Visual Arts](https://www.overport-ps.vic.edu.au/performing-and-visual-arts.html).  
- Active sport program with district and interschool representation (basketball, soccer, t‑ball, cricket, AFL, netball, swimming carnivals) plus a twice‑weekly Runners’ Club [citation:Overport Sport & P.E](https://www.overport-ps.vic.edu.au/sport--pe.html).

Frankston is a seaside regional centre with family amenities — large shopping hubs (Bayside Shopping Centre in the CBD and Karingal Hub) plus many parks, upgraded playgrounds and library/neighbourhood services, so families have easy access to shops and play spaces [citation:Karingal Hub Centre Info](https://karingalhub.com.au/about-the-centre/) [citation:Frankston City Playgrounds](https://www.frankston.vic.gov.au/Things-To-Do/Playgrounds).
