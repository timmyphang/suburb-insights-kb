1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Vermont South is an affluent, family-focused suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,944 (2021 Census), significantly above the Victorian median [citation:Vermont South Suburb Profile 2026 | Collings Real Estate](https://www.collings.com.au/vermont-south-suburb-profile-2026-lifestyle-demographics-market/). The median age is 46, older than the state median of 38, reflecting a settled population of owner-occupiers and long-term investors [citation:2021 Vermont South, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/211041273). The suburb is predominantly white-collar, with a low-density residential character and strong school catchment appeal [citation:Vermont South Suburb Profile 2026 | Collings Real Estate](https://www.collings.com.au/vermont-south-suburb-profile-2026-lifestyle-demographics-market/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas country of birth is China, with 14.2% of residents born there [citation:Ancestry & Birthplace in Vermont South — Census profile | CensusAtlas](https://censusatlas.au/region/sal/SAL22628/vermont-south/topic/ancestry-heritage). Overall, 51.3% of residents were born in Australia [citation:Ancestry & Birthplace in Vermont South — Census profile | CensusAtlas](https://censusatlas.au/region/sal/SAL22628/vermont-south/topic/ancestry-heritage).

3) **Working-parent support:** Weeden Heights Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by **After The Bell Aus Pty Ltd** [citation:After The Bell Aus- Weeden Heights PS | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/weeden-heights-primary-school-oshc). During school term, before-school care runs from **7:00 AM to 9:00 AM** and after-school care from **3:30 PM to 6:00 PM**, Monday to Friday [citation:After The Bell Aus- Weeden Heights PS | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/weeden-heights-primary-school-oshc).

The school's website mentions a "vibrant learning community" but does not name specific clubs, sports, or programs [citation:Our School - Weeden Heights Primary School | Vermont South](https://weedenheightsps.vic.edu.au/our-school/). 5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Vermont South has a quiet, leafy family vibe anchored by The Glen Shopping Centre in nearby Glen Waverley and local strip retail on Morack Road and at Vermont South shops [citation:Vermont South Suburb Profile 2026 | Collings Real Estate](https://www.collings.com.au/vermont-south-suburb-profile-2026-lifestyle-demographics-market/). For specialty groceries, **Rich Maha Asian Groceries** is located at Shop 4, 501 Burwood Highway in the Vermont South Shopping Centre, offering Asian products [citation:Indian Grocery Stores Vermont South, VIC](https://singh.com.au/indian-grocery-stores-vermont-south-vic/). **Bharat Superstore**, an online Indian grocery serving the area, operates from nearby Forest Hill [citation:THE BEST 10 Supermarkets and Grocery Stores in Vermont South, Victoria](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/victoria/vermont-south/shopping/supermarkets-and-grocery-stores/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** **Vermont Thai**, located at 503A Burwood Highway in the Vermont South Shopping Centre, offers authentic Thai cuisine including Pad Thai and Massamun Curry for takeaway or dine-in [citation:Vermont Thai | Best Restaurants Australia](https://www.bestrestaurants.com.au/vic/melbourne/vermont-south/restaurant/vermont-thai-vermont-south). For pizza, **Tripadvisor** reviews highlight that pizza from local options is highly rated, though a specific named pizzeria is not identified in the provided snippets [citation:THE 10 BEST Restaurants in Vermont South (Updated April 2026)](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g4398597-Vermont_South_Whitehorse_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Cloud Tuition** offers online tutoring for Year 2–12 in English, Maths, and Science, serving Vermont South students with virtual face-to-face lessons [citation:Tutoring Vermont South | Online Tutors for Years 2-12 | Cloud Tuition](https://www.cloudtuition.com.au/locations/tutoring-vic/vermont-south). **Lynn’s Learning** runs a Maths and English tuition centre at Glen Hill Community Church, 832–834 High Street Road, Glen Waverley, just a short drive from Vermont South, offering in-person sessions for Foundation to Year 10 at $40 per week [citation:Maths Tutoring in Vermont South · Foundation to Year 10 · Lynn’s Learning](https://lynnslearning.com.au/maths-tutoring/vermont-south).

8) **Commute:** Vermont South does not have a train station. The primary public transport option to the CBD is the **Route 75 tram**, which runs along Burwood Highway from Vermont South Shopping Centre to Flinders Street Station [citation:Flinders Street Station to Vermont South - 4 ways to travel via line 75 tram](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Vermont-South). The tram journey to Flinders Street Station typically takes approximately **1 hour and 42 minutes** [citation:Flinders Street Station to Vermont South - 4 ways to travel via line 75 tram](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Vermont-South).
