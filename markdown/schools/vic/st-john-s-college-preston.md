1) Preston is a middle-income, culturally diverse suburb with a mix of families and singles. According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income is $1,844, and the median age is 37 [citation:2021 Preston (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22121). The suburb has a balanced occupational mix, with 48.6% male and 51.4% female residents, and 8,624 families among the 33,790 population.

2) Preston is a multicultural suburb, with 66.4% of residents born in Australia [citation:2021 Preston (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22121). The most common overseas country of birth is not specifically listed in the available snippets, but other common birthplaces in similar Darebin areas include Italy, Greece, and China. 3) St John's College Preston offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) through VillageOSHC, operating Monday to Friday from 6:45am–8:30am (Before School Care) and 3:10pm–6:00pm (After School Care) for Prep to Year 6 students [citation:Before & After School Care - St John's College Preston](https://www.stjohnspreston.vic.edu.au/enrolments/before-and-after-school-care/). After School Care fees are $32.20 per session (permanent) or $36.44 (casual), with a $5.00 termly admin fee [citation:VillageOSHC St John's College Preston](https://villageoshc.lookedafter.com/dashboard/schools/SJCP3072). The service can be booked online via the VillageOSHC platform.

4) St John’s College Preston offers a strong music program including the school choir, the dance group, and the instrumental music program (with tuition in instruments like piano, guitar, saxophone, and bouzouki) [citation:Co-Curricular | Northern Suburbs Preston - St John's College](https://www.stjohnspreston.vic.edu.au/co-curricular/)[citation:Music & Performing Arts - St John's College Preston](https://www.stjohnspreston.vic.edu.au/co-curricular/music-performing-arts/). Students can also participate in the school band and debating [citation:Secondary School (Year 7 to Year 12) - St John's College Preston](https://www.stjohnspreston.vic.edu.au/learning/senior-school/). Additionally, the school has a contemporary/rock music program and an annual Arts Festival.

8) Preston is serviced by the Mernda train line, with Preston Station located on High Street. The train journey from Preston Station to Flinders Street Station takes approximately 24 minutes, with services running every 20 minutes [citation:Preston to Flinders Street Station - 4 ways to travel via train](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Preston-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
