1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Wantirna is a middle-to-upper-income, predominantly family-oriented suburb. According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income is $1,883, notably higher than the Victorian median of $1,759, and the median age is 41 years, older than the state median of 38 [citation:2021 Wantirna, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/211011259). The suburb has 4,111 families and a high rate of outright home ownership, with 90.1% of dwellings being houses, indicating a stable, white-collar, family-centric demographic [citation:Wantirna – SA2 211011259](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/wantirna).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** In Wantirna, the majority of residents were born in Australia; the 2021 Census shows 66.4% of people are Australian-born [citation:2021 Wantirna, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/211011259). The most common overseas country of birth is England (data not specified in available sources), while other significant overseas-born groups include people from China and India, but specific percentages for these were not found in the provided context.
