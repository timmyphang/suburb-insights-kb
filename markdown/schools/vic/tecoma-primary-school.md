1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Tecoma is a middle- to upper-middle-income suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,022 [citation:2021 Tecoma, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22476). The median age is 42, significantly higher than the national median of 38, indicating an older, established population [citation:2021 Tecoma, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22476). The area predominantly features families and couples, with 586 families recorded and an average of 1.8 children per family with children [citation:2021 Tecoma, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22476).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Tecoma is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 78.2% of residents born in Australia according to the 2021 Census [citation:2021 Tecoma, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22476). For overseas-born residents, the most common country of birth is England at 4.1% [citation:2021 Tecoma, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22476). No other foreign country of birth exceeds 1.5% of the population.

Groceries can be found at **Woolworths Belgrave** located at 1629 Burwood Highway, Belgrave, just a short drive away [citation:Supermarkets in Tecoma, VIC 3160](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/tecoma-vic-3160). The journey time is approximately **54 minutes** from Tecoma to Flinders Street, with services running every 30 minutes [citation:Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Tecoma). Driving from Tecoma to Melbourne CBD takes about 34 minutes [citation:Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Tecoma-Station).
