3) **Working-parent support:** Epping Primary School operates its own on-site Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) service [citation:Before & After School Care - Epping Views Primary School](https://www.eppingviewsps.vic.edu.au/page/11/Before-&-After-School-Care).
