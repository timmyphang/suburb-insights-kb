1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Grovedale is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,477 and a median age of 41 [citation:2021 Grovedale, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21114). The area shows a mix of white-collar and blue-collar residents, with a balanced family-versus-singles mix reflected in 4,011 families and 6,275 private dwellings [citation:2021 Grovedale, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21114).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Grovedale is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 64.4% of residents having both parents born in Australia and 18.3% having both parents born overseas [citation:2016 Grovedale, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21106). The most common overseas country of birth was not found in available 2021 sources, but 2016 data indicates England was a significant source [citation:2016 Grovedale, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/203021041).
