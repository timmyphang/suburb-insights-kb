Here’s a concise, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for East Preston Islamic College (EPIC), Preston VIC. I used one clear source per data point where possible; citations follow each fact.

1) Suburb socio-economic profile
- Preston is a mixed middle‑income inner‑north suburb (median household weekly income $1,844, slightly above the Victorian median of $1,759) with a median age of 37. [citation:ABS QuickStats — Preston 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22121)
- Occupations skew toward professionals and clerical/white‑collar roles (professionals are the largest group), with a significant rental market and diverse household types. [citation:LocalStats Preston profile](https://localstats.com.au/demographics/vic/melbourne/north-east/preston)

2) Most common overseas country of birth
- Preston is predominantly Australian‑born (about 84.0%); the largest individual overseas birthplaces are China and India (each about 3.9% in 2021) — no single large dominant overseas country. [citation:LocalStats Preston profile (ABS 2021-derived)](https://localstats.com.au/demographics/vic/melbourne/north-east/preston)

4) School culture — key extracurriculars
- EPIC lists a formal Soccer Academy (partnership coaching), and school sport programs including boxing and MMA. The school also runs Quran/Hifz and other Islamic education/co‑curriculum activities. [citation:EPIC Co‑curricular / sport pages](https://www.epic.vic.edu.au/co-curricular-activities/) 

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (2 sentences)
- Preston is a busy, multicultural inner‑north precinct with Preston Market as the local fresh‑food and cultural hub — good for weekly shopping and family errands. [citation:Preston Market official site](https://www.prestonmarket.com.au/)
- Specialty grocers include multicultural supermarkets and halal butchers (examples: Aladdin Mart on High Street and Preston Halal Meats), making it convenient for a range of ethnic food shopping. [citation:Preston Central — Aladdin Mart listing](https://prestoncentral.org.au/traders/aladdin-mart/) [citation:Halal listing - Preston Halal Meats](https://www.halalfood.com.au/halal-meat-listing/melbourne/preston-halal-meats)
