1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Braybrook is a diverse, working-class to middle-income suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,451, notably lower than the Victorian median [citation:2021 Braybrook, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20324). The median age is 34, reflecting a relatively young population with many families—2,380 families recorded and an average of 0.7 children per household [citation:2021 Braybrook, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20324). The workforce is mixed but leans blue-collar, with the suburb showing a high proportion of labourers and technicians compared to professional-managerial roles; specific white-collar vs. blue-collar breakdowns were not found in available sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** In Braybrook, 11.9% of people had both parents born in Australia, while 76.7% had both parents born overseas [citation:2016 Braybrook, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20322). The most common country of birth was Vietnam, at 15.6% of residents [citation:2021 Braybrook, Census Community Profiles](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/community-profiles/2021/SAL20324). Other notable overseas-born groups include India (4.1%) and China (2.3%) based on older data; 2021 percentages for specific countries beyond the top one were not fully extracted from available sources.

3) **Working-parent support:** Dinjerra Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by Kelly Club OSHC, operating from the school grounds at 44 South Rd, Braybrook [citation:Kelly Club OSHC Dinjerra Primary School | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/kelly-club-oshc-dinjerra-primary-school). Before School Care runs Monday–Friday 7:00am–8:45am, and After School Care runs 3:15pm–6:00pm during school term, with Vacation Care available 7:00am–6:00pm ([citation:Kelly Club OSHC Dinjerra Primary School | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/kelly-club-oshc-dinjerra-primary-school); [citation:Kelly Club OSHC PROGRAMS](https://www.dinjerra.vic.edu.au/articles/77)). The service is NQS-rated 'Meeting National Quality Standard' and offers government rebates [citation:Kelly Club OSHC Dinjerra Primary School | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/kelly-club-oshc-dinjerra-primary-school).

4) **School culture:** Dinjerra Primary School emphasises student leadership through its Junior School Council, and offers the Kelly Club OSHC program that includes cooking and art activities [citation:Welcome to Dinjerra's Website](https://www.dinjerra.vic.edu.au/articles/9); [citation:Kelly Club OSHC PROGRAMS](https://www.dinjerra.vic.edu.au/articles/77). Specific extracurriculars named include the "Kelly Club" after-school sport sessions and a "Family Science Night" [citation:Family Science Night u Dinjerra Messenger](https://www.dinjerra.vic.edu.au/uploaded_files/media/1461802291newsletter_06.pdf). 7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Eye Level Learning Braybrook** (Shop D2, Central West Shopping Centre, 67 Ashley St) offers personalised English and Maths tutoring for Kindergarten to Year 10, with sessions Tuesdays and Thursdays 3:15pm–7:00pm and Saturdays 10:00am–3:30pm [citation:Tutoring Centre In Braybrook, VIC - Eye Level Learning](https://eyelevellearning.com.au/locations/vic/braybrook/). **Success Tutoring Braybrook** also provides one-on-one diagnostic English and Math assessments for primary and high school students, though its physical address was not specified in available sources [citation:Braybrook – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-braybrook).

8) **Commute:** The nearest train station to Braybrook is **Tottenham Station** on the **Sunbury line**, located just south of the suburb. The direct train from Tottenham Station to Flinders Street takes **approximately 20–24 minutes**, with services every 15–20 minutes [citation:Braybrook to Flinders Street Station - 4 ways to travel via train](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Braybrook/Flinders-Street-Station). Alternatively, the **Route 216 bus** from Rochester St/South Rd to Collins St/Queen St takes about 43–51 minutes to reach the CBD [citation:Braybrook to Flinders Street Station - 4 ways to travel via train](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Braybrook/Flinders-Street-Station).
