Doveton College — short 8‑point briefing for families

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Doveton is a younger, lower‑income, family‑oriented suburb with a median age of 34 and many households with children; median weekly household income is around $1,260–$1,273 (below the Victorian median) [citation:2021 Doveton QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/212021295). The area is relatively working‑class with higher rental rates and observable socio‑economic disadvantage on SEIFA measures (local profiles note lower income percentiles) [citation:SEIFA 2021 release](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/people-and-communities/socio-economic-indexes-areas-seifa-australia/latest-release).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- The largest overseas‑born group referenced for Doveton is people born in Afghanistan (about ~11–12% of residents in 2021); a little over one‑third of residents are Australian‑born in the same profile [citation:Behind the Fence suburb profile summary / ABS QuickStats listing](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/doveton) [citation:2021 Doveton QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/212021295).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Doveton is a multicultural, family‑focused suburb with local parks, community services and basic retail strips rather than a large shopping centre; it’s practical and community‑oriented rather than upmarket [citation:2021 Doveton QuickStats / suburb profiles](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/212021295).  
- Notable local grocery options include Daily Halal Market (halal butcher/grocery), Friendly Grocer (Linden Place) and small greengrocers/rite‑way supermarkets on Autumn Place [citation:Daily Halal Market Doveton](https://newdhm.com.au/doveton/) [citation:Friendly Grocer Linden Place](https://friendlygrocer.com.au/linden-place-supermarket).
