1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Silvan is a middle-to-upper-income, family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,158, well above the Victorian median of $1,759, and the median age is 41, indicating an older population [citation:2021 Silvan, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22281). The suburb is predominantly composed of families, with 361 families counted and an average of 0.9 children per household [citation:2021 Silvan, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22281). Occupation breakdown is not available in the provided sources, but the high income and low unemployment suggestive of white-collar dominance.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Silvan is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 82.2% of residents born in Australia [citation:2021 Silvan, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22281). The most common overseas country of birth is the United Kingdom, though specific percentage is not available; the next most common are not listed in the snippet [citation:2021 Silvan, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22281).

3) **Working-parent support:** Silvan Primary School offers on-site Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) operated by the school council. Morning sessions run from 7:30 am to 8:45 am, and afternoon sessions run from 3:30 pm to 5:30 pm, Monday to Friday during school terms [citation:Silvan Primary OSHC](https://www.silvanps.vic.edu.au/page/31/Silvan-Primary-OSHC) [citation:Silvan P.S Outside School Hours Care | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/silvan-ps-outside-school-hours-care). The service has 15 approved places and is rated as Meeting the National Quality Standard [citation:Silvan P.S Outside School Hours Care | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/silvan-ps-outside-school-hours-care).

4) **School culture:** Silvan Primary School offers a range of named extracurricular activities, including the **Upper Yarra Primary Schools Sports Association** events (specifically kayaking, badminton, table tennis, archery) and a **Kitchen Garden Program** with cooking classes [citation:Our Programs](https://www.silvanps.vic.edu.au/page/12/Our-Programs). The school also runs a **Billy Cart Derby** and a **Restaurant Day** as part of its annual calendar [citation:Our Programs](https://www.silvanps.vic.edu.au/page/12/Our-Programs).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Silvan has a relaxed, semi-rural vibe with a strong community feel, known for its berry farms and natural attractions like Silvan Reservoir Park. For groceries, Silvan has a small general store, but the nearest major supermarkets are in nearby towns; no specialty/ethnic supermarkets are recorded within Silvan itself in the provided sources [citation:THE BEST Restaurants Near Silvan Reservoir Park - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/RestaurantsNear-g3373435-d9814530-Silvan_Reservoir_Park-Silvan_Victoria.html).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly takeaway and dining options in Silvan include **The Barn Owl** (Cafe, Australian) at 2.2 km from Silvan Reservoir Park, and **Pig & Whistle Tavern** (British, pub fare) in Olinda (4.6 km) [citation:THE BEST Restaurants Near Silvan Reservoir Park - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/RestaurantsNear-g3373435-d9814530-Silvan_Reservoir_Park-Silvan_Victoria.html). Additionally, **Tandoori Curry House** (Indian, BYO license) is located at 159 Monbulk Road, Silvan [citation:THE BEST 10 Restaurants in Silvan, Victoria](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/victoria/silvan/food-and-dining/restaurants/).

8) **Commute:** Silvan does not have a train station. The nearest station is **Lilydale** on the **Lilydale line**, approximately 8 km away. From Lilydale, the train to Flinders Street Station takes about 60–70 minutes [citation:Timetable Search | Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/timetables/). Alternatively, the **Route 663 bus** connects Silvan to Lilydale Station, with a travel time of about 15–20 minutes [citation:Victorian public transport timetables - Transport Victoria](https://transport.vic.gov.au/timetables).
