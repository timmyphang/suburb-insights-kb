1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Scoresby is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,167 and a median age of 40 years [citation:gdp.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/scoresby-vic). The suburb is predominantly composed of detached dwellings, with high rates of home ownership and stable employment [citation:Smart Property Investment](https://www.smartpropertyinvestment.com.au/data/vic/3179/scoresby). The population of 6,066 is split evenly between genders, reflecting a mature, settled community.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The most common overseas birthplace in Scoresby is China, accounting for 7.4% of residents [citation:gdp.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/scoresby-vic). The suburb is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 76.8% of residents born in Australia [citation:gdp.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/scoresby-vic). Other significant overseas-born groups include India (7.2%) and England (3.3%).

3) **Working-parent support**: Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) at Scoresby Primary School is provided by Camp Australia, offering before and after school care, pupil-free day care, and holiday programs [citation:Camp Australia](https://campaustralia.com.au/school/?accessCode=3DBA&doing_wp_cron=1733066562.8980040550231933593750). Specific operating hours are not specified in available sources, but the service is registered for before and after school care with a capacity of 30 children [citation:KindiCare](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3179/scoresby/camp-australia-scoresby-primary-school-oshc).

4) **School culture**: Scoresby Primary School offers a range of named extracurricular activities, including **Breakfast Club** (every Wednesday morning, 8:15–8:45am, providing cereal, toast, and fruit) [citation:Learning Beyond the Classroom](https://scoresbyps.vic.edu.au/our-students/learning-beyond-the-classroom/), **Running Club** (used as training for cross-country events) [citation:Learning Beyond the Classroom](https://scoresbyps.vic.edu.au/our-students/learning-beyond-the-classroom/), and the **Kitchen Garden** program [citation:Learning Beyond the Classroom](https://scoresbyps.vic.edu.au/our-students/learning-beyond-the-classroom/). The school also has **Inter School Sport**, **Instrumental Music**, and **School Productions** held every second year [citation:Learning Beyond the Classroom](https://scoresbyps.vic.edu.au/our-students/learning-beyond-the-classroom/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Scoresby is a quiet, family-friendly suburb with basic amenities. For groceries, a **Woolworths** supermarket is located at 1385 Ferntree Gully Road, and **Scoresby Convenience Store** at 82 Berrabri Drive offers everyday essentials [citation:Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/scoresby-vic-3179). For specialty ethnic groceries, **Prime Sun Asian Groceries** is at 1306 High Street Road, Wantirna South (nearby), and an Indian grocery store is also available in the area [citation:WOM](https://www.wordofmouth.com.au/vic/scoresby/groceries).

6) **Weeknight dining**: For casual family-friendly dining, **Scoresby Thai Restaurant** (10 Darryl St) offers authentic Thai cuisine including pad Thai, green curry, and spring rolls, with dine-in, takeaway, and delivery options [citation:Scoresby Thai](https://www.scoresbythai.com.au/). Additional takeaway options include pizza and Chinese cuisine available via delivery services, but specific restaurant names beyond Scoresby Thai are not found in available sources.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Nearby tutoring centres include **Kumon Wheelers Hill Education Centre** (190 Jells Road, Wheelers Hill) offering maths and English programs [citation:Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/tuition-tutoring/scoresby-vic-3179), and **Strive For Excellence Tutoring** (1593a Ferntree Gully Road, Knoxfield) providing academic support [citation:Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/tuition-tutoring/scoresby-vic-3179). **Alchemy Tuition** also offers in-home and online tutoring in Scoresby [citation:Alchemy Tuition](https://alchemytuition.com.au/melbourne-tutoring-scoresby/).

8) **Commute**: Scoresby does not have a train station. The best public transport option is to take the **bus route 693** from Scoresby to **Oakleigh Station**, then board the **Cranbourne/Pakenham line** train to **Flinders Street Station** in the Melbourne CBD. The total journey takes approximately **58 minutes** [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Scoresby/Flinders-Street-Station). Driving from Scoresby to Flinders Street takes about 22 minutes via the Monash Freeway [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Scoresby/Flinders-Street-Station).
