Trinity Grammar School (Kew) — short family briefing (primary)

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Kew is an affluent, largely white‑collar inner‑eastern suburb with high household incomes and older median age (median age 41; median weekly household income reported $2,497) — many professional/manager households and established families. [citation:2021 Kew (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21336) [citation:Home | City of Boroondara | Community profile - id](https://profile.id.com.au/boroondara)

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- Most residents are Australian‑born (about 66.1%); the largest overseas birthplace listed is China (~6.6%). [citation:Kew, VIC - Superb Suburb](https://superbsuburb.com.au/victoria/kew-3101/) [citation:Kew, Victoria - Wikiwand](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Kew%2C_Victoria)

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- Trinity runs an on‑site Outside School Hours Care / Before & After School Care service (listed with Extend and the national registers); vacation‑care available through the provider. [citation:Trinity Grammar School - Extend Before and After School Care](https://www.extend.com.au/school/trinity-grammar-school/) [citation:Trinity Grammar School OSHC-Extend - StartingBlocks.gov.au](https://www.startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01K7MHT6CF70012QDH6HXTY6H3/Trinity-Grammar-School-OSHC-Extend)

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars
- Music: extensive ensemble/instrumental program (many choirs, concert/jazz bands, strings/orchestra). [citation:Music - Trinity Grammar School](https://trinity.vic.edu.au/school-life/cocurricular/music/)  
- Sport: school sports program with interschool teams and PE pathways. [citation:Sport - Trinity Grammar School](https://trinity.vic.edu.au/school-life/cocurricular/sport/)  
- Drama / co‑curricular clubs also offered as part of the broader co‑curricular program. [citation:Co-curricular Life | Trinity Grammar School, Kew](https://trinity.vic.edu.au/school-life/cocurricular/)

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Kew Junction is a leafy, village‑style shopping strip popular with families — cafes, specialty food shops and local services are concentrated along High Street. [citation:Kew Junction | City of Boroondara](https://www.boroondara.vic.gov.au/explore-boroondara/markets-and-shopping/kew-junction)  
- Notable local grocers/specialty food shops include Toscano’s (fresh produce/Italian deli), De Maria’s Grocer and a local IGA; nearby specialty and deli options make fortnightly shopping easy for families. [citation:Toscano's - QUALITY FRUITERERS - Kew/Richmond/Toorak](https://toscanos.com.au/) [citation:Fruits, Vegetables, Italian Grocery, Deli & Artisan Cheese - Kew](https://www.demariasgrocer.com.au/areas-we-serve/kew/) [citation:IGA Local Grocer Kew](https://www.iga.com.au/stores/iga-local-grocer-kew/)

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 casual, family‑friendly options
- Vicky’s Italian Restaurant & Pizza (family‑friendly pizzas and takeaway). [citation:Dine-in or Takeaway | Vickys Italian Restaurant + Pizza](https://vickys.com.au/)  
- Kew Junction takeaway strip offers a variety of casual options (Asian and other takeaways such as Mama Thanh / Hot Pot House listed in local directory). [citation:Take Away Archives - Kew Junction](https://kewjunction.com.au/business-sector/take-away/)  
- Several cafés on High Street also provide easy takeaway dinners for families. [citation:Eat & Drink Archives - Kew Junction](https://kewjunction.com.au/business-category/eat-drink/)

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 local centres)

8) Commute — train / tram estimate to Melbourne CBD
- Kew does not have a Metro train station; the realistic public‑transport option is tram route 48 (High Street/Kew → city). Typical tram travel from Kew/High Street into the CBD to the Flinders Street/Collins St area is about 25–30 minutes (allow extra time for stops/traffic).
