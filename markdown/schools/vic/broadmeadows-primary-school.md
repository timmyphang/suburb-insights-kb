Families make up a significant portion, with 2,790 families recorded and an average of 2.9 people per household [citation:2021 Broadmeadows (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20346).

3) **Working-parent support:** Broadmeadows Primary School provides Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) run by CommunityOSH, operating after school from 3:30 pm to 6:00 pm [citation:OSHC - Broadmeadows Primary School](https://bps.vic.edu.au/oshc.html). The program offers before and after school care, with holiday programs also available, and families can claim the Child Care Subsidy [citation:OSHC - Broadmeadows Primary School](https://bps.vic.edu.au/oshc.html). Contact details for the program are provided, including a phone number of 0477 156 110 [citation:OSHC - Broadmeadows Primary School](https://bps.vic.edu.au/oshc.html).

4) **School culture:** Broadmeadows Primary School encourages students to "Aim High" and has an eSmart accreditation from the Alannah & Madeline Foundation [citation:Broadmeadows Primary School - Home](https://bps.vic.edu.au/); [citation:eSmart - Broadmeadows Primary School](https://bps.vic.edu.au/esmart.html). The school offers specific programs such as a Student Leadership program (awarding "Community Leaders" badges) [citation:Awards - Broadmeadows Primary School](http://www.bps.vic.edu.au/learning-behaviours/) and overnight camps at Camp Doxa for students in grades 3-6 [citation:Senior School - Broadmeadows Primary School](https://bps.vic.edu.au/senior-school.html). These activities are part of the school's focus on community, sport, and the arts.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Broadmeadows is a culturally diverse suburb with several specialty grocers catering to its multicultural population. Notably, Broadmeadows Phil-Asian Grocer & Mixed Business on Railway Crescent offers a wide range of Filipino and Asian products [citation:Broadmeadows Phil-Asian Grocer & Mixed Business - Broadmeadows - Nextdoor](https://au.nextdoor.com/pages/broadmeadows-phil-asian-grocer-mixed-business/). For general grocery needs, the suburb has major supermarkets including a Woolworths at Broadmeadows Shopping Centre.
