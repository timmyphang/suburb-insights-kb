1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
Berwick is a middle‑income, family‑oriented outer‑suburban area with a median household weekly income of about $2,113 and median age ~38. It’s primarily white‑collar/professional with many families and home‑owning households rather than a high-density singles precinct [citation:ABS QuickStats – Berwick 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20224).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
The majority of residents are Australian‑born (around 62%); the largest single overseas birthplace is India (about 6% of residents) in the 2021 Census data for Berwick [citation:ABS QuickStats – Berwick 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20224).
