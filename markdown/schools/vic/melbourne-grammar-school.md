Quick note: you asked for “Melbourne, Melbourne” primary — Melbourne Grammar’s primary campus is Grimwade House (Prep–Year 6) on Balaclava Rd in the Caulfield / Caulfield North area; I’ve used that campus/suburb for the suburb-specific items below and noted sources. Now the eight‑point family briefing (snippet‑first).

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile
- Caulfield North (Grimwade House area) has a median age ~37 and median weekly household income $2,205 — a relatively affluent, white‑collar suburb with a high share of professionals and managers (large proportion working in professional occupations). [citation:2021 Caulfield North QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20523) [citation:Caulfield North demographics (occupations)](https://localstats.com.au/demographics/vic/melbourne/bayside/caulfield-north).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- About 59% of residents are Australian‑born; ABS QuickStats lists the top overseas birthplaces for Caulfield North with South Africa, China and England among the leading overseas countries of birth (no single overwhelmingly dominant foreign birthplace). [citation:2021 Caulfield North QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20523) [citation:2016 Caulfield North QuickStats historic top responses](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20520).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- Grimwade House runs an approved Before/After School Care and Vacation Care (OSHC / Grimwade Club) on campus for Grimwade students. [citation:Grimwade House OSHC page](https://mgs.vic.edu.au/learning/campuses/grimwade-house/outside-classroom/and-after-school-care) [citation:StartingBlocks service listing](https://www.startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01HGCPSFYZ4BS6JC1FJSNQEGJY/Melbourne-Grammar-School-Grimwade-House).

4) School culture — extracurriculars (2–3 examples)
- Grimwade Club / OSHC offers an extended activities programme (over 40 activities) and the campus runs structured sport teams, an instrumental/music program and specialist activities including swimming, skateboarding and coding. [citation:Grimwade House overview](https://mgs.vic.edu.au/learning/campuses/grimwade-house) [citation:Grimwade Club / Outside the classroom](https://mgs.vic.edu.au/learning/campuses/grimwade-house/outside-classroom).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (2 sentences)
- Caulfield North is family‑oriented around Caulfield Park (multiple playgrounds, sports facilities) and local services such as Caulfield Library — comfortable, green inner‑suburban feel close to the city. [citation:Caulfield Park (Glen Eira)](https://www.gleneira.vic.gov.au/our-city/parks-and-playgrounds/caulfield-park) [citation:Caulfield Library](https://library.gleneira.vic.gov.au/about-us/locations-and-hours/caulfield-library).
- Grocery options nearby include mainstream supermarkets (Woolworths/Coles on Orrong Rd / Caulfield Plaza) plus specialty/ethnic shops and kosher/Asian grocers serving the local community (e.g., Tang Food Emporium, local kosher food listings). [citation:Woolworths Caulfield listing](https://woolworths.supermarketlocationmaps.com/en/location/21974-woolies-360-orrong-rd) [citation:Tang Food Emporium](https://www.tangfoodemporium.com.au/) [citation:Caulfield synagogue visitors/kosher listings](https://www.caulfieldshule.com.au/visitors).
