Below is a concise, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Darley Primary School (Darley, VIC). Each item is snippet‑first with a supporting citation.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)  
Darley is a family‑oriented suburb (median age 37) with a substantial share of children (5–14 years higher than state averages) and a median weekly household income of about $1,951 — consistent with a middle‑income, family suburb on the edge of Bacchus Marsh [citation:2021 Darley QuickStats (ABS)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20712).

2) Most common foreign country of birth  
Darley is overwhelmingly Australian‑born; the most common overseas birthplace reported is England (around ~3% of residents in suburb summaries) [citation:2021 Darley QuickStats (ABS)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20712) [citation:Darley VIC Suburb Profile — Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/darley).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
Darley Primary hosts an on‑site TheirCare program providing Before School Care (from ~6:00am), After School Care (until 6:00pm) and Holiday Care — families enroll via TheirCare [citation:TheirCare - Darley Primary School](https://darleyprimary.vic.edu.au/their-care/).

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars  
The school runs weekly Performing Arts for all students (music, dance, drama) and senior students (Years 5–6) participate in interschool sports including netball, AFL and soccer; the school also highlights STEM activities and regional athletics participation in newsletters [citation:Teaching & Learning - Darley Primary School](https://darleyprimary.vic.edu.au/teaching-learning/) [citation:Darley Primary newsletter (May 2026)](https://darleyprimary.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/22.05.26-Compressed-1.pdf).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)  
Darley has a small‑town, market and community feel (regular Darley Market for fresh produce) with everyday shopping served locally; the main supermarket is the local IGA (Darley/Bacchus Marsh) and there are nearby specialist ethnic grocers (e.g., AD Bacchus Marsh Supermarket for Sri Lankan/Indian/Asian groceries) [citation:Champions IGA Bacchus Marsh (IGA)](https://www.iga.com.au/stores/champions-iga-bacchus-marsh/) [citation:Darley Market overview (Welcome to Moorabool)](https://welcometomoorabool.com/darley-market-bacchus-marsh) [citation:AD Bacchus Marsh Supermarket (directory)](https://www.cylex-australia.com/company/ad-bacchus-marsh-supermarket---sri-lankan--indian---asian-grocery-store-25013524.html).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 family‑friendly options  
Local, casual family options include Darley Fish & Chips (classic takeaway), Darley General Store & Cafe (cafe/takeaway) and Darley Bakehouse (bakehouse/cafe) — good for quick family dinners and takeaway [citation:Darley Fish & Chips (Tripadvisor)](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g552131-d27675441-Reviews-Darley_Fish_Chips-Bacchus_Marsh_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html) [citation:Darley General Store & Cafe (Tripadvisor)](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g552131-d13372847-Reviews-Darley_general_store_CAFE-Bacchus_Marsh_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html) [citation:Darley Bakehouse (RestaurantGuru)](https://restaurantguru.com/Darley-Bakehouse-Bacchus-Marsh-Victoria-2).

7) Tuition and enrichment nearby  
Local options include Darley Neighbourhood House & Learning Centre (community learning programs) and small local tutors such as Bacchus Marsh / Darley maths tutoring services that advertise primary‑level tuition [citation:Darley Neighbourhood House & Learning Centre](https://www.darleylearning.com/) [citation:Bacchus Marsh Maths Tutoring (listing)](https://www.dlook.com.au/business/tutor-in-darley-vic-3340/bacchus-marsh-maths-tutoring).

8) Commute — train time to Melbourne CBD (note: Darley has no train station)  
Darley itself has no V/Line train station; families typically catch the V/Line from nearby Bacchus Marsh Station (reachable by local bus), then train to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street/Southern Cross) in roughly 50–65 minutes depending on the service — plan for an additional ~10–15 minute bus or drive from Darley to Bacchus Marsh station [citation:433 Maddingley - Darley via Bacchus Marsh Station (bus route)](https://transport.vic.gov.au/route/15892/433-maddingley-darley-via-bacchus-marsh-station) [citation:Bacchus Marsh Station (Transport Victoria)](https://transport.vic.gov.au/stop/4684/bacchus-marsh-station/3/regional-train) [citation:Bacchus Marsh to Flinders Street (Rome2rio)](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Bacchus-Marsh/Flinders-Street-Station).
