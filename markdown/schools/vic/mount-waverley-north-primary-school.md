Quick, family-facing eight-point briefing — Mount Waverley North Primary School (Mount Waverley, VIC)

1) Suburb socio-economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Mount Waverley is predominantly middle-to-affluent, with high household incomes and many professionals / white‑collar workers — median weekly household income ~$2,066 (2021) which is above the Victorian median. [citation:2021 Mount Waverley QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21816)  
- Local SEIFA/area profiles also show relatively low disadvantage in the City of Monash (Mount Waverley ranks among the less disadvantaged small areas) [citation:SEIFA by profile area | City of Monash](https://profile.id.com.au/monash/seifa-disadvantage-small-area).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- China is the largest single overseas country of birth recorded for Mount Waverley in recent Census outputs (China was the top overseas birthplace in 2016; 2021 QuickStats list country‑of‑birth data for the suburb) [citation:2016 Mount Waverley QuickStats (China 11.3% in 2016)](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21804) [citation:2021 Mount Waverley QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21816).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- The school hosts an on‑site OSHC program run by TeamKids offering Before School Care, After School Care and Vacation Care (CCS‑approved). [citation:Mount Waverley North Primary OSHC (school page)](https://www.mtwavnth.vic.edu.au/enrolments/out-of-school-hours-care-oshc/) [citation:TeamKids Mt Waverley North listing](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/mount-waverley-north-primary-oshc-5/).

4) School culture — 2–3 named extracurriculars
- Instrumental/private music tuition and an annual whole‑school community concert / performing‑arts program. [citation:Performing Arts & Music - Mount Waverley North Primary](https://www.mtwavnth.vic.edu.au/curriculum/specialist-programs/performing-arts-music/)  
- Team sports / weekly Physical Education with team opportunities and a Fitness Club; school camps program. [citation:Sports Program - Mount Waverley North Primary](https://www.mtwavnth.vic.edu.au/curriculum/beyond-the-classsroom/sports-program/) [citation:Beyond the Classsroom](https://www.mtwavnth.vic.edu.au/curriculum/beyond-the-classsroom/).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Mount Waverley is a family-oriented suburb with parks, playgrounds and a suburban shopping strip (Mount Waverley Village / Hamilton Place area) that supports local family life. [citation:2021 Mount Waverley QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21816)  
- Local grocery/ethnic options include TK Asian Supermarket in the village and Indian grocery K J L Spice Corner, alongside major supermarkets (Woolworths/Coles) nearby. [citation:TK Asian Supermarket listing](https://mountwaverley3149.com.au/stores/tk-asian-supermarket/) [citation:K J L Spice Corner listing](https://www.courtesyindia.com/resource-details/indian-grocery-store/australia/victoria/mt-waverley/k-j-l-spice-corner) [citation:Woolworths Mount Waverley listing](https://woolworths.supermarketlocationmaps.com/en/location/21997-woolies-64-virginia-street).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 family-friendly options (call out cuisines)
- Rossini’s Pizzaria — pizza/Italian, family takeaway/dine-in. [citation:Rossini's Pizzaria](https://rossinispizzaria.com.au/)  
- Piccolino Express — takeaway pizzas/pastas (family deals). [citation:Piccolino Express (takeaway)](https://www.piccolinoexpress.com.au/)  
- Proud Peacock — Vietnamese / Southeast Asian casual dining and takeaway. [citation:Proud Peacock](https://proudpeacock.com.au/)

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 centres)
- Math Minds — local maths tuition / learning centre in Mount Waverley. [citation:Math Minds Mount Waverley](https://mathminds.com.au/maths-tutor-mount-waverley/)  
- Kumon Mount Waverley — maths & English Kumon education centre (local centre listing). [citation:Kumon Mount Waverley](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-MOUNT-WAVERLEY-VIC)

8) Commute — train time to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)
- Mount Waverley is on the Glen Waverley line; a direct train from Mount Waverley to Flinders Street typically takes about 30–35 minutes (typical direct services ~30 minutes). [citation:Rome2rio example times (Mount Waverley → Flinders Street)](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Mount-Waverley/Flinders-Street-Station) [citation:Glen Waverley Line — Metro Trains / timetable info](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/lines/glen-waverley/).
