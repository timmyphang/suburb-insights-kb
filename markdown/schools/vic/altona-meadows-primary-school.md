1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Altona Meadows is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb. The 2021 Census shows a median weekly household income of $1,573 and a median age of 41, indicating a slightly older family demographic [citation:2021 Altona Meadows, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/213021342). The suburb has a predominantly suburban housing profile with above-average rates of outright home ownership [citation:Altona Meadows – SA2 213021342 | AreaSearch](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/altona_meadows). Occupation data specific to white-collar vs. blue-collar splits was not found in available sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** In the 2016 Census (the most recent data available for country of birth), the most common overseas country of birth was India at 4.2%, followed by England at 2.6% [citation:2016 Altona Meadows, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20035). Overall, 31.4% of people had both parents born in Australia, and 51.3% had both parents born overseas [citation:2016 Altona Meadows, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20035).

3) **Working-parent support:** Altona Meadows Primary School partners with Camp Australia to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) [citation:OSHC - CAMP AUSTRALIA | Altona Meadows Primary School](https://www.amps.vic.edu.au/ohsc-camp-australia). The service operates before school from 7:00 am to 8:45 am and after school, providing care on pupil-free days and during school holidays [citation:Camp Australia - Altona Meadows Primary School OSHC - Altona Meadows 3028](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3028/altona-meadows/camp-australia-altona-meadows-primary-school-oshc).

4) **School culture:** Altona Meadows Primary School offers a range of specific extracurriculars. A 'Just Brass' band program, run in partnership with the Salvation Army, provides brass instrument tuition and weekly after-school band practice [citation:PERFORMING ARTS | Altona Meadows Primary School](https://www.amps.vic.edu.au/performing-arts). The school also runs Breakfast and Fruit Clubs, and a Stephanie Alexander Kitchen Garden Program [citation:Altona Meadows Primary School - Melbourne](https://www.amps.vic.edu.au/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Altona Meadows has a suburban, family-friendly vibe with local amenities concentrated around Central Square Shopping Centre on Merton Street. Major supermarkets include Coles and IGA Friendly Grocers within the suburb [citation:Supermarkets in Altona Meadows, VIC 3028 — All Stores & Hours | Yellow Pages®](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/altona-meadows-vic-3028). For specialty groceries, there are Indian grocery stores such as Laverton Spices (16 Lohse Street, Laverton) and India Spice Corner (7 Borrack Sq, Altona North) nearby [citation:Indian Grocery Stores Altona, VIC](https://singh.com.au/indian-grocery-stores-altona-vic/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** La Porchetta Altona Meadows (Central Square Shopping Centre) offers family-friendly Italian dining with a kids' play area and takeaway [citation:Altona Meadows – La Porchetta](https://laporchetta.com.au/altonameadows/). Akshaya Tiffins And Biryanis (42 Alma Avenue) serves Indian cuisine for takeaway and delivery [citation:Altona Meadows 3028 Food Delivery and Takeaway VIC | ozfoodhunter.com.au](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/altona-meadows-3028). The Spice Yard (75 Pier St, Altona) is another nearby Indian restaurant offering takeaway [citation:Altona Meadows 3028 Food Delivery and Takeaway VIC | ozfoodhunter.com.au](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/altona-meadows-3028).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Innate Tutoring provides private tuition in Altona Meadows, offering VCE tutoring in subjects such as Maths, English, Biology, and Chemistry [citation:Tutoring Altona Meadows | A Leading Tutors at Innate Tutoring](https://www.innatetutoring.com.au/tutoring-altona-meadows/). Kip McGrath offers online English and maths tutoring for primary and secondary students, with live sessions from home [citation:Altona Meadows Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/altona-meadows).

8) **Commute:** Altona Meadows is not directly served by a train station. The nearest station is Altona Station on the Werribee line, approximately 3 km away. From Altona Station, the train journey to Flinders Street Station takes about 33 minutes [citation:Train Altona to Flinders Street Station from $3 | Tickets & Timetables | Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Altona-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). Alternatively, bus routes 411 and 412 connect Altona Meadows to Altona and Laverton stations [citation:Flinders Street Station to Altona Meadows - 5 ways to travel via , and train](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Altona-Meadows).
