1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Endeavour Hills is a middle‑income, family‑focused suburb. The 2021 Census recorded a median weekly household income of $1,740 and a median age of 39, with 6,842 families and an average of 1.8 children per family [citation:2021 Endeavour Hills, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20871). The socio‑economic index (IRSAD) is 990, indicating a solid middle‑band profile, and the suburb has a mix of trades, white‑collar, and service workers [citation:Endeavour Hills, VIC 3802 Property Market and House Prices 2026](https://www.htag.com.au/vic/vic213-casey-city/endeavour-hills-vic-3802/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** In 2021, the most common overseas country of birth among Endeavour Hills residents was India, at 8.5% of the population. The proportion born in Australia was 59.8% [citation:2021 Endeavour Hills, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20871). Other notable overseas birthplaces include Sri Lanka (5.5%) and the Philippines (2.1%).

3) **Working‑parent support:** Mossgiel Park Primary School partners with Camp Australia to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC). The before‑school program runs 7:00 am – 8:45 am Monday–Friday, and the after‑school program runs until 6:00 pm (end time stated on the Camp Australia site) [citation:OHSC – Camp Australia – Mossgiel Park Primary School](https://mossgielparkps.vic.edu.au/ohs/); [citation:Camp Australia - Mossgiel Park Primary School OSHC](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3802/endeavour-hills/camp-australia-mossgiel-park-primary-school-oshc). Pupil‑free day care is also available.

4) **School culture:** Mossgiel Park Primary School offers a range of named extracurricular activities. These include a **Choir**, an **African Drumming** program, a **Coding Club**, a **Soccer Club**, and a **Board Games Club** [citation:Specialist Programs – Mossgiel Park Primary School](https://mossgielparkps.vic.edu.au/specialist-programs/). Students can also join the **Library Club** and the **Maths Club** [citation:Specialist Programs – Mossgiel Park Primary School](https://mossgielparkps.vic.edu.au/specialist-programs/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Endeavour Hills has a community‑oriented, suburban feel with parks and a large shopping centre. For grocery shopping, the **Endeavour Hills Shopping Centre** houses Coles, Woolworths, Aldi, and Kmart [citation:Endeavour Hills Shopping Centre](https://www.endeavourhillsshopping.com.au/). Specialty options include **BestMart Asian Grocer** (located next to Coles), which offers a wide range of authentic Asian ingredients [citation:BestMart Asian Grocer - Endeavour Hills Shopping Centre](https://www.endeavourhillsshopping.com.au/stores/bestmart-asian-grocer/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Families can choose from several casual takeaway options within the shopping centre. Named restaurants include **Dancing Wok** (Chinese), **La Calabrese** (Italian/pizza), and **Subway** [citation:Dining & Takeaway - Endeavour Hills Shopping Centre](https://www.endeavourhillsshopping.com.au/dining-takeaway/). For fish and chips, **Heatherton Village Fish & Chips** delivers within the suburb [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Endeavour Hills, VIC 3803](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/endeavour-hills-vic-3803).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Lynn’s Learning Endeavour Hills** provides personalised maths and English tutoring for Foundation to Year 10, plus Selective & Scholarship preparation for Years 3–9. Sessions run Monday afternoons and Saturday early afternoons at the Endeavour Hills Neighbourhood Centre [citation:Endeavour Hills | English Tuition | Maths Tuition | Lynn's Learning](https://lynnslearning.com.au/locations/victoria-locations/lynns-learning-endeavour-hills-tuition-centre/). **Success Tutoring Endeavour Hills** also offers one‑on‑one and small‑group support [citation:Endeavour Hills – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-endeavour-hills).

8) **Commute:** Endeavour Hills does not have its own train station. The most efficient public‑transport route is to catch the **Bus 843** from Endeavour Hills Shopping Centre to **Dandenong Station**, then board the **Cranbourne/Pakenham line** train to **Flinders Street Station**. Total travel time is approximately **1 hour 14 minutes** [citation:Endeavour Hills to Flinders Street Station - 4 ways to travel](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Endeavour-Hills/Flinders-Street-Station). Driving takes about 26 minutes.
