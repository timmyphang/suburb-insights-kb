1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Blackburn South is an established, middle-to-upper-income residential area. The 2021 Census recorded a median weekly household income of $1,861 and a median age of 42, which is older than the Victorian average of 38 [citation:2021 Blackburn South, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/207031162). The suburb is primarily composed of families (2,933 families) living in detached houses (75.7%), indicating a strong family-oriented, white-collar demographic [citation:Blackburn South – SA2 207031162 | AreaSearch](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/blackburn_south).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The available data does not provide the dominant overseas country of birth for Blackburn South specifically. The 2021 QuickStats for Blackburn South omits the Country of Birth table in the provided context [citation:2021 Blackburn South, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/207031162). The 2016 Census for Blackburn South showed that 65.1% of people spoke only English at home, with Mandarin (9.6%) and Cantonese (4.9%) being the most common languages other than English [citation:2016 Blackburn South, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20253).

3) **Working-parent support:** St Luke the Evangelist School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) on its grounds at 46 Orchard Grove, Blackburn South [citation:St Luke the Evangelist Outside School Hours Care, Blackburn South | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/st-luke-evangelist-outside-school-hours-care-blackburn-south). The service is operated by MELBOURNE ARCHDIOCESE CATHOLIC SCHOOLS EARLY YEARS EDUCATION LTD (MACSEYE) and is branded as Kelly Club St Luke's the Evangelist Catholic Primary School [citation:Kelly Club St Luke's the Evangelist Catholic Primary School | Out-of-school Care](https://www.kellyclub.com.au/stlukes). During school term, before-school care runs from 7:00-8:50 am and after-school care from 3:25-6:00 pm, Monday to Friday [citation:St Luke the Evangelist Outside School Hours Care, Blackburn South | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/st-luke-evangelist-outside-school-hours-care-blackburn-south).

4) **School culture:** St Luke the Evangelist School offers a range of named extracurricular activities. Lunchtime clubs include **Lego Club**, **Yoga Club**, and **Art Club**, which are run by senior students [citation:Our Students | Stlukeevangelist](https://www.slblackburnsth.catholic.edu.au/ourstudents). The school also runs a **Social Justice Program** (**Mini Vinnies**) and provides **Instrumental Music Lessons** (keyboard, singing, drums, or ukulele) with an external provider [citation:Dynamic & Engaged | Catholic Primary School Blackburn](https://www.slblackburnsth.catholic.edu.au/dynamicengaged). Students can also participate in **Rosellas Netball** [citation:Dynamic & Engaged | Catholic Primary School Blackburn](https://www.slblackburnsth.catholic.edu.au/dynamicengaged).
