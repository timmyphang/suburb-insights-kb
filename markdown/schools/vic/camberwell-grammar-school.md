1) Suburb socio-economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Canterbury is an affluent, inner‑east suburb with a high median weekly household income (~$2,877) and high median mortgage repayments (~$3,500/month), and an older median age (46) — signalling established, family‑oriented, white‑collar households. [citation:2021 Canterbury (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20468)

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- The most common overseas birthplace in Canterbury is China (around 10% of residents); a majority of residents are Australian‑born but China is the largest single overseas birthplace in the suburb. [citation:2021 Canterbury (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20468) [citation:Canterbury VIC Suburb Profile — Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/canterbury)

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)

- Local grocery options include an IGA on Maling Road and speciality delis/provender shops (e.g., Wilson & Walsh, local cheese/deli cafes).
