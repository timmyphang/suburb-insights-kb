1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Coolaroo is a working-class suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,025 and a median age of 37, slightly younger than the national median [citation:2021 Coolaroo QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20624). The population of 3,193 is family-oriented, with 778 families and an average household size of 2.9 [citation:2021 Coolaroo QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20624). Employment data shows 19.2% work full-time and 15.3% part-time, with a 5.2% unemployment rate, reflecting a blue-collar local economy [citation:GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/coolaroo-vic).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Coolaroo is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 85.9% of residents born in Australia [citation:GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/coolaroo-vic). Among overseas-born residents, the most common country of birth is India at 6.5%, followed by Egypt (1.7%) and England (1.5%) [citation:GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/coolaroo-vic).

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care at Coolaroo South Primary School is provided by OSHClub, offering an after-school program called "Stay and Play" from bell time until 6:00 PM during term [citation:OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/coolaroo-south-primary-school/). Before-school care times are not specified in available sources; please contact the school for full hours.

4) **School culture:** Coolaroo South Primary School offers a Spanish language program for Foundation to Year 6, taught by Señor Toni through music and activities, along with weekly Arabic immersion classes for mother-tongue speakers [citation:Languages](http://www.coolaroosouthps.vic.edu.au/teaching-learning/languages/). In sport, students participate in inter-school competitions in netball, soccer, and cricket, plus annual athletics and cross-country events, with an after-school sports program also available [citation:Physical and Sport Education](https://www.coolaroosouthps.vic.edu.au/teaching-learning/physical-and-sport-education/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Coolaroo has a strong community feel with nearby parks and local shops. For groceries, residents can shop at Guru Bazaar, an Indian grocery store located at 3/30 Maffra Street, Coolaroo, offering spices, religious items, and everyday essentials [citation:Singh.com.au](https://singh.com.au/indian-grocery-stores-in-melbourne/). Tales of India provides online Indian grocery delivery to Coolaroo with same-day service for orders over $49 [citation:Tales of India](https://talesofindia.com.au/pages/indian-grocery-store-coolaroo-3048).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family-friendly meals, Kolapasi Indian Canteen at 1350 Pascoe Vale Road offers authentic South Indian comfort bowls (e.g., biryani, parotta) with no MSG, served quickly for takeaway [citation:Kolapasi](https://kolapasi.com.au/coolaroo/). The Coolaroo Hotel Bistro on Pascoe Vale Road serves pub-style meals in a family-friendly environment, with a dedicated dining area [citation:Coolaroo Hotel Dining](https://www.coolaroohotel.com.au/dining). Delivery options like My Mate's Pizza - Meadow Heights also service the area via Uber Eats [citation:Uber Eats](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/coolaroo-melbourne-vic).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Success Tutoring Coolaroo, located at Suite 307/1510 Pascoe Vale Road, offers personalised tutoring in Math and English aligned to the Victorian Curriculum, with flexible in-centre and online sessions [citation:Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/blog/success-tutoring-opens-in-coolaroo-to-support-local-students). The centre provides tailored learning plans and assessments for primary and secondary students [citation:Coolaroo Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/coolaroo-tutoring).

8) **Commute:** Coolaroo is served by Coolaroo station on the Craigieburn train line, with direct trains to Flinders Street Station running every 20 minutes [citation:Metro Trains Coolaroo](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/coolaroo/). The exact travel time to Melbourne CBD is not specified in available sources, but the route is a direct service and typical journey time is under 40 minutes (estimated). The station is in Zone 2 [citation:Metro Trains Coolaroo](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/coolaroo/).
