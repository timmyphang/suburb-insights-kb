Briefing: St Lawrence of Brindisi Catholic Primary School — Weir Views (for families)
(Strict, fact-first eight points; sources immediately after each fact.)

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Weir Views is a new, fast‑growing estate suburb with a very young median age (27) and a high share of children and young adults — consistent with many young families moving into new housing developments [citation:2021 Weir Views QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22740) and the City of Melton community profile [citation:Community Profile – Weir Views (Melton City Council)](https://www.melton.vic.gov.au/files/assets/public/v/1/council/about-the-city/demographics/community-profiles/community-profile-weir-views.pdf). Overall it reads as a family‑focused, middle‑income suburban estate rather than an established affluent inner suburb [citation:2021 Weir Views QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22740).

2) Most common overseas country of birth  
India is the most common overseas birthplace listed for Weir Views residents (top non‑Australian birthplace in 2021; roughly ~11% reported India as birthplace in local summaries) — Australia is still the single largest birthplace overall. Sources: ABS / local profile summaries [citation:2021 Weir Views QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22740) and Melton community profile [citation:Community Profile – Weir Views (Melton City Council)](https://www.melton.vic.gov.au/files/assets/public/v/1/council/about-the-city/demographics/community-profiles/community-profile-weir-views.pdf).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
Yes — there is on‑site Outside School Hours Care at the school (St Lawrence of Brindisi Children’s Hub / Kelly Club OSHC). The ACECQA National Register entry shows Before, After and Vacation Care with an approved 45 places and published term/holiday hours [citation:St Lawrence of Brindisi Children's Hub - ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/kelly-club-oshc-st-lawrence-brindisi-catholic-primary-school).

4) School culture — extracurriculars (2–3 specific offerings)  
- School sports / physical education programs and school sport events (school lists a Sports & PE teacher and runs programs such as AFL AusKick) [citation:Staff / Latest News — St Lawrence of Brindisi](https://www.slweirviews.catholic.edu.au/).  
- Performing arts / music — the school advertises a Performing Arts teacher and performing‑arts activities [citation:Staff — St Lawrence of Brindisi](https://www.slweirviews.catholic.edu.au/staff/).  
- Facilities and planned community sports infrastructure (oval, multi‑use synthetic soccer pitch and future indoor gymnasium) support organised sport and school carnivals [citation:Our Facilities — St Lawrence of Brindisi](https://www.slweirviews.catholic.edu.au/our-facilities/).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)  
Weir Views feels like a new family suburb centred on a small local shopping hub (Opalia Plaza) and playgrounds/parks — everyday shopping is local while larger retail is in neighbouring Caroline Springs / Melton [citation:Opalia Plaza stores / Opalia Shopping Centre](https://opaliaplaza.com.au/stores/, https://opaliaweirviews.com.au/opalia-shopping-centre/). For specialty/ethnic groceries, nearby Caroline Springs and local listings show Indian/South Asian grocery options (e.g., The Indian Superstore at WestSprings Central and local independent Indian supermarkets) serving the area’s South Asian community [citation:The Indian Superstore - WestSprings Central](https://www.westspringscentral.com.au/find-a-shop/the-indian-superstore) [citation:Yes Yes Indian Supermarket (local listing)](https://www.mapquest.com/au/victoria/yes-yes-indian-supermarket-794829508).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 casual family options  
Local, family‑friendly options at Opalia Plaza include McDonald’s and KFC for quick takeaway, plus Okami Japanese (all‑you‑can‑eat style) and The Jolly Miller cafe for casual meals/takeaway [citation:Opalia Plaza stores / Okami Japanese (Opalia Plaza)](https://opaliaplaza.com.au/stores/, https://opaliaplaza.com.au/storecategory/okami-japanese-restaurant/).

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 nearby centres)  
Nearby tutoring/enrichment options are in Caroline Springs: Kumon Caroline Springs North (Kumon centre) and local private providers such as Caroline Springs Tutoring and Brainworks (Caroline Springs) offering maths & English tutoring and small‑group/one‑to‑one support [citation:Kumon Caroline Springs North](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-CAROLINE-SPRINGS-NORTH-VIC) [citation:Caroline Springs Tutoring](https://carolinespringstutoring.com.au/) [citation:Brainworks Caroline Springs](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-caroline-springs).

8) Commute — realistic public‑transport estimate to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)  
Weir Views has no rail station of its own; typical public‑transport commutes use a local bus to Melton or Cobblebank and then a V/Line or suburban train into the city. Combined bus + V/Line/travel times commonly run around 60–75 minutes peak (public‑transport trip planners show combined journeys ~1h10–1h15), while direct driving is roughly 35–45 minutes depending on traffic [citation:Moovit (local bus routes & connections)](https://moovitapp.com/index/en-gb/public_transportation-Weir_Views-Melbourne-site_165153943-2803) [citation:Rome2rio (Weir Views → Melbourne typical combos)](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Weir-Views/Melbourne) [citation:V/Line - services between Melton and Southern Cross](https://www.vline.com.au/Plan-trip-buy-tickets).
