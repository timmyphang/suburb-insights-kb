Here’s a concise, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Aitken Hill Primary School (Craigieburn). I stuck to short fact snippets and added one inline citation per claim.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)  
Craigieburn is a fast‑growing northern Melbourne growth‑area with a young median age (32) and a high share of families — broadly family‑oriented rather than inner‑city singles [citation:2021 Craigieburn QuickStats (ABS)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20661). The area is culturally diverse and described by the City of Hume as a major growth corridor with mixed middle‑income / working‑family households [citation:City Profile - Hume City](https://www.hume.vic.gov.au/Your-Council/Our-City/City-Profile).

2) Most common foreign country of birth  
India is the most common overseas country of birth (about 13.5%); around 47% of residents were born in Australia at the 2021 Census [citation:Craigieburn suburb profile (Behind the Fence, citing ABS)](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/craigieburn).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
Yes — Aitken Hill PS hosts on‑site OSHC run by TheirCare offering Before School, After School and Vacation Care (contact/booking details listed by TheirCare and StartingBlocks) [citation:Aitken Hill PS — TheirCare / StartingBlocks listing](https://www.startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01HGCR1VF2Z0JYA2HN4RYZ13XG/Aitken-Hill-PS-TheirCare).

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars  
Inter‑school/team sport opportunities (Years 4–6 representation); Performing Arts (music, dance, drama); and STEM/robotics programs — the school also teaches Auslan as a specialist subject [citation:Specialist Classes — Aitken Hill Primary School](https://www.aitkenhillps.vic.edu.au/page/12/Specialist-Classes).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)  
Craigieburn feels like a suburban family precinct with shopping centres (Craigieburn Central / Craigieburn Plaza), parks and family services — typical of Melbourne’s growth corridors [citation:Craigieburn Central / Hume City profile](https://craigieburncentral.com.au/; https://www.hume.vic.gov.au/Your-Council/Our-City/City-Profile). Notable local specialty grocers include Pinoy Tayo (Filipino/Asian) and Tuan Phong Asian Grocery for ethnic ingredients [citation:Pinoy Tayo (Craigieburn Central) & Tuan Phong listings](https://craigieburncentral.com.au/retailer/pinoy-tayo/; https://au.showmelocal.com/profile.aspx?bid=17637280).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 casual, family‑friendly options  
Dosa Hut (Indian dosas and family meals), El Jannah (halal charcoal chicken / family packs), and local Indian options such as Thaali offer casual, family‑friendly takeaway/dine‑in choices [citation:Dosa Hut; El Jannah; Thaali listings](https://www.dosahut.net.au/indian-restaurant-in-craigieburn/; https://eljannah.com.au/locations/vic/craigieburn/; https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurant/thaali-130012).

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 centres)  
Local/school‑area options include Kumon Craigieburn (maths & English programmes) and Brainworks Craigieburn (one‑to‑one tutoring) for academic support/enrichment [citation:Kumon search; Brainworks Craigieburn](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-CRAIGIEBURN-VIC; https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-craigieburn).

8) Commute — Craigieburn station → Flinders Street (estimate)  
Train: Craigieburn is on the Craigieburn line; typical travel time to Flinders Street is about 40–45 minutes (many summaries list ~44 minutes as a common journey time) — check timetables for exact off‑peak/peak services [citation:Metro Trains Craigieburn line; Rome2rio journey time estimate](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/lines/craigieburn/; https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Craigieburn/Flinders-Street-Station).
