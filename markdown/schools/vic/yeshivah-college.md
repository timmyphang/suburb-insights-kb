1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** St Kilda East is an affluent, predominantly white-collar suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,020, well above the national median [citation:2021 St Kilda East QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22344). The median age is 34, reflecting a younger demographic [citation:2021 St Kilda East QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22344). The suburb has a mix of singles and families, with a low average of 0.5 children per household and 2,801 families recorded [citation:2021 St Kilda East QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22344).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas country of birth for St Kilda East residents is not specified in the available QuickStats data, and the Australian-born percentage (31.3% in 2006) is outdated [citation:2006 St Kilda East QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2006/SSC21639). The 2021 data shows that 33.6% of people had both parents born in Australia and 40.7% had both parents born overseas, but no single dominant overseas country is named [citation:2016 St Kilda East QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22333).
