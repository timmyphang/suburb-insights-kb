1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Tooradin is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a working-to-middle-class mix. The median weekly household income is $1,954, and the median age is 40 [citation:2021 Tooradin, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22546). The area has a high proportion of families, with 450 families recorded and an average of 2.9 people per household [citation:2021 Tooradin, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22546). Specific white-collar vs. blue-collar breakdowns were not found in the available sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Tooradin is overwhelmingly Australian-born. According to 2016 Census data (the latest available in the provided context), 61.5% of people had both parents born in Australia [citation:2016 Tooradin, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22533). The most common overseas country of birth was England at 3.8% [citation:2016 Tooradin, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22533). 2021 country-of-birth percentages were not available in the provided sources.
