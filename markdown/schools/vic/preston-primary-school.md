Here’s a short, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Preston Primary School (Preston, VIC). I used ABS QuickStats / council profiles where possible and opened at most one page per data point.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Preston is a mixed, multicultural inner‑north suburb (median age 37) with a lively mix of long‑term and newer residents and strong local retail/market precincts [citation:Preston QuickStats 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22121) [citation:Preston suburb guide (Darebin)](https://www.darebin.vic.gov.au/Events-and-facilities/Suburb-guides/Preston).  
- Median weekly household income in Preston was $1,844 (2021), and occupations skew toward professionals and clerical/administrative roles — more white‑collar than heavy industry [citation:Preston QuickStats 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22121) [citation:Preston demographics (LocalStats)](https://localstats.com.au/demographics/vic/melbourne/north-east/preston).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- Preston is majority Australian‑born (around 84% in 2021); the largest overseas birthplaces recorded were China (~3.9%) and India (~3.9%) (China is listed as a top overseas birthplace) [citation:Preston QuickStats 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22121) [citation:Preston suburb profile (ABS‑derived summary)](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/preston-vic).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- Preston Primary runs an on‑site OSHC program: Before Care from 7:00am and After Care 3:30–6:00pm (bookings/cancellation rules and late‑fee policy on the school OSHC page); the service is listed on the government StartingBlocks child‑care finder [citation:Preston Primary OSHC page](https://www.preston-ps.vic.edu.au/page/181/Out-of-School-Hours-Care) [citation:StartingBlocks listing](https://www.startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01HGCPSD9XFXWQP50FAG8XJHJT/Preston-Primary-School-OSHC).

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars
- Performing Arts program (Drama, Music, Dance; weekly sessions Prep–Yr3 and performance opportunities) [citation:Preston Performing Arts](https://www.preston-ps.vic.edu.au/page/192/Performing-Arts).  
- Interschool sport (Grades 5–6 weekly: summer — tee ball/rounders/cricket; winter — soccer, football, netball) plus district swimming, cross‑country and athletics carnivals [citation:Preston Physical Education](https://www.preston-ps.vic.edu.au/page/170/Physical-Education).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Preston has a busy, family‑friendly market and High Street precinct (Preston Market, cafes, parks and arts facilities) — lots of everyday family amenities close by [citation:Preston suburb guide (Darebin)](https://www.darebin.vic.gov.au/Events-and-facilities/Suburb-guides/Preston).  
- Notable specialty/ethnic grocers include multiple traders inside Preston Market (e.g., ARC Asian Grocer) and local stores such as Dong Thinh International Food Store on High Street for Asian ingredients [citation:Preston Market map / ARC Asian Grocer](https://www.prestonmarket.com.au/market-map/) [citation:Dong Thinh International Food Store (Preston Central)](https://prestoncentral.org.au/traders/dong-thinh-international-food-store/).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 casual, family‑friendly options
- Pad Cha Thai (Thai cuisine on High Street) — casual takeaway/dine‑in [citation:Pad Cha Thai Contemporary (Preston Central)](https://prestoncentral.org.au/traders/pad-cha-thai-contemporary/).  
- Noi Pizzeria / local pizza shops on High Street for family pizza takeaway/delivery [citation:Noi Pizzeria Preston (order site)](https://order.noipizzeria.com.au/) and a number of casual pizza/takeaway outlets on High St (e.g., TAKEAWAY PIZZA) [citation:TAKEAWAY PIZZA (High St)](https://www.takeawaypizza.melbourne/).
