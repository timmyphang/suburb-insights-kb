Below is a short, family-facing 8‑point briefing for Gilgai Plains Primary School (Kalkallo). Each item is a concise fact/snippet with source citations.

1) Suburb socio-economic profile  
- Kalkallo is a young, rapidly growing, family‑oriented suburb (median age 30; many couples with children) with median weekly household income $2,047 — above the national median — so broadly middle-to-higher income and family-focused. [citation:2021 Kalkallo QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21284) [citation:New Census insights on income in Australia](https://www.abs.gov.au/articles/new-census-insights-income-australia-using-administrative-data)

2) Most common overseas country of birth  
- The most common overseas birthplace in Kalkallo is India (about 25% of residents); a large share of the population is Australian‑born but India is the dominant overseas country. [citation:Behind the Fence Kalkallo profile](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/kalkallo) [citation:2021 Kalkallo QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21284)

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC) at this school  
- Gilgai Plains Primary partners with TheirCare to provide Before‑School care (6:30–8:50am), After‑School care (3:10–6:30pm) and Vacation/Holiday programs (typically 6:30–6:30), run on site. [citation:Parent & Community Portal — Gilgai Plains PS](https://gilgaiplainsps.vic.edu.au/parents/) [citation:TheirCare Gilgai Plains PS](https://theircare.com.au/holiday-programs/gilgai-plains-ps-theircare/)

- Sport/PE programs supported by a dedicated performing‑arts & PE building, indoor multi‑sport court, outdoor hardcourts and sports field; the school runs camps and excursions.
