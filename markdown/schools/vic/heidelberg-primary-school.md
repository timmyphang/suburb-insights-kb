Here’s a concise, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Heidelberg Primary School / the suburb of Heidelberg (Melbourne). I used one focused search per item and cite the source after each fact.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile
- Heidelberg is largely middle‑income and family‑oriented (median age 39; many married/de‑facto households and a strong share of primary‑age children). [citation:2021 Heidelberg QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21167)  
- Property/market summaries and council profiles describe it as an established inner‑northern suburb with a mix of professionals and long‑standing families (not a high‑poverty or heavy industrial area). [citation:Heidelberg VIC Suburb Profile — Domain](https://www.domain.com.au/suburb-profile/heidelberg-vic-3084)

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- Most residents are Australian‑born — roughly two‑thirds of residents are born in Australia (≈66–68%); there is no single overseas country that overwhelmingly dominates Heidelberg’s overseas‑born population. [citation:2021 3084 QuickStats (postcode) and 2021 Heidelberg QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/POA3084) [citation:2021 Heidelberg QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21167)

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- Heidelberg Primary School runs a licensed School‑Age Care (OSHC) program offering Before School Care (≈7:00–8:45am) and After School Care (≈3:20–6:00pm). [citation:School Age Care - Heidelberg Primary School](https://www.heidelbergps.vic.edu.au/school-age-care)  
- The program is listed on the national register with 140 approved places and an “Exceeding NQS” rating. [citation:ACECQA service register — Heidelberg Primary School School Age Care Program](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/heidelberg-primary-school-school-age-care-program)

4) School culture / extracurriculars
- Instrumental music program (onsite lessons: guitar, recorder, drums/keyboard via visiting teachers). [citation:Instrumental Music Program - Heidelberg Primary School](https://www.heidelbergps.vic.edu.au/instrumental-music-program)  
- Active sports/PE program with inter‑school team participation, plus performing arts and student leadership, camps and excursions. [citation:Specialist Programs - Heidelberg Primary School](https://www.heidelbergps.vic.edu.au/specialist-programs/)

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (2 sentences)
- Heidelberg has a village/main‑street feel around Burgundy Street and Warringal Shopping precincts, with parks and family amenities close by — convenient for local families. [citation:Heidelberg VIC Suburb Profile — Domain](https://www.domain.com.au/suburb-profile/heidelberg-vic-3084)  
- Main supermarkets include Coles at Warringal (Warringal Shopping Centre) and a Woolworths outlet nearby; there are also local specialty grocers serving South‑Asian and other cuisines (e.g., Vibhu Store — Indian/South Asian groceries). [citation:Coles Warringal Mall (Heidelberg)](https://www.coles.com.au/find-stores/coles/vic/heidelberg-545) [citation:Vibhu Store Heidelberg — Indian & South‑Asian Groceries](https://singh.com.au/vibhu-store-heidelberg-melbourne/)

6) Weeknight dining (casual, family‑friendly)
- Cafe Scintilla (all‑day breakfast/lunch; family‑friendly café on Burgundy St). [citation:Cafe Scintilla - Heidelberg](https://www.cafescintilla.com.au/)  
- The Pepper Tree (café/deli on Burgundy St) — casual dining/takeaway suitable for families. [citation:The Pepper Tree – Burgundy Street, Heidelberg](https://thepeppertree.co/)  
(There are additional takeaway options listed in local directories for pizza, Asian and fish‑and‑chips near the station/Burgundy St.) [citation:Restaurants in Heidelberg, VIC With Takeaway | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/heidelberg/takeaway-category)

7) Tuition and enrichment (nearby)
- Mindful Maths Tutoring — local maths/literacy tuition centre on Burgundy Street (primary/secondary support). [citation:Mindful Maths Tutoring | Heidelberg](https://www.mindfulmaths.net/)  
- Local franchise/independent tutors and larger providers are available (Kumon listings cover nearby centres; local private tutors also advertise services for primary students). [citation:Search for a Kumon Centre | Kumon Australia (VIC)](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/VIC)

8) Commute — train time to CBD
- Heidelberg has a train station on the Hurstbridge line; typical city‑bound journey to Flinders Street is about 25–30 minutes (commonly cited ≈26 minutes). [citation:Heidelberg Station - Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/heidelberg/) [citation:Heidelberg Station to Flinders Street — Rome2rio estimated travel time](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Heidelberg-Station-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station)
