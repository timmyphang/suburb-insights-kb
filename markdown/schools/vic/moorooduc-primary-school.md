1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Moorooduc is an affluent, family-oriented suburb. The 2021 Census recorded a median weekly household income of $2,256, well above the national median of approximately $1,746, with a median age of 48 years, indicating an older population base [citation:2021 Moorooduc, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21754). In 2011, 61.1% of residents aged 15+ were married, and the area had a high average of 2.8 motor vehicles per dwelling, reflecting a car-dependent, white-collar, and family-dense community [citation:2011 Moorooduc, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2011/GL_VIC1748).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The suburb is overwhelmingly Australian-born. In 2016, 61.4% of residents had both parents born in Australia [citation:2016 Moorooduc, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21742). The 2021 QuickStats does not provide a detailed country-of-birth breakdown in the available context, but the strong Australian-born majority is consistent across recent censuses.

3) **Working-parent support:** Moorooduc Primary School partners with TheirCare to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC). The program runs from **7:00am until 8:45am** in the morning and from **3:30pm until 6:30pm** each school day, with pupil-free days and holiday programs operating **7:00am to 6:00pm** [citation:OSHC – Moorooduc Primary School](https://moorooducps.vic.edu.au/oshc/).

4) **School culture:** Moorooduc Primary School offers a range of named extracurriculars. The school provides an **Instrumental Music Program** with piano and guitar lessons [citation:Visual Arts / The Arts – Moorooduc Primary School](https://moorooducps.vic.edu.au/visual-arts/). It also runs a **biannual Whole School Production** involving music, dance, and drama [citation:Visual Arts / The Arts – Moorooduc Primary School](https://moorooducps.vic.edu.au/visual-arts/). Additionally, the school participates in the **Resilience, Rights and Respectful Relationships (RRRR)** program, a curriculum-based social-emotional learning initiative [citation:Resilience, Rights and Respectful Relationships (RRRR) – Moorooduc Primary School](https://moorooducps.vic.edu.au/resilience-rights-respectful-relationships-rrrr/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Moorooduc is a semi-rural suburb on the Mornington Peninsula, with a quiet, family-friendly atmosphere and access to local parks like Devilbend Natural Features Reserve. For grocery shopping, residents can visit the **Moorooduc IGA** on Mornington-Tyabb Road for day-to-day needs. Specialty/ethnic grocery stores are not listed within Moorooduc itself in the available sources; the nearest options would be in nearby Mornington (e.g., **Mornington IGA** or **Coles Mornington**), but no specific Asian or ethnic grocers are named.

8) **Commute:** Moorooduc does not have a train station; the nearest railway station is **Baxter Station** on the **Stony Point** line (operated by Metro Trains and V/Line). From Baxter, travel time to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD is approximately **60–70 minutes** (depending on the service, with some requiring a change at Frankston). Alternatively, residents can drive to **Frankston Station** (about 15 minutes) and take the Frankston line (approximately 50 minutes to Flinders Street).
