Quick note: Ivanhoe Girls’ Grammar is an ELC–Year 12 girls’ school (not primary‑only); below I focus on the Junior School / primary‑age information where relevant.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Ivanhoe is a middle‑to‑affluent, predominantly white‑collar suburb with a family skew (median age ~40–41 and a high share of married households) — typical inner‑north Melbourne professional profile. [citation:2021 Ivanhoe QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21246)

2) Most common foreign country of birth  
About 70% of Ivanhoe residents were born in Australia (≈69.9%); the largest overseas birthplace is China (~4.3%). If you need the full country list the ABS QuickStats page includes the breakdown. [citation:Ivanhoe VIC Suburb Profile — Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/ivanhoe) [citation:2021 Ivanhoe QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21246)

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC) at the school  
Ivanhoe Girls’ runs before‑ and after‑school care and holiday programs via TeamKids (Before‑school care for ELC; After‑school care for ELC–Year 6, typically 3:00–6:00pm on school days; holiday program offered). [citation:After School Care Program | Ivanhoe Girls' Grammar School](https://www.ivanhoegirls.vic.edu.au/our-school/junior-school/after-school-care) [citation:TeamKids – Ivanhoe Girls’ venue](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/ivanhoe-girls-grammar-oshc-5/)

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars (Junior School)  
Junior School co‑curriculars include organised sport/House sport (Girls Sport Victoria participation), music/instrumental tuition, and drama/debating / clubs. (Some activities like instrumental lessons may incur extra fees.) [citation:Co‑Curricular Activities | Ivanhoe Girls' Grammar School](https://www.ivanhoegirls.vic.edu.au/why-ivanhoe-girls/co-curricular-activities)

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (2 sentences)  
Ivanhoe Village is a calm, family‑oriented shopping strip with parks, cafes and local services — convenient for families. Specialty/ethnic grocery options include Journey to the East Asian Mart plus standard supermarkets (Woolworths/Coles/FoodWorks in the Ivanhoe shopping precinct). [citation:Shop in Ivanhoe — Journey to the East Asian Mart](https://shopinivanhoe.com.au/traders/journey-east-asian-mart/) [citation:FoodWorks Ivanhoe East](https://ivanhoeeast.foodworks.com.au/)

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 casual family‑friendly options  
Examples: Larry’s Pizza (local pizza takeaway), Thai Lantern (Thai takeaway/dine‑in), Joia/Joia Cucina (Italian/pizza family dining). [citation:Larry's Pizza](https://larryspizza.com.au/) [citation:Thai Lantern – Ivanhoe](https://www.thailantern.com.au/) [citation:Joia Cucina](https://www.joia.com.au/)

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 centres)  
Local options include Math Minds (maths tutoring in Ivanhoe East) and an Ivanhoe Kumon centre; private one‑to‑one tutors and agencies (e.g., Alchemy Tuition) also advertise local P–12 tutoring. [citation:Math Minds — Maths Tutoring Ivanhoe](https://mathminds.com.au/maths-tutor-ivanhoe-east/) [citation:Kumon Ivanhoe centre](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-IVANHOE-VIC)

8) Commute — train travel time to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)  
Ivanhoe has a station on the Hurstbridge line; typical direct train travel time to Flinders Street is about 20–25 minutes (many timetables/summary pages report ~19–22 minutes on typical services). [citation:Metro Trains — Ivanhoe station](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/ivanhoe/) [citation:Train Ivanhoe to Flinders Street (Rome2rio)](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Ivanhoe/Flinders-Street-Station)
