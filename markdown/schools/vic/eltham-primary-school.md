1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:**
Eltham is an affluent, predominantly white-collar suburb with a strong family orientation. The median weekly household income is $2,391, well above the national median, and the median age is 43, indicating a mature population [citation:2021 Eltham (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20865). The area is characterised by a high proportion of professionals and above-average rates of outright home ownership, with 88.4% of housing being detached houses [citation:Eltham – SA2 209031209 | AreaSearch](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/eltham).
