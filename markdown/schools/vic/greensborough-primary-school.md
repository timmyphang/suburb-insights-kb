The area has a mix of 6,043 families and a significant proportion of residents in white-collar professional occupations, though a detailed breakdown of white-collar vs blue-collar is not available in the provided sources [citation:2021 Greensborough QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21104). Children aged 0–14 years made up 18.6% of the population in 2021, indicating a strong family presence [citation:2021 Greensborough QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21104).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Greensborough is predominantly Australian-born, with 75.7% of residents born in Australia according to the 2021 Census [citation:2021 Greensborough QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21104). The most common foreign country of birth is not specified in the provided snippets, and the available data do not list a dominant overseas country with its percentage share.

3) **Working-parent support:** Greensborough Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by OSHClub, operating from the school grounds at 130 Grimshaw Street [citation:Greensborough Primary School OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/greensborough-primary-school/). The "Rise then Shine" before-school program runs from 6:45 AM until bell time, and the "Stay and Play" after-school program runs from bell time until 6:00 PM [citation:Greensborough Primary School OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/greensborough-primary-school/). Holiday programs are also available from 6:45 AM to 6:00 PM [citation:Greensborough Primary School OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/greensborough-primary-school/).

4) **School culture:** Greensborough Primary School offers a choir program, which commenced in 2018, and private instrumental music lessons [citation:Learning Greensborough Primary School](https://greensborough.vic.edu.au/learning/). The school also runs a Buddy Program that pairs Prep children with Grade 5 or 6 students for cross-age classroom sessions and playground support [citation:Wellbeing Greensborough Primary School](https://greensborough.vic.edu.au/wellbeing/). Additionally, the school has a swimming program for all students Prep through Six [citation:Our School Greensborough Primary School](https://greensborough.vic.edu.au/our-school/).
