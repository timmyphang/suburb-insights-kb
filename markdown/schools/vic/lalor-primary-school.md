1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Lalor is a working-class suburb with a median weekly household income of approximately $1,374 (based on a monthly estimate of $5,956) and a median age of 38 in the Lalor-East SA2 area [citation:HTA](https://www.htag.com.au/vic/vic197-whittlesea-city/lalor-vic-3075/) [citation:ABS](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/209041529). The suburb is predominantly blue-collar, with a high proportion of families—the average household size is 2.8 people, and married couples make up 49.2% of residents [citation:HTA](https://www.htag.com.au/vic/vic197-whittlesea-city/lalor-vic-3075/). The 2025 estimated population is 24,097, with a density of 3,121 persons per square km [citation:profile.id](https://profile.id.com.au/whittlesea/about?WebID=150).

3) **Working-parent support**: Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is available at Lalor Primary School through the "Lalor Primary Combined OSHC" service, located at 26 Maxwell Street [citation:CareforKids](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/p2628/lalor-primary-combined-oshc-3075). The service is approved for centre-based care for up to 45 children, but specific operating hours and the provider name (e.g., Camp Australia or TheirCare) are not stated in the available sources [citation:ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/lalor-primary-combined-oshc). The school’s contact page lists a Before & After School Care Coordinator, Paula Yacoub, reachable at (03) 9464 7500 [citation:Contact](https://www.lalorps.vic.edu.au/contact/).

4) **School culture**: Lalor Primary School offers a Performing Arts program that includes music, dance, drama, and digital media, led by specialist Paul Zula [citation:Specialists](http://www.lalorps.vic.edu.au/specialists.html). The school also runs "Sport Rotations," where Year 3 and 4 students teach games to Year 1 and 2 students to promote physical activity [citation:Sport Rotations](http://www.lalorps.vic.edu.au/34-blog/12-sport-rotations). Additionally, the school emphasises student voice and agency as part of its strategic plan [citation:Principal's Blog](http://www.lalorps.vic.edu.au/the-principals-blog/category/all).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Lalor has a strong multicultural feel with a wide range of specialty grocery stores. Key options include Thien Lien Asian Grocery (29 May Road), Hai Phu Seafood (370 Station Street), and Gran Monte Supermarket (296 Station Street) for continental goods [citation:Lalor Shopping Centre](https://lalorshoppingcentre.au/store/thien-lien-asian-grocery/). Other notable grocers include Afghan Supermarket (322-324 Station Street) and Swagat Grocery Store (16 May Road) [citation:Lalor Shopping Centre](https://lalorshoppingcentre.au/store/thien-lien-asian-grocery/). The suburb also has a Coles supermarket at 47-71 May Road for mainstream shopping [citation:Lalor Shopping Centre](https://lalorshoppingcentre.au/store/thien-lien-asian-grocery/).

6) **Weeknight dining**: Family-friendly takeaway options in Lalor include Nacools Pizza (Italian/Pizza, available via TuckerFox) and Fat Wraps and Wings (Chicken & Chips) [citation:TuckerFox](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/lalor-vic-3075). Another local option is Ginos Pizza - Lalor, offering Italian and pizza takeaway [citation:TuckerFox](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/lalor-vic-3075). For a sit-down meal, there are also options like Muzzys Kebab and Grill (Coburg) and Duck & Dumpling House (Mill Park) available for delivery [citation:TuckerFox](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/lalor-vic-3075).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Brainworks Lalor offers one-to-one tutoring in Maths and English, located at the Lalor Library Complex (2A May Rd), and also prepares students for selective entry and scholarship exams [citation:Brainworks](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-lalor). Additionally, the Lalor Neighbourhood House provides an After School Tutoring program for primary and secondary students, held Monday–Wednesday from 4:00–7:00 pm, with qualified teachers offering personalised support in literacy, numeracy, and science [citation:After School Tutoring](https://lalornh.org.au/youth-and-childrens-programs/after-school-tutoring/).

8) **Commute**: Lalor is served by the Mernda train line, with Lalor Station located on May Road. The average train journey to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD takes approximately 37 minutes, with trains running every 15 minutes during peak times [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Lalor-Station/Flinders-Street-Station). The service is operated by Metro Trains Melbourne and runs direct with no transfers [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Lalor-Station/Flinders-Street-Station).
