Below is a short, family‑facing 8‑point briefing for St Francis of Assisi School (Mill Park). I used ABS QuickStats / community profiles for suburb facts and one small page load per school items when needed; snippets cited inline.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Mill Park (2021 pop. 28,712) is largely middle‑income: median weekly household income $1,735 and median age 40 — a mature, family‑oriented suburb with many married households and a substantial proportion of people attending primary school [citation:2021 Mill Park QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21683).  
2) Most common foreign country of birth
- Most residents are Australian‑born (ABS‑derived suburb profiles); the largest overseas birthplace shown in suburb summaries is India (about 4–5% of residents) as the top non‑Australian country [citation:GDP.com.au Mill Park profile](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/mill-park-vic) [citation:2021 Mill Park QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21683).  
3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- St Francis of Assisi runs an on‑site Out‑of‑School Hours Care (Before/After school care and Vacation Care); service listed on the school site and on the national StartingBlocks child‑care directory (CCS‑approved; sample hours ~6:40am–8:40am and ~3:15/3:30–6:00pm) [citation:OSHC | St Francis of Assisi School](https://sfmillpark.catholic.edu.au/oshc/) [citation:StartingBlocks OSHC listing](https://www.startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01HGCPT4KT1CMRRX8KWRDJJN1R/St-Francis-of-Assisi-Out-of-School-Care-Program).  
4) School culture — extracurriculars (2–3 named)
- Instrumental / music program (dedicated instrumental offerings); specialist Arts program (music, visual/media, drama); languages program (Italian P–6; Japanese in upper primary) [citation:Extra Curricular | St Francis of Assisi](https://sfmillpark.catholic.edu.au/extra-curricular/) [citation:Curriculum | St Francis of Assisi](https://sfmillpark.catholic.edu.au/curriculum/).  
5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Mill Park is a primarily residential, family‑focused suburb with local shopping and parks (e.g., Mill Park Village shopping centre anchored by Coles) serving everyday family needs [citation:Mill Park Village directory](https://millparkvillage.com.au/retail-directory/) [citation:City of Whittlesea place snapshot (Mill Park)](https://www.whittlesea.vic.gov.au/files/assets/public/v/1/documents/services/building-planning-and-development/place-snapshots/placesnapshot_millpark.pdf).  
- Local ethnic/specialty grocery options include an Asian grocer (East West Asian Grocery) and the Mill Park Village Coles for mainstream groceries [citation:East West Asian Grocery listing](https://www.dlook.com.au/business/asian-grocery-shop-in-mill-park-vic-3082/east-west-asian-grocery-store-japanese-grocery-store-korean-grocery-store) [citation:Mill Park Village directory](https://millparkvillage.com.au/retail-directory/).  
6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 family takeaway/dine options (with cuisine)
- Italia Pizza Mill Park / Juve’s Pizza & Pasta — family‑friendly Italian/pizza takeaway options [citation:Italia Pizza Mill Park](https://italiapizzamillpark.com.au/) [citation:Juve's Pizza & Pasta](https://juvespizza.com.au/).  
- Lucky 88 Kitchen — local Chinese/Asian takeaway/delivery option [citation:Lucky 88 Kitchen](https://www.lucky88kitchen.com.au/).  
7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 local centres)
- Kip McGrath Mill Park (K–12 English & Maths tuition centre) and local private tutors/tuition services (e.g., listings on Ezy Math / Superprof) — local in‑centre and private tutoring options are available [citation:Kip McGrath Mill Park](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/mill-park) [citation:Ezy Math Tutoring Mill Park listings](https://www.ezymathtutoring.com.au/tutors/mill-park-3082).  
8) Commute (train / realistic substitute)
- Mill Park itself does not have a train station. Typical public‑transport commutes use a local bus to South Morang (or nearby station) then the Mernda/South Morang rail line to Flinders Street — door‑to‑door this commonly runs in the order of ~40–60 minutes depending on the bus connection; one route planner estimate (including bus+train) is ~44 minutes [citation:PTV bus route info (example: 564 serving Mill Park → South Morang)](https://transitapp.com/en/region/melbourne/ptv-public-transport-victoria/bus-564) [citation:Rome2rio Mill Park → Flinders Street estimate](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Mill-Park/Flinders-Street-Station).
