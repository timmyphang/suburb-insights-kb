1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Sunshine North is a working-class to middle-income suburb with a mix of families and singles. The median weekly household income is $1,399, which is below the Victorian median, and the median age is 37 [citation:2021 Sunshine North, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22396). With 2,976 families and an average of 1.8 children per family, the area has a strong family presence, though employment data was not specified in the provided sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas country of birth is Vietnam. In the 2001 Census, 19.2% of residents were born in Vietnam, though updated 2021 figures for country of birth were not fully detailed in the available snippets [citation:2001 Sunshine North, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2001/SSC22506). The 2021 Census shows 27 different languages are spoken at Sunshine North Primary School, indicating a highly multicultural community [citation:Grade 3/4W](http://sunshinenps.vic.edu.au/?course=grade-34w).

3) **Working-parent support:** Sunshine North Primary School partners with TheirCare to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) [citation:OSHC (Outside School Hours Care)](https://sunshinenps.vic.edu.au/our-families/oshc/). Before School Care runs from 6:45 AM to 8:45 AM, and After School Care runs from 3:30 PM to 6:00 PM, with Pupil Free Day and Holiday Programs available from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM [citation:OSHC (Outside School Hours Care)](https://sunshinenps.vic.edu.au/our-families/oshc/). Eligible families can receive up to 90% off session fees through the Child Care Subsidy [citation:OSHC (Outside School Hours Care)](https://sunshinenps.vic.edu.au/our-families/oshc/).

4) **School culture:** Sunshine North Primary School offers a weekly Performing Arts enrichment program that includes drama, music, and dance for Prep to Grade 6 students (e.g., using djembe drums) [citation:Performing Arts](https://sunshinenps.vic.edu.au/our-learning/performing-arts/). The school also has a "Sunshine North Song" celebrating its multicultural community [citation:Sunshine North Song](http://sunshinenps.vic.edu.au/?page_id=13380). Additional named extracurriculars beyond these were not listed in the provided sources.
