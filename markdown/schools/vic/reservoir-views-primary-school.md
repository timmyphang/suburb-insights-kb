1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Reservoir is a middle-income, diverse suburb with a mix of families and singles. The median weekly household income is $1,541, and the median age is 38, indicating a relatively young population [citation:2021 Reservoir (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22161). The suburb has a balanced family-and-singles demographic, with 13,259 families recorded in the 2021 Census [citation:2021 Reservoir (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22161).

However, the suburb is known for its multicultural population, with many residents born in Australia and a significant proportion from various overseas countries. For precise data, refer to the ABS Community Profiles for Reservoir [citation:2021 Reservoir (Vic.), Census Community Profiles](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/community-profiles/2021/SAL22161).

3) **Working-parent support**: Reservoir Views Primary School partners with **TeamKids** to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC). Before-school care runs from **7:00am to 8:45am**, and after-school care from **3:30pm to 6:00pm**, with holiday care available from **7:00am to 6:00pm** [citation:Before and After School Care | Reservoir Views Primary School](https://www.reservoirviewsps.vic.edu.au/before-and-after-school-care). Families may be eligible for the government Child Care Subsidy to reduce costs [citation:Before and After School Care | Reservoir Views Primary School](https://www.reservoirviewsps.vic.edu.au/before-and-after-school-care).

4) **School culture**: Reservoir Views Primary School offers a range of named extracurricular activities. For Years 5–6, the **Interschool Sport** program includes football, soccer, netball, tee ball, bat tennis, rounders, and cricket [citation:Physical Education | Reservoir Views Primary School](https://www.reservoirviewsps.vic.edu.au/physical-education). The **Bike Education Program** (for Years 5–6) teaches cycling skills and road safety, supported by a fleet of bikes and new bike shed [citation:Physical Education | Reservoir Views Primary School](https://www.reservoirviewsps.vic.edu.au/physical-education); [citation:Home | Reservoir Views Primary School](https://www.reservoirviewsps.vic.edu.au/). The **Music Program** includes instrumental tuition on ukuleles, xylophones, keyboards, and percussion, with regular performances [citation:Music | Reservoir Views Primary School](https://www.reservoirviewsps.vic.edu.au/italian).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Reservoir has a lively, family-oriented feel with diverse grocery options. Specialty supermarkets include **Broadway Asian Supermarket Toko** (264 Broadway, Indonesian groceries) [citation:Broadway Asian Supermarket Toko](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/vic/reservoir/broadway-asian-supermarket-toko-15299659-listing.html), and **Star India Spices & Groceries** (3 Edwardes Street, Indian groceries) [citation:Indian Grocery Stores Reservoir, VIC](https://singh.com.au/indian-grocery-stores-reservoir-vic/). **Marco Fine Foods & Groceries** offers organic and eco-friendly produce, plus a butcher, bakery, and cafe [citation:Marco Fine Foods & Groceries, Reservoir](https://mammaknowsnorth.com.au/pop-to-the-shop/marco-fine-foods-reservoir).

6) **Weeknight dining**: Families can enjoy casual, family-friendly dining in Reservoir. **Phon Pi Sai** (Thai cuisine) is a popular local spot for takeaway [citation:Cheap Eats in Reservoir](https://melbz.com.au/reservoir/cheap-eats/). **Dosa Hut** offers Indian takeaway with a wide menu [citation:Dosa Hut](https://www.dosahut.net.au/indian-restaurant-reservoir/). **Indian Accent** provides fine-dining Indian cuisine suitable for dine-in or takeaway [citation:Indian Accent](https://indianaccentcuisine.com.au/).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Several tutoring centres operate in or near Reservoir. **The Tutor Place** (21 Taylor Avenue, Reservoir) offers private tutoring for primary and high school students [citation:Tuition Tutoring Reservoir](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/tuition-tutoring/reservoir-vic-3073). **InsideMath Tutoring Services** provides math tutoring in the Reservoir area [citation:Tuition Tutoring Reservoir](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/tuition-tutoring/reservoir-vic-3073). **Cluey Learning** offers online and in-home tutoring for Years 2–12 in English, Maths, and Science [citation:Tutoring Reservoir / Cluey Learning](https://clueylearning.com.au/lts/tutoring-reservoir/).

8) **Commute**: Reservoir is served by the **Mernda line** on the Metro Trains network. **Reservoir Station** provides direct trains to **Flinders Street Station** in the Melbourne CBD, with a typical travel time of **29 minutes** and services every 20 minutes [citation:Reservoir to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Reservoir/Flinders-Street-Station). The Mernda line runs via Clifton Hill and the City Loop [citation:Mernda line - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mernda_railway_line).
