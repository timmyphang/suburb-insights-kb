1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Alphington is an affluent, white-collar suburb popular with young families and professionals [citation:CompoundInvest](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/alphington/). According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income is $2,429 and the median age is 39 [citation:ABS QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20034).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The suburb is overwhelmingly Australian-born. In the 2021 Census, 75.7% of Alphington residents were born in Australia [citation:ABS QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20034). The most common overseas country of birth was England (3.6%), followed by Italy (2.2%) and New Zealand (1.6%) [citation:ABS QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20034).

3) **Working-parent support:** Alphington Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by TheirCare [citation:CareforKids](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/ng677/alphington-primary-school-oshclub-3078). The service operates before school from 7:00am to 9:00am and after school until 6:00pm, Monday to Friday [citation:Kindicare](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3078/alphington/alphington-primary-school-oshclub); vacation care is also available [citation:CareforKids](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/ng677/alphington-primary-school-oshclub-3078).

4) **School culture:** The school runs a well-established Out of Hours Music Program (OHMP) offering individual lessons in instruments like piano, violin, guitar, and voice, plus a School Ensemble that performs at assemblies [citation:Alphington PS - Out of Hours Music Program](https://www.alphinps.vic.edu.au/teaching-learning/out-of-hours-music-program/). A structured School Camp Program provides outdoor education from Foundation (a twilight evening) through to a Year 6 residential camp [citation:Alphington PS - School Camp Program](https://www.alphinps.vic.edu.au/teaching-learning/school-camp-program/). Sporting Programs include inter and intra school sporting activities throughout the year [citation:Alphington PS - Sporting Programs](https://www.alphinps.vic.edu.au/teaching-learning/sporting-programs/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Alphington is a leafy, family-oriented suburb with tree-lined streets, parks like Alphington Park and Darebin Parklands, and a thriving café culture [citation:CompoundInvest](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/alphington/). For groceries, residents have access to standard supermarkets such as Woolworths and Coles in nearby Fairfield Village and Northcote Plaza; specific specialty grocers in or near Alphington were not found in available sources.

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual, family-friendly dining, Alphington Social (7 Rowe St) offers a local pub-style menu suitable for families [citation:Alphington Social](https://www.alphingtonsocial.com.au/). The Alphington Foodstore serves café cuisine and is a popular spot for breakfast, lunch, or a relaxed dinner [citation:AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurant/the-alphington-foodstore-80348). Woodfire pizza is also available from local pizzerias noted by nearby residents [citation:Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g4324142-Alphington_Yarra_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Alchemy Tuition provides in-home and online one-on-one tutoring in Alphington, operating since 2005 with screened tutors [citation:Alchemy Tuition](https://alchemytuition.com.au/melbourne-tutoring-alphington/). Math Minds Learning Centre offers tailored maths tutoring in a centre located a 5-minute drive from Alphington, with programs for primary and secondary students [citation:Math Minds](https://mathminds.com.au/maths-tutor-alphington/).

8) **Commute:** Alphington Station is on the Hurstbridge train line, with trains running every 20 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Alphington). The journey from Alphington Station to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD takes approximately 19 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Alphington-Station/Melbourne). A direct train also runs from Flinders Street to Alphington in about 28 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Alphington).
