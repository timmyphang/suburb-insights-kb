1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Cranbourne is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb. The 2021 Census shows a median weekly household income of $1,477, a median age of 35, and 5,448 families, with an average of 1.9 children per family for families with children. This suggests a mix of working and middle-class households, though specific white-collar/blue-collar breakdown is not available.
