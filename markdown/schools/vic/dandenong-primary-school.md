1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
- Dandenong has a relatively young population (median age 33) and a median weekly household income of $1,267 — younger and lower‑income than many Melbourne suburbs, with a strong working/middle‑income profile. [citation:2021 Dandenong QuickStats (All persons)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20707)  
- The area is highly culturally diverse with many nationalities represented across the Greater Dandenong municipality. [citation:Statistics and Data - Greater Dandenong Council](https://www.greaterdandenong.vic.gov.au/about-us/social-statistics)

2) Most common overseas country of birth  
- The top overseas birthplace in the suburb is Afghanistan (about 11.5% of residents born there in 2021); only about 31% of residents were born in Australia. [citation:Dandenong VIC Suburb Profile — Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/dandenong)
