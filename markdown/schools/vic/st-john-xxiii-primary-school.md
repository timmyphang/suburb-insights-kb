1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Thomastown is a working-class suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,225 and a median age of 39, slightly older than the national median [citation:2021 Thomastown, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/209041223). The suburb has a mix of residential and industrial areas, and a high proportion of residents work in blue-collar industries, with 65% of employed people commuting by car in 2016 [citation:2016 Thomastown, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22492). The population is 20,234, with a density of 1,372 people per km² [citation:Thomastown VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/thomastown-vic).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas birthplace in Thomastown is India, accounting for 11.8% of the population. The majority of residents (73.9%) are Australian-born [citation:Thomastown VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/thomastown-vic).

3) **Working-parent support:** St John XXIII Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by OSHClub, operating before and after school at the school site on Regal Avenue. Specific operating hours are not listed in the available sources, but the service is available for before-school and after-school care [citation:St Johns XXIII OSHClub Thomastown East in Thomastown, VIC 3074 | CareforKids.com.au](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/pjw29/st-johns-xxiii-oshclub-thomastown-east-3074).

4) **School culture:** St John XXIII Primary School offers a Kitchen Garden program as part of the Stephanie Alexander Kitchen Garden Foundation, where students grow produce and prepare meals [citation:Curriculum Areas | St John XXIII Primary School Thomastown East](https://www.sjthomastowneast.catholic.edu.au/curriculumarea). The school also runs a Visual Arts program covering drawing, painting, collage, sculpture, textiles, and photography [citation:Curriculum Areas | St John XXIII Primary School Thomastown East](https://www.sjthomastowneast.catholic.edu.au/curriculumarea).
