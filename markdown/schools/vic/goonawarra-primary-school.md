1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Sunbury is a middle‑income, family‑oriented suburb with a median personal income of $854 per week and a median weekly household income of $2,106 [citation:2021 Sunbury, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/210041240)[citation:Sunbury VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunbury-vic). The median age is 38, the population is around 38,851, and the housing stock is predominantly separate houses with above‑average rates of outright home ownership [citation:Sunbury VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunbury-vic)[citation:Sunbury - South – SA2 210041540](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/sunbury_south). The suburb has a strong family demographic, reflected in its large proportion of children aged 0‑14 and low unemployment of 3.3% [citation:Sunbury VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunbury-vic).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Sunbury is overwhelmingly Australian‑born, with 92.4% of residents born in Australia [citation:Sunbury VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunbury-vic). The most common overseas birthplace is England (3.9%), followed by India (2.0%) [citation:Sunbury VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunbury-vic).

3) **Working‑parent support:** Goonawarra Primary School partners with TheirCare to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) on site [citation:Out Of School Care](https://www.goonawarra.vic.edu.au/page/73/Out-Of-School-Care). Before School Care runs from 6:30 AM to 8:45 AM, After School Care from 3:30 PM to 6:15 PM, and a Holiday/Pupil Free Day program operates 6:30 AM to 6:15 PM [citation:Out Of School Care](https://www.goonawarra.vic.edu.au/page/73/Out-Of-School-Care). The service is subsidised through the Child Care Subsidy, with eligible families receiving up to 90% off session fees [citation:Out Of School Care](https://www.goonawarra.vic.edu.au/page/73/Out-Of-School-Care).

4) **School culture:** Goonawarra Primary School offers a range of named extracurricular activities including a School Choir (which performs at the Sunbury Sunfest Battle of the Choirs) and a School Band (announced in school newsletters) [citation:School Choir Success At Sunbury Sunfest](https://www.goonawarra.vic.edu.au/page/100)[citation:2 – 6 Gullane Drive, Sunbury Vic 3429](https://www.goonawarra.vic.edu.au/uploaded_files/media/news2614.pdf). Students also participate in the Victorian State Schools Spectacular, a large‑scale choral and dance event [citation:The Victorian State Schools Spectacular](https://www.goonawarra.vic.edu.au/articles/147). In addition, the school runs a CLUBS program twice a year with 22 different activity options for mixed‑grade groups [citation:Search Through Site](https://www.goonawarra.vic.edu.au/search/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Sunbury has a relaxed suburban feel with several shopping centres. For specialty groceries, Asia Finest Grocery at Sunbury Square Shopping Centre (Shop 9, 2‑28 Evans Street) offers a wide range of Filipino, Indian, Pakistani, Chinese, Japanese, Thai, and other Asian ingredients, fresh vegetables, and condiments [citation:Asia Finest Grocery - Sunbury Square Shopping Centre](https://sunburysquare.com.au/asias-finest-grocery/). It is open daily 9:00 AM–6:00 PM [citation:Asia Finest Grocery - Sunbury Square Shopping Centre](https://sunburysquare.com.au/asias-finest-grocery/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Casual family‑friendly options in Sunbury include Coco Seed (Thai, at 54 Macedon Street), Goonawarra Pizza Shop (pizza and pasta, at 13/17 Dornoch Drive), and OBs Kebabs & Burgers (kebabs and burgers, at 3/90 Vineyard Road) [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Sunbury](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/sunbury). All three offer takeaway and delivery and are well‑rated for their quality and service.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Kip McGrath Sunbury (49a O'Shanassy Street) provides professional tuition in English and maths for primary and secondary students, using qualified teachers and individual learning programs [citation:KMEC Sunbury Tuition Centre](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/sunbury). Kumon Sunbury also offers personalised maths and English programmes designed to build strong academic skills [citation:Kumon Sunbury Education Centre](https://www.facebook.com/KumonSunbury). Both centres are located within Sunbury.

8) **Commute:** Sunbury is served by the Sunbury line on the Metro Trains network [citation:Sunbury](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/lines/sunbury/). From Sunbury Station, trains run directly to Flinders Street Station in the CBD, with a typical travel time of 49 minutes [citation:Sunbury to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Sunbury-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). Services run every 20–30 minutes throughout the day [citation:Sunbury to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Sunbury-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
