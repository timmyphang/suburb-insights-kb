QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22170). The workforce skews white-collar, given the suburb's proximity to the CBD and professional services hub.

3) **Working-parent support:** Richmond West Primary School provides after-school care from 3:30pm to 6:00pm each day, facilitated by the City of Yarra [citation:Out of school hours care - Richmond West Primary School](http://richmondwestps.vic.edu.au/out-of-school-hours-care/). Before-school care and vacation care are also offered at the school through the City of Yarra's Richmond West Outside School Hours Care program [citation:Richmond West Outside School Hours Care | Yarra City Council](https://www.yarracity.vic.gov.au/services/family-and-children/richmond-west-outside-school-hours-care). The specific operating hours for before-school care are not stated in the available school sources.

4) **School culture:** Richmond West Primary School runs a **Kitchen Garden Program** where students grow, harvest, and cook seasonal food [citation:Extra Curricular | Richmond West Primary School](https://richmondwestps.vic.edu.au/extra-curricular/). The school also has a **Brass Band Program** with three bands (Rookie, Intermediate, and Senior) that rehearse before school and perform at community events [citation:Extra Curricular | Richmond West Primary School](https://richmondwestps.vic.edu.au/extra-curricular/). Additionally, students in Years 3–6 participate in **Interschool Sport**, including athletics, swimming, and cross-country carnivals, and Year 5/6 teams play friendly matches against nearby schools [citation:Extra Curricular | Richmond West Primary School](https://richmondwestps.vic.edu.au/extra-curricular/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Richmond is a vibrant inner-city suburb with a lively food and retail scene. For groceries, **Richmond Traders** is a new urban market at the corner of Bridge Road and Church Street, featuring specialty providores and a Coles supermarket [citation:Richmond Traders | Fresh Food Market](https://richmondtraders.com.au/). Families can also find Asian grocery stores such as **Monkey King Thai** and other specialty grocers, though no specific ethnic supermarket names were listed in the provided search snippets.
