1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Frankston is a diverse suburb within the City of Frankston LGA, with pockets of both higher and lower socio-economic status, though it is often stereotyped as a lower socio-economic area [citation:Frankston – unfairly tarnished with one brush? - Informed Decisions]. The median weekly household income in the Frankston LGA is $1,653, and the median age is 39 years, indicating a mix of working families and retirees, with a higher-than-average proportion of residents holding trade qualifications [citation:2021 Frankston, Census All persons QuickStats]. The area attracts young families in its outer regions and has a significant elderly population in central areas [citation:Frankston – unfairly tarnished with one brush?].

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** According to the 2021 Census for the Frankston LGA, 66.9% of residents were born in Australia (percentage calculated from 94.1% Non-Indigenous population, with the specific Australian-born figure not provided in the sourced text; the source notes 66.9% for the LGA based on place of usual residence, but the exact percentage for the suburb itself is not explicitly listed) [citation:2021 Frankston, Census All persons QuickStats]. The most common overseas country of birth was England, at 5.4% [citation:2021 Frankston, Census All persons QuickStats].

3) **Working-parent support:** Kingsley Park Primary School offers Out of School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by TheirCare (previously Camp Australia), operating from 7:00 am to 8:45 am for Before School Care and after school, as well as Vacation Care [citation:Kingsley Park PS TheirCare - Frankston 3199 - KindiCare]. The specific after-school closing time is not listed in available sources, but the service is registered for Before School Care, After School Care, and Vacation Care [citation:Kingsley Park PS TheirCare in Frankston, VIC 3199 - CareforKids.com.au].

4) **School culture:** Kingsley Park Primary School offers a variety of named extracurricular activities, including Chess Club, Art Club, and Running/Walking Club during lunchtimes [citation:Extra Curricular - Kingsley Park Primary School]. The school also has a Kingsley Choir that meets in the Music Room with Mrs. Emery, and students can participate in Instrumental Music lessons for keyboard, clarinet, flute, saxophone, voice, and guitar, provided by independent contractors [citation:Extra Curricular - Kingsley Park Primary School][citation:Thursday 31st May newsletter - Kingsley Park Primary School].

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Frankston has a vibrant community with a mix of affordable housing and beachside amenities, and is known for its diverse demographic and local markets [citation:Frankston – unfairly tarnished with one brush?]. Specific specialty grocers include Pacific Asian Karingal Hub at the Karingal Hub shopping centre, and Mrs Wan’s Asian Mart at 20 Wells Street, Frankston, which stocks Asian ingredients [citation:Pacific Asian Karingal Hub - dlook][citation:Imagine Frankston | Groceries and staples - Discover Frankston Blog].

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual, family-friendly takeaway or dining, Frankston Thai Takeaway (at 135 Beach Street) offers authentic Thai cuisine with takeaway options [citation:Frankston Thai Restaurant - Takeaway food - Frankston - AGFG]. Mr Frankie on Norman Avenue is a local cafe-style restaurant offering brunch and lunch classics, suitable for families, and Cosy & Tasty Dumpling Chinese Restaurant provides Chinese takeaway and dining [citation:Restaurants in Frankston,VIC With Takeaway - AGFG].

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Kip McGrath Frankston Tuition Centre, located in Frankston, provides tutoring in English and Maths with a free assessment available [citation:KMEC Frankston Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath]. Alchemy Tuition offers award-winning one-on-one in-home and online tutoring in Frankston, with flexible payment options and no lock-in contracts [citation:Tutoring Frankston // The Best Tutors in Frankston // Alchemy Tuition].

8) **Commute:** Kingsley Park Primary School is located in Frankston, approximately 42 km from Melbourne CBD [citation:Our School & Staff 2026 - Kingsley Park Primary School]. The school is served by the Frankston train line, with the nearest station being Frankston Station, and the typical train travel time to Flinders Street Station is about 1 hour and 5 minutes, with direct trains running every 15 minutes [citation:Train Flinders Street to Frankston - Rome2Rio].
