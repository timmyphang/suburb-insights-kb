1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Endeavour Hills is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,740 [citation:ABS QuickStats 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20871). It has a median age of 39 years, with 6,842 families and an average of 1.8 children per family, indicating a strong family demographic [citation:ABS QuickStats 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20871). The suburb's IRSAD score of 990 suggests a solid socio-economic profile, neither affluent nor disadvantaged [citation:HTAG Property Market](https://www.htag.com.au/vic/vic213-casey-city/endeavour-hills-vic-3802/). 3) **Working-parent support:** St Paul Apostle North School's Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided by Camp Australia and operates at 76 Mossgiel Park Drive [citation:KindiCare](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3802/endeavour-hills/camp-australia-st-paul-apostle-north-oshc). The morning session runs from 6:30am to 8:45am Monday to Friday, and after-school care is also offered, though specific afternoon hours are not listed in the provided data [citation:KindiCare](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3802/endeavour-hills/camp-australia-st-paul-apostle-north-oshc). 4) **School culture:** St Paul Apostle North School offers a Performing Arts program with a specialist teacher, as well as Visual Arts and Physical Education [citation:Our School](https://www.spanhills.catholic.edu.au/ourschool). The school also provides a LOTE (Languages Other Than English) program in Mandarin [citation:Our School](https://www.spanhills.catholic.edu.au/ourschool). Additional extracurriculars mentioned include a Catholic Leadership Program for grades 5/6 students [citation:Learning & Teaching](https://www.spanhills.catholic.edu.au/learningteaching).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Endeavour Hills is a family-friendly suburb with the Endeavour Hills Shopping Centre hosting over 80 retailers, including Aldi, Coles, Kmart, and Woolworths [citation:Endeavour Hills Shopping Centre](https://www.endeavourhillsshopping.com.au/). For specialty groceries, BestMart Asian Grocer is located next to Coles in the shopping centre, offering a wide array of authentic Asian ingredients [citation:BestMart Asian Grocer](https://www.endeavourhillsshopping.com.au/stores/bestmart-asian-grocer/). Other specific ethnic supermarkets are not named in the available sources.

6) **Weeknight dining:** Casual family-friendly takeaway options in Endeavour Hills include Heatherton Village Fish & Chips, offering fish and chips [citation:TuckerFox](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/endeavour-hills-vic-3802). Additionally, Asian Kitchen in the Endeavour Hills Shopping Centre provides Asian cuisine takeaway [citation:Asian Kitchen](https://www.endeavourhillsshopping.com.au/stores/chinese-food/). A third named option is Zambrero, located outside the shopping centre, serving Mexican cuisine [citation:Stores - Endeavour Hills Shopping Centre](https://www.endeavourhillsshopping.com.au/stores/).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Lynn's Learning Endeavour Hills offers individualised Maths and English tutoring for Foundation to Year 10, held at the Endeavour Hills Neighbourhood Centre on Mondays and Saturdays [citation:Lynn's Learning](https://lynnslearning.com.au/locations/victoria-locations/lynns-learning-endeavour-hills-tuition-centre/). Success Tutoring also provides tutoring services in Endeavour Hills, offering personalised learning support with 24/7 access to academic resources [citation:Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-endeavour-hills).

8) **Commute:** Endeavour Hills does not have its own train station. The most efficient commute to Melbourne CBD involves taking the bus 843 from Endeavour Hills SC/Matthew Flinders Ave to Dandenong Station, then catching the Cranbourne train line to Flinders Street Station, with a total travel time of approximately 1 hour and 14 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Endeavour-Hills/Flinders-Street-Station). Alternatively, driving takes about 26 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Endeavour-Hills/Flinders-Street-Station).
