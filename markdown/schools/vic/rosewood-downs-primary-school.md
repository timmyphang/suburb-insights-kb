1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Dandenong North is a working-class, family-oriented suburb. The 2021 Census recorded a median weekly household income of $1,436 and a median age of 38 [citation:2021 Dandenong North, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20708). Household composition leans toward larger family groups, with an average of 2.8 people per household and 5,877 families reported [citation:2021 Dandenong North, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/212041312). The SEIFA index of disadvantage for the broader Dandenong postcode (3175) places it in the bottom 10% of Victorian suburbs, reflecting lower-to-middle income profiles [citation:Dandenong Suburb Profile 2026](https://www.collings.com.au/dandenong-suburb-profile-2026-demographics-schools-transport-and-lifestyle/).
