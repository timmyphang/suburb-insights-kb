1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Ascot Vale is a middle-to-upper-income, family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,192, and the median age is 37 years old [citation:2021 Ascot Vale, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20075). There are 3,910 families and 15,197 residents, suggesting a balanced mix of families and singles [citation:2021 Ascot Vale, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20075).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The specific country of birth percentages are not available in the provided QuickStats summary. However, the 2016 Census for Ascot Vale states that 41.3% of people had both parents born in Australia, and 39.8% had both parents born overseas [citation:2016 Ascot Vale, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20074). The 2021 data for the broader Statistical Area Level 2 shows 66.8% of people were born in Australia, but the most common overseas country is not listed in the provided snippets [citation:2021 Ascot Vale, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/206031113).

3) **Working-parent support:** Ascot Vale Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by Camp Australia [citation:Ascot Vale Primary School | Camp Australia](https://campaustralia.com.au/schools/02BD). The hours are Monday to Friday from 7:30 am – 8:45 am for before-school care and 3:30 pm – 6:00 pm for after-school care [citation:Out of School Hours Care - Ascot Vale Primary School](https://avps.vic.edu.au/out-of-school-hours-care.html).

4) **School culture:** Ascot Vale West Primary School offers several named extracurricular activities including the "Ascot Singers" choir and the "Moonee Valley Instrumental Music Program (MVIMP)" [citation:At Ascot Vale West Primary School we value our broad range of](https://avwps.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Performing-Arts-Ascot-Vale-West-PS.pdf) [citation:About Ascot Vale West Primary School](http://avwps.vic.edu.au/content/index.php?page=107). The school also runs a "Breakfast Club" program [citation:Ascot Vale West Primary School Student Wellbeing and Engagement Policy](https://avwps.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Student-Wellbeing-and-Engagement-Policy_Reviewed-2025.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Ascot Vale has a family-friendly suburban feel with local amenities including parks and shops. For groceries, a Woolworths Metro is located in Ascot Vale [citation:Ascot Vale Metro - Store locator](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/storelocator/vic-ascot-vale-3094), and the nearby suburb of Moonee Ponds offers Russo's Foodtown [citation:Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/ascot-vale-vic-3032). Asian grocery needs can be met at Happy Apple Ascot Vale, which offers a range of Asian products [citation:Happy Apple Ascot Vale](https://ascotvale.thehappyapple.com.au/category/asian-1).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly dining options in Ascot Vale include "Pacinos Italian Family Restaurant" (Italian, pizza) and "Hellenic Flame" (Greek, grill) [citation:THE 10 BEST Restaurants in Ascot Vale](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g1830095-Ascot_Vale_Moonee_Valley_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). For takeaway, "Pizza Minded" on Union Road is a local pizza option [citation:Restaurants in Ascot Vale,VIC With Takeaway | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/ascot-vale/takeaway-category).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Brainworks Ascot Vale offers one-to-one maths and English tutoring, including programs for selective entry and scholarship exams, held at the Farnham St. Neighbourhood Learning Centre in Flemington [citation:Brainworks Ascot Vale. One to One Tutoring For Your Child](https://mail.staging.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-ascot-vale). Success Tutoring Ascot Vale also provides primary and high school tutoring in the area [citation:Tutoring Ascot Vale | Primary & High School Tutors - Success Tutoring](https://www.successtutoring.com/tutoring-melbourne/ascot-vale).

8) **Commute:** Ascot Vale is served by the Craigieburn train line from Ascot Vale Station to Flinders Street Station [citation:Ascot Vale to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Ascot-Vale/Flinders-Street-Station). The typical train journey to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street) takes approximately 17 minutes [citation:Ascot Vale to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Ascot-Vale/Flinders-Street-Station).
