1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Blackburn is an affluent, middle-to-upper-income suburb with a predominantly white-collar workforce. The median weekly household income is $2,050, well above the national median, and the median age is 40, indicating a mature population with many established families [citation:2021 Blackburn, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/207031161). The area has a high proportion of families, with 6,001 families recorded and an average of 1.8 children per family with children [citation:2021 Blackburn, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/207031161). Detached houses dominate the housing stock at 75.7% in nearby Blackburn South, reinforcing the family-oriented character [citation:Blackburn South – SA2 207031162 | AreaSearch](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/blackburn_south).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Blackburn is predominantly Australian-born, with 65.5% of residents born in Australia (based on 2021 Census data for the Blackburn SA2) [citation:2021 Blackburn, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/207031161). The most common overseas country of birth is China (excluding SARs and Taiwan), accounting for 6.8% of residents [citation:2021 Blackburn, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/207031161).

3) **Working-parent support:** Blackburn Primary School partners with TeamKids to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) [citation:Out of School Hours Care](https://www.blps.vic.edu.au/page/145/Out-of-School-Hours-Care). Specific operating hours for before and after school care are not listed on the school's OSHC page, but TeamKids typically runs programs from 7:00 AM until 9:00 AM and from 3:00 PM until 6:00 PM on school days; parents are advised to confirm exact times directly with TeamKids [citation:TeamKids - Blackburn Lake Primary - Blackburn 3130](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3130/blackburn/camp-australia-blackburn-lake-primary-school-oshc). Holiday programs are also available through TeamKids [citation:Out of School Hours Care](https://www.blps.vic.edu.au/page/145/Out-of-School-Hours-Care).

4) **School culture:** Blackburn Primary School offers a diverse range of named extracurriculars including the BPS Band Program (for students in Grades 3-6, with individual instrument lessons and ensemble performances), Chess Club, and Coding Club [citation:Extra Curricular | Blackburn Primary School](https://blackburnps.vic.edu.au/extra-curricular/). Additional activities include a Choir, Tennis Lessons (before school), and Fizz Kids Science (after school) [citation:Extra Curricular | Blackburn Primary School](https://blackburnps.vic.edu.au/extra-curricular/). The school also facilitates clubs for netball, soccer, taekwondo, and dance [citation:Enrol | Blackburn Primary School](https://blackburnps.vic.edu.au/enrol/).
