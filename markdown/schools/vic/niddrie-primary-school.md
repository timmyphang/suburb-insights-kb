Niddrie is an affluent, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,213 and a median age of 40, according to the 2021 Census [citation:2021 Niddrie, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21942). The workforce is predominantly white-collar, supported by the suburb’s proximity to Melbourne’s professional services and CBD.

3) **Working-parent support**  
Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) at Niddrie Primary School is provided by TeamKids, offering Before School Care from 7:00 am to 9:00 am and After School Care from 3:30 pm to 6:30 pm, plus Vacation Care from 7:00 am to 6:00 pm [citation:Niddrie Primary | TeamKids](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/niddrie-primary-oshc-5/). The service operates on-site in the OSHC Room, with entry via Fullarton Road.

Niddrie Primary School offers several named extracurricular activities, including the annual Footy Day (raising funds for charities such as Challenge and Nick’s Wish), the Niddrie Clean-Up Day, and a camping program for Years 3 and 4 [citation:2020 Annual Report to The School Community](https://niddrieps.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/2020-NPS-Annual-Report.pdf).
