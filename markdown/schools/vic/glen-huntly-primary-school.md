1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Glen Huntly is an affluent, white-collar suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,877, well above the national median [citation:2021 Glen Huntly QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21009). The median age is 35, and the area has a mix of families and singles, with households most commonly couples without children [citation:Landchecker](https://landchecker.com.au/suburb/glen-huntly-vic-3163/). A significant percentage of residents are employed in professional and managerial roles [citation:CompoundInvest](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/glen-huntly/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The specific dominant overseas country of birth is not stated in the available ABS QuickStats pages for Glen Huntly. However, 2016 Census data shows that 65.4% of residents had both parents born overseas, indicating a highly multicultural community [citation:2016 Glen Huntly QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21002). The 2021 Census reports that only 20.7% of people had both parents born in Australia [citation:2021 Glen Huntly QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21009).

3) **Working-parent support:** Glen Huntly Primary School offers On-Site Before and After School Care delivered by TheirCare. The program operates every school day from 7:00 am to 9:00 am and 3:30 pm to 6:00 pm, and also runs on curriculum days and during school holidays [citation:Before & After School Care – GHPS](https://www.glenhuntlyps.vic.edu.au/ohsc-theircare/). Children are provided with breakfast, afternoon tea, and snacks, and are safely escorted to and from classrooms.

4) **School culture:** Glen Huntly Primary School offers a wide range of named extracurricular activities. These include the **Choir** (Grades 2–6), the school rock band **“The Party Hats”** (Grades 5–6), and a **Chess Club** open to all skill levels [citation:Extra Curricular activities – GHPS](https://www.glenhuntlyps.vic.edu.au/extra-curricular/). The school also runs a **Hens Care Program** where students care for the school’s four hens [citation:Extra Curricular activities – GHPS](https://www.glenhuntlyps.vic.edu.au/extra-curricular/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Glen Huntly has a village feel with parks such as Glen Huntly Park and Booran Reserve, and a thriving café culture. For specialty groceries, **Heng Sheng Asian Grocery** at 1126 Glen Huntly Road stocks a wide range of Asian products [citation:Heng Sheng Asian Grocery](https://www.whereis.com/vic/glen-huntly-3163/yellowId-15267924). **Subiksha Indian Grocery Store** and **Indian Big Bazaar** are also located in the suburb, offering Indian, Asian, and Nepali groceries [citation:Subiksha Indian Grocery](https://singh.com.au/subiksha-glen-huntly-melbourne/) [citation:Indian Big Bazaar](https://singh.com.au/indian-big-bazaar-indian-grocery-store-glen-huntly-melbourne/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Families can enjoy casual takeaway from **Burger Bliss** (1170 Glen Huntly Road), which serves burgers and fast food [citation:OzFoodHunter](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/glen-huntly-3163). **Garlic & Greens** (1168 Glen Huntly Road) offers modern Australian cuisine, and **Huntly Dumplings** (1161 Glen Huntly Road) is a popular Chinese dumpling spot [citation:Uber Eats](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/glen-huntly-melbourne-vic). All are located on Glen Huntly Road.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Cloud Tuition** provides online tutoring for Years 2–12, with personalised support in schoolwork, assessment, and exam preparation [citation:Cloud Tuition Glen Huntly](https://www.cloudtuition.com.au/locations/tutoring-vic/glen-huntly). **Top of the Class Private Home Tutoring** offers in-home tutoring programs tailored to each child’s learning style and strengths [citation:Top of the Class](https://topoftheclass.com.au/private-home-tutoring-glen-huntly.html). Both services are available to Glen Huntly families.

8) **Commute:** Glen Huntly is served by **Glen Huntly Railway Station** on the **Frankston line**. The direct train journey to Flinders Street Station takes approximately 31 minutes, with services running every 15 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Glen-Huntly-Station/Flinders-Street-Station). The station is within walking distance of the school.
