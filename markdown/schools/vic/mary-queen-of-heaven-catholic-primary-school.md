1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Greenvale is a middle-to-upper-income, primarily family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,262 (87.1st percentile nationally), and 72.2% of homes have four or more bedrooms, reflecting a market dominated by owner-occupiers with mortgages (55%) [citation:GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/greenvale-vic); [citation:DA Leads Australia](https://daleads.com.au/suburbs/greenvale-vic-3059/). The median age is 35, younger than the national median of 38, and the suburb has a very low rental rate of 12.5% [citation:ABS QuickStats 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21105).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The dominant overseas birthplace in Greenvale is India, accounting for 3.2% of residents [citation:GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/greenvale-vic). However, the suburb is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 89.9% of residents born in Australia [citation:GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/greenvale-vic).

3) **Working-parent support:** Mary Queen of Heaven partners with Big Childcare to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) [citation:Before & After Care](https://mqhgreenvale.catholic.edu.au/our-community/before-after-care/). The Before School program runs from 7:00–8:30am daily, and the After School program runs from 3:00–6:00pm on Monday–Tuesday and 3:15–6:00pm on Wednesday–Friday [citation:Before & After Care](https://mqhgreenvale.catholic.edu.au/our-community/before-after-care/).

4) **School culture:** Mary Queen of Heaven offers several named extracurriculars, including an Instrumental Music Program (keyboard, guitar, and singing lessons by external professionals) [citation:Extra-Curricular Activities](https://mqhgreenvale.catholic.edu.au/learning/extra-curricular-activities/). Lunchtime clubs include a Crochet Club and a Library Club [citation:Newsletter Term 2 Week 1](https://mqhgreenvale.catholic.edu.au/smartnews/mary-queen-of-heaven-newsletter-term-2-week-1/). Students in Years 3–6 can participate in extra sporting activities such as Athletic Carnivals and Cross Country Carnivals [citation:Extra-Curricular Activities](https://mqhgreenvale.catholic.edu.au/learning/extra-curricular-activities/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Greenvale is a car-dependent family suburb with a high rate of detached housing (97%) and large blocks [citation:DA Leads Australia](https://daleads.com.au/suburbs/greenvale-vic-3059/). Local supermarkets include a Coles at 1-11 Greenvale Drive and a Woolworths at 8-34 Gladstone Park Drive [citation:Yellow Pages](https://yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/greenvale-vic-3059). For specialty groceries, Philmart Asian Groceries is located at Shop 24, 250 Somerton Road, Roxburgh Park (nearby) [citation:Yellow Pages](https://yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/greenvale-vic-3059).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Several casual, family-friendly takeaway options are available in Greenvale. **Jessie's Pizza (Greenvale)** serves pizza [citation:TuckerFox](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/greenvale). **Tek Kebab (Greenvale)** offers kebabs and wraps [citation:TuckerFox](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/greenvale). **Greenvale Charcoal Chicken** provides chicken and chips [citation:TuckerFox](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/greenvale-vic-3059).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** A specific tutoring centre near Greenvale is **New Era Education**, located at 21 Cityside Drive, Mickleham (approximately a 10-minute drive from Greenvale) [citation:New Era Education](https://neweralearning.com.au/tutoring/greenvale). Another option is **Ezy Math Tutoring**, which provides in-home maths and English tutoring across Greenvale [citation:Ezy Math Tutoring](https://www.ezymathtutoring.com.au/tutors/greenvale-3059).

8) **Commute:** Greenvale does not have a train station; the nearest station is Broadmeadows on the **Craigieburn line**. From Broadmeadows, the train trip to Flinders Street Station takes approximately 30 minutes [citation:Metro Trains Timetable](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/timetables/). To reach Broadmeadows, residents typically take the **Route 484 bus** from Greenvale, and the combined bus and train journey to Flinders Street takes about 1 hour and 8 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Greenvale/Flinders-Street-Station).
