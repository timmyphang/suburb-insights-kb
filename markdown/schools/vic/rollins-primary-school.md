1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Bell Post Hill is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a blend of white-collar and blue-collar workers. The median weekly household income is $1,368, and the median age is 42, which is older than the national median of 38 [citation:2021 Bell Post Hill, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20192). With 1,452 families and an average of 0.7 children per household, the area shows a mix of family households and older residents.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The 2021 Census does not specify a dominant overseas country of birth for Bell Post Hill in the provided snippets. However, 43.1% of people had both parents born in Australia, and 38.7% had both parents born overseas [citation:2016 Bell Post Hill, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20191). The available 2001 data listed Croatia (4.9%), Macedonia (4.3%), and Italy (3.1%) as common birthplaces, but this is outdated [citation:2001 Bell Post Hill, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2001/SSC26011).

3) **Working-parent support:** Rollins Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by Camp Australia [citation:Vacancies, Enrol now at Camp Australia - Rollins Primary School OSHC](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/3xxoj/camp-australia-rollins-primary-school-oshc-bell-post-hill-3215). After-school care fees range from $24.03 to $34.06 per session, though specific before-school hours are not listed in the provided sources. The service operates Monday through Friday.

4) **School culture:** Rollins Primary School offers a range of extracurricular activities including choir and music lessons, as noted in a 2016 school newsletter [citation:From the Principal…. Thursday, 26 May 2016 – Issue 8](http://rollinsps.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2012/06/260516.pdf). The school also runs specialist programs in Physical Education, Visual Arts, Performing Arts, STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics), and LOTE (Languages Other Than English) within the classroom [citation:Rollins Primary School](https://rollinsps.vic.edu.au/). Additionally, it is a partner school for The Resilience Project, focusing on gratitude, empathy, and mindfulness.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Bell Post Hill is a residential suburb with a quiet, family-friendly vibe, close to local sporting clubs like Bell Post Hill Football & Netball Club and Geelong Rangers Soccer Club [citation:About Us](http://rollinsps.vic.edu.au/about-us/). For groceries, the Bell Post Hill Grocery & Liquor Co. at 12 Beauford Ave offers a local supermarket option [citation:Bell Post Hill's Best Supermarkets](https://www.dlook.com.au/find/grocery-store-in-bell-post-hill-vic-3215).
