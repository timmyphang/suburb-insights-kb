1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Lalor is a middle-to-working-class suburb with a mix of blue-collar and white-collar households. According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income in Lalor is approximately $1,489 (based on monthly income of $5,956) [citation:Lalor, VIC 3075 Property Market and House Prices 2026](https://www.htag.com.au/vic/vic197-whittlesea-city/lalor-vic-3075/). The median age is 32 years, and the area has a high proportion of families, with an average household size of 2.8 people [citation:2021 Lalor, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/CED228). 

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas country of birth for Lalor residents is not explicitly stated in the available sources. However, the school reports that 67.4% of its students have a Language Background Other Than English, drawn from 28 different cultural backgrounds, indicating strong multicultural diversity [citation:Our School | St Luke's Lalor](https://www.stlukelr.catholic.edu.au/ourschool).

3) **Working-parent support:** St Luke’s School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by Extend. Before School Care runs from 6:45 am to 8:45 am, and After School Care runs from 3:15 pm to 6:00 pm [citation:St Luke's Primary School - Extend Before and After School Care](https://www.extend.com.au/school/st-lukes-primary-school/). Holiday care is also available at nearby Extend locations [citation:St Luke's Primary School - Extend OSHC in Lalor, VIC 3075](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/5qdyo/st-lukes-primary-school-extend-oshc-lalor-3075).

4) **School culture:** St Luke’s fosters a faith-based, inclusive community with a focus on holistic development. Named extracurricular activities include a **Homework Club** for structured after-school support, as well as sport and arts-and-crafts programs [citation:Our School | St Luke's Lalor](https://www.stlukelr.catholic.edu.au/ourschool). Additional enrichment such as cooking, science, and drama is also offered through the school’s activity program.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Lalor has a vibrant, family-friendly atmosphere with a strong multicultural feel. The Lalor Shopping Centre on May Road hosts supermarkets like **Coles** and specialty grocers such as **Thien Lien Asian Grocery**, **Gran Monte Supermarket**, and **Afghan Supermarket** [citation:Thien Lien Asian Grocery – Lalor Shopping Centre](https://lalorshoppingcentre.au/store/thien-lien-asian-grocery/). Station Street also offers many ethnic grocers, including **Kim Oanh Asian Grocery** and **Hai Phu Seafood**.

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly takeaway and dining options in Lalor include **Nacools Pizza** (Italian/Pizza), **Fat Wraps and Wings** (chicken and chips), and **Ginos Pizza - Lalor** (Italian/Pizza) [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Lalor, VIC 3075](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/lalor-vic-3075). These venues offer casual dining and delivery for busy weeknights.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Two named tutoring centres in Lalor are **Brainworks Lalor**, which provides one-to-one maths and English tutoring at the Lalor Library Complex (2A May Rd) [citation:Brainworks Lalor. One to One Tutoring For Your Child](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-lalor), and the **After School Tutoring** program at Lalor Neighbourhood House (47A French Street), offering personalised literacy and numeracy support for primary and secondary students [citation:After School Tutoring - Lalor Neighbourhood House](https://lalornh.org.au/youth-and-childrens-programs/after-school-tutoring/).

8) **Commute:** Lalor is served by **Lalor Station** on the **Mernda line**. The average train travel time to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD is **37 minutes**, with services running every 15–20 minutes [citation:Lalor (Station) to Flinders Street Station Trains](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Lalor-Station/Flinders-Street-Station). Direct trains operate from early morning to late evening [citation:Lalor | Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/lalor/).
