1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Deepdene is an affluent, white-collar suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,243, well above the national median [citation:2021 Deepdene (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20728). The median age is 46, indicating a mature population, and the suburb has a strong family orientation with 575 families and an average of 0.7 children per household [citation:2021 Deepdene (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20728). The workforce is predominantly professional and managerial, reflecting the suburb’s prestige and high property values [citation:Deepdene, VIC 3103 Suburb Profile | CompoundInvest](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/deepdene/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The large majority of Deepdene residents are Australian-born, with 66.4% of people stating their country of birth as Australia [citation:2021 Deepdene (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20728). The most common overseas country of birth is China (excluding SARs and Taiwan), at 5.2% of the population [citation:2021 Deepdene (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20728).

3) **Working-parent support**: Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) at Deepdene Primary School is provided by OSHClub, operating Monday to Friday from 7:00 am to 9:00 am (before school) and after school until 6:00 pm, with vacation care also available [citation:Deepdene Primary School OSHClub - KindiCare](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3103/balwyn/deepdene-primary-school-oshclub). The service is located on Burke Road, Balwyn (adjacent to the school), and has a capacity of 60 children [citation:Deepdene Primary School OSHClub - KindiCare](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3103/balwyn/deepdene-primary-school-oshclub).

4) **School culture**: Deepdene Primary School offers a vibrant Instrumental Music program, with students able to join the Symphonic band, a Jazz band, or Chamber strings [citation:Our School | Deepdene Primary School](https://deepdeneps.vic.edu.au/our-school/). Every Friday during Terms 1 and 2, Level 5 and 6 students participate in Interschool Sports [citation:Level 5 and Level 6 | Deepdene Primary School](https://www.deepdeneps.vic.edu.au/page/169/Level-5-and-Level-6). The school also provides a Tutor Program, a targeted learning support program [citation:Learning | Deepdene Primary School](https://deepdeneps.vic.edu.au/learning/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Deepdene is a leafy, quiet suburb with heritage homes and a relaxed, family-friendly atmosphere, with residents enjoying local cafés and parks [citation:Deepdene, VIC 3103 Suburb Profile | CompoundInvest](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/deepdene/). For groceries, the suburb has a Coles supermarket and an ALDI at the nearby Balwyn shopping precinct [citation:Deepdene, VIC 3103 Suburb Profile | CompoundInvest](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/deepdene/). Specialty grocers near Deepdene include the Indian grocery store ‘Tales of India’ for Indian staples and the ‘Deep Indian Supermarket’ for Indian groceries, both in Melbourne’s eastern suburbs [citation:Indian Grocery Store Near Me | Melbourne Delivery | Tales of India](https://talesofindia.com.au/) [citation:Deep Indian Supermarket wholesale & Retail | Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/deepindiansupermarkett/).

6) **Weeknight dining**: di Francesco Cucina Italiana Woodfired Pizza on Whitehorse Road offers authentic Italian woodfired pizzas and is family-friendly, with a casual atmosphere [citation:The 3 Best Deepdene,VIC Restaurants | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/deepdene). District Pho serves Vietnamese pho and other noodle dishes in a relaxed setting [citation:The 3 Best Deepdene,VIC Restaurants | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/deepdene). Napa Thai Noodle offers Thai cuisine and is suitable for takeaway or casual dining [citation:The 3 Best Deepdene,VIC Restaurants | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/deepdene).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: ‘Cloud Tuition’ provides personalised 1-to-1 English tutoring for Years 2-12, with online sessions available for Deepdene and Balwyn North families [citation:English Tutors Deepdene Balwyn North: Years 2-12 | Cloud Tuition](https://www.cloudtuition.com.au/locations/english-tutors-deepdene-balwyn-north). ‘North Shore Coaching College’ has a tutoring centre in nearby Balwyn (approximately 2 km away), offering programs for primary and secondary students in maths, English, and preparation for selective school tests [citation:North Shore Coaching College | Tutoring Centre](https://www.north-shore.com.au/).

8) **Commute**: Deepdene is not served directly by a train station; the nearest station is Balwyn on the Belgrave/Lilydale line, approximately a 10-minute walk or short bus ride away [citation:Deepdene, VIC 3103 Suburb Profile | CompoundInvest](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/deepdene/). From Balwyn station, the train to Flinders Street Station takes approximately 20-25 minutes [citation:Timetable Search | Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/timetables/). Alternatively, the 200-series bus (e.g., route 200 or 207) runs along Whitehorse Road and connects to the city in about 30-40 minutes [citation:Victorian public transport timetables - Transport Victoria](https://transport.vic.gov.au/timetables).
