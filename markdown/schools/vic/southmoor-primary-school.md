3) **Working-parent support**: Southmoor Primary School’s Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided by TeamKids, operating from 6:45 am to 8:45 am (before school) and 3:30 pm to 6:15 pm (after school) on school days, with a holiday program from 7:30 am to 6:00 pm [citation:TeamKids Southmoor Primary](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/southmoor-primary-oshc/); [citation:ACECQA TeamKids](https://acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/southmoor-primary-school-oshc). The program is run in the school hall and includes breakfast and afternoon tea, as well as incursions such as Bunnings workshops and yoga [citation:Southmoor OSHC page](http://www.southmoor.vic.edu.au/oshc.php).

4) **School culture**: Southmoor Primary School offers a range of named extracurricular and enrichment programs, including an award-winning **Kitchen Garden program** where students grow vegetables and cook meals, a **STEAM program** that builds problem-solving and critical thinking through hands-on projects, and a **Performing Arts program** that includes music and creative expression [citation:Southmoor Teaching & Learning](https://southmoor.vic.edu.au/curriculum/); [citation:Southmoor Home](https://southmoor.vic.edu.au/). These programs are integral to the school’s hands-on, engaging learning environment.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Moorabbin offers family-friendly amenities such as Moorabbin Reserve, King George VI Memorial Reserve, and Joyce Park Wetlands, along with a thriving café culture [citation:CompoundInvest](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/post/moorabbin). For specialty groceries, families can visit **Raj Kamal Enterprises** (Indian and Asian groceries at 22 Station St) or **Great Asia Food Store** (a large Asian grocery in nearby Melbourne) [citation:Singh.com.au Indian Grocery](https://singh.com.au/indian-grocery-stores-moorabbin-vic/); [citation:Yelp International Grocery](https://www.yelp.com.au/search?cflt=intlgrocery&find_loc=Moorabbin+Victoria+3189).

6) **Weeknight dining**: Casual family-friendly takeaway options in Moorabbin include **Maharani Indian Restaurant** (authentic North and South Indian cuisine, delivery available), **Cool Bean Kitchen** (burgers and casual fare at 614 South Rd), and **Coconut House** (Malaysian cuisine) [citation:Localsearch Takeaway](https://www.localsearch.com.au/find/takeaway-food/moorabbin-vic-3189); [citation:AGFG Moorabbin](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/moorabbin/takeaway-category).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Families can access **Fruition Tuition**, offering one-on-one tutoring in maths, English, and selective entry exam preparation (located at 15-17 Scanlan St, Bentleigh East) [citation:Fruition Tuition Moorabbin](https://www.fruition.com.au/tutoring-moorabbin/). Another option is **Aura Tutoring**, based in Moorabbin, which provides tutoring in maths, drawing, arts, computer skills, and languages [citation:Aura Tutoring Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/AURATUTORING).

8) **Commute**: Moorabbin is served by **Moorabbin Station** on the **Frankston line**. Direct trains run every 15 minutes to Flinders Street Station, with an average travel time of **40 minutes** [citation:Rome2Rio Moorabbin to Flinders St](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Moorabbin/Flinders-Street-Station); [citation:Metro Trains Moorabbin](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/moorabbin/).
