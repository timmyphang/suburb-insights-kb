1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Berwick is a family‑oriented, middle‑to‑upper‑middle‑income commuter suburb with many married households and a high share of households with children; median weekly household income is around $2,100–$2,360 depending on the ABS area used, and mortgage/rent levels are higher than state averages — typical of outer‑metro family suburbs.
