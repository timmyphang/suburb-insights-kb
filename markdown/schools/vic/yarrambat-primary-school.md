Here’s a short, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Yarrambat Primary School (Yarrambat, VIC). I followed your snippet‑first, one‑search‑per‑data‑point workflow and cite the source after each fact.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Yarrambat / Plenty area is relatively affluent with a high median weekly household income ($2,554) and an older median age (43), and shows a strong family/household orientation (married households ~58%) — more white‑collar / family suburb than inner‑city singles or heavy blue‑collar industry [citation:2021 Plenty - Yarrambat QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/209031213).

2) Most common foreign country of birth  
The locality is overwhelmingly Australian‑born (no large single overseas community). In older ABS locality data England was the most common overseas birthplace at ~2.4% (2016); there is no large dominant overseas country in Yarrambat [citation:2016 Yarrambat QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22901). (Note: 2021 SA2 QuickStats covers Plenty–Yarrambat and likewise shows a high Australian‑born share [citation:2021 Plenty - Yarrambat QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/209031213).)

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
Yarrambat Primary runs an on‑site Outside School Hours Care (Before 7:00–8:45am; After 3:30–6:00pm) with a centre registration/approval (capacity ~90) — bookings/enrolment info are on the school OSHC page and the ACECQA register entry [citation:Yarrambat PS OSHC](https://www.yarrambatps.vic.edu.au/page/30/OSHC) [citation:Yarrambat Primary Out of School Hours Care (ACECQA)](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/yarrambat-primary-out-school-hours-care).

4) School culture — extracurriculars (2–3 named)  
The school offers instrumental music lessons via Northern Melbourne Music, tennis coaching, and other extra‑curricular programs (music/performance events and sport coaching) [citation:Extra‑Curricular Programs (Yarrambat PS)](https://www.yarrambatps.vic.edu.au/page/28/Extra-Curricular-Programs).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (2 sentences)  
Yarrambat is a semi‑rural, family‑oriented village with parks and a regular community market (Yarrambat Market) — a quiet, green outer‑northern feel suited to families [citation:DVCS Markets Yarrambat Market](https://www.dvcsmarkets.com.au/yarrambatmarket). For groceries there’s a local general store/foodstore (Yarrambat General Store / Rivers of Yarrambat foodstore) that serves everyday and specialty provisions close to the village hub [citation:Rivers of Yarrambat](https://riversofyarrambat.com/) [citation:Yarrambat General Store (TrueLocal)](https://www.truelocal.com.au/yarrambat-vic/tlp/yarrambat-general-store-580581402).

6) Weeknight dining (2–3 casual family options)  
Rivers Cafe & Foodstore (Rivers of Yarrambat) is the main casual, family‑friendly dining/takeaway option in the village (cafés/platters/coffee). The local general store also provides takeaway basics — there are few late‑night or diverse ethnic takeaway options in Yarrambat itself, so families often travel to nearby suburbs for a wider range [citation:Rivers of Yarrambat](https://riversofyarrambat.com/) [citation:Yarrambat General Store (TrueLocal)](https://www.truelocal.com.au/yarrambat-vic/tlp/yarrambat-general-store-580581402).

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 local centres)  
Local/private tuition is available (e.g., Success Tutoring lists services for Yarrambat) and nearby centres such as Brainworks (Kalkallo) or other tutoring providers service the area for English/maths tuition and exam prep [citation:Success Tutoring Yarrambat](https://www.successtutoring.com/tutoring-yarrambat) [citation:Brainworks Kalkallo](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-kalkallo).

8) Commute (train‑substitute estimate)  
Yarrambat has no train station; families typically use the PTV bus 385 (Yan Yean Road stops including Yarrambat Primary) to connect to Mernda/Greensborough train stations, then take a train to Flinders Street. Expect a combined bus+train door‑to‑door journey to the CBD of roughly 1 hour 15 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes depending on connections and time of day (example route & planner info: PTV bus 385 + multi‑leg journey planners) [citation:PTV bus 385 route](https://transport.vic.gov.au/route/16290/385-whittlesea-mernda-station-greensborough-station) [citation:Flinders Street Station to Yarrambat (Rome2rio)](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Yarrambat).
