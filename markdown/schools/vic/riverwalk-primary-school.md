Below is a short, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Riverwalk Primary School (Werribee). I used search snippets first and only cited pages where needed.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)  
Werribee is a broadly middle‑income, family‑oriented suburb with a median age of 35 and average household size ~2.7 — a mix of blue‑ and white‑collar households rather than exclusively affluent or disadvantaged [citation:2021 Werribee QuickStats (ABS)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22750). Local council demographic profiles and SEIFA resources treat Werribee as a large, diverse growth area within Wyndham [citation:.id Werribee demographics](https://profile.id.com.au/s_werribee/demographics).

2) Most common foreign country of birth  
The largest overseas‑born group in Werribee is people born in India (around 7% of residents); just under 60% of residents were born in Australia in 2021 [citation:Werribee QuickStats (ABS)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22750) [citation:Werribee suburb profile summary](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/werribee).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC availability)  
Riverwalk Primary has on‑site outside‑school‑hours care provided by TeamKids (before, after and vacation care) — the ACECQA register lists the Riverwalk PS OSHC service with approved places (example listing shows 75 places) and TeamKids operates the venue [citation:TeamKids — Riverwalk Primary OSHC](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/riverwalk-primary-oshc/) [citation:ACECQA register entry](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/big-childcare-riverwalk-ps-oshc).

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars offered  
- Instrumental music lessons through the Primary Music Institute (weekly on‑site lessons) [citation:Instrumental Music — Riverwalk PS](https://riverwalkps.vic.edu.au/community/instrumental-music/).  
- Performing arts / specialist PE programs supported by the school’s performing‑arts and PE facilities [citation:School Buildings Victoria — Riverwalk Primary School](https://www.schoolbuildings.vic.gov.au/riverwalk-primary-school).  
- Before/after‑school activity programs (OSHC holiday activities frequently include sport‑style programs such as netball) [citation:TeamKids — Riverwalk Primary OSHC](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/riverwalk-primary-oshc/).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)  
Werribee is a family‑focused growth area with town‑centre shopping, parks and community facilities (Watton Street dining precinct and Pacific Werribee shopping nearby) that suit regular family life [citation:Visit Werribee — Cafes & Street Eats](https://www.visitwerribee.com/food-wine/cafes-street-eats) [citation:Pacific Werribee centre directory](https://pacificwerribee.qicre.com/stores/retail-stores/pacific-asian-supermarket/). Notable specialty/ethnic grocers in the Werribee/3030 area include Pacific Asian Supermarket and Filipino/Asian grocery shops (e.g., Mabuhay Pinoy / Feast on Asian listings) [citation:Pacific Asian Supermarket (Pacific Werribee)](https://pacificwerribee.qicre.com/stores/retail-stores/pacific-asian-supermarket/) [citation:Mabuhay Pinoy listing](https://mabuhay-pinoy-asian-grocery.lucky-three-seven.com/).

6) Weeknight dining (2–3 casual, family‑friendly options)  
- Tocca — family‑friendly Italian (pizza, pasta) in Werribee [citation:Tocca Werribee](https://tocca.melbourne/).  
- Pacific Werribee Urban Diner / food‑court and local takeaways offering Asian, pizza and kebab options — a convenient family takeaway hub [citation:Visit Werribee — Cafes & Street Eats](https://www.visitwerribee.com/food-wine/cafes-street-eats).  
- Local top‑rated takeaway picks highlighted by local guides (e.g., MELBZ best‑takeaway list) for quick family dinners [citation:MELBZ — Best Takeaway in Werribee](https://melbz.com.au/werribee/best-takeaway/).

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 centres)  
Kumon Werribee (Maths & English) is available in town (listed Kumon Werribee Education Centre) and there are nearby Kumon/Wyndham Vale centres for supplementary tuition [citation:Kumon search — Kumon Werribee](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-WERRIBEE-VIC). (No notable school‑reported academic outcomes were cited in the search snippets.)

8) Commute — train travel time to CBD (Werribee → Flinders Street)  
Regular Metro Trains services run Werribee→Flinders Street; typical scheduled travel time is roughly 38–42 minutes depending on the service (about every 20 minutes off‑peak) [citation:Rome2rio — Werribee Station to Flinders Street](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Werribee-Station/Flinders-Street-Station) [citation:Metro Trains — Werribee line overview](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/lines/werribee/).
