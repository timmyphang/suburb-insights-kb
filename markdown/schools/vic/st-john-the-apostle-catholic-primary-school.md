Welcome to Narraweena

There’s a quietly settled feeling to Narraweena that lands like a familiar jacket: established streets, a middle‑income mix of families and singles, and a lot of people who’ve put down roots rather than hopping between inner‑city rentals. The median age sits around 43, which you’ll notice in the pace of the suburb — relaxed school runs, weekend lawn-mowing, local cafés with regulars — and home ownership is higher than you’d expect if you’re comparing this to busier city pockets. It’s the kind of place where white‑collar work and family life meet the sand-and-surf culture of the Northern Beaches, and if you’re moving here from overseas, the presence of multilingual and overseas‑born households around town is a small comfort — you’ll find neighbours who’ve navigated the same unsettled early days and local networks that understand the transition to a new country.

Inside the Classroom at St John the Apostle Catholic Primary School, Narraweena

St John the Apostle is a K–6 primary school with a close-knit feel; classrooms run from Kindergarten through Year 6 and the school places a clear emphasis on arts and active community life. Music gets a visible profile here: an on-site keyboard program run through Note by Note Music School complements a student Music Ensemble that performs during school events, and the school also points families toward after‑school dance opportunities with a local dance academy. Sport and movement aren’t boxed into physical education hours alone — the school regularly promotes local basketball, tennis and multi‑sport camps so kids can keep moving after the bell. All up, the vibe is one of hands‑on participation rather than competition for competition’s sake.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

If you’re juggling work and school pickups, the practical stuff matters. St John the Apostle runs on‑site Before and After School Care provided by OOSH Northern Beaches, opening from 7:00am and staying on until 6:00pm, and they also provide Vacation Care during school holidays. That’s Outside School Hours Care — on-site before and after school childcare — which means drop-offs before your first meeting and a reliable, supervised handover once the afternoon rush starts. Because spaces in before-and-after programs can fill, families I talk to often recommend getting in touch early to secure a place and understand the program’s routines and fees.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Narraweena keeps weeknight meals easy. For everyday grocery runs, the Friendly Grocer on Alfred Street covers the essentials, while a couple of specialty grocers give you the ingredients for a worthwhile home-cooked meal. If tonight is "short on time" night, local takeaway spots pick up the slack: there’s a wood‑fired barbecue joint perfect for casual family meals, solid pizza and kebab outlets for a fuss-free dinner, and Thai, Vietnamese or café options on local delivery platforms when you want variety without the prep. The result is a neighbourhood where routines are simple to maintain and the occasional takeaway feels like a treat rather than a plan B.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

For families thinking about extra academic support, local tutoring services are available that offer both in‑home and online options, and you can arrange assessments or regular sessions if you want targeted help in literacy, numeracy or exam prep. On the commute front, Narraweena doesn’t have a Sydney Trains station, so most trips into the central business district combine bus services with nearby rail interchanges. Typical door‑to‑Central journeys using public transport commonly run around 40 to 50 minutes depending on routes and connections, so it’s worth trialling your morning and afternoon trip at a time you’d usually travel to get the real-world feel of timings and transfers.

Your Next Steps

Step 1: Book a school tour with St John the Apostle to see classrooms and confirm K–6 enrolment requirements, including any parish or fee details, so you understand the paperwork early.  
Step 2: If you’ll rely on before or after school care, contact OOSH Northern Beaches as soon as possible to enquire and enrol for their 7:00am–6:00pm care and for Vacation Care spots during the holidays.  
Step 3: Ask the school about the Note by Note keyboard program, the Music Ensemble and Dance North Academy classes, and take a look at local tutoring options if you want extra academic support; while you’re at it, map out Keolis Northern Beaches bus routes and use trip planners to test your daily commute.
