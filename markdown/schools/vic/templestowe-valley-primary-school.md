1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Lower Templestowe is an affluent, family-oriented suburb with a high proportion of white-collar professionals. The median weekly household income is $1,919, well above the national median, and the median age is 45, reflecting an established community [citation:2021 Templestowe Lower, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/207021160). The area has a high rate of outright home ownership and 80.7% of dwellings are detached houses, with 4,082 families recorded in 2021 [citation:Templestowe Lower – SA2 207021160](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/templestowe_lower).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The suburb is predominantly Australian-born, with 57.9% of residents born in Australia according to the 2016 Census [citation:2016 Templestowe Lower, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22472). The most common overseas country of birth is China (excluding SARs and Taiwan), accounting for 9.2% of the population [citation:2016 Templestowe Lower, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/207021160).

3) **Working-parent support:** Templestowe Valley Primary School runs an Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) program directly on site. The service operates before school from 7:00 AM to 9:00 AM and after school from 3:30 PM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday during school terms [citation:Templestowe Valley Primary School - OSHC | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/templestowe-valley-primary-school-oshc). The provider is the school itself (Templestowe Valley Primary School Council) and the service has 80 approved places [citation:Templestowe Valley Primary School - OSHC | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/templestowe-valley-primary-school-oshc).

4) **School culture:** The school offers a range of named extracurricular activities including **Valley Voices** (a choir that rehearses weekly and performs at school and community events), a **TVPS Rock Band** for students learning musical instruments, and **bi-annual whole school musical theatre productions** where every student from Prep to Year 6 performs [citation:Curriculum](https://www.tempvalprimary.vic.edu.au/page/30/Curriculum) [citation:School Production](https://www.tempvalprimary.vic.edu.au/school-production/). The school also runs lunchtime clubs and a **MAGIC Groups** multi-age well-being program [citation:Curriculum](https://www.tempvalprimary.vic.edu.au/page/30/Curriculum).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Lower Templestowe is a leafy suburban area with a strong community feel, offering access to parks, the Yarra River trails, and local shopping at the Templestowe Village shopping centre. For groceries, residents can shop at **Woolworths Templestowe Lower** (Unit 19, 325 Manningham Road, open 7 AM–10 PM daily), **Persian Market** (Unit 2, 325 Manningham Road), and **Seasons Asian Grocery** for a diverse range of Asian products [citation:Supermarkets in Templestowe Lower](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/templestowe-lower-vic-3107) [citation:Seasons Asian Grocery](https://www.dlook.com.au/business/asian-grocery-shop-in-templestowe-lower-vic-3107/seasons-asian-grocery).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Families can enjoy casual, family-friendly dining at **Tawee Thai** (Thai cuisine, offering takeaway and delivery), **Rajbhog Indian** (Indian restaurant in Templestowe), and **Pizzaroni's Templestowe** (pizza, open from 5:00 PM) [citation:TAWEE THAI, Templestowe Lower](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurant_Review-g4398581-d25438180-Reviews-Tawee_Thai-Templestowe_Lower_Manningham_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html) [citation:Restaurants in Templestowe,VIC With Takeaway](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/templestowe/takeaway-category).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Local tutoring options include **TopScore Education Melbourne** (2a Ashford Street, Templestowe Lower) and **Begin Bright Doncaster** (Unit 11, 114 James Street, Templestowe) [citation:30 Best Local Tuition Tutoring in Templestowe Lower](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/tuition-tutoring/templestowe-lower-vic-3107). Alpha Omega Education, while not in Lower Templestowe itself, offers early years enrichment programs in the broader area [citation:Alpha Omega Education](https://www.alphaomega.vic.edu.au/).

8) **Commute:** Lower Templestowe has no train station. The most direct public transport option is the **Route 905 bus**, which runs from Swanston Street in the city to Tuscany Rise/Porter Street in Templestowe, with a typical travel time of approximately 40 minutes [citation:Flinders Street Station to Templestowe](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Templestowe). The closest train station is **Eltham Station** on the **Hurstbridge line**, with a journey from Flinders Street Station to Eltham taking about 1 hour 15 minutes, followed by a bus connection [citation:Flinders Street Station to Templestowe](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Templestowe).
