1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Doreen is a middle-to-upper-income, family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,151, and the median age is 33, reflecting a large population of young families [citation:2021 Doreen, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20779). Children aged 0–14 years made up 26.8% of the population in 2021 [citation:2021 Doreen, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20779). Employment breakdown (white-collar vs blue-collar) was not found in the available sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Doreen is predominantly Australian-born. In 2021, 72.3% of residents were born in Australia [citation:2021 Doreen, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20779). The most common overseas country of birth was India, at 3.5% of the population [citation:2021 Doreen, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20779).

3) **Working-parent support:** Doreen Primary School partners with Camp Australia to provide its Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) program [citation:Our Staff | Doreen PS](https://www.doreenps.vic.edu.au/our-staff). The program offers before-school, after-school, and pupil-free day care, though specific hours of operation are not listed on the school site [citation:Out of School Hours Care – St Paul the Apostle, Doreen](https://www.spadoreen.catholic.edu.au/enrolment-information/st-paul-the-apostle-school-doreen-out-of-school-hours-care/). For exact hours and fees, families should contact Camp Australia directly on 1300 105 343 [citation:Out of School Hours Care – St Paul the Apostle, Doreen](https://www.spadoreen.catholic.edu.au/enrolment-information/st-paul-the-apostle-school-doreen-out-of-school-hours-care/).

4) **School culture:** Doreen Primary School offers a school choir that meets weekly, a band program where senior students form rock bands, and an extensive instrumental program with lessons on drums, guitar, bass, piano/keyboard, violin, and voice [citation:Music | Doreen PS](https://www.doreenps.vic.edu.au/music). The school also runs a Junior School Council [citation:General Curriculum | Doreen PS](https://www.doreenps.vic.edu.au/curriculum). The whole school performs in an Annual School Concert [citation:Music | Doreen PS](https://www.doreenps.vic.edu.au/music).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Doreen has a growing retail precinct with a Woolworths supermarket at 1070 Hazel Glen Drive [citation:Supermarkets in Doreen, VIC 3754 — All Stores & Hours | Yellow Pages®](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/doreen-vic-3754/supermarkets-grocery-stores). For specialty groceries, nearby South Morang (a few minutes' drive) has Annapoorna Groceries for Indian products and Inang's Asiamart for Asian goods, both on McDonalds Road [citation:Supermarkets in Doreen, VIC 3754 — All Stores & Hours | Yellow Pages®](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/doreen-vic-3754/supermarkets-grocery-stores). The suburb features many parks and wetlands, such as Serle Wetland Park and Hilltop Park [citation:THE 5 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Doreen - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g8366368-zfp10601-Doreen_Yarra_Valley_Victoria.html).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family takeaway, **Laurimar Pizza** (Italian/Pizza) on Armidale Road is a popular choice [citation:Restaurants in Doreen,VIC With Takeaway | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/doreen/takeaway-category). **Slices - Doreen** offers Italian cuisine with takeaway [citation:Restaurants in Doreen,VIC With Takeaway | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/doreen/takeaway-category). **Aksorn Thai** (Thai) on Hazel Glen Drive provides takeaway and has a child-friendly rating on AGFG [citation:Restaurants in Doreen,VIC With Takeaway | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/doreen/takeaway-category); [citation:Restaurants in Doreen,VIC With Child Friendly | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/doreen/childfriendly-category).
