1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**  
Ormond is an affluent, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,062 (2021 Census), well above the national median of $1,746. The median age is 37, indicating a mix of families and working-age adults, while the population density is 4,650 persons per km². The suburb is predominantly white-collar, with many residents employed in professional, managerial, and technical roles. Divrei Emineh does not offer on-site Outside School Hours Care (OSHC). The ACECQA national register confirms that the service at 290 Glen Eira Road provides no before-school care, after-school care, or vacation care. Families will need to arrange alternative OSHC from nearby providers, such as those in Bentleigh or McKinnon. 5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**  
Ormond is a quiet, family-friendly suburb with convenient grocery options. Major supermarkets include IGA at 509 North Road and Woolworths at 524 Centre Road, Bentleigh. For specialty groceries, Heng Sheng Asian Grocery is located at 1126 Glen Huntly Road in nearby Glen Huntly, offering a wide range of Asian products. 6) **Weeknight dining**  
Ormond offers several casual, family-friendly takeaway options. **North Road Fish & Chips** (seafood) provides classic fish and chips for a quick dinner. **Bombay Club** serves authentic Indian and tandoori cuisine with takeaway available. **SUM Cafe and Thai eatery** offers Thai dishes for dine-in or takeaway. All are located on or near North Road. [citation:Restaurants in Ormond,VIC With Takeaway | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/ormond/takeaway-category)

7) **Tuition and enrichment**  
**Austin Education** has an Ormond campus offering tutoring from Year 1 to Year 12, including VCE Maths, English, Chemistry, and Biology. Their programs include small classes, one-on-one sessions, and scholarship preparation. This is a well-established centre with a track record of academic results. 8) **Commute**  
Ormond is served by **Ormond Station** on the **Frankston line**. Direct trains run to Flinders Street Station every 15 minutes, with a typical travel time of **33 minutes** (via direct train) or **29 minutes** via Malvern Station. The service operates daily, providing a straightforward commute to the CBD.
