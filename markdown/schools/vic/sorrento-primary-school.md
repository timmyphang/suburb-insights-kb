1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Sorrento is an affluent, older suburb with a median age of 64 years – 26 years older than the national median – and a median personal income of $936 per week. The median weekly household income is $1,658. Home ownership is very high: 76.4% of dwellings are owned outright, and only 23.6% have a mortgage [citation:GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sorrento-vic). The population of 2,013 (2021 Census) is evenly split male/female, and the suburb has a notably low proportion of families with children, reflecting its retiree-heavy demographic [citation:ABS QuickStats 2021 Sorrento (Vic.)](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22306).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: Sorrento is overwhelmingly Australian-born: 91.8% of residents were born in Australia. Among overseas-born residents, the largest group is from England at 6.1%. No other country of birth exceeds 0.6% [citation:GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sorrento-vic).

3) **Working-parent support**: Sorrento Primary School operates its own on-site before- and after-school care program – it is not outsourced to a named external provider. The school’s website states it provides “a safe, enjoyable and nurturing environment during non-school hours” but does not list specific operating hours in the available sources [citation:FOR PARENTS - Welcome to Sorrento Primary School](http://sorrentops.vic.edu.au/for-parents/).

4) **School culture**: Sorrento Primary School offers a choir program with two named ensembles: ‘Sorrento Jewels’ (open to Years 3–6) and ‘Sorrento Sings’ (by invitation/audition for Years 5–6) [citation:Choir-Sorrento Sings and Sorrento Jewels](https://www.sorrentops.vic.edu.au/page/92/Choir-Sorrento-Sings-and-Sorrento-Jewels). Senior students participate in the Winter/Summer Lightning Premiership sports program, and individual instrumental lessons are available (students report lessons spanning nearly six years) [citation:YEARS 5 & 6 - Welcome to Sorrento Primary School](https://sorrentops.vic.edu.au/years-5-6/). The school also runs a ‘Buddy Program’ pairing Year 6 students with Foundation children.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Sorrento is a coastal town on the Mornington Peninsula with a relaxed, beachside feel. Major grocery options include Coles Sorrento (open daily 7am–9pm) [citation:Coles Sorrento](http://www.sorrento.com.au/food-and-wine/groceries/coles-sorrento), IGA Sorrento Village [citation:IGA Sorrento Village](https://www.iga.com.au/stores/iga-sorrento-village/), and Sorrento Supermarket (Ritchies) at 108 Ocean Beach Road [citation:Sorrento Supermarket](https://sorrento.shop.ritchies.com.au/). For specialty groceries, Scicluna’s Real Food Merchants offers fresh fruit, vegetables, deli items, and artisan breads [citation:Scicluna's Sorrento](https://sciclunas.com.au/stores/sorrento/), and Sorrento Italian Market is also a local specialty grocer [citation:Sorrento Italian Market](http://sorrentoitalianmarket.com/).

6) **Weeknight dining**: For casual, family-friendly takeaway or dining, Sorrento Thai Restaurant & Takeaway (located in the Sorrento Portsea RSL) serves Thai cuisine with dine-in and takeaway options [citation:SORRENTO THAI RESTAURANT & TAKEAWAY](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurant_Review-g552257-d3656768-Reviews-Sorrento_Thai_Restaurant_Takeaway-Sorrento_Mornington_Peninsula_Victoria.html). Thai Rosy Restaurant on Hurley Street also offers Thai takeaway [citation:Thai take away - Sorrento, VIC - Thai Rosy Restaurant](https://local.thairosyrestaurant.com.au/thai-take-away/victoria/sorrento). Additional options include pizzerias and Indian restaurants listed by Tripadvisor, but specific named venues beyond the Thai restaurants are not specified in the available sources.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Two named tutoring options serve the Sorrento area. Sorrento Tutoring Services (STS) is a personalised tutoring centre offering educational programmes for primary and secondary students [citation:Sorrento Tutoring Services](https://sorrentotutoring.simplybook.me/v2/). Success Tutoring provides one-on-one diagnostic assessments in English and Math for students in Sorrento [citation:Tutoring Sorrento | Primary & High School Tutors](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-sorrento). Both are local to the suburb.

8) **Commute**: Sorrento has no train station. The nearest train station is Frankston on the Frankston line (approximately 50 minutes from Flinders Street Station), but specific bus routes connecting Frankston to Sorrento are not detailed in the provided sources. Families typically drive (around 90 minutes to Melbourne CBD) or take a V/Line coach service from Southern Cross Station or Frankston; exact timetable and travel time for the coach are not found in the available context [citation:V/Line - Regional public transport for Victoria](https://www.vline.com.au/Timetables/Train-coach-timetables).
