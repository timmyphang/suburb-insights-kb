1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**  
Highton is a middle-to-upper-income, family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,054, and the median age is 39, with separate houses and couples with children being common [citation:2021 Highton Census QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21187)[citation:HIGHTON suburb profile – Landchecker](https://landchecker.com.au/suburb/highton-vic-3216/). Employment is predominantly white-collar, with 87.7% of residents travelling to work by car [citation:HIGHTON suburb profile – Landchecker](https://landchecker.com.au/suburb/highton-vic-3216/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**  
The provided 2021 Census QuickStats do not specify the most common foreign country of birth for Highton. However, 2016 Census data show that 60.2% of residents had both parents born in Australia, and the most common overseas country of birth was England (7.2%) [citation:2016 Highton Census QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/203021042). More recent 2021 figures are not available in the provided sources.

3) **Working-parent support**  
Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) at Highton Primary School is provided by TeamKids. Before School Care runs from 7:00 am to 9:00 am, and After School Care runs from 3:15 pm to 6:30 pm, with Vacation Care also available [citation:TeamKids Highton Primary](https://teamkids.com.au/venues/highton-primary-oshc/). The service is located on-site in the Community Building near the school car park.

4) **School culture**  
Specific extracurricular activities at Highton Primary School are not named in the available sources. The school is an International Baccalaureate (IB) World School and offers a canteen menu, but no named clubs, sports teams, or enrichment programs are listed [citation:Highton Primary School](https://www.hightonps.vic.edu.au/)[citation:F.A.Q – Highton Primary School](https://www.hightonps.vic.edu.au/?p=12). Families seeking detailed extracurriculars should contact the school directly.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**  
Highton is a quiet residential suburb with easy access to major supermarkets and specialty grocers. Full-service supermarkets include Woolworths at 4-46 Province Boulevard and IGA at 53 North Valley Road [citation:Yellow Pages Supermarkets Highton](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/highton-vic-3216). For ethnic groceries, the Best Indian Grocery Store (63 Thomson Street, Belmont) and Gourmet Asian Grocery (Shop 5, 168 High Street, Belmont) are nearby [citation:Local Business Guide Supermarkets Highton](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/victoria/highton/shopping/Supermarkets-and-Grocery-Stores/).

6) **Weeknight dining**  
Casual family-friendly takeaway options in Highton include Black Basil Thai (Thai cuisine, opens 5:00 pm), Deccan Indian Kitchen (Indian cuisine, opens 4:00 pm), and Pizzas with Attitude Highton (pizza, opens 4:00 pm) [citation:AGFG Takeaway Highton](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/geelong-bellarine-peninsula/highton/takeaway-category). All offer takeaway and are suitable for weeknight meals.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**  
Learning Works Hub provides personalised Maths and English tutoring in Highton, using qualified teachers [citation:Learning Works Tutoring Highton](https://www.learningworkshub.com.au/tutoring-highton/). Kip McGrath Education Centres also operates nearby at Shop 5, 155 High Street, Belmont, offering literacy and numeracy support for primary and secondary students [citation:Yellow Pages Tuition Tutoring Highton](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/highton-vic/tuition-tutoring).

8) **Commute**  
Highton does not have its own train station; the nearest V/Line station is Geelong Railway Station, located approximately 3 km away. The Geelong line runs to Southern Cross Station in Melbourne, with a typical travel time of about 60 minutes (not explicitly stated in provided sources). From Southern Cross, Flinders Street Station is a 5-minute tram ride or a short walk.
