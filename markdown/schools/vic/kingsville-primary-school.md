Yarraville has a relatively high median weekly household income (≈$2,485/week) and a median age of 37 — overall it reads as an inner‑city, predominantly professional/white‑collar, family‑oriented suburb rather than a working‑class area [citation:2021 Yarraville QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22917).
