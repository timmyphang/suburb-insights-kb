1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Kealba is a working-class to middle-income suburb. According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income is $1,542, and the median age is 39 [citation:2021 Kealba, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21312). The suburb has a mix of families and singles, with 908 families and an average of 0.9 children per household [citation:2021 Kealba, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21312). Occupation data is not provided in the available sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The 2016 Census indicated that the most common country of birth overseas was Vietnam, with 8.4% of residents born there [citation:2016 Kealba, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21304). In 2016, 52.6% of people spoke only English at home, with other top languages including Vietnamese (12.6%) and Arabic (3.8%) [citation:2016 Kealba, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21304). 2021 data for country of birth was not found in the provided sources.
