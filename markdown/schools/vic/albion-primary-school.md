1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Albion is a working-to-middle-class suburb in Melbourne's west. The median weekly household income is $2,052, which is slightly above the national median but below many affluent eastern suburbs [citation:2021 Albion, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20021). The median age is 32 years, indicating a relatively young population with a mix of families and singles, though specific white-collar/blue-collar split figures are not provided in the available data [citation:2021 Albion, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20021).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** According to the 2021 Census, 86.2% of Albion residents were born in Australia, making it a predominantly Australian-born suburb [citation:Albion QLD — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/albion-qld). For those born overseas, the most common country of birth is England at 6.9% [citation:Albion QLD — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/albion-qld). Note: The available QuickStats for the Victorian Albion (SAL20021) do not list country of birth percentages, so QLD data is used as a proxy; the true Victorian Albion figure may differ slightly.

3) **Working-parent support:** Albion Primary School partners with St Theresa's Primary School to offer before and after school care through OSH Club [citation:OSH Club - Albion Primary School](https://www.albionps.vic.edu.au/articles/346). Specific operating hours (e.g., 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM) are not listed on the school's page. Families must register and book with OSH Club; children are walked between the two schools for care sessions [citation:OSH Club - Albion Primary School](https://www.albionps.vic.edu.au/articles/346).

4) **School culture:** Albion Primary School has a strong performing arts program, including home-grown musical productions (e.g., *Under the Big Top* in 2018) and an "Albion's Got Talent" afternoon [citation:Performing Arts - Albion Primary School](https://www.albionps.vic.edu.au/page/204/Performing-Arts). All students participate in music classes sponsored by the Australian Children's Music Foundation, with optional instrument lessons on a user-pays basis [citation:Performing Arts - Albion Primary School](https://www.albionps.vic.edu.au/page/204/Performing-Arts). The school also runs Interschool Sport, with fixtures such as a home game versus Ardeer PS [citation:Our Values: Respect, Honesty, Care, Learning and Achievement](https://www.albionps.vic.edu.au/uploaded_files/media/1519265337newsletter_no._1.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Albion is a family-oriented, multicultural neighbourhood with easy access to several supermarkets in nearby Sunshine. For everyday shopping, residents can visit Woolworths at 80 Harvester Road, Sunshine (3 km away) [citation:Supermarkets in Albion, VIC 3020 — All Stores & Hours | Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/albion-vic-3020/supermarkets-grocery-stores). Specialty groceries include SPICES & LENTILS- Srilankan and Indian grocery at Shop 50, 324-328 Hampshire Road, Sunshine, and Sagar Spices at Shop 11, 254 Hampshire Road, Sunshine [citation:Supermarkets in Albion, VIC 3020 — All Stores & Hours | Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/albion-vic-3020/supermarkets-grocery-stores).
