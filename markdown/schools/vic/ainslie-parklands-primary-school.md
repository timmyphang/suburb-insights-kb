1) Suburb socio-economic profile: Croydon is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a higher-than-average proportion of couples with children. The median weekly household income is $1,615, and the median age is 39, slightly above the national median of 38 [citation:2021 Croydon (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20682). The suburb has a strong family focus, with 7,785 families and an average of 1.8 children per family with children [citation:2021 Croydon (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20682).

2) Most common foreign country of birth: The suburb is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 86.3% of residents born in Australia [citation:Croydon VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/croydon-vic). The most common overseas country of birth is England, accounting for 5.1% of the population [citation:Croydon VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/croydon-vic).

3) Working-parent support: Ainslie Parklands Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by TheirCare. The service operates before school from 6:45am to 8:45am and after school from 3:30pm to 6:00pm on school days [citation:Out of School Hours Care - Ainslie Parklands Primary School](https://www.ainslieparklandsps.vic.edu.au/out-of-school-hours-care).

4) School culture: Ainslie Parklands Primary School provides a range of named extracurricular activities. The school offers a **Choir** for students in Years 2-6, who perform at every assembly [citation:Performing Arts - Ainslie Parklands Primary School](https://www.ainslieparklandsps.vic.edu.au/music). It also runs **Instrumental Lessons** on piano, guitar, drums, and brass instruments [citation:Performing Arts - Ainslie Parklands Primary School](https://www.ainslieparklandsps.vic.edu.au/music). Additional music programs include classroom-based **Music specialist lessons** from Foundation to Year 6, with a focus on practical instrument playing [citation:Performing Arts - Ainslie Parklands Primary School](https://www.ainslieparklandsps.vic.edu.au/music).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries: Croydon offers a family-friendly atmosphere with several shopping hubs, including Croydon Central shopping centre. For specialty groceries, **The Local Asian Grocer** is located inside Croydon Central opposite Coles [citation:The Local Asian Grocer | Croydon Central](https://www.croydoncentral.com.au/stores/local-asian-grocer). On Croydon Main Street, there are additional specialty stores including **Chin Star Asian Grocery** and **European Grocery Store** [citation:Grocery & Food Imports Traders - Croydon Main Street](https://www.croydonmainstreet.com.au/trader-category/grocery-food-imports/).

6) Weeknight dining: Croydon has several casual, family-friendly dining options. **Holy Basil Thai** is a highly-rated Thai restaurant offering takeaway [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Croydon - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g552158-zfp10601-Croydon_Maroondah_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). **Piyada Thai Restaurant** is another popular Thai takeaway option on Maroondah Highway [citation:Piyada Thai (Croydon) - Order Online](https://piyadathai.com.au/). For Mexican, **Carlos Cantina** provides a lively, child-friendly atmosphere for dinner [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Croydon - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g552158-zfp10601-Croydon_Maroondah_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).
