Below is a focused, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Springvale Rise Primary School (Springvale, VIC). Each item is short and fact‑first.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Springvale is a culturally diverse, lower‑SES suburb with relatively concentrated economic disadvantage (lower SEIFA/IRSD scores) and a younger median age (median 36) — more working‑class / high proportion of overseas movers than middle‑affluent suburbs [citation:2021 Springvale (Vic.), Census QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22328) [citation:Addressing concentrations of disadvantage: Springvale case study (AHURI)](https://www.ahuri.edu.au/sites/default/files/migration/documents/AHURI_Research_Paper_Addressing-concentrations-of-disadvantage-Springvale-case-study-report.pdf).

2) Most common overseas country of birth
- The largest overseas‑born group in Springvale is people born in Vietnam (around one‑in‑five residents; ~20% of residents reported Vietnam as country of birth in 2021 Census summaries) [citation:2021 Springvale, Census QuickStats (place-level summaries)](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/212041317) [citation:Springvale suburb profile summary (local summary)](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/springvale).

3) Working‑parent support — OSHC availability at the school
- Springvale Rise Primary School runs OSHC (Before School Care, After School Care and Vacation/Holiday programs) on site; the service is operated by TheirCare and is listed on the school’s parents page and TheirCare/ACECQA listings [citation:Daily Procedures — Springvale Rise PS (Parents)](https://springvaleriseps.vic.edu.au/parents/) [citation:Springvale Rise PS TheirCare (TheirCare listing)](https://theircare.com.au/holiday-programs/springvale-rise-ps-theircare/) [citation:Springvale Rise Primary School Council OSHC - ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/providers/springvale-rise-primary-school-council-oshc).

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars offered
- Sporting program with interschool competition (swimming, athletics, football, soccer, netball, volleyball, cross‑country) [citation:Curriculum — Springvale Rise PS](https://springvaleriseps.vic.edu.au/curriculum/).  
- Strong Visual Arts program / Arts activities and language learning (Vietnamese language activities noted) [citation:Curriculum — Springvale Rise PS](https://springvaleriseps.vic.edu.au/curriculum/).  
- The school also posts choral/performing‑arts events (school performances) online, indicating active music/choir involvement [citation:Springvale Rise Primary School | Voices for Reconciliation 2026 (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rAAWUxKr0wE).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Springvale is a bustling, family‑oriented multicultural precinct with large Asian markets and small‑business shopping around Springvale Station; good for affordable fresh produce and specialty Asian ingredients [citation:Springvale Market - WeekendNotes](https://www.weekendnotes.com/springvale-market/) [citation:Springvale Market: The Hub of Authentic Asian Flavours (Asian Inspirations)](https://asianinspirations.com.au/experiences/springvale-market-the-hub-of-authentic-asian-flavours/).  
- Notable local grocers/supermarkets include Binh Minh Asian Groceries (Spring Vale Shopping Centre), Num Lee Long Asian Groceries and LH Fresh (Asian supermarket chains) [citation:Spring Vale Shopping Centre - Binh Minh Asian Groceries](https://springvaleshoppingcentre.com.au/portfolio-items/binh-minh-asian-groceries/) [citation:Num Lee Long Asian Groceries](https://www.numleelong.com.au/).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 family‑friendly takeaway/dining options & cuisines
- Pho Nho (popular pho/ Vietnamese takeaway) — family‑friendly and on delivery platforms [citation:Order Pho Nho Springvale | Uber Eats](https://www.ubereats.com/au/store/pho-nho-springvale/uCG8qY9ASPO7ii-gb4BdwQ).  
- Pho Hung Vuong — well‑reviewed pho house (casual Vietnamese dining) [citation:PHO HUNG VUONG, Springvale - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurant_Review-g1214782-d4060000-Reviews-Pho_Hung_Vuong-Springvale_Dandenong_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).  
- Multiple casual Vietnamese bakeries/restaurants and cheap Asian takeaways cluster around Springvale Market — Vietnamese cuisine is a local standout [citation:The 18 Best Springvale, VIC Vietnamese Restaurants | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/springvale/vietnamese-cuisine).
