1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Keysborough is a largely family‑oriented, younger‑than‑average suburb (median age 36) with a high share of married households and an average household size around 3.2 people, consistent with many family households [citation:2021 Keysborough QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21339).  
- Median weekly household income was reported around $1,991 (2021 Census), which positions Keysborough as broadly middle‑income (mixed white‑ and blue‑collar employment across the area) rather than an affluent pocket [citation:2021 Keysborough QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21339).
