1) Ferny Creek is an affluent, family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,393, well above the national median of $1,746, and the median age is 45, indicating a mature population [citation:2021 Ferny Creek QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20918). There are 439 families and an average of 1.9 children per family, reinforcing a strong family demographic [citation:2021 Ferny Creek QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20918). The suburb is largely white-collar, set in the leafy Dandenong Ranges.

2) The suburb is overwhelmingly Australian-born. According to the 2011 Census, 79.3% of residents were born in Australia, with England (5.7%) being the most common overseas country of birth [citation:2011 Ferny Creek QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2011/SSC20487).
