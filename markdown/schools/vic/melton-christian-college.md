Here’s a concise, family-facing eight‑point briefing for Melton Christian College (Brookfield). I used search‑snippets first and opened pages only when needed; sources are cited after each fact.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Brookfield (City of Melton) is a young, family‑oriented outer‑suburban area with a median age ~32 and larger-than-average household size; median weekly household income ~$1,642 — broadly middle‑income, family-heavy suburb with many young white‑collar and trades/blue‑collar households as the area grows [citation:ABS QuickStats Brookfield 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20351) [citation:Melton City Council Brookfield community profile (draft)](https://www.melton.vic.gov.au/files/assets/public/v/1/council/about-the-city/demographics/community-profiles/community-profile-2023-infographics/community-profile-brookfield.pdf).

2) Most common foreign country of birth  
Majority born in Australia (~60.9%); the largest overseas birthplace is India (around 7–8% of residents) per 2021 Census summaries [citation:ABS QuickStats Brookfield 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20351) [citation:Brookfield demographics summary](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/brookfield).

3) Working‑parent support — OSHC availability at the school  
Melton Christian College partners with Camp Australia to provide before‑school, after‑school and vacation care (OSHC) at the Brookfield campus (listed on Camp Australia and StartingBlocks) — families can book OSHC through Camp Australia [citation:Camp Australia — Melton Christian College](https://campaustralia.com.au/school/?accessCode=2060&doing_wp_cron=1724091120.0411779880523681640625) [citation:StartingBlocks — Camp Australia Melton Christian College OSHC](https://www.startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01HGCQW1Q9M4BXW2K94B1V704Q/Camp-Australia-Melton-Christian-College-OSHC).

4) School culture — key extracurriculars (2–3 specific items)  
Inter‑school sport teams and carnivals (swimming, athletics, CSEN rally days); optional instrumental music / small instrumental program; school camps and an annual school musical — also junior farm activities for younger grades [citation:Melton Christian College Sports archive](https://mcc.vic.edu.au/category/sports/) [citation:SchoolChoice Melton Christian College profile](https://www.schoolchoice.com.au/melton-christian-college/).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (2 sentences)  
Brookfield is a fast‑growing, family‑focused outer suburb with local convenience shops but residents commonly use nearby Melton shopping centres for major groceries and services [citation:Melbz Brookfield guide](https://melbz.com.au/brookfield/best-restaurants/). Local grocery options include small convenience stores and specialty outlets such as Blue Nile Groceries (local African/Middle‑Eastern style grocer listing) and larger supermarkets in nearby Melton (Coles, Woolworths, ALDI) for full weekly shopping [citation:Blue Nile Groceries listing](https://australia.chamberofcommerce.com/business-directory/victoria/brookfield/grocery-store/3681116-blue-nile-groceries) [citation:Melton supermarkets directory](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/victoria/melton/Shopping/Supermarkets-and-Grocery-Stores/).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 family-friendly takeaway/dining options  
Brookfield Convenience & Takeaway (local fish & chips, burgers, wraps) and small local takeaway shops are the immediate options; for wider family dining choices (Asian, Middle Eastern, pizza) families usually go to Melton / Woodgrove shopping precincts a short drive away [citation:Uber Eats — Brookfield Convenience & Takeaway](https://www.ubereats.com/au/store/brookfield-convenience-%26-takeaway/IArVnqNQWCWh_pwGr-_Xgw) [citation:Melbz Brookfield guide](https://melbz.com.au/brookfield/best-restaurants/).

7) Tuition and enrichment nearby  
Melton Christian College runs its own after‑school tutoring/homework hub at the College; local private providers include Kip McGrath Melton and several independent tuition services (e.g., Learner’s Hub / Alchemy Tuition) serving Brookfield/Melton families [citation:Melton Christian College tutoring page](https://mcc.vic.edu.au/discover/tutoring/) [citation:Kip McGrath Melton](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/melton).

8) Commute — train/bus to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)  
Brookfield does not have an active metropolitan passenger train station; travel is typically by local bus to Melton or nearby rail hubs, then V/Line/Metro into the city. Typical door‑to‑door public‑transport journeys to Flinders Street are commonly about 60–80 minutes (bus + train), depending on connections; Rome2rio and local journey planners show ranges around 1–1.4 hours for Brookfield → Flinders St [citation:Moovit — Brookfield public transport (buses)](https://moovitapp.com/index/en-gb/public_transportation-Brookfield-Melbourne-city_34862-2803) [citation:Rome2rio Brookfield → Flinders Street Station estimate](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Brookfield-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
