1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Cranbourne West is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median age of 32 and a high proportion of young children (10.0% aged 0–4 years) [citation:2021 Cranbourne West QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20666). The median weekly household income is $1,856, above the national median of $1,746, and the housing stock is dominated by detached houses (83.2%) [citation:AreaSearch Cranbourne West](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/cranbourne_west). The suburb has 5,302 families, indicating a strong family presence, and a labour force mix that is likely a blend of white-collar and blue-collar workers (detailed occupation breakdowns were not found in available sources).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The most common overseas country of birth in Cranbourne West is India, accounting for 12.5% of residents. Overall, 45.9% of the population was born overseas, with other significant communities from Afghanistan (3.7%) and New Zealand (3.1%) [citation:Grokipedia Cranbourne West](https://grokipedia.com/page/cranbourne_west). The Australian-born percentage is not explicitly stated in available sources but is implied to be 54.1% (100% – 45.9%).

3) **Working-parent support**: Quarters Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by **TheirCare**. The program runs weekdays from **6:30am until 8:30am** in the morning and from **3:15pm until 6:30pm** in the afternoon; on pupil-free days and school holidays it operates from 6:30am to 6:30pm [citation:Before and After School Care](https://quartersps.vic.edu.au/parent-information/before-and-after-school-care/). Families may be eligible for the Child Care Subsidy, which can reduce session costs by up to 90%.

4) **School culture**: Quarters Primary School offers several named extracurricular activities, including **District Athletics** (held Wednesday 13 May), a **Grade 5/6 Camp** (Wednesday 17 – Friday 19 June), and a **Lamont Book Fair** (Monday 24 – Friday 28 August) [citation:Important Dates](https://quartersps.vic.edu.au/parent-information/important-dates/). The school also celebrates events such as Harmony Day and conducts Water Safety Week for Grades 1–4.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Cranbourne West is a growing family suburb with a local shopping centre on Morningside Boulevard that features a full-service **Woolworths** supermarket [citation:Cranbourne West Store Directory](https://cranbournewestshoppingcentre.com.au/store_directory/). For specialty groceries, the centre houses **Kuya JJ Asian Grocery** (Asian groceries including dumplings, noodles, and frozen goods) and **Homeland Spices** (Indian, Pakistani, and Sri Lankan groceries) [citation:Kuya JJ Asian Grocery](https://cranbournewestshoppingcentre.com.au/store/kuya-jj-asian-grocery/). An additional Indian grocery store, Wirsa Indian Groceries, also serves the area [citation:Indian Grocery Store Cranbourne](https://wirsa.com.au/indian-grocery-store-cranbourne/).

6) **Weeknight dining**: Family-friendly takeaway and dining options in Cranbourne West include **Bubba Pizza Cranbourne West** (4/85 Everlasting Blvd) for pizza, **Lala’s Kitchen** (34 Industrial Circuit) for Indian cuisine, and **St Germain Kebab, Fish & Chips** (Shop 4, Cranbourne West Shopping Centre) for kebabs and seafood [citation:OzFoodHunter Cranbourne West](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/cranbourne-west-3977) [citation:Cranbourne West Shopping Centre Directory](https://cranbournewestshoppingcentre.com.au/store_directory/). These offer both takeaway and delivery options.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: A named tutoring centre in Cranbourne West is **Edu Kingdom College Cranbourne West**, located at 6 Universal Way (opposite the 7-Eleven petrol station) [citation:Tutoring Centre Cranbourne West](https://www.tutoringcentre.net/cranbourne-west.html). It offers tutoring from Year 1 to Year 12, covering Maths, English, Science, and selective school preparation (e.g., for Melbourne High and Nossal High School). Another option is **Tuition Zone**, which provides a two-on-one tutoring model for Prep to Year 12 at its Cranbourne West location [citation:Tuition Zone Cranbourne West](https://tuitionzone.com.au/locations/tutoring-cranbourne-west).

8) **Commute**: Cranbourne West is served by the **Cranbourne railway line** on the Metro Trains network. The nearest station is **Cranbourne Station**, from which the train to **Flinders Street Station** takes approximately **56 minutes** (according to Rome2Rio) [citation:Rome2Rio Cranbourne to Flinders Street](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Cranbourne/Flinders-Street-Station). Driving from Cranbourne West to the CBD takes about 37 minutes via the Monash Freeway.
