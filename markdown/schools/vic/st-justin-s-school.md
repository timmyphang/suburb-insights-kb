1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Wheelers Hill is an affluent, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,818 and a median age of 48 [citation:2021 Wheelers Hill, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22766). The suburb has 5,934 families and an average of 0.8 children per household, indicating a mix of older families and empty-nesters, with a predominantly white-collar professional demographic [citation:2021 Wheelers Hill, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22766).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Country-of-birth data for 2021 is not fully broken down in available sources, but 2016 data shows that 59.0% of Wheelers Hill residents had both parents born overseas [citation:2016 Wheelers Hill, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22752). In 2021, 27.6% of people had both parents born in Australia, and the most common overseas countries of birth were not specified in the provided snippets [citation:2016 Wheelers Hill, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22752).

3) **Working-parent support:** St Justin's School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) operated by Camp Australia, with before-school care from 7:00am to 8:55am and after-school care from 3:15pm to 6:00pm on school days [citation:Camp Australia - St Justin's Catholic Primary School OSHC](https://campaustralia.com.au/school/?accessCode=1895), [citation:St Justin's OSHC Wheelers Hill | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/st-justins-oshc-wheelers-hill). The service is located at 42 Whalley Drive, Wheelers Hill, and is approved for up to 45 children [citation:St Justin's OSHC Wheelers Hill | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/st-justins-oshc-wheelers-hill).

4) **School culture:** St Justin's School offers several named extracurriculars including the **Kitchen Garden program** (held every Thursday for all year levels), the **St. Justin's Choir** (for Years 3-6, rehearsing weekly at lunchtime), and **Drumming Beats** (a performance group for Years 3-6 using "rubbish bin drums") [citation:Our Extra Activities | Stjustins](https://www.sjwheelershill.catholic.edu.au/ourextraactivities). The school also has a **Running Club** for Years 3-6 one morning per week and a **Swimming program** for Prep to Year 4 focused on water safety [citation:Our Extra Activities | Stjustins](https://www.sjwheelershill.catholic.edu.au/ourextraactivities).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Wheelers Hill is a quiet, leafy suburb with local shopping at Brandon Park Shopping Centre, featuring major supermarkets like Woolworths [citation:Wheelers Hill - Store locator](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/storelocator/vic-wheelers-hill-3146). Specialty options include **Astee Asian Grocery** in the Brandon Park centre, offering a wide range of Asian groceries, frozen foods, and fresh produce [citation:Astee Asian Grocer, Wheelers Hill](https://www.wordofmouth.com.au/reviews/astee-asian-grocer). Additional Chinese supermarkets are available nearby for traditional ingredients [citation:Wheelers Hill's Best Chinese grocery stores](https://www.dlook.com.au/find/chinese-supermarket-in-wheelers-hill-vic-3150).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly dining options in Wheelers Hill include **Wheelers Hill Hotel** (Family Bistro with views of the Dandenong Ranges, serving steaks and seafood) [citation:Wheelers Hill Hotel](https://www.wheelershillhotel.com.au/), **Young and Foolish** (a cafe offering Australian fare and take-home coffee) [citation:THE 10 BEST Restaurants in Wheelers Hill](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g2686202-Wheelers_Hill_Monash_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html), and **Uncle Jacks Kitchen** (an Asian, Chinese takeaway option) [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Wheelers Hill](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/wheelers-hill-vic-3150).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Local tutoring options include **Success Tutoring Wheelers Hill**, which offers personalised learning support with 24/7 access to resources [citation:Wheelers Hill – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-wheelers-hill), and **Math Minds Learning Centre**, which provides maths tutoring for students in the area [citation:Maths Tutoring Wheelers Hill — Math💡Minds • Learning Centre](https://mathminds.com.au/maths-tutor-wheelers-hill/). Online tutoring services like Tutero and Cloud Tuition also serve the suburb [citation:Online Tutoring in Wheelers Hill, VIC | Tutero](https://www.tutero.com/au/tutoringlp/tutoring-wheelers-hill).

8) **Commute:** Wheelers Hill does not have its own train station; the nearest major station is **Glen Waverley** on the **Glen Waverley line**, approximately 1 hour 3 minutes by train from Flinders Street Station [citation:Flinders Street Station to Wheelers Hill](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Wheelers-Hill). An alternative is the train to **Oakleigh Station** on the Pakenham/Cranbourne line, then bus route **693** to Lum Rd/Ferntree Gully Rd, taking about 1 hour 6 minutes [citation:Flinders Street Station to Wheelers Hill](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Wheelers-Hill). Driving from the CBD takes approximately 21 minutes [citation:Flinders Street Station to Wheelers Hill](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Wheelers-Hill).
