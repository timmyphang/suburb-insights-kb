1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Rosebud is a coastal suburb with a median age of 49, notably older than the Victorian median of 38, and a median weekly household income of $1,183, which is below the state average [citation:2021 Rosebud QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22198). The suburb has a mix of families and retirees, with 3,861 families and 8,717 private dwellings, and a higher proportion of female residents (52.7%) [citation:2021 Rosebud QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22198). The workforce mix is not explicitly detailed in the provided sources, but the area is considered a middle-income, coastal community with a blend of white-collar and blue-collar occupations.

3) **Working-parent support**: Our Lady of Fatima School partners with Camp Australia for Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) [citation:Camp Australia](https://campaustralia.com.au/school/?accessCode=987C). The specific hours of operation for this service are not listed in the provided sources, but Camp Australia typically offers before-school care from 7:00am and after-school care until 6:00pm at other schools. Vacation care is also likely available, but no exact timings are given for this school.

4) **School culture**: Our Lady of Fatima School offers a range of named extracurricular activities, including **choir**, **garden club**, and **Lego club** during playtimes, as noted on the school’s wellbeing page [citation:Wellbeing - Our Lady of Fatima](https://www.fatimarosebud.catholic.edu.au/about-3). The school also has a strong performing arts program with weekly lessons in dance, music, and drama, and students participate in performances [citation:Learning & Teaching - Our Lady of Fatima](https://www.fatimarosebud.catholic.edu.au/ourlearning). These activities are facilitated by student leaders and staff.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Rosebud has a suburban, family-friendly atmosphere with local amenities including Rosebud Plaza shopping centre, which houses Woolworths and specialty stores [citation:Rosebud Plaza](https://rosebudplaza.com.au/). For ethnic groceries, residents can visit **Great Asia Food Store** and **India at Home**, both located in Rosebud, as well as **Mr MAMA International Grocery** and **Masagana Filipino Asian Grocery** [citation:Yelp International Grocery](https://www.yelp.com/search?cflt=intlgrocery&find_loc=Rosebud+Victoria+3939). The area also has a Ritchies SUPA IGA for general groceries.

6) **Weeknight dining**: For casual family-friendly dining, **Rosebud Thai Restaurant** offers authentic Thai cuisine with dine-in, takeaway, and delivery options (883 Point Nepean Rd) [citation:Rosebud Thai Restaurant](https://www.rosebudthairestaurant.com.au/). **Jettys Pizza Rosebud** serves classic gourmet pizzas for takeaway, and **Papa Rolls Vietnamese** provides Vietnamese dishes such as pho and rolls, available via Uber Eats [citation:Uber Eats Rosebud](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/rosebud-melbourne-vic). These options are well-regarded for quick, affordable meals.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: **Rosebud Tutoring** offers in-person and online tutoring for primary through Year 12, covering all subjects with personalised learning plans [citation:Rosebud Tutoring](https://www.rosebudtutoring.com.au/). **Success Tutoring** also provides academic support in Rosebud, with 24/7 access to resources and tailored lessons for students of all ages [citation:Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-rosebud). Both services cater to local families seeking enrichment.

8) **Commute**: Rosebud does not have a train station; the nearest train service is via the **Frankston line** from Frankston Station. The most practical public transport option is the **788 bus** from Rosebud to Frankston Station, then a train on the Frankston line to Flinders Street Station, with a total travel time of approximately **1 hour 30 minutes** [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Rosebud/Flinders-Street-Station). Driving to Frankston Station and taking the train is also common.
