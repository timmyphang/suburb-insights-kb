1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Sunbury is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,106, and the median age is 38 years [citation:Sunbury - South – SA2 210041540 | AreaSearch](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/sunbury_south). The housing mix is predominantly separate houses (76.1%) with above-average rates of outright home ownership, indicating a stable, family-focused community [citation:Sunbury - South – SA2 210041540 | AreaSearch](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/sunbury_south).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The population is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 92.4% of residents born in Australia [citation:Sunbury VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunbury-vic). Of the overseas-born residents, the most common country of birth is England (3.9%) [citation:Sunbury VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunbury-vic).

3) **Working-parent support**: Kismet Park Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by TheirCare [citation:Before & After School Care](https://www.kismetparkps.vic.edu.au/page/88/Before-&-After-School-Care). The program runs from 6:30am until 8:45am in the morning and from 3:15pm until 6:30pm each school day [citation:Before & After School Care](https://www.kismetparkps.vic.edu.au/page/88/Before-&-After-School-Care).

4) **School culture**: Kismet Park Primary School offers a range of named extracurricular clubs. These include a Robotics Club, a Girls Wellbeing Club, and a Japanese Club, all running at lunchtimes [citation:Lunchtime Clubs](https://www.kismetparkps.vic.edu.au/page/240/Lunchtime-Clubs). The school also has a comprehensive Performing Arts program covering Dance, Drama, and Music [citation:Welcome from the Acting Principal](https://www.kismetparkps.vic.edu.au/page/53/Welcome-from-the-Principal).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Sunbury is a family-friendly suburb with a range of local amenities centred around the Sunbury Square Shopping Centre. For specialty groceries, families can visit Asia Finest Grocery, located at Shop 9 in Sunbury Square, which stocks Filipino, Indian, Chinese, Japanese, Thai, and other Asian products [citation:Asia Finest Grocery - Sunbury Square Shopping Centre](https://sunburysquare.com.au/asias-finest-grocery/).
