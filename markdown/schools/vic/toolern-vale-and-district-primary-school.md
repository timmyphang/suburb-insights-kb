1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Toolern Vale is a semi-rural, middle-income suburb. The median weekly household income is $1,908, and the median age is 44, notably older than the Victorian average of 38 [citation:2021 Toolern Vale, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22537). With 52.79% of residents married, an average household size of 2.9 people, and a high car ownership rate (3.1 vehicles per dwelling), the suburb has a strong family and owner-occupier character [citation:Toolern Vale, VIC 3337 Property Market and House Prices 2026](https://www.htag.com.au/vic/vic237-melton-city/toolern-vale-vic-3337/). Detailed occupations splits (white-collar vs blue-collar) were not found in available sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Toolern Vale is overwhelmingly Australian-born. The 2016 Census records that 60.3% of people had both parents born in Australia, and the most common overseas country of birth was England at 3.2% [citation:2016 Toolern Vale, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22524). More recent 2021 data for the broader Kurunjang - Toolern Vale SA2 area shows the Australian-born percentage is dominant, but the exact suburb-level Australian-born share for 2021 was not directly stated in the provided snippets.

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided at Toolern Vale and District Primary School by OSHClub. The programs are called 'Rise then Shine' (before school, operating from 6:00 AM to bell time) and 'Stay and Play' (after school, from bell time to 6:00 PM), and a Holiday HQ program runs from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM on pupil-free days [citation:Toolern Vale & District Primary School | OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/toolern-vale-and-district-primary-school/). The provider can be contacted at 0455 469 712 [citation:Toolern Vale & District Primary School | OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/toolern-vale-and-district-primary-school/).
