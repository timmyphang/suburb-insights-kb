1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Clayton is a youthful, middle-income suburb with a median age of 28 and a median weekly household income of $1,494 according to the 2021 Census [citation:2021 Clayton, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20569). The population of 18,988 includes a high proportion of young adults (22% aged 20–24 and 19.6% aged 25–29), reflecting the large student population from nearby Monash University. The suburb has a mix of white-collar professionals and blue-collar workers, but specific occupation data is not detailed in the provided sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas country of birth for Clayton residents is not explicitly stated in the provided search results. The 2021 Census QuickStats for Clayton does not list the top country of birth within the excerpts available [citation:2021 Clayton, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20569). However, the suburb is known to have large Chinese and Indian-born communities, as evidenced by numerous Asian grocery stores in the area.

3) **Working-parent support:** Clayton North Primary School offers a free Breakfast Club from 7:30 am to 8:30 am on school days, and a council-managed After School Care program from 3:30 pm to 6:30 pm [citation:Before and After School Care](https://www.claytonnorthps.vic.edu.au/page/240/Before-and-After-School-Care). The after-school care is run by the School Council, not by an external provider, and requires booking through the school office. A separate Camp Australia page also lists the school as a partner, but the school’s own site describes the service as council-managed [citation:Clayton North Primary School | Camp Australia](https://www.campaustralia.com.au/schools/83E8).

4) **School culture:** Clayton North Primary School offers several named extracurriculars, including the **School Band** (for Year 5–6 students performing fortnightly), the **School Choir** (performing weekly at assemblies), and a lunchtime **Art Club** run with support from Year 6 Art captains [citation:Specialist Programs](https://www.claytonnorthps.vic.edu.au/page/231/Specialist-Programs). The school also has a House System (Freeman, Dunlop, Wood, Namatjira) and encourages friendly competition through sport events and fundraising [citation:General Information](https://www.claytonnorthps.vic.edu.au/page/266/General-Information).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Clayton has a vibrant shopping strip along Clayton Road with several specialty grocers. Notable stores include **Hong Kong Supermarket** (359-365 Clayton Road), a large Asian grocery open daily 8am–8pm, and **Sarawan Spices** (335 Clayton Road), an Indian grocery [citation:Hong Kong Supermarket](https://www.hongkongsupermarket.com.au/); [citation:Groceries - Clayton Road](https://helloclayton.com.au/category/shop/groceries/). There are also two Coles supermarkets in Clayton, as well as Korean, Vietnamese, and halal butchers, making the suburb convenient for diverse family grocery needs.

6) **Weeknight dining:** Three family-friendly takeaway and dining options in Clayton are **Dosa Hut** (346 Clayton Road) serving authentic multi-cuisine Indian food, open daily 11am–10:30pm [citation:Indian Restaurant in Clayton - Dosa Hut](https://www.dosahut.net.au/indian-restaurant-clayton/); **Jay Bhavani** (also on Clayton Road) offering Indian vegetarian fare and desserts; and **Malaysia Garden** (Clayton) providing Asian/Chinese dishes via takeaway [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Clayton, VIC 3168 | TuckerFox AU](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/clayton-vic-3168). All are casual and suitable for families.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Specific tutoring centres in or near Clayton include **NQT Education** at Westall Primary School (17 Fairbank Road, Clayton South), which offers maths and English tutoring from Prep to Year 12 as well as selective school exam preparation [citation:Clayton South < Centres | NQT Education](https://www.nqt.com.au/centres/clayton-south). **Apex Tuition Australia** provides in-person and online tutoring across Clayton for VCE, IB, and primary subjects, with tutors who travel to homes [citation:Private Tutors in Clayton | Tutors Available for VCE, IB and High School](https://www.apextuitionaustralia.com/locations/clayton).

8) **Commute:** Clayton Station is on the **Cranbourne/Pakenham line** (part of the Metro Trains network). The train journey from Clayton Station to Flinders Street Station (via Town Hall) takes approximately **28–32 minutes**, with services departing every 10 minutes during peak times [citation:Clayton to Flinders Street Station - 5 ways to travel via train](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Clayton-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). The station is within walking distance of the school, about 1 km away.
