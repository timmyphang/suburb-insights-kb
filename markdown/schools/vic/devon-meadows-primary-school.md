1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Devon Meadows is a working-class to middle-income suburb. The median weekly household income is $1,790, and the median age is 44 [citation:2021 Devon Meadows, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20748). The area is predominantly home to couples with children, and the most common occupation is in trades [citation:Devon Meadows House Prices & Property Trends](https://www.propertyvalue.com.au/suburb/devon%20meadows-3977-vic). Most homes are owned outright by couples with children [citation:DEVON MEADOWS suburb profile](https://landchecker.com.au/suburb/devon-meadows-vic-3977/).
