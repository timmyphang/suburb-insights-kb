1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Mernda is a rapidly growing, family-oriented, middle-income suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,011, and the median age is 33, notably younger than the national median of 38 [citation:2021 Mernda, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21659). The suburb has a high proportion of families, with an average household size of 3 people [citation:2021 Mernda, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21659). The workforce is mixed, with 45.3% employed full-time and 22.7% part-time, and 27.6% not in the labour force, indicating a substantial number of stay-at-home parents or carers [citation:GDP Australia](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/mernda-vic).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** While a large majority (78.8%) of Mernda residents were born in Australia, the most common overseas country of birth is India, accounting for 14.0% of the population [citation:GDP Australia](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/mernda-vic). This is a significant shift from 2016, when India was the top overseas birthplace at 5.8% [citation:2016 Mernda, Census All persons QuickStats](https://quickstats.censusdata.abs.gov.au/census_services/getproduct/census/2016/quickstat/SSC21648).

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is available at Mernda Park Primary School, provided by the YMCA. Before school care runs Monday to Friday from 6:30am to 8:30am, and after school care runs from 3:15pm to 6:30pm [citation:Mernda Park OSHC | YMCA Outside School Hours Care](https://childrensprograms.ymca.org.au/outside-school-hours-care/mernda-park-primary). Permanent before school care is $30.50 per session, and permanent after school care is $39.10 per session (before Child Care Subsidy) [citation:Mernda Park OSHC | YMCA Outside School Hours Care](https://childrensprograms.ymca.org.au/outside-school-hours-care/mernda-park-primary).

4) **School culture:** Mernda Park Primary School offers several named extracurriculars. The school runs **Junior and Senior Choirs** as well as a **Ukulele group**, both of which have performed at community events like the Bunnings Christmas performance [citation:Lunchtime Clubs](https://www.merndaparkps.vic.edu.au/lunchtime-clubs). It also offers a **Sports Club** for students in Grades 4-6 to develop skills in various sports and leadership [citation:sports club so far...](https://www.merndaparkps.vic.edu.au/2023-physical-education-blog/sports-club-so-far). Additionally, the school participates in the **Bridge Inn North Interschool Sports competition**[citation:Physical Education Blog](https://www.merndaparkps.vic.edu.au/physical-education-blog).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Mernda is a family-friendly suburb with a village-like community centre and several major shopping options. For specialty groceries, **A&H Indian grocery store** is located at Unit 1/117 Regent St, offering Indian spices, fresh produce, and pantry staples [citation:A&H Indian grocery store](https://ah-indian-grocery-store.com-place.com/). **Tales of India** also delivers Indian groceries to Mernda [citation:Indian Grocery Store Mernda | Tales Of India](https://talesofindia.com.au/pages/indian-grocery-store-mernda-3754). General grocery needs are served by standard supermarkets in the area.

6) **Weeknight dining:** Several casual, family-friendly dining options are available in Mernda. **Na Songkhla Thai Mernda** offers Thai cuisine with takeaway and BYO wine options [citation:Na Songkhla Thai Mernda](https://www.facebook.com/NaSongkhlaMernda/). **Seven Star Pizza Pasta Grill Mernda** provides Italian-style pizza and pasta for takeaway or dine-in [citation:Seven Star Pizza Pasta Grill Mernda](https://sevenstarpizzeriamernda.com.au/). **Mernda Kebab House** at 1435 Plenty Rd offers takeaway and delivery options [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Mernda](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/mernda).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Several tutoring centres serve the Mernda area. **Brainworks Mernda** provides one-to-one maths and English tutoring for Year 1-10, including selective school and scholarship exam preparation, operating from the Mernda Village Community Centre [citation:Mernda Maths & English Tutoring - Brainworks](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-mernda). **Fruition Tuition** offers private one-on-one tutoring in multiple subjects, with services available in Mernda and a base at 110 Cooper St, Epping [citation:Private Tutoring Mernda - Fruition Tuition Centre](https://www.fruition.com.au/tutoring-mernda/).

8) **Commute:** Mernda is served by the **Mernda train line** on the Melbourne metropolitan rail network. The journey from **Mernda Station** to **Flinders Street Station** typically takes approximately **52-53 minutes**, with trains running every 20 minutes [citation:Train Mernda Station to Flinders Street Station | Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Mernda-Station/Flinders-Street-Station) [citation:Moovit](https://moovitapp.com/index/en-gb/public_transportation-line-mernda-Melbourne-2803-906713-318123819-2). The line is wheelchair accessible at all stations [citation:Metro Mernda Line - TransitApp](https://transitapp.com/en/region/melbourne/metro/commuter-rail-mernda).
