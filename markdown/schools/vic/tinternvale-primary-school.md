1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Ringwood East is a middle-income suburb with a family-friendly mix. The median weekly household income is $1,817, and the median age is 38, aligning with the national average [citation:2021 Ringwood East, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22175). The population of 10,764 includes a high proportion of families, with 2,882 families and an average household size of 2.5 people [citation:2021 Ringwood East, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22175). Home ownership is high (45.9% owned outright, 54.1% mortgaged), indicating a stable, established community [citation:Ringwood East VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/ringwood-east-vic).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas birthplace is China, at 5.2% of residents [citation:Ringwood East VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/ringwood-east-vic). The suburb is predominantly Australian-born, with 85.3% of residents born in Australia [citation:Ringwood East VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/ringwood-east-vic).

3) **Working-parent support:** Tinternvale Primary School partners with TheirCare to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC). The program operates from 7:00am to 8:45am for before-school care and from 3:30pm to 6:15pm for after-school care [citation:Outside School Hours Care](https://www.tinternvaleps.vic.edu.au/page/76/OSHClub). TheirCare also runs vacation care from 7:00am to 6:15pm on school holidays and pupil-free days [citation:Outside School Hours Care](https://www.tinternvaleps.vic.edu.au/page/76/OSHClub).

4) **School culture:** Tinternvale Primary School offers a range of named extracurricular activities. These include **Inter-school Sport** for students in Terms 1-2, **Joggers Club** (a before-school running program held twice a week), and **Junior, Senior and Specialist Choirs** [citation:2020 Annual Report to The School Community](https://www.tinternvaleps.vic.edu.au/uploaded_files/media/annual_report_2020_1.pdf); [citation:Physical Education](https://www.tinternvaleps.vic.edu.au/page/91/Physical-Education). The school also participates in the **Maroondah All Inclusive concert** with its Senior Choir and Percussion Group [citation:Tinternvale Primary School Tintalk Issue 11 27 April 2017](https://www.tinternvaleps.vic.edu.au/uploaded_files/media/issue_11_27_april_2017.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Ringwood East has a relaxed, family-oriented vibe with convenient local shopping along Railway Avenue. Specialty grocery options include **Chinland Asian Grocery** at 52/70a Railway Avenue, offering Asian ingredients, and **Fruitland Ringwood East**, a specialty grocery store at 54 Railway Avenue [citation:Food & Drink – Ringwood East - Railway Avenue](https://ringwood-east-railway-avenue.myshopify.com/pages/copy-of-food-drink). For a wider selection, the nearby Eastland Shopping Centre in Ringwood houses **Crown Asian Grocery** [citation:CROWN ASIAN GROCERY in Eastland Shopping Centre](https://www.australia-shoppings.com/malls-centres/victoria/ringwood/eastland-shopping-centre/crown-asian-grocery).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Ringwood East offers several casual, family-friendly takeaway and dining options. **Thai Ute** at 34 Railway Avenue serves Thai cuisine for dine-in or takeaway and is fully licensed [citation:Thai Ute](https://thaiute.com/). **Indians Again Restaurant** provides Indian takeaway and delivery [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Ringwood East, VIC 3135 | TuckerFox AU](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/ringwood-east-vic-3135). For pizza, **Eastwood Fish & Chips** is a popular local takeaway option [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Ringwood East, VIC 3135 | TuckerFox AU](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/ringwood-east-vic-3135).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Two named tutoring centres are located near Ringwood East. **Kip McGrath Ringwood** at 2 Murray Place, Ringwood, offers English and maths tuition for primary and secondary students, with in-centre and online sessions available [citation:KMEC Ringwood Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/ringwood). **NRICH Learning Ringwood** is the flagship centre at a nearby Ringwood location, providing small-group tuition in English, maths, science, and NAPLAN preparation for preschool to Year 12 [citation:Ringwood Centre - NRICH Learning](https://nrichlearning.com.au/centre/ringwood-centre/).

8) **Commute:** Ringwood East is served by the **Lilydale train line** via Ringwood East Station [citation:Train Ringwood East to Flinders Street Station from $4 | Tickets & Timetables | Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Ringwood-East/Flinders-Street-Station).
