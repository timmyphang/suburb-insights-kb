1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Tarneit is a rapidly growing, middle-income suburb on Melbourne's western fringe, with a strong family orientation. The median weekly household income is $2,103, and the median age is 30, reflecting a young population [citation:2021 Tarneit, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22451). An extraordinary 86.4% of households are families, and the suburb's SEIFA index sits at 1,000 (the national average), indicating a balanced socio-economic profile [citation:Crime & Safety in Tarneit VIC | Microburbs](https://www.microburbs.com.au/Crime-Disadvantage/Tarneit). The suburb features a predominantly suburban housing profile with a higher proportion of rental properties than the broader region [citation:Tarneit - North – SA2 213051584 | AreaSearch](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/tarneit_north).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas country of birth in Tarneit is India, though the exact percentage was not found in available sources. According to the 2021 Census, 94.5% of Tarneit residents are non-Indigenous, and 0.4% are Aboriginal and/or Torres Strait Islander [citation:2021 Tarneit, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22451). The suburb has a significant Indian community, reflected in the presence of multiple Indian supermarkets and restaurants.

3) **Working-parent support:** Barayip Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) through Kelly Club OSHC, located at the school [citation:Kelly Club OSHC Barayip Primary School in TARNEIT, VIC 3029 | CareforKids.com.au](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/1jrydz). The specific hours of operation are not listed in available sources, but the service provides Before School Care, After School Care, and Vacation Care.

4) **School culture:** Barayip Primary School offers several named extracurricular activities. Small group instrumental music lessons are available through Soundgarage [citation:Wellbeing](https://barayipps.vic.edu.au/wellbeing). Students have participated in a collaborative Art Workshop with artist Kobi Sainty to create the school's first significant art piece, which included storytelling and learning Bunurong language [citation:Wellbeing](https://barayipps.vic.edu.au/wellbeing). The school also has the Stand Up Project, which empowers students to identify bullying behaviours and develop effective responses [citation:Wellbeing](https://barayipps.vic.edu.au/wellbeing).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Tarneit is a family-oriented suburb with modern shopping amenities, anchored by Tarneit Central Shopping Centre. For specialty groceries, Tarneit Asian Supermarket offers a large range of produce from Japan, Korea, and China [citation:Tarneit Asian Supermarket](https://www.tarneitcentral.com.au/stores/supermarkets/tarneit-asian-supermarket). Yes Yes Indian Supermarket, located in Tarneit Central, caters to Indian, Pakistani, and other South Asian communities [citation:Yes Yes Indian supermarket | We Can’t Say No](https://www.yesyesindiansupermarket.com.au/). Apna Bazaar West at Tarneit West Village is another option for Indian speciality ingredients [citation:Apna Bazaar West Grocery store - Tarneit West Village](https://tarneitwestvillage.com.au/stores/apna-bazaar-west-grocery-store-i-open-trading/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual, family-friendly dining, Dosa Hut Tarneit (located in Wyndham Village Shopping Centre, 380 Sayers Road) serves authentic South Indian and multi-cuisine Indian dishes including dosa, biryani, and tandoori options [citation:Best Indian Restaurant in Tarneit | Authentic Indian Food](https://www.dosahut.net.au/indian-restaurant-tarneit/). Desi Dhaba Tarneit offers freshly made Indian dishes suitable for family dining and takeaway [citation:Desi Dhaba Tarneit | Indian Food & Takeaway](https://www.desidhaba.com.au/tarneit). Additional options include Tarneit Kebab House for Turkish cuisine and Pizza Club for pizza [citation:Restaurants in Tarneit,VIC With Takeaway | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/tarneit/takeaway-category).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Kip McGrath Tarneit (KMEC) at 239 Hogans Road, Hoppers Crossing provides personalised tutoring in Maths and English for primary and secondary students, with in-centre and online options [citation:KMEC Tarneit Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/tarneit). Brainworks Tarneit, located at Penrose Promenade Community Centre (83 Penrose Promenade), offers one-to-one tutoring in Maths and English, as well as selective entry exam preparation for Melbourne High, Mac.Robertson Girls' High, and Suzanne Cory High [citation:Brainworks Tarneit. One to One Tutoring For Your Child](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-tarneit).
