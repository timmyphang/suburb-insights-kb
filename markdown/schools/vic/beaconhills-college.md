Briefing: Beaconhills College — Pakenham (primary) — concise facts for families

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 lines)
- Pakenham is a fast‑growing, family‑oriented outer‑Melbourne suburb with a young median age of 33 and large shares of children (0–9 years notably high). Median weekly household income: $1,664 (2021). [citation:2021 Pakenham, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22027)

2) Most common overseas country of birth
- 67% born in Australia (2021); the most common overseas birthplace reported is India (~6.0%). [citation:Pakenham VIC Suburb Profile — Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/pakenham)

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- Beaconhills Pakenham offers on‑site before/after school care and a Little Beacons holiday program (OSHC/enrolment forms available). [citation:New parent resources | Beaconhills College](https://www.beaconhills.vic.edu.au/enrolment/new-parent-resources/)

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars
- Performing arts (Year 2 Strings, choirs, ensembles, college musicals), instrumental/private tuition (piano, guitar, brass, strings, etc.), and organised sport/camps (swimming lessons, outdoor camps). [citation:Junior School - Beaconhills College](https://www.beaconhills.vic.edu.au/learning/curriculum/junior-school/)

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 lines)
- Pakenham reads as a family suburb with growing new housing, parks and community food initiatives; common household type is “couples with children.” For ethnic/specialty groceries there are local Indian/Asian grocers (e.g., Ultimate Indian/Asian Supermarket) and community food outlets like The Community Grocer (weekly fresh‑food market). [citation:2021 Pakenham, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22027) [citation:Ultimate indian/Asian Supermarket Pakenham (Indian grocery)](https://www.everythingindian.com.au/ultimate-indianasian-supermarket-pakenham-indian-grocery) [citation:The Community Grocer - Pakenham - Open Food Network](https://openfoodnetwork.org.au/tcg-pakenham/shop)

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 family‑friendly takeaways
- Maloney’s Pizza (local pizza), Pakenham Fish & Chips (classic family takeaway), and Dosa Hut (South Indian dosas/takeaway) are common casual options. [citation:Maloney's Pizza - Pakenham](https://www.maloneys.net.au/) [citation:PAKENHAM FISH & CHIPS - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurant_Review-g552236-d5034203-Reviews-Pakenham_Fish_Chips-Pakenham_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html) [citation:Dosa Hut Pakenham menu](https://www.dosahut.net.au/menu/pakenham/)

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 local centres)
- Local options include Kip McGrath Pakenham (English & maths tuition) and Kumon Pakenham (maths & English programme). [citation:Kip McGrath Pakenham Tuition Centre](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/pakenham) [citation:Kumon Pakenham centre search](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/Kumon-Pakenham-John-Street-VIC)
