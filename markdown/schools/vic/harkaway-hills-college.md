For specialty groceries, Sari-Sari Asian Groceries is located at 26A Webb St in nearby Narre Warren, offering Asian products [citation:Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/vic/narre-warren/sari-sari-asian-groceries-14678447-listing.html), and Heli Asian Grocery on Narre Warren-Cranbourne Rd in Narre Warren South provides Chinese, Korean, Japanese, Filipino, Thai, and Malaysian foods [citation:Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/heli-asian-grocery-narre-warren-south).
