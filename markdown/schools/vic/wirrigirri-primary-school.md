7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Several tutoring centres serve the Wollert area. **Kumon Wollert Education Centre** offers English and mathematics tutoring programs [citation:Kumon Wollert Education Centre](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/Kumon-Wollert-VIC). **Brainworks Wollert** provides one-on-one maths and English tutoring for Year 1-10 students, and also offers selective school and scholarship exam preparation [citation:Brainworks Wollert](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-wollert).

8) **Commute:** The nearest train station to Wollert is **Craigieburn Station** on the **Craigieburn line** [citation:Wollert to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Wollert/Flinders-Street-Station). To reach the station, residents take the **route 390 bus** from Wollert to Craigieburn Station, then board the train to Flinders Street Station [citation:Wollert to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Wollert/Flinders-Street-Station).
