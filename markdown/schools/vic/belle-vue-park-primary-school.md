3) **Working‑parent support**  
Belle Vue Park Primary School offers an Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) program operated by **TheirCare**. The service runs from **7:00am to 8:45am** in the morning and from **3:15pm to 6:00pm** in the afternoon, and also provides school holiday care [citation:OOSH Care Program](http://www.bellevueparkps.vic.edu.au/oosh-care-program.html). Families can claim the government Child Care Subsidy to reduce costs, and bookings are made through the TheirCare website or by calling 1300 072 410 [citation:OOSH Care Program](http://www.bellevueparkps.vic.edu.au/oosh-care-program.html).

4) **School culture**  
The school’s distinct extracurricular offerings include the **free “Just Brass” band**, a **Green Screen Room** where children create video animations and documentaries, and a **S.T.E.A.M. program** integrating science, technology, engineering, arts and mathematics [citation:What does a parent want?](http://www.bellevueparkps.vic.edu.au/what-does-a-parent-want.html) [citation:Specialist Programs](http://www.bellevueparkps.vic.edu.au/specialist-programs.html).
