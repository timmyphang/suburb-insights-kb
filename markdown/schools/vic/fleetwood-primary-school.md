1) Suburb socio-economic profile: Narre Warren is a family-oriented, middle-income suburb with a median age of 35. The median weekly household income is $1,741, and the area is a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers, with a high proportion of families with children (an average of 0.9 children per household) [citation:2021 Narre Warren, Census QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21893). Reviews describe it as a "friendly town" with lots of parks and a "safe and welcoming" feel, though some note that the southern side of the freeway can feel "rough and nasty" with "hoons all night" [citation:Homely Narre Warren Review](https://www.homely.com.au/suburb-profile/narre-warren-vic-3805).

2) Most common foreign country of birth: Of Narre Warren residents, the most common overseas country of birth is India, at 11.6% of the population (based on the 2021 Census data for the broader Narre Warren area) [citation:2021 Narre Warren, Census QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21893). Australian-born residents account for 61.8% of the suburb's population [citation:2021 Narre Warren, Census QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21893).

3) Working-parent support: Fleetwood Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) through OSHClub, operating at 97 Fleetwood Drive, Narre Warren [citation:Fleetwood Primary School OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/fleetwood-primary-school/). The before-school program ("Rise then Shine") runs from 6:45 AM to bell time, and the after-school program ("Stay and Play") runs from bell time to 6:15 PM, Monday to Friday [citation:Fleetwood Primary School OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/fleetwood-primary-school/).

4) School culture: Fleetwood Primary School offers a variety of named extracurriculars including a Soccer Club, a Chess Club, and a Lego Club, with opportunities for students to help with gardening, raising the school flags, and taking out recycling bins [citation:Students Portal – Fleetwood Primary School](https://fleetwoodps.vic.edu.au/students-portal/). The school also runs fortnightly house-based activities where students are sorted into four houses for fun games [citation:Wellbeing – Fleetwood Primary School](https://fleetwoodps.vic.edu.au/wellbeing/).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries: Narre Warren is a family-friendly suburb with extensive amenities, including Westfield Fountain Gate shopping centre, Bunjil Place theatre, and numerous parks [citation:Homely Narre Warren Review](https://www.homely.com.au/suburb-profile/narre-warren-vic-3805). For specialty groceries, specific options include Sari Sari Asian Groceries at 26A Webb Street (Asian grocery) and East Easy Asian Grocer at Westfield Fountain Gate; Indian grocery shops are also available in nearby Narre Warren South [citation:Sari Sari Asian Groceries](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/vic/narre-warren/sari-sari-asian-groceries-14678447-listing.html) [citation:Indian Grocery in Narre Warren South](https://www.everythingindian.com.au/indian-grocery-shops-in-narre-warren-south-vic).

6) Weeknight dining: Casual, family-friendly dining options in Narre Warren include Dosa Hut, an Indian restaurant offering takeaway and delivery, and Casey Central Charcoal Chicken for charcoal chicken takeaway [citation:Indian Restaurant in Narre Warren – Dosa Hut](https://www.dosahut.net.au/indian-restaurant-narre-warren/) [citation:Takeaway in Narre Warren – TuckerFox](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/narre-warren-vic-3805). 7) Tuition and enrichment: Success Tutoring offers in-person tutoring at its Narre Warren centre, focusing on primary and high school students with personalised learning plans [citation:Success Tutoring Narre Warren](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-narre-warren/). Edu-Kingdom provides maths and English tutoring for Years 1 to 12 from a nearby branch, with a free assessment available [citation:Edu-Kingdom Maths Tutor Narre Warren](https://edukingdom.com.au/cm-maths-tutor-narre-warren/).

8) Commute: Narre Warren is served by the Pakenham line, with trains running from Narre Warren Station to Flinders Street Station typically taking 53 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio Narre Warren to Flinders Street](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Narre-Warren/Flinders-Street-Station).
