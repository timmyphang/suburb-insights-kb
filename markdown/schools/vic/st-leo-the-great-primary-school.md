1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Altona North is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,695 and a median age of 37 (2021 Census) [citation:2021 Altona North QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20037). The suburb has 3,385 families, an average of 0.8 children per household, and a mix of white-collar and blue-collar residents (specific occupation data not found). Just over 21% of the population is aged 65 and over, indicating a balanced mix of young families and older residents.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The dominant overseas country of birth is not explicitly stated in available sources, but the top non-English languages spoken at home are Arabic (13.1%) and Italian (6.1%) (2016 Census) [citation:2016 Altona North QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20036). Only 45.2% of residents speak only English at home, reflecting a culturally diverse community with strong Arabic-speaking and Italian-speaking populations.

3) **Working-parent support**: Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is available at St Leo the Great Primary School, provided by **Quantin Binnah / St Leo the Great OSHC** [citation:CareforKids](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/xz8qv/quantin-binnah-st-leo-the-great-oshc-altona-north-3025). The specific operating hours are not detailed in the provided sources; contact the school directly for before- and after-school care schedules.

4) **School culture**: St Leo the Great offers several named extracurricular programs, including **‘Family Circus’** – drama and music activities held under a big top [citation:Digital Technologies page](https://www.slaltonanth.catholic.edu.au/page/70/Digital-Technologies). The school also runs a **Buddy Program** pairing Prep students with Grade 6 students to support a smooth transition [citation:Buddy Program](https://www.slaltonanth.catholic.edu.au/page/122/Buddy-Program). Additionally, **Lent/Advent Family Activity Days** provide faith-based family engagement [citation:2024 Annual Report](https://www.slaltonanth.catholic.edu.au/uploaded_files/media/021690ar2024_1.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Altona North is a residential suburb with convenient family amenities, including the Altona Gate shopping centre. Specialty grocers include **Second Ave Grocer** (62-76 Second Avenue), stocking local produce and gourmet finds [citation:Second Ave Grocer](https://secondavegrocer.com.au/), and **India Spice Corner** (7 Borrack Square) for Indian and South Asian groceries [citation:Singh.com.au](https://singh.com.au/indian-grocery-stores-altona-vic/). The suburb also has a friendly mix of milk bars and Asian grocery stores along Empire Street.

6) **Weeknight dining**: For casual, family-friendly takeaway, consider **Altona Pines Fish & Chips** (fish and chips), **Try-Thai Altona North** (Thai cuisine), and **KURD Kebab** (kebabs) – all located in Altona North [citation:TuckerFox Altona North](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/altona-north-vic-3025). These options offer reliable meals for busy weeknights.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: **Brainworks Altona North** (at Altona North Community House, 7 Plover Drive) offers one-to-one tutoring in Maths and English for Years 3–9, as well as selective school exam preparation [citation:Brainworks Altona North](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-altona-north). **Success Tutoring Altona** provides customised K–12 tutoring in Maths and English, with a centre nearby in Altona [citation:Success Tutoring Altona](https://successtutoring.com.au/altona-tutoring).

8) **Commute**: Altona North does not have its own train station; the nearest station is **Altona** or **Westona** on the **Werribee line**. The train journey from Altona Station to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD takes approximately **33 minutes** [citation:Rome2Rio Altona to Flinders](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Altona-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). Bus route 903 connects Altona North to Altona Station for the train connection [citation:Metro Trains Altona](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/altona/).
