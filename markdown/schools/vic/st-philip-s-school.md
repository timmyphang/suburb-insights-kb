1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Blackburn North is an affluent, predominantly white-collar suburb with a strong family composition. The median weekly household income in 2021 was $2,021, well above the national median of $1,746, and the median age was 39 years [citation:2021 Census QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20253).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** According to the 2016 Census, 37.9% of Blackburn North residents had both parents born in Australia, and 47.3% had both parents born overseas [citation:2016 Census QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20252). The specific percentage for the most common foreign country of birth was not found in the provided sources; however, the suburb has a notable Asian-born community.

3) **Working-parent support:** St Philip's School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by Youth Leadership Victoria, located at 60 Junction Rd, Blackburn North [citation:CareforKids Youth Leadership Victoria OSHC](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/w8dre/youth-leadership-victoria-st-philips-oshc-blackburn-north-3130). The specific operating hours are not listed in the available sources, so please contact the school directly for details.

4) **School culture:** St Philip's Catholic Primary School fosters a community where every child is known and nurtured [citation:St Philip's School Website](https://www.stphilipbbn.catholic.edu.au/). The school also promotes mindfulness activities, as seen in their "Breathing Exercises" resources from Upschool [citation:Breathing Exercises Upschool](https://stphilipbbn.catholic.edu.au/upschool/tag/breathing-exercises/). Specific named extracurriculars beyond this were not found in the available sources.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Blackburn North has a village feel, anchored by the Blackburn Square shopping centre at the corner of Springfield and Williams Roads, which includes a Coles, Woolworths, and ALDI [citation:Blackburn Square Stores](https://www.blackburnsquare.com.au/our-stores/). For international groceries, residents can visit Lin's Asian Grocery at Blackburn Square or Satnam Indian Grocery nearby [citation:Lin's Asian Grocery](https://www.blackburnsquare.com.au/store/lins-asian-grocery/) [citation:Satnam Indian Grocery](https://www.satnamindiangrocery.com.au/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly dining options in and near Blackburn North include China Bar at Blackburn Square for Chinese cuisine, Souvlaki GR for Greek food, and NeNe Chicken for Korean fried chicken [citation:Blackburn Square Stores](https://www.blackburnsquare.com.au/our-stores/). These options offer easy takeaway and casual dining.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** For tutoring support, Success Tutoring Blackburn North offers personalised K-12 programs at local study hubs [citation:Success Tutoring Blackburn North](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-blackburn-north). Additionally, Ezy Math Tutoring lists numerous private tutors in Blackburn North for English and maths [citation:Ezy English Tutoring Blackburn North](https://www.ezymathtutoring.com.au/tutors/blackburn-north-3130/learn-english).

8) **Commute:** Blackburn North is served by Blackburn Station on the Belgrave and Lilydale train lines. The train journey from Blackburn Station to Flinders Street Station takes approximately 32 minutes, with services running every 10–15 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio Blackburn to Flinders Street](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Blackburn-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
