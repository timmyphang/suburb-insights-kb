1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Roxburgh Park is a working-class to middle-income suburb with a young, family-oriented demographic. According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income was $1,629, and the median age was 31, significantly lower than the national median of 38 [citation:2021 Roxburgh Park, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22208). The area has a high proportion of families, with 5,858 families recorded and an average of 2.3 children per family [citation:2021 Roxburgh Park, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22208).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The dominant overseas country of birth is Iraq. Based on the 2016 Census, 17.1% of residents were born in Iraq, followed by Turkey (5.4%) [citation:2016 Roxburgh Park, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22197). The percentage of Australian-born residents is not prominently stated in the 2021 sources, but the 2016 data indicates a highly multicultural population, with only 11.5% having both parents born in Australia [citation:2016 Roxburgh Park, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22197).

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided at Roxburgh Park Primary School by Kelly Club OSHC. Before-school care runs from 7:00 am to 8:45 am, and after-school care runs from 3:10 pm to 6:00 pm, Monday to Friday [citation:Before and After School Care - Roxburgh Park Primary School](https://roxburghparkps.vic.edu.au/v6/before-and-after-school-care/). The service also offers pupil-free day care from 7:00 am to 6:00 pm, and fees are eligible for Child Care Subsidy (CCS) of up to 90% [citation:Before and After School Care - Roxburgh Park Primary School](https://roxburghparkps.vic.edu.au/v6/before-and-after-school-care/).

4) **School culture:** Roxburgh Park Primary School offers a range of extracurricular activities, including interschool sports such as Soccer and district Athletics, where the school placed 3rd at interschool soccer and won multiple awards at district athletics [citation:Tutoring Program Term 2 - Roxburgh Park Primary School](https://roxburghparkps.vic.edu.au/v6/tutoring-program-term-2/page/2/?et_blog=).
