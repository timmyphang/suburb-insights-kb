1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Sunbury is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,106 and a median age of 39 years [citation:2021 Sunbury QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/210041240). The housing mix is predominantly separate houses (76.1% in Sunbury South), with above-average rates of outright home ownership [citation:Sunbury - South AreaSearch](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/sunbury_south). The suburb has a growing population of 40,675 (2023 estimate) and is characterised by a large proportion of families with children [citation:About the profile areas | Sunbury](https://profile.id.com.au/hume/about?WebID=240).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Sunbury is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 92.4% of residents born in Australia [citation:Sunbury VIC Demographics](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunbury-vic). The most common overseas birthplace is England (3.9%), followed by India (2.0%) [citation:Sunbury VIC Demographics](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunbury-vic).

3) **Working-parent support:** Holy Trinity Catholic Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by **Big Childcare** [citation:Big Childcare - Holy Trinity Catholic PS OSHC](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/4zr16/big-childcare-holy-trinity-catholic-ps-oshc-sunbury-3429). The program runs every morning from **6:30am** and in the afternoons until **6:30pm** on school days [citation:Out of Hours Care](https://htsunbury.catholic.edu.au/school-community/out-of-hours-care/).

4) **School culture:** Holy Trinity offers a **Choir** and **Sustainability Programs** as part of its extracurricular activities [citation:PARENT HANDBOOK](https://htsunbury.catholic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/HTCPS-Parent-Handbook.pdf). The school also hosts **The Music Bus**, which provides weekly private lessons in Keyboard, Drums, Guitar, Singing, Ukulele and Rock Band [citation:The Arts](https://htsunbury.catholic.edu.au/performing-arts/). Additionally, lunchtime clubs such as **netball** have been run by staff [citation:Meet our Prep teacher Nikki](https://htsunbury.catholic.edu.au/meet-second-prep-teacher/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Sunbury is a family-friendly suburb with ample parks, a town centre, and a strong community feel. For specialty groceries, **Asia Finest Grocery** at Sunbury Square Shopping Centre (Shop 9, 2–28 Evans St) stocks Filipino, Indian, Chinese, Japanese, Thai, and other Asian ingredients [citation:Asia Finest Grocery - Sunbury Square](https://sunburysquare.com.au/asias-finest-grocery/). **Add2Cart** offers an online Asian grocery store with local delivery and pickup from Sunbury [citation:Add2Cart](https://add2cartasiangrocery.com.au/). Major supermarkets such as Woolworths and Coles are also located in Sunbury.

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual, family-friendly takeaway or dining, **Coco Seed** (54 Macedon St) serves Thai cuisine and has received strong reviews for hot, fresh food [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Sunbury](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/sunbury). **Batman Pizza** (24 Batman Ave) offers Italian and pizza options with a thin, tasty base [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Sunbury](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/sunbury). **OBs Kebabs & Burgers** (3/90 Vineyard Rd) is another popular choice for kebabs and burgers with fresh ingredients [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Sunbury](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/sunbury).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Kip McGrath Sunbury** (49a O'Shanassy Street) provides professional English and maths tutoring for primary and secondary students, using qualified teachers and individual learning programmes [citation:KMEC Sunbury Tuition Centre](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/sunbury). **Kumon Sunbury Education Centre** offers individualised maths and English programmes aimed at building strong academic skills for children of all ages [citation:Kumon Sunbury](https://www.facebook.com/KumonSunbury). Online options such as **Tutero** also serve Sunbury students with personalised 1-on-1 tutoring aligned to the Victorian curriculum [citation:Tutero Sunbury](https://www.tutero.com/au/online-tutoring/locations/sunbury-vic).

8) **Commute:** Sunbury is served by **Sunbury Station** on the **Sunbury line** (Metro Trains Melbourne). The direct train to Flinders Street Station takes approximately **49 minutes** [citation:Sunbury to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Sunbury-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). Trains run every 20–30 minutes during peak times [citation:Sunbury to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Sunbury-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
