1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Clarinda is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,589 and a median age of 45, notably older than the Victorian median of 38 [citation:2021 Clarinda QuickStats]. The suburb is characterised by family housing and a mix of both white-collar and blue-collar occupations, though specific occupational breakdowns are not provided in the available sources [citation:Smart Property Investment]. Over half of households are families with children, and the population density is moderate.

3) **Working-parent support:** Clarinda Primary School partners with Camp Australia to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) [citation:Camp Australia - Clarinda]. Before-school care operates from 6:45 am to 8:45 am Monday to Friday [citation:KindiCare]. After-school care hours are not explicitly stated in the provided snippets, but Camp Australia typically offers care until around 6:00 pm; families should confirm directly.

4) **School culture:** Clarinda Primary School offers a range of named extracurricular activities, including the **Clarinda Singas** choir, **Interschool sport** (Years 5/6), and a **School Breakfast Club** that provides a healthy start each morning [citation:Beyond the Classroom][citation:2024 Annual Report][citation:School Breakfast Club]. Other lunchtime clubs include Chess, Drama, and Library clubs.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Clarinda has a quiet, family-friendly atmosphere with local parks and community facilities. For specialty groceries, the nearest major Asian supermarket is **Hong Kong Supermarket** in Clayton (approx. 3 km away), offering a wide range of Asian products [citation:Hong Kong Supermarket]. General supermarkets such as Woolworths and Coles are available in nearby shopping centres, though specific names in Clarinda are not listed in the provided sources.

6) **Weeknight dining:** Casual, family-friendly takeaway options near Clarinda include **Ammy Bangkok Restaurant** (Thai) in Springvale, **Captain Makos Fish & Chips** in Springvale, and **Charcoal Chicken Cheltenham** – all deliver to Clarinda [citation:TuckerFox]. These are popular choices for quick weeknight meals.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Success Tutoring** operates a centre in Clarinda, offering personalised learning support for primary and high school students [citation:Success Tutoring]. Additionally, **NQT Education** has a tutoring centre in Clayton South recommended for families in Clarinda, specialising in mathematics and English fundamentals [citation:NQT Education].

8) **Commute:** Clarinda does not have its own train station. The nearest station is **Clayton** on the **Cranbourne/Pakenham line**, approximately 2 km away. The typical train travel time from Clayton to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD is about 30 minutes, though precise timings vary by service [citation:Metro Trains]. Bus routes also connect Clarinda to Clayton station.
