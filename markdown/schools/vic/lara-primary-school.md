**1) Suburb socio-economic profile**
Lara is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a mix of blue- and white-collar workers. The 2021 Census reports a median weekly household income of $1,966, above the national median of $1,746, and a median age of 37, slightly below the national median of 38 [citation:ABS QuickStats Lara 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/203021043). Families with children are common: the average number of children per family is 1.9, and children aged 0–14 make up 19.3% of the population, well above the national share of 18.2% [citation:ABS QuickStats Lara 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/203021043).

**2) Most common foreign country of birth**
Lara is overwhelmingly Australian-born. In the 2021 Census, 72.7% of residents were born in Australia, compared to the national figure of 66.9% [citation:ABS QuickStats Lara 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/203021043). The most common overseas country of birth is England, at 4.8% of the population; no other single country exceeds 2% [citation:ABS QuickStats Lara 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/203021043).

**3) Working-parent support**
Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) at Lara Primary School is provided by Camp Australia, operating before and after school [citation:Camp Australia Lara Primary School](https://campaustralia.com.au/school/?accessCode=C7F0). Specific hours of operation are not listed in the available sources; for current times, families should contact Camp Australia directly or check the school’s website. At Lara Lake Primary School, the nearby OSHC program is run by TheirCare and operates from the school’s older hall [citation:Lara Lake PS OSHC](https://www.laralake.vic.edu.au/parent-resources/outside-school-hours-care).

**4) School culture**
Lara Primary School offers several named extracurricular programs. The **Breakfast Club** runs Monday–Thursday from 8:30–8:50am, providing healthy food in a social setting [citation:Lara PS Breakfast Club](https://www.laraps.vic.edu.au/news-events/breakfast-club). The **Serendip Program** is a unique environmental education initiative for all students [citation:Lara PS Serendip](https://www.laraps.vic.edu.au/teaching-learning/serendip). Upper primary students also take part in a **water activity day at Adventure Park** as part of the school’s annual swimming program [citation:Lara PS Extra Curricular](https://www.laraps.vic.edu.au/teaching-learning/extra-curricular-programs).

**5) Suburb vibe and groceries**
Lara is a growing, family-friendly suburb with a village feel, anchored by the **Lara Village Shopping Centre** at 120 Station Lake Road [citation:Lara Village Shopping Centre](https://laravillage.com.au/stores). The centre features a full-line **Coles Supermarket**, a Liquorland bottle shop, and specialty retailers including Routley’s Bakery and Mustafa’s Kebabs [citation:Lara Village Stores](https://laravillage.com.au/stores). The only dedicated ethnic grocer in Lara is **Asia Verse**, an Asian grocer offering products from Japan, Korea, China, India, and Southeast Asia (online store launching soon) [citation:Asia Verse](https://www.asiaverse.com.au). **Gracias Asian Products & Grocery** is also listed locally [citation:Gracias Asian Products](https://www.truelocal.com.au/business/gracias-asian-products-and-grocery/lara).

**6) Weeknight dining**
Family-friendly casual dining options in Lara include **Tuptim Thai Lara**, offering authentic Thai cuisine with dine-in/BYO, takeaway, and delivery [citation:Tuptim Thai Lara](https://tuptimthailara.com.au). **Lara Indian Restaurant** serves authentic Indian dishes (both vegetarian and non-vegetarian) with takeaway and home delivery, open daily from 5:00pm [citation:Lara Indian Restaurant](https://www.laraindian.com.au). **Town & Country, Pizza & Pasta** is praised by reviewers as the best pizza in Lara, offering reliable takeaway [citation:TripAdvisor Takeaway Lara](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g3331154-zfp10601-Lara_City_of_Greater_Geelong_Victoria.html).

**7) Tuition and enrichment**
**Flourish Tutoring Lara** provides 1:1 and small-group literacy and maths tuition for Foundation to Year 9, run by a primary teacher with 34 years’ experience; it also offers diagnostic assessments and support for diverse learning needs [citation:Flourish Tutoring Lara](https://www.flourishtutoring.com.au). **Cloud Tuition** offers personalised online English tutoring for Years 2–12, with curriculum-aligned lessons and flexible scheduling [citation:Cloud Tuition Lara](https://www.cloudtuition.com.au/locations/english-tutors-lara).

**8) Commute**
Lara is served by the **Geelong line** on the V/Line regional train network. The **Lara Railway Station** is located at the corner of Station Lake Road and Flinders Avenue. The typical train travel time from Lara Station to **Flinders Street Station** in the Melbourne CBD is approximately **50–55 minutes** [citation:V/Line Timetables](https://www.vline.com.au/Timetables/Train-coach-timetables).
