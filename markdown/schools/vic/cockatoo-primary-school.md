3) **Working-parent support:** Cockatoo Primary School offers an on-site Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) service managed by the Cockatoo Primary School Council. Before-school care operates from 7:00 am to 8:45 am, and after-school care runs from 3:30 pm to 6:30 pm on school days [citation:OSHC](https://www.cockatoops.vic.edu.au/page/175/OSHC). The service is approved for 45 children and provides breakfast and a nutritious snack as part of the program [citation:Cockatoo Primary School Council OSHC | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/cockatoo-primary-school-council-oshc).

4) **School culture:** Cockatoo Primary School runs a School Representative Council (SRC) that gives students a leadership voice in school decisions [citation:SRC](https://www.cockatoops.vic.edu.au/page/170/SRC). The school also participates in the Scholastic Book Club, allowing families to order books online through the Book Club LOOP platform [citation:Cockatoo Primary School](https://www.cockatoops.vic.edu.au/). Additional named extracurriculars beyond these two are not listed in the available page content.
