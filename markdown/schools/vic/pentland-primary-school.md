Here’s a short, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Pentland Primary School (Darley / Bacchus Marsh). I used one primary search per data point and opened pages only when the snippet lacked the needed fact.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Bacchus Marsh (town SAL area) is a middle‑income, family‑oriented town with a median age 39 and a high share of households with children (primary school attendance above state average) [citation:ABS QuickStats Bacchus Marsh (SAL20095)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20095). The area shows stable family households rather than a city singles profile, and local employment patterns reflect mixed white‑ and blue‑collar jobs typical of outer‑metro peri‑urban towns [citation:ABS QuickStats Bacchus Marsh (SAL20095)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20095).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- Bacchus Marsh is predominantly Australian‑born; ABS QuickStats shows Australia as the top birthplace and no single overseas country dominating the suburb (the area is largely Australian‑born) [citation:ABS QuickStats Bacchus Marsh (SAL20095)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20095).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- Pentland Primary has an on‑site Camp Australia Outside School Hours Care (before & after school + vacation care); ACECQA listing shows the service at the school address with Approved Places = 79 and a “Meeting NQS” rating [citation:ACECQA — Camp Australia Pentland Primary School OSHC](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/camp-australia-pentland-primary-school-oshc). Camp Australia’s school page also confirms before/after and holiday programs are run at the school [citation:Camp Australia Pentland Primary page](https://campaustralia.com.au/school/?accessCode=88F4).

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars
- School sport teams / district representation (students compete through the Bacchus Marsh Primary Schools Sports Association for swimming, athletics, cross‑country) [citation:Pentland Primary — Parents/Learning (school site snippet)](https://pentlandps.vic.edu.au/parents/).  
- On‑site music and STEAM facilities are noted in school job/profile listings (music rooms, art and STEAM spaces) — supporting instrumental/music and arts activities [citation:Graduate Teacher profile / Pentland Primary listing](https://au.gradconnection.com/employers/department-of-education-and-training-victoria/jobs/department-of-education-victoria-graduate-teacher-program-pentland-primary-school-6/).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Bacchus Marsh / Darley feels like a regional family town with local parks, markets and weekly community markets — suitable for families wanting outer‑metro space and community events [citation:Darley Community Market / Moorabool Shire Council](https://www.moorabool.vic.gov.au/Events/Darley-Community-Market).  
- Main grocery options include Coles and Champions IGA for everyday shopping, plus specialist South Asian/Indian grocery options such as Bacchus Mart and AD Bacchus Marsh Supermarket for ethnic ingredients [citation:Coles Bacchus Marsh store page](https://www.coles.com.au/find-stores/coles/vic/bacchus-marsh-672) [citation:Champions IGA Bacchus Marsh](https://www.iga.com.au/stores/champions-iga-bacchus-marsh/) [citation:Bacchus Mart (groceries & spice store)](https://www.thevillagebacchus.com.au/stores/bacchus-mart-groceries-spice-store).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 family takeaway/dining options
- Village Pizza & Pasta — family‑friendly pizzas & pasta on Main Street (good for quick takeaway) [citation:Village Pizza and Pasta](https://villagepizzaandpasta.com.au/).  
- Noodle Lovers — popular Chinese/Asian takeaway (noodles, rice) for quick family meals [citation:WelcometoMoorabool — takeaway list (Noodle Lovers entry)](https://welcometomoorabool.com/takeaway-food-bacchus-marsh-ballan-gordon-blackwood).  
- Darley Fish & Chips — classic fish & chips/takeaway local option [citation:WelcometoMoorabool — takeaway list (Darley Fish & Chips)](https://welcometomoorabool.com/takeaway-food-bacchus-marsh-ballan-gordon-blackwood).

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 centres)
- Kip McGrath Bacchus Marsh — local English & Maths tuition centre for primary and secondary students (NAPLAN & exam prep options) [citation:Kip McGrath Bacchus Marsh](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/bacchus-marsh).  
- Innate Tutoring — local tutoring services for maths, English and VCE subjects (local/online options) [citation:Innate Tutoring Bacchus Marsh page](https://www.innatetutoring.com.au/bacchus-marsh-tutoring/).

8) Commute — train time to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)
- Bacchus Marsh is served by V/Line Ballarat‑Melbourne trains. Typical travel time to Southern Cross is roughly 40–55 minutes depending on service; allowing a short transfer/walk/metro from Southern Cross to Flinders Street adds ~5–10 minutes, so expect about 50–65 minutes door‑to‑Flinders‑Street on most services [citation:Rome2rio Bacchus Marsh → Flinders St (typical journey times)](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Bacchus-Marsh/Flinders-Street-Station) [citation:V/Line timetables (Ballarat line services serve Bacchus Marsh)](https://www.vline.com.au/Timetables/Train-coach-timetables).
