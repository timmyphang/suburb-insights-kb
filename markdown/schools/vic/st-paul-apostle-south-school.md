1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Endeavour Hills is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,740 (2021 Census), above the national median [citation: 2021 Endeavour Hills QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20871). The median age is 39, and there are 6,842 families among 24,455 residents, indicating a strong family presence [citation: 2021 Endeavour Hills QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20871). The suburb has a solid socio-economic profile (IRSAD score of 990), reflecting a mix of white-collar and blue-collar households [citation: HTA Property Market](https://www.htag.com.au/vic/vic213-casey-city/endeavour-hills-vic-3802/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** According to the 2021 Census, 1,847 residents (7.6%) were born in India, making it the most common overseas birthplace [citation: 2021 Endeavour Hills QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20871). A total of 15,142 people (61.9%) were born in Australia [citation: 2021 Endeavour Hills QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20871).

3) **Working-parent support:** St Paul Apostle South School’s Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided by Extend, operating from 6:30am to 8:50am (before school) and 3:15pm to 6:00pm (after school) on school days [citation: Extend Before and After School Care](https://www.extend.com.au/school/st-paul-apostle-south-catholic-primary-school/). Vacation care is also available at the service [citation: ACECQA Service Register](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/st-paul-apostle-south-primary-school-oshc-extend).

4) **School culture:** St Paul Apostle South School offers a **Performing Arts program** where students learn music theory, instruments (ukulele, African drums, hand bells), drama, and dance, culminating in a whole-school concert every second year [citation: Performing Arts page](https://spsendeavourhills.catholic.edu.au/learning-teaching/performing-arts/). The school also runs **Health and Physical Education** classes weekly, emphasising sportsmanship and participation [citation: Physical Education page](https://spsendeavourhills.catholic.edu.au/learning-teaching/physical-education/). Additionally, students learn **Mandarin** as a language [citation: St Paul Apostle South homepage](https://spsendeavourhills.catholic.edu.au/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Endeavour Hills is a family-friendly suburb anchored by the **Endeavour Hills Shopping Centre**, which features major supermarkets Coles, Woolworths, Aldi, and Kmart [citation: Shopping Centre homepage](https://www.endeavourhillsshopping.com.au/). For specialty groceries, **BestMart Asian Grocer** (located next to Coles) offers a wide range of Asian ingredients [citation: BestMart Asian Grocer](https://www.endeavourhillsshopping.com.au/stores/bestmart-asian-grocer/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** The Endeavour Hills Shopping Centre offers several family-friendly takeaway options: **Domino’s Pizza** for pizza, **KFC** for fried chicken, and **Zambrero** for Mexican burritos and bowls [citation: Dining & Takeaway page](https://www.endeavourhillsshopping.com.au/dining-takeaway/). For fish and chips, **Heatherton Village Fish & Chips** provides delivery service in the area [citation: TuckerFox](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/endeavour-hills-vic-3803).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Lynn’s Learning Endeavour Hills** runs Maths and English tutoring for Foundation to Year 10, plus Selective Entry & Scholarship preparation for Years 3–9, at the Endeavour Hills Neighbourhood Centre on Mondays and Saturdays [citation: Lynn's Learning Endeavour Hills](https://lynnslearning.com.au/locations/victoria-locations/lynns-learning-endeavour-hills-tuition-centre/). **Success Tutoring Endeavour Hills** also offers personalised tutoring with 24/7 access to academic resources [citation: Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-endeavour-hills).

8) **Commute:** There is no train station in Endeavour Hills; the closest stations are **Dandenong Station** (on the Cranbourne/Pakenham lines) and **Hallam Station** (Pakenham line). The typical commute to Flinders Street Station involves catching bus **843** from Endeavour Hills to Dandenong Station, then a train taking approximately 1 hour 14 minutes total [citation: Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Endeavour-Hills/Flinders-Street-Station). Driving direct to the CBD takes about 26 minutes [citation: Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Endeavour-Hills/Flinders-Street-Station).
