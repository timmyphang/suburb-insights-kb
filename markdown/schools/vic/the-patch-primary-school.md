3) **Working-parent support:** The Patch Primary School’s Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided by OSHClub [citation:OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/the-patch-primary-school/). The ‘Rise then Shine’ before-school program operates from 7:00 AM until bell time, and the ‘Stay and Play’ after-school program runs from bell time to 6:00 PM [citation:OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/the-patch-primary-school/). Holiday programs (Holiday HQ) are also available from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM [citation:OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/the-patch-primary-school/).

4) **School culture:** The Patch Primary School offers a strong music program, including a **junior choir** and a **recorder club** for students in grades 3-6 [citation:Private Music Lessons](https://thepatchps.vic.edu.au/parents/private-music-lessons/) [citation:Level Two Information Booklet](https://www.thepatchps.vic.edu.au/files/6413/6253/6527/Level_2_Information_Booklet.pdf).
