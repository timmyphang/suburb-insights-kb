3) **Working-parent support**: Malvern Primary School partners with independent provider **TheirCare** for Outside School Hours Care [citation:Before and After School – Malvern Primary School](https://www.malvernps.vic.edu.au/parents/before-and-after-school-program/). Before-school care runs from **7:15 am to 8:45 am** and after-school care from **3:30 pm to 6:00 pm** each school day [citation:Before and After School – Malvern Primary School](https://www.malvernps.vic.edu.au/parents/before-and-after-school-program/). Vacation care is also available through the same provider.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: **Ezy Math Tutoring** offers in-home maths and English tutoring for K–12 students in Malvern East, with tutors including Olympiad medalists and experienced teachers [citation:301 Maths Tutors in Malvern East | Ezy Math Tutoring](https://www.ezymathtutoring.com.au/tutors/malvern-east-3145). **Malvern Tutoring**, run by Katherine Neale, provides primary-level tutoring with a focus on learning difficulties [citation:Malvern Tutoring | Malvern | Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/malverntutoring/).

8) **Commute**: The closest major station to Malvern East is **Caulfield Station** on the Cranbourne and Pakenham lines. A direct train from Caulfield to Flinders Street Station takes approximately **18 minutes** and runs every 10 minutes during peak times [citation:Malvern East to Flinders Street Station - 4 ways to travel ...](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Malvern-East/Flinders-Street-Station). Alternatively, **Malvern Station** (Glen Waverley line) is about 15 minutes away by train, though it is located in the adjacent suburb of Malvern.
