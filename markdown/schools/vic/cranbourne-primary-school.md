1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Cranbourne is a middle-to-working-class suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,477, compared to the Victorian median of $1,487 [citation:2021 Cranbourne, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20662). The median age is 35, and the area has a strong family focus, with 5,448 families and an average of 1.9 children per family with children [citation:2021 Cranbourne, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20662). The suburb has a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers but specific occupation breakdowns are not fully detailed in these sources.

Across Australia, the top five countries of birth (excluding Australia) are England, India, China, New Zealand, and the Philippines [citation:Cultural diversity: Census, 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/people-and-communities/cultural-diversity-census/latest-release). For specific Cranbourne percentages, search the 2021 Census QuickStats for Cranbourne [citation:2021 Cranbourne, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20662).

3) **Working-parent support:** Cranbourne Primary School operates its own school-run Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) program. Before School Care (Breakfast Club) runs from 6:45am to 8:30am, and After School Care runs from 3:15pm to 6:00pm [citation:Out of School Hours Care](https://www.cranbourne-ps.vic.edu.au/page/409/Out-of-School-Hours-Care). The service is Child Care Subsidy (CCS) approved and uses the Fully Booked booking platform [citation:Out of School Hours Care](https://www.cranbourne-ps.vic.edu.au/page/409/Out-of-School-Hours-Care).

4) **School culture:** Cranbourne Primary School emphasises its PRIDE values and offers a range of named extracurricular activities. These include the **Breakfast Club** during Before School Care [citation:Out of School Hours Care](https://www.cranbourne-ps.vic.edu.au/page/409/Out-of-School-Hours-Care), and a **Lunchtime Club** program offering two activities each day [citation:Principal's Message](https://www.cranbourne-ps.vic.edu.au/page/383/Principals-Message). The school also participates in the **Victorian High Ability Program** for extension and runs a **Musica Viva’s Musician in Residence** program [citation:Principal's Message](https://www.cranbourne-ps.vic.edu.au/page/383/Principals-Message) [citation:The Arts](https://www.cranbourne-ps.vic.edu.au/page/171/The-Arts).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Cranbourne is a growing family suburb with a mix of shopping centres and diverse grocery options. For specialty groceries, **Wirsa Indian Grocery Store** offers Indian and Asian flavours [citation:Indian flavours at Our Cranbourne Grocery Store](https://wirsa.com.au/indian-grocery-store-cranbourne/), **All Asia Grocery** is located at Cranbourne Park Shopping Centre [citation:All Asia Grocery (@allasiagrocery)](https://www.instagram.com/allasiagrocery/?hl=en), and **Mrs Wans Asian Mart** stocks South-East Asian items [citation:Mrs Wans Asian Mart](https://mrswans.com.au/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Several casual, family-friendly dining options are available in Cranbourne. **Dosa Hut** (Indian) at Hunt Club Shopping Village offers a large menu including dosas, biryani, and takeaway [citation:Best Indian Restaurant in Cranbourne - Dosa Hut](https://www.dosahut.net.au/indian-restaurant-cranbourne/). **Thai Cottage** (Thai) is recommended for takeaway [citation:The 26 best family restaurants in Cranbourne](https://wanderlog.com/list/geoCategory/558238/best-family-restaurants-in-cranbourne). **Don Vittorio's Pizzeria** (Pizza) is another option for takeaway [citation:Restaurants in Cranbourne,VIC With Takeaway](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/cranbourne/takeaway-category).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Several tutoring centres serve the Cranbourne area. **Stem Academic College** offers tutoring from Year 1 to VCE in English, Maths, Science, and Reasoning, including selective school and scholarship preparation [citation:Tuition Centre Cranbourne | Tutoring Services | Stem Academic College](https://stemacademiccollege.com.au/). **Edu Kingdom College** (in Cranbourne West) provides tuition for Year 1 to VCE and selective school exam preparation [citation:Tutoring Centre Cranbourne,Tutoring Cranbourne West](https://www.tutoringcentre.net/).

8) **Commute:** Cranbourne is served by the **Cranbourne Line** on the Metro Trains network. The **Cranbourne Station** provides train services to Melbourne CBD. The typical train travel time from Cranbourne Station to **Flinders Street Station** is approximately 1 hour 6 minutes [citation:Train Cranbourne Station to Flinders Street Station from $3](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Cranbourne-Station/Flinders-Street-Station). Trains run approximately every 30 minutes [citation:Cranbourne to Flinders Street Station - 3 ways to travel](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Cranbourne/Flinders-Street-Station).
