1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Panton Hill is a relatively affluent, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,367, notably above the Victorian median of $1,575 and the national median of $1,746 [citation:ABS QuickStats 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22032). The median age is 48, significantly older than the state and national averages of 38, and families comprise the majority of households, with an average of 0.9 children per all households and 1.8 per family with children [citation:ABS QuickStats 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22032). The suburb has 305 families among 1,063 residents, indicating a strong family presence, while occupation data (white-collar vs blue-collar) is not available from this source.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: Panton Hill is overwhelmingly Australian-born. According to the 2021 Census, 60.6% of people had both parents born in Australia, while 18.5% had both parents born overseas [citation:ABS QuickStats 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22032). The most common overseas country of birth in 2016 was England at 6.2%, followed by New Zealand at 1.3% and Germany at 1.3% [citation:ABS QuickStats 2016](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22020). Specific 2021 country-of-birth percentages for Panton Hill are not provided in the available sources.

3) **Working-parent support**: Panton Hill Primary School offers an Out of School Hours Care (OSHC) service managed by TheirCare. Before School Care runs from 6:45 am to 8:45 am, and After School Care from 3:20 pm to 6:00 pm, with a Holiday Program also available during school holidays [citation:Panton Hill Primary School OSHC](http://www.pantonhill.vic.edu.au/after-school-care/). The service operates from a dedicated building within the school grounds and has been established for over 20 years, with fees potentially reduced through Child Care Benefit via MyGov [citation:Panton Hill Primary School OSHC](http://www.pantonhill.vic.edu.au/after-school-care/).

4) **School culture**: Panton Hill Primary School offers a popular instrumental music program through external provider Whittlesea Music, with lessons available for Prep-6 students on Fridays, and the school also has a band that rehearses after school [citation:PHPS Info Book 2025](https://www.pantonhill.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/PHPS-Info-Book-2025.pdf) [citation:Panton Hill Primary School Gallery](http://www.pantonhill.vic.edu.au/classroom-news/). The school runs an interschool sports program for Years 4-6, competing in summer sports (Term 4) and winter sports (Term 2) including football, rounders, basketball, netball, T-ball, and cricket [citation:Panton Hill Primary School PE & Sport](http://www.pantonhill.vic.edu.au/pe-sport/). The school also hosts annual Swimming Sports, Athletics Sports, Hooptime, and Cross Country events [citation:Panton Hill Primary School PE & Sport](http://www.pantonhill.vic.edu.au/pe-sport/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Panton Hill is a semi-rural, family-friendly suburb with a quiet, community-oriented feel, featuring local amenities like Memorial Park, a community hall, tennis courts, and a football oval adjacent to the school [citation:Panton Hill Primary School About Us](https://www.pantonhill.vic.edu.au/about-us-overview/). Specific specialty or ethnic supermarkets in Panton Hill are not found in available sources; the nearest major grocery options are likely in nearby Hurstbridge or Eltham, with a Coles supermarket at 938 Main Road, Eltham. A library bus also visits the school area [citation:Panton Hill Primary School About Us](https://www.pantonhill.vic.edu.au/about-us-overview/).

6) **Weeknight dining**: The Panton Hill Hotel (also known as the Panton Hill Pub) offers a family-friendly pub dining experience with a menu catering to all food groups and dietary requirements, and features a play area for children [citation:Panton Hill Hotel](https://www.pantonhillhotel.com.au/) [citation:TripAdvisor Panton Hill Hotel](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurant_Review-g12828299-d10347835-Reviews-Panton_Hill_Hotel-Panton_Hill_Victoria.html). The Hill Thai Restaurant provides Thai cuisine with takeaway and delivery options, and is noted for curries and casual dining [citation:The Hill Thai Restaurant](https://thehillthairestaurant.com.au/). Additional takeaway options include Pizza and Chinese food available via Menulog and Uber Eats in Panton Hill postcode 3759 [citation:Menulog Panton Hill](https://www.menulog.com.au/area/3759-panton-hill).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Specific tutoring centres within Panton Hill are not listed in available sources; the closest tuition providers are found in the wider Nillumbik area, such as Kip McGrath Education Centres (Glen Waverley) and Teach Me Tutoring (Mount Waverley) [citation:Yellow Pages Tuition Tutoring Panton Hill](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/panton-hill-vic-3759/tuition-tutoring?page=3). Online or in-home tutoring services like Alchemy Tuition (serving Melbourne including Panton Hill) and Success Tutoring (Pennant Hills, NSW) are available, but no physical enrichment centre is named specifically for Panton Hill [citation:Alchemy Tuition](https://alchemytuition.com.au/tutoring-pennant-hills/).

8) **Commute**: Panton Hill does not have a train station; the nearest station is Hurstbridge on the Hurstbridge Line, approximately 8 km south. From Hurstbridge station, the train journey to Flinders Street Station in Melbourne CBD takes approximately 50–55 minutes, depending on the service [citation:Metro Trains Timetables](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/timetables/). The suburb is served by bus route 580 (Eltham–Hurstbridge), connecting to Hurstbridge station, with total travel time to the CBD (including bus and train) typically around 70–80 minutes.
