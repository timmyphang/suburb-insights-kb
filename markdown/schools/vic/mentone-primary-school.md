1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**  
Mentone is a middle-to-upper-income bayside suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,889 and a median age of 42, indicating a mix of established families and older couples [citation:2021 Mentone QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/208031190). The workforce skews white-collar, with many residents commuting to professional roles in Melbourne’s CBD [citation:2016 Mentone QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21637). The area has a higher proportion of families than the state average, though single-person households are also common.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**  
The most common overseas country of birth for Mentone residents is not explicitly listed in the available 2021 Census snippets. In 2016, 45.5% of people had both parents born in Australia, and 36.0% had both parents born overseas, but the single largest foreign country of birth is not specified [citation:2016 Mentone QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21637). The 2021 QuickStats data for Mentone does not include a country-of-birth table in the provided content.

3) **Working-parent support**  
Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) at Mentone Primary School is provided by TeamKids [citation:TeamKids – Mentone Primary](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/mentone-primary-oshc-5/). The specific operating hours are not stated in the available sources, but TeamKids typically offers before-school care from 7:00 am and after-school care until 6:00 pm. Parents should confirm exact times directly with the provider.

4) **School culture**  
Mentone Primary School runs a **Breakfast Club** that provides healthy meals before school to improve concentration and social skills [citation:Breakfast Club](https://www.mentoneps.vic.edu.au/page/51/Breakfast-Club). The **Student Excellence Program** offers enrichment and extension for high-ability students [citation:Student Excellence Program](https://www.mentoneps.vic.edu.au/page/64/Student-Excellence-Program). The school also hosts a **POTH (Battle of the Bands)** program, giving students a platform to perform and collaborate musically [citation:POTH Program](https://www.mentoneps.vic.edu.au/uploaded_files/media/poth_program__battle_of_the_bands.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**  
Mentone has a relaxed, family-friendly bayside atmosphere with local parks, a beach, and a village shopping strip. **Scicluna’s Real Food Merchants** on Mentone Parade is a large gourmet grocer offering fresh produce, deli items, and a café [citation:Scicluna's Mentone](https://sciclunas.com.au/stores/mentone/). For ethnic groceries, **Tales of India** delivers Indian staples such as spices, rice, and snacks to Mentone [citation:Tales of India](https://talesofindia.com.au/pages/indian-grocery-store-mentone-3194/).

6) **Weeknight dining**  
**La Porchetta Mentone** (121–123 Mentone Parade) serves authentic Italian pizzas, pastas, and schnitzels in a family-friendly setting with takeaway options [citation:La Porchetta Mentone](https://laporchetta.com.au/mentone/). **Thai Village Restaurant** on Mentone Parade offers classic Thai dishes like pad thai and green curry in a welcoming atmosphere [citation:Thai Village Restaurant](https://thecommunityforum.com.au/a-taste-of-thailand-in-mentone-the-enduring-charm-of-thai-village-restaurant/). **Adriano’s Pizza & Pasta** is a local favourite for affordable, no-fuss Italian takeaway [citation:Cheap Eats Mentone](https://melbz.com.au/mentone/cheap-eats/).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**  
**Kumon Mentone Education Centre** provides structured maths and English tutoring for primary and secondary students [citation:Kumon Mentone](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/Kumon-Mentone-VIC). Private in-home tutoring is also available through **Ezy Math Tutoring**, which offers one-on-one support for a range of subjects [citation:Ezy Math Tutoring Mentone](https://www.ezymathtutoring.com.au/tutors/mentone-3194).

8) **Commute**  
Mentone is served by **Mentone Station** on the **Frankston line**. Trains run every 15 minutes and the journey to **Flinders Street Station** (Melbourne CBD) takes approximately **48 minutes** [citation:Rome2Rio Trains](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Mentone-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). The station is within walking distance of the school (about 1 km).
