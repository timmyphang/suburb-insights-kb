With an average of 0.9 children per household and a population density of 905 persons per km², the suburb has a mix of families and older residents [citation:About the profile areas | Keilor | profile.id](https://profile.id.com.au/brimbank/about?WebID=180). The workforce profile is not detailed in the provided sources, but the income level suggests a solid middle‑class area with both white‑collar and blue‑collar households.

3) **Working-parent support:** St Augustine’s Primary School provides an Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) service operated by **Big Childcare**. Before‑school care runs from **6:30 am to 8:30 am** and after‑school care from **3:15 pm to 6:00 pm**, five days a week, with vacation care also available [citation:Outside School Care - St Augustine's Primary School](https://www.sakeilor.catholic.edu.au/school-community/outside-school-care/). The service is located in the school’s gymnasium (Cooinda) and can be contacted at 0426 203 858 or staugustines@bigchildcare.com.

4) **School culture:** St Augustine’s offers a wide range of named extracurricular activities. Examples include the **Super Uke Choir**, the **Chess Club**, and **Interschool Sport** (which includes events like the Twilight Athletics Carnival) [citation:Extra Curricular Activities - St Augustine's Primary School](https://www.sakeilor.catholic.edu.au/learning-teaching/extra-curricular-activities/). The school also runs a Student Leadership Program (with a Student Representative Council and Social Justice leaders) and a P–6 Camping Program.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Keilor has a family‑friendly feel with the **Keilor Central Shopping Centre** offering a Coles, an ALDI, and specialty stores such as **Pacific Asian** (Asian grocery) and **Harry’s Fresh** (fresh produce) [citation:Retail Directory - Keilor Central Shopping Centre](https://keilorcentral.com.au/retail-directory/). For Indian groceries, nearby **Dukaan Groceries** at 263 A Keilor Road in Niddrie stocks Indian and Pakistani products [citation:Dukaan Groceries - Indian Grocery Store Niddrie, Melbourne](https://singh.com.au/dukaan-groceries-indian-grocery-store-niddrie-melbourne/). The suburb is also close to the Maribyrnong River parklands.

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual, family‑friendly takeaway or dining, families can try **Keilor Village Kebab & Grill** (Middle Eastern, at 688 Old Calder Highway) [citation:Takeaway in Keilor - Order Food Delivery Near Me | Uber Eats](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/keilor-melbourne-vic), **Amalfi Pizza & Pasta** (Italian, 419 Keilor Road, Niddrie) [citation:Retail Directory - Keilor Central Shopping Centre](https://keilorcentral.com.au/retail-directory/), or **The Grillin’ Greeks** (Greek street food, inside Keilor Central). These options offer affordable meals suitable for families with primary‑school children.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Keilor Central Shopping Centre houses a **Kumon Education Centre** (providing maths and English enrichment) [citation:Retail Directory - Keilor Central Shopping Centre](https://keilorcentral.com.au/retail-directory/). **Success Tutoring** also has a centre in Keilor East (3033) offering primary and high school support in English and maths [citation:Keilor East – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-keilor-east). In‑home tutoring is available via Tutor2You and Ezy Math Tutoring in the area.

8) **Commute:** Keilor is served by **Keilor Plains Station** on the **Sunbury line**. The train journey from Keilor Plains to Flinders Street Station takes approximately **34–38 minutes** depending on the service [citation:Flinders Street Station to Keilor Plains Station - 4 ways](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Keilor-Plains-Station). Trains run frequently, with departures every 15 minutes during peak times.
