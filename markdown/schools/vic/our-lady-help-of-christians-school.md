Welcome to Hendra

Hendra settles comfortably between river bends and everyday family life. The median age here is around 39, and the suburb reads as a professional, white‑collar pocket of Brisbane where couples and families outnumber the singles-and-nightlife crowd. Households tend to be family-focused, and median weekly household income sits in the high two‑thousands (2021 figures), which shows up in neat postcodes, well-kept parks and an easygoing, suburban rhythm. If you’re migrating with kids and want quiet streets that still feel close to the city, Hendra gives you that blend: residential calm with practical proximity.

Inside the Classroom at Our Lady Help of Christians School

Our Lady Help of Christians School runs Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland), and the campus has the kind of small-school feel that many families find reassuring. Students can take individual instrumental music lessons on site through a local provider, and team sports are a visible part of the week — things like netball and Australian rules football, plus participation in an annual Gala Sports Day. After school the calendar is dotted with term-by-term options delivered by outside providers, from singing and dance to other activity programs. The vibe is community-focused: music, sport and arts are woven into the school day rather than tacked on, which helps children find their niche without parents having to ferry them all over town.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

If you need on-site before and after school childcare, the school’s Outside School Hours Care (that is, on-site before and after school childcare) is run by Catholic Early Education & Care. They operate before school from 7:00 to 8:30, after school from 3:00 to 6:00 and provide vacation care as well. The service is licensed for a limited number of places and is registered for the national Child Care Subsidy, so if you’ll rely on this care make that confirmation part of your enrolment checklist. The benefit of having care on campus is that it removes an extra handover in the afternoon and gives kids time to unwind with familiar faces before you collect them.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Grocery runs in Hendra are appropriately low-drama: there are convenience grocers and small supermarkets inside the suburb for quick top-ups, and a short drive opens up larger supermarkets and specialty food options. If you cook South Asian or East Asian recipes, nearby shopping strips in Clayfield and Nundah include specialty stores that make sourcing ingredients straightforward. When you don’t feel like cooking, Hendra’s local dining scene is very family-friendly: classic fish and chip shops, casual cafés that double as dinner spots, and the occasional modern Australian option that’ll feed a picky crew without fuss.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If your child needs extra academic support, the northern suburbs have several tutoring options a short drive or train ride away. Tailored maths and English programs and local private tutors can slot in around school hours, so you’re never far from extra help during exam season or when homework becomes a daily hurdle. For adults and older kids commuting to Brisbane’s CBD, Hendra sits on the Doomben inner northern rail line; typical scheduled train times to Brisbane Central are roughly 10 to 18 minutes depending on the service, so an off‑peak trip is often around fifteen minutes. That kind of short rail commute is a real advantage when you want the benefits of a calmer suburb without sacrificing quick access to the city.

Your Next Steps

First, book a school tour or enrolment meeting through the school website to confirm intake criteria and exact dates — seeing the classrooms and meeting staff will give you the clearest sense of fit. Second, connect directly with the on-site Outside School Hours Care provider to confirm enrolment capacity, operating hours and Child Care Subsidy details before you rely on before- or after‑school care. Third, ask the school for its current fee schedule and levies and, if programs like instrumental lessons or team sports matter to your child, check availability and costs for those offerings and any external providers now so you’re not caught out after term starts.
