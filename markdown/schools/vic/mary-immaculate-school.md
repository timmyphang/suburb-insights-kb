1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Ivanhoe is an affluent suburb, with a median weekly household income of $2,232, well above the Victorian median [citation:2021 Ivanhoe (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21246). The median age is 40, and the suburb has a high proportion of families, with an average of 1.7 children per family [citation:2021 Ivanhoe (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21246). The population is predominantly white-collar, with a large number of professionals and managers.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** In Ivanhoe, 72.8% of residents were born in Australia [citation:2021 Ivanhoe (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21246). Among overseas-born residents, the most common country of birth was England at 2.9%, followed by Italy at 2.2% and China at 2.1% [citation:2021 Ivanhoe (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21246).

3) **Working-parent support:** Mary Immaculate School provides Before and After School Care through TheirCare, an accredited provider [citation:Before and After School Care – TheirCare – Mary Immaculate, Ivanhoe](https://www.miivanhoe.catholic.edu.au/our-school/before-and-after-school-care-theircare/). The specific operating hours for this service are not listed in the provided search context, so check directly with the school.

4) **School culture:** Mary Immaculate School offers a range of named extracurriculars, including the **Garden Club** (student-led) and **Chess Club** [citation:ANNUAL REPORT TO THE SCHOOL COMMUNITY MARY IMMACULATE PRIMARY SCHOOL IVANHOE](http://www.miivanhoe.catholic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/Annual-Report-2019.pdf). Senior students also participate in the **Ivanhoe District School Sporting Association** inter-school sport competitions [citation:Wider Community – Mary Immaculate, Ivanhoe](https://www.miivanhoe.catholic.edu.au/school-community/wider-community/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Ivanhoe has a leafy, family-friendly vibe with easy access to the Darebin Parklands. For groceries, there is **Journey to the East Asian Mart**, a local Asian grocery store [citation:Journey to the East Asian Mart – Shop in Ivanhoe](https://shopinivanhoe.com.au/traders/journey-east-asian-mart/), and **FoodWorks East Ivanhoe** for everyday needs [citation:FoodWorks East Ivanhoe](https://eastivanhoe-shop.foodworks.com.au/). For fresh produce and bulk wholefoods, **Sage Bulk Wholefoods** is also listed in Ivanhoe’s shop directory.

6) **Weeknight dining:** For family-friendly takeaway or dining, **Woodfire Cafe Pizzeria** on Upper Heidelberg Road serves Italian wood-fired pizza and pasta [citation:AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/ivanhoe/takeaway-category). **Cafe Saffron** offers authentic Indian cuisine in a casual setting [citation:Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g1006537-zfp10601-Ivanhoe_Banyule_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). **Theo's Fish Bar** is a popular local spot for seafood and fast food [citation:Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g1006537-zfp10601-Ivanhoe_Banyule_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Math Minds** is a maths tutoring centre located at 181 Upper Heidelberg Road in Ivanhoe, serving grades 4 to VCE [citation:Maths Tutoring Ivanhoe East](https://mathminds.com.au/maths-tutor-ivanhoe-east/). **KIS Academics** provides English tutoring for Year 11-12 students in Ivanhoe [citation:Best Year 11-12 English Tutors in Ivanhoe](https://kisacademics.com/w/tutoring-locations/ivanhoe-english-tutoring).

8) **Commute:** Ivanhoe is served by the **Hurstbridge** train line, with trains departing from **Ivanhoe Station** to Flinders Street Station. The typical travel time is approximately 22 minutes [citation:Ivanhoe to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Ivanhoe/Flinders-Street-Station). Trains run every 20 minutes on average [citation:Ivanhoe to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Ivanhoe/Flinders-Street-Station).
