1. **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Nunawading is a middle-income suburb with a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers. The median weekly household income is $1,938, and the median age is 39, indicating a mature population with many families [citation:2021 Nunawading, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/211041271). The suburb has 3,270 families and an average of 0.7 children per household, suggesting a strong family presence alongside singles [citation:2021 Nunawading, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/211041271).

2. **Most common foreign country of birth**: In Nunawading, 38.8% of people had both parents born in Australia, while 44.7% had both parents born overseas, indicating a diverse population [citation:2016 Nunawading, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21973). The most common countries of birth were not specified in the available 2021 QuickStats, but the suburb has significant overseas-born residents.

3. **Working-parent support**: Nunawading Christian College offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) operating from 7:00am until school starts in the mornings and from 3:20pm to 6:00pm in the evenings, with a School Holiday Program available from 7:00am to 6:00pm [citation:Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) | Nunawading Christian College](https://nunawading.vic.edu.au/our-community/outside-school-hours-care). The provider is the school itself; families can register via the Xplor App [citation:Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) | Nunawading Christian College](https://nunawading.vic.edu.au/our-community/outside-school-hours-care). Breakfast and afternoon tea are included in the costs, which are $26 for permanent before-school care and $31 for permanent after-school care [citation:Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) | Nunawading Christian College](https://nunawading.vic.edu.au/our-community/outside-school-hours-care).
