1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Knoxfield is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb. According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income is $1,762, and the median age is 41 [citation:Knoxfield QuickStats 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21374). The suburb’s population is 7,645, with a high proportion of families (2,244 families) and an average of 2.6 people per household [citation:Knoxfield QuickStats 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21374). Detailed white-collar vs. blue-collar breakdown is not available in the provided sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** In Knoxfield, 69.5% of people speak only English at home, indicating a strong Australian-born majority [citation:2016 Knoxfield QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21366). The most common overseas country of birth is likely China (Mandarin speakers at 7.2%), followed by Sri Lanka (Sinhalese at 1.9%) [citation:2016 Knoxfield QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21366). The exact Australian-born percentage is not stated in the provided sources.

8) **Commute:** Knoxfield has no train station. The nearest station is **Ferntree Gully Station** on the Belgrave line. The total journey from Flinders Street Station to Knoxfield takes approximately 1 hour 39 minutes via the Belgrave line train to Ferntree Gully Station [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Knoxfield). An alternative is to take the train to Glen Waverley Station and then bus route 753 to Knoxfield, taking about 1 hour 34 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Knoxfield).
