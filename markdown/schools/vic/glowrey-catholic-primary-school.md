Here’s a concise eight‑point briefing for families considering Glowrey Catholic Primary School (Wollert). I used one targeted search per item and cite sources after each factual claim.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Wollert is a rapidly growing, family‑oriented suburb with a young median age (30) and a high share of couples with children — indicative of many new housing developments and young families moving in [citation:2021 Wollert QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22820). The area is primarily middle‑income and mixed‑occupational (a blend of professionals and trades) as a large master‑planned estate (Aurora) supplies new family amenities [citation:Aurora Village — Development Victoria](https://aurora-village.com.au/retailers/).

2) Most common overseas country of birth  
About 46% of Wollert residents were born in Australia (2021); the most common overseas birthplace is India (≈18.7% in 2021) [citation:Wollert suburb profile (2021)](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/wollert).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC) at Glowrey  
Glowrey offers on‑site Before & After School Care (Glowrey Children’s Hub) delivered by MACSEYE with before‑school (about 6:30–8:30am), after‑school (about 3:30–6:30pm) and holiday‑care options listed on the school/OSHC provider pages [citation:Glowrey Before & After School Care](https://glowrey.catholic.edu.au/our-school/before-after-school-care/) [citation:Glowrey Children's Hub — StartingBlocks](https://www.startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01KQWVGM59WNS066SYE41CV7DJ/Glowrey-Childrens-Hub-Wollert-OSHC).

4) School culture — extracurriculars / specialist programs  
Glowrey runs weekly specialist programs including Physical Education, Visual Arts, STEM and Japanese — these are explicitly listed as specialist lessons on the school site and parent handbook [citation:Our School (Glowrey)](https://glowrey.catholic.edu.au/our-school/) [citation:Glowrey Parent Handbook 2025 (pdf)](https://glowrey.catholic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Parent-Handbook-Glowrey-2025.pdf).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (2 sentences)  
Wollert/Aurora feels like a new master‑planned family precinct with playgrounds, parks and several childcare centres nearby (Aurora Playground, Edgars Creek Reserve and multiple early‑learning centres) [citation:Aurora Playground — MelbournePlaygrounds](https://www.melbourneplaygrounds.com.au/aurora-playground-cnr-craigieburn-east-road-and-edgars-road-wollert). Local supermarkets and ethnic grocers serving families include IGA Local Grocer Wollert and nearby Aurora Village supermarkets, plus specialist South Asian shops such as Hamara Bazaar and Ceylon Hub [citation:IGA Local Grocer Wollert](https://www.iga.com.au/stores/iga-local-grocer-wollert/) [citation:Hamara Bazaar listing (Aurora)](https://aurora-village.com.au/retailers/) [citation:Ceylon Hub listing](https://australia.chamberofcommerce.com/business-directory/victoria/wollert/asian-grocery-store/3934958-ceylon-hub).

6) Weeknight dining (2–3 casual/family options)  
- Wollert Take Away — local takeaway for quick family meals [citation:Wollert Take Away — Uber Eats](https://www.ubereats.com/au/store/wollert-take-away/6qzypl7_RqWxC0rn0hQOUA).  
- 4 Seeds Cafe — family‑friendly cafe serving breakfast/lunch and takeaway [citation:4 Seeds Cafe — Uber Eats](https://www.ubereats.com/au/store/4-seeds-cafe/IMHorzyzSmKufxBIDOPtfw).  
- Bluestone Restaurant & Functions — relaxed, family‑friendly dining and takeaway in Wollert (Mediterranean/modern Australian) [citation:Bluestone Wollert](https://www.bluestonewollert.com.au/).

7) Tuition and enrichment near Wollert  
Notable options include Brainworks (one‑to‑one tutoring in Wollert) and a local Kumon centre (Wollert) for maths/English practice [citation:Brainworks Wollert tutoring](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-wollert) [citation:Kumon Wollert listing](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-WOLLERT-VIC).

8) Commute — public transport to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)  
Wollert does not yet have a passenger train station; travel typically requires a local bus to a nearby station (Epping/Mernda/Craigieburn) then a train into the city. Typical combined bus+train commutes to Flinders Street are about 1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes depending on connections (Rome2rio/journey planners estimate ~1h09m as a typical option) and the Wollert precinct plan reserves land for a future rail extension [citation:Rome2rio (Wollert→Melbourne estimate)](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Wollert/Melbourne) [citation:Wollert precinct plan / future rail land (VPA)](https://vpa.vic.gov.au/wollert-precinct-structure-plan-approved/).
