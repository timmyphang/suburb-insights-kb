1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Richmond is a densely populated, relatively affluent inner-city suburb with a median age of 34, notably younger than the Victorian median of 38 [citation:2021 Richmond (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22170). The median weekly household income is $2,245, well above the state median, and the area has a high proportion of young professionals and singles, though families are present with 6,662 families recorded [citation:2021 Richmond (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22170). The workforce mix leans white-collar, reflecting the suburb's professional and managerial profile.

3) **Working-parent support:** Trinity Catholic School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by Extend, operating from the school grounds at 57-61 Davison St [citation:Trinity Catholic School OSHC - Extend](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/trinity-catholic-school-oshc-extend). The before-school care runs weekdays from 7:00 AM to 8:45 AM, and after-school care runs from 3:15 PM to 6:00 PM during school terms [citation:Trinity Catholic School OSHC - Extend](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/trinity-catholic-school-oshc-extend). Vacation care is also available.

4) **School culture:** Trinity Catholic School offers a range of named extracurricular activities, including **Ukulele Club**, where students learn to play an instrument and perform [citation:Student Activities – Trinity Primary School Richmond](https://tcs.catholic.edu.au/learning/student-activities/). The school also runs **Friendship Club** on Mondays and **Sports Club** on Mondays, as well as **Art Club** on Wednesdays and **Fit Club** on Thursday mornings in partnership with St Kevin’s Toorak [citation:Trinity Clubs – Trinity Primary School Richmond](https://tcs.catholic.edu.au/trinity-clubs/).
