1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Ringwood is best described as middle‑income with many established families (median age 38; median weekly household income $1,654). It’s a mixed white‑collar and service‑industry suburb with a substantial family presence (lots of households with children). 4) School culture — extracurriculars offered (2–3 specific examples)  
Kalinda offers performing arts (weekly lessons, choirs, instrumental lessons), Highland dancing (large participation), and a strong interschool sports program (basketball/netball, AFL/Hoop Time participation). 5) Suburb vibe and groceries (2 sentences)  
Ringwood is a busy outer‑east activity centre (Eastland shopping precinct) with family amenities including Ringwood Lake park and Realm library/cultural centre nearby — good for playgrounds, walking and short local trips. For groceries there are mainstream supermarkets (ALDI/Coles) plus Asian/ethnic grocers at Eastland such as Crown Asian Supermarket and several local specialty grocers.
