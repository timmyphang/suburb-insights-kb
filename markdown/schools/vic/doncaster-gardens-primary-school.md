Here’s a short, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Doncaster Gardens Primary School (Doncaster East). Each item uses one targeted source; I’ve kept snippets/facts only.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Doncaster East is generally affluent / upper‑middle income with a median household income about $1,792 per week and a higher median age (41) than Victoria overall [citation:2021 Doncaster East, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20772).

2) Most common overseas country of birth (Doncaster East residents)  
China is the largest overseas birthplace reported for Doncaster East (suburb summaries report ~14–15% born in China; around half the population are Australian‑born) [citation:Doncaster East, VIC — Suburb Demographics & Profile | Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/doncaster-east) [citation:2021 Doncaster East, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20772).

3) Working‑parent support / OSHC at this school  
Doncaster Gardens Primary runs on‑site Before & After School Care and Vacation Care through TheirCare (before school ~7:00–8:45am; after school ~3:30–6:15pm; holiday program offered) [citation:Out of Hours Care Program - Doncaster Gardens Primary School](https://www.dgps.vic.edu.au/page/22/Out-of-Hours-Care-Program) [citation:Doncaster Gardens PS TheirCare - ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/doncaster-gardens-ps-theircare).

4) School culture — extracurriculars (2–3 specific items)  
Senior choir and orchestra (Years 4–6) and junior choir (Years 2–3); specialist Arts program including Music, Drama and Visual Arts; regular school sports program/PE [citation:The Arts - dgps.vic.edu.au](https://www.dgps.vic.edu.au/page/77/The-Arts) [citation:Health and Physical Education - dgps.vic.edu.au](https://www.dgps.vic.edu.au/page/10/Health-and-Physical-Education).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)  
Doncaster East is family‑oriented with shopping hubs (Devon Plaza, Doncaster East Village / Tunstall Square), parks and local playgrounds nearby. For groceries there are major supermarkets at Devon Plaza (Woolworths/Coles) and several Asian specialty grocers such as KFL Supermarket and Prosperity Asian Grocery in the Doncaster East shopping precincts [citation:Doncaster East suburb profile (PDF) - City of Manningham](https://www.manningham.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/2024-06/doncaster_east_suburb_profile.pdf) [citation:Doncaster East KFL | The Asian Food Specialist](https://www.kflsupermarkets.com.au/doncaster-east-vic).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 family‑friendly options / cuisines  
Tunstall Square and Devon Plaza provide quick family options: Grill’d (burgers), Sengs Kitchen (Chinese takeaway) and food‑court outlets at Devon Plaza — a mix of Australian fast‑casual plus Chinese/Asian takeaway choices [citation:Grill'd Tunstall Square - Grill'd Healthy Burgers](https://grilld.com.au/restaurants/victoria/tunstall-square) [citation:Sengs Kitchen, Doncaster East (Chinese Cuisine)](https://sengskitchen.tuckerfox.com.au/).

7) Tuition and enrichment near Doncaster East  
Kumon Doncaster East (maths/English tuition) and a local Kip McGrath / KMEC Doncaster tuition centre are available for primary‑level tutoring and enrichment [citation:Search for a Kumon Centre | Kumon Australia](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-DONCASTER-EAST-VIC) [citation:KMEC Doncaster Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/doncaster).

8) Commute to Melbourne CBD (to Flinders Street) — realistic option  
Doncaster East has no suburban train station; the realistic public transport commute is by bus (not train). The high‑frequency 907/route into the city runs along Doncaster Rd and typically takes about 40–50 minutes to the city (times vary by traffic/timetable) — express‑style orbital buses connect Doncaster Rd stops to the CBD [citation:907 City - Mitcham via Eastern Fwy and Doncaster Rd - Transport Victoria](https://transport.vic.gov.au/route/16482/907-city-mitcham-via-eastern-fwy-and-doncaster-rd) [citation:Doncaster East to Flinders Street Station - 5 ways to travel via train (Rome2rio)](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Doncaster-East/Flinders-Street-Station).
