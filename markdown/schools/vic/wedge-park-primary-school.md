- Melton West has a median age of 36 and a 2021 median weekly household income of $1,450 — lower than many inner‑Melbourne suburbs, consistent with a lower‑to‑middle household income profile [citation:ABS QuickStats Melton West 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21644).

- Local grocery options include Melton International Grocery at the Woodgrove precinct for ethnic/international ingredients and a FoodWorks serving Melton West for everyday groceries [citation:Melton International Grocery (Woodgrove listing)](https://woodgroveshoppingcentre.com.au/woodgrove/store-directory/melton-international-grocery/) and [citation:FoodWorks Melton West](https://meltonwest.foodworks.com.au/).
