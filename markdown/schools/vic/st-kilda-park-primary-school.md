1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: St Kilda is an inner-city suburb with a median age of 36 and a median weekly household income of $1,779, slightly above the national median [citation:2021 St Kilda (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22343). The population skews young and professional (white-collar), with a high proportion of singles—only 0.2 children per household and 4,075 families out of 19,490 people [citation:2021 St Kilda (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22343). Families with children are present but form a smaller share compared to typical outer suburbs.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The exact dominant overseas country of birth for St Kilda in 2021 is not available in the provided sources [citation:2021 St Kilda (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22343). In 2016, 33.3% of residents had both parents born in Australia and 41.0% had both parents born overseas, with the most common overseas birthplaces being England, New Zealand, and India [citation:2016 St Kilda, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/206051133). For the most recent 2021 data, please refer directly to the ABS Community Profiles.

3) **Working-parent support**: St Kilda Park Primary School’s Out of School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided by **TeamKids** on-site [citation:Out of School Hours Care | St Kilda Park Primary School](http://www.skipps.vic.edu.au/programs/OSH.html). Before-school care runs from **7:00 am until 8:45 am** (with breakfast if needed) and after-school care from **3:30 pm until 6:15 pm** (afternoon tea provided) [citation:Out of School Hours Care | St Kilda Park Primary School](http://www.skipps.vic.edu.au/programs/OSH.html). TeamKids also operates a holiday program with extended hours.

4) **School culture**: St Kilda Park Primary School offers a **School Choir** (Foundation to Year 6) and a **Senior Choir** for older students, performing at assemblies and community events [citation:Performing Arts | St Kilda Park Primary School](https://www.skipps.vic.edu.au/learning/performing-arts/). The school also runs **Chess Club** and **Coding Club** as part of its extracurricular program, along with **Orchestra** and instrumental music tuition [citation:ST KILDA PARK PRIMARY SCHOOL 68 FITZROY STREET](https://www.skipps.vic.edu.au/assets/uploads/2023/11/Yr-34_parent-payments-arrangements.pdf). These activities are explicitly named in the school’s parent payments arrangement document.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: St Kilda is a lively beachside suburb with a mix of cafes, parks, and family-friendly amenities such as the St Kilda Adventure Playground and the St Kilda Sea Baths. For specialty groceries, **St Kilda Family Mart** (Asian grocery) and **Great Asia Food Store** offer a wide range of Asian products, while **Tales of India** provides Indian grocery delivery to St Kilda [citation:THE BEST 10 INTERNATIONAL GROCERY near ST KILDA](https://www.yelp.com/search?cflt=intlgrocery&find_loc=St+Kilda,+Melbourne+Victoria) [citation:Indian Grocery Store St Kilda Road Central](https://talesofindia.com.au/pages/st-kilda-road-central-3004). There is also a **Woolworths** and **Coles** in the area for everyday essentials.

6) **Weeknight dining**: For casual family-friendly dining, **Rococo** (Italian pizza and pasta, on Fitzroy St) is a popular choice with takeaway available [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in St Kilda - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g255360-zfp10601-St_Kilda_Port_Phillip_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). **Galleon Cafe** offers relaxed Australian cafe fare and is great for an easy meal [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in St Kilda - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g255360-zfp10601-St_Kilda_Port_Phillip_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). **Cher Thai St Kilda** provides Thai takeaway and delivery, with a menu including curries and stir-fries [citation:Cher Thai St Kilda](https://cherthaistkilda.yumbojumbo.com.au/).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: **Kip McGrath St Kilda** offers English and maths tutoring for primary and secondary students at its centre [citation:Kip McGrath St Kilda English and Maths Tutoring](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/st-kilda). **Success Tutoring St Kilda** provides personalised primary and high school tutoring in English and maths, with customised learning plans [citation:Tutoring St Kilda | Primary & High School Tutors](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-st-kilda). Both are located in or near the suburb.

8) **Commute**: St Kilda does not have a train station; the most direct public transport to Melbourne CBD is **tram route 16** (Kew–St Kilda Beach) or **tram route 96** (East Brunswick–St Kilda Beach), both running along St Kilda Road [citation:Flinders Street Station to St Kilda](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/St-Kilda). The estimated tram travel time from St Kilda Junction to Flinders Street Station is approximately **21–27 minutes** [citation:Flinders Street Station to St Kilda](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/St-Kilda). Alternatively, bus routes such as the 246 also connect to the city.
