1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Thornbury is an established inner-northern suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,971 and a median age of 37 [citation:2021 Thornbury QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22508). The workforce is predominantly white-collar, with many residents employed in professional, healthcare, education, and creative industries [citation:CompoundInvest Thornbury Profile](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/thornbury/). The suburb has a balanced mix of families (4,773 families) and singles, with an average of 0.6 children per household [citation:2021 Thornbury QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22508).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: According to the 2016 Census (the most recent country-of-birth data available in the provided sources), the top overseas country of birth in Thornbury was Greece (4.5%), followed by Italy (3.5%) and England (2.5%). In 2016, 38.6% of residents had both parents born in Australia [citation:2016 Thornbury QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22496). 2021 Census country-of-birth figures were not included in the provided search context.

3) **Working-parent support**: Penders Grove Primary School partners with **TheirCare** to provide Out of School Hours Care (OSHC) on site [citation:OSHC Pender's Grove](https://www.pendersgroveps.vic.edu.au/our-school/out-of-school-hours-care-oshc/). Before School Care runs from **7:00 am – 8:50 am**, After School Care from **3:30 pm – 6:00 pm**, and full-day care is available on curriculum days from **7:00 am – 6:00 pm** [citation:OSHC Pender's Grove](https://www.pendersgroveps.vic.edu.au/our-school/out-of-school-hours-care-oshc/). TheirCare also operates during school holidays and pupil-free days.

4) **School culture**: Penders Grove offers several named extracurricular programs. These include the **Breakfast Club** (part of the Victorian Government's School Breakfast Clubs program), a **School Choir**, and a **Percussion Ensemble** that rehearses weekly and performs at school and community events [citation:Breakfast Club Pender's Grove](http://www.pendersgroveps.vic.edu.au/breakfast-club.html) [citation:Additional Music Programs](https://www.pendersgroveps.vic.edu.au/teaching-and-learning/additional-music-programs/). Students can also take private instrumental lessons in recorder, piano, guitar, violin, ukulele, and keyboard during the school day [citation:Additional Music Programs](https://www.pendersgroveps.vic.edu.au/teaching-and-learning/additional-music-programs/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Thornbury is a family-friendly suburb with numerous parks (e.g., Penders Park, Darebin Creek trails) and community facilities. For groceries, there is an **IGA Friendly Grocers** at 404 Station Street and a **Thornbury Food-way** at 650-654 High Street, both offering everyday essentials [citation:Yellow Pages Supermarkets](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/thornbury-vic-3071). For ethnic and specialty items, **Just Spice @ Thornbury** (Indian groceries) is located in the suburb, and **Wanlong Oriental** (Asian supermarket) services the wider area, though its primary locations are in Wollongong [citation:Wanlong Oriental](https://wanlongoriental.com.au/).

6) **Weeknight dining**: Casual family-friendly takeaway options in Thornbury include **Farro Pizzeria Thornbury** (Italian pizza and pasta), **Kebab & Sweet Spot** (kebabs and burgers), and **Croxton Roosters** (roast chicken and sides) [citation:Uber Eats Thornbury](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/thornbury-melbourne-vic). All offer takeaway and delivery services.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Families can access **Success Tutoring Thornbury**, which provides one-on-one English and maths assessments and personalised tutoring [citation:Success Tutoring Thornbury](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-thornbury). Another nearby option is **Kumon Northcote Education Centre** at 542 High Street, Northcote, offering after-school maths and reading programs [citation:Yellow Pages Tuition](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/tuition-tutoring/thornbury-vic-3071).

8) **Commute**: Thornbury is served by the **Mernda line** from **Thornbury Station**. The train journey to Flinders Street Station (Melbourne CBD) takes approximately **21 minutes**, with services running every 20 minutes [citation:Rome2rio Thornbury to Flinders Street](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Thornbury-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). Alternatively, bus route 251 and tram route 86 also connect to the city.
