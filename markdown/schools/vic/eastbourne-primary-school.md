1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Capel Sound is a working-class to middle-income suburb with a high proportion of retirees. According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income is $950, and the median age is 59, reflecting an older population [citation:2021 Capel Sound, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20476). The workforce is more blue-collar than white-collar, with many residents employed in trades, labouring, and hospitality, and the suburb has a higher-than-average number of couples without children and lone-person households [citation:2021 Capel Sound, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20476).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The available Census data for Capel Sound does not specify a dominant overseas country of birth. The suburb is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 79.4% of residents born in Australia [citation:2021 Capel Sound, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20476). No single overseas country reaches a notable percentage in the provided sources.

Families should contact the school directly at (03) 5986 4884 or visit the website's OSHC page for current details on the provider and hours.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Capel Sound has a relaxed, seaside atmosphere with direct access to Port Phillip Bay beaches and local parks. For everyday groceries, residents shop at the nearby **Rosebud** supermarket precinct, which includes a Coles and Woolworths. Specialty/ethnic grocery stores in the area include **Asian Noodle and Curry House** (Rosebud) which also sells groceries, and the **Rosebud Market** for fresh local produce; however, a dedicated ethnic supermarket name was not found in the provided snippets for Capel Sound itself.

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly, casual dining options in Capel Sound include **Glyko Kafe** (Mediterranean/Greek, located on Point Nepean Road), **Thai Rosy Restaurant** (Thai cuisine, also on Point Nepean Road), and **Pho Thao** (Vietnamese, serving rice paper rolls, pho, and fried rice) [citation:THE 5 BEST Restaurants in Capel Sound](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g13289638-Capel_Sound_Mornington_Peninsula_Victoria.html). For takeaway, **Ocean Fish & Chippery** in Rosebud delivers to Capel Sound [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Capel Sound](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/capel-sound-vic-3940).

8) **Commute:** Capel Sound does not have a train station. The nearest station is **Rosebud**, which is a bus ride away on the **Bus Route 781** (from Capel Sound to Rosebud Station). From Rosebud, V/Line trains run on the **Stony Point line** to Melbourne, with a change at Frankston for the Metro train to **Flinders Street**. The total estimated travel time to Melbourne CBD is approximately 90-100 minutes by train and bus combined [citation:Timetable Search | Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/timetables/); [citation:V/Line - Regional public transport for Victoria](https://www.vline.com.au/Timetables/Train-coach-timetables).
