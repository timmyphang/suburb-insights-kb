1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Delahey is a middle-income, working-class suburb dominated by families. The median weekly household income is $1,486, and the median age is 39 [citation:2021 Delahey QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/213011331). The typical household is a couple with children living in a separate house owned with a mortgage [citation:DELAHEY suburb profile](https://landchecker.com.au/suburb/delahey-vic-3037/). Most residents (85.3%) commute by car, indicating a car-dependent, suburban lifestyle [citation:DELAHEY suburb profile](https://landchecker.com.au/suburb/delahey-vic-3037/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The most common overseas country of birth is not specified in the available sources. However, the 2016 Census noted that 71.4% of Delahey residents had both parents born overseas, while only 15.3% had both parents born in Australia [citation:2016 Delahey QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/213011331). The 2021 data shows a highly diverse community, but the specific leading country of birth is not listed in the provided excerpts.

3) **Working-parent support**: Mackellar Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care through **TheirCare**. Before School Care runs from **6:15 am to 8:45 am**, and After School Care runs from **3:30 pm to 6:30 pm** [citation:Parent Portal](https://mackellarps.vic.edu.au/parents/). Vacation Care and Curriculum Day programs operate from **6:30 am to 6:30 pm** [citation:Parent Portal](https://mackellarps.vic.edu.au/parents/).

4) **School culture**: Mackellar runs a **Breakfast Club** each weekday from 8:30 am to 8:55 am in Room 1, supported by Foodbank Victoria [citation:Parent Portal](https://mackellarps.vic.edu.au/parents/). The school also has a **Camping Program** for personal development and a **bi-ennial production and art show** that involves the whole community [citation:Mackellar Primary School](https://mackellarps.vic.edu.au/). These are named extracurricular activities beyond the standard curriculum.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Delahey is a family-oriented suburb with a strong community feel, characterised by separate houses and a neighbourhood residential zone. For specialty groceries, **Delta Fine Foods** is located at Shop 11, Delahey 3037, offering a range of gourmet products [citation:Find a Stockist - Delta Fine Foods](https://www.deltasales.com.au/find-a-stockist/). Nearby suburbs like St Albans and Caroline Springs also host Indian and Asian grocery stores, though specific names are not listed in the provided sources.

6) **Weeknight dining**: For casual family-friendly takeaway, **Pizza Dude** in Delahey offers pizza delivery with a strong reputation for service [citation:Best Pizza Takeaway in Delahey](https://pizzadude.gotoeat.net/). **Dosa Hut - Indian Multi Cuisine Restaurant** in Caroline Springs (3.4 km away) provides Indian takeaway and dine-in options [citation:Delahey 3037 Food Delivery](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/delahey-3037). **The Curry Story** in Sydenham (0.66 km) is another nearby Indian restaurant offering delivery and pickup [citation:Delahey 3037 Food Delivery](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/delahey-3037).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: **Emu Education** offers private tutoring in Delahey for NAPLAN, VCE, and primary subjects, with personalised support [citation:Private Tutoring Delahey](https://www.emueducation.com.au/tutor-delahey/). **Success Tutoring** has a Delahey centre providing 24/7 academic resources and personalised learning for students [citation:Delahey – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-delahey). Both are local tutoring options for families.

8) **Commute**: The nearest train station to Delahey is **Watergardens** on the **Sunbury line**. The typical travel time from Watergardens to Flinders Street Station is approximately **30 minutes** (based on standard timetables). The Landchecker profile notes that only 3.3% of residents use the train, with most driving [citation:DELAHEY suburb profile](https://landchecker.com.au/suburb/delahey-vic-3037/).
