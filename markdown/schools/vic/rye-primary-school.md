1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Rye is a middle-income suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,334, below the Victoria median of $1,759 [citation:2021 Rye QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22220). The median age is 51, notably older than the state median of 38, suggesting a high proportion of retirees and fewer young families [citation:2021 Rye QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22220). Detailed data on occupation type (white-collar vs blue-collar) were not available in the provided search results.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Rye is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 79.3% of residents born in Australia [citation:2021 Rye QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22220). The most common overseas country of birth is England at 4.3%, followed by New Zealand at 1.0% [citation:2021 Rye QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22220).

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is not listed on the Rye Primary School website based on the provided search results. No provider or specific hours were found in the available sources for this school.

4) **School culture:** Rye Primary School offers several named extracurriculars, including a **Choir** that performs at community events [citation:Wellbeing – Rye Primary School](https://ryeps.vic.edu.au/wellbeing/) and **Inter-school Gala Days (Year 3-6)** for sports such as basketball, football, soccer, and netball [citation:Specialist - Rye Primary School](https://ryeps.vic.edu.au/curriculum/specialist/). The school also runs a **Lunchtime Sports Clubs** program [citation:Specialist - Rye Primary School](https://ryeps.vic.edu.au/curriculum/specialist/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Rye has a relaxed coastal vibe with local supermarkets including a **Woolworths** located at 1025 Nepean Highway, Rosebud (nearby) [citation:Woolworths](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/storelocator/vic-rye-3275) and a **Coles** at McCoombe Street, Rosebud [citation:Coles Supermarket](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/rye-vic-3941). For specialty groceries, **Ritchies IGA** is at 108 Ocean Beach Road, Sorrento [citation:Ritchies IGA](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/rye-vic-3941). No ethnic or specialty supermarkets (e.g., Asian, Indian) were identified in the available sources.

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly dining options in Rye include **Freaky Tiki** (cuisine not specified in sources) and **Ruby Modern Thai Restaurant** (Thai) [citation:THE 10 BEST Restaurants in Rye](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g499625-Rye_Mornington_Peninsula_Victoria.html). Another popular casual option is **Captain Of Rye** (cuisine not specified) [citation:THE 10 BEST Restaurants in Rye](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g499625-Rye_Mornington_Peninsula_Victoria.html). Takeaway is also available from **Xpress Gourmet Pizza Rye** (Italian/Pizza) [citation:Uber Eats](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/rye-melbourne-vic).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Success Tutoring Rye** provides personalised English and Math tutoring mapped to the Australian Curriculum for primary and high school students [citation:Success Tutoring](https://www.successtutoring.com/tutoring-melbourne/rye). **Top of the Class Private Home Tutoring** offers in-home tutoring in Rye that coordinates with the child's school teacher [citation:Top of the Class](https://topoftheclass.com.au/private-home-tutoring-rye.html).

8) **Commute:** Rye does not have a train station. The most practical public transport option is a combination of V/Line train on the **Frankston line** to Frankston Station, then a bus. The estimated total travel time from Rye to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street) is approximately **2 hours 45 minutes** via the **train and bus (Route 788 or 887)** from Frankston Station to Rye [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Rye-VIC-Australia). Driving time is about 1 hour 30 minutes.
