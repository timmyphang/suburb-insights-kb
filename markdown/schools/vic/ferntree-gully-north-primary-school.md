3) **Working-parent support**: Ferntree Gully North Primary School partners with TheirCare to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) [citation:TheirCare (OSHC)](https://www.ferntree.vic.edu.au/page/257/TheirCare-(OSHC)). The program runs from 7:00 am to 8:45 am in the morning and from 3:30 pm to 6:00 pm each school day [citation:TheirCare OSHC Parent Information](https://www.ferntree.vic.edu.au/articles/532). This service is available for all children at the school, offering before- and after-school care.

4) **School culture**: The school offers several named extracurriculars, including The Chime Choir, a singing group mentioned in the school newsletter [citation:Newsletter #19 25th November 2021](https://www.ferntree.vic.edu.au/enews/details/180). The Junior School Council provides student leadership opportunities and is active in community projects [citation:Newsletter #20 8th December 2022](https://www.ferntree.vic.edu.au/enews/details/241). The school also runs a whole-school Christmas Concert and a biennial school production, such as the 2026 production "The Amazing Adventures of Superstan" [citation:Newsletter #20 8th December 2022](https://www.ferntree.vic.edu.au/enews/details/241).

Flame Trees Burger Bar - Ferntree Gully serves burgers, and Indian Rogan Josh offers Indian takeaway [citation:Restaurants in Ferntree Gully,VIC With Takeaway](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/ferntree-gully/takeaway-category).
