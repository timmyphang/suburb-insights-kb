Here’s a short, family‑facing 8‑point briefing for Brighton Grammar School (Brighton, VIC). I used official ABS suburb data and school/community pages; I substituted the nearest named train station (Brighton Beach) for “Brighton station” because there is no station called simply “Brighton” on the Metro network — noted at item 8.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Brighton is an affluent Bayside suburb: 2021 median weekly household income $2,710 (well above state averages) and a high median age (48) indicating many established households [citation:2021 Brighton (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20337).  
- The area is predominantly professional/white‑collar and family‑oriented with many married couples and fewer young adults compared with Melbourne averages [citation:2021 Brighton (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20337).

2) Most common overseas country of birth
- Brighton is mainly Australian‑born; the leading overseas country of birth is England (England is the largest overseas birthplace group in Brighton’s Census tables) [citation:2021 Brighton (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20337).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC / before & after school care)
- Brighton Grammar runs on‑site before/after school care and holiday programs (Share Community Campus / Extend) for ELC and Junior School students; after‑school care is available (typical hours ~3:20–6:00pm) [citation:Before & After School Care - Brighton Grammar](https://www.brightongrammar.vic.edu.au/learnwithus/our-school/beforeafterschoolcare/).

4) School culture — 2–3 specific extracurriculars
- Music: large co‑curricular music program — over 30 ensembles/choirs/orchestras; Year 3–4 students learn a string instrument (strings program) [citation:Music Program | Brighton Grammar School](https://www.brightongrammar.vic.edu.au/learnwithus/boy-centered-learning/music-program/).  
- Sport: structured Junior School sport with APS fixtures (examples include athletics, cricket, swimming and Aussie Rules programs) [citation:BGS Sport | Brighton Grammar](https://www.brightongrammar.vic.edu.au/connect/sports/).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Brighton is a bayside, village‑style suburb with a busy Bay Street retail strip, beaches/foreshore playgrounds and family amenities — popular for seaside family outings [citation:Bay Street retail village | Bayside City Council](https://www.bayside.vic.gov.au/services/business-and-commerce/bay-street-retail-village).  
- Local grocery options include full‑line supermarkets (Coles/Woolworths/Foodland on Bay St) and premium specialty grocers (e.g., Brighton Grocer / Brighton Foodland) for deli and specialty produce [citation:Brighton Foodland](https://brightonfoodland.com.au/) [citation:Coles Brighton - Bay Street](https://www.coles.com.au/find-stores/coles/vic/brighton-7819).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 casual family‑friendly takeaway/dining options
- Captain & Co Fish Bar — popular fish & chips takeaway on Bay Street (seafood) [citation:Captain and Co Fish Bar](https://www.captainandcofishbar.com.au/).  
- Bay Street cafés and casual restaurants offer family‑friendly takeaway/dine‑in options across multiple cuisines (pizza, cafés, seafood and bistros) — the Bay Street strip has many casual choices convenient for weeknights [citation:Bay Street retail village | Bayside City Council](https://www.bayside.vic.gov.au/services/business-and-commerce/bay-street-retail-village).

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 local centres)
- Bayside Academics — local 1:1 tutoring (Prep–Year 12) in Brighton [citation:Bayside Academics - Premium Tutoring in Brighton, VIC](https://baysideacademics.com.au/).  
- Kumon Brighton — franchise tutoring centre (maths and English) in Brighton [citation:Kumon Brighton Education Centre](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-BRIGHTON-VIC).

8) Commute — train time to Melbourne CBD
- Substitution note: there is no Metro station called simply “Brighton”; the nearest named stations are Brighton Beach / Middle Brighton. Using Brighton Beach Station (Sandringham line) as the realistic reference, train travel into Flinders Street Station is typically around 20–30 minutes (commonly reported ~25 minutes) depending on service and time of day [citation:Brighton Beach station (Sandringham line) — Melbourne Metro](https://melbournemetro.org/sandringham-line/brighton-beach/) [citation:Brighton Beach to Flinders Street - Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Brighton-Beach-Station/Flinders-Street-Station).
