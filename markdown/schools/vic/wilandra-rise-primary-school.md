1) Suburb socio‑economic profile
- Clyde North is a fast‑growing, predominantly residential suburb with a very young population (median age 30) and many families with children; household sizes are larger than the state average. Median weekly household income is $2,163 (well above Victoria’s $1,759), indicating middle‑to‑upper‑middle household incomes in the area [citation:2021 Clyde North QuickStats (ABS)](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20582). The City of Casey describes Clyde North as a residential growth precinct guided by a precinct structure plan [citation:City of Casey — Clyde North](https://www.casey.vic.gov.au/clyde-north).
