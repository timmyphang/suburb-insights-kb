1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Sunbury is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median age of 38 years and a median weekly household income of $2,106 (for the Sunbury SA2 area) [citation:2021 Sunbury, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/210041240). The wider Sunbury suburb has a median personal income of $854 per week and a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers, with 42.4% employed full-time [citation:Sunbury VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026)](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunbury-vic). Housing tenure is split between outright ownership (39.6%) and mortgage (60.4%), reflecting a stable family population [citation:Sunbury VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026)](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunbury-vic).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: Sunbury is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 92.4% of residents born in Australia [citation:Sunbury VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026)](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunbury-vic). The most common overseas country of birth is England, at 3.9% of the population [citation:Sunbury VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026)](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunbury-vic).

3) **Working-parent support**: Sunbury West Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by **Complete Kids OSHC** [citation:Outside School Hours Care (OSHC)](https://www.sunburywestps.vic.edu.au/page/175/Outside-School-Hours-Care-(OSHC)). The service operates before school from **6:30am to 8:45am** and after school from **3:30pm to 6:30pm** [citation:Before and After School Care - Sunbury - COMPLETE KIDS](https://www.completekids.net/before-and-after-school-care). Vacation care is also available [citation:Outside School Hours Care (OSHC)](https://www.sunburywestps.vic.edu.au/page/175/Outside-School-Hours-Care-(OSHC)).

4) **School culture**: Sunbury West Primary School offers several named extracurriculars including **Wakakirri** (a nationwide story-dance program for Years 3–6) and **Junior School Council** [citation:Specialist Programs](https://www.sunburywestps.vic.edu.au/page/216/Specialist-Programs)[citation:School Profile](https://www.sunburywestps.vic.edu.au/page/164/School-Profile). The school also runs an **instrumental program** as part of its extra-curricular offerings [citation:School Profile](https://www.sunburywestps.vic.edu.au/page/164/School-Profile).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Sunbury is a family-friendly suburb with a range of amenities including parks, shops, and the Sunbury Square Shopping Centre. For specialty groceries, **Asia Finest Grocery** at Shop 9, Sunbury Square Centre, 2–28 Evans St, offers Filipino, Indian, Chinese, Japanese, Thai, and other Asian ingredients [citation:Asia Finest Grocery - Sunbury Square Shopping Centre](https://sunburysquare.com.au/asias-finest-grocery/). An Indian grocery store, **Tales of India**, also provides online ordering and free delivery in Sunbury [citation:Indian Grocery Store Sunbury](https://talesofindia.com.au/pages/indian-grocery-store-sunbury-3429/).

6) **Weeknight dining**: Family-friendly takeaway and dining options in Sunbury include **Mas Thai Restaurant** (95A Evans St) for Thai cuisine, **John and Marios Pizza** (68-80 Evans St) for Italian and pizza, and **OBs Kebabs & Burgers** (3/90 Vineyard Rd) for kebabs and burgers [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Sunbury](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/sunbury). **Coco Seed** (54 Macedon St) offers Thai takeaway with free delivery [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Sunbury](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/sunbury).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: **Kip McGrath Sunbury** at 49a O'Shanassy Street offers English and maths tutoring for primary and secondary students, with sessions run by qualified teachers [citation:KMEC Sunbury Tuition Centre](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/sunbury). **Kumon Sunbury Education Centre** also provides individualised Maths and English programmes for children of all ages [citation:Kumon Sunbury Education Centre](https://www.facebook.com/KumonSunbury).

8) **Commute**: Sunbury is served by the **Sunbury train line** on the Metro Trains network, with services from **Sunbury Station** to **Flinders Street Station**. The typical train journey to Flinders Street takes approximately **49 minutes**, with trains running every 20–30 minutes [citation:Sunbury to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Sunbury-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station)[citation:Sunbury line Train Timetable](https://railmaps.com.au/routedetails.php?TableSelect=302).
