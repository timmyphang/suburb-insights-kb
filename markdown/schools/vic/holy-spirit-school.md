Welcome to New Farm

If you’re drawn to inner‑city living with a leafy, village feel, New Farm is the kind of suburb that makes you want to linger over a second flat white. It’s relatively affluent, with a strong white‑collar professional and managerial presence and a noticeably young, urban tilt — lots of residents in their 20s to 40s, a healthy mix of singles and families, and a higher share of people who’ve never married than you might find in outer suburbs. About two‑thirds of locals were born in Australia, and the most common overseas birthplace is England, which means if your family is newly arrived from overseas you’ll find plenty of neighbours who understand the practicalities of adjusting to a new country; that shared international experience can be a real comfort as you settle in.

Inside the Classroom at Holy Spirit School

Holy Spirit School welcomes children from Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland). Classrooms here feel busy in the best way — there’s a broad co‑curricular program designed to let kids find their thing. Instrumental music runs alongside private and group lessons, and creative options include choir, dance and a whole‑school musical. For kids who love tinkering and tech, there’s robotics and coding. Sports are well supported with netball, soccer, swimming and athletics, while quieter clubs such as chess and gardening give students alternative ways to connect. The culture reads as active and inclusive; families who want a school day that mixes academic routine with plenty of extracurricular flavour will likely feel at home.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families juggling the 9-to-5, the school’s on‑site Outside School Hours Care — run by Care For Kyds and Catholic Early EdCare — is a practical game changer. Before‑school care starts very early, from around 6:30am, and after‑school programs run until about 6pm, with vacation‑care options during the school holidays. That kind of wraparound care makes it realistic to manage a workday without constant timetable stress, and it’s reassuring to know the school has formal arrangements in place rather than leaving you to cobble something together.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Eating in New Farm is casual and reliably good for weeknight family life. For groceries, Merthyr Village handles the everyday shop with a Coles and easy convenience. If you’re after specialty items or to treat the kids to something a bit different, Sourced Grocer and the Powerhouse Farmers Market are nearby for fresh, artisanal produce and deli finds. When you don’t feel like cooking there are plenty of family‑friendly takeaways clustered around the school and park: classic fish and chips at New Farm Seafood, familiar Italian at Johnny Gio’s, and Roman‑style pizza at 075 Pinsa Romana. It’s the sort of neighbourhood where you can pop out for dinner with the stroller and be back before the little ones lose patience.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you’re thinking ahead about academic enrichment, New Farm has local tutoring options that include in‑home and centre tutors as well as national programs like Kumon — useful if you want maths or English extension or a little help preparing for national assessments. On the commute front, New Farm doesn’t have a suburban train station, but the river ferry — the CityCat/CityFerry from New Farm Park or Sydney Street — is a popular and scenic way into the Brisbane CBD. Typical travel time on the ferry is around 15 to 20 minutes; do a couple of door‑to‑door trials at peak and off‑peak times to build realistic school‑run plans.

Your Next Steps

First, book a school tour or open day to see classrooms, ask about fees and class sizes, and review the co‑curricular program so you can picture where your child will fit. Second, ring the on‑site Outside School Hours Care provider to confirm registration, hours and capacity for before/after school and vacation care — that clarity will make workdays manageable. Third, trial the ferry commute and wander local shopping spots like Merthyr Village and the Powerhouse Farmers Market so you know how long the school run and the weekly grocery shop will really take.
