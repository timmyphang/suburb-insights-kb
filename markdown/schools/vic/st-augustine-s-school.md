1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Frankston South is an affluent, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,038, well above the wider Frankston LGA figure of $1,653. The median age is 44, reflecting a mix of established families and older residents, with 5,253 families and an average of 1.8 children per family [citation: 2021 Frankston South, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20949). Owner-occupancy is predominant, and the suburb ranks relatively high on socio-economic advantage compared to neighbouring areas [citation: FRANKSTON CITY HOUSING STRATEGY BACKGROUND ANALYSIS](https://hdp-au-prod-app-frank-engage-files.s3.ap-southeast-2.amazonaws.com/1116/8957/5455/Frankston_City_Housing_Strategy_-_Background_Analysis.pdf).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The largest overseas-born group in Frankston South is from the United Kingdom, at 9.0% of the population, compared to 6.5% across the Frankston City LGA [citation: Birthplace | Frankston City | Community profile](https://profile.id.com.au/frankston/birthplace?WebID=140). The suburb is predominantly Australian-born, though an exact Australian-born percentage is not provided in the available sources.

3) **Working-parent support**: St Augustine’s School operates on-site Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by MACSEYE. Before School Care runs from 6:30–8:45 am, After School Care from 3:15–6:00 pm, and Vacation Care from 6:30 am–6:00 pm [citation: Out of School Hours Care](https://www.safranksth.catholic.edu.au/page/170/Out-of-School-Hours-Care).

4) **School culture**: Extracurriculars at St Augustine’s include a Kitchen/Garden program for all students, a Biennial School Musical Production, and the Mini Vinnies social justice initiative. The school also offers Interschool sports for seniors, a Camping Program for Years 3–6, and additional instrumental tuition through Kool Kidz Rock (keyboard, guitar, singing) [citation: School Profile](https://www.safranksth.catholic.edu.au/page/212/School-Profile).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Frankston South has a quiet, family-friendly feel with bushland reserves and local shops. For groceries, families can use Friendly Grocer Frankston South for everyday essentials, while Asian ingredients are available at Haodi Asian Supermarket and Hometown Asian Supermarket in nearby Frankston [citation: TOP 10 BEST Asian Supermarket near Frankston South Victoria 3199, Australia - Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/search?find_desc=Asian+Supermarket&find_loc=Frankston+South+Victoria+3199) [citation: Friendly Grocer Frankston South](https://friendlygrocer.com.au/friendly-grocer-frankston-south).

6) **Weeknight dining**: Family-friendly takeaway options include Ginseng (Chinese, Asian) on Nepean Highway, Hooked on Thai (Thai) also in Frankston, and Iron River Bar & Grill (American-style steaks, burgers, and parmas) on Cranbourne Road [citation: THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Frankston - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g552173-zfp10601-Frankston_Mornington_Peninsula_Victoria.html). These venues offer both dine-in and takeaway.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Aspire Tutoring offers in-person sessions at a dedicated tuition room in Frankston South, as well as online options [citation: Aspire Tutoring](https://www.aspiretutoring.com.au/). Kip McGrath also operates a Frankston tuition centre (KMEC) providing English and Maths support for primary and secondary students [citation: KMEC Frankston Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/frankston).

8) **Commute**: Frankston South is served by Frankston Station on the Frankston line. Trains run every 15 minutes to Flinders Street Station, with a typical travel time of 65 minutes (1 hour 5 minutes) [citation: Train Flinders Street to Frankston | Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Flinders-Street/Frankston). The journey is direct with no transfers required.
