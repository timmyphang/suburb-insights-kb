1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Newtown (Vic.) is a middle-to-upper-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,092 and a median age of 40 [citation:2021 Newtown (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/203021046). The workforce is predominantly white-collar, and the suburb has a higher proportion of families with children compared to the state average, with 0.7 children per household.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The majority of residents were born in Australia; 80.4% of people in Newtown (Vic.) are Australian-born, based on the most recent available data [citation:2021 Newtown (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/203021046). The most common overseas country of birth is England, but the precise percentage share for this country was not found in available sources.

3) **Working-parent support:** Newtown Primary School's Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is operated by **Kelly Club** [citation:Newtown Primary School | Out of Hours School Care](http://newtownps.vic.edu.au/out-of-school-hours-care/). The service runs **Before School Care from 7:00am to 8:50am** (including breakfast) and **After School Care from 3:30pm to 6:00pm** (including afternoon tea), as well as Vacation Care and Pupil Free Day programs from 7:00am to 6:00pm [citation:Newtown Primary School | Out of Hours School Care](http://newtownps.vic.edu.au/out-of-school-hours-care/).

4) **School culture:** Newtown Primary School offers a **Visual Arts program** in a dedicated Art room with an annual **Art Show** [citation:Specialist Programs - Newtown Primary School](https://newtownps.vic.edu.au/specialist-programs/). Students also participate in **Indonesian language and culture**, including a whole-school **Indonesian Day** event, and senior students can compete in inter-school sports like **basketball, cricket, football, and volleyball** through the **Lightning Premiership** [citation:Specialist Programs - Newtown Primary School](https://newtownps.vic.edu.au/specialist-programs/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Newtown has a strong community vibe with active parent participation in school events, concerts, and the School Council [citation:Newtown Primary School | School Profile](https://newtownps.vic.edu.au/profile/). Local supermarket options include a **Woolworths** at Marrickville Shopping Centre and **QE Foodstores Newtown** [citation:Supermarkets Grocery Stores in Newtown, NSW 2042](https://www.whereis.com/nsw/newtown-2042/category-supermarkets---grocery-stores); however, specific ethnic supermarkets in Newtown (Vic.) were not found in available sources.

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family dining, **It’s Time for Thai** at 233 King St offers authentic Thai cuisine with vegetarian/vegan options and takeaway/delivery [citation:It's Time for Thai Newtown](https://timeforthai.com.au/thai-newtown.html). **Thai Pothong** is a well-rated Thai restaurant in Newtown offering takeaway [citation:10 Best Delivery Restaurants in Newtown (Sydney)](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g255060-zfn7318723-zfp19-Sydney_New_South_Wales.html). For Italian, **Divino Osteria** serves pizza and pasta, and **Alba Italian restaurant** is also available for takeaway [citation:10 Best Delivery Restaurants in Newtown (Sydney)](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g255060-zfn7318723-zfp19-Sydney_New_South_Wales.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** In Newtown (Vic.), **Bravehearts** (36 Saffron St) and **Crackerjack Kids** (8 Holyrood Ave) both offer tutoring services for primary-aged students [citation:10 BEST Tutoring in Newtown VIC 3220](https://www.localsearch.com.au/find/tutoring/newtown-vic-3220). In Newtown (NSW), **Global Education Academy** provides tutoring for Kindergarten to High School students, covering the Australian Curriculum [citation:Newtown - Global Education Academy](https://globaleducationacademy.com.au/Suburb/newtown/).

8) **Commute:** Newtown (Vic.) does not have a train station; the closest major station is **Geelong Station**, serviced by **V/Line** trains on the **Geelong line**. The typical travel time from Geelong to Melbourne’s **Flinders Street Station** is approximately **60–75 minutes** by train, depending on the service [citation:V/Line - Regional public transport for Victoria](https://www.vline.com.au/Timetables/Train-coach-timetables).
