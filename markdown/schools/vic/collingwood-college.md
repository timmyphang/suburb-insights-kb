1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Inner‑city, relatively young and higher‑income suburb (median age 33). Median household and personal incomes are well above state averages (household ≈ $2,130/week; personal ≈ $1,338/week in 2021) — a mix of professionals, creatives and young couples rather than traditional blue‑collar suburbs [citation:2021 Collingwood QuickStats (ABS)](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20616) [citation:Collingwood, VIC - Suburb Profile (inthesuburbs)](https://www.inthesuburbs.com.au/suburb/collingwood-vic-3066.html).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- 58.4% of residents were born in Australia (2021); the next most common overseas birthplace is England (about 4.3%) — there is no single large non‑Australian community that dominates the suburb [citation:Collingwood, Victoria - Wikipedia (citing 2021 data)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collingwood%2C_Victoria) [citation:2021 Collingwood QuickStats (ABS)](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20616).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- Collingwood College runs before‑school, after‑school and vacation care in partnership with TheirCare (hours typically ~7:00–8:50am and 3:15–6:15pm on school days; holiday program ~7:00–6:15pm) — families may be eligible for Child Care Subsidy [citation:Before School, After School and Vacation Care | Collingwood College](https://www.collingwood.vic.edu.au/teaching-learning/primary/before-after-school-care/) [citation:TheirCare — Collingwood College listing](https://theircare.com.au/holiday-programs/collingwood-college-theircare/).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Collingwood is a dense, inner‑city, culturally lively precinct (Smith Street / Johnston Street) with cafes, shops and good public transport; more of an urban, mixed family‑and‑single vibe than a quiet suburban streetscape [citation:Collingwood, VIC - Suburb Profile (inthesuburbs)](https://www.inthesuburbs.com.au/suburb/collingwood-vic-3066.html).  
- Grocery options include a Coles supermarket on Smith Street (everyday groceries) and independent specialty grocers such as Sonsa Markets (Mediterranean/Butcher/Bakery) on Smith Street; nearby Fitzroy specialist stores (e.g., bulk/health foods) also service Collingwood residents [citation:Coles Collingwood (store listing)](https://www.coles.com.au/find-stores/coles/vic/collingwood-7842) [citation:Sonsa Markets (Smith St)](https://sonsamarkets.com.au/) [citation:The Source Bulk Foods (Fitzroy, serves Collingwood)](https://fitzroy.thesourcebulkfoods.com.au/).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 casual family‑friendly takeaway/dining options
