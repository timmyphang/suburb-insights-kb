1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Malvern East is an affluent, predominantly white-collar suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,386 [citation:2021 Malvern East, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/208041195). The median age is 38, and 48.8% of the population is male and 51.2% female, indicating a balanced mix of families and singles [citation:2021 Malvern East, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/208041195). The suburb has a population of 21,923 and an average of 0.7 children per household [citation:2021 Malvern East, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/208041195).
