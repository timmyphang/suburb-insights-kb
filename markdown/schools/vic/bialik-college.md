1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Hawthorn is an inner‑Melbourne, generally well‑off suburb with a relatively young median age (34) and high median household income (about $2,145/week), and a strong white‑collar/professional workforce — many renters and a high share of professionals vs trades/labour. Hawthorn has a lively Auburn/Glenferrie shopping strip, parks and family amenities while remaining inner‑city and walkable to public transport.
