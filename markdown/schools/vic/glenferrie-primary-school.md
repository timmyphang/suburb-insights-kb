1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Hawthorn is an affluent, white-collar suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,145, well above the national median [citation:2021 Hawthorn (Vic.) Census QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21152). The median age is 34, reflecting a mix of young professionals and families, though singles are more common (average household size is 2.1). The suburb has a high proportion of professionals and managers, with few blue-collar workers.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: Hawthorn is predominantly Australian-born, with 65.8% of residents born in Australia [citation:2021 Hawthorn (Vic.) Census QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21152). Among overseas-born residents, the most common country of birth is China (excludes SARs and Taiwan) at 5.4%, followed by India at 3.2% [citation:2021 Hawthorn (Vic.) Census QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21152).

3) **Working-parent support**: Glenferrie Primary School’s Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided by TeamKids (formerly OSHClub) [citation:TeamKids - Glenferrie Primary](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/n82wm/glenferrie-ps-oshclub-hawthorn-3122). The program offers Before School Care, After School Care, and Vacation Care, though specific operating hours were not listed in the available sources [citation:TeamKids - Glenferrie Primary](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/n82wm/glenferrie-ps-oshclub-hawthorn-3122).

4) **School culture**: Glenferrie Primary School offers a weekly one-hour Performing Arts lesson for all students in Semester 2, including plays, singing, and instruments [citation:Music and Performing Arts - Glenferrie Primary School](https://glenferrieps.vic.edu.au/curriculum/music/). The school also has a Lunchtime Italian Club [citation:Languages - Glenferrie Primary School](https://glenferrieps.vic.edu.au/curriculum/languages/). Additionally, Years 3–6 students can attend school camps aligned with the Victorian curriculum [citation:Camp - Glenferrie Primary School](https://glenferrieps.vic.edu.au/programs/camp/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Hawthorn has a vibrant, family-friendly atmosphere with parks, cafes, and boutique shopping along Glenferrie Road and Burwood Road. For specialty groceries, there is an Asian Grocery Supermarket on Glenferrie Road near Mary Street [citation:Asian Grocery Supermarket at Swinburne Hawthorn](https://studentvip.com.au/swinburne/hawthorn/maps/126740), and Demaria’s Grocer offers Italian groceries and artisan cheese [citation:Demaria's Grocer Hawthorn](https://www.demariasgrocer.com.au/areas-we-serve/hawthorn). McCoppins gourmet supermarket in nearby Hawthorn East provides fresh bread and an extensive deli [citation:McCoppins Hawthorn East](https://mccoppins.com.au/stores/hawthorn-east/).

6) **Weeknight dining**: For casual family-friendly dining, Santoni Pizza Bar on Glenferrie Road serves Italian pizza and pasta [citation:Santoni Pizza Bar on Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g844564-zfp10601-Hawthorn_Boroondara_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). Tao Tao House offers Chinese yum cha and other dishes [citation:Tao Tao House on Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g844564-zfp10601-Hawthorn_Boroondara_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). Lulo provides Indian fusion cuisine, also suitable for takeaways [citation:Lulo on Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g844564-zfp10601-Hawthorn_Boroondara_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Success Tutoring in Hawthorn offers in-person and online tutoring for K-12 students in subjects including Math and English, with tailored learning plans [citation:Success Tutoring Hawthorn](https://successtutoring.com.au/hawthorn-tutoring). Alchemy Tuition provides in-home and online tutoring in Hawthorn for primary through VCE students [citation:Alchemy Tuition Hawthorn](https://alchemytuition.com.au/melbourne-tutoring-hawthorn/).

8) **Commute**: Hawthorn is served by the Belgrave, Lilydale, and Glen Waverley train lines via Hawthorn Station [citation:Hawthorn Station Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/hawthorn/). The train to Flinders Street Station takes approximately 10 minutes and runs every 15 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio Hawthorn to Flinders Street](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Hawthorn-Station/Flinders-Street-Station).
