Briefing: Methodist Ladies’ College (Junior School), Kew — family facts (concise)

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Kew is an affluent, predominantly white‑collar suburb with high socio‑economic advantage (top decile on SEIFA/IRSAD). Median age ~41 and a population ~24,500 (2021) — a family‑oriented, established inner‑east suburb within Boroondara [citation:2021 Kew (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21336) [citation:Kew, VIC 3101 - Suburb Profile | DA Leads Australia](https://daleads.com.au/suburbs/kew-vic-3101/).

2) Most common overseas country of birth  
About 66.1% of Kew residents were Australian‑born (2021); the largest overseas birthplace is China (~6.6%) with England, Malaysia and India following [citation:2021 3101, VIC, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/POA3101).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
MLC Junior School offers Before, After and Vacation Care through a partnered provider (TeamKids); Before School from 7:00 am and After School care to ~6:30 pm on campus [citation:Before and After School Care | Methodist Ladies' College Melbourne](https://www.mlc.vic.edu.au/about-us/junior-school/before-and-after-school-care/).

4) School culture — key extracurriculars (2–3 specific examples)  
Instrumental music program with multiple junior ensembles (Year 2 Strings, Year 5 Band, junior choirs); extensive sport program (member of Girls’ Sport Victoria with >18 Junior School sports); wide performing‑arts and student clubs offering regular co‑curricular participation [citation:Music | MLC](https://www.mlc.vic.edu.au/the-mlc-difference/co-curricular/music/) [citation:Junior School - Methodist Ladies' College](https://www.mlc.vic.edu.au/about-us/junior-school/).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (2 sentences)  
Kew is an established inner‑east neighbourhood with leafy streets, family parks and a strong High St/Church St shopping strip—convenient for families. Major supermarkets (Coles, Woolworths, IGA) serve the suburb and there are local specialty/ethnic grocers on High Street (e.g., Q Asian Store) [citation:Coles Kew | Store Directions & Opening Hours](https://www.coles.com.au/find-stores/coles/vic/kew-7766) [citation:Q Asian Store in Kew, VIC 3101 - Cybo](https://www.cybo.com/AU-biz/q-asian-store).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 casual family options (ethnic highlights)  
Spice on High (Indian, High St) — popular for takeaway/delivery; plus a mix of family‑friendly cafes and takeaway outlets along High Street (pizza, sushi, modern Aussie)—local roundups list Kew’s takeaway scene and kid‑friendly restaurants [citation:Spice on High Indian Restaurant Kew](https://www.spiceonhigh.com.au/) [citation:Best Takeaway in Kew — 2026 Guide | MELBZ](https://www.melbz.com.au/kew/best-takeaway/).

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 centres)  
Maths-focused tutoring available nearby (Math Minds — Kew area) and private/agency tutors and small centres servicing Kew (Alchemy Tuition/Cluey provide in‑home/online programs) [citation:Math Minds Learning Centre — Maths Tutoring Kew](https://mathminds.com.au/maths-tutoring-kew/) [citation:Alchemy Tuition - Tutoring Kew](https://alchemytuition.com.au/melbourne-tutoring-kew/).

8) Commute — train/tram estimate to CBD (substitution note)  
Kew does not have an active heavy‑rail station (the historical Kew railway station closed mid‑20th century); realistic public‑transport option is tram (route 109) or buses. Tram 109 from Kew/High St into the CBD typically takes roughly 30–35 minutes to Flinders Street depending on traffic/time, per route timetables and journey planners [citation:Kew railway station, Melbourne - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kew_railway_station%2C_Melbourne) [citation:109 Box Hill - Port Melbourne - Transport Victoria](https://transport.vic.gov.au/route/722/109-box-hill-port-melbourne) [citation:Kew to Flinders Street Station - Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Kew-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
