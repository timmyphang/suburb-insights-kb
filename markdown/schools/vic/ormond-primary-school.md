1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Ormond is an affluent, white-collar suburb with a strong family presence. The median weekly household income is $2,062, well above the Victorian median, and the median age is 37 [citation:2021 Ormond QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22016). Families with children are common, with an average of 1.8 children per family and 0.7 children per household [citation:2021 Ormond QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22016). The suburb has a mix of families and singles, given the significant proportion of residents aged 25–34 (15.9%) alongside strong cohorts aged 0–14 (16.8%) [citation:2021 Ormond QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22016).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** In Ormond, 72.0% of residents were born in Australia [citation:2021 Ormond QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22016). Among overseas-born residents, the most common country of birth is China (excluding SARs and Taiwan), at 3.5% of the total population [citation:2021 Ormond QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22016).

3) **Working-parent support:** Ormond Primary School uses TheirCare for its Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) program [citation:Parents | Ormond Primary School](https://ormondps.vic.edu.au/parents/). The service is located at 121 Wheatley Road, Ormond, and standard operating hours are from 3:30 pm to 6:00 pm on weekdays for after-school care [citation:Ormond Primary School Out of School Hours Care](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3204/ormond/ormond-primary-school-out-of-school-hours-care). Specific before-school care hours were not found in available sources, but the centre has a capacity of 80 children [citation:Ormond Primary School Out of School Hours Care](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3204/ormond/ormond-primary-school-out-of-school-hours-care).

4) **School culture:** Ormond Primary School offers a lively Performing Arts program that includes singing, instrumental music, dance, drama, and performance for Foundation to Year 6 [citation:Learning | Ormond Primary School](https://ormondps.vic.edu.au/learning/). The Student Representative Council (SRC) organises a Chess Club, a Craft Club, and zumba dance sessions for students [citation:Students | Ormond Primary School](https://ormondps.vic.edu.au/students/). The annual report also mentions extracurricular clubs such as chess, lego, and stop motion, as well as after-school music lessons [citation:2024 Annual Report](https://ormondps.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/OPS_Annual-Report_2024.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Ormond has a local, family-friendly vibe with convenient grocery options. An IGA supermarket is located at 509 North Road, Ormond [citation:Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/ormond-vic-3204). For specialty/ethnic groceries, the area offers Heng Sheng Asian Grocery at 1126 Glen Huntly Road in nearby Glen Huntly [citation:Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/ormond-vic-3204), and in nearby Bentleigh East, Mackie Road Supermarket provides a local alternative at 75 Mackie Road [citation:Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/ormond-vic-3204).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Steak Bank Charcoal Grill at Ormond offers Australian steakhouse cuisine and is noted as a family favourite for takeaways [citation:TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g1931045-Ormond_Glen_Eira_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). Remezzo serves Italian and pizza dining, with good value serves that appeal to families [citation:TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g1931045-Ormond_Glen_Eira_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). Bombay Club is a popular Indian restaurant, also well-suited for family dining and takeaway [citation:TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g1931045-Ormond_Glen_Eira_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Austin Education has a dedicated Ormond campus offering tutoring for Year 1 to VCE in subjects including Maths, English, Chemistry, and Biology [citation:Austin Education](https://www.austineducation.com.au/en/campuses/ormond). They provide specialist classes such as AMC Competition Class, Scholarship Class, and Selective School Class for primary and secondary students [citation:Austin Education](https://www.austineducation.com.au/en/campuses/ormond).

8) **Commute:** Ormond Station is on the Frankston railway line, providing direct trains to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD [citation:Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/ormond/). The typical train travel time from Ormond Station to Flinders Street Station is approximately 23 minutes, with services running every 15 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Ormond).
