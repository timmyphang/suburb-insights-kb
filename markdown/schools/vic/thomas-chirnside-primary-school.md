1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Werribee is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,645 and a median age of 35, according to the 2021 Census [citation:2021 Werribee QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22750). The suburb has a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers, with a high proportion of families—13,202 families recorded in 2021—and a larger-than-average household size of 2.7 people [citation:2021 Werribee QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22750).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The most common overseas country of birth is not explicitly stated in the available sources. The 2016 Census showed that 66.6% of Werribee residents spoke only English at home, and the most common non-English languages spoken were Karen (3.4%), Italian (2.9%), and Punjabi (1.9%) [citation:2016 Werribee QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22736). The 2021 Census data for country of birth is not provided in the search results.

3) **Working-parent support**: Thomas Chirnside Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) including Before School Care, After School Care, and Vacation Care [citation:CareforKids](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/n1o0z/thomas-chirnside-primary-school-outside-school-hours-care-werribee-3030). The After School Care operates from 3:00 pm to 6:30 pm on weekdays, but specific hours for Before School Care are not listed in the available sources [citation:KindiCare](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3030/werribee/thomas-chirnside-primary-school-outside-school-hours-care). The Vacation Care program is open to all Wyndham residents, with priority given to Thomas Chirnside students [citation:Wyndham City](https://www.wyndham.vic.gov.au/directory/thomas-chirnside-primary-school).

4) **School culture**: Thomas Chirnside Primary School offers a West African Drumming Band, a Choir, and a Stephanie Alexander Kitchen Garden program as named extracurricular activities [citation:Principal’s Welcome](http://thomaschirnsideps.vic.edu.au/principals-welcome/) [citation:The Arts](http://thomaschirnsideps.vic.edu.au/the-arts/) [citation:Stephanie Alexander Kitchen Garden](http://thomaschirnsideps.vic.edu.au/stephanie-alexander-kitchen/). The school also runs a comprehensive Physical Education program including Athletics, Swimming, and Cross Country events, as well as interschool sporting competitions [citation:Physical Education & Sport](http://thomaschirnsideps.vic.edu.au/physical-education-health/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Werribee has a strong family-friendly atmosphere with major shopping at Pacific Werribee, which houses a Pacific Asian Supermarket [citation:Pacific Asian Supermarket](https://pacificwerribee.qicre.com/stores/retail-stores/pacific-asian-supermarket/). Specialty grocers include Mabuhay Binoy Asian Grocery at 51 Synnot Street for Filipino products [citation:Mabuhay Binoy Asian Grocery](https://www.whitepages.com.au/mabuhay-binoy-asian-grocery-11719862/werribee-vic-11719861B), and Indian grocery stores such as Patel Brothers and Uttam Indian Supermarket on Watton Street [citation:Indian Grocery Stores Werribee](https://singh.com.au/indian-grocery-stores-werribee-vic/).

6) **Weeknight dining**: For casual family-friendly dining, Thaigerlily offers authentic Thai cuisine with takeaway and dine-in options at 48 Watton Street [citation:Thaigerlily](https://www.thaigerlily.com.au/). Tocca is an Italian restaurant on Watton Street specialising in pizza and family meals [citation:Italian Restaurant Werribee](https://tocca.melbourne/). Ages Pizza on Watton Street is another popular takeaway option for pizza and other Italian dishes [citation:AGFG Werribee Restaurants](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/werribee/takeaway-category).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Success Tutoring has a centre at 48C Watton Street, Werribee, offering personalised tutoring in English, Maths, and other subjects aligned with the VIC curriculum [citation:Success Tutoring Werribee](https://successtutoring.com.au/werribee-tutoring). Kip McGrath Werribee provides specialised English and Maths tutoring for primary and secondary students, with a free assessment available [citation:KMEC Werribee Tuition Centre](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/werribee).

8) **Commute**: Werribee is served by the Werribee train line, with Werribee Station as the main stop. Trains run every 20 minutes to Flinders Street Station, with a typical travel time of 39 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Werribee/Flinders-Street-Station). The direct service operates daily, and a single ticket costs approximately $4 [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Werribee/Flinders-Street-Station).
