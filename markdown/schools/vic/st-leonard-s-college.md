Below is a short, family‑facing briefing on St Leonard’s College (Junior/Primary campus — Brighton East). I used ABS QuickStats and local sources; each fact has a source cited.  

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)  
Brighton East is an affluent, predominantly white‑collar suburb with an older median age (mid‑40s) and high household incomes (median weekly household income well above the Victorian average). Large share of married households and smaller average household size than some Melbourne suburbs [citation:2021 Brighton East QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20338).  

2) Most common foreign country of birth  
The area is majority Australian‑born (around two‑thirds); the ABS QuickStats shows Australia as the dominant birthplace for residents (no single overseas country dominates the suburb) [citation:2021 Brighton East QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20338).  

3) Working‑parent support — OSHC availability at this school  
St Leonard’s College (Brighton) offers Out‑of‑School‑Hours Care / before‑ and after‑school programs; OSHC at the Brighton campus is provided through TeamKids (and ELC OSHC is run by ELC staff) [citation:Out of School Hours Care - St Leonard's College](https://www.stleonards.vic.edu.au/entry/out-of-school-hours-care/) [citation:St Leonard’s College – Brighton East - TeamKids](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/st-leonards-college-brighton-east-oshc-5/).  

4) School culture — 2–3 specific extracurriculars offered  
- Private/instrumental music lessons (on‑campus instrumental and vocal tuition) [citation:Private Music Lessons - St Leonard's College](https://www.stleonards.vic.edu.au/education/cocurricular-opportunities/music/private-music-lessons/).  
- “Lennie’s Sports” after‑school club sport for Junior School students [citation:Lennie's Sports - St Leonard's College](https://www.stleonards.vic.edu.au/education/cocurricular-opportunities/club-sport/lennies-sports/).  
- Interschool sport program (ACS junior sport / gala days for Years 5–6) [citation:Interschool Sport - St Leonard's College](https://www.stleonards.vic.edu.au/education/cocurricular-opportunities/interschool-sport/).  

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)  
Brighton East is a bayside, family‑oriented suburb with easy access to beaches, parks (e.g. Dendy Street Beach / Dendy Park) and playgrounds — quiet residential streets popular with families. Local grocery options include an IGA Local Grocer in Brighton East and larger supermarkets in nearby Middle/North Brighton (Woolworths/Coles serving the area) [citation:Bayside City Council — Brighton East](https://www.bayside.vic.gov.au/term/location/brighton-east) [citation:IGA Local Grocer Brighton East](https://www.iga.com.au/stores/iga-local-grocer-brighton-east/).  

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 casual, family‑friendly takeaways / cuisines

- Beach House Brighton / Dendy Street beachfront café — casual seaside takeaway/dining [citation:Beach House Brighton — About](https://www.beachhousebrighton.com.au/about).

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 centres near Brighton East)

8) Commute — train travel time to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)  
Brighton East does not have its own named train station — the nearest Sandringham‑line stations (e.g. Brighton Beach / Middle Brighton) serve the area. From Brighton Beach station the direct train to Flinders Street typically takes about 20–30 minutes (commonly ~25 minutes) depending on service; this is a straightforward city commute on the Sandringham line [citation:Brighton Beach | Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/brighton-beach/) [citation:Train Brighton Beach to Flinders Street (Rome2rio)](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Brighton-Beach-Station/Flinders-Street-Station).
