1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** St Helena is a relatively affluent, middle-to-upper-income suburb with a strong family orientation. The median weekly household income is $2,256, well above the Victorian median, and the median age is 46, reflecting a mature population [citation:2021 St Helena, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22339). The suburb is primarily residential with a mix of white-collar professionals and families; 52.9% of residents are female and 47.1% male, with 812 families recorded in 2021 [citation:2021 St Helena, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22339).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The suburb is overwhelmingly Australian-born. In the 2021 Census, 80.5% of residents were born in Australia [citation:2021 St Helena, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22339). The most common overseas country of birth is not explicitly stated in the available sources, but the data shows a very low proportion of overseas-born residents overall.

3) **Working-parent support:** North-Eastern Montessori School operates its own Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) program, managed directly by the school. The service runs from 7:30am to 6:30pm, covering both Before School Care and After School Care [citation:Outside School Hours Care | North Eastern Montessori School](https://northeastern.vic.edu.au/community/oshc/). Permanent bookings for a full term are available at a discounted rate, and casual bookings can be made by contacting oshc@northeastern.vic.edu.au [citation:Outside School Hours Care | North Eastern Montessori School](https://northeastern.vic.edu.au/community/oshc/).

4) **School culture:** North-Eastern Montessori School offers several named extracurricular activities that build community and life skills. These include a **Year 6 Graduation Event planning group** where students organise fundraising activities like an **Online Auction** of student artwork, a **Stall of hand-painted planter pots**, and a **“Guess the number of snakes” jar game** [citation:Year 6 Graduation - a collaborative approach | North Eastern Montessori School](https://northeastern.vic.edu.au/year-6-collaborative-graduation/). The school also has a **Music program** covering a variety of instruments and genres, as detailed in its Music Handbook [citation:MUSIC HANDBOOK 2023](https://northeastern.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/2023-Music-Handbook-4.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** St Helena is a leafy, quiet suburb with a family-friendly feel, centred around parks and nature reserves. For groceries, the main local option is not specifically named in the available sources, but nearby Greensborough (approx. 3 km away) offers a Coles and Woolworths supermarket. No specialty or ethnic supermarkets are specifically named in St Helena itself in the provided search results.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Several tutoring services are available in St Helena, though specific named centres are not listed in the available sources. **Ezy Math Tutoring** and **Ezy English Tutoring** offer in-home tutoring across all subjects for primary through VCE students in St Helena [citation:70 English Tutors in St Helena | Ezy English Tutoring](https://www.ezymathtutoring.com.au/tutors/st-helena-3088/learn-english). 8) **Commute:** St Helena does not have its own train station; the closest station is **Greensborough Station** on the **Hurstbridge line**. The typical train travel time from Greensborough Station to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD is approximately **40–50 minutes** [citation:Timetable Search | Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/timetables/). Buses (e.g., Route 516) connect St Helena to Greensborough Station, adding around 10–15 minutes to the total commute.
