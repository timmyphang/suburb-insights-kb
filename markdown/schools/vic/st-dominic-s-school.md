1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Melton (the suburb) is a working-class, family-oriented area with a median age of 40 and a median weekly household income of $1,058, well below the Victorian median of $1,759 [citation:2021 Melton (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21642). The suburb has a high proportion of families—1,992 families recorded in 2021—and an average of 1.8 children per family, reflecting a strong family presence [citation:2021 Melton (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21642). Occupations are not specified in available sources, but the low income and high child density suggest a predominantly blue‑collar and lower‑middle‑income demographic.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The specific percentage share of the dominant overseas country of birth for the suburb of Melton is not provided in the available sources. Across the wider City of Melton LGA, 62.4% of residents were born in Australia, with the most common overseas birthplaces being India (4.1%), the Philippines (2.0%), and New Zealand (1.7%) [citation:2021 Melton, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/LGA24650). For the suburb of Melton itself, the Australian‑born proportion is not separately stated in the snippets.

3) **Working-parent support**: St Dominic’s School does not operate its own on‑site Outside School Hours Care; instead, children access the program at Melton Primary School, located directly across the road [citation:Out of School Hour Care - St Dominic's Melton](http://www.sdmelton.catholic.edu.au/school-community/out-of-school-hour-care/). The care is run by OSH Club, a national provider, but specific operating hours (e.g., before‑school start time or after‑school finish time) are not listed in the available sources [citation:Out of School Hour Care - St Dominic's Melton](http://www.sdmelton.catholic.edu.au/school-community/out-of-school-hour-care/). Registration forms can be collected from the school office.

4) **School culture**: St Dominic’s Primary School runs a **Breakfast Club** each Wednesday morning, offering free food to students before class [citation:St. Dominic's Primary School NEWSLETTER](https://www.sdmelton.catholic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/July-24.pdf). Students also participate in **inter‑school sport** activities, which are organised as part of the senior classes’ physical education program [citation:Health & Physical Education - St Dominic's Melton](http://www.sdmelton.catholic.edu.au/learning-teaching/health-physical-education/). Additionally, the school hosts a weekly **Playgroup** for pre‑school families, building community connections [citation:Playgroup - St Dominic's Melton](https://www.sdmelton.catholic.edu.au/playgroup/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Melton is a growing outer‑suburban community with a mix of shopping centres and specialty grocers catering to its multicultural population. At **Woodgrove Shopping Centre** (533‑555 High Street), families can find **Melton International Grocery**, stocking Asian and international products [citation:Woodgrove – Feel Good, Shop Local](https://woodgrove.qicre.com/Stores/Retail-Stores/Melton-International-Grocery). For Indian, Pakistani and Asian groceries, **OM Indian Grocery Shop** at 8B Station Road, Melton South, is a dedicated store, while **Lee Merchants – Asian Grocery** on High Street specialises in Vietnamese, Filipino, Korean and Samoan goods [citation:OM Indian Grocery Shop Melton South](https://singh.com.au/om-indian-grocery-shop-melton-south-melbourne/) and [citation:Lee Merchants - Asian Grocery Melton](https://www.facebook.com/leemerchants/).

6) **Weeknight dining**: Families have several casual takeaway and dining options in Melton. **Melton Seafoods Fish & Chips** (on High Street) is a well‑rated local fish‑and‑chip shop offering burgers and seafood [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Melton - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g552210-zfp10601-Melton_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). **Pad Thai Takeaway and Restaurant** serves affordable Thai cuisine, popular for its pad thai and curries [citation:Pad Thai Takeway & Restaurant - Melton](https://www.padthaimelton.com.au/). **Guneet's Indian Restaurant** is another option, offering freshly prepared Indian dishes for dine‑in or takeaway [citation:THE 10 BEST Indian Restaurants in Melton (Updated 2026)](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g552210-c24-Melton_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Two well‑known tutoring centres operate in Melton. **Kip McGrath Melton**, located at 83‑85 Unitt Street, provides English and Maths tuition for primary and secondary students, with personalised learning plans and NAPLAN preparation [citation:KMEC Melton Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/melton). **Learner’s Hub** is an award‑winning education centre that offers numeracy and literacy classes from Foundation to Year 9, as well as selective school preparation and holiday STEM workshops [citation:Learner's Hub Melton City Council](https://www.melton.vic.gov.au/Out-n-About/Local-Directory-Listing/Learners-Hub).

8) **Commute**: Melton is served by **V/Line** trains on the **Ballarat line** from **Melton Station**. To reach Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street Station), passengers typically take a V/Line train to Footscray Station (approx. 30 minutes) and then transfer to a Metro train for the remaining 10‑15 minutes, giving a total journey time of around **55 minutes** [citation:Train Melton (Station) to Flinders Street Station from $8 | Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Melton-Station-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). Alternatively, a direct V/Line service from Melton to Southern Cross Station takes 37 minutes, followed by a short walk (or one stop on the loop) to Flinders Street [citation:Flinders Street Station to Melton - 5 ways to travel via train](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Melton).
