Loreto Mandeville Hall (Toorak) — short family briefing (8 items)

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
- Toorak is an affluent inner‑Melbourne suburb with a high share of professional/white‑collar residents and an older median age (median age 47). [citation:2021 Toorak QuickStats (ABS)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22547) [citation:Toorak Village economic profile (City of Stonnington)](https://www.stonnington.vic.gov.au/Business/Economic-Data/Toorak-Village-economic-profile)

2) Most common foreign country of birth  
- Toorak is predominantly Australian‑born (the majority of residents were born in Australia in 2021). [citation:2021 Toorak QuickStats (ABS)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22547)

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
- Outside School Hours Care is available on site via an external provider (Extend): before‑school care from 7:00am and after‑school care (Prep–Yr6: ~3:10pm–6:00pm) — enrolments/bookings managed through the provider. [citation:Before and After School Care — Loreto Mandeville Hall](https://www.loretotoorak.vic.edu.au/admissions/before-and-after-school-care) [citation:Extend — Loreto Mandeville Hall listing](https://www.extend.com.au/school/loreto-mandeville-hall/)

4) School culture / extracurriculars (2–3 specific offerings)  
- Instrumental music program (weekly lessons; multiple bands, choirs and ensembles). [citation:Music — Loreto Mandeville Hall](https://www.loretotoorak.vic.edu.au/clendon-precinct-7-12/programs/music)  
- Competitive sport via Girls Sport Victoria pathways and many school sport offerings. [citation:GSV Sport — Loreto Mandeville Hall](https://www.loretotoorak.vic.edu.au/clendon-precinct-7-12/programs/gsv-sport)  
- Extra clubs and extension activities (drama, debating, lunchtime clubs / subject extension). [citation:Programs — Loreto Mandeville Hall](https://www.loretotoorak.vic.edu.au/clendon-precinct-7-12/programs)

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)  
- Toorak Village is a boutique shopping strip of cafes, restaurants and services — quiet, upscale village feel with limited large‑format retail in the immediate strip. [citation:Toorak Village](https://www.toorakvillage.com.au/)  
- Local grocery options include Coles Local Toorak Village and Woolworths (Tok H centre/Toorak Village area); larger or specialist ethnic supermarkets are generally found in nearby precincts (e.g., South Yarra). [citation:Coles Local Toorak Village](https://www.coles.com.au/find-stores/coles-local/vic/toorak-7670) [citation:Woolworths Toorak (Toorak Village directory)](https://www.toorakvillage.com.au/business-directory/woolworths-toorak)

6) Weeknight dining (2–3 casual, family‑friendly options + cuisine callouts)  
- Thai @ Toorak — casual Thai (takeaway/dine‑in). [citation:Thai @ Toorak](https://www.thaiattoorak.com.au/)  
- Romeo’s of Toorak — family‑friendly Italian / neighbourhood eatery (takeaway & dine‑in). [citation:Romeo's of Toorak](https://www.romeosoftoorak.com.au/)  
- Cucina Toorak — local Italian option with takeaway available. [citation:Cucina Toorak](https://cucinatoorak.com.au/)

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 centres nearby)  
- Success Tutoring (Toorak / inner‑east) — in‑person and online tuition for primary and high‑school subjects and holiday programs. [citation:Success Tutoring — Toorak](https://www.successtutoring.com/tutoring-toorak)  
- Apex Tuition / private tutors serving Toorak — private one‑to‑one tuition and subject support. [citation:Apex Tuition — Toorak](https://www.apextuitionaustralia.com/locations/toorak)

8) Commute (Toorak station → Flinders Street)  
- Train from Toorak station to Flinders Street Station typically about 10–12 minutes (around 11 minutes reported). If you plan regular CBD commuting, Toorak station is the local rail option. [citation:Toorak railway station — Wikipedia (journey time)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toorak_railway_station) [citation:Toorak to Flinders Street - Rome2rio (summary)](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Toorak-Station/Flinders-Street-Station)
