1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Caulfield South is an affluent, white-collar suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,289, and 38.9% of residents earn over $3,000 weekly, indicating prosperity [citation:2021 Caulfield - South QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/208021178) [citation:AreaSearch Caulfield South](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/caulfield_south). The median age is 41, and the suburb has a strong family presence, with 4,857 families and an average of 0.8 children per household [citation:2021 Caulfield - South QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/208021178).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: Specific percentages for the most common foreign country of birth in Caulfield South are not available in the provided sources. However, 2016 Census data indicated that 50.8% of people had both parents born overseas, and the most common countries of birth included those from non-English speaking backgrounds [citation:2016 Caulfield South QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20521). Families should consult the 2021 Census QuickStats for detailed country-of-birth data.

3) **Working-parent support**: Melbourne Montessori College partners with TheirCare for after-school care at the Caulfield campus [citation:TheirCare After School Care](https://parents.mmc.vic.edu.au/after-school-care/). The program runs after school until families collect their children, with afternoon tea and child-led play included. Specific hours of operation are not detailed in the provided content; families should check TheirCare’s website at TheirCare.com.au for exact times and booking information.

4) **School culture**: Melbourne Montessori College offers a range of named extracurriculars. Students can join the **Melbourne Montessori Choir** and the **Melbourne Montessori College Band Program**, which rehearse weekly and perform publicly [citation:Cycle 3](https://mmc.vic.edu.au/junior-school/cycle-3-years-4-5-6/). Additionally, a **Dungeons & Dragons club** is part of the extracurricular program, alongside other options like chess and movie clubs [citation:5-Star School](https://mmc.vic.edu.au/news-and-events/its-official-were-a-5-star-school/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Caulfield South has a family-friendly village feel with convenient local shopping. For groceries, families can visit **Friendly Grocer Caulfield South** for everyday essentials [citation:Friendly Grocer](https://friendlygrocer.com.au/friendly-grocer-caulfield-south). Specialty Asian groceries are available at **Maita by Tang** (7-9 Normanby Road, Caulfield North) and **Lin’s Asian Grocery** within Monash University’s Caulfield campus [citation:Tang Food Emporium](https://www.tangfoodemporium.com.au/) [citation:Lin's Asian Grocery](https://studentvip.com.au/monash/caulfield/maps/118230).

6) **Weeknight dining**: Casual family-friendly dining options in Caulfield South include **Sala Thai Kitchen** (614 Glen Huntly Road), offering Thai takeaway and delivery [citation:Sala Thai Kitchen](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/sala-thai-kitchen-caulfield-south). Another option is **Curry Queen** (Indian cuisine), which provides dine-in, takeaway, and delivery with a menu featuring tandoori dishes and curries [citation:Curry Queen](https://www.curryqueen.com.au/indian-restaurant-caulfield-south/). Both are within the suburb and cater to families with casual atmospheres.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Two specific tutoring centres in Caulfield South are **Kip McGrath Caulfield South** (Suite 2, Level 1, 342 Hawthorn Road), offering English and maths tuition for primary and secondary students [citation:Kip McGrath Caulfield South](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/caulfield-south). **Success Tutoring Caulfield South** also provides personalised English and maths tutoring mapped to the Australian Curriculum [citation:Success Tutoring Caulfield South](https://www.successtutoring.com/tutoring-melbourne/caulfield-south).

8) **Commute**: Caulfield South is served by **Caulfield Station** on the **Frankston, Cranbourne, and Pakenham lines**. The train journey from Caulfield Station to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD takes approximately **19 minutes** [citation:Metro Trains Caulfield](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/caulfield/). Trains run every 10 minutes during peak times, providing a reliable commute for working parents.
