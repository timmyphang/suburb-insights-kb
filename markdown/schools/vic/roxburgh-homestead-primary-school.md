The suburb has a much younger population than the Victorian median age of 38, with 23.0% of residents aged 0–14 years and a high average of 3.7 people per household [citation:2021 Roxburgh Park, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22208).
