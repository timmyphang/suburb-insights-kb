1) Narre Warren is a middle-to-lower-income suburban area with a median weekly household income of $1,741 (2021 Census), which is below the Victorian median of roughly $1,750 [citation:2021 Narre Warren, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21893). The median age is 35, and the area has a high proportion of families (7,476 families out of 27,689 people), indicating a strong family-oriented demographic [citation:2021 Narre Warren, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21893). Employment is mixed blue- and white-collar, with many residents working in retail and manufacturing sectors nearby.

2) In Narre Warren, the most common country of birth other than Australia is India. Australian-born residents make up approximately 63.5% of the population, while Indian-born residents account for about 6.5% (the largest overseas-born group) [citation:2021 Narre Warren, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21893). This reflects significant South Asian community presence in the area.

3) Outside School Hours Care at Don Bosco School is provided by Camp Australia, operating before and after school. Before-school care runs Monday–Friday from 6:30 am to 8:40 am, and after-school care runs Monday–Friday from 3:10 pm to 6:30 pm during school terms [citation:Camp Australia - Don Bosco Primary School OSHC](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/ylv-don-bosco-oshc). Vacation care is also available on weekdays from 7:00 am to 6:30 pm.

4) Don Bosco School offers a range of named extracurriculars, including a Year 5/6 inter-school sports program (competing against other local Catholic schools) and a senior student camp at Waratah Beach Camp in Waratah Bay [citation:Principal's Message](https://www.dbnarre.catholic.edu.au/newsletter/article/125?skin=1). The school also runs a Kitchen Garden program and a choir, as noted in school newsletters (though specific choir name not found in available sources). These activities are complemented by Advent prayer services and other faith-based events.

5) Narre Warren has ample family amenities including the major Westfield Fountain Gate shopping centre with Woolworths, Coles, and Aldi supermarkets. Specialty grocery options include Sari-Sari Asian Groceries at 26A Webb Street, which stocks Filipino and pan-Asian products [citation:Sari Sari Asian Groceries - Supermarket](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/vic/narre-warren/sari-sari-asian-groceries-14678447-listing.html), and AR Afghan Butcher & Supermarket offering Middle Eastern and Asian goods. Ritchies IGA is at 14 Webb Street for everyday needs.

6) Casual family-friendly dining options in Narre Warren include La Bella Pizza Beaconsfield (pizza, delivery available) and Oatlands Fish and Chips (fish and chips, takeaway) [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Narre Warren, VIC 3805](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/narre-warren-vic-3805). Berwick Palace Chinese Restaurant offers Chinese takeaway and dine-in, located nearby on the Princes Highway.

7) Two specific tutoring centres available near Don Bosco School are Edu-Kingdom (focused on Maths and English for Years 1–12) at their Narre Warren branch, and Success Tutoring Narre Warren, which offers personalised learning support and 24/7 online resources [citation:Maths Tutor Narre Warren](https://edukingdom.com.au/cm-maths-tutor-narre-warren/) [citation:Narre Warren – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-narre-warren). Both provide in-centre and online options.

8) The commute from Narre Warren to Melbourne CBD is via the Pakenham line from Narre Warren Station. The typical direct train to Flinders Street Station takes about 53 minutes, with trains running every 20 minutes on weekdays [citation:Narre Warren to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Narre-Warren/Flinders-Street-Station). Narre Warren Station is located on the Princes Highway, a short walk or drive from the school.
