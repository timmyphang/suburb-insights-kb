1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Surrey Hills is an affluent, predominantly white-collar suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,503, and the median age is 42, indicating a mature, family-oriented population [citation:2021 Surrey Hills, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22399). The suburb is characterized by a mix of families, professionals, and retirees, with many period homes and a strong sense of community [citation:Surrey Hills, VIC 3127 Suburb Profile](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/surrey-hills/).

3) **Working-parent support:** Our Holy Redeemer School partners with Camp Australia to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC). The program operates Monday to Friday, with morning care from 7:00am to 8:30am and afternoon care until 6:00pm [citation:Camp Australia Our Holy Redeemer School OSHC](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3127/surrey-hills/camp-australia-our-holy-redeemer-school-oshc). 4) **School culture:** Our Holy Redeemer School offers a chess club and musical instrument lessons as named extracurricular activities [citation:Testimonials | Our Holy Redeemer Primary School](https://www.ohrsurreyhills.catholic.edu.au/testimonials/).
