1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Sunshine North is a working-class, culturally diverse suburb. According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income was $1,399 [citation:2021 Sunshine North QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22396), which is below the Victorian median. Blue-collar and manufacturing employment is common, and the median age is 37 years, with a mix of families and singles [citation:2021 Sunshine North QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22396).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The suburb has a high proportion of overseas-born residents; in the 2001 Census, the most common foreign country of birth was Vietnam at 19.2% [citation:2001 Sunshine North QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2001/SSC22506). More recent 2021 data for Sunshine North shows a median age of 37 and a non-Indigenous population of 93.9%, but the most recent country-of-birth breakdown was not found in the available sources [citation:2021 Sunshine North QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22396).

3) **Working-parent support:** Albion North Primary School partners with TheirCare to provide Outside School Hours Care. The program runs every school day from 6:30am to 8:45am (Before School Care) and 3:15pm to 6:30pm (After School Care), and from 6:30am to 6:30pm on Pupil Free Days [citation:OSHC - Albion North Primary School](https://www.albionnorthps.vic.edu.au/oshc/).
