Here’s a short, fact‑first briefing for families considering Australian International Academy of Education (AIA) — Coburg (King Khalid Coburg Campus / Prep–5). I’ve kept each data point concise and cited sources directly.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Coburg is generally middle‑to‑affluent, with household incomes above the Victorian median and a high share of university‑educated adults (41.7% bachelor degree+). It’s professionally oriented (many white‑collar workers), relatively young, and family‑focused though renting is common [citation:2021 Coburg, Census All persons QuickStats - Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20596) [citation:Coburg (Suburb / Locality) — Census profile | CensusAtlas](https://censusatlas.nickstallman.net/region/sal/SAL20596/coburg).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- Majority Australian‑born (~67.1%); the largest overseas birthplace listed is Italy (≈4.4%) — Italy is the single most common foreign country of birth in Coburg’s 2021 Census snapshot [citation:Coburg, Victoria - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coburg%2C_Victoria) [citation:2021 Coburg, Census All persons QuickStats - Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20596).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- Yes — on‑site Before/After School Care and Vacation Care run at the AIA King Khalid Coburg Campus via Amana OSHC (653 Sydney Rd); listing shows before/after and holiday programs and contact/enrolment details [citation:OSHC at Australian International Academy Coburg (KKCC), Coburg | Amana OSHC](https://amanaoshc.com.au/centres/aia-kkcc).

4) School culture — 2–3 specific extracurriculars
- Competitive school sport teams (football/soccer, basketball, netball, cricket/softball). 
- Music/performing‑arts programs / instrumental activities (school media/videos show music activity; AIA cites a broad co‑curricular programme).
These offerings are described on AIA school pages and the school sports listing [citation:Sports - AIA](https://www.aia.vic.edu.au/school-life/sports) [citation:Australian International Academy of Education - IB World School](https://ibo.org/en/school/000790).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Coburg is an inner‑north, multicultural shopping strip neighbourhood (Sydney Rd) with parks, family services and good public transport—appeals to families and professionals. Local specialty grocers include Asian supermarkets (KFL Supermarket on Sydney Rd), halal butchers/markets and Middle Eastern bakeries/delis (e.g., Coburg Halal Meats, Zaatar) [citation:Coburg (Suburb / Locality) — Census profile | CensusAtlas](https://censusatlas.nickstallman.net/region/sal/SAL20596/coburg) [citation:COBURG - VIC | KFL Supermarket (The Asian Food Specialist)](https://www.kflsupermarkets.com.au/coburg-vic) [citation:Coburg Halal Meats — Ummah Directory listing](https://ummahdirectory.com.au/food-and-beverage/halal-butchers/coburg-halal-meats/).
