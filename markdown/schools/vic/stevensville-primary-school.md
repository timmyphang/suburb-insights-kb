1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: St Albans is a working-class to middle-income suburb with a diverse, family-oriented population. The median weekly household income is $1,205, and the median age is 36, indicating a relatively young community with many families [citation:2021 St Albans (Vic.) QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22330). The suburb has a mix of blue-collar and service industry workers, with a large migrant and refugee community.

3) **Working-parent support**: Stevensville Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) through the provider **TheirCare**. The program runs from **7:00 am to 8:45 am** (before school) and from **3:30 pm to 6:00 pm** (after school) [citation:Stevensville Primary School website](https://www.steveps.vic.edu.au/).

4) **School culture**: The school’s Community Hub runs a **Breakfast Club** and **cooking and gardening groups** for families and children, fostering community engagement [citation:Community Hub page](https://www.steveps.vic.edu.au/page/179/Community-Hub). The Physical Education program includes a named **gymnastics unit**, which is a core part of student activity [citation:Physical Education page](https://www.steveps.vic.edu.au/page/171/Physical-Education). Specific named sporting clubs or teams are not detailed in the provided materials.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: St Albans has a vibrant, multicultural town centre with many Asian grocers. Key specialty supermarkets include **Pahal Grocers** (282 Main Road East, South Asian groceries) [citation:Pahal Grocers](https://www.stalbanstowncentre.com.au/news-stories/pahal-grocers), **KFL Supermarket** (330 Main Road East, Asian foods) [citation:St Albans KFL](https://www.kflsupermarkets.com.au/st-albans-vic), and **Super Pinoy** (Filipino/Asian groceries) [citation:Super Pinoy](https://www.stalbanstowncentre.com.au/directory/super-pinoy). The area offers a range of fresh produce and international ingredients.

6) **Weeknight dining**: Family-friendly takeaway and dining options include **Phi Phi Vietnamese & Chinese Restaurant** (Vietnamese/Chinese) [citation:Tripadvisor St Albans](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g4358126-St_Albans_Brimbank_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html), **Shahi Darbaar** (Indian, on East Esplanade) [citation:ozfoodhunter St Albans](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/st-albans-3021), and **Pho Kim Long** (Vietnamese) [citation:Tripadvisor St Albans](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g4358126-St_Albans_Brimbank_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). These are popular choices for casual meals.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Tutoring centres in the area include **Innate Tutoring** (offers VCE English, Maths, Biology, and Chemistry tutoring in St Albans) [citation:Innate Tutoring](https://www.innatetutoring.com.au/st-albans-tutoring/) and **Success Tutoring St Albans** (provides personalised K-12 tutoring) [citation:Success Tutoring St Albans](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-st-albans/). Both offer in-person and online sessions.

8) **Commute**: St Albans is served by **St Albans Station** on the **Sunbury line**. The average train travel time to Flinders Street Station (Melbourne CBD) is **31 minutes**, with trains running every 15 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio St Albans to Flinders Street](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/St-Albans-Station/Flinders-Street-Station).
