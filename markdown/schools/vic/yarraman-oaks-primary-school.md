1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Noble Park is a culturally diverse, working-class to middle-income suburb with a median age of 35. The median weekly household income is $1,382, and the suburb has a high proportion of families (7,975 families, average 1.7 children per family for families with children) [citation:2021 Noble Park, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21952). The suburb features a mix of housing, with 89.8% houses and 10.2% other dwellings in Noble Park North and 71.8% houses in Noble Park West [citation:Noble Park North – SA2 212041316 | AreaSearch](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/noble_park_north) [citation:Noble Park - West – SA2 212041460 | AreaSearch](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/noble_park_west). Reviews note it is a "vibrant, multicultural community" with affordable housing and strong community spirit, but some describe it as "very low socioeconomic status" with unfriendly neighbours [citation:Noble Park, VIC 3174 - Suburb Profile & Local Reviews](https://www.homely.com.au/suburb-profile/noble-park-vic-3174).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Noble Park is a highly multicultural suburb; only 31.4% of residents were born in Australia [citation:2021 Noble Park, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21952). The most common foreign country of birth is India, at 11.5% of the population [citation:2021 Noble Park, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21952).
