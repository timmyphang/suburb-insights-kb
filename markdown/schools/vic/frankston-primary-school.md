1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Frankston City is a middle-to-working-class area with a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers. The median weekly household income is $1,653, and the median age is 39 [citation:2021 Frankston, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/LGA22170). SEIFA data indicates that Frankston Central and Frankston North have relatively low socio-economic status, while areas like Sandhurst are more advantaged [citation:SEIFA by profile area | Frankston City | Community profile](https://profile.id.com.au/frankston/seifa-disadvantage-small-area). The suburb has a mix of families and singles, with 37,590 families counted in 2021 [citation:2021 Frankston, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/LGA22170).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Frankston is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 73.9% of residents born in Australia (data from the LGA QuickStats, not the suburb SA1 level). Among overseas-born residents, the most common country of birth is England, at 4.6% [citation:2021 Frankston, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/LGA22170).

3) **Working-parent support:** Frankston Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) operated by Camp Australia. The program runs Monday to Friday from 7:00am to 8:45am and 3:30pm to 6:30pm [citation:Before & After School Care | Frankston Primary School](https://frankstonps.vic.edu.au/parent-information/before-after-school-care/). Vacation care is also available through Camp Australia at nearby venues, and families should contact Camp Australia directly for details [citation:Before & After School Care | Frankston Primary School](https://frankstonps.vic.edu.au/parent-information/before-after-school-care/).

4) **School culture:** Frankston Primary School offers a range of extracurriculars including a Breakfast Club, a Choir program, and House Sports Day (including Junior House Sports and 3–6 House Sports Day) [citation:Frankston Primary School: Term 1 Week 2 2026](https://frankstonps.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/2026-Term-1-WEEK-2.pdf) [citation:Student Voice | Frankston Primary School](https://frankstonps.vic.edu.au/student-learning/student-voice/). These activities are led by House Captains and supported by the Student Representative Council [citation:Student Voice | Frankston Primary School](https://frankstonps.vic.edu.au/student-learning/student-voice/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Frankston has a coastal, family-oriented vibe with amenities like Beauty Park, George Pentland Botanical Gardens, and the Frankston Beach foreshore [citation:Specialist Program | Frankston Primary School](https://frankstonps.vic.edu.au/student-learning/specialist-program/). Specialty grocers include MicMacs Oriental Grocer (Asian grocer), Mrs Wan's Asian Mart at 20 Wells Street, and Pacific Asian Karingal Hub at Karingal Hub [citation:MicMacs Oriental Grocer | Frankston VIC | Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/micmacsorientalgrocer/). Frankston Spices Asian Groceries & Takeaway is another option for Asian ingredients [citation:Frankston Spices Asian Groceries & Takeaway, Frankston](https://www.trueindia.com.au/business/supermarket-and-grocery-stores/5139/frankston/frankston-spices-asian-groceries-and-takeaway).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family takeaway, try Frankston Thai Restaurant for Thai cuisine (online ordering available) or Mr Frankie Cafe on Norman Avenue for Modern Australian brunch and lunch options [citation:Restaurants in Frankston,VIC With Takeaway | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/frankston/takeaway-category). Another option is Lucky Inn Chinese restaurant, offering Chinese takeaway [citation:Restaurants in Frankston,VIC With Takeaway | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/frankston/takeaway-category).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Alchemy Tuition offers in-home and online tutoring in Frankston since 2005, with one-on-one sessions [citation:Tutoring Frankston // The Best Tutors in Frankston // Alchemy Tuition](https://alchemytuition.com.au/melbourne-tutoring-frankston/). Kip McGrath Frankston (at Lot 3, Shop 4, 300 Heatherhill Road) provides professional tutoring in English and maths for primary and secondary students [citation:KMEC Frankston Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/frankston).

8) **Commute:** Frankston is on the Frankston line, with services from Frankston Station to Flinders Street Station. The average train travel time is 59-65 minutes, with direct trains running every 15 minutes [citation:Train Flinders Street to Frankston from $4 | Tickets & Timetables | Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Flinders-Street/Frankston). The line serves stations including Seaford, Kananook, and Frankston [citation:Frankston line | Train timetable | Metro Trains Melbourne](https://railmaps.com.au/routedetails.php?TableSelect=320).
