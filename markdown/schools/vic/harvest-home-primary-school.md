Here’s a short, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Harvest Home Primary School (Epping). I used ABS QuickStats / community profile snippets and the school/provider pages; each point is a short fact with source citations.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Epping (Vic.) median age 35 and median weekly household income $1,671 (2021 Census) — relatively young, family‑oriented suburb with many households and children [citation:2021 Epping (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20878).  
- The child-age brackets (0–9) are slightly above the Victoria share, supporting a family-heavy mix [citation:2021 Epping (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20878).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- The most common overseas birthplace recorded for Epping residents (2021 Census) is India [citation:2021 Epping (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20878).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- Harvest Home Primary runs an on‑site YMCA Outside School Hours Care: Before School, After School and Vacation Care at 365 Harvest Home Rd; ACECQA records an approved capacity of 120 places [citation:Out of School Hours Care - Harvest Home Primary School](https://www.harvesthomeps.vic.edu.au/page/46/Out-of-School-Hours-Care) [citation:YMCA Harvest Home Primary School OSHC - ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/ymca-harvest-home-primary-school-oshc).

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars
- Performing Arts (Drama & Music) program for Prep–Year 6 [citation:Performing Arts - Harvest Home Primary School](https://www.harvesthomeps.vic.edu.au/page/63/Performing-Arts).  
- Interschool sport and a broad PE program (Years 5–6 compete in weekly interschool sport; school runs basketball, football, netball, cricket, athletics) [citation:Physical Education - Harvest Home Primary School](https://www.harvesthomeps.vic.edu.au/page/56/Physical-Education).  
- School handbook also lists STEM and Spanish specialist programs (useful for enrichment) [citation:School Handbook (2026) - Harvest Home Primary School](https://www.harvesthomeps.vic.edu.au/uploaded_files/media/2026_hhps_school_handbook.pdf).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Epping is a family suburb with shopping and community facilities (Pacific Epping / High Street precincts and parks), making everyday errands and family activities straightforward [citation:Epping | Explore Whittlesea](https://www.explore.whittlesea.vic.gov.au/Food-and-drink/Caf%C3%A9s-and-takeaways/Epping).  
- For specialty groceries there are several Indian/Asian grocery options around Epping (examples: Indiagate Spices & Groceries; local Indian supermarkets listed for the area) [citation:indiagate Spices & Groceries in Epping, VIC](https://australia.chamberofcommerce.com/business-directory/victoria/epping/indian-grocery-store/3907686-indiagate-spices-groceries) [citation:Indian Grocery Shops in Epping, VIC Australia - Everything Indian](https://www.everythingindian.com.au/indian-grocery-shops-in-epping-vic).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 family‑friendly options
- La Famiglia (Italian on High Street) — family‑friendly pizzas/pasta and takeaway options [citation:La Famiglia Italian Restaurant](https://www.lafamigliaepping.com.au/).  
- Local High Street/Pacific Epping takeaway mix — pizza, fish & chips and a variety of quick international takeaways common in the precinct (good for weeknights) [citation:Epping | Explore Whittlesea](https://www.explore.whittlesea.vic.gov.au/Food-and-drink/Caf%C3%A9s-and-takeaways/Epping).

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 local centres)
- Kumon Epping (maths & English tuition centre serving Epping) [citation:Search for a Kumon Centre | Kumon Australia (KUMON EPPING VIC)](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-EPPING-VIC).

8) Commute — train time to CBD
- Epping has a Metro train station on the Mernda/Epping line; typical peak journey time to Flinders Street is about 40 minutes by train (direct services to the city) [citation:Epping | Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/epping/) [citation:Epping to Flinders Street Station (FromTo.travel)](https://fromto.travel/en/australia/epping-station/flinders-street-station).
