St Paul the Apostle Catholic Primary School — Doreen (short family briefing)

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
- Doreen is a young, family‑oriented growth suburb (median age 33) with many couples-with-children households and a high share of owner‑occupied family homes. Median weekly household income is comparatively high (~$2,151/week), putting the area in a comfortable/middle‑to‑affluent family bracket overall [citation:2021 Doreen QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20779) [citation:Doreen suburb profile](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/doreen).

2) Most common foreign country of birth  
- Most residents (about 77.6%) were born in Australia; the largest overseas birthplace is India (~3.9% of residents) — no single large overseas group dominates the suburb [citation:Doreen suburb profile](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/doreen) [citation:2021 Doreen QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20779).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
- The school offers OSHC through Camp Australia (before school, after school and holiday programs); families register with Camp Australia for bookings and fees [citation:St Paul the Apostle OSHC page](https://www.spadoreen.catholic.edu.au/enrolment-information/st-paul-the-apostle-school-doreen-out-of-school-hours-care/) [citation:Camp Australia school listing](https://campaustralia.com.au/school/?accessCode=75E0&doing_wp_cron=1730824048.2318570613861083984375).

- Active school sport program including playground/multi‑sport facilities and inter‑school/team sport (school has organised netball teams — the "Doreen Saints" are cited as a new netball team started at the school) [citation:Our new play areas / sport at SPA](https://www.spadoreen.catholic.edu.au/our-new-play-areas-are-ready/) [citation:Parliament Hansard mention of Doreen Saints](https://www.parliament.vic.gov.au/parliamentary-activity/hansard/hansard-details/HANSARD-2145855009-21220).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (2 sentences)  
- Doreen / Laurimar is a family‑focused, new‑housing growth area with many parks, playgrounds and community reserves (e.g., Laurimar Recreation Reserve) making it convenient for young families [citation:Laurimar Recreation Reserve](https://www.whittlesea.vic.gov.au/Things-to-see-and-do/Parks-trails-and-playgrounds/Local-parks-and-reserves/Laurimar-Recreation-Reserve) [citation:Doreen family guide](https://melbz.com.au/doreen/family-guide/).

8) Commute (to Melbourne CBD) — realistic option substitution: bus + train  
- Doreen does not have a metro train station; residents typically take a local bus to Mernda Station and catch the Mernda‑line train to Flinders Street. Bus route 388 (and other local bus routes) links Doreen/Laurimar to Mernda Station [citation:Bus 388 timetable](https://transport.vic.gov.au/route/timetable/15693/388-mernda-station-doreen-mernda-station) [citation:Moovit / Mernda connections](https://moovitapp.com/index/en-gb/public_transportation-Laurimar_Boulevard-Melbourne-street_6860109-2803).  
- Approximate train time from Mernda to Flinders Street is about 50–52 minutes (total door‑to‑platform time will add the Doreen → Mernda bus leg, typically 10–20 minutes depending on stop) [citation:Mernda Line info](https://transport.vic.gov.au/route/5/mernda-line) [citation:Mernda to Flinders Street travel time ~52 min](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Mernda/Flinders-Street-Station).
