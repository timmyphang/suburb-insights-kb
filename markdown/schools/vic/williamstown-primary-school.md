Here’s a compact, family-facing eight‑point briefing for Williamstown Primary School (Williamstown, VIC). Each data point uses a single public source (citation after the fact).

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile — Williamstown is an upper‑middle / relatively affluent bayside suburb (median household weekly income about $2,411) with an older median age (45) and a strong professional/white‑collar presence and many established families. [citation:ABS QuickStats — Williamstown (Vic.) 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22792)

2) Most common foreign country of birth — Most residents are Australian‑born (about 74%); the largest overseas birthplace is England (~5% of residents). [citation:BehindTheFence suburb profile summarising ABS 2021; ABS community profile page](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/williamstown) [citation:ABS Community Profiles — Williamstown (Vic.) 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/community-profiles/2021/SAL22792)

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC) — Williamstown Primary has OSHC run through Camp Australia (before‑school and after‑school care plus school‑holiday programs listed). [citation:Camp Australia — Williamstown Primary School OSHC](https://campaustralia.com.au/school/?accessCode=0177)

4) School culture — Active performing‑arts and music programs (Choir, Marimba Band, Rock Band) plus a structured PE program with interschool sport opportunities. [citation:Williamstown Primary — Performing Arts](https://www.williamstownps.vic.edu.au/page/49/Performing-Art) [citation:Williamstown Primary — Physical Education](https://www.williamstownps.vic.edu.au/page/48/Physical-Education)

5) Suburb vibe and groceries — Williamstown is a historic seaside village with foreshore parks, cafes and boutique shops popular with families and weekend visitors. Main supermarkets on the Douglas Parade/Kororoit Creek Road strip include Coles and Woolworths; specialty/continental groceries (e.g., Marazita Bros delicatessen) and smaller ethnic grocers serve the area. [citation:Wikipedia — Williamstown (general/village vibe)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williamstown%2C_Victoria) [citation:Whereis — supermarkets & local grocers listing](https://www.whereis.com/williamstown-vic-3016/supermarkets-grocery-stores) [citation:Marazita Bros Delicatessen — local specialty grocer](https://www.marazitabros.com.au/)

6) Weeknight dining — Casual family options along Douglas Parade/Kororoit Creek Rd include local fish & chips and takeaway (Golden Catch / Fish Bites / Off The Pier) and a range of cafés; seafood and classic Aussie fish‑and‑chips are local standouts. [citation:Golden Catch Fish & Chips listing](https://restaurantguru.com/Golden-Catch-Fish-and-Chips-Williamstown) [citation:Batterbing Fish (Douglas Parade) listing](https://www.doordash.com/en-AU/store/batterbing-fish-williamstown-969178) [citation:Off The Pier — TripAdvisor listing](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurant_Review-g261669-d7805124-Reviews-Off_the_Pier_Fish_and_Chippery-Williamstown_Hobsons_Bay_Greater_Melbourne_Victori.html)

7) Tuition and enrichment — Local options include a Kumon learning centre in Williamstown (maths/reading programmes) and online/national providers that advertise services to Williamstown families (e.g., Cluey Learning). [citation:Kumon Williamstown centre listing](https://www.kumon.com/Williamstown) [citation:Cluey Learning — Williamstown school page / offers](https://clueylearning.com.au/en/lp/school/?sch=Williamstown+Primary+School)

8) Commute — Williamstown has its own suburban train station on the Williamstown line; the trip to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street) is roughly 20–30 minutes depending on service (typical published estimates ~24 minutes). [citation:Rome2rio trip summary ~24 min estimate](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Williamstown/Flinders-Street-Station) [citation:Transport Victoria — Williamstown Line (timetables/routes)](https://transport.vic.gov.au/route/timetable/17/williamstown-line)
