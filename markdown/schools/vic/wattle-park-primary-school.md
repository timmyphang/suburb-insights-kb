1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Burwood is a middle-to-upper-income, family-friendly suburb in Melbourne’s eastern suburbs, with a mix of families, professionals, and retirees. The median weekly household income is $1,564, and the median age is 33, indicating a relatively young population [citation:2021 Burwood (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/207031165). The suburb is characterised by leafy streets, heritage homes, and modern developments, with a strong white-collar professional base drawn to its proximity to the city and quality schools [citation:Burwood, VIC 3125 Suburb Profile](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/post/burwood).

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) at Wattle Park Primary School is provided by TeamKids. Before school care operates from 7:00 am to 9:00 am, and after school care runs from 3:30 pm to 6:00 pm, all located in the school hall on Banksia Street [citation:Wattle Park Primary - Burwood | TeamKids](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/wattle-park-primary-burwood-oshc-2/). TeamKids also offers vacation care from 7:00 am to 6:00 pm during school holidays [citation:Wattle Park Primary - Burwood | TeamKids](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/wattle-park-primary-burwood-oshc-2/).

4) **School culture:** Wattle Park Primary School offers a Stephanie Alexander Kitchen Garden Program, which gives students hands-on experience growing and cooking fresh food [citation:Wattle Park Primary School](https://www.wattleparkps.vic.edu.au/). The school also runs the Sounds Write phonics program to support early reading and writing [citation:Wattle Park Primary School](https://www.wattleparkps.vic.edu.au/). Other specific extracurriculars were not named in the available sources, but the school website lists a focus on these two key programs.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Burwood has a leafy, family-friendly vibe with large parks like Wattle Park and the Gardiners Creek Trail for outdoor activities [citation:Burwood, VIC 3125 Suburb Profile](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/post/burwood). For groceries, families can shop at GR'buy Asian Supermarket at Westfield Burwood, which stocks products from Japan, China, Korea, and Thailand, or visit Hu Hui Supermarket at Burwood Brickworks for fresh Asian produce [citation:GR'buy Asian Supermarket at Westfield Burwood](https://www.westfield.com.au/burwood/store/4GBfHyr2QMWciXn6hm092q/gr-buy-supermarket) [citation:Hu Hui Supermarket - Asian Grocery | Burwood Brickworks](https://www.burwoodbrickworks.shopping/stores/hu-hui-supermarket). Standard supermarkets such as Coles are also available at Westfield Burwood.

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family dining, families can try Sengs Kitchen at 34 Tunstall Square for Cantonese-style Chinese food, or Sabaidee Lao & Thai Restaurant at 161 McKinnon Road for authentic Thai [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Burwood | TuckerFox AU](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/burwood). Another option is Heathmont Fish & Grill, which is popular for fish and chips and kebabs [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Burwood | TuckerFox AU](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/burwood).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** For tutoring, Dr. Du Education has a Burwood campus at Level 8, 11-15 Deane Street, offering Maths, Science, and English tutoring for students from Year 9 to HSC [citation:Burwood Tutoring | Maths, Science & English | Dr. Du Education](https://www.drdueducation.com.au/burwood-tutoring/). 8) **Commute:** The commute from Burwood to Melbourne CBD is served by the Belgrave/Lilydale train line from Burwood Station. The journey to Flinders Street Station is direct, taking approximately 34 to 46 minutes, with trains running every 15 minutes [citation:Burwood to Flinders Street Station Trains](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Burwood-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). Additionally, the Route 75 tram along Burwood Highway provides an alternative service to the city, taking about 48 minutes [citation:Burwood to Flinders Street Station Trains](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Burwood-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
