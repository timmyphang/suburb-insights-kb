1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Coldstream is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $1,913, notably higher than the Victorian median of $1,759, and the median age is 37, reflecting a relatively young population [citation:QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20613). The area has a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers, with a strong family focus, as indicated by 625 families and an average of 2.8 people per household [citation:QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20613).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Coldstream is overwhelmingly Australian-born. According to the 2021 Census, 79.8% of residents were born in Australia [citation:QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20613). The most common overseas country of birth is not listed in the available sources for the suburb alone, but for the broader Lilydale-Coldstream area, the top birthplace after Australia was England at 3.7% [citation:QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/211051278).

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is available at Coldstream Primary School, provided by “Coldstream Primary School OSHC & Early Years” [citation:ChildcareDB](http://www.childcaredb.com.au/service/3579/coldstream_primary_school_oshc_early_years). The service operates from 6:30 am to 6:00 pm on weekdays, offering Before School Care, After School Care, and Vacation Care [citation:KindiCare](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3770/coldstream/coldstream-primary-school-oshc-early-years).

4) **School culture:** Coldstream Primary School offers a strong arts program with named extracurriculars including **School Productions** and **Choir** [citation:The Arts](https://www.coldstreamps.vic.edu.au/the-arts). The school is also a **Lego Champion School** and participates as a **Scientist in Schools affiliate**, providing additional enrichment opportunities for students [citation:Principal Welcome Message](https://www.coldstreamps.vic.edu.au/principal-welcome-message).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Coldstream has a relaxed, rural-village feel within the Yarra Valley, with a strong community focus. For everyday groceries, the main local option is **FoodWorks Coldstream** [citation:Blue Business Guide](https://www.bluebusinessguide.com.au/browse/supermarket-and-grocer/coldstream/foodworks). For specialty or ethnic groceries, residents typically drive to nearby suburbs such as Lilydale (e.g., Woolworths Lilydale) or Chirnside Park (Coles or Woolworths); specific ethnic supermarkets in Coldstream were not found in available sources [citation:Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/coldstream-vic-3770).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual, family-friendly dining in Coldstream, **Sassy Thai Coldstream** offers Thai cuisine with dine-in, takeaway, and Uber Eats delivery [citation:Sassy Thai](https://sassythaicoldstream.com/). Another option is **Dosa Hut**, an Indian takeaway restaurant available for takeaway orders near Coldstream [citation:Dosa Hut](https://www.dosahut.net.au/indian-restaurant-coldstream/). Additional details on other specific venues were not found in available sources.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Specific tutoring or enrichment centres located directly in Coldstream were not found in available sources. However, nearby options include **Kumon Mooroolbark Education Centre** in Mooroolbark (approx. 10 minutes’ drive) and **Future Track Pty Ltd** in Lilydale (approx. 5 minutes’ drive) [citation:Whereis](https://www.whereis.com/coldstream-vic-3770/tuition-tutoring).

8) **Commute:** Coldstream does not have its own train station. The nearest station is **Lilydale**, which is the terminus of the **Lilydale train line** (operated by Metro Trains). Commuters can drive to Lilydale Station (approx. 5-10 minutes) and then take a train to Flinders Street, with a typical travel time of about **55-65 minutes** [citation:Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/timetables/). V/Line coach services also serve the broader region but detailed times are not specified [citation:V/Line](https://www.vline.com.au/Timetables/Train-coach-timetables).
