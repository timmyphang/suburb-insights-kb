1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** South Morang is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,078, and the median age is 36 years, which is younger than the national median of 38 [citation:2021 South Morang, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22311). The area has a high proportion of families, with an average of 3 people per household and 1.1 children per household [citation:2021 South Morang, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22311).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** While 84.1% of South Morang residents were born in Australia, the most common overseas country of birth is India, accounting for 8.9% of the population [citation:South Morang VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026)](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/south-morang-vic). Other notable overseas birthplaces include Iran (1.7%), England (1.6%), and China (1.3%) [citation:South Morang VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026)](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/south-morang-vic).

3) **Working-parent support:** Morang South Primary School offers an Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) program operated by Their Care on the school site [citation:OSHC - Morang South Primary School](https://www.morangsouthps.vic.edu.au/oshc/). The Before Care program runs from 6:45 AM to 8:45 AM, and the After Care program runs from 3:30 PM to 6:15 PM [citation:OSHC - Morang South Primary School](https://www.morangsouthps.vic.edu.au/oshc/). Holiday programs and Curriculum Day/Pupil Free Day programs are also available, subject to sufficient numbers [citation:OSHC - Morang South Primary School](https://www.morangsouthps.vic.edu.au/oshc/).

4) **School culture:** Morang South Primary School offers a range of named extracurricular activities. The school runs a **Gardening Club** for students [citation:Clubs – Gorge News](https://gorge.morangsouthps.vic.edu.au/tag/clubs/). Through its Arts program, students participate in a biannual **school concert** held at the Plenty Valley Arts and Convention Centre, which includes dance tuition through the **Footsteps dance program** [citation:The Arts – Morang South Primary School](https://www.morangsouthps.vic.edu.au/the-arts/). The school also facilitates **sports clinics and events** throughout the year [citation:Celebrating learning – Morang South Primary School](https://www.morangsouthps.vic.edu.au/celebrating-learning/).
