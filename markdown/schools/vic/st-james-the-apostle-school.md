1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Hoppers Crossing is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb. According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income is $1,580, and the median age is 37 [citation:2021 Hoppers Crossing, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21203). Over three-quarters of the population are families, and the workforce is a mix of white-collar and blue-collar occupations, with the suburb classified as solidly middle-income [citation:Hoppers Crossing Suburb Profile, VIC 3029 | Satterley](https://satterley.com.au/bluestone/about/hoppers-crossing-vic-3029-suburb-profile/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** In Hoppers Crossing, 60.2% of residents were born in Australia, and the most common overseas country of birth is India, at 10.9% [citation:2021 Hoppers Crossing, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21203).

3) **Working-parent support:** St James the Apostle School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by Camp Australia. The before-school care runs Monday to Friday from 7:00 AM to 8:30 AM, and the after-school care runs from 3:05 PM to 6:00 PM, both during school terms [citation:Camp Australia - St James the Apostle Primary School OSHC | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/camp-australia-st-james-apostle-primary-school-oshc). Vacation care is also available but not listed with specific hours on the ACECQA register [citation:Camp Australia - St James the Apostle Primary School OSHC | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/camp-australia-st-james-apostle-primary-school-oshc).

4) **School culture:** St James the Apostle School offers a range of extracurriculars, including the Student Leaders program, a Senior Camp at Lady Northcote Discovery Camp, and participation in the National Simultaneous Storytime [citation:School Organisation & Information - St James the Apostle, Hoppers Crossing](https://www.sjhcn.catholic.edu.au/our-school/school-organisation/)[citation:Senior Camp - St James the Apostle, Hoppers Crossing](https://www.sjhcn.catholic.edu.au/senior-camp/)[citation:St James the Apostle, Hoppers Crossing](https://www.sjhcn.catholic.edu.au/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Hoppers Crossing has a strong family vibe with multiple parks, comprehensive shopping facilities, and safe streets [citation:Hoppers Crossing Suburb Profile, VIC 3029 | Satterley](https://satterley.com.au/bluestone/about/hoppers-crossing-vic-3029-suburb-profile/). For specialty groceries, families can shop at Grocer-Eez (The Indian Supermarket) at 5/428 Old Geelong Road, or Fiji Produce at 3/10 Hammer Court, both offering Indian, Asian, and Fijian groceries [citation:Grocer-Eez The Indian Supermarket in Hoppers Crossing, VIC 3029](https://australia.chamberofcommerce.com/business-directory/victoria/hoppers-crossing/indian-grocery-store/3536291-grocer-eez-the-indian-supermarket)[citation:Indian Grocery Stores Hoppers Crossing, VIC](https://singh.com.au/indian-grocery-stores-hoppers-crossing-vic/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family-friendly dining, Hanuman Thai Restaurant offers Asian and Thai cuisine, Jai Ho Indian Restaurant provides Indian dishes, and Hoppers Kebab House serves Middle Eastern fast food [citation:THE 10 BEST Restaurants in Hoppers Crossing (Updated July 2026)](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g4063929-Hoppers_Crossing_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). All are suitable for takeaways and relaxed meals.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Alchemy Tuition offers one-on-one in-home and online tutoring in Hoppers Crossing, covering all subjects [citation:Tutoring Hoppers Crossing // The Best Tutors in Hoppers Crossing // Alchemy Tuition](https://alchemytuition.com.au/melbourne-tutoring-hoppers-crossing/). Kumon Hoppers Crossing Education Centre provides after-school English and mathematics programmes for children aged 3-14 [citation:Kumon Hoppers Crossing Education Centre](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/Kumon-Hoppers-Crossing-VIC).

8) **Commute:** Hoppers Crossing is served by Hoppers Crossing Station on the Werribee train line, operated by Metro Trains Melbourne.
