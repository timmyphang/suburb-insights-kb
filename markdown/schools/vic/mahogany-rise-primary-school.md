1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Frankston North is a working-class, low socio-economic area with a notable amount of social housing [citation:Frankston – unfairly tarnished with one brush? | Informed Decisions](https://www.id.com.au/insights/articles/frankston-unfairly-tarnished-with-one-brush/). The median weekly household income is $1,128, which is below the Victorian median of $1,653 for the wider Frankston LGA [citation:2021 Frankston North, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20948). The median age is 37, and although the suburb has many families, the mix also includes a significant number of single-person households [citation:2021 Frankston North, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20948).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Frankston North is overwhelmingly Australian-born. In 2021, 72.9% of residents were born in Australia, while the top overseas country of birth was England at 3.5% [citation:2021 Frankston North, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20948).

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is available at Mahogany Rise Primary School, provided by **Elysium OSHC - Mahogany Rise [citation:Elysium OSHC - Mahogany Rise in Frankston North, VIC 3200 | CareforKids.com.au](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/52dqm/elysium-oshc-mahogany-rise-frankston-north-3200). The service runs Monday to Friday from **7:00 am to 6:00 pm**, offering Before School Care, After School Care, and Vacation Care [citation:Elysium OSHC - Mahogany Rise | ChildcareDB](http://www.childcaredb.com.au/service/12395/elysium_oshc_mahogany_rise).

4) **School culture:** Mahogany Rise Primary School offers a range of named extracurricular lunchtime clubs, including **Netball**, **Soccer**, **Art Club**, **Garden Club**, and **Choir** [citation:Mahogany Rise Primary School](https://www.mahoganyriseps.vic.edu.au/). The school also participates in a **Kitchen Garden program** and hosts **House activities** as part of its student wellbeing and engagement framework [citation:Student Wellbeing and Engagement Policy Tabled Ratified Review Date](https://www.mahoganyriseps.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/MRPS-Student-Wellbeing-and-Engagement-Policy-2025.docx.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Frankston North is a family-oriented suburb with a mix of public housing and affordable homes.
