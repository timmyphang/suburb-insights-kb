Here’s a concise, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Nearnung Primary School (Tarneit). I used short web snippets and opened pages only when needed; each data point below cites the source used.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)  
Tarneit is a rapidly growing, young family suburb with a median age of 30 and very high shares of young children — a clear family‑oriented area [citation:ABS QuickStats Tarneit 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22451). Employment/occupation trends show a large and growing Professionals cohort, so the area reads as predominantly middle‑income with a rising white‑collar presence alongside trades and service jobs [citation:CityCompass Tarneit occupations summary / Wyndham infographic](https://citycompass.com.au/wyndham-c/community/tarneit/employment/occupations).

2) Most common foreign country of birth  
India is the leading overseas birthplace for Tarneit residents — roughly ~28–29% of residents were born in India in 2021 (Australia-born is lower than many suburbs; see ABS QuickStats summary) [citation:BehindTheFence summary / ABS QuickStats Tarneit 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22451).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
Yes — an OSHC provider (TeamKids) runs before/after‑school care and holiday programs at Nearnung Primary (TeamKids venue listing + ACECQA / StartingBlocks regulatory entry) [citation:TeamKids Nearnung Primary + ACECQA listing](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/nearnung-primary-tarneit-oshc-5/).

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars offered  
- Specialist music & drama space / programs (school facilities list) [citation:Nearnung Primary “Our Learning Spaces”](https://www.nearnungps.vic.edu.au/page/4/Our-Learning-Spaces).  
- Sport teams / use of gym, hardcourts and sports field (school facilities and local club use) [citation:Nearnung Primary facilities & Premier’s announcement about after‑hours sport use](https://www.nearnungps.vic.edu.au/page/4/Our-Learning-Spaces) [citation:Premier of Victoria facilities access pilot](https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/families-save-time-using-school-facilities-close-home).  
(Also community clubs such as karate/basketball have been using the gym after hours.)

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)  
Tarneit feels like a suburban, family‑focused growth area with several shopping centres, playgrounds and community facilities (Tarneit Central, Tarneit Gardens) serving everyday family needs [citation:Tarneit Central / Tarneit Gardens listings](https://www.tarneitcentral.com.au/). For ethnic/specialty groceries there are local options inside Tarneit Central and nearby (e.g., Yes Yes Indian Supermarket and other South‑Asian/Asian grocers listed in local directories) [citation:Tarneit Central store listing (Yes Yes Indian Supermarket) / local directories](https://www.tarneitcentral.com.au/stores/supermarkets/yes-yes-indian-supermarket).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 family takeaway/dining options (standout cuisines)  
- Kesari Tarneit — Indian (Tarneit Gardens) [citation:Kesari Tarneit listing](https://kesari.com.au/kesari-tarneit/)  
- Desi Dhaba Tarneit — Indian takeaway/dine‑in [citation:Desi Dhaba Tarneit](https://www.desidhaba.com.au/tarneit)  
- El Jannah Tarneit — halal Lebanese charcoal chicken (family‑friendly takeaway) [citation:El Jannah Tarneit](https://eljannah.com.au/locations/vic/tarneit/)

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 local options)  
- Kumon Tarneit (maths & English learning centre) — local Kumon centre listing [citation:Kumon Tarneit centre listing](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-TARNEIT-VIC).  
- Kip McGrath / KMEC Tarneit tuition (English & Maths tutoring) — local centre listing [citation:Kip McGrath Tarneit](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/tarneit).

8) Commute — train travel time to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)  
Tarneit is served by V/Line regional trains (Tarneit → Southern Cross). Estimated total travel time to Flinders Street (including the typical transfer/walk/tram from Southern Cross) is about 40–45 minutes (Rome2rio estimates ~41 minutes); check live V/Line timetables for specific service times [citation:Transport Victoria Tarneit station info / Rome2rio journey estimate](https://transport.vic.gov.au/stop/1599/tarneit-station/3/regional-train) [citation:Rome2rio Tarneit → Flinders Street estimate](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Tarneit-Station/Flinders-Street-Station).
