Below is a short, family‑facing briefing for Truganina P‑9 College (Truganina, VIC) covering the eight requested data points. I used ABS QuickStats and local profiles/snippets first and opened school pages only when needed.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Truganina is a rapidly growing, young, family‑oriented suburb — median age 30 and a very high share of children (0–9 years well above state averages) [citation:2021 Truganina QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22582).  
- Local council/profile notes rapid residential growth alongside an industrial precinct, giving a mix of young family households and workforce/industrial employment — best described as young, family‑heavy with middle‑income to working‑class characteristics [citation:Truganina suburb profile (Wyndham City)](https://www.wyndham.vic.gov.au/about-council/advocacy-and-government-relations/way-forward-wyndham/truganina-railway-station).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- India is the largest overseas birthplace among Truganina residents (about 19% reported as India in recent local community summaries derived from the 2021 Census) [citation:Draft Community Profile - Truganina (Melton City)](https://www.melton.vic.gov.au/files/assets/public/v/1/council/about-the-city/demographics/community-profiles/community-profile-2023-infographics/community-profile-truganina.pdf).  
- (ABS QuickStats for Truganina also lists country‑of‑birth tables for reference) [citation:2021 Truganina QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22582).

3) Working‑parent support — OSHC availability at this school
- Yes — Truganina P‑9 College hosts on‑site Outside School Hours Care (Before, After and Vacation Care) operated via TeamKids / listed as an OSHC venue (contact TeamKids for bookings) [citation:TeamKids — Truganina P‑9 College venue](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/truganina-p-9-college-oshc/) [citation:StartingBlocks - TeamKids Truganina P_9 College](https://www.startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01JJYGKE488SX6T97GHJZWZ2H6/TeamKids-Truganina-P9-College).

4) School culture — 2–3 specific extracurriculars
- Instrumental music lessons through Soundgarage (keyboard, guitar, singing, drums, ukulele — lessons during school hours) [citation:Extra Curricular | Truganina P-9 College](https://truganinap9.vic.edu.au/curriculum/extra-curricular/).  
- Competitive school sports / sport‑house events and interschool teams (examples include boys rugby league entries and school volleyball videos) [citation:Welcome | Truganina P-9 College](https://truganinap9.vic.edu.au/welcome/) [citation:PlayRugbyLeague — Truganina P-9 College team](https://www.playrugbyleague.com/Competitions/Competition/2025-cameron-smith-shield-u14s-boys---north-west-gala-day-63786375?team=63785869).  
- Active school library and reading programs (regular library access for all students) [citation:Extra Curricular | Truganina P-9 College](https://truganinap9.vic.edu.au/curriculum/extra-curricular/).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Truganina feels new‑suburb / family‑focused with many parks, playgrounds and local services for young families and easy access to Wyndham library services [citation:Extra Curricular | Truganina P-9 College](https://truganinap9.vic.edu.au/curriculum/extra-curricular/) [citation:Wyndham Libraries](https://www.wyndham.vic.gov.au/services/libraries).  
- Local grocery options include major supermarkets (Coles) plus community/ethnic grocers such as Abu Rashed’s Friendly Grocer and Indian subcontinental/“Yes Yes” Indian supermarket and halal‑focused grocers (WQM Quality Supermarket) serving multicultural and halal shopping needs [citation:Coles Truganina](https://www.coles.com.au/find-stores/coles/vic/truganina-7685) [citation:Abu Rashed's Truganina - Friendly Grocer](https://friendlygrocer.com.au/abu-rasheds-truganina) [citation:Yes Yes Indian Supermarket (MapQuest)](https://www.mapquest.com/au/victoria/yes-yes-indian-supermarket-794822812).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 casual, family‑friendly options + standout cuisines
- Chaskah (Indian takeaway / family meal deals) — family meal packs and party bundles make it a clear family takeaway option [citation:Chaskah - Truganina (Menu)](https://chaskah.com.au/stores/23x3shfi7q/menu).  
- Stella Pizza Truganina (pizza / casual family dining) and other local takeaways listed on local directories (pizza and fast‑casual) [citation:Tripadvisor - Restaurants in Truganina](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g12370552-Truganina_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).  
- Several halal and South Asian takeaway options are prominent locally (useful for families seeking halal/Indian/Pakistani cuisines) [citation:HalalHQ Truganina listings](https://halalhq.io/au/vic/truganina).

7) Tuition and enrichment — 1–2 local centres
- Success Tutoring — local centre offering primary and secondary tutoring aligned to Victorian Curriculum in Truganina (in‑centre and online options) [citation:Truganina Tutoring – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/truganina-tutoring).  
- Brainworks (one‑to‑one maths & English tutoring) / other private tutors listed locally (Brainworks venue for Truganina) [citation:Brainworks Truganina](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-truganina-south).

8) Commute — train time to Melbourne CBD
- There is currently no operational Truganina passenger train station; Truganina station is planned but not yet open, so the realistic nearest rail option is Tarneit Station (~7 km away) [citation:Truganina Railway Station | Wyndham City](https://www.wyndham.vic.gov.au/about-council/advocacy-and-government-relations/way-forward-wyndham/truganina-railway-station).  
- Typical train travel from Tarneit to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street / Southern Cross) is roughly 30–40 minutes by regional/metro services depending on the run and transfers (many sources show averages ~30–41 minutes to central stations) — expect about 30–40 min rail time from the nearest station plus local travel to the station [citation:Tarneit Station to Flinders Street Station - Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Tarneit-Station/Flinders-Street-Station) [citation:Tarneit Railway Station - Transport Victoria](https://transport.vic.gov.au/stop/1599/tarneit-station/3/regional-train).
