1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Altona North is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,695 (2021 Census) and a median age of 37 [citation:2021 QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20037). The population includes a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers, with 3,385 families and an average of 2.6 people per household, indicating a strong family presence [citation:2021 QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20037). The area has a notable proportion of residents aged 0-14 (20.2%) and 65+ (21.8%), reflecting a mix of young families and older households [citation:2016 QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20036).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The most common overseas country of birth is not explicitly stated in the available census excerpts. However, the top non-English language spoken at home is Arabic (13.1% in 2016), followed by Italian (6.1%) and Greek (5.7%), which may indicate significant communities from Arabic-speaking countries, Italy, and Greece [citation:2016 QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20036). In 2016, 45.2% of residents spoke only English at home [citation:2016 QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20036).

3) **Working-parent support**: Altona North Primary School’s Out of School Hours Program is provided by **Camp Australia** and operates Monday to Friday during school terms [citation:Before and After School Care](https://www.altonanorthps.vic.edu.au/page/65/Before-and-After-School-Care). Before-school care runs from **7:00am to 8:30am** and after-school care from **3:15pm to 6:15pm** [citation:Before and After School Care](https://www.altonanorthps.vic.edu.au/page/65/Before-and-After-School-Care). The program is registered for Child Care Subsidy, and families can contact Camp Australia directly for enrolment [citation:Camp Australia Altona North](https://campaustralia.com.au/school/?accessCode=030D).

4) **School culture**: Altona North Primary School offers several named extracurriculars through its Performing Arts extension programs, including **Choir**, **Dance Crew**, **Drama Group**, and **Instrumental Band** [citation:Performing Arts](https://www.altonanorthps.vic.edu.au/page/124/Performing-Arts). Additionally, **Sports X** runs a **basketball program** every Monday from 3:20-4:20pm during term [citation:Sports X](https://www.altonanorthps.vic.edu.au/articles/52). These programs provide opportunities for students to perform at local events such as Hobson’s Voice and the annual school concert [citation:Performing Arts](https://www.altonanorthps.vic.edu.au/page/124/Performing-Arts).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Altona North has a suburban feel with local amenities like **Borrack Square** and **Altona Gate** shopping centres. Specialty grocers include **Second Ave Grocer** (62-76 Second Avenue), offering local produce and gourmet finds [citation:Second Ave Grocer](https://secondavegrocer.com.au/). For international groceries, **India Spice Corner** (7 Borrack Square) stocks Indian and Asian products [citation:Indian Grocery Stores](https://singh.com.au/indian-grocery-stores-altona-vic/). The suburb also has a variety of supermarkets and fresh food outlets.

6) **Weeknight dining**: Family-friendly takeaway and dining options in Altona North include **Try-Thai Altona North** (Thai cuisine) and **Window Cafe** (Vietnamese/Asian) at Borrack Square [citation:TuckerFox Altona North](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/altona-north-vic-3025). **KURD Kebab** (kebab shop) is another popular local option for casual takeaway [citation:TuckerFox Altona North](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/altona-north-vic-3025). These venues offer easy takeaway and delivery, ideal for busy weeknights.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: **Brainworks Altona North** provides one-to-one tutoring in Maths and English for Years 3–9, as well as selective school and scholarship exam preparation, located at Altona North Community House, 7 Plover Drive [citation:Brainworks Altona North](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-altona-north). **Success Tutoring** operates in Altona, offering customised tutoring for Kindergarten to Year 12 in subjects like Math and English, with a focus on the VIC curriculum [citation:Success Tutoring Altona](https://successtutoring.com.au/altona-tutoring). Both centres provide in-person and online options.

8) **Commute**: Altona North is served by **Altona Station** on the **Werribee line** (Metro Trains). The train journey from Altona Station to Flinders Street Station takes approximately **33 minutes**, with trains running every 20 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio Altona to Flinders](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Altona-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). Alternatively, bus route **903** connects Altona North to Altona Station, and the total commute via train and bus may take around 45 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio Altona North to Flinders](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Altona-North/Flinders-Street-Station).
