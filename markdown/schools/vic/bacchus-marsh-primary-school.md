Here’s a compact, family-facing eight‑point briefing for Bacchus Marsh Primary School (Bacchus Marsh, VIC). Each item is a short fact/snippet with source citations.

1) Suburb socio-economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Bacchus Marsh is a regional commuter town with a mixed middle‑income profile; the SAL suburb median weekly household income is $1,508 (urban centre median ~ $1,745), indicating household incomes a little below the larger urban centre/Melbourne‑area median [citation:2021 Bacchus Marsh QuickStats (SAL20095)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20095) and [citation:2021 Bacchus Marsh QuickStats (UCL212001)](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/UCL212001).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- Bacchus Marsh is overwhelmingly Australian‑born; overseas‑born residents are a small share. England appears as the largest single overseas birthplace (low single‑digit percent) in suburb summaries [citation:Bacchus Marsh VIC — Suburb Profile - gdp.com.au (sourcing ABS 2021)](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/bacchus-marsh-vic) and the ABS QuickStats overview for the area [citation:2021 Bacchus Marsh QuickStats (SAL20095)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20095).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- Bacchus Marsh Primary School has on‑site OSHClub before‑ and after‑school care and holiday programs (OSHClub runs the school’s before/after school care and holiday program) [citation:OSHClub — Bacchus Marsh Primary School](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/bacchus-marsh-primary-school/) and the school’s policies page notes OSHClub provision [citation:Policies – Bacchus Marsh Primary School](https://bacchusmarshps.vic.edu.au/policies).

4) School culture — extracurriculars (2–3 specific items)
- Choir (Years 2–6), Dance and Drama clubs (lunchtime/semesters) and an annual Year‑6 musical production; Year 4 students learn recorder and Year 5 students learn ukulele, with private instrumental lessons available via local providers [citation:Performing Arts — Bacchus Marsh Primary School](https://www.bacchusmarshps.vic.edu.au/teaching-learning/performing-arts) and the Physical Education program [citation:Physical Education — Bacchus Marsh Primary School](https://www.bacchusmarshps.vic.edu.au/teaching-learning/physical-education).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Bacchus Marsh feels like a family‑oriented regional town with cafes, farm‑gate/food producers and parks; the local Visit Bacchus Marsh guide highlights cafés, farm‑fresh producers and wineries nearby [citation:Food & Drink Guide | Visit Bacchus Marsh](https://www.visitbacchusmarsh.com.au/food-drink/).  
- For specialty groceries there are local South Asian/Indian/Sri Lankan grocers (e.g., Bacchus Mart Groceries & Spice Store; AD Bacchus Marsh Supermarket) on/near Main Street [citation:The Village — Bacchus Mart Groceries & Spice Store](https://www.thevillagebacchus.com.au/stores/bacchus-mart-groceries-spice-store) and [citation:AD Bacchus Marsh Supermarket listing](https://srilankadirectory.com/listing/ad-bacchus-marsh-supermarket-sri-lankan-indian-asian-grocery-store/).
