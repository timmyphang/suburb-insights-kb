1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Footscray has a median age of 34 and a median weekly household income of $1,763, which is below the national median [citation:2021 Footscray, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20935). The area has a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers, with a significant proportion of renters and a relatively young population. Footscray is diverse and dynamic, with a strong student presence and a growing number of families [citation:Spotlight on Footscray's changing housing market](https://www.id.com.au/insights/articles/spotlight-on-footscrays-changing-housing-market/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** According to the 2021 Census, the most common country of birth outside Australia for Footscray residents is Vietnam [citation:2021 Footscray, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20935). Specific percentage data is not available in the provided snippets, but it is known that Footscray has a large Vietnamese-born community. Overall, 24.6% of people had both parents born in Australia [citation:2016 Footscray, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20929).

3) **Working-parent support:** St Monica's School offers an on-site Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) program [citation:St Monica's Parish School | OSHC](https://www.stmonica.catholic.edu.au/community/oshc). Before School Care runs from 7:00 am to 8:20 am, and After School Care runs from 3:00 pm to 6:00 pm [citation:St Monica's Parish School | OSHC](https://www.stmonica.catholic.edu.au/community/oshc). Vacation Care is also available from 7:00 am to 5:30 pm [citation:St Monica's Parish School | OSHC](https://www.stmonica.catholic.edu.au/community/oshc).

4) **School culture:** St Monica's School offers a Kitchen Garden program where students grow, harvest, and prepare food [citation:Specialists - St Monica's Primary School](https://www.smfootscray.catholic.edu.au/learning-teaching/specialists/). The school also has a Choir and a Chess Club, as noted in the 2024 Annual Report [citation:2024 St Monica's School 2 Wingfield Street, FOOTSCRAY 3011](https://www.smfootscray.catholic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/ARSC-Final-.pdf). Physical Education includes sports like Tennis, Soccer, and Hockey clinics [citation:Specialists - St Monica's Primary School](https://www.smfootscray.catholic.edu.au/learning-teaching/specialists/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Footscray has a vibrant, multicultural atmosphere with a strong Asian food culture. Specific grocery stores include KFL Supermarket (an Asian food specialist) at 176-180 Barkly Street [citation:Footscray KFL | The Asian Food Specialist](https://www.kflsupermarkets.com.au/footscray-vic) and Shoon Loi Grocery (an Asian grocer) at the Footscray Market [citation:Grocery & Produces — Footscray Market](https://www.footscraymkt.com.au/grocery-producers). For Indian groceries, Vel Spices and Tales of India offer online ordering and delivery [citation:Online Grocery Footscray | Grocery Footscray Online Store – Vel Spices](https://velspices.com.au/pages/online-grocery-footscray) [citation:Indian Grocery Store Footscray | Free Delivery | Tales Of India](https://talesofindia.com.au/pages/indian-grocery-store-footscray-3011).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Nhu Lan Bakery is a popular, family-friendly spot for Vietnamese banh mi and is very affordable [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Footscray - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g2062777-zfp10601-Footscray_Maribyrnong_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). 8bit offers American-style burgers and is known for its family-friendly and takeaway options [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Footscray - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g2062777-zfp10601-Footscray_Maribyrnong_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). Cafe Lalibela serves Ethiopian cuisine, is inexpensive, and has a welcoming atmosphere for families [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Footscray - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g2062777-zfp10601-Footscray_Maribyrnong_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Kip McGrath Footscray, located at Level 1, 3/250 Barkly Street, provides English and maths tuition for primary and secondary students, with qualified teachers and tailored learning plans [citation:KMEC Footscray Tuition Centre](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/footscray). NQT Education offers professional tutoring in mathematics and English, with classes held at Footscray High School - Pilgrim Campus, and specializes in scholarship and selective school exam preparation [citation:Footscray < Centres | NQT Education](https://www.nqt.com.au/centres/footscray).

8) **Commute:** Footscray is served by Footscray Station on the Sunbury, Werribee, and Williamstown lines [citation:Footscray | Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/footscray/). The train journey from Footscray Station to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD takes approximately 13 minutes, with trains running every 10 minutes [citation:Footscray to Flinders Street Station - 4 ways to travel via train](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Footscray/Flinders-Street-Station).
