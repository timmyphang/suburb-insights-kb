1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Hurstbridge is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,168 and a median age of 41, according to the 2021 Census [citation:2021 Hurstbridge QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21223). The suburb has a high proportion of families with children (19.9% of residents are aged 0–14 years), and most employed residents travel to work by car (67.5% in 2016), suggesting a largely white-collar commuting population [citation:2016 Hurstbridge QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21214). Detailed occupation breakdowns are not available in the provided sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Hurstbridge is overwhelmingly Australian-born. In the 2016 Census, 64.5% of people had both parents born in Australia [citation:2016 Hurstbridge QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21214). For the 2021 Census, the specific country-of-birth percentages are not displayed in the available snippet, but the 2001 data showed England as the most common overseas birthplace at 4.6% [citation:2001 Hurstbridge QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2001/UCL222400).

3) **Working-parent support:** Hurstbridge Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) before and after school, provided by TheirCare [citation:Hurstbridge PS OSHC](https://www.hbridgeps.vic.edu.au/page/201/Out-of-School-Hours-Care). The program is located near the community entrance next to the Multi Purpose Room. Specific operating hours are not listed on the school’s OSHC page, but bookings can be made online via the TheirCare parent portal (www.theircare.com.au) or by calling 0457 222 124.

4) **School culture:** Hurstbridge Primary School offers a wide range of named extracurricular activities. Specific examples include a **Chess Club** run by a professional chess company, a **School Band** supported by instrumental music lessons, and the **Stephanie Alexander Garden & Sustainability** program [citation:Teaching & Learning at Hurstbridge PS](https://www.hbridgeps.vic.edu.au/page/192/Teaching-&-Learning-at-Hurstbridge-Primary-School). These activities are listed alongside other programs such as Junior School Council and interschool sports.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Hurstbridge has a village feel with several local grocers. For everyday supplies, the IGA Friendly Grocers is located at 796 Main Road [citation:Yellow Pages Hurstbridge Supermarkets](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/hurstbridge-vic-3099). Specialty options include **Hurstbridge Deli & Larder** (4 Parker Rd), which offers artisan cheeses, fresh pasta from Pasta Classica, and locally sourced meats, and **Nature’s Harvest** (Shop 3/920 Main Rd), a traditional health food store with bulk organic grains and a resident naturopath on Saturdays [citation:Hurstbridge Village Groceries](https://www.hurstbridgevillage.com.au/groceries).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family-friendly takeaway or dining, Hurstbridge offers **Henry Hurst’s Pizza & Pasta Restaurant**, established for over 15 years and serving Italian cuisine [citation:Hurstbridge Village EAT](https://www.hurstbridgevillage.com.au/eat-restaurants-cafes-takeaway). Another option is **Tasty Thai 927**, which provides Thai takeaway and delivery via platforms like TuckerFox [citation:TuckerFox Tasty Thai](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/tasty-thai-927-hurstbridge). Both are suitable for weeknight family meals.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Specific named tutoring centres in Hurstbridge are not listed in the available sources. However, families can access individual tutors through online platforms such as **Ezy Math Tutoring** and **Ezy English Tutoring**, which screen and match local tutors for in-home or online sessions covering primary subjects including maths and English [citation:Ezy Math Tutoring Hurstbridge](https://www.ezymathtutoring.com.au/tutors/hurstbridge-3099) and [citation:Ezy English Tutoring Hurstbridge](https://www.ezymathtutoring.com.au/tutors/hurstbridge-3099/learn-english). No stand-alone enrichment centre name was found.

8) **Commute:** Hurstbridge is served by the **Hurstbridge train line** on the Metro Trains network. The journey from **Hurstbridge Station** to **Flinders Street Station** (Melbourne CBD) takes approximately **61 minutes**, with direct trains running every 30 minutes during the day [citation:Melbourne Metro Hurstbridge Line](https://melbournemetro.org/hurstbridge-line/) and [citation:Transitapp Hurstbridge Line](https://transitapp.com/en/region/melbourne/metro/commuter-rail-hurstbridge). The line operates from early morning to around midnight.
