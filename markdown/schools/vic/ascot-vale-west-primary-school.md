1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Ascot Vale is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers. The median age is 37, and the median weekly household income is $2,192, which is slightly above the national median [citation:2021 Ascot Vale, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20075). Families represent a significant portion of the population, with 3,910 families and an average of 1.7 children per family [citation:2021 Ascot Vale, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20075).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most recent 2021 Census data shows that 41.3% of Ascot Vale residents had both parents born in Australia [citation:2016 Ascot Vale, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20074). 3) **Working-parent support:** Ascot Vale West Primary School’s Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided by Camp Australia [citation:OSHC – Camp Australia | Ascot Vale West Primary School](https://avwps.vic.edu.au/parents/oshc/). The Before Care Program runs Monday to Friday from 7:30 am to 8:45 am, and the After Care Program runs Monday to Friday from 3:15 pm to 6:00 pm [citation:OSHC – Camp Australia | Ascot Vale West Primary School](https://avwps.vic.edu.au/parents/oshc/).
