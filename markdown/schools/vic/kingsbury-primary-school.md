3) **Working-parent support:** Kingsbury Primary School partners with TheirCare to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC). The service operates from 7:00 am to 8:45 am for before-school care and from 3:30 pm to 6:00 pm for after-school care, with vacation care available from 7:00 am to 6:00 pm [citation:For Parents - Kingsbury Primary School](https://www.kingsburyps.vic.edu.au/for-parents/). Families can access the Child Care Subsidy to reduce costs, and the provider can be contacted via their website or at 1300 072 410 [citation:For Parents - Kingsbury Primary School](https://www.kingsburyps.vic.edu.au/for-parents/).

4) **School culture:** Kingsbury Primary School offers specific extracurricular activities including lunchtime clubs in Visual Arts, Technology, and sports [citation:2023 Annual Report to the School Community](https://www.kingsburyps.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/Kingsbury-Primary-School-Annual-Report-2023.pdf). 8) **Commute:** Kingsbury does not have its own train station. The nearest station is Macleod, located on the Hurstbridge line, approximately 2.5 km from Kingsbury Primary School. Travel time from Macleod to Flinders Street Station is approximately 30–35 minutes, based on typical Metro Trains schedules [citation:Metro Trains Timetable](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/timetables/). Bus services along Plenty Road (such as Route 561 or 566) connect Kingsbury to Macleod station, with the bus trip taking about 10–15 minutes.
