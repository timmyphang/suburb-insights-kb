1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Sunbury is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median personal income of $854/week and a median age of 38, matching the national median [citation:Sunbury VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunbury-vic). The area has a high proportion of families – average household size is 2.6 – and housing tenure shows 39.6% owned outright and 60.4% owned with a mortgage, indicating a stable, home-owning community with a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers [citation:Sunbury VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunbury-vic).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The most common overseas birthplace in Sunbury is England, accounting for 3.9% of residents. The suburb is overwhelmingly Australian-born (92.4%) [citation:Sunbury VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunbury-vic).

3) **Working-parent support**: St Anne’s School offers an Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) program run by OSHClub, which provides before school care, after school care, and vacation care on site [citation:St Anne's School Sunbury | OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/st-anne-s-school-sunbury/); [citation:Our Community | St Anne's Catholic Primary School Sunbury](https://www.sasunbury.catholic.edu.au/ourcommunity).

4) **School culture**: St Anne’s School offers a range of extracurriculars. The school choir performs at local events such as the Sunbury Show and Sunfest, and the School Leadership program actively involves students in representing the school in the wider community [citation:2025 St Anne's School 16-24 Stewarts Lane, SUNBURY 3429](https://www.vrqa.vic.gov.au/StateRegister/Provider.aspx/GetFile?Type=AnnualReport&EntityID=2&SchoolNumber=1758&requestFileType=Pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Sunbury is a family-friendly suburb with ample local amenities, including parks, shopping centres (e.g., Sunbury Square), and several specialty grocery stores. Asia Finest Grocery at Sunbury Square offers Asian, Filipino, Indian, Chinese, Japanese, Thai, Singaporean, and Malaysian products, and there are Indian grocery stores like Tales of India for home delivery [citation:Asia Finest Grocery | Sunbury VIC](https://www.facebook.com/AsiaFinestGrocery/); [citation:Indian Grocery Store Sunbury | Free Delivery | Tales Of India](https://talesofindia.com.au/pages/indian-grocery-store-sunbury-3429/).

6) **Weeknight dining**: Sunbury has many casual, family-friendly takeaway and dining options. Popular choices include Batman Pizza (Italian/pizza), Coco Seed (Thai), and Cheeky Kebabs (Turkish) [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Sunbury | TuckerFox AU](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/sunbury); [citation:Restaurants in Sunbury,VIC With Child Friendly | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/sunbury/childfriendly-category).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Several tutoring and enrichment centres are located in Sunbury. Kip McGrath Sunbury (49a O'Shanassy Street) offers English and maths tuition for primary and secondary students using qualified teachers, and Kumon Sunbury also provides maths and English programmes [citation:KMEC Sunbury Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/sunbury); [citation:Kumon Sunbury Education Centre | Sunbury VIC](https://www.facebook.com/KumonSunbury).

8) **Commute**: Sunbury Station to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street Station) takes approximately 49 minutes by train, operated by Metro Trains Melbourne with services every 30 minutes [citation:Sunbury to Flinders Street Station - 4 ways to travel](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Sunbury-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
