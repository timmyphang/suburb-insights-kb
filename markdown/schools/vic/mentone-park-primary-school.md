1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:**
Mentone is a middle-to-upper-income, established bayside suburb with a strong family presence. The median weekly household income is $1,889, and the median age is 42, which is older than the Victorian median of 38, reflecting a mature population [citation:2021 Mentone, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/208031190). The suburb has a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers, but specific occupational breakdowns (e.g., professional vs. trades) are not detailed in the available sources. With 3,700 families and an average of 0.6 children per household, it attracts both families and couples [citation:2021 Mentone, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/208031190).

The available 2021 QuickStats for Mentone does not list the top countries of birth (e.g., England, India). 3) **Working-parent support:**
Mentone Park Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) through the provider **TheirCare**. The program runs from **7:00am to 8:45am** for Before School Care and from **3:30pm to 6:00pm** for After School Care [citation:Before and After School Care](https://www.mentonepark.vic.edu.au/page/31/Before-and-After-School-Care). Vacation Care is also available [citation:Mentone Park Primary School- VillageOSHC in Mentone, VIC 3194](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/n93e3/mentone-park-oshclub-3194).

4) **School culture:**
Mentone Park Primary School offers several named extracurricular activities. The school has a **Choir** open to students in Grades 3–6 [citation:E-news Web](https://www.mentonepark.vic.edu.au/enews/details/69). They also run a **Green Gang** program that gives environmental updates at school assemblies [citation:September - Issue 14](https://www.mentonepark.vic.edu.au/enews/details/53). Additionally, the school participates in the **Victorian High-Ability Program (VHAP)** for Years 5–6 students in English and Maths [citation:Student Excellence Program](https://www.mentonepark.vic.edu.au/page/40/Student-Excellence-Program).

6) **Weeknight dining:**

8) **Commute:**
Mentone is served by the **Frankston line** on the Metro Trains Melbourne network. The nearest station is **Mentone Station**, and the typical train travel time to **Flinders Street Station** in the Melbourne CBD is approximately **39–49 minutes** [citation:Train Mentone to Flinders Street Station from $3](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Mentone-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). Trains run frequently, every 15 minutes during the day [citation:Mentone to Flinders Street Station - 3 ways to travel via](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Mentone-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
