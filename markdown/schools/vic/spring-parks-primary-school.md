1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Springvale is a socio-economically under-privileged, working-class suburb with a high proportion of two-parent families and overseas movers [citation:Report Addressing concentrations of disadvantage Springvale case study report](https://www.ahuri.edu.au/sites/default/files/migration/documents/AHURI_Research_Paper_Addressing-concentrations-of-disadvantage-Springvale-case-study-report.pdf). The median weekly household income is $1,402, and the median age is 36 [citation:2021 Springvale (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22328). This income is below the Victorian median.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** In Springvale, only 17.6% of people spoke only English at home, and 25.0% spoke Vietnamese at home, making Vietnam the dominant overseas-born community [citation:2016 Springvale (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22317). The suburb is overwhelmingly overseas-born, not Australian-born.

3) **Working-parent support:** Spring Parks Primary School partners with TheirCare to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) [citation:Parents & community | Spring Parks Primary School](https://springparksps.vic.edu.au/parents/). TheirCare operates after school care from 3:30 PM to 6:00 PM on school days and offers a full-day program from 6:45 AM to 6:00 PM on pupil-free days [citation:Parents & community | Spring Parks Primary School](https://springparksps.vic.edu.au/parents/).

4) **School culture:** The school offers a **Breakfast Club** for students before school [citation:Parents & community | Spring Parks Primary School](https://springparksps.vic.edu.au/parents/). Students can participate in **Running Club**, which runs daily to encourage exercise, and **Badminton Club**, run by senior students [citation:Students | Spring Parks Primary School](https://springparksps.vic.edu.au/students/). House teams (Kestrels, Falcons, Hawks, Eagles) compete in whole-school competitions.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Springvale has a vibrant, multicultural vibe centered on its Asian grocery scene. Key supermarkets include **KFL Supermarket**, an Asian food specialist at the Springvale Shopping Centre [citation:Springvale KFL](https://www.kflsupermarkets.com.au/springvale-vic), and **Num Lee Long Asian Groceries**, a wholesale and retail grocer [citation:Home | Num Lee Long Asian Groceries](https://www.numleelong.com.au/). **Lien Hung Asian Grocery** is also within the shopping centre [citation:Spring Vale Shopping Centre - Lien Hung Asian Grocery](http://springvaleshoppingcentre.com.au/portfolio-items/lien-hung-asian-grocery/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly takeaway and dining options in Springvale include **Ammy Bangkok Restaurant** (Thai) on Buckingham Avenue [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Springvale](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/springvale), **Al Baik** (Burgers, Pizza) for takeaway [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Springvale](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/springvale), and **Bharawan Da Dhaba** (Indian) for a casual sit-down meal [citation:THE 5 BEST Indian Restaurants in Springvale](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g1214782-c24-Springvale_Dandenong_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Success Tutoring** in Springvale offers customised tutoring from Kindergarten to Year 12, covering subjects like Math and English [citation:Springvale Tutoring – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/springvale-tutoring). **DT Education**, also in Springvale, provides small-group VCE tuition in Maths, English, and Science for Years 7–12 [citation:DT Education Springvale](https://www.dt-education.com.au/).

8) **Commute:** Springvale has a railway station on the Cranbourne/Pakenham line, served by Metro Trains Melbourne [citation:Springvale to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Springvale/Flinders-Street-Station). The train from Springvale Station to Flinders Street Station (Town Hall Station) takes approximately 33–37 minutes [citation:Springvale to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Springvale/Flinders-Street-Station).
