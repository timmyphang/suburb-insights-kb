Ardeer Station is on the **V/Line Ballarat line**, with direct trains to Southern Cross Station taking approximately 23–28 minutes [citation:Ardeer (Station) to Flinders Street Station - 5 ways to travel via train](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Ardeer-Station/Flinders-Street-Station).
