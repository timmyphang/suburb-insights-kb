1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Richmond is an affluent, inner-urban suburb with a young professional skew. The median weekly household income is $2,245, and the median age is 34 [citation:2021 Richmond (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22170). The population is balanced at 48.6% male and 51.4% female, with a relatively low average of 0.3 children per household, indicating a high proportion of singles and couples without children [citation:2021 Richmond (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22170).

3) **Working-parent support:** Richmond Primary School’s OSHC is run by CommunityOSH. Before School Care operates from 7:00am to 9:00am, and After School Care from 3:30pm to 6:00pm [citation:Richmond Primary Outside School Hours Care Program](https://www.commosh.edu.au/richmondps). They also offer a Holiday Program (7:30am–6:00pm) and an End of Term Early Finish option (2:00pm–6:00pm) [citation:Richmond Primary Outside School Hours Care Program](https://www.commosh.edu.au/richmondps).

4) **School culture:** Richmond Primary School offers several named extracurriculars. The school hosts a ‘Parents Battle of the Bands’ competition to raise funds [citation:RPS Chatter May 2025](https://richmondps.vic.edu.au/news/rps-chatter-may-2025/). It also runs a ‘Pizza Day’ organized by Grade 6 students [citation:RPS Chatter September 2025](https://richmondps.vic.edu.au/news/rps-chatter-september-2025/). Additionally, the school has a ‘Whole School Colour Run’ and ‘House Sports Day’ [citation:RPS Chatter October 2025](https://richmondps.vic.edu.au/news/rps-chatter-october-2025/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Richmond has a vibrant, family-friendly atmosphere with excellent food shopping. The new ‘Richmond Traders’ fresh food market at the corner of Bridge Road and Church Street offers specialty providores and a Coles supermarket [citation:Richmond Traders | Fresh Food Market](https://richmondtraders.com.au/). For ethnic groceries, the suburb has several Asian grocery stores, with options like those listed on dlook.com.au providing access to aromatic spices and exotic fruits not found in mainstream supermarkets [citation:Richmond's Best Asian Grocery Stores [2026]](https://www.dlook.com.au/find/asian-grocery-shop-in-richmond-vic-3121).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Richmond has many casual, family-friendly takeaway options. ‘Monkey King Thai’ offers Thai takeaway and delivery using fresh ingredients [citation:Thai Restaurant Richmond | Best Thai Takeaway & Delivery in Richmond — Monkey King Thai](https://monkeykingthai.com.au/thai-restaurant-richmond). ‘I Love Pho’ is a popular Vietnamese restaurant serving authentic pho, highly rated by families [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Richmond - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g635736-zfp10601-Richmond_Yarra_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). ‘St Domenico Pizza Bar’ provides Italian pizza in a cozy setting [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Richmond - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g635736-zfp10601-Richmond_Yarra_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Richmond has several tutoring centres to support student learning. ‘Kip McGrath Richmond Tuition Centre’ provides tutoring in English and maths for K-12 students, with a free assessment to create a tailored program [citation:KMEC Richmond Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/richmond). ‘Alchemy Tuition’ offers in-home and online tutoring in Richmond, focusing on building confidence and academic performance [citation:Richmond Tutoring | The Best Tutors in Richmond | Alchemy Tuition](https://alchemytuition.com.au/richmond-tutoring/).

8) **Commute:** Richmond is well-connected to the Melbourne CBD by train. Trains from West Richmond Station on the Hurstbridge line run directly to Flinders Street Station, with a typical travel time of 5 minutes [citation:Richmond to Flinders Street Station - 4 ways to travel ...](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Richmond-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). Services depart every 10 minutes on weekdays, making the commute quick and reliable [citation:Richmond to Flinders Street Station - 4 ways to travel ...](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Richmond-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). Alternatively, tram route 75 from Richmond Town Hall/Bridge Road takes about 13 minutes [citation:Richmond to Flinders Street Station - 4 ways to travel ...](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Richmond-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
