1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Bayswater is a middle-income suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,598, slightly below the Victorian median of $1,759 [citation:2021 Bayswater (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20173). The median age is 38, in line with the national median, and 80.0% of residents were born in Australia [citation:Bayswater VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026)](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/bayswater-vic). The area has a mix of families and singles, with 3,381 families and an average household size of 2.4 people [citation:2021 Bayswater (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20173).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas birthplace in Bayswater is China, at 6.2% of the population [citation:Bayswater VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026)](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/bayswater-vic). Australia is the country of birth for 80.0% of residents, followed by India at 5.3% and England at 3.6% [citation:Bayswater VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026)](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/bayswater-vic).

3) **Working-parent support:** Bayswater West Primary School offers its own Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) program, run by the school with a dedicated out-of-hours room [citation:Before & After School Care - Bayswater West Primary School](https://baywestps.vic.edu.au/before--after-school-care.html). Before school care runs from 7:00am to 8:45am, and after school care runs from 3:30pm to 6:00pm [citation:Before & After School Care - Bayswater West Primary School](https://baywestps.vic.edu.au/before--after-school-care.html). Contact for enquiries is Donna on 0448 986 041 [citation:Before & After School Care - Bayswater West Primary School](https://baywestps.vic.edu.au/before--after-school-care.html).

4) **School culture:** Bayswater West Primary School offers named extracurriculars including Dance/Choir/Bands, where students perform concerts [citation:School Brochure pdf](https://baywestps.vic.edu.au/uploads/3/2/3/4/3234469/school_brochure_general_-compressed.pdf). The school also runs a Victorian High Ability Program (VHAP) for Years 5-6 students in English and Mathematics, offering online extension classes [citation:Student Excellence and Extension - Bayswater West Primary School](https://baywestps.vic.edu.au/student-excellence-and-extension.html). Additionally, students can participate in the Victorian Challenge and Enrichment Series (VCES), which provides enrichment activities across the curriculum for high-ability students from Prep to Year 12 [citation:Student Excellence and Extension - Bayswater West Primary School](https://baywestps.vic.edu.au/student-excellence-and-extension.html).
