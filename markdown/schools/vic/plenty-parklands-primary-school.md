Below is a short, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Plenty Parklands Primary School (Mill Park, VIC). I used one focused search per data point and cite sources inline.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
Mill Park is a broadly middle‑income, family‑oriented suburb (median weekly household income $1,735; median age 40) with a high share of married households and many children in primary school age brackets [citation:2021 Mill Park, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21683).

2) Most common overseas country of birth
Most residents are Australia‑born (around 80–83%); the largest overseas birthplace shown in local summaries is India (roughly 4–5% of residents) as the biggest single overseas‑born group [citation:2021 Mill Park, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21683) [citation:Mill Park VIC — Suburb Profile (GDP.com.au)](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/mill-park-vic).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
Plenty Parklands PS runs an on‑site Outside School Hours Care program run by TheirCare (before school, after school and vacation care; hours listed on the school site) — families are directed to TheirCare for enrolment/details [citation:Plenty Parklands Primary School :: Outside School Hours Care](https://www.plentyparklands-ps.vic.edu.au/forms-notices/outside-school-hours-care/) [citation:Plenty Parklands PS TheirCare - ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/plenty-parklands-ps-theircare).

4) School culture — extracurriculars
Plenty Parklands lists specialist and extracurricular offerings including: Music program (specialist), Sport/Physical Education (school teams and use of courts/fields) and Languages (Indonesian) [citation:Plenty Parklands Primary School :: Learning Structure](https://www.plentyparklands-ps.vic.edu.au/about-us/learning-structure/) [citation:Plenty Parklands Primary School :: Facilities](https://www.plentyparklands-ps.vic.edu.au/about-us/facilities/).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
Mill Park feels like a family suburb with parks, community centres and local shopping precincts (Mill Park Village / The Stables) suitable for weekly errands and kid activities [citation:Mill Park Village — Store Directory Map](https://millparkvillage.com.au/floorplan/). Major supermarkets include Woolworths at The Stables; there are also local/ethnic grocery options listed in local directories (e.g., regional listings of specialist grocers) [citation:Woolworths Supermarket location in Mill Park](https://woolworths.supermarketlocationmaps.com/en/location/21784-woolies-cnr-childs-road-and-redleap-avenue) [citation:35 BEST local Supermarkets & Grocery Stores in Mill Park VIC | Yellow Pages®](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/mill-park-vic-3082).

6) Weeknight dining (2–3 casual/family options)
Local family‑friendly takeaway/dinner options include Amalfi Pizza & Pasta (Italian), Angie's Dumpling Bar (Asian/dumplings) and Viet Chopsticks (Vietnamese) — Mill Park’s takeaway scene is multicultural and value‑oriented [citation:Top 7 restaurants in Mill Park, January 2026 - Restaurant Guru](https://restaurantguru.com/Mill-Park) [citation:Best Takeaway in Mill Park — 2026 Guide | MELBZ](https://melbz.com.au/mill-park/best-takeaway/).

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 centres)
Notable local providers: Kip McGrath Mill Park (English & Maths tuition) and Kumon Mill Park (Kumon maths/English programmes) — both list Mill Park centres for primary‑school tuition [citation:KMEC Mill Park Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/mill-park) [citation:Kumon Mill Park Education Centre](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-MILL-PARK-VIC).

8) Commute — train/bus to CBD (Flinders Street)
Mill Park does not have its own heavy‑rail station; residents typically use local buses to connect to the Mernda/South Morang train line. A typical train leg from South Morang to Flinders Street is about 40–45 minutes (plus bus connection time from Mill Park to the station) [citation:Public Transport in Mill Park - Moovit](https://moovitapp.com/index/en-gb/public_transportation-Mill_Park-Melbourne-city_35081-2803) [citation:Mill Park to Flinders Street Station - 6 ways to travel via train (Rome2rio)](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Mill-Park/Flinders-Street-Station).
