1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Bittern is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median age of 46, older than the national median of 38 [citation:2021 Bittern, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20248). The median weekly household income is $1,615, below the Victorian average of $1,759 [citation:2021 Bittern, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20248). The area has a mix of family households (average 2.5 people per dwelling) and a notable retired population, with 22.8% of residents aged 65 and over [citation:2021 Bittern, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20248). Specific white-collar vs blue-collar occupation data is not available in the provided sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Bittern is overwhelmingly Australian-born. According to the 2021 Census, 83.2% of residents were born in Australia [citation:2021 Bittern, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20248). The most common overseas country of birth is England, at 3.9% of residents [citation:2021 Bittern, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20248).

3) **Working-parent support:** Bittern Primary School hosts Elysium OSHC - Bittern on-site at 10 Portsmouth Rd [citation:Elysium OSHC - Bittern - ChildcareDB](http://www.childcaredb.com.au/service/12394/elysium_oshc_bittern). The service operates Monday to Friday from 7:00 am to 6:00 pm, offering before-school care (7:00–8:45 am) and after-school care (3:15–6:00 pm), as well as vacation care [citation:Elysium OSHC - Bittern | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/elysium-oshc-bittern).
