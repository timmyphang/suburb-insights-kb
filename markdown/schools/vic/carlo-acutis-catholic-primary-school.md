1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Wollert is a rapidly growing, middle-income family suburb. The 2021 Census shows a median weekly household income of $1,979, a median age of just 30, and 88% of dwellings are houses [citation:2021 Wollert, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22820). The population is predominantly family-oriented with a high proportion of children aged 0–14 years (29%) and a low proportion of people over 65 (4.4%) [citation:2021 Wollert, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22820). Detailed white-collar vs. blue-collar breakdown not found in available sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Wollert is an ethnically diverse suburb. The 2016 Census (most recent detailed country-of-birth data available in the snippets) indicated 17.0% of people had both parents born in Australia, while 67.8% had both parents born overseas [citation:2016 Wollert, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22805). Specific percentage share for the dominant overseas country of birth in 2021 was not found in the provided sources.

3) **Working-parent support:** Carlo Acutis Catholic Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by MACSEYE (Melbourne Archdiocese Catholic Schools Early Years Education) [citation:OSHC | Carlo Acutis](https://carloacutis.catholic.edu.au/oshc/). After School Care began operating from Term 1, 2025; specific session hours and before-school care availability were not detailed in the provided sources [citation:OSHC | Carlo Acutis](https://carloacutis.catholic.edu.au/oshc/).

4) **School culture:** The school offers specialist extracurricular subjects including Physical Education, Languages other than English, Art, and Science as part of its curriculum [citation:Carlo Acutis story | Carlo Acutis](https://carloacutis.catholic.edu.au/carlo-acutis-story/). As a new school opening in 2025, additional named extracurriculars (e.g., sports teams, band, choir) are not yet listed as they ‘will be added as the school continues to grow’ [citation:Carlo Acutis story | Carlo Acutis](https://carloacutis.catholic.edu.au/carlo-acutis-story/). Student leadership opportunities are encouraged for all students [citation:Carlo Acutis | Catholic Primary School Wollert](https://carloacutis.catholic.edu.au/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Wollert has a suburban, family-oriented vibe with modern housing estates and growing local amenities. Specialty grocery options include Tales of India, which delivers Indian groceries including spices, snacks, and fresh produce directly to Wollert [citation:Indian Grocery Store Wollert | Free Delivery | Tales Of India](https://talesofindia.com.au/pages/indian-grocery-store-wollert-3750/). There is also a local South Asian grocery, Everest Grocer, located in Wollert [citation:Everest Grocer | Wollert VIC](https://www.facebook.com/EverestGrocerWollert/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly takeaway and dining options in Wollert include Smokin Joe's Pizza & Grill, located at 250 Epping Rd, offering pizza [citation:Wollert 3750 Food Delivery and Takeaway VIC - Pizza](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/wollert-3750). For Indian cuisine, Dosa Hut at Shop D1, 420-440 Craigieburn Road, Craigieburn (a short drive from Wollert), provides takeaway and delivery with a wide menu of North and South Indian dishes [citation:Indian Restaurant Near Wollert | Indian Food Takeaway Menu Wollert - Dosa Hut](https://www.dosahut.net.au/indian-restaurant-wollert/). Additional casual dining options were not named in the provided sources.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Several tutoring centres operate in or near Wollert. Brainworks Wollert offers face-to-face maths and English tutoring for Year 1–10 students, including preparation for private school and selective school exams [citation:Brainworks Wollert. One to One Tutoring For Your Child](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-wollert). There is also a Kumon Wollert Education Centre, providing the Kumon Method in English and mathematics to help build fundamental skills [citation:Kumon Wollert Education Centre](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/Kumon-Wollert-VIC).

8) **Commute:** There is no train station in Wollert. To reach Melbourne CBD, you can take the bus route 390 from Wollert to Craigieburn Station, then board a train on the Craigieburn line to Flinders Street Station [citation:Wollert to Flinders Street Station - 3 ways to travel via train](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Wollert/Flinders-Street-Station). The total journey time is approximately 50–60 minutes, depending on connections.
