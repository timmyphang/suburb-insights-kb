1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Knoxfield is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median age of 41 [citation:2021 Knoxfield, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21374). The median weekly household income is $1,762, and the most common household type is couples with children living in separate houses [citation:2021 Knoxfield, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21374); [citation:KNOXFIELD suburb profile](https://landchecker.com.au/suburb/knoxfield-vic-3180/). Employment data from the Census indicates a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers, but specific occupation breakdowns are not detailed in the available snippets.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** According to the 2021 Census, 42.0% of Knoxfield residents had both parents born in Australia, while 43.1% had both parents born overseas [citation:2016 Knoxfield, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21366). The most common overseas country of birth for Knoxfield residents was not specified in the available quickstats or search snippets.

3) **Working-parent support:** Knox Park Primary School's Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided by Camp Australia [citation:Camp Australia - Knox Park Primary School OSHC](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/camp-australia-knox-park-primary-school-oshc). The program operates before school from 7:00 am to 8:45 am, and after school from 3:30 pm to 6:00 pm [citation:Parents | Knox Park Primary School](https://knoxparkps.vic.edu.au/parents/).

4) **School culture:** Knox Park Primary School offers a choir that practices weekly and performs regularly [citation:Teaching & Learning | Knox Park Primary School](https://knoxparkps.vic.edu.au/teaching-learning/). The school also provides externally provided dance group sessions and instrumental tuition lessons [citation:Teaching & Learning | Knox Park Primary School](https://knoxparkps.vic.edu.au/teaching-learning/). Additionally, the school runs a transition program for future kindergarten children and their parents [citation:Enrolment | Knox Park Primary School](https://knoxparkps.vic.edu.au/enrolment/).
