Occupation and class breakdown are not detailed in the available sources, but the income and family structure suggest a mix of working-class and middle-class households.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Melton offers a range of specialty grocers catering to diverse communities. **OM Indian Grocery Shop** (8B Station Road, Melton South) stocks Indian, Pakistani, and Asian groceries, including spices, masalas, and pooja items [citation:OM Indian Grocery Shop Melton South](https://singh.com.au/om-indian-grocery-shop-melton-south-melbourne/). **Lee Merchants – Asian Grocery** (Melton) specialises in products from Vietnam, Philippines, Samoa, and Korea [citation:Lee Merchants - Asian Grocery Melton](https://www.facebook.com/leemerchants/). **Melton International Grocery** is located within the Woodgrove Shopping Centre (533-555 High Street) [citation:Woodgrove – Feel Good, Shop Local](https://woodgrove.qicre.com/Stores/Retail-Stores/Melton-International-Grocery). Major supermarkets like Woolworths and Coles are also available in the area.

6) **Weeknight dining**: For casual family-friendly dining and takeaway, Melton has several well-reviewed options. **Guneet's Indian Restaurant** (Indian cuisine, on High Street) offers dine-in and takeaway, known for freshly prepared dishes [citation:THE 10 BEST Indian Restaurants in Melton](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g552210-c24-Melton_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). **Thai Delight Restaurant** (Thai cuisine) is popular for its consistent quality and family-friendly atmosphere [citation:THE 10 BEST Restaurants in Melton](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g552210-Melton_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). **Melton Seafoods Fish & Chips** provides classic takeaway seafood and burgers, with a children's menu available [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Melton](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g552210-zfp10601-Melton_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Two specific tutoring centres in Melton are **Kip McGrath Melton** (83-85 Unitt Street), offering English and maths tuition from qualified teachers with personalised learning plans, plus NAPLAN and entrance exam preparation [citation:KMEC Melton Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/melton). **Learner's Hub** (Melton) provides tutoring for Foundation to Year 9 in numeracy and literacy, as well as SEAL and selective school preparation, and runs workshops during school holidays [citation:Learner's Hub Melton City Council](https://www.melton.vic.gov.au/Out-n-About/Local-Directory-Listing/Learners-Hub).

8) **Commute**: The closest train station is **Melton Station**, located on the **V/Line Ballarat line**. Services run every 30 minutes to **Southern Cross Station** in the CBD, with a typical travel time of about 37 minutes [citation:Flinders Street Station to Melton - 5 ways to travel via train](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Melton). From Southern Cross, Flinders Street Station is a short walk or one tram stop away, making the total journey approximately 44–55 minutes [citation:Train Melton (Station) to Flinders Street Station from $8 | Tickets & Timetables | Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Melton-Station-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
