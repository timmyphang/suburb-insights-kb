Here’s a short, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Altona Primary School (Altona, VIC). I used snippet‑first sources and opened only official pages as needed.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Altona is a generally middle‑income to relatively advantaged bayside suburb with an older median age (median 42) and median weekly household income around $1,826 — indicating established families rather than a young singles scene [citation:ABS QuickStats - Altona (Vic.) 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20035). SEIFA/IRSAD indicators also rank Altona toward the higher end, consistent with a largely white‑collar / mixed professional community [citation:Economy & Advantage in Altona — CensusAtlas](https://censusatlas.nickstallman.net/region/sa2/213021341/altona/topic/economy-advantage).

2) Most common foreign country of birth  
Most residents are Australian‑born (about two‑thirds); the most common overseas birthplace is England (around 4–5% of residents) — there’s no single large non‑English overseas community dominating the suburb [citation:ABS QuickStats - Altona (Vic.) 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20035) [citation:Altona suburb profile (summary)](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/altona).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
Altona Primary School offers on‑site TheirCare OSHC: before‑school care 7:00–9:00am, after‑school care 3:15–6:00pm and school holiday programs 7:00am–6:00pm (TheirCare coordinator and bookings details on the school OSHC page) [citation:OSHC - Altona PS (TheirCare)](https://www.altonaps.vic.edu.au/oshc).

4) School culture — extracurriculars  
Notable programs include STEM (coding & robotics), Performing Arts / music, and Auslan (LOTE) as timetabled specialist or extracurricular offerings; there is also an active school music program (student recordings present online) [citation:Specialists - Altona PS](https://www.altonaps.vic.edu.au/specialists) [citation:Altona Primary School music page](https://altonaprimaryschool.bandcamp.com/).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries  
Altona is a family‑friendly bayside suburb with parks, foreshore access and local shopping strips — good for parents who want nearby playgrounds, cafes and beach walks [citation:Hobsons Bay - Best Kid‑Friendly Cafes/Restaurants (Hobsons Bay Council)](https://www.hobsonsbay.vic.gov.au/visit/Blog/Best-Kid-Friendly-Cafes-Restaurants-in-Hobsons-Bay). For groceries look to local specialty/ethnic options such as India Spice Corner and Vk’s Spice Centre plus continental deli/cheese butchers like Altona Fresh Fine Foods in nearby shopping centres [citation:India Spice Corner - Altona listing](https://singh.com.au/india-spice-corner/) [citation:Vk’s Spice Centre listing](https://singh.com.au/vks-spice-centre-altona-melbourne/).

6) Weeknight dining (casual, family‑friendly)  
Family‑friendly takeaway/dining options include local fish & chips (Altona Bay Fish and Chips; Fish and Chips on Civic) and a popular Thai takeaway (Siam Secret Thai in Harrington Square) — straightforward, kid‑friendly choices for weeknights [citation:Altona Bay Fish and Chips (AGFG)](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurant/altona-bay-fish-and-chips-83747) [citation:Fish and Chips on Civic](https://fishnchipsoncivic.com.au/) [citation:Siam Secret Thai (takeaway listing)](https://www.ubereats.com/au/store/siam-secret-thai-restaurant/8IRb2bpeR6CdCskmPhGMvA).

7) Tuition and enrichment nearby  
Local tutoring/enrichment providers serving Altona include Success Tutoring (local branch) and Tutoring Victoria (face‑to‑face and online options for primary students) — standard local tutoring choices for literacy/numeracy support [citation:Success Tutoring — Altona](https://successtutoring.com.au/altona-tutoring) [citation:Tutoring Victoria — Altona tutoring](https://www.tutoringvictoria.com.au/tutoring-altona).

8) Commute — Altona station to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)  
Altona has a train station (Altona Station). Typical scheduled train travel time to Flinders Street is roughly 30–40 minutes (journey planners and travel summaries commonly list ≈34 minutes as a typical trip; frequencies vary, so check PTV/Metro for exact times) [citation:Altona | Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/altona/) [citation:Altona to Flinders Street - Rome2rio summary](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Altona-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
