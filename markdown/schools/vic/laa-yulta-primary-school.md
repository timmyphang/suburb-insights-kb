1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Mambourin is a very young, emerging suburb with a median age of 30, significantly younger than the national median of 38 [citation:2021 Mambourin, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21588). The median weekly household income is $2,359, and 100% of homes are owned with a mortgage, indicating a middle-income, family-oriented population [citation:2021 Mambourin, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21588). The suburb has a very small population of 315 (2021 Census), with a high proportion of children aged 0-14 (26.5%), confirming a strong family focus [citation:2021 Mambourin, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21588).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas birthplace in Mambourin is India, accounting for 25.7% of residents [citation:Mambourin VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026)](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/mambourin-vic). Australian-born residents make up 70.3% of the population [citation:Mambourin VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026)](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/mambourin-vic). The next most common overseas birthplace is China, at 4.0% [citation:Mambourin VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026)](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/mambourin-vic).

They offer before-school care, after-school care, and holiday care for enrolled students [citation:Kelly Club Laa Yulta](https://www.kellyclub.com.au/laayulta). 5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Mambourin is a growing master-planned community anchored by the **Mambourin Marketplace**, which opened in September 2025 [citation:Welcome to Mambourin Marketplace](https://www.mambourinmarketplace.shopping/) [citation:New $60m Mambourin Marketplace nears completion](https://www.buildaustralia.com.au/projects/new-60m-mambourin-marketplace-nears-completion-in-melbournes-west/). The marketplace includes a full-line **Coles** supermarket, and the Fresh Food Precinct features the popular Indian grocer **Total Fresh Produce** [citation:Welcome to Mambourin Marketplace](https://www.mambourinmarketplace.shopping/). Other nearby amenities include the award-winning **Club Mambourin** with a 25-metre pool and café, plus three major parks [citation:New $60m Mambourin Marketplace nears completion](https://www.buildaustralia.com.au/projects/new-60m-mambourin-marketplace-nears-completion-in-melbournes-west/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Mambourin Marketplace offers several casual, family-friendly dining options. **Smokin' Joe's Pizza & Grill** serves pizzas, slow-cooked meats, and pastas [citation:New Werribee Restaurants at Mambourin Marketplace](https://www.mambourinmarketplace.shopping/news/new-werribee-restaurants-at-mambourin-marketplace). **The Bangkok Express** provides authentic Thai flavours with generous portions and fast service [citation:The Bangkok Express](https://www.mambourinmarketplace.shopping/stores/the-bangkok-express). **Noodle Box** offers cooked-to-order Asian noodle bowls and stir-fries for takeaway [citation:Noodlebox at Mambourin Marketplace](https://www.mambourinmarketplace.shopping/stores/noodlebox).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** For families seeking academic support, **Success Tutoring Mambourin** offers one-on-one diagnostic English and Math assessments for primary and high school students [citation:Mambourin – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-mambourin). 8) **Commute:** Mambourin does not have its own train station. The nearest train station is **Werribee Station** on the **Werribee line**, approximately a 10-minute drive from Mambourin via the Princes Freeway [citation:New Werribee Restaurants at Mambourin Marketplace](https://www.mambourinmarketplace.shopping/news/new-werribee-restaurants-at-mambourin-marketplace). The train journey from Werribee Station to Flinders Street Station typically takes about **35–40 minutes** (not found in provided search snippets, based on standard Metro Trains timetable data). The **Ison Road overpass extension**, set to open in 2026, will improve connectivity to Werribee and the freeway [citation:New $60m Mambourin Marketplace nears completion](https://www.buildaustralia.com.au/projects/new-60m-mambourin-marketplace-nears-completion-in-melbournes-west/).
