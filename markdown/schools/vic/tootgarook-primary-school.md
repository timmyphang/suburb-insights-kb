1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Tootgarook is a working-to-middle-income area with a median weekly household income of $1,294.[citation:2021 Tootgarook, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22550) The median age is 46, older than the Victorian median of 38, and the suburb has a mix of families and older couples, with 69% of homes owner-occupied and a high proportion of couples without children.[citation:2021 Tootgarook, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22550)[citation:Micromarkets](https://www.micromarkets.ai/suburbs/vic/mornington-peninsula/tootgarook-3941) Employment is a mix of blue-collar and white-collar roles, with 88.8% of workers commuting by car.[citation:landchecker.com.au](https://landchecker.com.au/suburb/tootgarook-vic-3941/)

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Tootgarook is overwhelmingly Australian-born; 2006 Census data indicated 48.8% of residents were born in Australia, with the next most common being England at 3.9%, Italy at 3.1%, and Greece at 1.0%.[citation:2006 Tootgarook, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2006/SSC21679) The specific Australian-born percentage for the 2021 Census is not available in the provided search snippets, but 2021 QuickStats show a non-Indigenous population of 92.2%.[citation:2021 Tootgarook, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22550)

3) **Working-parent support:** Tootgarook Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by TheirCare.[citation:TheirCare (OSHC)](https://www.tootps.vic.edu.au/page/231/TheirCare-(OSHC)) The program runs from 7:00am to 8:45am for before-school care, and from 3:15pm to 6:00pm for after-school care on school days.[citation:TheirCare (OSHC)](https://www.tootps.vic.edu.au/page/231/TheirCare-(OSHC))

4) **School culture:** The school offers a highly regarded school band that performs at weekly assemblies and community events, and a weekly choir practice open to all students.[citation:Music Curriculum](http://www.tootps.vic.edu.au/page/75/Music-Curriculum) Students also enjoy lunchtime clubs including STEM Club, Art Club, Sports Club, Hoop Time, Bike Riding, and Band.[citation:Community | Tootgarook Primary School](https://tootps.vic.edu.au/community/) Additionally, students can participate in interschool sport, a school production (held every second year), and instrumental tuition for piano, woodwind, drums, and guitar.[citation:Learning | Tootgarook Primary School](https://tootps.vic.edu.au/learning/)
