1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Somerville is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a broad mix of occupations. The median age is 40, with 18.6% of the population aged 0-14 years, indicating a strong family presence [citation:2021 Somerville (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22305). The median weekly household income is $1,781, slightly below the Victorian median of $1,759, and the suburb has a higher proportion of couples with children than the state average [citation:2021 Somerville (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22305). Employment data shows a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers, with major industries including construction, retail trade, and health care [citation:2021 Somerville (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22305).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Somerville’s population is overwhelmingly Australian-born. In the 2021 Census, 79.8% of residents reported being born in Australia [citation:2021 Somerville (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22305). The most common overseas country of birth is England, at 5.1% of the population, followed by New Zealand at 1.5% [citation:2021 Somerville (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22305).

3) **Working-parent support:** St Brendan’s School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) on-site, operated by TheirCare [citation:St Brendan's Somerville TheirCare | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/st-brendans-somerville-theircare). The service provides before-school care from 6:30 AM to 9:00 AM and after-school care from 2:30 PM to 6:00 PM on school days, as well as vacation care during school holidays [citation:St Brendan's Somerville TheirCare | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/st-brendans-somerville-theircare). Specific hours for vacation care are listed as 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM Monday to Friday [citation:St Brendan's Somerville TheirCare | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/st-brendans-somerville-theircare).
