1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Vermont is an affluent, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,042 and a median age of 40 [citation:ABS QuickStats 2021 Vermont](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22627). The suburb has 3,082 families and an average of 1.8 children per family, indicating a strong family demographic [citation:ABS QuickStats 2021 Vermont](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22627). Vermont is predominantly white-collar, with a high proportion of professionals and managers living in established, leafy streets [citation:Collings Real Estate Vermont South Profile](https://www.collings.com.au/vermont-south-suburb-profile-2026-lifestyle-demographics-market/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: In Vermont, 41.3% of people had both parents born in Australia, while 43.9% had both parents born overseas [citation:2016 ABS QuickStats Vermont](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22614). The most common overseas country of birth is not explicitly stated in the available 2021 QuickStats, but the suburb is highly multicultural with significant communities from China and India as typical for eastern Melbourne.

3) **Working-parent support**: St James' School OSHC is provided by Camp Australia (operated by MACSEYE) and runs before school from 7:00 am to 8:50 am and after school from 3:20 pm to 6:15 pm, Monday to Friday [citation:StartingBlocks St James OSHC](https://startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01JJSAGREFT4RNESF9W3QEYJ6R/St-James-School-OSHC-Vermont) [citation:ACECQA Camp Australia St James](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/camp-australia-st-james-primary-school-vermont-oshc). The service is located at 51 Centre Road, Vermont, and currently holds a 'Working towards National Quality Standard' rating [citation:StartingBlocks St James OSHC](https://startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01JJSAGREFT4RNESF9W3QEYJ6R/St-James-School-OSHC-Vermont).

4) **School culture**: St James offers a range of named extracurriculars including **Marathon Club**, **School Choir**, and an **Instrumental Music Program** (with lessons for various instruments) [citation:School Profile St James](https://sjvermont.catholic.edu.au/schoolprofile/). Additional activities include Gardening Club and Outdoor Education, while specialist classes cover Physical Education, Visual Arts, Performing Arts, Auslan, and STEM [citation:School Profile St James](https://sjvermont.catholic.edu.au/schoolprofile/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Vermont is a leafy, family-friendly suburb with well-regarded schools and ample parklands such as Dandenong Creek Parklands [citation:Collings Real Estate Vermont South Profile](https://www.collings.com.au/vermont-south-suburb-profile-2026-lifestyle-demographics-market/). Local grocery shopping is available at the Vermont South Shopping Centre on Morack Road (including a Coles supermarket) and at The Glen Shopping Centre in nearby Glen Waverley [citation:Collings Real Estate Vermont South Profile](https://www.collings.com.au/vermont-south-suburb-profile-2026-lifestyle-demographics-market/). Specific ethnic supermarkets in the immediate area are not named in the provided sources; however, nearby Forest Hill has Bharat Superstore for Indian groceries [citation:LocalBusinessGuide Vermont South](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/victoria/vermont-south/shopping/supermarkets-and-grocery-stores/).

6) **Weeknight dining**: **Vermont Thai** at 503A Burwood Highway, Vermont South, offers casual Thai takeaway and dine-in with popular dishes like Pad Thai and Massamun Curry [citation:Vermont Thai](https://vermontthai.com.au/) [citation:Best Restaurants Vermont Thai](https://www.bestrestaurants.com.au/vic/melbourne/vermont-south/restaurant/vermont-thai-vermont-south). Other specific family-friendly restaurants in Vermont are not detailed in the provided search results.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: **Fruition Tuition Vermont** at Shop 5, 580-584 Canterbury Road, Vermont, provides personalised tutoring for early childhood through secondary school, including Maths, English, and Science [citation:Fruition Tuition Vermont](http://www.fruition.com.au/location/vermont/). **Top of the Class Private Home Tutoring** also serves the Vermont area, offering one-on-one tutoring tailored to the school curriculum [citation:Top of the Class Vermont](https://topoftheclass.com.au/private-home-tutoring-vermont.html).

8) **Commute**: The nearest train station is **Heatherdale Station** on the **Lilydale/Belgrave lines**, with a travel time of approximately **1 hour 13 minutes** to Flinders Street Station [citation:Rome2Rio Flinders to Vermont](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Vermont-VIC-Australia). Metro Trains operates services every 15 minutes. Alternatively, the **907 bus** from Swanston Street to Mitcham Station takes about 1 hour 22 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio Flinders to Vermont](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Vermont-VIC-Australia).
