1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Sunshine West is a working-class to middle-income suburb with a mixed economy of blue-collar and white-collar workers. The median weekly household income is $1,416, and the median age is 38 years [citation:2021 Sunshine West Census QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/213011338). Families make up a significant portion of the population, with the average household size being 2.8 people [citation:2021 Sunshine West Census QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/213011338).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The dominant overseas country of birth in Sunshine West is India, accounting for 6.5% of residents [citation:Sunshine West Suburb Profile](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunshine-west-vic). A large majority of residents (80.3%) were born in Australia [citation:Sunshine West Suburb Profile](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunshine-west-vic).

3) **Working-parent support:** Ardeer South Primary School provides Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) through the provider TheirCare [citation:Ardeer South Primary School](https://www.ardeersthps.vic.edu.au/). The specific operating hours for the program are not listed in the available sources, but the school's website links to TheirCare's information page [citation:Ardeer South Primary School](https://www.ardeersthps.vic.edu.au/).

4) **School culture:** Ardeer South Primary School offers several named extracurriculars, including a free Breakfast Club for all students from 8:30am to 8:45am [citation:Ardeer South Primary School](https://www.ardeersthps.vic.edu.au/). The school also runs interschool sport initiatives and school camps [citation:Curriculum Framework](https://www.ardeersthps.vic.edu.au/page/252/Curriculum-Framework-). Student leadership opportunities include House Captains for the four school houses (Red, Gold, Green, Blue) [citation:Student Leadership](https://www.ardeersthps.vic.edu.au/page/258/Student-Leadership).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Sunshine West has a diverse, family-oriented feel with a strong multicultural community. Local grocery options include an IGA Everyday supermarket at 25 Talintyre Road [citation:Supermarkets in Sunshine West](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/sunshine-west-vic-3020). For specialty groceries, Tai Phat Asian Grocery at 286b Hampshire Road in neighbouring Sunshine offers fresh Asian produce and groceries, while Spices & Lentils at Shop 50, 324-328 Hampshire Road provides Sri Lankan and Indian grocery items [citation:Tai Phat Asian Grocery](https://www.sunshinebusiness.com.au/business_listing/tai-phat-asian-grocery/) [citation:Supermarkets in Sunshine West](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/sunshine-west-vic-3020).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family dining, Glengala Hot Food Take-Away at 86 Glengala Road offers takeaway meals, while Tallintyre Pizza & Fish at Shop 1, 2 Tallintyre Road serves pizza and fish and chips [citation:Take Away Food in Sunshine West](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/victoria/sunshine-west/food-and-dining/take-away-food/). Bell Street Pizza at 1A Bell Street provides another pizza option for a quick family dinner [citation:Take Away Food in Sunshine West](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/victoria/sunshine-west/food-and-dining/take-away-food/).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Brainworks West Sunshine offers one-to-one tutoring in Maths and English for students from Grade 1 to 10 at the West Sunshine Community Centre, 25 Kermeen Street [citation:Brainworks West Sunshine Tutoring](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-west-sunshine). Ezy Math Tutoring provides in-home or online maths tutoring in Sunshine West [citation:Maths Tutors in Sunshine West](https://www.ezymathtutoring.com.au/tutors/sunshine-west-3020).

8) **Commute:** The nearest train station is Sunshine Station, which is on the Sunbury line. The train journey from Sunshine Station to Flinders Street Station typically takes 24 minutes [citation:Sunshine Station to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Sunshine-Station/Flinders-Street-Station). Trains run frequently, approximately every 15-20 minutes [citation:Sunshine Station to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Sunshine-Station/Flinders-Street-Station).
