1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Mount Waverley is a middle-to-upper-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,066 and a median age of 40 [citation:2021 Mount Waverley, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21816). The suburb has a high proportion of families (9,879 families) and a mix of white-collar and professional residents, with a strong community feel [citation:People & Lifestyle Mount Waverley Vic 3149](https://www.microburbs.com.au/People-Lifestyle/Mount-Waverley). The population density is 2,440 persons per square km [citation:About the profile areas | City of Monash | Community profile](https://profile.id.com.au/monash/about?WebID=150).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The available sources do not provide the specific percentage for the most common overseas country of birth for Mount Waverley. However, the suburb is diverse, with a significant proportion of residents born overseas, and Australian-born residents make up a notable share [citation:2021 Mount Waverley, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21816). For exact figures, refer to the full Census data.

3) **Working-parent support:** Syndal South Primary School’s Outside School Hours Care is provided by TeamKids [citation:Syndal South Primary - Mount Waverley | TeamKids](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/syndal-south-primary-oshc-5/). Before School Care runs from 7:00 am to 9:00 am, After School Care from 3:30 pm to 6:30 pm, and Vacation Care from 7:00 am to 6:30 pm, with fees starting at $21.27 for before-school sessions [citation:Syndal South Primary - Mount Waverley | TeamKids](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/syndal-south-primary-oshc-5/). The program is available for Prep to Year 6 students.

4) **School culture:** Syndal South Primary School offers a range of named extracurricular activities. These include an **Instrumental Music Program** where students can learn guitar, piano, violin, cello, or ukulele in small groups [citation:Instrumental Music Program](https://www.syndalsp.vic.edu.au/page/208/Instrumental-Music-Program). The school also runs a **Gardening Club** and **Interschool Sport** for Years 5 and 6 [citation:Extra Curricular Activities](https://www.syndalsp.vic.edu.au/page/184/Extra-Curricular-Activities). Additional offerings from past annual reports include Chess Club, Coding, and Environmental Club [citation:2019 Annual Report to The School Community](https://www.syndalsp.vic.edu.au/uploaded_files/media/2019_syndal_south_primary_school_annual_report.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Mount Waverley is a family-friendly suburb with several shopping centres, including Pinewood Shopping Centre on Blackburn Road. For specialty groceries, families can visit **LionCity Asian Grocery** at 15 Centreway, Mount Waverley [citation:LionCity Asian Grocery | Mount Waverley VIC](https://www.facebook.com/lioncityasiangrocery/), or **TK Asian Supermarket** for Asian ingredients [citation:TK Asian Supermarket](https://www.menulog.com.au/restaurants-tk-asian-supermarket). Standard supermarkets include Coles Pinewood [citation:Coles Pinewood from local business guide](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/find/grocery-stores/mount-waverley/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Several casual, family-friendly takeaway options are available in Mount Waverley. **Siam Village Thai** offers authentic Thai food for dine-in or takeaway [citation:Restaurants in Mount Waverley,VIC With Takeaway | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/mt-waverley/takeaway-category). **Rossini’s Pizzaria & Cafe** serves pizza and Italian fare, while **The Proud Peacock** provides Vietnamese dishes [citation:Restaurants in Mount Waverley,VIC With Takeaway | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/mt-waverley/takeaway-category). All are located within the suburb and offer takeaway.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Mount Waverley has several tutoring options. **Success Tutoring Mount Waverley** offers personalised support for primary and secondary students [citation:Mount Waverley – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-mount-waverley). **Strive Academy** provides in-person small-group maths tutoring (Years 7–12) with a focus on VCE Maths Methods [citation:Tutors Mount Waverley | VCE Maths & English Tutoring Services - Strive Academy](https://www.striveacademytutoring.com.au/mount-waverley-tutors). Both are located in or near Mount Waverley.

8) **Commute:** Mount Waverley is served by **Mount Waverley Station** on the **Glen Waverley line**. The train ride to Flinders Street Station takes approximately **31 minutes**, with trains running every 20 minutes [citation:Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Mount-Waverley/Flinders-Street-Station). Metro Trains Melbourne operates this service [citation:Glen Waverley | Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/lines/glen-waverley/).
