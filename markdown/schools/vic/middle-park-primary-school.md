1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Middle Park is an older, relatively affluent inner‑suburb with a high median age (45) and a large share of married / professional households — more white‑collar than blue‑collar [citation:ABS QuickStats Middle Park 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21677). The area has fewer children proportionally than outer suburbs and is popular with established families and couples.

2) Most common foreign country of birth  
Most residents were born in Australia; the top overseas birthplace is England (small single‑digit share of residents) — Australia is the dominant birthplace [citation:ABS QuickStats Middle Park 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21677).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC) at this school  
Middle Park Primary runs a before/after school and vacation program through TeamKids — before school and after‑school care plus school holiday (vacation) programs are available [citation:MPPS OSHC (TeamKids)](https://www.middleparkps.vic.edu.au/oosh).

4) School culture — extracurriculars (2–3 specific offerings)  
Music program with a school orchestra and two student “rock bands” (junior & senior), plus private instrumental lessons (piano, violin, flute, drums, guitar) [citation:MPPS Music](https://www.middleparkps.vic.edu.au/music). The school also runs specialist programs and activities such as French, physical education / sport and robotics/camps [citation:MPPS Community Involvement](https://www.middleparkps.vic.edu.au/community-involvement).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (2 sentences)  
Middle Park is a quiet, village‑style bayside suburb with waterfront parks, cafés and small retail strips serving local families. Local grocery options include a village IGA and the Metro Middle Park shopping precinct (market‑style Coles + specialty shops) and smaller specialty greengrocers / produce shops [citation:IGA Middle Park](https://www.iga.com.au/stores/iga-middle-park/) [citation:Metro Middle Park centre](https://metromiddlepark.com.au/).
