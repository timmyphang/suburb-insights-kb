1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Officer is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb. According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income is $2,125, and the median age is 32 years, indicating a young, working-age population [citation:2021 Officer QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22006). The suburb has a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers, with a high proportion of families (couples with children) compared to singles [citation:2021 Officer QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22006). Detailed occupation and industry breakdowns are available in the full ABS Community Profile.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** In Officer, the most common overseas country of birth is India, accounting for 20.5% of residents [citation:2021 Officer QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22006). The proportion of Australian-born residents is 59.5% [citation:2021 Officer QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22006).

3) **Working-parent support:** Kurmile Primary School has Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by Kelly Club Australia. Before school care runs from 6:45 AM to 8:45 AM, and after school care runs from 3:15 PM to 6:15 PM, Monday to Friday during school terms [citation:Kelly Club OSHC Kurmile Primary School](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/kelly-club-oshc-kurmile-primary-school). Vacation care is not listed as currently offered at this service [citation:Kelly Club OSHC Kurmile Primary School](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/kelly-club-oshc-kurmile-primary-school).

4) **School culture:** Kurmile Primary School offers several named extracurricular activities including a "Breakfast Club" and "Kitchen Garden program" as mentioned in school newsletters [citation:E-news Web](https://www.kurmileps.vic.edu.au/enews/details/57). The school also holds a "Swimming Program" during term time [citation:E-news Web](https://www.kurmileps.vic.edu.au/enews/details/57). A "Student Leadership" program is part of the school's strategic plan [citation:School Strategic Plan 2025-2029](https://www.kurmileps.vic.edu.au/source/Kurmile%20Primary%20School%20(5602)%20-%202025-2028%20-%20School%20Strategic%20Plan.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Officer is a growing family suburb with local amenities including the Lakeside Square Shopping Centre. Major supermarkets include Woolworths and Coles, with a specific Asian grocery option being Wanlong Oriental in nearby Pakenham [citation:Wanlong Oriental](https://wanlongoriental.com.au/). There is also a Noone School Wear store at 9 Siding Avenue, Officer, for school uniforms [citation:Kurmile Primary School](https://www.kurmileps.vic.edu.au/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly dining options in Officer include **Dosa Hut** (Indian, at Lakeside Square Shopping Centre, Pakenham, offering takeaway and dine-in) [citation:Dosa Hut Officer](https://www.dosahut.net.au/indian-restaurant-officer/). **Royal Golden Spoon** is another Indian restaurant offering takeaway and catering in the area [citation:Royal Golden Spoon](https://www.royalgoldenspoon.com.au/). Several pizza and takeaway options are also listed on local business guides [citation:Take Away Food Officer](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/victoria/officer/food-and-dining/take-away-food/).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Success Tutoring** has a centre in Officer offering English and Math tutoring for primary and high school students [citation:Success Tutoring Officer](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-officer). **Realus Education** provides online tutoring services with programs in Mathematics, English, and Science, including preparation for NAPLAN and selective school exams [citation:Realus Education](https://realus.com.au/).

8) **Commute:** Officer is serviced by the **Pakenham line** on the Metro Trains network. The journey from Officer Station to Flinders Street Station takes approximately **66 minutes** by direct train, with services running every 20 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio Flinders Street to Officer](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Officer-Station). Driving from Officer to the Melbourne CBD takes about 39 minutes via the M1 freeway [citation:Rome2Rio Flinders Street to Officer](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Officer-Station).
