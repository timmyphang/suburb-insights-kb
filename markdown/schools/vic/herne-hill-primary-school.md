1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Hamlyn Heights is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,643, slightly below the national median of $1,746 [citation:ABS QuickStats 2021 Hamlyn Heights](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21130). The median age is 38, and the suburb has 1,730 families, with a mix of couple families with and without children [citation:ABS QuickStats 2021 Hamlyn Heights](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21130). The majority of homes are separate houses, and most residents travel to work by car (87.7%) [citation:Landchecker Hamlyn Heights profile](https://landchecker.com.au/suburb/hamlyn-heights-vic-3215/).

Before school care runs from 6:45 am to 9:00 am, and after school care runs from 3:30 pm to 6:15 pm [citation:TeamKids Herne Hill Primary](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/herne-hill-primary-hamlyn-heights-oshc-5/). 4) **School culture:** Herne Hill Primary School offers weekly specialist classes in Performing Arts/Auslan (including music, movement, dance, drama, and Australian Sign Language) and Visual Arts [citation:Programs – Herne Hill Primary School](https://hernehillk6.vic.edu.au/programs/). Students can also participate in the State's School Spectacular (massed dancers section) when selected, and a whole-school concert is held every second year in Term 3 [citation:Performing Arts – Herne Hill Primary School](http://hernehillk6.vic.edu.au/programs/performing-arts/).
