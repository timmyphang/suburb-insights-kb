1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Dandenong is a working-class, culturally diverse suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,267, significantly lower than the Greater Melbourne median of approximately $1,750 [citation:2021 Dandenong, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20707). The SEIFA Index of Relative Socio-Economic Disadvantage score for the Dandenong postcode (3175) is approximately 870, placing it in the bottom 10 per cent of Victorian suburbs [citation:Dandenong Suburb Profile 2026 | Demographics & Lifestyle](https://www.collings.com.au/dandenong-suburb-profile-2026-demographics-schools-transport-and-lifestyle/). The median age is 33 years, and household composition leans toward larger family groups, with an average household size of 2.8 persons [citation:2021 Dandenong, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20707).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** According to the 2021 Census, the most common country of birth for Dandenong residents is India, with 19.4% of the population born there [citation:2021 Dandenong, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20707). Australian-born residents account for 37.5% of the population [citation:2021 Dandenong, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20707).
