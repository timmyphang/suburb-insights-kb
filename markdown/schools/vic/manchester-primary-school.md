1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Mooroolbark is a middle-income suburb with a strong family orientation. The 2021 Census shows a median weekly household income of $1,992, and the median age is 37 years [citation:2021 Mooroolbark, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21755). The average household size is 2.75 people, indicating a majority family composition [citation:Population and dwellings | Yarra Ranges Council | Community profile](https://profile.id.com.au/yarra-ranges/population?WebID=190). Specific white-collar vs. blue-collar breakdown is not detailed in the provided search snippets.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Mooroolbark is predominantly Australian-born, with 53.9% of people having both parents born in Australia [citation:2016 Mooroolbark, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21743). The 2021 QuickStats do not list the top overseas countries of birth in the provided snippets, so the specific dominant overseas country and percentage are not available from this search.

3) **Working-parent support:** Manchester Primary School partners with TheirCare to provide outside school hours care on-site [citation:Outside of School Hours Care – Manchester Primary School](https://manchesterps.vic.edu.au/outside-of-school-hours-care/). TheirCare operates Before School Care from 7:00 am to 8:45 am and After School Care from 3:30 pm to 6:30 pm [citation:Outside of School Hours Care – Manchester Primary School](https://manchesterps.vic.edu.au/outside-of-school-hours-care/). Families can contact TheirCare at 1300 072 410 for enrollment and government rebate details.

4) **School culture:** Manchester Primary School offers a Grade 1–6 choir that meets during lunchtime, and provides an instrumental lessons program through an external on-site provider [citation:The Arts – Manchester Primary School](https://manchesterps.vic.edu.au/the-arts/). The school also runs a Buddies Program, pairing Year 6 students with Foundation students for activities like art and craft, outdoor games, and reading [citation:Buddies Program – Manchester Primary School](https://manchesterps.vic.edu.au/buddies-program/). Additionally, the school has a STEAM program integrating Science, Technology, Engineering, Art, and Math [citation:STEAM – Manchester Primary School](https://manchesterps.vic.edu.au/steam/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Mooroolbark has a family-friendly feel with major supermarkets like Coles at 89 Brice Avenue [citation:KKJ Grocery](https://www.mooroolbark.vic.au/listing/moy-asian-grocery/). For specialty groceries, Kow-kah (KKJ) Grocery at 62 Manchester Road offers a wide range of Asian ingredients [citation:MOY ASIAN GROCERY](https://www.yelp.com/biz/moy-asian-grocery-mooroolbark). Masala Pantry is the area's first Indian grocery store, catering to South Asian cooking needs [citation:Masala Pantry](https://www.masalapantry.com.au/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family-friendly takeaway, Flavours of India at 60 Brice Avenue offers Indian delivery from 5:00 pm [citation:Mooroolbark Food Delivery and Takeaway VIC](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/777-thai-take-away). Mooroolbark Pizza & Pasta, at 41 Manchester Road, provides Italian pizzas and pasta ready for delivery or pick-up [citation:Mooroolbark Food Delivery and Takeaway VIC](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/mooroolbark-3138). Sampe's Pizza is also noted as a popular family takeaway option in Mooroolbark [citation:THE 10 BEST Restaurants in Mooroolbark](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g4271274-Mooroolbark_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Success Tutoring has a centre in Mooroolbark, offering personalised academic support [citation:Mooroolbark – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-mooroolbark). Kumon Mooroolbark Education Centre is also available, providing English and mathematics programmes to build fundamental skills [citation:Kumon Mooroolbark Education Centre](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/Kumon-Mooroolbark-VIC). Cluey Learning offers tutoring for Mooroolbark students, with both online and local tutor options [citation:Tutoring Mooroolbark / Top Private Primary & High School Tutors](https://clueylearning.com.au/lts/tutoring-mooroolbark/).

8) **Commute:** Mooroolbark Station is on the Lilydale train line, part of the Metro Trains Melbourne network [citation:Mooroolbark (Station) to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Mooroolbark-Station/Flinders-Street-Station). A direct train from Mooroolbark Station to Flinders Street Station runs every 30 minutes, with a typical travel time of approximately 53 minutes [citation:Mooroolbark (Station) to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Mooroolbark-Station/Flinders-Street-Station). Tickets cost around $4 per trip.
