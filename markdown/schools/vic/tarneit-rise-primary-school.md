Here’s a short, fact‑first briefing on Tarneit Rise Primary School (for families considering enrolment). Each point gives the key numbers/facts and a source.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Tarneit is a young, family‑oriented suburb: median age 30 and average household size ~3.4 people [citation:2021 Tarneit, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22451).  
- Median weekly household income about $2,103 — broadly a middle‑income, growing outer‑suburban area with many young families and homeowners [citation:2021 Tarneit, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22451).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- India is the most common overseas birthplace among Tarneit residents (reported as the top overseas country of birth in local ABS‑derived summaries) [citation:Tarneit VIC Suburb Profile — Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/tarneit).
