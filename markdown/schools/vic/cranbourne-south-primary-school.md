1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Cranbourne South is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,278, and the median age is 35, indicating a relatively young population [citation:2021 Cranbourne South, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20665). There are 941 families and an average of 1.8 children per family with children, confirming a strong family presence [citation:2021 Cranbourne South, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20665). 4) **School culture:** Cranbourne South Primary School offers several named extracurricular programs. The Dance Fever program runs weekly in Term 1, teaching styles such as Hip Hop, Jive, and Tango to all year levels, culminating in a parent performance [citation:Dance Fever | Cranbourne South Primary School](https://cranbournesouth.vic.edu.au/project/dance-fever/). The school also holds a House Athletics Carnival where senior students compete in track and field events while junior students enjoy tabloid sports [citation:Athletics Day | Cranbourne South Primary School](https://cranbournesouth.vic.edu.au/project/athletics-day/). In addition, the school participates in Premiers Active April, a state-wide initiative encouraging 30 minutes of daily physical activity [citation:Archives | Cranbourne South Primary School](https://cranbournesouth.vic.edu.au/2019/03/).
