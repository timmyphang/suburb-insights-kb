1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Ashburton is an affluent, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,743, well above the national median [citation:2021 Ashburton (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/207011146). The median age is 41, and families make up a significant proportion of households, with 2,142 families recorded in the 2021 Census [citation:2021 Ashburton (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/207011146). The suburb has undergone gentrification and is characterised by tree-lined streets, period homes, and a strong sense of community [citation:Ashburton, VIC 3147 Suburb Profile | Buyers Agency | Real Estate - CompoundInvest](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/ashburton/).

3) **Working-parent support:** Solway Primary School’s Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided by TeamKids, operating from a dedicated OSHC Hall on the school grounds [citation:Solway Primary - Ashburton | TeamKids](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/solway-primary-ashburton-oshc-5/) [citation:Solway Primary School | Victoria School Guides](https://victoriaschoolguides.starcommunity.com.au/schools/primary-schools/solway-primary-school/). TeamKids runs before-school care from 7:00 am and after-school care until 6:00 pm, with exact hours not specified in the provided sources [citation:Solway Primary - Ashburton | TeamKids](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/solway-primary-ashburton-oshc-5/). A Holiday Program is also offered during school vacations [citation:Solway Primary School | Victoria School Guides](https://victoriaschoolguides.starcommunity.com.au/schools/primary-schools/solway-primary-school/).

4) **School culture:** Solway Primary School offers a Senior Choir for Years 5 and 6, meeting Friday mornings at 8:00 am to perform at school and community events [citation:Performing Arts](https://www.solwayps.vic.edu.au/page/68/Performing-Arts). The school also has a School Band for Years 5 and 6 students learning a musical instrument [citation:Performing Arts](https://www.solwayps.vic.edu.au/page/68/Performing-Arts). Students participate in Interschool Sport through the Glen Iris District School Sports Association (GIDSSA), competing in sports including football, soccer, netball, cricket, and tennis [citation:Interschool Sport](https://www.solwayps.vic.edu.au/page/67/Interschool-Sport).
