Mount Eliza Primary School — short briefing for families (eight quick facts)

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Mount Eliza is an affluent, family‑oriented bayside suburb: median age 45 and a high share of married/couple households; many households are families with children [citation:2021 Mount Eliza QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21793).  
- Median weekly household income is well above state average (reported about $2,547/week in 2021), supporting an overall white‑collar / professional profile [citation:Mount Eliza VIC suburb profile](https://www.openagent.com.au/suburb-profiles/mount-eliza-3930).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- Most residents are Australian‑born (~74–75%); the largest overseas birthplace is England (about 9% of residents) [citation:Mount Eliza VIC Suburb Profile (country of birth)](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/mount-eliza) [citation:2021 Mount Eliza QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21793).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- Mount Eliza Primary runs school OSHC delivered by Camp Australia (before‑school, after‑school and vacation care listed for the school) — families can book via Camp Australia; the service also appears on the ACECQA register [citation:Camp Australia — Mount Eliza Primary School](https://campaustralia.com.au/school/?accessCode=5FA6) [citation:ACECQA national register listing](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/camp-australia-mount-eliza-primary-school-oshc).

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars (specific)
- Soccer program (Kids Unlimited / KU lunchtime/after‑school sessions at the school) [citation:KU flyer for Mt Eliza Primary (KU extra‑curricular)](https://mtelizaps.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/KU-flyer-for-Mt-Eliza-Primary.pdf).  
- Robotics / coding clubs listed in the school’s extra‑curricular flyers (Kids Unlimited offerings) [citation:KU flyer for Mt Eliza Primary (KU extra‑curricular)](https://mtelizaps.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/KU-flyer-for-Mt-Eliza-Primary.pdf).  
- Performing arts / end‑of‑year concerts (school runs music/performances and public concerts) [citation:Mt Eliza Primary School — newsletter / Facebook posts](https://mtelizaps.vic.edu.au/) [citation:Mt Eliza Primary Facebook End of Year Concert post](https://www.facebook.com/mtelizaps/posts/859383926187975/).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Mount Eliza has a relaxed, seaside village vibe with family parks, a monthly farmers market and a small village shopping strip serving daily family needs [citation:Mount Eliza shopping & markets (Travel Victoria)](https://www.travelvictoria.com.au/mounteliza/shopping/).  
- Main local grocery options include the Mt Eliza village supermarket (Ritchies / Mt Eliza Supermarket) and specialty greengrocers/delis such as Palamara Village Fruits in the village [citation:Ritchies Village (Mt Eliza)](https://www.ritchiesvillage.com.au/) [citation:Palamara Village Fruits — about](https://shop.mtelizavillagefruits.com/aboutus).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 casual, family‑friendly options (call out cuisines)
- Eliza Village Fish & Chips — family‑friendly fish & chips takeaway in the village [citation:Eliza Village Fish & Chips](https://elizavillagefishandchips.au/).  
- Slim’s Hot Pizza / local pizzerias — quick pizza takeaway options in Mt Eliza [citation:Restaurant listings — Mt Eliza (RestaurantGuru)](https://restaurantguru.com/Mount-Eliza).  
- Thai and other casual options available in the village (e.g., local Thai eateries highlighted in local guides) [citation:Mt Eliza takeaway roundup (MELBZ / RestaurantGuru)](https://melbz.com.au/mount-eliza/best-takeaway/) [citation:Restaurant listings — Mt Eliza (RestaurantGuru)](https://restaurantguru.com/Mount-Eliza).

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 local options)
- Alchemy Tuition (private tutoring servicing Mount Eliza / in‑home & online tuition for P–12) [citation:Tutoring Mount Eliza — Alchemy Tuition](https://alchemytuition.com.au/melbourne-tutoring-mount-eliza/).  
- Cloud Tuition (online / local‑area tutoring in English, Maths & Science listing for Mount Eliza) [citation:Cloud Tuition — Mount Eliza location](https://www.cloudtuition.com.au/locations/tutoring-vic/mount-eliza).

8) Commute (train substitution) — estimated travel time to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)
- Mount Eliza has no train station. The usual public route is local bus from Mount Eliza into Frankston, then Frankston → Flinders Street by train. Bus route 785 (Frankston–Mornington via Mt Eliza) connects to Frankston Station; Frankston‑to‑Flinders Street train services are on the Frankston line [citation:785 Frankston Station — Mornington East via Mount Eliza (route page)](https://transport.vic.gov.au/route/19090/785-frankston-station-mornington-east-via-mount-eliza-and-mornington) [citation:Frankston Line timetable (Transport Victoria)](https://transport.vic.gov.au/route/timetable/6/frankston-line).  
- Typical door‑to‑door public travel time is roughly 1 hour to 1 hour 30 minutes depending on bus stop and transfer wait (bus to Frankston ~20–35 min, then Frankston→Flinders Street train ~50–65 min), so plan around 1¼ hours peak‑to‑peak [citation:Journey summaries (Rome2rio) and timetables](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Mount-Eliza/Flinders-Street-Station) [citation:Frankston Line timetable (Transport Victoria)](https://transport.vic.gov.au/route/timetable/6/frankston-line).
