1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Fawkner is a working-to-middle-income suburb with a mix of blue-collar and white-collar workers. The median weekly household income is $1,436, and the median age is 35, reflecting a relatively young population with many families [citation:2021 Fawkner, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/210031237). With 3,394 families and an average of 2.8 people per household, Fawkner has a strong family-oriented character.

3) **Working-parent support:** Moomba Park Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by the YMCA. Before-school care operates Monday to Friday from 7:00 AM to 8:40 AM, and after-school care runs from 3:10 PM to 6:00 PM, with holiday care also available from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM [citation:YMCA - Moomba Park Primary School OSHC | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/ymca-moomba-park-primary-school-oshc). This service is based at the school on Anderson Road and also welcomes families from nearby St Matthew’s Primary School [citation:YMCA - Moomba Park Primary School OSHC](https://childrensprograms.ymca.org.au/outside-school-hours-care/moomba-park-primary-fawkner).

4) **School culture:** Moomba Park Primary School offers a School Choir for students in Grades 1 to 6, which performs at assemblies and in the local community [citation:General 1 — Moomba Park Primary School](https://www.moombaparkps.vic.edu.au/music). The school also runs an Instrumental Program facilitated by Experience Music, allowing students to learn individual instruments. Additionally, students in Grades 5 and 6 attend a camp program featuring activities like canoeing and surfing [citation:Camp Program](https://www.moombaparkps.vic.edu.au/camp-program).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Fawkner offers a community-oriented feel with convenient grocery options. The Friendly Grocer at 94 Lynch Road is open daily from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM for general groceries [citation:Friendly Grocer Fawkner](https://friendlygrocer.com.au/friendly-grocer-fawkner). For specialty items, several Indian grocery stores serve the suburb’s diverse community, providing spices, lentils, and authentic ingredients [citation:Fawkner's Best Indian Grocery Stores](https://www.dlook.com.au/find/indian-grocery-shop-in-fawkner-vic-3060).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Fawkner Pizza at 47 Bonwick Street is a long-standing (since 1975) Italian pizza takeaway option, open from 4:00 PM to midnight daily [citation:FAWKNER PIZZA - Updated 2026 Restaurant Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurant_Review-g4371557-d8565014-Reviews-Fawkner_Pizza-Fawkner_Moreland_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). Fawkner Kebab House offers authentic pizza and kebab takeaway [citation:Fawkner Kebab House](https://fawknerkebabhouse.com.au/). For fish and chips, Delivery Cons Fish and Chips provides casual takeaway delivery to the area [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Fawkner](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/fawkner).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Success Tutoring Fawkner offers one-on-one diagnostic assessments and tutoring in English and Math for primary and high school students [citation:Tutoring Fawkner | Primary & High School Tutors](https://www.successtutoring.com/tutoring-melbourne/fawkner). Spectrum Tuition also has a presence in the Brunswick and Fawkner area, providing tutoring services [citation:Spectrum Tuition Brunswick & Fawkner](https://www.facebook.com/spectrumtuitionBrunswick.Fawkner/).

8) **Commute:** Fawkner Station is on the Upfield train line. A direct train from Fawkner Station to Flinders Street Station takes approximately 31 minutes and runs every 30 minutes [citation:Fawkner (Station) to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Fawkner-Station/Flinders-Street-Station). The service operates daily, providing a reliable commute to the Melbourne CBD.
