1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Williamstown is an affluent, primarily white-collar suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,411 as of the 2021 Census [citation:2021 Williamstown (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22792). The median age is 45, which is notably older than the Victorian median of 38, and the suburb has a high proportion of families with children (e.g., 7.9% of residents are aged 10–14) [citation:2021 Williamstown (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22792). Income has shown steady growth from $1,329 in 2006 to $2,411 in 2021, reflecting increasing prosperity [citation:Williamstown, VIC 3016 Suburb Profile](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/williamstown/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: Over 60% of residents claim English, Scottish, or Irish ancestry, and a notable number of residents have moved from New Zealand [citation:Ancestry & Birthplace in Williamstown — Census profile](https://censusatlas.au/region/sal/SAL51615/williamstown/topic/ancestry-heritage). However, specific overseas-born country percentages are not fully detailed in the available sources; the Australian-born percentage is notably high, given the suburb's strong British Isles heritage.

3) **Working-parent support**: Newmark Primary offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by Extend, located at 126-128 Nelson Pl, Williamstown [citation:Vacancies, Enrol now at Newmark Primary OSHC - Extend](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/30zl8/newmark-primary-oshc-extend-williamstown-3016). Specific hours of operation are not listed in the available sources, so families should contact Extend directly for exact before- and after-school care timings.

4) **School culture**: Newmark Primary emphasises project-based, community-connected learning rather than traditional extracurriculars, but its programs include a multi-day Camp Program for Foundation to Year 6 (ranging from night-time activities to adventure camps) as a central part of the curriculum [citation:Our fees](https://www.newmark.vic.edu.au/fees). The school also offers a scholarship program for families based on income and circumstances [citation:Apply for a Scholarship](https://www.newmark.vic.edu.au/scholarship-application). Additional named extracurricular activities were not explicitly listed on the school's website beyond the Camp Program.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Williamstown offers a coastal, family-friendly vibe with a village atmosphere and access to Port Phillip Bay beaches. For specialty groceries, Tales Of India provides Indian grocery delivery to Williamstown, with free same-day delivery for orders over $49 [citation:Indian Grocery Store Williamstown](https://talesofindia.com.au/pages/indian-grocery-store-williamstown-3016). Wanlong Oriental is an Asian supermarket that serves the broader region, though its physical stores are in Wollongong and Norwa, not directly in Williamstown [citation:Wanlong Oriental](https://wanlongoriental.com.au/).

6) **Weeknight dining**: Casual, family-friendly options in Williamstown include A Day In BKK Thai restaurant, which offers dine-in, delivery, and takeaway [citation:Asian & Thai Food In Williamstown](https://adayinbkk.com.au/williamstown/). Other takeaway options include Pizza d'Asporto Williamstown for Italian and Hot Wings Charcoal Chicken & Salad Bar for burgers [citation:Restaurants in Williamstown,VIC With Takeaway](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/williamstown/takeaway-category). Feeling Thai Cuisine is another Thai takeaway option at 9/71-79 Kororoit Creek Rd [citation:Feeling Thai Cuisine, Williamstown](https://feelingthai.yumbojumbo.com.au/).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Kip McGrath Williamstown (owned by Oskar Balog) offers English and maths tutoring for primary and secondary students, with in-centre sessions at 18 Mallard Drive, Altona North, and operates Monday–Saturday [citation:KMEC Williamstown Tuition Centre](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/williamstown). Success Tutoring also provides primary and high school tutoring in Williamstown, with 24/7 access to academic resources [citation:Tutoring Williamstown](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-williamstown).

8) **Commute**: Williamstown is served by the Williamstown railway line, operated by Metro Trains Melbourne, with stations including Williamstown, Williamstown Beach, and North Williamstown [citation:Williamstown | Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/lines/williamstown/). The train from North Williamstown Station to Flinders Street Station takes approximately 24 minutes [citation:Williamstown to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Williamstown/Flinders-Street-Station). Trains run every 30 minutes throughout the day [citation:Williamstown to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Williamstown/Flinders-Street-Station).
