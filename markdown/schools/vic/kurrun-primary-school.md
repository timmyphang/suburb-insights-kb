Before-school care runs from **6:30am to 8:45am** and after-school care from **3:15pm to 6:30pm**, Monday–Friday during school terms. DS Sports also offers holiday care and curriculum day programs [citation:Outside School Hours Care | Kurrun Primary School](https://kurrunps.vic.edu.au/community/outside-school-hours-care/).
