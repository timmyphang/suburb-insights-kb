1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Spotswood is a middle-to-upper income, family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,460, well above the Victorian median, and the median age is 37, indicating a mature demographic [citation:2021 Spotswood, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22319). The suburb has a high proportion of families, with 775 families recorded and an average of 1.8 children per family with children [citation:2021 Spotswood, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22319). Detailed white-collar vs. blue-collar breakdown was not found in available sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Spotswood is predominantly Australian-born. According to 2021 Census data, 68.6% of residents were born in Australia [citation:2011 Spotswood, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2011/SSC21242). The most common overseas country of birth was England at 3.6%, followed by New Zealand at 2.6% [citation:2011 Spotswood, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2011/SSC21242). More recent 2021 percentages for country of birth were not detailed in the provided snippets, but the 68.6% Australian-born figure suggests a largely locally-born population.

3) **Working-parent support:** St Margaret Mary's School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by Camp Australia. The before-school program runs from 7:00am to 8:30am, and the after-school program runs from 3:15pm to 6:30pm [citation:FAQs - St Margaret Mary’s Catholic Primary School](https://www.smmspotswood.catholic.edu.au/faqs). Camp Australia also operates a holiday program called Rocketeers during school breaks [citation:St Margaret Mary's School | Out of School Hours Care](https://www.smms.catholic.edu.au/our-school/out-of-school-hours-care).
