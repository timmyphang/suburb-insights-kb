1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Thomastown is a working-class suburb with a mix of blue-collar and white-collar residents. The median weekly household income is $1,225, and the median age is 39 years old, slightly older than the national median of 38 [citation:2021 Thomastown, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22504). Families are common, with 5,580 families recorded and an average household size of 2.6 people [citation:2021 Thomastown, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22504).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas birthplace in Thomastown is India, accounting for 11.8% of residents [citation:GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/thomastown-vic). Australian-born residents make up 73.9% of the population [citation:GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/thomastown-vic).

3) **Working-parent support:** Thomastown Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by Camp Australia [citation:Camp Australia](https://campaustralia.com.au/school/?accessCode=7F0E&doing_wp_cron=1731136601.0624060630798339843750). Specific operating hours for the program were not found in the available sources.

4) **School culture:** Thomastown Primary School offers a range of named extracurriculars, including Breakfast Club (every Monday and Thursday), Interschool Sports (Terms 2 & 4), and a Soccer club (held every lunch time) [citation:Extra Curricular Activities and Additional Services](https://www.thomastownps.vic.edu.au/page/121/Extra-Curricular-Activities-and-Additional-Services). Other activities include LEGO Club, Drama club, and Art club [citation:Extra Curricular Activities and Additional Services](https://www.thomastownps.vic.edu.au/page/121/Extra-Curricular-Activities-and-Additional-Services).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Thomastown is a culturally diverse suburb with a mix of residential and industrial areas, offering many specialty grocers. For Indian and Asian groceries, families can visit Kiren Indian/Asian Groceries at 12 Wood St or Shri Ram Groceries at 175 High St [citation:THE BEST 10 Supermarkets and Grocery Stores in Thomastown, Victoria](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/victoria/thomastown/shopping/supermarkets-and-grocery-stores/). Mainstream supermarkets include Supa IGA at 122 Main St and Woolworths at the corner of High and Hurtle Streets [citation:THE BEST 10 Supermarkets and Grocery Stores in Thomastown, Victoria](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/victoria/thomastown/shopping/supermarkets-and-grocery-stores/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Casual family-friendly takeaway options in Thomastown include Taste of Thai (Thai cuisine) at 260-268 High St, and Royal Restaurant and Cafe (Middle Eastern cuisine) at 287 High St [citation:TASTE OF THAI, Thomastown](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurant_Review-g8177999-d12900013-Reviews-Taste_of_Thai-Thomastown_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html) [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Thomastown](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/thomastown). For Indian food, Dosa Hut in nearby Mill Park at Shop R10, 415 McDonalds Road offers takeaway and delivery [citation:Indian Restaurant near Thomastown](https://www.dosahut.net.au/indian-restaurant-thomastown/).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Tutoring options in Thomastown include Spectrum Tuition, which offers Maths and English tutoring, and Fruition Tuition Centre, which provides after-school tutoring for all levels and subjects [citation:English & Maths Tutor Thomastown](https://spectrumtuition.com/thomastown/) [citation:Private Tutors Thomastown](https://www.fruition.com.au/tutoring-thomastown/). Both are accessible for local families.

8) **Commute:** Thomastown is served by the Mernda train line via Thomastown Station. The average train travel time from Thomastown Station to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD is 35 minutes, with trains running every 15 minutes [citation:Thomastown (Station) to Flinders Street Station Trains](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Thomastown-Station/Flinders-Street-Station).
