1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Seaford is a middle-income suburb with a mix of families and singles. The median weekly household income is $1,500, and the median age is 40 [citation:2021 Seaford (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/214011375). The suburb has a slightly higher proportion of females (51%) than males (49%), and average household size is 2.3 people, indicating a family-friendly demographic [citation:2021 Seaford (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/214011375).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Seaford is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 83.0% of residents born in Australia [citation:Seaford, VIC 3198 - Suburb Profile](https://www.inthesuburbs.com.au/suburb/seaford-vic-3198.html). The most common overseas birthplace is England, though a specific percentage for England is not found in available sources for the Victorian Seaford; the South Australian Seaford reports England at 15.1% [citation:Seaford SA — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026)](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/seaford-sa).

3) **Working-parent support:** Seaford Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by Camp Australia [citation:Camp Australia - Seaford Primary School OSHC](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/camp-australia-seaford-primary-school-oshc). Specific hours of operation are not listed in available sources, but typical Camp Australia programs run before and after school.

4) **School culture:** Seaford Primary School offers a range of named extracurriculars including the Breakfast Club, Excel Keyboard and Guitar lessons, and the School Choir participating in the State School Spectacular [citation:Principal's Welcome - Seaford Primary School](https://www.seafordps.vic.edu.au/page/76/). The school also runs a LEAP program and Interschool Sport [citation:Principal's Welcome - Seaford Primary School](https://www.seafordps.vic.edu.au/page/76/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Seaford has a coastal, family-friendly vibe with parks and bay access. For groceries, locals can shop at Ritchies Supermarket at 280 Seaford Road [citation:Seaford Supermarket - Ritchies](https://seaford.shop.ritchies.com.au/) and Woolworths Seaford [citation:Seaford, VIC | Woolworths](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/storelocator/vic-seaford-3176). 6) **Weeknight dining:** Families can enjoy Thai cuisine at Seaford Thai Takeaway (3a/366 Frankston-Dandenong Rd) [citation:Home » Seaford Thai Takeaway](https://seafordthai.com/) or Thai Beach Cafe (delivery and pick-up) [citation:Thai Beach Cafe - Food delivery - Seaford](https://www.thaibeachseaford.com.au/).
