3) Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is offered at Rangebank Primary School, operated by Rangebank Primary School OSHC (Tatoah Pty Ltd) [citation:OSHC | Rangebank Primary School](https://rangebankps.vic.edu.au/oshc). Before school care runs from 6:30am to 8:45am, and after school care from 3:15pm to 6:30pm on school days. Vacation care and curriculum day care are also available from 7:00am to 6:00pm [citation:Rangebank Primary School OSHC - ChildcareDB](http://www.childcaredb.com.au/service/10245/rangebank_primary_school_oshc).

5) Cranbourne offers a range of family amenities including major shopping centres like Cranbourne Park and local specialty grocers. For ethnic groceries, families can visit Wirsa Indian Grocery for Indian and Asian flavours [citation:Indian flavours at Our Cranbourne Grocery Store](https://wirsa.com.au/indian-grocery-store-cranbourne/), Mrs Wans Asian Mart for a wide variety of South-East Asian items [citation:Mrs Wans Asian Mart](https://mrswans.com.au/), and Kuya JJ Asian Grocery for Filipino and Asian treats [citation:Kuya JJ Asian Grocery - Cranbourne West](https://cranbournewestshoppingcentre.com.au/store/kuya-jj-asian-grocery/). 7) Several tutoring and enrichment centres operate in Cranbourne. 8) Cranbourne is served by the Cranbourne Line railway. The nearest station is Cranbourne Station, with trains running approximately every 30 minutes to Melbourne CBD. The estimated travel time from Cranbourne Station to Flinders Street Station is about 1 hour 6 minutes [citation:Train Cranbourne Station to Flinders Street Station from $3 | Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Cranbourne-Station/Flinders-Street-Station).
