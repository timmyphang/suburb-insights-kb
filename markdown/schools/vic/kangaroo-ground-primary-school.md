1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Kangaroo Ground is an affluent, family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $2,909, well above the Victorian median, and the median age is 44, with a high proportion of families (312 families out of 1,208 residents) [citation:2021 Kangaroo Ground, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21294). The average household size is 3.1 people, and 59.05% of residents are married, indicating a strong family demographic [citation:htag](https://www.htag.com.au/vic/vic216-nillumbik-shire/kangaroo-ground-vic-3097/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: Not found in available sources. The 2021 QuickStats page does not include a country-of-birth table in the provided content. However, 2016 data shows 60.1% of people had both parents born in Australia [citation:2016 Kangaroo Ground, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21286). The 2021 data indicates 94.0% of residents are non-Indigenous, but specific overseas-born percentages are not listed.

3) **Working-parent support**: Kangaroo Ground Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) run by the school's own Kangaroo Ground Primary Combined OSHC program. Before School Care operates from 7:15 am to 8:45 am, and After School Care runs from 3:20 pm to 6:00 pm (with adjusted hours on early dismissal days) [citation:OSHC — Kangaroo Ground Primary School](http://www.kangaroogroundps.vic.edu.au/ohsc). The program is held in the community hall next to the pre-school.

4) **School culture**: The school culture is enriched by several named extracurricular activities. Students can join the Chess Club, which has seen eight students reach state finals, and the Rock Band, where senior students perform as a band under a private music teacher [citation:History](http://www.kangaroogroundps.vic.edu.au/history) and [citation:Rock Band](http://www.kangaroogroundps.vic.edu.au/rock-band). Additionally, the school offers an extensive Instrumental Music Program covering instruments such as piano, guitar, drums, and trumpet [citation:Instrumental music](http://www.kangaroogroundps.vic.edu.au/instrumental-music/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Kangaroo Ground is a rural suburb with limited local shopping. For groceries, residents typically travel to nearby Eltham, which has major supermarkets like Woolworths and Coles. Specialty ethnic grocers are not found in the immediate suburb; the nearest Asian supermarkets are in Eltham or Greensborough, but specific names are not listed in provided sources.

6) **Weeknight dining**: For weeknight dining, Kangaroo Ground has a few casual options. The Kangaroo Ground General Store offers takeaway and cafe-style meals [citation:TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/RestaurantsNear-g6158019-d10130490-Massaros-Kangaroo_Ground_Yarra_Valley_Victoria.html). Fondata 1872, located 1.6 km away, serves Italian cuisine in a rustic setting, and Inglewood Estate, 1.9 km away, offers cafe and international fare [citation:TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/RestaurantsNear-g6158019-d10130490-Massaros-Kangaroo_Ground_Yarra_Valley_Victoria.html). All are family-friendly.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Tutoring and enrichment options include Success Tutoring Kangaroo Ground, which provides personalised English and Math tutoring mapped to the Australian Curriculum [citation:Success Tutoring Kangaroo Ground](https://www.successtutoring.com/tutoring-melbourne/kangaroo-ground/). Additionally, Ezy Math Tutoring offers in-home and online maths tutoring with a network of local tutors, including a seasoned primary school teacher [citation:Ezy Math Tutoring](https://www.ezymathtutoring.com.au/tutors/kangaroo-ground-3097).

8) **Commute**: Kangaroo Ground does not have a train station. The nearest station is Eltham on the Hurstbridge line, approximately 8 km away. The travel time from Eltham to Flinders Street is about 35 minutes. Bus route 578 connects Kangaroo Ground to Eltham station, but specific timetables are not provided in available sources. [citation:Metro Trains Timetable](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/timetables/) provides general timetable information.
