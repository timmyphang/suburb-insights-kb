1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Greensborough is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb. The 2021 Census recorded a median weekly household income of $2,075 and a median age of 41 [citation:2021 Greensborough, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21104). Families made up 6,043 of the 8,355 private dwellings, with children aged 0–14 years comprising 18.6% of the population [citation:2021 Greensborough, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21104). The workforce is predominantly white-collar, with car as driver being the main mode of travel to work (63.7% in 2016) [citation:2016 Greensborough, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21096).
