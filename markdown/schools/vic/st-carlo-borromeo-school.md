### 1) Suburb socio-economic profile
Greenvale is a middle-to-upper income, family-oriented suburb. 97% of dwellings are detached houses and 72% have 4+ bedrooms, with 55% of homes under a mortgage and only 12.5% rented. The median weekly household income is $2,262 (87.1st percentile) and the median house price is $920,000, reflecting a predominantly owner-occupier, mortgage-led market [citation:DA Leads Australia](https://daleads.com.au/suburbs/greenvale-vic-3059/).

### 2) Most common foreign country of birth
Specific data on the dominant overseas country of birth is not available in the provided search results. However, 34.8% of Greenvale residents were born overseas, well above the national average of 21.6% [citation:DA Leads Australia](https://daleads.com.au/suburbs/greenvale-vic-3059/).

### 3) Working-parent support – OSHC
St Carlo Borromeo School offers on-site Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) through MACSEYE, operating before school from 7:00 am and after school until 6:00 pm, Monday to Friday. Younger children are escorted to/from care [citation:St Carlo Borromeo Primary School - Before & After School Care](https://www.scgreenvale.catholic.edu.au/before-after-school-care).

### 4) School culture – extracurriculars
The school provides a range of extracurricular activities including a performing arts program, school choir, student leadership team, and student-led assemblies. Italian is taught to all classes, and a Perceptual Motor Program is offered [citation:MACS School Information](https://www.macs.vic.edu.au/SchoolInformation.aspx?School_ENumber=E1347).

### 5) Suburb vibe and groceries
Greenvale is a car-dependent family suburb with a Coles supermarket and several local grocers. Ethnic specialty options include Philmart Asian Groceries in nearby Roxburgh Park and online Indian grocery delivery via Tales of India [citation:Yellow Pages](https://yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/greenvale-vic-3059) [citation:Tales of India](https://talesofindia.com.au/pages/indian-grocery-store-greenvale-3059).

### 6) Weeknight dining
Casual, family-friendly takeaway options include Jessie’s Pizza (Greenvale), Tek Kebab, and Greenvale Charcoal Chicken. Don Luigis Pizza Spaghetti Bar in nearby Gladstone Park is also popular [citation:TuckerFox](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/greenvale).

### 7) Tuition and enrichment
New Era Education, located a 10-minute drive away in Mickleham, offers small-group and one-on-one tutoring with VIT-registered teachers in English and maths [citation:New Era Education](https://neweralearning.com.au/tutoring/greenvale). Other tutoring services (e.g., Success Tutoring) also serve Greenvale.

### 8) Commute
Greenvale does not have a train station. The typical journey to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street) involves a bus to Broadmeadows or Craigieburn station then a train, taking approximately 1 hour 8 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Greenvale/Flinders-Street-Station).
