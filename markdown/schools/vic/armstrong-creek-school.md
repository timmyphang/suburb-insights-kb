Brief family-facing eight‑point briefing — Armstrong Creek School (Armstrong Creek, VIC)

1) Suburb socio-economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Median age 30 with a large share of young children (11% aged 0–4) and ~3,033 families — a clearly family‑oriented, fast‑growing suburb. Median weekly household income reported $2,200, and average household size ~2.9 people, consistent with middle‑to‑upper‑middle income young family demographics in a growth area [citation:ABS QuickStats Armstrong Creek 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20068) and local growth‑area description [citation:Armstrong Creek (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armstrong_Creek%2C_Victoria).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- Armstrong Creek is predominantly Australian‑born (~89% reported born in Australia in 2021); the largest overseas birthplace reported is India (about 5–6% of residents) [citation:Suburb demographics summary (uses ABS 2021)](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/armstrong-creek-vic) [citation:ABS QuickStats Armstrong Creek 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20068).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- Armstrong Creek School partners with the YMCA to provide on‑site Before & After School Care and vacation care (YMCA OSHC at the school; address listed as 100 Central Blvd) — before‑school and after‑school sessions are published on the YMCA/StartingBlocks listings [citation:YMCA program listing](https://childrensprograms.ymca.org.au/children-program/children/home/outside-school-hours-care/armstrong-creek-school) [citation:School OSHC page (school site)](https://www.armstrongcreekschool.vic.edu.au/page/45/YMCA-School-Care).

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars (specific)
- Performing Arts: Dance, Drama and Music program for all students. Instrumental/ensemble opportunities are noted in the performing‑arts program [citation:Performing Arts (school)](https://www.armstrongcreekschool.vic.edu.au/page/9/Performing-Arts).
- Sport / Physical Education: regular PE program and school team sport activities (fundamental movement and team sport focus) [citation:Physical Education (school)](https://www.armstrongcreekschool.vic.edu.au/page/14/Physical-Education).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Armstrong Creek (Warralily town centre) is a new growth‑area neighbourhood with family amenities: a local shopping centre, medical and community services and parks — a typical suburban family hub. Grocery options include a full‑line Woolworths at the Village Warralily centre plus local specialty greengrocers and ethnic outlets such as “Armstrong Creek Fresh Fruit Market” and an Indian grocer (Guru’s Indian Groceries) in the town centre [citation:Village Warralily shopping centre](https://villagewarralily.com.au/) [citation:Guru’s Indian Groceries listing](https://home-co.com.au/armstrong-creek-town-centre/gurus-indian-groceries-and-sweets).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 family‑friendly takeaways / casual options
- Village Warralily options include Town & Country Pizza (family pizza), Kebab & Burger Spot (fast casual kebabs/burgers) and sushi/Asian takeaway options listed in the centre directory — family‑friendly takeaway choices and a few café/dine‑in venues nearby [citation:Eat‑in & Takeaway Food — Village Warralily directory](https://villagewarralily.com.au/store-categories/eat-in-takeaway-food/).

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 centres)
- Learning Works Hub offers local primary Maths & English tutoring for Armstrong Creek students (tailored one‑to‑one support) [citation:Learning Works Hub tutoring Armstrong Creek](https://www.learningworkshub.com.au/tutoring-armstrong-creek/).
- Kumon previously opened an Armstrong Creek centre (now operating/relocated to nearby Charlemont/Charlemont Rise shopping precinct per the Kumon announcement) — local Kumon/charlemont centre options for maths/reading are available to nearby families [citation:Kumon Armstrong Creek / Charlemont notice](https://au.kumonglobal.com/kumon-opening-soon-in-armstrong-creek-vic/).

8) Commute — train time to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)
- Armstrong Creek has no rail station. Realistic public‑transport route: take local bus Route 45 from Armstrong Creek/Warralily to Waurn Ponds Station, then V/Line train from Waurn Ponds to Melbourne (Southern Cross). Typical Waurn Ponds → Southern Cross V/Line trip is about 1h 15–1h 20 (approx. 75–80 minutes); add ~5–10 minutes to reach Flinders Street from Southern Cross — full travel time roughly 1 hr 25 min to 1 hr 30 min door‑to‑door on public transport. (Substituted bus+regional train because there is no Armstrong Creek train station.)
