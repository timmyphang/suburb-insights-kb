Below is a concise, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Melbourne Rudolf Steiner School (Warranwood). Each item uses one focused web source (snippet‑first); sources are cited inline and collected at the end.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile
- Warranwood is relatively affluent / family‑oriented with a higher median household income and older median age (median age 41) — many married households and family households dominate the suburb [citation:2021 Warranwood, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of ...](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22704) [citation:Warranwood Property Market and Trends | OpenAgent](https://openagent.com.au/suburb-profiles/warranwood-3134).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- The suburb is overwhelmingly Australian‑born (≈80%); the largest overseas birthplace is England (~3.9%) — no large single non‑Australian community dominates [citation:Warranwood VIC Suburb Profile — Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/warranwood) [citation:2021 Warranwood, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of ...](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22704).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- Melbourne Rudolf Steiner School runs on‑site Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) at 213 Wonga Rd with before‑ and after‑school sessions (afternoon OSHC listed 15:00–18:00; fees/sessions listed on StartingBlocks) [citation:Melbourne Rudolf Steiner School OSHC - StartingBlocks.gov.au](https://www.startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01HGCQXNP2VGCSEGMWEEC1JGGF/Melbourne-Rudolf-Steiner-School-OSHC).

4) School culture / extracurriculars
- Key extras: an active Instrumental Music program (tuition, ensembles and instrument hire), singing/choirs and regular music/performances; plus a substantial outdoor‑education program (camping, bush/nature programs) [citation:Music – MRSS](https://mrss.vic.edu.au/education/music-and-the-arts/) [citation:MRSS – Melbourne Rudolf Steiner School](https://mrss.vic.edu.au/).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries
- Warranwood is a quiet, leafy, family suburb (close to native bushland and small local cafés) with larger supermarket/food options in nearby Ringwood/Croydon shopping centres. For specialty/ethnic groceries you’ll typically go to nearby Ringwood (e.g., Soon Lee Asian Grocery) or Croydon Central which hosts Asian grocers and major supermarkets [citation:Warranwood — Complete Local Guide | MELBZ](https://melbz.com.au/warranwood/suburb-guide/) [citation:Stores - Croydon Central](https://www.croydoncentral.com.au/stores) [citation:Soon Lee Asian Grocery - Asian Grocery Shop in Ringwood, VIC 3134 - dlook](https://www.dlook.com.au/business/asian-grocery-shop-in-ringwood-vic-3134/soon-lee-asian-grocery).

6) Weeknight dining (casual / family takeaway)
- Local family options include Warranwood General Store Café (café/ take‑away, casual dining) and Parkwood Fish & Chips (casual takeaway); more variety is a short drive to Ringwood/Croydon for Asian and multicultural takeaways [citation:Warranwood General Store Cafe | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurant/warranwood-general-store-cafe-87188) [citation:Parkwood Fish and Chips, Warranwood - RestaurantGuru](https://restaurantguru.com/Parkwood-Fish-and-Chips-Park-Orchards).

7) Tuition and enrichment (nearby)
- Nearby options include Kip McGrath Ringwood (maths & English tuition centre) and Kumon Mooroolbark (Kumon maths/English programme); private tutor networks also list tutors available in Warranwood [citation:KMEC Ringwood Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/ringwood) [citation:Kumon Mooroolbark Education Centre | Kumon Australia](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-MOOROOLBARK-VIC).

8) Commute (train/bus to CBD)
- Warranwood has no train station; commuters typically catch a local bus into Ringwood then a train to Flinders Street. Ringwood → Flinders St train services typically take about 33–41 minutes (fastest ~33 min); add a bus ride to Ringwood (~10–20 min depending on stop), so expect roughly 50–65 minutes door‑to‑door to the CBD by public transport [citation:Warranwood Rd/Wonga Rd - Transport Victoria](https://transport.vic.gov.au/stop/24646/warranwood-rdwonga-rd/2/bus) [citation:Ringwood to Flinders Street Station - 4 ways to travel via train (Rome2rio)](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Ringwood-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
