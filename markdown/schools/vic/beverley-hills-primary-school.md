Here’s a concise, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Beverley Hills Primary School (Doncaster East). I used one primary source per data point where possible and opened pages only when needed — citations follow each fact.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
- Doncaster East is generally middle‑to‑upper income with a median age of 41 and a high share of married/family households (58.8% married). Median weekly household income: about $1,792/week (2021 Census). [citation:2021 Doncaster East QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20772)

2) Most common overseas birthplace (suburb)  
- The largest overseas birthplace is China — roughly 15% of residents (Australia is the largest single birthplace at ~45%). [citation:2021 Doncaster East QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20772)

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
- On‑site OSHC (before, after school and vacation care) is available at Beverley Hills PS run by TeamKids (Before 7:00–9:00; After 3:15–6:30; Vacation care 7:00–18:00). [citation:Beverley Hills Primary - TeamKids venue page](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/beverley-hills-primary-doncaster-east-oshc-6/)

4) School culture / extracurriculars  
- Active music program: school choir plus recorder program and a whole‑school concert. [citation:Music — Beverley Hills Primary School](https://www.bevhillsps.vic.edu.au/music.html)  
- Strong sport/PE program: participation in district sports (rounders, cricket, volleyball), swimming carnival and external clinics (Hockey Victoria, local football club, Collingwood FC). [citation:PE — Beverley Hills Primary School](https://www.bevhillsps.vic.edu.au/pe.html)

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (2 sentences)  
- Doncaster East is a family‑oriented, residential suburb with local shopping precincts (Tunstall Square) and larger retail at nearby Westfield Doncaster. [citation:2021 Doncaster East QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20772)  
- Notable specialty/ethnic grocers nearby include KFL Supermarkets (Asian food specialist) and local Asian grocers listed in Doncaster East (No.1 Asian Supermarket / Prosperity Asian Grocery); Westfield Doncaster also hosts Asian food retailers. [citation:KFL Supermarkets Doncaster East](https://www.kflsupermarkets.com.au/doncaster-east-vic) [citation:No.1 Asian Supermarket listing](https://australia.chamberofcommerce.com/business-directory/victoria/doncaster-east/asian-grocery-store/4798680-no-1-asian-supermarket) [citation:Shining Asia — Westfield Doncaster](https://www.westfield.com.au/doncaster/store/4UVzopNU92QW2e0iMQy8SM/shining-asia)

6) Weeknight dining (casual, family‑friendly)  
- Tunstall Square options: Saporito (Italian), Ikoi Cafe (Japanese/sushi/cafe) and Grill’d (burgers) are easy family choices for takeaway or casual meals. [citation:Saporito Ristorante Italiano](https://www.saporitorestaurant.com.au/) [citation:Ikoi Cafe](https://ikoicafe.com.au/) [citation:Grill'd Tunstall Square](https://grilld.com.au/restaurants/victoria/tunstall-square)

7) Tuition and enrichment near the school  
- Local tuition options include Kumon Doncaster East (English & maths worksheets/program) and Kip McGrath / other tutoring centres in Doncaster servicing primary students. [citation:Kumon Doncaster East centre listing](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-DONCASTER-EAST-VIC) [citation:Kip McGrath Doncaster tuition centre](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/doncaster)

8) Commute to Melbourne CBD (train vs bus) — substitution note  
- Doncaster East has no suburban train station; the realistic public‑transport option to the CBD is bus (SmartBus 907 / Doncaster Rd corridor). Typical bus travel time to Flinders Street is about 40–45 minutes depending on traffic; timetabled SmartBus services run frequently along Doncaster Rd. [citation:907 SmartBus route (PTV)](https://transport.vic.gov.au/route/16482/907-city-mitcham-via-eastern-fwy-and-doncaster-rd) [citation:Doncaster East → Flinders St time estimate (Rome2rio)](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Doncaster-East/Flinders-Street-Station)  
- (Council advocacy pages also note there is currently no train/tram serving Doncaster/Doncaster East.) [citation:Manningham Council - rail advocacy / no current train](https://www.manningham.vic.gov.au/about-council/strategies-and-action-plans/advocacy/suburban-rail-loop-srl)
