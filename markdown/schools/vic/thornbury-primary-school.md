1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Thornbury is a middle-income, predominantly white-collar suburb with a strong family focus. The median weekly household income is $1,971, and the median age is 37, reflecting a mix of young families and established residents [citation:2021 Thornbury QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22508). A significant proportion of residents work in professional, healthcare, education, retail, and creative industries [citation:CompoundInvest Thornbury Profile](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/thornbury/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** According to the most recent available data (2016 Census), the suburb is moderately diverse: the most common overseas country of birth is Greece (4.5%), followed by Italy (3.5%) [citation:2016 Thornbury QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22496). Australian-born residents make up a substantial portion, with 38.6% of people having both parents born in Australia [citation:2016 Thornbury QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22496).

3) **Working-parent support:** Thornbury Primary School’s Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided by TeamKids. The school’s website directs families to book children into the “TEAM Kids” program for before and after school care [citation:Thornbury Primary School Information](https://thornburyps.vic.edu.au/information). Specific hours of operation are not listed in the available sources, but the service is listed as “Before School Care” and “After School Care” on the CareforKids directory [citation:CareforKids Thornbury Primary School Combined OSHC](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/w7m8e/thornbury-primary-school-combined-oshc-3071).

4) **School culture:** Thornbury Primary School offers a range of named extracurricular activities. Lunchtime clubs include **Choir**, **Band**, and **Basketball** [citation:Thornbury Primary School Learning](https://thornburyps.vic.edu.au/learning) [citation:Thornbury Primary School Wellbeing](https://thornburyps.vic.edu.au/wellbeing). The school also runs a **Winter Solstice Lantern Walk** and a **Parent Battle of the Bands** event, which involve the school community [citation:Thornbury Primary School Home](https://thornburyps.vic.edu.au/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Thornbury has a family-friendly, village-like atmosphere with several local grocery options. For everyday shopping, there is **IGA Thornbury** at 404 Station Street and **Thornbury Food-way** at 650-654 High Street [citation:Yellow Pages Supermarkets Thornbury](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/thornbury-vic-3071). For specialty groceries, **Just Spice** offers Indian and Sri Lankan products, and **CERES Fair Food** delivers organic and local produce [citation:Indian Grocery Shops Melbourne](https://singh.com.au/indian-grocery-stores-in-melbourne/) [citation:Yellow Pages Supermarkets Thornbury](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/thornbury-vic-3071).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Families can choose from several casual, family-friendly takeaway options. **Farro Pizzeria Thornbury** (Italian pizza) is a popular choice, as is **Hecho en Mexico** (Mexican) for tacos and burritos [citation:Uber Eats Thornbury](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/thornbury-melbourne-vic). **Croxton Roosters** offers roast chicken and sides, ideal for a quick weeknight meal [citation:Uber Eats Thornbury](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/thornbury-melbourne-vic).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** For academic support, **Success Tutoring Thornbury** provides personalised English and Maths assessments and tutoring [citation:Success Tutoring Thornbury](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-thornbury). Another option is **Kumon Northcote Education Centre** (542 High Street, Northcote), which offers after-school Maths and English programs [citation:Yellow Pages Tuition Tutoring Thornbury](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/tuition-tutoring/thornbury-vic-3071).

8) **Commute:** Thornbury is served by **Thornbury Station** on the **Mernda line**. The train journey from Thornbury Station to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD takes approximately **21 minutes** [citation:Rome2Rio Thornbury to Flinders Street](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Thornbury-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). Trains run every 20 minutes during peak times [citation:Rome2Rio Thornbury to Flinders Street](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Thornbury-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
