Here’s a short, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Edithvale Primary School (Edithvale, VIC). Each item is fact‑first; sources are cited inline and listed at the end.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Edithvale is a bayside, established family suburb with a median age of 40 and a relatively high median weekly household income of $2,101 (ABS 2021) — overall a middle-to-upper‑middle, commuter/white‑collar family area rather than a working‑class zone [citation:2021 Edithvale QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20848).

2) Most common overseas country of birth  
Most residents are Australian‑born (≈77%); the largest overseas birthplace is England (about 4% of residents) in 2021 [citation:2021 Edithvale QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20848).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
Edithvale Primary runs before‑school, after‑school and holiday programs via TeamKids — before school 6:45–8:45, after school 3:30–6:15, plus vacation care (bookings/fees via TeamKids) [citation:Before & After Care — Edithvale PS](https://edithvaleps.vic.edu.au/before-and-after-care/) [citation:TeamKids venue — Edithvale Primary OSHC](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/edithvale-primary-oshc-6/).

4) School culture / extracurriculars  
The school runs a comprehensive Performing Arts program (Drama, Dance, Music) with classroom percussion, ukulele and guitar and a whole‑school production; the site also highlights sport and other optional extra‑curricular activities [citation:Music & Performing Arts — Edithvale PS](https://edithvaleps.vic.edu.au/music-performing-arts/) [citation:Edithvale Primary homepage](https://edithvaleps.vic.edu.au/).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries  
Edithvale is a small beachside, family‑oriented suburb with local shops along Nepean Hwy and easy access to coastal reserves — popular with families and commuters. Local grocery options include IGA / IGA Local Grocer and a Friendly Grocer on Nepean Hwy, plus small greengrocer/community outlets [citation:Edithvale — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edithvale) [citation:IGA Local Grocer Edithvale](https://www.iga.com.au/stores/iga-local-grocer-edithvale/) [citation:Friendly Grocer Edithvale](https://friendlygrocer.com.au/friendly-grocer-edithvale).

6) Weeknight dining (casual, family‑friendly)  
Nearby family takeaway/dinner spots include Edithvale Fish & Chips (classic fish’n’chips), Saloon Barcade / pizza options, and Fairy Bird (Chinese/Malaysian‑style takeaway) — all suitable for quick weeknight meals [citation:Edithvale Fish & Chips listing](https://australia.chamberofcommerce.com/business-directory/victoria/edithvale/fish-chips-restaurant/3607137-edithvale-fish-chips) [citation:Saloon Barcade menu](https://saloonbarcade.com.au/menu/) [citation:Fairy Bird menu](https://www.fairybird-edithvale.com.au/menu).
