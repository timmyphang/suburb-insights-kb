Welcome to Kedron

Kedron feels like a well-kept secret for families who want inner‑city convenience without the chaos. The median age sits around 35 and the neighbourhood skews toward young professionals and growing families, not retirees, which means playgrounds, cafés and family‑friendly services are part of the everyday landscape. Weekends along Kedron Brook are a highlight: Bradbury Park’s playscape keeps kids happily exhausted while Kalinga Park’s bikeway is perfect for scooters, bikes and slow morning rides with coffee in hand. Household incomes are solidly upper‑middle, so you’ll see a lot of renovated weatherboard homes and young parents swapping tips at the local green spaces.

Inside the Classroom at St Anthony’s Primary School (Kedron)

St Anthony’s has a distinctly busy, engaged feel: their music program runs from junior to senior choir, beginner and junior strings, and a concert band that gives parents something to look forward to at assembly and end‑of‑year concerts. Sport is active and social, with gala days for touch and netball and opportunities to join wider Catholic school competitions. If your child is into tinkering and problem solving, the school’s STEM clubs — coding and robotics included — are lively pockets of curiosity. They also run chess, tennis and soccer programs so there’s a bit of everything for different interests. The school covers primary grades; I’d ring the office to confirm exact year levels when you call about enrolment.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

If you’re juggling work and school pickups, St Anthony’s has on‑site Outside School Hours Care (on‑site before and after school childcare) called Little Flower School Age Care, run by Catholic Early EdCare. They provide before‑school, after‑school and vacation care programs, which means less deadline panic on hectic mornings and reliable supervision on pupil‑free days. For families who both work, having care on site is a real quality‑of‑life advantage — you can drop, head into the city or your home office, and trust the routine is covered.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Kedron’s grocery scene is quietly multicultural, which is great if you like cooking from scratch. For Asian ingredients there’s Kapamilya Asian Shop, and for Indian and South Asian staples Kedron Spice Centre is the obvious go‑to — both make it easy to keep favourites in the pantry so weeknight meals don’t become takeout every night. Speaking of takeout, when pizza is the plan, Smoky Pizza Woodfired does solid wood‑fired pies. For the nights when kids demand fish and chips, local shops such as The Cod Father and other neighbourhood takeaways have you covered. There’s enough variety for quick fixes and for adventurous midweek cooking alike.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you’re thinking about extra academic support, Kedron has local tutoring options like Alchemy Tuition and franchise services such as Apex Tuition/Tutors Australia that will come to the house or host small sessions nearby. Commuting into the Brisbane CBD is straightforward: the nearest heavy‑rail interchange is Eagle Junction, and the train from Eagle Junction to Brisbane Central typically takes about 10 to 15 minutes. From door to CBD you should allow roughly 10 to 20 minutes, depending on how you get to the station and whether it’s peak time — it’s an easy option for parents who work downtown but want a calmer residential base.

Your Next Steps

First, call St Anthony’s to confirm exactly which primary years they cover, ask about the enrolment process, and book a tour so you can see the music rooms and STEM spaces in action. Second, if on‑site before‑and‑after school care matters to your routine, register or enquire with Little Flower School Age Care through Catholic Early EdCare to check availability for before‑school, after‑school and vacation programs. Third, plan your commute by testing train times from Eagle Junction to Brisbane Central and allowing 10 to 20 minutes door‑to‑door; while you’re at it, scope local tuition options like Alchemy or Apex if you think extra support will help during the school year.
