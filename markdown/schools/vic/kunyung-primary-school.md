Below is a short, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Kunyung Primary School (Mount Eliza, VIC). Each point is snippet‑first with source citations.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Mount Eliza is an affluent, family‑oriented suburb with a high median household income ($2,547/week) and an older median age (45), with many married households — a strong white‑collar/professional tilt and lots of established families rather than singles [citation:2021 Mount Eliza QuickStats (SAL21793)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21793).

2) Most common overseas birthplace (if any)  
About three quarters of residents are Australian‑born (~74–75%); the most common overseas birthplace is England (around 9% of residents) — England is the single largest non‑Australian birthplace [citation:Mount Eliza QuickStats (SAL21793)](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21793) [citation:Mount Eliza profile (summary)](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/mount-eliza).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
Kunyung Primary has on‑site OSHC (Before School Care, After School Care and Vacation Care) run by TeamKids (Before: 7:00–9:00; After: 3:30–6:00; Vacation care 7:00–18:00) — bookings and fees shown on the TeamKids venue page [citation:TeamKids – Kunyung Primary (OSHC)](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/kunyung-primary-mt-eliza-oshc-5/).

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars offered  
Kunyung is an IB PYP school with specialist music provision (partnered with Hands On Music / specialist music teachers), large whole‑school music/dance events, and active community co‑curricular activities such as kitchen‑garden/volunteer committees and other parent‑run clubs [citation:Kunyung Learning (IB + music)](https://kunyung.vic.edu.au/learning/) [citation:Kunyung Community / Parent handbook](https://kunyung.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Parent-Information-Handbook-2024.pdf) [citation:Kunyung whole‑school dance (video)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lo2Vx7IJ7j4).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (2 sentences)  
Mount Eliza has a relaxed, seaside family vibe with a village centre, regular farmers’ market and playgrounds — popular with families and retirees. Main local grocery options include Ritchies IGA (Mt Eliza village), Woolworths in Eliza Square and long‑standing local greengrocers (Palamara Village Fruits); there’s also a monthly Mount Eliza Farmers’ Market for local produce [citation:Ritchies IGA Mt Eliza](https://www.iga.com.au/stores/ritchies-iga-mt-eliza/) [citation:Woolworths Mt Eliza listing](https://www.openinghoursau.com/woolworths-mount-eliza) [citation:Mt Eliza Village Fruits](https://www.mtelizavillagefruits.com/) [citation:Mt Eliza Farmers' Market](https://www.mtelizafarmersmarket.com.au/).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 family‑friendly options / standout cuisines  
Family‑friendly takeaway/dining options include Eliza Village Fish & Chips (classic family fish’n’chips), Ezra Mt Eliza (casual bistro / local seafood & Modern Australian) and Mt. Terrasse / cafes in Eliza Village for casual meals and takeaway coffee/sweets [citation:Eliza Village Fish & Chips](https://elizavillagefishandchips.au/) [citation:Ezra Mt Eliza](https://www.ezramteliza.com.au/) [citation:Mt. Terrasse listing](https://mt-terrasse.square.site/).

7) Tuition and enrichment nearby  
Local tutoring/enrichment providers include Alchemy Tuition (in‑home & online tutoring serving Mount Eliza) and Ezy Math / Cloud Tuition listings for local maths tutors and one‑to‑one tuition in the area — local private tutors and small tuition services are commonly used by families here [citation:Alchemy Tuition Mount Eliza](https://alchemytuition.com.au/melbourne-tutoring-mount-eliza/) [citation:Ezy Math Tutoring listings (Mount Eliza)](https://www.ezymathtutoring.com.au/tutors/mount-eliza-3930).

8) Commute — train/bus estimate to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)  
Mount Eliza does not have a heavy‑rail station; the nearest Metro rail is Frankston (approx. 4–6 km). Typical public trips use a local bus to Frankston then the Frankston line to Flinders Street — the Frankston→Flinders Street train leg is about 1h10–1h15, and adding a bus from Mount Eliza to Frankston (~15–25 minutes depending on route and connection) gives a door‑to‑Flinders Street public‑transport time of roughly 1h25–1h45 in many trips (allow more at peak or with waits) [citation:Mount Eliza transport summary (nearest heavy rail Frankston)](https://www.travelvictoria.com.au/mounteliza/transport/) [citation:Frankston line info (Metro Trains)](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/lines/frankston/) [citation:Rome2rio travel summary Frankston→Flinders St (example times)](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Mount-Eliza/Flinders-Street-Station).
