1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Diamond Creek is a middle-to-upper income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,508 and a median age of 39 [citation:2021 Diamond Creek QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20752). The suburb has a high proportion of families (3,588 families) and an average of 2.9 people per household, indicating a strong family focus [citation:2021 Diamond Creek QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20752). Occupation data is not directly available in the provided sources, but the income level and family composition suggest a predominantly white-collar, professional community.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Diamond Creek is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 61.5% of residents having both parents born in Australia [citation:2016 Diamond Creek QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20746). The most common overseas country of birth is England at 4.3%, followed by New Zealand at 0.9% [citation:2016 Diamond Creek QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20746). (2021 data on country of birth was not available in the provided sources.)

3) **Working-parent support:** Diamond Creek East Primary School partners with independent provider **TheirCare** to run Before and After School Care and Holiday Care [citation:OSHC | Diamond Creek EastPS](https://www.dceps.vic.edu.au/oshc). Before-school hours are 7:00 am to 8:45 am, after-school hours are 3:30 pm to 6:30 pm, and holiday programs run from 7:00 am to 6:30 pm [citation:OSHC | Diamond Creek EastPS](https://www.dceps.vic.edu.au/oshc). Families can also access a government rebate to reduce session costs [citation:OSHC | Diamond Creek EastPS](https://www.dceps.vic.edu.au/oshc).

4) **School culture:** The school offers a range of named extracurricular activities including **Smash Hits** (the school rock band that performs at festivals), **interschool sport**, and **JETS Gymnastics** [citation:School Profile and History | Diamond Creek EastPS](https://www.dceps.vic.edu.au/profile). Other programs include swimming, bike education, camping, a whole school concert, DCE Live, student leadership programs, and a perceptual motor program [citation:School Profile and History | Diamond Creek EastPS](https://www.dceps.vic.edu.au/profile).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Diamond Creek has a family-friendly village feel with several supermarkets including **Coles** (67 Main Hurstbridge Road) and **Ritchies Supa IGA** (64 Main Hurstbridge Road), plus dedicated butchers like **DC Meats** and bakeries such as **Ferguson Plarre Bakehouse** [citation:Fresh Food & Supermarkets Diamond Creek](https://www.diamondcreekshopping.com.au/places/category/fresh-food-supermarkets/). Specialty ethnic supermarkets were not found in the available sources; however, the large **Coles** and **Ritchies IGA** stock a wide range of everyday groceries.

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family takeaway, consider **Da Vinci Pizza & Pasta Gallery** (2/16 Chute Street) for Italian, **Aksorn Thai Restaurant** (1/72 Main Hurstbridge Road) for Thai, and **Indian Hub** (4/24 Chute Street) for Indian cuisine [citation:Take Away Food Diamond Creek](https://www.diamondcreekshopping.com.au/places/category/take-away-food/). All offer dine-in or takeaway options and are popular with local families.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** External tutoring options include **Cloud Tuition**, which provides online tutoring for Years 2–12 in English, Maths, and Science with personalised lessons [citation:Cloud Tuition Diamond Creek](https://www.cloudtuition.com.au/locations/tutoring-vic/diamond-creek). **Alchemy Tuition** offers in-home and online tutoring in Diamond Creek and has been serving the area since 2005 [citation:Alchemy Tuition Diamond Creek](https://alchemytuition.com.au/melbourne-tutoring-diamond-creek/).

8) **Commute:** Diamond Creek is served by the **Hurstbridge line** with trains from **Diamond Creek Station** to **Flinders Street Station** taking approximately **52 minutes** [citation:Rome2Rio Diamond Creek to Flinders Street](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Diamond-Creek/Flinders-Street-Station). Trains run hourly and tickets cost around $4 one-way [citation:Rome2Rio Diamond Creek to Flinders Street](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Diamond-Creek/Flinders-Street-Station).
