Here’s a concise, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for St Michael’s School (Berwick). I ran one targeted search per item and used official/local sources (ABS, school site, ACECQA, etc.). Sources are cited inline and collected at the end.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile
Berwick is a largely family‑oriented, middle‑income outer‑suburban area with steady growth and a high share of married couples and households with children—median age 38. It sits in a growth corridor of Melbourne’s southeast and shows moderate professional/white‑collar representation alongside ongoing residential development [citation:2021 Berwick QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20224) [citation:Berwick – Suburb (AreaSearch)](https://areasearch.com.au/suburb/berwick).

2) Most common overseas country of birth
Most residents are Australian‑born (about 62%); the largest single overseas birthplace is India (approx. 6.1% of residents) [citation:Berwick VIC Suburb Profile — Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/berwick) [citation:2021 Berwick QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20224).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
St Michael’s offers on‑site Outside School Hours Care run by Camp Australia (before‑school, after‑school and pupil‑free day programs). The ACECQA entry shows the Camp Australia service at the school with an approved capacity (54 places) and standard morning/evening hours [citation:St. Michael's OSHC (school page)](https://www.stmberwick.catholic.edu.au/outside-of-school-hours-care) [citation:Camp Australia — ACECQA listing](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/camp-australia-st-michaels-primary-school-berwick-oshc).

4) School culture / extracurriculars
The school runs sport via School Sport Victoria membership (inter‑school sport), music/church‑music involvement and other extra‑curricular activities listed in the school materials and handbook (e.g., instrumental/choir and team sports) [citation:St. Michael's Primary School (site)](https://www.stmberwick.catholic.edu.au/) [citation:Parent Handbook (school PDF)](https://stmberwick.catholic.edu.au/_file/media/2429/handbook_2026_parents.pdf).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries
Berwick feels like a family suburb with parks, schools and expanding residential precincts and established shopping hubs (Berwick Central / Berwick Village) for everyday needs [citation:City of Casey — Berwick page](https://www.casey.vic.gov.au/berwick) . Major supermarkets (Coles, Woolworths) are in suburb centres; several South Asian / Indian grocers and specialist stores also operate in town (e.g., local Indian grocery listings) [citation:Coles Berwick Central](https://www.coles.com.au/find-stores/coles/vic/berwick-7695) [citation:Budget Grocery — local Indian grocery listing](https://au.showmelocal.com/35104376-budget-grocery-berwick).

6) Weeknight dining (casual, family‑friendly)
Good family options include The Hill (casual family restaurant), The Main (cafe/restaurant) and a strong local takeaway mix (pizza, Thai, Indian and fish & chips) around Berwick Central and nearby strips [citation:The Hill — Berwick](https://thehillrestaurant.com.au/) [citation:The Main Berwick](https://www.themainberwick.com.au/) [citation:Tripadvisor — Berwick takeaways](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g552140-zfp10601-Berwick_City_of_Casey_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) Tuition and enrichment nearby
Local tutoring/enrichment centres include Kumon (Berwick centre) and Kip McGrath Berwick (English & Maths tutoring), both offering regular after‑school programs for primary students [citation:Kumon Berwick listing](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-BERWICK-VIC) [citation:Kip McGrath Berwick](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/berwick).

8) Commute: public transport to CBD
By train from Berwick station to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street) is typically about 55–60 minutes (typical trip times ~55–57 minutes); services run regularly (approx. every 20 minutes at peak) — driving is faster off‑peak but trains are the common public‑transport option [citation:Berwick to Flinders Street — Rome2rio (typical time)](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Berwick-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station) [citation:Metro Trains timetables](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/timetables/).
