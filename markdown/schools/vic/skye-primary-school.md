Here’s a concise, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Skye Primary School (Skye VIC). I used a single search per item and only opened pages when needed; citations follow each fact and a short Sources list is at the end.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
Skye is a family‑oriented, middle‑income suburb with a median age of 36 and a noticeably higher share of children (0–14) than Victoria overall — consistent with many young families living locally [citation:2021 Skye QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22291). The profile data suggest a mixed white‑ and blue‑collar workforce with many households comprising couples with children (family‑dominant household structure) [citation:2021 Skye QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22291).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
The suburb is predominantly Australian‑born (around three quarters); the most common overseas birthplace reported is England (≈4–5% of residents) [citation:Skye suburb profile — BehindTheFence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/skye) and supported by ABS area QuickStats [citation:2021 Skye QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22291).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
Yes — on‑site OSHC is provided by TeamKids at Skye Primary (Before School Care, After School Care and Vacation Care; before 6:30–8:45am, after 3:15–6:30pm; vacation care hours listed) [citation:TeamKids — Skye Primary OSHC](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/skye-primary-oshc/).

4) School culture / extracurriculars (2–3 specific items)
Skye Primary runs specialist and co‑curricular programs including: Music (school music updates/newsletters), STEAM (hands‑on STEAM program), and a Kitchen Garden program; the school also has a new gym/library hub that supports on‑site sport and PE activities [citation:Skye Primary — Teaching & Learning / Newsletter / School Buildings VIC](https://skyeps.vic.edu.au/teaching-learning/; https://skyeps.vic.edu.au/community/; https://www.schoolbuildings.vic.gov.au/skye-primary-school).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
Skye feels suburban and family‑focused with parks, new school facilities and local convenience services; it’s a practical outer‑metro neighbourhood rather than a boutique shopping strip [citation:2021 Skye QuickStats; Skye family guide](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22291; https://www.melbz.com.au/skye/). Local grocery options include community grocers such as McCormicks Food Store (local supermarket / fish & chips/ takeaway at McCormicks Rd) and nearby larger supermarkets in adjacent centres [citation:McCormicks listing / local supermarkets directory](https://au.showmelocal.com/profile.aspx?bid=17781780; https://www.localsearch.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/skye-vic-3977).

6) Weeknight dining (2–3 casual, family‑friendly options)
Local family take‑away/dining options include McCormicks Fish & Chips (local takeaway staple), Lucky Star Chinese Takeaway, and small cafes/shops such as Homestyle Cafe — a mix of fish‑and‑chips, Chinese and cafe‑style takeaway suited to families [citation:Local Business Guide / Localsearch listings](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/victoria/skye/food-and-dining/Take-Away-Food/; https://www.localsearch.com.au/find/takeaway-food/skye-vic-3977).

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 centres)
Local tutoring options include in‑area/home/online tutors listed via Tutors Australia and LearnMate (local primary/secondary tutors who service Skye), and small local private tutors advertised for primary subjects; these are mostly in‑home/online or in neighbouring suburbs rather than big centre chains inside Skye itself [citation:Tutors Australia Skye / LearnMate Skye listings](https://www.tutorsaustralia.com.au/skye-tutors/; https://learnmate.com.au/cmloc-skye-vic-3977/).

8) Commute (train / substitute transport)
Skye has no suburban train station. Typical public‑transport commutes to Melbourne CBD use a local bus to a nearby station (eg. Frankston) then the Frankston line into the city; a door‑to‑door bus+train trip to Flinders Street is commonly around 1 hour 15–90 minutes depending on connections (many journey‑planner summaries estimate ~1h25) [substitution: bus + train used because Skye has no train station] [citation:Moovit public‑transport for Skye; Rome2rio Skye→Melbourne summary](https://moovitapp.com/index/en-gb/public_transportation-Skye-Melbourne-city_35177-2803; https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Skye-VIC-Australia/Melbourne).
