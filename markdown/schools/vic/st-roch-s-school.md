1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Glen Iris is an affluent suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,491, well above the Victorian median, and a median age of 40 [citation:2021 Glen Iris (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21010). The suburb is predominantly white-collar, with a mix of families and professionals; it has a population of about 26,000 and a family-oriented character [citation:GLEN IRIS VIC, AU SUBURB PROFILE - FREE REPORT](https://www.smartpropertyinvestment.com.au/data/vic/3146/glen-iris). Nearly 80% of households are family households, and educational attainment is above metropolitan averages [citation:GLEN IRIS suburb profile](https://landchecker.com.au/suburb/glen-iris-vic-3146/).

3) **Working-parent support:** St Roch’s School offers Out of School Hours Care (OSHC) operated by Melbourne Archdiocese Catholic Schools Early Years Education Ltd and administered by Youth Leadership Victoria (YLV) [citation:St Roch's OSHC, Glen Iris](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/st-rochs-oshc-glen-iris) [citation:Community - Catholic Primary School](https://www.srgleniris.catholic.edu.au/community). Before-school care runs 07:00–09:00 and after-school care runs 15:30–18:30 on school days; holiday care is listed as 07:00–18:00 but the ACECQA register marks “Vacation Care: No,” so families should confirm directly [citation:St Roch's OSHC, Glen Iris](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/st-rochs-oshc-glen-iris).

4) **School culture:** St Roch’s offers a wide range of named extracurriculars including **Interschool Sport** (summer and winter district competitions), **Rock With You Music Lessons** (instrumental tuition), and **Dance Club** [citation:Extra Curricular](https://www.srgleniris.catholic.edu.au/extra-curricular). The school also runs a biennial whole-school production and a **GRIP Leadership Program** for student leaders [citation:Extra Curricular](https://www.srgleniris.catholic.edu.au/extra-curricular).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Glen Iris is a leafy, family-friendly suburb with parks, good schools, and the **The Glen Shopping Centre** offering major supermarkets (Coles, ALDI) and specialty grocers [citation:Supermarkets & Fresh Food - The Glen](https://www.theglen.com.au/shopping/supermarkets-fresh-food). For ethnic groceries, **The Glen Asian Grocery** stocks Asian produce, **Haiku Future** (Japanese grocery) is on Toorak Road in nearby Camberwell, and **The South African Shop** is in Caulfield South [citation:The Glen Asian Grocery](https://www.theglen.com.au/stores/the-glen-asian-grocery) [citation:THE BEST 10 Supermarkets and Grocery Stores in Glen Iris, Victoria](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/victoria/glen-iris/shopping/supermarkets-and-grocery-stores/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Casual family-friendly options include **Little Thai Princess** (Thai cuisine on Burke Road), **That Greek Tavern** (Greek), and **Fathima's Indian Kitchen** (Indian) [citation:Restaurants in Glen Iris,VIC With Takeaway](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/glen-iris/takeaway-category) [citation:Authentic Indian Cuisine, Traditional Recipes, and Flavorful Dishes |Fathima - Glen Eris](https://glen.fathimasindiankitchen.com.au/). All offer takeaway and dine-in, with Little Thai Princess open from 5:30 PM.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Math Minds Learning Centre** is located a 1-minute walk from Glen Iris Station at the corner of High St and Malvern Rd, offering maths tutoring [citation:Maths Tutoring Glen Iris • Math💡Minds – Learning Centre](https://mathminds.com.au/maths-tutor-glen-iris/). **KIS Academics** provides tutoring in English and other subjects at various locations in the area [citation:Best Year 11-12 English Tutors in Glen iris, VIC 3146](https://kisacademics.com/w/tutoring-locations/glen-iris-english-tutoring).

8) **Commute:** Glen Iris Station is on the **Glen Waverley line**. The train to Flinders Street Station takes approximately **20 minutes** and runs every 20 minutes [citation:Glen Iris (Station) to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Glen-Iris-Station/Flinders-Street-Station) [citation:Timetable Search | Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/timetables/). Alternatively, tram route 6 along Malvern Rd/High St reaches the city in about 38 minutes [citation:Glen Iris (Station) to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Glen-Iris-Station/Flinders-Street-Station).
