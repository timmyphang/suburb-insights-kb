1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** St Albans is a predominantly working-class, blue-collar suburb with a high proportion of families. The median weekly household income is $1,205, notably below the Victorian median, and the median age is 36 [citation:2021 St Albans (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22330). The area has a high density of 2,935 persons per square km and a strong family presence, with 9,391 families recorded [citation:About the profile areas | St Albans | profile.id](https://profile.id.com.au/brimbank/about?WebID=290).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas country of birth in St Albans is Vietnam. Census data shows that 22.8% of residents were born in Vietnam, making it the dominant migrant community, followed by India at 4.1% [citation:2021 St Albans (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22330). Only 44.7% of residents were born in Australia, well below the national average [citation:2021 St Albans (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22330).

3) **Working-parent support:** St Albans East Primary School partners with Camp Australia to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) [citation:St Albans East Primary School](https://www.saeps.vic.edu.au/). The service is located at 7-17 Station Avenue, St Albans [citation:Camp Australia - St Albans East Primary School OSHC - St Albans 3021](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3021/st-albans/camp-australia-st-albans-east-primary-school-oshc). Specific operating hours were not found in the available sources, but the service offers before and after school care with a late pick-up fee of $15 per 15 minutes [citation:St Albans East Primary School | Camp Australia](https://campaustralia.com.au/school/?accessCode=2731).

4) **School culture:** St Albans East Primary School offers a Clubs Program, where students participate in multi-age club groupings such as craft, sport, and cultural activities, culminating in a whole-school showcase for parents [citation:Clubs Program](https://www.saeps.vic.edu.au/page/156/Clubs-Program). The school also provides a Primary Music Institute (PMI) program offering instrumental lessons in Keyboard and Concert Band, as well as private/small group music instruction [citation:PMI-Primary Music Institute](https://www.saeps.vic.edu.au/page/153/PMI-Primary-Music-Institute). Additionally, the school runs a Sporting School Program, a Swimming Program, a Choir, and a Vietnamese Dance group [citation:School Strategic Plan 2018-2022](https://www.saeps.vic.edu.au/uploaded_files/media/st_albans_east_primary_school_4741__20182021__school_strategic_plan.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** St Albans has a vibrant, multicultural town centre with many specialty grocers catering to Vietnamese, Indian, Filipino and other Asian cuisines. Specific stores include Pahal Grocers (282 Main Road East) for South Asian groceries, KP Asian Grocery (12 Alfrieda Street) for Korean, Japanese, and Southeast Asian products, and KFL Supermarket (330 Main Road East) for a wide range of Asian staples [citation:Pahal Grocers: South Asian Flavours and Everyday Essentials in St Albans - St Albans Town Centre](https://www.stalbanstowncentre.com.au/news-stories/pahal-grocers) [citation:KP Asian Grocery - St Albans Town Centre](https://www.stalbanstowncentre.com.au/directory/kp-asian-grocery) [citation:St Albans KFL | The Asian Food Specialist](https://www.kflsupermarkets.com.au/st-albans-vic). Super Pinoy and Saint Filo Grocer also serve the Filipino community [citation:Super Pinoy - St Albans Town Centre](https://www.stalbanstowncentre.com.au/directory/super-pinoy) [citation:Saint Filo Grocer - St Albans Town Centre](https://www.stalbanstowncentre.com.au/directory/saint-filo-grocer).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Families can enjoy casual Vietnamese dining at Phi Phi Vietnamese & Chinese Restaurant (on Main Road East), known for its affordable and generous portions [citation:THE 10 BEST Restaurants in St Albans (Updated July 2026)](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g4358126-St_Albans_Brimbank_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). For Indian cuisine, Shahi Darbaar (10A East Esplanade) offers takeaway and delivery with a 5-star rating [citation:St Albans 3021 Food Delivery and Takeaway VIC | ozfoodhunter.com.au](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/st-albans-3021). Another option is Pizza Del Mondo, a pizza restaurant with a 4.4 rating and family-friendly appeal [citation:THE 10 BEST Restaurants in St Albans (Updated July 2026)](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g4358126-St_Albans_Brimbank_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Two named tutoring centres in St Albans are Success Tutoring St Albans, offering personalised K-12 programs at local study hubs [citation:St Albans – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-st-albans), and Innate Tutoring, which provides VCE and Pre-VCE tuition in English, Maths, Biology, and Chemistry [citation:Tutoring St Albans | VCE Tutors, English, Online Biology & Chemistry Help](https://www.innatetutoring.com.au/st-albans-tutoring/). 8) **Commute:** St Albans is served by the Sunbury train line. The journey from St Albans Station to Flinders Street Station takes approximately 31 minutes, with trains running at least every 15 minutes [citation:Train St Albans Station to Flinders Street Station from $4 | Tickets & Timetables | Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/St-Albans-Station/Flinders-Street-Station). The direct train service operates daily, and a myki fare is around $4 per trip [citation:Train St Albans Station to Flinders Street Station from $4 | Tickets & Timetables | Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/St-Albans-Station/Flinders-Street-Station).
