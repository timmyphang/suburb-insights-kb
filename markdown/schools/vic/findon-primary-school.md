3) **Working-parent support:** Findon Primary School partners with TheirCare to provide Outside School Hours Care [citation:Out of School Hours Care – Findon Primary School](https://www.findonps.vic.edu.au/index.php/out-of-school-hours-care/). The program runs from 6:45am to 8:45am for before-school care and from 3:30pm to 6:00pm for after-school care [citation:Out of School Hours Care – Findon Primary School](https://www.findonps.vic.edu.au/index.php/out-of-school-hours-care/).

4) **School culture:** Findon Primary School offers a School Choir and instrument opportunities as part of its Performing Arts program [citation:The Arts – Findon Primary School](https://www.findonps.vic.edu.au/index.php/performing-arts/). The school also has a comprehensive interschool sports program where students compete in various team sports against neighbouring schools [citation:Physical Education – Findon Primary School](https://www.findonps.vic.edu.au/index.php/physical-education/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Mill Park offers several specialty grocery options, including Shriji Supermarket at 1/7 Development Boulevard, which stocks Indian groceries, spices, and fresh produce [citation:Shriji Supermarket Mill Park, Melbourne](https://singh.com.au/shriji-supermarket-mill-park-melbourne/). Eastwestmart is located at Westfield Plenty Valley (Shop 77/415 McDonalds Rd), providing Asian groceries [citation:Plenty Valley - Eastwestmart](https://eastwestmart.com.au/plenty-valley/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For family-friendly dining, try La Porchetta Mill Park for Italian and pizza [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Mill Park](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g2023223-zfp10601-Mill_Park_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).
