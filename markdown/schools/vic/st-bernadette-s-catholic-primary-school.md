1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** The Basin is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb. According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income is $2,019, and the median age is 40, which is slightly older than the state median of 38 [citation:2021 The Basin (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22496). The suburb has a higher proportion of married residents and a significant number of families with children, contributing to a stable, community-focused demographic.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The suburb is predominantly Australian-born, with 80.4% of residents born in Australia, according to the 2021 Census [citation:2021 The Basin (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22496). The most common overseas country of birth is not explicitly stated in the provided source, but the data shows a very high Australian-born percentage, indicating limited overseas-born diversity.

3) **Working-parent support:** St Bernadette's Catholic Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) through the provider OSHCLUB [citation:St Bernadette's Primary School - The Basin - Prep Enrolments Open 2023](https://sbthebasin.catholic.edu.au/). Before school care operates from 7:00 am, and after school care runs until 6:00 pm [citation:St Bernadette's Primary School - The Basin - Prep Enrolments Open 2023](https://sbthebasin.catholic.edu.au/). Holiday programs are also provided by OSHCLUB [citation:St Bernadette's Primary School - The Basin - Prep Enrolments Open 2023](https://sbthebasin.catholic.edu.au/).
