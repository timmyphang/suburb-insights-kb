Short briefing — Greenvale Primary School (Greenvale VIC) — 8 quick points for families

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
- Middle‑income, family‑oriented suburb (median age 35; high share of families/married households). [citation:ABS QuickStats Greenvale 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21105)  
- Local suburb profiles cite a median household income in the ~$110–120k range, consistent with a mixed white‑ and blue‑collar community rather than high‑affluence. [citation:InTheSuburbs Greenvale profile](https://www.inthesuburbs.com.au/suburb/greenvale-vic-3059.html)

2) Most common overseas country of birth  
- Most residents are Australian‑born (~two‑thirds); the top overseas birthplace reported for Greenvale is Italy (small single‑digit % share reported in Census‑derived suburb profiles). [citation:LocalStats Greenvale demographics](https://localstats.com.au/demographics/vic/melbourne/north-west/greenvale) [citation:SuperbSuburb Greenvale summary](https://superbsuburb.com.au/victoria/greenvale-3059/)

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC / before & after school care)  
- Greenvale Primary runs on‑site Outside School Hours Care provided by TheirCare (Before School Care, After School Care and Vacation/Holiday programs). Contact: TheirCare / school page. [citation:Greenvale PS After Hours Care](https://greenvaleps.vic.edu.au/after-hours-care/) [citation:TheirCare Greenvale service](https://theircare.com.au/holiday-programs/greenvale-ps-theircare/)

4) School culture — extracurriculars (2–3 specific offerings)  
- Performing arts / biannual school concert (whole‑school concerts). [citation:Greenvale PS Performing Arts News](https://greenvaleps.vic.edu.au/performing-arts-news/)  
- Regular athletics / inter‑school sport (annual athletics carnival, district competitions). [citation:Greenvale PS Athletics 2025](https://greenvaleps.vic.edu.au/athletics-2025/)  
- Instrumental/music programs referenced in the school handbook/policies (instrumental tuition listed). [citation:Greenvale PS Information Handbook 2023](https://greenvaleps.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/2023-Greenvale-Information-Handbook.pdf)

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)  
- Greenvale feels like a practical, family‑centred outer‑north suburb with neighbourhood shopping and parks (local primary‑school catchments, playgrounds and community services). [citation:Wikipedia Greenvale overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenvale%2C_Victoria)  
- Main supermarkets: Coles at Greenvale Shopping Centre and an IGA Local Grocer (Liberty Greenvale); specialty/ethnic fresh‑food options are limited in‑centre, with nearby suburbs offering a wider range. [citation:Coles Greenvale store](https://www.coles.com.au/find-stores/coles/vic/greenvale-608) [citation:IGA Local Grocer Liberty Greenvale](https://www.iga.com.au/stores/iga-local-grocer-liberty-greenvale/)

6) Weeknight casual family dining / takeaway (2–3 options)  
- Jessie's Pizza (Greenvale Shopping Centre) — pizza & pasta takeaway/delivery. [citation:Jessies Pizza Greenvale menu](https://www.jessiespizza-greenvale.com.au/menu)  
- Tek Kebab (Turkish/halal kebab takeaway) in Greenvale Shopping Centre — popular local ethnic takeaway. [citation:Tek Kebab Greenvale listing](https://tekkebab.com/)

7) Tuition & enrichment (local options)  
- Local private tutors and tutoring platforms service Greenvale (Superprof / local one‑to‑one tutors and math tutors listed). [citation:Superprof Greenvale tutors](https://www.superprof.com.au/lessons/all-tutors/greenvale/)  
- Several small local tutoring providers/directories and home tutors (Ezy Math / private tutors) advertise in the Greenvale area for primary‑level maths/English support. [citation:Ezy Math Tutoring Greenvale listings](https://www.ezymathtutoring.com.au/tutors/greenvale-3059)

8) Commute to CBD — train/bus estimate (Flinders Street)  
- Greenvale has no metropolitan train station; public access to the rail network is by local bus to nearby Roxburgh Park (or Broadmeadows) then train. [citation:Transport Victoria bus route 484 via Greenvale](https://transport.vic.gov.au/route/18909/484-broadmeadows-roxburgh-park-via-greenvale)  
- Typical rail time Roxburgh Park → Flinders Street is ~38–42 minutes; allowing ~10–25 minutes bus + transfer, expect around 50–70 minutes door‑to‑door to Flinders Street by public transport. [citation:Rome2rio Roxburgh Park to Flinders St (~41 min)](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Roxburgh-Park-Station/Flinders-Street-Station) [citation:Metro Trains Roxburgh Park station](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/stations/roxburgh-park/)
