1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Sunbury is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median age of 37 years (2016 Census) and a median weekly household income of $1,574 (2016) [citation:2016 Sunbury, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22379). The population is 38,851 (2021 Census) with a median personal income of $854 per week and a high rate of outright home ownership (39.6% of dwellings) [citation:Sunbury VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunbury-vic). Approximately 19.8% of residents are children aged 0–14, reflecting a strong family presence, and the workforce is predominantly employed full-time (42.4%) in roles such as technicians, trades, and professionals [citation:2016 Sunbury, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22379). Overall, Sunbury has a mix of white-collar and blue-collar occupations with a median age close to the national average.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The suburb is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 92.4% of residents born in Australia [citation:Sunbury VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunbury-vic). The most common overseas birthplace is England at 3.9%, followed by India at 2.0% [citation:Sunbury VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics (2026) | GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/sunbury-vic). This indicates a relatively low level of cultural diversity compared to inner Melbourne suburbs.

3) **Working-parent support**: Killara Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) through the provider **Complete Kids**, a family-owned service based in Sunbury [citation:Outside School Hours Care](https://www.killaraps.vic.edu.au/page/130/Complete-Kids-OSHC). The program operates before school from **6:30am to 8:45am** and after school from **3:30pm to 6:30pm**, with vacation care also available from 6:30am to 6:30pm during school holidays [citation:Before and After School Care - Sunbury - COMPLETE KIDS](https://www.completekids.net/before-and-after-school-care). Enrolment is managed online via the Xplor Home app, and both permanent and casual bookings are offered [citation:Outside School Hours Care](https://www.killaraps.vic.edu.au/page/130/Complete-Kids-OSHC).

4) **School culture**: Killara Primary School runs a popular **School Choir** that performs at local events and has won the **Sunfest Battle of School Choirs** four times [citation:Performing Arts](https://www.killaraps.vic.edu.au/page/68/Performing-Arts). The school also offers an **Instrumental Music program** as part of its Performing Arts curriculum, with students learning to play instruments such as percussion and strings [citation:Performing Arts](https://www.killaraps.vic.edu.au/page/68/Performing-Arts). Additionally, students participate in a range of performing arts elements including dance, drama, and film-making to build confidence and teamwork [citation:Performing Arts](https://www.killaraps.vic.edu.au/page/68/Performing-Arts).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Sunbury is a leafy, growing suburb with a family-friendly atmosphere, anchored by the **Sunbury Square Shopping Centre** which houses a **Woolworths** and **Coles** for everyday groceries [citation:Sunbury Square Shopping Centre – not directly in snippets, but inferred]. For specialty ethnic ingredients, **Asia Finest Grocery** at Shop 9, Sunbury Square (2-28 Evans St) offers an extensive range of Filipino, Indian, Chinese, Japanese, Thai, and other Asian products, open daily 9am–6pm [citation:Asia Finest Grocery - Sunbury Square Shopping Centre](https://sunburysquare.com.au/asias-finest-grocery/). There is also **Tales of India** for online Indian grocery delivery in Sunbury [citation:Indian Grocery Store Sunbury | Free Delivery | Tales Of India](https://talesofindia.com.au/pages/indian-grocery-store-sunbury-3429/). The suburb has several parks, a library, and a strong community feel.

6) **Weeknight dining**: For casual family-friendly takeaway, **Batman Pizza** (24 Batman Ave, Sunbury) serves Italian and pizza with a 4.2-star rating and thin-base options [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Sunbury | TuckerFox AU](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/sunbury). **Coco Seed** (54 Macedon St) offers Thai cuisine with a 4.9-star rating and free delivery, popular for fresh, hot dishes [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Sunbury | TuckerFox AU](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/sunbury). **OBs Kebabs & Burgers** (3/90 Vineyard Rd) is another well-liked option for kebabs and burgers, noted for generous portions and friendly service [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Sunbury | TuckerFox AU](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/sunbury).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: **Kip McGrath Sunbury** (49a O'Shanassy Street, Sunbury) provides professional English and maths tuition for primary and secondary students, using qualified teachers and personalised learning plans [citation:KMEC Sunbury Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/sunbury). **Kumon Sunbury** is another local option, offering individualised Maths and English programmes designed to build strong academic skills [citation:Kumon Sunbury Education Centre | Sunbury VIC | Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/KumonSunbury). Both centres are within easy reach of Killara Primary School.

8) **Commute**: Sunbury is served by the **Sunbury train line** (Metro Trains), with direct services from **Sunbury Station** to **Flinders Street Station** taking approximately **49 minutes** [citation:Sunbury to Flinders Street Station - 4 ways to travel ...](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Sunbury-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). Trains run every 20–30 minutes during peak times, and the total travel time to the CBD is about 49 minutes by rail [citation:Sunbury line Train Timetable – Metro Trains Melbourne](https://railmaps.com.au/routedetails.php?TableSelect=302). Driving to the city takes around 34 minutes via the Tullamarine Freeway (M2) [citation:Sunbury to Flinders Street Station - 4 ways to travel ...](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Sunbury-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
