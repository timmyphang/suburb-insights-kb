1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Frankston is a middle-to-lower income suburb with a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers. The median weekly household income is $1,653, and the median age is 39 [citation:2021 Frankston, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/LGA22170). Frankston Central and Frankston North have relatively low socio-economic scores (SEIFA disadvantage), while nearby Sandhurst is more advantaged [citation:SEIFA by profile area | Frankston City](https://profile.id.com.au/frankston/seifa-disadvantage-small-area). The area includes a significant proportion of families, with 37,590 families recorded in the 2021 Census [citation:2021 Frankston, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/LGA22170).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The suburb is predominantly Australian-born, with 71.0% of residents born in Australia according to the 2021 Census [citation:2021 Frankston, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/LGA22170). The most common overseas country of birth is England at 3.9% of the population [citation:2021 Frankston, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/LGA22170).

3) **Working-parent support:** Frankston East Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by **Camp Australia** [citation:Before & After School Care](https://www.frankstoneastps.vic.edu.au/page/20/Before-&-After-School-Care). The program runs Monday to Friday, from 6:30am to 9:00am (before school) and from 3:15pm to 6:30pm (after school) [citation:Before & After School Care](https://www.frankstoneastps.vic.edu.au/page/20/Before-&-After-School-Care). Holiday programs are also available through Camp Australia at other nearby venues.

4) **School culture:** Frankston East Primary School offers several named extracurriculars, including a **Band program** (keyboards, bass, guitar, drums, vocals), a **Choir** that meets at lunchtime on Tuesdays, and a **Running Club** that meets before school once a week [citation:Music & Performing Arts](https://www.frankstoneastps.vic.edu.au/page/17/Music-&-Performing-Arts) [citation:Monitoring and assessment - 2024](https://www.frankstoneastps.vic.edu.au/source/BUILD/Frankston%20East%20Primary%20School%20(4682)%20-%202024%20-%20AIP%20-%20Actions%20Outcomes%20and%20Monitoring.pdf). The school also runs daily clubs including Lego, chess, book club, fitness, and netball [citation:Monitoring and assessment - 2024](https://www.frankstoneastps.vic.edu.au/source/BUILD/Frankston%20East%20Primary%20School%20(4682)%20-%202024%20-%20AIP%20-%20Actions%20Outcomes%20and%20Monitoring.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Frankston has a suburban, family-oriented vibe with coastal access, including the Frankston Waterfront and a prominent central shopping area. Specialty grocers include **MicMacs Oriental Grocer** for Asian ingredients [citation:MicMacs Oriental Grocer | Frankston VIC](https://www.facebook.com/micmacsorientalgrocer/), **Mrs Wan’s Asian Mart** at 20 Wells Street, and **Frankston Spices Asian Groceries & Takeaway** [citation:Imagine Frankston](https://www.imaginefrankston.com.au/about-frankston/local-love/freshfooddelivered) [citation:Frankston Spices Asian Groceries & Takeaway, Frankston](https://www.trueindia.com.au/business/supermarket-and-grocery-stores/5139/frankston/frankston-spices-asian-groceries-and-takeaway). Mainstream supermarkets like Woolworths and Coles are also available.

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly takeaway and dining options include **Frankston Thai Takeaway** for authentic Thai cuisine [citation:Frankston Thai Takeaway, Frankston](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurant/frankston-thai-takeaway-70832), **Mr Frankie** for modern Australian cafe food (lunch and brunch) [citation:Restaurants in Frankston,VIC With Takeaway](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/frankston/takeaway-category), and **Geonbae Korean BBQ** for Korean cuisine [citation:Restaurants in Frankston,VIC With Takeaway](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/frankston/takeaway-category). Many of these offer takeaway and delivery services.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Kip McGrath Frankston**, located at Lot 3 (Shop 4), 300 Heatherhill Road, provides in-centre tutoring in English and maths for primary and secondary students, using qualified teachers and individual learning plans [citation:KMEC Frankston Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/frankston). **Alchemy Tuition** offers one-on-one in-home and online tutoring for Frankston families, matching students with screened tutors with no lock-in contracts [citation:Tutoring Frankston // The Best Tutors in Frankston // Alchemy Tuition](https://alchemytuition.com.au/melbourne-tutoring-frankston/).

8) **Commute:** Frankston is on the **Frankston line** of Melbourne’s Metro Trains network, with trains operating from **Frankston Station** directly into **Flinders Street Station** in the CBD [citation:Train Flinders Street to Frankston from $4 | Tickets & Timetables](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Flinders-Street/Frankston). The typical journey time is approximately **1 hour 5 minutes**, with trains departing every 15 minutes [citation:Train Flinders Street to Frankston from $4 | Tickets & Timetables](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Flinders-Street/Frankston). All services are direct with no transfers required.
