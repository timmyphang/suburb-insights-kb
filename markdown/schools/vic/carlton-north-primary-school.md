1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**  
Carlton North is an affluent inner-city suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,400, well above the national median [citation:2021 Carlton North, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20496). The population is young (median age 33) and professional, with a mix of singles and families – 1,462 families counted in 2021. The suburb is characterised by a high proportion of white-collar residents and a strong sense of community.
