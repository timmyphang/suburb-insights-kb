1) Suburb socio-economic profile
- Greenvale is a family-oriented, middle‑income suburb with a relatively young median age (35) and high rates of married households — consistent with many growing outer‑north suburbs [citation:2021 Greenvale (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21105). Median weekly household income (2021) is around $2,262, indicating a generally comfortable middle‑income profile for the area [citation:2021 Greenvale (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21105).
