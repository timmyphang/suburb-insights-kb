1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Cheltenham is a middle-to-upper-income suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,919, above the national average [citation:2021 Cheltenham (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20539). The median age is 40, and families with children make up a significant proportion of the population [citation:CompoundInvest](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/cheltenham/) [citation:2021 Cheltenham (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20539). The suburb has undergone gentrification and is considered a desirable residential area, with a mix of white-collar professionals and a strong family demographic [citation:CompoundInvest](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/cheltenham/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Specific percentages for the most common foreign country of birth for Cheltenham VIC are not found in available sources. However, the suburb is predominantly Australian-born; the 2021 Census shows 96.4% of residents are non-Indigenous, and while overseas-born data is available in the full QuickStats table, the top specific country share is not detailed in the provided snippets [citation:2021 Cheltenham (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20539).

3) **Working-parent support:** Cheltenham East Primary School partners with TeamKids to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) [citation:TeamKids - Cheltenham East Primary](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3192/cheltenham/camp-australia-cheltenham-east-primary-school-oshc). The service operates from 3:30 PM to 6:00 PM on weekdays for after-school care; before-school care hours are not specified in the available sources [citation:TeamKids - Cheltenham East Primary](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3192/cheltenham/camp-australia-cheltenham-east-primary-school-oshc). Note: Cheltenham Primary School (a different school) partners with Camp Australia for OSHC [citation:Camp Australia Before and After School Care (OSHC) | Cheltenham Primary School](https://cheltenhamps.vic.edu.au/special-programs/camp-australia-before-and-after-school-care-oshc/).

4) **School culture:** Cheltenham East Primary School offers a lunchtime Choir for students to develop singing and performance skills [citation:Curriculum | Cheltenham East Primary School](https://cheltenhameast.vic.edu.au/curriculum/). The school also runs lunchtime clubs led by Year 6 students, including a Book Club, Gardening Club, and a Health and Wellness Group [citation:2021 Newsletter](https://cheltenhameast.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/9-March-26-2021.pdf). Private instrumental tuition is available for instruments such as guitar, piano, flute, and violin [citation:Cheltenham East Primary School PROSPECTUS](https://cheltenhameast.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/CEPS-Prospectus-March-2015.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Cheltenham offers a family-friendly atmosphere with parks like Cheltenham Park and Kingston Heath Reserve, and a thriving café culture [citation:CompoundInvest](https://www.compoundinvest.com.au/insights/suburb-profiles/cheltenham/). The suburb hosts a Cheltenham Farmers’ Market and has several specialty grocers, including Orient-Asia Foods for Thai, Indian, and Middle Eastern items, and Ocho Ocho Asian Goods for Asian foods and beauty products [citation:Orient-Asia Foods](https://www.facebook.com/orientasiafoodsltd/) [citation:Ocho Ocho Asian Goods](https://www.facebook.com/ochoochoasiangoods/). Tales of India offers online delivery of Indian groceries to Cheltenham [citation:Tales of India](https://talesofindia.com.au/pages/indian-grocery-store-cheltenham-3192).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual, family-friendly takeaway, Out4Thai offers Thai cuisine for dine-in or takeaway [citation:Out4Thai](https://out4thai.com.au/). Magic Pizza provides pizza, parmas, and pasta for takeaway and delivery [citation:Magic Pizza](https://www.magicpizza.com.au/). Indian Burrp is a popular Indian takeaway option with a 4.4-star rating on Tripadvisor [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Cheltenham](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g4357995-zfp10601-Cheltenham_Bayside_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Success Tutoring has a centre in Cheltenham offering personalised tutoring for primary and secondary students, with 24/7 access to academic resources [citation:Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-cheltenham). Kip McGrath’s KMEC Cheltenham East Tuition Centre provides tutoring in English and maths for primary-aged students [citation:KMEC Cheltenham East Tuition Centre](https://www.kipmcgrath.co.uk/cheltenham-east).

8) **Commute:** Cheltenham Station is on the Frankston railway line, with trains running every 15 minutes to Flinders Street Station [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Cheltenham-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). The average train journey is 45 minutes, though the fastest services take 36 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Cheltenham-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
