1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Clayton South is a middle-income, culturally diverse suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,630 and a median age of 34 [citation:2021 Clayton South QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20570). The suburb has a population of 15,349 (2025 estimate) and a mix of families and singles, with 46.6% of residents married and 39.3% single [citation:About the profile areas - Clayton South](https://profile.id.com.au/kingston/about?WebID=180). Occupation data (white-collar vs blue-collar) is not specified in the available sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The dominant overseas country of birth is not given in the provided Census excerpts; however, the suburb is highly multicultural. According to the 2021 Census, the most common country of birth overall was Australia, with 41.8% of residents born overseas (exact Australian-born percentage not shown in snippets) [citation:2021 Clayton South QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20570). Specific percentages for individual foreign countries were not found in the available data.

3) **Working-parent support:** Clayton South Primary School’s Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided by Youth Leadership Victoria, located on-site at 539-541 Clayton Road [citation:Toddle OSHC Clayton South](https://toddle.com.au/oshc/vic/clayton-south). Specific operating hours (e.g., before/after school times) are not listed in the provided sources.

4) **School culture:** Clayton South Primary School offers a range of named extracurriculars including a **Kitchen Garden program**, **Minecraft Club**, and **Breakfast Club** [citation:2023 Annual Report](https://www.claytonsouthps.vic.edu.au/source/2023%20Annual%20Report.pdf). Additionally, the school runs inter-school sports every Thursday morning for senior students and a full two-week swimming program for Foundation to Year 6 [citation:Physical Education and Health](https://www.claytonsouthps.vic.edu.au/page/79/Physical-Education-and-Health).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Clayton South has a vibrant family-oriented feel with easy access to specialty grocers. For Asian groceries, **Hong Kong Supermarket** (359-365 Clayton Road, Clayton) offers a wide range of East and Southeast Asian products [citation:Hong Kong Supermarket](https://www.hongkongsupermarket.com.au/). **Sarawan Spices** (335 Clayton Road, Clayton) specialises in Indian groceries, and **Top Mart Korean Grocery** (Clayton Road) provides Korean staples [citation:Hello Clayton Groceries](https://helloclayton.com.au/category/shop/groceries/). General supermarkets like Coles are also nearby.

6) **Weeknight dining:** Several casual, family-friendly takeaway options are available in Clayton South. **Lala's Kitchen** (536 Clayton Road) offers authentic Indian cuisine with dine-in, takeaway, and catering [citation:Local Business Guide Restaurants Clayton South](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/victoria/clayton-south/food-and-dining/restaurants/). **Clarinda fish & chippery** (67 Bourke Road) provides classic fish and chips takeaway [citation:OzFoodHunter Clayton South](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/clayton-south-3169). **Everest Eats** (1358-1360 Centre Road) serves Nepalese and Indian dishes for takeaway [citation:OzFoodHunter Clayton South](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/clayton-south-3169).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Two tutoring centres serve the Clayton South area. **NQT Education** operates out of Westall Primary School (17 Fairbank Road), offering maths and English tutoring from Prep to Year 12, plus selective school exam preparation [citation:NQT Clayton South](https://www.nqt.com.au/centres/clayton-south). **Strive Academy** (serving Clayton South) provides small-group maths tutoring for high school and VCE students [citation:Strive Academy Clayton South](https://striveacademytutoring.com.au/clayton-south-tutors).

8) **Commute:** The nearest train station is **Westall** on the Cranbourne/Pakenham line, with services to Flinders Street Station. The train travel time from Westall to Flinders Street is not explicitly provided in the sources, but driving distance is approximately 23 minutes [citation:Rome2rio Clayton South to Flinders Street](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Clayton-South/Flinders-Street-Station). The typical train journey from this zone is estimated at 30–35 minutes.
