Here’s a short, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Beth Rivkah Ladies College (primary campus) in St Kilda East. I used official ABS QuickStats and local school pages; citations follow each fact and a Sources list is at the end.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
- St Kilda East is a relatively young, singles‑heavy inner‑Melbourne suburb (median age 34; 57% of adults report never married) with many residents in the 25–34 and 30–34 age bands — a mix of young professionals and families rather than a predominantly working‑class area [citation:2021 St Kilda East QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22344).  
- Local summary profiles describe a majority Australian‑born population and a community with both long‑term residents and newer, middle‑income households [citation:St Kilda East VIC Suburb Profile — Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/st-kilda-east).

2) Most common foreign country of birth  
- A majority (about 64.6%) of St Kilda East residents were born in Australia (2021); QuickStats/community summaries do not show a single overseas country that dominates the remainder, so Australian‑born is the principal birthplace group [citation:St Kilda East VIC Suburb Profile — Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/st-kilda-east) [citation:2021 St Kilda East QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22344).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
- Yeshivah / Beth Rivkah runs an approved Outside School Hours Care program (“YBR Cares”) offering before‑ and after‑school care and vacation care at the St Kilda East campus — suitable for working parents [citation:Beth Rivkah Primary — Yeshivah](https://ybr.vic.edu.au/beth-rivkah-primary) [citation:Yeshivah Beth Rivkah Cares - StartingBlocks](https://www.startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01HGCR57NHQ6X0WGFMQ2NAFH95/Yeshivah-Beth-Rivkah-Cares).

4) School culture — main extracurriculars (2–3 items)  
- Annual Athletics Carnival (house teams) and school Swimming Carnival [citation:Beth Rivkah Primary — Yeshivah](https://ybr.vic.edu.au/beth-rivkah-primary).  
- Lunchtime clubs / After School Program clubs (age‑tailored BR Girls Club / Teen Club) and an instrumental/music program (students learn instruments and perform) [citation:After School Program | Beth Rivkah Schools](https://www.bethrivkah.edu/asp) [citation:Beth Rivkah Primary — Yeshivah](https://ybr.vic.edu.au/beth-rivkah-primary).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)  
- St Kilda East has an inner‑city, café/strip‑shopping vibe (Carlisle St / Inkerman St / Balaclava nearby) with a mix of family amenities and many cafés — parks and local shops are within short distances for families.  
- There are several kosher and Jewish‑community food suppliers and butchers serving the area (examples listed for nearby St Kilda / Balaclava), so kosher groceries and specialist suppliers are readily available close to the school [citation:Contact Us - melbkosher.com.au](https://melbkosher.com.au/contact-us/) [citation:Kimberley Gardens | Kosher Food in Saint Kilda East](https://www.gokosher.com/en/f99058).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 family‑friendly options / standout cuisines  
- Balaclava Hotel (Carlisle St) — pub/bistro with pizzas and family meals on Carlisle Street [citation:Balaclava Hotel, St Kilda East, VIC](https://www.thebalaclavahotel.com.au/).  
- Carlisle Street quick‑bites corridor — pizza, burgers, sushi and casual takeaway options along Carlisle Street (family‑friendly, variety of cuisines) [citation:Quick Bites & Takeaway Food Carlisle Street | Balaclava & St Kilda](https://carlislestreet.com.au/industries/quick-bites-takeaway/).  
(Plenty of nearby cafés and kosher catering/takeaway options also serve the local community.)

7) Tuition and enrichment near St Kilda East  
- Success Tutoring (local St Kilda East tutoring service offering primary English/Math tuition and holiday programs) [citation:Tutoring St Kilda East | Success Tutoring](https://www.successtutoring.com/tutoring-st-kilda-east).  
- Private tutors and small‑group tutors listed on Superprof / local directories for one‑on‑one help in the area (common options for families seeking extra academic support) [citation:Private Tutoring Saint Kilda East - Superprof](https://www.superprof.com.au/lessons/all-tutors/saint-kilda-east/).

8) Commute — train/tram time to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street)  
- St Kilda East has no train station inside the suburb; the realistic public‑transport options are tram or the nearest train at Balaclava. Nearest train: Balaclava Station (Sandringham line) — Balaclava → Flinders Street is about 13 minutes by train (typical service) [citation:St Kilda East to Flinders Street Station - Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/St-Kilda-East/Flinders-Street-Station).  
- Tram options from Hotham St / Carlisle St into the CBD typically take longer (commonly ~25–30 minutes depending on route and traffic) — check PTV journey planner for exact timetables [citation:Alma Rd / Hotham St (St Kilda East) stop - Moovit](https://moovitapp.com/index/en-gb/public_transportation-Alma_Rd_Hotham_St_St_Kilda_East-Melbourne-stop_7975153-2803) [citation:Journey planner - Public Transport Victoria](https://transport.vic.gov.au/journey).
