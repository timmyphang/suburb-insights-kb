Specific white-collar vs. blue-collar breakdowns are not available in the provided sources.

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) at Chalcot Lodge Primary School is provided by Camp Australia, operating from 7:00 am to 9:00 am for before-school care and presumably after-school care until approximately 6:00 pm (specific afternoon closing time not listed) [citation:Camp Australia - Chalcot Lodge Primary School OSHC](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3802/endeavour-hills/camp-australia-chalcot-lodge-primary-school-oshc). The service also offers pupil-free day care and school holiday programs [citation:Chalcot Lodge Primary School | Camp Australia](https://campaustralia.com.au/school/?accessCode=C734).

4) **School culture:** Chalcot Lodge Primary School offers a range of named extracurricular activities including a Vegetable Garden Program, a Cookery & Etiquette program, and a Choir [citation:Search Through Site](https://www.chalcotps.vic.edu.au/search/). The school also has a Cheer Squad and a Perceptual Motor Program for Prep students [citation:Front](https://www.chalcotps.vic.edu.au/articles/39) [citation:Helpers](https://chalcotps.vic.edu.au/p/helpers).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Endeavour Hills is a family-friendly suburb anchored by the Endeavour Hills Shopping Centre, which features major supermarkets Coles, Woolworths, Aldi, and Kmart [citation:Endeavour Hills Shopping Centre home to over 80 retailers](https://www.endeavourhillsshopping.com.au/). For specialty groceries, there is BestMart Asian Grocer located next to Coles, offering a wide range of authentic Asian ingredients [citation:BestMart Asian Grocer - Endeavour Hills Shopping Centre](https://www.endeavourhillsshopping.com.au/stores/bestmart-asian-grocer/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Casual, family-friendly dining options in Endeavour Hills include Dancing Wok (Chinese) and Domino's Pizza, both located within the Endeavour Hills Shopping Centre [citation:Dining & Takeaway - Endeavour Hills Shopping Centre](https://www.endeavourhillsshopping.com.au/dining-takeaway/). Additionally, Heatherton Village Fish & Chips offers classic fish and chips for takeaway in the area [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Endeavour Hills, VIC 3802 | TuckerFox AU](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/endeavour-hills-vic-3802).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Lynn's Learning runs a dedicated tutoring centre in Endeavour Hills, offering personalised Maths and English tuition for Foundation to Year 10, as well as Selective & Scholarship preparation for Years 3–9 [citation:Endeavour Hills | English Tuition | Maths Tuition | Lynn's Learning](https://lynnslearning.com.au/locations/victoria-locations/lynns-learning-endeavour-hills-tuition-centre/). Sessions are held at the Endeavour Hills Neighbourhood Centre on Monday afternoons and Saturday early afternoons [citation:Maths Tutor - Endeavour Hills Neighbourhood Centre](https://ehillsnc.org.au/maths-tutor/).

8) **Commute:** Endeavour Hills does not have its own train station. The most efficient public transport route to Melbourne CBD involves taking the bus (route 843) from Endeavour Hills Shopping Centre to Dandenong Station, then catching the Cranbourne or Pakenham line train to Flinders Street Station [citation:Endeavour Hills to Flinders Street Station - 4 ways to travel](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Endeavour-Hills/Flinders-Street-Station). The total journey time is approximately 1 hour 14 minutes [citation:Endeavour Hills to Flinders Street Station - 4 ways to travel](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Endeavour-Hills/Flinders-Street-Station).
