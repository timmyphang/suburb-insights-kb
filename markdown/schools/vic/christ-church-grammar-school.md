1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** South Yarra is an affluent, white-collar suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,063—significantly higher than the national median. The median age is 33 years, younger than the national average of 38, reflecting a large population of professionals and a lower proportion of families; the average household size is just 1.7 people [citation:2021 South Yarra, Census QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22314). The suburb scores highly on socio-economic advantage indices, and most residents work in professional or managerial roles [citation:South Yarra VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/south-yarra-vic).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** While 79.9% of South Yarra residents are Australian-born, the most common overseas birthplace is England, accounting for 7.3% of the population [citation:South Yarra VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/south-yarra-vic). The next most common overseas-born groups hail from China (4.7%) and India (3.1%) [citation:2021 South Yarra, Census QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22314).

3) **Working-parent support:** Christ Church Grammar School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by TeamKids, operating Monday to Friday from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM during school terms [citation:TeamKids - Christ Church Grammar | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/christ-church-grammar-oshc-extend). TeamKids also runs vacation care from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekdays during school holidays [citation:TeamKids - Christ Church Grammar | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/christ-church-grammar-oshc-extend).

4) **School culture:** Christ Church Grammar School offers a vibrant co-curricular program including the **Chapel Choir** (for Years 5–6), a **Year 2 String Program** (violin, viola, cello), and a **Chess Club** that competes in interschool competitions [citation:Music](https://www.ccgs.vic.edu.au/discover/co-curricular/music) [citation:Extra-curricular](https://www.ccgs.vic.edu.au/discover/extra-curricular). Other named electives for Years 4–6 include **Indoor Rock Climbing**, **Robotics Club**, and **Coding Club** [citation:Extra-curricular](https://www.ccgs.vic.edu.au/discover/extra-curricular).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** South Yarra is a vibrant, inner-city suburb with a mix of boutiques, parks, and cafes. For specialty groceries, **Demaria’s Grocer** (corner of Bridge Rd and Church St) is a traditional Italian grocery and deli [citation:Fruits, Vegetables, Italian Grocery, Deli & Artisan Cheese - South Yarra](https://www.demariasgrocer.com.au/areas-we-serve/south-yarra). **In the Pantry** on Chapel Street is a family-run Asian grocery, and **Tales of India** delivers Indian groceries to the area [citation:In The Pantry | Prahran VIC | Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/inthepantry/) [citation:Indian Grocery Store South Yarra | Free Delivery | Tales Of India](https://talesofindia.com.au/pages/indian-grocery-store-south-yarra-3141).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly casual options include **Fratellino Pizzeria** (Italian, on Chapel Street), **The Yarra Indian Restaurant** for authentic Indian cuisine on Toorak Road, and **Pacific Seafood BBQ House** for Chinese takeaway [citation:Takeaway in South Yarra | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/south-yarra/takeaway-category) [citation:The Yarra Indian Restaurant](https://theyarraindian.com.au/). Many of these offer takeaway for busy weeknights.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Kip McGrath South Yarra** offers English and maths tutoring with personalised programs for primary students [citation:Kip McGrath South Yarra English and Maths Tutoring](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/south-yarra). **Apex Tuition Australia** provides in-person and online tutoring across South Yarra, including for Christ Church Grammar students, covering VCE and primary foundations [citation:Private Tutors in South Yarra | Apex Tuition Australia](https://www.apextuitionaustralia.com/locations/south-yarra).

8) **Commute:** South Yarra Station is served by the Sandringham, Pakenham, Cranbourne, and Frankston train lines. The train journey to Flinders Street Station takes approximately **7 minutes**, with frequent services every 5–15 minutes [citation:South Yarra to Flinders Street Station - Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/South-Yarra/Flinders-Street-Station). For door-to-door convenience, the Line 58 tram also runs from South Yarra to the city in about 25 minutes [citation:South Yarra to Flinders Street Station - Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/South-Yarra/Flinders-Street-Station).
