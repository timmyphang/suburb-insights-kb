1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Glen Waverley is a largely affluent, family-oriented suburb. The median weekly household income is $1,918, and the median age is 40 [citation:2021 Glen Waverley, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21013). 78.3% of households are families, and the area is highly diverse, with only 35% of residents speaking English only at home [citation:Who Lives in Glen Waverley? | People & Lifestyle Profile | Microburbs Research](https://www.microburbs.com.au/People-Lifestyle/Glen-Waverley).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The specific dominant overseas country of birth and its percentage share are not available in the provided sources. However, the suburb is highly multicultural, with only 35% of residents speaking only English at home, indicating a large proportion of overseas-born residents, particularly from Asian countries [citation:Who Lives in Glen Waverley? | People & Lifestyle Profile | Microburbs Research](https://www.microburbs.com.au/People-Lifestyle/Glen-Waverley).

3) **Working-parent support:** Glen Waverley South Primary School runs its own Out of School Hours Care (OSHC) program, managed by the School Council. Before School Care operates from 7:00am to 8:45am, and After School Care from 3:30pm to 6:00pm, with a cooked breakfast provided once a fortnight [citation:Out of School Hours Care - Glen Waverley South Primary School](https://www.gwsps.vic.edu.au/oshc). Holiday programs run from 7:00am to 6:00pm during school breaks [citation:Out of School Hours Care - Glen Waverley South Primary School](https://www.gwsps.vic.edu.au/oshc).

4) **School culture:** Glen Waverley South Primary School offers a range of named extracurricular activities. These include a **Choir** for students in Levels 3–6, a **Rock Band**, and a **Lunchtime STEAM Club** [citation:Looking for the right school for your child? - Glen Waverley South Primary School](https://www.gwsps.vic.edu.au/latest-news/looking-for-the-right-school-for-your-child_117s33). The school also has a sports house system (Goolagong, Landy, Bradman, Cuthbert) and a school musical [citation:Wellbeing - Glen Waverley South Primary School](https://www.gwsps.vic.edu.au/wellbeing).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Glen Waverley is a bustling suburban hub centred around The Glen shopping centre, which contains major supermarkets like Coles, Woolworths, and ALDI [citation:Supermarkets & Fresh Food - The Glen](https://www.theglen.com.au/shopping/supermarkets-fresh-food). For specialty ethnic groceries, **The Glen Asian Grocery** offers a wide range of Asian fresh produce and condiments, and **Astee Asian Groceries & Butcher** on Railway Parade provides Asian groceries and butcher services [citation:The Glen Asian Grocery - The Glen](https://www.theglen.com.au/stores/the-glen-asian-grocery) [citation:ASTEE ASIAN GROCERIES & BUTCHER - Updated April 2026 - Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/astee-asian-groceries-and-butcher-glen-waverley).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Several casual, family-friendly takeaway/dining options are available in Glen Waverley. **Thai Garden Restaurant & Takeaway** (690 High Street Road) offers authentic Thai cuisine for dine-in or takeaway [citation:Thai Garden Restaurant & Takeaway - Order Online](https://thaigardenglenwaverley.com.au/). **Scusa Mi Italiano** (Springvale Road) serves Italian classics in a casual setting, and **Meze Restaurant & Bar** (Springvale Road) offers a modern take on Indian and Middle Eastern dishes [citation:Restaurants in Glen Waverley,VIC With Takeaway | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/glen-waverley/takeaway-category).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Two named tutoring centres in Glen Waverley are **BT Education** (Level 1, Shop 5 – Dining Terrace, The Glen Shopping Centre), which offers VCE and enrichment programs for Years 3–12, and **NumberWorks'nWords** (191 Coleman Parade), which specialises in maths and English tutoring for children aged 5–16 [citation:GLEN WAVERLEY | Bteducation](https://bt-education.com/glen-waverley/) [citation:Tutoring in Glen Waverley | NumberWorks'nWords](https://numberworksnwords.com/au/our-locations/australia/glen-waverley).

8) **Commute:** Glen Waverley is serviced by the **Glen Waverley train line** from **Glen Waverley Station**. The train journey to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD takes approximately **35 minutes** [citation:Metro Glen Waverley Line - Melbourne (Mel): Timetable and Information](https://transitapp.com/en/region/melbourne/metro/commuter-rail-glen-waverley). Trains run every 20 minutes during peak times [citation:Glen Waverley to Flinders Street Station - 3 ways to travel ...](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Glen-Waverley/Flinders-Street-Station).
