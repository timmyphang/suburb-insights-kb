1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Croydon North is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,156 and a median age of 40 [citation:2021 Croydon North QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20684). Of its 8,092 residents, 2,320 are families, and the average household size is 2.7 people, with 2.1 motor vehicles per dwelling, indicating a strong family and car-dependent profile. Occupational breakdown (white-collar vs. blue-collar) is not specified in the available sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The specific percentage share of the dominant overseas country of birth is not detailed in the provided sources. The suburb is largely Australian-born—further breakdowns (e.g., England, India) were not available in the extracted ABS QuickStats data for Croydon North.

3) **Working-parent support**: Yarra Road Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by **TheirCare Pty Ltd** [citation:Yarra Road PS TheirCare - ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/yarra-road-ps-theircare). Before-school care runs Monday–Friday **6:30 am – 8:45 am**, and after-school care runs **3:30 pm – 6:00 pm** during school terms; vacation care is also available from 7:00 am – 6:00 pm [citation:Yarra Road PS TheirCare - ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/yarra-road-ps-theircare).

4) **School culture**: Yarra Road Primary School offers several named extracurriculars including **Junior & Senior Choir**, **After School Basketball & Hoop-time**, and an **Instrumental Program** [citation:2019 Parent Handbook - Yarra Road PS](https://yarrardps.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/2019-Parent-Handbook-FINAL-YRPS.pdf). Additionally, lunchtime clubs such as **Purple Quills (writing club)** and **Board Games** are available throughout the year [citation:Community Programs 2025 - Yarra Road PS](https://yarrardps.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Community-Programs-2025-1-1.pdf). The school also hosts a bi-annual whole school production.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Croydon North is a leafy, family-friendly suburb with local amenities centred around **Croydon Central Shopping Centre**. For specialty groceries, **The Local Asian Grocer** is located inside the centre (opposite Coles) and stocks a wide range of Asian ingredients [citation:The Local Asian Grocer - Croydon Central](https://www.croydoncentral.com.au/stores/local-asian-grocer). Another option is **Chin Star Asian Grocery** on Croydon Main Street, offering fresh vegetables, frozen seafood, and party supplies [citation:Chin Star Asian Grocery - Croydon Main Street](https://www.croydonmainstreet.com.au/trader/chin-star-asian-grocery/).

6) **Weeknight dining**: Casual family-friendly takeaway options include **Piyada Thai** (412 Maroondah Hwy, Croydon North) serving authentic Thai meals for pickup [citation:Piyada Thai (Croydon)](https://piyadathai.com.au/). In nearby Croydon, **Little Bad Wolf** offers Mediterranean cuisine and **Thai Lemon Tree** provides another Thai takeaway option, both open for dinner [citation:Restaurants in Croydon with Takeaway - AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/croydon/takeaway-category).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: For after-school academic support, **ATop Education** in Croydon offers tutoring in Maths, English, Science, and coding up to HSC level [citation:ATop Education](https://atopeducation.com.au/). **Cluey Learning** also has a Croydon location providing primary and high school tutoring with qualified teachers [citation:Cluey Learning Croydon](https://clueylearning.com.au/lts/tutoring-croydon/). Both are within a short drive of Croydon North.

8) **Commute**: Croydon North is served by the **Lilydale line** via **Croydon Station**. The average train travel time from Croydon Station to Flinders Street Station is **48 minutes**, with the fastest services taking 42 minutes and trains running every 30 minutes [citation:Croydon Station to Flinders Street Station - Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Croydon-Station-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). Buses from Primrose Street also connect to Croydon Station.
