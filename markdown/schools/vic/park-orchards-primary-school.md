1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Park Orchards is an affluent, family-oriented suburb. The 2021 Census recorded a median weekly household income of $3,155 and a median age of 45 [citation:2021 Park Orchards QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22036). With 1,058 families and an average of 2.1 children per family, the suburb is predominantly composed of families rather than singles [citation:2021 Park Orchards QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22036).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The available 2016 Census data indicates that 57.2% of Park Orchards residents had both parents born in Australia [citation:2016 Park Orchards QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22024). A specific percentage for the most common overseas country of birth was not found in the provided sources.

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is available at Park Orchards Primary School, operated by TheirCare [citation:OSHC in Park Orchards - Toddle](https://toddle.com.au/oshc/vic/park-orchards). The service runs from 7:00 am to 8:45 am in the morning and from 3:30 pm to 6:30 pm each school day [citation:Parents - Park Orchards Primary School](https://pops.vic.edu.au/parents/).

4) **School culture:** Park Orchards Primary School offers a range of named extracurriculars including an Instrumental Music Program and Junior and Senior school choirs that rehearse weekly [citation:Curriculum - Park Orchards Primary School](https://pops.vic.edu.au/curriculum/). Lunchtime clubs also include Chess Club and Minecraft Club [citation:2022 Annual Report](https://pops.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/AR-POPS-2022.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Park Orchards is a quiet, leafy suburb with a strong family feel and a median age of 45. Specific specialty or ethnic supermarkets within Park Orchards were not named in the provided sources; major supermarkets are available in nearby suburbs.

6) **Weeknight dining:** Specific family-friendly takeaway or dining options located within Park Orchards were not listed in the provided sources. Nearby suburbs such as Ringwood offer a variety of casual dining cuisines including Thai, Chinese, and Indian.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** The Park Orchards Community House & Learning Centre offers a range of recreational courses and activities for children and adults [citation:Park Orchards Community House](https://www.parkorchards.org.au/). For academic support, Top of the Class Coaching provides maths tutoring in Park Orchards [citation:Private Maths Tutor Park Orchards - Top of the Class](https://topoftheclass.com.au/maths-tutoring-park-orchards.html).

8) **Commute:** Park Orchards does not have its own train station. The nearest station is Blackburn on the Belgrave/Lilydale line; the train journey from Flinders Street to Blackburn takes approximately 30 minutes [citation:Flinders Street to Park Orchards - Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Park-Orchards). From Blackburn, bus route 271 connects to Park Orchards.
