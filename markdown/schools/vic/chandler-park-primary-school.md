Below is a concise, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Chandler Park Primary School (Keysborough). I ran one search per data point and opened pages only when needed; sources are cited inline and collected at the end.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile — middle‑income, family oriented
Keysborough is a broadly middle‑income, family‑oriented suburb with a median age of 36 and a high share of married households (≈54% of adults) — signalling many established families rather than a singles precinct [citation:ABS QuickStats Keysborough 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21339). 

2) Most common overseas birthplace
The largest overseas birthplace in Keysborough is Vietnam (around 8–9% of residents), followed closely by Cambodia; Australia-born residents are under half of the suburb’s population (~43%) [citation:ABS QuickStats Keysborough 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21339).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
Chandler Park Primary hosts an on‑site TheirCare centre providing Before School Care, After School Care and Vacation/Holiday programs (term and holiday hours listed by the provider) — so formal OSHC is available at the school [citation:TheirCare Chandler Park PS](https://theircare.com.au/holiday-programs/chandler-park-ps-theircare/) [citation:StartingBlocks Chandler Park PS TheirCare](https://www.startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01HGCPSHXAKC6PEYVQ9YJTX7BG/Chandler-Park-PS-TheirCare).

4) School culture — key extracurriculars
Chandler Park runs specialist and extracurricular programs in Performing Arts, STEAM (science/technology/engineering/arts/maths) and Mandarin language classes (listed as school specialist programs) [citation:Chandler Park Primary — Learning/Curriculum](https://chandlerparkps.vic.edu.au/learning/) .

5) Suburb vibe and groceries
Keysborough is suburban and family‑oriented with local shopping and community facilities clustered around Parkmore Shopping Centre and nearby parks; it’s a practical family suburb rather than a nightlife hub [citation:ABS QuickStats Keysborough 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21339) [citation:Parkmore Shopping Centre stores](https://www.parkmoreshopping.com.au/stores-services?category=food+%26+dining). Specialty/ethnic groceries nearby include All Asia Grocery at Parkmore and several Asian/Filipino grocers serving the local community [citation:All Asia Grocery Parkmore](https://www.allasiagrocery.com.au/) [citation:Place Advisor — Keysborough supermarkets](https://keysborough-vic.place-advisor.com/supermarket/).

6) Weeknight dining — family‑friendly picks
Parkmore Shopping Centre offers easy family takeaway/dine‑in options: Nando’s (peri‑peri/Portuguese), Sushi/Sushi/Japanese outlets, and Mad Mex (Mexican) — convenient for weeknight meals [citation:Parkmore Shopping Centre stores](https://www.parkmoreshopping.com.au/stores-services?category=food+%26+dining) [citation:Sushi Sushi Parkmore](https://www.sushisushi.com.au/our-stores/parkmore-keysborough-9).

7) Tuition and enrichment near Keysborough
Local options include Kumon (Keysborough centre) and Kip McGrath (Keysborough) for maths and English tuition/enrichment programs [citation:Kumon Keysborough centre](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-KEYSBOROUGH-VIC) [citation:Kip McGrath Keysborough](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/keysborough).

8) Commute — public transport to Melbourne CBD
Keysborough does not have its own train station; the nearest major rail hub is Dandenong (≈3–4 km). Typical public‑transport trips use a local bus to Dandenong then a train to Flinders Street — total travel time to the CBD commonly falls in the ~45–60 minute range depending on connections and time of day [citation:Rome2rio Dandenong ↔ Keysborough](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Dandenong-Station/Keysborough) [citation:Moovit Keysborough public transport](https://moovitapp.com/index/en-gb/public_transportation-Keysborough-Melbourne-city_35030-2803).
