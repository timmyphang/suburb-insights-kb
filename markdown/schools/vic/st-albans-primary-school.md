1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** St Albans is a working-class, culturally diverse suburb, with a median weekly household income of $1,205, significantly lower than the Victorian median [citation:2021 St Albans (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22330). The median age is 36, and the suburb has a strong family presence, with 9,391 families and an average of 1.8 children per family with children [citation:2021 St Albans (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22330).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas country of birth is Vietnam, with 22.5% of residents born there, making it the dominant birthplace outside Australia [citation:2021 St Albans (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22330). Only 38.3% of residents were born in Australia, well below the national average, reflecting the suburb's high migrant concentration [citation:2021 St Albans (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22330).
