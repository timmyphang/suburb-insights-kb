1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Diamond Creek is a middle-to-upper-income, family-oriented suburb with a strong white-collar workforce. The median weekly household income is $2,508, well above the Victorian median of $1,759, and the median age is 39 years, reflecting a mature family demographic [citation:QuickStats 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20752). The area has a high proportion of families, with 3,588 families recorded, and a low proportion of single-parent households [citation:QuickStats 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20752).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** According to the 2016 Census, the most common overseas country of birth in Diamond Creek was England at 4.3%, followed by New Zealand at 0.9% [citation:2016 QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20746). The suburb is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 61.5% of residents having both parents born in Australia [citation:2016 QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20746).

3) **Working-parent support:** Diamond Creek Primary School partners with Thrive Outside School Hours Care to provide OSHC services [citation:Information for Parents](https://www.diamondcreekps.vic.edu.au/parents). Specific hours for before and after school care are not published on the school website, but the provider typically offers 7:00 AM–8:45 AM and 3:30 PM–6:30 PM care, with holiday programs from 7:00 AM–6:30 PM [citation:OSHC DCEPS](https://www.dceps.vic.edu.au/oshc). Note: the source for hours is from Diamond Creek East Primary School (a different school), as exact hours for Diamond Creek Primary School were not found in available sources.

4) **School culture:** Diamond Creek Primary School offers a Human Powered Vehicle (HPV) program, which involves designing and racing a custom vehicle at the Maryborough event [citation:Extracurricular Activities](https://www.diamondcreekps.vic.edu.au/extracurricular-activities). The school also runs a Junior Rockers instrumental music program providing weekly tuition in guitar, keyboard, drums, and vocals [citation:Junior Rockers](http://www.diamondcreekps.vic.edu.au/junior_rockers.html). Additionally, students can participate in the Victorian State School Spectacular Massed Choir each year [citation:Curriculum](https://www.diamondcreekps.vic.edu.au/curriculum).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Diamond Creek has a village-like feel with a strong community focus, offering a choice of supermarkets including Coles at 67 Main Hurstbridge Road and Ritchies Supa IGA at 64 Main Hurstbridge Road [citation:Fresh Food & Supermarkets](https://www.diamondcreekshopping.com.au/places/category/fresh-food-supermarkets/). Specialty grocers include DC Meats (butcher) at 6/72 Main Hurstbridge Road and Bakers Delight at 62B Hurstbridge Road [citation:Fresh Food & Supermarkets](https://www.diamondcreekshopping.com.au/places/category/fresh-food-supermarkets/). No international or ethnic supermarkets were identified within Diamond Creek in the available sources, though Wanlong Oriental Supermarket is located in nearby Wollongong (not in Diamond Creek) [citation:Wanlong Oriental](https://wanlongoriental.com.au/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Diamond Creek offers several casual, family-friendly takeaway options. La Sera Pizza, Pasta & Ribs (6/75 Main Hurstbridge Road) serves Italian cuisine with dine-in and takeaway [citation:Take Away Food](https://www.diamondcreekshopping.com.au/places/category/take-away-food/). Indian Hub (4/24 Chute Street) provides Indian takeaway and delivery [citation:Indian Hub](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/indian-hub-diamond-creek). Aksorn Thai Restaurant (1/72 Main Hurstbridge Road) offers authentic Thai takeaway and dining [citation:Restaurants](https://www.diamondcreekshopping.com.au/places/category/restaurant-take-away-food/).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Diamond Creek Primary School itself offers a school-based Learning Enhancement Program (Tutoring) for students needing additional literacy and numeracy support [citation:Learning](https://www.diamondcreekps.vic.edu.au/learning). For private tutoring, Cloud Tuition provides online tutoring for Years 2–12 in English, Maths, and Science, with personalised plans for Diamond Creek students [citation:Cloud Tuition](https://www.cloudtuition.com.au/locations/tutoring-vic/diamond-creek). Another option is Heather Ronngard Educational Consultancy, which offers both remedial and enrichment assistance for primary to mature-age students (listed on Yellow Pages) [citation:Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/diamond-creek-vic/tuition-tutoring).

8) **Commute:** Diamond Creek is served by the Hurstbridge train line, with Diamond Creek Station located on the line. The typical train travel time from Diamond Creek Station to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD is approximately 52 minutes, with trains running hourly and costing $4 each way [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Diamond-Creek/Flinders-Street-Station).
