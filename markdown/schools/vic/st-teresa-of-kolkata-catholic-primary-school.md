1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Tarneit North is a young, middle-income, family-oriented area. The median age is just 29, and the median weekly household income is $2,198, which is above the Australian national median [citation:2021 Tarneit - North, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/213051584). Housing is overwhelmingly detached houses (99.2%), and households average 3.4 people, indicating a strong family presence [citation:Tarneit - North – SA2 213051584 | AreaSearch](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/tarneit_north). The population is 7,197 (2021 Census), and the suburb is characterised by a high proportion of rental properties compared to the broader region [citation:Tarneit - North – SA2 213051584 | AreaSearch](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/tarneit_north).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** For Tarneit North specifically, the top country of birth for overseas-born residents was not listed in the available SA2-level QuickStats [citation:2021 Tarneit - North, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/213051584). However, for the broader suburb of Tarneit (which includes Tarneit North), the most common country of birth was India at 18.8% (2016 Census data), and only 17.9% of people had both parents born in Australia [citation:2016 Tarneit, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22439).

3) **Working-parent support:** St Teresa of Kolkata Catholic Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by **Big Child Care** [citation:Before & After School Care - St Teresa of Kolkata Primary](https://stktarneitnorth.catholic.edu.au/parents/before-after-school-care/). During school term, Before School Care operates from **6:30am to 8:45am**, and After School Care runs from **3:15pm to 6:30pm** [citation:Before & After School Care - St Teresa of Kolkata Primary](https://stktarneitnorth.catholic.edu.au/parents/before-after-school-care/). Holiday care is also available from **7:00am to 6:00pm** [citation:Before & After School Care - St Teresa of Kolkata Primary](https://stktarneitnorth.catholic.edu.au/parents/before-after-school-care/).

4) **School culture:** St Teresa of Kolkata Catholic Primary School provides a broad curriculum including specialist subjects in **Physical Education**, **Languages other than English (LOTE)**, **Art**, and **Science** [citation:Our School - St Teresa of Kolkata Primary](https://stktarneitnorth.catholic.edu.au/our-school/). The school also implements the **Respectful Relationships** program, developed by Deakin University and the University of Melbourne, to build social skills [citation:Respectful Relationships - St Teresa of Kolkata Primary](https://stktarneitnorth.catholic.edu.au/student-wellbeing/respectful-relationships/). The school’s Religious Education program is central to its Catholic identity, drawing from the MACS Religious Education Curriculum Framework [citation:Our School - St Teresa of Kolkata Primary](https://stktarneitnorth.catholic.edu.au/our-school/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Tarneit North is a new and growing suburb with a suburban, family-friendly feel. For specialty groceries, the **Tarneit Central Shopping Centre** (on Derrimut Road) houses **Tarneit Asian Supermarket** (offering Japanese, Korean, and Chinese produce) and **Yes Yes Indian Supermarket** (catering to Indian, Pakistani, Sri Lankan, and other South Asian communities) [citation:Yes Yes Indian supermarket | We Can’t Say No](https://www.yesyesindiansupermarket.com.au/); [citation:Tarneit Asian Supermarket](https://www.tarneitcentral.com.au/stores/supermarkets/tarneit-asian-supermarket). Other local Indian grocery options include **Tales of India** for online delivery and **India Together** on Sayers Road [citation:Indian Grocery Stores Tarneit, VIC](https://singh.com.au/indian-grocery-stores-tarneit-vic/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Casual family-friendly dining options in Tarneit include **Dosa Hut Tarneit** (Indian cuisine, located at Wyndham Village Shopping Centre, 380 Sayers Road) offering dine-in, takeaway, and delivery [citation:Best Indian Restaurant in Tarneit | Authentic Indian Food](https://www.dosahut.net.au/indian-restaurant-tarneit/). Another option is **Kesari Tarneit** (Indian, providing dine-in, takeaway, and catering) [citation:Kesari Tarneit - Kesari Indian Kitchen](https://kesari.com.au/kesari-tarneit/). For pizza, **Pizza Kings (Tarneit)** offers home delivery and pick-up [citation:Pizza Kings (Tarneit) - Order Online](https://pizzakingstarneit.com.au/).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Brainworks Tarneit** provides one-to-one tutoring in Maths and English, and offers scholarship and selective entry exam preparation (e.g., for Melbourne High and Suzanne Cory High) at the Penrose Promenade Community Centre [citation:Brainworks Tarneit. One to One Tutoring For Your Child](https://www.brainworks.com.au/venues/tutoring-tarneit). **Kip McGrath Tarneit** (based at 239 Hogans Road, Hoppers Crossing) offers Maths and English tuition for primary and secondary students, as well as NAPLAN preparation and early years programmes, with options for in-centre or online sessions [citation:KMEC Tarneit Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/tarneit).

8) **Commute:** Commuters from Tarneit North can use **Tarneit Railway Station** on the **Geelong line** (V/Line service) [citation:Tarneit Station to Flinders Street Station - 6 ways to travel](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Tarneit-Station/Flinders-Street-Station). The train journey from Tarneit Station to Southern Cross Station (in the CBD) takes approximately **31 minutes**, and the total travel time to Flinders Street Station via a transfer at Footscray is around **42 minutes** [citation:Tarneit Station to Flinders Street Station Trains | 7-Day Timetable](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Tarneit-Station/Flinders-Street-Station). Trains run at least every 30 minutes [citation:Tarneit Station to Flinders Street Station Trains | 7-Day Timetable](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Tarneit-Station/Flinders-Street-Station).
