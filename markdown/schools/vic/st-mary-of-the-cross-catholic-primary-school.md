1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Point Cook is a middle-to-upper-middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,392 and a median age of 33 [citation:2021 Point Cook, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22086). It has 18,022 families and an average of 1.8 children per family, reflecting a strong family demographic. The workforce is predominantly white-collar, with many residents employed in professional, managerial, and administrative roles.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The most common overseas country of birth is not explicitly listed in the available Census data, but Point Cook is notably multicultural with a large Indian-born community. ABC News reports that the suburb has a significant Indian Fijian population and also notes a relatively large Papua New Guinean presence [citation:Census 2021 data reveals Australia's most multicultural suburbs and growing diversity - ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2022-07-07/multicultural-suburbs-cultural-diversity-census-point-cook/101200006). The presence of numerous Indian grocery stores further supports the strong Indian community [citation:Indian Grocery Shops in Point Cook, VIC](https://singh.com.au/location/point-cook_vic/category/indian-grocery-shops/).

3) **Working-parent support**: Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) at St Mary of the Cross Catholic Primary School is provided by OSHClub [citation:St Mary of the Cross Catholic Primary School | OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/st-mary-of-the-cross-catholic-primary-school/). The service operates before school from 6:30am to 8:45am and after school until 6:00pm (hours confirmed by KindiCare for the centre) [citation:St Mary of the Cross OSHClub - Point Cook 3030](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3030/point-cook/st-mary-of-the-cross-oshclub). Vacation care is also available.

4) **School culture**: The school’s website states it offers a “wide range of extracurricular activities,” but specific named programs are not listed in the available sources [citation:Home - St Mary of the Cross Catholic Primary School Point Cook](https://www.smocptcook.catholic.edu.au/). The Parents and Friends Community (PFC) organises social events like school discos and trivia nights, but these are community fundraisers rather than student extracurriculars [citation:PFC - St Mary of the Cross Catholic Primary School Point Cook](https://www.smocptcook.catholic.edu.au/pfc). For named extracurriculars, please contact the school directly, as the provided materials do not include details.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Point Cook has a vibrant, family-friendly atmosphere with a mix of parks, schools, and shopping centres. Stockland Point Cook shopping centre houses specialty grocers such as **East West Marketplace** (Asian and multicultural groceries) and **Glory Asian Grocery** [citation:East West Marketplace - Point Cook](https://www.stockland.com.au/shopping-centres/centres/stockland-point-cook/stores/east-west-marketplace)[citation:Stores](https://www.stockland.com.au/shopping-centres/centres/stockland-point-cook/stores/glory-asian-grocery). Independent Indian grocery stores like **Karma Indian Grocery Store** and **India Supermarket Point Cook** also serve the community [citation:Indian Grocery Shops in Point Cook, VIC](https://singh.com.au/location/point-cook_vic/category/indian-grocery-shops/).

6) **Weeknight dining**: Families have several casual dining options in Point Cook. **Priya Indian Cuisine** (Shop 2/300 Point Cook Rd) offers authentic North Indian dishes with takeaway and dine-in [citation:Priya Indian Cuisine - Point Cook](https://pointcook.priyaindiancuisine.com.au/). **Kampung Story** (45/300 Point Cook Rd) serves Malaysian-Asian food and is popular for family takeaway [citation:Order Thai Food in Point Cook](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/point-cook/thai). For pizza and fish-and-chips, **Jessie’s Pizza** is a local favourite, as noted on TripAdvisor [citation:THE 10 BEST Restaurants in Point Cook](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g659928-Point_Cook_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Two well-known tutoring centres in Point Cook are **Success Tutoring Point Cook** (Suite 706, 2 Main St, Stockland Shopping Centre) and **Pre Uni Victoria Tutoring** (also located in Point Cook). Success Tutoring offers personalised programs for primary and high school students, while Pre Uni Victoria provides NAPLAN and selective school test preparation [citation:Point Cook Tutoring – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/point-cook-tutoring)[citation:Point Cook Tutoring | Best Primary & High School Tutors Melbourne](https://preunivictoria.com.au/).

8) **Commute**: The nearest train station to Point Cook is **Williams Landing Station** on the **Werribee line**. The train journey from Williams Landing to Flinders Street Station takes approximately **50 minutes** [citation:Point Cook to Melbourne - 3 ways to travel via train, taxi](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Point-Cook/Melbourne). Trains run every 20 minutes during peak times, and a single fare costs about $8.
