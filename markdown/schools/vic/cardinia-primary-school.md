1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Cardinia is a growing, middle-income suburb within Cardinia Shire, with a median weekly household income of $1,874 and a median age of 34, indicating a young, family-oriented population [citation:ABS QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/LGA21450). The shire is a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers, with many residents working in nearby Pakenham and Melbourne. The area is characterised by new housing developments and a strong sense of community.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: Specific data on the most common overseas country of birth for Cardinia suburb is not found in the available sources. The broader Cardinia Shire is predominantly Australian-born, with a median age of 34 and a population that includes growing numbers of families from English-speaking and Asian backgrounds.

3) **Working-parent support**: Cardinia Primary School offers on-site Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by DS Sport [citation:Cardinia PS Before and After School Care](https://www.cardiniaps.vic.edu.au/page/103/Before-and-After-School-Care). Before school care runs from 6:30 am until the school bell, and after school care operates from the bell until 6:30 pm, Monday to Friday during term.

4) **School culture**: Cardinia Primary School runs several named extracurriculars, including the **Choir** (performs at the annual Art & Science Show), **Buddy Activities** (e.g., Tabloid Sports where older and younger students pair up), and a **Whole School Camp** (annual camp for Foundation to Grade 6 at a farm, beach, city, or bush location) [citation:Cardinia PS Whole School](https://www.cardiniaps.vic.edu.au/page/78/Whole-School). The school also hosts a Whole School Sleepover with a disco and movie.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Cardinia itself is a small rural-residential locality with limited shopping; the nearest major amenities are in Pakenham, about 5 km away. No specific ethnic or specialty supermarkets are listed in Cardinia in the provided sources, but residents can access Asian groceries at **Wanlong Oriental** (Wollongong) or Indian groceries via online searches – none within Cardinia itself.

6) **Weeknight dining**: Within Cardinia, dining options are scarce, but nearby Pakenham offers family-friendly venues such as **Cardinia Club** (modern Australian bistro with a kids’ zone, open for lunch and dinner) [citation:Cardinia Club](https://cardiniaclub.com.au/) and **Shanikas Pakenham** (Italian restaurant with takeaway). Both are casual and suitable for families.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: **Ezy Math Tutoring** provides in-home English and maths tutoring for Cardinia students, with tutors who are qualified teachers or high-achieving graduates [citation:Ezy Math Tutoring](https://www.ezymathtutoring.com.au/tutors/cardinia-3978/learn-english). No physical tutoring centre within Cardinia itself is named in the provided sources.

8) **Commute**: The nearest train station is **Cardinia Road Station** on the **Pakenham line** (Metro Trains). The direct train journey from Cardinia Road Station to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD takes approximately **1 hour 5–7 minutes** [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Cardinia-Road-Station). Trains run every 20 minutes during peak times.
