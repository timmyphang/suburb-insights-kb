1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Greenvale is a middle-to-upper-income, family-dominated suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,262 and a median age of 35 [citation:2021 Greenvale (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21105). The housing stock is overwhelmingly detached (97%) and large (72% have 4+ bedrooms), with 55% of homes owned with a mortgage and only 12.5% rented, reflecting an owner-occupier, mortgage-led market [citation:Greenvale, VIC 3059 - Suburb Profile](https://daleads.com.au/suburbs/greenvale-vic-3059/). The median house price in early 2024 was $920,000, indicating an established middle-income area with strong family orientation [citation:Greenvale, VIC 3059 - Suburb Profile](https://daleads.com.au/suburbs/greenvale-vic-3059/).

The most common overseas country of birth is India, accounting for 3.2% of residents, followed by England (1.3%) and Egypt (1.2%) [citation:Greenvale VIC — Population, Median Income, Rent & Demographics](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/greenvale-vic).
