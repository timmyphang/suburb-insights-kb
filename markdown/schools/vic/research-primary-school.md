1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Research is an affluent, predominantly family-oriented suburb in Melbourne's outer north-east. The median weekly household income is $2,194, and the median age is 41 years, reflecting a mature, established population with a high proportion of white-collar professionals [citation:Research suburb profile](https://profile.id.com.au/s_research/suburb-profile). The suburb scores very highly on the SEIFA index of advantage, indicating significantly higher socio-economic status than the Australian average.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Research is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 83.6% of residents born in Australia [citation:Research suburb profile](https://profile.id.com.au/s_research/suburb-profile). The largest overseas-born group is from England, accounting for 5.2% of the population, followed by New Zealand at 1.2% and Germany at 0.5%.

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided at Research Primary School by the national provider OSHClub [citation:Students | Research Primary School](https://researchps.vic.edu.au/students/). The service operates before school from 7:00 am to 8:45 am and after school from 3:30 pm to 6:00 pm on school days, with vacation care also available during school holidays.

4) **School culture:** Research Primary School offers a vibrant enrichment program that includes a **Whole School Production** (with past shows like *Rumble in the Jungle*), a **Camping Program for Years 3–6**, and **Interschool Sport** [citation:Students | Research Primary School](https://researchps.vic.edu.au/students/). Instrumental music lessons are also available, including a piano and keyboards program, and a guitar, ukulele, and drum program.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Research has a quiet, semi-rural bushland feel with strong community spirit, featuring a general store, bakery, and local parks. For major grocery shopping, the nearest supermarket is a Coles at **Eltham** (about 5 km away), and specialty grocers are found in nearby **Eltham** and **Greensborough** (e.g., an Asian grocery and an IGA). No dedicated ethnic supermarkets are located within Research itself.

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual, family-friendly dining, options in nearby Eltham include **Pizza Art** (Italian-style pizza and pasta), **Eltham Chinese Takeaway** (Chinese cuisine), and **Eltham Fish & Chips** (Australian fish and chips). Within Research, the **Research Hotel** offers a family-friendly bistro with classic pub meals.

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** In nearby Eltham, **Kip McGrath Eltham** provides tutoring in English and maths for primary and secondary students. **Eltham North Primary School** also hosts a **Maths Club** enrichment program. No tutoring centres are located directly in Research.

8) **Commute:** Research is served by the **Hurstbridge** train line, with the nearest station being **Eltham Station** (approximately 3 km from the school). The train journey from Eltham Station to Flinders Street Station in the CBD takes about **35–40 minutes** [citation:Timetable Search | Metro Trains](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/timetables/). A bus route (Route 578 from Research to Eltham Station) connects the suburb to the train.
