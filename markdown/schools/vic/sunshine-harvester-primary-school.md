1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Sunshine is a working-class, culturally diverse suburb with a mix of families and singles. The median weekly household income is $1,566, which is below both the Victorian ($1,759) and Australian ($1,746) medians [citation:2021 Sunshine (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22395).
