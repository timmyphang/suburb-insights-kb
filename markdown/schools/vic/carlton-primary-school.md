1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Carlton is a densely populated inner-city suburb with a median age of 27, indicating a very young population dominated by students and young professionals. The median weekly household income is $1,292, which is below the Victorian median, and the average household size is just 1.8 people, reflecting many single and couple-only households [citation:2021 Carlton (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20495). With only 2,533 families in the suburb and a very low 0.2 children per household, Carlton is not a traditional family-oriented suburb but is home to a mix of white-collar workers and students [citation:2021 Carlton (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20495).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Carlton is a vibrant, multicultural suburb with a strong Italian heritage and a lively cafe scene around Lygon Street. For groceries, families can shop at Mr Dad Asian Supermarket, which has locations at 168 Victoria St and Shop 1, 557 Swanston St, both open daily from 10am–11pm [citation:Mr Dad Asian Supermarket](https://mrdadasiansupermarket.com.au/).
