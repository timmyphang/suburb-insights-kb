1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Belgrave South is a middle-to-high-income, family-dominated suburb with a semi-rural character. The median weekly household income is $2,382, and the median age is 44, reflecting a mature population of long-term residents [citation:2021 Belgrave South, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20190). Family households are the norm, with 486 families recorded and an average of 1.9 children per family, while homeownership rates are high and rental housing is limited [citation:BELGRAVE SOUTH VIC, AU SUBURB PROFILE - FREE REPORT - Smart Property Investment](https://www.smartpropertyinvestment.com.au/data/vic/3160/belgrave-south).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** According to the 2016 Census, 59.5% of Belgrave South residents had both parents born in Australia, and 21.8% had both parents born overseas [citation:2016 Belgrave South, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC20189). The most common individual overseas country of birth for 2021 is not specified in the available sources; however, the suburb is overwhelmingly Australian-born.

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care at Belgrave South Primary School is provided by OSH Club. Before-school care ("Rise then Shine") runs from 7:00 AM until the school bell, and after-school care ("Stay and Play") runs from bell time until 6:00 PM, with holiday programs also available [citation:Before & After School Care - Belgrave South Primary School](https://www.belgravesouthps.vic.edu.au/before-after-school-care/) [citation:Belgrave South Primary School | OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/belgrave-south-primary-school/).

4) **School culture:** Belgrave South Primary School offers an extensive program of lunchtime clubs. Named examples include Basketball Club (run by Year 6 House Captains), Dance Club (student-led for Years 3–6), and Lego Club (open to all year levels) [citation:Lunchtime Clubs - Belgrave South Primary School](https://www.belgravesouthps.vic.edu.au/lunchtime-clubs/). Additional offerings include Instrumental Music, inter-school sports, and an intensive swimming program [citation:About Us - Belgrave South Primary School](https://www.belgravesouthps.vic.edu.au/about-us/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Belgrave South is a quiet, low-density suburb set among bushland, with residents relying on nearby Belgrave for most retail needs. The main local grocery is IGA Belgrave South, a friendly supermarket offering fresh meat, deli, and liquor [citation:IGA Belgrave South | Belgrave South VIC | Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/IGABELGRAVESOUTH/). Larger supermarkets such as Woolworths are available in Belgrave, about a 5-minute drive away [citation:19 BEST local Supermarkets & Grocery Stores in Belgrave South, VIC | Yellow Pages®](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/belgrave-south-vic-3160).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Casual family-friendly dining options are found in nearby Belgrave. Babaji’s Kerala Kitchen offers authentic South Indian curries and dosa (dog-friendly) [citation:Belgrave Indian Restaurant | Babaji's Kerala Kitchen Belgrave](https://www.babajiskitchen.com.au/our-restaurants/belgrave.html).
