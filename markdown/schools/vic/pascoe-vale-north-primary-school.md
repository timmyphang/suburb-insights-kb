1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Pascoe Vale is a middle-to-upper-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,025 (2021 Census) and a median age of 36, indicating a mix of young families and working professionals [citation:2021 Pascoe Vale, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22041). The suburb has a strong owner-occupier rate of 58% and a rental proportion of 42%, with a resident base of dual-income households and a property market that is increasingly competitive [citation:Pascoe Vale Suburb Profile 2026 | Lifestyle & Market](https://www.collings.com.au/pascoe-vale-suburb-profile-2026-lifestyle-demographics-market/). It sits roughly 10 km north of Melbourne’s CBD and is described as a well-connected, family-friendly area with tree-lined streets [citation:Pascoe Vale Suburb Profile 2026 | Lifestyle & Market](https://www.collings.com.au/pascoe-vale-suburb-profile-2026-lifestyle-demographics-market/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: According to the 2021 Census, 66% of Pascoe Vale residents were born in Australia [citation:2021 Pascoe Vale, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22041). Among overseas-born residents, Italian and Lebanese communities are prominent, though the exact percentage for a single country is not specified in the available sources [citation:Pascoe Vale Suburb Profile 2026 | Lifestyle & Market](https://www.collings.com.au/pascoe-vale-suburb-profile-2026-lifestyle-demographics-market/). More recently, Vietnamese and Chinese communities have also grown in the suburb.

3) **Working-parent support**: Pascoe Vale North Primary School’s Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is provided by TheirCare, as indicated by the school’s OSHC page linking to theircare.com.au [citation:OSHC — Pascoe Vale North Primary School](https://www.pvnps.vic.edu.au/oshc). Specific operating hours are not listed on the school website; parents are advised to contact the school directly at (03) 9354 3343 for exact before- and after-school times. The service is based on the school grounds for the convenience of families.

4) **School culture**: Pascoe Vale North Primary School offers a **Rock band program** for Grades 3–6, providing students with opportunities to perform in a band setting [citation:Pascoe Vale North Primary Specialists — Pascoe Vale North Primary School](https://www.pvnps.vic.edu.au/specialists). During lunchtimes, the **Sports Garage** is opened several times a week, supervised by student leaders, where children can borrow sports equipment for active play [citation:PVNPS is a learning hub… — Pascoe Vale North Primary School](https://www.pvnps.vic.edu.au/school-profile). The school also runs a **Junior School Council** and student-led lunchtime clubs to foster voice and leadership [citation:Student Voice — Pascoe Vale North Primary School](https://www.pvnps.vic.edu.au/student-voice).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Pascoe Vale has a strong community feel with several grocery options catering to diverse tastes. **Pascoe Vale Road Market** (on Pascoe Vale Road, Glenroy) offers a wide range of fresh produce, international foods and Mediterranean groceries [citation:PASCOE VALE ROAD MARKET - Grocery store in Glenroy](https://pascoe-vale-road-market.com-place.com/). For Italian-style shopping, **Piedimontes Supermarket** at 366–368 Bell Street, Pascoe Vale South is a popular choice, while **Wingstar Asian Grocery** at Union Square Shopping Centre in nearby Brunswick West supplies Asian staples [citation:THE BEST 10 Supermarkets and Grocery Stores in Pascoe Vale South, Victoria](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/victoria/pascoe-vale-south/shopping/supermarkets-and-grocery-stores/). A **FoodWorks** supermarket is also located at 173 Derby Street in Pascoe Vale.

6) **Weeknight dining**: Families have several casual, family-friendly options in Pascoe Vale. **Circa 900 Pizzeria Napoletana** (on Reynard Street, Pascoe Vale South) was voted Best Pizza in Melbourne 2025 and offers authentic Neapolitan-style pizza in a relaxed setting [citation:Restaurants in Pascoe Vale,VIC With Takeaway | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/pascoe-vale/takeaway-category). **Shop 225 Pizzeria** (Sussex Street) is another award-winning Italian option, known for handcrafted pizzas ranked among the world’s top 30. For a more casual takeaway, **Pascoe Vale Seafoods** (available on Uber Eats) provides fish and chips and other seafood [citation:Takeaway in Pascoe Vale - Order Food Delivery Near Me | Uber Eats](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/pascoe-vale-melbourne-vic).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: **Kip McGrath Pascoe Vale** (also known as KMEC Pascoe Vale Tuition Centre) is located at 1/266 Sussex Street, Pascoe Vale, offering professional tuition in English and maths for primary and secondary students, both in-centre and online [citation:KMEC Pascoe Vale Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/pascoe-vale). The centre provides obligation-free assessments and personalised learning plans with qualified teachers. For broader subject support, **Ezy Math Tutoring** connects families with tutors in Pascoe Vale who cover maths, science, English and more, with in-home or online options [citation:55 Maths Tutors in Pascoe Vale | Ezy Math Tutoring](https://www.ezymathtutoring.com.au/tutors/pascoe-vale-3044).

8) **Commute**: Pascoe Vale is served by **Pascoe Vale Station** on the **Craigieburn line** (Metro Trains Melbourne). A direct train from Pascoe Vale Station to Flinders Street Station takes approximately **27 minutes**, with services running every 20 minutes [citation:Pascoe Vale Station to Flinders Street Station - 3 ways](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Pascoe-Vale-Station/Flinders-Street-Station). The station is a short walk or drive from most parts of the suburb, making the CBD easily accessible for commuting workers.
