1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Scoresby is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $2,167 and a median age of 40 years, which is slightly older than the national median of 38 [citation:GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/scoresby-vic). The population is 6,066, and dwellings are predominantly owned outright (45.9%) or with a mortgage (54.1%), indicating a stable, home-owning community [citation:GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/scoresby-vic). The workforce is primarily white-collar, with 40.5% employed full-time and 22.7% part-time, reflecting a mix of professional and trade occupations [citation:GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/scoresby-vic).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** While 76.8% of Scoresby residents were born in Australia, the most common overseas country of birth is China, accounting for 7.4% of the population [citation:GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/scoresby-vic). India is the second most common overseas birthplace at 7.2%, followed by England at 3.3% [citation:GDP.com.au](https://gdp.com.au/suburb/scoresby-vic).

3) **Working-parent support:** St Jude the Apostle School partners with Camp Australia to run its Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) program [citation:Before & After Care](https://www.sjscoresby.catholic.edu.au/before-and-after-care/). The program operates before school (morning) and after school from 3:00 pm to 6:00 pm on weekdays, with pupil-free day and school holiday programs also available [citation:Camp Australia St Jude](https://campaustralia.com.au/school/?accessCode=94D6). [citation:Kindicare](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3179/scoresby/camp-australia-st-jude-the-apostle-school-oshc).

4) **School culture:** St Jude’s offers a variety of named extracurricular activities, including chess, robotics, and coding clubs [citation:Curriculum](https://sjscoresby.catholic.edu.au/curriculum/). Students can also participate in karate, gardening, and the school’s running club [citation:Curriculum](https://sjscoresby.catholic.edu.au/curriculum/). The school participates in external competitions such as STEM challenges, Tournament of the Minds, and debating and chess competitions [citation:How We Support](https://www.sjscoresby.catholic.edu.au/how-we-support/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Scoresby is a quiet, family-friendly suburb with convenient shopping at Woolworths on Ferntree Gully Road and Scoresby Convenience Store on Berrabri Drive [citation:Yellow Pages Supermarkets](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/scoresby-vic-3179). For specialty groceries, the area includes Prime Sun Asian Groceries in nearby Wantirna South and Knoxgate Foodworks [citation:Yellow Pages Supermarkets](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/scoresby-vic-3179). An Asian grocery store is also listed for sale in Scoresby, indicating local availability of ethnic foods [citation:Asian Grocery Store](https://www.commercialrealestate.com.au/business-for-sale/asian-grocery-store-bd-0918-2017825381).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Scoresby Thai on 10 Darryl St is a popular family-friendly restaurant offering authentic Thai cuisine with takeaway and delivery options [citation:Scoresby Thai](https://www.scoresbythai.com.au/). Reviewer favorites include the Scoresby fried rice and Thai green curry, noted as kid-friendly [citation:Scoresby Thai](https://www.scoresbythai.com.au/). Menulog lists additional takeaway options in the area, though specific names beyond Scoresby Thai could not be confirmed from the available sources [citation:Menulog](https://www.menulog.com.au/area/3179-scoresby/thai).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Specific tutoring centres in Scoresby include Kumon Wheelers Hill Education Centre, located nearby on Jells Road in Wheelers Hill [citation:Yellow Pages Tutoring](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/tuition-tutoring/scoresby-vic-3179). Strive For Excellence Tutoring is another option, found on Ferntree Gully Road in adjacent Knoxfield [citation:Yellow Pages Tutoring](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/tuition-tutoring/scoresby-vic-3179). Additional in-home and online services like Alchemy Tuition also serve the Scoresby area [citation:Alchemy Tuition](https://alchemytuition.com.au/melbourne-tutoring-scoresby/).

8) **Commute:** Scoresby does not have its own train station. To reach Melbourne CBD, families can take the line 693 bus from Caribbean Drive/Ferntree Gully Road to Oakleigh Station, then board a Cranbourne or Pakenham line train to Flinders Street Station, with a total travel time of approximately 58 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Scoresby/Flinders-Street-Station). Alternatively, the line 753 bus connects to Glen Waverley Station, arriving at Flinders Street Station in about 62 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Scoresby/Flinders-Street-Station). By car, the drive to the city is roughly 22 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Scoresby/Flinders-Street-Station).
