Below is a short, practical briefing for families considering enrolling at Virtual School Victoria (VSV) in Thornbury.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
Thornbury is an inner‑north Melbourne suburb that has seen noticeable gentrification over the past decade and is best described as middle‑income with a strong mix of young professionals, creatives and families (a shift from its earlier working‑class character) [citation:Home | City of Darebin | Community profile](https://profile.id.com.au/darebin/) [citation:Northcote vs Thornbury: 7 Best Investment Tips 2026](https://www.collings.com.au/northcote-vs-thornbury-property-comparison/).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
The dominant overseas birthplace reported for Thornbury is Greece — Census summaries indicate a small but visible Greek‑born community (around ~4% of residents in past counts; third‑party 2021 suburb summaries list Greece at ~4.2%) [citation:2016 Thornbury, Census All persons QuickStats - Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22496) [citation:Thornbury VIC Suburb Profile — Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/thornbury).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
Virtual School Victoria is a state F–12 virtual/distance education school; VSV’s website emphasises that learning is delivered online and that parents/carers or nominated school supervisors support students studying outside normal school hours. VSV does not advertise an on‑site Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) program for routine before/after‑school childcare in the way a mainstream primary usually does — families should plan for home supervision or third‑party care if needed [citation:Parents and supervisors - Virtual School Victoria](https://www.vsv.vic.edu.au/parents-supervisors/) [citation:Contact - Virtual School Victoria](https://www.vsv.vic.edu.au/contact/).

4) School culture — extracurriculars
VSV offers a range of co‑curricular clubs and activities (examples: Music Club and subject‑level instrumental/performance options, Duke of Edinburgh Award, and inter‑school sport competitions), plus online student clubs (Art, Book, Games, Philosophy/Debating, etc.) to build community for remote learners [citation:School clubs - Virtual School Victoria](https://www.vsv.vic.edu.au/students/activities/school-clubs/) [citation:Year 12 Music Repertoire Performance Units 3 and 4 - Virtual School Victoria](https://www.vsv.vic.edu.au/subject/music-repertoire-performance-unit-3-and-4/).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
Thornbury has a village/high‑street feel (High Street & surrounding streets) with good local parks, bike and creek trails (Merri/Darebin creek corridors) that suit families and outdoor activity, alongside cafe culture and creative businesses [citation:Thornbury City of Darebin](https://www.darebin.vic.gov.au/Events-and-facilities/Suburb-guides/Thornbury). Local grocery options include a neighbourhood IGA and small Mediterranean/Greek grocers (examples: IGA Thornbury and Psarakos Market); larger Mediterranean‑style wholesalers are nearby in Brunswick [citation:IGA Thornbury | IGA Supermarkets](https://www.iga.com.au/stores/iga-thornbury/) [citation:Psarakos Market in Thornbury, VIC 3071 - business listing](https://australia.chamberofcommerce.com/business-directory/victoria/thornbury/supermarket/3435971-psarakos-market) [citation:Mediterranean Wholesalers | Melbourne - Brunswick](https://mediterraneanwholesalers.com.au/).
