1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Springvale is a working-class, multicultural suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,402 (2021 Census), below the Victorian median of $1,759 [citation:2021 Springvale (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22328). The median age is 36, and the suburb has a high proportion of two-parent families and overseas-born residents, often classified as a 'Type 2' disadvantaged area [citation:Report Addressing concentrations of disadvantage Springvale case study report](https://www.ahuri.edu.au/sites/default/files/migration/documents/AHURI_Research_Paper_Addressing-concentrations-of-disadvantage-Springvale-case-study-report.pdf). Housing is predominantly blue-collar, with many residents working in trades (51.9% of occupations in 2011) [citation:Demographics | Springvale Ethnographic Research](https://springvaledeakin.wordpress.com/demographics/).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The most common overseas birthplace is Vietnam, with 25.0% of residents speaking Vietnamese at home (2016 Census) [citation:2016 Springvale (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22317). Only 17.6% of people speak only English at home, reflecting the suburb's strong Asian-migrant community [citation:2016 Springvale (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC22317). More recent 2021 data on country of birth is not available in the provided sources.

3) **Working-parent support**: Heatherhill Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by **Extend** [citation:Heatherhill PS OSHC - Extend | Springvale VIC Child Care](https://www.childcare.net.au/childcare/after-school-care/vic/springvale/heatherhill-ps-oshc-extend/41478). Specific operating hours (before and after school) are not listed in the available search results. Families should contact the school directly for detailed timings and fees.

4) **School culture**: Heatherhill Primary School runs several named extracurricular programs, including a daily **Breakfast Club** (funded by Red Cross, starting at 7:45 am) [citation:Heatherhill Primary School | Information | Newsletters](http://www.heatherhillps.vic.edu.au/news-090217.html), **Boys on the Bounce** to build resilience in boys, and **Girls on the Go** for girls' social and emotional development [citation:Heatherhill Primary School | Our School](http://www.heatherhillps.vic.edu.au/ourschool.html). The school also offers a **Perceptual Motor Program** for physical skill development [citation:Heatherhill Primary School | Principal's Message](http://www.heatherhillps.vic.edu.au/principal.html).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Springvale is a vibrant, family-friendly suburb with a strong Asian-community feel, centred around the Springvale Shopping Centre. For specialty groceries, families can shop at **KFL Supermarket** (Asian food specialist, located in Springvale Shopping Centre) [citation:Springvale KFL | The Asian Food Specialist](https://www.kflsupermarkets.com.au/springvale-vic) and **Num Lee Long Asian Groceries** (known for fresh Asian greens and frozen goods) [citation:Num Lee Long Asian Groceries](https://www.numleelong.com.au/). Other options include **Lien Hung Asian Grocery** and **An-Dong Asian Grocery** for a wide range of imported ingredients [citation:The Best 10 Grocery near An-Dong Asian Grocery in Springvale Victoria - Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/search?cflt=grocery&find_near=an-dong-asian-grocery-springvale).

6) **Weeknight dining**: Casual family-friendly dining in Springvale includes **E Laenoi Lao Restaurant** (Lao and Thai cuisine, 1-3 St Johns Ave) [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Springvale | TuckerFox AU](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/springvale), **Ammy Bangkok Restaurant** (Thai food, 16 Buckingham Ave) [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Springvale | TuckerFox AU](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/springvale), and **Dapur Indo Indonesia Cuisine** (Indonesian, 231 Springvale Rd) [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Springvale | TuckerFox AU](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/takeaway-delivery/springvale). These venues offer takeaway and eat-in options suitable for families.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Two tutoring centres in Springvale are **Success Tutoring Springvale** (Kindergarten to Year 12, focusing on Maths and English) [citation:Springvale Tutoring – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/springvale-tutoring) and **Strive Academy** (specialising in high school Maths and VCE Maths Methods, with small-group in-person classes) [citation:Tutors Springvale | The Best VCE Maths & English Tutoring Services - Strive Academy](https://www.striveacademytutoring.com.au/springvale-tutors). Both centres follow the Victorian curriculum and offer flexible scheduling.

8) **Commute**: Springvale Station is on the **Cranbourne line** (part of the Pakenham/Cranbourne group). The train journey from Springvale Station to Flinders Street Station takes approximately **37 minutes** via direct service [citation:Springvale (Station) to Flinders Street Station - 4 ways ...](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Springvale/Flinders-Street-Station). Trains run every 10–15 minutes during peak times.
