The school also lists extracurricular activities such as **cooking, bubble soccer, art attack, coding, and STEM days** through its OSHC program [citation:Out of School Hours Care](https://www.aberfeldieps.vic.edu.au/out-of-school-hours-care).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Essendon has a family-friendly vibe with several supermarkets including a **Coles** on Mount Alexander Road and an **IGA** on Rose Street [citation:Supermarkets in Essendon](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/essendon-vic-3040). For specialty groceries, **LaManna Supermarket**, an Italian supermarket, is located at 10 English Street in Essendon Fields [citation:LaManna - Italian Supermarket](https://www.lamanna.com.au/). Asian groceries can be found at **Wanlong Oriental** (though its listed addresses are in Wollongong and Norwa, not Essendon) or at **Hometown Asian Supermarket** and **Golden Mart Asian Grocery** mentioned in local searches [citation:Asian Grocery Stores near Essendon North](https://www.yelp.com/search?find_desc=Asian+Grocery+Stores&find_loc=Essendon+North+Victoria+3041).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly dining options in Essendon include **Max's Corner** (Italian, pizza) on Mt Alexander Road [citation:Takeaway Restaurants in Essendon](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g2019289-zfp10601-Essendon_Moonee_Valley_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). **Ipoh Garden Kitchen** offers Asian cuisine, and **Tutti Trattoria** provides Italian meals, both noted as casual and family-friendly [citation:Family-friendly Restaurants in Essendon](https://www.quandoo.com.au/melbourne-essendon/tags/family-friendly-restaurants). **LaManna Cafe** also offers takeaway Italian options [citation:Takeaway Restaurants in Essendon](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g2019289-zfp10601-Essendon_Moonee_Valley_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Tutoring centres in Essendon include **Aldon Tutoring Centres Essendon**, offering one-to-one tuition in literacy and numeracy for students from Prep to Year 12 [citation:Aldon Tutoring Centres Essendon](https://www.aldontutoringcentre.com/). **Kip McGrath Essendon** (KMEC), located at Suite 6/59 Fletcher St, provides tutoring in English, Maths, and entrance exam preparation for Primary and Secondary students [citation:KMEC Essendon Tuition Centre](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/essendon).

8) **Commute:** Essendon is served by **Essendon Station** on the **Craigieburn** train line [citation:Essendon Station to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Essendon-Station/Flinders-Street-Station).
