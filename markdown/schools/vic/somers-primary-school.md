1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Somers is an affluent, older coastal community. The median weekly household income is $1,906, and the median age is 53, significantly higher than the Victorian median of 38 [citation:2021 Somers, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22303). The suburb has a higher proportion of residents aged 55+ and a lower proportion of children under 15 compared to state averages, indicating a mix of established families and retirees [citation:2021 Somers, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22303). Occupation breakdown (white-collar vs blue-collar) is not explicitly stated in the available QuickStats.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: Somers is overwhelmingly Australian-born. According to the 2021 Census QuickStats, 76.5% of residents were born in Australia [citation:2021 Somers, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22303). The most common overseas country of birth is England at 7.1% [citation:2021 Somers, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22303).

3) **Working-parent support**: Somers Primary School operates its own Before and After School Care program, run by the school council [citation:OSHC | Somers Primary School](https://somersps.vic.edu.au/oshc/); [citation:Somers Primary School OSHC Program – BSC/ASC | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/somers-primary-school-oshc-program-bscasc). The service is open from 7:00am to 8:45am in the morning and from 3:30pm to 6:00pm after school, with approved places for 30 children [citation:OSHC | Somers Primary School](https://somersps.vic.edu.au/oshc/); [citation:Somers Primary School OSHC Program – BSC/ASC | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/somers-primary-school-oshc-program-bscasc).

4) **School culture**: Somers Primary School offers a musical instrument program and a Book Circle Program as named extracurriculars [citation:Curriculum | Somers Primary School](https://somersps.vic.edu.au/curriculum/); [citation:1 last updated: 20-02-2023 SOMERS PRIMARY SCHOOL General Information](https://somersps.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/GENERAL-INFORMATION-FOR-PARENTS.pdf). The school also runs the Somers Arts Fair, a major annual fundraiser, and specialist programs including Performing Arts, where students have explored Hip Hop culture, rap writing, and West African music [citation:About | Somers Primary School](https://somersps.vic.edu.au/about/); [citation:Curriculum | Somers Primary School](https://somersps.vic.edu.au/curriculum/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Somers has a tight-knit beachside community feel, located 250 metres from Westernport Bay [citation:Somers Primary School](https://somersps.vic.edu.au/). For groceries, there is no major supermarket in Somers itself; the closest options are Woolworths and Coles in Hastings (approx. 10 km away) at 11-23 Victoria Street, Hastings, and 9 Queen Street, Hastings respectively [citation:15 BEST local Supermarkets & Grocery Stores in Somers, VIC | Yellow Pages®](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/supermarkets-grocery-stores/somers-vic-3927). No specialty or ethnic supermarkets were found in Somers in available sources.

8) **Commute**: Somers does not have a train station. The nearest station is Hastings, on the Stony Point line, with V/Line services providing connections to Frankston and the Melbourne CBD. The typical journey from Hastings Station to Flinders Street Station takes approximately 1 hour 20 minutes by train, not including the bus connection from Somers to Hastings [citation:Somers to Flinders Street Station - 5 ways to travel via train, bus, and taxi](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Somers-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
