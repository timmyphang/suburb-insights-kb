1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Seaford (VIC) is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median age of 40 and a median weekly household income of $1,500 (equivalent to $78,000 per year) [citation:2021 Seaford (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/214011375). The population is 17,215, with 4,580 families and an average household size of 2.3, indicating a mix of families and couples [citation:2021 Seaford (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/214011375). Occupation data (white-collar vs blue-collar) is not specified in the available sources.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The most common overseas country of birth for Seaford (VIC) is not provided in the available search results. The ABS QuickStats for Seaford VIC does not include a country-of-birth breakdown in the extracted content [citation:2021 Seaford (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/214011375). For comparison, Seaford (SA) is 83.0% Australian-born with England at 15.1%, but that data is for a different state [citation:2021 Seaford (SA), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL41315).

3) **Working-parent support**: Seaford Park Primary School’s Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is operated by Team Kids. The program runs from the Performing Arts room between 7:00–8:45 am (before school) and 3:20–6:00 pm (after school) every day [citation:Outside Hours School Care](https://www.seafordpark.vic.edu.au/page/93/Outside-Hours-School-Care). Contact the Team Kids Director of Service, Lily, on 0460 273 776 for registration.

4) **School culture**: Seaford Park Primary School offers several named extracurricular activities. The Breakfast Club operates from the BER building every morning [citation:School Facilities](https://www.seafordpark.vic.edu.au/page/63/School-Facilities). The school also has a strong Student Leadership program for Years 5 and 6, who meet weekly with the Principal [citation:Student Leaders](https://www.seafordpark.vic.edu.au/page/221/Student-Leaders). Additionally, a swimming program is included in the Year 5 curriculum [citation:Seaford Park Primary School](https://www.seafordpark.vic.edu.au/uploaded_files/media/year_5_2025.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Seaford has a relaxed coastal vibe with family-friendly parks and beaches. Mainstream grocery options include Ritchies Seaford Supermarket at 280 Seaford Road (open 7:30 am–10:00 pm weekdays) and Woolworths Seaford [citation:Seaford Supermarket - Ritchies](https://seaford.shop.ritchies.com.au/). Specific ethnic or specialty grocers are not named in the available sources; however, residents can access Asian grocery stores in nearby suburbs like Chelsea or Springvale.

6) **Weeknight dining**: Seaford offers casual family-friendly takeaway and dining. Seaford Thai Takeaway (3a/366 Frankston-Dandenong Road) provides Thai cuisine for takeaway or delivery, open Tuesday–Sunday 11:30 am–9:00 pm [citation:Home » Seaford Thai Takeaway](https://seafordthai.com/). Thai Beach Cafe (also in Seaford) offers dine-in, pick-up, and delivery of Thai food, with the same hours [citation:Thai Beach Cafe](https://www.thaibeachseaford.com.au/). For fast food, Hungry Jacks and Subway are available via Uber Eats [citation:Takeaway in Seaford - Order Food Delivery Near Me | Uber Eats](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/seaford-adelaide-sa).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Success Tutoring has a Seaford centre offering personalised learning support for primary and secondary students, with 24/7 access to resources [citation:Seaford – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-seaford). Cloud Tuition provides online tutoring for Years 2–12 in English, Maths, and Science, with free trial lessons available [citation:Tutoring Seaford | Online Tutors for Years 2-12 | Cloud Tuition](https://www.cloudtuition.com.au/locations/tutoring-vic/seaford).

8) **Commute**: Seaford Station is on the Frankston line. Trains run direct to Flinders Street Station every 15 minutes, with a typical travel time of 1 hour 9 minutes [citation:Seaford to Flinders Street Station - 3 ways to travel via train](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Seaford-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). The driving distance is about 36 minutes.
