1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** St Albans is a working-class, family-oriented suburb with a mix of blue- and white-collar workers. The median weekly household income is $1,205, which is below the national median, and the median age is 36 years, reflecting a relatively young population [citation:2021 Census QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22330). There are 9,391 families in the area, and the average household size is 2.8 people, indicating a strong family presence [citation:2021 Census QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22330).

The specific top country of birth and its percentage share are not found in the provided sources.

The specific operating hours for before and after school care are not listed in the provided search snippets, but the service is confirmed to offer after-school care for children aged 5 to 12 years [citation:Camp Australia - Kings Park Primary School OSHC](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/n31wj/camp-australia-kings-park-primary-school-oshc-st-albans-3021).

4) **School culture:** Kings Park Primary School offers named extracurriculars including a **Choir** for grades 4–6, which performs at assemblies and teaches breathing techniques [citation:Choir](https://www.kpps.vic.edu.au/page/88/Choir). The school also runs **Inter-school Sports** for grades 5–6, with summer sports like cricket and basketball and winter sports like football and netball [citation:Inter-school Sports](https://www.kpps.vic.edu.au/page/81/Inter-school-Sports). Additionally, a **Junior School Council** (JSC) leads lunchtime clubs such as dancing, sport, Lego, and DIY club [citation:Junior School Council](https://www.kpps.vic.edu.au/page/72/Junior-School-Council).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** St Albans has a vibrant, multicultural atmosphere with a town centre offering diverse Asian and South Asian grocery stores. Notable speciality grocers include **Pahal Grocers** at 282 Main Road East, which stocks Sri Lankan, Pakistani, Indian, and other Asian products [citation:Pahal Grocers](https://www.stalbanstowncentre.com.au/news-stories/pahal-grocers), and **KP Asian Grocery** at 12 Alfrieda Street, offering Korean, Japanese, Vietnamese, Thai, and Malaysian foods [citation:KP Asian Grocery](https://www.stalbanstowncentre.com.au/directory/kp-asian-grocery). The **St Albans Town Centre** also hosts **Super Pinoy** for Filipino groceries and **Kim Minh Asian Grocery** for fresh produce and dry goods [citation:Super Pinoy](https://www.stalbanstowncentre.com.au/directory/super-pinoy); [citation:Kim Minh Asian Grocery](https://www.stalbanstowncentre.com.au/directory/kim-minh-asian-grocery).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly dining options in St Albans include **Phi Phi Vietnamese & Chinese Restaurant**, known for Asian cuisine and takeaway [citation:Phi Phi Vietnamese & Chinese Restaurant](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g4358126-St_Albans_Brimbank_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). **Shahi Darbaar**, located at 10A East Esplanade, offers Indian cuisine with a 5-star rating and 5% off for first-time orders [citation:Shahi Darbaar](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/st-albans-3021). **Pizza Del Mondo** is a casual pizzeria popular with families, rated 4.4 out of 5 on TripAdvisor [citation:Pizza Del Mondo](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g4358126-St_Albans_Brimbank_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Two specific tutoring centres in St Albans are **Innate Tutoring**, offering personalised VCE tutoring in English, math, biology, and chemistry for secondary students [citation:Innate Tutoring](https://www.innatetutoring.com.au/st-albans-tutoring/). Another option is **Success Tutoring St Albans**, which provides K–12 tutoring with customised learning plans and flexible booking times [citation:Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-st-albans/).

8) **Commute:** St Albans is served by the **Sunbury line** on the Metro Trains Melbourne network, with direct trains from **St Albans Station** to Flinders Street Station. The average train travel time to the CBD is **31 minutes**, with trains running every 15 minutes and fares starting at $4 [citation:Train St Albans Station to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/St-Albans-Station/Flinders-Street-Station). The journey time is typically 27–36 minutes depending on the service [citation:St Albans Station to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/St-Albans-Station/Flinders-Street-Station).
