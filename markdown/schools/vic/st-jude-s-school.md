1) Suburb socio-economic profile: Langwarrin is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,950 and a median age of 38 [citation:2021 Langwarrin QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/214011374). The suburb is predominantly suburban housing (83.4% houses) with above-average rates of outright home ownership, and a mix of white-collar and blue-collar residents, with 6,997 families and 2.7 average people per household [citation:2021 Langwarrin QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/214011374); [citation:AreaSearch Langwarrin](https://areasearch.com.au/vic/langwarrin).

2) Most common foreign country of birth: Based on the 2016 Census, the most common overseas country of birth in Langwarrin was England at 5.9% of the population, followed by New Zealand at 1.7% [citation:2016 Langwarrin QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/214011374). Overall, 57.0% of people had both parents born in Australia [citation:2016 Langwarrin QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/214011374). More recent 2021 Census data on country of birth was not provided in the available sources.

3) Working-parent support: St Jude's Primary School Langwarrin partners with TheirCare to provide Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) on-site in the school hall [citation:O.H.S.C - Their Care](https://www.sjlangwarrin.catholic.edu.au/about-5-4); [citation:ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/st-judes-ps-langwarrin-theircare). Specific operating hours were not found in the available sources, but TheirCare typically offers before-school, after-school, and holiday programs.

4) School culture: St Jude's offers several named extracurriculars including the St. Jude’s Flames Basketball Club (FDBA) for interschool competition [citation:Home St Jude's Langwarrin](https://www.sjlangwarrin.catholic.edu.au/). The school also runs Interschool Sport and Social Justice Days as part of its community calendar [citation:Community St Jude's Langwarrin](https://www.sjlangwarrin.catholic.edu.au/ourcommunity). Additionally, the school has a wellbeing dog program called "St Jude's HUB Maisie" featured in school videos [citation:Home St Jude's Langwarrin](https://www.sjlangwarrin.catholic.edu.au/).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries: Langwarrin has a suburban, family-friendly feel with large chain supermarkets including Coles (Southgateway) and Woolworths (Cranbourne-Frankston Road) serving everyday grocery needs [citation:Yellow Pages Supermarkets](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/langwarrin-vic-3910/supermarkets-grocery-stores). No specific specialty or ethnic grocery stores were identified in the provided sources for Langwarrin itself; nearby options may exist in Frankston.

6) Weeknight dining: While specific restaurants within Langwarrin are not named in the provided sources, popular family-friendly takeaway and dining options are available a short drive away in nearby Frankston. These include Frankston Thai Restaurant (Thai), Tower Hill Fish & Chips (fish and chips), and Betty's Burgers (burgers) [citation:Localsearch Langwarrin Takeaway](https://www.localsearch.com.au/find/takeaway-food/langwarrin-vic-3910).

7) Tuition and enrichment: Success Tutoring Langwarrin offers personalised English and Math tutoring mapped to the Australian Curriculum for primary and high school students [citation:Success Tutoring Langwarrin](https://www.successtutoring.com/tutoring-melbourne/langwarrin). Alchemy Tuition also provides in-home and online tutoring in Langwarrin, covering a range of subjects [citation:Alchemy Tuition Langwarrin](https://alchemytuition.com.au/melbourne-tutoring-langwarrin/).

8) Commute: Langwarrin does not have its own train station; the nearest station is Frankston on the Frankston line. The train journey from Flinders Street Station to Frankston Station takes approximately 1 hour 25 minutes [citation:Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Langwarrin). From Frankston Station, local bus services (e.g., routes 775 or 776) connect to Langwarrin, or driving takes about 39 minutes from the CBD [citation:Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Langwarrin).
