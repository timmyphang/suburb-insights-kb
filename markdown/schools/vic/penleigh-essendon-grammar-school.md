1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Keilor East is a middle‑income, established suburb with an older median age (median age 43) and a high share of married/couple households — broadly family‑oriented rather than a young singles area [citation:2021 Keilor East QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/210011534).
