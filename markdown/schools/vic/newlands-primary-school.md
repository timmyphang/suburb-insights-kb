1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Preston is a middle-income, family-friendly suburb. According to the 2021 Census, the median weekly household income is $1,844 and the median age is 37, with 8,624 families and an average of 1.7 children per family [citation:2021 Preston (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22121). The population is 33,790, and while specific occupation breakdowns are not provided, the income level suggests a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The available sources do not specify the most common overseas country of birth or its percentage for Preston. The 2021 Census indicates 95.6% of residents are non-Indigenous, but no breakdown of Australian-born versus overseas-born percentages is included in the provided data [citation:2021 Preston (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22121).

3) **Working-parent support**: Newlands Primary School offers an on-site Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) program, operated by the school council (community-based). According to ACECQA, before-school care runs from 7:00am to 9:00am and after-school care from 3:30pm to 6:00pm on school days, with a capacity of 195 children per session [citation:Newlands Out of School Hours Program | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/newlands-out-school-hours-program).

4) **School culture**: Newlands Primary School offers several named extracurricular activities, including the School Rock Band, School Choir, and Dance Clubs [citation:Learning | Newlands Primary School](https://newlandsps.vic.edu.au/learning/). The school also has an instrumental music room and a performance group. Additionally, Newlands is Australia’s only Spanish-bilingual primary school, with immersion in both English and Spanish.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Preston is known for its multicultural vibe and the bustling Preston Market, which features specialty grocers such as ARC Asian Grocer (offering a large variety of packaged goods and fresh produce) [citation:ARC Asian Grocer – Preston Market](https://www.prestonmarket.com.au/listing/arc-asian-grocer/). Other nearby options include Dong Thinh International Food Store on High Street and Shi Dai Asian Supermarket, both catering to Asian grocery needs [citation:Dong Thinh International Food Store - Preston Central](https://prestoncentral.org.au/traders/dong-thinh-international-food-store/).

6) **Weeknight dining**: Family-friendly takeaway options in Preston include Takeaway Pizza, a popular spot for pizza and cider with a kid-friendly atmosphere [citation:TAKEAWAY PIZZA, Preston - Restaurant Reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurant_Review-g850584-d14196172-Reviews-Takeaway_Pizza-Preston_Darebin_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). Another option is Gipis Pizza House, which offers Italian and pizza takeaway and delivery in Preston [citation:Takeaway & Delivery in Preston, VIC 3072 | TuckerFox AU](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/preston-vic-3072).

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Local tutoring centres include S & S Math Tutors at 9 Parker Street, Preston, specialising in mathematics [citation:S & S Math Tutors | Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/tuition-tutoring/preston-vic-3072). Kumon Northcote Education Centre at 542 High Street, Northcote (adjacent to Preston) offers after-school maths and reading programs [citation:Kumon Northcote Education Centre | Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com.au/find/tuition-tutoring/preston-vic-3072).

8) **Commute**: Preston Station is on the Mernda line, with trains running every 20 minutes. The journey to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD takes approximately 24 minutes [citation:Preston to Flinders Street Station - Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Preston-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station). An alternative is the line 251 bus from Mornane St/Victoria St to Little Collins St/Queen St, which takes about 57 minutes.
