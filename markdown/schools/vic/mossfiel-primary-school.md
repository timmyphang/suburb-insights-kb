1) **Suburb socio-economic profile**: Hoppers Crossing is a middle‑income, family‑oriented suburb. The 2021 Census reports a median weekly household income of $1,580 and a median age of 37 [citation:2021 Hoppers Crossing QuickStats]. Over three‑quarters of the population are families, and the 2023 estimated resident population is 37,097 with a density of 2,104 persons per km² [citation:Satterley suburb profile][citation:About the profile areas].

2) **Most common foreign country of birth**: The most common overseas country of birth is not explicitly listed in the provided census snippets. According to the 2016 Census, 33.6% of residents had both parents born in Australia and 50.1% had both parents born overseas, indicating a highly diverse population [citation:2016 Hoppers Crossing QuickStats].

3) **Working‑parent support**: Mossfiel Primary School has an Outside School Hours Care program operated by **Big Childcare** [citation:Mossfiel Primary School Wyndham]. Before‑school care runs from **6:30 am to 8:45 am** on weekdays [citation:Big Childcare Mossfiel PS OSHC]. After‑school care hours are not specified in the available sources, but the service is confirmed as catering for Mossfiel students [citation:Vacancies Big Childcare].

4) **School culture**: Mossfiel Primary School offers a **Learn to Swim Program** for Foundation to Year 2 students [citation:14 JULY 2022 newsletter]. No other specific extracurricular activities (e.g., sports teams, band, choir, kitchen garden) were named in the available school newsletters or website.

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries**: Hoppers Crossing is a safe, family‑friendly suburb with ample parks and comprehensive shopping [citation:Satterley suburb profile]. For specialty groceries, **Mohini and Mani Indian Supermarket** (302‑306 Old Geelong Road) and **Grocer‑Eez** (5/428 Old Geelong Road) offer Indian, Asian, and Fijian products [citation:Indian Grocery Shops][citation:Grocer‑Eez]. The nearby Pacific Werribee shopping centre provides additional major supermarkets and retail.

6) **Weeknight dining**: Family‑friendly takeaway and dining options in Hoppers Crossing include **Hanuman Thai Restaurant** (Asian/Thai), **Michael’s Fish Shop** (fish and chips), and **Biryani House** (authentic Indian cuisine) [citation:Tripadvisor Restaurants][citation:AGFG Restaurants]. All are rated well for casual meals.

7) **Tuition and enrichment**: Parents can access **Alchemy Tuition**, which provides in‑home and online tutoring in Hoppers Crossing, and **Kumon Hoppers Crossing Education Centre**, offering English and mathematics programmes for primary‑aged children [citation:Alchemy Tuition][citation:Kumon Hoppers Crossing].

8) **Commute**: Hoppers Crossing is served by the **Werribee train line**. The direct journey from **Hoppers Crossing Station** to **Flinders Street Station** takes approximately **35 minutes**, with trains departing every 20 minutes on weekdays [citation:Rome2Rio Flinders Street to Hoppers Crossing][citation:Metro Trains Hoppers Crossing Station].
