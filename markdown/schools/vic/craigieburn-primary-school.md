Here’s a concise, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Craigieburn Primary School (Craigieburn, VIC). Each point gives the key fact(s) with a primary-source citation.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile  
Craigieburn is a younger, family‑oriented, middle‑income growth suburb (median age 32; large shares of 0–14 year olds), with many married households (55.5%) and a high proportion of families compared with state averages [citation:ABS QuickStats Craigieburn 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20661).

2) Most common foreign country of birth  
India is the largest overseas birthplace in Craigieburn (the most common overseas country of birth; ~13–14% in 2021) — Australia remains the single largest birthplace overall [citation:ABS QuickStats Craigieburn 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20661).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)  
Craigieburn Primary has an on‑site Outside School Hours Care program run by TheirCare: before‑school 7:00–9:00am and after‑school 3:30–6:30pm, plus Pupil‑free day and holiday care (7:00–6:30pm) [citation:Craigieburn Primary School – Outside School Hours Program](https://www.craigieburnps.vic.edu.au/page/27/Outside-School-Hours-Program).

4) School culture / extracurriculars  
Sport: weekly specialist PE lessons and Grade 3–6 opportunities in district athletics, cross‑country and inter‑school sport (Grade 6 inter‑school competition). Music: on‑site instrumental lessons via Metro Music and a visiting “Music Bus” program for keyboard, guitar, drums and ukulele [citation:Craigieburn Primary – Physical Education](https://www.craigieburnps.vic.edu.au/page/36/Physical-Education) [citation:Metro Music at Craigieburn PS](https://www.craigieburnps.vic.edu.au/page/45/) [citation:The Music Bus announcement](https://www.craigieburnps.vic.edu.au/articles/774).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (2 sentences)  
Craigieburn is driven by suburban family life with a major shopping hub (Craigieburn Central) and local parks/playgrounds — handy for weekly shopping and family outings [citation:Craigieburn Central – Find a Store](https://craigieburncentral.com.au/find-stores/). Notable local/specialty grocers include Greenland Fresh Market (fresh produce & Halal), Tuan Phong Asian Grocery and Al Safa meat/grocer options in and around Craigieburn Central [citation:Greenland Fresh Market (Craigieburn Central)](https://craigieburncentral.com.au/retailer/greenland-fresh-market/).

6) Weeknight dining (casual, family‑friendly)  
Examples: VHM Bros (Vietnamese / pho & banh mi at Craigieburn Central), Desi Dhaba (Indian curries/tandoori at Craigieburn Central), and Craigieburn Pizza Bar for affordable pizza/takeaway — family‑friendly, takeaway‑oriented choices close to the school area [citation:VHM Bros (Craigieburn Central)](https://craigieburncentral.com.au/retailer/vhm-bros-vietnamese-restaurant/) [citation:Desi Dhaba Craigieburn](https://www.desidhaba.com.au/craigieburn) [citation:Craigieburn Pizza Bar listing](https://restaurantguru.com/Craigieburn-Pizza-Bar-Craigieburn).

7) Tuition and enrichment near the school  
Local options include Kumon (Craigieburn centre for maths/English) and Mathnasium Craigieburn for maths tutoring; private tutors and small local learning centres (e.g., Shehon Learning Centre) are also active in the suburb [citation:Kumon Craigieburn centre listing](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/KUMON-CRAIGIEBURN-VIC) [citation:Mathnasium Craigieburn](https://www.mathnasium.com/au/maths-centres/craigieburn).

8) Commute (train) to CBD  
Craigieburn has its own station on the Craigieburn line; typical train travel time to Melbourne CBD (Flinders Street) is roughly 40–45 minutes (regular services on the Craigieburn line) [citation:Metro Trains – Craigieburn line](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/lines/craigieburn/) [citation:Typical journey estimate Craigieburn → Flinders Street (example timetable/summary)](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Craigieburn-Station/Flinders-Street-Station).
