1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Rowville is a middle-to-upper-income, family-oriented suburb. The 2021 Census shows a median weekly household income of $2,205, above the Victorian average, and a median age of 41, indicating an older family demographic [citation:2021 Rowville, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22207). The suburb has a high proportion of families, with 9,779 families and an average of 1.8 children per family [citation:2021 Rowville, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22207). The workforce is primarily white-collar, with a significant number of professionals and managers, though specific occupation data is not provided in the available snippets.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Rowville is predominantly Australian-born. The 2021 Census indicates that 63.6% of residents were born in Australia [citation:2021 Rowville, Census All persons QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL22207). The most common overseas country of birth is England, but its exact percentage share is not specified in the available search snippets.

3) **Working-parent support:** Heany Park Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) through OSHClub [citation:Heany Park Primary School | OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/heany-park-primary-school/). The 'Rise then Shine' program runs from 7:00 AM until the school bell, and the 'Stay and Play' program runs from bell time until 6:30 PM [citation:Heany Park Primary School | OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/heany-park-primary-school/). Holiday programs, called 'Holiday HQ', are also available from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM [citation:Heany Park Primary School | OSHClub](https://www.oshclub.com.au/find-us/heany-park-primary-school/).

4) **School culture:** Heany Park Primary School offers a diverse array of named extracurricular activities. Students can join the school's **Choir**, which meets at lunchtime, and participate in **inter-school sport** competitions including Swimming, Athletics, Cross Country, Basketball, AFL, and Cricket [citation:Learning | Heany Park Primary School](https://heanyparkps.vic.edu.au/curriculum/)[citation:Heany Park Primary School Newsletter August 25, 2022 Edition 14](https://heanyparkps.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Edition-14-25th-August.docx.pdf). The school also runs a **Lap Club** for running endurance training [citation:HEADING 8 4 March 2021 1 SPECIALIST LEARNING IN THE CLASSROOM](https://heanyparkps.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Specialist-Learning-in-the-Classroom-March.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Rowville has a suburban, family-friendly feel with ample local amenities. For grocery shopping, there are several specialty options including **Spice World** (Asian grocery) at 2/1221 Stud Road, and **Rowville Supermarket** (a Ritchies IGA) at Shop 18, 1100 Wellington Road [citation:Asian grocery store in Rowville](https://www.australianplanet.com/rowville/asian+grocery+store)[citation:Rowville Supermarket - Ritchies](https://rowville.shop.ritchies.com.au/). 6) **Weeknight dining:** Several casual, family-friendly dining options are available in Rowville. **Eating House** offers modern Australian cuisine with a kids-eat-free promotion on certain nights [citation:Eating House - Restaurant Cafe Bar Functions Catering Pizza Rowville](https://eatinghouse.com.au/). **Big Al's Pizza - Rowville** is a popular choice for pizza takeaway [citation:Takeaway in Rowville - Order Food Delivery Near Me | Uber Eats](https://www.ubereats.com/au/neighborhood/rowville-melbourne-vic). **Dosa Hut** provides Indian food for takeaway or delivery [citation:Indian Restaurant Near Rowville | Indian Food Takeaway Menu Rowville - Dosa Hut](https://www.dosahut.net.au/indian-restaurant-rowville/).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** There are specific tutoring centres available for local families. **DW Tuition** provides one-on-one tutoring in Maths, English, and VCE subjects at its Rowville centre [citation:Group & Private Maths Tutoring Rowville | DW Tuition](https://www.dwtuition.com.au/). **Success Tutoring** also offers one-on-one diagnostic assessments and tutoring in English and Math for Rowville students [citation:Tutoring Rowville | Primary & High School Tutors](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-rowville).

8) **Commute:** Rowville does not have a train station. Residents typically use bus services to connect to nearby train stations. For example, taking a bus from Rowville to **Glen Waverley Station** (on the Glen Waverley line) takes about 10-15 minutes, and then the train from Glen Waverley to **Flinders Street** station takes approximately 27 minutes [citation:Rowville to Flinders Street Station - 5 ways to travel via train](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Rowville/Flinders-Street-Station). The total journey time from Rowville to the Melbourne CBD is generally around 35-45 minutes by public transport or about 24 minutes by car [citation:Flinders Street Station to Rowville - 6 ways to travel via train](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Rowville).
