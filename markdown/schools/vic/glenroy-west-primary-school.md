1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Glenroy is a diverse, middle-income suburb with a median weekly household income of $1,655 and a median age of 34, according to the 2021 Census [citation:2021 Glenroy (Vic.) QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21047). The suburb has a mix of families and singles, with a population of 23,792 and an average of 0.7 children per household [citation:2021 Glenroy (Vic.) QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21047). While specific occupational breakdown is not provided in available sources, the suburb’s housing profile (median home price $861,000) and rental yields suggest a working-to-middle-class demographic [citation:SmartPropertyInvestment](https://www.smartpropertyinvestment.com.au/data/vic/3046/glenroy).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common foreign country of birth is not explicitly listed in the provided sources, but 8.9% of Glenroy residents speak Arabic at home—the largest non-English language—followed by Italian (7.7%) and Turkish (4.1%) [citation:SmartPropertyInvestment](https://www.smartpropertyinvestment.com.au/data/vic/3046/glenroy). This suggests a significant Lebanese-born community, though the official Australian-born percentage is not stated in available data.

3) **Working-parent support:** Glenroy West Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by VillageOSHC [citation:Outside School Hours Care Program](https://www.glenroywestps.vic.edu.au/page/171/Outside-School-Hours-Care-Program). Before school care runs from 7:00 am to 8:50 am (fee $20.85 per session), and after school care runs from 3:15 pm to 6:00 pm (fee $23.50 per session) [citation:Outside School Hours Care Program](https://www.glenroywestps.vic.edu.au/page/171/Outside-School-Hours-Care-Program). The service also offers holiday care from 8:00 am to 6:00 pm [citation:ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/glenroy-west-primary-school-villageoshc).

4) **School culture:** Glenroy West Primary School offers several named extracurricular programs. These include **Breakfast Club** (a morning meal program), **Homework Club** (after-school academic support), and **Genesis Music School** (instrumental and group music lessons on site) [citation:Glenroy West Primary School homepage](https://www.glenroywestps.vic.edu.au/). The school also runs lunchtime **Coding Club** and **Library Club** for interested students [citation:GWPS Information Booklet](https://www.glenroywestps.vic.edu.au/uploaded_files/media/2021_gwps_handbook.pdf).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Glenroy offers a range of family amenities including parks and a local shopping strip along Pascoe Vale Road. Specialty grocers in the suburb include **Touch Of Asia Pacific Glenroy** (Indian and Asian groceries at 826a Pascoe Vale Rd) [citation:Touch Of Asia Pacific Glenroy](https://touch-of-asia-pacific-glenroy.wheree.com/), **Glenroy Asian Food Centre** (at 796 Pascoe Vale Rd) [citation:Glenroy Asian Food Centre](https://www.yelp.com/biz/glenroy-asian-food-centre-glenroy), and **Indreni Glenroy Supermarket** (at 796K Pascoe Vale Rd) [citation:Indreni Glenroy Supermarket](https://www.indreniglenroysupermarket.com.au/). Online Indian grocery delivery is also available from **Tales of India** [citation:Tales of India](https://talesofindia.com.au/pages/indian-grocery-store-glenroy-3046/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** Family-friendly takeaway and dining options in Glenroy include **Indian Essence** (Indian cuisine, available for takeaway and delivery) [citation:TuckerFox](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/glenroy-vic-3046), **Nonna Rosa Family Restaurant** (Italian) [citation:TuckerFox](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/glenroy-vic-3046), and **Gabbies Pizza-Pasta-Kitchen** (pizza and pasta) [citation:TuckerFox](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/glenroy-vic-3046). **The Chicken Machine** is another popular choice for chicken and chips [citation:TuckerFox](https://www.tuckerfox.com.au/restaurant-takeaway-delivery/glenroy-vic-3046).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Tutoring services available in or near Glenroy include **Cluey Learning**, which offers online private tutoring for primary and high school students in English, Maths, and Chemistry with qualified teachers [citation:Cluey Learning](https://clueylearning.com.au/lts/tutoring-glenroy/). **Tutero** provides personalised one-on-one online tutoring aligned to the Victorian curriculum for Glenroy students [citation:Tutero](https://www.tutero.com/au/online-tutoring/locations/glenroy-vic). Both services match students with expert tutors for ongoing support.

8) **Commute:** Glenroy is served by **Glenroy Station** on the **Craigieburn line**. The direct train to Flinders Street Station takes approximately 31 minutes, with trains running every 15–20 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Glenroy-Station/Flinders-Street-Station). The station is a short walk from Glenroy West Primary School on York Street.
