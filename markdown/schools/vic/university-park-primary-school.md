The suburb has a population of 38,042 and a high proportion of families, with an average of 2.8 people per household. The workforce is diverse, with a mix of blue-collar and white-collar occupations, though specific occupational breakdowns are not provided in the available sources.

3) **Working-parent support:** University Park Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by TheirCare, operating from the school’s full-size gym [citation:U Park PS TheirCare](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/y8mdq/u-park-ps-theircare-st-albans-3021). The specific hours of operation are not listed in the available sources, but typical OSHC services run before and after school (e.g., 7:00–9:00 AM and 3:00–6:00 PM) and during school holidays. The service is listed on Toddle as “U Park PS TheirCare” at the school’s Lister Street address [citation:OSHC in St Albans, VIC 3021](https://toddle.com.au/oshc/vic/st-albans).

4) **School culture:** University Park Primary School runs several named extracurricular programs, including the **Kitchen Garden Program** (using a fully equipped kitchen-garden space) and the **Buddy Program**, which pairs older students with younger ones to foster peer connections and reduce bullying [citation:Student Engagement Programs](https://universityparkps.vic.edu.au/wellbeing/student-engagement-programs/) [citation:Buddy Program](https://universityparkps.vic.edu.au/wellbeing/buddy-program/). The school also offers targeted **Social Skills Groups** that may include Bluearth sessions focused on physical challenges and resilience [citation:Student Engagement Programs](https://universityparkps.vic.edu.au/wellbeing/student-engagement-programs/).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** St Albans has a lively, multicultural town centre with a strong Asian and South Asian influence. Local specialty grocers include **Pahal Grocers** (282 Main Road East) offering Indian, Sri Lankan, and Pakistani products, **KP Asian Grocery** (12 Alfrieda Street) stocking Korean, Japanese, and Vietnamese items, and **KFL Supermarket** (330 Main Road East) providing a wide range of Asian groceries [citation:St Albans Town Centre](https://www.stalbanstowncentre.com.au/news-stories/pahal-grocers) [citation:KP Asian Grocery](https://www.stalbanstowncentre.com.au/directory/kp-asian-grocery) [citation:St Albans KFL](https://www.kflsupermarkets.com.au/st-albans-vic). The area is family-friendly with parks, playgrounds, and community services.

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family-friendly dining, **Phi Phi Vietnamese & Chinese Restaurant** (on Main Road East) serves Vietnamese and Chinese dishes and is popular for takeaway [citation:THE 10 BEST Restaurants in St Albans](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g4358126-St_Albans_Brimbank_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html). **Shahi Darbaar** (10A East Esplanade) offers Indian cuisine with a 5-star rating and is available for takeaway [citation:St Albans 3021 Food Delivery](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/st-albans-3021). **Pizza Del Mondo** is another local favourite for pizza, often enjoyed by families [citation:THE 10 BEST Restaurants in St Albans](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g4358126-St_Albans_Brimbank_Greater_Melbourne_Victoria.html).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Innate Tutoring** (online and in-person) provides VCE and pre-VCE tutoring in English, Maths, Biology, and Chemistry for St Albans students [citation:Tutoring St Albans](https://www.innatetutoring.com.au/st-albans-tutoring/). **Success Tutoring St Albans** offers English and Maths tutoring in a dedicated learning centre, with positive reviews from local families [citation:St Albans – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-st-albans).

8) **Commute:** St Albans is served by **St Albans Station** on the **Sunbury line** (Metro Trains Melbourne). The direct train to Flinders Street Station takes approximately **31–36 minutes** on average, with services running every 15 minutes [citation:St Albans Station to Flinders Street Station](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/St-Albans-Station/Flinders-Street-Station). The fastest journey can be as short as 27 minutes.
