1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Warrandyte is an affluent suburb with a strong family focus. The median weekly household income is $2,742, well above the national median, and the median age is 45 years, reflecting an older, established population [citation:2021 Warrandyte, Census All persons QuickStats]. The area has a high proportion of families, with an average of 1.9 children per family and 1.1 children per household, and a very high average of 2.5 motor vehicles per dwelling, indicating a car-dependent, higher-income demographic [citation:2021 Warrandyte, Census All persons QuickStats].

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** Warrandyte is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 80.1% of residents born in Australia [citation:2021 Warrandyte, Census All persons QuickStats]. Among overseas-born residents, the most common country of birth is England, at 4.2% of the population [citation:2021 Warrandyte, Census All persons QuickStats].

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) is available at Warrandyte Primary School, provided by Our Patch OSHC [citation:Vacancies, Enrol now at Our Patch OSHC at Warrandyte Primary School](https://www.careforkids.com.au/child-care/p53dm/our-patch-oshc-at-warrandyte-primary-school-3113). A key information sheet from the school states the program is operated by CommunityOSH, though specific hours for the Warrandyte site were not detailed in the available context [citation:WARRANDYTE PRIMARY SCHOOL OSHC T H E D A I L Y P E E P](https://warrandyteps.vic.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/OSHC-Key-Information-Sheet.pdf).

4) **School culture:** Warrandyte Primary School offers several named extracurricular activities. The school has a **Kitchen Garden program** and an active **fundraising committee** that organizes events throughout the year [citation:2025 Annual Report to the School Community](https://www.vrqa.vic.gov.au/StateRegister/Provider.aspx/GetFile?Type=AnnualReport&EntityID=1&SchoolNumber=12&requestFileType=Pdf). The annual report also notes parent volunteering in classrooms, supporting a strong community culture [citation:2025 Annual Report to the School Community].

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Warrandyte has a village-like, family-friendly atmosphere centered around the Yarra River, with local amenities including the Warrandyte Riverside Market. For grocery shopping, there is an **IGA** supermarket in the town centre, and families can access specialty foods via services like **Tales of India**, which delivers Indian groceries to the area [citation:Indian Grocery Store Warrandyte | Free Delivery | Tales Of India](https://talesofindia.com.au/pages/indian-grocery-store-warrandyte-3113). Additional Asian groceries can be found at Wanlong Oriental, though its primary locations are in Wollongong and Norwa, not Warrandyte itself [citation:Wanlong Oriental – Get your fresh food](https://wanlongoriental.com.au/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family dining, **Indian Affair Restaurant** at 35 Drysdale Road offers Indian cuisine with takeaway and delivery options [citation:Warrandyte 3113 Food Delivery and Takeaway VIC](https://www.ozfoodhunter.com.au/takeaway/warrandyte-3113). Another option is **Vanilla Orchid** on Yarra Street, which serves authentic Thai cuisine with French-style desserts and is noted for its child-friendly atmosphere [citation:Restaurants in Warrandyte,VIC With Child Friendly | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/warrandyte/childfriendly-category). **Warrandyte Café** on Yarra Road is also a popular, family-friendly dining spot overlooking the Yarra River [citation:Restaurants in Warrandyte,VIC With Child Friendly | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/melbourne/warrandyte/childfriendly-category).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** **Success Tutoring** offers in-centre and online tutoring services for primary and high school students in Warrandyte, focusing on English, maths, and other core subjects [citation:Warrandyte – Success Tutoring](https://www.successtutoring.com.au/tutoring-Warrandyte). For online tutoring specifically, **Cloud Tuition** provides one-on-one, virtual tutoring for Years 2–12 in English, maths, and science, with lessons available from 9 AM to 9 PM [citation:Tutoring Warrandyte | Online Tutors for Years 2-12 | Cloud Tuition](https://www.cloudtuition.com.au/locations/tutoring-vic/warrandyte).

8) **Commute:** Warrandyte does not have a train station. The nearest train station is **Ringwood Station** on the **Belgrave and Lilydale lines**, approximately a 10–15 minute drive from Warrandyte. From Ringwood Station, the train journey to Flinders Street Station in the Melbourne CBD takes approximately **35–40 minutes**. Alternatively, residents can use bus route **579** (Warrandyte to The Pines Shopping Centre) or route **364** (Warrandyte to Ringwood Station) to connect to the train network.
