1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Norlane is a working-class suburb and, according to the 2021 SEIFA index, it was ranked as the most disadvantaged small area in Victoria [citation: The Geelong suburb of Norlane is Victoria's most disadvantaged area, census data shows - ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2023-05-04/norlane-most-disadvantaged-area-in-victoria-abs-census-data/102291206). The median weekly household income is $909, significantly lower than the state and national averages, and the median age is 37 [citation: 2021 Norlane, Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21960). The area is predominantly a family-oriented suburb with a mix of couples with children and single-parent families, though specific white-collar vs. blue-collar breakdowns were not found in the provided sources.

The specific operating hours for the before and after school programs are not detailed in the provided sources, but the service operates Monday to Friday and eligible families may receive the Child Care Subsidy [citation: Before & After School Care - St Thomas Aquinas](https://stanorlane.catholic.edu.au/before-and-after-school-care/). Contact the school for exact hours.

4) **School culture:** St Thomas Aquinas School offers a **"Positive Behaviours for Learning (PBL)"** program where students earn tokens for rewards such as "baking in the school kitchen" [citation: Positive Behaviours for Learning (PBL) - St Thomas Aquinas](https://stanorlane.catholic.edu.au/positive-behaviours-for-learning/). The school also runs a **F.I.R.E Carrier Program** (the specifics of which are not detailed in the provided text) [citation: Our Community - St Thomas Aquinas](https://stanorlane.catholic.edu.au/our-community/). Additionally, a **community garden** is available for volunteers, as mentioned in an ABC News article [citation: The Geelong suburb of Norlane is Victoria's most disadvantaged area, census data shows - ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2023-05-04/norlane-most-disadvantaged-area-in-victoria-abs-census-data/102291206).
