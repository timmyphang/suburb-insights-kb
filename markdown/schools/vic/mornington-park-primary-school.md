1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Mornington has a median age of 50, notably older than the Victorian median of 38, and a median weekly household income of $1,451 [citation:2021 Mornington (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21763). Families make up a significant portion, with 7,088 families recorded and an average of 0.6 children per household [citation:2021 Mornington (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21763). The suburb has a higher proportion of female residents (53.6%) and is generally considered a middle-to-upper-middle-income area with a mix of retirees, professionals, and families.

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The suburb is overwhelmingly Australian-born, with 95.7% of residents identifying as non-Indigenous [citation:2021 Mornington (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21763). Specific percentages for the most common foreign country of birth were not found in available sources, but the data indicates a very low proportion of overseas-born residents.

3) **Working-parent support:** Mornington Park Primary School offers Outside School Hours Care (OSHC) provided by Little Wonder Kids [citation:Before & After School Care](https://www.morningtonparkps.vic.edu.au/page/227/Before-&-After-School-Care). The before-school care operates from 6:45 AM to 8:45 AM, and after-school care runs from 3:30 PM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday during school terms [citation:Mornington Primary School OSHC | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/mornington-primary-school-oshc). Vacation care is not listed as available at this service [citation:Mornington Primary School OSHC | ACECQA](https://www.acecqa.gov.au/resources/national-registers/services/mornington-primary-school-oshc).

4) **School culture:** Mornington Park Primary School has a music program that includes Recorder Karate and participation in the Junior and Whole School choirs, which perform at the Mornington Peninsula Harmonies Unite Choral Festival [citation:Music](https://www.morningtonparkps.vic.edu.au/page/242/Music). The school runs a Breakfast Club for students and a strong instrumental program offering lessons in piano, violin, cello, flute, clarinet, saxophone, trumpet, singing, guitar, and drums [citation:Music](https://www.morningtonparkps.vic.edu.au/page/242/Music) [citation:Mornington Park Primary School - Mornington Peninsula Shire](https://www.mornpen.vic.gov.au/Services/Communities/Community-Directory/Mornington-Park-Primary-School). The school also offers a Steiner stream with specialist subjects such as eurythmy and craft (knitting, sewing, crochet) [citation:The Steiner Curriculum](https://www.morningtonparkps.vic.edu.au/page/237/The-Steiner-Curriculum).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Mornington has a relaxed, family-friendly coastal vibe with a mix of boutique shops, cafes, and beaches. For groceries, residents can shop at Henrys Mercato Mornington, a local green grocer, and find Asian specialty items at Great Asia Food Store or Mr MAMA International Grocery in the broader Mornington Peninsula area [citation:Henrys Mercato Mornington | Mornington VIC | Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/henrysmercatomornington) [citation:THE BEST 10 INTERNATIONAL GROCERY in MORNINGTON PENINSULA SHIRE VICTORIA, AUSTRALIA - Updated 2026 - Hours - Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/search?cflt=intlgrocery&find_loc=Mornington+Peninsula+Shire+Victoria). Mainstream supermarkets such as Woolworths and Coles are also available in the suburb.

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual, family-friendly dining and takeaway, D.O.C. Pizza & Mozzarella Bar offers authentic Italian pizza and takeaway options [citation:THE 10 BEST Takeaway Restaurants in Mornington Peninsula - Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurants-g1055530-zfp10601-Mornington_Peninsula_Victoria.html). Tadka Club Mornington serves Indian cuisine with a coastal twist, suitable for dine-in or takeaway [citation:Tadka Club Mornington | Best Indian Restaurant](https://mornington.thetadkaclub.com.au/). Dava Thai Mornington provides authentic Thai takeaway and dining [citation:Restaurants in Mornington,VIC With Takeaway | AGFG](https://www.agfg.com.au/restaurants/mornington-peninsula/mornington/takeaway-category).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** Kip McGrath Mornington offers English and maths tutoring for primary and secondary students, with a free assessment and tailored programs, located at Suite 3, 19 Bruce Street, Mornington [citation:KMEC Mornington Tuition Centre | Kip McGrath](https://www.kipmcgrath.com.au/mornington). Primary Skills Tutoring provides specialist support for students with learning difficulties on the Mornington Peninsula [citation:Primary Skills Tutoring | Specialist support for students with learning difficulties](https://www.primaryskillstutoring.com.au/).

8) **Commute:** Mornington does not have a train station. Commuters must take a bus to Frankston Station, then catch the Frankston line train to Flinders Street Station in Melbourne CBD. The total travel time is approximately 1 hour 30 minutes to 2 hours, depending on connections, with the journey involving a bus and then train [citation:Mornington to Flinders Street Station - 4 ways to travel via ...](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Mornington-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
