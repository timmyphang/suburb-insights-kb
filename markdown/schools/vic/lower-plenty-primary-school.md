3) **Working-parent support:** Lower Plenty Primary School operates its own self-managed Out of School Hours Care (OSHC) program, called the Lower Plenty Primary OSHC program [citation:Before & After Care](https://lowerplentyps.vic.edu.au/before-and-after-care/). After-school care runs from 2:45pm to 6:00pm, costing $26.00 per session, and families are eligible for the Child Care Subsidy (CCS) [citation:Before & After Care](https://lowerplentyps.vic.edu.au/before-and-after-care/). The program is child-centred, with activities based on art, cooking, sport, and science [citation:Before & After Care](https://lowerplentyps.vic.edu.au/before-and-after-care/).

4) **School culture:** Lower Plenty Primary School offers an extensive music and performing arts program, with weekly specialist lessons and a strong focus on performance [citation:Curriculum](https://lowerplentyps.vic.edu.au/curriculum/).
