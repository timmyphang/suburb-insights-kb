1) **Suburb socio-economic profile:** Keilor Downs is a middle-income, family-oriented suburb with a mix of white-collar and blue-collar workers. The median weekly household income is $1,553 [citation:ABS QuickStats 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/213011332). The median age is 42, and the suburb has a high proportion of families, with an average of 0.9 children per household [citation:ABS QuickStats 2021](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/213011332).

2) **Most common foreign country of birth:** The most common overseas country of birth for residents is not specified in the available sources, but 21.4% of people had both parents born in Australia and 63.2% had both parents born overseas [citation:2016 Keilor Downs QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC21307). A large proportion of the suburb is born overseas, with Vietnam, India, and Malta being common birthplaces, but exact percentages for Keilor Downs are not available in the provided text.

3) **Working-parent support:** Outside School Hours Care at Monmia Primary School is provided by Camp Australia and is located at Copernicus Way, Keilor Downs [citation:Camp Australia - Monmia Primary School OSHC](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3038/keilor-downs/camp-australia-monmia-primary-school-oshc). The service offers after-school care from 3:30 pm to 6:30 pm, Monday to Friday, and before-school care hours are not specified in the provided source [citation:Camp Australia - Monmia Primary School OSHC](https://kindicare.com/childcare/centres/Australia/VIC/3038/keilor-downs/camp-australia-monmia-primary-school-oshc).

4) **School culture:** Monmia Primary School offers a PMI Music Program, which provides instrumental lessons in keyboard, guitar, and concert band through the Primary Music Institute [citation:PMI Music Program](https://www.monmia.vic.edu.au/page/21/PMI-Music-Program). The school also has a Student Leadership Program with House Leaders (Green, Blue, Red, and Gold) who organize sporting activities like House Athletics [citation:Student Leadership](https://www.monmia.vic.edu.au/page/11/Student-Leadership). Additionally, students participate in Camp Programs, including overnight sleepovers at the school with activities, dinner, and a guest performance [citation:Camp Programs](https://www.monmia.vic.edu.au/page/20/Camp-Programs).

5) **Suburb vibe and groceries:** Keilor Downs has a family-friendly vibe centered around the Keilor Central Shopping Centre, which includes major supermarkets like Coles and ALDI [citation:Retail Directory - Keilor Central](https://keilorcentral.com.au/retail-directory/). For specialty groceries, there is Pacific Asian, an Asian grocery store stocking pantry essentials and snacks, located in the Keilor Central centre [citation:Pacific Asian - Keilor Central](https://keilorcentral.com.au/stores/pacific-asian/). There is also Keilor Downs Asian Food, offering takeaway Asian dishes like dim sims and lemon chicken, located at G-047 Keilor Central [citation:Keilor Downs Asian Food](https://keilorcentral.com.au/stores/keilor-downs-asian-food/).

6) **Weeknight dining:** For casual family-friendly dining, try Keilor Downs Asian Food at Keilor Central, offering Asian takeaway such as dim sims and Mongolian beef [citation:Keilor Downs Asian Food](https://keilorcentral.com.au/stores/keilor-downs-asian-food/). Another option is Sharma Sweets & Indian Cuisine at 350 Taylors Road, Delahey (near Keilor Downs), serving traditional Indian dishes and sweets [citation:Top 118 Restaurants in Keilor Downs](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/find/restaurants/keilor-downs/). For pizza and pasta, Hometown Pizza & Pasta in nearby Taylors Hill offers gourmet pizzas and creamy mushroom pasta [citation:Top 118 Restaurants in Keilor Downs](https://www.localbusinessguide.com.au/find/restaurants/keilor-downs/).

7) **Tuition and enrichment:** A specific enrichment centre is the Kumon Keilor Downs Education Centre, which offers English and mathematics tutoring programs to help master fundamental skills [citation:Kumon Keilor Downs Education Centre](https://www.kumonsearch.com.au/Centre/Kumon-Keilor-Downs-VIC). Another option is Innate Tutoring, which provides VCE tutoring in subjects like English, Biology, and Chemistry, with a focus on personalised learning for secondary students [citation:Innate Tutoring Keilor Downs](https://www.innatetutoring.com.au/keilor-downs-tutoring/).

8) **Commute:** The closest train station is Keilor Plains Station on the Sunbury line, served by Metro Trains Melbourne [citation:Rome2Rio Flinders Street to Keilor Downs](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Keilor-Downs). The train journey from Flinders Street Station to Keilor Plains Station takes approximately 34-38 minutes, with trains departing every 15-20 minutes [citation:Rome2Rio Flinders Street to Keilor Downs](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Flinders-Street-Station/Keilor-Downs).
