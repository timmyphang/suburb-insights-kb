Here’s a short, family‑facing eight‑point briefing for Mont Albert Primary School (Mont Albert, VIC). I used official ABS and direct school/provider pages where possible; each item is a short fact/snippet with a source.

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
- Mont Albert is an established, relatively affluent, family‑oriented suburb — median age 42 and median weekly household income reported as high relative to state averages [citation:2021 Mont Albert QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21732).  
- The area ranks toward the advantaged end on SEIFA indexes (higher relative socio‑economic advantage) [citation:SEIFA 2021](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/people-and-communities/socio-economic-indexes-areas-seifa-australia/latest-release).

2) Most common foreign country of birth
- The ABS QuickStats for Mont Albert show that the most common overseas birthplace is China (i.e. Chinese‑born residents are the largest non‑Australian birthplace group for the suburb) [citation:2021 Mont Albert QuickStats](https://abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL21732).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
- Mont Albert Primary has on‑site OSHC (Before School Care, After School Care and Vacation Care) run by TeamKids (hours listed: before school from 7:00 am, after school until 6:00 pm; vacation care offered) [citation:TeamKids Mont Albert OSHC](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/mont-albert-primary-oshc-5/).

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars
- Performing arts program (singing, classroom instruments such as recorder and tuned percussion) [citation:Mont Albert Primary Performing Arts](https://www.maps.vic.edu.au/performing-arts.html).  
- Specialist physical education program and school sports (specialist PE teacher, team games, sports clinics) [citation:Mont Albert Primary Physical Education](https://www.maps.vic.edu.au/physical-education.html).  
- Environmental/after‑school clubs (Environmental Action Group, school gardens) noted on the school extracurricular/profile pages [citation:Mont Albert Primary School Profile](https://www.maps.vic.edu.au/school-profile.html).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
- Mont Albert is a leafy, village‑style suburb with a compact Hamilton Street shopping strip and parks — popular with families for local cafes, small retailers and primary‑school catchment living [citation:FoodWorks Mont Albert](https://montalbert.foodworks.com.au/).  
- Local grocery options include Mont Albert FoodWorks on Hamilton Street and small local grocers / delivery services (Mont Albert Fresh) serving the area [citation:FoodWorks Mont Albert](https://montalbert.foodworks.com.au/) [citation:Mont Albert Fresh](https://montalbertfresh.com.au/).

6) Weeknight dining — 2–3 casual family options
- Churchill Cafe (Hamilton St) — family‑friendly café with takeaway and casual meals [citation:Churchill Cafe](https://www.churchillcafe.com.au/).  
- Mont Albert Fish & Chips (Hamilton St) — classic takeaway option for families [citation:Mont Albert Fish & Chips](https://montalbertfishandchips.business.site/).  
- Cantina Centrale — local Italian/pizza option on Hamilton St for evening dining/takeaway [citation:Cantina Centrale](http://cantinacentrale.com/).

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 centres)
- Success Tutoring lists services for Mont Albert (local primary/secondary tutoring and exam/homework support) [citation:Success Tutoring Mont Albert](https://www.successtutoring.com/tutoring-mont-albert).  
- There are also several private tutors and platforms (Superprof / local one‑to‑one tutors) advertising in Mont Albert for in‑home or online tutoring [citation:Superprof Mont Albert](https://www.superprof.com.au/lessons/all-tutors/mont-albert/).

8) Commute — train travel time to Flinders Street
- Note: Mont Albert railway station was closed as part of level‑crossing removal works (station closure recorded in 2023) — therefore Mont Albert no longer has the old station in service; I’m substituting the nearest active local station (Surrey Hills or Box Hill) for travel estimates and noting the substitution. - From Surrey Hills (nearest active station to the west) to Flinders Street by Belgrave/Lilydale line trains is typically about 20–25 minutes; from Box Hill (to the east) it’s roughly 25–30 minutes depending on stopping pattern and time of day [citation:Rome2rio Mont Albert to Flinders St (journey estimates) and Metro trains timetables](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Mont-Albert-Station/Flinders-Street-Station) [citation:Metro Trains timetables](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/timetables/).
