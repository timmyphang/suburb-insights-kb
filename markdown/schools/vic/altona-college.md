Quick note: Altona College is a combined government P–12 college (not only a primary) — I’ll treat the primary years within that P–12 campus below [citation:Altona College | vic.gov.au](https://www.vic.gov.au/altona-college-number-8857).

1) Suburb socio‑economic profile (2–3 sentences)
Altona is broadly middle‑to‑upper‑middle in socio‑economic terms with an older median age (median age 42). It’s a family‑oriented bayside suburb with a mix of white‑collar and trades/blue‑collar households rather than a high‑affluence pocket [citation:2021 Altona (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20035) [citation:Home | Hobsons Bay City | Community profile - id](https://profile.id.com.au/hobsons-bay).

2) Most common overseas country of birth
About two‑thirds of residents were born in Australia (≈67%); the most common overseas birthplace is England (~4–5%) in local 2021 QuickStats for Altona [citation:2021 Altona (Vic.), Census All persons QuickStats | Australian Bureau of Statistics](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL20035) [citation:Altona, VIC — Suburb Demographics & Profile | Behind the Fence](https://www.behindthefence.com.au/suburbs/vic/altona).

3) Working‑parent support (OSHC)
Altona College runs on‑site Outside School Hours Care (Before, After and Vacation Care) run by TeamKids (service transition noted from Extend → TeamKids; TeamKids venue page linked from the school) [citation:Outside School Hours Care - altonacollege.vic.edu.au](https://www.altonacollege.vic.edu.au/page/176/Outside-School-Hours-Care) [citation:Altona College - TeamKids](https://www.teamkids.com.au/venues/altona-college-oshc/).

4) School culture — 2–3 extracurriculars
- Instrumental / music program and scheduled instrumental tuition (school newsletters and music timetables show an active instrumental music program).  
- School music/VCE music pathways (music performance subjects / Certificate II in Music are offered at senior levels).  
- Competitive school sport (the annual report highlights strong sport engagement, notably volleyball).  
Sources: school pages, newsletter and annual report [citation:Altona College Newsletter - Instrumental timetable (Issue 4)](https://newsletters.naavi.com/i/v5zo6WN/issue-4/page/14) [citation:Certificate II in Music (Performance) - Altona College Pathways Guide](https://sites.google.com/education.vic.gov.au/altonacollegesenior/explore-subjects/vocational-educational-training-options/certificate-ii-in-music-performance) [citation:Annual Report to the School Community 2023 - Altona College](https://www.altonacollege.vic.edu.au/uploaded_files/media/arc_2023_8857.pdf).

5) Suburb vibe and groceries (≈2 sentences)
Altona is a bayside suburb with foreshore reserves, parks and a relaxed family‑friendly vibe plus local shopping strips (Altona Central / Civic Parade). Main supermarket options include Coles at Altona Central and local IGAs; there are also ethnic grocers nearby (e.g., Indian/Spice grocers serving the western suburbs) [citation:Home | Hobsons Bay City | Community profile - id](https://profile.id.com.au/hobsons-bay) [citation:Coles - Altona Central Square, VIC](https://www.openinghoursau.com/coles-altona-central-square) [citation:India Spice Corner Grocery Shops Altona North Melbourne VIC](https://singh.com.au/india-spice-corner/).

7) Tuition and enrichment (1–2 centres)
Local tutoring options include a newly listed Success Tutoring centre in Altona and specialist maths tutors (Perdocere / private tutoring services) operating in the Altona area — these offer K–12 tuition and curriculum‑mapped programs [citation:Altona Tutoring – Success Tutoring](https://successtutoring.com.au/altona-tutoring) [citation:Altona Math Tutoring • Perdocere](https://www.perdocere.com/altona-math-tutoring/).

8) Commute — Altona station → Flinders Street (estimate)
Altona is served by Altona station on the Werribee line (Altona Loop). Typical train travel to Flinders Street takes about 30–40 minutes (roughly ~35 minutes is a common estimate depending on time of day and whether the service runs via the Altona Loop) [citation:Altona station (Werribee line) — Melbourne Metro](https://melbournemetro.org/werribee-line/altona/) [citation:Altona to Flinders Street Station - Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Altona-VIC-Australia/Flinders-Street-Station).
