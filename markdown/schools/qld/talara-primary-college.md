# Welcome to Currimundi

If you’re picturing a middle‑income coastal suburb where weekends are as likely to be spent at the beach as catching up with neighbours, you’ve landed in the right place. Currimundi has a comfortable family and retiree mix, a median weekly household income around $1,467 (2021 Census) and a higher share of married and family households than a young‑singles precinct. It’s the kind of place where the sea breeze shapes your routine and local rhythms—markets on the weekend, a friendly pub for quick dinners, and schools that feel like the social hub of the community.

# Inside the Classroom at Talara Primary College

Talara Primary College serves children from Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland). The school has a lively mix of curricular and extra‑curricular life: an instrumental music program that links into regional extension workshops and ensembles, strong sport pathways including a swimming program and athletics carnivals, and Performing Arts “Excellence Teams” in dance, drama and choir for kids who want to commit and go further. It’s the kind of school where music and sport aren’t just extras but pathways for kids to find their people and shine.

# Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

Talara has on‑site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare, plus vacation care) run by Helping Hands. That means you can arrange before‑school care, after‑school pick‑up and supervised holiday programs in the same place your child goes to class—handy if you’re juggling work hours and school runs. Check their enrolment and fee details directly with Helping Hands so you know how bookings and holiday programs work for your family.

# The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Groceries and feeding a family around here are a straightforward, friendly affair. Currimundi Marketplace has a Woolworths for weekly shopping, there are nearby IGAs and the Currimundi Markets for fresh community produce and vendors when you want something more artisanal. If you need specialty or ethnic ingredients, nearby Sunshine Coast Asian supermarkets are a short drive away. For nights when cooking feels like too much, local staples include Italia Pizza & Pasta for takeaway, Pacific Haven Bakery for quick meals and breakfast pastries, and the Currimundi Hotel for casual pub meals and alfresco seating.

# Tuition and the Daily Commute

If your child needs a bit of academic support, local tutoring options include Kip McGrath in Caloundra and regional centres that offer maths and English tuition and enrichment programs. For parents who commute, Currimundi doesn’t have a heavy‑rail Queensland Rail station; public transport relies on regional buses that connect to Sunshine Coast train stations or coach services. Expect door‑to‑door public‑transport journeys to Brisbane central to be roughly two to three hours depending on connections, while driving off‑peak is typically around one to one and a quarter hours. It’s worth trialling the commute at the times you’d use it so you know whether the timing will work for your household.

# Your Next Steps

Pick a morning and tour Talara to see classrooms, ask about the instrumental music sign‑up, the Performing Arts “Excellence Teams” and representative sport pathways. Call Helping Hands to confirm outside school hours care availability, fees and holiday‑care bookings and complete enrolment if you’ll need before/after or vacation care. If after‑school academic support is likely, reach out to local tutors and take a drive through the shopping and dining spots—Currimundi Markets, Woolworths, Sunlit Asian Supermarket and the bakery or pub—to see how daily life feels for your family.
