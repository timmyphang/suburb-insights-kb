# Welcome to Wellington Point

If you picture bayside living with a quietly confident, family-first vibe, Wellington Point fits the bill. It’s a middle-to-upper‑middle‑income suburb where people tend to stay put: home ownership and mortgages are strong, the median age sits around 43 and households report a healthy weekly income that supports an easy family lifestyle. About three quarters of residents were born in Australia, and the most common overseas birthplace is England, which means newcomers often find a familiar English-speaking community waiting to help with that first grocery run or school pickup. The bay, local parks and a settled neighbourhood culture make this a comforting first base for families moving to South East Queensland.

# Inside the Classroom at Wellington Point State School

Wellington Point State School teaches children from Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland). The school has a bright, active feel — you’ll hear strings warming up for a rehearsal or the choir getting ready for a concert, and the school’s sporting program has picking-up trophies and gala days as part of its proud tradition. These arts and sports pathways give kids a range of ways to belong, whether your child is learning an instrument, singing in a group, or representing the school at sport. That combination of music, choir and award-winning sport makes it an easy place for children to find their people and settle in.

# Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families juggling work and school runs, Wellington Point State School offers on-site Outside School Hours Care (that’s on-site before and after school childcare, and holiday programs) run by Camp Australia. The service is listed on the national childcare register and runs the kind of before-school, after-school and school‑holiday sessions that help working parents keep routines intact without extra driving. Because the program is right at the school, your after‑3 pm handover is simple: your child can move straight from class into supervised activities, homework help or play, and you get to focus on fitting the day’s logistics around your work schedule.

# The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Weeknight dinner in Wellington Point leans into fresh, easy and unfussy. For pantry top-ups you’ve got local options like IGA and FoodWorks, plus a nearby farm market where you can pick up the kind of seasonal fruit and vegetables that make for simple, wholesome family meals. When peeling out of the car and not wanting to cook, the local takeaway scene covers all the basics: classic fish and chips and seafood, family-friendly pizza from Manny’s Italian, and Indian and Thai takeaways that are perfect for a low-stress weeknight. It’s the kind of place where you mix a fresh-market salad with a takeaway pizza and everyone walks away happy.

# Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you want extra academic support, Wellington Point has local tutoring and enrichment options that work around family life, from in-home and online providers to tutor‑matching services tailored to your child’s year level. For parents commuting into the city, Wellington Point station sits on the Cleveland train line and a trip to Brisbane’s central station typically takes about an hour, so plan for roughly 55–60 minutes door to platform when scheduling work days. That rail link gives you a reliable balance: the calm of bayside living with a straightforward train into the CBD when you need it.
