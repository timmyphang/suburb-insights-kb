# Welcome to Highland Park, QLD (Gold Coast hinterland)

Highland Park sits quietly in the Gold Coast hinterland with a slightly older, family-first feel—think median age 42 and neighbours who mostly settled here for the leafy streets and space for kids to play. Household incomes run a little under the Queensland median, and you’ll find a practical mix of technicians and tradespeople alongside professionals. For newcomers, the fact that most households are Australian‑born is a small but steady comfort: it often means everyday rhythms, services and community events are straightforward when you’re still learning the local ropes.

# Inside the Classroom at William Duncan State School

William Duncan State School is that kind of local primary where sport, music and hands‑on experiences shape the week. The school runs inter‑school sport, house carnivals and competitive teams, so your child will be welcomed into a calendar of active events. There’s an instrumental music program with band and string options, and regular camps and excursions that push learning beyond the classroom walls. Check the school website for exact grade ranges and enrolment details, but expect a warm, community‑minded primary that balances play, performance and outdoor learning.

# Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families juggling work, William Duncan has an on‑site Outside School Hours Care program run by Camp Australia, covering before‑school care, after‑school care and school‑holiday programs. That on‑site setup makes drop-off and pick‑up routines much easier, whether you’re heading to an early shift or picking up late from the office. If you need steady childcare that bridges the school day, this is a convenient option to explore early—spaces can fill up in term time and over school holidays.

# The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Highland Park Shopping Plaza is the local one‑stop for groceries, anchored by Spano’s IGA and a few specialty fresh‑food retailers like an organic butchery, which makes fuelling a family dinner simple. For evenings when you’d rather not cook, the choices around here lean comforting and familiar: Aromas of India for curries and quick dine‑in options, Highland Court if you’re craving Chinese takeaway, and Pagliacci for pizza nights. These spots keep weeknights easy when homework and after‑school activities take precedence.

# Tuition and the Daily Commute

If your child could use extra academic support, there are local tutoring options within easy reach—ATK Tutoring and Reach Your Potential run sessions in the Nerang area and serve Highland Park families. Commuting to Brisbane or the wider region is doable: Nerang train station is about five to six kilometres away, and the Gold Coast Line services typically take around one hour and fifteen minutes to reach Brisbane’s central station, though you’ll want to consult the journey planner for exact timetables that line up with your schedule. Try a trial run of the trip at the time you’d commute to get a real feel for door‑to‑platform timings.

# Your Next Steps

First, head to the William Duncan State School website to confirm grade levels and enrolment requirements, and if possible book a tour so you can see the classes and meet staff in person. Second, if before‑school, after‑school or holiday care matters to your routine, contact Camp Australia about the on‑site program to check availability and enrolment details. Third, test your likely commute to Nerang station, familiarise yourself with Spano’s IGA and the shopping plaza, and reach out to local tutoring providers if you want to line up extra learning support before term starts.
