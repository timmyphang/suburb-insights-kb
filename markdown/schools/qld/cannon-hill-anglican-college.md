# Welcome to Cannon Hill

Cannon Hill feels like the kind of suburb that grows up around families: the median age sits at 34, lots of households include kids, and the weekly household income tends to reflect white‑collar, professional work. Most people here were born in Australia — roughly seven in ten — with New Zealand and England the next most common birthplaces, so you’ll meet plenty of familiar accents alongside fresh ones as neighbours settle in. It’s a place where the rhythm of school terms, community sport and local cafés sets the pace of life.

# Inside the Classroom at Cannon Hill Anglican College

Cannon Hill Anglican College, in its primary years, runs from Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland). The school puts a noticeable emphasis on creative and co‑curricular life: performance music is prominent, with tuition options, ensembles, concerts and even recording facilities, while drama and stage productions get used to shaping confident kids who love an audience. Sport is also a steady part of the week, with primary students taking part in the junior interschool competition and college carnivals such as swimming, cross‑country and athletics. If your child leans toward music, theatre or sport, the school facilities and programming give genuine room for growth and regular public performance.

# Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families balancing work and school, the College offers on‑site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) run by School Plus. That service covers before‑school, after‑school and vacation care and takes the practical stress out of juggling pick‑ups and meetings. It’s a sensible setup for parents who need reliable wrap‑around care on school days and during the holidays; the enrolment process is handled through the school’s OSHC resources or via the School‑Plus listing so you can confirm spots ahead of term starts.

# The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Cannon Hill makes weeknight meals straightforward. You’ll find the usual big supermarkets nearby along with local IGA and Friendly Grocer options for quick top‑ups, and an Indian grocery and takeaway delicatessen on Wynnum Road if you want to add regional ingredients or spice mixes to a midweek curry. When time is short, local takeaways do a steady trade: Chinese takeaway, a seafood and fish‑and‑chip shop and a family‑style Indian takeaway cover most cravings and make dinner logistics easier after sports practice or music lessons.

# Tuition and the Daily Commute

If extra academic support is part of your plan, there are established options in Cannon Hill: a local one‑to‑one tutoring centre and a Kumon maths and English program both serve families in the area, so you can arrange targeted help without long drives. For parents commuting to central Brisbane, the train from Cannon Hill station to Brisbane Central typically runs about 18 to 25 minutes depending on the service, which makes the commute surprisingly manageable for professionals who want shorter travel times and more family time in the evenings. Factor the school drop‑off and pick‑up windows into your daily routine and you’ll find a good balance between work, tutoring runs and after‑school activities.

# Your Next Steps

First, secure your child’s wrap‑around care early: enrol in the College’s on‑site Outside School Hours Care via the school resources or the School‑Plus listing so you’re not left juggling last‑minute arrangements. Second, book a visit to the College, join an open day or take a tour to see the music, arts and sports facilities in person and match them to what lights your child up. Third, check train timetables for the commute window you’d use and line up any after‑school academic support — local tutors and the Kumon centre make it easy to slot in one‑to‑one learning without long transfers.
