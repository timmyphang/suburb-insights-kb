# Welcome to Eagleby

Eagleby has that friendly, down-to-earth feel families want when they’re planting roots. It’s a community where neighborhood parks and the family-focused vibe are part of daily life, and you’ll notice a strong practical streak in the local economy: many residents work in trades, services and hands-on roles. Household incomes trend lower than some neighbouring suburbs, which shows up in affordable shopping options and a visible share of state housing, but for newcomers—especially families from overseas—there’s comfort in knowing Eagleby already welcomes a sizeable community born abroad, with New Zealand being the most common overseas birthplace. That makes settling in and finding friends who get the transition to a new country a little easier.

# Inside the Classroom at Eagleby South State School

Eagleby South State School is your neighborhood primary school where classroom life blends the core curriculum with lively extras. The school runs a standard primary program and supports music learners with instrumental strings, sends teams to local sports and competitions like Rugby League, Touch Football and soccer, and gets creative in Tournament of the Minds and school musical productions. If your kid likes performing or playing in a group, there are obvious outlets for both. The school’s Annual Report and curriculum overview give a good picture of what is offered each year, so you can check whether the specific instruments, teams and event schedules match your child’s interests.

# Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

Working parents will appreciate that on-site before- and after-school care is provided by PCYC’s “Fun Squad.” This is an approved outside school hours care program—on-site before and after school childcare—so you can drop kids with confidence around standard start and finish times. It’s worth confirming enrolment capacity and booking details early, because these programs can fill up quickly.

# The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

When it comes to groceries and quick meals, Eagleby keeps things straightforward. There’s a local Fresh & Save for weekly shopping and an Eagleby Asian Grocery that stocks Southeast Asian and Filipino staples if you’re cooking regional favourites. For those nights when you don’t want to cook, the takeaway scene is comforting and familiar: fish-and-chips and fried chicken shops, kebab and pita places, and Mediterranean-style fast food that kids usually rate highly. It’s a practical food mix that suits busy families and multicultural palates.

# Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you’re considering extra academic support, the Logan area has tutoring options like Alchemy Tuition and Kip McGrath that work with primary-aged students. These providers can be a sensible complement to school-based programs when you want targeted help with literacy or numeracy. Commuting into the Brisbane central business district involves a short drive or bus to the nearest train hub at Beenleigh, since Eagleby doesn’t have its own station. From Beenleigh, trains to Brisbane typically take around 50 to 60 minutes depending on the service, so plan a commute test from your specific address to see how that fits with your family’s rhythm.

# Your Next Steps

First, contact PCYC’s Fun Squad to confirm OSHC (outside school hours care) hours, enrolment capacity and booking process so before- and after-school care is settled. Second, read the school’s Whole School Curriculum Overview and recent Annual Report to verify the music, sports and other extracurriculars you care about and to check event timing. Third, do a commute trial from your address to Beenleigh station and check train timetables for the 50–60 minute trip to Brisbane to see how school drop-offs and your workday will flow.
