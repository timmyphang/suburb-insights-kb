Welcome to Upper Coomera

Upper Coomera feels like the kind of place that greets you with a playground and a coffee shop within walking distance. With a median age of 33, it’s a family‑oriented, middle‑income suburb where owner‑occupied family homes and a healthy share of children set the rhythm of the streets. The local workforce blends professionals and tradespeople, so weekend sport, after‑school activities and DIY projects all fit naturally into the neighbourhood tempo. If you’re picturing tree‑lined streets, active families and a sense that your kids will quickly find playmates, you’re on the right track.

Inside the Classroom at Assisi Catholic College

Assisi Catholic College in Upper Coomera teaches children from Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland). The school has a lively performing‑arts program with college choirs, instrumental ensembles including strings and guitar, private tuition across instruments and voice, and an audition‑based band excellence pathway for kids who want to dive deeper. Sport is structured and broad: primary interschool competition and participation in the Catholic colleges sport cluster mean options like netball, soccer, rugby league, futsal and basketball are built into the calendar, along with carnival days that parents learn to favour in their family diaries.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For working parents, Assisi runs on‑site registered Outside School Hours Care (on‑site before and after school childcare) that’s set up to match a typical workday. Before School Care starts at 7:00 and runs until school begins; After School Care picks up at 3:30 and runs until 6:30. Vacation Care stretches the day further, roughly 7:00 to 6:30 in the evening, giving families reliable coverage through school holidays. The service is listed on the national enrolment portal for childcare, which makes booking and admin straightforward.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Grocery runs are easy to manage with mainstream supermarkets at the local centres—Woolworths and Coles at nearby Coomera and Upper Coomera—and plenty of specialty Asian markets, like the Hanaro Mart at Westfield Coomera, if you cook with specific ethnic ingredients. When life is busy, family‑friendly takeaways make weeknights simple: Ocean Bite Fish n Chips scrubs dinner into a quick family pack, Sushi Break Express covers speedy sushi orders, and Gill’s Indian Cuisine gives you curry and vegetarian options that travel well. It’s the kind of local food scene that keeps weeknights relaxed without much planning.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

For extra academic help or enrichment, you’ll find after‑school options nearby such as Kumon for maths and English and private tutors through the Coomera Learning Centre for one‑to‑one or small group support. Commuting into Brisbane requires a short drive or bus to Coomera Station—Upper Coomera doesn’t have its own heavy‑rail stop—and the train from Coomera to Brisbane Central typically takes about 60 to 70 minutes depending on the service. Families who commute tend to build the station timetable into morning and evening routines, so planning around peak services makes life much smoother.

Your Next Steps

First, book a school tour at Assisi Catholic College and confirm Prep to Year 6 enrolment steps so you know deadlines and requirements. Second, secure your childcare by enrolling early in the school’s Outside School Hours Care for before, after and vacation care through the college’s OSHC page or the national childcare portal. Third, map your commute via Coomera Station and sign kids up early for music auditions, interschool sport and any extra tutoring so you have routines in place from week one.
