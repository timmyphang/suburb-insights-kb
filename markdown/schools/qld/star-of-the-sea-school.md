Welcome to Merrimac, QLD

Merrimac feels like the kind of suburb that greets you with a backyard BBQ and a friendly wave. The median age sits around 38 and the median household income reflects a comfortable, busy family rhythm — a place where professionals and managers live alongside a healthy share of tradies and technical workers. It’s very family-oriented, with lots of households raising kids, and while most people here were born in Australia, you’ll notice a steady stream of New Zealand and English accents — and a reassuringly diverse mix of other overseas-born families. If you’re moving from overseas, that blend of local and international backgrounds makes settling in easier: there are familiar faces and newcomers finding their feet at the same time.

Inside the Classroom at Star of the Sea School

Star of the Sea in Merrimac is that small-school vibe with big opportunities. While grade ranges weren’t specified in the briefing, what stands out is a commitment to music and performance for the upper primary years: instrumental lessons and beginner and intermediate bands for Years 3 to Year 6, plus choir and handbells. Kids get the chance to play in small groups, have one-on-one tuition and perform in eisteddfods and community events, which is lovely if you want your child to build confidence on stage. Sport and other co‑curricular activities are offered term-by-term through external providers, so there’s always something on the calendar to keep active kids engaged. The school’s culture leans into participation and community, and that shows in regular performances and sporting rotations.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

If you rely on before- or after-school care, Star of the Sea has on-site Outside School Hours Care (that’s on-site before and after school childcare, plus vacation care) operated by Catholic Early EdCare. Having care right at the school removes a lot of the afternoon logistics stress — you can drop, run to work or pick up a grocery order, and know your child is in supervised care without a long commute. Do check hours and current enrolment capacity before making plans, though; services can fill up quickly and it’s best to confirm the details directly with the provider.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Merrimac’s food scene is warm and practical for family life. For quick weeknight meals you’ll find Merrimac Chinese Takeaway on Jura Parade and the ever-reliable Merrimac House of Pizza for family-friendly pizza nights. If you want to cook up something different, there are specialty options nearby: Churras brings South American and Latin groceries and a butcher’s touch, SPAR Express covers convenience essentials, and broader Asian grocery options servicing the Gold Coast — such as Asian Emporium — make it easy to source ingredients for more adventurous dinners. Local cafes and the broader Gold Coast dining scene are close enough for a weekend treat.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

For extra academic support, local options include Kumon in Robina and private tutors like ATK Tutoring, also operating out of Robina. They’re a sensible choice if you want targeted reading or maths practice outside school. On the commute front, Merrimac doesn’t yet have an operational passenger rail station — a new Merrimac station is under construction as part of the Cross River Rail upgrades. In the meantime, Robina Station is the nearest existing rail link; trains from Robina to Brisbane Central typically take around one hour and fifteen minutes to one hour and thirty minutes depending on the service. If you’re planning work commutes, use Robina for now and keep an eye on Cross River Rail and Translink updates so you can switch to the local station once it opens.

Your Next Steps

1) Ring Catholic Early EdCare or check the StartingBlocks listing to confirm the outside school hours care hours, enrolment process and whether there’s space before you rely on it.  
2) Book a school tour or an enrolment meeting and ask about the Years 3 to Year 6 instrumental music program, choir and the term-by-term sport offerings — if your child likes music or sport, come prepared to ask about how they progress from beginner to band.  
3) For commute planning, use Robina Station for now and sign up for Cross River Rail and Translink updates so you’ll know when Merrimac’s new station opens; meanwhile, explore Kumon or ATK Robina if you’re considering extra tuition.
