# Welcome to Crestmead

If you’re picturing a young, busy suburb where families are the visible heartbeat, Crestmead fits that picture. The median age here is 29 and households average about three people, so you’ll see plenty of prams, school bags and after-school chatter at the local caf?. It’s largely a working- and middle-income neighbourhood: mixed blue- and white-collar jobs are common, incomes sit around a median weekly household figure of about $1,470, and renting is frequent—so it’s a practical, down-to-earth place for families starting out or settling into their next chapter. The community energy is refreshingly family-focused, which makes it an easy fit if you’re prioritising a primary‑school catchment and everyday convenience over glossy suburban quiet.

# Inside the Classroom at St Francis College

St Francis College accepts students in the typical Queensland primary intake: Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland). The school has a clear emphasis on sporting development through its Sports Academy, which offers structured pathways in futsal, football (soccer), netball and representative teams—so if your child loves kicking a ball or wants to chase representative opportunities, there’s a defined route for that interest. Music and the arts are represented, too: on-site private small-group instrumental lessons span piano, guitar, ukulele, strings, woodwind and brass, and those options are woven into a broader co-curricular program. Together, the Sports Academy and the instrumental offerings give kids both performance and creative outlets within the school day and beyond.

# Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families balancing paid work and school pickups, St Francis has on-site Outside School Hours Care run by Catholic Early EdCare. That service covers before-school, after-school and vacation care and is listed on the national register for early‑childhood services, so you’re not left improvising care arrangements on short notice. It’s the kind of setup that takes some of the evening logistics off your plate: whether you need a spot for early starts or for the days when after-work runs late, having registered, on-site care makes the school day feel a lot more manageable.

# The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

When dinner planning needs to be efficient, Crestmead’s local shops and takeaways keep things pleasantly stress-free. There’s an IGA and a Friendly Grocer for fresh staples and last‑minute school-lunch needs, and a Yi Asian Grocery if you prefer to cook with a broader range of Asian ingredients. For nights when you’d rather not cook, Asian Kitchen at Crestmead Central does family-friendly Vietnamese and Chinese takeaway with family packs and delivery options, and Stellarossa provides a reliable local café and drive‑thru for coffees, snack meals and quick pick-me-ups. Those nearby options mean fewer detours on the school run and more predictable weekday evenings.

# Tuition and the Daily Commute

If supplementary academic support matters, there are local maths tutors serving the area, including Cloud Tuition and Ezy Math Tutoring, so after‑school catch-up or extension sessions are doable without long travel. Commuting to the Brisbane CBD is realistic by bus—TransLink Route 551 is the main public-transport option from Crestmead, and one-way journeys typically land in the 60–75 minute range depending on time of day; a sample trip is about 68 minutes. There isn’t a train station in Crestmead itself, so if you rely on public transport, factor in that bus link and the variability of peak-hour traffic when planning workdays.

# Your Next Steps

1) Ring the school to confirm Prep and primary start dates and to book an open day or school tour so you can see classrooms and meet teachers in person.  
2) Check availability and fees for the on-site Outside School Hours Care with Catholic Early EdCare and the national service register, and ask about enrolment steps so your after‑school care is organised before term starts.  
3) Ask the school about Sports Academy entry rules, representative pathway expectations and any extra costs, and clarify how the on-site instrumental lessons are scheduled and billed so you can decide which activities fit your family routine.
