# Welcome to Clontarf (Moreton Bay / Redcliffe Peninsula)

Clontarf has the easy-going, water-side feel you picture when you think of the Redcliffe Peninsula: a median age around 46 and a community that leans toward middle-income families and older households. Most people here were born in Australia, with England the most common overseas birthplace, so if your family is arriving from abroad you’ll find plenty of neighbours who understand starting again in a new country. The pace is calmer than the inner city, and household incomes sit around the local median, which keeps the suburb practical and family-friendly without feeling crowded.

# Inside the Classroom at Grace Lutheran Primary School

Grace Lutheran Primary School in Clontarf teaches Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland), and it’s the kind of small-school environment where kids quickly become known by name. The school puts a lot of love and resources into performing arts: music, choir, dance and optional instrumental lessons are a noticeable part of the week, so children who light up on stage or with an instrument have real scope to grow. Sport and physical education are active, with inter-school opportunities for kids who enjoy friendly competition. Specialist STEM classes and Japanese lessons broaden the week too, so whether your child is into coding experiments or language learning, there’s structured enrichment on offer.

# Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

Grace runs on-site Outside School Hours Care — called Grace Outside School Hours Care — which means before-school, after-school and vacation care are available on the school grounds. The program publishes its hours and enrolment details, so parents who work standard office hours or shift patterns can plan reliably. Having an on-site program means pickups are simpler, kids can stay in a familiar school environment for homework or play, and working parents don’t have to juggle multiple pickup points across town.

# The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Groceries are easy to organise around family life here. There’s a local IGA for everyday shopping and fresh staples, and Lek’s Asian Market if you’re after specialty ingredients for curries, dumplings or other international favourites. When it’s been one of those days and you don’t want to cook, the waterfront café Bramble Bay General is perfect for a relaxed family dinner with a view, and Zorbas Pizza & Pasta covers the quick pizza-and-pasta nights that please hungry kids. The peninsula’s cafes and takeaways give you options for both casual family dining and takeaway convenience.

# Tuition and the Daily Commute

If your kids need extra study support, local tutoring options such as Fruition Tuition and Alchemy Tuition are popular with Clontarf families for targeted help after school. For parents commuting to Brisbane’s central business district, remember Clontarf doesn’t have a city train station. Most public-transport trips use local buses that connect to the peninsula rail line at Kippa‑Ring, and typical door-to-destination travel times to Brisbane Central are roughly 75 to 90 minutes depending on connections and time of day. It’s worth running a journey plan for your exact address to see the best daily options and timetables.

# Your Next Steps

First, check Grace’s Outside School Hours Care hours and confirm enrolment requirements so you’ve secured before/after-school coverage. Next, contact the school for the current fees, enrolment numbers or a prospectus to understand capacity and curriculum details. Finally, run a Translink journey plan from your Clontarf address to map commute times and, if you think your child would benefit, look into local tutors like Fruition or Alchemy for after-school academic support.
