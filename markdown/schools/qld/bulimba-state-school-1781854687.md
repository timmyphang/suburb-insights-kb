Inside the Classroom at Bulimba State School

Bulimba State School brings a lively arts and music vibe to the school day. Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

If your household needs care that stretches beyond the school bell, Bulimba State School runs on‑site Outside School Hours Care that covers before‑school, after‑school and vacation care, with typical published hours starting as early as about 6:30am for before‑school care and running until around 6:00pm after school. The service is operated as a business unit of the school’s Parents and Citizens association and is listed with an ‘Exceeding’ rating. If you’re juggling meetings, trains, or the odd late return, this program provides a reliable on‑site option to keep your routine smooth. The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners
