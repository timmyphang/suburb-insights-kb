Welcome to Pelican Waters

Pelican Waters feels like the kind of place you slow down to notice. With a median age of 55, the suburb leans towards mature and established households—retirees, empty‑nesters and long‑standing professionals who’ve chosen calm coastal living over the city rush. That doesn’t mean you’ll be on your own as a newcomer. A noticeable share of residents were born overseas, especially in England, which can make the social transition easier for families coming from abroad: familiar accents, shared customs and a welcoming community rhythm help new arrivals find common ground quickly. Income levels sit around the middle range for Queensland, and daily life here orbits local conveniences and outdoor time rather than late‑night city thrills.

Inside the Classroom at Caloundra City Private School — Pelican Waters campus

Caloundra City Private School’s Pelican Waters campus is focused on Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland), and it’s the kind of school where the day is full of options beyond reading and arithmetic. Kids can join choir or sign up for private instrument lessons—guitar, piano and drums are on offer—and sports include volleyball and tennis. Lunchtimes are often busy with STEM, Chess and Lego clubs, and the school’s outdoor programs take advantage of the Sunshine Coast lifestyle with beach and outdoor education activities. The vibe is active and varied: if your child is the kind who likes music one term and science the next, the campus is set up to let them try a lot of different things without having to travel across town.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families juggling work and school runs, the campus runs an on‑site licensed Outside School Hours Care called Pelican Waters Kid’s Club. After school care operates Monday to Friday from roughly 3:00pm until 6:00pm, and holiday or vacation care is available from about 8:00am to 6:00pm. The service is approved for the Australian government’s Child Care Subsidy, uses Xplor for bookings and is registered with the national regulatory register, so paperwork and subsidy eligibility are handled through familiar channels. That on-site convenience changes the daily logistics: you can plan work commitments around a predictable pickup window and leave extracurricular pickups for later in the week.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Groceries and dinner take a neighborhood-friendly tone here. Pelican Waters Shopping Village is the everyday hub, with both Coles and Woolworths for staples and one‑stop weekly shops. If your family likes to cook dishes from different cuisines, specialty and ethnic groceries are easy to reach in nearby Caloundra—places that stock the kinds of ingredients that make weeknight cooking feel like home. When you don’t feel like peeling vegetables, the suburb’s casual dining options are reliably local: the Pelican Waters Tavern offers family-friendly bistro meals and takeaway, The Cove Cafe is a great spot for a quick bite or coffee, and the Pelican Bowls Club provides Chinese takeaway and dining when you want something comforting and familiar.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you’re thinking about extra academic support, Pelican Waters has tutoring options close by, from math tutors who list Pelican Waters in their service area to private tutors available through national platforms for K–6 support and enrichment. For parents balancing work in Brisbane, plan for public‑transport door‑to‑door times that are commonly around two hours. There isn’t a local train station, so most families take a local bus to reach the Sunshine Coast rail line (Landsborough is a common rail connection), then the train into Brisbane’s central stations—train time from Landsborough to Brisbane is roughly one hour and fifteen to twenty‑five minutes, and once you add the local bus leg the full journey commonly stretches to around one hour fifty minutes to two hours. It’s worth testing exact connections on the regional transit site before locking in daily commutes.

Your Next Steps

First, check the school’s Outside School Hours Care details, confirm Child Care Subsidy eligibility if you need it, and ask about the enrolment and booking process through Xplor so there are no surprises on day one. Second, take a look at the school’s co‑curriculum pages and pick a couple of activities your child might love—music lessons, choir or a sports program can make the first term feel fun and fast‑moving. Third, if you’ll be commuting to Brisbane regularly, factor in roughly two hours each way on public transport and test the specific bus‑to‑train connections on TransLink so your workday planning is realistic.
