Welcome to Ascot

Ascot sits comfortably close to the city, a suburb that feels both established and quietly lively. Household incomes here trend higher than the Brisbane local government area average, and the neighbourhood attracts a healthy mix of professionals, managers, long-standing family homes and singles or young adults who enjoy being near Racecourse Road’s cafés and shops. You’ll still find an English influence in the birthplace data for residents, but most people are Australian-born, so families moving in should feel the balance of familiar neighbourhood rhythms and a gentle urban energy. It’s a suburb where school runs, weekend markets and a stroll down to a local deli all fit into the same easy morning.

Inside the Classroom at St Margaret’s Anglican Girls School — Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland)

St Margaret’s Prep to Year 6 campus in Ascot brings a classic girls’ school approach with a lively co‑curricular heartbeat. If your child is into music, you’ll find an extensive offering: instrumental lessons, ensembles, bands and choirs all run through the primary years. The performing arts program is similarly broad, with drama and debating opportunities and even stage and rock band experiences for younger students. Sport is part of the daily fabric too, so whether your child’s energy is musical, theatrical or athletic, there’s scope to plug in and grow. Before you enrol, it’s worth reviewing the school’s overview and the music handbook to match their programs to what excites your child.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families juggling work and school, the campus offers practical care options. Before school care runs from 7:00 to 8:15 am and after school care from 2:45 to 6:00 pm, both delivered through organized on-site programs. Vacation care is available on the primary campus too, provided by an external specialist provider, TeamKids, which requires family registration. Some families may be eligible for the Australian government’s Child Care Subsidy depending on circumstances, so it’s wise to check eligibility and session availability as part of your planning. These arrangements make the school day manageable for parents with full-time jobs or shifting schedules.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Racecourse Road is the spine of Ascot’s casual dining scene. On a busy weeknight you might pick up a roast-style takeaway or a quick pizza, or head to one of the neighbourhood cafés for something a touch more interesting—Petrichor & Co brings Latin and Colombian‑influenced cafés vibes while Verandah on Racecourse blends Australian and Vietnamese flavours for family dinners that feel both comforting and a little adventurous. For grocery runs, there’s an IGA Marketplace on Alexandra Road and a Woolworths in Racecourse Village Shopping Centre, with specialty delis and fresh produce suppliers scattered along Racecourse Road for when you want a handcrafted meal or artisanal ingredients.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you think your child might benefit from extra learning support, Ascot is served by local tutors who do in-home sessions and subject-specific work, particularly in maths. That local tutoring network makes after-school or weekend enrichment straightforward without long drives. For parents commuting to Brisbane’s central business district, Ascot’s train station is handy: the trip to Brisbane Central is typically around 20 minutes on frequent services—always worth checking current timetables for peak-hour differences. Driving times will vary with traffic, but whether you’re catching a train or coordinating school pick-up and work, the suburb’s proximity to the city keeps daily logistics doable.

Your Next Steps

First, take a careful look at St Margaret’s “Our School” page and the music handbook so you can confirm the co‑curricular options that matter most to your child. Second, if you’ll need before, after or vacation care, get in touch with TeamKids to register and check session availability and any childcare subsidy details. Third, trial a weekday commute and a quick swing down Racecourse Road to the IGA or Woolworths so you can see how shopping, dinner options and drop‑off/pick‑up fit your family’s rhythm.
