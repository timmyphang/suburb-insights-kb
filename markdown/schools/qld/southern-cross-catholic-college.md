Welcome to Scarborough

If your picture of coastal Queensland includes salty air, a slower pace and a streetscape dotted with weathered fibro and low‑set bungalows, Scarborough will probably feel familiar the moment you arrive. It’s an older, seaside suburb where the median age sits in the early fifties and homeownership and retirement are more common than the school‑run chaos you find in younger suburbs. Household incomes are moderate and the local workforce is a mixed bag of professionals, trades and service workers — so think middle‑income, down‑to‑earth neighbours rather than glossy new developments. That mix means families who move here get the benefits of a settled community, plenty of local character and close access to the water, while still tapping services that support kids and school life.

Inside the Classroom at Southern Cross Catholic College

Southern Cross Catholic College’s Scarborough campus runs Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland), and the school leans into a broad co‑curricular program that will appeal to a variety of interests. If your child loves music, the instrumental program offers one‑on‑one tuition and ensemble opportunities across several instruments, so there’s a pathway from first lessons through to performance. Sport is a full offering too — swimming, athletics, soccer, netball, rugby league and futsal mean kids can try lots of things and find a team. The performing arts are active as well, with drama, dance and an annual cultural showcase giving children chances to build confidence on stage. Overall it feels like a school that values participation and giving kids multiple ways to belong, whether that’s in a practice room, on the field or under the stage lights.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families juggling work and school pickups, the Scarborough campus provides Outside School Hours Care — that’s on‑site before‑and‑after‑school childcare — run in partnership with Catholic Early EdCare. If your calendar also needs school‑holiday coverage, vacation care is available through the college’s Kippa Ring campus. Those arrangements make life much easier for households where both parents work or for single parents needing reliable, supervised care into the late afternoon. It’s worth checking the specific hours and fees with Catholic Early EdCare when you enrol, because exact start/finish times and holiday care locations can change from year to year.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Scarborough’s food scene is refreshingly straightforward. For weekly grocery runs there’s a Friendly Grocer right in town for staples, and a short trip into the wider Redcliffe area gets you specialty and Asian ingredients at places like Lek’s Asian Market. When you want takeaways the neighbourhood leans family‑friendly: Bay Boats Corner Shop does reliable fish‑and‑chips, burgers and ice‑cream; The Boat Shed keeps seafood simple and kid‑approved; and Charm Thai offers quick Thai classics that travel well. Those local options make weeknight meals easy after a busy afternoon of sport, music lessons or homework.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you anticipate extra academic support, you won’t be stuck — Kip McGrath Scarborough runs English and maths tuition for primary and secondary students, and there are local private tutor listings for targeted help in areas like mathematics. Commuting into Brisbane’s CBD is doable but takes planning: Scarborough doesn’t have a train station. Most families use a local bus to Kippa‑Ring (commonly 15–25 minutes depending on route and timing) and then the Redcliffe Peninsula Line train into Brisbane Central, which is roughly a 54–55 minute train ride. In practice expect about 70–85 minutes door‑to‑door on public transport depending on connections and the time of day, so trial the run a few times at the hours you’d travel before committing.

Your Next Steps

First, ring or email Southern Cross Catholic College to confirm enrolment steps, book a campus tour and get the latest fee schedule; double‑check Outside School Hours Care hours and fees with Catholic Early EdCare if you’ll need before‑or‑after‑school care or holiday programs. Second, look through the college’s co‑curricular pages and decide which programs — instrumental music, sport, the arts — matter most to your child so you can ask about sign‑ups and any associated costs. Third, trial the commute from your door to Kippa‑Ring and on to Brisbane Central at the times you’ll be travelling, and check current bus and train timetables to lock in realistic door‑to‑door times.
