Welcome to Annerley

Annerley sits snugly in Brisbane’s inner south, where tree-lined streets meet a lively mix of young professionals and growing families. It leans more inner‑city and middle‑income than its industrial past, which you’ll notice in the steady buzz of cafés, short commutes to the city and a median age of 33. The suburb has a big cohort of 20–34 year‑olds and a noticeable “never married” population, so first‑time parents and couples new to Australia often find neighbours on similar life stages — a comforting backdrop when you’re still unpacking boxes and learning local rhythms.

Inside the Classroom at Junction Park State School

Junction Park State School runs from Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland). The school’s vibe mixes a strong community focus with a surprisingly broad extra‑curricular program for a neighbourhood school. Music is a real draw here: strings, woodwind, brass and percussion lessons feed into concert bands and string ensembles, while younger kids join Mini Hearts for choral fun and older primary students audition for the Senior Choir. Sport is active and organised, too, with interschool teams in netball, touch and soccer that train regularly and compete across the district. If your child loves performing or being part of a team, Junction Park gives them a lot to choose from.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families juggling work and school hours, Junction Park’s community‑run Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) is run by the P&C and offers before‑school, after‑school and vacation care. That single on‑site provider model makes it straightforward to manage enrolment, pick‑ups and weekly routines without bouncing between multiple childcare operators. Before you finalise care arrangements, confirm session times, fees and available spots directly with the P&C — those details tend to determine whether you can keep your current work schedule or need to tweak it.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Ipswich Road anchors Annerley’s local shopping strip and it’s where dinner decisions get easy. Coco’s Annerley and a cluster of small supermarkets and ethnic grocers make grabbing fresh produce and specialty ingredients simple, so weeknight cooking doesn’t feel like a chore. For nights you don’t want to cook, Indian Brothers serves family‑friendly Indian dine‑in and takeaway, The Annerley Kitchen covers casual takeaway and delivery, and the usual fast‑food options are nearby for quick pickups. With groceries and affordable takeout close to the school, evenings can remain flexible even when schedules are full.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If extra academic support is on your radar, Kumon in Annerley offers maths and English tuition and there’s a healthy market of local private tutors for targeted help. For parents commuting into the CBD, Annerley benefits from the Beenleigh train line; the trip from Annerley station to Brisbane Central is commonly quoted at about 15–20 minutes with frequent weekday services. That short rail commute makes it possible to work in the city without sacrificing evening routines or long drop‑offs.

Your Next Steps

1) Ring the Junction Park P&C OSHC to check current hours, fees and enrolment availability so you can lock in care before school starts.  
2) Book a school tour or call the office to discuss Prep enrolment, catchment matters and spots in music ensembles, choir or interschool sport.  
3) Test a door‑to‑door commute using the Beenleigh line timetable and, if you want extra learning support, reach out to Kumon or a local tutor to plan a fallback study routine.
