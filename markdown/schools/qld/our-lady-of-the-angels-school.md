Welcome to Wavell Heights

Wavell Heights feels like the kind of suburb families move to when they want comfortable homes, a strong community and a predictable weekday rhythm. The median age sits at 37 and many households are made up of married couples and families, with a workforce leaning toward professionals and clerical or administrative roles. Household incomes are healthy by Brisbane standards — the 2021 median weekly household income was around $2,615 — and most residents were born in Australia, with New Zealand and England the next most common birthplaces. It’s a neighbourhood that reads as quietly settled and family-focused, which helps when you’re juggling school runs, after-school activities and evening dinners.

Inside the Classroom at Our Lady of the Angels School

Our Lady of the Angels School teaches children from Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland). The school has a lively arts and music culture: there are band and strings ensembles, multiple choirs that start as early as Year 3, and instrument tuition available on a user-pays basis. Kids who prefer quieter brain work will find chess club and a homework club, and debating appears on the activity list too. That mix of creative and academic options makes it an easy place to plug in a child who enjoys music or who benefits from extra structure after class.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For working parents, the school’s on-site outside school hours care is a practical lifeline. Wavell Outside School Hours Care, run by Catholic Early EdCare, offers before-school sessions starting around 6:30am, after-school care until about 6:00pm and vacation care during the school holidays. Having a program on the school site means you can coordinate drop-offs and pickups without extra detours, and you’ll be able to confirm usual hours, enrolment steps and fees directly with the provider as you finalize your routine.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

When life is busy, the local food options are a comfort. You’ll find independent grocers and specialty shops — including OM Spice Grocery & Fresh Meat and a handful of small Asian and ethnic food stores — if you like to cook or want specific ingredients. For nights when cooking feels like too much, there are reliable takeaways nearby: Thai On Rode for quick Thai takeaway, Spotted Cod for fish and chips, and the Wavell Inn for Chinese takeaway. Those places make it easy to feed a hungry family without complicated logistics.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you’re thinking about extra academic support, local tutoring services can match you with a tutor for ongoing primary-level help or enrichment; Tutors Australia and Alchemy Tuition both list services covering Wavell Heights. Commuting to Brisbane’s CBD requires a little planning because Wavell Heights doesn’t have its own train station. Families commonly travel to Toombul or Nundah stations by bus or a short drive, and from Toombul the train to Brisbane Central can be about 12–15 minutes — so allow extra time for that first bus or car leg when you’re timing a morning run.

Your Next Steps

First, check the outside school hours care hours, enrolment process and any fees directly with Wavell Outside School Hours Care / Catholic Early EdCare so you know how before- and after-school care will fit into your week. Second, book a school visit to watch a rehearsal or meet staff — ask about choir and band schedules and instrument-tuition costs while you’re there. Third, trial the commute: map a route to Toombul or Nundah, include the bus or short drive from Wavell Heights, and time the whole trip so you’re not surprised on day one.
