Welcome to Southport (Gold Coast, QLD)

If you’re picturing a coastal regional centre where surf-side leisure meets everyday convenience, Southport fits that mental map. Household incomes here sit around the median weekly level for the area, and the local workforce is a comfortable mix of office-based roles and service-sector jobs that keep a seaside town ticking. You’ll meet a fair number of families who were born overseas — New Zealand is the largest non-Australian birthplace after Australia — which makes settling in easier for newcomers: multicultural shops, restaurants and friendly neighbours mean your family won’t feel like the only ones adjusting to a new country.

Inside the Classroom at St Hilda’s School

St Hilda’s junior campus covers Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland), and the feel here is very much “whole-child” rather than purely academic. The school leans into sport with strong programs in netball, swimming and rowing, and there are explicit pathways for students who want to step up into high-performance training. Music is also well catered for: instrumental tuition across strings, woodwind, brass, percussion, piano and guitar sits alongside voice lessons and ensemble opportunities. After-school clubs — from dance to chess to tennis — give younger students low-pressure ways to explore interests and make friends.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families juggling work and school schedules, St Hilda’s runs on-site Outside School Hours Care, which includes before-school care, after-school care and vacation care. You can book permanent sessions if your routine is steady, or casual spots when plans change. That on-campus childcare model removes a lot of daily logistics parents worry about: drop-off and pick-up stay on familiar grounds and you don’t have to ferry kids between a separate daycare and the school. If you’re easing into a new job or arranging shift swaps, knowing there’s a school-operated program on site is an enormous practical relief.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Southport’s food landscape is family-friendly and refreshingly varied. For quick midweek dinners, you’ll find Korean barbecue and casual Korean spots that make takeout evenings easy, plus family-focused seafood options like The Fish Shak near the Broadwater when you want a fuss-free dine-in. If you cook at home, you won’t be short of ingredients: there are several Asian and Korean grocers — the kind of Hanaro/K-Town style stores where you can find fresh produce, sauces and snacks that make international families feel at home. A shopping run to Australia Fair combined with a stop at a specialty supermarket will sort out weeknight staples in one trip.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you’re thinking about extra academic support, Southport has established options. Kumon runs local English and mathematics programs, and independent tutors offer targeted literacy, numeracy and test-prep help for primary-school kids. If your work takes you to Brisbane’s central business district, a direct train from Southport doesn’t exist; families commonly take the local tram or bus to the Helensvale interchange and transfer to the Gold Coast line train. Expect typical door-to-door travel time to be around an hour and a half to an hour and forty minutes depending on connections and the time of day, so it’s worth trialling the commute at the hours you’d be travelling before you lock in a permanent routine.

Your Next Steps

1) Call or check the school’s Outside School Hours Care page to confirm hours, fees and how permanent versus casual bookings work so your childcare plan matches your work roster.

2) Book a school tour or open morning, ask for the Prep to Year 6 campus map and the school’s pick-up/drop-off traffic procedures, and while you’re there enquire about trial sessions for the sports or music programs you care about.

3) If you’ll be commuting to Brisbane, do a timed trial of the public-transport route during the hours you expect to travel and check the train and tram timetables for connection windows at Helensvale.
