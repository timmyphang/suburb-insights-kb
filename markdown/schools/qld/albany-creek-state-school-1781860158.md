Inside the Classroom at Albany Creek State School

Albany Creek State School runs primary years from Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland), and it’s the sort of school that balances strong class routines with real opportunities for kids to explore music and the arts [citation:Out of school hours care - Albany Creek State School]. Children with an interest in instruments will find a formal Instrumental Music Program that introduces specialist tuition and ensembles around Years 3–4, so families who want band, strings or woodwind options can plan ahead for sign‑ups and practice timetables [citation:Instrumental Music - Albany Creek State School]. Sports are active and well‑organised, with interschool competitions in soccer, netball, cricket, rugby league and touch football, and the school also runs choirs, ensembles and a Year 5–6 Dance Club for kids who prefer performing arts to team sports [citation:Sports - Albany Creek State School] [citation:Extra-curricular - Albany Creek State School].
