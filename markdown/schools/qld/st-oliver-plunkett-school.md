Welcome to Cannon Hill

Cannon Hill has the kind of energy that makes young families breathe easier: a median age of 34 and a strong representation of adults in their mid‑20s to late‑30s means playground chatter, prams on footpaths and cafés doing a steady trade in after‑school coffee. The workforce here leans toward white‑collar jobs and service and retail roles rather than heavy industry, so you’ll find professionals and local small‑business owners side by side. It’s a neighbourhood that feels intentionally lived‑in rather than staged — useful when you’re choosing a place to raise kids and build routines.

Inside the Classroom at St Oliver Plunkett School (Prep to Year 6, note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland)

St Oliver Plunkett caters from Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland). The school has a lively extracurricular palette that stretches well beyond the classroom. If your child likes robots, collaborative problem‑solving or competitive thinking, programs such as RoboCup, Opti‑Minds and Tournament of the Minds are on offer. Creative kids can join choir, instrumental groups or speech and drama, while sport options range from structured swimming lessons and clubs to representative programs in tennis, aerobics and even chess. The overall feel is one of broad opportunity: the academic and creative tracks run in parallel, so there’s a real chance to find a niche for almost every child.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families juggling work and school runs, the school’s on‑site Outside School Hours Care (on‑site before and after school childcare) run by Catholic Early EdCare is a major practical win. Typical care windows follow the familiar rhythms of busy households, with before‑school sessions beginning in the early morning and after‑school care stretching into the early evening; vacation programs are also available. The service is registered with the Australian Children's Education & Care Quality Authority, which provides an extra layer of reassurance about standards and compliance. In practice, that means you can make the 9am sprint to the office without the mid‑afternoon panic — and still pick up a child who’s had a safe, structured afternoon.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Cannon Hill’s local shopping hub, Cannon Central, makes weeknight logistics simple: you’ll find a supermarket, a butcher, a pharmacy and a handful of casual dining options within easy reach. If you cook from home and like to spice things up, Santoshi Spice Market and Santoshi India House offer Indian groceries and pantry staples that can turn a regular weeknight into something a little more adventurous. Everyday runs to the shop are covered by Friendly Grocer and nearby IGA so you won’t be hunting for basics. When you don’t feel like cooking, family‑friendly dining is on hand: Satay Ria serves Malaysian‑Chinese dishes that travel well for takeout, and local fish and chip spots such as Crustaceans Seafood and Sea Change are reliable weeknight crowd pleasers.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you think your child might benefit from extra academic support, Cannon Hill has local tutoring options from one‑to‑one centres to maths specialists for Years 2 through 12. Those services make it easy to slot a weekly session around school and work. For commuting parents, Cannon Hill station on the Cleveland line is a genuine asset: typical scheduled travel to Brisbane Central takes around 20 to 25 minutes depending on the service, with frequent peak‑time trains that make a city commute manageable without a car. It’s the kind of commute that preserves family time while keeping city jobs accessible.

Your Next Steps

First, book a school visit and confirm the Prep to Year 6 enrolment process while you check the extracurricular list on the school site to see how your child might fit. Second, call Catholic Early EdCare to verify Outside School Hours Care hours, booking procedures and vacancies, and confirm the service registration details with the national regulator. Third, test the Cannon Hill to Brisbane Central timetable during your likely peak times, then swing by Cannon Central and the nearby grocers and eateries to see how evenings might feel for your family.
