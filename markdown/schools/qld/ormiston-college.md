# Welcome to Ormiston

If you're picturing a bayside suburb where tree-lined streets meet family-friendly shops and weekend playground hangs, Ormiston fits that picture neatly. With a median age around 47, it’s a relatively affluent, stable neighbourhood where many households are owner-occupiers and white-collar professionals — the sort of place where families settle in for the long haul. Weekly household incomes sit well above the national average, and while about two-thirds of residents are Australian-born, a noticeable share come from overseas, most commonly England. For newcomers, that mix of local and international households can make the transition feel more comfortable: you’ll bump into other families who’ve navigated the move before and exchanged tips on everything from school enrolment to the best weekend walks along the bay.

# Inside the Classroom at Ormiston College

Ormiston College’s lower-school setup is framed as Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland), and the classroom experience reflects a strong push beyond the basics. Academic life is matched by a wide co‑curricular program: music is a particular highlight, with choirs, concert and jazz bands, string groups and individual instrumental tuition mapped into students’ options. Sport is equally broad, from swimming and tennis to the usual school favourites like soccer, netball and rugby, and there are extra clubs such as chess and opportunities to join The Duke of Edinburgh’s International Award. If you value a school that encourages kids to try lots of atividades outside the classroom, Ormiston College makes those pathways easy to find.

# Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families juggling work and school runs, the practical stuff matters. Ormiston College runs on-site Outside School Hours Care — that’s before-school care from 6:30am and after-school care until 6:00pm, with vacation-care programs through the school holidays. Bookings can be casual or permanent, which is great if your work roster changes from week to week. Having that on-site before- and after-school childcare removes a lot of weekday stress: you can drop the kids early, head into the city, or pick them up after work without an extra childcare handoff.

# The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Ormiston village keeps weeknight dinners simple and local. For a quick family takeaway you’ll find a Chinese/Asian option and a familiar fast-food option, while a standalone Thai restaurant brings something a little more flavourful if you want to go out. If you prefer to cook, the suburb has easy access to fresh produce — think The Big Mushroom Fruit Barn and a handful of neighbourhood grocers — so weeknight meals can be wholesome without a long supermarket run. When the weather allows, families often end the day with a stroll to one of the waterfront reserves; Raby Bay Esplanade Park and Apex Park are favourites for kicking a ball, watching the light on the water, and letting kids burn off the last of their energy before homework.

# Tuition and the Daily Commute

If extra academic support is on your radar, there are local private tutoring options offering in-home and online sessions, including general tuition and 1:1 maths help. For parents commuting into Brisbane’s CBD, Ormiston is served by the Cleveland line via Ormiston station. Train times to Brisbane Central typically sit around 54 to 63 minutes, so most families should budget roughly an hour door-to-door depending on your exact start and finish points. It’s a realistic daily commute for many, and easy to test in advance to see how it fits your rhythm.

# Your Next Steps

Start by confirming the practical details: check current Outside School Hours Care hours, fees and vacancy information on the school’s OSHC page and the government childcare directory so you know what’s available before you commit. Book a school tour or meet staff to see the facilities and chat about the Prep to Year 6 co‑curricular options that matter most to your child, whether that’s music ensembles, sport or clubs. Finally, test a likely commute on your chosen schedule and spend an afternoon visiting Ormiston Village, the local parks and grocers — nothing replaces a real walk around the neighbourhood when you’re deciding if it feels like home.
