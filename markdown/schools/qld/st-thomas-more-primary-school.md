Welcome to Sunshine Beach

Sunshine Beach feels like a well‑kept secret that the coast quietly shares with families who like sand, high amenities and a slower rhythm. It reads as an older, established seaside village: the median age nudges toward 49 and household incomes sit above many nearby pockets, which shows in the tidy homes, local shops and the mellow, family‑oriented vibe. You’ll find plenty of married couples and retirees alongside young families who prize being minutes from the surf. Most people here were born in Australia, with England the most common overseas birthplace for newcomers, so you’ll settle into a community that’s familiar-feeling without being homogenised.

Inside the Classroom at St Thomas More Primary School

St Thomas More Primary School at Sunshine Beach teaches Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland). Classrooms are complemented by a lively creative program: the school runs an instrumental music program with junior and senior bands and small ensembles for Years 3 to 6, and it stages regular choir performances and full school musicals. Dance troupes of different ages perform throughout the year, and a specialist physical education program feeds into school carnivals with a Year 6 swimming and surf skills program so kids learn ocean respect as part of their curriculum. Those creative and active options make it easy for children to find a niche — whether that’s brass band, chorus or the surf safety squad.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families juggling work and school hours, St Thomas More has on‑site Outside School Hours Care run by Catholic Early EdCare. This service provides before‑school, after‑school and vacation care, and you can check availability and book sessions directly with the provider. Having on‑site care simplifies handoffs for parents who need consistent pick‑up or who travel for work occasionally, and it keeps children in a familiar environment where staff already know the school’s routines.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Sunshine Beach has the intimacy of small village grocers for everyday needs and a couple of specialty suppliers nearby if you lean organic or eat Asian staples. For quick dinners, Frankie’s Trattoria is the local Italian that caters to families with takeout options, Marble Bar Bistro serves relaxed bistro and tapas for an easy evening out or takeaway, and The Deck is the dependable family café doing all‑day breakfasts and lunches. For specialty pantry runs, Organika covers organic and health‑food needs and Asian Frenzy supplies broader Asian ingredients for those evenings when you want something different.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you want extra academic support, Noosa Tutoring Centre offers one‑to‑one tuition and Kip McGrath Noosa provides focused English and maths help for primary and secondary students. Plan ahead with assessments and block sessions early in the year if your child is starting a new school level. Commuting beyond the coast needs a little planning: there’s no train station at Sunshine Beach itself, and the nearest Sunshine Coast line stations sit inland. Door‑to‑door public‑transport trips to Brisbane Central — Brisbane’s central business district — are commonly around three to four hours, while driving to Brisbane is roughly one hour 35 minutes to one hour 45 minutes depending on traffic. For parents who travel into the city occasionally, it’s worth trialling a drive versus coach or train combo so you know which option fits your schedule.

Your Next Steps

First, ring St Thomas More to confirm enrolment processes, ask for the current fee schedule and find the next open day or visit time. Second, if you need before, after or vacation care, contact the on‑site Outside School Hours Care to check availability and reserve sessions early. Third, if music, dance or sport matters to your child, ask about the instrumental program for Years 3 to 6, the choir and musical productions, the dance troupes and the Year 6 surf and swimming program — these activities fill quickly, so sign up as soon as you decide.
