Welcome to Kenmore (Brisbane), QLD

Kenmore feels like the kind of riverside suburb where families have been putting down roots for decades. With a median age around 40 and a high share of married households, the streets and parks hum with school runs, weekend sport and the kind of neighbourly chats that make a place feel settled quickly. Household incomes here sit well above the Queensland average, which shows up in well-maintained properties, plenty of local amenities and a strong white‑collar, family‑focused vibe. If you’re arriving from interstate or overseas and want a calm, family‑first neighbourhood that still has easy access to Brisbane’s inner city, Kenmore ticks a lot of boxes.

Inside the Classroom at Our Lady of the Rosary School (Prep to Year 6, note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland)

Our Lady of the Rosary is a small, community‑centred Catholic primary that runs from Prep to Year 6. It’s very much tuned to families who want a broad, creative curriculum alongside solid classroom basics. Music is a standout: every class has music time, there’s a school choir, a Year 4 band and structured instrumental lessons that start with strings in the early years and move to band instruments from Year 3. Outside the music room you’ll find a full roster of co‑curricular choices—running and athletics, tennis and futsal training through a local academy, chess club, speech and drama, and musical theatre. That mix means there’s something for children who want to try a bit of everything without needing to travel across town for lessons.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

The school hosts on‑site Outside School Hours Care, run by Catholic Early EdCare, which covers before‑school, after‑school and vacation care. Having care physically on the school grounds gives a lot of practical comfort to working parents: children don’t need to be transferred to a separate centre, the handover is smoother, and holiday care solves the usual school‑holiday logistics. Before you lock plans around it, check the exact hours, fees and enrolment steps with Catholic Early EdCare so you know how it will fit into your workday.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

For everyday groceries you’ll gravitate between Kenmore Plaza—home to mainstream supermarkets like Woolworths and Aldi—and Kenmore Village, where you’ll find fresh produce and independent grocers. If you like cooking with specific spices and ingredients from home, there’s also an Indian grocery in town. For those evenings when dinner needs to be quick and family‑friendly, the local fish and chip shops are reliable favourites; Village Seafood in the Village and Kenmore Plaza Seafoods are both popular with families. Kenmore Plaza’s food outlets cover pizza, Asian takeaways and easy weeknight options, and you can also use delivery apps for a night off the kitchen.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If your child could benefit from extra academic support, Kenmore has local tutoring options for maths and reading as well as combined English and maths tuition—convenient when you want short lessons that don’t cost you an hour on the road. On commute days, remember Kenmore doesn’t have a train station; the realistic public transport option into Brisbane’s central business district is by bus. Typical journey times are around half an hour, with common planners quoting somewhere in the high 20s to mid‑30s minutes. It’s an easy commute, but worth testing during peak school‑run times before making a daily routine out of it.

Your Next Steps

First, ring Catholic Early EdCare and confirm the before‑school, after‑school and vacation care times, fees and how enrolment works so that your workday planning is rock solid. Second, browse the school’s music and co‑curricular pages and ask about sign‑up windows for band, choir, sport and drama—popular activities fill quickly. Third, if you think your child might need extra practice in maths or reading, contact the local tuition centres for a trial lesson and compare schedules against school and care hours.
