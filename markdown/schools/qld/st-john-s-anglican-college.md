# Welcome to Forest Lake

If you’re picturing a tidy, family‑focused neighbourhood where the weekends revolve around local parks and school events, Forest Lake fits the bill. This masterplanned suburb of about 22,700 people sits squarely in the middle‑income range, with a lot of established families and a workforce that mixes white‑collar roles with service and trades. The median weekly household income sits around $1,921 and the community skews youngish, with a median age of 37. It’s the kind of place where houses feel lived‑in rather than show‑home new, and where neighbours in school drop‑off lines tend to know each other by first name.

# Inside the Classroom at St John’s Anglican College

St John’s Anglican College runs from Prep through Year 12, so once you’re in, you can stay for the long haul. The Junior School has an on‑site Outside School Hours Care offering before‑school care, after‑school care and vacation care, which makes juggling work and school routines a lot easier for busy parents. Musically inclined kids will find a full programme: orchestral instrumental tuition begins early and there are ensembles and choirs for students who love to play and sing. Sport is also a big part of life here, with a strong team‑sports culture—rugby, soccer, netball, basketball, plus swimming and athletics—so children who like competition or a team environment can jump straight in. The clubs programme is wide-ranging too, from STEM and technology groups to drama and debating, giving kids choices beyond the classroom to discover what lights them up.

# Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For parents who finish work at 5 and hope to catch the last school buses, St John’s on‑site Outside School Hours Care is one of those conveniences that reduces daily friction. Having before and after care, plus vacation care available on campus, means you won’t be juggling a long list of drop‑offs. It also supports families who need reliable supervision tied to the school day rather than a separate provider, which can be a real stress reducer when your calendar fills up with extracurriculars and after‑school rehearsals or trainings.

# The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Forest Lake Shopping Centre is the local hub when dinner needs to be sorted quickly: major supermarkets like Woolworths, Coles and ALDI are all in range, and there’s a Kmart for the odd household errand. If you’re craving something simple and family‑friendly, Khao Thai does reliable Thai takeaway and dine‑in options, Punjab Curry Club offers North Indian favourites for nights when you want bold flavours with minimal prep, and the local Chinese takeaway keeps weeknights moving with familiar dishes the kids usually approve of. For more specialised Asian or ethnic groceries you might need for a particular recipe, neighbouring suburbs are where you’ll find a broader selection of specialist stores.

# Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you want extra academic support, Forest Lake has options close to home: Kumon for workbook‑style English and mathematics practice and Kip McGrath for targeted English and maths tutoring. These centres are familiar faces in the suburbs for families who want steady, ongoing help rather than one‑off coaching. Commuting to Brisbane’s CBD is doable but expect it to take time: there’s no train station in Forest Lake, so the main public transport link is the TransLink bus route 100 directly to the city. Depending on traffic and stops, door‑to‑door on public transport is commonly in the neighbourhood of an hour, so factor that into school pick‑up windows and your own daily commute plans.

# Your Next Steps

Call the Junior School to confirm how to enrol in the on‑site Outside School Hours Care, what the hours are, and how the sign‑up process works so you’re not left guessing on the first week of term. Look through St John’s music, sport and clubs listings with your child and pick a couple of activities to trial so they start school with something to look forward to. Try the bus route 100 at the time you’d normally travel to see how long the trip really takes and whether driving or a school carpool might be a better fit for your family rhythm.
