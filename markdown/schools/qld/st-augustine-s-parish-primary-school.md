Welcome to Currumbin Waters, QLD

Nestled inland from the coast, Currumbin Waters has that settled, family-ready feel where well-kept homes, tree-lined streets and steady property growth are part of the everyday backdrop. The suburb leans middle to upper-middle in income, with a 2021 median household windfall of about $1,636 a week and house values that have been on the rise, so you’re looking at a neighbourhood many families choose when they want space and stability. The local workforce is a healthy mix of professionals, tradies and service workers, which gives the community both a comfortable, white-collar rhythm during weekdays and a practical, hands-on vibe. About 78% of residents were born in Australia, with England the most common overseas birthplace; for families moving from abroad, that mix—enough people with international roots to make newcomers feel at home—tends to be a real comfort when settling into a new country.

Inside the Classroom at St Augustine’s Parish Primary School

St Augustine’s Parish Primary School teaches from Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland), and it’s the kind of Catholic primary where classroom life is balanced with a strong emphasis on culture, the arts and sport. Alongside the everyday literacy and numeracy program you’ll find specialist classes in music, visual art and dance and drama, plus French lessons for younger learners—so creative kids get plenty of runway. Sport is big here: house teams, swimming, cross‑country and athletics carnivals, term surfing and representative pathways mean most students are moving every week. The school’s calendar and annual reports also highlight performing‑arts events and community celebrations, which is where families really see the school’s identity come to life.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For working families, St Augustine’s has on-site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) operated by Catholic Early EdCare. The program runs before school from about 6:30am and continues after school until roughly 6:00pm, with vacation‑care options across the school holidays. It’s approved for childcare subsidy support, which is useful if both parents are juggling work and pick-ups. If you need consistent before- or after-school supervision without an extra commute, having care embedded on campus is one of those small practical comforts that changes daily logistics for the better.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Daily life in Currumbin Waters keeps things convenient. For your routine supermarket shop there’s IGA Currumbin Fair close by, and if you love weekend wandering or picking up multicultural ingredients, the Sanctuary Markets at Currumbin are a favourite for market-style stalls and specialty finds. Weeknight dining leans family-friendly and informal: local cafés and takeaway spots such as The Galleon 4223 make dinners easy after sport practice or late work finishes, and the wider Currumbin strip offers a good mix of fish and chips, cafés and small restaurants for low‑fuss family meals.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If your child wants extra help with schoolwork, Currumbin Waters plugs you into a decent local tutoring scene—options like Cloud Tuition and Alchemy Tuition offer personalised primary and secondary support, with in‑home or online lessons to fit busy timetables. Commuting to the Brisbane CBD is doable but requires planning: Currumbin Waters doesn’t have a heavy‑rail station inside the suburb, so most families drive or catch a local link to nearby stations such as Varsity Lakes or Tugun. From Varsity Lakes the direct train to Brisbane Central is commonly about one hour and twenty‑three minutes, so expect a short road transfer plus the train time if you’re doing the weekday city run.

Your Next Steps

First, book a leadership tour with St Augustine’s and ask to see classrooms and the on-site Outside School Hours Care facilities so you can picture a full school day from drop-off to pick-up. Second, use the visit to confirm logistics: check the exact operating hours and enrolment steps for the Catholic Early EdCare program, ask about childcare subsidy eligibility and how waitlists or bookings are handled, and request the school’s most recent annual report and enrolment trends. Third, talk through classroom sizes, pastoral care and behaviour or support frameworks, and confirm specialist offerings and extracurricular expectations—those details will tell you how your child will be supported academically, socially and in sport or the arts.
