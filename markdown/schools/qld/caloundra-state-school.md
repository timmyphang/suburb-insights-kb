Welcome to Caloundra

There’s a gentle, seaside calm to Caloundra that shows up in the rhythm of daily life: early morning walks on the Esplanade, a steady stream of retirees and long‑term locals, and a town that leans into beachside convenience rather than urban rush. The suburb itself skews older, with a lot of residents in retirement, and household incomes in the immediate suburb sit below the broader Caloundra area—but widen your view to the whole Caloundra region and you’ll see a more mixed, higher‑income picture. Most people here were born in Australia, with England the most common overseas birthplace among the smaller group who weren’t. For families moving in, that means a place where friendly, well‑established neighbourhoods meet the lived comforts of a coastal township.

Inside the Classroom at Caloundra State School

Caloundra State School is a genuine community primary school with a broad co‑curricular life. While the school’s extra‑curricular listings focus on upper primary cohorts, you’ll find programs that give children a lot of ways to plug in: Instrumental Music for Years 4 to 6, a Strings Program that runs from Years 3 to 6, and active dance ensembles like CalStars and CalCrew. Sport is part of the day-to-day culture too, from rugby squads to swim and surf programs, and there’s a push on STEM with robotics clubs that kids love tinkering in. These activities sit alongside core classroom learning in a way that feels inclusive rather than elite—an appealing mix if you want your child to try music, sport or robotics without travelling far.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

The school has on‑site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) provided by Helping Hands, covering before‑school, after‑school and vacation programs. That on-campus continuity makes the logistics of a busy week much easier: drop-offs that don’t require a long detour, a familiar setting for kids to unwind and structured holiday programs that keep school‑age children occupied when parents are working. For many families relocating here, having a trusted on‑site provider is one less thing to worry about when balancing work and school runs.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

When it comes to groceries and eating out, Caloundra feels small but surprisingly complete. You’ll find mainstream supermarkets and smaller fresh‑produce markets where you can pick up just-caught seafood or seasonal fruit, alongside specialty shops such as Asian Food Land that make everyday meal planning easier for anyone craving a particular ingredient. After a day at the beach or a late practice, the Bulcock Beach and Esplanade area is where the town loosens up: Beachview Seafoods does the classic beachfront fish and chips that kids will happily devour, while there are solid Thai options—Wung Nam and Tasty Thai among them—if you want something quicker and family‑friendly. Dinner here tends to favour relaxed, informal spots where kids are welcome and the view is part of the seasoning.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If extra academic support is on your mind, Caloundra has local options for tuition in primary English and maths, plus broader K–12 tutoring that can be arranged in‑home or online. Those services make it straightforward to bridge curriculum differences if you’re coming from overseas or just want targeted help around assessment time. Commuting to Brisbane is doable but not quick: there’s no active heavy‑rail station in town right now, although a Caloundra station is planned as part of a regional rail project. For now, expect a typical public‑transport journey to Brisbane’s centre to take around two to two‑and‑a‑half hours door‑to‑door depending on connections. Many families settle the school‑run logistics by mapping a few sample trips with the regional public‑transport planner and deciding whether a flexible work arrangement or occasional carpool will be part of the weekly pattern.

Your Next Steps

First, pull the school prospectus or the one‑page summary of fees and enrolment rules from the school or the Department of Education profile so you’re clear on zones, policies and any costs. Second, ring Helping Hands to confirm exact Outside School Hours Care hours, fees and vacation program details so you can line up before‑ and after‑school care that actually fits your workweek. Third, run a sample school‑run commute for your address and usual drop‑off time through the regional public‑transport journey planner to get a realistic door‑to‑door estimate and see whether driving, public transport or a blended approach will suit your family.
