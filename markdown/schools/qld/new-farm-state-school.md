# Welcome to New Farm, QLD

If you’re picturing leafy streets, weekend markets and a neighbourhood that somehow feels both buzzy and relaxed, you’re thinking of New Farm. The median age here sits around 39 and the weekly household income reflects a comfortable, lifestyle-oriented community where a lot of residents are young professionals or singletons carving out city-fluent lives. About two-thirds of locals were born in Australia, with England the next most common overseas birthplace, which gives the suburb a familiar, easygoing vibe for families arriving from overseas.

# Inside the Classroom at New Farm State School

New Farm State School runs from Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland), and it feels like a community school with city energy. The campus is built for active kids: there’s a school pool, a grassed oval and courts for regular sport and swimming programs, and specialist classes in the arts, technology and languages. Parents and kids talk about an enrichment program that lets students stretch beyond the standard curriculum, and the school’s choir is a real community staple — weekly rehearsals and performances pop up at Harmony Day, ANZAC Day and the Teneriffe Festival, so families quickly see their children participating in neighbourhood life.

# Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families juggling work with school pick-ups, New Farm State School offers on-site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) operated by School Plus. It covers before- and after-school sessions as well as vacation programs, though places require enrolment. That on-site option makes late afternoons and school holiday planning so much easier when you need reliable, familiar care right where the kids are learning.

# The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Groceries and weeknight dinners are remarkably simple here. For everyday shopping, there’s a Coles at Merthyr Village — handy for quick runs and pantry staples. If you prefer artisan produce and counter-service charm, the long-running New Farm Deli and Café and nearby specialty shops in the Teneriffe and Newstead precincts will become favourites. On busy nights, local takeaways and casual eateries save the day: pizza from Crust on Brunswick Street, fish and chips or seafood from New Farm Seafood, and a relaxed Thai at Thai On New Farm are all within easy reach.

# Tuition and the Daily Commute

If extra academic support is on your radar, New Farm has local tutoring options including one-to-one providers who work in-home or online, as well as directories connecting you with subject specialists for exam preparation. Commuting into the central business district (CBD) usually means a pleasant ferry ride from New Farm Park — roughly a 10 to 14 minute trip depending on the service — or a bus if that works better for your route. There isn’t a heavy rail station inside New Farm itself; the closest train access is Fortitude Valley, which is roughly a 15 to 25 minute walk from parts of the suburb or a short bus trip if you prefer trains.

# Your Next Steps

1) Contact New Farm State School to confirm enrolment and Prep intake details, and ask about the specialist and enrichment programs so you know how your child might fit in.  
2) Enrol early in the on-site Outside School Hours Care (School Plus) and pin down exact before, after and vacation hours so your workweek has fewer surprises.  
3) Test your commute plans: check the ferry timetables from New Farm Park for realistic pickup and drop-off windows and keep Fortitude Valley as a train-backup if needed; if you expect extra academic help, reach out to a local tutor to lock in support before term ramps up.
