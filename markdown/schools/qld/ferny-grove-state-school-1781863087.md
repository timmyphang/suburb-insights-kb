Welcome to Ferny Grove, Brisbane QLD
Ferny Grove feels like one of those suburbs built for family life: established, quietly leafy, and sitting in Brisbane’s outer north‑west with a median age around 41. Inside the Classroom at Ferny Grove State School
Ferny Grove State School is the local hub for primary education and runs an active program that keeps kids moving: athletics, cross‑country and swimming carnivals are staples, and older primary students in Years 5 to 6 get opportunities to represent the school in district interschool sport. [citation:School Sport - Ferny Grove State School] Music and performing arts are alive here too, with both junior and senior school bands giving children a stage and a sense of belonging. [citation:Activities (Extra-curricular) - Ferny Grove State School] If you’re checking grade placement, ask the school directly about exact grade structures and whether your child starts in Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland).
