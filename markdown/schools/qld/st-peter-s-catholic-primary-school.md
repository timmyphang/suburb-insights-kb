Welcome to Caboolture

If you're picturing a family-friendly, regional base an easy train ride from Brisbane, Caboolture fits the bill. The median age sits around 36, and the suburb leans into a blue-collar, hardworking character with household incomes hovering near metropolitan averages—comfortable, down-to-earth and built around families rather than inner-city flash. You’ll find the Caboolture Hub for library visits and community events, a charming historical village for weekend wanderings, an aquatic centre for summer laps or kids’ lessons, and busy weekend markets that local families swear by. About three-quarters of residents were born in Australia, with New Zealand and England the next most common birthplaces, which means new arrivals often find a familiar mix of cultures rather than a sharply foreign environment—comforting when you’re settling into a new country.

Inside the Classroom at St Peter’s Catholic Primary School

St Peter’s teaches Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland), and the school hums with a blend of faith-based values and practical opportunities for kids to try new things. The music program is a standout: guitar lessons, junior and senior bands, and choir ensembles that get out to festivals. Sport is part of the daily rhythm too, with swimming lessons, interschool and gala sports and lively interhouse carnivals. Dance troupes, student leadership programs, camps and the parish sacramental program round out a school culture that’s active, community-focused and rich in extracurricular pathways.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For busy families, the school’s on-site Outside School Hours Care, operated by Catholic Early EdCare, is a real lifesaver. Before-school care starts from 6:30am, after-school care runs until 6:30pm, and vacation care covers school breaks. The program is located at the rear of the campus with its own gate access, making handovers quick and less stressful on those rushed mornings or late afternoons when both parents are juggling work and activities.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Caboolture keeps weeknight dinners interesting without a trek. Major supermarkets like Coles and Woolworths cover the big-shop essentials, and you’ll also find specialty grocers such as Singh for Indian ingredients and CU Mart for Asian pantry staples—helpful if you want to recreate a favourite family curry or noodle dish. When you prefer to eat out or grab takeaway after sport practice, there are family-friendly options nearby: Dhamaka for Indian meals, Bellygood for Chinese and Malaysian takeaway, and Sweet Basil for relaxed Thai dining.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you’re thinking about extra academic support, Caboolture has local tuition options that focus on English and mathematics and NAPLAN preparation—centres like Kip McGrath and The Bright Tutors are part of the local mix, alongside online providers that serve the area. Commuting into Brisbane is feasible for those working in the city: the Caboolture train station sits on the main line and typical scheduled journeys to Brisbane Central run in the 45–50 minute range depending on the service, which makes day commuting realistic while keeping evenings and weekends at home.
