Welcome to Victoria Point

There’s a calm, settled rhythm to Victoria Point that families feel the moment they arrive: a median age around 49, steady household incomes, and a workforce stitched together from trades, clerical roles and a healthy share of retirees and well-established families. It’s not a precinct of late-night bars and single-share apartments; it’s quieter, friendly and intentionally livable. For parents arriving from overseas, you’ll notice about three quarters of residents were born in Australia but there’s a reassuring international thread here too — England and New Zealand are the most common overseas birthplaces — which makes settling in and finding familiar cultural touchpoints a little easier when you’re adapting to a new country.

Inside the Classroom at Faith Lutheran College – Redlands (Junior campus, Victoria Point)

The junior campus teaches children from Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland). The school leans into a “whole child” approach. If your child loves running around and team play, Active@Faith is the school-wide sports program that offers coached team sports and structured physical activities. If they prefer quieter focus or the stage, Create@Faith gives them access to drama, visual arts and instrumental music with ensemble and tuition opportunities. Those two strands — active and creative — mean most kids can find a niche without losing the daily rhythm of classroom learning.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For working families the campus runs its own on-site Outside School Hours Care, which covers before-school, after-school and full-day vacation care. The morning window opens early (around 6:30am) to match early starts, and after-school care typically runs until about 6:00pm. Because the provider is the school’s own OSHC program, there’s a continuity of staff, site familiarity and the convenience of having care on campus — but a practical note: confirm current hours, vacancy and booking procedures before relying on it for day-to-day coverage, because places and start times do change from year to year.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Victoria Point makes weeknight life easy. For regular groceries, you’ll find a mix of specialty and fresh produce options — Spice Asian Grocery for pantry staples and region-specific ingredients, and Skippy’s Fresh Frootz for straight-to-the-point fruit and veg. After a busy cohort of afternoon activities, the local dinner scene leans toward family-friendly takeaway: the kiosk and café does classic fish-and-chips plus quick café options, while Thai Tamarind and Golden Bowl cover reliable Thai and Chinese meals when you need something fast and comforting. It’s the kind of local food map that keeps weeknights low-stress and gives you a few reliable saves for those homework-to-bed squeezes.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you think your child might benefit from extra academic support, there are local tutoring choices that service Victoria Point and the Redlands area, including KeirEd Tutoring and Alchemy Tuition, both of which offer flexible in-home or online options. Factor in the commute if your family works in Brisbane City: there’s no train station at Victoria Point, so most people use direct TransLink buses. Expect roughly 75 minutes to the Brisbane central business district depending on route and time of day, which is why many parents trial the home-to-school and work commute during peak hours before they commit to a morning schedule.

Your Next Steps

First, book a campus tour or a Prep information session and ask for up-to-date enrolment and fee details so you know exactly where your child fits in. Second, confirm the on-site Outside School Hours Care hours, vacancy and booking procedure directly with the school — check the early morning start (around 6:30am), after-school finish (about 6:00pm) and vacation care availability before you rely on it for full work-day coverage. Third, review the co-curricular programs (Active@Faith, Create@Faith, and instrumental music), put your child into trials or waitlists if needed, and contact local tutors early if you want a regular academic boost.
