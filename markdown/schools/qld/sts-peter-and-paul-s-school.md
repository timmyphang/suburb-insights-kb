Welcome to Bulimba

There’s a quietly confident, riverside rhythm to Bulimba that many families fall in love with fast. With a median age hovering around 40 and household incomes well above Brisbane averages, this inner‑suburban pocket feels settled and neighbourhood‑driven, centred on the boutiques and cafés of Oxford Street and the riverfront parks that double as weekend playgrounds. If you’re picturing tree‑lined streets, pram‑friendly footpaths and an easygoing weekend vibe where you can grab groceries, a coffee and a playground in a single loop, that’s Bulimba.

Inside the Classroom at Sts Peter and Paul’s School

Sts Peter and Paul’s is a Catholic primary school teaching Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland). The school balances traditional classroom rhythms with creative and extracurricular stitching: choir and music lessons — including individual piano and guitar tuition — sit alongside a Chess Club and after‑school tennis coaching. Older primary students can access extension pathways such as participation in the Maths Olympiad and debating, so the school feels like a place where both everyday learning and academic stretch are possible without sending your child across town.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

One of the most practical features for busy families is on‑site Outside School Hours Care, which operates before school, after school and during vacation time through the Catholic provider. Published session times and fee details are available through the national child‑care listings and the provider, so you can check availability and booking routines before you commit. That on‑site childcare makes juggling work runs and evening activities much easier: kids are supervised right where they study, and you don’t have to coordinate separate pickups across different suburbs.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Oxford Street keeps things convenient when dinner time rolls around. There’s a full Woolworths for the weekly shop, and specialty stores such as Market Organics and The Source Bulk Foods if you like to stock the pantry with wholefoods and low‑waste ingredients. For nights when you’d rather not cook, Bulimba’s local eateries make weeknight dining a breeze: Thai Legend is a reliable takeaway for busy evenings, Luckies Kitchen delivers modern East Asian flavours, and family‑friendly pubs like Oxford 152 and Old Mr Rabbit offer simple, child‑friendly plates and takeaway options so everyone gets fed without drama.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If your child benefits from extra academic support, local options include in‑home and online tutoring services and a nearby Kumon centre for structured maths and English programs. Commuting to the Brisbane central business district doesn’t have to be a battle: Bulimba doesn’t have a passenger train station, but the Oxford Street ferry services provide a pleasant river crossing with typical travel times into the CBD around 20 to 30 minutes depending on the schedule. It’s worth doing a timed run at the hours you would travel to see how the rhythm fits your family’s mornings and evenings.

Your Next Steps

First, confirm the Outside School Hours Care hours, session fees and enrolment details through the national child‑care listing and the Catholic provider so you know how before‑ and after‑school care would actually work for your week. Second, book a school tour and come with specific questions about OSHC capacity and waitlists, music lesson availability, extension programs like Maths Olympiad and debating, and after‑school sports. Third, do a timed commute from your home during your planned travel times — check ferry timetables — and pop into Oxford Street to try a grocery run and a weeknight dinner so you can feel how Bulimba fits your daily life.
