Welcome to Windsor (inner‑north Brisbane)

If you like a neighbourhood that feels young, walkable and quietly energetic, Windsor has that down. The median age sits around 32, which you’ll notice in the mix of young singles, couples and families filling the cafés and weekend playgrounds. A strong professional workforce gives the suburb a white‑collar, weekday buzz, and while most residents were born in Australia (about 69–70% at the last detailed count), the area still feels welcoming to newcomers — the local rhythm and services make it easy to settle in.

Inside the Classroom at St Mary of the Cross School — Windsor (Brisbane)

If you’re looking at primary school life in Windsor, St Mary of the Cross has historically been a hub of active arts and specialist learning. The school was known for its instrumental program — violin, ukulele and keyboard featured regularly — and annual concerts that families loved. Specialist Spanish lessons, sport and broader arts activities were also part of the mix, giving kids a varied weekly timetable and plenty of chances to try something new. Important note: the school website carries a closure notice indicating the school closed on 5 December 2025. That means any enrolment, program availability or on‑site arrangements should be confirmed directly with the school or Brisbane Catholic Education before you make plans.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families juggling the school run and work, Windsor’s before‑and‑after school care scene has historically been covered by a nearby provider, Windsor School Age Care Centre, which offered on‑site before and after school programs as well as vacation care. With the school’s closure notice in place, you’ll want to confirm whether that same provider is still running collection or drop‑off services for the local community or whether you’ll need an alternative. Outside School Hours Care (that’s on‑site before and after school childcare) is a lifesaver for working parents; reach out early to understand pickup logistics and holiday program options so you’re not caught out on a busy workday.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Grocery runs and easy weeknight options are straightforward in Windsor. Mainstream shopping is centred around Newmarket Village, where Coles anchors the centre, and there’s a Woolworths at the Lutwyche City Shopping Centre a short drive away. If you cook Asian home meals or like specialty ingredients, the inner‑north’s Sunlit market options (Fortitude Valley and surrounds) are commonly used by locals. Dining out tends toward cafes and casual, family‑friendly spots: Outside In Cafe is a solid takeaway and child‑friendly option, Espresso House Windsor fits the neighbourhood grab‑and‑go vibe, and Kafe Krave is where brunches linger into the afternoon. Between the supermarkets and cafés, weeknight dinner rhythms are easy to manage — grab fresh produce at Newmarket, swing by a specialty for a specific ingredient, and rely on the cafés for a quick dinner rescue.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you’re thinking about extra academic support, there are local tutoring options right in Windsor: Head of the Class and Alchemy Tuition run programs and one‑on‑one support for school subjects and study skills. For commuting into the Brisbane CBD, Windsor station is a genuine convenience: it’s a single short train ride to Brisbane Central, typically in the order of five to ten minutes depending on the service pattern, which makes the suburb particularly attractive for parents who work in the city and want a short, predictable school‑run route. Driving times vary with traffic, so check local maps when you’re planning morning pickups.

Your Next Steps

First, contact Brisbane Catholic Education and check the St Mary of the Cross website to confirm the current school status and your enrolment options given the closure notice. Second, ring Windsor School Age Care Centre and a couple of alternative care providers to sort out before‑and‑after school care or vacation care arrangements now that the school’s future is changing. Third, reach out to the school parent contacts or listed program providers to verify whether the instrumental music, Spanish and arts offerings you’re interested in are still running or available elsewhere in the area.
