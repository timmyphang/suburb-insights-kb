# Welcome to Rochedale South

Rochedale South feels like the kind of suburb families choose because they want space, quiet streets and a neighbourhood that actually works for kids. The median age here is about 37, and it’s very much a family-focused place rather than a singles or inner‑city scene. Household incomes sit a notch above many nearby suburbs, which shows up in the tidy homes and well-kept parks. Around seven in ten people were born in Australia, with England the most common overseas birthplace after that, so if your family is new to the country you’ll find plenty of neighbours who’ve gone through the same settling-in process — that multicultural mix is a real comfort when you’re adjusting to a new life.

# Inside the Classroom at Springwood Road State School

Springwood Road State School teaches children from Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland), and the vibe is firmly about giving kids a rounded, active foundation. They run interschool sport for Years 4 to 6, so kids who love footy, athletics or team competition have pathways into district and regional sport. If your child likes music or performing, the school offers instrumental music and choir, plus professional dance lessons — not just one-off workshops but ongoing programs that feed into concerts and school carnivals. Swimming and athletics carnivals are regular calendar highlights, and the school’s emphasis on extracurricular pathways means coaches and teachers can point enthusiastic kids toward district-level opportunities as they grow.

# Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

The school has on-site Outside School Hours Care (this is on-site before and after school childcare) run by the parent group, so families who work full days can rely on structured care without a huge commute. Before school care opens from 7:00 to 8:25am, after school care runs from 2:30 to 6:00pm, and vacation care operates broadly from 7:00 to 6:00pm. Because the program is P&C‑managed, booking details and contact information are published on the school site; in practice that means you’ll want to lock in regular spots early in term or before school holidays to avoid disappointment.

# The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Groceries and quick dinners are straightforward in Rochedale South. For everyday supermarket runs the local Rochedale Shopping Village has a Drakes and a handful of family-friendly takeaways — pizza, Thai, sushi and kebab shops that make weeknights far less stressful. If you cook more internationally or want a broader range of Asian ingredients, there are larger Asian supermarkets a short drive away in Underwood and Sunnybank where you can find everything from fresh produce to specialty sauces. For coffee and a quick meet-up, little neighbourhood places like Gathering Spot Cafe give the suburb that friendly, local feel where baristas know your order and kids can grab a muffin after school.

# Tuition and the Daily Commute

If your child needs extra academic help, Rochedale South is served by a mix of private tutors and local tutoring marketplaces offering maths and English support. You’ll find private options for one-on-one lessons as well as listings connecting you with regional tutors — useful whether you want weekly help or exam-term boost sessions. Commuting to the Brisbane central business district is doable by bus and the nearby busway/park‑and‑ride; there isn’t a suburban train station in Rochedale South itself, so most people plan trips by bus. Typical travel time to the CBD is in the 45–50 minute range depending on traffic and which service you catch, so it’s a reasonable balance if you work in the city but prefer a family suburb feel at home.

# Your Next Steps

First, check the school website to confirm current enrolment zones and contact details before you apply or pop in for a visit. Second, if you’ll need before or after school care, book the on-site Outside School Hours Care early — it’s well used, and the P&C publishes the session times and booking information you’ll need. Third, arrange a school tour or open visit and ask specific questions about interschool sport, instrumental music and dance so you can see how the school’s activities match your child’s interests — and try a pick-up run at school times to trial your commute and parking.
