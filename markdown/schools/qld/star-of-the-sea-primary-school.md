# Welcome to Cleveland (QLD)

Cleveland feels like a bayside town that decided to slow down and keep smiling. The median age is about 51, and you’ll notice a comfortable mix of mature households and retirees alongside families who’ve chosen this quieter, green‑edged life. The foreshore parks and playgrounds around Raby Bay and Cleveland Point make it easy to imagine weekend picnics and scooter runs, and the public library sits right in the rhythm of community life. Most people living here were born in Australia—roughly two‑thirds of residents—with England the most common overseas birthplace, so newcomers often find a friendly, familiar vibe rather than a hard cultural leap. Household incomes sit around a middle‑income level, and the housing mix includes long‑term owner‑occupiers and some renters, which keeps the neighbourhood grounded and stable.

# Inside the Classroom at Star of the Sea Primary School

Star of the Sea Primary School in Cleveland teaches Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland). Inside the school you’ll find a curriculum that balances core classroom work with a lively range of specialist offerings. There’s a STEM Club and project work for kids who like tinkering, designing and experimenting, plus weekly music and performing‑arts sessions and a school choir that brings the community together at events. For students who need a little extra time with concepts, Homework Club and learning‑enhancement support are available. Language lessons include Auslan as a specialist language program; Auslan is the Australian Sign Language used by the Deaf community, and learning it here gives students practical communication skills as well as inclusivity experience.

# Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

If you need care beyond the school bell, the campus runs on‑site Outside School Hours Care (on‑site before and after school childcare) and vacation care through Catholic Early EdCare. That setup means morning drop‑offs and late shifts can be managed without a long drive to a separate childcare hub, and vacation programs keep the kids engaged when school isn’t in session. The on‑site nature of the service also makes it straightforward to coordinate emergency pickups, extracurricular commitments or a last‑minute change of plans.

# The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Grocery runs are pleasantly simple: Cleveland Central houses mainstream options like Coles and Woolworths plus an IGA, so pantry staples are easy to tick off. If you’re after more specialist ingredients, there are Asian groceries serving the wider Redlands area—options in nearby suburbs such as Capalaba and Victoria Point carry the sauces and spices you might miss from home. For nights when cooking feels optional, Viet’s Roll at Cleveland Central is the go‑to for banh mi and quick, flavourful rolls. Mamma Mia Trattoria Pizzeria at Raby Bay does family‑friendly wood‑fired pizzas and takeaway, while Samson Fish Seafood by the marina is the classic fish‑and‑chips stop with water views—perfect for a low‑fuss dinner after sport or tutoring.

# Tuition and the Daily Commute

For families looking for a little extra academic support, there are local tutoring options including a branch of Kip McGrath offering English and maths tuition and Cluey Learning’s blended online and face‑to‑face programs. Commuting for work is realistic for Brisbane central business district commuters: Cleveland is the terminus of the Cleveland train line and trips into Brisbane central business district usually run about 50 to 60 minutes depending on the service. That makes daily city work possible while keeping the benefits of a bayside lifestyle at home.

# Your Next Steps

First, ask the school for a one‑page printable enrolment summary you can tuck into your enrolment folder—handy for open days and quick comparisons. Second, request the school’s enrolment and contact webpage link and ask them to confirm the current Outside School Hours Care fees and operating hours so you know exactly what the sessions cost and when they run. Third, plan a quick visit: stroll the Raby Bay foreshore, pop into Cleveland Central to check grocery and takeaway options, and time a train so you can picture the real‑world commute.
