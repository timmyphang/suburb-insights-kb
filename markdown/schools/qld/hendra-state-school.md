# Welcome to Hendra

If you picture a leafy, inner-north Brisbane pocket where families outnumber flatmates and weekend bike rides are as normal as coffee on the corner, you’re imagining Hendra. It’s a relatively affluent, family-oriented suburb where many households are couples with children and the median weekly household income is notably high. Most residents were born in Australia, with England the most common overseas birthplace, so newcomers often find a comfortable mix of familiarity and gentle multiculturalism as they settle in.

# Inside the Classroom at Hendra State School

Hendra State School teaches from Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland) and feels like the kind of local school that becomes part of a family’s daily rhythm. The school has a strong extracurricular culture: instrumental music and lunchtime clubs like chess give kids creative outlets, while district sporting trials and specialist coaching introduce them to disciplines such as Taekwondo, Ultimate Frisbee and Australian Rules football. There’s a structured swimming program, student leadership pathways and tailored support for higher-achieving students, so whether your child is into music, sport or academic challenges, there’s usually something to catch their interest.

# Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For parents juggling work and school pickups, the campus hosts an on-site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) program run by Jabiru. After-school care operates on weekdays from around 2:30pm to 6:00pm and is based in “The Hive” on campus, which makes that 3pm scramble far less stressful. It’s a practical setup for families who need consistent, school-linked childcare without the extra commute—definitely worth a call if you’re planning to work full days or need occasional backup.

# The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Walkable food options mean dinner doesn’t have to be a production every night. Pinkies Groceries serves as the local supermarket/grocer for routine shopping, while nearby chain supermarkets and Asian specialty stores along Nudgee Road and in neighbouring suburbs make it easy to find fresh produce and familiar ingredients. For casual dining, Hendra Café and Nudgee Road Kitchen are family-friendly spots for breakfast and lunch, Asian Republic provides reliable Thai and Asian takeaway, and the local fish and chip shops are the kind of quick, beloved options that save evenings when homework and bath time collide.

# Tuition and the Daily Commute

If extra academic support looks likely, there are tutoring options close by. NumberWorks’nWords in Clayfield covers maths and English tuition, and CloudTuition connects families with online and local tutors who serve Hendra students. Commuting into the CBD is straightforward for most families: the Doomben line stops at Hendra station, and trains to Brisbane Central typically take around 15 to 20 minutes—many services are about 17–18 minutes—so city jobs and weekend trips are genuinely convenient from here.

# Your Next Steps

First, take a good look at the school website and the Outside School Hours Care page to confirm current enrolment details, opening hours and how bookings or waitlists work, and check Prep enrolment dates so you don’t miss key deadlines. Second, book a school tour or go to an open day so you can see The Hive, meet staff, and watch the extracurriculars in action—having that on-the-ground feel makes decisions much easier. Third, ring Jabiru to confirm before- and after-school care availability and, if you think your child will need extra academic help, reach out to NumberWorks’nWords or CloudTuition to discuss options.
