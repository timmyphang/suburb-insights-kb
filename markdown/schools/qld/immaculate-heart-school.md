Welcome to Leichhardt (City of Ipswich)

Leichhardt feels like one of those neighbourhoods that wears its heart on its sleeve: young, family‑centred and practical. The median age here is 31, and while household incomes tend to sit below the Queensland median, that only underscores the suburb’s strong community vibe — a mix of younger adults, a higher‑than‑average Indigenous presence, a noticeable share of one‑parent households and pockets of social housing. For families arriving from interstate or overseas, that mix often translates into an immediate sense of belonging: people from different backgrounds and life stages living side by side, looking out for each other.

Inside the Classroom at Immaculate Heart School

Immaculate Heart is a Prep to Year 6 school (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland) that leans into a small‑school, community atmosphere. The specialist program lineup adds real texture to the school day: your child can expect regular Physical Education for active lessons and sports, a choir for anyone who hums on the way to class, and Auslan — Australian Sign Language — as part of the communication and language offering. Those specialist subjects are helpful not just for enrichment but for building friendships across different year levels and interests.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

If your weekday rhythm needs before‑ and after‑school care, Immaculate Heart runs on‑site Outside School Hours Care through Catholic Early EdCare. They open early for before‑school care from 6:30 a.m. to 8:30 a.m., and after‑school care runs until 6:00 p.m., with vacation care available as well. Having on‑site care that ties directly into the school day makes logistics far simpler for working parents and helps kids stay in familiar surroundings while you finish the workday or commute.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Leichhardt’s food options are what you’d expect from a close‑knit suburban pocket: small grocery outlets like Leichhardt Four Square for pantry basics, and nearby suppliers such as Wilpak Wholesale Meats if you like to stock up on fresh protein. When two‑minute dinners are the order of the evening, there are family‑friendly takeaways along Toongarra Road — Leichhardt Take Away is a reliable go‑to — plus other fast‑food and Asian takeaway choices dotted along the Old Toowoomba/Toongarra Road strip. It’s the kind of place where weeknight meals are uncomplicated: groceries at the local corner store, and a hot takeaway when the calendar gets away from you.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If your child needs a bit of extra academic support, Ipswich hosts tuition options such as Kip McGrath and Fruition Tuition that cater to primary students. Commuting to Brisbane for work is realistic but needs planning: Leichhardt itself has no rail station, so most families make their way to Ipswich station or nearby East Ipswich or Wulkuraka to catch the train. Typical door‑to‑CBD travel time from Ipswich to Brisbane Central is roughly an hour, so allow about 55–60 minutes on the train and a little extra for getting to the station.

Your Next Steps

Step 1 — Lock in before/after care: ring Immaculate Heart’s on‑site Outside School Hours Care to confirm enrolment, hours and any vacancy for before‑school, after‑school or vacation care, especially if you’ll rely on it for workdays.  
Step 2 — Match the extras to your child: take a look at the school’s specialist programs — Physical Education, Choir and Auslan — and decide which ones you want your child to attend or be prioritised for.  
Step 3 — Plan the commute and support: check Ipswich train timetables for your likely journey times, and if extra tutoring looks helpful, contact local providers like Kip McGrath or Fruition to discuss options.
