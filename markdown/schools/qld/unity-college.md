# Welcome to Caloundra West

If you’re picturing a family-friendly pocket of the Sunshine Coast where playground chatter and weekend bike rides set the rhythm of life, Caloundra West fits that picture nicely. The median age sits around 37 and many households here include children, so there’s a very lived-in, family-focused feel to the streets. Household incomes trend a touch below the Queensland median, which helps explain why the suburb has a relaxed, practical atmosphere rather than anything flashy. Most residents were born in Australia, with England the largest identified overseas birthplace where people were born elsewhere; for parents arriving from overseas, that mix can be comforting — you’ll find neighbours and school families who’ve navigated moves and cultural adjustments before.

# Inside the Classroom at Unity College (Caloundra)

Unity College runs from Prep to Year 12, so you can realistically plan your child’s journey here from first year of primary through to the end of high school. If your child is joining the younger years, note that we say "Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland)" so you know exactly where they slot in. The college punches well above its weight in extracurricular culture. The instrumental music program spans all year levels, offering weekly tuition and an ensemble scene that reads like a mini conservatory: concert and stage bands, strings, guitar groups, choirs and even a rock band. Sport is equally broad: everything from rugby, netball and basketball to cricket, tennis, touch football and mountain biking, with genuine inter-school pathways if your child wants to play competitively. For families who value both arts and athletics, Unity offers options to keep kids meaningfully busy and socially connected.

# Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For working parents, the practical stuff matters more than ever. Unity College hosts on-site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) that opens early — around 6:15am — and runs after school until about 6:00pm, with vacation and pupil-free day care as well. That program is delivered through Catholic Early EdCare and is registered for the national Child Care Subsidy, which can make morning drop-offs and late finishes much easier to manage. With daily care available on campus, you can realistically keep work schedules intact without relying on long drives or juggling multiple pickup points.

# The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

When the kids are home and homework is on the table, you want food options that feel local and friendly. There’s an IGA serving Caloundra West for the essentials, a specialty/organic option at The Real Grocer not far away, and a local fruit and veg outlet called Youngs that’s handy for quick fresh produce runs. For nights when cooking feels like too much, Amici Restaurant Pizzeria delivers reliably to the area if you’re craving a simple pasta or pizza, while Beachview Seafoods at Bulcock Beach is a classic family-friendly fish-and-chips takeaway. Between the supermarket, the greengrocer and a couple of trusted takeaways, weeknights can be practical and low-stress.

# Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you’re thinking extra academic support would be useful, Caloundra has local choices such as Kip McGrath for English and maths tuition and an independent option in Cloud Tuition. Both are set up to assess learning needs and offer ongoing support if you’re worried about literacy or numeracy gaps. Commuting to Brisbane’s CBD is doable but requires planning: Caloundra West doesn’t have a rail station of its own, so most families factor in a drive or local bus trip to Landsborough station and then a train that’s typically in the order of about one hour and twenty minutes to Brisbane Central. That transfer time to and from Landsborough is important when you’re scheduling work hours or thinking about weekly city trips.

# Your Next Steps

1) Book a visit to Unity College, ask specifically about Prep to Year 6 routines and enrolment steps, and while you’re there mention music and sport so you can get the scoop on weekly lessons, ensembles and inter-school pathways.  
2) Lock in your child care logistics: confirm Outside School Hours Care enrolment, hours and Child Care Subsidy details with Catholic Early EdCare, and while you’re at it compare local tuition options like Kip McGrath or Cloud Tuition if you think after-school academic support might help.  
3) Map your weekday logistics — factor in roughly 1 hour 20 minutes on the train from Landsborough to Brisbane Central plus the trip to Landsborough station — and familiarise yourself with nearby grocery and takeaway options so weeknight routines feel smooth from day one.
