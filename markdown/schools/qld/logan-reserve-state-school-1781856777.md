Welcome to Logan Reserve

If you’re picturing a family-focused pocket on Brisbane’s southern fringe, Logan Reserve fits that vibe. The median age here is just 27, so expect a neighbourhood buzzing with young parents, kids on scooters and a fair bit of backyard laughter; many households are lower‑to‑middle income and the local workforce mixes hands‑on trades with office jobs, which keeps the place grounded and practical [citation:2021 Logan Reserve QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL31678) [citation:SEIFA by profile area | City of Logan](https://profile.id.com.au/logan/seifa-disadvantage). Most residents were born in Australia, with New Zealand the most common overseas birthplace in earlier counts, and the overall population profile leans young and family‑centred, so arriving with kids often means joining an immediate community of peers [citation:2016 Logan Reserve QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2016/SSC31693) [citation:2021 Logan Reserve QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/SAL31678).

Inside the Classroom at Logan Reserve State School

Logan Reserve State School is the local state primary, teaching from Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland); check with the school for the latest grade arrangements and enrolment details [citation:Logan Reserve State School (home)](https://loganreservess.eq.edu.au/). The school has a lively extracurricular life: musically minded kids can join the Band and Strings instrumental program from Year 3 and get involved with the school choir, while sporty families will find Gala Day representative sports across soccer, basketball, rugby league, netball, volleyball and OzTag, plus traditional athletics and cross‑country carnivals that fill the school calendar [citation:Music Activities — Logan Reserve State School](https://loganreservess.eq.edu.au/extra-curricular/music-activities) [citation:Sports — Logan Reserve State School](https://loganreservess.eq.edu.au/ExtraCurricular/Sports).

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

If you work standard hours, know that Outside School Hours Care (on‑site before and after school childcare) options operate alongside the school through local providers. Seedlings & Co. is one named provider and there are other after‑school care services listed for the area; the sensible next step is to contact the school office and the care providers directly about bookings, availability and sign‑in procedures because spaces can fill quickly [citation:Seedlings & Co. (StartingBlocks listing)](https://www.startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01JM7QEPG0P4Z84QMVYA0JFSJ2/Seedlings-and-Co-Logan-Reserve) [citation:OSHC listings (CareForKids)](https://www.careforkids.com.au/after-school-care/logan-reserve/4133) [citation:Logan Reserve State School (home)](https://loganreservess.eq.edu.au/). For families juggling shifts or hybrid work patterns, having that local care infrastructure is the difference between frazzled evenings and manageable routines.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

When it’s 6pm and everyone’s asking what’s for dinner, Logan Reserve Shopping Village—with Woolworths as the anchor—handles the weekly grocery run and the pantry staples with the kind of convenience you want when you’ve got tired kids and homework to shepherd [citation:Logan Reserve Shopping Village (Woolworths)](https://loganreserveshoppingvillage.com.au/) [citation:Woolworths - Logan Reserve](https://loganreserveshoppingvillage.com.au/shop/woolworths/). If your family cooks with Southeast Asian flavours or needs specialist produce, Mekong Asian Grocery in Waterford West and the Letsgo Asian Supermarket chain nearby are good calls for fresh herbs, noodles and the pantry items that turn a quick stir‑fry into something comforting and familiar [citation:Mekong Asian Grocery (Waterford West listing)](https://www.truelocal.com.au/qld/waterford-west/mekong-asian-grocery-13785265) [citation:Letsgo Asian Supermarket (chain listing)](https://www.letsgoretail.com/). And on those nights when everyone’s exhausted and no one wants to cook, Logan Reserve’s takeaway scene has the essentials: Domino’s for pizza, Lanny’s for fish and chips, and Happy Panda for a quick Chinese order—perfect for chaotic, little‑league practice evenings [citation:Domino’s Logan Reserve](https://www.dominos.com.au/store/qld-logan-reserve-97519) [citation:Lanny’s Fish & Chips Takeaway](https://lannysfishandchipstakeaway.yumbojumbo.com.au/) [citation:Happy Panda Chinese Take‑Away listing](https://australia.chamberofcommerce.com/business-directory/queensland/logan-reserve/asian-restaurant/3681390-happy-panda-chinese-take-away).

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If your child benefits from a bit of extra academic support or you want someone to help boost confidence before tests, Cloud Tuition and The Bright Tutors serve Logan Reserve with in‑home or matched private tutoring options that are easy to arrange and flexible around family schedules [citation:Cloud Tuition — Logan Reserve listing](https://www.cloudtuition.com.au/locations/city/logan-reserve) [citation:The Bright Tutors — Logan Reserve](https://www.thebrighttutors.com.au/locations/logan-reserve-3). For commuting to Brisbane’s CBD, Logan Reserve doesn’t have its own train station; the nearest suburban rail access is Loganlea on the Beenleigh line, and typical Loganlea to Roma Street commuter trains run about 38 to 50 minutes depending on the service—use TransLink timetables to plan reliable morning and afternoon trips [citation:Loganlea station — Queensland Rail](https://www.queenslandrail.com.au/Customers/Stations%20and%20Maps/Pages/AllStations.aspx?StationName=Loganlea) [citation:Beenleigh line timetable (TransLink)](https://jp.translink.com.au/plan-your-journey/timetables/train/T/beenleigh-line). If you need to travel between Brisbane and Sydney by train, be aware that the long‑distance XPT service means a journey on the order of 14 to 14.5 hours when you account for getting from Logan Reserve to Loganlea and the city connection—always check current timetables when planning extended trips [citation:NSW XPT overview (Transport NSW)](https://transportnsw.info/regional-travel/travelling-onboard/xpt-regional-trains) [citation:Sydney–Brisbane XPT time example (Transport NSW route)](https://transportnsw.info/destination-route/SYD/BNE) [citation:Loganlea to Roma Street estimate (FromTo)](https://fromto.travel/en/australia/loganlea-queensland/roma-street-station-queensland).

Your Next Steps

First, call Logan Reserve State School to confirm enrolment details, the school’s Prep‑to‑Year‑6 structure and how they handle Outside School Hours Care so you know where your child fits and how care can be arranged [citation:Logan Reserve State School (home)](https://loganreservess.eq.edu.au/). Second, if you’ll need before‑ or after‑school care, contact Seedlings & Co. and compare the other local providers listed on CareForKids and StartingBlocks—book early if you need a regular spot [citation:Seedlings & Co. (StartingBlocks listing)](https://www.startingblocks.gov.au/find-child-care/01JM7QEPG0P4Z84QMVYA0JFSJ2/Seedlings-and-Co-Logan-Reserve) [citation:OSHC listings (CareForKids)](https://www.careforkids.com.au/after-school-care/logan-reserve/4133). Third, if your child likes music or sport, ask about joining the Band & Strings program from Year 3, the choir and the Gala Day sports sign‑ups so you can plan uniforms, practice times and carnival dates early in the term [citation:Music Activities — Logan Reserve State School](https://loganreservess.eq.edu.au/extra-curricular/music-activities) [citation:Sports — Logan Reserve State School](https://loganreservess.eq.edu.au/ExtraCurricular/Sports).
