# Welcome to Shailer Park, QLD

If you’re picturing a family-friendly pocket on Brisbane’s outer south, you’re close: Shailer Park is that settled, middle‑class suburb where established households and weekend routines set the rhythm. The median age sits around 39 and household incomes are a touch above the Queensland average, which shows up in tidy streets, well‑kept yards and a comfortable local vibe. About two thirds of residents were born in Australia, and seeing a noticeable number of families from New Zealand and England makes this an easy place for newcomers — that mix of backgrounds is actually one of the quiet comforts when you’re settling into a new country: familiar accents at the school gate, multicultural food choices nearby, and a community used to people arriving from overseas.

# Inside the Classroom at Kimberley Park State School

Kimberley Park State School welcomes children from Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland), and the classroom culture leans into creative and active learning as much as the basics. Music is a big part of school life: weekly lessons are complemented by ensembles that include a string orchestra, concert band, choir and even a rock band, so kids who love noise and rhythm get plenty of outlets. There’s also a busy dance program with performance opportunities — think Dance Showcase, Wakakirri and the occasional school musical — and a Sport Excellence stream for children who thrive on competition and team training. If your child lights up for music, drama or sport, Kimberley Park builds room for that to grow alongside literacy and numeracy.

# Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families juggling work and school hours, Kimberley Park runs on‑site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) through Camp Australia, offering before‑school, after‑school and vacation care. That means you can plan a commute and still have supervised wraparound care without driving all over town. Do double‑check current enrolment, operating hours and fees on the school’s OSHC page or the Camp Australia listing before you rely on that service, since programs and capacities change from year to year.

# The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

When life gets busy, the Logan Hyperdome becomes the default dinner and shopping loop: the food court handles everyone from picky primary schoolers to tired parents, and there are family‑friendly takeaway options nearby like Greenery Cafe for Chinese and Thai dishes or Penang Palace if you crave Malaysian flavours. If you cook at home and want specific ingredients, the area has specialist and ethnic grocers — Hanaromart near the Hyperdome and Metro Asian Groceries in Loganholme are the obvious stops for pantry items that aren’t always in mainstream supermarkets. In short, whether you’re picking up a quick feed after choir practice or doing a proper weekend shop, the choices are convenient and varied.

# Tuition and the Daily Commute

If your child needs extra academic support, there are local tutoring options to explore: Cloud Tuition advertises maths tutors serving Shailer Park, and Alchemy runs tutoring services across the Logan area, alongside independent tutors and small centres advertising primary maths and literacy support. Commuting to Brisbane’s CBD is doable but usually involves a short bus trip to a nearby station. Shailer Park itself doesn’t have a train station, so families typically bus to Loganlea or the Hyperdome and then catch Beenleigh line services into Brisbane; the train ride from Loganlea to Brisbane Central is often around 40 minutes on faster services, though timetables vary. Factor that combined bus‑plus‑train time into early morning routines, and give yourself a trial run during peak hours before committing to a daily schedule.

# Your Next Steps

1) Check the Kimberley Park OSHC page and the Camp Australia listing to confirm before‑ and after‑school care hours, current fees and how to enrol.  
2) Book a school tour or attend a performance or Sport Excellence session so you can see the music, dance and sport programs in action.  
3) Map your address to local bus links and Loganlea train times, then try a week of your planned commute to see how the morning rhythm actually feels.
