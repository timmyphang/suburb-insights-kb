Welcome to Merrimac

If you’re picturing a family-friendly pocket of the Gold Coast with easy access to shops and schools, Merrimac fits that image neatly. It’s broadly middle-income and feels lived-in rather than flashy: the median household income sits around A$1,688 a week and the median age hovers near 38, so you’ll find plenty of families, tradespeople, retail and health-care workers and a steady community pace. Many locals were born in Australia, with New Zealand historically the largest overseas birthplace, which helps give the suburb a familiar, neighbourly vibe for Kiwis and Australians alike.

Inside the Classroom at All Saints Anglican School

All Saints Anglican School’s Junior School runs from Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland). Walk into the classrooms and you’ll notice a school that leans heavily on co-curricular life as part of its academic identity. Music starts young, with instrumental lessons, ensembles and singing opportunities introduced from the earliest years. Sport is part of the weekly rhythm too: inter-house swimming, athletics and cross-country meet the team sports and fitness programs that keep kids active. The junior years also feature dance, drama, chess and specialist art and technology sessions, so children get a broad set of ways to discover what they enjoy and where they shine.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families juggling work, the school hosts on-site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) through a partnered accredited provider. Typical before-school care runs from about 6:30am to 8:15am, after-school care from about 3:00pm to 6:00pm, and vacation care generally stretches roughly 7:00am to 6:00pm during school breaks. That continuity—before school, after school and holiday coverage—makes the day-to-day logistics of two working parents or shift workers much simpler than piecing together ad-hoc care.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Merrimac’s grocery landscape supports everyday family life: there’s a nearby Coles for staples, Woolworths over in Robina for a larger supermarket run, and local IGA options if you want something quick. If your kitchen experiments lean toward Asian flavours, Asian Emporium is within reasonable driving distance for specialty ingredients. When you’re not cooking, the suburb’s casual dining hits the right notes for weeknights: Merrimac Tavern is a reliable family-friendly bistro, Counter Eatery is a solid local café and takeaway option, and a scattering of cafes and small takeaways across Merrimac and neighbouring Robina fill in the rest.

Tuition and the daily commute to Brisbane

If you’re thinking about extra academic support, there are familiar options close by: Kumon in Robina for structured maths and English, plus private tutors and small local tutoring centres for one-to-one or small-group coaching. For work commutes into the Brisbane central business district (Brisbane Central), note that Merrimac does not yet have a functioning local train station, although a new Merrimac station is planned as part of rail upgrades. In the meantime most commuters use Robina station; allow roughly 75 to 90 minutes from Robina to Brisbane Central depending on the service you catch. Mapping a realistic door-to-platform routine—drive, park and train—before you commit will save surprises on a Monday morning.

Your Next Steps

First, book a Junior School tour at All Saints Anglican so you can see the Prep to Year 6 classrooms and watch music and sport in action. Second, ring the on-site Outside School Hours Care provider to confirm how before- and after-school bookings and vacation care work, including any enrolment steps and costs. Third, try the commute you’d use on a weekday—drive to Robina station and check the train timetable—so you know how the timing fits your family’s routine.
