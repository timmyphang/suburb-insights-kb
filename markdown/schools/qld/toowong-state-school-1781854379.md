Welcome to Toowong (Brisbane, QLD)

If you’re moving into Toowong, expect the kind of riverside, inner‑city vibe that reads less like a suburb and more like a compact, professional neighbourhood with a young pulse. The median age here is 30, noticeably younger than the Queensland average of 38, and you’ll see a high share of never‑married residents, students, young professionals and small families living in apartments a short hop from the Brisbane CBD — so think morning coffee queues, an easy commute, and plenty of single‑household energy in the streetscape [citation:2021 Toowong, Census All persons QuickStats](https://www.abs.gov.au/census/find-census-data/quickstats/2021/305041137). Living here feels convenient: local retail and eateries cluster around Toowong Village, making those missing‑the‑grocer moments mercifully short [citation:Stores - Toowong Village](https://www.toowongvillage.com.au/stores).

Inside the Classroom at Toowong State School

Toowong State School brings a lively primary program with clear opportunities for kids to plug into music and sport. The school runs a strong Instrumental Music Program and a school band, alongside junior and senior choirs — one of which is an Auslan signing choir for kids learning Australian Sign Language — so children who love performing have real pathways to belong [citation:Extra-Curricular](https://toowongss.eq.edu.au/extra-curricular). On the sporting side, older primary students play interschool sport in Years 5 and 6, covering basketball, soccer and netball, which is a tried‑and‑true way for newcomers to make friends on the field and off [citation:Sports - Toowong State School](https://toowongss.eq.edu.au/ExtraCurricular/Sports). The school’s out‑of‑school hours care program, Toowong Out of School Hours Care, is supported by the local Parents and Citizens association and runs before‑school care from about 6:45am, after‑school care until about 6:00pm, plus vacation‑care and pupil‑free day programs, so families juggling work schedules have robust on‑site childcare options to explore [citation:Toowong Out of School Hours Care (TOSHC) - Toowong State School](https://toowongss.eq.edu.au/facilities/out-of-school-hours-care) [citation:Toowong Outside School Hours Care - childcaredb.com.au](https://www.childcaredb.com.au/service/532/toowong_outside_school_hours_care).

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

If your afternoons are packed, Toowong State School’s on‑site Outside School Hours Care (often shortened to “before and after school care”) is a real lifeline — it kicks off early in the morning, keeps kids until about 6pm, and fills the usual gaps with vacation and pupil‑free day programs so you don’t have to scramble when your workday runs long [citation:Toowong Out of School Hours Care (TOSHC) - Toowong State School](https://toowongss.eq.edu.au/facilities/out-of-school-hours-care) [citation:Toowong Outside School Hours Care - childcaredb.com.au](https://www.childcaredb.com.au/service/532/toowong_outside_school_hours_care). Signing your child up for the school band, choir (including the Auslan choir) or the interschool sports teams is also one of the fastest ways to build a weekday routine that blends supervised extracurricular learning with social connection, so those afternoons feel settled rather than rushed [citation:Extra-Curricular](https://toowongss.eq.edu.au/extra-curricular) [citation:Sports - Toowong State School](https://toowongss.eq.edu.au/ExtraCurricular/Sports).

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

When you need dinner on the run, Toowong Village is where you’ll end up most evenings. The food court’s Lucky Chopsticks is a reliable spot for Chinese takeaway or a casual dine‑in, while Thai Origin and A Night in India offer family‑friendly Thai and Indian options that work well on busy weeknights [citation:Lucky Chopsticks | Toowong Village](https://www.toowongvillage.com.au/stores/lucky-chopsticks?location=G67A) [citation:Thai Origin](https://thaioriginbrisbane.com.au/) [citation:ORDER - Night In India](https://www.anightinindia.com.au/order/). Commutes to the CBD are seriously short: trains from Toowong station into Brisbane’s central station are typically around seven to nine minutes, with frequent direct services — just remember to add the walk to the station and a bit of platform time into your morning schedule so you’re not sprinting with a lunchbox in hand [citation:Toowong to Brisbane Central Station Train Times (Train O'Clock)](https://www.trainoclock.com/en-AU/traintimes/toowong-brisbanecentralstation) [citation:Toowong to Brisbane CBD - Rome2rio summary](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Toowong/Brisbane-CBD).
