Welcome to Arundel

If you're picturing a family suburb where neighbours know each other by name and the weekly routine still centres around school pick-ups and sports practice, Arundel fits that image neatly. It sits firmly in the middle-income bracket with a median age of 41 and a median household income that reflects steady, comfortable family life. Married couples and family households are more common here than in the wider state, and you’ll meet plenty of people whose roots start overseas — New Zealand is the most common birthplace after Australia — which is reassuring if you’re new to the country. That mix of established family rhythms and a welcoming international presence makes settling in feel straightforward: playground conversations, weekend markets and school routines help new arrivals find their feet quickly.

Inside the Classroom at A.B. Paterson College

A.B. Paterson College focuses on Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland), and it’s the kind of place that keeps young learners busy beyond the core subjects. The co-curricular program is lively and broad — think instrumental music lessons that feed into school bands and ensembles, choirs and dance, theatre and musicals, alongside an expansive team-sport program that includes soccer, netball, basketball, cricket, Australian rules football and touch. If your child loves performing or needs an outlet for energetic afternoons, the College has structured options to channel that energy into teamwork and confidence-building. The school culture leans into both creative and sporting pathways, so there’s usually something for every kid to latch onto.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families juggling work and school hours, the on-site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) — Banjo & Friends — is a real practical win. They run before-school care from 7:00 to 8:15 am and after-school care from 3:15 to 6:00 pm, plus vacation care with extended hours for early-childhood services. The program includes homework stations and structured activities designed specifically with working parents in mind, so you don’t have to worry about the afternoon shuffle between practices and meetings. Having that consistent, school-based childcare makes juggling full-time work and family life much easier, especially during school holidays.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Weeknight dinners in Arundel are flexible and family-focused. For quick pickups after sport or rehearsals, Arundel Plaza has reliable takeaway options like Cool Sushi, Arundel Pizza and Arundel Chinese, and the Arundel Tavern is the familiar family-friendly pub for a casual meal where kids can burn off energy. If you cook at home and like to source specialty ingredients, there’s Exotic Asian Grocery in the Harbour Town/Brisbane Road area, and bigger Asian supermarket options at Helensvale such as Hanaro Mart. These choices mean you can keep dinners simple or experiment with broader flavours without a long drive, and the nearby larger shopping centres around Harbour Town and Westfield Helensvale expand your weeknight cuisine options when you want them.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you’re thinking about extra academic support, there are established tutoring providers showing up in the local listings — everything from 1:1 tuition with Elite Tutoring & Mentoring to regional services like ATK Tutoring and Tutor2You for subject help and homework support. They’re handy if you want personalised attention or a confidence boost outside school hours. For commuters, Arundel doesn’t have its own Queensland Rail station; the nearest major interchange is Helensvale station, which connects train, tram and bus services. Typical train travel from Helensvale to Brisbane Central is roughly 60 to 75 minutes depending on the service, so if you’ll be commuting into the city regularly, plan your daily routine around those timetables and allow a bit of buffer time.

Your Next Steps

First, book a school tour or open day at A.B. Paterson College to see the Prep classrooms, meet staff and check entry requirements and fees so you know exactly what’s involved. Second, confirm Banjo & Friends Outside School Hours Care places and hours to match your work schedule and holiday needs — it’s worth locking that in early. Third, if music or sport matters to your family, ask about instrumental lesson availability, ensembles and junior sports pathways during your visit so you can map out after-school commitments from day one.
