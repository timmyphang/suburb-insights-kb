Welcome to Camp Hill (inner‑east suburb of Brisbane)

Camp Hill feels like the kind of inner‑east neighbourhood where leafy streets, village shops and professional families come together without the fuss. Household incomes here are well above many Queensland suburbs, and you’ll quickly notice a stronger share of managers and professionals in the workforce, which shapes local services, after‑school offerings and the feel of weekend markets. Most residents were born in Australia, with England and New Zealand the most common overseas birthplaces among those who weren’t, so if you’re relocating from abroad you’ll find a comfortable mix of local familiarity and a few expat faces to lean on.

Inside the Classroom at St Thomas’ School

St Thomas’ is a Catholic primary that teaches Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland). The school’s day is complemented by a lively menu of extracurricular activities: an instrumental music program run in partnership with a local music provider, private piano lessons, regular school sport, and visiting after‑school coaches for things like tennis. There are also options like chess coaching and dance, so children who want to pick up a hobby or keep up an existing skill can do so without crossing town. For families who want structure and a sense of community, that combination of classroom stability and on‑site activities makes it easy to find your child’s niche.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

If you need before‑ or after‑school care, St Thomas’ runs an on‑site Outside School Hours Care (on‑site before and after school childcare) service through Catholic Early EdCare, including vacation care. That means you can drop into school in the morning and be confident there’s a supervised program until you finish work, and the vacation care option keeps routines intact during school breaks. Before enrolling, it’s worth checking current session times, booking procedures and fees with the provider so you can sync your work schedule and childcare bookings without surprises.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Camp Hill Marketplace is the local hub for grocery runs, anchored by a large supermarket and surrounded by specialists for fresh produce and pantry treats — think a dedicated fruit bowl, boutique provisions stores and a small selection of gourmet outlets that make weeknight cooking a pleasure rather than a chore. When you don’t feel like unpacking bags, the food choices are refreshingly diverse: a Japanese spot that does karaage, katsu and poke for fast, flavourful dinners; a Korean‑style fried chicken takeaway when you need something crowd‑pleasing; and a Vietnamese place dishing up pho and rice bowls for colder or quicker nights. It’s the kind of neighbourhood where you can swing by for fresh ingredients or grab a reliable takeaway on the way home.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you think extra academic support will be useful, there are local tutoring options that cover early years through Year 12, including in‑home and online tutors as well as local tutoring centres and broker services to match you with the right tutor. As for getting into the Brisbane central business district, Camp Hill doesn’t have an active train station; historically there was a tram and station, but today most families commute by bus to the CBD or hop to the nearest train stations a short drive or bus ride away. Typical rail travel from the nearest station to Brisbane Central is around 15–20 minutes, and direct bus trips from Camp Hill to the city commonly take roughly 20–30 minutes depending on route and traffic — a realistic commute for many working parents.

Your Next Steps

1) Book a school tour to meet teachers and see the classrooms so you can feel the vibe and ask about extracurricular sign‑ups.  
2) Confirm Outside School Hours Care hours, booking rules and current session fees with the Catholic Early EdCare provider before finalising enrolment.  
3) Try the commute at the times you’ll travel and visit the local marketplace to make sure the grocery and dinner options suit your family rhythm.
