# Welcome to Holland Park

Holland Park feels like that neighborhood you hope to find when you’re moving with kids: inner-south Brisbane charm, tree-lined streets and a clear family focus. The median age sits around 37, and the suburb skews toward middle-to-upper income, white‑collar households with plenty of children and middle‑aged adults. Most residents were born in Australia, with New Zealand the next most common birthplace, which helps Holland Park keep a familiar, settled vibe for families arriving from overseas. If you’re after a place that’s close to the city but still unmistakably residential, this is the sort of pocket that balances convenience with calm.

# Inside the Classroom at Seville Road State School

Seville Road State School is the local go-to for early years, offering Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland). The school leans into a broad, creative curriculum: performing arts are a visible strength with choir, strings and brass programs and school participation in Creative Generation; there’s a healthy STEM and robotics club for kids who like building and coding; and art and sport options cover everything from cricket and soccer to frisbee and swimming. That mix means your child can explore music or robotics without sacrificing weekend sport, and there’s a real sense that the school supports both creativity and academic curiosity.

# Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

If your household needs supervised care around work hours, Seville Road has an on-site Outside School Hours Care provider called After The Bell. They run before-school, after-school and vacation care programs right at the school, so drop-off and pick-up logistics are straightforward and you don’t need to juggle multiple locations. It’s worth contacting After The Bell early to check availability, hours and enrolment procedures, particularly if you plan to rely on before-school care on a regular basis.

# The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

For groceries and quick essentials there’s a neighbourhood feel: Wilbur’s General Store pulls double duty as a local café and grab-and-go spot, handy for those rushed school mornings. If you’re after specific ingredients or Asian pantry items, Sunlit Asian Supermarket services the inner-south Brisbane area and will be useful for dinner projects that need specialty sauces or noodles. When you’d rather leave the cooking to someone else, Holland Park’s weeknight dining options are reliably comforting: Wooden Spoon Thai Kitchen has solid homestyle Thai, Gnocchi Gnocchi Brothers leans hard into Italian comfort with a gnocchi-first menu, and Noodle City is the quick noodle takeaway favourite for kids and adults who want something warm and fast.

# Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you think your child might benefit from extra academic support, there are local tutoring options that cover curriculum-aligned maths and other subjects. Cloud Tuition provides structured maths help for primary and secondary students in the area, and Ezy Math and local private tutors offer broader listings if you want one-on-one support. Commuting to the Brisbane central business district from Holland Park is mostly a bus-based affair since there’s no Queensland Rail heavy-rail station in the immediate suburb. The South East Busway, including Holland Park West stops, connects you to the city and typical travel time into the CBD is around 20 to 35 minutes depending on the route and time of day. If you’re planning a routine commute, it’s a good idea to trial the bus trip at peak times so you can plan drop-off and pick-up windows around school and work schedules.

# Your Next Steps

First, ring the school or check the Department of Education site to confirm you’re in the enrolment zone, find current term dates and get the uniform rules so you don’t miss a detail when you apply. Second, contact After The Bell to lock in before‑school, after‑school or vacation care places and then arrange to tour the school or pop along to an open day so you can see the performing arts spaces, robotics gear and sports facilities in person. Third, trial the peak-time bus commute between Holland Park West and the CBD, and if academic support looks useful, reach out to Cloud Tuition or local tutors to arrange an assessment.
