Welcome to Haberfield

Haberfield feels like a well‑worn linen shirt turned crisp for the weekend: established, leafy and quietly affluent. It’s one of Sydney’s inner‑west suburbs where tree‑lined streets and confident period homes meet a strong village vibe on Ramsay Street. The neighbourhood skews a little older on average and household incomes tend to sit above many other Sydney suburbs, which shows up in higher housing prices and a large proportion of owner‑occupied homes. Couples with children are common, but you’ll also notice a comfortable presence of older residents, and about seven in ten people were born in Australia. The suburb’s Italian heritage is part of its personality — you can feel it in the delis, the Saturday food runs and the way local life orbits the corner cafes and groceries.

Inside the Classroom at Dobroyd Point Public School

Dobroyd Point Public School, often called Haberfield K–6, teaches kindergarten through Year 6 and has a familiar small‑school energy. The school runs a lively co‑curricular program so your child can try representative school sport, sing in choir, pick up a ukulele or join drama, and even dive into chess, coding or the gardening club. For families who value a well‑rounded primary experience — sport, music and STEM — the school offers visible opportunities to explore those interests while maintaining a neighbourhood, community feel. If you’re looking for a school where kids can move from classroom to club without long commutes, this is the kind of setup that works.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For working families who need care beyond the school day, Dobroyd Point has an on‑site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) service. Dobroyd Point OSHC operates before school, after school and during vacation periods through a registered provider based at the school, and it’s approved for the government Child Care Subsidy where eligible. Having that childcare physically on campus makes afternoons and school drop‑offs feel far more manageable — you don’t have to race between locations to collect the kids, and the familiarity of being on the school grounds helps younger children settle.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Ramsay Street is where Haberfield shows its Italian heart. Lamonica’s IGA and Zanetti’s deli anchor the local grocery scene with large Italian‑style supermarket choices, fresh breads, specialty cheeses and a steady parade of pasta essentials. When weeknight life gets busy, you won’t be stuck for easy, family‑friendly options: pizza and takeaway from La Disfida, a relaxed cafe meal at Happyfield, or one of the many small takeaway shops along Ramsay Street will get dinner on the table without stress. The practical benefit here is that everyday shopping and dinner runs fit into the rhythm of school pickup rather than becoming an extra chore.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you’re thinking about extra academic support, Haberfield has convenient local options. Primary tutoring in English and maths is available through local providers like Success Tutoring, and after‑school coding and STEM programs such as Code Camp run EOI (expressions of interest) options at nearby Dobroyd venues. Commuting to the Sydney CBD is straightforward: Haberfield station connects you to frequent inner‑west line services and the trip into Central takes roughly 13 to 16 minutes by train, so parents working in the city can genuinely consider a sensible daily commute without eating too much family time.

Your Next Steps

First, double‑check the current enrolment zone and catchment details on the school website or with the local education office so you understand your eligibility. Next, call the onsite Outside School Hours Care provider to confirm their current operating hours, fees and whether you qualify for the government Child Care Subsidy, especially for before/after school and vacation care. Finally, take a school tour or attend an open day to see the co‑curricular offerings in action, then stroll Ramsay Street to test groceries, takeaways and train times from your address so you know how the daily routine will feel.
