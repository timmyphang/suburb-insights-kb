Welcome to Stokers Siding

If you’re picturing a tightly knit country pocket with a relaxed pace, you’ve found it: Stokers Siding is a small rural village where the median age sits higher than in an inner‑city suburb and the population is under 700. Long‑term residents and retirees shape much of the local character, so newcomers can expect a quieter semi‑rural rhythm and a friendly, familiar community feel. Household incomes hover around the middle range for the region, which matches the easygoing, down‑to‑earth lifestyle here — think local produce, a slower commute, and neighbours who know each other by name.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

For everyday groceries, Stokers Siding relies on nearby Murwillumbah for full‑service supermarkets like the Tweed Valley IGA, while the village and surrounding properties make the most of local produce outlets and greengrocers for fresh, seasonal fruit and veggies. For a wider selection of cafés and casual options, Murwillumbah’s platform and station precincts along the Northern Rivers Rail Trail give you a pleasant short drive and a choice of weekend brunches or after‑school treats.
