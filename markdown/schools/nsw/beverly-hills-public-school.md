Welcome to Beverly Hills (New South Wales)

Beverly Hills feels like the kind of established, family-minded suburb that keeps a soft, reliable rhythm. With a median weekly household income slightly above the state average and a median age of about 40, it’s a middle-income neighbourhood where married couples and families make up a large share of the population. The streets have mature trees, local shops that have been serving generations, and enough multicultural flavour in the shops and services to make new arrivals feel comfortable quickly — if you’ve come from overseas, finding familiar food, languages and faces here will help you settle in.

Inside the Classroom at Beverly Hills Public School

Beverly Hills Public School has a lively performing-arts focus: think choir, band, dance and an instrumental training band that gives kids real chances to shine on stage. The school also runs organised sports including athletics and inter-school competitions, so there are outlets for both the theatrically inclined and the sporty child. If you want precise grade listings and enrolment details, the school asks parents to confirm directly — the site and school office are the places to request the handbook and any specific entry requirements.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

Working families will be pleased to know there’s on-site outside school hours care — that’s before- and after-school childcare, plus vacation programs — run by TeamKids. Having the childcare run on the school grounds removes a lot of pick-up logistics and keeps afternoons straightforward: children go from classroom to supervised care without a separate commute. It’s worth calling TeamKids to check current operating hours, fees and how bookings are handled so you can plan your workday and holiday care with confidence.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Groceries and takeaways in Beverly Hills mirror the suburb’s cultural mix. A handy IGA sits on Tooronga Terrace for everyday essentials, while several Asian and specialty grocers make it easy to cook the flavours you miss from home. On weeknights when everyone needs dinner fast, you have good local options: a Turkish pide and kebab house that’s excellent for sharing, a family-friendly Pizza Hut for an easy win, and Chinese takeaway favourites that the kids will recognise. Those familiar shopfronts and food choices make settling in feel more like home cooking than a culinary adventure every night.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you’re thinking about extra academic support, there are local tutoring options from small independent tutors to organised centres like MPower Tuition and Helix Tutoring, which can be handy for anything from homework help to exam prep. For city commuters, Beverly Hills has a suburban rail station and the train journey into Sydney Central is commonly about 28 minutes as a practical guide. That makes this suburb a sensible balance of quieter family life and an achievable commute to the CBD.

Your Next Steps

1) Call or email Beverly Hills Public School to confirm the grades they offer, ask about the enrolment process and request the school handbook so you’ve got the official timeline and requirements.

2) Ring TeamKids to check current availability, hours, fees and how to enrol or book before- and after-school care and vacation programs so your childcare is locked in.

3) Ask the school about performing-arts and sports enrolment — choir, band, dance and inter-school sport have places to join, and a quick conversation with the school will show you how kids sign up.
