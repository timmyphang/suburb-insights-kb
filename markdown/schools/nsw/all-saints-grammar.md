Welcome to Belmore

If you’re picturing a lively, inner‑south‑west Sydney suburb where family life hums against a backdrop of Mediterranean bakeries and community sport, you’re thinking of Belmore. It’s a mixed, middle‑income pocket with a median age around 38 and a typical household income that reflects steady, working families. The workforce is a blend of white‑ and blue‑collar roles — clerical and professional jobs alongside trades, labour and manufacturing — and the neighbourhood’s cultural mix is part of what makes settling in easier: roughly 42% Australia‑born with one of the largest overseas communities tracing heritage to Greece. That multilingual, multicultural fabric is a real comfort for families moving from overseas; you’ll find familiar foods, neighbourhood faces, and community rhythms to lean on while you settle.

Inside the Classroom at All Saints Grammar

All Saints Grammar’s primary campus runs Kindergarten to Year 6 (note: in Australia, Kindergarten is the first year of primary school). The campus leans into a broad extra‑curricular program that gives kids room to discover their thing — Band, Choir and Chess are steady favourites, and the school also runs Coding, Dance, Debating and Robotics among other activities. For parents who want a bit of arts, logic or tech woven into the school day, that variety helps children explore what clicks for them without having to chase external clubs every afternoon.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

If your week looks like school drop‑offs and a race to get to work, All Saints has a practical setup you’ll want to know about. The school offers on‑site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) through a partner provider, Extend. Extend manages bookings, fees and holiday‑program hours, so families who need reliable before‑ and after‑school care can book directly through that service rather than juggling separate providers. It’s a tidy solution for working parents who need a consistent handoff at the end of the day.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Belmore is a treat for anyone who loves family meals that don’t require a formal reservation. The local food scene tilts Mediterranean and Middle Eastern, so your shop list will quickly include village‑style olives, fresh pita and a go‑to souvlaki spot. Sal’s IGA and the nearby Greek deli and souvlaki shops are local staples for quick ingredients, while family pizza and takeaway on Burwood Road make weeknight dinners simple — think grab‑and‑go pizza or a kebab and pide when the school day runs long. Long‑running Greek cafes and family souvlaki shops mean you can keep dinners familiar for kids missing flavours from home.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you’re thinking about extra academic support, Belmore has local options. There are maths‑focused tutors and broader K–12 centres nearby that offer both face‑to‑face and online tutoring for maths, English and the sciences. For families balancing work and study goals, those local tutors can be a gentle, short commute away. Speaking of commuting, Belmore sits on the Bankstown line with a typical train trip to Sydney’s Central Station of roughly 20–25 minutes depending on the service and any waiting or transfers, which keeps city access surprisingly convenient for a suburb with such a village feel.
