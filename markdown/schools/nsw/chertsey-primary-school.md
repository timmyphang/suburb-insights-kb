# Welcome to Springfield (Central Coast - SAL13639), NSW 2250

There’s a quietly confident hum to life in Springfield. Tucked into the Central Coast near Gosford, it’s the kind of low‑density village that still feels like a neighbourhood where kids ride scooters to the shops and families wave across the street. The median age sits around 37 and household sizes are what you’d expect in a family suburb — comfortable, middle‑income households rather than either very affluent or industrially focused. Population is small and local, which means you get the calm of a village without being stranded from amenities in nearby Gosford or Erina.

# Inside the Classroom at Chertsey Primary School

Chertsey Primary School teaches Kindergarten to Year 6 and reads like a classic community school with modern heartbeat. The classroom culture leans into active participation: school sport across athletics, swimming and team events, inter‑school competitions, and a strong creative edge through music, drama and concerts. Students are encouraged to find their voice through debating and public speaking and to take on responsibilities via leadership programs. Lunchtime clubs and cultural mentoring—like the Dhinewan program—add softer, meaningful rhythms to the school day, so learning happens both in lessons and in belonging.

# Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

If your family needs reliable care outside the school day, Chertsey has on‑site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) run by Coastwide OOSH. They provide before‑school, after‑school and vacation care sessions that are eligible for childcare subsidy where applicable. The presence of a registered provider listed on national childcare registers means you can arrange care without daily commutes, which is a practical relief on workdays or during school holidays.

# The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

For weekly groceries, Springfield’s hub is FoodWorks in the local Springfield Shopping Centre. It’s convenient for everyday shopping and deli needs, and there’s a neighbouring café for takeaway coffee and quick meals when you need to get dinner on the table fast. If you’re hunting for specialist or ethnic ingredients, or want a wider range of family dining options, a short drive into Gosford or Erina opens up more choices. In practice that means most weekday dinners here are a blend of easy supermarket finds and occasional drives for something new.

# Tuition and the Daily Commute

If academic extra support is on your radar, there are local private tutors and tutor‑matching services that visit or operate around Springfield, for example small maths tutors, and broader learning centres tend to be based in Gosford or delivered online. For work commutes to Sydney, Springfield doesn’t have its own intercity rail station; the nearest major hub is Gosford. When planning, allow roughly an hour and twenty minutes on intercity trains from the Central Coast into Sydney’s Central station, keeping in mind service variations.

# Your Next Steps

First, check Chertsey Primary School’s website for enrolment details, contact information and school policies so you can get a sense of intake and routines. Second, register or enquire with Coastwide OOSH about session times for before, after and vacation care and confirm childcare subsidy eligibility if relevant. Third, start with FoodWorks Springfield for your weekly groceries and use the local café for quick meals while planning drives into Gosford or Erina when you need specialty shops, dining variety or formal tuition centres.
