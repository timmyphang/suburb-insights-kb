Welcome to Seven Hills, NSW

Seven Hills sits like a quietly confident family suburb on Sydney’s western fringe. The median age here is 37 and household budgets tend to be comfortable—median weekly household income was around $1,892 at the last census—so you’ll find streets of young families and working parents rather than retirement enclaves. Couples with children are the most common household type, and the local workforce is mixed rather than skewing only blue-collar or white-collar. One of the things that makes settling in easier is the strong multicultural thread through the neighbourhood: a significant number of residents were born overseas, with India the most common overseas birthplace at about 12.2 percent. For families moving from abroad, that cultural diversity is a real comfort—shops, community connections and school friendships form faster when others in the suburb share language, food and family experiences.

Inside the Classroom at Bert Oldfield Public School

Bert Oldfield Public School teaches from Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland), and it projects the friendly, activity-rich vibe you want for early school years. The school runs a full menu of extracurriculars: school sport and inter-school athletics, creative and performing arts with music and drama and regular concerts, plus public speaking and debating opportunities and lunchtime interest clubs that help kids find their tribe. Those programs help children build confidence well beyond the classroom and give parents a steady stream of after-school performances and events to enjoy.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

If you need before- and after-school care, Bert Oldfield has an on-site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) program run by Inspire. Their schedules typically include a before-school window from about 7:00 until school starts and after-school care that usually runs until around 6:00 p.m., with vacation care options during school holidays. For dual-working families or parents with shifting shifts, that structure is invaluable: drop-off, pick-up and holiday coverage are all handled in one familiar place, which takes a lot of logistics stress off your plate.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Shopping and quick dinners around here are pleasantly straightforward. Big supermarkets like Woolworths and ALDI sit in Seven Hills Plaza for your weekly shop, and the plaza also houses smaller specialty stores so you can pick up South Asian spices or Sri Lankan staples without a long drive—Kate Foods and several Indian and Asian grocers make that easy. When tonight needs to be quick, Akira Sushi in the food court does sushi bentos and grab-and-go boxes, Thai Spice handles takeaway cravings, and Brothers Fish Market or the local fish-and-chips spots in the plaza are the kind of reliable weeknight wins kids usually love.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you’re thinking about extra academic support, there are established local options offering tutoring and holiday programs. Master Tutor covers tutoring from primary right through to Year 12 and also works with preparation for high‑stakes tests, while Success Tutoring focuses on personalised English and maths support and dedicated holiday programs. For parents commuting into the city, Seven Hills station connects to Sydney Central on the T1 Western Line in roughly 35 to 40 minutes depending on the specific service and time of day—an entirely doable daily commute if you’re balancing work in the CBD with school drop-off routines.

Your Next Steps

First, book a school tour or an information session and have a look through the school’s extracurriculars page so you’re clear on the clubs and programs your child might want. Second, call or email Inspire and the school to confirm exact Outside School Hours Care hours, availability for before/after programs and the enrolment steps for vacation care. Third, factor the roughly 35–40 minute train commute into your weekday planning and, if you want academic support, reach out to Master Tutor or Success Tutoring to discuss tailored programs.
