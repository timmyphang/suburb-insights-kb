Welcome to Kenthurst

If you’re picturing leafy streets, large family homes and a village main street where everyone seems to know each other, you’re imagining Kenthurst pretty accurately. This outer-northwest Sydney pocket leans affluent and family-first: the median household income sits well above the metro average and the community skews a little older, with lots of married couples and white‑collar professionals choosing this quieter lifestyle while still keeping access to the city when they need it. The village feel is real — local shops, a post office, a butcher and cafes form the social hub, and you’ll find fresh produce at Kenthurst Village Fruit & Veg and everyday groceries at the local FoodWorks.

Inside the Classroom at The Hills Grammar — Junior Campus (Kenthurst)

The Hills Grammar Junior Campus in Kenthurst teaches Kindergarten to Year 6 and keeps a distinctly hands‑on, creative focus for younger learners. Music is a clear strength here: junior choirs, small ensembles, one‑on‑one instrumental lessons and regular performance events give kids lots of chances to build confidence onstage as they learn. Sport is just as active, with school football and club pathways, a broad co‑curricular calendar and even specialist offerings such as equestrian for families who want something beyond the usual weekend fixtures. The campus culture feels tuned to families who want both structure and extra‑curricular breadth — if your child loves music or wants an unusual sport like riding, this campus will make those options easy to explore.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

Working parents will appreciate that Camp Australia runs Outside School Hours Care (on‑site before and after school childcare) at the Junior Campus, with vacation care also available. That means you can pick a school where your child is already on campus before you arrive at work and stays in a supervised, familiar environment after the last bell, rather than having to shuttle them between locations. Camp Australia manages the session times and enrolment process, so starting paperwork early is worth it if you need guaranteed before‑ or after‑school care.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Kenthurst’s village vibe extends to dinner plans. On those nights when you don’t want to cook, you can grab a quick pizza from Pizzarelli’s, pick up Chinese takeaway from Kim Wah, or drop into Hidden Jem for casual cafe meals and coffee. For a slightly more formal meal out, places like The Hills Grove and Heritage House offer relaxed dining without the city drive. For grocery runs and fresh fruit and veg, the local FoodWorks and Kenthurst Village Fruit & Veg will sort most weekly lists — the pattern here is practical village convenience rather than big‑box shopping.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you’re thinking about extra academic support, there are nearby options like Pacific Tutoring and private tutors from agencies such as Alchemy Tuition for K–12 lessons and later stage HSC work. Commuting to central Sydney requires a little planning: Kenthurst doesn’t have a train station so most families combine the local bus (route 639) with a train or metro connection from a hub such as Castle Hill or Cherrybrook. Door‑to‑door public transport trips to Sydney’s Central Station commonly take around one to two hours depending on connections, so many households either time the commute carefully or drive to a rail hub to shave minutes off peak travel.

Your Next Steps

First, book a Junior Campus tour and review the Kindergarten entry information on The Hills Grammar website so you know which documentation and timelines matter. Second, confirm Outside School Hours Care enrolment, session times and vacancy with Camp Australia — families who need before‑ or after‑school care should lock that in early. Third, ask the school about Junior music, sport and equestrian pathways, and while you’re at it trial the door‑to‑door commute at school drop‑off and pick‑up times (bus 639 plus the rail link) to make sure the daily rhythm works for your household; if you want extra subject support, reach out to local tutors like Pacific Tutoring or Alchemy.
