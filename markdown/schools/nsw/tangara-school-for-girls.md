Welcome to Cherrybrook, NSW (Sydney’s Hills District)

Cherrybrook feels like the kind of place you drive into and instantly understand why families stay. With a median age around 43 and a workforce made up mostly of white-collar professionals, it’s an affluent, owner-occupied, family-first suburb where tree-lined streets and backyard cricket feel entirely at home. Many households here are married couples with children, which means playground chatter, weekend sport and school-run rhythms shape the neighbourhood. If you’re arriving from overseas, you’ll also find comfort in Cherrybrook’s multicultural fabric — having neighbours from a mix of backgrounds helps make settling in a little less of a leap.

Inside the Classroom at Tangara School for Girls

Tangara School for Girls teaches children from Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland). The school’s culture leans into a broad, arts-and-sport-rich experience. Music is a big part of daily life through the Tangara Music Academy: think choirs, chamber groups, wind and string ensembles, contemporary bands and one-on-one instrument lessons. Sport is similarly organised and active, with structured school sport programs, athletics and inter-school carnival pathways — netball teams are a familiar sight. Clubs, leadership programs and performing‑arts activities round out a classroom experience that’s both structured and creative.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

Tangara offers on-site Outside School Hours Care — that’s on-site before-and-after-school childcare — operated by Extend. Families can book sessions through Extend and via the national childcare portal; after‑school care typically runs until about 6pm. For working parents, that on-site provision removes a lot of the evening juggling: pick-ups, short commutes to childcare centers, and the frantic search for someone to mind the kids while you finish work. The booking systems make spots easier to secure, especially if you register early in the enrolment process.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

When it comes to feeding a busy family, Cherrybrook Village is your reliable hub. Woolworths anchors the shopping precinct for big weekly runs, while local independent retailers and Martelli’s Market offer fresh produce if you prefer hand‑selecting seasonal fruit and vegetables. When nights are too full for cooking, local dining options are pleasantly suburban: The Brook Eatery serves modern Australian, family-friendly meals; The Burrow is a casual café-diner that’s good for takeaway; and there are several sushi and takeaway outlets for nights when you want something quick and familiar. Between pantry staples and a few local favourites, weeknight dinners here are straightforward and forgiving.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you want extra academic support, Cherrybrook has options on the doorstep. Kumon Cherrybrook offers structured maths and English tuition, while Cherrybrook Maths Coaching provides more personalised maths help. These programs slot in well around school and after‑school care routines. For parents commuting to Sydney’s central business district — Sydney CBD refers to the city’s central business area — Cherrybrook is very commuter-friendly. Cherrybrook Station on the Sydney Metro North West line gives a direct connection into Central Station, with a typical journey time of around 40 to 45 minutes. That makes a daily commute entirely doable without erasing family dinner time.

Your Next Steps

First, get in touch with Tangara’s admissions team to confirm enrolment requirements and to book a school visit; the school website is the best place to start. Second, register for the on-site Outside School Hours Care through Extend so you have before-and-after-school care sorted — after‑school sessions generally finish around 6pm. Third, sign kids up for co‑curriculars you care about early: the Music Academy, peripatetic lessons and school sport all have enrolment processes you’ll want to tick off while spaces are available.
