Welcome to Bexley

If you're picturing an inner‑south Sydney suburb where tree‑lined streets meet friendly front porches, you're close. Bexley feels quietly established: the median age sits around 40 and you’ll find a neighbourhood made up of families buying into long‑term homes alongside a mix of renters. It’s the kind of place where professionals, office workers and tradies rub shoulders, all feeding into that lived‑in, community vibe. You’ll find everyday conveniences on Forest Road and pockets of specialty stores that hint at the suburb’s cultural mix — handy when you’re settling in and stocking a new kitchen.

Inside the Classroom at Bexley Public School

Bexley Public School covers primary years through to Year 6 and brings a very practical, community‑minded energy to school life. Kids bounce between sport — everything from soccer and Australian rules football to inter‑school competitions — and creative outlets like a Year 6 ukulele group, choir performances and school showcases. There are quieter corners too: gardening groups, crochet and interest clubs where children can pick up hands‑on skills and new friends. The vibe is active but not frantic, with plenty of chances for kids to try things and parents to stay connected to the school community.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families juggling work and school runs, the school offers on‑site Outside School Hours Care, which is the on-site before‑and‑after school childcare program. Parents typically contact the school to confirm current session times, hours and the booking process; providers and exact schedules can change, so a quick call before enrolment will save you questions. The existence of on‑site care makes afternoon logistics a lot simpler for those days when meetings overrun or public transport is slow.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Weeknight dinners in Bexley are a relaxed affair. There’s a local IGA for the staples and a handful of ethnic grocery options — including Filipino and broader Asian specialty stores — that make it easy to source familiar ingredients. When you need something faster, the local takeaways deliver solid family favourites: Bexley Pizzeria for an easy pizza night, the classic fish and chips on Forest Road when everyone wants something comforting, and The Hot Spot BBQ & Grill if you’re after Lebanese charcoal grill flavours and halal options. It’s the sort of culinary mix that helps new arrivals feel at home quickly, whether you’re recreating a family recipe or stretching dinner into conversation after a busy day.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you’re thinking about tutoring or extra academic support, there are local options that serve primary and secondary students. Markitup Tuition runs local programs across K–12, exam preparation and subject help, and nearby Smart Space Tutoring supports families in Rockdale, Kogarah and Bexley. For parents commuting to the CBD, Bexley North station sits on the T8 Airport & South train line, and typical travel to Central Station is roughly 20 to 25 minutes depending on the service. That short rail link makes city access doable for daily commuters while keeping daily life comfortably suburban.

Your Next Steps

1) Call Bexley Public School to confirm Outside School Hours Care session times, booking details and any provider information so you can plan before enrolment.  
2) Peek at the school’s student opportunities page to see current co‑curricular options — sport, music groups like ukulele and choir, gardening and craft clubs — and to note any Year 6‑specific activities.  
3) If you want tutoring or extra support, email or ring Markitup Tuition or Smart Space Tutoring to check programs and availability, and scope local groceries and takeaways for the weeknight meals that will keep your family fed while you settle in.
