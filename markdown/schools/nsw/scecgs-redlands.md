Welcome to Cremorne (NSW)

Cremorne on the Lower North Shore has that quietly accomplished, family-friendly feel that makes parents breathe a little easier. Think tree-lined streets, smart cafés and shops along Military Road, and the kind of household incomes that signal a strong professional community — plenty of consultants, managers and clinicians who want good schools and easy commutes. Most residents were born in Australia, with England the most common place of birth among overseas-born households, so while it’s an established, Anglo‑Australian pocket of Sydney, you’ll find the familiar mix of local conveniences and city-adjacent calm that families crave.

Inside the Classroom at SCECGS Redlands

SCECGS Redlands is the kind of school that leans into broad childhoods: alongside solid classroom teaching you’ll find a structured primary music program (there’s an established Year 2 strings program and multiple junior ensembles) and a robust sports offering that ranges from basketball and swimming to tennis, sailing and rowing. If your child lights up at a violin or dreams of weekend representative sport, Redlands gives those options room to grow. The primary years are supported by clear pathways into co‑curricular ensembles and team sports, so the classroom day usually extends into purposeful music rehearsals or training sessions rather than one-off activities.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families juggling work and pick‑ups, SCECGS Redlands runs an on‑site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) program that covers after‑school care and vacation care. That means you can enrol your child in structured after‑school activities without the daily dash to an off-site provider; it’s a practical relief for full‑time working parents who need consistent care and a short walk from classrooms to supervision. Check availability early — these on-site spots are often popular with Redlands families.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Military Road is your weekday lifeline for groceries and quick dinners. The local IGA (Supamart) handles the essentials, while nearby Asian grocers make whipping up stir‑fries and noodle bowls easy on a weeknight. When you want zero cooking, Cremorne’s casual takeaway scene delivers: wood‑fired pizzas from the local joints and reliable Thai and other takeaways that kids will approve of. The strip is quietly family-oriented, with parks and cafés nearby so a midweek pizza can double as a playground reward.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If extra academic support becomes part of your plan, Cremorne has local options — from C3 Education’s campus offering primary and secondary tuition and selective-entry preparation, to listings for in-home or online tutors through broader networks. Commute-wise, Cremorne doesn’t have its own heavy‑rail station but it’s well served by frequent buses along Military Road and has nearby ferry and train interchanges. Expect typical public‑transport trips into the CBD to be around 15 to 25 minutes by bus depending on traffic and timing; it’s close enough for professional parents who need reliable, short travel times to central Sydney.

Your Next Steps

1) Ring Redlands to confirm which primary grades are currently enrolling, ask specifically about primary placements and the Year 2 strings and junior ensemble pathways so you can plan auditions or instrument needs.  
2) Enrol in the school’s on‑site Outside School Hours Care (after‑school care and vacation care) if you need reliable wraparound care — check the StartingBlocks listing and lock in spots early.  
3) Test your commute: try the Military Road bus routes at the times you’ll travel, and use trip planners to confirm door‑to‑CBD times before you sign a lease or buy.
