Welcome to Strathfield

If you’re picturing a lively, family-focused suburb that still feels close to the city, Strathfield fits that picture. The median age here is 32 and the median weekly household income sits above the New South Wales average, so you’ll find many young professionals and growing families rather than an older, industrial catchment. The community’s multicultural fabric is part of its comfort: only about one third of residents were born in Australia, with the largest overseas birthplace being Nepal (around 13.6%), then China and India. For families arriving from abroad, that mix makes it easier to settle in—shops, neighbours and schoolmates who speak other languages mean you’re less likely to feel isolated while everyone adjusts.

Inside the Classroom at St Martha’s Catholic Primary School, Strathfield

St Martha’s offers a traditional primary program with a strong co‑curricular heartbeat. Sport is organised into teams and seasonal programs, and there’s a lively performing‑arts scene—music, dance and instrumental opportunities sit alongside more cerebral options like an after‑school chess enrichment program. The atmosphere feels active and inclusive: kids are encouraged to stretch on the sports field, try an instrument or audition for a performance, and there are structured clubs for those who want extra academic stimulation. If you value a school that balances movement, creativity and chess‑style strategy, St Martha’s has that mix.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

If you’re juggling work and school runs, St Martha’s has practical on-site arrangements. Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) and vacation care are provided by Sydney Catholic Early Childhood Services. That means drop-off before the bell and pick-up after training, rehearsals or homework time can all be handled on campus, which removes a lot of daily logistical friction. Before you commit, check hours, fees and any waitlist details with the provider so you know whether you’ll need backup plans for busy weeks or school holidays.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Strathfield’s food scene is quietly convenient for families who cook at home and for those who grab something easy near the station. For everyday groceries there’s a Woolworths in Strathfield Plaza, and if you need Asian or South Asian ingredients you’ll find specialist grocers like GR Buy Asian Supermarket and other Indian, Pakistani and East Asian stores nearby. When you’d rather not cook, the restaurant strips around the station are friendly and casual: Thai at Baan Raun, Vietnamese at Saigon Things and plenty of Korean and Japanese options that suit takeaway, kids and weekday dinners. That variety is great for families who want both familiar pantry staples and the comforts of home-cuisine ingredients.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you think extra academic support might be needed down the track, Strathfield has established options. Kumon offers maths and English tuition locally, and Matrix Education runs structured tutoring and HSC support from a Strathfield campus. For parents planning school‑day logistics, the train from Strathfield to Sydney Central is frequent and fast—typical journey times are roughly 12 to 16 minutes depending on the service—so commuting into the CBD for work or extracurriculars is realistic. Test actual journey times from prospective homes and check live timetables for school‑day planning to avoid surprises.

Your Next Steps

1) Call or email St Martha’s to confirm the exact year levels they accept, the enrolment criteria and timing—every school handles applications slightly differently.  
2) Ring Sydney Catholic Early Childhood Services to check Outside School Hours Care hours, fees, vacancies and holiday care arrangements so you can plan daily drop-offs and school‑holiday coverage.  
3) Arrange a school visit or attend an open day to see the sports teams, music and performing‑arts activities and the chess program in action; you’ll get a much better feel walking the playground and watching a class or rehearsal.
