Welcome to Como (SAL10996)

If you’re picturing a family-friendly pocket with tree-lined streets, waterfront glimpses and a decidedly suburban rhythm, Como fits that image well. The median age is around 40 and most households are married or couple families with kids, which gives the suburb a settled, white-collar feel rather than a singles or inner-city buzz. Household incomes tend to be above average, and while most residents were born in Australia, there’s a healthy sprinkling of families from England and elsewhere — that mix can be a real comfort if you’re new to the country, because it usually means local playgroups, school cohorts and community events are used to accommodating different backgrounds. Local shopping is convenient: think neighbourhood supermarkets, delis and the kinds of market and specialty stalls that make weeknight meal runs quick and varied rather than a chore.

Inside the Classroom at Como Public School (K–6)

Como Public School is the local primary school serving Kindergarten to Year 6, with a community-minded vibe that leans into sport, music and public speaking. Kids here get plenty of chances to run in athletics and inter-school competitions, sing or perform through the Sutherland Shire Schools Music Festival and sharpen their confidence in debating and public speaking. There are interest-based clubs and clear pathways for student leadership, so your child can find their niche whether they’re into performance, team sports or leading projects. The school’s focus on well-rounded opportunities means parents can expect a curriculum balanced with extra-curricular experiences that build both skills and friendships.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families juggling work and school runs, Como Public School offers on-site Outside School Hours Care — that’s before- and after-school childcare plus vacation care — run by providers such as OSHClub alongside the school’s own Before & After School Care program. Having on-site care saves the scramble between the classroom and your next meeting, and it’s particularly handy for parents who work shifts or have unpredictable days. It’s wise to register early: these programs are popular and places fill up, so checking availability with OSHClub and the school’s program listing gives you peace of mind and a clear routine for afternoons and school holidays.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Evenings in Como are pleasantly low-fuss. If you want to grab dinner without a long drive, the marina precinct has family-friendly options like Blackfish Cafe & Grill and the Como Marina Bay Cafe, both waterside and easy with kids. There’s also Thai Rim Nam and other casual takeaway options that make weeknight meal planning simple. Grocery shopping tends to revolve around neighbourhood supermarkets, the local butcher and deli counters, and market vendors who pop up in local listings. Together, these options mean you can put a decent mid-week meal on the table without spending your entire evening shopping or cooking.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If extra academic support is on your radar, there are local tutoring options for primary school subjects like English and maths; providers such as Kip McGrath in nearby Miranda and small private tutors through local education services can step in for assessments and ongoing sessions. Commuting from Como to Sydney’s central business district is straightforward for working parents: Como Station sits on the Sydney Trains line serving the Eastern Suburbs and Illawarra, and many scheduled services take about 30 to 35 minutes to reach Sydney Central depending on the time of day. Testing your likely travel time during a school-week morning or afternoon helps you plan drop-offs, pick-ups and any hybrid work arrangements.

Your Next Steps

First, check the school catchment and book a tour or open-day visit so you can feel the classrooms, meet teachers and get a sense of whether the school culture fits your child. Second, secure your after‑school care early by contacting OSHClub and the school’s Before & After School Care listing to register and confirm spots for before, after and vacation care. Third, choose extracurriculars and any extra academic help by reviewing the school’s clubs in sport, music and debating, and reach out to local tutors if you want assessment or regular lessons.
