Welcome to Davidson

If you’re picturing quiet streets lined with leafy reserves and a neighbourhood where the morning school run looks like a parade of tidy backpacks and coffee cups, you’re not far off. Davidson sits comfortably in the family-friendly end of the North Shore, home to a largely professional, white-collar community where the median age is around 45 and the typical household earns well above the national average. Most residents were born in Australia with a noticeable cluster from England, which gives the suburb a familiar, easygoing feel for newcomers from similar places. It’s the kind of place where neighbours know each other’s kids by name, and weekend markets and local cafés feel like part of the weekly routine.

Inside the Classroom at St Martin’s Catholic Primary School (Davidson, NSW)

St Martin’s runs Kindergarten to Year 6, so your child will move through the full primary cycle here. The classrooms strike a balance between traditional literacy and numeracy focus and hands-on enrichment. Music is well supported — there’s both a Training Band and a Concert Band — and if your child likes strategy and quiet concentration they’ll find a thriving chess culture with competition opportunities. Technology isn’t an afterthought: every year level has a weekly coding lesson through a Code4Fun program, so kids graduate comfortable with basic computational thinking. There’s also choir, dance club and teams for maths and public speaking, so children with different interests can find their niche. The school feels like a place where extra-curriculars are treated as part of a full education rather than optional add-ons.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

If you need care beyond the school bell, St Martin’s has on-site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) run by CatholicCare. Before-school care opens at 7:00am and runs until the start of the school day; after-school care runs from 3:00pm until 6:00pm, and there’s vacation care during the school holidays. The convenience of having supervised, structured care on-site can make a big difference for working parents — drop-off before a morning commute and pick-up straight from the school grounds in the evening keeps afternoons calmer and logistics simpler.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

When weeknights are tight and you need groceries or a quick meal, Davidson punches above its weight. Davidson Specialty Foods offers a local specialty grocer experience for ready-to-go items and pantry top-ups, while several supermarkets and grocery stores nearby cover the basics. For takeaway, Imbue Cafe is a friendly local option for coffee and quick bites, and the suburb supports a scattering of family-friendly pizzerias and café-style takeaways that deliver. In practice, that means you can plan a healthy midweek dinner without a long detour — or order something the kids love when a late meeting runs into dinner time.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If extra academic support is on your radar, you’ve got local options. Davidson Tutoring provides tailored tutoring and test-prep locally, and regional providers like Success Tutoring also serve the area for subject-specific help or catch-up programs. Commuting into central Sydney is straightforward but bus-focused: Davidson doesn’t have a train station, so most families rely on buses to Chatswood or other rail interchanges and then catch a train into the city. Typical door-to-destination times into central Sydney land around 35 to 45 minutes depending on connections and time of day — for example, a 20–30 minute bus to Chatswood followed by a 15–20 minute train. It’s a predictable, manageable commute, but it’s worth mapping your exact route at peak times to know which bus to aim for and where you’ll transfer.

Your Next Steps

1) Check the school’s enrolment page to confirm required documents, deadlines and fees so you can lock in a spot without last-minute scrambling.  
2) Call CatholicCare via the school’s OSHC page to confirm how vacation care and regular before/after-school bookings work, including fees and capacity.  
3) Ask the school about signing up for band, chess and coding: find out costs, schedules and whether there are waiting lists so you can plan instruments, uniforms or practice time.
