Welcome to Leichhardt (includes Leichhardt North)

Leichhardt feels like a comfortable, well‑dressed neighbourhood that still knows how to feed you a perfect pizza. The median age here sits around 37, and you'll find a mix of young professionals, families and single adults who have chosen Leichhardt for its village feel within easy reach of the city. Household incomes are on the higher side, which shows up in neat terraces, renovated cottages and a lively local café culture along Norton Street. If you're moving in with kids, there's a reassuring mix of settled families and energetic newcomers that makes settling in feel normal rather than unusual.

Inside the Classroom at St Columba’s Catholic Primary School (Leichhardt North)

St Columba’s in Leichhardt North feels like a small school with big heartbeats. The school’s parent information highlights the standard primary year offerings, and what stands out most is a strong commitment to music and sport: instrumental music lessons, choir, and an active sports calendar that includes interschool football and other team competitions. For families who want their kids singing, playing an instrument, or wearing a team singlet on the weekend, St Columba’s programs give plenty of everyday opportunities to belong and grow.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For working parents the logistics matter, and St Columba’s flags before‑ and after‑school care on its parent pages. Many families in the area use on‑site or closely affiliated providers, and local operators such as LOOSH run outside school hours care (on‑site before and after school childcare) options nearby that help bridge the gap between the school bell and your commute home. That kind of setup makes juggling drop‑offs and late finishes a lot less stressful, especially during those weeks when rehearsals, tutoring or weekend sport take over the family calendar.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Norton Street and Norton Plaza are the beating culinary heart of Leichhardt. For pantry basics you have a Coles at Norton Plaza and an IGA around the corner, and for those days when you want proper Italian deli items or continental cheeses you can pop across to the Haberfield delis that stock prosciutto, fresh pasta and antipasti. When dinner needs to be quick and delicious, Gioia and Moretti serve wood‑fired, thin‑crust pizzas that make weeknight cooking feel optional, and Norton Asian House covers the speedy, flavourful takeaway evenings. Between supermarket runs and pizzeria stops, weeknight life here has the comforting flexibility that busy families crave.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If your child could use a bit of extra help with maths or English, Leichhardt has local tuition options like NumberWorks’nWords and independent tutors such as Alchemy Tuition who offer assessment‑based support and flexible scheduling. Commuting into the CBD from Leichhardt North is straightforward: the L1 light‑rail runs directly towards Central Station and typical trip times are in the 25–35 minute range depending on the time of day. That combination of local academic support and a predictable light‑rail link makes Leichhardt an attractive base for families who need both strong neighbourhood schools and manageable access to the city.

Your Next Steps

First, check the school’s Parent Information page to confirm before‑and‑after‑school care hours and enrolment steps so you know what fits your routine. Second, call local outside school hours care providers like LOOSH to confirm availability and whether they run on‑site programs or close drop‑off arrangements. Third, spend an afternoon walking Norton Street and Norton Plaza to eyeball groceries, sample a pizza and note any tutoring centres you might want to contact for assessments.
