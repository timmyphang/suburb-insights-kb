Welcome to Fingal Head

If your move includes a tiny coastal village with big heart, Fingal Head is the place you’ll want to explore. With a population of roughly 615 people, a median age of 46 and a median weekly household income a touch below the New South Wales average, this is a middle‑income seaside community where older residents and young families live side by side. It’s the kind of place where the ocean is a part of daily life, mornings can mean a walk along the headland and the local rhythms feel calm compared with the nearby hustle of Kingscliff or Tweed Heads.

Inside the Classroom at Fingal Head Public School

Fingal Head Public School teaches Kindergarten to Year 6, and the classroom culture is quietly active: students get weekly specialist music and creative‑arts lessons, regular school sport including athletics and cross‑country, and opportunities to join inter‑school competitions. The school leans into outdoor learning with environmental and gardening projects, and it encourages student voice through public speaking, leadership programs and STEM enrichment. Parents who want a school that balances the basics with creative and practical experiences will find that the timetable is designed to give kids a variety of ways to engage beyond textbook work.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

The school website does not list an on‑site Outside School Hours Care provider (that’s on‑site before and after school childcare), so families should plan on arranging after‑school care through local providers. Aggregator listings and local services in the Fingal Head postcode make it possible to find care, but it’s a good idea to check directly with the school for the latest arrangements and to ring individual providers early to confirm hours, fees and availability. If your family needs supervised care at the end of the school day, expect to line up a spot through a nearby provider rather than relying on an on‑site program at the school.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

For groceries and a quick bite, Fingal Head keeps things friendly and small. The Fingal General Store & Cellars covers local convenience needs, while Saltstone and Sheoak offer that artisanal, beachside café vibe where you can grab baked goods, takeaway and a bit of gallery charm. For bigger supermarket shops you’ll head into Kingscliff or Tweed Heads for Coles, Woolworths or ALDI. When weeknights are busy, family‑friendly local spots like the Aboriginal‑owned Miimis café and the relaxed Saltstone/Sheoak Shack make dinner easy without losing the coastal feel.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If your child benefits from extra tuition, local options include Tweed Coast Tutoring for targeted maths support and Kip McGrath Tweed Heads for structured English and maths programs; many families also tap providers in Kingscliff and Tweed Heads for regular sessions. Commuting beyond the region requires planning: Fingal Head doesn’t have a train station, and trips to Sydney by public coach can run 15 to 16 hours depending on connections. Most families prefer to drive or fly for faster travel when longer journeys are involved.

Your Next Steps

First, call or visit the school website to confirm enrolment requirements, the school‑day hours and bell times so you know how the day fits with your routine. Second, ask the school whether they have added any on‑site Outside School Hours Care provider recently and, if not, use aggregator listings to contact local after‑school care services to compare hours, fees and vacancy. Third, if extra academic support is on your mind, ring Tweed Coast Tutoring or Kip McGrath Tweed Heads to check what programs and schedules will suit your child.
