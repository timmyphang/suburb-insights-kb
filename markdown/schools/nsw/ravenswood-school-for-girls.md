Welcome to Gordon (NSW)

Settle in, and you’ll quickly notice why families love Gordon on Sydney’s Upper North Shore. It’s an affluent, quietly polished suburb where the median household pulls in around $2,460 a week and many people work in professional and managerial roles. The streets are leafy, the parks feel made for weekend picnics, and the neighbourhood hums with a family-first rhythm that makes school drop-offs, sports pickups and after‑school routines feel effortless.

Inside the Classroom at Ravenswood School for Girls — Junior School

Ravenswood’s Junior School, for Kindergarten to Year 6, feels like a small-town conservatory wrapped in the resources of a well‑resourced independent school. Music is a clear heartbeat here: orchestras, multiple bands and choirs give kids real ensemble experience rather than token exposure. Sport is broad and purposeful, with a school pool, tennis, swimming and diving options, netball and soccer, gymnastics and even pathways into equestrian programs and representative sport for kids who want to compete. If your child leans toward tinkering and problem-solving, clubs in coding and robotics (VEX) sit alongside homework and extension clubs so there’s always a place to push curiosity. It’s the kind of place where a love of learning is built through both performance and play.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

Parents juggling full schedules will appreciate that Ravenswood runs on-site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) through TheirCare. After-school care runs to 6:00 pm on school days, and holiday programs cover early morning through to 6:00 pm, which takes the pressure off those long workdays. Many families may be eligible for the Australian government’s Child Care Subsidy, which can reduce out-of-pocket costs — always worth checking your eligibility when you’re budgeting.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Gordon’s food scene is built for families who want reliable, honest meals after a busy day. If you cook at home, you’ll find specialty Asian grocers like Gordon Asian Grocery and Jin Asian Grocery that make Mandarin and wider Chinese ingredients easy to source — a real comfort if you’re adjusting to life here and missing familiar flavours. For takeaway and quick family dinners, Mehfil Indian serves family-friendly Indian fare, Thai Riffic covers authentic Thai for those nights you don’t want to cook, and Gordon Fish & Chips is the dependable, kid-approved option. For something a little more grown-up on date night, Grill @ Gordon offers the family-style grill and steak choices that locals return to.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you want a little extra academic support, the suburb has established options like Kumon in Gordon for maths and English, plus local private tutors and centres offering selective exam and national assessment preparation, including Better Tuition Academy and S.A.M Singapore Maths. For parents commuting to the central business district, Gordon Station sits on Sydney Trains’ Northern line, with practical weeknight journeys to Central taking roughly 35 minutes depending on the service — fast enough to keep your workday manageable while still coming home to dinner and bedtime stories.
