Welcome to Bexley, NSW

If you picture a leafy St George suburb where the median age sits around 40 and the streets hum with the friendly rhythm of family life, you’re picturing Bexley. It’s a place where half of households are family‑oriented and around half of adults are married, a comfortable middle ground for couples with kids and parents balancing white‑collar jobs and skilled trades. For families arriving from overseas, Bexley is quietly reassuring: just over half the residents were born in Australia, and a strong Chinese community is the largest overseas-born group. That mix of backgrounds means neighbours, playmates and schoolrooms that are used to multilingual families and the small, practical kindnesses that help new arrivals settle in quickly.

Inside the Classroom at Carlton Public School

Carlton Public School teaches Kindergarten through Year 6 and feels like the kind of local primary where teachers know the kids’ names and parents recognise each other at the gate. The school runs a full suite of extracurriculars: school sport including athletics and inter‑school competitions, performing arts in both music and drama, debating and public‑speaking, lunchtime clubs and student leadership programs. Those activities do more than fill afternoons — they create easy ways for children to find their tribe, explore new interests and build confidence before moving on to high school.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

Working parents will appreciate practical after‑school options. Carlton has on‑site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) and vacation care run by the YMCA, known locally as The Y. That arrangement means handoffs at the school feel seamless: kids can move straight from class into supervised care without you having to sprint across town. If your family needs structured afternoons, occasional late finishes or school holiday cover, the routine and consistency of on‑site care make life a lot easier.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Forest Road is Bexley’s everyday main street, and it’s where you’ll run into neighbours and pick up groceries between school pickups. There’s an IGA for weekly staples, a fresh greengrocer like All Seasons Fruit Market for seasonal produce, and smaller community shops that stock specialty items if you’re hunting for a particular ingredient. When you don’t feel like cooking, family‑friendly takeaways are plentiful: a local pizzeria on Forest Road and Mediterranean‑style outlets offer quick, kid-approved dinners that make weeknights feel manageable without sacrificing flavour.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If your child benefits from extra academic support, there are local tutoring and selective‑entry coaching options for maths, English and exam preparation — useful whether you want a confidence boost or are planning ahead for selective high school entry. For commuters, Bexley North station on the T8 line connects to Sydney Central in roughly 20–25 minutes depending on the service, and regular buses run along Forest Road for local cross‑suburb trips and station access. It’s a short, practical commute for parents who work in the city, and easy enough to test before you commit to a daily routine.

Your Next Steps

First, peek at the Carlton Public School website to check enrolment zones, contact details and program notes so you know how the school handles new applications and classroom placements. Second, ring The Y at the Carlton Outside School Hours Care to confirm before‑and‑after school and vacation care hours, vacancies and enrolment steps so you can plan workdays with confidence. Third, ask the school about the specific extracurriculars and leadership opportunities your child might join — it’s the quickest way to match your child’s interests to programs and feel confident about the social fit.
