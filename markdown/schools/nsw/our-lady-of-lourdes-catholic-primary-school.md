Welcome to Earlwood

Earlwood feels a little like a well-loved neighbourhood you stumble across while daydreaming about stability and good coffee. The median age here sits around 44, and the suburb is mainly built around established families and homeowners rather than students or singles. Household incomes tend to be higher than the metropolitan average, which shows in the tidy streets, family-friendly parks and dependable local services. One of the things that makes settling in easier is the suburb’s strong Greek-born community — roughly 7% of residents — which brings Mediterranean groceries, weekend baking rituals and a warm multicultural rhythm that’s a big comfort if you’ve moved from overseas.

Inside the Classroom at Our Lady of Lourdes Catholic Primary School (Earlwood)

Our Lady of Lourdes is a K–6 Catholic primary where community and co-curricular life are as much a part of the school day as reading and maths. The school runs a lively program of music — choir, instrumental lessons and even a drum and lyre-style band — alongside drama, chess, public speaking and inter-school sport. That mix means your child can try a few things without being pushed into a single lane, and it creates multiple small communities within the school: kids who sing together, kids who play sport together, kids who meet over chess. If you want data on academic progress, ask for the latest annual report; that will usually include staffing, program detail and results from the National Assessment Program — Literacy and Numeracy — which is Australia’s standard testing snapshot.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

If you’re juggling drop-offs, office hours and after-school activities, the school’s on-site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) operated by SCECS is designed to make afternoons and early mornings manageable. Before-school care typically starts around 7:00am and runs until the start of the school day, and after-school care usually covers the window from dismissal until around 6:00pm. They provide meals, homework support and play programs, so you don’t have to race between work and the classroom bell. It’s worth confirming current hours, capacity and fees with the provider, but having this wraparound option on campus is a huge peace-of-mind win for busy families.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Homer Street is essentially Earlwood’s living room for groceries and takeout. If you miss Mediterranean flavours, there are Greek and Cypriot grocers and specialist stores stocking olives, regional cheeses and pantry staples the way your nonna would approve. For weeknight dinners, the street keeps things casual and family-friendly: pizza and pasta spots do a reliable job on busy weeknights, while a local taverna brings authentic Greek dishes that are perfect for introducing kids to shared plates. For nights when someone just needs a quick, comforting meal, there’s Thai and other takeaway options within a short walk or drive.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

For families thinking about extra academic support, there are local tutoring services offering one-to-one tuition and small-group programs that work around school hours. These providers can help fill gaps or extend learning beyond the classroom, particularly in the lead-up to important assessment years. On the commute front, Earlwood doesn’t have its own train station, but several nearby stations and frequent bus routes connect to Sydney’s Central Station. Depending on where you join the rail network, typical train journeys into the city can be around 12 to 20 minutes from the nearest stations, which makes daily commutes manageable if you plan your morning route.

Your Next Steps

First, ask the school for its most recent annual report and a current newsletter so you can see up-to-date enrolment, staffing and program details, including results from the National Assessment Program — Literacy and Numeracy. Second, get the enrolment policy and fee schedule and confirm the Outside School Hours Care hours, capacity and costs with SCECS to make sure before- and after-school care fits your work routine. Third, book a school tour and chat with leadership about co-curricular commitments like music and sport, and how homework support is handled — then, if you want, I can fetch the annual report or turn what you learn into a short pros-and-cons checklist.
