Welcome to Maroubra Junction (Maroubra)

If you’re picturing the kind of beachside suburb where surf culture and weekday work routines rub shoulders, you’re pretty much there. Maroubra Junction sits in Maroubra, a mixed‑income pocket where established families live nearer the sand while apartment-lined main roads house younger professionals and students. You’ll find white‑collar commuters next to long-standing public-housing communities, which gives the area both stability and variety. Most residents were born in Australia (about 55–56% at the 2021 Census), and the neighbourhood hums with the everyday comforts families want: local cafés, quick shopping, and that easy access to the beach for weekends.

Inside the Classroom at Lycée Condorcet — The International French School of Sydney

Lycée Condorcet serves children in Prep to Year 6 (note for international parents: Prep is the first year of primary school in Queensland) and feels like a small international village. The school’s semestered AES — Activités Extra‑Scolaires — programme is a proper highlight: think choir and instrumental workshops for kids who love music, soccer and basketball for the sporty ones, and rotating clubs such as robotics and dance for makers and movers. That structure tends to give families flexibility across the year while keeping children engaged with different pursuits. If your child loves variety, the AES catalogue shows plenty of options that can fit into a weekly rhythm without overwhelming the school day.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For parents juggling work and school pick‑ups, Lycée Condorcet runs on‑site Outside School Hours Care (on-site before and after school childcare) through TeamKids. After‑school care operates until 6:00pm, which covers that crucial late-afternoon window for most working families, and there are school-holiday programs too. Having a familiar, on‑site provider makes transitions easier: children stay on campus with structured activities right where their friends are, and you can breathe a little more easily about late meetings or unexpected overtime.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

When it comes to groceries and kiddo-friendly meals, Pacific Square is your local go‑to. It anchors with Coles and Aldi for everyday staples and is surrounded by family‑friendly quick options like sushi, pizzerias and cafés — perfect for nights when everyone is wiped out but still needs feeding. If you want to do a proper shop or pick up specialty items, Harris Farm Markets is nearby for fruit and veg, The Source Bulk Foods covers pantry staples in larger quantities, and Jasmine Asia Market is a handy stop for Asian ingredients. Between Pacific Square’s ready-to-eat choices and these specialty shops, weeknight dinner can be as simple or as adventurous as your schedule allows.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If your child needs a little academic boost, local tutoring services such as Success Tutoring and Art of Smart operate in the Maroubra area and can slot into afternoons or weekends without a massive commute. Getting to the Sydney CBD from Maroubra Junction is primarily a bus trip — there’s no train station at the junction itself — and direct services to Central Station or the city typically range from about 28 to 35 minutes depending on traffic and time of day. The 396 along Anzac Parade is one of the frequent routes people test when planning their daily commute; trying the trip at your usual travel time is the best way to know what your days will actually look like.

Your Next Steps

First, ring the school to confirm enrolment details, sort out grade placement, and take a look at the AES timetable so you can slot your child into the activities they’ll love. Second, if after‑school supervision or holiday care matters to your family, register with TeamKids early — spots can fill and you’ll want to confirm session times that match your work hours. Third, treat Pacific Square as your local lifeline for groceries and quick dinners, and keep the Harris Farm, The Source and Jasmine Asia Market addresses handy for special recipes or cultural ingredients.
