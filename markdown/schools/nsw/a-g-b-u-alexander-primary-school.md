Welcome to Duffys Forest

If you’re picturing tree-lined streets, established family homes and a slower pace that still sits within reach of the city, Duffys Forest fits that picture. The neighbourhood tilts older than a typical inner‑city pocket, with a median age around 43 and lots of married couples and long‑standing family households. Income levels sit comfortably in the upper‑middle range, which helps explain the well‑kept gardens, specialty food shops nearby and a general feeling of stability. About seven in ten residents were born in Australia, with England the most common overseas birthplace among the remainder — a small international thread in an otherwise locally rooted community that makes settling in smoother for many new arrivals.

Inside the Classroom at A.G.B.U. Alexander Primary School

A.G.B.U. Alexander Primary School teaches Kindergarten to Year 6 and carries a reputation for blending academic stretch with cultural life. Kids can expect opportunities in debating and public speaking, chess teams, and entry into external academic competitions led by bodies such as the University of New South Wales. Creative outlets are strong too: a school choir, private piano and guitar tuition, instrumental ensembles and even folkloric Armenian dance groups sit alongside the regular sports program and the school sports carnival. That mix suits families who want both solid classroom basics and a chance for children to explore interests beyond the core curriculum.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

Before and after school care is one of the practical questions families ask first. The school’s public pages don’t advertise on‑site Outside School Hours Care (on‑site before and after school childcare), so many families either check directly with the school for current arrangements or use nearby community providers. That means you’ll want to plan whether a neighbour, carpool, local provider or a mix of options fits your household routine. For busy families, lining up named local providers and understanding pickup points makes the afternoon shuffle much calmer.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Grocery runs feel a little boutique here. Forestway Fresh is the local gourmet providore with a café attached, and Terrey Hills has several providore options and small supermarkets serving the 2084 area. For an easy family night out or takeaway, Stonehenge Pizza in Terrey Hills and the casual café meals at The Tasting Deck inside Forestway Fresh are reliable low‑stress choices. Between the providore goods and nearby small takeaways and cafés, you can stitch together weeknight meals that range from fast and familiar to a notch more crafted when time allows.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

If extra academic support is part of your plan, there are local tutoring options serving the upper Northern Beaches area — from multi‑subject centres to independent maths tutors who do in‑home or online lessons. Families commonly use providers in Belrose and Terrey Hills for specialised tuition and exam preparation. Commuting to the Sydney CBD usually means taking a local bus to a major interchange such as Chatswood, then continuing by train. Door‑to‑door travel time typically sits around 60 to 75 minutes depending on connections, so factor that into your daily rhythm if someone in the household is commuting into the city for work.

Your Next Steps

1) Contact the school through its enrolment or contact page to confirm the current before and after‑school childcare options and any changes to pickup arrangements.  
2) Put together a short enrolment visit checklist: ask about bus routes and pickup spots, the availability and structure of the Armenian language program, current class sizes, and the co‑curricular offerings like music, dance, debating and sport.  
3) Line up local support: identify Nearby outside‑school‑hours care providers and tutoring options, and run a timed commute from your home to Chatswood and into the city so the school‑run and work commute sit comfortably together.
