Welcome to Cherrybrook, NSW

Cherrybrook often feels like a postcard for settled family life: tree-lined streets, generous established homes and a community that skews a little older than the city’s average, with a median age around 43. This is an upper‑middle‑income neighbourhood where many households are white‑collar and family‑oriented, and you’ll see that reflected in the local parks, weekend sports and school catchment conversations. If you’re moving from overseas, you’ll find plenty of familiar faces—substantial Indian and Chinese communities mean bilingual neighbours, shops and cultural touchpoints, which can be a real comfort when you’re settling into a new country.

Inside the Classroom at John Purchase Public School

John Purchase Public School takes children from Kindergarten through Year 6, and the classroom culture feels active and creative. Academic days are balanced with a broad extracurricular menu: students regularly participate in inter‑school sport and athletics through the local school sports association, there are strong creative and performing arts offerings, and the school runs a band and instrumental music program that many families love. If you want a school where choir practice, assembly performances and weekend sports fixtures are part of the rhythm of family life, this is the sort of place that cultivates those opportunities.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

The school hosts an on‑site outside school hours care program run by TheirCare, with before‑school sessions starting at 7:00am, after‑school care until about 6:30pm, plus vacation‑time programs. Places are limited, so parents who need wraparound care should contact TheirCare early to secure a spot and check whether you’re eligible for the federal Child Care Subsidy, which can help with costs. For families juggling commutes and meetings, having an on‑campus provider makes pickups and drop‑offs less frantic.

The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Cherrybrook Village is the local heartbeat for groceries and last‑minute dinners. There’s a Woolworths for one‑stop shopping and Martelli’s Market when you want the freshest produce. If your kitchen leans towards South Asian flavours, India Homes and the spice specialists at Green Valley Spices stock ingredients that make cooking from scratch easy. For nights when you need something quick and delicious, Meet Bun’s dumplings and buns, Cherrybrook Village Indian’s curries for a family takeaway, or Costi’s Catch for fish and chips will get dinner on the table without drama.

Tuition and the Daily Commute

For families thinking about academic enrichment, Cherrybrook has local options for tutoring and exam preparation. Cherrybrook Maths Coaching is a longstanding choice for students wanting extra support, and there are several private providers that focus on selective school and opportunity class preparation as well as NAPLAN practice. Commuting into the Sydney CBD is straightforward: Cherrybrook Metro station links you to frequent metro services and typically takes about 30–35 minutes to Central Station, which makes the suburb practical for parents who work in the city but want a quieter family base.

Your Next Steps

Book a school tour or information session and ask about Kindergarten–Year 6 classrooms, the inter‑school sports program, performing arts and the band so you get a feel for the school day and extracurricular options. Contact TheirCare as soon as you can to check availability for before‑ and after‑school care and vacation programs, and confirm whether you qualify for the Child Care Subsidy. If you’re thinking about selective school entry or extra NAPLAN preparation, reach out to Cherrybrook Maths Coaching or local tutoring directories to arrange an assessment and start dates.
