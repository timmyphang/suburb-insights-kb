# Welcome to Leichhardt

Leichhardt feels like the inner‑west Sydney everyone imagines: tree‑lined streets, neighbourhood cafés, and a quietly affluent, white‑collar hum. It attracts families and professionals who value quick access to the city without giving up local character. The population leans toward Australian‑born residents, with a mix of long‑standing local families and a touch of historic English migration showing up in older census breakdowns. If you’re drawn to a suburb with solid amenities, good schools and a lively food scene on Norton Street, Leichhardt is worth a close look.

# Inside the Classroom at Kegworth Public School

Kegworth Public School (Kindergarten to Year 6) is the kind of local school that makes parents smile when they drive past. The school has a strong music culture — ensembles for junior and senior concert band, strings groups, ukulele and recorder ensembles, choir options, instrument hire and access to tutors — so if your child lights up around music they’ll find plenty of ways to join in. Kegworth also offers debating and public speaking, inter‑school sport and an active environmental or eco club, which means students have lots of chances to explore beyond the classroom and to develop confidence in front of an audience or on the sports field.

# Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For families balancing work and school runs, Kegworth runs on‑site Outside School Hours Care (that’s on‑site before and after school childcare) and vacation care through TeamKids. Term‑time hours typically start from about 7:00am until the bell and resume from around 3:00pm to 6:00pm, and vacation programs commonly run longer, often from early morning until 6:00pm. If you need predictable childcare that slots into a standard workday, having a TeamKids venue based at the school takes a lot of logistics off your plate — but you’ll want to book early, especially for vacation weeks.

# The Kitchen Table and Weeknight Dinners

Norton Street is the heart of easy family food runs. For weekly shopping you’ve got Norton Plaza with a full Coles, Harris Farm for fresh produce and AM‑Mart for hard‑to‑find Far‑East ingredients. If tonight needs to be quick and happy, the strip’s wood‑fired pizza places and family‑friendly takeaways make weeknight dinners effortless: think Gioia Cafe’s wood‑fired pies, Mario’s Pizza Bar’s reliable family pizzas, or Aperitivo Pizza & Pasta when you want casual sit‑down Italian. Between a quality supermarket and plenty of dependable takeaway, dinnertime in Leichhardt is the kind that feels like home after a school day.

# Tuition and the Daily Commute

If you want extra academic support, Leichhardt offers options close to home: local centres provide remedial literacy and holiday programs, maths and English tuition for primary students, and several private tutors who’ll do one‑on‑one work tailored to your child. Commuting to the Sydney CBD is straightforward enough: the suburb is served by light rail and buses, and the nearest regular Sydney Trains station that most families use is Petersham, roughly two kilometres away — a short bus ride or walk — with about a ten‑minute train into Central. It’s a practical setup for parents who work in the city but want to come home to a community school.

# Your Next Steps

1) Lock in your child’s spot with the school’s on‑site Outside School Hours Care provider early — check exact hours and enrolment requirements so you’re covered for term time and school holidays.  
2) If music is a priority, reach out to the Kegworth parent group about ensemble sign‑ups, instrument hire and tutor options so your child can join from day one.  
3) Plan grocery and dinner routines around Norton Street and Norton Plaza, and line up any extra tutoring or holiday programs now so you have a ready rhythm once term starts.
