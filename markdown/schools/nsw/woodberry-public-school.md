Welcome to Woodberry, NSW (postcode 2322)

Woodberry feels like a place built for growing families. It’s a lower‑to‑middle income, family‑oriented suburb with a younger median age of 36 and more children than the New South Wales average, so playground chatter and school runs are part of the everyday rhythm. Household incomes here run below the state median, which keeps the local vibe practical and community‑minded rather than polished. You’ll also find a notably higher proportion of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander residents, which shows up in local school programs and community life and can be a real comfort to newcomers: there’s visible cultural continuity and recognised First Nations presence that helps make the place feel rooted and welcoming. Most residents are Australian‑born, with a small historic representation from England among overseas‑born residents. If you like a neighbourhood that leans family-first without pretense, Woodberry will feel familiar quickly.

Inside the Classroom at Woodberry Public School

Woodberry Public School is a K–6 school that leans into both tradition and hands‑on learning. The co‑curricular program blends sport and the creative and performing arts with a few standout offerings that give the school personality: a Wonnarua Country Aboriginal language program that connects students to local Indigenous culture, a Bike Hub where kids restore bikes and pick up practical mechanical skills, and a committed chess team that keeps the brain gears turning after class. These programs give children multiple ways to belong — whether they’re sporty, crafty, curious about culture, or love a quiet strategy game. If you’re moving with children, those options are the kinds of everyday extras that help kids settle in quickly.

Life After 3 PM: Working Parent Support

For working parents, Woodberry Public School runs an on‑site Outside School Hours Care program (on-site before and after school childcare) provided by Fair Play OOSH. That means you can enrol your kids for supervised before‑school, after‑school and vacation care directly through the school’s OOSH page or Fair Play’s service listing. Having an on‑site provider takes a lot of anxiety out of juggling work and school pickup, and it’s worth enrolling early — spaces in school-based care services can fill fast at the start of the year.

Tuition and the Daily Commute
