# Meadow Heights (VIC)

Slug: `meadow-heights-vic`

We know what they say about Meadow Heights. “It’s where the northern growth corridor forgets to grow.” “The suburb that makes Coolaroo look like South Yarra.” And honestly? They’re not wrong. But they also missed the bit where our only real claim to fame is getting lumped into emergency alert lists.

Back in 2017, the night buses got axed on New Year’s Eve. While trams and trains ran all night, we were left staring at an 8am return bus if we dared stay up past midnight. As Broadsheet reported, residents from Meadow Heights to Rosebud were “the most heavily affected.” Translation: if you wanted to watch the fireworks from the CBD, you either drove back or faced Uber surge. And nobody’s Ubering from a suburb where the main street is still waiting for its first proper cafe.

Then came 2020. Storms knocked out some Yarra Valley Water infrastructure, and suddenly 88 suburbs — including us — were told to boil our tap water before drinking, brushing teeth, or making baby formula. The warning list read like a game of bingo: Attwood, Broadmeadows, Coolaroo, Dallas, Fawkner, Meadow Heights. We made the cut alongside Greensborough and Glen Waverley. Finally, some recognition. The water company said the risk of gastro was “low,” but let’s be honest: Meadow Heights has been boiling its water for years just out of habit.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who wants a late-night bus home after a date. Anyone who expects a local coffee shop to have a name beyond “Milk Bar.” If your idea of a good winter date involves “moody wine bars” and “vinyl-only listening sessions” — see Sitchu’s list for Brunswick East — you’d be lost in our world of 24-hour 7-Elevens and the occasional kebab truck.

But who would love it? People who don’t need a night bus because they’re already home by 9pm. People who appreciate a quiet suburb where the biggest drama is whether the tap water is safe to drink. We’re not flashy. We’re the 88th suburb on a list you’ll scroll past. And we’re fine with that. Just don’t try to catch a bus home after midnight.
