# Mount Waverley (VIC)

Slug: `mount-waverley-vic`

You’re weaving through the queue at **Son of Tucci** — tucked away in a shady walkway, always buzzing — while a bloke in activewear attempts his third parallel park on Blackburn Rd. The clock hits 7:45am. Congratulations, you’ve just lost the war for a park at Mount Waverley station. According to Whirlpool forum elders, spots are gone by 7:45 — show up at 7:46 and you’re basically circling until your kids graduate from **Mount Waverley Secondary College**.

Welcome to our leafy, car-obsessed corner of the south-east. We’ve got **Hamlet** on Marianne Way — a refurbished corner milk bar serving fried-chicken eggs Benny and banana-Milo waffles, because why choose between savoury and dessert at 9am? (Julien Moussi and his crew from Inglewood Coffee Roasters know the drill.) We’ve got **South Society** for elegant mid-week dinners, and **Piccolino** — John Dib’s little takeaway on Blackburn Rd that’s been slinging the city’s best pizzas since Bill Clinton was President and movie tickets cost $6.

But the real local drama isn’t the food. It’s the **Telstra 5G home internet**. According to a local on Whirlpool, it drops out three times a day for 15–20 minutes. Just enough to ruin your Zoom, your streaming, and your faith in the NBN. And don’t ask about the 2020 water warning — 88 suburbs, including ours, told to boil our water. We still eye the tap suspiciously.

**Who would hate it here?** Anyone expecting nightlife. Our ‘thriving after dark’ is the **Mount Waverley Community Centre** (a stone’s throw from the station, but BYO party). And if you’re not a dumpling fanatic, you’ll struggle — **Dumplings Prime** at Hamilton Place serves some of Melbourne’s best steamed parcels (June 2025, Sitchu can confirm), and you’ll be judged for ordering spring rolls.

**Who would love it?** Families who want a peaceful, leafy suburb with top-tier public schools in the catchment. Kids can burn off energy at **Risky Kids** parkour on Ricketts Road. And if you’ve got a spare $30k for a sofa, **Jardan**’s annual sale (yes, 2012, but we’re still talking about it) is a rite of passage.

Mount Waverley — where the parking’s a nightmare, the Wi-Fi’s a gamble, but the dumplings are worth it.
