# Aintree (VIC)

Slug: `aintree-vic`

We know what you’re thinking: “Aintree? That’s the one with the rainforest, right?” Wrong. That’s Daintree. We’re the one with the never-ending traffic, a shopping centre called Woodlea Town, and a sunflower field that pops up once a year to remind Melburnians we exist.

A Saturday morning here looks like: you’ve finally fought your way through the Luxford Road traffic to get to Woodlea Town Shopping Centre, which Time Out said was “bursting with possibilities” back in 2021. Possibilities like a chemist, a supermarket, and a parking lot that’s always full. If you spot a sunflower in the distance, you’re at the Rockbank field—150,000 of them, all blooming for your Insta grid. But good luck getting a shot without a Ford Ranger photobombing you.

The local drama? According to the Whirlpool forums, the big debate is choosing between us and Tarneit. “The estate developer actually delivers on their promises,” one user said. “Traffic in both are horrendous.” That’s our claim to fame: a developer who doesn’t ghost you, and a traffic jam that makes you question your life choices. There’s also the Cheeky Monkeys Playgroup at Timbertop Children’s & Community Centre on Timbertop Parade, where mums probably swap tips on how to survive the morning school run without losing their minds.

Who would hate it here? Someone who wants a late-night coffee or a bar that isn’t attached to a shopping centre. Also anyone who thinks “quiet suburban bliss” means zero traffic noise. Spoiler: it doesn’t.

Who would love it? Families who want a Georgian-style house with a promise of a park that might actually get built. People who don’t mind a 45-minute commute to the city because, hey, at least the developer delivered the footpath. And anyone who needs a photo of 150,000 sunflowers to prove they went somewhere this year. Welcome to Aintree: we’re not a rainforest, but we’ve got a bloody good field. 🌻
