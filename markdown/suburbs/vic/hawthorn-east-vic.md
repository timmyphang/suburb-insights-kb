# Hawthorn East (VIC)

Slug: `hawthorn-east-vic`

Hawthorn East — where your morning starts with a life crisis: “Do I go to Le Flâneur for a siphon coffee and pretend I’m in a Wes Anderson film, or do I hit Love Athletica for a Reformer Pilates class that promises to give me the LA look of a ‘toned greyhound’?” The answer is always pastitsio.

Yes, we’ve finally got a Greek renaissance of our own. Voula’s Taverna just landed on Burwood Road, 160 seats, rooftop bar, and a saganaki menu section that serves flaming cheese four ways — including with a shot of Metaxa poured tableside. Owner Nick Flaounas named it after his mum, so you know the pettiness is baked in. Meanwhile, the old guard at the Auburn Hotel (formerly The Geebung, and yes we’re still processing that identity crisis) celebrated its 130th birthday with free Messina and a ripper Sunday session. Try getting that past the Love Athletica crowd.

The real local drama? Where to park your inflated sense of wellness. East End Wine Bar, housed in a 1930s auction house and co-owned by Demon’s ruckman Max Gawn, is now the place to see and be seen. And Pizza Religion on Camberwell Road is still slinging award-winning slices from a hole-in-the-wall — but directly across from the Rivoli Cinema, so you can argue about whether Deco Pizza Bar is actually better. We’ve got opinions, and they’re strong.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks a “private farm in Gippsland” is overkill for a cafe. Mr & Mrs Anderson’s menu nods to Nordic and South-East Asian cuisine, and its chef trained at Attica — so if you want a simple bacon and egg roll, maybe stick to the servo. Also, if you’re allergic to heritage overlays and Emma Tulloch Architects, look away.

Who would love it? Families who want tree-lined streets and Anderson Park picnics, foodies chasing the next big thing (Voula’s does traditional Greek breakfast, people), and anyone who’s ever uttered “but can we get a DGtek alternative to NBN?” We’re leafy, we’re prosperous, and we’ve got a Pilates studio that thinks it’s in Laurel Canyon. Get on board or get out of the way of the push-up planks.
