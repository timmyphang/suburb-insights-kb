# Spotswood (VIC)

Slug: `spotswood-vic`

We moved to Spotswood for the same reason everyone does: we got priced out of Yarraville but still wanted to tell people we live “near the river.” The West Gate toll is our rent. Every morning we queue at **Candied Bakery** on Hudsons Road for a cinnamon scroll so sticky it could double as a hair gel — as Urban List put it, “beware tackling this sugary number can get messy.” Then we walk past **Stan’s Grill** where they’re firing up cevapi on a tiny grill visible through the window, and we think “ah yes, Balkan sausage sizzle at 9am — that’s Spotswood.”

The **Duchess of Spotswood** is still the queen of the strip, though Broadsheet’s 2012 article says she started doing dinner with “ox-cheek braised in Guinness.” We’re pretty sure some of that Guinness is still braising. The couple who owns it lives upstairs — which means they can hear us arguing about whether to go to **Grazeland** or just stay home. Grazeland, by the way, is a permanent food truck park on Booker Street that Time Out calls “Melbourne’s ultimate foodie playground.” It’s got shipping containers, Greek doughnuts from Lokma Lokma, and free entry for kids under twelve. Which is good, because you’ll need that $20 for the toll home.

But not everything is sweet. A Whirlpool forum user once warned about a Spotswood street built on an “ex-toxic site,” which explains why our garden grows nothing but weeds and existential dread. And the **Spotswood-Kingsville RSL** — founded 1916 — is where we go to remember a time before the bakery hype. As Time Out notes, it’s still providing support to veterans. Respect.

**Who would hate it here?** Anyone who thinks “emerging food scene” means never hearing a truck engine. The industrial relics still hum. The West Gate traffic does not care about your pastry plans.

**Who would love it?** The person who wants a Balkan sausage, a Lebanese-twist knafeh croissant from **Creme de la Creme**, 200 craft beers from **Hudsons Road Wine & Beer**, and a view of the city skyline from a food truck park — all within 500 metres. Spotswood is the west’s sweet spot. It’s just a bit sticky.
