# Kallista (VIC)

Slug: `kallista-vic`

**Kallista, VIC — where the Dandenong Ranges meets the ‘I’m basically a mountain person now’ crowd.**

Look, we know what you think. You drive past the Kallista Tea Rooms on your way to the Puffing Billy run and think, “cute, but who actually lives here?” We do, mate. We live here. In a suburb where the biggest local controversy is whether the composting toilet at Larnook counts as “luxury” or “performative off-grid.”

Back in the day — and by that we mean 2022, which is basically ancient history up here — Broadsheet discovered Larnook, a shipping-container hideaway on Grantulla Road that promised “off-grid getaways where the camping-averse could escape the tedium of the daily grind.” Two shipping containers clamped together, a composting toilet, and an outdoor shower that apparently works just as well in frost as it does in summer. We’re still arguing about whether that’s aspirational or just expensive glamping with extra steps.

Speaking of aspirational: Alkira Eco-Glamping Retreat out on Kallista-Emerald Road (yes, it’s technically Emerald, but let’s not split fern fronds). That 40-acre farm with the straw-bale villa, the alpacas, and the gumboots? Emily Stygall’s family has been running it for decades. You can order a picnic basket from Mrs Schmick in Emerald if you’re feeling fancy. We just point at the vineyard and pretend we own it.

And then there’s Windmill Gardens — 175 Monbulk Road, which Sitchu called “vibrant and whimsical” for weddings. Nothing says “till death do us part” like a fern gully and a windmill. We’ve all been to at least three weddings there where the bride wore gumboots and the groom spent the reception staring at the solar panels.

Time Out once named Kallista one of Melbourne’s most popular suburbs. Which makes sense, if you think “popular” means “people drive here for scones and then leave.” The Kallista Tea Rooms have been doing that for years — locally sourced, organic, and the perfect spot to watch tourists try to parallel park on a hill.

**Who’d hate it here?** Anyone who thinks “glamping” is just camping with a credit card, or who expects a coffee shop open past 2pm. Also people who don’t own a puffer jacket.

**Who’d love it?** Wedding planners, compost enthusiasts, anyone who’s ever said “we really need to disconnect.” And sheep. Lots of sheep.
