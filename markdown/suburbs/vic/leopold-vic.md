# Leopold (VIC)

Slug: `leopold-vic`

Leopold, VIC – the Bellarine’s quiet retirement planning capital. We’re the suburb where the biggest local news in recent memory is that, as one Whirlpool forum user put it, “a fair few” lifestyle villages “have been built, at Leopold.” Thrilling stuff. Forget a bustling cafe strip – we have a postcode (3224, if you’re writing) and a playgroup with a 2023 copyright. That’s our cultural output.

Back when the data was fresher, you could head to the Leopold Playgroup website and maybe buy a tote bag? Now we just sit quietly while Geelong hogs all the spotlight. The only other Leopold in the news is a Belgian king from the 1800s or a character in a Netflix supernatural drama. Not exactly our brand.

Who would hate it here? Anyone looking for a trendy brunch spot or a night out past 8pm. We’re a suburb designed for the sensible art of downsizing – no stamp duty on those lifestyle villages, apparently. You want drama? Go to Torquay. We’ll be here, planning our 55+ future and wondering if the playgroup is still running.

Who would love it? Retirees who genuinely appreciate a quiet life, families who want space without the Ocean Grove price tag, and anyone whose idea of a big Saturday is a bit of lawn bowling followed by a council newsletter. We know we’re not the suburb they write about in Broadsheet. But we’re a good spot to settle down and wait for the next shampoo-and-set.
