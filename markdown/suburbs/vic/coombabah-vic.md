# Coombabah (VIC)

Slug: `coombabah-vic`

You know you’re in Coombabah when the most exciting local landmark is a six-acre garden centre that’s been growing petunias longer than you’ve been alive. Ross Evans Garden Centre, set amongst six lush acres with over 40 years in the game — half their stock is literally grown on site. That’s commitment. No need for trendy nurseries in New Farm when you can wander through an actual institution and buy a pot the size of your car.

Our Saturday routine? Hit the AWLQ op shop — as Urban List noted, every Gold Coast op shop crawl from Burleigh to Helensvale includes our local Coombabah branch. Then a bush walk through the Coombabah Lakelands: towering eucalypts, a full boardwalk, and if you squint hard enough, you might spot a koala. They’re shy. As are we, which is why you won’t find us on the crowded beaches — we’re kayaking Coombabah Creek, meeting Eastern Curlews and Brahminy Kites like some kind of David Attenborough remake.

Yeah, we know what they say — that we’re the forgotten northern pocket of the Gold Coast, the place you drive through to get to anywhere else. And they’re not wrong. Our 21-year-old from Coombabah made news for the wrong reasons — charged with GBH over a Brisbane coward punch attack. We don’t claim him. We do claim The Estuary at 2/38 Kangaroo Avenue though — property listings don’t lie.

Who’d hate it here? Anyone who needs a craft beer bar on every corner, or a brunch spot where the eggs benny costs more than your electricity bill. Who’d love it? Plant parents who want to spend a whole Saturday at Ross Evans, op shoppers on a mission, and families who know a good bush trail when they see one. We’re not flashy. But at least we’ve got six acres of ferns and a creek full of eagles to prove we exist.
