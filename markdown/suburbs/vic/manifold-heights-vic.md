# Manifold Heights (VIC)

Slug: `manifold-heights-vic`

There’s a moment on a Saturday morning in Manifold Heights when the entirety of Shannon Avenue holds its breath. You’re outside what used to be House Iberia — that Spanish deli with the terracotta flooring and the jamon Serrano that made us feel like we’d actually left Geelong — and you’re wondering if it’s still open. The article data is hazy on that. Back in the day, owner Rosie Mahoney turned a three-month Spanish holiday into a pincho paradise with Reverence Specialty Coffee, croquettes, and a Manchego cheeseburger that broke the internet (locally). Now? Frankie Say Relax at 139-141 is still there, selling coastal aesthetic and Robert Gordon ceramics from a shipping container origin story. And Mano Deli — the retro gem — sits at 137, serving "good old fashioned service" and mouthwatering menus according to Sitchu. But we say "according to" because we’re not sure they’ve updated their hours since 2022.

This is the drama of Manifold Heights: nothing happens here, and we’re fine with that. No council feuds, no barista war. Just a quiet strip where the biggest controversy is whether the paella at House Iberia is still being made. Spoiler: we don’t know. The concrete evidence is sparse. But we own it.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a 24-hour gym, a late-night kebab, or a reason to leave their house after 5pm. This suburb is for people who look at Newtown and think, “Too much going on.” For those who want a lifestyle store with Australian candles but no queue.

Who’d love it? You. If you remember the good old days of House Iberia and want to live in a place where the only thing you fight over is parking outside Frankie Say Relax on a Saturday at 10:01am. Welcome to the Heights. It’s quiet. It’s fine. It’s ours.
