# Hadfield (VIC)

Slug: `hadfield-vic`

**Hadfield, VIC – the postcode that spent 2020 in the news for all the wrong reasons, and has been keeping its head down ever since.**

We’re the suburb that existed in a permanent state of “Glenroy, Hadfield, Oak Park” like we’re the middle child nobody remembers. Every time the Premier opened his mouth, we were back in lockdown. 3046 had 65 active cases at one point – we were trendsetters before anyone knew what a trend was.

But here’s the thing: we quietly had one of the best cafes in the north. Middle Street Food & Coffee on Middle Street (creative, we know). Back when Simon Polkinghorne – apprenticed to Teage Ezard, no less – was churning out Allpress coffee, Dench pastries and a $25 Sunday roast that would make your nan weep. It doubled as a food store selling Jonai Farms pork and Warialda beef. Meanwhile, out the back, BBQ Bloke was catering your cousin’s wedding.

We also had Pehlivan, the family-run Turkish spot on Middle Street that we get possessive about. The room is small, the Adana is serious, and if you say you prefer a different Turkish restaurant within earshot, you’ll be escorted to the postcode border.

The big local drama? Our council was urged to rename itself because “Moreland” was named after a Jamaican slave estate. We went from “apparently named after a slave plantation” to “yeah that tracks” in record time. The Wurundjeri elders pointed it out, the council mayor called it “painful and wrong,” and we all nodded while wondering if we’d still get our bins collected.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who relies on a vibrant nightlife, or thinks “I’ll just pop into the city” is a casual 20-minute drive. It’s not. The Upfield line is fine if you’ve got time to read a novel.

Who would love it? Someone who wants a genuinely good cup of coffee, a reliable Turkish feed, and a suburb that weathered the pandemic with quiet dignity. We’re not flashy, but we’re not trying to be. And when the planes fly overhead to Melbourne Airport, we just wave. ✈️
