# Doveton (VIC)

Slug: `doveton-vic`

**Doveton, VIC — where the mail gets sent to the wrong place and nobody even bothers correcting it.**

Look, we know what you’re thinking. “Doveton? Isn’t that where Google Maps gives up and suggests you just turn around?” And yeah, fair cop. We’re the suburb that real estate agents describe as “adjacent to Hallam” like that’s a selling point.

Our claim to fame? Back in 2022, the nation got a good look at us when a TikTok went viral about a rental at 12 Oleander Street — a three-bedder with gas cooking and a private backyard that cost $1,280 a month. The internet lost its mind. We just shrugged. That’s the going rate for “landlord might fix the hot water by Christmas.”

But here’s the thing nobody tells you about Doveton. We’ve got the best kept secret in the south-east: the Doveton Pool in the Park. It’s basically a water park with a side of community Centre that’s been holding it down since the 70s. When the mercury hits 40 and everywhere else is charging $15 to sit in a puddle, we’re out here living our best lives on the slides.

And yes, we know what they say about us. We’re the suburb that makes Narre Warren look fancy. But you know what? We don’t care. We’ve got the Doveton Neighbourhood Learning Centre doing actual good work, and the Doveton Reserve where every Saturday you’ll see the full spectrum of humanity: kids playing cricket, dads pretending they could’ve made the AFL, and someone’s nan power-walking like she’s training for the Olympics.

**Who would hate it here?** Anyone who needs their latte art to look like a swan. There’s no “artisanal small-batch whatever” — we respect the bakery that’s been selling vanilla slices since 1985 and doesn’t apologise for it.

**Who would love it?** People who know that a $1,280/month rental in 2022 was actually a bargain. People who don’t need a culture war every time they buy milk. And people who appreciate that when you say you’re from Doveton, most Melburnians just nod politely and change the subject.

We call that freedom.
