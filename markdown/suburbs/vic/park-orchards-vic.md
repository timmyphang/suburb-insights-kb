# Park Orchards (VIC)

Slug: `park-orchards-vic`

Park Orchards, we are the water warning capital of Manningham. In August 2020, Yarra Valley Water rang us up to say, “Bit of a drama with the pipes, might need to boil everything for a few days.” And we did. We boiled our bubba’s formula, boiled our teeth-brushing water, boiled the kettle twice for good measure. And then we got on with it, because that’s who we are — a suburb that takes a "potential water quality issue" in its stride, then goes back to quietly enjoying our leafy blocks and the occasional fruit-picking trip up the road.

We’re listed in the same breath as Bulleen, Warrandyte, and Templestowe in the “Around Manningham” guide, but we rarely get our own paragraph. We’re the suburb that shows up in the 88-name roll call of boil notices, the "Playgroup Victoria" directory (shout-out to Park Orchards Community House, still going since 2020), and the Sitchu home search dropdown. That’s our three claims to fame. We don’t have a flash cafe that made the Broadsheet hot list. We don’t have a nightly brawl at the local pub. Our biggest scandal was when the water tasted a bit funny for a week.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs validation from a trendy brunch menu. If your weekend plans rely on a 4.8-star fusion restaurant and a personalised latte art, do not move to Park Orchards. You will be stuck boiling water and driving 15 minutes to Doncaster for a flat white. Also, if you think “community” means a street party, not a playgroup in a community house, you’ll find us very boring.

But who would love it? Families who want space, nature, and the kind of quiet that lets you hear your own thoughts. People who love the Yarra Valley fruit-picking season without the crowds of the tourist farms. And anyone who, when Yarra Valley Water calls, just boils the tap water and gets on with life. We’re the suburb that doesn’t need to be on anyone’s radar. And we’re perfectly fine with that.
