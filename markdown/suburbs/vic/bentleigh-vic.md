# Bentleigh (VIC)

Slug: `bentleigh-vic`

Saturday morning in Bentleigh and you’re already in a queue. Not for a smashed avo on sourdough – oh no, we’re classier than that. We’re lined up outside Artisanal Bakehouse for a garlic butter croissant that two French chefs brought here after swapping Michelin-starred Paris for the gentle hum of Centre Road. That’s Bentleigh in a nutshell: a suburb so calm it makes French pastry chefs retire early.

Our main drag was a foodie testing ground. Mr Burger opened their first concept restaurant here – a place to “test things”. Good Times Milk Bar gave away free burger buns to celebrate their offshoot. For a suburb that’s “family-friendly” and “leafy”, we’ve quietly hosted more culinary experiments than a MasterChef pantry. Friday was Neri’s Hummus Bar day. Saturday belonged to the croissant. And if you needed Russian groceries, Kazachok Deli had you covered. Even the locals at CK Foodstore near ALDI knew the best Chinese produce.

We’re not flashy. Bentleigh station scored 8.32 out of 10 in the RACV survey – one of Melbourne’s best. We’re proud of our underpasses, which a commuter once called “pure genius”. But don’t mistake calm for boring. When East Bentleigh copped flash flooding, we didn’t panic – we just waded to Seven Stars for the dim sims that are “the talk of Bentleigh”. Our biggest local controversy? Whether the steamed or fried dim sim wins. (It’s steamed, but don’t tell the fried loyalists.)

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a nightclub, a DJ, or a reason to leave the house after 9pm. Bentleigh’s vibe is “let’s get the kids to bed and watch Married At First Sight”. (Speaking of – MAFS 2025 couple Jacqui and Clint got married, but not here. We were too busy with the sorriso at Pronto 478.)

Who would love it? Families who appreciate a train station that doesn’t feel dangerous. Food nerds who want Roman street food via Pronto 478 without the Sydney prices. And anyone who understands that a quiet suburb with a French bakery, a Russian deli, and a Friday-only hummus bar is the real Melbourne. We don’t need a scene. We’ve got garlic butter croissants.
