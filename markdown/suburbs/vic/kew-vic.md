# Kew (VIC)

Slug: `kew-vic`

We know what you’re thinking: “Kew? Isn’t that where your nan moved after she sold the family home in Hawthorn?” And yes, we do have more Italian restaurants than parking spots, and every second car is a German sedan with a Genazzano scholarship sticker. But mate, we’re not just a leafy retirement village for private school parents — we’ve got layers.

Saturday morning starts at **Gilbert’s** for a yia yia-approved spanakopita and a $10 mimosa, because nothing says “I’m a relaxed local” like day-drinking before 10am on High Street. Then you pop into **Frank & Harri** for a croissant that costs more than your weekly groceries, admire the yellow espresso machine at **Percy’s Aeroplane**, and pretend you’re going to buy ethical, small-batch bacon from **Cannings Butchers** (you won’t — you’ll just stand there hoping they hand out samples).

The real drama? Our former state MP **Tim Smith** tried to rig a Facebook poll against Dan Andrews and still lost — a truly elite level of failing upwards. He later resigned after a DUI, because nothing says “member for Kew” like crashing the luxury sedan into a gutter on the way home from the pub. We’ve also had the **Studley Park Boathouse** get a $5.8 million revamp, which is just classic Kew: spend millions on a place to hire a rowboat and eat overpriced fish and chips while a magpie steals your chips.

Look, if you’re after a night out where the biggest decision is **Centonove** vs **Mister Bianco** vs **Di Palma’s** (which has been serving the same gnocchi since 1988 and we love it for that), then welcome home. And if you want a quiet omakase at **Sushi On** where you perch on velvet stools for 23 courses, go for it — but don’t tell anyone how much you spent. Kew is for people who want to live near the city but never have to deal with a tram full of uni students. We’ve got the grand trees, the old-school charm, and a butcher that tells you the name of the cow you’re about to eat. Come for the **Ora** coffee, stay for the absolute certainty that you’re better than everyone in Camberwell.
