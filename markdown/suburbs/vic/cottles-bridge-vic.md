# Cottles Bridge (VIC)

Slug: `cottles-bridge-vic`

We drive up past Eltham, past the mud-brick houses and the last of the bitumen, and suddenly we're in Cottles Bridge — where the Yarra still laughs and your phone signal definitely doesn't.

This is Dunmoochin country. Back in the day, painter Clifton Pugh set up an artist community at 105 Barreenong Road, and not much has changed since. The Yarra here is clean for swimming, if you don't mind the brown from suspended clay. Locals still float downriver in old tyres on hot days, because why buy a kayak when you've got a Bridgestone from 1992? As Broadsheet noted in 2013 (the last time anyone wrote about us), you can lounge in rock pools and mini rapids before heading to Montsalvat for coffee. Except Montsalvat is in Eltham, so really, Cottles Bridge is just the scenic route to somewhere with proper coffee.

We don't have a cafe. Or a shop. Or a phone tower. What we do have is a 145-metre tunnel that diverts the Yarra through a mountain at Pound Bend, because apparently regular river wasn't dramatic enough. And if you need art supplies, you drive 20 minutes to Victorian Artists Supplies in Eltham — an institution, but also proof we are not an institution.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who wants a supermarket within walking distance. Anyone who needs to check Instagram every five minutes. Anyone who thinks "artist community" means kombucha on tap and a co-working space. We don't have any of that. We have bush, mud bricks, and the distinct possibility that your neighbour is a painter who hasn't spoken to anyone since 2008.

Who would love it? Artists, hermits, people who genuinely enjoy floating in brown river water. If you've ever thought "I'd like to live in a place that looks exactly like it did when settlers first misheard the Wurundjeri word for 'laughing waters'", congratulations — you've found your suburb. Just bring your own coffee. And a tyre.
