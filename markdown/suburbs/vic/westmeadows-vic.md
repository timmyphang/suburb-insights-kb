# Westmeadows (VIC)

Slug: `westmeadows-vic`

Westmeadows, you absolute gem of a postcode. We’re the suburb that made it into Yarra Valley Water’s 2020 boil-water advisory alongside 87 other suburbs – and we’ve been trying to live that down ever since. As Broadsheet noted, we had to boil every drop before brushing our teeth. “Better safe than sorry,” they said. We’re still sorry.

But look, we’ve got layers. Whirlpool forums put it best: “Westmeadows can either be quite nice or an absolute ghetto depending on what pocket you’re in.” That’s not a warning, that’s a map. Stay off Erinbank Crescent unless you’re there for the adventure. Stick near Mickelham Road if you like your footpath free of drama.

Back in the day (2019, to be specific), we had West Espresso Brewers on Fawkner Street – a former barbershop turned coffee hub for cyclists and dog walkers. Those were the days of puppy-cinos and gelato on summer nights. We also had The Natural Pasta House bringing nonna-style pasta to the Autumn Flavour Exchange, and a restaurant with a dedicated prayer area during Ramadan, as Time Out confirmed.

And let’s not forget: John Tummino, the man behind Brunswick’s Madonna Electric pizzeria, grew up right here. His restaurant is filled with Franco Cozzo furniture and kitschy plates – nostalgia, not irony. That’s the Westmeadows spirit: we’re not joking, we’re just Sicilian.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “pocket” means a trendy wine bar. Who would love it? People who don’t mind a bit of character, a boil-water memory or two, and a community that knows exactly how to make thin-crust pizza feel like home. We’re rough around the edges, but the pasta is real.
