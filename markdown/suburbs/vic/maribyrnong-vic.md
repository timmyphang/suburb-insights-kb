# Maribyrnong (VIC)

Slug: `maribyrnong-vic`

We don’t just live in Maribyrnong — we survive it. You know you’re a local when you’ve had to explain to a visitor from the inner east how to pronounce our suburb. It’s “Marrah bah-nong”, not “Marry-burn-ong”. It’s also the place where a seal once showed up in the river to show off its fish-eating skills (2020, thank you Alicia Pavlis). Even the wildlife here has more stage presence than most of the coffee spots.

On a Saturday morning, you’ll find us at Anglers Tavern on Anglers Way — yes, it’s a pub with a kids’ play area, an iPad station, and enough trikes to keep your little ones entertained while you pretend you’re having an adult conversation. It’s the kind of place where the river views are nice, but the real draw is that your toddler won’t escape. Meanwhile, burger enthusiasts have to hunt down St. Burgs, hidden inside an apartment complex with no signage — basically an Easter egg for people who love smashed patties and poor directions. It’s been around for a while now, but we still treat it like a secret handshake.

Then there’s the drama. Remember October 2022? Flood-affected residents claimed the Flemington Racecourse flood wall — built 15 years prior — made the Maribyrnong River floods worse. About 30 homes evacuated, one death recorded. We’re still not over it. Meanwhile, our beloved Jack’s Magazine, a 1870s munitions storage facility, now hosts art exhibitions. Nothing says west-side gentrification like turning a gunpowder bunker into a sound installation space. And let’s not forget the truck-eating bridge that has its own action group. We don’t have a beach, but we have a bluestone wall and a lot of opinions.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who can’t handle a bit of industrial heritage, a flood risk, or the fact that Highpoint is our cultural centre. Who would love it here? Families who want a pub with a playground, runners who love the Maribyrnong River Trail, and anyone who thinks an abandoned munitions magazine is the perfect date spot. Welcome to Maribyrnong: we’ve got seals, burgers, and unresolved council grudges.
