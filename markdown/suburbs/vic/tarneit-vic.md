# Tarneit (VIC)

Slug: `tarneit-vic`

Welcome to Tarneit, 3029 — where the train station opening is the most exciting thing to happen since... well, the last train station opening.

We’re Melbourne’s fastest-growing frontier, but don’t mistake growth for polish. Our daily routine? A morning V/Line commute that’s one points failure at Sunshine away from a full-blown existential crisis. As one brave Whirlpool forum user put it: “the resulting chaos revealed serious failings of the railway’s systems.” Yeah, mate, we know.

Infrastructure is our love language. The government just unveiled final designs for a *new* West Tarneit station (October 2025, if you’re counting), which is great — we’ll finally have two stations to miss our trains from. Meanwhile, forum veterans are still debating “Tarneit vs Aintree” like it’s the new Civil War. The verdict? “Tarneit has better facilities and is closer to the station.” Small wins.

School enrolment here is a contact sport. Good News Lutheran requires you to submit an application *right after your child’s birth*. We’re not kidding — one new parent on Whirlpool was “so choked to see” that. Welcome to the birth certificate lottery.

Our other major cultural institution: Bunnings Tarneit. The click-and-collect bays have a maximum wait of 10 minutes. We brag about that. “There’s only about 3 or 4 cars waiting.” It’s our version of a farmers’ market.

Look, we know what they say about outer west suburbs. They’re not wrong — the traffic is awful, the V/Line is unreliable, and you’ll spend your first year fighting a school waiting list. But who else can offer you a new train station, a Bunnings that respects your time, and the smug satisfaction of knowing you picked Tarneit over Point Cook? Exactly.

Tarneit: we’re building our dream, one delayed train at a time. 🚂🏗️
