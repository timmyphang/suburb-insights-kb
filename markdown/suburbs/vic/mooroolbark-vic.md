# Mooroolbark (VIC)

Slug: `mooroolbark-vic`

Woke up this morning to the familiar sound of: a Yarra Valley Water alert telling us to boil our tap water. Again. Look, we know Mooroolbark made the list of 88 suburbs with “potential water quality issues” back in 2020, and frankly, we’re still not sure if it’s fixed. We just boil everything now. Our kettle hasn’t had a day off in six years.

For a suburb that makes the news mostly for being on lists nobody wants to be on, we do have one claim to fame that actually sparkles: Boyd Close. Every December, locals deck their entire property in a “winter wonderland section, beloved blow-up cartoon characters and metres of dazzling lights” — it’s actually worth the trip, even if you have to dodge potholes on the drive over. As Urban List says, “Experience a festive wonderland.” We live the rest of the year wondering why there’s no good restaurant.

The local drama? We’re not just about Christmas lights. In September 2025, a 23-year-old from Mooroolbark was arrested alongside a Balwyn man in relation to the Camp Sovereignty attack. Pedestrian.TV reported it. So yes, we have the bad boy energy to go with the community centre energy.

Speaking of which, the Mooroolbark Community Centre at 125 Brice Avenue is open late Tues-Thurs until 9pm, which is basically our nightlife. You want bottomless brunch? Head to Brisbane. We’ve got the community centre and a Whirlpool thread asking “Thoughts on Croydon, Mooroolbark and Kilsyth” for family homes near public transport. That’s our culture.

You’d hate it here if you’re after a “food scene”. The only omakase in Mooroolbark is whatever you find at the servo. You’d love it here if you enjoy a quiet life, low rent, and a Christmas display that actually makes the news. Just bring a bottle of boiled water.
