# Grovedale (VIC)

Slug: `grovedale-vic`

Grovedale, VIC — population: pit-stop chic.

You know the vibe. You’re bombing down the Surfcoast Highway, Torquay-bound, windows down, Saltwater Kid on the stereo, and you see the sign: Grovedale. You don’t slow down. You don’t even blink. But we locals know — this is where the coast begins to whisper, not shout.

The morning starts at **Booln Booln Café** (410 Surfcoast Highway, since August 2025). The Blak Brews crew set up shop here and suddenly Grovedale had a reason to exist beyond the Maccas drive-thru. Indigenous flavours, proper coffee, and a view that’s mostly a car park but we’ll take it. As *Time Out* noted, it’s “on the way to Torquay” — which is basically our whole identity.

Weekends mean the **Grovedale Community Hub** for a playgroup that’s “culturally safe, inclusive and welcoming” — because nothing says Saturday morning like a circle of parents pretending they’re not exhausted. The **Grovedale United Services Memorial Hall** hosts RSVP events, which is about as wild as it gets. Don’t ask what happened at the **Grovedale** pub during the Bulldog Gin G&T promotion back in September — we’re not entirely sure, but someone definitely brought a sugar skull.

**Who would hate it here?** Anyone who thinks a “night out” means options. Our dining scene is one Indigenous cafe and a pub that hosted a Day of the Dead event once. You want a 10-course tasting menu? Try Coogee. We’ve got the **Grovedale West Primary School** playgroup and a lot of roundabouts.

**Who would love it?** Families who know that the best surf beaches are 15 minutes away, and the best coffee is right here. People who appreciate a suburb that doesn’t pretend to be anything but what it is: a quiet, decent place to raise kids, with one absolutely banging café and a community hall that’s hosted every wedding, reunion, and elec‑tion barbecue since 1952. We’re not trying to be Torquay. We’re just glad you’re passing through — and maybe stopping for a pie.
