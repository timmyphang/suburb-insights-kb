# Kings Park (VIC)

Slug: `kings-park-vic`

**Kings Park, VIC — the other Kings Park. The one that doesn’t get 24,000 wildflowers or a Broadsheet guide. We’re the Kings Park that shares a postcode with St Albans and Kealba (3021 represent) and whose biggest claim to fame is a couple of Christmas light displays on Gillepse Road that once got a mention on Concrete Playground. Yes, we know what they say: “Kings Park? The one in Perth?” No. The one where your wheel repairer—Virgin Wheels, apparently a proper joint for smash repairs—has to close over Xmas because even the wheel guys need a break.**

**Our morning routine? Not a flat white at a botanic garden cafe. It’s a trip to the local KFC (yes, there’s one here—they did State of Origin burgers once) and then maybe a wander past the NBN node that Whirlpool forums still argue about. We’ve got bushland? Not really. We’ve got the quiet hum of the Calder Freeway and a lot of people wondering if they’re in Sydenham or Kings Park. (Pro tip: if your mail keeps getting delivered to Perth, you’re here.)**

**Who’d hate it here? Anyone expecting a park, a botanical garden, or any of the stuff that Time Out and Urban List rave about. We’re the suburb that exists in the shadow of a much more famous namesake. It’s like being the other Brad Pitt. There’s no Moonlight Cinema here—we have moonlight filtered through powerlines. The only “lightscape” is the glow from the petrol station.**

**But who’d love it? People who don’t need a lifestyle guide. People who know that a good Christmas light display on Gillepse Road is peak community spirit. People who appreciate a no-fuss wheel repair shop and a postcode that’s just close enough to the city to commute, but far enough to have a backyard. We’re not trying to impress anyone. Kings Park, VIC: we’re not the main attraction, but we’ve got your spare tyre sorted.**
