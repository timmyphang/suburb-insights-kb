# Dingley Village (VIC)

Slug: `dingley-village-vic`

We’re still not sure what’s stranger: that Dingley Village got its first inner-city-style cafe in 2017, or that it’s called Strange Servant and serves “bashed avo.” But here we are, queueing up on Centre Dandenong Road for a coffee brewed with Fitzroy’s Industry Beans while the blokes inside debate whether the mural by WA WA WA Designs is art or a warrant for a second latte.

Our morning routine is simple: grab a bashed avo (with baba ganoush, because why not), pretend we’re in Brunswick, then remember we’re 25 minutes from the city and about 10 from Moorabbin Airport. If the feed is still slow, we might pop over to Mr Patterson in Patterson Lakes – same owner, Sam Elmazi, but with dusty pink ceilings and “Gaytime ice-cream” on French toast. Very fancy for a suburb that still gets called “the village” unironically.

Petty local drama? Oh, we’ve got it. For six years we’ve been promised the Southern Hemisphere’s largest indoor-outdoor waterpark, Surf n Play. The developer Pellicano submitted plans in 2019. It got the green light in 2024, according to an Urban List update. But we’re still waiting on a wave machine that generates six waves per minute. Meanwhile, our other claim to fame was breaking news in 2023: two blokes from Dingley Village and Lysterfield got stabbed in the CBD. We made the Pedestrian TV site, so… thanks, I guess?

If you hate the roar of a USAF aircraft during your morning stroll, Dingley isn’t for you. Delta Force Paintball uses Hollywood-style props (including a real aircraft) and you can hear the explosions from our kitchens – adds a certain je ne sais quoi to your bashed avo. Also, avoid if you need a proper nightlife: our “vibrant” scene is a cafe that closes by 3pm and a paintball arena that’s probably better suited for corporate teambuilding than a Friday night.

Who would love it here? Families who want a lazy river that might open by 2027. People who enjoy being “unassuming” – our suburb profile on Sitchu literally lists us as a tag in other suburbs’ profiles. We’re the place you pass on the way to Mordialloc Beach. But hey, we’ve got bashed avo, a paintball field built for a movie set, and a waterpark that exists mostly in a real estate brochure. That’s village enough.
