# Gladstone Park (VIC)

Slug: `gladstone-park-vic`

You know you’re in Gladstone Park when your Google Maps is genuinely confused because the article data keeps sending us to Balmain, Dollis Hill, and Chicago. We’re not a suburb, we’re a glitch in the matrix.

Welcome to 3043, where the Whirlpool forum archive is basically our local history society. One brave soul asked “Is Gladstone Park (Victoria) a good suburb?” and the answer was a symphony of “pretty sure the bus loops through” and links to second-hand Yamaha speakers on Gumtree. That’s it. That’s our legacy.

The Sitchu home search results confirm we exist as a postcode between Aberfoyle Park and Gladesville, which is about the most exciting thing we’ve got. The concrete playground data? That’s in Sydney. The Time Out bowling club? Also Sydney. The giant tyre fire? Broadmeadows, not us, though we did feel the heat. The only Pedestrian TV shoutout we get is a fleeting mention of “Gladdy’s” for cheap eats, and even that might be in another Gladstone Park.

Back in the day, the Whirlpool forum had a theory: the bus loops right through on the main streets, goes one way to Broadmeadows station, the other to Airport West for a tram to the city. That’s the most detailed tourism guide we’ve got.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who arrives expecting a harbourside view, a duck pond, or a baroque painting on a subway wall. We’re a car-dependent pocket with a freeway on ramp, a postcode that copy-pastes into real estate searches, and a local chat that mostly revolves around whether the bus is running.

Who would love it? Someone who just needs a place to plug in a Yamaha receiver and speakers, a quiet corner with cheap rent, and a genuine appreciation for the fact that at least we’re not on fire (that was Broadmeadows). We’ve got the freeway right there, the bus goes somewhere, and the Gumtree listings are top-notch. Welcome to the park that isn’t a park.
