# Balwyn (VIC)

Slug: `balwyn-vic`

We see you, Balwyn. We see you with your Maranoa Gardens picnics and your quiet obsession with school zones — specifically, whether renting an apartment gets your kid into Balwyn High or if you need to actually fork out for a mortgage. The Whirlpool forums are full of you, trying to game the system.

Let’s set the scene. It’s a Saturday. You’re at Chai Garden on Belmore Road, sipping a kulhad of filter coffee and wondering if the Bombay sandwich is healthy enough to cancel out the fact you just spent $85 on a single-origin roast at the Proud Mary stand. Or maybe you’re sandwiched between a Pizza Hut and a kids art school at Little Nonna, where the menu is handwritten and the chef used to be at Vue de Monde. It’s all very “we’re refined, but we still love a panini that went viral during lockdown.”

Then there’s the drama. You remember when a DiDi driver allegedly “nearly abducted” a woman — by taking her on a series of random wrong turns towards Balwyn North? Yes, we know what they say about us. They’re not wrong. But also, we had a 32-year-old from Balwyn arrested in that Camp Sovereignty thing. So, we’re not just bougie — we’re occasionally newsworthy for the wrong reasons.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “leafy and refined” means exciting nightlife. Sorry, but Enoteca Boccaccio’s 400-strong wine list is as wild as it gets. If you can’t get excited about a black-sesame latte from White Mojo, or the fact that Eddy (the guy behind Regulars’ viral -85°C dirty coffee) roasts all his beans somewhere in Balwyn, you’re not ready for our pace.

Who would love it? Families who want tree-lined streets, a good playgroup at North Balwyn Baptist Church, and the quiet satisfaction of knowing your kid might get into Balwyn High if you buy the right house. We’re not flashy. We’re just… really, really into coffee and school catchment areas.
