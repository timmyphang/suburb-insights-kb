# Sandringham (VIC)

Slug: `sandringham-vic`

Port of Call at 7 Bay Road used to be a real estate agency, which tracks because the only thing we sell here is the dream of a quiet life. We’re the suburb where your morning routine is: grab a lemon & almond waffle at Port of Call, nod at the same three locals, then ponder whether to walk 90 seconds to Sandringham Beach or just stand in the carpentry workshop admiring the terrazzo floors. The owners behind Touchwood and Tall Timber brought their A-game to this ex-agency, so the coffee’s good, the cauliflower tacos are legit, and the mums with prams have formed a second committee (the first one meets at the Sandringham Playgroup at the back of the Community Centre, $2 donation requested).

Let’s talk transport. Our train line was ranked worst in Melbourne by a guy who clearly didn’t need a hobby. Barely any stations, feels unfinished. When a stray spark at Richmond shuts us down, we simply accept our fate and walk to the beach. We have a Museum of Play and Art now (co-funded by the Arts Council, because we needed more reasons to stay indoors), a Sandy Twilight Market on the foreshore, and Sparrows Bar for when you crave a proper cocktail and a stool that doesn’t wobble. But as Sitchu kindly noted: “the only things Sandringham lacks are restaurants and nightlife.” Yes. We’re outdoorsy. We bike. We paddle. We stare at the bay like it owes us money.

If you need a Tuesday dancefloor or a kebab at 2am, do not move here. You will shrivel. But if you want clean air, a local tiramisu shop (Tiramisu Galleria says hi), and the kind of beachside passeggiata that Italians would approve of, welcome. Just don’t complain about the train. We’ve already accepted it.
