# Bayswater North (VIC)

Slug: `bayswater-north-vic`

So Bakery Hill Distillery packed up its stills after 25 years and moved to Kensington. As Broadsheet noted, it took three years to relocate. Which means we’ve been living next to a literal heritage whisky operation for a quarter-century and most of us only found out when it left. Classic Bayswater North – we don't brag, we just let the good things quietly evaporate.

Our Saturday routine used to be: head down Canterbury Rd to Keith Fagg Tiles for some vintage bathroom inspiration, then pop over to Al’s Autoworks for a yarn about the car. Now Keith Fagg’s is a patchwork quilting shop (the tiles are still there, just gathering dust under fat quarters), and Al’s is still going strong – funny guy, that Alex. On the other side of town, Import Monster on Bayfield Rd knows their JDM cars back to front. If you need a part for your Nissan Silvia, we’re your suburb.

The biggest news in recent memory? A Wendy’s finally opened on the corner of Dorset and Canterbury. Yes, a square burger joint sharing a carpark with Guzman y Gomez is our moment of culinary glory. Meanwhile, one Whirlpool forum user kindly advised first-home buyers to “highly recommend Boronia instead – better shops, some good independent restaurants and cinema”. Fair call, mate. You do you.

We also made the list of 88 suburbs told to boil our water after a storm in 2020. Yarra Valley Water said the risk was low, but we still boiled everything for a week – even the kettle water. You can never be too careful.

Who would *hate* it here? Anyone hoping to tour a distillery on a Saturday afternoon. Bakery Hill is gone, mate. The only whisky you’ll find now is the bottle you bring back from Kensington. Also anyone expecting a thriving café scene – our local food review is literally a Wendy’s construction update from 2022.

Who would *love* it here? Someone who values a mechanic who knows his stuff, a tile shop that’s been frozen in time, and a community that doesn’t need a Kenzo Crawl to have a good day. We’re the suburb you drive through on the way to somewhere flashier, and we’re perfectly fine with that. The water’s safe again. Come for the quilting, stay for the patience.
