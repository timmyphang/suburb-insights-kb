# Sorrento (VIC)

Slug: `sorrento-vic`

We’ve got a rooftop bar at Hotel Sorrento that seats 320 people, which is roughly the entire summer population of the Mornington Peninsula who aren’t already lined up at the Conti’s new $100 million spa. Yes, we’ve become a “luxe resort” – just ask the Pitt family, who’ve run the pub for 40 years and now serve spritz carafes and lobster rolls with a retractable roof for when the bay breeze turns into a southerly buster. As Broadsheet put it, the rooftop is inspired by the French Riviera and photographer Willy Rizzo. Sure, Willy would have loved photographing Jack Nicholson here – but he’d probably be more fascinated by the bloke in board shorts arguing with his wife about whether the blueberry-vodka spritz is too sweet.

The real drama? The Conti. After a $100M+ makeover, six levels, five buildings, and a chef’s grandma-named fine diner (Audrey’s), they swapped Scott Pickett’s two-hatter for a bold new offering called Ember in 2025. That’s a 150-year legacy getting a menu refresh before you’ve even finished your first float at Aurora Spa. Meanwhile, the Sorrento Writers Festival just announced 155 events for 2026. Yes, 155. We’ve got more speakers than parking spots.

Who’d hate it here? Anyone who doesn’t own a Kia Sorrento PHEV (yes, we’re aware of the forum threads about towing capacity) or anyone who thinks “beachside village” shouldn’t require a $300-a-night hotel booking to get a slice of ocean view. Also, people who don’t enjoy a 90-minute drive just to hear Tina Arena’s “Sorrento Moon” played at the pub – because it will be, and we will all sing along.

Who’d love it? You, if your ideal weekend involves a 320-person rooftop, an igloo ice room at Aurora, and a writers festival where you can argue about literary theory while the surf pounds at Sorrento Back Beach. We’re the Mornington Peninsula’s version of the Côte d’Azur – if the Côte d’Azur had a retractable roof and a loyal Kia dealership. Come for the rock pools, stay for the $25 cocktail. We’ll save you a seat.
