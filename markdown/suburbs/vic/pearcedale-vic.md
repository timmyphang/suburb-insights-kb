# Pearcedale (VIC)

Slug: `pearcedale-vic`

We knew something was up when the bloke down the road started measuring his switchboard with the intensity of a man about to solve a cold case. Turns out, upgrading to three-phase power in Pearcedale is a journey. As one local hero discovered, United only gives you 63 amps per phase, no matter how many grow lights you’ve got in the shed. It’s a state-wide limit, apparently. So if you’re running a welder and a hot water system at the same time, good luck.

Our morning routine? A trip to Moonlit Sanctuary to see the dingo pack and a few cuddly pythons. Time Out said it’s worth dropping by, and they’re not wrong. It’s the one place in Pearcedale where the wildlife outnumbers the utes. After that, maybe a visit to the Pearcedale Public Hall for playgroup—because that’s where the real action is. The hall’s got more events than the main street, which is saying something given the main street has about three things.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “nightlife” means a bar open past 9pm. Or anyone who gets excited by a new cafe opening. We don’t get new cafes. We get new chook sheds. And we’re fine with that.

But you’ll love Pearcedale if your ideal Saturday involves patting a wallaby, not fighting for a park. If you think “public transport” is a bus that comes every 90 minutes—if you’re lucky. And if you’ve ever said, “I reckon I could wire up a three-phase myself.” Welcome home. Just don’t expect a banh mi.
