# Seabrook (VIC)

Slug: `seabrook-vic`

**SEABROOK, VIC — the suburb that’s more of a question than an answer.**

We are Seabrook. And honestly, most of Melbourne has never heard of us. That’s fine. We’re the quiet sibling of the western suburbs — no nightlife, no drama, and the only thing that’s ever made it onto a list is a single-level Airbnb called “Family Retreat Seabrook.” Urban List called it “ample room for the littlies to play without stairs getting in the way.” Which sums up our entire identity: we’re the suburb you choose when you’re sick of stairs.

There’s no cafe to name-drop here. No charcoal chicken hotspot, no Greek restaurant, no wine bar. Sure, there’s a “Seabrook” wine in Adelaide, but that’s not us — just a wine producer who stole our name. We’re what happens when a developer dreams of a peaceful estate and forgets to add a single destination. Our local heroes? The family who booked that Airbnb and posted a photo of their kid playing on the floor.

The misfits who’d hate it here: anyone looking for a “scene.” A coffee culture. A pub with a decent parma. If your weekend plan involves more than “maybe we’ll watch the neighbour’s cat walk across the driveway,” you’ll go mad. There’s no petty local drama — because there’s no one here to fight with.

But who would love it? People who mean it when they say “we just need a place to sleep.” Families with “small peeps,” as Urban List put it. Anyone who’s done their time in Point Cook traffic and just wants a single-level sanctuary. We’re not a suburb for stories. We’re a footnote in the Melbourne rental market. And honestly? We’re okay with that.
