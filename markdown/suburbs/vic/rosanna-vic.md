# Rosanna (VIC)

Slug: `rosanna-vic`

We’re Rosanna. Yes, we know what they say. “One of those ‘growth suburbs’.” Which is basically code for: we’ve got a train line, a Bunnings-sized roundabout, and a speed camera on Rosanna Road that’s caught more locals than the census. The Gatso at Rosanna/Darebin? It’s our most famous resident. We’d put a plaque on it, but we’re still paying the fines.

Look, we’re not here to pretend we’re some north-side enclave with a station-side micro-roastery. Broadsheet did a whole article about “station-side cafes” in 2013 – they talked about Hampton, Fairfield, Elsternwick. Not a single mention of Rosanna. We got a train station. We got a kebab shop. The closest we came to a coffee trend was when someone opened a window at the servo.

But we do have Sideshow Burgers – a no-nonsense joint from hospo pros Abhish Gupta and Julian Chew. No smashed avo on sourdough. Just burgers. And then there’s the gluten-free bakery that started in someone’s Rosanna kitchen. Urban List says they now have two storefronts and the Coeliac tick of approval. That’s us: from humble kitchen to two stores. Meanwhile, we’re still trying to figure out what to do with the empty shop next to the station.

Petty local drama? The Rosanna Road speed camera, of course. There’s a Whirlpool thread dedicated to trying to outsmart it. “I didn’t even know the area was 40,” one poor soul wrote. Mate, we all found out the hard way. It’s like the council’s way of saying “welcome to the growth suburb”.

Who would hate it here? Anyone looking for a station-side cafe with a courtyard where kids can watch trains. We don’t have that. We have a bus shelter and a lot of patience.

Who would love it? Anyone who understands that a “growth suburb” means you can still buy a house without a second mortgage, you can get a good gluten-free pie, and you know exactly where the speed camera is. It’s not flashy. But it’s ours. And we’ve got burgers.
