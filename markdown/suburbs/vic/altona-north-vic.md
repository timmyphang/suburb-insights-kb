# Altona North (VIC)

Slug: `altona-north-vic`

Altona North: where the West Gate Toll is still a rumour and the NBN has been “coming soon” since 2015.  

We wake up to the sound of trucks on Millers Rd and the faint hope that today might be the day we finally get fibre. Across town they’re sipping flat whites in Fitzroy. Here we’re at **Café Cubin** on Misten Avenue — a cabinet-maker’s showroom that doubles as a cafe, because nothing says Altona North like eating smashed avo surrounded by two Chinese warrior statues and a Day of the Dead mural. Coffee’s by Reverence, milkshakes are 1950s style, and kids and dogs are welcome. It’s the most genteel thing within 5km of a factory fire — and yes, we’ve had those. In 2018, a 100m x 200m blaze in West Footscray sent toxic smoke our way. The EPA said close your windows. We said “pass the bagel”.  

**Petty local drama**: Our parks have had asbestos in the mulch. And a creep was targeting women exercising in the west — specifically Newport and Altona North. So we added “don’t jog after 6pm” to the list of things we didn’t need to worry about in 2025. Meanwhile, the **Cliffhanger Climbing Gym** offers 27m walls as therapy. If you can get there without buffering — because our NBN is still on a Whirlpool forum thread from 2015 where someone’s on Optus 4G and it’s “barely 3mb in the evening”.  

**Who’d hate it here**: Anyone who thinks “proximity to the West Gate Tunnel opening 2025” is a selling point. Anyone expecting a park without a suspicious layer of mulch. Anyone who wants a direct bus to the city with USB charging — we got those in 2019, but they only go to Fitzroy.  

**Who’d love it**: Dessert fiends hitting **The Confectionist** for Dubai chocolate cannoli. Bouldering nerds at Cliffhanger. Families who know **Baby Sensory** classes run in Essendon, Williamstown, Footscray and here — because we’re the only suburb that’s both industrial enough for a bikie gang mention on Whirlpool AND wholesome enough for a baby development class on a Tuesday morning.  

We’re not trying to be Williamstown. We’re the place you drive through to get to the beach, stop for a sanga at **Matteo’s** (technically Altona, but we claim it), and then forget about. And that’s fine. We’ll be here, waiting for that West Gate Tunnel, one USB bus at a time.
