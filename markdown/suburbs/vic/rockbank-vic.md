# Rockbank (VIC)

Slug: `rockbank-vic`

Saturday morning in Rockbank and you’re standing in a sunflower field that looks like someone spilled a bucket of golden highlighter on the Western Freeway. 150,000 of the blooming beauties, part of the Blooming West Initiative at Woodlea. It’s the one time of year the rest of Melbourne remembers we exist.

Back in 2016, a bloke behind Entrecôte and Second Home thought, “You know what this nascent suburb of half-poured foundations needs? A Scandinavian café with rosewood, snow-white marble and an ‘Obligatory Smashed Avocado.’” So Go West opened at 27 Woodlea Boulevard. It served Rosso Coffee and Ottolenghi-style salads, delivered to your door via app. How very north-of-the-river. Of course, it’s permanently closed now. But we still have the sunflower selfies.

The real local drama? It’s not about who stole whose dog. It’s the Whirlpool forums. My wife argues that eventually the traffic will be heavier as Rockbank develops, but I think it would still not be as bad as the northwest. Mate, we’ve got train platform extensions coming at Cobblebank, Rockbank, Caroline Springs and Deer Park. Longer and faster trains, Time Out says. Meanwhile, parents are fighting for prep spots at BMG Rockbank Campus. Both schools mentioned they have been overloaded with applications. Half the kids in Woodlea will have to wait for the next estate phase.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks a “night out” means a rooftop bar with harbour views. We’ve got the Sunflower Field (seasonal), a permanently closed café, and plans for a massive shopping centre next to the station in Bridgefield – plans they’ve been talking about since the Serenity Rockbank forum threads. Come for the cheap land, stay because you signed a mortgage and it’s 35 minutes to the city on a good day.

Who would love it? Families who want a brand-new house in a display village that feels like a real town, eventually. Train geeks who love platform extensions. And anyone who just really, really loves sunflowers. We do. Even if the barista with the denim cushions has moved on.
