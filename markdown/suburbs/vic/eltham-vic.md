# Eltham (VIC)

Slug: `eltham-vic`

**Eltham, VIC — where the mud-brick never goes out of style and the gut health is now a full-time job.**

We’ve always been the suburb that makes you say “why have I never been here before?” — usually while floating down Laughing Waters on a lilo, trying not to think about the suspended clay. Ben Mendelsohn went to high school here. Emily Browning too. We’ve been punching above our artistic weight since Justus Jörgensen built Montsalvat in the 1930s, and Alistair Knox gave us buildings that look like they’re made of dried Weet-Bix.

These days, our morning routine is a study in contradictions. You start at **Second Home** — a renovated 1973 Alistair Knox warehouse that feels like a treehouse for grown-ups. You order the triple-cheese toast and the “famous” herby folded eggs, and if it’s the weekend, you flag down the *libations trolley* like a cocktail-crazed flapper. Then you pop next door to **Little Drop of Poison** for a pumpkin cake that’s “gluten-free but we don’t tell people that” — because nothing says wellness like lying about your cake.

But the real transformation? We’ve got a **BP/David Jones bougie servo** coming (yes, really), and **THERAPY** — a 5000-square-metre wellness powerhouse that opened in October 2025. Think cryotherapy, infrared saunas, hyperbaric oxygen chambers, and a restaurant where you can have hydrogen therapy with your lunch. Membership starts at $79 a week and goes up to $495 if you want to feel really, truly okay about your mortgage.

The drama? The old guard — the artists, the mud-brick purists, the people who remember when Eltham was just a bushland escape — are side-eyeing the new wave of gut-health disciples. “There’s a transformation brewing,” said the Sitchu article. Yeah, from “home among the gumtrees” to “vagus nerve stimulation with your smashed avo.”

**Who would hate it here?** Anyone who thinks wellness is a scam, or who can’t handle a cafe that also functions as an art gallery. Also, people who don’t own a lilo.

**Who would love it?** Artists who want a cheap studio, families who want a train line to the city, and anyone who’s ever wanted to combine a gin masterclass at **Naught Distilling** with a cold plunge. We’re still a quiet upgrade, but we’ve got a libations trolley and a whole lot of mud-brick. Come for the pumpkin cake, stay for the 5000-square-metre gut overhaul.
