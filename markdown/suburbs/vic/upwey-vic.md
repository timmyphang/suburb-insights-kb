# Upwey (VIC)

Slug: `upwey-vic`

Upwey — it’s the suburb where every visitor’s first thought upon cresting the hill is “how many koalas are hiding in those trees, and will I have phone reception to post about it?” We know the answer, and it’s *at least* three, and *no* — unless you’ve got the repeater tower pointed juuust right. As our Whirlpool forums eternally debate: “Cant receive FTA Channel 7? From the 3150 postcode you’re well away from the shadows of the foothills… no need for the repeaters at FTG and Upwey.” The irony is thick enough to paint the blue board on your new structure — yes, someone literally asked on Whirlpool for a “proper job for rendering blue board (New structure) in Upwey VIC.” We are a suburb that lives online, troubleshooting our own DIY.

Our Main Street routine used to involve a pilgrimage to **Pearl Garden** — that “leafy and hilly” spot Urban List swore by for decades of dim sims. Then **Maria** appeared: “couple Josh O’Brien and Omar Viramontes… combining Aussie brunch and traditional Mexican dishes.” That’s peak Upwey — a breakfast burrito baffling your mum while you duck into **Burrinja Gallery** (corner Glenfern Rd & Matson Dr) to stare at art that’s actually better than anything in the city. We are a culture pit stop between bushland and bottomless mimosas.

The petty drama? There is none—except the eternal question of whether the TV repeater or the PTV bus will fail you first. We don’t have a feud; we have a shared headache.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who expects a 24-hour supermarket, a bar open past 10pm, or a flat road. If your ideal weekend involves a tram that arrives every three minutes, stay in Richmond. We measure distances in “how many kookaburras you see before you hit the servo.”

Who would love it? People who want the Dandenongs without the tourist tax. Families who don’t mind driving for groceries, artists who actually use Burrinja Gallery, and anyone who thinks “cracking blue board render” is a valid weekend plan. We are the suburb where you can own a plot of land that feels like a national park, then complain on Whirlpool about your antenna. And we wouldn’t have it any other way.
