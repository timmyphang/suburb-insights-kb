# Ashburton (VIC)

Slug: `ashburton-vic`

**Ashburton, VIC — where the biggest drama is whether Joe Frank’s crumpets are worth the wait.**

Every Saturday morning, we do the same thing. Roll out of bed, wander across the road from Ashburton Station, and join the queue at Joe Frank. The siblings behind Mr Tucci and Son of Tucci took over the old Resident Cafe space, and now we all pretend we’re in Nonno’s garden while inhaling baba ghanoush at 9am. The house-made crumpets with mulled strawberries and zabaglione custard? Mate, we don’t share. You’ve been warned.

Back in the day, the big news was *Kiss With Style* at 281 High Street — a “refined Ashburton showroom” for designer homewares. It was the kind of place you’d buy a candle for someone you barely liked. Now? It’s a ghost of High Street retail past, alongside the lingering memory of Hyde n Seek in nearby Ashwood, where we used to get falafel wraps in a bike-themed cafe. The Urban List called it “homely.” We called it Tuesday.

The Hot Chocolate Festival comes to “around Victoria, Ashburton” according to Time Out, which is about as specific as our council’s plans for the golf course. We know what they say about us: the quiet cousin of Glen Iris, the suburb you drive through to get to Chadstone. They’re not wrong. But they missed the bit where we have a dog-friendly courtyard, a train station that actually works, and zero pretension.

Ashburton is not for you if you need a late-night scene, a Sourdough Kitchen (that’s in Seddon), or a Nico’s Sandwich Deli (CBD). It’s for the people who want to eat crumpets named after someone’s dad, pat a dog, and be home by 11am. Welcome to the middle. It’s honestly fine.
