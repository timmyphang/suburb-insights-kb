# Mount Martha (VIC)

Slug: `mount-martha-vic`

Saturday morning in Mount Martha: you’ve just wrestled a park near the South Beach boxes, slathered on the SPF 50, and mentally rehearsed your “no, we’re not leaving the Pillars until sunset” speech. The water’s that unreal aquamarine that makes you forget you’re only 45 minutes from the Westgate Bridge. But the traffic on Nepean Highway? That’s the real magic trick.

For years, we’d grab a coffee at Cibo on Lochiel Avenue — the upmarket Italian deli Bianca Fazzari opened because she was sick of fish’n’chips. As chef Jett Alexander once said, the banana bread at Cibo Deli was “unrivalled.” We’re still chasing that high. And if you’re a vanilla slice purist, Mount Martha Fine Foods had the thickest custard this side of a maths textbook.

Back in 2023, our primary school made national news when it banned hugging. Yes, kids were getting injured from too much love. We still don’t know whether to be proud or concerned. Meanwhile, The Briars turned into Hogwarts for a few months in 2024 with the Harry Potter Forbidden Forest Experience — locals suddenly found themselves surrounded by wand-waving muggles hunting nifflers. We let it slide because the normal tourist traffic to The Pillars is chaos enough.

If you can’t handle sand in every crevice of your car and a summer queue that takes longer than a school council meeting, Mount Martha will break you. The luxury stay at Martha Beach House costs $1000 a night — and that’s eight people sharing. The master ensuite alone is a mini day spa, but the mortgage to buy here is a nightmare.

But if you want a beach that Tourism Australia ranked eighth in the country for 2026 — complete with those iconic coloured huts and a swim spot that feels like the Med — then welcome. Just watch your back at drop-off. The no-hugging rule is still unspoken.
