# Caulfield North (VIC)

Slug: `caulfield-north-vic`

We know what they say about Caulfield North — that it’s one of those south‑eastern suburbs you could drive through for years without noticing. But between the heritage corners and the leafy walks, we’ve quietly become the most alluring pocket of Melbourne you’re not visiting enough.

Look, a Saturday morning here is a masterclass in peaceful competition. You’ll see the Baker Bleu queue snaking out 119 Hawthorn Rd before 7am — 48‑hour fermented sourdough that restaurants like Attica and Gimlet get shipped in. Over at 98 Hawthorn Rd, Banksia is serving tuna crudo with stracciatella and coffee oil from a former Cutler chef who almost quit the industry. And if you’re after something a bit more sentimental, Ruzia’s Wine on Balaclava Rd is a Polish‑Jewish love letter to grandma’s chicken meatballs with kasha — the kind of place where the uncle bakes the Basque cheesecake and the niece’s dad curated the wine list. We don’t do loud. We do refined and slightly offbeat.

Now, let’s talk drama. Woolworths Caulfield North decided to go cashless — a trial that risked privacy and created poverty, according to Junkee. Then there’s 193 Balaclava Rd, home to Probe Group, where residents on Whirlpool report being chased for debts that aren’t even theirs. As one forum user put it: “stranger danger writes... I can’t think of what I could be owing.” Classic.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a pulse after 9pm. Our bars are tiny and mostly only open three nights a week. If you want a rowdy club, you’ve missed the point.

Who would love it? Food nerds who appreciate a chef who says “fine dining says seafood and cheese don’t go together — well, that can’t be true.” Families who want a bakery that does a crookie and a kids’ menu without chicken nuggets. And anyone who likes a suburb that doesn’t try to compete — because we already won.
