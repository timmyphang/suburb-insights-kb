# Glen Waverley (VIC)

Slug: `glen-waverley-vic`

We remember when Glen Waverley was just the place you went for a mediocre food court feed before catching the train. Now look at us. We’ve got a former Kisume chef serving teishoku breakfast sets at Andzero on High Street Road — no avo toast in sight, just house pickles, onsen eggs and Wagyu hamburg. We’ve got Jerry Mai’s Bia Hoi at The Glen, where you can grab a three-litre beer tower and pretend you’re in Hanoi while the kids fight over ramen at Ramen Ramen (get the matcha soft serve swirl for $4, you’ll thank us later).

But let’s be real: we’re still the suburb where the NBN goes down so often you’ve memorised the iiNet 4G backup reboot sequence. And if you think our local drama is just about parking at The Glen, you clearly missed when Glen Waverley FC made the “Biggest Scandals of 2022” list — yes, our local football club had to release a statement “saddened and disappointed” at their own players’ antics. As Pedestrian TV put it, the train line is full of “school children and normies” — but at least you won’t find “chaotic people” on it. Unless you count the bloke who tried to ride his motorised scooter down the Monash Freeway in 2019. He ended up at Glen Waverley Police Station. Legend.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “Asian food” means just one cuisine. Glen Waverley has more Japanese restaurants than Glen Iris has roundabouts — Niku Shiki, Marble Yakiniku, Wagyu Ya with its licensed Kobe beef, Kariton’s Filipino, The Boiling Crab’s Louisiana-style. If your idea of dining out is a steak and salad at a pub, mate, you’re lost. Also, if you need a functioning NBN connection to work from home, good luck. As one Whirlpool user noted: “NBN internet down – Melbourne, Glen Waverley – although the NBN Modem shows all four lights.” Classic.

Who would love it? Anyone who wants to eat their way through Asia without leaving the south-east. The Glen is basically a food court that grew up and got ambitious. We’ve got frozen yoghurt shops doing proper burgers (YOMG, we see you), slick teppanyaki spots, and a pho steamboat that can feed four. Sure, the BYD dealership in Glen Waverley is now taking orders for the Atto 2 — the electric future has arrived, and our NBN might still be down, but at least we can charge the car. Glen Waverley: come for the ramen, stay because you can’t get a train to the city on weekends.
