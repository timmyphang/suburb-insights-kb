# Hampton Park (VIC)

Slug: `hampton-park-vic`

We know what you’re thinking: “Hampton? Must be nice, with the beach and the $8 lattes.” Wrong suburb, mate. This is Hampton **Park** — the one where the closest you’ll get to a marina is the puddle in the M420 bus shelter after it rains.

We’re the cheaper, sprawlier, ducted-heating cousin that gets left off the map. As one Whirlpool forum noble put it: “Hampton Park is pretty average, public transport is limited but if that's more affordable it's not the worst place to live.” High praise from Australia’s harshest critics.

Our claim to fame? Air conditioner sizing debates. Head to the local forum and you’ll find a single-storey 12-square house asking if a 15KW Braemar unit with seven ducts will do the job. That’s our version of coastal luxury.

Yes, the developers keep trying to sell us as “Hampton adjacent.” No, we don’t have a Kalye Marinas seafood boil or a Cornerstone & Co. — that’s the *other* Hampton, the one with Ludstone Street and artisan pastries. Our local bakery? The one that’s been “opening soon” since 2019.

We’ve got the Bunnings, the industrial estates, and the occasional pest that blows in from Cranbourne. But you know what? The rent is (kind of) still reasonable, the neighbours keep to themselves, and the 15KW Braemar keeps the place toasty.

You’d hate it here if you expect beachside glam or a tram line. You’d love it if you just want a roof, a back yard, and the quiet satisfaction of proving the Whirlpool critics wrong.
