# Fairfield (VIC)

Slug: `fairfield-vic`

We used to just rock up to the Grandview Hotel for a cold beer and a parma before heading into the city. Now, behind that 1888 facade, there’s charcoal smoke, pomegranate, and arak — and the family that’s owned the pub since 2000 has opened Sama, a Lebanese restaurant where the hospitality “doesn’t whisper. It feeds you.” As co-owner Norah El-Fahkri put it, “as the area shifted – with Station Street thriving and High Street expanding, the venue had to evolve.” So yeah, we evolved straight into a $65-per-person banquet with lamb nayyeh scattered with pistachio and rose petals.

Saturday morning means a queue at Oasis Bakery on Station Street for za’atar bread with beetroot tahini and a Turkish coffee. Then you grab a box of baklava from the grocery section and a Kinder Bueno doughnut (International Doughnut Day, anyone?). If you’re feeling fancy, you swing by Thaali — the second outpost of Ishu Wadhawan’s North Indian spot, where they do Fairfield-only naan with jalapeno and burrata. MasterChef’s Andy Allen dropped in for a thali and papri chaat and stayed for hours. We get it.

But here’s the drama: the old Grandview regulars are still out front nursing a schooner, wondering where the parma went. Meanwhile, the rest of us are signing up for Wildsoul Wellness’s new seventh studio — reformer, contrast therapy, red light pod, and compression boots. It’s the closest Wildsoul has come to the CBD, and we’re not sure how we feel about having a $200 Akme Beauty voucher giveaway when the servo across the road still sells $5 dim sims.

You’d hate it here if you just want a quiet beer without a side of pomegranate molasses or a wellness discussion. You’d love it if you want to eat your way from Lebanon to Chandigarh to Brunswick East without leaving the 3078. As Broadsheet noted, “Northcote too hectic for you? We have just the thing.” We’re the chill cousin who got a glow-up and a charcoal grill. Come for the jalapeno naan, stay for the arak.
