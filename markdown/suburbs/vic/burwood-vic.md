# Burwood (VIC)

Slug: `burwood-vic`

Let’s be honest, Burwood East is the kind of suburb that makes you do a double-take. A rooftop farm on a shopping centre? Quail coops and worm farms above a Dan Murphy’s? That’s the sort of thing we expect from Fitzroy, not a place where the biggest local controversy is Telstra signal at Westfield.

Our Saturday morning: hit Rombe for farm-driven fare with views of the Dandenongs – never mind that you’re technically parked on top of a shopping centre. Or duck into Eastwood on the corner for a plate of lamington hotcakes so pillowy you’ll need a lie-down after. Later, take your pick from the roaming yum cha carts at Gold Leaf. Yes, we have a “second Chinatown” energy, but with suburban parking.

The petty drama? We had a whole 2500-square-metre rooftop farm at Acre. It grew its own strawberries, quail eggs and micro-greens. And then, like a bad hangover, it closed. Permanently. January 2025. Now we’ve got Rombe in its place, which is lovely, but we still haven’t forgiven the universe for taking away those $5 kids meals from Urban List’s Eat Street launch back in 2021.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who despises the smell of compost on a warm day. Or needs reliable phone reception at Burwood Westfield – good luck with that, Telstra. Also, if you’re a tradie who thinks a “rooftop farm” is code for a car park with extra steps, you’ll be fuming.

Who would love it? Families who want good schools (PLC, Deakin Uni), a train station that might actually exist one day (SRL East, we’re waiting), and a Sunday where you can eat yum cha from Gold Leaf’s roaming carts without leaving the car park. Burwood East: we’re a little bit country, a little bit shopping centre, and permanently confused about what decade we’re in.
