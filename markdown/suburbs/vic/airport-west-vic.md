# Airport West (VIC)

Slug: `airport-west-vic`

We don't have planes landing in our backyards — that’s Tullamarine’s problem. But when a 737 rattles your windows at 6am, you learn to live with it. Airport West: where the suburb’s name is its most honest feature. We’re the bit between the tarmac and the housing commission flats, and we’ve got the tram route 59 to prove we exist.

A Saturday morning here? You start at the Backhaus Bakery factory on Hood Street for a 48-hour fermented sourdough that’s so good it gets shipped ten minutes down the road to Essendon. Then you grab a bag of arancini from Melbourne Arancini — run by Paul and Laura Muscara, who turned a family Christmas tradition into a permanent spot at 33A Hood Street. Six clicks from the terminal, and a million miles from the airport’s overpriced toasties.

Need a tint on your Merc? Sam’s Window Tinting is your bloke. Timber for a deck? Essendon Timber used to be the go-to before Bunnings priced everyone out. And if you’re after a “niche little adult store in the most random spot in Victoria” — well, Sexyland has been serving Airport West and Tullamarine since someone decided you need a vibe before a flight.

The drama? We were “mainly working class” — now suddenly we’re “commanding high prices” thanks to proximity to the CBD. The real locals watch the Jaecoo demo cars roll into the showroom and wonder if the new owners will still buy evaporative cooler parts from the Cool Breeze distributor. Probably not.

Who would hate it here? People who want a quiet life. You can’t escape the hum of engines, and the tram sometimes stops because of a “police request” on Route 59. Also anyone who thinks a 48-hour sourdough should cost less than $12 — sorry, that’s Essendon pricing now.

Who would love it? Anyone who likes their suburb honest. We’ve got a bakery that started as a wholesale business in 2022, a family-run Italian joint that’s been dishing out arancini since 2016, and an adult store that’s been called “hyper-niche” by Pedestrian. We’re not trying to be the next hotspot. We’re just Airport West — and that’s exactly the point.
