# Campbellfield (VIC)

Slug: `campbellfield-vic`

Campbellfield: where the air smells like industrial waste and freshly fried dough 🍩💨

We don’t have a main street. We have *Cooper Street* – a glorious corridor of factories, tyre joints, and the occasional beacon of hope: **That’s Alotta Donuts** at 882 Cooper. Urban List told Australia we’re an “undiscovered foodie destination” and we’re still laughing. Discovered? More like you’ve driven past us on the Hume and thought “hope I don’t get stuck at the lights.”

The local gossip? We had a massive industrial fire at 16 Thornycroft Street. 100 firefighters, black smoke over Broadmeadows. The factory was Bradbury Industrial Services – a hazardous waste joint that already had its licence suspended. Classic Campbellfield: we make the news for being on fire, not for our food scene. But hey, we got a mention in Broadsheet – turns out **Perla yerba maté soda** is *made here*. Elderflower, lemon myrtle, zero per cent alc, one hundred per cent industrial park energy.

We were *supposed* to get an El Jannah in 2023 – Pedestrian TV said so. Still waiting. In the meantime, we’ve got Campbellfield Market, which Time Out says “reopens after a nifty facelift”. We’ll believe it when the car park’s not a pothole maze.

**Who would hate it here?** Anyone who thinks “mixed-use zoning” means a nice café next to a pharmacy, not a donut shop next to a waste storage facility. Also people who don’t like the smell of burning plastic on a Tuesday morning.

**Who would love it?** The person who sees a factory fire as character. The one who knows that behind every corrugated iron fence is a gem like Perla’s production line. The true northside warrior who can navigate the roundabouts of Camp Road without losing their soul.

Campbellfield: we’re not pretty, but we’re on the map. And at least our donuts are alotta. 🍩
