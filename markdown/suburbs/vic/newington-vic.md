# Newington (VIC)

Slug: `newington-vic`

So you’ve moved to Newington, Ballarat. Congratulations — you now share a postcode with a ramen shop in Sydney and a historic armory complex you can’t actually visit. We’re the quiet corner of town where TPG and Optus come to die. Ask anyone here — the Whirlpool forum thread on reception is basically our local newspaper. “Just moved to Newington, what’s everyone’s reception like?” Answer: two bars if you stand on the bin near the letterbox.

But don’t write us off. Once a year, Victoria Park transforms into something unrecognisable — Strummingbird Festival rolls into town and suddenly our dead-zoned streets are full of people trying to stream Post Malone on 3G. October 10, 2026 — mark it in your calendar (and screenshot your tickets, because you won’t be loading them at the gate).

Other than that? We’ve got Kosuke Ramen in Sydney’s version of Newington — but hey, we can claim it as ours on a technicality. We’ve got local legends who know every pothole on the way to Ballarat CBD by heart. And we’ve got that sweet, sweet quiet.

Who’d hate it here? Anyone who needs to make a phone call. Any delivery driver who types “Newington” into Google Maps and ends up in a shipping container beer garden in Petersham. Any influencer who can’t upload a mirror selfie.

Who’d love it? Festival-goers who like a little bush atmosphere with their bass drops. People who think “reception issues” is a small price to pay for a cheap house. Locals who know that the best thing about Newington is the bit where nobody’s quite sure which Newington you mean — and you’re happy to keep it that way.
