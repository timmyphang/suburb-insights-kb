# Yering (VIC)

Slug: `yering-vic`

We live in Yering, which is basically a wine-soaked fever dream where your neighbours are alpacas and the postcode has more cellar doors than letterboxes. Yes, we’ve got the prestige — Yarra Yering got crowned Australia’s number one winery for 2026, which is nice, but let’s be honest: the only reason you’re here is to stumble between tasting rooms and pretend you know what “tannins” means.

A typical Saturday? You wake up at The George — that luxury farmhouse with six bedrooms, balcony views, apple trees and grazing alpacas. You head to Yering Farm for a “pruner’s platter” because apparently that’s what we call a cheeseboard now. Then you roll over to Chateau Yering (250 acres, feels like a 19th-century time warp) for a Melba Suite and a charcuterie board that costs more than your car. But the real local drama? Yarra Valley Dairy got fined over nine grand by the EPA for its “nasty smell.” Yes, our cheese factory stank so bad the government had to step in. That’s Yering: world-class pinot, but don’t breathe too deep near the dairy.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who doesn’t drink. Also people who want a decent coffee after 3pm — good luck. And if you’re not on a wine tour, you’re basically trespassing.

But who would love it? The person who wants to say “I stayed at Chateau Yering” while wearing linen. The designated driver who gets to watch everyone else slurp their way through Yering Station’s historic cellar door. And definitely the type who thinks a farmers market (established 1999, still going strong) is a weekend highlight. We don’t have much — but we’ve got alpacas, vines, and the faint whiff of dairy controversy. Cheers.
