# Laverton (VIC)

Slug: `laverton-vic`

So you're scrolling realestate.com.au and you stumble on Laverton. $350k for a house. “Zone 1?” you whisper hopefully. Then you spot it: Aircraft station, rated the second-worst train station in all of Melbourne with a score of 3.43 out of 10. As Broadsheet reported back in 2020, South Kensington was bottom, but Aircraft was right behind it – and we’ve never let it live down. That’s our welcome mat.

We know what you’re thinking: “Why is Laverton so cheap?” As a Whirlpool forum legend once asked: “Anyone knows why Laverton cheaper?” Mate, we have answers. It’s cheaper because you get the dulcet hum of planes overhead, the occasional aroma from the industrial estates, and a train station that commuters ranked worse than Broadmeadows. But here’s the bit they missed: Laverton North has a little jewellery workshop where you can learn to make a precious item with no more than five other people, as Urban List noted. We’ve got quiet creativity hiding behind the servo fridges.

Misfits: Don’t move here if you need a express train every five minutes, or a latte that costs more than your rent. If you flinch when someone says “Aircraft” and think of a runway, not a suburb identity, this isn’t your stop.

Welcome to Laverton if you want to buy in before the developers sniff around but also don’t mind a bit of grit. We’ve got cheap houses, a train station that’s only second-worst, and a secret community of jewellers making art in an industrial shed. It’s not much, but it’s ours – and you can afford it.
