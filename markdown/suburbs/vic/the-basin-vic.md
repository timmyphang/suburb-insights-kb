# The Basin (VIC)

Slug: `the-basin-vic`

You know you’re from The Basin when you tell someone where you live and they go, “Oh, the camping spot near Palm Beach?” No. That’s the other Basin. The one with the snorkelling and the ferries. Ours is the one where the Mountain Highway winds up through the fern gullies and you’re only a lung-busting bike ride from Sassafras.

We’re basically the quiet, unassuming gateway to the Dandenongs. If you’re a “serious cyclist only”, as Concrete Playground once warned, you’ll know our stretch of Mountain Highway intimately. The rest of us just wave from the car as we head up to the pie shop in Sassafras.

For years, our claim to fame was being the suburb with the name that sounds like a sink. Not a trendy one — just the basin you clean your toothbrush over. But we own it. We’re not flashy. There’s no Rebel Rebel restaurant here (that’s in Canberra). No beachfront cocktails. No Havza pop-up. We’ve got bushland, a train station, and the distinct feeling that if you blink you’ll miss the main street.

Who would hate it here? Anyone looking for a destination. We’re not a destination; we’re the suburb you pass through to get to the real destination. Also, cyclists who aren’t serious. Don’t attempt that Mountain Highway climb if you’re a newbie. Seriously.

Who would love it? People who like their suburb quiet, green, and a little bit forgotten. People who don’t need their morning coffee delivered by a barista with a curated playlist. We’re The Basin — unpretentious, leafy, and happy to be mistaken for somewhere else. At least we’re not Bents Basin. That one floods.
