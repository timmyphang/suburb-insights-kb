# Kealba (VIC)

Slug: `kealba-vic`

We’re Kealba — the 3021 suburb you’ve never heard of but definitely drove through on the way to somewhere else. Our biggest claim to fame? Getting named in a July 2020 COVID lockdown alongside St Albans and Kings Park. “Hot zone” status was the most attention we’d had since the post office got a digital scale.

Back in the day, our local highlights were… well, that’s the thing. Concrete Playground and Time Out only ever mention us as part of a postcode list. Broadsheet wrote about cafes in Richmond, Sydney, Brisbane — not a single flat white poured in Kealba. The only time we made the news was when we had to stay home. And frankly, we didn’t miss much.

Our morning routine? There’s no Ritual Espresso or artisan bakery. We’ve got a servo and a bottle shop, and the most heated local debate is whether the 408 bus actually comes or if it’s a government prank. The petty drama in Kealba is who forgot to put their bin out — again. We don’t have street art or laneway bars. We have powerlines and a roundabout that takes you to Sunshine.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who thinks “vibrant dining precinct” means more than one kebab shop open past 9pm. If your Instagram captions rely on exposed brick, communal tables, or single-origin pour-overs, you will starve. You’ll also get weird looks for wearing activewear to the post office.

Who would love it? People who want a quiet life, a backyard big enough for a Hills Hoist, and zero fear of missing out — because there’s nothing to miss. We’re the suburb you forget to mention when listing western suburbs, and we’re okay with that. We’ve got good footpaths for walking the dog, and the neighbours mind their own business. Plus, when the next lockdown hits — and it will — we’ll be ready. We’ve had practice.
