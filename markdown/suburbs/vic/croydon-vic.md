# Croydon (VIC)

Slug: `croydon-vic`

We know what you’re thinking: Croydon? Isn’t that the grey bit on the train line between Ringwood and Lilydale where the air smells like wet lawn clippings and deferred maintenance? Correct on all counts, but stay with us.

Every Saturday morning, we perform the same ritual: press our face against the window of MrT Deli on Main Street, waiting for them to perfect the art of the thick sanga. As Urban List noted back in May 2026, they’ve been kicking around for a few years now, and we’ve made it our life’s mission to try every single focaccia, bagel, and breakfast bun that passes through those doors. It is, unironically, the only unifying force in this suburb.

The real drama? Try finding a park near the station on a weekday. You will circle Luxford Road until you question every life choice that brought you here. Then you’ll slink off to The Cellar Door by The Public Brewery — a three-story temple to Yarra Valley hops that we genuinely claim as our own. It’s the only place where you can get a proper craft beer and watch three kids go feral on a slide at the same time. As Urban List says, “this unique venue is the latest brewery from Croydon favourite The Public Brewery.” We like that they called us a favourite.

Look, if you need high-end retail or a barista who remembers your name, you will hate it here. There is no ‘scene’. There is just the reliable hum of the M3, the occasional Christmas light display on Mulgrave Way that runs nightly from December 1st to the 26th, and the eternal question of whether the chicken parma at the local will ever be as good as the one you had last time.

But you know who would love it? People who understand that a suburb doesn’t need a festival to have personality. People who respect a long-term sandwich deli. People who know that when you say “I’m from Croydon,” what you really mean is “I’ve found a place that’s perfectly fine, and I’m not leaving.”
