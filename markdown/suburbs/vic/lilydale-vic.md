# Lilydale (VIC)

Slug: `lilydale-vic`

Lilydale, VIC — home of the hidden platform pub, the Yarra Valley’s forgotten gateway, and more identity crises than a teenager on the Lilydale to Warburton Rail Trail.

We know what you’re thinking: “Lilydale? Isn’t that a chicken brand?” Yes, mate, and also a place where you can knock off work, hop on the 3:08pm from Flinders, and be at a heritage-listed pub on the actual station platform by 3:48pm. The Lilydale VRI bar and museum has been there since 1914, open every Friday 3–8pm, and we’ve been keeping it to ourselves. Until TikTok found out. Now there’s queues at the bar between train announcements. Thanks, internet.

Our mornings? A toss-up between a ride along that 40km rail trail (Northlane even wrote a song about the “tough, muddy terrain”) or grabbing a raw salted caramel tart from Lilydale General. And if you’re fancy, Madeleine Butter in Lilydale is making cultured butter from grass-fed Jersey cows that’ll ruin supermarket butter for life. Yes, we have artisanal butter. No, we haven’t forgotten the 2020 water warning when Yarra Valley Water told us to boil everything. That’s just how we roll — gourmet butter one year, gastro risk the next.

Petty local drama? Try the Lilydale High School kids trying to navigate the new station construction while the old one slowly decomissions. Or the constant confusion between Lilydale VIC and Lilydale TAS — no, we don’t have Four Pillars Gin distillery, that’s Healesville. But we do have a Hello Harry on Main St and a York on Lilydale that “has a little bit of everything”. Including, apparently, Mt Evelyn residents.

Who’d hate it here? Anyone who needs a late-night train after 8pm. Or anyone who can’t handle the smell of spent cooling liquid from stills — wait, that’s Healesville. Our version is the faint whiff of diesel from the rail yard and the perfume of Lilydale Showgrounds after a busy weekend.

Who’d love it? Cyclists, butter snobs, heritage pub enthusiasts, and anyone who likes the idea of a city fringe town that still remembers when it was just a railway stop. We’re Lilydale — a bit rough, a bit buttery, and hiding a pub on the platform. Come for the rail trail, stay for the… well, the 3:08pm train back.
