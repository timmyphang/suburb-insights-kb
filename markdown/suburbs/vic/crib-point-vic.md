# Crib Point (VIC)

Slug: `crib-point-vic`

We call Crib Point home. You’ve never heard of it? That’s fine — we prefer it that way. We’re the bit of the Mornington Peninsula you drive through on the way to Stony Point, wondering if you missed the turn-off for a decent coffee.

Our claim to fame? Fighting an AGL gas import terminal with all the fury of 54 artists and a fundraising exhibition called "Art is in our Nature." David Larwill, Rosie Weiss, Juan Ford — they all joined the campaign. We took on Big Gas with watercolours and a petition. As Time Out noted, we’re "80km from the CBD" and we’ve got penguins, dolphins, seals and mangroves worth protecting. They called it a "devastating impact on the environment." We call it Tuesday.

Our morning routine? Back in 2020, the foreshore campsites were closed "until February 2021" — so you’d find us at the council-run site at Crib Point Stony Point, trying to pitch a tent without shared toilets. The Shire CEO John Baker worried about "transmission risk." We worried about having to explain to tourists that yes, this is a campground, no, there isn’t a bar. Just the bay and the bracing wind.

The local real estate? A Whirlpool user once noted land around here is "worth about 110-150K" and that a house has "a lot of renovation potential." We call it character. You call it a fixer-upper. We call it ours.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting a Rosebud-style beach strip with ice-cream shops and pedestrian crossings. Anyone who needs a battery charge on their phone to Google "cafes near me." We don’t have a Ritual Espresso or a Luxford Road. We have the Westernport Bay, the gas terminal protest signs, and the quiet satisfaction of being a suburb nobody puts on their home-search list.

Who would love it? People who own a tent, a sense of purpose, and a deep appreciation for a place that will fight a multinational corporation with art. People who understand that "foreshore camping" means you might have to BYO everything except the view. And people who don’t mind that our biggest news story in years was a fundraising exhibition.

Crib Point: we’re not for everyone. But we’re for someone. And if you want to camp here, check the council website first — because we’re still figuring out the toilets.
