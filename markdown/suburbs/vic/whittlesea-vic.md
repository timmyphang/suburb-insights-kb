# Whittlesea (VIC)

Slug: `whittlesea-vic`

Whittlesea — where the big drama isn’t the council being put under administration in 2020, it’s whether your rates notice arrived in July or August. We’re still waiting, council. And you didn’t even reply. Rude.

But look, we get it. We’re the suburb that’s 40km north of Melbourne but feels closer to a tropical resort — as long as that resort is Funfields. Opened in 1985 as the Alpine Toboggan Park, we’ve since evolved into a water wonderland with a $10 million upgrade that brought us the Lava Lagoon Lazy River. Two million litres of water, 5000 tonnes of earth shifted, wave generators from Scotland. We even have Volcano Beach, Melbourne’s first outdoor heated wave pool. So yes, we’re basically the Gold Coast, minus the property prices and with more palm trees.

Speaking of property — good luck changing your street address. One of our locals bought 1 Seville Close and wanted to switch it back to 1 Flanders Street. Council? Crickets. We’re still trying to figure out if we live on a close or a street, but honestly, we’re too busy floating down the lazy river in our VIP cabana.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks a theme park should have a cafe serving single-origin pour-overs. We don’t do that. We do bumper cars and a 137-metre water slide called Typhoon. If you want a flat white, go back to Fitzroy.

Who would love it? Families who remember coming here as kids in the ‘80s and now want to relive the magic while their own children splash around. Also, anyone who enjoys a good council drama thread on Whirlpool. That’s our culture.

Welcome to Whittlesea. Bring your rates notice, a towel, and a willingness to accept that your street name might change without warning.
