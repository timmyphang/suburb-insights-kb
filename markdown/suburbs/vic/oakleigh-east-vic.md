# Oakleigh East (VIC)

Slug: `oakleigh-east-vic`

Oakleigh East, VIC — we’re Oakleigh’s quiet, responsible sibling who’s always got a spare nappy and hasn’t had a proper night out since Y2K.

Our morning routine is a carefully calibrated logistics dance: drop the kids at **Guardian Childcare & Education on Ferntree Gully Rd** (25 out of 28 centres have vacancies, so there’s always a spot), then speed past the turn-off to Oakleigh’s famous Greek strip because we have to get back for the 10am nap window. The average cost of $154.26/day? That’s our contribution to the local economy. You’ll find us at **Oakleigh Early Learning Journey** comparing trim levels on our people movers, not ordering $17 taramasalata with Yarra Valley caviar.

Speaking of which — just over the boundary, Oakleigh got a rooftop wine bar called **Olympia** back in the day, from former DJ Nick Flaounas. As Concrete Playground noted, “Greek restaurants are a dime a dozen in the diasporic heartland of Oakleigh, but Greek wine bars are a less common phenomenon.” Us in Oakleigh East? We got the dime-a-dozen childcare centres. The only rooftop we’re converting is the one over the Hills Hoist.

The petty local drama? That **New York Times** shoutout for a “humble gyro from Oakleigh” did not mention Oakleigh East once. Not once. We’re the silent, pram-pushing majority who keep the suburb running while Oakleigh gets all the press. The Broadsheet guide lists **Nikos Quality Cakes** (since 1987), **Mythos GR**, **Kalimera Souvlaki Art** — all Oakleigh. We have to drive a whole 2km for a loukoumades fix.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting the Greek-island vibes of Olympia’s vinyl DJ booth and olive-tree-lined rooftop. As Time Out put it, “the outlook of power lines and palm trees are decidedly Melbourne” — and in Oakleigh East, it’s more power lines and triple-storey townhouses called “The Olympia” but with no rooftop.

Who would love it? The person who looks at 28 childcare options and thinks “finally, choice.” The family who knows that “Greek influence” in Oakleigh East means the takeaway souvlaki from **Orexi Souvlaki Bar Café** after soccer on Saturday. The local who quietly enjoys the peace while Oakleigh hogs the spotlight. We don’t need a retractable roof — we’ve got a retractable baby gate.
