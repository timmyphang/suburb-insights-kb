# Richmond (VIC)

Slug: `richmond-vic`

We’ve been waiting for the day Richmond got its own dippable sanga drama, and May Bakes on New Street just delivered a “pho-wich” that makes us forget every other dipping beef situation. Shio pan, pork pâté from Nguyen’s mum’s recipe, and a side of northern pho broth so rich you’ll want to move into the bakery. But let’s be real — we’re not here for the 2026 food trends alone. We’re here for the contradictions that make this suburb a beautiful, glorious mess.

Yes, we’re the suburb where AFL players either get caught leaking nudes or brawling and then ask the club to pay their $75k COVID fines. We’ve got the Tigers, the MCG, and the full post-match tsunami down Swan Street to Piccolina Gelateria. We’ve also got Nixie Nox — Richmond’s smallest pub, which opened in April 2025 to prove you can have a beer garden and a balcony without being a multi-level monstrosity. Meanwhile, Flour Child on Bridge Road is slinging 22 pizza combos and gnocchi that make the Richmond Club Hotel’s century-old institution look like yesterday’s news.

But let’s talk about our real identity crisis: we’re the inner-east that embraced the safe injecting room trial before most suburbs would even say the words. No nimbyism here — council and traders backed it, because we know what’s what. We’re also the suburb where Hamish Blake and Zoë Foster Blake dropped $4.25m on a Grand Designs home back in 2013, then sold it, because even multimillionaires can’t resist the pull of Victoria Street’s gritty charm.

If you hate footy scarf wall art, drunken post-game crowds at every pub, and the smell of pho and brake pads mingling, Richmond will break you. But if you want to live 10 minutes from the CBD, get your coffee from Black Gold Richmond, and watch an actual pho-wich debate unfold on your high street, welcome home. Just don’t ask us to park.
