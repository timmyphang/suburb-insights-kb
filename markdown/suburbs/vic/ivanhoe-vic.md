# Ivanhoe (VIC)

Slug: `ivanhoe-vic`

You know a suburb’s hit peak Ivanhoe when your Sunday morning starts at Lucci for the family-run sourdough, then you grab a focaccia from Baba’s Deli (soft opening Wednesday 13 May, apparently), and by arvo you’re queueing at Fairy Hills Ice Cream for one of their seven Royal Melbourne Fine Food Award-winning scoops. All before you’ve even touched Upper Heidelberg Road.

But let’s be real—we’re not just leafy green tranquillity and osteopath-founded day spas (shout out Relinque Urban Retreat & Spa at 19 Upper Heidelberg). We’ve got drama. Remember when All Are Welcome in Ivanhoe East called it quits in June 2025, owner Boris Portnoy allegedly owing staff $243k in super and $125k in leave? The landlord locked them out on a Tuesday evening. Very on-brand for a suburb that also got a cease-and-desist from Kanye West over College Dropout Burgers. Yes, a burger joint with a self-described “Inspired By Ye” mantra had to shake up its entire menu after Ye’s lawyers came knocking. Esteemed Ivanhoe, indeed.

Vinoshis used to be the cosy wine bar in the old Biviano and Sons grocer—winemaking couple Jasmine and Yuri pouring natural Sicilian drops and showing photos of volcanic vineyards. Lip Cafe on Waterdale Road was the Middle Eastern-meets-Malaysian gem we kept to ourselves. And L’Artigiano on Upper Heidelberg? Still there, we think. It’s hard to keep track when businesses have the lifespan of a sourdough starter.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who doesn’t appreciate that our local gelato has seven awards, our day spas are run by osteopaths, and our biggest scandal involves unpaid super and a global rapper’s legal team. If you can’t handle the intersection of hamptons-chic pub grub and Kirribilli-style ice cream queues, maybe stick to the city.

Who would love it? Families who want a quiet tree-change without leaving Melbourne. Foodies who can name every bakery closure. People who unironically use the phrase “self-care” and mean the full Relinque package. And anyone who thinks a suburb with a burgery that got Ye-d and a bakery that went bust owing a quarter mil is just a little bit iconic.

Welcome to Ivanhoe. We’ve got plants, pilates, and pending litigation.
