# Helensvale (VIC)

Slug: `helensvale-vic`

Look, we know what you're thinking — "Helensvale, VIC?" And fair call, because Helensvale is about as Victorian as a Byron Bay chai latte. But between you and us, this Gold Coast hybrid suburb doesn't mind living rent-free in your head. It's the northern end of the glitter strip where the cool cafes are "surprisingly few and far between," as Urban List so gently put it. Which is why we're clinging to Bear Boy Espresso like a life raft — terracotta splashes, potato waffle benedict, and a vibe that makes you forget you're a 15-minute drive from anything remotely edgy.

A typical Saturday? Up at 7, grab a Blackboard Coffee from Funnybone Coffee over in The Surrounds, then realise you've got two hours to kill before the kids are done at the indoor playground. Head to Helensvale Westfield — where our library is currently camping out, like it's on a gap year. Pop into Alpha H skin clinic for a facial that costs more than your weekly shop, because if you can't afford a Palm Beach address, at least you can afford to look like you belong there.

Petty local drama: Remember when the Swell Sculpture Festival plonked a five-metre inflatable head that was half Homer Simpson, half Ancient Greek poet outside the Helensvale Library? We had the one cultural event of the year, and it was basically a meme on Main Street. Also, the Whirlpool forums are still arguing about whether we're a "quiet family suburb" or a "ghetto to avoid." Honestly, we're both — you'll find a $2.5 million Queenslander next to a housing commission block, and everyone's just trying to navigate the tram smart-ticketing trial without missing their stop.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting late-night energy, a laneway cocktail bar, or a public transport system that doesn't require a Go Card and a prayer. This is a place where your biggest decision is whether to hit the NightQuarter food market or just go to bed at 8pm.

Who would love it? Families who want a big backyard, good schools, and the assurance that their car won't get keyed at the shops. Also, people who genuinely believe that a Bear Boy coffee and a quick facial is a perfect Saturday. It's not trying to be Coolangatta. It's just Helensvale, QLD — and we're fine with that.
