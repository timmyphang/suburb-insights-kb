# Wollert (VIC)

Slug: `wollert-vic`

We’ll be real — you haven’t thought about Wollert. That’s fine. Most Melburnians haven’t either. We’re that suburb that appears as a ghost postcode in your search filter when you’re trying to buy a house in Epping and accidentally scroll too far. Wollert 3750 — the “far up” north where, as one Whirlpool legend put it, “it’s all new estate cheap and has NBN”. And honestly? They’re not wrong.

Our claim to fame is a bloke who did full hotel quarantine in South Australia, came back to his Wollert home on May 4th, and then tested positive for COVID. That’s it. That’s our headline. No trendy bars, no op shop guides, no dumpling roundups — Broadsheet wrote about fashion in Melbourne and Perth instead of us. Time Out talked about burgers and time zones instead of us. Even Sitchu just listed our postcode next to Windsor like we were an afterthought.

But we have our own soul. Every Tuesday morning you’ll find the mums at ONE Wollert playgroup, running on cold coffee and the hope that the kid doesn’t scream before snack time. If you need a real community fix, Creeds Farm Living & Learning Centre is the beating heart — a neighbourhood house that serves both Epping and us, because apparently we don’t have enough foot traffic to justify our own. They do playgroups, courses, all that good stuff. It’s basically our town square, minus the square.

Who would hate it here? Anyone looking for a “vibe”. There is no skating rink, no listening bar, no champagne video store nostalgia. If your weekend plans involve “popping into a small batch ceramics studio” you are lost. This is suburbia in its rawest form: new estates, roundabouts, and the eternal question of when the next chicken shop will open.

Who would love it? People who look at a block of land and see potential. People who don’t need a breakfast cafe to feel alive. People who are okay with their suburb’s Google result being a single COVID article and a playgroup link. Because that’s Wollert — we’re new, we’re cheap, we’ve got NBN, and we’re still figuring out the rest. Come for the affordable house, stay for the Creeds Farm craft group.
