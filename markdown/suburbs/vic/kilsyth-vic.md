# Kilsyth (VIC)

Slug: `kilsyth-vic`

We knew Kilsyth was a quiet achiever when the biggest local controversy was a Yarra Valley Water boil-water alert after a storm. Yep, we were on the list of 88 suburbs told to boil our tap water because “some water may have entered our drinking water supply network that had not been treated to the usual standard.” Classic Kilsyth – even our water supply is low-key dramatic.

Back in the day, if you wanted a creative outlet, you’d head to SaJo Ceramics at 11 Lillypilly Lane in Kilsyth South. A pottery class in a lane named after a flower – that’s about as bougie as we get. Meanwhile, the Whirlpool forums tell the real story: one prospective buyer “cooled off on the area simply because the access to public transport wasn't as good as other areas” and ended up in Croydon North. Oof. They’re not wrong – you need a car and a solid sense of adventure to navigate our bus timetable.

But don’t sleep on us. We’ve got basketball. As Junkee reported back in 2025, Lexi Rodgers wrote in her diary about driving to Kilsyth straight after an eight-hour shift to play. “It will be a gameday, and restrictions will force me to cheer my teammates from the bench as an inactive player.” That’s the Kilsyth spirit – turning up even when you’re benched.

Who’d hate it here? Someone who expects a train at their doorstep and a cafe that serves flat whites with a side of irony. Also anyone who panics when the water gets a bit dodgy for an afternoon.

Who’d love it? Pottery enthusiasts, basketball die-hards, and people who appreciate a suburb that keeps its head down, boils its water when told, and lets the Croydon North folks have the public transport. We know what they say – and they’re not wrong. But they missed the part where we’re just quietly getting on with it, one clay mug at a time.
