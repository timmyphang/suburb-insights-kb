# Boronia (VIC)

Slug: `boronia-vic`

Welcome to Boronia, where the local economy runs on three things: pies, development speculation, and discarded shopping lists that end up as NGV installations.

Our morning ritual? Head to Country Cob Bakery at 951 Mountain Highway—if you can find a park. The carpark is always full, and for good reason. This place has been crowned Australia’s best pie multiple times (pepper-beef, seafood-satay, caramelised-pork-and-pepper, and Cambodian fish amok). We don’t say “how was your day,” we ask “pie or vanilla slice?” And yes, the custard-to-pastry ratio is near-perfect.

After that, maybe a walk up Boronia Peak Walk through Messmate forest—if you can handle a steady climb. Or if you’re Kenny Pittock, you spend your teen years trolley-pushing at the local supermarket, collecting shopping lists. Now his ceramic replicas are in the NGV. That’s the Boronia hustle: turn trash into art.

Petty local drama? Optus once started pulling down their HFC network here and the Whirlpool forums went into meltdown. We’re a suburb that gets genuinely invested in cable removal. Also, there’s a never-ending debate about whether the Belgrave line development is a blessing or a curse. “Boronia is getting a lot of development,” one forum user noted. Yes, we know—we’ve been hearing that since 2016.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who doesn’t like pie (good luck), people who want a nightlife beyond the bakery, and snobs who think outer east is too far from Collingwood. Also, anyone allergic to the word “development.”

Who would love it? Pie pilgrims, playgroup parents (Haering Road Playgroup has been running since 1999), hikers who don’t mind a bit of Messmate, and aspiring artists collecting shopping lists. We’re a suburb that’s rough around the edges, obsessed with baked goods, and proud of our weird little achievements. Come for the Pepper Beef Pie, stay for the council debate.
