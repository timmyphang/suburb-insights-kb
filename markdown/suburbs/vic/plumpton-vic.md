# Plumpton (VIC)

Slug: `plumpton-vic`

Plumpton, VIC — we are the fastest-growing suburb you’ve never heard of, and we’re not sure whether to be proud or put a second driveway in.

Time Out just told Australia that Fraser Rise–Plumpton added 4,300 people in a year. That’s basically a small country town moving into your neighbour’s back shed. Our streets are a blur of brick veneer, scaffolding, and For Sale signs that go up faster than the render dries. Every block is either a new estate or a paddock that’s about to become one. Whirlpool forums call us “Deanside” and “Kororoit” depending on which side of the drainage ditch you’re building on. We don’t have a town centre — we have a Target Country and a dream.

Come Saturday, you’ll find us at the real local attraction: 319 Leakes Road. That’s the Christmas tree farm Sitchu raves about. We load a $320 pine onto the roof of the Prado and pretend we’re rural. Back home, we unpack it in a living room that still smells like glue from the floorboards. The kids play at the Plumpton Children’s and Community Centre while we stare at the estate plan and wonder if they’ll ever build the promised park.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks a suburb needs a pub, a cafe, or a reason to leave the house after 6pm. Anyone who gets anxious when every second car is a dual-cab ute with a trailer full of Laminex.

Who would love it? Families who want a four-bedroom house under a million and don’t mind driving 20 minutes for a loaf of sourdough. People who believe “community hub” means the Bunnings car park on a Saturday morning. And anyone who’s ever said “Melbourne’s growing too fast” — because we are the growth. Buckle up, we’re bringing 4,300 new neighbours. And they all need a coffee shop.
