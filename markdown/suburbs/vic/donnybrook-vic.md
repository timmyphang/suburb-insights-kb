# Donnybrook (VIC)

Slug: `donnybrook-vic`

Donnybrook, VIC — we don’t have a main street, but we do have a bottleneck. And a train station that scored 3.44 out of 10 for “safety, comfort, convenience and access.” That’s right: we were voted the fifth worst station in Melbourne, just ahead of Broadmeadows and Hastings. We wear that like a badge of honour, because at least we’re not South Kensington.

Back in the day, when the housing estate dreams were still fresh, a Whirlpool forum user summed us up: “Donnybrook is out the middle of nowhere with a bottleneck in & out, like Point Cook. It's a growth suburb with limited public transport etc.” They weren’t wrong. They also missed the bit where the bottleneck is basically our town square — where you can wave at your neighbours while crawling towards the Hume. It’s community.

The data says we’re one of Melbourne’s most vacant-suburb hotspots, along with Craigieburn, Kalkallo and Mickleham. So yes, there’s plenty of room for all our opinions on the colour of the new brick veneers. And because we’re a growth suburb, the only local business most of us know is the servo that sells overpriced milk. As for a cafe? Broadsheet never reviewed one here. But we did get a mention in a Time Out article about vacant homes, so that’s something.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a reliable train, a footpath that goes somewhere interesting, or a coffee that isn’t from a 7-Eleven. Also: people who object to staring at bare paddles waiting for the next housing stage to drop.

Who would love it? People who enjoy a blank canvas — literally, because the development’s still being painted in. People who think “limited public transport” is code for “peace and quiet.” And anyone who wants to be part of a community that’s still deciding whether we’re a suburb or a very large car park.

Welcome to Donnybrook, 3064. We’ve got the bottlenecks, the vacant homes, and a train station that makes South Kensington look like a five-star hotel. But hey — at least we’re not Point Cook.
