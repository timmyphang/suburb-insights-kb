# Toolern Vale (VIC)

Slug: `toolern-vale-vic`

**Toolern Vale, VIC — where the dingoes outnumber the baristas and the only flat white you’re getting is from a Bunnings sausage sizzle.**

We woke up in postcode 3337 back in July 2020 and Broadsheet decided we were one of Victoria’s hardest-hit COVID suburbs. 79 active cases, they said. But here’s the thing — when you live on the outskirts of Melton, you don’t measure community by case numbers. You measure it by how many people you see at the Dingo Discovery Sanctuary on a Saturday arvo. And let’s be real: the only queue we’ve ever had is for the booking-only tour of 40 acres of state forest. As Time Out put it, “meet some of Australia’s most adorable native fuzz balls.” That’s our culture mate.

There is no Union Road. No Bay Street. No Maribyrnong River Trail. There is dirt. There is sky. There is a dingo enclosure that’s been a bigger drawcard than any café in the western corridor. The closest we get to a “brunch scene” is a packet of Shapes and a can of Mother at the servo on the highway.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “village vibe” means a laneway latte and a vintage store. If your idea of a weekend is browsing boutiques, don’t bother. We don’t have boutiques. We have paddocks and a healthy respect for apex predators.

Who would love it here? People who understand that “community spirit” is not a marketing line — it’s knowing exactly which neighbour’s dog will chase your car, and which dingo is the boss of the pack. We took COVID on the chin, we showed up for the dingo tours, and we’re still here. Unpretentious. Wild. Ourselves. Come for the sanctuary, stay because your GPS can’t find the way back.
