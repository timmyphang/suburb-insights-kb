# Ardeer (VIC)

Slug: `ardeer-vic`

Ardeer, VIC — we don’t have a high street. We have Ballarat Road. And if you’re looking for a morning routine, ours involves a pilgrimage to the 14,252-square-metre Costco at 740 Ballarat Road, praying the 18-pump petrol line isn’t longer than the checkout queue. Back in the day, Brimbank Council had to demolish an existing warehouse just to make room for our temple of bulk-buy. That’s the closest thing we’ve got to a heritage listing.

Yes, we know what they say about us: Ardeer is where Melbourne’s west goes to buy a year’s worth of toilet paper and then disappear. The only “nightlife” is the fluorescent hum of the warehouse and the occasional Aldi carpark showdown. But we own it. When we want a night out, we drive past the old industrial blocks and thank our lucky stars we don’t live in a suburb with a “best new bar” list.

Petty local drama? The suburb made the news when a 20-year-old local was arrested alongside two others in relation to the Camp Sovereignty attack. We don’t sugarcoat it – that’s the kind of headline you get when your postcode is best known for a giant concrete box and a post office that closes at 2pm.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “charcoal chicken” is a personality trait. People who want a coffee shop where the barista remembers your name, not just your membership number. If you need “vibes” and “laneways”, Ardeer is not for you. We have roundabouts and a lot of gravel.

Who would love it here? Savers. People who understand that the best weekend is one where you save $40 on laundry powder, fill the tank, and still have time to glare at the Ford Ranger reversing into a park. It’s quiet, it’s cheap, and we’ve got enough bulk-bought snacks to survive any apocalypse. Just don’t ask us where to get a smashed avo – we’ve got a pallet of them in the freezer.
