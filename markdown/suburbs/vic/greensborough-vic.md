# Greensborough (VIC)

Slug: `greensborough-vic`

**Greensborough, VIC — where the Plenty River Trail meets the drive-thru Woolies at the end of a very long Hurstbridge Line commute.**

We wake up in Greensborough and we know exactly what we’re getting: a 30-minute train ride that feels like a lifetime, a bagel from a place on Main Street that someone once called “a bagel bar that balances tradition with playful irreverence” (Sitchu said it, not us), and the eternal hope that today might be the day a culinary hotspot actually pops up and stays.

Remember when Broadsheet warned us not to drink the tap water? 2020. 88 suburbs. Greensborough was on the list. We boiled everything for a week and honestly, it was the most exciting thing that happened that year. The water was fine. But we still talk about it like it defined us.

And Dulce Gelateria? Fast forward a few years and “it’s Greensborough’s turn” they said. Urban List called us “relatively unassuming.” We’re still waiting for our turn, but at least we got the gelato. And Ben & Jerry’s Free Cone Day at Hoyts Greensborough — that’s our Super Bowl.

The real drama? A Whirlpool forum user once asked, “Where to buy? Eltham, Greensborough or Ringwood?” The answer was probably “Ringwood.” We know. We’ve accepted it. We also lost Video Games Heaven at Greensborough, and we still mourn it in the Whirlpool “Retailers you miss” thread.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting a nightlife. Our drag bingo was a one-off event at Mabel Jones — a sparkling afternoon, sure, but then it was over. The grassfire in 2019 that scared everyone into thinking we were next? That was over too.

But who would love it? People who want a 30-minute run on the Plenty River Trail, a $69 bottomless brunch with drag bingo nostalgia, and a drive-thru Woolies so you never have to unbuckle your seatbelt. We’re not trendy. We’re not unassuming. We’re Greensborough — and we’re fine with that.
