# Grange (VIC)

Slug: `grange-vic`

**GRANGE, VIC** — The suburb that makes you check Google Maps three times because surely this is Brisbane. Or Adelaide. Or maybe it’s just the universe’s way of testing your sense of place.

We’re the ones who’ve been living in this delicious identity crisis for years. One minute you’re at **Bora** on **6 Jetty Street** knocking back a Caipirinha at 9am like a Brazilian beach god, the next you’re wondering if you’re in the same state as the **Penfolds Grange** wine we share a name with but can’t afford ($800 a bottle? We’ll take the $10 cleanskin thanks).

Our local legend? **Bella & Tortie**, the bakery that closed in December 2022 and got 550 Instagram comments of grief. People *lined up* for the last canelés and crullers like it was the end of civilisation. Owner Suzi Unwin had to open as an online bakery just to escape the emotional baggage. That’s the level of drama we serve with our iced vovos.

The misfits who’d hate it here? Anyone who wants a single identity. We’re a three-state puzzle — the Brisbane **Kedron Brook Bikeway** crowd, the Adelaide beach life, the Melbourne wine snobs who only know us from a $149 Shiraz Challenge. Try explaining that to your Uber driver. “Just head towards the confusion mate.”

**Cody Lavigne**, the tradie from Pedestrian TV’s 30 Under 30, described himself as “humble, considerate, and charismatic.” That’s us. We’re a suburb that’s been called “pleasant suburban living” by Sitchu while a Whirlpool forum user said they’d never live in the neighbouring suburb “if they were giving away houses.” We’re the nice one. The one with weatherboard homes and a quiet superiority complex.

Who would love it here? People who enjoy a good existential crisis with their brunch. Brazilians, bakers, tradies with emotional intelligence, and anyone who’s ever said “I’m from Grange” and watched the other person’s face go blank. We’re not famous. We’re not forgotten. We’re Grange — and we’re fine with that.

*Now if you’ll excuse us, we have a date with a cruller and a map.* 🥐🗺️
