# Cranbourne West (VIC)

Slug: `cranbourne-west-vic`

Emu Bush Drive, Cranbourne West — where the streets are named after flora and the internet is held together by a single, exhausted prayer.  

We wake up here in the Casiana Grove Estate, part of the great Ambrosia-Alarah-Clarinda-Casiana cluster, and the first thing we do is check our Opticomm connection. Not for emails. Not for Netflix. To see if it’s still alive. Thank you, @Matt E on Whirlpool, for speaking our truth.  

By mid-morning, we’re parked outside the biggest textile recycling facility in Australia: Upparel’s 10,500-square-metre warehouse off some industrial road. It opened in November 2025 and can process 10,000 tonnes of material a year. We’re not entirely sure what they do with our old socks, but we’re proud they do it here instead of sending our shame offshore. As Broadsheet noted, the B Corp has “pushed forward the future of textile recycling.” We’re just pushing forward the future of driving everywhere because there’s no train.  

Our main street? If you can call it that. The data doesn’t name one, because we don’t have a main street. We have a Bunnings somewhere over the horizon, a few servo pies, and the faint sound of a lawnmower at 7am on a Sunday.  

The petty local drama? It’s not about a barista feud — we don’t have a cafe. It’s about the internet. We’re split between NBN and Opticomm, and if you’re on Opticomm, you’re probably on Whirlpool complaining about speed tests. As one local wrote: “Our side is with Opticomm. I submitted my order with Leaptel… so far it is not moved to…” We know how that sentence ends: with a shrug and a hotspot.  

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs reliable WiFi before 10am. Anyone who thinks “warehouse district” isn’t a vibe. Anyone who wants a croissant within walking distance.  

Who would love it? People who are genuinely excited about textile circularity. People who don’t mind a 20-minute drive for milk. People who look at a 10,500sqm recycling facility and think, “That’s the energy we need.”  

Cranbourne West: we’re growing, we’re recycling, and our internet is… pending. Come for the estate names, stay for the Upparel tour.
