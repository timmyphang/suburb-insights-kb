# Pacific Pines (VIC)

Slug: `pacific-pines-vic`

**Pacific Pines, QLD** — wait, someone put “VIC”? Yeah nah, we’re on the Gold Coast, and we’re still trying to figure out if that’s a flex or a typo. But here we are: the suburb that Whirlpool forums once called “nice looking, but plenty of crime” and where a bloke in his backyard nearly had a brown snake join his morning coffee. That’s Pacific Pines for you.

Come December, the whole street turns into a traffic jam because 21 Springwood Avenue has the best Christmas lights on the Coast — multiple-time winner of the Gold Coast Bulletin comp. You can’t move for minivans and kids in PJs. But the rest of the year? You’re more likely to cop a security camera moment like our local legend who posted his own snake-under-the-chair footage. Classic Pines.

We’ve got Brockman Park for the kids, an Airbnb with a pool and BBQ for the extended family, and apparently Pandora Crescent has a house that Pedestrian TV called a “House of Horrors” — your guess is as good as ours whether it’s for Halloween or just a Tuesday.

You’d hate it here if you want hipster cafes and a quiet life. You might also hate it if a brown snake sliding past your ankles is your idea of a bad day. But you’d love it if you want a huge Christmas lights display, a free park for the ankle-biters, and a community that’s been called “ghetto” online but still shows up for carols in Central Park. Just watch your step in the backyard.
