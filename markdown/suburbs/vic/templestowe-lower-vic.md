# Templestowe Lower (VIC)

Slug: `templestowe-lower-vic`

We don’t have a train line, but we do have a pub where kids can play arcade games while you sink a craft beer. Welcome to Templestowe Lower—the corner of Melbourne where the Yarra bends, the buses are elusive, and the tap water once required a rolling boil.

Our morning routine starts at Macedon Square, where Riddik sits inside an old ANZ branch like a glorious bank heist of brunch. The mural of a woman by street artist Tarek stares at you while you order the coffee-rubbed beef burger marinated in Code Black grinds. The breakfast board changes daily, which is code for “we will judge you if you come at 11:25am.” Before that, Power Plant Café’s manager once told Time Out that people love our parks. We do. Westerfolds Park has 123 hectares of kangaroos, echidnas, and platypus. Also, Wombat Bend has a playground that makes kids forget about iPads for a solid 45 minutes.

Petty local drama? In 2020, Yarra Valley Water told 88 suburbs—including ours—to boil our tap water after storms caused electrical damage. “Potential water quality issue,” they said. We all boiled like it was a camping trip, then found out the risk was low. Classic. We still eye the kettle suspiciously.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who relies on a train. We have buses—the 281 to Box Hill, the 905 to the city via Pines Shopping Centre. That’s it. No trams. If you’re late, you’re walking the Main Yarra Trail 33km to Southbank. Also, if you hate kids, avoid the Templestowe Hotel on Parker Street—it has an indoor playground, arcade games, and computer stations. Your quiet pint will be soundtracked by a six-year-old winning at Mario Kart.

Who would love it? Families. Petty’s Orchard for apple picking. Down The Rabbit Hole’s bronuts for picnic fuel. The Templestowe Hotel where your offspring can conquer the play HQ while you pretend you’re still cool. We’re sleepy, we’re a bit forgotten between Doncaster and Eltham, and our water history is questionable. But the parks are green, the coffee is strong, and nobody bothers you. Lower Templestowe: the suburb that’s just low-key enough.
