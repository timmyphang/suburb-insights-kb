# Wheelers Hill (VIC)

Slug: `wheelers-hill-vic`

Saturday morning in Wheelers Hill and you’ve got two choices: hit the Monash Gallery of Art for a Harry Seidler-designed culture hit with a side of Dukes coffee from Chumanchu, or queue at Kerrie Road Bakery for a schnitzel sandwich with beetroot and heaps of onion – just like chef Esther Sun’s been doing since kinder. The MGA’s still an artwork itself, views over the Dandenongs and all. But let’s be honest, Chumanchu’s owner once said we were “missing out on a good cup of coffee” and that the local scene was “saturated with bog-standard cafe options.” He wasn’t wrong.

There was a time some bright spark tried to rebrand this place the “Golden Mile” to bump up house prices. One Whirlpool user called it “artificial inflation” – we’re not fooling anyone. And if you’ve ever been pulled over near Springvale and Whitehorse Road by a bloke in a white van pushing “Denmark Audio” speakers, congratulations – you’ve experienced the true Wheelers Hill sales pitch.

The old Wheelers Hill Hotel, where 19-year-olds lined up for garage band gigs for decades, is now The Rogue Squire – a family pub with actual slides. We’ve swapped mosh pits for playgrounds. Meanwhile our pride and joy, Jells Park, just got $5.9 million worth of new walking and cycling trails, solar lighting and a seat. On-leash only for the doggos, but 127 hectares of sniffing is still a win.

Who would hate it here? Anyone hoping for a late-night dining scene or artisan roasteries. The Chumanchu owner said it best: “Dining options are few.” We’re still a work in progress.

Who would love it? Families who want a gallery, a legendary bakery, and a park that makes other suburbs jealous. Just leave the Golden Mile delusion at the door.
