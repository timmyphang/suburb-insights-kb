# Cardinia (VIC)

Slug: `cardinia-vic`

Cardinia, VIC — the suburb that’s mostly a farm that supplies a restaurant in another suburb. Yes, we are basically the pantry for Beaconsfield’s finest degustation, and we’re fine with that.

Let’s paint the picture. Saturday morning in Cardinia means one of two things: you’re at Cardinia Reservoir Park watching the eastern grey kangaroos graze at sunrise like some David Attenborough special, or you’re stuck in the queue on the Monash Freeway wondering why the state government promised us those extra lanes between Clyde and Cardinia roads back in 2022. As Broadsheet reminded us, the upgrade was supposed to shave nine minutes off the Pakenham commute. We’re still waiting.

Our big claim to culinary fame is O.My, the hatted fine diner in Beaconsfield where chefs Blayne and Chayse Bertoncello grow all their fruit, veg, eggs and honey on their two-acre farm in nearby Cardinia. That’s right, our suburb is basically the restaurant’s veggie patch. Concrete Playground called it a “one-of-a-kind cabin” on farmland near the reservoir. We call it our identity.

Petty drama? The Cardinia Shire welcomes around six families a day and 28 newborns each week, according to a Casey Cardinia Kids survey. That’s a lot of nappies and not nearly enough infrastructure. We’re growing faster than the weeds on the farm, and the only thing expanding quicker is the Monash Freeway — set to hit 10 lanes between here and the city.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks a “night out” means more than one choice of fine dining within a 20-minute drive. Also people who don’t like kangaroos or the smell of compost.

Who would love it? Nature lovers who want a picnic at Cardinia Reservoir Park, farm-to-table obsessives who don’t mind driving to Beaconsfield for dinner, and anyone who considers “peace and quiet” a feature, not a bug. We’re not a suburb with a main street — we’re a state of mind. And a very good tomato.
