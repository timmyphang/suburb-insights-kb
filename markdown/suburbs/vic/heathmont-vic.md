# Heathmont (VIC)

Slug: `heathmont-vic`

You know you’re in Heathmont when your big local drama is a water warning that hit 88 suburbs back in 2020, and yours made the list purely by alphabetical accident. We were wedged between Greenvale and Kalorama like the shy kid at the school photo. Yarra Valley Water said boil our water because some storms messed with the pipes. And we did. We boiled everything. Even the jug got a second boil. That’s the level of excitement here—we once had to pre-boil before we could make our tea.

A typical Saturday morning? You’ll find us at the Heathmont Farmers’ Market, fourth Sunday of the month, 8am–1pm at Great Ryrie Primary School. We grab our golden jars of Heathmont Honey and nod at the same three people we nod at every month. As Broadsheet never wrote about us: we’re the suburb that exists so Ringwood can say “look how close we are to the Dandenongs.” Time Out’s 2021 Ringwood guide mentioned us as “below Ringwood.” Cheers, mate. Real altitude descriptor.

The petty drama? It’s the Heathmont College catchment war. Whirlpool forums have families stressing because their house borders Ringwood but the school zone might shunt them here. Imagine: you buy a home thinking you’re getting Ringwood Secondary College, but the distance algorithm says “sorry, you’re Heathmont.” That’s a whole thread of panic. We own it. Our kids get an education and a healthy dose of being the underdog.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting a laneway cafe or a bar that doesn’t close at 4pm. Don’t come looking for a 400 Gradi pizza—that’s at Crown. The closest you’ll get to high-end is knowing that Heathmont’s own Our Place bakery supplies bread to a fancy Malvern East corner store called Breadcetera. We’re proud, but also, that’s the peak.

Who would love it? Families who want a quiet life, a farmers market, and a hot cross bun so good that Sitchu said it was worth “a drive out to Heathmont” back in 2026. That’s us. We’re the suburb you drive to for a bun. And we’re fine with that. Boil the kettle. Stay for the honey.
