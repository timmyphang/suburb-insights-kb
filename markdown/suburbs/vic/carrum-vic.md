# Carrum (VIC)

Slug: `carrum-vic`

**Carrum, VIC — where the beach is clean, the bakery’s from a neighbouring suburb, and the most exciting thing that’s happened lately was a kid on a motorised esky in Carrum Downs.**

We’re a bayside town that the Concrete Playground bike bloggers remember exists only as the last 27 kilometres of a ride from Brighton. “If there’s more fuel in those legs, keep riding to Carrum,” they say — like we’re the penalty box of the Bayside Trail. Thanks for that.

Our beaches are officially clean. Ranked equal 10th by the EPA in 2016, alongside South Melbourne. We’re proud to beat St Kilda — who wouldn’t be — but it’s still “Carrum beach, pretty decent” energy. Not Hampton. Not Seaford. Carrum. The “fine, I guess” of bayside swimming.

For years, our gastronomic claim to fame has been sourdough from Lievito in Carrum Downs, shipped to a cafe in Albert Park. As Broadsheet noted, Halcyon Days — a cafe in Albert Park, not Carrum — built half its menu around loaves from our neighbour’s bakery. We’re the bread basket for people who live somewhere else. It’s a whole identity.

There was Dainton Family Brewery in Carrum Downs, which opened in 2016 in a warehouse with a food truck and table tennis. It took owner Dan Dainton seven years to convince his dad to let him brew. But that’s the other suburb. We don’t even get the brewery; we just live near the road sign for it.

**The local vibe:** Mums in activewear pretending they’re Mornington Peninsula, kids at Funtopia indoor play centre (also Carrum Downs — we share everything), and a lot of people who thought renting here was cheaper until they realised the train takes an hour.

**Who would hate it here:** Anyone who wants a late-night anything. We close at 3:30pm, like Halcyon Days. Anyone who pronounces it “Carrum” with any sense of urgency. We’re not going anywhere fast.

**Who’d love it:** Cyclists with strong legs. People who like clean-ish water and zero pretension. Folks who want to say they live “on the bay” without paying Brighton prices. And anyone who genuinely believes Lievito sourdough tastes better when it’s been trucked 40 minutes south before you eat it.

Welcome to Carrum. We’re the nice-but-forgettable middle child of the Port Phillip coast. And honestly? The beach is fine.
