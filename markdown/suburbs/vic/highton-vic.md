# Highton (VIC)

Slug: `highton-vic`

Highton, we are the suburb that Geelong forgets exists until they need a shortcut to the ring road. We live on the quiet side of the Barwon, where the biggest controversy is whether 63 Degrees is still the best cafe in town—and the answer, since 2016, has been a firm "well, it used to be."

Back when Carly and Simon Bryant opened at 87 Barabool Road in 2006, we had our very own practice run for the CBD dream. That 2016 renovation gave us a clean, fresh, leafy vibe—and we clung to it like a roasted marshmallow hot chocolate in winter. The menu changed seasonally, but the crispy-chicken wrap and smashed avo never left. Coffee from Clark St Coffee Roasters? Yes we did. Martini-style shaken iced lattes? You bet. But these days, your best bet is to remember it fondly, because the hospitality scene moved on and we stayed put.

Our local drama is entirely digital. Whirlpool forums document our constant HFC dropouts on Westnet Cable—one heroic user reports 100-170 Mbps down, but we all know that's a lie told at 3am when the kids are asleep. The rest of the time, Netflix buffers just as you reach the climax.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks Geelong is a "cultural hub." We don't have a craft beer bottle shop from Concrete Playground's list. We don't have a waterfront bar. We have Roslyn Playgroup for 0-5 year olds and a postcode that gets confused with Highett. If you need nightlife, you'll cry.

Who would love it? People who understand that anonymity is the ultimate luxury. We're the suburb where you can buy a house without a mortgage, complain about internet speeds on a forum, and still feel smug when you drive past the Pakington Street traffic. We're not trying to be cool. We're just comfortable. And that's exactly how we like it.
