# Craigieburn (VIC)

Slug: `craigieburn-vic`

If the Craigieburn train line was a person, it’d be that bloke who peaked in 2014, still wears a cap backwards and yells at the Myki machine for fun. Pedestrian TV ranked it dead last in Melbourne — “the view is atrocious” and you’re just staring at “your typical run-of-the-mill eshay”. Fair call, but we’ve embraced it. You haven’t lived until you’ve watched a sunrise over a vacant block from the window of a V/Line carriage that smells like a servo pasty.

Back in the day, our big news was when Mark Wahlberg’s Wahlburgers planned to open inside United Cinemas here. Still waiting, Mark. But at least we’ve got Bonchon — the Korean fried chicken spot that’s been holding it down since 2023. And a Red Rooster. And, if you’re feeling fancy, barbaros Turkish restaurant. That’s basically our food scene, and we’re not sorry.

Petty local drama? The Whirlpool forum asking “Is Craigieburn an unsafe suburb?” got 47 replies of “Yeah, but it’s cheap”. Meanwhile, Time Out revealed we had one of the biggest increases in vacant homes in Melbourne — so many empty houses you could play hide and seek for a week. And the crime reputation? We don’t sugarcoat it. Yes, there’s housing stress. Yes, the eshays are real. But the Highlands estate has all the amenities you need, and the multicultural playgroup at Craigieburn South Primary is genuinely lovely.

You’d hate it here if your idea of a weekend is a Broadsheet-approved wine bar with a retractable roof. Sorry, our pub is the United Cinemas food court. But you’d love it here if you’re a first-home buyer who’s done the maths, you love a decent feed of fried chicken, and you don’t mind that your morning commute involves a guaranteed contactless payment trial and a front-row seat to Melbourne’s finest flog parade. Welcome to Craigieburn, mate. Don’t leave your bike unlocked.
