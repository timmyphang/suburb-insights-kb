# Delahey (VIC)

Slug: `delahey-vic`

**Delahey, VIC — population: we're still standing**

We know what you’re thinking: “Wait, where’s Delahey?” Exactly. We’re the quiet corner of postcode 3037, forever living in Sydenham’s shadow and Hillside’s… well, actual hills. The closest we got to national fame was when Broadsheet named us one of Melbourne’s hardest-hit COVID suburbs back in 2020. 119 active cases. Not a proud record, but hey — we still showed up and did the right thing, masks and all. The Department of Health called us out by name. We still frame that data drop on our fridges.

Our Friday night? Nothing. Our Saturday morning? Also nothing. But that’s the point. We don’t have a Tyler’s Milk Bar (that’s Preston’s thing). We don’t have a single cafe that made the “World’s 100 Best Coffee Shops” list. We don’t even have a karaoke bar. Our most exciting local event was when someone on Whirlpool complained about parcel customs delays. Twice.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a brunch spot within walking distance. Anyone who Googles “things to do in Delahey” and gets zero results. Anyone expecting hipster charm or heritage architecture. We’ve got the Calder Park race track as our landmark and a lot of TFI Fridays on empty blocks.

But who would love it? People who want quiet. People who appreciate that when Sitchu listed us in their suburb profiles, they didn’t even bother writing a description — just our postcode and a link to Deepdene. We’re the suburb that knows its place. No drama, no fuss, no artisanal pickle shop.

We’re Delahey. We’ve seen harder times than 119 cases. And we’re still here. Just don’t ask us where the good coffee is.
