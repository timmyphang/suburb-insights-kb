# Wattle Glen (VIC)

Slug: `wattle-glen-vic`

Wattle Glen: where the NBN rollout is still a more gripping saga than any Netflix series, and the local Wi-Fi signal is held together by the ghosts of dial-up.

We’re the suburb that makes you earn your internet. Back in 2017, we were in the 3HUR exchange footprint — Diamond Creek, Hurstbridge, and us, the forgotten middle child. The forums were lit: “anything beats 3.4mbps,” one local wrote. “I’m about 650 metres from the node, hoping for 60.” We saw fibre laid along Heidelberg-Kinglake Road, right up near Wattle Glen Station. Green spools on trucks. Hope. And then… well, did it actually arrive? We’re still not sure. The copper is like a prayer.

Weekend routine? You’re either on a kilometre-long waitlist for a table at Warran Glen Garden Centre (which, let’s be real, is in Warrandyte — we just claim it because our own garden options are a Bunnings trip through Diamond Creek) or you’re staring at the "loading" spinner on your NBN connection, wondering if that fibre ever reached your street.

Petty local drama: the years-long debate about whether NBN Co would bless us with FTTC or just leave us on FTTN purgatory. “Two addresses within 100 metres of each other — one FTTN, one FTTC. Doesn’t make any sense.” That’s Wattle Glen in a nutshell: random, inconsistent, and we love it anyway.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who needs to upload a file before 2026. Tradies trying to stream footy on the job. Deliveroo drivers who can’t find us because Google Maps thinks we’re part of the bush.

Who would love it? People who see 3.4mbps as a character-building exercise. The kind of folks who enjoy watching possums argue outside the window while they wait for a webpage to load. Locals who know that the real high-speed connection is the friends you make at the station platform.

We’ve got trees, silence, and a community forum that’s been arguing about nodes for eight years. Welcome to Wattle Glen — better bring a book.
