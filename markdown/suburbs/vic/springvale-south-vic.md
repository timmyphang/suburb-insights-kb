# Springvale South (VIC)

Slug: `springvale-south-vic`

Springvale South, we don’t have a main street, we have a queue. And that queue is for bánh mì. At Bun Bun Bakery, the five well-organised women behind the counter churn out between 600 and one-thousand rolls a day, unfazed, while the rest of us stand there sweating in the sun wondering if we’ll make it to the front before our lunch break ends. The best-selling roasted pork in barbecue sauce roll? Stuffed with marinated cubes of crispy pork and caramelised onions. Worth every second of the wait. Over at Spring Hot Bread, Anna Nguyen and her parents have been perfecting their fried chicken bánh mì since 2003 – the julienned carrots may not win prizes for uniformity, but they cut through the richness of the cold cuts, chicken liver pâté and butter-slathered insides of the mixed ham roll like a proper local hero.

Yes, we know what they say about Springvale South. It’s the quiet cousin that gets lumped in with Springvale proper – the one with the massive Asian grocery shops and the chaos of the market. But we’ve got our own rhythm. Every December, the streets light up with Christmas displays that make the rest of Melbourne drive through with their kids yelling “Look, that one has a blow-up Grinch!” As Urban List noted, the best Christmas lights in Melbourne are dotted all over – and yes, that includes us. We don’t fuss about it.

But who would hate it here? Anyone who can’t handle a 20-minute wait for a $6 roll. Or someone who needs a lily-white café with single-origin pour-overs and a resident barista called Bodhi. That’s not our vibe. We don’t have a “village shopping strip” – we have Luxford Road and a lot of ambition.

Who would love it? Anyone who understands that the best food comes from family-run bakeries, not franchise kitchens. Anyone who knows that a good bánh mì is a cultural institution, not a trend. And anyone who’s happy to stand in line with the rest of the suburb, because we all know what’s waiting at the other end. Springvale South – we’re not fancy, but we’re full.
