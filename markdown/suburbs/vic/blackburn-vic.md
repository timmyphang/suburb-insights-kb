# Blackburn (VIC)

Slug: `blackburn-vic`

Every time we tell someone we’re from Blackburn, they say, “Oh, the one with the rural vibe just 16km from the CBD?” Yes, Sitchu said that. And we’ve been leaning into it ever since. But here’s the thing — you can’t exactly call it rural when Urban Climb dropped a 2800-square-metre, five-storey climbing wall on Alfred Street back in 2022. That’s not a farm silo, mate — that’s 12 kilometres of steel and a sauna. We’re not milking cows; we’re milking chalk dust.

Our Saturday morning routine is a finely tuned negotiation. Do we queue at Aunt Billies, hidden away from the main road, for a brunch that’s “fast, fresh and friendly”? Or do we hit Natural Science Wine & Liquor on Salisbury Avenue for a bottle of something natural and pretend we’re in Collingwood? Maybe grab a viral drink from Haru Bakery House — because apparently 2026 is the year of the Instagrammable takeaway. And if you’re really feeling adventurous, there’s always The Honey Thief Bakery in Blackburn South, doing the best bread in the state, allegedly. (We haven’t verified this. But the Urban List said it, so it must be true.)

The real local drama? It’s not about council rates or the new townhouses. It’s the Whirlpool forum user “Blackburn BBQers” — our unofficial suburb sage — going to war over Kia batteries, Mercedes van steering wheels, and the tragic state of modern utes. We’ve got a whole online personality dedicated to car feuds. That’s our version of a neighbourhood watch.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting a bustling main strip. Half the businesses on “Blackburn Road” are actually in Glen Waverley or Mount Waverley. But that’s okay — we’re used to being a warm-up act for the eastern suburbs.

Who would love it? Climbers who want to practise at an Olympic-standard wall without leaving the eastern suburbs, wine snobs who like a curated bottle shop, and anyone who thinks “rural vibe” means plenty of street parking and a Woolies that’s never too busy. You’ll fit right in. Just don’t bring up your car battery.
