# Kalkallo (VIC)

Slug: `kalkallo-vic`

We know what you’re thinking: “Kalkallo? Isn’t that just the place you drive past on the Hume Highway to get to somewhere else?” And yeah, you’re not wrong. We’re the suburb that exists because the postcode 3064 needed to bulk out a COVID-19 lockdown list back in 2020. We were in that Concrete Playground article – the one where Daniel Andrews rattled off “Craigieburn, Donnybrook, Mickleham, Roxburgh Park and Kalkallo” like he was reading a roll call at a very depressing high school. We were famous for a minute. Then we went back to being a speck on the map.

Every morning, our most dedicated resident is a concreter named Noel. Back in 2020, he was up at 5am to drive 67 kilometres to his worksite here, after getting a $1652 fine for driving 32km for butter chicken. As Broadsheet reported, he ended up with a year’s supply of Desi Dhaba curry. We still talk about that. It’s our biggest cultural event since the lockdown.

Our housing strategy is simple: you can get a 350sqm house and land package in Kalkallo for the same price as 315sqm in Greenvale. The Whirlpool forums are still arguing about which one has better capital growth. Spoiler: neither. We’re a sea of new estates, single driveway per block, and a waiting list for a PlayBuddies program that might happen one day.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a bar that isn’t a servo, a cafe that isn’t a Maccas, or a delivery zone that doesn’t charge extra. Also anyone who doesn’t own a tradie ute and a story about driving past Melbourne to get to work.

But who would love it? People who understand that “local” means knowing the concreter who got famous for butter chicken, and that “community” is a Whirlpool thread about house prices. We’re not much, but we’re honest. And we’re one of the few places where you can still buy a block of dirt and call it a future. Just don’t expect a coffee shop. We’re still waiting for that one.
