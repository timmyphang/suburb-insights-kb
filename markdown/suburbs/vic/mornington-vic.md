# Mornington (VIC)

Slug: `mornington-vic`

We call it the 'Ninch' if we’re being lazy, or just "the Peninsula" if we’re trying to sound like we own it. Which we do, sort of — at least until the Melbourne weekenders pile off the freeway with their esky full of natural wine and a booking at Jackalope.

A proper Mornington morning starts at Dreamer, where Ben and Melisa have been making Turkish eggs with pomegranate labneh since 2023, and where the coffee comes from Commonfolk — because we’re not animals. By 10am you’ll see the same faces: the mum from Avocado Baby yoga, a bloke comparing EV range anxiety on Whirlpool, and someone’s dad trying to decide between the tuna melt and the salmon tartine. The whole place runs on relationships, not just great coffee, as Dreamer’s owner put it. And we believe him, because we’ve been coming here since before the second cafe opened in Balnarring.

Speaking of arrivals: Andrew Bolt moved to the Peninsula, and we’re still not sure if that’s a compliment or a threat. Meanwhile, over in Mount Eliza, The Block 2026 has property experts waving red flags about what Peninsula buyers actually want. We’re watching from a distance, sipping a spiced cacao latte, mildly offended that anyone would build a house here without a dedicated surfboard rack.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks a day trip to Peninsula Hot Springs counts as "living local". The Lululemon crowd who book Alba Thermal Springs and then complain about the drive back to St Kilda. People who say "Mornington is just a wine region" — mate, we have a 15-tonne log splitter discussion on Whirlpool, you don’t get that in the Yarra Valley.

Who would love it? People who understand that "relaxed lifestyle" means you can spend Saturday morning at Dreamer, afternoon at Pond Bathhouse, and evening watching the tuna boats come back in. We’re not fancy — we’ve just got the best hot springs, a cafe that remembers your order, and the quiet satisfaction of knowing the block tourists are somewhere else. Come for the Bathhouse, stay for the banana bread.
