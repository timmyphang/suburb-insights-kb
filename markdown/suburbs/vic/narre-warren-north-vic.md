# Narre Warren North (VIC)

Slug: `narre-warren-north-vic`

**Narre Warren North — it’s not the other Narre Warren, okay?**

We don’t have a cafés section in the Broadsheet guide, which tells you everything. Our cultural highlight is the Banana Bus stop. Yes, the *festival* bus that departs from here and also from St Albans, because we know how to party — as long as that party starts with a designated driver and a complimentary condom in a “survival kit.”

**A morning here:** you wake up, check if the bus from 2017 is still running (no one knows), and then spend 20 minutes driving to anywhere that sells a coffee that isn’t from a servo. There’s no “vibrant community café scene” to write home about, unless you count the local Macca’s as a cultural institution. As Broadsheet noted once, a decade ago, we were lumped in with Dandenong and Cranbourne as “festival bus departure points.” We’ve been dining out on that clout ever since.

**The local drama:** we get mistaken for Narre Warren South. Or just *Narre Warren*. A letter addressed to “Narre Warren North” arrives at the wrong house three suburbs over. The council thinks we’re a nature reserve. We have no Sitchu profile, no “Top 10 Things To Do,” no home tours featuring tasteful rugs from Double or Miss Amara. Our aesthetic is “slightly rural, slightly confused, definitely not in the Broadsheet newsletter.”

**Who would hate it here:** anyone who wants to live in an actual North Melbourne or Carlton North, with Rathdowne Street bakeries and Errol Street wine bars. Anyone who wants a rug delivered that isn’t from Temple & Webster because it was the only place that would ship this far out.

**Who would love it:** people who cherish anonymity. People who think a “festival survival kit” is a valid personality trait. People who want to live five minutes from the M1 but also five minutes from a paddock that may or may not have alpacas. We are the suburb of “just enough.” And honestly? We’re fine with that. The Banana Bus is booked.
