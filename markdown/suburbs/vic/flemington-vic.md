# Flemington (VIC)

Slug: `flemington-vic`

You know a suburb’s got range when one day you’re licking a handrail at the Melbourne Cup and the next you’re debating the merits of Ghanaian vs Nigerian jollof at Evette Quoibia’s Jollof Vibe on Racecourse Road. That’s Flemington, baby.

Our Saturday morning routine? Start at Laksa King (6‑12 Pin‑oak Crescent) for a bowl of that coconut‑milk curry laksa that’s been the real local MVP since 1998. Then wander past the old Escape Room in someone’s mum’s backyard — yes, Melbourne’s first escape room was literally in a Flemington bungalow, and yes we’re still proud of that. Grab a banana pudding matcha from Local Goods, which has finally made the glow‑up from pop‑up to permanent spot. And if the day’s feeling fancy, we’ll hit The Fox & Daisy on Mount Alexander Road for $35 wine‑and‑pâté deal and a vinyl‑soaked Sunday session.

But let’s not pretend we’re all elegant racecourse champagne sippers. This is the suburb where a streaker gallivanted around Flemington during #Soaksday and a TikTok princess licked an escalator handrail at Cup. We’ve had refugee activists shutting down the main entrance on Epson Rd, a car spray‑painted on train tracks, and an oil fire that forced Laksa King to evacuate mid‑lunch. The public housing towers — 23 cases across 12 households in the COVID days — remind everyone we’re not all Ascot Vale renovation queens. As Concrete Playground reported, we were literally locked down as a hotspot. We wear it.

Who’d hate it here? Anyone who can’t handle their laksa with a side of housing‑estate reality. If you need your suburb to be all polished terraces and oat‑milk lattes with zero Festival Hall chaos, try Camberwell.

Who’d love it? The food‑obsessed, the festival‑goers (Laneway, Good Things, K‑pop in 2026 — all at the racecourse), and anyone who wants nine different jollofs, a wine bar with $5 pots, and a community that actually showed up with mutual aid during lockdown. Flemington: we’re a mess of contradictions, and we wouldn’t have it any other way. 🏁✨
