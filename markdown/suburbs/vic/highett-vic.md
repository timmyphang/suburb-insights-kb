# Highett (VIC)

Slug: `highett-vic`

Saturday morning in Highett and you’ve got options. Do you queue for the house-milled sourdough at The Flour House before it sells out? Or head to The Diplomat for a burger and pretend it’s health food? Decisions, decisions.

The buzzing junction around Highett Rd and Railway Parade is “throwing up some really cool stuff,” says Urban List. And they’re not wrong. We’ve got Abbiocco doing paccheri and Aperol Margaritas, Railway Wine Bar pouring minimal-intervention wine in a former aquarium shop, and Third Wheel – which won the Toby’s Estate Local Legends award just months after opening in 2023. Not bad for a place that Whirlpool forums reckon is “truly a better place to live in than it was 5 or 6 years ago.”

But let’s not pretend the gentrification fairy waved a wand. That same forum thread admits there’s “still a few ugly parts and a fair amount of social housing dotted around.” And our restaurant strip has a bit of a revolving door. Remember Hot Lips Hacienda? The 2017 Mexican pioneer that was going to bring the south back? “Everything has gone bang over the last two years,” the owners said. Well, it’s now a memory – replaced by Chilpa, which as of May 2025 is “permanently closed.” The space is a question mark. That’s Highett: one step forward, one taco stand shuttered.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting a seamless bayside bubble. We’re not Hampton or Sandringham – we’ve still got the odd social housing block and a newsagent-turned-restaurant down the road. If you need your brunch to come with zero grit, try further south.

Who would love it? People who want a genuinely good feed without the inner-city rent. The pizza from Teo’s, the ceramics at Urban Clay, the wine bar in a former aquarium shop – it’s all happening. We’re not polished, but we’ve got personality. Come for the margarita, stay for the mess. #Highett #BaysideComeback
