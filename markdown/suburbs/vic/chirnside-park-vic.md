# Chirnside Park (VIC)

Slug: `chirnside-park-vic`

**Chirnside Park, VIC — the Yarra Valley’s gloriously unglamorous little sibling.**

You know that moment when you’re cruising up the Maroondah Highway, past the wineries and the rolling hills, and then suddenly you’re faced with a Bunnings so big it could house a small country town? That’s us. Welcome to Chirnside Park — where the main attraction is a shopping centre that does Santa photos from July, and the closest thing we have to a night out is a battle on the mini golf at Maroondah Adventure Golf Park (363 Maroondah Hwy, if you’re keeping score).

Our Saturday routine? Up early for a gluten-free hot cross bun from Just Yummy on Meadowgate Drive — because yes, being 100% gluten, wheat and nut free makes it a celiac’s dream, but also means we’re all eating brownies that taste like cardboard. Then it’s straight to the Chirnside Park Shopping Centre to argue about whether to get Zero95 pizza or Oz Tenpin bowling. Spoiler: we do both, because that’s the level of chaos we live for.

Petty local drama? Back in August 2020, Yarra Valley Water told 88 suburbs to boil their tap water. We were on that list. Everyone in Melbourne panicked; we just shrugged and kept drinking from the garden hose. “It builds character,” we said. And yes, three of us got mild gastro. Worth it.

Who would hate it here? Anyone looking for a “curated” Yarra Valley experience. If your idea of a good weekend is a charcuterie board at a winery with views, you’ll be deeply disappointed by our main street’s “quite a few tourist cafes and some normal ones” — plus a fish and chip shop that’s been there so long it’s basically heritage listed. Also, if you’re easily offended by the sight of a giant Bunnings, look away.

Who would love it? Families who want mini golf, tenpin bowling, and a gluten-free bakery all within a five-minute drive. Locals who know that the real Yarra Valley isn’t about the fancy restaurants — it’s about a Saturday arvo at Maroondah Adventure Golf, a slice of Zero95 pizza, and never having to leave the safety of the shopping centre. We’re not trying to be Healesville. We’re just Chirnside Park, and we’re fine with that. Mostly.
