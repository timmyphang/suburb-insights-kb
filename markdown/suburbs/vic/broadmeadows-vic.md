# Broadmeadows (VIC)

Slug: `broadmeadows-vic`

Broadmeadows Train Station: you pull in, the doors open, and suddenly you’re living inside a 3.74 out of 10 RACV review. The only thing lower is the vibe on the Craigieburn line, where the view is “a typical run-of-the-mill eshay” — according to Pedestrian TV, not me. But we wear it like a badge.

A Saturday morning in Broady: you grab a coffee at Humble Harry Roasting Co. on Pascoe Vale Rd (if it’s still roasting — let’s be real, the article’s from the Urban List and who knows when). Then you wander into Broadmeadows Central, where Bonchon once gave away $1 wings back in 2022. We still talk about it. Or you hit the Chatime for bubble tea, because yes, we have a Chatime. We have everything a five-kilometre radius needs.

Petty local drama: earlier this year, a woman was charged over an alleged assault at a Melbourne cafe, and she’ll be facing the music at the **Broadmeadows Magistrates’ Court** on 17 August 2026. That court’s seen it all — from Lidia Thorpe protesting at the immigration detention centre to every eshay who got caught tagging the Civic Plaza. Speaking of which, the **Broadmeadows Town Hall** — once nicknamed “The Pink Elephant” — won the top Victorian Architecture Medal in 2020. Yes, our council building has more awards than our train station.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a platform 9¾ for white-collar snobs. If you want a station that feels safe after dark, good luck. If you’re scared of “above average burglary rates” (thanks Whirlpool), maybe stick to Brighton. Also, if you think high-rise development is scary — the Vic Government just proposed us for massive towers. Get ready, Camberwell.

But who would love it? Anyone who wants the **most affordable and liveable suburb in Melbourne** — median house price $568k (Time Out, 2024). Families who hit the **Broadmeadows Community Hub** playgroup. Migrant communities who make the **Broadmeadows Fiesta** the best party north of the ring road. And anyone who knows that behind the knocked-up train station and the 2020 water warning, there’s a real, honest, multicultural ‘burban heart.

We’re not trying to be South Kensington. We’re just trying to get to the city without staring at empty blocks and a corner shop. And we’re doing it our way — $1 wings, Pink Elephant, and all.
