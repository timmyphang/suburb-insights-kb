# Murrumbeena (VIC)

Slug: `murrumbeena-vic`

We knew Murrumbeena was having a moment when Oasis Bakery started giving away 10,000 free doughnuts in 2018. Classic jam, Nutella, or just your suburban pride swelling to dangerous levels. That’s our Murrumbeena — a place where the biggest local drama is whether you queue for Oasis at 8am or try to sneak in at 7:59.

Let’s set the scene. Saturday morning. You’re wedged between car yards and a servo on Nepean Highway — but really you’re just heading to Oasis, the Middle Eastern bakery, cafe and grocery store that’s been a Murrumbeena staple for almost 25 years. It’s a three-in-one cult destination, according to Time Out. You grab a Lebanese breakfast (scrambled eggs with soujouk, ful medammas, grilled haloumi, chilli labneh balls — the works). You feel sophisticated. Then you walk past the car yards and reality hits.

But we do have our little gems. Murrumbeena Wine Bar, female-owned, right next to the station. It showcases female musicians and serves all-Australian beverages. Good luck getting a seat though — the whole suburb turns up on a Friday. And speaking of the station: we survived the $1.6 billion sky rail project. Four level crossings gone — Murrumbeena Road, Grange Road, Poath Road, Koornang Road. Two new stations. More services. We now have a fancy elevated train line that gives us a great view of... more car yards.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks a suburb should have more than one iconic food destination. Sorry, Oasis is the main event. If you want variety, try the next suburb over. Also: if you can’t handle the occasional middle-of-nowhere feeling while being 15km from the city.

Who would love it? Families who appreciate a good bakery, a wine bar with soul, and a train that actually runs (now). Also anyone who wants to say “I go to Oasis” like it’s a personality trait. Because it is.
