# Hastings (VIC)

Slug: `hastings-vic`

We don’t have a Hastings Street in Hastings, VIC. We have Marine Parade and a dream. And by dream we mean the sound of a fishing boat reversing at 5am and the polite NBN forum argument that’s been running since 2020.

Let’s be real: our Hastings is not Noosa. When Masterchef Jamie Fleming said “the only place to go on the coast is Hastings Street in Noosa” he definitely wasn’t talking about our Western Port wind tunnels. Back in the day, Cafe Pelikan Societe at 2 Marine Parade was the undisputed brunch champion – “one of the best spots on the Mornington Peninsula for brunch or lunch with a view,” Concrete Playground said in 2024. Still there, still serving, still the only place where you can watch a container ship while eating eggs. It’s our entire hospitality scene in one reliable building.

Petty local drama? We’ve got one piece and it’s the NBN. There’s an archived Whirlpool forum post from 2020 titled “NBN Problems 3915 Hastings” with over 1,000 posts of people arguing about dropouts on Aussie Broadband. That’s our version of a council planning war. Someone posts “anyone else’s internet down?” and boom, 47 replies later we’ve solved nothing. But we’ve bonded.

You’d hate it here if you need a Hastings Street piazza or a late-night anything. We don’t have a Cibaria or a Misschu Upstairs. We have the Hastings Pier, a pub, and a bakery that closes at 2pm sharp. If you’ve ever said “I’m bored” within earshot of a local, prepare for a 20-minute monologue about how you should be grateful for the fresh air.

But you’d love it if you own a boat, a fishing rod, or a deep appreciation for a suburb that refuses to be anything other than itself. We’re the quiet, salty older sibling that lets Noosa have the spotlight. Our drama is NBN forums. Our nightlife is a bag of chips on the jetty. And honestly? We wouldn’t swap it for a flat white on Hastings Street.
