# Preston (VIC)

Slug: `preston-vic`

**Preston, VIC — where else can you fight for your life at the market, then fight for a park at the pho joint?**

It’s Saturday morning, and we’re already sweating. Not from the 5K — from the decision: do we grab a borek from Tammy’s at the Preston Market (29 Mary Street, where mother-daughter duo Tammy and Tanya are feeding vegans and carnivores alike with plant-based pastries that even the most committed meat-lover forgives), or do we queue at **Eat Cannoli** for a gluten-free cannoli piped with ricotta and honey from the owners’ own rooftop beehive? Either way, we’re spending a tenner and taking 40 minutes to find a park. That’s Preston.

Yes, we know what they say about us. After threats from developer **Salta Properties** to close the 53-year-old market back in May 2023, we spent a year freaking out — until November 2023, when vendors announced a five-year lease extension to at least 2028. So the market’s staying, but so is the redevelopment drama. We’re used to it. Meanwhile, **Northland Shopping Centre** made national news in May 2025 after an alleged knife fight involving 10 people. We don’t pretend we’re Camberwell. We’re the suburb where you can get a $14 bowl of pho at **Pho Hung**, then walk past a **Pinky’s** on Gilbert Rd — a pastel wonderland from artist Emily Green and stylist Beckie Littler — and all of it feels normal.

The ironic thing is, we’ve quietly become a food destination. **Bar Mexico** opened in August 2024 inside the former Northside Food Hall on High Street, pouring 50 tequilas sourced straight from Mexico. And **Jamsheed Urban Winery** — founded by Elika Rowell and Gary Mills — just hired John Cassie as head chef in June 2026, bringing CWA-style curried sausages and wood-fired everything. Even **Moon Dog World** is hiding in a secret warehouse with 72 taps, a lagoon, and a hidden tiki bar. As Concrete Playground noted back in 2018, we were “fairly short on applaudable dining destinations.” Not anymore.

You’d hate it here if you need a serious cocktail bar — we’ve got none, just beer gardens and a **Wellness Social Club** with a hyperbaric chamber if the hangover’s bad. And if you’re scared of housing commission blocks or a bit of local colour? Stay in Fitzroy. But if you want a suburb that’s messy, real, and actually has a soul — where the **Preston Lions** play in the A-League Men and your March involves both a stabbing lockdown and a gluten-free cannoli — welcome home. Just bring a tenner and your sense of humour. You’ll need both.
