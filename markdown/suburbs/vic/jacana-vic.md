# Jacana (VIC)

Slug: `jacana-vic`

Jacana, VIC — the suburb that only ever makes the news when we’re being sent back into lockdown. And honestly? We’re still not sure anyone actually knows where we are.

You say “3047” and people think Broadmeadows… Dallas… oh right, Jacana. The one wedged between the train line and the Western Ring Road, where the only thing that moves faster than the traffic is the gossip about who’s testing positive. Back in July 2020, when Premier Dan Andrews announced the Stage Three postcodes, Jacana got a name-drop alongside Glenroy and Fawkner. That was our fifteen minutes. We’ve been quiet ever since.

Our morning routine? Not much has changed since those lockdown days. We still know the four reasons to leave the house: shop for essentials, exercise, medical care, work/study. The local park gets a morning lap, the servo gets a visit for milk and bread, and if you’re feeling adventurous, you might grab a takeaway from the fish-and-chip shop that’s been there since the 90s. We don’t have a Ritual Espresso or a 1800 Lasagne delivery — we have a ’91 Holden Barina parked outside a house that’s seen better days.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “vibrant food scene” means more than hot chips and a chicken shop. Anyone who expects a brunch spot with exposed bulbs and a “truffle mushroom toast.” We’re not that suburb. We’re the suburb where the biggest local drama was in 2020, when we got lumped in with the wrong postcode restrictions and nobody noticed.

But who would love it? People who want space, a back deck, a flat block, and zero pretense. People who know that a quiet life beats a trendy one. We’re not on any “best cafes in Australia” list — and we don’t care. We’re just here, minding our business, waiting for the next lockdown announcement to remind everyone we exist.
