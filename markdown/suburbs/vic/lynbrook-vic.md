# Lynbrook (VIC)

Slug: `lynbrook-vic`

Lynbrook, VIC — we don’t make the “best new bars” lists. We make the “warehouse assistant wanted” lists. And honestly? We’re fine with that.

Picture this: it’s Saturday morning. You’re not queueing for a saké den on Little La Trobe Street. You’re not hunting down a listening bar with vintage Tannoy speakers. You’re in Lynbrook, where the biggest local news since the estate went up is that “Robb & Lulu, an Australian owned retail fashion business” is looking for a casual Warehouse Assistant to join their small team based in Lynbrook. Yes, that’s our whole cultural calendar — a clothing warehouse gig. The Pedestrian TV job board saw fit to mention us. We’ll take it.

Back in the day, when Broadsheet was busy profiling champagne bars and potato salad with nori powder, we were here, minding our own business, wondering when the next bus to the station would arrive. No cocktail bars, no rooftop infinity pools. Just the quiet hum of the South East growth corridor and the occasional sound of a 4WD reversing out of a driveway.

Who would *hate* it here? Anyone who thinks a suburb isn’t legit unless it has a “concept store” or a “negroni bianco” on tap. Come to Lynbrook for the vibes, leave because there’s no bar. The closest you’ll get to a night out is the servo at the top of the street.

But who would *love* it? People who understand that a suburb doesn’t need to be in a magazine to be a good place to live. Families who want a front yard, a backyard, and a warehouse job close by. People who hear “Lynbrook” and think “peaceful, affordable, and nobody’s going to interrupt my weekend with a rooftop party.”

Yes, the media forgot us. But we know where we are. And we’ve got a casual warehouse position open if you’re interested.
