# Bennettswood (VIC)

Slug: `bennettswood-vic`

Saturday in Bennettswood looks like this: we wander down Greenwood Street to the old Neighbourhood House at 7 Greenwood Street — back in the day it wore its inclusivity like a badge, offering affordable classes and a welcoming suburban vibe for everyone, no fuss. Then we slow to a standstill outside 179 Station Street where the Bennettswood Bowling Club used to hold its business: Gala Presentation Nights (yes, there was a 2026 one with awards, a bar and footy on the big screens) and the kind of pool tournaments that settle arguments about whose team actually trained.

Our weekend soundtrack is Book Week parades from St Scholastica’s at 4/8 Starling Street — we still grin remembering the 2025 costumes. For years the fabric of this place has been local groups and modest rituals rather than boutique frills, which suits us fine.

Who would hate it here? If your idea of fun is midnight DJ sets and boutique everything, Bennettswood will feel like a sleepy footnote. If you need constant novelty, you’ll be disappointed — we’re more committee noticeboard than café crawl.

Who’d love it here? Families who value a proper school parade, people who’ll happily spend an evening watching footy on a big screen in a club that knows your name, and anyone who likes a neighbourhood that still runs on activities, not hype. We’re small, a little earnest, and proud of it — and yes, we can bowl better than we look.
