# Parkdale (VIC)

Slug: `parkdale-vic`

Parkdale, VIC — where the boldest thing anyone has ever said about us is "probably isn't the first place that springs to mind when you think of high-end Italian food."

We live in a quiet seaside suburb. So quiet that when Broadsheet realised Buono Restaurant & Bar existed here, they basically wrote a whole article marvelling that we could have nice things. And fine — they were right. We *were* surprised. A smart-casual Italian joint with teal banquettes and an aqua bar that looks more Mediterranean than Port Phillip's brackish waters? In our postcode? Fabio Magliano, Cinzia Buono and head chef Daniele Ruffolo clearly had other ideas. And we thank them for the marine hues and a twice-cooked octopus that made us feel cosmopolitan for one evening.

Then there’s Mi Peru D’Carmen — back in the day, a favourite of South American expats serving home-style stews like aji de gallina and some of the best ceviche in Melbourne. We didn't know what half of that meant, but we lined up anyway because anything that breaks the fish-and-chip monotony is a win.

But let’s be honest: most weekends we’re still just walking the dog along the beach, pretending we live in Mentone or Mordialloc. Our main identity crisis is whether we're "bayside" or just "that bit before Chelsea". We don't have a dramatic local feud — the biggest scandal was when the new cafe in Mentone (Busy Boy, from the Drip team) stole all our brunch crowd.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting a nightlife scene. Or a secret laneway bar. Or a reason to leave the house after 8pm that isn’t a Coles run.

Who would love it? People who understand that "quiet seaside suburb" is both an apology and a flex. We’re the suburb that doesn’t need to be the destination. We just let Buono and a good Peruvian feed do the talking — then go back to watching the tide.
