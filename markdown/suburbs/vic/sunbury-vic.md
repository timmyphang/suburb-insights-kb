# Sunbury (VIC)

Slug: `sunbury-vic`

You know you’re in Sunbury when the biggest local legend isn’t a footy star or a winemaker—it’s a bloke who sent a drone to Bunnings to grab a snag while sitting in his hot tub. Yes, that actually happened. CASA fined him up to nine grand, but honestly? Worth it. That’s the energy we bring.

A Saturday here starts on the Sunbury line, where you’ve got a 50-50 chance of a 30-minute delay due to a “police operation” or just the wind blowing a bit hard. But once you’re home, you head to the Ball Court Hotel on Macedon Street—our pub for over 150 years, now with a 600sqm beer garden that’s basically a small park with schooners. Kids eat half-price on Sundays, and they can run wild on the turf while you kick back and pretend you’re not in mortgage stress. Meanwhile, the Craiglee Shiraz 2002 from our own soil is good enough to appear on wine lists in Geelong. We’re classy like that.

The local drama? A bloke used a drone to order a Bunnings snag from his outdoor spa. The video went viral, then got deleted. We’re still not sure if we should be proud or embarrassed. Probably both.

Who would hate it here? The Whirlpool user who said they can’t wait to move interstate. They’re not wrong—the development is massive, every second block is a display home from Arden Homes costing $470k, and the train line rattles with freight at night. If you need inner-city grit, you’ll find us too “country town” and too far from a decent late-night bar that isn’t the Ball Court.

But who would love it? Anyone who wants a friendly, affordable spot that still feels like a country town but has a Metro Tunnel whisking you into town every 10 minutes. We’re the suburb that inspired a MIFF award-winning film about a John Farnham musical at a specialist school. We’re where Michael Gudinski sold watermelons to parched hippies at Sunbury ’72. We’re a bit rough, a bit proud, and we’ve got a Bunnings snag story that will never, ever die.
