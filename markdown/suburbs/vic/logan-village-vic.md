# Logan Village (VIC)

Slug: `logan-village-vic`

Saturday morning in Logan Village, and we’re at the Logan Village Hotel nursing a cold one on the new deck, watching the grass grow while our phone stubbornly refuses to load a single bar. Welcome to our pocket of paradise—where the country pub-with-rooms energy is strong, but the mobile signal is weaker than a flat soda. As one Whirlpool warrior put it, we’re basically living in a blackspot gully with trees and wetlands conspiring against our 5G dreams. Even the Optus tower north of us at 900MHz can’t save you when the power goes out and your NBN battery gives you 45 minutes tops. The council opened an evacuation centre here during the 2025 floods, which is handy because you’ll need to drive five minutes up the road just to call for help.

The real local drama? It’s not between neighbours—it’s between you and your telco. We had an MP and a news outlet involved, and they said a new tower would fix it by 2023. Spoiler: it didn’t. Meanwhile, the Doo Dropp Inn cottage on the Village Green is flogging locally made craft, and the Scout Group over on Waterford Tamborine Road in Buccan is full of friendly people with fun activities. Myhealth Logan Village is here to patch you up after you trip over a power cord trying to find a signal.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs to work from home without a backup generator. Or snobs from Brisbane who read that Whirlpool thread calling us a “ghetto superstar” to avoid. They’ve got a list longer than Yarrabilba’s commute, and guess who’s on it? Us. “Logan Village used to be a nice part of the world,” one forum mourned. Used to be? Mate, we still are—just don’t expect to post an Instagram story from your backyard.

Who would love it? People who want a crisp pub feed and a bed upstairs, who don’t mind a bit of radio silence, and who appreciate a community that rallies around a craft cottage and a scout hall. We’re not for everyone. But if you can handle the occasional power cut and the eternal phone signal quest, welcome. Just bring a booster antenna.
