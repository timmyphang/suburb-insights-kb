# Scarborough (VIC)

Slug: `scarborough-vic`

You know you’re in Scarborough when the big local drama involves a king tide flooding your driveway and someone on Whirlpool is complaining about a $1m house build leaving their kids with a “never ending mortgage.”

Welcome to the beachy, slightly confused tip of the Redcliffe Peninsula, where we are surrounded by “picturesque” Moreton Bay views and the occasional flash flood courtesy of a 2.74-metre king tide. Yes, the same council that hands out sandbags at Darra also watches as Scarborough goes under. But we bounce back. Because Scarborough residents are nothing if not resilient — we’ve got one of Brisbane’s best seafood markets, a beach pool for those who “aren’t keen on all that sand,” and a bike trail that’ll take you from Shorncliffe right to our doorstep if you’ve got the legs for it.

Petty local drama? We’ve got a classic one: the eternal wait for someone to actually open a bar in that old bank building at Hobart Street and Scarborough Beach Road. Broadsheet promised us Sonny’s wine bar in October 2022. It’s 2026. We’re still looking at a “building site” with towering trees and a neighbouring yoga studio. Meanwhile, the Paddington Ale House across the corner stays packed. We just want to sit on the streets of Scarborough “smoking a dart and having a drink” like they do in Paris — without needing to drive to Mount Hawthorn.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who thinks “pet-packed suburb” means just one dog. We’re officially WA’s most pet-packed suburb — you can’t walk three metres without tripping over a greyhound named something fancy. Also, if you don’t like the Indi Bar’s “thrashing roster of local bands” that has been going since 1972, you will not survive a Friday night.

Who would love it here? People who think “beachy ‘burbs” is a lifestyle, not a real estate category. Who happily walk to Ronnies Pizza, then the beach, then back to Ronnies again. Who don’t mind that the hottest new restaurant might actually be a bowls club with Taiwanese-inspired focaccia. We’re Scarborough — we’ve got the views, the floods, and an inheritance of a never-ending mortgage. Welcome to paradise.
