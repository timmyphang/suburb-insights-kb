# Cranbourne South (VIC)

Slug: `cranbourne-south-vic`

Cranbourne South — where the main drag is a general store that once made national headlines for tinned margaritas, and that’s about the most exciting thing that’s happened since the postcode was assigned.

Look, we love our little slice of semi-rural quiet. We really do. But let’s be honest: when Pedestrian TV name-checked the Cranbourne South General Store for fizzy cocktail tins, half the suburb didn’t even know it was famous until someone’s nephew saw it on Instagram. That’s us: accidentally viral, totally unbothered.

We don’t have a café scene. We don’t have a market. We don’t even have a main street worth arguing about. What we have is paddocks, a few Christmas light displays that make the Urban List every few years, and the occasional aurora hunter pulling over on the dirt road thinking they’ve found dark skies — only to realise the closest thing to a view is the glow from the servo down the highway.

If you want inner-city convenience, move to South Melbourne (which people keep confusing with us). If you want heritage charm, try Blackburn South. We’re the 3977 that’s always an afterthought on those real estate profiles. Our biggest local drama? Someone parked a ute too close to the mailboxes.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “nightlife” means more than watching the possums fight over the rubbish bin. Anyone who expects a barista to know their name. Anyone who spells “relaxing” as “boring.”

But for the quiet life? For the peace of knowing your nearest neighbour is a cow? For the kind of dark sky that makes the Aurora Australis actually worth the drive? Cranbourne South is heaven. Just bring your own tinned margaritas — the general store might be sold out.
