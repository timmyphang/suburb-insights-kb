# Mordialloc (VIC)

Slug: `mordialloc-vic`

Down here in Mordi, we’ve got two speeds: sunrise at the pier with the tide humming under the timber planks, or a debrief at Milhouse Canteen over a sanga and a pie. We know the EPA ranked our beach 25th cleanest – that’s not bottom of the class, that’s “room for improvement with a view.” And we own it. 

Our weekend ritual? A vinyasa flow at Warrior One (formerly Mordy Surf Shop, because we’re beachy on the inside too), then a fried Sichuan eggplant at Bang Bang – the one that made them famous enough to open in Hampton. Or you’ll find us at the new Tommy Ruff flagship on Main Street, because the old spot got a glow-up. And if you’ve got energy, there’s La Cabra coming soon to Main Street, plus the Wall to Wall street art precinct that slapped colour over the old industrial bits. We’re suddenly cool, and we don’t know what to do with ourselves.

But let’s be honest – the train station has a reputation. The Whirlpool forums say Mordi is the last stop on the Frankston line where you feel safe at night. Past Carrum? Sketchy. So yes, we’ve got “undesirables” hanging at the beach, but they’re mostly just kids with a skateboard and a vape. The real drama is that Bang Bang’s $2 oyster happy hour is so popular, we’re now fighting Hampton for a table.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a 24-hour 7-Eleven or thinks a “street art precinct” is a euphemism for gentrification. Also, if you don’t like sand in your coffee – Hendriks Cafe on White Street has windows overlooking the golf course, but the sand follows you home.

Who would love it? Families who want Braeside Brewing Co. for Sunday arvo, foodies who can’t decide between Chiki Chan’s Asian-fusion and Bang Bang’s hawker-style, and anyone who wants to watch the sun rise from a pier that’s “long and lonely” – then complain about it on a forum. That’s us.
