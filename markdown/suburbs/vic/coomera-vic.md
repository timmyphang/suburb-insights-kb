# Coomera (VIC)

Slug: `coomera-vic`

Let’s be real: tell anyone you’re from Coomera and they say, “Oh, Victoria, right?” No, mate – we’re the Gold Coast’s favourite overflow suburb. Where every second house is a brand-new build, every third car has a dog hanging out the window with its tongue in the breeze, and your weekend plans revolve around either Costco or Dreamworld. Yes, we own that.

Our Saturday routine goes like this: queue at Pause Button Cafe in the Coomera Collective, run by the same crew behind Southport’s Humble Cafe. If you’re feeling bougie you hit Black Sheep Bistro for their quirky modern-Australian menu – it was booming on weekends, apparently. But by the time the kids have done the playground at Coomera Westfield, you’ll eat anything that’s not a happy meal. And if you’re really desperate, Doughnut Time was supposed to open near Dreamworld – we’re still waiting, lads.

The biggest local drama? Sophie Monk’s parents reportedly moved out of their Coomera River home over wedding “tension.” That’s right – we made the tabloids over a wedding feud in a suburb where the biggest excitement used to be the mobile phone detection camera copping half the postcode with a phone on their lap. “I was just using the loudspeaker,” said every Coomera driver, to the tune of $400.

If you hate theme park traffic, dogs in prams, and constant construction updates about a new $75 million Dreamworld Resort, you’ll hate it here. Also, good luck setting up your Opticomm fibre – we’ve got the Whirlpool threads to prove it.

But look. We’ve got the Coomera Lodge Hotel, a pub that’s been pouring since 1885 – and now they’ve done it up with cabins so you can roll from the chicken parmi straight into bed. We’ve got Costco, which is basically our town square. And according to Time Out, we’re one of the most pet-packed suburbs in Queensland. So if you love cheap bulk groceries, a dog that thinks it owns the couch, and a chilli dog from a 140-year-old pub, welcome to Coomera. Just put your phone in the glovebox first.
