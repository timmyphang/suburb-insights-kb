# Windsor (VIC)

Slug: `windsor-vic`

So Time Out went and named us the tenth coolest neighbourhood in the world in 2024. Tenth. The only Australian suburb in the top ten, apparently. And while we appreciate the nod, we can't help but laugh — because Windsor is basically a single strip of Chapel Street that got lost on its way to Prahran and decided to stay.

Here’s your typical Windsor morning: you wake up in a nineteenth-century terrace you’re renting for $600 a week (no parking, of course), wander down to what was once Good Nights for some beef brisket pho before it said good night in 2023, and accept that all the cool new stuff is happening in Brunswick now. But we’ve got Belle’s Hot Chicken — opened a third store right on Chapel Street so you can smash some fried chicken and natural wine for under $12 a glass. That’s the Windsor bargain: cheap wine, expensive rent.

The drama? Well, our defining Vietnamese diner closed its doors because the lease came up and the owner decided to consolidate his brand up north. As Broadsheet noted, Good Nights was the “more playful and fun” offsider — but it’s gone. That’s Windsor in a nutshell: we make the world’s coolest list, but we’re small enough that one restaurant closure is headline news.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “cool” means having a laneway bar on every corner. We’ve got a doughnut shop (Bistro Morgan gives you a free mini doughnut on National Doughnut Day) and Messina for gelato if you’re a 90s kid. But if you want a backyard bigger than a postage stamp, or a street that isn’t clogged with Ubers at 2am, this isn’t your vibe.

Who would love it? Those who understand that what Windsor lacks in size, it makes up for in attitude — as Sitchu put it, this place has “just the right amount of edge.” It’s the suburb that sits between South Yarra’s gloss and St Kilda’s chaos, and we’ve perfected the art of being overlooked. We’re cool enough for Time Out, but honest enough to know we’re still just a skinny Chapel Street pocket with a killer fried chicken sandwich.
