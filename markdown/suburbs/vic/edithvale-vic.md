# Edithvale (VIC)

Slug: `edithvale-vic`

Edithvale, VIC — where the beach is so secret even the sand is suspicious of newcomers. "Edithvale Beach? Never heard of it," we say, shielding our eyes from the sun while hogging the dunes like they’re the last lifeboats off the Titanic. Urban List says we’re “sand-hungry locals” keeping it all to ourselves — and yes, that’s an accurate description of a Saturday morning.

Our local pillar? The Edithvale General Store, a former milk bar that’s now half café, half shop — because nothing says community like splitting your identity down the middle. As Urban List asks, “Can a business be a pillar of the community?” Mate, it’s got a side of general store and a side of caffeine. That’s two pillars. 

Back in the day (2020, per Broadsheet), we had Soul Press for Instagram-worthy green smoothies and bowls that looked like edible gardens. But that’s old news — now we’re getting Swedish buns delivered from Semla in Seaford between Edithvale and Mornington, because nothing says beachside chic like cardamom buns from a garage micro-bakery.

For drama, we’ve got the Whirlpool forums revealing 67 Rae Avenue is a real estate experiment with four different bankers — BOQ, Westpac, NAB, and a cash buyer. That’s more bankers than a Monopoly game. And we’ve got the Edithvale Life Saving Club — so new it was launched in 2020 with free tours and afternoon tea. We’re still not sure if it’s a club or a showroom.

Who’d hate it here? Anyone who expects a buzzy nightlife or a café that stays open past 2pm. We’re a three-café town with a general store and a fire brigade that does the BBQ at Carols by Kingston. If you want “vibes,” head to Chelsea.

Who’d love it? People who want to paddle-board in silence, eat a cinnamon bun in their car, and feel like they’ve discovered the last real beach in Melbourne. Just don’t tell anyone we told you.
