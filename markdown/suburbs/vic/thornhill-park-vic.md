# Thornhill Park (VIC)

Slug: `thornhill-park-vic`

Thornhill Park, VIC — where the closest thing to a natural wine bar is the multipurpose room at the Thornhill Park Children’s and Community Centre, and let’s be honest, the kombucha at playgroup is probably sparkling water with a whiff of cordial. We don’t have a wine bar. We don’t have a cafe. We have a community hub with “ample natural light” and a kitchen, and we’re proud of it. Yes, Broadsheet, Time Out and Urban List all wrote guides to Melbourne’s best wine bars — and none of them came within 15km of us. We know.

But we do have a story. Back in 2025, a 19-year-old from Thornhill Park made headlines — not for opening a wine bar, but for being one of seven arrested over a wild machete brawl at Northland Shopping Centre. “A Thornhill Park man,” the article said. We didn’t ask for that representation, but here we are. The other arrests included an 18-year-old from Wollert, a 15-year-old, and three 16-year-olds. The north-west really said “let’s make the news together.”

Meanwhile, our real heartbeat is the Thornhill Park Children’s and Community Centre — an integrated hub with maternal and child health services, kindergarten programs, and a community room so spacious you could host a machete-free playgroup in it. The playgroup reviews say it has “ample natural light and environmental stimulation to move, engage and play.” That’s us. No wine list. Just good, honest, well-lit community vibes.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who types “best wine bar near me” into Google and expects results. Anyone who wants a smashed avo that costs $22 and comes with a side of social cachet. The only pairing happening here is a nappy and a wet wipe.

Who would love it? Families who value a real community centre over a wine list. Parents who want to know the names of the other mums and dads at playgroup. People who don’t need a “fun place to drink good wine” because they’re already having fun chasing a toddler around a room with an onsite kitchen. Thornhill Park — it’s not for everyone. But the community room is available for hire. BYO sparkling water.
