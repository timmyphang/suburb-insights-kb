# Melton South (VIC)

Slug: `melton-south-vic`

We’re Melton South. We’re the suburb Canberra suburbs use as a punchline (“Tuggeranong is like the Melton South of the ACT”), which is rich coming from a place where the main attraction is a roundabout with a flagpole.

Back in the day, our NBN saga was a suburban legend. We got aerial fibre lead-ins strung between poles — not underground, not romantic, just fibre swinging in 100km/h winds like a tightrope walker with a death wish. The Whirlpool forums spent years debating whether optic fibre could handle the bend. Spoiler: it could. But we still had to believe it would arrive by April 2020. Classic Melton South: we’ll believe it when we see it.

For community, we’ve got Grace Community — formed from three old churches, celebrating over 150 years of worship. They run basketball teams at Cobblebank Stadium in the Melton Basketball Association. And every March, the World Day of Prayer gathers at St Anthony’s on Exford Road. This year’s focus was Taiwan. You’ll find us there at 10:30am, after the NBN has buffered.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs their suburb to be a personality trait. People who think “aerial lead-in” is an architectural feature. If you need a Lululemon within walking distance and a cafe called “Kindred & Soul,” you’re going to have a bad time.

Who would love it? People who just want a house, a basketball court nearby, and a church that says “come as you are” and actually means it. People who are used to waiting — for NBN, for trains, for the next development to maybe one day bring a proper supermarket. Melton South is not trying to impress you. And frankly, that’s the whole point.
