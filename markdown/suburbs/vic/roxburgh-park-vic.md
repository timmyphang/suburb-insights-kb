# Roxburgh Park (VIC)

Slug: `roxburgh-park-vic`

We’re Roxburgh Park, the suburb that spent 2020 on the nightly news as postcode 3064 — the COVID hotspot that made Craigieburn look like Byron Bay. Our claim to fame? Daniel Andrews had more excuses to mention us than we had bogans at the Roxburgh Park Shopping Centre entrance. And trust us, that’s a lot.

A Saturday morning here is a vibe: you take the kids to Airborn Indoor Trampoline Park (open 11am, because we’re not waking up before 10 on a weekend), then hit the Roxburgh Rise Community Hub on Bluebird Way for the Assyrian/Chaldean Playgroup at 9:30 — but you’re late. You grab a coffee from nowhere because there’s literally no café mentioned in any article about us since 2020. So you boil your tap water, just like Yarra Valley Water told 88 suburbs to do back in 2020. Yes, we’re still cautious.

The petty drama? We had 286 active COVID cases in July 2020 — second-highest in Victoria. Then a 2024 report showed we also lead the state in vacant homes. So we’ve got empty houses AND a trampoline park. Make it make sense. Also, our Telstra exchange apparently still has high pings at peak times. The Whirlpool forums are savage: “bogans drinking at the shop entrance, fully sik bros in old Commodores, wanna-be arab gangsters.” Look, they’re not wrong, but they also missed the bit where Good People Act Now meets Thursdays to end violence against women. We’ve got layers.

Who’d hate it here? Someone who wants a flat white from a place called “Ritual” and assumes “Roxburgh Park” is a nature reserve. It’s not. It’s a subdivision with 20 different builders all arguing on Whirlpool about who forgot to install the toilet.

Who’d love it? Families who want a quiet residential feel, a primary school with a Sri Lankan playgroup on Wednesdays, and Christmas lights that made the Urban List in 2025. We’re not flashy, but we’re real. And we’ve got Airborn. What more do you need?
