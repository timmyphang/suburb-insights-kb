# Eltham North (VIC)

Slug: `eltham-north-vic`

Eltham North, VIC — we’re so quiet we make Eltham look like the CBD. Back in 2020 the government promised us an off-leash dog park as part of some grand $154 million Suburban Parks Program. Five years on and we’re still waiting to see if our dogs are meant to roam the *undeveloped parklands* or just guard the barbecue shelters at Eltham North Parklands.

That’s our main attraction: a running/cycling track, a few BBQs, and a small playground. As Time Out put it, the parklands are “undeveloped.” Not a euphemism — they mean there’s a track and a picnic table. Our Saturday morning routine is a lap of that oval, a wave to the four other humans who made the same mistake, and a quiet drive to Eltham for a coffee because we don’t have a café.

The drama? Local Facebook groups still arguing whether the dog park ever actually got built. Some say it’s an empty paddock. Others claim it’s a state secret. We’re looking at you, Suburban Parks Program.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a barista within walking distance. Anyone who thinks “undeveloped” is an insult. Anyone who asks “where should we grab dinner?” and enjoys a conversation longer than “there’s the Zen Den in Eltham.”

Who would love it? People who genuinely enjoy an empty oval. People who want to raise their kids in a suburb where the biggest news is a wellness centre opening in the next suburb over (THERAPY, gut health, the works — but it’s in Eltham, not here). People who define a good time as a quiet barbecue with no queues. We’re low-maintenance, and we’re proud. Now if you’ll excuse us, we’ve got a dog park to find.
