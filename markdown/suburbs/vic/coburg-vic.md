# Coburg (VIC)

Slug: `coburg-vic`

**Coburg, VIC — Where the Pole Dancers Don’t Give a Fork About Pentridge’s Past**

There’s two Coburgs now, and they’re in an awkward, sweaty slow-dance right on Sydney Road.

On one hand, you’ve still got the Tibaldi’s factory churning out frankfurts for the Whirlpool forums to argue about. On the other, you’ve got a former wholesale Lebanese bakery that’s now **Hotel Lombardo** — a 100-seat Italo-American dive bar where you can “enter via a back alleyway” and find a pole dancing centrepiece inspired by a Bushwick nightclub. Yes, that pole. The one that “only takes one person jumping on to get the party started.” If your upper-body strength isn’t there yet, order the Calypso Mortadel pizza with stewed peaches and treacle — that’ll get you through.

But the real local genius move? **Sweet Nectar Inn** at 140 Nicholson Street. Devon Hunter from California and Sam O’Farrell (who spent six months hand-painting 300 tiles for the bar top) have turned a former hair salon into a hybrid Aussie milk bar meets American dive. It’s so Coburg you can grab a City Larder pate, some Acide pickles, a flagon of Fin Wines and call it a “DIY picnic situation” on the footpath.

And of course, the elephant in the room — **Pentridge Prison**. We’ll never escape it. The site now has BrewDog in the old lockup, **The Interlude** (19 hotel rooms in former cells with bluestone walls), **The Glass Den** doing breakfast with Thai infusions, and **North and Common** dishing up hatted dinners in the mess hall. As Sitchu put it: “Once synonymous with Pentridge Prison, the site now anchors a lively precinct... blending heritage gravitas with modern flair.” Translation: we’ve turned actual jail cells into a boutique hotel and still can’t decide if that’s cool or deeply weird.

**Who would hate it here?** Anyone who wants a quiet Saturday evening. Because between Lombardo’s disco ball, the bin chickens at Coburg Lake Reserve (yes, Time Out calls it **Bin Chicken Island** — a colony of ibis the council can’t shift), and the Coburg Night Market returning for 2025, there’s nowhere to hide.

**Who would love it?** The person who genuinely can’t decide if they’re a bogan, a hipster, or a former prisoner guide. We’re all of them, and somehow, the party’s just starting.
