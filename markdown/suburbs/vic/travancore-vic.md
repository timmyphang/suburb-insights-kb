# Travancore (VIC)

Slug: `travancore-vic`

Travancore, VIC — the postcode that exists purely so real estate agents can write “leafy inner-north pocket” without actually mentioning we’re wedged between the Tullamarine Freeway and a tram depot. We’re so small Google Maps sometimes just gives up and labels us “Ascot Vale anyway.”

Our claim to fame? Phat Milk. That’s it. One Middle Eastern breakfast joint that Urban List once deigned to mention — and we’ve been riding that high ever since. You want a flat white? Fine. But if Phat Milk is closed, you’re driving to Moonee Ponds like a peasant.

And yes, we were in that COVID lockdown postcode — 3032, baby! Tossed in with Ascot Vale and Maribyrnong like the awkward cousin at the family barbecue. The whole country learned we exist for five seconds, then promptly forgot.

The rest of the article data? All about Elwood and Brisbane and some ice-cream shop called Fluffy Torpedo in Fitzroy. That’s the Melbourne media for you — they’ll write 5,000 words on a scroll bakery in the CBD but Travancore gets a footnote in a stay-at-home order.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who wants a nightlife, a second café, or a street that doesn’t double as a rat run for people trying to avoid the freeway tolls.

Who would love it? People who want to say they live in a suburb with a royal-sounding name but no actual palace, no tram stop, and a single breakfast spot that’s doing the Lord’s work. We’re not much, but we’re ours. And we’ve got Phat Milk. For now.
