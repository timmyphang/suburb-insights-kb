# Beveridge (VIC)

Slug: `beveridge-vic`

We know our postcode. We know the developers have plans — 15,000 new homes, four town centres, eight schools, green public space as far as the eye can see. That’s the future. That’s Beveridge North West.

Right now? You’re standing on a dirt patch off Old Sydney Road wondering where the servo went.

But we’ve always been a place of quiet ambition. For years, the only reason anyone outside the 3753 knew our name was because of a Christmas tree farm at 445 Old Sydney Road — Coburg Christmas Trees, if you’re feeling festive in July. And because every time you tell a city mate you live in Beveridge, they ask if you’re related to Luke Beveridge (Bulldogs coach) or Michael Beveridge (Big Brother 2012). No, we’re not. But we do have a strong naming consultant community apparently.

The petty drama? There isn’t much yet. We don’t have a cafe to fight over. Our main local news is that a massive estate is coming, and we’re all waiting to see if the town centre will have a bakery or just another display village. The biggest controversy so far is whether the new suburb should be called “Beveridge North West” or just “Beveridge II: The Reckoning”. Council hasn’t returned our calls.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who wants immediate gratification. If you need a brunch spot with oat milk on tap and a resident sourdough whisperer, you’re in the wrong paddock. Also, anyone who thinks “15,000 homes” means we’ll have a train station this decade. Cute.

Who would love it? People who see a blank canvas and think, “I could build something there.” Homeowners who want a block of dirt and a promise of parks. Families who don’t mind driving 20 minutes for milk because the school zone is worth it. And anyone who enjoys telling friends, “Yeah, it’s up past Wallan, you’ll know it when you see the Christmas trees.”

We’re not a suburb yet — we’re a spare room in Melbourne’s future. And honestly? We’re okay with that. The estate agents are going to need a lot of coffee.
