# Geelong (VIC)

Slug: `geelong-vic`

We wake up at 6:45am to catch the Geelong Flyer ferry from Steampacket Quay, because nothing says "I’m a sophisticated regional commuter" like a 90-minute cruise to Docklands with free Wi-Fi and an onboard cafe. Sure, the train’s an hour, but the ferry feels like a micro-holiday before you even hit the office. By the time we’re back, we’re grabbing a pastry from the sleek Geelong CBD outpost of that Wallington bakery — get there early or it’s sold out. Then we roll into Yoga 213 in Newtown, where Sammy Veall has an oversized painting of Biggie Smalls above the front desk and the music is turned up louder than any normal yoga class. Because nothing says zen like Downward Dog to Drake.

But let’s talk about our identity crisis. Time Out ranked Geelong the third least romantic city in Australia — right behind Newcastle-Maitland and Bunbury. Cupid’s arrow missed us entirely. Meanwhile, we were supposed to host the 2026 Commonwealth Games alongside Bendigo, Ballarat and Gippsland, but Premier Dan Andrews pulled the plug when the cost ballooned from $2.6 billion to at least $6 billion. So now we’ve got $2 billion in promised upgrades and 13,000 new homes, but no athletes’ village. Typical Geelong: we get the budget blowout without the actual event.

Then there’s the Chin Chin pop-up. Chris Lucas grew up here — his dad ran pubs in the ’60s — and he’s finally bringing a yearlong Club Chin Chin to the GMHBA Stadium grandstand as part of the $142 million Joel Selwood Stand. It’s not a commercial exercise, he says, just a bit of fun. But we know the truth: Geelong has been desperate for a proper late-night noodle joint since forever. We’ll take it.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks a prohibition-style speakeasy at 18th Amendment on Little Malop Street is too try-hard, or that a wine shop like Bottles & Barrels with leather couches and Lethbridge Wines isn’t a real bottle-o. Also, anyone who can’t handle the ferry timetable changing on public holidays.

Who would love it? People who want a Crowne Plaza waterfront glow-up, a yoga class named after Snoop Dogg’s group, and a city that’s still figuring out if it’s a small town or a bustling metropolis. We’re both, and that’s the charm. Just don’t ask us for romance — we’re too busy queueing at the bakery.
