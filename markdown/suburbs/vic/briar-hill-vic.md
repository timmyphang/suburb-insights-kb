# Briar Hill (VIC)

Slug: `briar-hill-vic`

Yes, we’re Briar Hill. The suburb that made #5 on a 2021 PWC liveability list but still can’t get a café that stays open past 2pm. We share our postcode with Montmorency like a shy cousin at a wedding — “Briar Hill? Oh, you mean near the fancy shopping centre?” No, we mean the pocket of winding streets where every second house has a trampoline and a dog that barks at possums.

Our claim to fame? The snot block. Urban List crowned our vanilla slice the best in Victoria in 2023, and honestly? We’ve been riding that custardy high ever since. Half of Melbourne apparently called them to sing our praises. We still don’t know which bakery it was — the data doesn’t say — but every local knows the bakery at the Plenty Road shops used to have a mean one. Used to. Because nothing in Briar Hill stays forever. The milk bar that sold lollies by the scoop? Gone. The video store that saved you on Friday nights? A memory.

We get it. We’re quiet. Too quiet for the inner-north crowd who need a new brewery every month. Come here and you’ll miss your smashed avo — there isn’t even a proper brunch spot. You’ll spend your weekends driving to Greensborough for a coffee that doesn’t taste like regret. You’ll wonder why every street is named after a tree and why no one knows how to merge onto the Greensborough Highway.

But if you want a genuine two-storey brick veneer on a quarter-acre block with a Hills Hoist and a kookaburra on the fence? Mate, this is heaven. We don’t have pretension — we have the Warringal Parklands, a decent bus route, and a community Facebook group that loses its mind over bin collection changes. The people who love it here? They’re the ones who bought in 2021 and now smile smugly as the report gets screen-shotted again. Welcome to Briar Hill: it’s not much, but it’s ours.
