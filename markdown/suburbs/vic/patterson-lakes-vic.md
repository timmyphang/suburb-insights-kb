# Patterson Lakes (VIC)

Slug: `patterson-lakes-vic`

Ah, Patterson Lakes. You know us — we’re the suburb that gave Australia the Day-Knight residence, a fake Fountain Lakes postcode, and a very real 4 Lagoon Place that’s now a pile of Dig Dig Demolition rubble. Yes, we’ve read our ‘istory books. The Kath & Kim house was demolished in 2019 and the horn-bags are still emotional about it. But we’re not just a TV set. We’re a place where you can still get a Pai Mei Death Treat from The Famished Wolf — loaded dim sims with kewpie, sweet soy, sambal oelek, crispy shallots, and spring onions. That’s the kind of elevated bogan cuisine we’re about. 

On a Saturday morning, you’ll find us at Mr Patterson on Thompsons Road, ordering the ricotta-stuffed brioche French toast with Golden Gaytime ice-cream and “gay dust” — because nothing says breakfast like white-chocolate-snow crumble at 9am. The walls are dusty pink, the flowers are on the counter, and the coffee is Industry Beans. It’s homey. It’s warm. It’s a world away from the lagoon estates that give us our name.

But let’s talk drama. The biggest news to hit Patterson Lakes in years? The demolition of 4 Lagoon Place. We had an open day in 2019 — the last chance to sticky beak at the original set — and then poof, gone. Some locals still drive past and mutter “it’s noice, it’s different, it’s unusual.” Others are still trying to sell the dream with Sanctuary Lakes vibes. But we know the truth: we’re the quieter, wetter cousin of the bayside suburbs, with a community centre on Playgroup Victoria’s radar and not much else making headlines.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting a thriving nightlife or a mini golf hotspot. Time Out’s guide mentions Forest Hill Chase for Birdies — not us. Concrete Playground lists events that are just… blank. We don’t even have a proper pub in the data. You want excitement? Head to the Queen Vic Market’s Brick Lane Brewing — that’s not us either.

But who would love it? People who want a dead quiet life by the lakes, who appreciate a killer dim sim, who don’t mind their suburb’s claim to fame being a demolished TV set. We’ve got water views, we’ve got Mr Patterson, and we’ve got the legacy of Kath Day-Knight. What more do you need? Noice.
