# Hallam (VIC)

Slug: `hallam-vic`

There’s a moment, around 9pm on a Thursday night in Hallam, when the karaoke microphone at Cha’s Cabin gets passed from the owners to a bloke who’s had one too many Scotches on the rocks. You’re there for the all-you-can-eat Sri Lankan buffet — yellow rice, kottu roti, hoppers, thosai — and suddenly you’re also part of a live performance. As Broadsheet noted, Lankans love nothing more than a drink and singing into the wee hours, and at Cha’s Cabin on Spring Square, that’s not a vibe, it’s a roster.

We are Hallam. We are the suburb that got name-checked in the 2017 platform extensions for the Cranbourne-Pakenham line, because our train station needed to fit supersized trains. So if you’re waiting on the platform for a V/Line that’s 20% longer, remember: that’s government money spent on *us*. We are also the suburb that, according to Time Out, was a “priority suburb” for mobile COVID testing vans, right alongside Broadmeadows and Sunshine West. Take that as you will.

The local drama? It’s the Cha’s Cabin Friday-Saturday “Eastern and Western buffet,” where your plate gets lasagne next to your rice and curry. Purists are furious. The rest of us are just trying to figure out if the karaoke machine accepts requests for Chisel.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks a night out involves a small-batch negroni and a side of judgement. If your ideal weekend is a farmers market and a discussion about single-origin beans, you will not survive the Thursday night singalong.

Who would love it? People who understand that the best night of the week involves a $27 buffet, a mic drop, and a walk past the Christmas tree farm on Belgrave-Hallam Road in December. We’re not fancy. We’re just full.
