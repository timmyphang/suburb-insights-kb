# Noble Park (VIC)

Slug: `noble-park-vic`

**Noble Park, VIC**

We are the suburb they yell about from the right-hand lane of the Monash Freeway. The one where trucks got banned from that lane back in 2018, and we still haven't forgiven the government for taking away our favourite overtaking companion. Yes, we know what they say about us. “Bogan.” “Blackest.” “The place you pass through on the way to Dandenong.” They are not wrong, but they also missed the bit where Jacksons Road now links directly with EastLink and we have 600 construction jobs to show for it.

Our morning routine: grab a banh mi roll from Hoa Hiep Hot Bread (the same ones For Heaven’s Steaks uses for their perfect paninis), then head to Lollipop's Playland to let the kids run wild in the heated, air-conditioned cavern while you dream of the days when Noble Park Swim Centre had the largest water slide in Melbourne. That slide was a legend. We’d queue for 20 minutes, come down in 10 seconds, and somehow it felt like a full-day adventure. Now we just drive past the pool and reminisce.

The drama? The state government wants to turn us into a 12-storey activity centre. We’re getting high rises, baby. But also we’re getting 11 MCGs worth of new parklands under the Skyrail, including a fenced dog park in our own backyard. So we’ll take the density if it comes with 4000 extra trees and a walking track all the way to Caulfield.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks a “suburban upgrade” means a new wine bar instead of a freeway lane. Also people who can’t handle the smell of fresh banh mi at 8am or the sound of kids screaming at Lollipop’s. And definitely anyone who gets stressed about a truck drifting into their lane. Stay on Huntingdale Road, thanks.

Who would love it? Families who want a house under $700k, a big pool with a giant slide, and a park that’s about to get 11 MCGs of open space. Also anyone who appreciates a good op-shop rummage — Alice Wormald came all the way from the city to hit our secondhand stores. We’re not fancy. But we’ve got the largest water slide in Melbourne, a direct link to EastLink, and a banh mi that’ll change your life. Come for the development, stay for the bread.
