# Keilor (VIC)

Slug: `keilor-vic`

**Keilor, VIC — where the wine is cool-climate and the welcome is equally cool**

We’ve got a suburb that’s basically three suburbs in a trenchcoat: Keilor proper (bluestone wineries and underworld shootings), Keilor East (a quarry that finally got its cafe after 15 years of broken promises), and Keilor Downs (where we once made the state’s COVID hotspot list alongside Broadmeadows — we own it). 

Sunday morning in Keilor means one thing: driving past the Organ Pipes National Park thinking “we should really go there one day,” then heading to Perry’s (Peregrine Social Club) on Valley Lake Boulevard for a parma and a pint of their house brew. The owners promised locals a cafe when they bought into the estate back in 2009, so when Perry’s finally opened in February 2024, it was less a grand opening and more a “FINALLY” audible from Niddrie to Tullamarine. We don’t hold grudges — we just hold our coffee cups with quiet triumph.

The real drama? Back in 2023, a daylight shooting at a cafe in Keilor Village got Melbourne’s underworld buzzing. Gavin “Capable” Preston became the talk of the town — and not the good talk, like when Arundel Farm Estate’s 150-seat restaurant does a weekend pasta special with their own red wine ragu. Yes, Keilor has a 151-year-old bluestone winery overlooking the Maribyrnong Valley, and yes, we also have the kind of headlines that make your mum text you “are you OK?” from Queensland.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting an inner-north hipster scene. Our coffee beans might come from Industry Beans, but our vibe is more “quiet streets, good blocks, no through traffic” — as one Whirlpool local put it. We don’t do flashy. We do 400 Gradi’s margherita gelato (yes, it exists, and yes, it’s as weird as it sounds).

Who would love it? People who want a 25-minute drive to the city, a winery with actual horses, a community centre with playgroup, and a cafe that doubles as a social club with plans for movie nights on the cliff face. We’re the suburb that survived a pandemic hotspot and a cafe shooting and still showed up for brunch at Little Sister Cafe. That’s Keilor for you: bluestone tough, pinot grigio smooth.
