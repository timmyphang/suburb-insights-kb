# Watsonia North (VIC)

Slug: `watsonia-north-vic`

We have a single claim to fame in Watsonia North, and it’s a man who spent six years stealing shoes off porches. 2300 pairs of work boots and sneakers. “The Great Shoe-dini,” they called him. We’re not even mad, that’s impressive dedication. Who has a room that big? And who in Watsonia North is ordering that many boots online between 11pm and 7am? We all have questions.

Our morning routine doesn’t involve a trendy café with oat milk art. It’s more like: get in the car, drive past Myrtle Court and Middleton Street (the forum said they were nice, thanks Whirlpool), and try to find a bulk billing medical centre that’s not a myth. We’re still looking, mate.

The local drama is the shoe heist. For six years, police got reports from Doreen, Watsonia North, Craigieburn, and Mernda. We were robbed of our dignity and our Dunlop Volleys. The Great Shoe-dini was charged with theft and handling stolen goods, released on bail, and due in Heidelberg Magistrates’ Court in April 2018. We assume he’s either reformed or has a really impressive collection at home. We never got our boots back.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who expects a “vibrant community” or a “buzzing Errol Street.” We have a postcode that Sitchu accidentally tagged onto Fitzroy North articles. We are not trendy. We are a suburb that made news only because of a man with a footwear obsession. If you need lifestyle features, go read about Carlton North’s Rathdowne Street. We’ll be here, watching our porches.

Who would love it? People who want a quiet pocket near the Greensborough Highway, where the biggest news in six years is a shoe theft ring. People who don’t mind that the only local legend is a man who allegedly couldn’t resist a free pair of Blundstones. It’s honest. It’s us. And if you see a shoe on your doorstep, just leave it. It’s probably ours anyway.
