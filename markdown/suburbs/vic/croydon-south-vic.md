# Croydon South (VIC)

Slug: `croydon-south-vic`

Croydon South, VIC — where the most exciting thing to happen in recent memory was a boil-water alert that named us alongside 87 other suburbs. Yes, the 2020 Yarra Valley Water drama hit our taps, and we boiled up like a good kettle. That’s the level of buzz we’re dealing with.

Our local claim to fame? Goldeluck’s Bakeshop, at 15 The Mall, back when it was the place to grab a doughnut that was “juuuust right.” Urban List mentioned us as a spot “with time to kill” — and mate, we’ve got plenty of that. The bakeshop may still be kicking, but the data’s so quiet we can’t guarantee it. That’s Croydon South for you.

Petty local drama? Try your NBN dropping out for 12 hours. One poor soul on Whirlpool logged a fault in August 2020, power-cycling their Arris CM8200 while all the lights stayed on. We feel their pain. That’s the kind of excitement that gets a forum thread going here.

Who would hate living here? Anyone hoping for a nightlife, a food scene, or even a working internet connection at peak hour. We’re a residential pocket wedged between the train line and EastLink, where “popping out” means driving to Croydon proper. If you need a cocktail bar or a wine list with 20 by-the-glass options… keep driving to Bassendean. Yes, we’re referencing a Perth suburb because that’s how little local coverage we get.

Who would love it? Families who just want a decent playgroup. Local Mums Online lists Croydon playgroups for “getting out of the house” — and we take that seriously. If your idea of a big Saturday is a doughnut from a bakeshop and a calm afternoon at a park with no fuss, welcome home. We’re the suburb that doesn’t need a profile on Sitchu. We’re just here, boiling water when told, and hoping the NBN stays on long enough to finish this post.
