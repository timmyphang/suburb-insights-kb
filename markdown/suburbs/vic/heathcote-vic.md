# Heathcote (VIC)

Slug: `heathcote-vic`

📍 **Heathcote, VIC** – the wine-obsessed country cousin who’s quietly convinced it’s the French Riviera, but with more utes and fewer berets.

Let us set the scene: it’s a Saturday morning. You’re at the **Heathcote Region Farmers’ Market** at **Barrack Reserve**, clutching a plastic-free tote full of **Hope Farm Bakery** sourdough and **McIvor Farm** pork snags. A local is arguing with a stallholder about whether the romanesco is *actually* seasonal. This is the energy. Meanwhile, over at **Chauncy**, chef Louis Naepels is whisking someone into the kitchen garden for dessert like it’s a full-blown proposal. We don’t have a Lululemon crowd – we have people who drive an hour just to argue about Cambrian soil and shiraz clones.

But look, we’re not pretending we’re fancy. The region’s best-known for **Pink Cliffs** and mountain bike trails, and our idea of a big night is stargazing at **Mount Wombat Lookout** because the pub closed at 8. And now, thanks to the state government, **Heathcote is losing piped gas by the end of 2026**. Nothing says “country charm” like watching your gas stove get ripped out while you sip a grenache from **Sanguine Estate**.

The petty drama? We’re constantly confused with other Heathcotes. Sydney’s got a national park. Perth’s got a museum. And Bella Heathcote? She’s an actress, not our mayor. We get all the name recognition and none of the royalties.

Who would hate it here? People who need a functioning gas connection, or who think “wine region” means you can get a decent coffee after 3pm. Not happening, champ.

Who would love it? The sort of person who sees “Ten Victorian towns to lose piped gas” and thinks “perfect, now I can justify that induction cooktop.” The dog owners who book **The Barn** on six acres and never leave. The bike riders who actually *like* powdery pink dirt in their teeth.

Heathcote – we’re not trying to be the next Yarra Valley. We’re just here for the shiraz, the snags, and the smug satisfaction of knowing your region’s soil is older than your grandmother’s temper. 🍷
