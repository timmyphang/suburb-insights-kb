# Bentleigh East (VIC)

Slug: `bentleigh-east-vic`

We know what you think of Bentleigh East. It's the suburb you drive through on Centre Road to get somewhere *better*. The one where the biggest local drama is whether the falafel waffle at **Time & Place** is still as good as it was back in 2019 (it is, but the Aperol spritzes are now a cherished memory). Yes, we're the people who get excited when a former dry cleaner becomes an all-day diner with royal-blue umbrellas and a courtyard. That's us.

Our morning routine is a minefield of loyalty: do we queue at **Boosa** for the panko za'atar eggs and a smoky lamb slider under a glass cloche (thanks, Eitam's Moroccan grandmother Jacqueline), or do we hit **Neri's Hummus Bar** on a Friday only – because nothing says 'weekend flex' like a $15 hummus bowl from the factory front? And let's not forget **Hello SAM** at 691 Centre Road for a burger twist, or the wholesale-turned-public **Tahini Neri** operation. We have *options*, people.

The real petty drama? Coffee allegiances. When **The Merchants Guild** (queues out the door, remember?) used one roaster, and **Boosa** deliberately switched to 5 Senses just to be different. And then the Merchants Guild team opened *Walk Don't Run* in Armadale. We felt betrayed. But we moved on. We're resilient – we have to be, with no train station in the suburb proper.

You'd hate it here if you need a nightlife past 9pm, or you think a 'trendy' cafe means anything other than a stripped-back room with Scandinavian chairs and a falafel waffle. But you'll love it if you're a family who appreciates a good beeswax wrap from a local artisan (yes, made right here in Bentleigh East using Australian beeswax), or if you want to join a running club that's produced Olympic medal winners – yes, *that* running club counts us as alumni. We don't need your hipster validation. We've got hummus on Fridays and a vibe that says "we were here before the *Burnt Red Exterior* article." And we'll still be here when the next fad moves on.
