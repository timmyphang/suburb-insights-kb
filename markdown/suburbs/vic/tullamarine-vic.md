# Tullamarine (VIC)

Slug: `tullamarine-vic`

Tullamarine – where your GPS gives up before you even hit the M80. We’re the suburb that exists so planes have somewhere to land and so drivers can experience the existential dread of a 10pm freeway closure between the Calder and the Ring Road. As Broadsheet once reminded us, the Tulla Freeway shut in *both* directions for three days one summer. Three days. That’s not a detour, that’s a pilgrimage.

Back when we had a life beyond the terminal, Pasta Al Dente on Assembly Drive was the real reason to stop. Handmade arancini that took three days to perfect – until a fire next door sent 60 firefighters and a wave of water run-off their way. Business as usual the next day, because Tullamarine doesn’t flinch. Now the arancini kitchen still cranks, but you’ll find the real local drama at the Uber pickup zone. Concrete Playground called us out: “the correct rideshare area, looking at you Tullamarine.” Yes, we know we’re Australia’s worst-rated airport for convenience. But we’ve also been voted the best in the region for 2025 by Time Out. Make it make sense.

If you hate the smell of jet fuel, roundabouts that lead nowhere, and the constant low hum of “is my flight delayed?”, you’ll hate it here. We’ve got a wave park called URBNSURF that’s been open, closed, reopened, and now hosts Three Blue Ducks – but good luck getting there without hitting a pothole the state government promised to fix with $1 billion. And yes, there’s an adult store in the airport terminal. For “thinking” apparently.

But if you love a suburb that wears its working-class heart on its sleeve, where the local Italian grocer is tougher than a mezzaluna loaf, and where you can catch a wave, grab a reuben, and still make your 6am Jetstar to Sydney? Welcome home. Just bring snacks for the freeway.
