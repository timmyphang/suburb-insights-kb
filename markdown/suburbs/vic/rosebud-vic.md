# Rosebud (VIC)

Slug: `rosebud-vic`

There’s a specific light in Rosebud that Matthew Sleeth captured on medium-format film between 2002 and 2006 — teenagers leaning on cars, sun glowing through plastic milk-bar strips. That’s us. Then and now. Just with more retirees and fewer flannos.

We start our mornings at **Blue Mini** (arrive hungry for local produce, not bowling shoes like the ’90s), or duck into **Zarb & Ru** — a hole-in-the-wall on Point Nepean Road that’s all soul and no sign. If we’re feeling vintage, we hit **Reload Records**; the owner’s been selling vinyl since 1976, so he’s seen every trend come and go — including us.

Petty local grievance? We’re still livid about the night bus being axed on New Year’s Eve 2017. Last bus from the CBD at 9pm? We had to wait until 8am or cop an Uber surge. Thanks PTV. And yes, the summer traffic is a nightmare — but that’s the price of “beautiful bay views and easy access to conveniences,” as Time Out puts it.

Who’d hate it here? Anyone seeking a late-night scene. Our main strip offers **OH YA** Indian (opened 2023, first-time restaurateur Sarthak Bansal, legit) and an Irish pub on Point Nepean Road. That’s your nightlife. Also, if you’re allergic to retirees, look away — Time Out named us one of Victoria’s best retirement towns, and we wear that badge with pride.

But if you love a quiet seaside village with a film festival on the village green (shout-out to local actor **Steve Bastoni** for founding the Peninsula Film Festival), a record store that’s older than most residents, and that golden late-afternoon light that makes everything feel like a lost Matthew Sleeth photo — come on down. Just bring your own wheels. Buses stop early.
