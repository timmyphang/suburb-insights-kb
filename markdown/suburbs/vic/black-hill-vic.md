# Black Hill (VIC)

Slug: `black-hill-vic`

Black Hill, VIC — we couldn't find enough local stories for a proper roast this week. Try a bigger postcode.

Wait — actually, scrap that. Because if you're from *actual* Black Hill (South Australia, not Victoria — Broadsheet knows the difference), you know our suburb’s entire personality fits inside a daypack. We are a 4.2km, two-hour, “hard difficulty” hike with panoramic views of the Adelaide Plains and zero places to grab a coffee at the end. As Time Out put it, the Black Hill Summit Track is a “quick and easy” way to spot kangaroos or koalas — provided you survive the anticlockwise route that “get[s] the steep section done early” before you have to “climb down the rocks later.” So either way, you’re crawling.

This is a suburb where your weekend routine is either “I’m doing the summit” or “I’m recovering from the summit.” There’s no in-between. No cafe. No pub. No corner shop selling overpriced protein bars. Just eucalypts, loose scree, and the faint sound of someone from Athelstone complaining about parking. As Broadsheet noted, if you’re looking for cheap summer fun, you can “try a more challenging trail at Morialta or Black Hill” — and that’s it. That’s the entire entry. We are a footnote in a list of nine other things to do for under $10.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “the summit” is a craft brewery. Anyone who wants to walk to a gelato shop. Anyone who searches “Black Hill” on Sitchu and finds a $1.75M property in NSW with “groomed grounds” — and realises that’s a different universe. Our black hill is rocky, sweaty, and you share it with kangaroos who look at you like you’re the tourist.

But who would love it? People who understand that a suburb isn’t defined by its strip of shops — it’s defined by its one good trail. People who don’t need a barista to validate their existence. People who say “I’ll meet you at the top” and actually mean it. We’re not much, Black Hill. But the view? It’s ours.
