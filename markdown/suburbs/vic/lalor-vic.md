# Lalor (VIC)

Slug: `lalor-vic`

Welcome to Lalor — where your bank account breathes a sigh of relief, and your GPS pretends it’s still part of Melbourne.

Let’s be real. When Time Out named us one of the “10 most affordable and liveable suburbs in Melbourne” in 2024, they weren’t wrong. A median price of around $700,000? That’s not a bargain — that’s a miracle within 18km of the CBD. We’re the suburb where your first home deposit actually buys a home, not a car space in Brunswick East.

Our morning routine is simple: head to the Lalor Library on May Road. As Time Out noted, this branch of Yarra Plenty Regional Libraries in the City of Whittlesea is genuinely a thing to do in Lalor. And honestly? The air conditioning is free, the books are quiet, and nobody judges you for spending an hour reading the newspaper. It’s our cultural heartbeat, and we’re fine with that.

But let’s talk about the real vibe. Out here, the only cocktail bar drama involves whether you’ll get a table at the local pub or just grab a six-pack from the servo. The Broadsheet guide to Melbourne’s best cocktail bars? That’s for the CBD tourists. We’ve got a different kind of mixology — blending budget-friendly living with a genuine community spirit.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a Lululemon on every corner or a rooftop bar with a Negroni menu. If you think “going out” means a curated cocktail experience, you will feel personally victimised by our low-key lifestyle.

Who would love it? Everyone who’s tired of paying $2,000 a month for a shoebox in Fitzroy. We’re the affordable, liveable, library-loving locals who know that home isn’t about the address — it’s about the 18km commute that leaves you enough cash for a life. Come for the house prices, stay for the peace.
