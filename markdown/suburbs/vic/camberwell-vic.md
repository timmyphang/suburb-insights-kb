# Camberwell (VIC)

Slug: `camberwell-vic`

Saturday midday on Burke Road and you spot a grey coat. It’s 26 degrees. Is that Jacob Elordi? Nah, just another private school dad who hasn’t updated his wardrobe since 2015. Welcome to Camberwell.

We start our mornings at Collective Espresso on Cookson Street – an unassuming presence that brings a pleasant slice of the suburban ecosystem, as Concrete Playground put it. Then we’re off to the Camberwell Sunday Market, trawling for vintage treasures at Market 755 before the private school pickup chaos begins. Meanwhile, Kirishima on Camberwell Road is prepping 23 courses of omakase for the evening crowd – Kumamoto A5 Wagyu draped over sushi rice, truffle shavings, the works. Yes, we do both: bargain hunting and $200 dinners, often in the same day. As Urban List noted, “Camberwell is not just for avid thrifters – it’s a foodie haven.”

The real local drama? The Middle Camberwell EV charger on Riversdale Road. 200kW, four bays, 65c/kWh from August. But good luck getting a spot – it’s permanently ICEd by a Range Rover Sport with grammar school stickers. Also, Chargrill Charlie’s is finally opening at 10 Prospect Hill Road – our Melbourne editor nearly had a breakdown at the news, says Pedestrian TV. We’re just confused why we’d queue for Sydney chicken when Tandoori Den has been doing proper Indian since 1981.

You’ll hate it here if you don’t own a private school tie, a yoga hammock from Little Mandarin on Burke Road, or a strong opinion on whether Carey or Camberwell Grammar is superior. Also, don’t mention the Camberwell Free Film Festival – that’s in London, mate.

But you’ll love it if you want fog-island omakase and a vintage lamp from the Sunday Market in the same afternoon. We’re contradictions: misty peaks and market dust. And if you see Elordi again, tell him we forgive the coat. Just.
