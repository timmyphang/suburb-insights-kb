# Tootgarook (VIC)

Slug: `tootgarook-vic`

Tootgarook — you know, the bit of the Mornington Peninsula you zoom past at 90km/h on Point Nepean Road, windows up, car full of wine bottles from Red Hill, muttering “ooh look at that old motel” as you fly through to Sorrento. Well, surprise — that old motel is now actually cool.

The Keith Motel at 1929 Point Nepean Road has gone and pulled a saltwater-chic reno, and suddenly we Tootgarook locals have somewhere to send our visiting city mates that isn’t just “the primary school hall” where we hold playgroup. Yes, Tootgarook Primary School Hall — hosting Playgroup Victoria since at least July 2025, because that’s peak excitement around here.

We’ve also got Shoreline Hideaway, a pet-friendly seaside cottage dream near the water, because even your golden retriever deserves a weekend away from the shame of being walked past all those $4M Sorrento mansions. And if you’re after a bigger group, you can upgrade to Blue Haven in McCrae or Glen Mor in Rye — all part of Ocean Blue Coastal Retreats. Basically, we’re the Peninsula’s practical, understated cousin who lets you crash at their place while everyone else fights over the good beachfront.

Honestly, if you can’t handle the quiet hum of a suburb whose main claim to fame is “it has a primary school and a motel that used to be daggy,” then Tootgarook will break you. There’s no hype, no queues, no influencer pop-ups. Just a hall, a beach shack, and a motel that’s trying its best.

But if you love the idea of being five minutes from Sorrento’s smug but paying half the price for a holiday rental where your dog can dig up the garden — welcome home, mate. We’ve been waiting for someone to notice.
