# Upper Ferntree Gully (VIC)

Slug: `upper-ferntree-gully-vic`

**Upper Ferntree Gully, VIC — where the Dandenongs meet a health food store that looks like a witches' kitchen had a baby with a farmers' market.**

Morning routine in Upper Gully: you catch the Belgrave line to Upper Ferntree Gully Station, wait half an hour for the 688 bus (it comes “every half hour or so,” which is a generous interpretation), and by the time you arrive at Wholefood Merchants you’re already in need of a carob-mushroom hot chocolate to recover. Yes, that’s a real drink. It’s not a fever dream — it’s the cafe inside one of Melbourne’s largest health food stores, sitting right there in Ferntree Gully, serving organic cauliflower fritters and a low-FODMAP bowl so green it looks like a lawn in a good way.

The drama: we are a suburb at war with itself. On one hand, we have the Ferntree Gully Hotel, where live music Friday to Sunday in Flanagan’s room means you can get a refreshing ale while touring bands play in the Middle room. On the other hand, Wholefood Merchants is selling crystals, tarot cards and dandelion lattes. There is no middle ground. You are either weekending in the hills with a Devonshire tea at Ripe (avoiding Miss Marple’s, as any self-respecting local knows) or you are arguing on Whirlpool about ADSL2+ availability because you moved here expecting broadband and got a dandelion latte instead.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “organic” is a personality trait. Also anyone who doesn’t own a blanket cocoon and a strong opinions about whether the 688 bus is reliable (it is not). Also people who want to camp in the Dandenongs in winter — the article literally warns you it’s frosty. You have been warned.

Who would love it? People who want to feel like they’re in a tiny European village but can’t afford a plane ticket. People who want to buy bulk food and a tarot reading in one trip. People who aren’t afraid of a mushroom in their hot chocolate.

Welcome to Upper Ferntree Gully. It’s cold, it’s crunchy, and the bus is never on time — but at least you can get a crystal while you wait.
