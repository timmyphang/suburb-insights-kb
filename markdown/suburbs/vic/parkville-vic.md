# Parkville (VIC)

Slug: `parkville-vic`

**Parkville: where the hospital corridors smell like floor polish and deferred dreams**

Morning in Parkville starts with a cha-cha of medical students, grieving parents, and architecture nerds all queueing for a single filter coffee at Bureau of Provisions & Periodicals on Royal Parade. Jay Clough runs a one-man show — a newsagent for “the next gen” that stocks indie food mags, Mat’s Hot Shop Peachango Tango sauce, and absolute slabs of butter on jambon-beurre. We’re not a suburb, we’re a pit stop between a sick kid and a lecture hall.

The real action? A 17-storey tower approved for the old Commonwealth Games Village, despite locals pointing out it’ll cast a shadow over Royal Park that could make a possum suicidal. The original plan capped buildings at 11 storeys, but developers lobbied hard, and Planning Minister Richard Wynne said “how about 17 then.” So now we’ve got 900 homes huddled under a high-rise that’ll block long views of the Trin Warren Tam-boore wetlands. The jewel in the crown is now the shadow in the car park.

Then there’s Residence, a restaurant at the Potter Museum of Art with no menu and no head chef — just a vibe. Nathen Doyle and Cameron Earl want to swap chefs every year like TV seasons. The debut, Cherrywood, served devilled egg with smoked eel and skirt steak with ox tongue, which is exactly what you want after visiting a museum. It’s permanently closed now, but the concept lives on: a rotating door of ambitious chefs who don’t own a restaurant yet. We don’t have a pub, but we have a restaurant that’s basically a hospitality TAFE with a wine list.

The Metro Tunnel’s shiny new Parkville station will finally connect us to hospitals and Uni, but we’re fighting over who gets the shade first. 

**Who’d hate it here?** Anyone who thinks a suburb should have a pub that wasn’t illegally bulldozed, or a cafe with more than one staff member.

**Who’d love it?** Architecture students who appreciate a good shadow study, and food writers who like watching a restaurant concept decompose in real time.
