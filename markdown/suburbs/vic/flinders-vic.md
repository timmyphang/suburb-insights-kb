# Flinders (VIC)

Slug: `flinders-vic`

**Flinders, VIC** — The suburb that’s basically the shy cousin at the Mornington Peninsula family barbecue. You know the one. Hides down near Bass Strait, looks completely different to the bayside posers, and everyone says it’s a “gem” but nobody can actually name a cafe there.

We’re Flinders. The “completely different look and feel” suburb, as Urban List once called us. And they weren’t wrong. While Sorrento is busy polishing its yacht club membership and Rye is arguing about parking, we’re just sitting here with our rugged coastline, pretending we don’t notice the tourists driving past to the hot springs.

Back in the day, you could roll in for a quiet weekend and feel like you’d discovered something secret. The kind of place where the main attraction is the view and the local gossip is about whether the waves are any good. We don’t have a famous bakery, we don’t have a main street with a thousand boutiques — we have the ocean and a sense of relief that we’re not Blairgowrie.

If you hate peace and quiet, don’t come here. If you need a brunch spot open past 2pm, you’re out of luck. If your idea of a good time is a traffic jam on the Nepean Highway, stay in Frankston.

But if you want to sit on the edge of the continent, watch the swell roll in, and feel like you’ve escaped the whole Mornington Peninsula circus for a minute? We’re your spot. Just bring your own snacks — we don’t have a Woolies.
