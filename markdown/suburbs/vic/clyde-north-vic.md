# Clyde North (VIC)

Slug: `clyde-north-vic`

We don’t have a main street. We don’t have a heritage pub. But by god, we’ve got a golf simulator and a community hub that’s seen more playgroups than a daycare chain. Welcome to Clyde North—where the Friday night lights are six projectors beaming a virtual St Andrews onto foam turf at X-Golf, and the biggest social event of the week is “Playgroup at Manna Gum.”

The local morning rush isn’t about latte lines—it’s about whether you can snag a park at the Selandra Community Hub before the mums with double prams camp the good spots. Drop the kids at One Early Education Clyde North’s special playgroup for mothers and little ones (yes, that’s the data—a “relaxed, child-friendly environment”), then head to the Manna Gum Family and Community Centre, which opened back in 2021 as the “new hub servicing the Clyde North community.” It’s still servicing. Hard.

Our biggest local drama? When the X-Golf simulators were fully booked on a Saturday. We are not kidding—that was the talk of the parenting Facebook group. “Six simulators? Should’ve booked ahead, Karen.” We’ve got 611 square metres of sports-entertainment hybrid with a food and drink offering, and locals treat it like it’s the MCG. Because in Clyde North, it basically is.

If you need a craft brewery on every corner or a laneway cafe with a single-origin pour-over, you’re going to struggle. We’ve got fresh air, a Volvo-worthy number of family hubs, and a golf game that doesn’t require actual weather. The misfit here is anyone who thinks “village vibe” means cobblestones and a wine bar. We’re a housing estate that grew up fast, and we own it.

But if you’re starting a family, want a big house on a new estate, and think a parma at a pub you’ll drive 15 minutes to is a perfect weekend—welcome home. The community is real, the playgroups are thriving, and the most exciting thing in the neighbourhood is a world-class golf sim. And honestly? We wouldn’t have it any other way.
