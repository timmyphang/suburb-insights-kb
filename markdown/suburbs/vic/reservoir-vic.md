# Reservoir (VIC)

Slug: `reservoir-vic`

Reservoir, VIC — we have no actual reservoir. The name is a beautiful lie, like calling your suburb “Beachside” when you’re 45 minutes from the coast. But we own it. We’ve got La Pinta on High Street doing wallaby tartare and pumpkin agnolotti off a chalked-up menu, and Sardinas across the road selling flowers and lo-fi wine in concrete shelves. Yes, we have a florist-bar. We also have a bánh mì shop in Reservoir Village that Time Out says makes “huge” baguettes in “fluffy, soft, perfect bread.” That’s our cultural diversity in a nutshell: Spanish tapas and crusty pork rolls, no passport required.

The gentrification is real. Old-school Italian espresso bar turned into La Pinta, billiard room turned into a boutique wine bar. As Broadsheet noted, getting a seat at La Pinta is “a bit of a challenge” — even on a weeknight. Meanwhile, Luther’s Scoops opened its third shop here in 2025, so you can queue for butterscotch ripple alongside families who’ve been here since the Ford days. And Bonnie and Neil is hiring a pick/pack dispatch assistant for their Reservoir studio — they make fabric, we pack it, and everyone pretends we’re just another creative hub.

Misfits would hate it. Anyone who moves here expecting a literal reservoir will be deeply disappointed. Also anyone who can’t handle the 553 bus crawl down Plenty Road or the fact that our “nightlife” is essentially “which tapas bar has a table free at 7pm.” If you need a lake for your dog to swim in, go to Bundoora Park.

Who should move here? People who understand that bang-for-your-buck is a constant — whether it’s a $4 bánh mì or a $25 wallaby tartare. We’re Thornbury without the smugness, Preston without the overpriced townhouses. We’re a suburb that finally has good coffee and still keeps the servo open late. Come for the irony, stay for the Basque burnt cheesecake. Just don’t ask where the water is.
