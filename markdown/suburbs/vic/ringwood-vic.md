# Ringwood (VIC)

Slug: `ringwood-vic`

Our weekend starts at 6am with a deer doing $100,000 worth of damage to the local funeral home. That’s Ringwood for you. The wild deer that made its way down the Yarra corridor found our funeral home particularly inviting — Broadsheet called it “$100k worth of damage” back in 2021. We call it a Tuesday.

Then it’s off to Mr Lee’s Foods for some pork-heavy Korean-Australian institution vibes (Time Out called it “well worth the trip” — they’re not wrong). Or if you’re after something newer, Hoshino at Ringwood East has been serving Japanese on Railway Avenue since May 2025. The Urban List reckons it’s one of the best Japanese cafes in Melbourne. We just reckon it beats queuing at Eastland.

Ah, Eastland. Our $665 million shopping centre redevelopment that promised a Sage Hotel, an upgraded Ringwood station, and the Realm community hub with a library and art space. Tourists in Ringwood? The hotel started construction in 2016. We’re still waiting for the tourists. Meanwhile, The Cellar Door by The Public Brewery is a short walk from the station — a rooftop bar for parents who finally escaped the playground at one of those 21 Melbourne pubs with awesome play areas. Yes, we have parmas. Yes, we have bottomless brunch at The Firehouse Smokery and Bar. The Time Out guide knows.

But Ringwood isn’t just shopping and smashed interior design. We’ve got a 50-year-old playgroup (North Ringwood Playgroup, founded 1974) that’s still going strong with a paid facilitator. We’ve got Natskin, a day spa Urban List calls one of the best massages in Melbourne. We’ve got mental health pop-ups at Each, because sometimes you need a break from the deer funerals and water warnings (remember 2020 when Yarra Valley Water told 88 suburbs to boil their tap water? We were on that list).

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks a $100k deer is a liability and not local legend material. Anyone who expects their hotel to have opened before 2017. Anyone who can’t handle a spreadsheet scandal at the exclusive Ringwood private school (Pedestrian TV reported Year 11 students were expelled in 2024 for a Discord list — yes, we have that too).

Who would love it? Families who want a proper playgroup, a 15-minute train to the city, and a spa next to a shopping centre.  Anyone who saw Car City Ringwood in Aunty Donna’s Netflix show and thought “ah, a hyper-local Melbourne reference”. We’re not fancy. We’re just the suburb where a deer can break into a funeral home and everyone nods, says “that’s Ringwood”, and goes back to their bottomless brunch.
