# Mulgrave (VIC)

Slug: `mulgrave-vic`

You know you’re in Mulgrave when your biggest local claim to fame is a duck you need to order 48 hours in advance and a footy ground that hosted the 1991 AFL grand final stand construction. We’re the suburb that makes the southeast feel like a business park with a conscience — and we’re fine with that.

The morning routine? Forget brunch. You’re heading to Chopstick Delight on Mackie Road — paper tablecloths, handwritten menus, a fish tank in the back. The whole-roast duck arrives golden, and you’ve been mentally preparing for it since Tuesday. As Broadsheet would say if they ever visited: “Off the beaten track” is our permanent address. Meanwhile, Waverley Park is now a housing estate, but we still remember when the southern stand was built here in ’91. History, mate.

Local drama? We’ve got a One Nation candidate who offered to resign after a scandal, then got outraged when his resignation was accepted. That’s peak Mulgrave — can’t even quit properly. And if you’re on the NBN, you know the pain: HFC connections stopped in Eltham, Mulgrave and Doncaster, according to the Whirlpool forums. Best NBN news we ever received, they said. We’re still waiting for a signal that works.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting a silk pillowcase from a Broadsheet guide. We don’t do luxury throws. Our idea of cosy is a servo blanket and a working heater. Hipsters looking for street art? Try AC/DC Lane. We’ve got the Mulgrave Country Club and Little Groover’s concerts for the mums. That’s your culture.

Who would love it? People who appreciate a hidden gem that stays hidden. Families who want Guardian Early Learning at 211 Wellington Road and don’t need a latte art competition. Workers at the NEC/NextEP site who just need the NBN to not drop out. We’re Mulgrave — we’ve got a duck worth waiting for, a scandal worth forgetting, and a postcode that’s never going to be on any “best of” list. And we’re okay with that.
