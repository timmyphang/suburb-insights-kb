# Yallambie (VIC)

Slug: `yallambie-vic`

Yallambie, 3085 — the suburb that exists mostly as a postcode on other suburbs’ profiles. If you’ve heard of us, it’s probably because your water was dodgy in 2020 (shoutout Yarra Valley Water for putting us on the boil-water list alongside 87 other suburbs — we made the big leagues). Or you’re on Whirlpool forums at 2am trying to figure out which exchange services this place, because Naked DSL is apparently a myth here.

We don’t have a Broadsheet guide. We don’t have a bakery named Bread Club or a vodka-pasta controversy. What we have is the quiet honour of being a cul-de-sac-shaped blip between Viewbank and Watsonia. Back in the day, moving here meant you were either upgrading from a commission flat or hiding from an ex. Now it’s all “heritage overlay” and “where’s the nearest cafe?”

The internet drama is real. For years, your connection depended on which side of the creek you lived on, and the answer was always “no.” You want fibre? Mate, we’re still arguing about the exchange location on Whirlpool threads from 2014. The most exciting local event is when someone spots a delivery driver who hasn’t cancelled the order.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who expects a local bar, a decent brunch spot, or a pavement that doesn’t crack after autumn. Also people who enjoy being able to order a pizza that arrives hot.

Who would love it? People who think “quietly green” is a personality trait. Retirees who measure excitement by the number of magpies in the backyard. Families who treat a trip to Northland Shopping Centre as a day out. And anyone who’s ever said, “I don’t need a community, I just need a back deck and a spot to park my ute.”

Yallambie: we’re not the suburb you choose — we’re the one you end up in when you forget to exit at Greensborough. And honestly? The water’s fine now. Mostly.
