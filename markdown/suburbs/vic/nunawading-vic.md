# Nunawading (VIC)

Slug: `nunawading-vic`

**Nunawading: Where the only thing climbing faster than property prices is the NBN outage count**

Let us take you on a journey. It’s Saturday morning. You’ve just stumbled off the train at Nunawading Station, past *Station Street Café* — sure, convenient, but you’re not here for a quick toastie. You’re here for the big leagues: *Hardrock Climbing* on Varman Court. $12 gets you a full day of pretending you’re Spider-Man, but your arms will ache tomorrow. And after? You head to *Peddler Cafe* for pork belly with parsnip puree — because nothing says “I just fell off a wall” like confit fennel.

But let’s be real: the real drama in Nunawading isn’t the climbing routes — it’s the internet. “Anyone connected to Nunawading POI?” cried a local on Whirlpool. “Absolute crap with 3 outages in 3 days — out for 7 hours straight.” The HFC network here is more unreliable than a MasterChef dessert in a power outage. Speaking of which, we finally got a sweet treat coming: *Jess Lemon* — MasterChef’s own Jess Liemantara — is opening at 10 Market Street. Pastry classes, dessert degustations, croissants. But good luck booking — “councils gonna council,” she said, and we felt that in our bones.

Then there’s the car scene. “Please avoid Nunawading Toyota,” warns another forum ghost. If you can’t get satisfaction, fight them — or just take your Corolla to Harvey Norman Nunawading and buy a Samsung 3D Plasma instead. (Yes, that’s a real complaint from 2010.) And don’t even start on JB Hi-Fi moving to Nunawading from Doncaster — “very inconvenient,” muttered a user.

**Who would hate it here?** Anyone who expects their water to be safe without boiling. Back in 2020, Yarra Valley Water put 88 suburbs on alert, including ours. You couldn’t drink, brush teeth, or make baby formula without a rolling boil. Also: people who think “industrial estate” means “arts precinct.” We have a performing arts centre called *The Round* — but it’s in Ringwood, not here.

**Who would love it?** The kind of person who loves a good multi‑phasic bakery opening (workshops in May, cafe in July, degustations in October — maybe). The climber who wants to lead‑climb 16m walls without a partner. The soul who sees “Nunawading” on a temperature chart and thinks, “Bet my block has never had a water quality issue.” We’re not fancy, but we’ve got pork belly waffles, a communal table at *The Peddler*, and an NBN that finally works… for now. Welcome to the ’Wading.
