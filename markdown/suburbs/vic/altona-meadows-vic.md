# Altona Meadows (VIC)

Slug: `altona-meadows-vic`

Altona Meadows — where the Christmas lights are so bright you need sunnies, but the night buses stop at 9pm on NYE so you’re walking home in the glow. 🎄🚶‍♂️

We know what they say about us. “Altona Meadows? Bit rough, bit west, bit… smelly?” Look, the water treatment plant does let out a few honest pongs a couple times a year — as one brave local put it on Whirlpool, “It gets a bit stinky a few times a year.” But they also said they love living down near Altona Green, so take that, snobs.

Our weekend routine? Well, there’s no Euro-style deli on our doorstep (that’s Pier Street in Altona proper, get it right). Instead, we hit up the Altona Meadows Library and Learning Centre — proper community vibes with collections in Arabic and Chinese, because our diversity is “really evenly spread,” as one forum user put it. Then maybe we cycle the Bay Trail — 69km from here to Seaford, though we usually just puff as far as the wetlands and call it a win.

Speaking of wetlands — we back onto them, not the beach. “Nice open spaces but not ‘beach’,” as Whirlpool diplomatically noted. Yeah, we know. If you want beach, you pay Altona prices. We get the wetlands, the occasional whiff of seaweed, and the satisfaction of knowing our Christmas lights game is elite. Urban List says we’ve got “potentially one of Melbourne’s brightest” displays — two-storey, tens of thousands of lights, inflatables, bubble machines. Over a month of prep. We take our festive obligations seriously.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a flashy cafe on every corner or a 2am train. Back in 2017, Broadsheet reported our NYE buses got canned because it fell on a Sunday. Last bus from the CBD at 9pm? Try again, champ.

Who would love it? Families who want space, a decent library, and a street where your neighbours have been lighting up for 20 years. We’re not fancy. We’re just bright. ✨
