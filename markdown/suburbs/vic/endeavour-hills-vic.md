# Endeavour Hills (VIC)

Slug: `endeavour-hills-vic`

There’s a specific kind of quiet that settles over Endeavour Hills around 9pm on a Sunday. It’s the sound of no night bus. Back in 2017, they axed the New Year’s Eve service—meaning if you wanted to see the CBD fireworks and get home, you had to leave before the clock struck twelve or fork out for an Uber surge. We made our own fireworks that night: arguing over who had to be the designated driver.

We are the suburb that made the news for all the wrong reasons in 2015—first a counter-terrorism raid, then a tragic homicide at Kassan Gardens. Yes, that happened here. And we’re still here. You want to know what else happened in Endeavour Hills? In 2019, the shopping centre got an Italian pizza and pasta joint. That was our big food news. Before that, the big attraction was a Play School Craft Village in the mall. We take our milestones where we can get them.

The Endeavour Hills Shopping Centre is the beating heart of the community—and we mean that literally, because the Community Corner and the Endeavour Kids Corner are where local mums go to not strangle each other during school holidays. They even have Easter egg hunts there. Phone 9700 2374 if you want to book a spot. No, really, that’s the number.

We’ve also got a cosmetic clinic—one of 13 across Melbourne, according to Time Out. Because when you live in a suburb where the biggest night out is the shopping centre food court from 2019, you earn those anti-wrinkle injections.

And during Ramadan, there’s a gem here that opens for iftar and suhoor. We won’t name it because Broadsheet didn’t either—but it exists, and it’s ours.

So who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a late-night taxi rank, a third-wave coffee roastery, or a street that doesn’t end in a cul-de-sac with a view of another cul-de-sac.

Who would love it? People who understand that community corner isn’t just a marketing gimmick—it’s where your kids make Play School crafts while you pretend the news headlines from 2015 are ancient history. We’re Endeavour Hills. We’ve got a shopping centre, a past, and a phone number you can actually call. What more do you want?
