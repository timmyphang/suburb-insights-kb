# Forest Hill (VIC)

Slug: `forest-hill-vic`

Forest Hill: where the biggest local controversy is whether the Macca’s McPlant actually counts as a vegetable.

We know what you’re thinking. Forest Hill? Isn’t that just the place you drive through to get to Box Hill? Yes. And we own it. Our main street is a glorious stretch of car yards, a very persistent Macca’s (the one that Instagrammed the McPlant like it was a Michelin star), and a waiting room for the Belgrave line.

Our morning routine is simple: wake up, wonder why Forest Hill College got a “stay away” warning on Whirlpool Forums, then head to the Boutique Homes display village to fantasise about a life where you can afford a proper kitchen. Which brings us to our one brush with glamour: Broadsheet is hosting a supper club with Victor Liong (of Lee Ho Fook fame) inside an elegant Boutique Homes property. A three-course menu, matched wines, and you move through “spaces designed for hosting” – which in Forest Hill usually means moving from the lounge room to the rumpus room via a sliding door. We’ve never been more confused. Are we the new Fitzroy? Did someone put fairy lights on the Luxford roundabout?

The petty drama that still haunts us? Someone’s friend went to a meeting at Forest Hill that “went for about an hour” and was convinced it wasn’t a pyramid scheme. Mate, it was a pyramid scheme. We still see people wearing lanyards near the post office.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “nightlife” should involve more than a 6pm dinner at the RSL and a game of chance for a double pass to a supper club. Anyone allergic to mediocrity.

Who would love it? People who appreciate that Forest Hill is the suburb equivalent of a shy kid at a party who’s actually really nice once you get past the fact they’ve never done anything remarkable. We’ve got a Boutique Homes supper club. We’ve got a Macca’s that tried to go plant-based. We’re trying, okay?
