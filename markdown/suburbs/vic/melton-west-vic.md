# Melton West (VIC)

Slug: `melton-west-vic`

The year is 2020 and Melton West is copping it from all angles — we’re postcode 3337, 79 active COVID cases, and the only thing hotter than us that winter was the rage in the Metricon Facebook groups. Yes, they ordered the tradie to rebuild a four-year-old home here because the slab cracked. Classic Westie welcome.

But step outside the headlines and you’ll find us living the real dream: a quick drive to absolute nowhere, an hour from the city if the Western Freeway is being nice, and the most affordable median house price in Melbourne according to Pedestrian TV. We own that title like we own our oversized Bunnings utes. “Affordable” means you can buy a house here without selling a kidney — and maybe still afford the funeral when the foundations give way.

For the kids, there’s the Melton West Children and Community Centre on 2 West Melton Drive. For the adults, there’s… the West Melton Drive. That’s the big night out. You want natural hot springs? Concrete Playground and Time Out rave about them — but they’re in Hepburn or Mornington. We get a tap at home, mate, and it’s lukewarm.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who wants a latte with a side of urban grit. There’s no Ritual Espresso, no laneway arcade. Just a loyal community that knows the real soil of the west — reactive clay, mostly. We’re not pretending to be Fitzroy. We’re Melton West. We survived COVID and a slab full of cracks. Come for the affordable mortgage, stay for the community centre and the quiet pride of a suburb that’s always been the punchline — and learned to laugh first.
