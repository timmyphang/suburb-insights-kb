# Oakleigh South (VIC)

Slug: `oakleigh-south-vic`

**Welcome to Oakleigh South, where your Saturday plans go from Olympic ice skating to a fetish dungeon in 2.4 kilometres.**  

We’re the suburb that makes other Melbourne enclaves look one-dimensional. Need to burn off some energy? Head to **La Roca** bouldering gym — where former Amaru head chef Cam Tay-Yap once ran his **Pebble** pop-up from the carpark, feeding climbers fried potato flatbread from a mobile cool room. Now that’s multi-tasking.  

Got kids? **Olympic Ice Skating Centre** at 1080 Centre Road does casual laps Wednesday and Friday afternoons ($28 with skate hire). Family action. But if that’s too vanilla, wander into the industrial backstreets where Sir James runs his dungeon — as documented in Jenny Valentish’s book. “Almost indistinguishable from the auto-repair shops”, they said. We call it character.  

The real local drama? Australia Post keeps losing our parcels. One forum user complained their delivery was “stuck” at **OAKLEIGH SOUTH VIC** for a week. Welcome to the queue, mate. Meanwhile, **Fenton & Fenton** holds its annual warehouse sale — 70% off bold sofas and statement objects. That’s our idea of a treasure hunt.  

Who would hate it here? Anyone who can’t handle a suburb where a Greek doughnut run (shout-out to **TODCO** in nearby Oakleigh) might involve passing a cross-dressing room. Or parents who get weird about explaining “the dungeon next to the kinder” to their kids.  

Who would love it? Climbers, skaters, kink explorers, bargain hunters, and anyone who appreciates a suburb that refuses to pick a lane. We’re industrial, we’re sweet, we’re strange — and yes, our mail is delayed. But you’ll never be bored.
