# Hughesdale (VIC)

Slug: `hughesdale-vic`

Hughesdale, you beautiful little nowhere. We’re the suburb that Broadsheet and Urban List only mention when they’re reminiscing about where a cafe used to be. Knafeh Nabulseyeh started here in 2015, making cheese-and-pistachio pastry that had people driving past Hughesdale station without noticing the sign. Then it moved to Coburg in 2023. Classic.

But we had our moment. Urban List once got excited about a “newly opened” café called Temperance Society with brulee yoghurt and black tea marbled eggs — though nobody seems to remember if it’s still there. And Trish Pryse used to own Brew here before she moved on to Daniel Son in Murrumbeena. We’re basically a breeding ground for cafe talent that leaves the nest.

We sit quietly between Oakleigh (where everyone actually goes for souvlaki) and Chadstone (where everyone actually goes for life). As one Whirlpool user put it: “Hughesdale is a bit more affordable, and close to Oakleigh which has great restaurants (mostly Greek).” Thanks, mate. That’s our whole vibe — “affordable and close to better places.”

The only drama we get is people asking about all-day parking at Hughesdale station at 8am. It’s a 2P zone and the locals have sharp elbows. Forget about a park after 7.30.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who wants a nightlife, a Facebook group, or a reason to leave the house other than Woolies and Coles at Oakleigh Plaza. Also anyone who thinks a suburb should have its own identity instead of being “the quiet one between Oakleigh and Murrumbeena.”

Who would love it? People who want a house they can actually afford in the south-east, access to Poath Road shortcuts, and a train station that gets you to the city in 30 minutes while you zone out. We’re the suburb for people who don’t want to be a suburb — just somewhere to crash between better meals.
