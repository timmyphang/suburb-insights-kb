# Lysterfield (VIC)

Slug: `lysterfield-vic`

Lysterfield, VIC — we’re the suburb that has one thing going for it, and it’s a lake. Not even a lake with jet skis or swan pedal boats — just a gentle 82‑hectare expanse where “non‑powered boating” is the wildest thing you can do. As Concrete Playground put it, we’ve got “calm waters and wide sandy beaches” and “even total novices can feel at ease.” Translation: if you want thrills, head somewhere else. We’re here for the slow paddle, the family picnic, the occasional nap on the shore while the kids throw sticks in the water.

But let’s be real — Lysterfield is basically the dressing room for the lake. There’s no Coles, no Woolies, no café. The only local drama we’ve got is when someone’s solar panels don’t generate enough juice in winter (shout out to the Whirlpool user tracking 11.43kWh per day in June). And yeah, we made the news in 2023 for a CBD stabbing involving a local — but that’s not our story; that’s the city’s mess that happened to have a Lysterfield postcode attached.

You want a morning routine here? You wake up, drive to the lake, kayak for an hour, then… drive to Narre Warren for a coffee. That’s the lifestyle. There’s a walking trail, a swimming bay, and a whole lot of quiet. If you’re the type who needs a flat white within 200 metres of your front door, you’ll last about 48 hours before you start muttering about moving to Fitzroy North.

But if you’re after a spot where your biggest decision of the day is whether to launch your canoe from the boat ramp or the beach, and you don’t mind the closest restaurant being a 10‑minute drive away — welcome home, mate. We’ve got your lake, your peace, and no queues. Just don’t ask what’s for dinner.
