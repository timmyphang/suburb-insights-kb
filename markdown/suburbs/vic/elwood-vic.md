# Elwood (VIC)

Slug: `elwood-vic`

We wake up in Elwood like we’re starring in our own moody beach montage. First stop: Combi, where we order a raw zucchini spaghetti with cashew sauce and pretend we enjoyed it. Then we shuffle down Ormond Road to Repeat Offender for a gluten-free Latin tapas — because nothing says “I live near the beach” like paying $28 for a corn chip that’s ethically sourced. The Milton? Permanently closed. RIP to that wine bar on the corner — we hardly knew ye, but we did love your 120-varietal list and exposed brick.

The drama: an extremely horny ship rudely woke us all up last year with an unexpected morning foghorn. Victoria Police said it was a false alarm. We’re still not over it. Also, back in the day, the local milkbars stopped putting arcade machines in because the Clicker hack fad let kids play for free. We haven’t trusted technology since.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who doesn’t own a keep cup, thinks “wellness emporium” is a warning sign, or refuses to eat waffles made of beetroot and teff. Also, anyone who doesn’t enjoy being sandwiched between St Kilda’s chaos and Brighton’s mansion envy. If you want a pub with a pokies room and a parma the size of your head, go to Bentleigh.

Who would love it? The person who describes themselves as “breezy” and “coastal chic”. The kind of local who buys organic bulk foods at The Staple Store, then strolls the St Kilda Foreshore Trail with a raw caramel slice from Combi. The one who genuinely believes Elwood Bathers is the best restaurant on the bay — and honestly, with that view, they might be right. We’re a good-looking suburb, full of sun-drenched Art Deco and golden sand. Just don’t ask us to share our secret beach spot. It’s already too crowded.
