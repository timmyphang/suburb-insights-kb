# Pascoe Vale (VIC)

Slug: `pascoe-vale-vic`

Pascoe Vale: where the bagels are reborn in a former butcher’s shop, the gelato is buffalo-milk and open 365 days a year, and you still can’t get served at MSY in under 45 minutes.

Our Saturday morning routine: roll into **Bagels Baby** at the corner of Pascoe Vale (the one that used to be a butcher’s, because of course it did) and get a lox with Gruyere. Then wander over to **Augustus Gelatery** (formerly Augustus Gloop, but they clearly hired a lawyer) for a scoop of burnt fig gelato, because Emma Nicholas-Jennis grew up in a gelato store so she knows what she’s doing. If you need a caffeine fix, **George Jones** is the all-day eatery we apparently needed, from the same bloke who did Cheeky Monkey and Rubber Duck. And if you’re gluten-free, pack the car and head to **Shop225** in Pascoe Vale South – Australia’s best pizza, 28th best in the world, and Coeliac Australia accredited. They even developed their own vegan cheese. We are not worthy.

But for all the new cool, this suburb still holds a grudge. Remember **Bar Tobala**? The “American diner meets roadside taco stand” on Melville Road? Permanently closed. RIP neon and mezcal. And if you’ve ever tried to get a drive test at Broadmeadows VicRoads, you’ve probably copped an ITE on Pascoe Vale Road coming off Barry Road. The Whirlpool forums have felt your pain since 2014.

Who would hate Pascoe Vale? Anyone who thinks “all-day eatery” means something other than cinnamon scroll pancakes and boba cold brew. Anyone who can’t handle the delicate dance of dodging the Western Ring Road smash near the on-ramp. And definitely anyone who expects to walk into MSY and get served in under 45 minutes. Sorry, that’s Glenroy’s vibe.

Who would love it? The family who wants gelato in July, the pickleball enthusiast who needs five indoor courts at 2 Attercliffe Avenue, the gluten-free pizza connoisseur, and anyone who thinks “we used to be a butcher’s” is the best backstory for a bagel joint. Yes, we’re a bit north of Bell Street. No, we don’t care. Our gelato is buffalo-milk and our pizzeria is world-ranked. Catch us at the Pascoe Vale Outdoor Pools come summer. We’ll be the ones with the forgotten toy stashed in the greenery wall at Parkstone Cafe.
