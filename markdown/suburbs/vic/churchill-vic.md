# Churchill (VIC)

Slug: `churchill-vic`

**Churchill, VIC — The Suburb That NBN Forgot (But Is Now Getting FTTP)**

We live in a place where the most exciting local news is a Whirlpool forum thread about Rural Fibre Co doing FTTP for the 420 houses on Fixed Wireless. Yeah, that’s us. While Armadale gets a 120-seat Victor Churchill steak palace with dry-aged Wagyu and a Himalayan salt wall, we get… a discussion about whether our NBN upgrade is coming this decade. 

Yes, we know — everyone else thinks of Churchill Island or Winston Churchill when they hear the name. We’re not that Churchill. We’re the one where someone on Whirlpool asked if “Churchill edu” is just an easy RPL cash grab. We still don’t have an answer. 

Our local economy runs on two things: the University (we assume — not in the data) and the sheer thrill of watching a fibre cable go in. We’re the quiet achiever of the Latrobe Valley. No drama, no feuds, no late-night barista showdowns — just 420 houses waiting patiently for that sweet, sweet fixed-line internet. 

Who belongs here? People who think “fast broadband” is a valid personality trait. People who enjoy the peace of a suburb that never makes the news — unless it’s about a butcher in Armadale with the same name. 

Who would hate it? Anyone looking for a world-class steak, a rooftop bar, or a single Instagrammable cafe. We’ve got Woolies, we’ve got a servo, and we’ve got a forum thread with 64 posts about Chinese car batteries. That’s the vibe. 

Come for the NBN upgrade. Stay because you don’t know how to leave.
