# Blackburn South (VIC)

Slug: `blackburn-south-vic`

Blackburn South — where you move when you want to tell people you’re from Blackburn, but don’t quite have the deposit for Blackburn proper.

We’re the quiet, leafy younger sibling that gets mentioned third in the water contamination alerts. Yes, in August 2020, Yarra Valley Water told 88 suburbs to boil their water after storms caused electrical damage, and there we were, right between Blackburn and Boronia. Classic us. Always getting lumped in, never getting our own headline.

Back in the day, the Honey Thief Bakery used to produce some of the best bread in Melbourne — sourdough purists and fruit loaf fanatics would queue like it was a Waitlist for a nightclub. But that was then. Now we mostly just talk about it like a war story. “Remember when we had that bread?” Yeah, we remember.

Life here revolves around the careful art of being just fine. The Wurundjeri Walk Trail is a lovely creek-side stroll. Eley Park has a tennis club. Mirrabooka Oval has cricket. There’s a preschool called Indra and a primary school called Laburnum. Everything is pleasant in the way a dentist’s waiting room is pleasant — serviceable, clean, and you wouldn’t want to live there permanently.

Who’d hate it here? Anyone under 30 who still wants to pretend they live in an exciting postcode. Our median age is 42. We go to bed at 9pm. The most exciting thing that happens on Canterbury Road is a Woolworths run. If you need a 24-hour kebab, you’ve got to drive to Box Hill.

But for you — the person who wants a four-bedroom weatherboard with a Hills Hoist, thinks “leafy streets” is the sexiest phrase in the English language, and genuinely enjoys the walk to Laburnum Station — we’re your people. We’re the suburb that says “yes” to dinner at 6pm and “no” to street parking after dark.

Blackburn South: we’re not the main character. But we always boil our water on time.
