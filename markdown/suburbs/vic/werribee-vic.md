# Werribee (VIC)

Slug: `werribee-vic`

Let’s be honest, when people hear “Werribee” they think of two things: the Open Range Zoo and the sewage ponds. And they’re not wrong. We’ve got a literal celebrity duck that took an 8000km wrong turn and ended up in the Western Treatment Plant – the “goth duck” with a Nick Cave quiff. Twitchers from all over camped out to see it. Meanwhile, we’ve also had a bloke name a lost Officeworks trolley “Trevor” and teach it to take selfies. That’s peak Werribee.

A Saturday morning here? You’re either at the Bridge Hotel on Watton Street (that heritage art deco beauty that reopened in 2020 with fireplaces and rotating taps) getting in early for a $20 steak night on Thursday, or you’re doing the 10km Werribee Gorge circuit. But fair warning: “at least one person has been swept from the rocks” – so maybe skip the swim. If you’re feeling fancy, L.M Spa at the Lancemore Mansion Hotel is our idea of boujee, but we’re equally comfortable at Catalyst Community Services’ Family Hub on Heaths Road for a free coffee and a chat.

Now for the local spice. We’ve had drag queen story time protests at Werribee Plaza Library, anti-Muslim vigilante groups patrolling, and a bloke fined for driving 30km to the city for a butter chicken. The Werribee line? Good luck – major delays when the weather so much as sneezes. And don’t get us started on the NBN: the CVC graph is “certainly getting real close” according to Whirlpool nerds.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who wants a quick, reliable train ride, or thinks a “precinct” is just a fancy word for a carpark. Also anyone who doesn’t appreciate the unique perfume of a sewage treatment plant at sunrise.

But who would love it? The kind of person who wants a $25 Sunday roast at the Bridge, a morning safari where you can glamp with the rhinos, and a community that names trolleys and ducks. We’re halfway between Melbourne and Geelong, so we’re everyone’s meeting spot. And if you don’t like it? The gorge is right there – go for a hike.
