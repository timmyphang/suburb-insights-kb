# Hillside (VIC)

Slug: `hillside-vic`

**Hillside, VIC — Where even the name is aspirational geography**

We live in Hillside, and let’s be honest: the only thing actually on a hillside around here is the Hillside Hotel, which briefly had a Milky Lane pop-up serving American food. That’s it. That’s the peak. The rest of us are on a gentle slope that estate agents call “elevated” and everyone else calls “slightly closer to the Western Ring Road noise.”

The data says we’re a suburb that appears in articles about other places’ hillsides. TarraWarra’s cellar door is built into *their* hillside. Fortitude Valley’s Agnes restaurant sits in an ancient *hillside* warehouse. There's a cosy cafe in the Adelaide Hills where people queue 20-deep for a seat. But here? In Hillside, VIC? We’ve got a hotel that borrowed Bondi’s leftovers for three days.

Back in 2017, a Whirlpool forum user mentioned their place being “cut into the hillside” with a sloping garden. Another forum ghost wrote about “little boxes on the hillside, the ones made of ticky tacky.” We feel seen. Our hillside is less scenic vista, more “hope the retaining wall holds.”

**Who would hate it here:** Anyone who Googled “Hillside VIC attractions” and got directed to articles about Rio de Janeiro slums and Thailand resorts. The suburb’s entire online presence is a geography error. You want hillside views? Try Adelaide. You want cellar doors? Try the Yarra Valley. You want a working-class Melbourne suburb where the most exciting recent event was a pop-up kitchen at the local pub? Welcome home.

**Who would love it:** People who appreciate that our identity crisis is the whole point. We’re Hillside — named for what we’re not. No pretension, no wine regions, no 20-deep cafe queues. Just good honest suburbia on a modest slope, where the only thing “perched on a hillside” is the hope that Woolies has the good mince on special.
