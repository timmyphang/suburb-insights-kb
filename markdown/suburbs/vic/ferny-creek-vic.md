# Ferny Creek (VIC)

Slug: `ferny-creek-vic`

There's a specific kind of Saturday in Ferny Creek where you wake up, pull on some hiking shorts you swear you'll wash this week, and drive fifteen minutes to Sassafras because the real bakery is actually next door. Head to Proserpina Bakehouse—well, *used to* head there, back when Carolyn Deutsher was milling Victorian biodynamic flour and baking the sourdough that had the whole Dandenong Ranges talking. You'd grab a Silva Coffee, a flaky almond croissant (the talk of the town, apparently), maybe a slice of that famous cheesecake with the oaty base and local berries. Then you'd hit the trails, because that's why you live here.

Olinda Falls and Valley Picnic Loop is a gentle 3.4km that makes you feel like a bushwalking influencer, all lush fern gullies and cascading water. If you're feeling slightly more ambitious—or masochistic—there's the 1000 Steps in the Dandenong Ranges National Park. The Pedestrian crew called it "genuinely mystical," and yeah, the tall trees and woodfire scent are nice. But the real local drama? Dogs aren't permitted. That's it. That's the scandal. You'll see people parking illegally on Mountain Highway just to let their beagle sniff a tree before being shooed away by a ranger. Honestly, the most exciting thing to happen here since the 2019 opening of a bakery in the next suburb.

Would you hate living here? Absolutely, if you don't own a car. The nearest proper shop is a winding drive away, and the wifi at home is just fast enough to load a recipe but not fast enough to stream it. Also, if you're not into the constant sound of lycra-clad runners panting up the 1000 Steps at 7am, this place will drive you batty.

But if you want to live inside a postcard—where the air actually smells different, where a 3.4km loop is considered a big day out, and where the only unsolved mystery is whether the Proserpina almond croissant is still as good as it was in 2019—Ferny Creek is your weird, wonderful, slightly sleepy paradise. Just bring your own dog.
