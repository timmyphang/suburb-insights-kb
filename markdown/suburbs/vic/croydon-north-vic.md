# Croydon North (VIC)

Slug: `croydon-north-vic`

We know what they say about Croydon North. “Most of it’s very nice,” they murmur on Whirlpool, before adding “central Croydon is pretty scummy.” Classic. We exist in the shadow of Croydon Hills (too expensive) and the shadow of Ringwood (too chaotic). We’re the polite middle child of the 3136 postcode, and frankly, we’re fine with that.

Back in 2020, we made headlines alongside 87 other suburbs because our tap water needed a rolling boil before we could brush our teeth. Yarra Valley Water said it was “better to be safe than sorry.” So we boiled, we waited, we lived. It was a bonding moment – nothing brings a suburb together like collective paranoia about gastro.

When December rolls around, we come alive. According to Christmas Light Search Australia, there’s a display here “filled with an abundance of lights and music—and possibly Santa himself.” Possibly Santa. We don’t make promises, but if you drive past and see a bloke in a red suit sweating under a blow-up sleigh, that’s him. It’s the most exciting thing that’s happened since the water warning.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting a bustling café strip or a trendy bar. We don’t have Auction Rooms (that’s North Melbourne). We don’t have Errol Street. We have a Sitchu page that just lists our postcode and moves on. If you need your chia pudding served on a wooden board, keep driving up the Maroondah Highway.

Who would love it? People who like a bit of space, a bit of quiet, and a suburb that’s been called “very nice” by strangers on the internet. We’re the place that says “yes, we’re Croydon, but the good bit.” And if you come at Christmas, you might even meet Santa. Possibly.
