# Box Hill South (VIC)

Slug: `box-hill-south-vic`

**Ilza and duck and K Home, oh my.**

Let’s be real: we’re the awkward middle child of the eastern suburbs. Not quite our flashy parent Box Hill, not quite the leafy serenity of Burwood. We’re Box Hill South — the suburb that keeps its head down and lets the food do the talking.

A Saturday morning here starts with a quiet pilgrimage. You skip the big shopping centres. Instead, you head to **Wattle Park Green Grocer** at 194 Elgar Road with your own containers like a smug eco-warrior, grabbing organic fruit and low FODMAP specialty products. Then, because you’ve earned it, you swing by **Ilza Japanese Cafe** — a Tokyo-inspired spot from ex-Nobu duo Han Oh and Erin Kim. You order the cheeseburger curry udon and a strawberry-matcha latte and pretend you’re not sitting in a car park.

Speaking of which, we’re about to enter our villain era. **Kmart** is opening a massive 3,800-square-metre K Home concept store here in June 2026, taking on Ikea. Our local car parks will never recover.

**The petty local drama:** The internet is divided on where to get the best ramen. Concrete Playground mentions “an old family recipe perfected over decades makes for delicious ramen at this Box Hill spot.” We have no idea which spot. No one will tell us. The secret ramen is a shadowy figure pulling noodles in the night. Meanwhile, chef Esther Sun told Broadsheet that **Simons Peking Duck** in Box Hill South is “hands-down my favourite restaurant in the world.” She once ate a whole banquet here alone. Iconic.

**Who would hate it here:** People who need a proper town centre. We don’t have one. Our “vibes” come from the **Camera Exchange** on Middleborough Road (a Whirlpool-approved institution for decades) and the **Acorn Nursery** that’s been here since 1985. If you need a main street with a barista who knows your name, you’re moving to Camberwell.

**Who would love it:** Anyone who understands that the best things come in unassuming packages. You don’t come to Box Hill South for a scene. You come for the best duck of your life, fresh peanuts in a bulk bin, and the quiet, dignified knowledge that your local Kmart might just be the first to out-Ikea Ikea. We’re humble. We’re hungry. We’ve got a K Home.
