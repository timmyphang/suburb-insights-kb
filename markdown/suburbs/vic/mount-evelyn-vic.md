# Mount Evelyn (VIC)

Slug: `mount-evelyn-vic`

**Mount Evelyn, VIC — we are the suburb that got its 15 minutes of fame by being one of 88 suburbs told to boil our tap water back in 2020, and we’ve been milking that aesthetic ever since.**

Here’s the morning routine: you wake up, check if Yarra Valley Water has put us on the boil notice again (they haven’t, but we keep the kettle ready), then you walk the dog past the historic Mount Evelyn Aqueduct. It’s 1930s stone terraces and pergolas — basically Instagram bait for people who call a day trip “an escape.” We nod at the other dog walker who definitely also got the water alert email. We’ve bonded over gastro risk speculation.

Our big drama? Back in August 2020, a storm knocked out power, and “some water entered our drinking water supply system that had not been treated to the usual standard.” As Broadsheet noted, we were on the list alongside 87 other suburbs. We were lumped in with Attwood and Coolaroo. No special treatment. Just “boil your water, maybe see a GP if you get the runs.” We’re still not sure if our taps are fully trustworthy, but the aqueduct looks nice.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who expects a functioning water supply without a side of anxiety. Also people who think a “weekend getaway” requires a proper town centre — we’re mostly bush, reservoirs, and the echo of that 2020 storm.

But who would love it? The kind of person who reads “Six Dam Good Reservoirs” articles and thinks “yes, that’s my summer.” You, the reservoir enthusiast. The one who appreciates a good aqueduct and doesn’t mind if your drinking water has a mildly suspicious past. We’re the suburb that says: the water might be dodgy, but the views are dam good.
