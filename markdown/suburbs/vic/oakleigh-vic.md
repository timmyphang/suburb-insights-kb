# Oakleigh (VIC)

Slug: `oakleigh-vic`

Saturday morning in Oakleigh means one thing: the line at Vanilla Cakes and Lounge snakes halfway down Eaton Mall while you try to decide if the tiropita is worth the wait. Spoiler: it always is. We’re the suburb that gave the world Nikos Quality Cakes, Kalimera Souvlaki Art, and a crepe shop that thought moussaka belonged in a crepe (Mykonos Cafe and Crepes – we see you, madman Thomas Deliopoulos). But recently, we’ve also got Olympia, a Greek-inspired wine bar above the market with a rooftop that makes you forget you’re parked next to a car park that used to be a car park. The Metro Tunnel now spits you out at Oakleigh Station every 10 minutes, so you can roll straight into Coté Terra for a wagyu burger and a brew.

Petty local drama? It’s the parking at Oakleigh Station. If you arrive after 8am, you’re circling like a seagull at a chip shop. The Whirlpool forums are littered with souls asking “All day parking?” – answer: no, not really. Also, the Oakleigh Motel is now townhouses, so you can’t even pretend you’re in a retro Tim Ross photo shoot anymore.

Who would hate it here? Someone who thinks “Greek” is just a brand of yoghurt. Someone who doesn’t understand why the cake cabinet at Vanilla deserves its own quarterly magazine. Someone who panics when they see a lineup for doughnuts at TODCO (The Oakleigh Doughnut Co – get it? Todd-coe). And definitely anyone who can’t handle the fact that we have a warehouse where you can smash stuff (Smash Splash) and a Fenton & Fenton sale that brings out the inner hoarder.

Who would love it? Anyone who believes a suburb should smell like souvlaki, baklava, and fresh coffee. Families who want a playgroup at Oakleigh South Primary School and a fish and chipper that cuts chips daily. Locals who remember when Oakleigh was just Greek cakes and now appreciate the wave of West African eateries from Ghana, Nigeria, and Cameroon. We’re a mashup of old-school institutions and new-wave rooftop bars – and if you can’t find a park, just get the train. We did.
