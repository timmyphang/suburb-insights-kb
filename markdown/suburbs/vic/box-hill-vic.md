# Box Hill (VIC)

Slug: `box-hill-vic`

So you’ve somehow ended up at Box Hill Central on a Saturday arvo, and you’re trying to navigate the Pop Mart while dodging a granddad wheeling a trolley of bok choy. Welcome to our roundabout of prosperity. As Stephen Liu of The Penny Drop put it back in 2016: “It’s Box Hill, so everything’s Asian.” Not wrong, mate. Roundness means prosperity – and we’ve got round marble bars, round dumplings, and roundabout traffic that’ll test your karma.

Our morning routine used to be a pilgrimage to Zero Mode for that famous self-collapsing bubble tea cake and a side of chicken-ginseng beer broth. Or you’d grab a Japanese cabbage pancake at Second Wife – because nothing says “healthy” like a katsuobushi avalanche. Then you’d wander past the Tax Office (the real centre of the universe) and wonder if anyone actually works there, or if they’re all just waiting for the SRL to be finished in 2050.

The petty drama? Try finding a standalone house under two million. As Whirlpool forums reckon, “if you are looking for a standalone house with a good amount of land in Box Hill, it will easily be north of 2m+.” Oh, and don’t even get us started on Box Hill TAFE. One cyber security grad said they “wont look favorably” on the Cert IV from here. Brutal, but fair.

Who’d hate living here? Anyone who thinks the only good thing about Whitehorse Road is leaving it. Or people who get stressed by the sheer volume of yakiniku options at Katori and the $151 Hibiki. Also: anyone who doesn’t want a Kmart K Home concept store in Box Hill South. Sorry, Ikea – we’re coming for you.

But who’d love it? The person who wants a four-table bistro like Una Una that serves roast duck and a Dirty Smoked Olive Martini at night, and a foccacia by day. The kind of local who treats The Chen’s rooftop pool like a public amenity. And anyone who knows that yes, Box Hill is trying to be Melbourne’s second CBD – but we still have the best dumplings and the worst parking. Come for the trains, stay for the six different Asian grocery stores in one block.
