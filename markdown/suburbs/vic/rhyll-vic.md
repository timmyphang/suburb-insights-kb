# Rhyll (VIC)

Slug: `rhyll-vic`

**Rhyll, VIC — where the wetlands are world-famous and the locals are world-class at pretending a pelican isn't staring at your chips.**

There's a moment every Saturday morning in Rhyll. You're paddling through the serene Rhyll Wetlands, trying to spot a wading bird that's flown thousands of kilometres just to feed here, when you realise: you are the entertainment. The local pelicans have seen it all — hungover campers, eager birdwatchers, and families who thought "coastal walk" meant a flat path with a coffee at the end.

The data says this place is a "must-visit when coming to Phillip Island." And sure, the wetlands are laden with mangroves and mudflats, a significant site for wading birds. But we locals know the real show is at Wild Food Farm on Rhyll-Newhaven Road. As Broadsheet noted back in 2021, it showcases over 40 native bush food varieties. Breakfast means muesli with Davidson plum compote, or a brekkie burger with warragal greens and bush-tomato chutney. Is it still the same operation now? We reckon a place that serves chocolate wattleseed brownies is timeless.

**The petty drama that splits the town:** whether the Mornington black mussel dish with kakadu plum coconut curry is better than the weekend barbecue. We have no formal council records of this feud. We don't need them. The side-eye at the gift shop over the last jar of bush-tomato chutney tells you everything.

**Who would hate it here?** Anyone who thinks "getaway" means five-star service, not a self-contained cottage where you still have to make your own bed. The website may mention Rhyll Haven Luxury B&B and Silverwater Resort, but let's be real: you're here for the mudflats, not the minibar. If you need a DJ and bottomless prosecco on a Saturday night, stick to Cowes.

**Who would love it?** The bird nerd who brings binoculars to brunch. The family who packs a picnic and calls it adventure. The couple who stays at Wild Food Farm, buys three bush rubs, and genuinely believes they'll cook with wattleseed at home. Welcome to our quiet corner of the island. The pelicans are waiting.
