# Bundoora (VIC)

Slug: `bundoora-vic`

There’s a moment every Bundoora local knows: you’re standing in the Jump! Swim School carpark after a 9am lesson, wondering if the water at home is safe to boil, while a $5 billion La Trobe Uni masterplan hums somewhere in the distance. That’s us. We’re a suburb of contradictions — and we own it.

Saturday mornings we BYO picnic to Bundoora Park, as Time Out suggests, swinging by Bundoora Homestead Café (open Wed–Fri 11–4, Sat–Sun 12–5, because we respect a good heritage-listed afternoon). Or we hit Swing & Serve on Plenty Road for pickleball — $45/hour for non-members, which is still cheaper than a therapy session after reading a Whirlpool forum about BMW dealers. Speaking of: our VicRoads office is “quite big and not very crowded,” per Whirlpool. Finally, a win.

The Bundoora Hotel? It’s got a 4.02-star rating on its own website and Google reviews like “Enjoyed Zagames in Reservoir much more.” We felt that. We also felt the 2020 water warning — Yarra Valley Water told 88 suburbs to boil, and we nodded like “yep, sounds about right.” And yes, we’re still processing the 2019 tragedy that made national headlines. It’s part of our story, and we don’t forget.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting a Lululemon on every corner or a barista who knows your oat milk order. We don’t have new cocktail dens — we have a Jump! Swim School and a Homestead with scones.

Who would love it? Students on a budget, pickleball obsessives, anyone who thinks a $5 billion campus transformation is “exciting,” and people who appreciate a VicRoads queue that moves. Come for the cheap court hire, stay for the boiled water. We’re rough, we’re real, and we’re not moving.
