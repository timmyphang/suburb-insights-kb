# Clayton (VIC)

Slug: `clayton-vic`

Clayton — where Monash University students, PC builders, and the occasional protester scaling an RMIT building all call home. We’ve got the Church of Secular Coffee on campus (St Ali’s answer to uni caffeination, affordable and decent), Top Tea’s Clayton outpost winning Best Cafe Design (yes, we’re fancy now), and a strip of Taiwanese restaurants on Clayton Road that fuel more assignment all-nighters than we’d like to admit.

Our morning routine? Queueing at the Church for a flat white before a lecture, pretending we’re worldly because the barista knows our order. Then a quick stop at CentreCom on the way home when your “perfectly fine” hard drive suddenly dies — because nothing says Clayton like a Whirlpool-level argument about warranty replacements. We’ve all swapped salty stories about Scorptec’s Clayton store, where a faulty KC2500 gets “upgraded” to a KC3000 and you still leave wondering if you won or lost.

The local drama? Not much, but we did have one of our own — a bloke from Clayton who scaled RMIT in 2025 with a harness, protesting “corrupt…” something. We never quite worked out what, but we respected the commitment. Also, our Christmas lights game is strong enough that Yalta Court, Clayton South made Time Out’s national map — so don’t tell us we’re not festive.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “student suburb” means quiet weekends. Clayton is a constant hum of Monash traffic, PC parts shipments, and the occasional sirens from the nearby hospital. If you need serenity, head to the Dandenongs — we’ll be here arguing about whether CentreCom or Scorptec has better customer service.

Who would love it? Students who never want to leave the uni bubble, budget-conscious café hoppers, and anyone who needs a dependable Taiwanese feed at 9pm on a Wednesday. Clayton isn’t glamorous, but it’s got our backs — and a surprisingly good coffee scene for a suburb that’s mostly known for its postcode.
