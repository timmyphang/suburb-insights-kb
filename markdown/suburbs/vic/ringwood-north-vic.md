# Ringwood North (VIC)

Slug: `ringwood-north-vic`

We know what you’re thinking: Ringwood North. Is that the bit of Ringwood where they keep the spare trees and the mysterious humming noise?

Yes, we are the suburb that made the news in 2020 when Yarra Valley Water told 88 suburbs to boil their water, and we were on that list — right between Ringwood and Ringwood East. Classy. We’re also the suburb that made the Urban List in 2026 for having one of the best massages in Melbourne at 176 Warrandyte Road. That’s our big claim to fame: a really good rub-down in a leafy street. Welcome.

Our morning routine? You take the kids to North Ringwood Playgroup at the Scout Hall on Wonga Road — 50 years old in 2024, so it’s been running since before your mum was in nappies. Then you swing by Frida’s Sip n’ Paint on opening weekend, because we do have a cultural life — it’s just that it’s BYO wine and you leave with a painting of a cockatoo that looks mildly disappointed.

The local drama? There’s a hum. A low, persistent hum. As one local on Laughlans Hill put it on Whirlpool: “My wife and I hear exactly what you have described for the past couple of years… I reckon the noise comes from the 'ground' as it is louder in the house esp when you lay in bed.” We’ve got a suburban mystery that keeps us up at night, and it’s not the possums.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting a nightlife. Our most romantic restaurant? We don’t have one — don’t look at that OpenTable list, we’re not on it. Our idea of a hot date is a couples’ infrared sauna session in the city and a massage back home.

Who would love it? Families who want space, a playgroup with actual history, and a quiet life punctuated only by unexplained geological vibrations. Come for the trees, stay for the hum.
