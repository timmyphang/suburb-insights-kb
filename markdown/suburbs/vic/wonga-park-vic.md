# Wonga Park (VIC)

Slug: `wonga-park-vic`

**Wonga Park, VIC** — we are the suburb that exists on the very edge of civilisation, and we mean that geographically *and* socially.

Our morning routine is a tightrope walk between serenity and survival. We start with a bracing dip in the Yarra — because, as Pedestrian.TV confirmed, us Wonga Park folk are one of the few places on the river that actually get the “scientific tick” for swimming. Then we grab a coffee (no, there’s no cafe mentioned in our data, so don’t ask) and ponder our annual water quality crisis. In 2020, Yarra Valley Water told 88 suburbs to boil their tap water after a storm. We were on that list. We’re always on that list. It’s part of our charm.

Our claim to fame is the **Mount Lofty Loop** — the 6.6-kilometre circuit that Time Out and AllTrails named *Australia’s most popular winter hike of 2024*. We are not a suburb of cafes. We are a suburb of *hikes*. The only other place you’ll find us in a guide is the **Kellybrook Cider Festival**, which has been happening the first weekend of May for nearly 30 years. If you come for the cider, stay for the vibe of a winery perched in old Yarra Valley orchard territory.

But we know what they say about us. We’re a water-warning suburb. We’re the place where the Yarra is clean enough to swim but your tap water might give you gastro. We’re sandwiched between Warrandyte and Yering, and let’s be honest, nobody even knows our postcode.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a proper brunch joint. Anyone who thinks “highly rated winter hike” is not a personality trait. Anyone who wants a corner store open past 3pm.

Who would love it here? People who own hiking boots, a kayak from Concrete Playground’s inflatable kayak deal, and a deep appreciation for a suburb that lets you swim in the Yarra without getting tetanus. We’re low-key. We’re bushland. And if you boil our water, we’re perfect.
