# Chelsea Heights (VIC)

Slug: `chelsea-heights-vic`

Chelsea Heights, VIC — where you type the name into Google and spend the next hour trying to convince the algorithm you don't want a hotel in Hong Kong, a gallery district in NYC, or a haircut on the NSW South Coast. We get it. We're the "other" Chelsea. The one where our biggest cultural export is a 2013 Homeland episode that used a fight outside our pub as a metaphor for terrorism.

Yes, the Chelsea Heights Hotel is the beating heart of this suburb — a place Time Out accurately describes as "traditional pub down the Mornington Peninsula, with kids entertainment and a nightclub for us big kids." So on Saturday arvo you're watching your ankle-biters navigate the newly renovated jungle gym (climbing nets, slides, kids menu up to age 12 — thanks Urban List), and by 11pm you're back on the same dancefloor pretending you can still party like it's 2007. The hotel staff have seen it all. Especially that brickie who, as Junkee once noted, had "a blue with the bouncers" — a line so iconic it made scriptwriters for Homeland go "yeah, that's the vibe we need."

Beyond the pub, our community runs on the Chelsea Heights Community Centre, which at least offers a free Hey Dee Ho Music concert for preschoolers and a $250 Power Saving Bonus (if you got in before June 2022). For childcare, we've got Chelsea Occasional Childcare — serving families from Chelsea, Chelsea Heights, Bonbeach, Edithvale, Seaford, Carrum, Patterson Lakes and Frankston, because apparently our suburb's identity is so porous we absorb everyone else's kids.

If you want a proper cultural outing, you can catch Semla the Bakery (a Swedish micro-bakery from Seaford) setting up at Chelsea Bicentennial Park on Scotch Parade once a month. It's the closest we get to a foodie scene, and we'll take it.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who typed "Chelsea Heights" hoping for the High Line, the Whitney Museum, or a Starbucks Reserve Roastery. Sorry, you're getting a pub with a bouncy castle and a community centre that smells like Milo and damp carpet.

Who would love it? People who want a pub with a dedicated kids menu AND a nightclub in the same building. Families who call their local the "Chelsea Heights Hotel" and mean it with pride. And anyone who's ever been in a blue with a bouncer and lived to tell the tale. We're not much, but we're real.
