# Wallan (VIC)

Slug: `wallan-vic`

Stop us if you’ve heard this one: it’s 7:42am on the V/Line, you’ve already had three “ongoing rail equipment fault at Wallan” announcements, and you’re mentally reciting your rainwater tank compliance specs. That’s just a Thursday in the fastest-growing commuter town north of Melbourne — where the growth rate is 0.029 per cent, as Time Out just confirmed, and so is the blood pressure.

Saturday morning doesn’t mean brunch. It means kitting up at Wallan Paintball — the largest paintball venue in Victoria, packages starting at $30 for four hours of shooting your neighbours. Because why pat a labradoodle when you can simulate a tactical breach in a paddock? It’s not gentrification; it’s adrenaline.

The local drama? It’s on Whirlpool, where someone building in Wallan just discovered they need 5,000 litres of rainwater tank and double glazing to comply with developer design guidelines. Meanwhile, old-school locals mutter about how the new estates are “lowering the class” of the place — you know, back when Wallan was a proper country feel, not a housing estate with a train derailment history. (Pedestrian TV reminded us of the 2020 derailment on a track that needed maintenance. They fixed it eventually. Probably.)

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “country feel” means a calm tree change and not a 4am wake-up to catch a train that might get cancelled. Or anyone who expects a local cafe that isn’t a paintball field.

Who would love it? People who want a four-bedroom house on a block big enough for a rainwater tank and a dream, who don’t mind a bit of delayed V/Line banter. Come for the growth rate, stay for the paintball. Just bring your own double glazing.
