# Ormeau (VIC)

Slug: `ormeau-vic`

Ormeau, VIC — we’re the suburb that exists in a permanent state of “just driving through.” Actually, we’re the reason you’re sitting in 10km of M1 traffic from Beenleigh to Ormeau, wondering if you’ll ever make it home. Broadsheet called that “a multi-vehicle crash causing major delays,” but we call it Tuesday morning.

Yes, we know what they say about us. One Whirlpool forum put it this way: “Ormeau is the equivalent of Rooty Hill or Blacktown in Sydney. There’s redevelopment and estates going in, but it’s still got a lot of bogans floating around.” And we won’t argue. We’ve got the housing estates, the gravel driveways, and the smoke alarm legislation debates that go for 47 pages because no one can agree if the chirping is worth the risk of dying in a fire. (Thanks, Whirlpool.)

But here’s the thing: come December, we turn into a glittering, musical menace. Urban List says our “refreshed 7th edition of the annual epic Christmas lights musical display” is “one of our personal favourite displays.” We take that seriously. You’ll find families piled into utes, thermos of Milo in hand, crawling past houses that look like they’ve been electrocuted by Santa himself. It’s community, mate. Or at least, it’s a good excuse to judge each other’s LED investments.

And if you need a feed after all that staring? Garlic Clove Indian Restaurant at Shop 15A, 37 Eggersdorf Rd does the business. Urban List calls it “the real deal” with “sumptuous Indian cuisine made from recipes passed down through generations.” No oat milk latte here. Just butter chicken and a side of “you’re a long way from Melbourne, aren’t you?”

Who’d hate it here? Anyone who’s allergic to bogans, traffic, or council debates about fire alarms. But if you love Christmas lights that look like a transformer had a seizure, don’t mind the M1 becoming a carpark, and think “bogan” is a term of endearment? Pull up a chair. We’ll save you a spot in the queue.
