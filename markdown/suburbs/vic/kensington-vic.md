# Kensington (VIC)

Slug: `kensington-vic`

Kensington, VIC – where we’ve got specialty coffee, a community garden, and a local bloke making the news for heckling the ANZAC Day dawn service. Yes, that happened. But we’re not defined by our worst moments – we’re defined by how many times we can queue at Rumble Coffee Roasters before noon.

A Saturday morning here? You’re starting at Double Dutch Bakery for a butter croissant that’s flakier than the rental market. Then it’s a banh mi from Rollin169 near the station because you deserve something good after fighting for a park. If you’re feeling fancy, Arnold’s across from the station is pouring natural wine from Manze’s Nagesh Seethiah – a local lad who made good. And if you want to pretend you’re in Fitzroy without the attitude, The Abyssinian’s injera will set you straight.

The Doot – Doutta Galla Hotel to the uninitiated – reopened last year after a major glow-up, and we’ve collectively decided it’s still our pub, just with better lights. Meanwhile, the Kensington Rec Centre got an Olympic-sized makeover (Emma McKeon approved) and locals are thrilled they can swim without fear of tetanus.

But let’s be real: Kensington is in its “quietly becoming” era. Broadsheet called it “one of Melbourne’s most livable suburbs” and we’re both smug and terrified. The new Local: Kensington apartments offer five-year leases and a pet-friendly co-working space – basically a resort for people who don’t want to leave the suburb. Meanwhile, Younghusband Market pops up with artisan everything, and we dutifully show up to buy $12 candles.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a 24-hour supermarket. Anyone who thinks “leafy streets” means actual nightlife. And definitely anyone who doesn’t want to hear about the Kensington Stockyard Food Garden for the fifth time this week.

Who would love it? Renovation nostalgics who miss when the Doot had sticky carpets. Uni students who can walk to class. And anyone who wants to live two kilometres from the city but still pretend they’re in the country. Welcome to Kensington – we’ve got tacos at La Tortilleria, raves at The Third Day, and a firmly held opinion that Laksa King is worth the Flemington queue.
