# Glen Iris (VIC)

Slug: `glen-iris-vic`

We’ve got the "Milan end of Burke Road" and let’s be honest, it’s more like the Milan end of a very long, leafy driveway where the cars are nicer than your house. Welcome to Glen Iris, where the white picket fences are real and the only drama is whether you can get a booking at Grazia before 2028.

A weekend here: you start at Baba Sus for an Asian-inspired coffee and a slight existential crisis over the menu’s “twists.” Then you wander to Pantry Glen Iris, which by sunset morphs into Too Good Talker – a rotating sign does the heavy lifting while you switch from gingerbread porridge to lamb biang biang noodles. Fancy. But if you’re really feeling yourself, you’ll head to Huzzah (hidden behind the station, obviously) for bocce and a pint of NeighbourGOOD, the house beer that donates proceeds to local clubs. Because nothing says Glen Iris like feeling virtuous while throwing darts.

Petty drama? Oh, we’ve got layers. First, Deeds Brewing opened a swanky taproom in what Urban List called “one of the driest spots in Melbourne” – which is a sick burn on our rainfall, thanks. Then in 2024 it went into administration. But don’t cry: Stomping Ground co-founder Steve Jeffares bought the space and also opened Huzzah. So the same bloke now owns half our social life. And last October, a hundred girls from Sacre Coeur school flooded a local Macca’s in yellow jumpsuits and afro wigs for muck-up day. That’s our front page.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks a $4.7 million lottery prize home is “reasonable” or who gets stressed by the sheer concentration of marble, terrazzo, and heritage-listed light-filled spaces. Also, if you don’t love hearing “our physio is also a yoga guru with a Himalayan vision,” Universal Practice might be too much.

Who would love it? People who want a neighbourhood that feels like a lifestyle magazine shoot – but with a working brewery, a beer garden that handles four seasons in one day, and a Greek wine bar named after the owner’s wife. We’re not a suburb, we’re a carefully curated dining precinct with really good schools and a side of wholesome chaos.
