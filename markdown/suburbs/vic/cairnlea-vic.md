# Cairnlea (VIC)

Slug: `cairnlea-vic`

Cairnlea, VIC — you’ve never heard of us, and honestly? That’s how we like it.

We’re the suburb that exists between the Western Freeway and the Caroline Springs roundabouts. The place where the Corkman Pub’s asbestos ended up after those developers illegally demolished it — yes, that was us. We didn’t ask for it, we didn’t want it, but here we are, forever known as “that Cairnlea lot” in planning tribunal documents.

Our fifteen minutes of fame? A bloke pulled over by the Safer Communities Task Force with $2,000 worth of stolen meat and seafood in his car. As one local put it on the Whirlpool forums: “I’ve just moved from London… staying with the other half’s parents in Cairnlea.” Welcome to Australia, mate. Hope you like meat pies and police chases.

Back in the day, we had a property at 17 Sunnyside Way briefly mentioned in a Sitchu profile — but that’s about as glamorous as our real estate gets. We don’t have a cafe scene. We don’t have a late-night economy. The closest thing to a local landmark is the Brimbank City Council offices and a servo that sells slightly stale pasties.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “community hub” means something other than the IGA carpark. If you need a cold drip coffee at 7am, move to Fitzroy. If you value heritage architecture, maybe don’t live in the suburb where heritage building waste was illegally dumped.

Who would love it? Ex-Londoners who want to experience what “quiet” really means. People who appreciate a good Whirlpool ADSL argument. And anyone who thinks a busted meat haul is peak local entertainment. We’re not much, but we’re honest. And we’ve got plenty of space for your stolen seafood.
