# Derrimut (VIC)

Slug: `derrimut-vic`

We've got 3 playgroups and a dream — welcome to Derrimut, VIC, where the local nightlife is a toddler’s birthday party at the Derrimut Community Centre that finishes by 11am.

Back in the day, when the blog was hot, you’d find us next door to Derrimut Primary School, trying to remember whether we signed little Timmy up for Playgroup 1, 2, or 3. The real drama? Forgetting your playgroup number 103727 on the term fee form — that’s our version of a council scandal.

There is no “ritual espresso.” There is no “club 77.” There is only a community centre with vacancies and a laminated timetable. We know what they say about us: Derrimut’s just a roundabout with a Bunnings on the way to Werribee. And they’re not wrong, but they missed the best part — our local economy runs on stale Anzac biscuits and warm cask wine at the playground.

Misfits, stay away unless you’ve got a child under 5 and a burning desire to pay term fees for the privilege of sitting on a tiny plastic chair. If you’re after a late-night eatery like Supper Inn? Forget it. Our closest thing to a 2.30am classic is the 7-Eleven on Ballarat Road.

But who would love it here? Any parent who knows that three playgroups mean three chances to avoid folding the laundry. Come for the “vegetable garden” that’s mostly weeds, stay for the complete absence of hipster pretension. We’re not so much a suburb as a state of mind — and that mindset is “can I please just get five minutes of silence while the kids do jigsaw puzzles.” Welcome to our creaky, carpeted, chaos-filled kingdom.
