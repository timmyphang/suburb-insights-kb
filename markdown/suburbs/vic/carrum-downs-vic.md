# Carrum Downs (VIC)

Slug: `carrum-downs-vic`

We know what they say about Carrum Downs. "Carrum Downs? Oh, the bit you drive past on the way to the Peninsula." And yeah, we are the suburb you speed through on Frankston-Dandenong Road, but you’re missing the real action – and the motorised esky chase.

A Saturday morning here? You’ll find us at Dainton Family Brewery, the warehouse that Dan Dainton and his old man Kevin opened after looking everywhere from Milawa to Shepparton. We settled for the site beside the freeway – perfect for families and people who want to sink a Red Eye Rye while the kids play table tennis under the giant catwalk. Or maybe you’re grabbing a sourdough loaf from Lievito – the bakery so good even Albert Park cafes brag about it. Then it’s Bayside Rock for a climb designed by an actual Australian champion, or Funtopia for the kids to go feral in Australia’s largest indoor play centre.

But we have our moments. You know the 17-year-old who got chased by a police helicopter through Carrum Downs in a motorised esky packed with weed? That was us. Our local colour. Also our Whirlpool forum threads are a vibe: “Getting a lot more worried about Carrum Downs with lots of car burglaries,” one user wrote. Meanwhile the Kmart carpark is “at capacity on the weekend.” Priorities.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks the Peninsula Link is a “scenic drive.” People who want a corner cafe with exposed brick and oat milk art, not a brewery food truck and a climbing wall. City dwellers who clutch their pearls at the words “Hall Road.”

Who would love it? Families who want a warehouse brewery where the kids can run feral and you can sip a Bad Daughter choc-orange porter. Tradies who need a Kmart run and a brew within 500 metres. Locals like Barry, who still huffs about “the sheilas” on AFLW but secretly loves that we’ve got one of the best climbing centres in Melbourne. We’re Carrum Downs – we’re not pretty, but we’ve got beer, bread, and a great esky story.
