# Greenvale (VIC)

Slug: `greenvale-vic`

If you’ve ever wanted to live in a suburb that’s equal parts “tree-lined streets” and “please boil your water before brushing your teeth,” welcome to Greenvale. Yes, we made Time Out’s 2024 list of Melbourne’s most affordable and liveable suburbs — $873,750 median price, spacious living, community feel — but we also made Yarra Valley Water’s 2020 “boil alert” list alongside 87 other unlucky postcodes. That’s balance.

Our crowning glory? Woodlands Historic Park. It’s genuinely stunning — rolling bushland, wild kangaroos, more fauna and flora than you can shake a bin chicken at. Visitors think it’s a nature wonderland. Locals know it’s where you take relatives from Craigieburn when you need an hour of peace. The roos don’t judge your lawn.

Come December, Glencairn Drive transforms into the Clark Griswold Memorial Spectacular. The Urban List named it one of Melbourne’s best Christmas light streets, which means suburb-wide street cred and a 300% increase in Toyota Camrys crawling past at 2km/h. We don’t have a fancy café strip. We have a park with kangaroos and a street that goes nuclear for six weeks. It’s a vibe.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who needs a late-night small bar or a brunch spot that serves activated charcoal something. If your ideal Saturday involves a reso at a place called “Ritual Espresso” — sorry, that’s not here. We don’t have one. Our most famous culinary export is “Greenvale Farm pork tenderloin” — and that’s served in the Grampians, not here.

Who would love it? Families who want a backyard bigger than a postage stamp, don’t mind the occasional boil-water Tuesday, and are ready to embrace a suburb where the biggest local character is a wild kangaroo named Greg. We’re affordable, we’re tree-y, and we’ve got a Christmas street that puts Myer’s window to shame. Just don’t drink the tap water without a warning first.
