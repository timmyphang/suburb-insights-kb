# Wantirna South (VIC)

Slug: `wantirna-south-vic`

Wantirna South — where your weekend plans begin and end with a carpark at Westfield Knox. We’re the suburb that made the top 20 liveable list in 2021 (#12, thank you very much) and we’ve been riding that high ever since, mostly from the food court at Ozone.

Our Saturday routine? Start with a sugar hit at Augustus Gelatery (cotton candy gelato before 10am? Absolutely). Then a few rounds of axe throwing or pickleball at Level Up because we’re aggressively active between bargain-hunting at Big W. Lunch is a dilemma: do you hit Oushou for truffle Wagyu fried rice or Chi-La Hunan Cuisine for stinky tofu that clears the entire level? Then you settle it over a tasting flight at Urban Alley Brewery, pretending you’re cultured while the kids stack tickets at Timezone.

Yes, we’re basically a shopping centre with a postcode. But we’ve got layers. Back in the day, Straits Cafe was the go-to for anyone wanting to impress a Sydney friend — as Broadsheet noted, one leading Melburnian planned to take their mate there post-lockdown. The Lyrebird Pop-Up Bar and The Bavarian’s bottomless meat and cheese boards came and went, but we’ve still got URD for your Subaru servicing when you’re sick of the dealer upsell.

We’ve also made the news for reasons beyond retail: hot air balloons crash-landing near Eastlink, lightning strikes knocking out power to 100,000 homes, and a 15-year-old student calling out racism at The Knox School. We’re not just a carpark — we’re a carpark with drama.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “culture” is a Fitzroy laneway and gets anxious when they can’t find a single-origin pour-over within 50 metres. Also, people who hate roundabouts. We have so many roundabouts.

Who would love it? Families who want everything in one sprawling, air-conditioned ecosystem. People who consider a great day out “doing a lap of Knox” and meaning it affectionately. If your ideal Saturday involves a Japanese feast, a gelato, a brewery, and laser tag without ever stepping outside — welcome home. We’ve got your whole life under one roof.
