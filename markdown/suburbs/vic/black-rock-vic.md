# Black Rock (VIC)

Slug: `black-rock-vic`

Black Rock, VIC — where brunch is taken so seriously we’ve got a dedicated village for it, and the only thing browner than the sand is the coffee at your local.

Saturday morning starts at **Cafeteria** on Bluff Road, where Irish chef Eugene Lavery fled to our sleepy suburb for a “quiet life” and somehow ended up running a hotspot. We’re still not sure if we’re meant to gatekeep this place or charge tourists admission. One dinner service a week? That’s not hospitality, that’s a power move. The rosti with salmon and poached egg is the closest we come to being transported to Italy — until you remember gelato is also a local religion.

Then there’s **Winston’s**, the European bistro that landed in 2024 and made Baysiders reconsider the hike into the city. “Primo dining with Euro flair,” they said. We say it’s just nice to have somewhere to wear a collared shirt that isn’t a funeral. Over on Beach Road, **True South** offers water views and private dining rooms for up to 200 guests — perfect for when you need to explain to your relatives why you still live in your hometown.

And let’s not forget **Elichi**, the modern Indian eatery from the Babu Ji team, serving truffle naan and brussels sprouts with curry leaf. Because nothing says “laid-back coastal suburb” like a Punjabi-inspired cardamom haven. We’re not complaining — just wondering when the gelato truck will start serving vindaloo popsicles.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “affluent” and “laid-back” are contradictions. City slickers who need a new club every weekend. People who don’t understand why we’d rather snorkel at **Half Moon Bay** than queue for a rooftop bar.

Who’d love it? The kind of person who takes brunch very seriously, owns more than one swimsuit, and believes a beachside stroll is the only cardio you’ll ever need. Welcome to the Rock. Don’t tell everyone.
