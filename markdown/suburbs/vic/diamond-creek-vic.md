# Diamond Creek (VIC)

Slug: `diamond-creek-vic`

**Welcome to Diamond Creek: Where Your Only Social Outlet Is a Brewery That Almost Opened Into a Lockdown**

We wake up every morning in Diamond Creek and the first question we ask ourselves is: "Should we drive to the brewery, or should we walk to the brewery?" Because let's be honest, that's pretty much the full social calendar here.

Our crown jewel is **Golden Hills Brewery** on Station Street — which, as Broadsheet kindly documented, opened the day before Victoria's first lockdown in 2020. Legend has it the owner Tyson Chambers basically had to beg favours to can the beer that was meant for pints and serve it through a bottle shop window. That's the most Diamond Creek origin story imaginable. We didn't get a grand opening. We got a "please form an orderly queue at the roller door."

These days Golden Hills has 11 taps pouring everything from a feijoa sour to a ginger and lime gose — which is exactly the kind of adventurous drinking you need when your other option is watching the speed cameras on **Luxford Road**. And sure, the same bloke also owns a hotel in South Melbourne now, but we pretend he's still ours.

But here's the thing: the area's real claim to fame might be the Moon Dog Brewery origin story. According to Broadsheet, one of the founders was working at the **Diamond Creek Tavern bottle shop** — the Snake Pit — back in the day, scoring Chimay and dreaming of something bigger. And now Moon Dog has a 900-person venue in Footscray. So we're basically the spiritual birthplace of Melbourne's craft beer scene. We just happen to be the bit where you can still park.

If craft beer isn't your thing, there's **Wild Heart Woodcraft** making handcrafted pens and homewares from epoxy resin — which is great if you're a tradie who's also into calligraphy. And you can pop down to **CherryHill Orchards** in the Yarra Valley for fruit picking, provided you book ahead online and don't just show up like some sort of spontaneous city person.

**Who would hate it here:** Anyone who wants to go out for a spontaneous dinner that isn't parma-adjacent. Or anyone who doesn't drink beer. There's a charming bakery and a train station, but this is not a place where you "stumble upon" a late-night taco joint.

**Who would love it here:** People who've accepted that life is about finding one good brewery, one good bottle shop (RIP Snake Pit legacy), and a really good view of the Plenty River while you wait for the coffee to kick in. We're not much, but we're honest. And we've got a feijoa sour with your name on it.
