# Armstrong Creek (VIC)

Slug: `armstrong-creek-vic`

**Armstrong Creek, VIC — The Suburb That's Still Loading**

You know that feeling when you move into a brand-new estate and the render on your neighbour’s house is still curing? That's Armstrong Creek. We've been on the Geelong growth frontier for a decade now, and our main community event is still the playgroup at the Armstrong Creek Community Hub. As the Playgroup Victoria page notes, we've got an "indoor/outdoor community room" — and frankly, that's our nightlife sorted.

Back in the day, we thought we were getting a proper town centre. Instead, we got an NBN that still makes the Whirlpool forums weep. Someone on the iiNet thread complained about speeds dropping from "40-60mbps to 3mbps" on WiFi while sitting ten feet from the router. That's us. We're the ones restarting the modem before the kids' Peppa Pig buffer finishes.

There's no café. No pub. No bakery. Our only named culinary export so far is something called "Armstrong Barbecue" — which actually launched in a Melbourne food hall, not here. So if you're craving brisket, you're driving 20 minutes into Geelong. Or, you know, firing up the Weber in the driveway and pretending you're at Il Mercato Centrale.

The misfits? Anyone who wants a barista within walking distance. Anyone who doesn't own a pram. Anyone who thinks a "hub" should include more than a room with a kettle and a stack of plastic dinosaurs.

But for families? This place is a blank canvas. The Lutheran College has a German-language playgroup. The streets are wide enough to forget your neighbour's name. And if you love the smell of fresh asphalt and the sound of a WiFi router being power-cycled, welcome home. Just bring your own coffee. And patience.
