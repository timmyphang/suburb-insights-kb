# Carlton North (VIC)

Slug: `carlton-north-vic`

We love a good queue in Carlton North. Not the kind for a train — we don’t have one, and we like it that way — but the kind that wraps around the block at **Florian** on Rathdowne Street. By 8am you’ll find us shuffling past the new **Florian To Go** (opened 2026, takeaway salads and ham baguettes on Iris the Bakery bread) while muttering about how it’s the only gym we’ll ever join. Even *Wuthering Heights* star Jacob Elordi has been spotted here for bacon and soft-boiled eggs — which is more than you can say for most inner-north postcodes.

Our morning routine is a pilgrimage: hit **Amann Patisserie** for a maple bacon danish (the French couple Yohann and Alizée somehow make it work), then mooch next door to **Florian Home** for ceramics we definitely don’t need but “feel like an extension of the brand”. If you’re feeling bready, **Monforte Viennoiserie** sorts you out, though the proper barista coffee they finally added was a game-changer. Or head to **North Carlton Canteen** on the border of Brunswick East for pumpkin fetta fritters and a picnic box — $30 for two paninis and two sweets, basically the cheapest therapy in town.

**Petty local drama:** The NIMBYs are right about one thing — Florian is never not packed. But they also fought tooth and nail against anything new, so when **Thirty Eight Chairs** took over the old Paragon Cafe site (the one with the colourful history — stolen ute through the window in 2014, fire in 2015, underworld ties to Mick Gatto), you could hear the sigh of relief from Rathdowne Street all the way to the Elvis Presley Memorial. Yes, that’s a thing — a tiny garden shrine to The King on Canning Street. It’s our most niche attraction, and we’re proud of it.

**Who would hate it here:** Anyone without a tolerance for queues, heritage overlays, and paying $30 for a picnic you could assemble at home. If you think Lygon Street is the only game in town, you’ll be lost. Also, if you’re offended by a bar called **Henry Sugar** serving kangaroo tail rillettes (affectionately known as “kangarhubarb”) — this might not be your speed.

**Who would love it:** People who treat brunch as a competitive sport. Fans of tiny bakeries with huge reputations. Anyone who wants to live in a village that feels like a movie set — complete with a wine bar (**Brico**), a sweets-soaked namesake (**Calle Bakery** for croissant wheels), and a wellness space that promises euphoria (**Ma Saj** on Nicholson Street). We’re basically a food court disguised as a suburb, and we wouldn’t have it any other way.
