# Melton (VIC)

Slug: `melton-vic`

There’s a moment every Melton local knows — you’re staring at the reactive clay crack in your driveway, wondering if the $900 million Melton Hospital (first fully electric in Victoria — very fancy) will open before your slab splits in two. You head to Woodgrove Shopping Centre for some DC EV charging. Still waiting. So you do what any of us would: swing past Buddy’s Bakery, grab Australia’s Best Plain Meat Pie (yes, 2024 baby, we beat the whole country) and sit in the car wondering why we’re technically “metro Melbourne” but still get treated like regional cousins.

Speaking of cousins — let’s talk about the nana who grew weed in her secondhand RV in a Melton backyard to keep her sons off the street. The County Court heard about it. That’s our local Breaks Bad energy. We don’t have a secret laneway jazz den (sorry Urban List date ideas), but we do have a backyard operation that’s more dedicated than Aether Brewing’s ginger beer recall.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “Melton” is just a wine producer from South Australia. Anyone who Googles “Melton” and gets *May December* instead of a council permit for a deck. Anyone who wants their soil to stay still.

Who would love it? People who appreciate a champion pie, a basketball team supported by Grace Community Melton Uniting Church, and a suburb that’s building its own hospital while dealing with the same clay problems your grandparents’ house had. We’re not trying to be the next Footscray. We’re just trying to get some decent charging at the shops and keep our nan out of the news.
