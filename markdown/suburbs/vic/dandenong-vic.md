# Dandenong (VIC)

Slug: `dandenong-vic`

We know what you’re thinking: “Dandenong? Isn’t that where you go for scones and steam trains?” Mate, that’s the *Ranges*. We’re the other Dandenong – the one with a neon pig, a six-seat hidden diner that’s actually in Cockatoo, and enough cultural density to make Footscray blush.

A morning here starts at the Dandenong Market (Cleeland Street, since 1866 – older than your suburb’s collective imagination). “We’re about the hustle and the bustle,” says the marketing manager. We are. You want five million visitors a year? That’s us. Grab a bag of fresh curry leaves from Little India on Foster Street, then eye off the Bombay Halwa at Calcutta Sweets. It’s the Punjabi curries that keep us coming back, not the Insta-worthy lighting.

**Petty Local Drama:** The old Carl’s Jr on the Princes Highway closed. For months, Whirlpool forums were buzzing: “Wendy’s is coming!” “No, it’s not.” “Yes, it is – the sign’s up!” It’s still a drive-thru ghost. Meanwhile, PCCatz – a boutique PC shop we all loved – vanished into the digital ether. Magg’s Emporium (pulleys, tractor parts, tins of dust) closed in the ’80s but we still bring it up.

**Misfit alert:** If you’re here for the Dandenong Ranges’ Miss Marple’s Tea Room, you will be very confused. We’ve got Brick Lane Brewery in Dandenong South (award-winning dark hoppy ale) and Dandee Donuts’ gold-and-purple van handing out free jam doughnuts. There’s a WOTSO coworking space now. This is not a Tudor cottage.

**Who would love it here?** Anyone who wants a proper feed – Afghan bread from clay ovens, Sri Lankan hoppers, authentic everything. The Dandenong Film Festival is legit. The Festival of Lights at Greaves Reserve is a month of silk lanterns. We’re 40 minutes from the city by train, and we’ve got the Drum Theatre, not a taxidermy shop. Come for the market, stay for the bread. Just don’t ask where the steam train is.
