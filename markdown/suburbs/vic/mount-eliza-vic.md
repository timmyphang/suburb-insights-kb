# Mount Eliza (VIC)

Slug: `mount-eliza-vic`

You know you’re in Mount Eliza when the local news cycle is split between The Block’s “red flag” mansions and a bakery finally opening in a strip that’s had a ‘Coming Soon’ sign since 2022.

Our Saturday routine? Start at **Sunnyside North Beach** — a serene little cove adored by locals who definitely don’t tell tourists about it. Then you wander up to the village for a squiz at **Bau Bau Dining**, where they farm rare Italian veggies in Daylesford and charge $90 for lunch. “Woof woof” means Italian, apparently, and the parking situation means you’ll need a four-wheel drive just to parallel park.

But the real drama? **The Block 2026** rolled in with five fresh-faced teams, multimillion-dollar water views, and a “red flag” warning from property insiders who reckon they’ve completely misread what Peninsula buyers want. Meanwhile, we’ve been living with the **Mount Eliza ADSL2+ exchange** — notoriously overloaded since the dial-up era. You can renovate a mansion for The Block, but we can’t stream Netflix on a Saturday night without buffering.

Our luxury cred is legit though: **The Bay Crest Manor** has old-world charm, two acres of gardens, and a giant chess board. If you’re not staying there, you’re probably at **Slim’s** — a retro, rowdy Italo-American pizza parlour by the Wowee Zowee team. Yes, we have a pizza place named after a 1970s diet plan.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks a “village atmosphere” means walking distance to a train station. We don’t have trains. We have a bus that turns up three times a day and a Whirlpool thread dedicated to our exchange’s latency issues.

But honestly? If you’ve got a spare million, don’t mind a bit of ADSL nostalgia, and want to watch The Block contestants argue over window frames while sipping a Negroni at Bau Bau — welcome to the peninsula’s poshest hot mess. Just don’t ask about the internet.
