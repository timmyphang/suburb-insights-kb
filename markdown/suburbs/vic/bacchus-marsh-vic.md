# Bacchus Marsh (VIC)

Slug: `bacchus-marsh-vic`

We know what you’re thinking: “Bacchus Marsh? Isn’t that just the turn-off for Lerderderg Gorge?” And yeah, sure, the 89-kilometre walking track from here to Daylesford starts at our doorstep – we’ve got ancient rock formations and glacial sediment to prove we’re older than your inner-city apartment. But we’re more than a pit stop for hikers who’ve just passed the Werribee Gorge Circuit.

Our Saturday morning routine? Swing by the Bacchus Marsh Newsagency at 138 Main Street for a scratchie, then hit up Bacchus Marsh FoodWorks on Bennett Street for pre-walk sushi (yes, our supermarket does sushi). If you’re feeling fancy, you’ll find us at the new Local Taproom Bacchus on Grant Street – the first craft beer taproom in town, opened by locals who finally got sick of driving to Ballarat for a decent IPA. Before that, the most exciting new business was Mudpuppies Grooming, and frankly, the dogs are better dressed than half the adults.

Now for the petty drama. Everyone knows the real blood sport in Bacchus Marsh isn’t footy – it’s school waiting lists. You’ve got parents queuing for years at Heathdale Christian College, Good News Lutheran, and of course Bacchus Marsh Grammar. The Whirlpool forums still burn with arguments: “PEGS vs Bacchus Marsh Grammar – the academic gap is less than you think.” Meanwhile, our grammar school hit national headlines in 2024 when AI-generated fake nudes of 50 female students were circulated. We’re not proud, but at least we’re famous for something other than the aquifer that waters Peninsula Hot Springs.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting a late-night scene – The Local Taproom closes at a reasonable hour, and the most heated debate at the Bacchus Marsh Lawn Tennis Club on Taverner Street is whether to book the court for an extra half hour. Also, if your NBN drops out (hello, iiNet users), you’ll be on Whirlpool yelling about the optical light being red for days.

Who would love it? Families who want a big backyard, a good state school, and a lawn bowls club that actually runs an intra-club match when Deer Park cancels. We’ve got the Racecourse Reserve for footy, the Lerderderg River for swimming, and a FoodWorks with a deli counter. We’re the kind of town where the playgroup has been running for 20 years and the annual meeting nominations cause genuine tension. Welcome to Bacchus Marsh – we’ve got minerals, mudpuppies, and a very strong opinion about which grammar school is overpriced.
