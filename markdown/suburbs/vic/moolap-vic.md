# Moolap (VIC)

Slug: `moolap-vic`

**Moolap, VIC — where the flowers are treated with reverence, but your internet is treated with contempt.**

We don’t have a cafe strip. We don’t have a main street. We have the Geelong Flower Farm, where buckets spill over with seasonal stems and “unfussy bunches,” and we have a 2.7km cable length to the MOLP exchange that synced at 10,500kbps if you were lucky. That’s the full Moolap experience: you can buy a beautiful arrangement of native foliage, then go home and wait three minutes for a webpage to load while you stare at the ADSL2+ light.

Yes, we’re the suburb the NBN forgot. Back in 2020, we were on Whirlpool forums arguing about Optus off-net vs Telstra ADSL1. TPG didn’t even have us on their to-do list. The most heated local debate of the past decade wasn’t about a development application — it was whether Exetel was better than the copper. Spoiler: neither is.

But we’ve got the flower farm. As Sitchu noted, flowers are treated “with reverence, not fuss” out here. That’s Moolap in a nutshell. We’re the fuss-free, reverent fringe of Geelong. You won’t find us on any “best suburbs” list. You’ll find us on a speed test result that makes you weep.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a strong 4G signal. Anyone who thinks “local” means a laneway espresso bar. Anyone who wants to post a TikTok without buffering for 90 seconds.

Who would love it? Flower arrangers who appreciate horticultural know-how. People who think “unfussy” is a compliment. And anyone who’s fine with the internet being a throwback — because the flowers are worth the wait.
