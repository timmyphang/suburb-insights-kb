# Altona (VIC)

Slug: `altona-vic`

*ROASTING ALTONA: THE BAYSIDE UNDERDOG THAT INVENTED "ALMOST HAVING IT ALL"*

Let's be honest, we Altonians have been living in Williamstown's shadow for so long we've got permanent shade on our faces. While Willy's got the yacht club and the heritage vibe, we've got... a haunted homestead from 1842 where an apparition peers out the window? Yeah, Junkee says it's one of Australia's most-haunted spots. Perfect for when you want a ghost AND a 20-minute drive from the city.

But look, we're on the rise. Two Bros on Blyth opened at 51 Blyth Street and gave us that cultural injection Concrete Playground raved about — espresso martinis upstairs, breakfast downstairs. And BAM Street Food? Started with owner Cal smashing patties in his garage, now they've got a brick-and-mortar in Harrington Square doing lacey-edged smash burgers. As Urban List noted, "the fuss all started when owner Cal began (literally) smashing patties in his garage." That's Altona energy right there.

And Matteo's Delicatessen on Pier Street? Pastrami, provolone, cannoli from Cannoleria, and Industry Beans coffee. The sandwiches are so loaded the Cold Cuts one is "just as meaty as you think."

The drama? Our recycling bins got the new purple lid before half of Melbourne. Hobsons Bay Council introduced the fourth-bin system earlier than most, as Broadsheet noted. We're environmental pioneers, apparently.

Who'd hate it here? Anyone who needs a proper nightlife without driving to the city. Also that Whirlpool user who warned about "random large volcanic rocks" when digging in Altona. And the poor jokers on the train between Seaholme and Altona — as Whirlpool flagged — who "start moving from the fourth carriage to the last carriage" trying to get off faster, "juggling two doors, bags and the train jinking left and right."

Who'd love it? People who want a bayside suburb where you can still afford a unit in Altona Meadows (as those Whirlpool buyers were keen on), where your dog can run wild at Burn's Reserve at low tide, and where the fish and chips at the westside institution on Ford Road is treated like "a treasure," per Sitchu.

We're not Williamstown. We're Altona. We've got ghosts, smash burgers, a purple recycling bin, and volcanic rocks. And honestly? We wouldn't have it any other way.
