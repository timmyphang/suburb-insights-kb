# Balwyn North (VIC)

Slug: `balwyn-north-vic`

**Balwyn North: where the Italian grocers have been family-run since 1963 and the only thing moving faster than the traffic on Burke Road is the rumour that Little Nonna might come back (spoiler: it won’t).**

We’re the suburb that says “I live in Balwyn” and then quietly whispers “North” when no one’s listening. And honestly? We like it that way.

A Saturday morning here starts at **Peddler** — the café that’s been a staple for years and still has a weekend queue that makes you question your life choices. If you’re feeling fancy, you swing by **Matta** in Balwyn North for a Japanese brunch that isn’t your standard eggs Benny. Or, if you’re homesick for Mumbai, you head to **Chai Garden** for a grilled Bombay sandwich and a masala chai that hits different.

Then it’s off to **Boccaccio Cellars** — the Italian grocer that’s been on Burke Road since 1963. But we don’t just shop downstairs anymore. We’ve got **Enoteca Boccaccio** upstairs now, a wine bar with two cellars’ worth of Italian vino and an official head of cheese. Yes, a *head of cheese*. That’s the kind of job title we respect.

The drama? Well, there was that one time a DiDi driver allegedly took a wrong turn towards Balwyn North. We’re still not sure if it was an attempted abduction or just someone trying to avoid the Doncaster Road traffic. Either way, we’ve got opinions about it in the Whirlpool forums, right next to the decade-old thread about the ADSL2+ exchange at 269 Doncaster Road.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “leafy streets” means “interesting nightlife.” We’ve got one wine bar, a butcher that sells $40 steaks, and a bakery called **The Caterer’s Kitchen** that’s “nifty.” That’s it. If you need a club, try Fitzroy.

Who would love it? Families who value top schools, long drives down Belmore Road, and the quiet satisfaction of knowing your local grocer has better prosciutto than your inner-city mate’s entire suburb.

We’re not flashy. We’re just Balwyn North — the slightly less famous sibling who still knows where the good olives are.
