# Montrose (VIC)

Slug: `montrose-vic`

So you’ve driven 45 minutes from the city, past the Pines, past the pie shop that’s been there since 1987, and now you’re in Montrose. The roundabout has a big sculpture of a leaf. We’re very proud of it. We call it “The Leaf.” Don’t ask why.

On a Saturday morning, we’re either queuing at **Montrose Christmas Tree Farm** (they started with 50 trees, now they sell 1,500. We don’t do anything by halves) or we’re booking a private mineral bath at **Japanese Mountain Retreat** on Mountain Crescent. Yes, we have a luxury Japanese hot spring hidden behind the servo. You can soak alone in a cedar-lined bath while your mate pretends they’re in Hakone. The reality: you’re 200 metres from the BP. We own the contradiction.

And if you’re not soaking, you’re stuffing your face at **Mary Eats Cake**. They’ve got locations in Brunswick and Montrose, and we’re still not sure which one is more surprised to exist. Here you can get a bottomless high tea with mimosas, fluffy scones, and a welcome cocktail. The scones are famous. The gossip is hotter than the bathhouse.

The petty drama? Someone once complained that the Christmas tree farm sold out of the “premium” trees before November. The Montrose Facebook group went nuclear. “It’s not a competition” said one mum. “Yes it is” said the mum who got the last 7ft pine.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a train station. We’ve got a bus. One bus. It comes every hour. Also anyone who doesn’t like the smell of eucalyptus mixed with someone’s weekend firepit. The air is smoky, damp, and people are unapologetically hanging laundry on a line.

Who would love it? People who want to soak in private peace, eat cake, and buy a Christmas tree in a place where the roundabout has a leaf. We know we’re a weird mix of luxury spa and suburban bogan. But we’ve got the best high tea in the hills and the only Japanese retreat you don’t have to share with strangers. So come for the bath, stay for the scone. Just bring a jumper. It gets cold up here.
