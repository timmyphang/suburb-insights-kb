# Silvan (VIC)

Slug: `silvan-vic`

Silvan, VIC — the suburb that exists so Melburnians can say “we went berry-picking in the hills” without actually going to the Yarra Valley. We are a 20-acre fruit bowl with a side of reservoir paranoia, and we wouldn’t have it any other way.

Saturday morning in Silvan: you’re at Blue Hills Berries & Cherries on Parker Road, elbowing tourists for the last bucket of raspberries. The Chapman family’s been here since the 1800s, opened to the public in the ‘70s, and they’ve got strawberries, raspberries, blueberries, boysenberries, cherries — basically a vitamin aisle. But here’s the catch: berry-picking is Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Saturdays only. Show up on a Wednesday and you might as well head to the Silvan Reservoir and stare at the water. Which, by the way, is exactly what the Broadsheet people told us to do. “Six Dam Good Reservoirs,” they said. Ours helps supply metropolitan Melbourne. Good for them.

Petty local drama? Back in August 2020, storms knocked out power at the Silvan Dam and Yarra Valley Water told 88 suburbs — including us — to boil their tap water. “Potential water quality issue,” they said. Low risk of gastro, but better safe than sorry. So for a week, half the street was boiling billy cans like it was 1880. Classic Silvan: your drinkable water comes from a dam, your identity comes from a berry patch, and your sprayer comes from a brand literally named after us. As one Whirlpool forum user put it, “Silvan Prograde 5L works very well, but don’t overtighten the top fitting.” Solid life advice.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who wants a late-night anything. Our nightlife is watching the possums fight over fallen cherries. If you need a bar with a humidor or a cocktail list — head to Siglo in the city. That’s not us.

Who would love it? Families with $20 entry fees and a punnet bucket. Couples who think “all-you-can-eat cherries” is a personality trait. People who don’t mind checking a daily fruit report the night before. Welcome to Silvan. Bring a sprayer. Check the water. Don’t come on Wednesday.
