# Taylors Hill (VIC)

Slug: `taylors-hill-vic`

We are Taylors Hill, the Gen X capital of Melbourne—and no, not the Sri Lankan hotel, though we appreciate the confusion. We’re the suburb that had 119 active COVID cases back in July 2020 (postcode 3037, if you’re scoring at home), which means we were briefly famous for something other than being the place between Watergardens and Melton’s eventual ghetto-ification. 

Our main claim to fame? Swanston Court. Every night from 8:30pm to 10pm during December, the entire front of one house becomes a luminous wonderland with animated Santa and Mrs. Claus. We bring the Christmas spirit harder than anyone in the north-west, and we don’t even charge for parking. 

Whirlpool forums say we’ve got the highest percentage of Gen X of any Melbourne suburb. That tracks—we’re the middle child of the western corridor. Not young and trendy like Point Cook, not old and established like Taylors Lakes. We’re just here, upgrading our mortgages and yelling at kids to get off our nature strips. 

Local drama? Brimbank Council has a reputation for being “money hungry and corrupt as hell,” according to our own residents on Whirlpool. We take pride in that, honestly. Also, the crime stats are higher than the eastern suburbs, but it’s mostly car break-ins and the occasional home invasion—keeps us on our toes. And no, we don’t dress “ghetto,” cheers for that unsolicited fashion advice. 

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks a 45-minute train ride to the CBD is a dealbreaker. Anyone who wants a cafe open past 3pm. Anyone who sees a Bunnings and thinks “that’s too far.” 

Who would love it? If you’re a Gen X-er who wants a four-bedroom house under a million, a 15-minute drive to Watergardens, and a community that goes hard on Christmas lights while ignoring the Council’s latest rate rise—welcome home. Just mind the active COVID cases from 2020. We’re still talking about it.
