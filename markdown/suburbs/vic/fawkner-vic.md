# Fawkner (VIC)

Slug: `fawkner-vic`

We’re in Fawkner, postcode 3060, and we’ve got a Krispy Kreme drive-thru, a community farm, and a reputation that’s half “hot zone,” half “hey, nice Merri Creek trail.”

Let’s start with the obvious: we made the COVID lockdown lists. Concrete Playground and Pedestrian TV both named us a “priority suburb” for mobile testing vans. We were right there with Broadmeadows and Sunshine West. No complaints — we all stayed home, watched the docs on Urban List, and thanked the Premier for the shout-out.

Our biggest claim to fame? The Fawkner Food Bowls. It’s a community urban farm run by volunteers, part of Merri-Bek’s Food System Strategy. Broadsheet says it supplies seasonal produce to a Palestinian food truck called Falastini. So while South Yarra has Fawkner Park Tennis Centre (six synthetic flood-lit courts, tram accessible), we’ve got volunteer-run market gardens and a working bee. Different vibes.

On the drama front? Well, a Sonsa Market associate had two empty cars set alight at a Fawkner home around 5.30am on a Saturday. The Age got the tip. We don’t know the bloke, but we all raised an eyebrow.

Our beloved Krispy Kreme drive-thru — listed on Pedestrian TV as one of the few spots to get Doggie Doughnuts — is basically our town square. The other town square is the Merri Creek Trail, which starts up here and runs 19 kilometres to the Yarra. Perfect for an e-bike ride, if you can dodge the potholes on Sydney Road.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who thinks a “tennis centre” means something other than a synthetic court that’s been there since 2011. Or anyone who expects a flat white and a charcoal croissant on every corner. That’s not us. We’re more “pick up a silog from the food truck at Greenvale Rec” energy.

Who would love it? People who want a real community — urban farm, playgroup, and enough car torching gossip to keep the Whirlpool forums interesting. We’re Fawkner. We’re not fancy, but we’re real. And we’ve got doughnuts.
