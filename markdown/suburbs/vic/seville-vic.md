# Seville (VIC)

Slug: `seville-vic`

We know what you’re thinking. “Seville? The one with the orange trees and flamenco?” Nah, mate. That’s Spain. We’re Seville, VIC — an hour up the Maroondah Highway, where the only oranges are in the chef’s pickled heirloom veg at Seville Estate.

Our morning routine is telling tourists, “No, there’s no Royal Alcázar here. But we do have a whalebone-shaped dining room with picture windows looking out at a paddock.” As Broadsheet put it, the red soil means “citrus, wildflowers and berries thrive, and chooks, pigs and eggs are all farmed nearby.” We don’t have a Metropol Parasol, but we have a four-bedroom homestead restored to highlight mid-century architecture. Take that, Juergen Mayer H.

Petty local drama? There’s no drama. The only thing that’s ever happened was someone on Whirlpool trying to change their street address from Seville Close back to Flanders Street in 2001. Riveting stuff. Meanwhile, other articles about “Seville” are actually about a bloody bar in Brisbane called Savile Row, or a wooden structure in Spain. We’re used to being the footnote.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting tapas and sangria. The Urban List recommended “El Rinconcillo, Seville’s oldest bar dating back to 1670” — but that’s in Spain. Our oldest bar is the cellar door at Seville Estate, which started in 1972. Sorry, no chalked-up bills on the countertop.

Who would love it? Wine nerds who want to “enjoy a drop of its revered barrel-fermented chardonnay, or complex old-vine shiraz in the bright and spacious dining room.” Also anyone who wants to stay overnight in a restored homestead with a pool and native gardens, as Sitchu noted. Just don’t ask for the Barber of Seville. You’ll get a bloke pruning vines with a buzzcut.
