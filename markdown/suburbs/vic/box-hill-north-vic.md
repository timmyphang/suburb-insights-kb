# Box Hill North (VIC)

Slug: `box-hill-north-vic`

It’s 8am on Whitehorse Road and we’re already fielding the question: “Oh, you live in Box Hill?” No, mate — Box Hill *North*. We’re the quiet sibling who borrows the good clothes. You’ll find us two blocks north of Haidilao’s dancing noodles, but don’t expect a Molly Tea queue to form on *our* corner.

A proper morning here means swinging past **Wattle Park Green Grocer** for organic produce and vegan dips, then grabbing a **Ms Bean** egg tart from the Sky Square precinct if we’re feeling fancy. The real move? Walking through **Box Hill Gardens** with a takeaway coffee, watching the ducks judge our life choices. We’ve got the 109 tram, express trains, and a brand-new bar — **Bar Nearby** — that opened in a former mechanics shop on Whitehorse Road late 2024. A local boozer in what was a dry zone for nearly a century. You love to see it.

But let’s talk about our real claim to fame: August 28, 2020. Remember when Yarra Valley Water told 88 suburbs to boil their tap water? We made the list. Blacktown, Box Hill, Box Hill North — all lumped together in storm-related shame. “Potential water quality issue,” they said. We boiled our billy like it was 1880. That’s the kind of camaraderie you can’t buy.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who wants to say “I live in Box Hill” without the asterisk. People who get stressed explaining that yes, we have a different postcode. Tourists who wander up from Station Street expecting a second Sky One Plaza and find… a very nice green grocer.

Who would love it? Those who know the best part of a vibrant suburb is a five-minute walk from the chaos. We’ve got the restaurants, the parks, the cinema room in the new **Local: Box Hill** apartments, and a mahjong room to settle scores in. We’re Box Hill’s back porch — less foot traffic, better parking. Don’t tell everyone.
