# Ashwood (VIC)

Slug: `ashwood-vic`

Ashwood — where even the name sounds like a polite cough in a library.

Morning routine? Here’s how it goes in our suburb: you head to the hidden cafe Hyde ’n Seek, which Broadsheet once noted was “hidden away in the streets of Ashwood” — and then you remember it was sold years ago. Now it’s just a bicycle-themed ghost haunting a brick and timber counter. Semir Elamazi brought his Moroccan-Turkish magic here first, then moved on to Ivanhoe. Classic Ashwood: the hipster training ground nobody writes home about.

We don’t have a local drama so much as a quiet existential crisis. What *is* Ashwood? It’s the zone for Ashwood High School — one of Melbourne’s top ten, apparently, if you believe the Sitchu real estate listings. The Boulevarde looks like a boulevard, but the vibe is more “time to move to Malvern East.” We’re not Richmond, we’re not Ivanhoe. We’re the placeholder suburb between Chadstone and the M1, where your GPS says “you have arrived” and you still don’t believe it.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting a coffee scene. Unless you loved Hyde ’n Seek circa 2017, you’re out of luck. Anyone who wants a pub. Anyone who wants a reason to exist on a map beyond a school zone and an ex-cafe.

But honestly, who would love it? Someone who wants to live in the Ashwood High School catchment without the price tag of Malvern. Someone who appreciates a suburb that doesn’t pretend to be cool, because it never was. We know what they say: "Ashwood is just a speed bump on High Street Road." Fair call. But the speed bump is where you stop, realise you’ve got a decent house with a backyard, and that’s enough. No drama, no fuss, just a school zone and a memory of a good coffee. 🏡☕️🚲
