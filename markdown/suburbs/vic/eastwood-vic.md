# Eastwood (VIC)

Slug: `eastwood-vic`

We know what you’re thinking — “Eastwood? Isn’t that the one with the Granny Smith apples and the midnight dumplings?” Or maybe “Oh, the Adelaide bakery that’s been around since 1992?” Nope. We’re Eastwood, VIC. The forgotten corner on Burwood East that even the data gets wrong. You search “Eastwood” and you get Clint Eastwood movies, a Sydney nightlife upgrade, and a deli on Glen Osmond Road. Meanwhile we’re here, quietly serving lamington hotcakes that’ll put you in a food coma by 10am.

Our morning ritual is simple: shuffle down to the cafe on the Burwood East corner, order the pillowy stack of hotcakes loaded with cream, coconut and jammy sweetness — because we believe brunch should occasionally require a lie-down. That’s it. That’s the drama. No Korean fried chicken rivalry, no late-night bus routes. Just a plate of hotcakes and the quiet satisfaction of knowing you’re in the Eastwood that nobody’s heard of.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who came looking for the Sydney Eastwood’s soju and street food, or the Adelaide Eastwood’s cult bakery lines. They’ll find a low-profile corner, a single standout cafe, and absolutely no nightlife. Developers won’t bother us — we’re too low-key for a “major nightlife upgrade.” Even the real estate listings are confused: one Sitchu profile raves about a “vibrant multicultural hub” — but that’s the other Eastwood. Ours is just a Burwood East corner.

But if you love a plate of hotcakes that tastes like a warm hug, no queues, no fuss, and the pleasure of being the only person in your group who’s actually been to Eastwood, VIC — come on down. We’ll be the ones on the corner, not the ones in the headlines.
