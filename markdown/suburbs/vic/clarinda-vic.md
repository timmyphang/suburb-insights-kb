# Clarinda (VIC)

Slug: `clarinda-vic`

Clarinda, VIC — we don’t have much to go on, and honestly? That’s the whole vibe.

We searched high and low for local lore and found… a Whirlpool forum post about a property on “4 Clarinda Rd, Essendon.” Classic. Even the internet can’t tell us apart from a completely different suburb. We’re the ghost in Melbourne’s southeast that the data forgot.

Back in the day (and we mean *back*), maybe there was a Christmas light display on Warwick Close that drew a crowd. The Urban List says someone’s been lighting up for over 20 years, with a nativity scene, inflatables, and a smoke machine. That’s our peak cultural calendar — and we don’t even know if it’s still going. It’s the Clarinda way: “Maybe it’s still there. Maybe not. Ask your neighbour.”

There’s no Broadsheet guide to our cafes because we don’t have cafes that Broadsheet knows about. There’s no best breakfast list, no cocktail bar roundup. We’re the suburb you drive through on your way to Chadstone, wondering if the servo does a decent pie.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a “scene.” If your idea of a big night is a Negroni at a bar with a waistcoat-wearing bartender, you will be very, very disappointed. We don’t have cocktail bars. We have a Woolies and a post office.

Who would love it here? People who genuinely enjoy not being on any list. People who appreciate a quiet street where the most exciting thing is whether the Christmas lights are on. People who pronounce “Clarinda” with two syllables (Clare-in-da, never Clare-ree-nda). We’re not for everyone. But we’re okay with that. We know who we are — or we would if anyone told us.
