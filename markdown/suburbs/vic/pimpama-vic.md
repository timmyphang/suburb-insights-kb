# Pimpama (VIC)

Slug: `pimpama-vic`

We don’t have a town centre in Pimpama. We have a drive-thru ramen window and a dream. Australia’s first, they say — Ramen Danbo on Pimpama Jacobs Well Road. You can customise your tonkotsu broth without even turning off the engine. That’s us. We’re the suburb where your lunch break is a lap of the servo car park.

Mornings here start with a prayer to the M1 gods. As Broadsheet noted, “congestion between Pimpama and Loganholme” is a daily ritual — a truck crash, a stray leaf, someone sneezes in Beenleigh and suddenly we’re parked between home and work for two hours. We don’t have a main street. We have an off-ramp and a long wait.

The big news? Urban List says Pimpama is home to Australia’s first drive-thru ramen. Ramen Danbo’s vegan tomkotsu got the nod for 2026. Meanwhile, The Fish Tank opened a shop on Soldier Bird Ave — because nothing says “Gold Coast hinterland” like battered flathead from a tank you can’t see into. And if you’re feeling adventurous, you can kayak Pimpama River and “catch koalas foraging in the eucalypts,” according to Urban List. So you can slurp noodles, queue for fish, and paddle past wildlife — all before you’ve seen another human face.

Pet drama? Time Out revealed we’re one of Queensland’s most pet-packed suburbs. Every second ute has a drooling staffy in the tray, and every third house has a “Beware of Dog” sign that’s really just a warning about the owner’s cooking.

Who would hate it here? Someone who wants to walk to a café. Someone who thinks “cultural precinct” means something other than the Ramen Danbo drive-thru and a baubles shop. A person who values footpaths over dual carriageways.

Who would love it? Any family who just finished an IKEA trip and needs a tonkotsu IV drip. Any commuter who treats the M1 like a second lounge room. Anyone who hears “Australia’s first drive-thru ramen” and thinks: finally, my car and my soul can dine together.

Welcome to Pimpama — where the ramen flows and the traffic doesn’t.
