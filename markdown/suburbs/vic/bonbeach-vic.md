# Bonbeach (VIC)

Slug: `bonbeach-vic`

**We tried to gatekeep Bonbeach, but Sitchu readers went and spilled the beans.**  

Let’s be honest – Bonbeach is the quiet kid in the class who gets perfect attendance but no one remembers. “One of Melbourne’s best-kept secrets,” the Sitchu faithful whisper, and they’re not wrong. We’re the beach that Chelsea’s older, more introverted sibling. Our sand is just as good – just less crowded, less smug, less likely to be featured on a tourism poster.  

Back in the day, you could grab a croissant at **The Little French Deli** on Nepean Highway – run by Romain, Aurelia and Gaspard, who brought baguettes to the bogan coast. That was 2012, and we still miss it. Now? The Nepean Highway offers you Macca's and a servo. But the beach remains: dog-friendly, quiet, and perfect for pretending you’re not 40 minutes from Flinders Street.  

**Our one bit of local drama?** Nobody can agree on whether Bonbeach is actually part of the Chelsea beach strip or its own independent sovereign state. The Whirlpool forums are silent on this matter – which tells you everything about the intensity of our local politics.  

**Who would hate it here?** Anyone who thrives on drama. There’s no council feud, no barista with a podcast, no pop-up wine bar. If your idea of excitement is a new bubble tea shop opening, you’ll be bored stiff. Also, anyone who relies on late-night public transport – we’re basically a ghost town after 9pm.  

**Who would love it?** People who want a beach without the performance. Dog owners who just need water bowls and a patch of sand (bonus points if you bring your own doggie biscuits). Retirees and remote workers who appreciate that “best-kept secret” energy. As Katrina Wright told Urban List, Bonbeach is one of three places she couldn’t live without – and we’re right up there with the Botanical Gardens and Yarra Valley wineries. That’s the vibe.  

Bonbeach: the suburb so low-key, even our council meetings run on time. Come for the quiet, stay because you missed the turn to Chelsea.
