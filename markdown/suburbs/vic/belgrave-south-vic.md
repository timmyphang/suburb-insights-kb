# Belgrave South (VIC)

Slug: `belgrave-south-vic`

**Belgrave South, VIC — where the Christmas trees are real, the community house is still standing, and your neighbour’s infinity pool is basically a public spectacle.**

We don’t have a café strip. We don’t have a main street. What we *do* have is a 16-metre infinity pool at 75 Altson Road, as flagged by *Sitchu* in 2026, and honestly? That’s everyone’s weekend daydream. “Should we go for a bushwalk or just stand outside that fence and pretend we can afford an agent?” You know the vibes.

Come November, we become the undisputed Christmas tree capital of the hills. Ports Christmas Tree Farm on Belgrave-Hallam Road — operating since 1997 — transforms into a pine-scented pilgrimage site. *Sitchu* called it “a place designed to spark tradition.” We call it “the reason your car smells like a forest until February.” And yes, we go every year. Even if we don’t buy a tree, we’ll stand there, breathe deep, and pretend we’re living in a Hallmark movie.

The *Belgrave South Community House* is our cultural heartbeat. As *Time Out* described it, it’s “playing host to educational and leisure activities for children and adults.” Translation: macrame classes, toddler playgroups, and the occasional heated debate about whether the R.U.T.H run should start at 8am or 9am. (For the uninitiated, that’s the fundraising run for Mums of the Hills — and yes, there’s a best-dressed prize.)

Our local drama? Well, we don’t have much, which is the drama. The nutritionist Megs moved here from Emerald and now we all know way too much about gut health. The 1st Belgrave South Scouts movie fundraiser is basically the social event of the season. And if you miss the Christmas tree drop-off at Belgrave Library, you’ll have to hear about it from Karen at the Community House.

Who would *hate* it here? Anyone who needs a 24-hour convenience store, a bar that stays open past 9pm, or a street that isn’t on a 45-degree incline. Also: people who think “Christmas tree farm” is just a novelty, not a lifestyle.

But who would *love* it? Families who want the Dandenongs without the weekend tourist traffic. People who drive a station wagon, own a Tupperware container from 1995, and believe that a real Christmas tree is worth the pine needles in every crevice. Welcome to Belgrave South — we’ve got an infinity pool we can’t afford, a community house that holds it together, and a Christmas tree farm that’s basically our town square. Come for the pines, stay for the passive-aggressive newsletter. 🎄🏠
