# Deanside (VIC)

Slug: `deanside-vic`

Deanside, VIC 🏡 — where your address sounds like a developer’s fever dream and your commute is a personality trait.  

We’ll be honest: most of Melbourne still thinks we’re a paddock with a bus stop. And they’re not entirely wrong. But as Junkee so kindly pointed out, people are moving here because the cost of a CBD shoebox equals a two-storey townhouse in our postcode. The catch? “Sacrifice hours to go to work every day” is our local greeting.  

Back in the before times, we were called Plumpton. Then Kororoit. Now Deanside. On Whirlpool forums, people genuinely debated whether to pick Fraser Rise or Deanside like they were choosing between two gas station pies. We’re an estate, not a suburb — and boy, do we wear it.  

There’s no corner café because we haven’t aged enough to grow one. No heritage pub, no “local” anything. Our main street is a construction sign. Our community vibe is half-built fences and “when’s the park coming?” WhatsApp groups.  

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “a short stroll to amenities” doesn’t involve a 40-minute hike to a bottle-o that’s definitely closing early today.  

Who would love it? Someone who understands that a home with a second toilet is worth every minute of that Western Freeway crawl. Someone who looks at a dirt lot and sees “future parkland” – or at least knows the developer’s brochure promised it.  

We’re Deanside. We’re still finding our feet. But at least our shoes are affordable.
