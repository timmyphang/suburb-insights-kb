# Canterbury (VIC)

Slug: `canterbury-vic`

We’re the suburb where the old post office now dishes out black pork curry, and the most heated debate isn’t about the Sri Lankan takeover – it’s about the bamboo.

A Saturday morning in Canterbury: you queue at **The Maling Room** (heritage building, 1908 post office, still has the stained-glass “Money Order” window) for a coffee roasted upstairs. Andrew Lew’s Synesso machines steam milk at exactly 65 degrees – order it hotter and you’ll get a look. Then a wander down **Maling Road**, an Edwardian strip so perfectly preserved it could be a film set. Pop into **Lennox Wine** (opened 2023, exclusively Aussie list, grilled peaches with burrata) – owners Gosia and James are usually behind the bar, pouring something natural and telling you where the grapes were grown.

Now for the drama. **Canterbury Council banned bamboo planting.** Yes, the very thing that gives our tree-lined boulevards that “leafy Melbourne neighbourhood” vibe was almost outlawed. Good luck keeping your neighbour’s *Fargesia* from staging a hostile takeover over the property line. The real tension though? Whether the old post office’s conversion into **The Havelock Place** (new Sri Lankan spot, black pork curry, pan rolls, Lion beer) counts as gentrification or just a very civilised upgrade. Locals are split: half love the hibachi-grilled sous-vide lamb; the other half miss the stamp counter.

You’d absolutely *hate* it here if your idea of a good time involves neon signs, kebabs after 10pm, or a house under $2 million. The streets are so quiet you can hear a hedge trimmer from three blocks away. And if you dare park on Canterbury Road during school pickup – forget it.

But you’ll *love* it if your idea of heaven is a heritage-listed coffee roastery, a wine bar where the owner remembers your name, and a community that went all-in on a new curry joint in a 1908 post office. We’re posh, we’re leafy, and we’ve got bamboo drama. Don’t hate us because we’re beautiful – hate us because we banned your running bamboo.
