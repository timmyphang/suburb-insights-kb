# Vermont South (VIC)

Slug: `vermont-south-vic`

Welcome to Vermont South, where the most dramatic thing to happen in the last five years was a hot air balloon crash landing and we thought it was an earthquake. Yes, a local resident told 3AW they “mistook the crash landing for an earthquake” — and honestly, that’s the most excitement we’ve had since the 2020 water boil alert made us realise our tap water could give us a “gastro type of illness.” We boiled everything. For weeks. Even the ice cubes.

Our morning routine? You’ll find us at the Vermont South Learning Centre playground at 9am with a pram and a coffee from… well, we’ll let you know if a cafe ever gets a write-up in Broadsheet. For now it’s the Neighbourhood House cuppa or nothing. We’re the suburb that launched a thousand Neighbours tours — literally, the fictional Erinsborough is here. Adele once bumped into the cast while visiting, and we still talk about it like it happened yesterday.

The drama? We’ve got two camps: the HFC NBN haves and the ADSL have-nots. The Whirlpool forums are our town square. “Planned with no date” is our catchphrase. And the 75 tram? It’s the only way out, taking us from our leafy cul-de-sacs all the way to Docklands — but only from January, apparently, and only as a trial. We’ll believe it when we see it.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who wants a bar that’s not attached to a bowling club, a gig that’s not a primary school fete, or a public transport service that doesn’t require a spreadsheet. Also anyone who doesn’t own a bubbler for emergency water boiling.

But who would love it? Families who think “quiet” is a compliment. Kids who want to grow up where the biggest scandal is a rogue balloon. And anyone who secretly loves that we’re the Melbourne suburb that’s one big film set for a TV show that never ends. We’re not exciting. We’re Vermont South. And we’ve got a Learning Centre with a proper classroom. What more do you need?
