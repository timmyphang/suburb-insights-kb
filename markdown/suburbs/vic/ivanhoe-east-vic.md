# Ivanhoe East (VIC)

Slug: `ivanhoe-east-vic`

Here in Ivanhoe East, we don’t have a main street. We have a “village”. Lower Heidelberg Road, a strip that Time Out called “contemporary with village charm” back in 2012, which in local terms means an eyecare shop, a pharmacy, a skin clinic, and a Foodworks that proudly announced it’d be open 8am-8pm on Australia Day. That’s our nightlife sorted.

And yet, somehow, we’ve got a wine bar that a proper CBD chef reckons has “nailed the vibe”. Vinoshis, on that same humble strip, is where Chef Elias Salomonsson (he of Circl and ex-Vue de Monde) sinks Sunday afternoons with cheese and charcuterie. He calls it his local. You know what that means? A guy who made smoked eel tart at a 1500-bottle wine house comes to Ivanhoe East for a drink. We’ll take the win.

But let’s not kid ourselves – we also have All Are Welcome. The bakery that welcomed everyone except its own staff, allegedly racking up $360,000 in unpaid super and leave, plus $1.4 million to the ATO. Founder Boris Portnoy bought it back under a new entity called Landrance, because nothing says fresh start like a name change. The drama was so juicy even the ABC got involved. Now we just walk past the Ivanhoe East outpost and wonder if our cinnamon scroll is subsidised by someone’s unpaid entitlements.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting a latte with a side of buzz. Our “cafes” are basically the Foodworks deli counter. If you need a fika, you’re driving to Butter Days in the city. The excitement peaked when East Ivanhoe Pharmacy offered stylish frames for your eye exam.

Who would love it? The kind of person who calls a 2012 shopping strip “charming” and means it. People who want a wine bar with zero queue, a bakery with a backstory, and a GP who can also fix your glasses. We’re not trying to be anything – and frankly, that’s the vibe.
