# Frankston North (VIC)

Slug: `frankston-north-vic`

Back in the day we’d haul the tiny feet down to the Frankston North Community Centre like it was mission control for small people. Playgroup Victoria used to list the place for a reason: a purpose-built playgroup room, an enclosed playground and — yes, believe it — a venue with a cafe, library and programs. It felt like someone finally designed childhood with a sensible floorplan.

We’re the kind of suburb that doesn’t court hype. As the Playgroup Victoria archive and even the PlayConnect+ sites PDF once showed, this was where the pushchairs parked and the community swapped advice about naps and nonsense. No pretence, just a plain old set-up that actually works when you’ve got a toddler with opinions.

Who would hate it here? People who need their suburbs to come with a six-stage Instagram narrative and a million new openings every month. We’re not about that flash noise. We’re fine being the reliable option on a map: community centre, play spaces, programs that keep the tiny dictators entertained.

Who would love it? Families who want somewhere that remembers children exist and made room for them — literally. Parents who want a covered playgroup room, a safe enclosed playground and a library for the post-meltdown escape will find it quietly excellent. We’ll keep the sandpit, the colouring in and the sensible espresso stand in the old cafe (as Playgroup Victoria used to note) — and if that sounds like heaven, welcome to Frankston North.
