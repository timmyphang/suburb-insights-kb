# Ascot Vale (VIC)

Slug: `ascot-vale-vic`

We reckon Ascot Vale is Melbourne’s most underrated identity crisis — and we wouldn’t have it any other way.

A Saturday morning here goes something like this: you queue at the new Gordon Street Bakery on Union Street (April 2026, thank you very much) for a croissant that took 72 hours and a cheeky splash of rum. Then you wander past Little Byrd — no obvious signage, just a wooden bird box in the window — and wonder if it’s still the same travel-inspired cafe owners Jordan and Effie keep reinventing every January. By lunch, you’re debating whether to hit Ascot Food & Wine for lobster rolls with Kewpie mayo (yes, the one Megan Gale co-owns now) or to grab a Double Up and Down from The Pickle and The Patty, a tiny burger spot that started life as a food truck at The Ascot Lot.

Speaking of The Ascot Lot — that permanent food truck park on a car park has seen more festivals than our social calendar. Poutine Festival, Gnocchi Festival, Bottomless Drinks ’n’ Doggos… we’ve stuffed ourselves silly in that gravel pit. And if that’s not enough, you can always order a bottle of rum with your Red Rooster delivery from the Ascot Vale store. Class.

The real local drama? We can’t decide if we’re a sleepy old milk bar suburb or a bistro destination. Ascot Food & Wine spent seven years trying to get a liquor licence, then six lockdowns, and now it’s serving grown-up Viennetta. Meanwhile, Motorhome Majestic came and went (RIP those spiked milkshakes), but a ramen master from Tokyo just opened Cafe Ogawa here in 2026. Make up your mind, Ascot Vale!

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a proper nightclub or a shopping strip that isn’t half hardware stores. The traffic on Mount Alexander Road will test your patience, and the “nightlife” is basically The Laurel Hotel’s renovated beer garden.

Who would love it? Young families who want the Maribyrnong River Trail on their doorstep, foodies who appreciate a bakery that bakes all day, and dog owners who never miss a bottomless brunch with their pup. It’s not fancy, but it’s ours.
