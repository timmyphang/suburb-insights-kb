# Elsternwick (VIC)

Slug: `elsternwick-vic`

We know what you’re thinking – “Elsternwick? Isn’t that just the suburb you drive through to get to Elwood?” And yeah, fair call. But pull over for a sec, because this pocket 9km south-east of the CBD has quietly become Melbourne’s most competitive Italian-gnocchi-charcoal-chicken-sushi battleground.

Let’s set the scene: it’s Saturday morning. You head to **Elster** – a European bistro in a former 1930s service station, because nothing says “suburban convenience” like a Bloody Mary with your steak frites at 11am. Owner Julien Moussi clearly wants you to feel like you’re in Tuscany, but the Tudor architecture roots and black countertops scream “IKEA showroom meets old pub.” Still, you take a panna cotta from **48h Pizza e Gnocchi Bar** next door to **Classic Cinemas** (built 1889, theatre since 1911 – yes, older than your nanna’s curtains). The gnocchi is “della nonna” style, gluten-free and blackened with charcoal. Nonna would be horrified. We love it.

After the movie, you pop into **After the Tears** for a pre- or post-film drink, then wander to **Bang Bang at the Rifle Club** – an Asian-inspired spot literally within shooting distance of Elsternwick Station. The Riverland Group’s Richie Ludbrook and Dave Sharry clearly thought “what if we put bottomless brunch in a former rifle club?” The answer: a two-hour feast with old-school charm and new-era polish. Perfect for when you need to settle a bet about who’s the best shot.

But here’s the real drama: we’ve got a **Hinoki Pantry** outpost now, called **Shoten**, opened at the end of 2025 on Glen Huntly Road. The north side has had its revered Japanese grocer-sushi-bar on Smith Street forever, and they finally gave in to “regulars pleading for something on the other side of town.” The locals here act like it’s the second coming of yuzu kosho. The made-to-order makizushi? Divine. The fact that you can buy Mino ware ceramics next to your takeaway chirashi? That’s peak Elsternwick – where every shopping trip feels like an interior design Pinterest board.

Who would HATE it here? Anyone who thinks a suburb should have a single identity. Elsternwick can’t decide if it’s a sleepy bayside retirement village or the latest foodie frontier. One day you’re at **Chargrill Charlie’s** for a cult-followed charcoal chicken, the next you’re at **Shoten** debating the merits of shiso Genovese sauce. It’s confusing. Also, if you don’t like walking past pottery classes at **Céramiques** while trying to get your morning coffee from **One Square Espresso** – well, stay in St Kilda.

Who would LOVE it? You, if you appreciate a suburb that’s old enough to have a 1911 cinema, competitive enough to have a world-ranked pizza (48h was named one of Oceania’s best), and weird enough to have a former VFL stadium (**Elsternwick Park**) hosting amateur footy while locals sip Aperol Spritzes at **Elster**. It’s the Goldilocks zone: not too close to the city, not too far from the beach, and just confusing enough to keep things interesting.

Come for the charcoal gnocchi, stay for the existential question: “Am I in a European piazza or a Glen Huntly Road carpark?” Either way, there’s a drink at **Antique Bar** with your name on it. 🍝🎬🇮🇹
