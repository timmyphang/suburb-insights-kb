# Capel Sound (VIC)

Slug: `capel-sound-vic`

We're Capel Sound, formerly Rosebud West — the Mornington Peninsula's most successful rebrand since someone tried calling TAC ads "edgy public service announcements." They say we changed our name to sound fancier. They're not wrong. But here's the thing: it didn't work.

The Whirlpool forums had a field day when we dropped "Rosebud West" and picked up Capel Sound in a posh voice. "Maybe next year they can recruit a team from Rosebud West....err sorry, I mean the newly renamed *Capel Sound*," one user quipped. And honestly, fair call. You can dress us up in a new postcode, but we're still the suburb where the council closed the foreshore camping grounds until February 2021 because "shared toilet blocks are a COVID risk" — while the non-Shire-managed sites like ours were meant to open by December 2020. We're the wild frontier of the Peninsula camping scene.

Back in the day, our big local drama was whether you could pitch a tent without sharing a shower block with strangers. The Mornington Peninsula Shire CEO John Baker said it wasn't feasible. We said: just don't shower. That's the Capel Sound way.

These days, you'll find us at the Capel Sound Community Hub (run by Playgroup Victoria since 2025) or walking the Capel Sound Bay Trail — a dreamy stroll that Sitchu called one of the best walks on the Peninsula. But let's be real: we're not Millionaires' Walk in Sorrento. We're the walk where you step over someone's abandoned camping chair and wonder if that's a bong or a Nalgene bottle.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a proper foreshore campsite open before February 2021. Anyone who expects their suburb's name change to actually fool anyone. Anyone who thinks "Capel Sound" sounds like a luxury eco-retreat and arrives to find a community hub, a bay trail, and a whole lot of people who still call it Rosebud West.

Who would love it here? The kind of local who knows that renaming yourself doesn't change your soul. We're still the underdog of the Peninsula, still the first to cop a joke, still camping with minimal amenities and maximum character. And honestly? We wouldn't have it any other way. Welcome to Capel Sound — don't forget your own toilet paper.
