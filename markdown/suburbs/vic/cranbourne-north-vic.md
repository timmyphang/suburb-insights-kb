# Cranbourne North (VIC)

Slug: `cranbourne-north-vic`

We’ve got one thing in Cranbourne North, and it’s not a decent café or a train that runs on time—it’s the Christmas lights on Middlesex Court. Every December, we roll out tens of thousands of coloured bulbs, a nativity scene, inflatables, and a bubble machine that doubles as a smoke alarm for the neighbours. People drive from as far as Dandenong to see our display. They get out, take photos, and then get back in their cars and head straight home. That’s the Cranbourne North experience: all spectacle, zero nightlife.

During the day, you’ll find us at Funtopia—one of Australia’s largest indoor play centres. It’s a maze of climbing adventures and huge play zones, which is code for “kids screaming while parents scroll through real estate listings wondering if they can still afford a house in Cranbourne North.” Back in the day (and by that we mean a Pedestrian TV article from the before times), our median house price jumped 14.9% in three months—from $451k to $518k. Now it’s probably the price of a small car. We’re not sure, because nobody’s asked us since.

Petty local drama? Try the Leaptel internet outage that had a Whirlpool user in Cranbourne North (3KEY Keysborough POI) screaming at 9am because customer service didn’t open until then. “That’s helpful, not!” they typed, and we all nodded from our dead wifi. We’re a suburb that needs a solid connection to look up other suburbs to visit.

If you hate peace and quiet and long drives to anywhere interesting, you’d hate it here. If you love Christmas lights, indoor trampolines, and the gentle hum of a housing estate where the biggest controversy is whether the smoke machine is too foggy—welcome, we’ve been expecting you.
