# Clyde (VIC)

Slug: `clyde-vic`

You know that special feeling when you’ve just moved into your brand-new Clyde estate, the paint’s still drying, and you sit down to stream some Netflix… only for the Opticomm connection to drop out for the third time that hour? Yeah, we know it too. Welcome to Clyde, where the most exciting local drama is a Whirlpool forum thread about Aussie Broadband dropouts in Clyde North. “Constant dropouts & extremely slow performance” — that’s our civic anthem.

Back in the day (the day being sometime in the last couple of years), someone had the bright idea to open X-Golf Clyde North. And honestly? That’s pretty much it. One world-class golf simulator to serve an entire suburb of new houses. We’re not saying we’re a barren cultural wasteland, but when a virtual putting green is our headline attraction, you know the local cafes haven’t quite arrived yet. If you want a flat white, you’re driving. If you want dinner out, you’re driving. If you want any evidence that this isn’t just a sea of concrete slabs and “coming soon” signs… you guessed it.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who thinks a suburb should have a single, walkable shop or a tree planted before 2029. Also, anyone who relies on a stable internet connection for work — the Opticomm dropouts will have you longing for the dial-up era.

Who would love it here? People who genuinely love the concept of a golf simulator and don’t mind driving 20 minutes for a coffee. Also, anyone who dreams of one day owning a house where the streets are named after every possible shade of grey. We’re not there yet, but by golly, we’ve got the NBN complaints down pat.
