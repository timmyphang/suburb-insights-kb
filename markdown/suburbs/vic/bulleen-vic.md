# Bulleen (VIC)

Slug: `bulleen-vic`

Welcome to Bulleen, where the most exciting thing that’s happened since the Krispy Kreme drive-thru started selling doggie doughnuts is a multimillionaire giving away houses on reality TV. That’s right—we’ve got a Hot-Dwelling Auction™ controversy courtesy of Adrian Portelli and *My Reno Rules*, with the local homes allegedly not going to auction after all. Chaos in suburbia: someone tell the neighbours to put the kettle down.

Our mornings are a study in contrasts. You can swing by **The French Lettuce** on Templestowe Road for a vanilla slice once voted best in Melbourne (they’re still proud, we’re still smug), or grab a box of glazed for 16c if your birthday’s in September. Then it’s off to **Heide Museum of Modern Art** at 7 Templestowe Road to stare at Man Ray vs Max Dupain, or learn about the Yaluk Langa community garden. We go from “I’ll have the Basque cheesecake from Sugar Nomad” to “quick, find a possum fur ball for Marn Grook” in the space of a single free community day.

But let’s be real: for a suburb that’s “quiet, safe and family-friendly” (cheers, Sitchu), we’ve got a surprising amount of artistic pretension. Heide brings in the bohemian crowd who wander through *Blak In-Justice: Incarceration and Resilience* and then spend 20 minutes photographing the sculpture garden. Meanwhile, the rest of us are just trying to get to Bulleen Park to watch women’s footy without getting stuck behind a Prado on its way to the dealer’s wife.

Would you hate it here? Absolutely, if you’re after nightlife. Our “nightlife” is a Krispy Kreme drive-thru and the occasional gallery opening that ends by 8pm. You’ll be bored senseless.

Who’d love it? People who want a three-bedroom house with a backyard, a patisserie that does wedding cakes, and a museum that talks to Man Ray. We’re not flashy, but we’ve got a vanilla slice that will change your life—and a mild real estate scandal to keep the gossip mill humming. Welcome to Bulleen: where the art is world-class and the drama is strictly domestic.
