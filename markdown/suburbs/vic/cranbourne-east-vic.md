# Cranbourne East (VIC)

Slug: `cranbourne-east-vic`

**Cranbourne East, VIC — 3977**

We are the fastest-growing suburb in Australia and we have the traffic, the housing estates, and the 63 child care centres to prove it. Every day, 350 people move to Melbourne and apparently half of them pull into Cranbourne East and say "this looks like a good spot to raise my three children and also never leave my car again."

We don't have a cafe culture. We have a "we used the concrete playground article to justify building more concrete" culture. When Broadsheet or whoever comes here, they write about the Royal Botanic Gardens Cranbourne, which is lovely — but it's the one place the developers haven't got to yet. The rest of us are out here on Race Drive, watching the pony club transform into a townhouse complex.

The local drama? It's all Whirlpool forum level: "Cranbourne East vs Officer — which estate has better land price?" The answer is Hunt Club. If you've lived in the Hunt Club for two years, you know it's the "best kept secret in the South East," but you also know the secret is that you will never be able to do a left turn out of your driveway again.

A misfit here? Anyone who doesn't have a pre-schooler. We have 63 child care centres, 58 after-school care services, and a new open space on Main Street and Race Drive that used to be the Casey Safety Village — perfect for your kid to practice their scooter on the old road markings while you question every life choice that led you to a suburb where the main event is a botanic garden that's technically in Cranbourne proper. If you're a single person without children, you are basically a tourist.

Who would love it? Anyone who wants to live in a suburb where the most exciting new development is a dog park coming in 2021-ish, and where the local community centre is called "TeamKids Casey Grammar" and it's basically a classroom full of flags. You'll love it if your idea of a big night out is arguing on Whirlpool about whether Cranbourne East or Officer has better land price. Welcome home, you beautiful, child-obsessed, traffic-crushed legend. You're going to need that dog park.
