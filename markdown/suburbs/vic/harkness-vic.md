# Harkness (VIC)

Slug: `harkness-vic`

**Harkness, VIC — the suburb that made the news exactly once, and it was for having 79 active COVID cases back in 2020.** That’s our stat. Postcode 3337, rounding out the top 20 hardest-hit postcodes in Victoria, alongside Kurunjang, Melton, Toolern Vale and Melton West. We were a footnote in a DHHS spreadsheet. And honestly? That’s the most attention we’ve ever wanted.

Every other time you hear “Harkness” it’s either a chef in Bendigo (Jay Harkness, doing classical French at Alium Dining in a heritage bank), a Marvel witch (Agatha Harkness, winking her way through *WandaVision*), or a *Doctor Who* immortal (Captain Jack Harkness). Meanwhile, our Harkness has no restaurants, no bars, no cafes — not even a servo that made the data. We’re a housing estate on the western fringe that exists so estate agents can say “close to Melton” without actually saying Melton.

If you live here, you know the drill: you commute, you take the kids to the park, you watch the housing developments go up, and you quietly appreciate that nobody’s writing a Broadsheet article about us. There’s no hipster listening bar, no spiritual sequel to a Northcote bakery. There’s just… 3337.

**Who would hate it here?** Anyone who expects a suburb to have a *thing*. A main street. A local legend. A café called Ritual Espresso. We don’t have any of that. If you need validation from Melbourne-centric media, Harkness will disappoint you.

**Who’d love it?** People who want to live in a place where your biggest moment was a 2020 health statistic. Where your name is shared with a warlock, a witch, and a fine dining chef — none of whom actually live here. Where you can say “I’m from Harkness” and watch them google it.

Low-key. Quiet. And proud of it. 🤫
