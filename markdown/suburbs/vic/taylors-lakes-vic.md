# Taylors Lakes (VIC)

Slug: `taylors-lakes-vic`

We know what you’re thinking. Taylors Lakes – that big roundabout on the way to Watergardens where the only traffic jam is between the Aldi trolley and the Cannoleria cart. We are here to defend nothing, but we are also not wrong.

Our Saturday routine is a prayer: can we get a park near the new Adore Beauty at Watergardens before the Tim Tams run out? Yes, they’re opening a second store here (Broadsheet confirmed it). Meanwhile, The Marketplace promised us a $50 million food precinct back in 2020 with 8bit burgers, Old Man Pho, and a cannoli pop-up. It’s attached to the centre, but we’re still waiting for that “vibrant” street-food vibe. We do have a Butcher Club Direct, which says “local family since the 60s” – but most of our houses are from the 80s and needing a reno (Whirlpool knows).

Now for the petty drama we don’t have to invent. Pedestrian TV named Professionals Taylors Lakes the most cooked landlord of 2023 for sending tenants a friendly email calling Christmas “eviction season.” We aren’t making that up. It’s why we still don’t wave at the real estate agents.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks a trip to the CBD should take 20 minutes. It’s 20km out, and if you try a 4:30am Uber to the airport, you’ll be on hold from Taylors Lakes to Flemington before anyone answers (Whirlpool, again). Also, if you’re a fan of trendy inner-north co-working spaces – we don’t have one. We have a Dick Smith that won’t price match.

But who would love it? People who remember 2020 lockdowns when our postcode (3038) was a hotspot and we couldn’t even go to Niddrie. We got through it with a cannoli in one hand and a Tim Tam in the other. This place is for folks who want a big backyard, a house from the 80s that they can slowly fix up, and a shopping centre that’s trying really hard. And if you need a laugh, just remember: it’s always eviction season somewhere.
