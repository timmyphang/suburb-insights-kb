# Oak Park (VIC)

Slug: `oak-park-vic`

We don’t have a lot going on here in Oak Park, and we’re fine with that. The biggest local controversy? Whether the orange-and-blue slide at the Oak Park Sports and Aquatic Centre is thrilling enough to justify the 15-minute queue on a 35-degree Saturday. (Spoiler: it is, barely.) Back in 2021, Time Out called it the place to be, and we still hold onto that like a medal.

Our morning routine is simple: a run or pram-walk along the Moonee Ponds Creek trail, then a detour to Francis Winifred on Winifred Street. It’s bright, buzzy, with a green-tiled frontage that shouts “I’m a proper Melbourne cafe.” You order the cilbir or shakshuka, sip an Allpress latte spiked with beetroot if you’re feeling fancy, and pretend you’re not just 20 minutes from the city. The house-made Snickers slice is non-negotiable. This place has been our pride since 2023, and we’ll milk it for years.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who craves nightlife, a tram every five minutes, or a street art tour. We have none of that. Our idea of a big Friday is sliding into the aquatic centre then grabbing a takeaway from the cafe before it closes at 3pm.

Who would love it? Cyclists, pram-pushers, runners, and anyone who thinks “a popular detour” is the highest compliment. We’re the suburb that makes Moonee Ponds look edgy. And we’re totally okay with that.
