# Bittern (VIC)

Slug: `bittern-vic`

**Bittern, VIC — Postcode 3918, population: roughly 12 humans, 400 birds, and a winery that’s technically in Balnarring.**

Friday arvo in Bittern and we’re staring at the letterbox wondering if the mail has been cancelled again. Our claim to fame? We share a name with a shy, swamp-dwelling bird that once had a run in Australia’s *Bird of the Year* comp. The *Australasian bittern* didn’t win, but neither do we — and that’s fine by us.

Back in the day, Quealy Winemakers on Bittern-Dromana Road put us on the map. Kevin McCarthy and Kathleen Quealy — legends of the Peninsula — started making pinot grigio here before selling up and starting again in ’06. They do organic wines, Italian varietals, and pét-nat that’ll make a city slicker weep. Even had a Pizza Luna pop-up residency this summer. But let’s be honest: the cellar door is technically in **Balnarring**. We just let them borrow our name so they sound rustic.

The good news: we’ve got a sandal named after us. Bared Footwear’s *Bittern Sandal* — a chunky fisherman’s number that “broke the internet” in 2014. That’s our level of excitement: a shoe that peaked before Instagram Reels existed.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting a cafe with oat milk art. We don’t have a coffee shop. We don’t have a pub. We have a silo and a sense of quiet so deep you can hear your neighbour sneeze from two paddocks away. Tourists drive through looking for the winery, see our single street sign, and do a U-turn back to Red Hill.

Who would love it? Anyone who’s over the Mornington Peninsula’s “discover the hinterland” aesthetic. You want a vineyard tasting without the crowds? Go to Quealy. You want to tell your friends you live in a suburb named after a bird that hides in wetlands? That’s us. We’re the anti-hype. Bittern — where the wine is world-class and the postcode is just a number.
