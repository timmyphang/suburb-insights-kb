# Deer Park (VIC)

Slug: `deer-park-vic`

Deer Park, VIC — we’ve finally got a train station that’s won a design award. Yes, Deer Park Station just scored at the 2025 National Landscape Architecture Awards. It’s the nicest thing to happen to the 3023 postcode since COVID taught us all what “hardest hit” really means. (177 active cases back in 2020, thanks for the data.)

Our Saturday routine: you skip the station’s fancy architecture because you’re driving your Geely EX5 — the new Chinese EV you pre‑ordered from that salesperson at the Deer Park dealer. “Test drives in 2–3 weeks,” they promised. You’re still waiting, but at least the charger installation guy from the Whirlpool forum finally showed up.

Breakfast is at Latin Foods and Wine — back in the day it was La Morenita in Sunshine, then they moved here and now it’s a whole empanada‑pisco‑chimichurri‑burger empire. The menu’s on a whiteboard, and you’ll spend 10 minutes deciding between baked or fried. Worth it.

Petty local drama: There’s that bloke on a bike who’s been mugging people in the underground carpark with a plastic gun. Threatened a 51‑year‑old woman on March 16. Cyclists — ruining our reputation one plastic weapon at a time.

You’d hate it here if you need a train that doesn’t bottleneck at Deer Park West junction. Every V/Line passenger knows the rage. Also, if you want a nice park — we don’t have one. The only “deer park” is in New Zealand.

But you’d love it here if you appreciate a suburb that owns its reputation. We’ve got a killer Latin grocer, a design‑award station, and a community that survived the worst of it. Just keep an eye out for cyclists with fake guns. Welcome to Deer Park.
