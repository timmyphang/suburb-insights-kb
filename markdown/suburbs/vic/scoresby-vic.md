# Scoresby (VIC)

Slug: `scoresby-vic`

Scoresby — where our main claim to fame is that we share a road name with a bunch of bakeries in Bayswater. Yes, Scoresby Road runs through us, but the moment you mention Dröm Bakery or The Hatter and the Hare, everyone assumes you mean the ones in Bayswater. Classic.

A Saturday morning here? You’re either lining up at 7am on Scoresby Road for a spherical choc hazelnut ‘Moonrock’ pastry (that’s from Dröm, 1/19 Scoresby Road — we’ll claim it as ours even if the postcode says Bayswater), or you’re at Scoresby Primary School’s playgroup, running out of the BER building with a toddler who’s just discovered the joy of finger painting. Two very different vibes, both valid.

The local drama? It’s not a feud over a new development — it’s the safety camera on Scoresby Road, Bayswater. Every local knows exactly where the 60 sign is, 500 metres before the intersection, and whether you’re northbound or southbound changes your entire driving strategy. Whirlpool forums have been debating this for years. It’s our version of a town square.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks a suburb should have its own destination cafe — not just be the quiet backdrop for other suburbs’ foodie pilgrimages. Also, anyone who doesn’t enjoy watching Instagrammers take 47 photos of a crescent croissant while you’re just trying to get a pie.

Who would love it? People who appreciate a suburb that doesn’t try too hard. We’ve got a playgroup, a primary school, a safety camera, and a road that leads to some of Melbourne’s best pastries — even if they don’t technically belong to us. We’re the reliable friend who lets Bayswater borrow our street name. And we’re fine with that. Mostly.
