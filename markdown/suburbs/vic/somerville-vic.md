# Somerville (VIC)

Slug: `somerville-vic`

You know you’ve made it as a Somerville local when your suburb’s biggest claim to fame is being the hometown of a MAFS contestant. Yes, Melissa Rawson put us on the map when Bryce Ruthven was spotted lurking around our streets. We’ve been trying to live that down ever since.

Our Saturday morning routine? A leisurely stroll down to Yaringa Boat Harbour, where Cptn Jack’s at 1 Lumeah Road serves up the kind of seafood chowder that makes you forget you’re only 45 minutes from the city. No one’s rushing here. The boats bob. The dog walkers nod. The only drama is whether the seagulls will stage a takeover.

Speaking of drama — the locals still whisper about that time a MAFS camera crew was spotted near the boat ramp. We’re a quiet bunch, but we’ll never forgive the disruption to our parking flow. As Broadsheet noted, a colossal fire once closed both sides of Somerville and Paramount roads — but honestly, the MAFS invasion was more traumatic.

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting a buzzing cafe scene, an art gallery, or a barista who knows your name and your oat milk preference. We have one pub, one boat harbour, and a postcode that gets confused with the one for Somerville in Massachusetts. Our “culture” is watching the tide come in.

Who would love it? People who want to own a house without selling a kidney. People who think “nightlife” is a twilight cruise on Western Port. People who look at a map and think, “Yes, somewhere between Mornington and nowhere looks perfect.”

We’re not fancy. We’re not famous. But we have Cptn Jack’s, we have a boat ramp, and we had a MAFS star. That’s enough.
