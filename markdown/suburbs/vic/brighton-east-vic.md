# Brighton East (VIC)

Slug: `brighton-east-vic`

Brighton East – we’re Brighton’s quieter cousin who brings a blanket to Landcox Park instead of a towel to the beach. That handsome 10-acre spot? It’s our community lounge room. You’ll find us there on a Saturday, dodging kids on the playground, pretending not to notice the duck pond drama.

Our morning routine is a pilgrimage down Hawthorn Road. First, Primrose Hill Deli at 765B for Syndicate coffee and hummingbird cake – back when it was the neighbourhood’s quiet gem. These days, Rustica’s opened a 125-seat bakery just over the line in Brighton proper (corner of Hampton and Pine, if you’re counting). We act like it’s ours. Let us have this.

Then there’s RawLove Superfoods at 760 Hawthorn Rd – because nothing says “Brighton East mum” like a raw chef’s certified holistic breakfast. And when we need a proper burger, The Royale Brothers has been proclaimed some of the best in Melbourne. Soft milk bun, charred beef, oozy Kraft cheese – yes, we still eat cheese that comes individually wrapped. We’re not savages.

Come Halloween, Carpenter Street transforms into a living haunted village. Every house goes full horror. It’s the one time our quiet streets get more attention than the beach.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who wants a nightlife, a beachfront apartment, or a flat white without a side of real estate talk. We’re all school zones (Gardenvale Primary, Brighton Secondary) and “tranquil pockets” – as the Sitchu listings never tire of telling you.

Who would love it? Families who want a 10-acre park with barbeques and a duck pond, a bookstore that delivers for free (Benn’s Books in Bentleigh will come to us), and a community that goes all out for Halloween. We’re not flashy. We’re just the suburb where you can still buy a Samsung TV from The Good Guys and call it a big weekend.

Welcome to Brighton East. Bring a picnic rug. The ducks are waiting.
