# Manor Lakes (VIC)

Slug: `manor-lakes-vic`

We’ve got a welcome sign at Manor Lakes that’s been broken so long it’s basically a permanent art installation. The water feature at Coles? Same. The company contracted to maintain the parks went broke, so now the local kids just play in the potholes. Welcome to the western fringe, where the Wendy’s arrived before the train station did.

Our morning routine: roll past the broken sign, grab a coffee at the Manor Lakes Central Shopping Centre (455 Ballan Rd, if you’re navigating), then head to the Community Learning Centre for playgroup. As the Whirlpool forum noted, “Manor Lakes is right on the Western fringe of Melbourne’s suburbs.” We know. We live it. The new railway line to Tarneit promised a station at Wyndham Vale — maybe Manor Lakes, maybe not. We’re still waiting.

The drama? Our suburb made the Broadsheet COVID data in 2020 with 94 active cases in postcode 3024. Not a flex, but at least we got a statistic. The forum crowd debates whether Truganina is better — closer to the CBD and has a station. We just remind them our land titles release like a cookie cutter. “The process pretty much down pat,” said one builder. We’re efficient, if nothing else.

Who’d hate it here? Anyone who expects a manicured estate with working water features. The welcome sign is a metaphor — if you need constant validation from infrastructure, go to Sanctuary Lakes. We’re not for you.

Who’d love it? Families who want a house and land package without the fuss. People who find charm in a broken sign because it’s *our* broken sign. And anyone who’s ever had to explain to their mates why Wendy’s chose Manor Lakes for their first Victorian store — “It’s like Taco Bell in Ellenbrook,” we say. You get it.

We’re Manor Lakes. The water feature might be on the fritz, but the playgroup’s still going strong.
