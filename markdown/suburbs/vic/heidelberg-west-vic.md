# Heidelberg West (VIC)

Slug: `heidelberg-west-vic`

Alright, let’s be real: when you tell people you’re in Heidelberg West, they still do that little eyebrow raise. “Is that the scary one?” they ask. And we say, “Used to be. Now it’s where you come for brisket and cannoli.”

On a Friday afternoon, you’ll find us lining up at 39 Mologa Road for The Bearded Pig – pitmaster Matt Antonello’s spiritual-experience brisket, smoked 16 hours over Victorian red gum. The burnt ends sell out within hours, but that just makes the hunt sweeter. As Broadsheet noted, even Matt’s partner Hayley thought he’d never shift 10 extra trays. He did.

Back in 2021, Dario Di Clerico opened Ugo Cucina Popolare at 69 Sheehan Road – a tiny panini bar in a warehouse. The porchetta roll with sticky-sweet crackling? Still haunts our dreams. Right next door, Cannoleria’s factory pumps out 500 square metres of fresh ricotta cannoli. Industrial estate? More like industrial foodie paradise.

But let’s not pretend it’s all artisan panini. The Whirlpool forums have a whole thread called “West Heidelberg – Thoughts?” with gems like “it seemed like a good future investment… now it’s beginning to make sense why they were cheaper.” And the classic: “It's heidelberg west that you have to worry about.” We own it – the housing commission blocks, the quiet streets that go real quiet at night, the dares you make with yourself to walk home after dark. It’s real.

If you need a yoga studio on every corner and a tulip festival, this ain’t it. You’ll hate the lack of a proper supermarket, the truck rumble, and that your new neighbours might be a glass-blowing studio (HotHaus) or Bounce’s indoor trampoline park on Waterdale Road. Also, the name game: Heidelberg West, West Heidelberg – even the postcode can’t decide.

But if you want a median house price under $800k (Time Out says $770k – most affordable and liveable 2024), and you’re the kind of person who gets excited about a converted van serving burnt ends, welcome. We’ll save you a spot in line.
