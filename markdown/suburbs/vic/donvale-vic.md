# Donvale (VIC)

Slug: `donvale-vic`

Donvale: where the biggest drama is either a boil-water alert or a bullet-riddled van.

We know what you think. We're that sleepy wedge between Doncaster and Warrandyte where the biggest local export is leaf blowers at 7am on a Saturday. But back in 2020, we made headlines when Yarra Valley Water put us on a boil-water warning alongside 87 other suburbs — because storms knocked out power and “untreated water entered the drinking supply.” So we spent a week boiling water like it was 1850. Very on-brand.

But the real story? Pedestrian TV reported that a body was found in a bullet-riddled van near the Mullum Mullum Tunnel, with the shooting occurring at Donvale. Emergency services got the call at 2.20am. So yes, our quiet nights occasionally involve crime scene tape and a freeway closure. We don’t make the news for our brunch scene — Broadsheet’s best Chinese restaurants guide doesn’t mention a single Donvale spot. Urban List’s best cafés guide? Also silent. Concrete Playground’s best ice cream? Not here. We’re not on the list.

So who would hate living here? Anyone who needs a “curated guide” to their Saturday. If you want a flat white with a side of influencer lighting, sorry — we’ve got the Mullum Mullam Creek trail and a petrol station. If you need a new omakase bar every week, you’ll be miserable.

Who would love it? People who value a big block with a gum tree and don’t mind that the nearest “thing to do” is a 10-minute drive. People who remember when the suburb was just farms and are fine with it still feeling like that. People who take a water warning in their stride and know exactly where the Mullum Tunnel is — because you drive through it to get anywhere decent.

Donvale, 3111. We’re not trendy. We’re not dangerous. We’re just… here. With a boiled kettle and a strong opinion about the speed cameras on Springvale Road.
