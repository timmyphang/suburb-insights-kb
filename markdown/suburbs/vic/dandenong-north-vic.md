# Dandenong North (VIC)

Slug: `dandenong-north-vic`

**Dandenong North, VIC — Where the pizza is wood-fired, the vanilla slice is a "snot block," and we're still shaking off a 2020 stat.**

We wake up on a Saturday morning in Dandenong North. The air smells of wood-fired pizza — because apparently, Pizza Strada & Bar, our suburb’s first proper pizzeria, has hit the market. They told Urban List it’s the "first wood-fired pizzeria in Dandenong North." We’re not sure if that’s a flex or a confession. Either way, it's a relaxed environment for a reasonable price. Too good to... well, you know.

Meanwhile, our culinary identity crisis is real. Urban List once tried to sell us a "snot block" (their words) from Saul's, but that’s a deli sanga joint, not here. The only vanilla slice in our postcode 3175 is the one you grab from a servo on the way to Endeavour Hills, hoping the custard doesn't explode on your lap.

Speaking of stats — we’ve got baggage. Broadsheet reminded the world in 2020 that postcode 3175 (Dandenong, Dandenong South, Dandenong North, Bangholme) was sitting pretty with 112 active COVID cases. That’s the kind of top 20 list no one wants to top. As one Broadsheet data reveal put it: we were "hardest hit." We owned it. We wore masks. We also probably ordered a lot of pizza.

**WHO’D HATE IT HERE?**
Anyone comparing us to Carlton North or Caulfield. Our "Forest-like foliage" is a bottle shop awning. Our "charming streets" involve dodging a hoon on Stud Road. The only "Rathdowne Street" we know is the one on the way to the dump.

**WHO GETS IT?**
The person who reads a Whirlpool forum thread titled "Dandenong North vs Endeavour Hills" and thinks, "That's a legitimate life choice." The family moving into Rosewood Downs because the schools are good, the park is fine, and the neighbour has a pizza oven.

We’re not flashy. We’re not leafy (sorry, Sitchu). We’re the 3175 — the suburb that got a pizza place and a pandemic mention in the same decade. Come for the gluten-free accreditation, stay because the rent is cheaper than Endeavour Hills.
