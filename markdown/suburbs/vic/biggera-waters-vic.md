# Biggera Waters (VIC)

Slug: `biggera-waters-vic`

We call it the industrial heart of the Gold Coast, but don't let the Bunnings-adjacent vibes fool you — Biggera Waters is the suburb that keeps our livers employed and our wardrobes outlet-priced.

A typical Saturday morning here? Grab a bubble tea from Cha Time at Harbour Town — because you need something to sip while you wander the 147 Brisbane Rd precinct pretending you're gonna buy a Hugo Boss suit. Then it's off to Yum Cha Cuisine for a trolley of dumplings that makes the queue at the food court worth it. If you're truly living, you swing by White Oat Distillery, where the idea was conceptualised over a beer (as all great ideas are). And if you're a beer snob? You know Black Hops II is the main production site — they make the eggnog stout that haunts your Christmas dreams.

Speaking of Black Hops: we don't like to brag, but our suburb's brewery was so beloved it nearly went under with $7.32 million in debt and had to be saved by a mysterious consortium called Black Hops Craft. Classic Biggera Waters energy — we're the underdog that crowdfunded its way to a $65 million valuation, then almost lost it all, then got rescued at the 11th hour by "passionate supporters." As Broadsheet noted, Deloitte said the business was insolvent from as early as December 2022, but we're still here, pouring hops like nothing happened. That's northern Gold Coast resilience, baby.

Who would HATE living here? Anyone who thinks "waters" in a suburb name means beachfront living. We're an industrial estate with a shopping centre and a brewery — the only water here is the condensation on your pint glass. If you want ocean views, go to Burleigh. We have Harbour Town and a distillery in a warehouse.

Who would LOVE it here? People who understand that greatness comes in industrial estates. You, the craft beer enthusiast who doesn't need a beach — just a solid hazy IPA and a discount on last season's sneakers. We're the suburb that keeps it real: boozy, bubbly, and slightly in debt. Welcome to Biggera Waters — bring your thirst and your patience for traffic on Brisbane Rd.
