# Pascoe Vale South (VIC)

Slug: `pascoe-vale-south-vic`

We’re Pascoe Vale South, and we’re the quiet achiever that’s been quietly gluten-freeing your face off since before it was cool.

On a Saturday morning, you’ll find us at Parkstone Cafe, 9 Parkstone Ave, where manager Ashlee Edwards is the dedicated doggy chef whipping up free biscuits for your pooch, while the kids tear around the greenery wall finding forgotten toys. The menu’s got a Supercalifragilisticexpialidocious nourish bowl and carrot-cake pancakes, and yeah, the Brazilian-born chef Leandro Mello is flexing. It’s all very lush and creative, just like Concrete Playground said back in 2018. But let’s be real – we’re not here for the brunch.

We’re here for the pizza. Specifically, Shop225 at 225 Melville Rd, where Lorenzo Tron and Roberto Davoli turned a neighbourhood joint into Australia’s best pizza (50 Top Pizza, 2025), climbing 27 spots in a year. It’s the first pizzeria in the country to be Coeliac Australia accredited, with a separate kitchen zone for gluten-free, and they developed their own vegan cheeses with a small non-dairy cheesemaker called Damona in Coburg. The Zio Pino (mushroom, Grana Padano, truffle oil) is the flagship, and the Verdi is not to be sniffed at. Time Out called it “arguably one of Melbourne’s best pizza joints” and it’s ranked fifth in Asia-Pacific. We’re not showing off, but we’re showing off.

The petty drama? Bar Tobala, the cocktail-forward Mexican diner that opened with all the fanfare – now permanently closed, according to Broadsheet’s June 2026 update. We used to get excited about “American diner meets roadside taco stand,” but now it’s just a memory. Meanwhile, Emil’s Cafe down the road took over the old milk bar that was the El-Khoury family childhood home, serving modern Australian-Arabic brunch and cocktails. Good on them, but we’re still trying to decide if it’s a destination or just a really nice milk bar.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks gluten-free pizza is a fad. Or anyone who can’t handle a suburb where the biggest international accolade is for a pizzeria and the second biggest is a gelato shop called Miinot (burnt fig, trust us). Also, if you don’t like dogs in cafes, you’re going to have a bad time – Parkstone has a dedicated doggy chef, for crying out loud.

Who would love it? Families who want a genuine neighbourhood vibe, coeliacs who’ve never had a real pizza, and anyone who wants to say “my local pizza place is ranked fifth in the Asia-Pacific” while eating it in a shopping strip. We’re not fancy, we’re just really, really good at making dough. And we’ll let our kids stash toys in the fake greenery while we sip an Aperol spritz at 10am. Welcome to the ‘Vale. Gluten-free optional.
