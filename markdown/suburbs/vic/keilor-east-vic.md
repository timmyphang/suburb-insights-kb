# Keilor East (VIC)

Slug: `keilor-east-vic`

We finally got our cafe. Only 15 years late, but who’s counting?

Welcome to Keilor East, where the big story is a quarry that became a lake, and the even bigger story is that we actually got the meeting place we were promised when we bought into the Valley Lake estate back in 2009. Peregrine Social Club – or Perry’s, as we call it – finally opened on the boardwalk in February 2024, serving wattleseed iced coffees and “puppacinos” while peregrine falcons judge us from the cliffs. The developers sold us on that building being a cafe one day. One day finally came. We’re not bitter. We’re just grateful we can now get a chicken parma without leaving the suburb.

But let’s be real – Keilor East’s food scene runs on a weird pendulum. There’s Houston’s Barbecue, a Texas-style joint tucked in an industrial estate that’s only open two days a week and has 12 seats outside. You want brisket? You better plan your entire week around it. Then there’s Classe 90 Pizzeria on Wyong Street, run by a former soccer player who imports everything from Italy and plays Serie A radio over the wood-fired oven. Amazing pizza, but good luck getting a table if you’re not part of the Italian football banter club.

And for the parents: Brimbank Park is where we go to pretend we’re in the country, with wallabies and wetlands and a river trail that Concrete Playground says is perfect for interval training. We mostly use it for pushing prams and nodding at other exhausted locals. The Maribyrnong Aquatic Centre is there too, if you want to swim laps while dreaming of Highpoint Shopping Centre.

Look, you’d hate it here if you need nightlife past 9pm or a bar that isn’t Perry’s Draught (brewed by Moon Dog specially for us). But if you want a suburb where the biggest drama is a cafe opening a decade and a half late, where you can rent a dress from Ivana Grande’s boutique or get a wellness hub on Centreway, and where your dog gets a puppacino and you get a view of a former quarry – welcome. Just don’t ask about the 15-year wait. We’ve moved on. Mostly.
