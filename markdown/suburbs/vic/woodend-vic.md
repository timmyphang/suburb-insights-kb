# Woodend (VIC)

Slug: `woodend-vic`

Woodend, VIC — where the Calder Highway dumps you into a town that can’t decide if it’s a country escape or an artist commune with a pie addiction.

We wake up to the smell of woodsmoke and the distant sound of someone arguing about whether Bourkie’s Bakehouse vanilla slice is *actually* the best in Victoria (32 years, multiple trophies, three permanent slices — they don’t even need a specials board). Then we wander down to Holgate Brewhouse, which has been pulling pints in the old Keatings Hotel since forever — that historic red-brick beauty built in 1897, now with a swanky taproom and a Temptress Choc Porter that makes city visitors forget they have to drive home.

Speaking of visitors: every June, the Woodend Winter Arts Festival rolls in and suddenly everyone’s wearing scarves and pretending they understand contemporary sculpture. Time Out once said Woodend has “more artists per capita than anywhere else in Victoria” — which explains why the local IGA has a gallery in the fruit aisle.

The real drama? The eternal debate about which baked good rules this town. Country Cob Bakery won Australia’s Best Pie in 2026 (that Malaysian prawn pie is legendary), but they’re in Kyneton, not here. Bourkie’s holds it down for the vanilla slice faithful. And don’t get us started on the food trucks — apparently Dhaba curries were spotted here back in 2012, and we still talk about it like it was yesterday.

Who would *hate* living here? Anyone who expects a train after 10pm. The V/Line is reliable, but only if you define “reliable” as “it will eventually arrive, possibly on time, possibly not.” Also, people who think “country living” means cheap rent. Mate, have you seen the renovation inspo on Brahman Perera’s place? This is not your grandmother’s farmstead.

Who would *love* it? Families who want a tree change, foodies who can handle a four-person queue at a bakery, and anyone who thinks a weekend itinerary of pie, beer, and bushwalks is peak civilisation.

Yes, we know what they say — “Woodend’s just a stop on the way to Daylesford”. But we’ve got the brew, the slice, and the arts festival. And we’re not even mad about the train delays, because that just means another round of Roadtrip IPA. See you at The Victoria Hotel after your Hanging Rock wander. 🍺🥧
