# Mickleham (VIC)

Slug: `mickleham-vic`

Back in the day we used to pretend we lived in the country by driving 35 minutes north of the CBD and calling it a weekend. We’d pull up at 2335 Mickleham Road and swivel our chairs to watch camels graze while alpacas judged our outfits — because yes, Marnong Estate was that kind of place. Broadsheet called it a 16‑hectare winery and working farm; Time Out and Sitchu called it idyllic. We called it “where the bistecca meets the bovines.”

Marnong’s roster — La Vètta (the elevated Italian diner that once had an ex‑Scopri/Bar Bambi chef), Caffe Vista and Cucina 3064 — used to be our excuse for long lunches, cellar‑door tasting and pretending we were on a mini‑staycation in one of the luxury cabins. The estate’s roaming Angus cattle and wedding capacities (up to 200 seated, 250 cocktail, as one weddings roundup noted) meant you could get hitched with a vineyard backdrop and a camel cameo if you were lucky.

There isn’t much petty council gossip in the files we have — mostly guides and event listings — so the local drama is more a delightful contradiction: on one side we’re rolling paddocks named for the murnong (the little yam daisy that gave the place its name), on the other Time Out was already listing Mickleham–Yuroke among the most up‑and‑coming suburbs for 2026. It’s the kind of place that can go from alpaca to avocado dreams (well, Time Out said “up‑and‑coming”; we’ll leave the avocado to the trend pieces).

Who would hate it here? If you can’t stomach a 35‑minute escape, or you prefer your date nights to involve neon and tram noises rather than panoramic paddocks, this isn’t your vibe. If the thought of camels at sunset unsettles you, move on.

Who’d love it? Families — Playgroup Victoria listed our Mickleham North Community Centre at the corner of Donnybrook Rd & Blackmore Rd, plus outdoor spots like Ace Reserve (Rosehill Boulevard & Monomeath Dr) and Summit Park (7 Tremaine Ave) — and anyone who wants winery weekends without the airport queue. We’re the suburban paddock where weddings, wine and weekend wanderings collide, and frankly, we’ll miss the people who thought we’d never grow up.
