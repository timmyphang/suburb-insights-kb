# St Albans (VIC)

Slug: `st-albans-vic`

We know what they say about St Albans. The western suburban cousin no one talks about at dinner parties. But let’s be honest — you’ve never experienced a real Melbourne multicultural festival until you’ve hit the St Albans Lunar New Year shindig on Alfrieda Street. First held in 1998, it’s the city’s largest and longest-running Lunar New Year bash, and we’re not about to let that crown slip.

A typical Saturday here? You’ll find us lining up at Phở Sắc for a bowl of Northern-style pho that puts half the city’s overpriced broth to shame. The Braybrook, CBD and St Albans outpost ladles the real deal, no pretentious garnishes. Later, swing past Gotcha for bubble tea — reusable cups and gold straws included. We’re basically the western suburbs’ food and festival capital, minus the Eastside pricing.

But here’s where the roast gets real: Our local news doesn’t mince words. A 14-year-old was stabbed walking from Ginifer Station. A bloke on a bike robbed a servo with a plastic gun. Buku Khamis played his junior footy here before hitting the Bulldogs — but even he’s probably seen some stuff. We own the tough edges. Housing stress, meth, break-ins. That’s St Albans. And we’re still here, still smiling, still hosting the city’s best fireworks display.

Who’d hate it here? Anyone expecting a “CABN eco-cabin on Kangaroo Island’s Cape St Albans” vibe. St Albans, VIC, is not a luxury spa retreat. It’s real, messy, and gloriously unpretentious. If you need gold straws and designer alpaca throws, head to Fitzroy.

Who’d love it? Families craving a genuine multicultural scene where the Lunar New Year festival closes streets and fills the sky with fireworks. Pho lovers who know the best bowl isn’t in Richmond. And anyone tired of suburbs that try too hard.

Welcome to St Albans. We’re not posh. But we’re proper.
