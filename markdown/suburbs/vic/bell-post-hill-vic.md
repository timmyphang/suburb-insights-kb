# Bell Post Hill (VIC)

Slug: `bell-post-hill-vic`

We’ve all been there – Monday morning, 8:45am, doing the school run limbo on Ernest Street. You’ve got one kid heading to Bell Post Hill Kindergarten Inc (58 Ernest St, don’t be late) and the other you’re dropping at Kardinia Kids (5-31 Anakie Road) because apparently you need two different drop-offs in a suburb with one main drag. The Safeway car park at 290/300 Anakie Rd is basically our community centre – we wave, we sigh, we buy the same milk we forgot yesterday.

Back in the day – and by that we mean 2021 – Geelong Coast Kids Today magazine was the hottest read in town. You could grab a free copy at Kardinia Kids or the Safeway, and it was the only way to find out which playgroup was accepting new members that month. Speaking of playgroups, the Kardinia Playgroup at 1-11 Kardinia Drive still runs sessions – we assume – because their admin email is active and someone named Olivia knows the password. The rest of Geelong gets boutique bakeries and wine bars; we get a playgroup and a Safeway. And we’re fine with that. Mostly.

Petty local drama? There is none, because nothing ever happens here. The most controversial decision in Bell Post Hill is whether to go left or right on Anakie Road to avoid the roundabout. The Mamils (Middle-Aged Men in Lycra) occasionally cut through on their way to the You Yangs, and we still talk about that one time a tandem bike went past. Thrilling.

If you hate peace, quiet, and the sound of your own breathing, you’d hate it here. There’s no third-wave coffee, no laneway bar, no weekend market. The only “event” is the biannual distribution of a free parenting magazine. If you need constant stimulation, move to Geelong West and leave our Safeway car park alone.

But if you want an affordable house with a backyard, a kindergarten your kid can walk to, and the kind of neighbour who’ll grab your mail when you’re away – Bell Post Hill is your spot. We’re not flashy, but we’ve got a playgroup, a Safeway, and absolutely zero pretension. Come for the low crime rate, stay for the Kardinia Kids drop-off lane. Just don’t expect a flat white.
