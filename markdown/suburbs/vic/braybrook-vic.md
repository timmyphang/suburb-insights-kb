# Braybrook, VIC

There is a specific Braybrook morning ritual happening at El Jannah now that they’ve rolled out the breakfast menu — the Kaki Charcoal Breakfast Sandwich, available from 6am, because nothing says “good morning” like a sesame pocket grilled over hot coals with haloumi and garlic sauce at dawn. As Broadsheet noted, yes, they also do scrambled eggs with brioche, but we know what you’re really there for at 6:15am on a Tuesday.

Braybrook is the kind of suburb where your weekend calendar is a beautiful mess — you could be at the Rampest Indoor Skatepark for Braybrook BMX Fest 2022 catching world-class riders launching off ramps, or you could be at PowerPlay’s newest electric go-karting track “taking the wheel” as Urban List described it, screaming past your mate from the commission blocks. Meanwhile, after that adrenaline you can bowl at Zone Bowling for $16.90 and add on laser tag because why not.

The real flex is West of Kin — Andy and Tram Tran’s sprawling sister venue to Kin on Chapel Street — plonked out here among the “Conite” commission homes that Whirlpool forum veterans remember from the 60s. There are a lot of these Commission homes in Braybrook, they noted, and yes, we know what they say about us. “Much of Footscray’s drug population has moved further west, Sunshine Station is still dodgy,” they say. They are not wrong. But they also missed the bit where the Braybrook Community Hub runs playgroups for your toddlers and the Sports Pavilion hosts the kind of local cricket tea that makes you remember why you moved west in the first place.

Time Out rated us among the 10 most affordable and liveable suburbs in 2024 with a median of $721,000 — punching above our weight, they said. That is rich coming from a publication that spends most of its time in Fitzroy.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who flinches when they see conite cladding, or who needs a cold brew and a yoga class on every corner. Who would love it? Anyone who wants a charcoal chicken breakfast, a BMX track, a bowling alley, a go-kart circuit, and a sprawling Vietnamese-Filipino fusion dinner all within a 5km radius and no parking inspectors in sight. Welcome to real Melbourne.
