# Coolaroo (VIC)

Slug: `coolaroo-vic`

**Coolaroo, VIC — postcode 3048, where the sky’s a permanent shade of “is that smoke from the SKM Recycling plant again?”**

There’s a certain poetry to starting your Saturday morning in Coolaroo. You pack the kids into the car, head to the **Coolaroo Hotel** for a parma, and let them loose on the carpeted indoor play area. Because if there’s one thing we know, it’s that kids need to burn off energy while the adults try to forget the Whirlpool forums called our suburb “pretty depressing post-war housing populated by lower socioeconomic inhabitants.” Cheers, mate. We know.

Back in the day, the big local drama wasn’t a controversial cafe menu — it was the **giant tyre fire at SKM Recycling** that sent toxic smoke over Dallas and had residents fleeing their homes. Oh, and that time police found a woman’s body in a bin and arrested a **45-year-old Coolaroo man** in Glenroy. As Broadsheet would say, “you may also like” to mind your own business.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who googled “Melbourne’s best restaurants 2026” and got directed to Barangaroo. There’s no flat white with oat milk and a side of smashed avo in Coolaroo. There’s the **Coolaroo Hotel’s** indoor play zone and a recycling plant that occasionally doubles as a spectacle.

But here’s the thing: we’re real. No one’s pretending to be something they’re not. The rent is actually affordable — Sitchu lists us right between Congupna and Connewarre, which is more than most people can say for their suburb’s identity. We’ve got Bunnings studs for your rugs and roller blinds that allegedly reduce heat transfer by 30%. We’re functional. We survive.

Would we have it any other way? Nah. Though we could do without the next tyre fire.
