# Beaumaris (VIC)

Slug: `beaumaris-vic`

Beaumaris: where the biggest secret isn’t the bay views, it’s that we actually have places to go.

We’re the suburb that “unless you grew up there, you’d rarely go to”, according to Urban List — and they’re not wrong. But if you *did* grow up here, you know the old pub on the corner got a glow-up. Beau Kitchen & Cellar took one old, dilapidated hotel, redeveloped the bottom floor and installed a banging eatery with a bottle-shop attached. Check, check. The locals lost their minds — finally, somewhere to get a drink that doesn’t smell like mothballs and regret.

Then there’s Le Bar Supper Club, tucked away on North Concourse, where you can raise a crystal glass to French food, fancy drinks and burlesque shows in Beaumaris of all places. Gold-trimmed mirrors, diamond chandeliers, caviar bumps — it’s like the 1920s threw up inside a beachside postcode. We still don’t know how we got it, but we’re not asking questions.

Sure, we lost Madoljai — the Thai restaurant that served King Rama V’s favourite soup and Yarra Valley caviar on watermelon rice crackers. Permanently closed, says Broadsheet. A stained-glass “restaurant” sign now points to an empty room. We’re still not over it.

And if you want a drinkable Dubai chocolate, Beau’s has you covered, because apparently Beaumaris is now a destination for decadence.

Who’d hate it here? Anyone who wants a buzzing nightlife that doesn’t involve a crystal chandelier or a burlesque dancer’s heel. We’re sleepy, we’re fancy, and we’re fine with it.

Who’d love it? The person who says “I live in Beaumaris” and watches you try to remember where that is. The person who knows the secret is worth the drive. The person who grew up here and never left because, frankly, why would you?
