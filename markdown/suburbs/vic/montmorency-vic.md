# Montmorency (VIC)

Slug: `montmorency-vic`

The year is 2021. PwC drops its City Pulse report and suddenly Montmorency is the fifth-best suburb in Melbourne. Fifth! We shot up past Blackburn, past Kew, right behind Wantirna. Local real estate agents still haven’t recovered — they keep the PDF pinned to the fridge between the kids’ swimming certificates and the Coles catalogue.

Back then, we had it all. Alex and Cathy Kamvissis were running Espresso 3094, then they opened Montibello in late 2018 (the Urban List will confirm). Mediterranean vibes, same crew, proper local energy. That was our golden era. You’d roll down to the station, grab a coffee, and feel like you’d cracked the code — all the space of Eltham without the artisanal smugness.

But let’s be real: Montmorency is the middle child of the north-east. We’re not as flashy as Greensborough (sorry, Greensborough — you know you’re trying too hard). We’re not as bush-boho as Eltham. We’re just… here. Leafy. Decent schools. A train line that mostly works. We’ve got the Montmorency Hotel and a Macca’s that’s basically a community centre. That’s our cultural precinct.

Who would hate it? Anyone who needs 24-hour nightlife. If your idea of a big Friday night is something that isn’t the 7-Eleven on Hawdon Street, do not move here. Also, if you think the Plenty Road traffic is a “character-building experience,” you’re lying.

Who would love it? Families who want a backyard and a good primary school without selling a kidney. People who genuinely enjoy telling others “we’re sort of between Greensborough and Eltham” and watching their eyes glaze over. We’re not trying to be cool. We just quietly ranked top five while nobody was watching. That’s the Montmorency way.
