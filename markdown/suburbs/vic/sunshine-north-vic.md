# Sunshine North (VIC)

Slug: `sunshine-north-vic`

Sunshine North: where our biggest claim to fame is a go-kart track that went up in flames, and our most anticipated development is a quarry that *might* one day be a park. We’re not here to pretend we’re Fitzroy. We’re here for the pastizzi.

Start your day at **Cafe Sunshine & Salamatea** on Dickson Street, where co-owners Hamed Allahyari and Jen Morillas are serving Persian breakfast staples like date omelette (khorma) and “giggle tea” (sour-cherry saffron – it’s supposedly an anti-depressant, and mate, you’ll need it after reading house prices in the inner north). This place is a social enterprise helping refugees and asylum seekers upskill, because even after escaping Iran by boat and ending up on Christmas Island, Allahyari couldn’t get a job until he volunteered at the Asylum Seeker Resource Centre. Now he runs a zero-waste eatery with in-house almond milk and books from diverse authors. We’re not crying, you’re crying.

Later, swing by **The Original Maltese Pastizzi Co** on McIntyre Road – Jacqui Challinor (exec chef at Reine & La Rue) calls it the best pastizzi she’s ever had, and if it’s good enough for her, it’s good enough for us. Grab a ricotta-and-pea one and eat it while staring at the tilt-slab warehouse that used to be Fun City, an indoor go-kart track that burned down in 2017 with 80 firefighters and 30 trucks. We still talk about it like it was yesterday. “Remember the smoke? I had to close my windows.”

If you’re after a night out, there’s **Double Deuce Bar and Billiards** on McIntyre Road – open 6pm-1am, which is basically our version of a club district. 

You’d hate it here if you need polished streetscapes, a train station that doesn’t feel like a bus stop, or a nightlife beyond pool tables. You’d love it here if you value real food, real stories, and don’t mind living next to a quarry that might become a park (River Valley Estate, we’re looking at you). We’re not trendy. We’re just… ourselves. And that’s enough.
