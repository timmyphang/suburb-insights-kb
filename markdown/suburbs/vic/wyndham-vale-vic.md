# Wyndham Vale (VIC)

Slug: `wyndham-vale-vic`

Wyndham Vale: where your mortgage is cheap but your commute is a personality trait. We’re the suburb that makes the phrase “only 40 minutes from the city” sound like a lie when it’s actually 90 in peak hour. And we wear that badge with pride.

A typical Saturday morning for us? Not brunch at some hipster joint. We’re either doing a double lap of Manor Lakes Central car park looking for a spot, or we’re driving past the empty shops on the new estate wondering when the cafe will arrive. But hey, we’ve got The Manor – that’s our pub. And we’ve got massive house-and-land packages that cost less than a deposit on a Brunswick apartment. Affordability? Absolutely. As Pedestrian TV noted, we’re one of the “most affordable areas” – if you don’t mind being “extraordinarily large distances away from the CBD.” We don’t mind. We’ve got Netflix.

In 2020, we hit the headlines for all the wrong reasons. Broadsheet’s COVID data put postcode 3024 – Wyndham Vale, Manor Lakes, Mambourin, Mount Cottrell, Fieldstone – at 94 active cases. We were right up there with the hotspots. Whole streets went into lockdown. But we got through it. And now we’re just trying to get our lives back.

Our neighbours in Werribee get a glowing wonderland called “Lit at Wyndham Park” this March with giant bubbles and light trails. We see the ads. We think about going. Then we remember parking.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who thinks “public transport” means a train every 10 minutes. Anyone who wants a corner store that sells organic quinoa. Anyone who values their time over their bank balance.

Who would love it? People who want a backyard. People who understand that 45 minutes in the car is a fair trade for a four-bedroom house. People who know the Werribee Plaza like the back of their hand. We’re not fancy. We’re just Wyndham Vale. And we’re fine with that.
