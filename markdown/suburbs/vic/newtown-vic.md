# Newtown (VIC)

Slug: `newtown-vic`

We’d love to tell you about a morning routine in Newtown, VIC, but honestly? We’d have to drive to Newtown, NSW to find a decent latte. Here in the Geelong pocket, our big cultural moment is the Gold Diggers Arms – an old-school pub in the back streets that’s been “an…” (the Urban List snippet cut out, which sums us up perfectly). We are the suburb that time forgot, but in a cosy, not-holding-any-grudge kind of way.

The most dramatic thing that’s happened here recently is a Sitchu home tour of a house styled by Simone Haag, described as “a bit LA, aspirational but liveable”. Yes, our biggest local claim to fame is that someone’s kitchen looked nice. No council feuds, no late-night kebab drama, no mural painted over in protest. Just quiet streets, a pub that may or may not still be open, and the faint hum of Geelong traffic.

You would hate it here if you need a standing-room-only Japanese bar or a late-night burrito. There is no White Moon Bar, no Young Henrys bioreactor, no Guzman y Gomez mural – those are all Sydney’s Newtown. We are the Newtown that gets asked “VIC or NSW?” every single time. The answer: the one with fewer options and more peace.

You would love it here if you want a house that looks like an LA influencer’s Instagram, a quiet pint at the Gold Diggers, and zero risk of running into a street artist. We’re the low-key, no-fuss Newtown – the one that doesn’t need a mural to tell you where you are.
