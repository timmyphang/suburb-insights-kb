# Bell Park (VIC)

Slug: `bell-park-vic`

We still call it "the bakery" even though La Madre on Milton Street stopped being our big claim to fame about 12 years ago. Broadsheet once called it artisan—hand-shaped sourdough, natural leven, the whole thing—and for a suburb that sits quietly in Geelong’s shadow, that was basically our Olympics. Now you walk past and it’s just a memory, like our NBN speed.

Bell Park is the suburb Geelong forgets to mention. We’ve got houses, a few roundabouts, and that one bakery that put us on the map for exactly one Broadsheet article in 2010. Our local drama? None. The biggest controversy is whether we’re actually Bell Park or just an extension of Bell Post Hill. We don’t have a park called Bell Park, either. We have a shopping centre car park that serves as the village green.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who wants a nightlife. Or a coffee shop. Or a reason to leave the house after 6pm. You’ll drive to Geelong for everything—groceries, friends, entertainment. We’re the suburb where you live because you got a good deal on rent, not because you chose it.

Who would love it here? People who understand that quiet isn’t boring. Families who want a backyard and a 15-minute drive to the beach. Those who remember La Madre’s seeded sourdough and still talk about it like it’s a living legend. We’re not trying to be cool—we’re just trying to get a decent loaf without leaving the postcode. And we’re still waiting.
