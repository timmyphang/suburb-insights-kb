# Keysborough (VIC)

Slug: `keysborough-vic`

Keysborough. The suburb where your GPS confidently says “you have arrived” and you’re still staring at a paddock and a Bounce trampoline park. We’re the proud home of the Keysborough Christmas Tree Farm — the only time we get mentioned in a lifestyle guide is when you need a pine tree in November and don’t want to drive past Cranbourne.

Our Saturday starts at Springers Leisure Centre on Cheltenham Road — not for a swim, but because it’s the only place with air conditioning that isn’t a Bunnings. We do our laps, then head to the Keysborough Gardens Primary School library for a playgroup that’s been running since Term 4, 2024. Thrilling stuff. 

Petty local drama? Oh, we’ve got it. Remember when the vice president of the Keysborough branch of the Liberal Party got busted for calling lesbians “butchy” on Facebook? That was us. Good ol’ Tim Dark, representing the suburb like a champ. And if you needed a night bus on NYE? Forget it. Residents of Keysborough were left without late-night services while the rest of Melbourne partied. We walked home. In the dark. Past the No Fuss factory. 

Who would hate it here? Anyone expecting a coffee shop that isn’t inside a school hall. Broadsheet and Concrete Playground have written extensively about dumplings in Sydney and Spanish restaurants in Sydney — not a single mention of our local cuisine. We don’t have a cafe. We have a factory that hires event staff. That’s the vibe.

Who would love it? Families who think Haileybury’s software systems are consistent between campuses. People who enjoy a Christmas tree farm that’s been providing “high-quality pines for over two and a half decades.” People whose ideal Friday night involves watching the I’m A Celebrity cast clues from Pedestrian TV while bouncing at Bounce. 

Keysborough: we’re not on any “Best Beer Gardens” list, but we’ve got a leisure centre that’s open until 11pm. Come for the trampolines, stay because the Night Bus doesn’t run here.
