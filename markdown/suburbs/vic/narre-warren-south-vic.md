# Narre Warren South (VIC)

Slug: `narre-warren-south-vic`

We’ve all heard the whispers. Narre Warren South: where the internet is dial-up slow, the main road is a car park from 3pm, and we’re sandwiched between the bogan enclaves of Narre Warren, Cranbourne, and Hampton Park. And you know what? They’re not wrong. But they also missed the bit where you can get a four-bedroom house for the price of a Berwick shoebox, and your kids can walk to WCC.

Our Thursday mornings look like this: drop the little ones at Narre Warren Kidz Playgroup (we’re inclusive – dads, grandparents, even carers welcome, as long as you don’t complain about the Wi-Fi), then hit the Aldi at Amberley Park SC. Afternoon? Stuck behind a single-lane nightmare on Narre Warren Cranbourne Rd, wondering if Casey Council will ever upgrade anything. As one local put it, “Narre is always the last suburb in Casey to get any infrastructure.” We feel that.

The drama? Oh, we’ve got it. The Whirlpool forums LIVE for us. “Cranbourne = Crimebourne,” they laugh. But honestly, our real drama is the NBN. HFC connection? Good luck. One resident reported “very flakey” service; another said ADSL is “as slow as treacle.” And don’t get us started on the TPG/Vocus outage of June 2026 that knocked out half the suburb. We’re still waiting for our BGP sessions to stabilise.

Who would hate it here? Berwick snobs who clutch their pearls at the word “bogan.” Anyone who needs fibre-to-the-premises yesterday. Or people who think “teenage gangsters” are a dealbreaker – yeah, we’ve got some, but they’re mostly just bored kids with nowhere to go. As one forum sage noted, “There are bogan areas everywhere.”

Who would love it? Families who want space, value, and walking distance to a good private school. People who don’t mind a bit of roughness – the newer estates off Clyde Rd are genuinely lovely. And anyone who can laugh at themselves. Welcome to Narre Warren South. Yes, the internet is terrible. But your backyard is huge.
