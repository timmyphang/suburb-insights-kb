# Wandin North (VIC)

Slug: `wandin-north-vic`

**Wandin North, VIC**

Here's the thing about living in Wandin North: we're not Wandin. We're *North*. That little distinction is the difference between being known for a pub and being known for 10,000 lavender plants and a hedge maze that's older than your nan's perm.

Yes, we know what they say. "Bit quiet out there." "Is that near Lilydale?" "Do they have electricity yet?" They are not wrong, but they also missed the bit where we've got Warratina Lavender Farm — a quaint little farm with over 10,000 lavender plants, a sprawling homestead garden, and, crucially, lavender ice cream. As Concrete Playground noted, entry is $10 for adults and goes straight to the Wandin Country Fire Authority. So you can spend the day frolicking among purple rows, dodging bees, and calling it a charity donation. Win-win.

But let's talk about *time*. The open garden weekend was a 2016 trend, okay? We're still riding that wave. Meanwhile, Time Out says Wandin Park is in Wandin North, except it's apparently in Gruyere? The boundaries here are a suggestion, like the speed limit on the Warburton Highway. We've got Australia's oldest hedge maze, a lavender maze, a rose maze — we've got more mazes than a maths textbook. But nobody can agree on which suburb we are.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs a proper directional sign, someone who thinks "lavender ice cream" sounds like a fever dream, or a person who expects a pub to stay open past 8pm. We don't have a pub. We have a thousand lavender plants and a sausage sizzle on a good weekend. That's it.

Who would love it? People who own a bike (Reid Cycles is based in North Melbourne but we're loyal), a dog on a lead, and a picnic basket. Also: bees. They love it here. Honestly, drive an hour east, bring a basket, eat lavender ice cream, get lost in a hedge maze, and tell your friends you went to Wandin. You don't have to say North. We'll know.
