# Templestowe (VIC)

Slug: `templestowe-vic`

**"We’re Templestowe. Yes, we’ve got the views. And the cold case."**

Let’s be honest — we’re not the suburb that comes up at dinner parties unless someone’s talking about real estate or true crime. We know what they say. And they’re not wrong. But they also missed the bit where we’ve got yum cha so good it’s “widely revered as home to one of Melbourne’s best offerings” (Time Out, not us), and a pub where the kids can play arcade games right next to your craft beer. The Templestowe Hotel — where parenting and pints coexist in perfect, sweaty harmony.

Our morning routine? You’re either queueing at Miss Kneady on James Street for a Neapolitan pizza that’s been fermenting for 72 hours (because we have time for that), or you’re at Riddik in an old ANZ branch in Lower Templestowe, ordering eggs six ways while sitting under a massive street-art mural. We’re fancy, but we’re also the kind of place where a cafe used to do “six types of eggs benny” and called it a day.

Yes, we sit 50 metres above sea level, giving us “some of the best views of the sparkling cityscape” — as Sitchu kindly noted. But we also sit on the Yarra, which, as of 2025, Dr Imberger says is finally swimmable near us. Finally — all those years of “potential water quality issues” (shoutout Broadsheet’s 2020 boil-water advisory for 88 suburbs) and now we can actually dip in without side-eyeing the pipes.

And look, we can’t ignore the shadow. Mr Cruel. The abduction of 13-year-old Karmein Chan in Templestowe. It’s part of our story — a dark, unsolved one that Pedestrian TV reminds us of every time the anniversary rolls around. We don’t pretend it away. It’s in the soil.

**Who would hate it here?** Anyone who thinks a suburb needs a nightclub. We’ve got Bellini cocktails on tap at Miss Kneady and then… bed. That’s the arc.

**Who would love it?** The person who wants a quiet life with a view, a world-class yum cha spot, and a river they can finally swim in. We’re not trendy. We’re Templestowe. And we knead you to accept that.
