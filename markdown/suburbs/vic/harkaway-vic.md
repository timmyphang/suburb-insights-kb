# Harkaway (VIC)

Slug: `harkaway-vic`

Harkaway, VIC — we’re the suburb that time forgot, and we’re perfectly fine with that.

You think you’ve got issues with your NBN? Try moving to Harkaway. As one local put it on a forum recently: “it’s really strange, there are solid connections in all the surrounding areas but Harkaway just kind of lives in a land that time forgot.” And they’re not wrong. Our internet is so bad we still use carrier pigeons. The Starlink dish on your roof isn’t a status symbol — it’s survival.

We don’t have a main street. We don’t have a cafe. We don’t have a pub. What we do have is postcode 3806, which apparently only exists so Sitchu can list it as a search filter. “Harkaway 3806” — it’s real, we checked. But ask anyone from the city where we are and they’ll say “isn’t that near… somewhere?”

Our biggest claim to fame is that one time someone on Whirlpool asked if Harkaway Homes builds in NSW. That’s it. That’s our local drama.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who relies on Deliveroo. Or has a deadline. Or wants to stream Netflix in HD without buffering. Basically, if you need fast internet and a decent flat white, don’t bother.

Who would love it? People who genuinely enjoy having no fixed line. People who look at a paddock and think “yes, this is where I belong.” People who want their internet delivered via satellite dish and their mail via the kindness of a neighbour’s 4WD. We’re not for everyone. But if you want to live in a place that’s literally off the grid — by accident — welcome to Harkaway. Just don’t ask about the NBN.
