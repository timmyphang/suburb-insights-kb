# Skye (VIC)

Slug: `skye-vic`

We call it the 3975 postal void. Skye, VIC — where even the internet can’t decide if you exist. Punch "Skye" into Broadsheet and you get a Supper on Skye event from some forgotten era (no, not the Isle, not the Bug Lady, not the influencer — just a lonely link with no date and zero follow-up). That’s the sum total of our local food scene: a single, undated supper. We’ve got the same number of listed cafés as we have cricket farms (zero), which is ironic given Skye Blackburn farms bugs in Sydney. But we’re not Sydney.

Living here means telling delivery drivers “no, not the Scottish island, not the I’m A Celeb winner, not the nail salon in North Sydney — just the housing estate between Cranbourne and Carrum Downs.” Our landmark? A Coles and a servo that everyone calls “the old one.” The closest thing to local drama is a Whirlpool forum user named “Skye!” who swears by Moogoo cream for eczema. That’s it. That’s the community.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who googled “Skye VIC food” and got truffle pasta in Tokyo. Also influencers expecting a backdrop — we don’t have a Cadenza Floral Cafe under the Skye apartments; we have roundabouts and a drainage ditch.

Who would love it? People who genuinely don’t mind being the answer to a trivia question. If your idea of a good weekend is staring at a roundabout and thinking “this could be worse,” welcome home. We’re the suburb that the data forgot — and we’re fine with that. As long as Moogoo stays in stock at the chemist.
