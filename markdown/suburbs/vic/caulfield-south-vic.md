# Caulfield South (VIC)

Slug: `caulfield-south-vic`

Alright, gather 'round, Caulfield South. We need to talk about our little pocket of 3162.

Saturday morning: you’re either in the queue at Sebby’s Scrolls on North Road before 8.30am, watching the team pump out 2500 cinnamon scrolls a week from a kitchen that starts baking at 3.30am, or you’re lying. The cream cheese icing is basically a religious experience, and the line moves fast but builds quicker. Then you’re off to Princes Park — not the north-side one, our 25-acre one — to let the dog speed-date at the off-leash area while you pretend to read a book.

The real local drama? It’s the eternal beef between Big Boy BBQ and Marmaris II. Lance Rosen brought an authentic Texas smoker from the States and opened Melbourne’s first American BBQ joint right here. Friday brisket, Sunday Southern Grace Diner, cow print decals. But then you’ve got Marmaris II on Glen Huntly Road — a Turkish takeaway that inspires “fierce local devotion”. Fresh bread baked daily, smoky dips. You cannot be loyal to both. Pick a side. (We’re Marmaris, but don’t tell the Zee Man burger.)

And yes, we know our suburban cafe scene runs on a rotation of the same few spots: Cedar Street Cafe where the barista knows your order, Mr Brightside on Booran Road (mock-timber house frame, Coffee Supreme blends), and Turquoise Eatery with its op-shop decor and charred eggplant tahini. Nothing too fancy — exactly how we like it.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs nightlife past 9pm. Hendrick’s Burgers closes, Big Boy is only two days a week, and Turquoise only does dinner Wednesday-Thursday. This is not a place for late-night decisions.

Who would love it? Scroll addicts, dog owners who value a good park bin, and people who want a fierce local debate that doesn’t involve politics — just brisket vs. borek. Come for the queue, stay for the quiet. Just don’t confuse us with Caulfield North. We’re the south side. We know our scrolls.
