# South Melbourne (VIC)

Slug: `south-melbourne-vic`

**South Melbourne, VIC — where the heritage terraces cost more than your first marriage, but the dim sims are still $4.50 and the locals have strong opinions about bridge height.**

Morning in South Melbourne. We’re up before the sun, jogging Albert Park Lake with 12,000 other smug residents who know they’re living in one of Melbourne’s oldest suburbs. Then it’s off to The Kettle Black on Albert Road for blueberry ricotta hotcakes and a coffee that costs more than a night at the old Star Hotel. Speaking of pubs — the Hotel South Melbourne on Clarendon Street has been a rock venue, a nightclub, and a BYO food hole. Now it’s serving Earl Grey IPA and cacio e pepe croquettes. Progress.

Yes, we have the South Melbourne Market — a sacred site where MasterChef contestants go to buy fat pigs and we go for the dim sims at South Melbourne Market Dim Sims. *“No visit is complete without a bag full of dimmies,”* says Urban List. We agree. Also, gozleme from Köy. And cannoli from Cannoleria. But don’t forget: this isn’t just a foodie paradise — it’s a battleground for trucks. The Montague Street bridge trauma has now spread: a truck recently slammed into the City Road light rail underpass, just 700m away. We call it the South Melbourne Bridge Curse.

And then there’s the petty drama: Channel 9 bought an Art Deco factory on Ingles Street to be the base for *The Block*. No doubt some renovation will ruin the local character. Meanwhile, a bloke got fined $1,600 during COVID for visiting his “semi-intimate partner” past curfew in South Melbourne. The police know we have feelings, apparently.

Who would HATE it here? Anyone who doesn’t own a dog, a slow cooker, or a tolerance for trucks scraping bridges. Also people who think “historic charm” means “no parking”.

Who would LOVE it? Dim sim enthusiasts, craft beer fiends (hi Brewmanity, Westside Ale Works, Hotel South Melbourne), and anyone who wants to live in a suburb where you can go from a Turkish flatbread to a French pastry to a rooftop boozy brunch at Mirror Mirror without changing postcode. And yes, we’ve got a new kid on the block — Upper Middle on Eastern Road, opened May 2026 by a France-Soir veteran serving pizza and baguettes with St Ali coffee. Welcome.

South Melbourne: we’ve been evolving since 1854. Still no parking, but plenty of dimmies. Come for the lake, stay for the bridge rage.
