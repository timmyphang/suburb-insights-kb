# South Morang (VIC)

Slug: `south-morang-vic`

Welcome to South Morang, where we’ve gone from a pub on the corner and a Maccas to a Westfield, 17 housing estates, and a play centre so good it’s open until 10pm. Yes, we boomed. But did anyone ask the kangaroos?

Our morning routine: fight the tree on the Hurstbridge line (again), grab a coffee from… well, we’ll just grab a Maccas drive-through because the only cafe mentioned by name in our data is **Farm Vigano** – and that’s a “fuel up after Plenty Gorge” place for out-of-towners. We save our appetite for **Hoopla** on McDonalds Road, where the kids run wild and parents pretend they’re enjoying it. It’s $12 a head and open till 10pm Friday. That’s a date night.

Petty local drama: the roos at **Hawkstone Park** have declared war on cyclists. One bloke wandered onto their turf and somehow lived – Pedestrian TV called it “Turf Of Death”. Meanwhile, a fallen tree on our train line was the most excitement we’ve had since the **Plenty Ranges Arts & Convention Centre** was a vaccination hub in 2021. Also, someone on Whirlpool is still asking about ADSL2+ speeds on **Beringarra Street**. Welcome to the future.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks a 3-hour commute is “unacceptable”. A Junkee article told the story of a counsellor who commutes from South Morang to a private school – then picks up extra shifts at the MCG just to save a few bucks. If you want a barista who knows your name, move to South Melbourne. We have a pub called **Commercial Hotel** that once hosted a band called Rancid Eddie. That’s our culture.

Who would love it? Families who want space, a **Plenty Gorge** walk that actually makes you feel like you’re in the bush, and a shopping centre that has everything you need except a decent pho (the 2026 guide didn’t mention us). And hey, if you can handle the roos, the trains, and the occasional burst water main, welcome to the ’Rang. We’ll save you a seat at Hoopla.
