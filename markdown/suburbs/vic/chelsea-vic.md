# Chelsea (VIC)

Slug: `chelsea-vic`

Chelsea’s got one address that shows up in the data: 8–10 Swanpool Avenue. That’s where Chelsea Occasional Childcare lives — flexible, affordable, short-term care. And honestly, that’s pretty much the whole vibe of our suburb: occasional. We’re not here every day, but when we are, we’re reliable and cheap.

Back in the day, you’d hear “Chelsea” and think bay views, salty air, weekend fish and chips. Now? You hear “Chelsea” and assume someone’s about to recommend a boot style or a New York hotel. The internet thinks we’re a fashion brand, a garden show, or a Chelsea Clinton hot take. Meanwhile we’re just here, dropping kids off at Swanpool Avenue between shifts.

Who would hate living here? Anyone searching for “Chelsea” on Broadsheet hoping to find a cafe review. Sorry — you’ll only get articles about modelling agents fighting AI. We don’t have a Ritual Espresso. We have a childcare centre with playgroup sessions and a car park that’s free if you don’t mind the pram jostling.

Who would love it here? Someone who understands that a suburb’s identity isn’t a restaurant guide — it’s the fact that our only listed business is a daycare with a “Manage Separation Anxiety” blog post. That’s the real Chelsea, VIC: low-key, practical, and fully accepting that we’re just the occasional stop on the Frankston line.
