# Cheltenham (VIC)

Slug: `cheltenham-vic`

Saturday morning in Cheltenham and you’ll find us at Bad Shepherd Brewing Co. on Reserve Road, scoffing half a pound of Angus brisket at 11am because that’s just how we roll. If we’re feeling fancy, we’ll detour past Frank’s on Cavanagh Street for a vegan scramble that tastes nothing like regret, or grab a loaded sanga from Robin’s Deli (yes, from the same team behind Merrymen in Hampton – we’re not complete animals). And when we need a new sofa that isn’t flat-pack, we hit Huset on Centre Dandenong Road for Danish furniture that costs more than our car.

Let’s be honest: we’re a suburb that lives in the shadow of both Southland and our own industrial estate. The locals on Whirlpool will happily debate the merits of the Anytime Fitness cardio equipment vs Brighton’s, while the GAC Aion dealership down the road lets you test drive a Chinese EV and then forget to turn off the 360 camera manually. Our biggest claim to fame? The Hot Bird – a charcoal chicken shop rooted in the 70s that makes you question every other chook in your life. And Bambam Eatery on Keys Road runs on solar power and a mostly vegan menu that changes daily, because apparently we’re bougie now.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who thinks “industrial chic” shouldn’t involve actual forklifts. People who get stressed trying to find parking near the new Cinnabon (it’s opening in Melbourne, not here, but still). And definitely anyone who doesn’t appreciate a good Hazelnut Brown Ale while watching kids run wild at a brewpub.

But we love it. Because where else can you buy a real Le Corbusier chair from Huset, grab a $12 bagel loaded with smoked trout from Bambam, and then wash it all down with house-brewed beer at Bad Shepherd? Cheltenham: we’ve got a slice of Paris (Bar Savarin), a dash of Denmark, and a whole lot of industrial soul. Come for the beer, stay for the incomprehensible sense of suburban pride.
