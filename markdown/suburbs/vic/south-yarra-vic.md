# South Yarra (VIC)

Slug: `south-yarra-vic`

You know you’ve picked the right suburb when your Saturday morning starts with a line out the door at **Tivoli Road Bakery**, a quick lap of the **Tan** in activewear, then a simmering argument at **Prahran Market** over whether the organic avocados are worth $8 each. We’re **South Yarra**, the postcode where the skyline went from “heritage mansions” to “CBD junior” in about ten minutes, and we’ve absolutely earned every bit of the reputation.

The drama? Oh, nothing much. Just a **Toorak woman allegedly smashing into a South Yarra home, then reversing out and driving off without tyres**. Classic “oops, wrong gear, darling” energy. And if that’s not enough, the **Greens once advertised on Grindr** to locals in Prahran and South Yarra — because nothing says “vote for climate action” like geo-targeting thirsty singles at 10pm. We’ve also got a new **Bar Yarra** (named Australia’s most romantic restaurant by OpenTable, no pressure), a **Baker Bleu** selling $12 sourdough with soft serve, and a **Soak Bathhouse** for when the 29-cheese gnocchi at that pop-up gets too real.

But if you hate high-rise towers, “Chap Laps” clogging Chapel Street at 1am, or people who ask if you’ve tried the **Koko Black chocolate wall**, you’ll loathe it here. This is a suburb where the tennis club membership application feels like a CEO interview, and **South Yarra Primary** has one class per year level — good luck fitting twins in.

Who *should* move here? Anyone who wants to feel like they’re in a 24-hour city but still has a mansion on Domain Road. We’re loud, we’re expensive, and we have a bathtub in a closet. But we’re also the best-dressed mess you’ll ever meet. 💅
