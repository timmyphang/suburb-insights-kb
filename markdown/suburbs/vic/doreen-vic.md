# Doreen (VIC)

Slug: `doreen-vic`

**Doreen, VIC — where your front porch doubles as a display window for the local footwear enthusiast.**

Look, we’re not saying Doreen has a crime problem. But between 2011 and 2017, police allegedly found over 2,300 pairs of stolen work boots and sneakers at a bloke’s house up the road in Mernda. They called him the “Great Shoe-dini.” And according to the reports, he was allegedly hitting porches in Doreen, Watsonia North, Craigieburn and Mernda between 11pm and 7am. So if you’ve ever wondered why your Dunlop Volleys keep vanishing, wonder no more. We’re not paranoid — we’re just checking our Ring doorbell footage before bed.

---

**The local routine**

On a good day in Doreen, you start your morning knowing the nearest gym options are “extremely limited” (as one Whirlpool forum user put it). You either drive 25 minutes inbound to South Morang for a proper commercial setup, or you make do with what’s here. Your choice of yoga or pilates? Sitchu reckons there’s a spot in Doreen, Ringwood North and Mill Park. Take what you can get.

For serious shopping? You’re heading to the Central Markets — but not the one in Adelaide with the famous laksa joint run by a woman named Doreen. Different Doreen. Our Doreen gets a mention as the closest The Bottle-O for road trips. Priorities.

---

**Petty local drama**

The absolute state of our internet migration. One poor soul on Whirlpool asked about moving from Velocity to Opticomm in Doreen, and the thread is basically a ghost town. Meanwhile, someone else noted that moving from the Velocity network is a nightmare of “max speed variation.” We’ve got shoe thieves but can’t get consistent NBN. Classic northern fringe living.

---

**Who would hate it here?**

Anyone who wants a 24-hour gym within walking distance. Anyone who values their footwear staying on their porch overnight. Anyone who thinks “Doreen” is a suburb named after a 1950s baby name trend — because it literally is. The name meaning pages are more relevant to our identity than any Melbourne lifestyle guide.

---

**Who would love it here?**

People who appreciate a quiet life where the biggest local headline was a shoe-stealing spree that lasted six years. People who don’t mind driving 20 minutes for a decent gym and 40 minutes for a good coffee. People who look at a suburb with a postcode (3754) and think “well, at least it’s not Mernda.” As Broadsheet noted back in 2017, our Great Shoe-dini was charged with theft and handling stolen goods. He got bail. That’s Doreen — we’re not dangerous, we’re just… missing our sneakers.
