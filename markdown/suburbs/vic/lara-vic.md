# Lara (VIC)

Slug: `lara-vic`

There is no escaping it — we are the suburb that gets remembered only because of a bus route. The new bus from Lara V/Line station to Avalon Airport is our claim to fame, and honestly, we will take it. That bus runs nine times a day from 6am to 10pm, and we have memorised the timetable the way other suburbs memorise brunch menus. Because that is what we have: a one-way ticket to Sydney and a whole lot of spare time.

Mornings here start with a choice: catch the 7:15 bus and be at the airport by 7:30, or wait for the 8:00 and risk missing your flight. No cafes, no community gardens, no “Lara locals” — just a V/Line platform and the distant roar of landing gear. Try searching “Lara” online and you get Lara Worthington’s skincare routine, Lara Dignam’s cocktail bar in Sydney, and Lara Croft’s tomb raiding. Not one mention of our bus shelter. That is the pettiest drama we have: we are less famous than a fictional archaeologist.

You would hate it here if you expect a scene. No barista curating single-origin, no pop-up galleries. The main event is watching the V/Line departures board flicker. But if you need affordable housing near Geelong, or you work at the airport and crave a three-minute door-to-terminal commute — welcome. We are the suburb that knows exactly where you are going, because we have absolutely nothing keeping you here.
