# Watsonia (VIC)

Slug: `watsonia-vic`

We’ve got a train station, a main road, and apparently — according to the crime logs — a bloke who spent six years nicking shoes off porches in Doreen, Watsonia North, Craigieburn and Mernda. Yes, the Great Shoe-dini was finally caught in 2017, with 2,300 pairs of work boots and sneakers stacked in his Mernda home. We’re still checking our own doorstep every morning. If you see a single Blundstone on the nature strip, you know who to blame.

Mornings here start with a decision: drive to the station and pray for a park, or catch the bus from Elder Street and hope the Hurstbridge line upgrade actually means something. Just when you thought we couldn’t get any more thrilling, they went and extended that tunnel between Grimshaw Street and Elder Street. $15.8 billion and we got a hole in the ground that makes the train run five minutes faster. Worth it.

Speaking of drama — remember the grassfire in December 2019? Spot fires and embers flying up to a kilometre, threatening Bundoora, Watsonia, Greensborough. We all stood on the back deck clutching a hose and a can of Coke. That’s the Watsonia way: ready for anything, but we’d rather not.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who expects a late-night hopper joint or a boudoir high tea. We’ve got a fish ’n’ chip shop next to the milk bar and a real estate agent who still says “potential”. The Whirlpool forum sums it up: “I know Watsonia has got a station and that’s a very positive thing for me.” That’s the headline.

But who would love it? Families who want a footy oval, a takeaway joint, and a neighbourhood where the biggest crime story is a shoe thief. We own our quiet, bare 3087. Come for the tunnel, stay for the parking spot you got at 7am.
