# Lockridge (NSW)

Slug: `lockridge-nsw`

We’ll say it straight: mornings in Lockridge used to mean a convoy of backpacks, metal lunch boxes and one very determined flock of hens. Back in the day we dropped the kids at the Child Australia Lockridge Campus — the Early Learning Centre and OSHC tucked into the parklands at 36 Diana Crescent — and watched them go off to a kitchen garden lesson like it was normal suburbia stuff. (Yes, chickens. Yes, sustainability practices. No, we did not expect to be learning how to compost before coffee.)

If you ever found yourself on the Lockridge centre tour brochure, you’d have memorised the opening hours — 7:00AM to 6:00PM — and possibly the phone numbers: (08) 9377 1676 / 0467 677 465, or the email lockridgecampus@childaustralia.org.au. That brochure was surprisingly reassuring to nervous first-time parents: glossy photos, a description of the newly designed outdoor play environment, and family testimonials about staff and the veggie-laden lunches.

We’ll admit we’re not a suburb of flashy awards or endless write-ups — the mentions that did pop up in our search were mostly Urban List lists about Tasmanian hotels and Netflix crime shows, and Sitchu’s wider Perth food roundups. In other words: national culture moved on while we perfected our worm farm.

If you hate the idea of kids learning leadership by harvesting carrots, or you can’t stand the thought of a community that actually lists its family services, health and wellbeing providers, kids’ health services and even carpet suppliers on MumsPages — then Lockridge will feel like a very small, very stubborn-ville. You’d be bored within a week.

But for families who prize an Outside School Hours Care that plans around play, for anyone who values parklands, a kitchen garden and staff who actually seem to know your kid’s name, Lockridge has quietly been doing its thing. Back in the day, that counted for a lot — and it still does for us.
