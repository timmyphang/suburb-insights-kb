# Dalkeith (NSW)

Slug: `dalkeith-nsw`

Dalkeith — we still remember that exact moment the sky goes orangey-pink at Point Resolution (Victoria Ave & Jutland Parade) and you feel morally superior for choosing a bench over the foreshore. Back in the day all the guides called it a “quiet piece of paradise” and honestly, they weren’t wrong — if your idea of nightlife is an expertly judged sunset and the occasional wandering dog.

Our morning routine used to be gloriously simple: shuffle past the leafier bits — Masons Gardens, Beaton Park — and pretend we were doing laps for fitness while actually scouting a picnic spot. If we were feeling social, we’d saunter (very saunter) to the Dalkeith Nedlands Bowling Club for a sausage sizzle and some barefoot bowls. Urban List raved about the club’s cheap beers and year-round social comp; we liked that it let us sweat under a hat without having to talk about mortgages.

We don’t have fresh scoops — all the clippings are a bit of a nostalgia reel — so consider this less of a “where to” and more of a “remember when.” Sitchu used to paint Dalkeith as the leafy, riverside one: luxury homes, river vistas, Jo Wheatley All Abilities Play Space for the ankle-biters, and cafés allegedly within a polite stroll. Mumsnet’s chatter remembered Dalkeith Country Park with its adventure playground, café and “fab shop” — useful if you like your country park served with a side of artisan jam.

Who would hate living here? If you crave the buzz of late-night scenes, cluster events, or someone to explain the appeal of watching bowls being rolled in near-silence, pass on us. If you measure a suburb by the number of neon signs and 24/7 options, Dalkeith’s pastoral, river-wrapped calm will bore you.

Who’ll love it? Families who want green spaces, people who value a sentimental sunset at Point Resolution, anyone who appreciates a good sausage sizzle at the bowling club. We’ll keep watching the river, waving to joggers we know by their headbands, and pretending the sun sets just for us.
