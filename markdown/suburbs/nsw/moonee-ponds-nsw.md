# Moonee Ponds (NSW)

Slug: `moonee-ponds-nsw`

Back in the day we’d start a Saturday on Puckle Street like it was a very polite pilgrimage — strolling past Luke’s Banh Mi, nodding to Brother Hen, maybe pretending we’d pop into Philhellene for something sensible. Palace Penny Lane turned up in December 2023 with recliners and a rooftop cinema, and for a while the local argument was whether to queue for the 4K picture or the gelato from Brunetti Classico’s massive Penny Lane outpost. 

Our weekend used to look like this: Queens Park for dog-free picnics, the Maribyrnong River for a riverside detour to The Boathouse, and Hall Street or Puckle Street for a circuit of ramen, pizza and craft-beer experimentation — Parco’s neon-lit bowls, Farro’s spelt pizzas and Holmes Hall’s 400-seat mess hall all made an era of big menus and bigger waits. We even had Convoy arrive after an ambitious reno and feel very pleased with itself.

Petty local drama? Oh, we’ve got receipts. Pedestrian TV once reported a MAFS sighting — Jamie allegedly spotted at a Moonee Ponds restaurant — and Junkee kindly reminded us of Shannon Noll’s Cox Plate singalong at Moonee Valley Racecourse, which still lives rent-free in our group chat. Meanwhile the Moonee Valley Festival used to pack Queens Park with folks who liked community spirit and questionable weather choices, and Time Out even pencilled the Water Lantern Festival into Queens Park plans for late May.

Who’d hate living here? If you can’t handle a suburban main street trying to be a mini inner-city (or you object to heritage houses elbowing in on brunch culture), Moonee Ponds will feel like everyone else’s weekend. If rooftop cinemas and 25-tap beer bars are a personal affront, well — you’re probably happier somewhere quieter.

Who’d love it? Families and young professionals who appreciate walkable streets, parks and an embarrassment of eateries; people who want a bit of riverside calm plus the occasional cinematic recliner upstairs. We’re proud locals — we know every lane, every reopened pub, every new pasticceria that expanded into Penny Lane — and we’ll argue until the next festival that Moonee Ponds is quietly very good at being itself.
