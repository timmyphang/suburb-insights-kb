# Selby (NSW)

Slug: `selby-nsw`

We sometimes joke that Selby, NSW is like a spare word people drop into articles when they’re hunting for a neat headline. Back in the day our claim to fame wasn’t a glossy cafe or a foodie festival — it was a name that turned up in books, bars and cabins scattered around the place.

Our morning routine is mostly making sure the mailbox hasn’t been hijacked by someone mistaking us for Todd Selby. As Broadsheet noted, Todd Selby’s Edible Selby and The Selby projects made a habit of wandering the world photographing creative homes and food people — and yes, we cheer on anyone who gives our postcode a literary cameo. Broadsheet even ran the Svbscription collaboration that felt like a love letter to “home”; we’ll take the compliment, even if it was aimed at a photographer, not our local bakery.

Weekends? We’re realistic. If you want to escape the suburb without actually going anywhere dramatic, Urban List once recommended the Selby Cabin in Victoria — a cosy timber getaway close to the Puffing Billy. We like that idea: a nearby fantasy of rustic glamour we can scroll through and then go back to mowing the lawn.

The most dramatic local chapter was a bar that borrowed our name and set up on the corner of Elizabeth and Redfern Street, looking over Redfern Park. Time Out and Broadsheet wrote about Selby Road’s balcony and its American-style pub snacks — and then, before we could get attached, Broadsheet marked it “permanently closed” in 2019. That’s our pace: briefly glamorous, then quietly back to status quo.

Who would hate living here? People who need constant novelty or like every street to have an artisanal concept store on the corner. They’ll be disappointed when the local claim to fame is a photographer’s book, an Adelaide lane called Selby Street with a Lantern by Nu tucked away, or a cabin you can’t actually get to without leaving town.

Who would love it here? Folks who enjoy low-key pride, a good name coincidence, and a place that wears being unflashy like a badge. We’ll keep the lawn tidy, nicknames in rotation, and a soft spot for any publication that bothers to mention “Selby” at all.
