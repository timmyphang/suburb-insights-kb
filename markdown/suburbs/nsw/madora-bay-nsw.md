# Madora Bay (NSW)

Slug: `madora-bay-nsw`

Back in the day — or at least according to the older local listings we could dig up — Madora Bay made the shortlist for Most Liveable Estate in Mandurah and honestly, it’s all because of the beach. We know this place by its sand, not its scandal.

Our Saturday starts with a walk past 39 Lord Hobart Drive in the vague direction of the shoreline, then a detour to wave at the primary school at 60 Huntington Street. The school used to run pop‑up Rhyme Time with Lakelands Library, because that’s the sort of wholesome thing our noticeboards celebrated. The community playgroup listing promised session details and limited spots — the suburb’s version of a social calendar.

If you’re nostalgic for proper local institutions, you’ll appreciate that the Madora Bay Tavern was recorded as a classic old‑school Aussie pub: founded in 1980, run by the same owners since the 1970s, and once offering pub meals, cold drinks, a TAB, a bottle shop and a deli. That reads less like a menu and more like a municipal hug.

Drama? The archives are scandal‑light. The most contentious thing we found was whether playgroup sessions had room. If you crave bar fights or council controversies, you’ll be bored. If you love sand, sensible pubs and a place that keeps its calendar on community listings rather than gossip blogs, you’ll be smugly content.

Who would hate it: clubbers and anyone allergic to serenity. Who would love it: families, beach walkers and people who appreciate a tavern that remembers when a TAB was a thing. We’ll see you at the shore — quietly, please.
