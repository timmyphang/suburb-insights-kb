# Richmond (NSW)

Slug: `richmond-nsw`

We start the day the only Richmond way we know how: squinting at the horizon wondering which drama will roll out of the bush this week. Back in the day the headlines around here were about footy and factories; now our claim to dramatic fame is a $8 million weed crop that NSW Police torched after someone noticed suspicious vehicles. As Pedestrian.TV reported, officers from Richmond Local Area Command and the NPWS found the site after cars were seen driving into Bundjalung — and honestly, that's peak local theatre.

Our morning ritual is modest. We check the feeds, watch the helicopters if there’s a follow-up, and then get back to the small things that actually matter: tracks to walk, paddocks to glare at, the sort of local chatter that sounds loud to us and insignificant to everyone else. There aren’t a lot of glossy lifestyle roundups in our feed — which suits us fine. We’ve never needed a press list to remind us where Bundjalung starts and the rest of the map ends.

Petty local drama? We’ll take the bush-caper over a boutique spat any day. Sure, headlines about police operations make for good coffee-shop gossip, but we also know the quieter stories: the slow comings and goings of a place that doesn’t pretend to be everything to everyone. If you’re after a suburb that wants to be famous, you’ll be frustrated. If you like your neighbourhood with a side of low-key mystery and the odd agricultural scandal, welcome home.

Who would hate living here: anyone who needs a constant stream of curated brunch content and late-night rooftop DJs. Who would love it: people who value room to breathe, who enjoy the kind of local news that involves National Parks and police taskforces rather than cocktail menus. We’re not a postcard — we’re a bit scrappy, sometimes strangely newsworthy, and very much our own.
