# Warwick (NSW)

Slug: `warwick-nsw`

We’ll level with you: living in Warwick, NSW feels a bit like being part of a long-running identity gag. Some days the internet treats us like the Warwick that’s a historic Southern Downs town with a Jumpers & Jazz winter festival; other days we’re apparently Warwick Farm — the one Broadsheet has lovingly catalogued for its karadjordjeva at Madera Kafe and the 40‑tequila list at El Topo Cantina. Back in the day these were the sorts of mentions that made us squint at the map and think, “Which Warwick are we again?”

Our morning routine is low-key because, well, the clippings are. We don’t have a shiny, recent sit-down listicle to parade; the archive hands us Broadsheet entries and travel pieces. As Broadsheet noted, Warwick Farm used to be where you could point at a menu and say “serbian schnitzel roll” in good faith, and “tex‑mex experimental” without rolling your eyes. Concrete Playground, too, remembers Warwick for being a decent day‑trip base on the road south — Stanthorpe and the Granite Belt got the winking shout-out.

Local drama? The historical files are more “miscellany” than scandal. Pedestrian TV’s archives flagged counterfeit $50 notes hitting businesses in a Queensland Warwick — so if you love a good bogus-bill yarn, the Warwick-name universe has you covered. Junkee, Time Out and Concrete Playground keep bringing up the name Warwick in a very different register: Warwick Thornton, whose films Samson & Delilah and The New Boy keep popping up in reviews and festival pieces. So yes, our postcode shares a name with someone who wins festival applause. Fancy that.

Who would hate it here: people who want up‑to‑the‑minute café guides, clout-by-association Instagram feeds, or a postcode that always shows up first in Google searches. Who would love it: anyone who enjoys a low-key place that isn’t trying too hard, film buffs who enjoy tripping over Warwick Thornton references, and road‑trippers using the old guides to plan detours to Stanthorpe or the coast.

We’ll admit it — the internet’s confused us with farms, festivals and a Fiji resort. We’re okay with that. We’ll keep being Warwick: quietly stubborn, oddly worldly by name, and stubbornly unbothered by the need to be “Gram‑worthy” every day.
