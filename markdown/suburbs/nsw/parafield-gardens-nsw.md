# Parafield Gardens (NSW)

Slug: `parafield-gardens-nsw`

We start our mornings like everyone who’s lived here long enough to know the shortcuts: drop the littlies at Parafield Gardens Primary School on Shepherdson Rd (yes, Sara Broster runs the place), swing past the Parafield Gardens Children’s Centre on Casuarina Dr if the kinder needs a last-minute hug, and pretend the District Centre Outlet at 22/24 Nobby Buckley Drive is a boutique precinct.

Full disclosure: our file of local press is mostly nostalgic. Back in the day (and by that we mean the pre-2024 clippings we still quote at BBQs), Parafield Gardens was the sort of place Sitchu landmarks on school zoning advice — the Mawson Lakes listing reminding everyone we feed into Parafield Gardens High or, for the aspirational, Endeavour College. Playgroup SA once issued that unforgettable 19 March 2020 notice that closed intergenerational playgroups; parents still compare lockdown stories like sporting highlights.

We’ve got community services you brag about when relatives visit: Novita on Salisbury Hwy does speech and physio work you don’t shout about until it changes your kid’s day; Adelaide Bounce-A-Round supplies the jumping castles for every birthday that ends in sticky lollies and loud regret. And yes, Sitchu even remembered to put Parafield on the Christmas-tree farm map — somewhere between Norwood and Glenelg East, apparently we grow festive cheer.

Petty local drama? Mostly paperwork and timetables. The real tiff is whether the OSHC/Vac Care at Parafield Gardens Primary is better organised than your cousin’s rostered school-run — a debate that burns longer than it should.

Who would hate living here? If you need 24/7 late-night restaurants, inner-city skyline selfies, or a parade of ever-changing cafés to judge, you’ll find us politely provincial. We don’t pretend to be somewhere we’re not.

Who would love it? Families who know the names of every teacher, carers who value Novita’s services, parents who turn up to PlayConnect sessions hosted by people like Martine Stockwell and leave “full of joy.” We’re not glamorous. We are useful, loud at 3pm, and memorably ours. If that sounds like home, come for a school drop-off and stay for the birthday jumping castle.
