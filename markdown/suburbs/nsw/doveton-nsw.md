# Doveton (NSW)

Slug: `doveton-nsw`

Back in the day we used to scroll through the internet and wonder if everyone meant the same Doveton as us. MumsPages had whole categories for baby support and services, psychologists and counsellors, dentists and pet services in Doveton (3177) — so mornings for us looked like a convoy of prams and appointments, and a quick check of the local directory before we left the driveway. As the Sitchu listing reminded us, some of us came for the three-bedroom family homes: gas cooking, separate living, central bathroom, a lock-up garage and a private backyard big enough for a dodgy trampoline and genuine regrets.

Then PedestrianTV did that thing it does and pulled apart a "studio unit" in Doveton that was advertised at $320 a week — $1,280 a month — which made us laugh, cry, and then measure our spare rooms for rent potential. The internet is unromantic but useful: one minute it’s telling us we have dentists and pet-services on tap, the next it’s offering hot takes on rental listings.

Meanwhile, Broadsheet had our name in lights — sort of — writing about Mitchell Harris Wine Bar at 38 Doveton Street North and Underbar at 3 Doveton Street North. Both sound delightful, and both are on Doveton Street in Ballarat, which is a confusing flex the internet likes to make. So if you arrived expecting a 28-seat weekly degustation or a repurposed 1870s cellar door on our high street, you’re probably looking at the wrong postcode. We appreciate the aspiration, though.

Who would hate living here? If your entire identity depends on boutique cellar doors being in walking distance or you require a constant rotation of 28-seat degustations, this isn’t your scene — google maps will keep failing you. Who would love it here? Young families, pet owners, people who actually want a backyard and a garage, and anyone who enjoys the practical comforts a Sitchu listing promises and the community services MumsPages catalogues.

We’re Doveton: modest, oddly named on the internet, useful for families, and quietly proud that our biggest dramas are rental listings and the occasional misdirected food critic.
