# Chatswood (NSW)

Slug: `chatswood-nsw`

We’ll be blunt: if you think Chatswood is just another strip of grey office towers, you haven’t walked Victoria Avenue at lunch. We’re the place where your neighbourhood yum cha habit collides with a David Jones flagship and a shiny new Joie Brasserie — because someone decided premium French dining belongs beside the kebab shop. The $625 million Chatswood Chase glow-up means more retailers, more windows to stare into and, yes, more reasons to get lost on Level 3.

Our morning moves are very specific. We wander past The Concourse at 409 Victoria Avenue on the way to whatever market stall is on duty — the Chatswood Mall Market on Victoria Avenue still serves up global snack artillery from 9am–9pm — and then drop into Westfield Chatswood to browse the newly opened Kinokuniya (they opened on April 9, 2026). If you’re here for the culinary staples, we’ve long been a multicultural smorgasbord: Broadsheet and Time Out have both pointed to our now-famous dining precincts — Amah and Mama Mulan get name-checked for a reason, and Star Capital Seafood still packs them in for weekend yum cha.

Petty local drama? We’ve got viral moments instead of neighbourhood feuds. Remember the metro door that stayed open until Crows Nest? Classic Chatswood. And who could forget the GentleMinions suit brigade taking over Westfield — only in our mall would that be a thing people tweet about.

Who would hate it here? If you crave pastoral silence, Chatswood’s constant churn of shoppers, shoppers-in-training (we have an Urban Escape Playground on Level 3) and a David Jones-level mall energy will make you nostalgic for tumbleweed. If 25,000-ish residents and a 15-minute-ish commute to the CBD feels like chaos, you’ll file us under “too much happening.”

Who actually loves it here? Families who live off Bales Park Playground and Kidsday Cafe, culture folks who book The Concourse, bibliophiles finally thrilled by a Kinokuniya within reach, golfers intrigued by Newgreens’ new 12-hole course, and anyone who worships variety at Chatswood Chase’s food precinct. We’re loud, slightly polished, proudly impatient — and we wouldn’t have it any other way.
