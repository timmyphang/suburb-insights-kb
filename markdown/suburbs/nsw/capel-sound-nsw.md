# Capel Sound (NSW)

Slug: `capel-sound-nsw`

We start our mornings like everyone else in Capel Sound: pretending we’re just popping in for a quick query at the Community Hub and somehow leaving three hours later, two craft projects poorer and emotionally responsible for a succulant. Back in the day the place billed itself as “where everyone belongs and every story matters” on the Hub website, and yes — they weren’t bluffing.

We used to roll up for Playgroup Victoria sessions at the Seawinds hub (Playgroup’s venue page even urged people to “Join the Fun! Become a member today.”), which meant more colour-coded activities than our entire street has excuses for missing the bin night. The Hub’s history is almost wholesome enough to make you suspicious: it grew out of the Rosebud West community renewal project and quietly rebranded from Seawinds into the Capel Sound Community Hub. Change with a conscience, if you like.

There’s no local scandal in our files — only the mercifully mundane reality that our claim to fame is organised belonging and a website that pays respect to the Bunurong/Boon Wurrung people. Which is, frankly, a nicer brag than some suburbs can muster.

Who would hate it here? People who need a 24/7 espresso crawl or a skyline to snapchat. Who would love it? Families, lifelong learners and anyone who secretly likes hummus that’s been judged by committee. We’re small, a bit earnest and proudly community-run — and we wouldn’t have it any other way.
