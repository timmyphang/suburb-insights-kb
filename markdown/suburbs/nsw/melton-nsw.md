# Melton (NSW)

Slug: `melton-nsw`

Back in the day we’d start a Saturday in Melton by arguing about pies and public infrastructure like it was a spectator sport. As Concrete Playground and Broadsheet noted, Buddy’s Bakery in Melton (Victoria) once took home top honours at the Great Aussie Pie Competition — so yes, we’ve got bragging rights even if the trophy shelf is in a different postcode’s photo.

Our weekend loop used to be gloriously domestic: a pit stop past High Street where Total Tools sits (169 High Street), a detour to the Melton Community Hall on High Street for whatever meeting is happening that week, and a pilgrimage to the Melton Library — the one Time Out said was the first Australian library awarded a five-star Green Star rating, all floor-to-ceiling windows and timber beams, pretending we’re into sustainability and architecture when really we just want the aircon. If the kids were with us, Playpoint Melton Indoor Sports at 65 Reserve Road was the “let them run until they collapse” option.

Petty local drama? Oh, we’ve had our moments. There was the pop-up drive-thru vaccination hub at 149 Barries Road — at the former Bunnings site — which Concrete Playground covered. We argued about queues like it was the Melbourne Cup. And our lotto shop (Melton Lotto & TSG) quietly flexed by selling 62 division-one tickets over 20 years, which PedestrianTV found mildly astonishing and seriously useful in bingo-game conversations.

Who would hate living here: people who need rooftop bars on every corner, instant nine-car VLocity trains (the upgrade for the Melton line was only due to roll out by 2027 and a new Melton Station was slated for 2026, according to Time Out), or anyone allergic to actual community infrastructure like playgroups and halls.

Who would love it: families and organisers. PlayConnect sessions were listed at Melton West Children and Community Centre (2 West Melton Drive) on Tuesdays 10–12 with contact details even named in the session list — practical, not performative. Grace Community Melton and Combined Churches Caring Melton (CCCM) quietly do the heavy lifting around town. We’ve got a historical society that runs ghost hunts at Willows Homestead, plans for a $900 million hospital were announced to start in 2024, and affordable housing keeps the place lively. In short: we’re proudly ordinary, slightly optimistic, and spectacularly ready to feed you a pie while we explain why our council map is a conversation starter.
