# Oyster Bay (NSW)

Slug: `oyster-bay-nsw`

We live here, in Oyster Bay (2225), and yes — we know the internet has identity issues. Back in the day, every time someone typed "Oyster Bay" they meant Dramatic Pink Granite Mountains and Freycinet, not our cul‑de‑sacs. Travel guides used to fangirl over Freycinet Marine Farm and Oyster Bay Tours — you know, the ones where oysters were shucked to order in Great Oyster Bay, and Piermont Retreat felt like a slow‑paced European village hugging the water. All very romantic. All very Tassie. We wave to them from across the map and keep our esky closed.

Our weekend routine is less Euro‑retreat, more local‑logistics. We scroll MumsPages for photographers and videographers, or for face painters and swimming lessons for the kids — because yes, we actually have tiny humans who need organising, and those directory listings were the kind of lifeline other suburbs brag about having as a town square. Concrete Playground once partnered with Oyster Bay Sparkling Cuvée for a listicle and, honestly, we still keep a bottle in the cupboard for when the neighbour successfully parallel parks on the first try.

If you came here to find oyster shucking tours and kayak tastings like the ones on the east coast of Tasmania, you’ll be disappointed — those delights were covered in Time Out, Urban List and Sitchu about Freycinet and Melshell Oyster Shack, not our local boat ramp. We’re quieter, less postcard, more practical: face‑painter bookings, piano recitals of the toddler kind, and the calming certainty that someone on the street knows a decent photographer for family portraits.

Who would hate living here? People who need to be the centre of weekend travel blog posts, or those who demand daily seaside vistas of the Hazards. Who would love it? Families who actually use the swimming‑lesson listings, people who enjoy a low‑key postcode that doesn’t pretend to be a national park, and anyone who appreciates that our name has a fancy cousin in Tassie — and we’re perfectly okay letting them hog the oysters.
