# Mayfield (NSW)

Slug: `mayfield-nsw`

Back in the day we bragged that our biggest flex wasn’t a rooftop bar but a garden you could get lost in — Mayfield Garden out at Oberon. We used to pile into the car for the few‑hour drive from Sydney and wander a Monet‑inspired water garden, wander hedges like they were set pieces for a BBC period drama, and argue over which bridge deserved a photo for the ‘gram. Broadsheet even said it was open 363 days a year — the sort of uselessly specific fact we parade at dinner parties.

For a spell the place mucked about with glamping pop‑ups (Simple Pleasures Camping Co. pitched luxury tents on the grounds), and Concrete Playground remembers goat‑yoga retreats turning the estate into a very fancy petting zoo. Time Out flagged those seasonal openings too: the Hawkins family’s private grounds were only cracked open for the public a handful of times each year for festivals, which made us feel exclusive and slightly unhinged at once.

Closer to town, the high street had its own modest wins: Sitchu name‑checked Estabar at 1/5 Maitland Road among Newcastle’s good cafes, and listings kept reminding us there’s a Mayfield Woolworths if you ever need to buy emergency milk after the garden has melted your will to cook. Property ads bragged that some of us were “just over 10 minutes from Newcastle’s vibrant CBD,” which is a nice way of saying we enjoy both quiet and the occasional CBD sprint.

Who would hate living here? If you need a tramline to a late‑night cocktail bar and think weekends exist only to queue for new restaurants, this place will drive you mad. We’re not remote‑by‑accident, but we’re also very happy when the horizon is fields and plane trees rather than yet another laneway mural.

Who would love it? People who want wide skies, a garden that doubles as a theme park for picnickers, weekend glampers, and anyone who enjoys festivals and seasonal openings where you can pretend you discovered the place before all the articles did. We’ll keep waving you in — just bring sensible shoes.
