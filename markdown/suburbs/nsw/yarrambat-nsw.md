# Yarrambat (NSW)

Slug: `yarrambat-nsw`

We wake up, put the kettle on, and do the thing every sensible Yarrambat local does: glance at the tap like it might have a secret. Back in the day Broadsheet noted that Yarra Valley Water issued a precautionary water warning that listed 88 Melbourne suburbs — and yes, it included Yarrambat. As Broadsheet noted, it was a precautionary alert, not the end of civilisation, but that hasn’t stopped us from treating it like front-page drama.

Morning routine here is equal parts tea and mild suspicion. We’ll water the garden, but not before we’ve discussed the correct procedure for boiling water at length with anyone who’ll listen. Neighbours form unofficial committees made of good intentions and excessive crockery.

The petty local drama: nothing quite thrills the street like a municipal advisory. It’s less “riot in the streets” and more “window-gazing, nuanced debate about whether to use bottled or tap for pasta.” No council coups, just the warm hum of community organising around the kitchen sink.

Who would hate living here? People who need constant city fuss and ultra-urgent headlines. If your ideal weekend involves a trending hashtag and a daily scandal, you’ll find Yarrambat a little… serene.

Who would love it? Folks who appreciate a suburb that quietly makes the news for sensible precautions, then gets back to mowing, catching up with neighbours and pretending we weren’t a tiny item in Broadsheet’s list of 88. We’re modest, slightly paranoid about our water, and very proud of it.
