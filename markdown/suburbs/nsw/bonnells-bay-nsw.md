# Bonnells Bay (NSW)

Slug: `bonnells-bay-nsw`

Back in the day we used to pride ourselves on having everything you actually need and nothing you don’t. Mornings here start with a brisk walk down to Pendlebury Park — bounded by Grand Parade and the Lake — where dogs go full Baywatch because, yes, it’s officially an off‑leash stretch (as Newy With Kids helpfully lists). If you’ve ever wanted your Labrador to have better hair than you, this is the place.

By mid‑morning the kids are commandeering 275 Fishery Point Road — enter via the Youth and Community Centre car park if you want to avoid circling like it’s peak shopping mall — and we’re not ashamed to admit we cheer. The Bonnells Bay Playground has a web net, cargo net and rock wall that promise scraped knees and durable memories, and beside it the Bonnells Bay Skate Park does its best work as one of the smaller, modular local‑level parks in Lake Macquarie: six‑foot quarter pipes, flat banks, a fun box, a rail and ramps that suit learners and those who still think they’re twenty (as Lake Mac Family Life describes).

If you’ve ever wondered where the youth activities hide, check the basketball court at 275 Fishery Point Road — Newy With Kids even had it down for a Youth Week 2026 event listing. So yes, our civic planning includes things that keep young people upright and marginally less bored.

For parents juggling budget and sanity, Lake Mac Family Life once included the Bonnells Bay Hotel (The Bay Hotel Motel) in a Kids Eat Free guide — the Boatshed Restaurant was listed as offering kids’ meals with ice‑cream, Paddle Pop and activity packs. That was the kind of practical hospitality we appreciate: generous, uncomplicated, and with dessert that requires no cutlery.

Who would hate it here? If you need neon nightlife, artisan everything or 24/7 delivery of obscure brunch accoutrements, Bonnells Bay will politely confuse you. Who would love it? Families who like parks that actually fit in an afternoon, skaters who relish a compact challenge, dog owners who need a proper swim spot, and anyone who prefers real lake air to fake city breeze.

As locals, we’ll keep the skateboards rolling, the dogs splashing and the kids climbing — and the rest of Australia can keep guessing where the best Paddle Pop is hidden.
