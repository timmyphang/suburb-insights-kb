# Newcastle (NSW)

Slug: `newcastle-nsw`

We wake up to the same ocean soundtrack everyone else tries to Instagram — except we actually know where the good vantage is. We stand on the headland, look back at Nobbys Beach and shrug: yes, the Pasha Bulker ran aground here once and we still bring visitors to gawp at the spot Junkee made into a legend. Then it’s a stroll past Merewether Ocean Baths — 50m by 100m, allegedly the biggest ocean pool in the Southern Hemisphere (Time Out told you so), where half the suburb learns to swim and the other half perfects the art of not getting sand in everything.

Weekends used to look like this: an art hit at the newly renovated Newcastle Art Gallery — Broadsheet covered the relaunch and that extra 1,600 square metres worth of stuff (Brett Whiteley, Albert Namatjira, Archie Moore — proper jaw-dropping works). Concrete Playground wrote about the big new shows too, including Torres Strait Islander works that made the gallery feel like ours again. After that, food: Urban List and Concrete Playground used to point out hotspots across Hamilton, New Lambton, Wickham and Honeysuckle — places like St. Lucia and Peregrin were on the lists, and Crumb in Lambton opened in 2025 with artisanal pastries and single-origin coffee, if you like to queue for provenance.

Our petty civic drama? The usual: a proposed 12,000-seat arena for Broadmeadow and plans to hand The Station Newcastle a 99-year lease to become a bakery/wine-bar/gastropub precinct — Urban List said the restoration plans were aiming for 2027, which either excites or terrifies depending on how much you like heritage buildings being repurposed.

Who would hate living here: people allergic to wide horizons, big ocean pools, or spontaneous family-friendly chaos. If you require a sleepy, detached cul-de-sac with no festivals, we’re not your vibe — we had an Easter Fair at the Racecourse in 2026 and Strummingbird chose our stages for its lineup.

Who would love it: families (Newy with Kids and Newcastlemums keep telling us why), art lovers, beach lovers and anyone who thinks underrated is code for “it’s ours, leave it alone” — Time Out once put us in the world’s top 10 most underrated spots, and frankly we plan to keep it that way.
