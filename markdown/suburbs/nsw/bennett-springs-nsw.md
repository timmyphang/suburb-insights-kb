# Bennett Springs (NSW)

Slug: `bennett-springs-nsw`

We’re the sort of locals who rate suburbs by their dog park, so here’s our take on Bennett Springs — or at least the Bennett Springs our inbox found. Back in the day the listings pointed to the 6063 lot over in WA: Slate Cafe at 12 Dulwich Street was the thing everyone wrote home about.

Mornings used to look predictably civilised: we’d fold the leashes, shuffle the kids into the car and amble to Slate Cafe — Urban List called it “modern Australian with Asian influences,” Concrete Playground liked that it had a fully enclosed, grassed dog playground. It was the sort of place where the espresso machine and the squeaky ball had an uneasy truce. The address — 12 Dulwich Street, Bennett Springs — even made the maps feel official.

There’s no juicy council scandal in the files we found, just practical suburbia: MumsPages lists for Fitness, Family Support and even Carpet in Bennett Springs, plus Galleria Art Studio noting it among its service areas. Translation: this place is organised, domestic and slightly obsessed with activity timetables.

Who’d hate living here? People who crave late-night drama, neon nightlife or a postcode that doubles as a punchline. If you despise grassed dog runs, community classes and the soothing mundanity of “which carpet shall we choose?”, move along.

Who’d love it? Families, dog people and anyone who likes their suburb with a schedule — fitness classes, family support services and an art studio that does kids’ programs. For years the local listings said it all; we’ll defend that wholesome predictability with a soft, knowing snort.
