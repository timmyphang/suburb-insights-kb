# Bentleigh (NSW)

Slug: `bentleigh-nsw`

Back in the day we’d ease into a Bentleigh weekend like a ritual: Centre Road hums, the kids are half-asleep in the back and we queue for something dangerously flaky. Artisanal Bakehouse on Centre Road used to draw those lines — cinnamon scrolls, garlic‑butter croissants and St Ali coffee in hand as we strutted past the shops that make up our little spine.

Centre Road is our main drag and it tells the story: Saul’s Diner sits on the corner of Mackie and Centre Road, Merchant’s Guild used to tempt us at 680 Centre Road with doughnut French toast, and the eternally smoky Centre Charcoal Chicken (they rotate the bird over coal for an hour and 20 minutes) kept the takeaway runs honest. For those who like their brunch with a side of design, District Brewer quietly did its thing on Brewer Road; Time Out even wrote nice things about the place.

We remember the corner that used to be a rundown convenience store at Tucker and Patterson Roads — it got the pink makeover and Good Times Milk Bar moved in, all powder‑pink and Golden Gaytime cheekiness. Little Z arrived in Patterson Road in 2023, a proper family labour of love by Georgia and Greg Poliwodzinski; their spiced‑pumpkin toasties and Polish cheesecake often sold out before we could blink. Then there’s the Eastern European cornerstones — Kazachok Deli and Fine Gourmet Deli — stacked with hams, caviar and things we pretend we understand.

There was a minor soap-opera moment when Mr Burger set up at 416 Centre Road promising craft beer and outdoor space — and then the listing was marked closed. No pitched battles, just the gentle ebb and flow of local hospitality.

If you hate queues, kids, or the smell of coal, you’ll probably hate living here. If you’re after a place that’s quietly family‑friendly — McKinnon Secondary College in the area, playgroups at St Paul’s Primary, and even fitness options like Moving Through Motherhood on 9 Scanlan St for new mums — Bentleigh has that suburban rhythm. Heck, we even had PicklePlay at 1 South Drive in Bentleigh East for anyone keen on indoor racket chaos.

Who would love us? Young families, people who rate walkable shops over skyline views, and anyone who thinks Centre Road’s mix of bakeries, delis and stodgy charcoal chicken is the whole point of life. We know our quirks. We like them.
