# Bligh Park (NSW)

Slug: `bligh-park-nsw`

We start our Saturday at Tiningi Hall — 139 Colonial Drive — with the Hawkesbury Busy Bees doing their Tuesday ritual (yes, we know the calendar better than the council). If you’ve ever judged a suburb by its playgroup roll call, you’ll understand why we’re quietly smug: our kids can sing nursery rhymes and locate the nearest community centre in under a minute.

Back in the day most of our press mentions were the sort that make you proud rather than famous — RCSI’s Bligh Park Community Services quietly holding the fort, the “Stronger Together” network keeping neighbourhood centres honest, and a MumsPages listing that reads like a directory of survival strategies for parents. If the internet had a heart, it would be our community services page.

We do get headlines, but only the wholesome kind. Concrete Playground once noted that Bligh & Barney Reserve featured in a list when alcohol restrictions were temporarily lifted — yes, there was a time the park got news coverage for reasons other than which family forgot the picnic rug. And if you remember the Urban List tease about AURA Spa & Bathhouse opening in the Hawkesbury, well — that was a glamour shot for the wider region, more “one-hour escape” than “we’ll see you between the cul-de-sacs”.

We’ll admit it: our media footprint is small and sensible. No late‑night club reviews; no restaurant feuds. Mostly playgroups, parks, and community support pages. That’s our vibe, and we own it.

Who would hate living here? People who treat their postcode like a nightlife username — if you need three Michelin stars and a cocktail menu written in Italian, we’ll wave you off with love and a map to Parramatta. Who would love it here? Families who want Tiningi Hall on speed dial, carers who actually use the Bligh Park Community Services, and anyone who thinks a good Saturday involves a playgroup, a patch of grass at Bligh & Barney Reserve, and neighbours who bring an extra blanket.

We won’t pretend we’re viral — our glory is quieter. We’ll take it.
