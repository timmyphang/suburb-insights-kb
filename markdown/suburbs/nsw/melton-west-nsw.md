# Melton West (NSW)

Slug: `melton-west-nsw`

We know exactly where the best carpeted patch of suburbia is: the Melton West Children and Community Centre. Back in the day the Playgroup Victoria pages read like a love letter to sensible community infrastructure — a small carpeted room with access to an outdoor children’s play space, kindergarten and maternal and child health services. Riveting stuff, really.

Our weekend routine was never brunch or barre — it was carting the small people to that exact carpet, watching them conquer the slide, then pretending to read the noticeboard. PlayConnect+ used to list a Melton Tuesday 10:00am–12:00pm session at 2 West Melton Drive, and if you wanted the human touch the PDF named Tara De Bono as the contact. That is very much community admin porn.

There’s no local scandal here — the public record is essentially playgroup timetables and lovely descriptions of communal carpet. If you were hoping for a juicy feud, you’ll have to settle for the thrilling controversy of whether the play mat should face north.

Who would hate living here? Someone who judges suburbs by the number of artisanal donut shops they can Instagram instead of by the quality of the nursery rhyme acoustics in the small community room. Who would love it? Parents who value kindergarten, maternal and child health services and an honest-to-goodness outdoor children’s play space over headline-making nightlife.

Melton West: we’re modest, carpeted and utterly dependable. Come for the playgroup, stay for the laminated newsletter.
