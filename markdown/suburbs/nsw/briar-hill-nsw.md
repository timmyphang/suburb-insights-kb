# Briar Hill (NSW)

Slug: `briar-hill-nsw`

We know how this looks: a sleepy postcode that grandparents brag about and city types paste into a weekend driving orbit. Back in the day Broadsheet bundled us into a PWC City Pulse write-up — "Montmorency – Briar Hill" landed at number five of Melbourne's best suburbs, and the piece said suburbs with bigger blocks and more open space were the new flex. As locals, we still like saying that out loud when someone asks where we're from.

Our chief claim to fame — at least in print — was pastry-based. Urban List once wrote up Melbourne's best vanilla slices and Briar Hill Traditional Bakehouse got a shout: our vanilla slice was even nicknamed a "snot block" by admirers. We will neither confirm nor deny the glory of that description, only that we've worn it like a badge.

There isn't much tabloid fodder here — the press mentions are older, so this is more nostalgia than a current hot take. We aren't pretending there's a craft beer bar on every corner or a mural on the train bridge; what we do have is space that used to make lists and a bakery that once made strangers very opinionated.

Who would hate it: anyone who needs a 24/7 cultural soundtrack, rooftops with DJ booths or the latest café drop. Who would love it: people after quieter streets, actual yards, and a vanilla slice with a personality strong enough to be remembered in a listicle. We like it that way.
