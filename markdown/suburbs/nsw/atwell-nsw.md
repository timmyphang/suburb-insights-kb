# Atwell (NSW)

Slug: `atwell-nsw`

Back in the day The Urban List put our little patch on the map for two very serious reasons: one house at 6 Aromatic Crescent was, allegedly, auditioning to be a theme park with its multi‑coloured inflatables, and a pastel‑drenched café called Flora & Fauna used to be tucked into Atwell Arcade, slinging the sort of vegan brunches that made people take photos they’d pretend were candid. We still drive past the lights — like visiting a celebrity who never asked to be famous.

Our weekend loop, according to the dusty files of local internet lore, went something like this: a cautious detour to Atwell Arcade to ogle Flora & Fauna (3 Cantonment St), a slow circling of Stargate Shopping Centre so someone can remember if the dentist is still there, and a mental check that Harvest Lakes Medical Centre on 23 Gibbs Road is still the sort of place local parents used to rely on for bulk‑billing if things went awry. The My Community Directory tells us we pack roughly 3.6 km² of suburbs into this postcode, surrounded by Aubin Grove, Banjup, Cockburn Central, Hammond Park, Jandakot, Success and Treeby — which is a very tidy way of saying we’re quietly positioned in the City of Cockburn, state electorate of Jandakot, federal electorate of Fremantle.

Petty local drama? The one we can point to with confidence is that Atwell Playgroup has closed. That’s in the record. No dramatic council meltdowns, no celebrity cafés opening and immediately closing — just a playgroup gone, and the kind of small‑town inconvenience that makes parents circulate email chains like contraband.

Who would hate living here: anyone who needs late‑night ramen, Michelin‑adjacent theatre, or a permanent lineup outside a bar they can brag about. Who would love it: families and people who appreciate practical things — an Atwell Medical Centre that was listed at Shop 10, Stargate Shopping Centre, 129 Lydon Boulevard, a local physiotherapy clinic, and a neighbourhood that’s content to be cozy rather than loud.

We’re proud of our understated sort of famous: a house that takes Christmas seriously, a café that once moved in and made us look fashionable, and the sensible little services that keep us ticking. Welcome to Atwell — small, slightly glowing at night, and quietly useful.
