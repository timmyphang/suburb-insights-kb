# Greenfield Park (NSW)

Slug: `greenfield-park-nsw`

We start the day like everyone else — coffee, inbox, a desperate scroll to see if anyone finally wrote about Greenfield Park. Spoiler: they didn’t. What showed up instead were other Greenfields doing the hard work for us.

Back in the day Concrete Playground ran a Lunar New Year guide and flagged the Hoi Cho Tet Vietnamese Lunar New Year Festival at CJ Greenfield Complex Park — 340 Freeman Road, Richlands — with the lot: food stalls, lion dances, fireworks and calligraphy. As Concrete Playground noted, that was Richlands’ moment in the sun, not ours. Meanwhile, a stateside parenting blog, WilmingtonMom, kept reminding us that “Greenfield Lake Park” exists over in Wilmington and that you should “watch out for alligators” if you go paddling. We are flattered by the global identity crisis.

Petty local drama? Our whole feud is with Google autocomplete. We measure success by how long it takes a courier to realise they’ve got the wrong Greenfield. Once you’re on the receiving end of “Did you mean Richlands?” three times a week, you develop a dry sense of humour and a collection of screenshots.

Who would hate living here: people who need to be on every festival list, or anyone allergic to ambiguity. If your life plan involves frequent mentions in lifestyle roundups or concerns about alligators during weekend paddle-boarding, you’ll be disappointed — Concrete Playground’s parade was Richlands’, and Wilmington’s lake keeps the gators on the other side of the planet.

Who would love it here: people who like being quietly mistaken for somewhere else. We enjoy the anonymity — it’s surprisingly liberating. We get the small-town vibes without having to compete for headline festivals, and we can mock the mix-ups with the best of them. If you appreciate a suburb that lets other places have the glory while you keep the peace, you’ll fit right in.
