# Millers Forest (NSW)

Slug: `millers-forest-nsw`

Back in the day our lot was a 29 km² shrug on the map and we’ve been owning that shrug ever since. We’re Millers Forest — proud, sprawling and smugly listed in My Community Directory as part of the Maitland family. If you want a postcode that says “room to breathe” on the tin, this is it.

Our morning consists of a reconnaissance loop through the suburbs that surround us – Berry Park, Duckenfield, Heatherbrae, Hinton, Nelsons Plains, Osterley, Raymond Terrace, Thornton and Woodberry – because why go to the gym when you can judge neighbouring fences for free? The community directory dutifully lists our health services, dentists and local clubs, which is handy when someone inevitably sprains their ego, if not an actual limb.

Pettiest local drama? For years the main squabble has been whether the directory’s search filters call us “rural” or “suburban.” We argue about it at the barbecue, then Google it later because what else are we going to do on a Thursday?

Who would hate living here: anyone who needs 24/7 neon-lit convenience, a constant stream of late-night options, or a postcode that doubles as a personality. Who would love it: people after space, neighbours who know your name without being creepy, and anyone who likes being part of the Maitland council patch — state electorate of Maitland, don’t forget — but still having enough scenery to pretend you’re off the grid.

We don’t need to be flashy. We’re Millers Forest: preferable to chaos, preferable to suburbia, and listed for your convenience.
