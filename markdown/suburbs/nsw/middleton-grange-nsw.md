# Middleton Grange (NSW)

Slug: `middleton-grange-nsw`

Back in the day we’d drag the crew out of bed, pile into the inevitable carpool and aim for the aeroplane at Stante Reserve — the water playground that opened in late 2021 and became our unofficial training ground for sticky hair and soggy socks. ParraParents made sure everyone knew about that one; if you’ve got kids, you’ve been there, or at least queued outside.

We know our patch by smell and by path: the southern edge of Western Sydney Parklands is basically our backyard. There’s a scenic loop between Middleton Grange and West Hoxton that Concrete Playground flagged — easy enough for prams, interesting enough for people who still pretend they like bushland. Then there’s the big-hearted walk that Time Out wrote up: the Gabrugal Yana 11km trail that winds through Cumberland Plain woodland, dotted with six landmark trees hosting Aboriginal artworks and audio pieces developed with Dharug cultural consultants and First Nations artists. It’s the kind of trail where the phone dies and the local humility kicks in.

If you’ve ever tried to get anything organised here, you’ll have met the parenting-industrial complex: Community Kids had a centre listed at 10–12 Broadbent Ave, and yes, directories like MumsPages and Community Kids carted us all through choices, CCS calculators and program lists until we could recite opening hours in our sleep. They even listed policy bits like the 3‑Day Guarantee update — thrilling dinner party conversation, frankly.

Petty local drama? We don’t need it. Our real conflict is the perennial family tug-of-war: playground time vs. nap time vs. whether the aeroplane is ‘our’ aeroplane. No council ruckus necessary; we’ll bicker over the swing rotation and call it community engagement.

Who would hate it here: people who crave a slick café crawl, late-night chic or a postcode that pretends it’s somewhere else. Who would love it: families who want space, folk who hike the Gabrugal Yana and nod at the artworks, and anyone who measures a good weekend by how glittery the kids’ hair is at 4pm.

Middleton Grange — a little wet, a lot grounded, and ours to mock with affection.
