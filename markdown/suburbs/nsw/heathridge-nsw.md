# Heathridge (NSW)

Slug: `heathridge-nsw`

We like to start our weekends like every proud local: pretending we live in a sleepy burb while queueing for the thing that made the northside interesting for a hot minute. Back in the day the headlines loved Runyeh Coffee at 11/99 Caridean St — Josh McGinnity’s roastery that was billed as a northern burbs favourite, “always heaving” and even the place to get a Mont Blanc iced coffee treat. We remember the hype like it was yesterday, even if the column bylines are from an era that now reads like vintage newsprint.

For years the local child-rearing beat was equally well covered — Whitford Family Centre used to be the go‑to name in our corner: pre‑kindy, playgroup, out of school care and a toy library, all listed under “we serve Heathridge” on their pages. There was even an Expression of Interest noted for 2027 pre‑kindy programs — which, now that we’ve dug through the archives, feels like an optimistic bookmark.

Petty local drama? The press mostly ran feature pieces, not feuds, so our scandal is mild: we fight over benches at Runyeh (when it was full) and who gets the last table near the playground. That’s our version of a council debate. If you were hoping for a saga about development or a Very Public Dispute, sorry — the internet gave us listicles and family-centre timetables instead.

Who would hate living here: anyone allergic to small talk at the school gate, or people who think a suburb without a front-page scandal is “boring.” Also, if you despise kids and coffee-fuelled weekends, Heathridge will feel like a relentless sunrise of playgroups and café queues.

Who would love it here: families who like the idea of a local toy library, parents who cherished the Whitford Family Centre’s wraparound programs, and anyone who remembers Runyeh as the northern burbs’ boast — a place that felt quietly alive without needing daily headlines. We’ll take that, archival articles and all.
