# Donvale (NSW)

Slug: `donvale-nsw`

Back in the day we used to joke Donvale was the kind of place drones fancied: quiet, tidy, and full of people who’d wave at a thing that hummed over the gumtrees. There haven’t been many fresh write-ups lately, so we’re leaning on the old news — remember when Wing teamed with DoorDash to drone-deliver from chains like Schnitz, Sharetea, Laurent Bakery, Gelatissimo and Betty’s Burgers to our postcode? It felt like the future, for about a week, then life carried on.

Mornings here are very suburban: the apartments on Mitcham Road are a short stumble from Tunstall Square shops, the kids still head to Donvale Primary and Mullauna Secondary College, and Donvale Reserve is where weekend footy gets trodden into the grass. We know every shortcut and which side of the reserve the sun hits at 4pm.

Our petty drama? That time Knox Football Club had to apologise after reported sexist behaviour toward Donvale Football Club players — 3AW’s Jacqui Felgate covered it and we had front-row seats to the awkward community clean-up. We’re not pretending we’re scandal-free; we just don’t make a habit of turning it into reality TV.

Who would hate living here: if you crave late-night cafes and a skyline view, you’ll get bored fast. Who would love it: families who appreciate good schools, anyone who likes a proper local reserve, and people who want a postcode that quietly gets on with things — even when the drones fly by.
