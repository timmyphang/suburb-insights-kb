# Arcadia Vale (NSW)

Slug: `arcadia-vale-nsw`

We know exactly what time the suburb wakes up: Tuesday, 9:30am. Back in the day (and in the listings that keep showing up on Playgroup NSW and Newy With Kids), Arcadia Vale Mini Kookas used to assemble at Arcadia Vale Public School — 31A Glade Street — like a tiny, polite invasion. We’ve been there. We’ve navigated the stroller parking. We’ve judged the thermos game.

As Playgroup NSW notes, the Mini Kookas met weekly and, if you were sensible, you emailed arcadiavaleplaygroup@gmail.com before wandering in. Newy With Kids also reminded everyone the group didn’t run during school holidays, because even the toddlers need a break from our civic duty of sharing snacks.

There isn’t much gossip in the files — which suits us. We prefer quiet cheeky competence to manufactured kerfuffle. The local P&C kept the organisational backbone — see the Arcadia Vale P&C page — which means this place runs on goodwill, paperwork and someone who volunteers twice a term and is secretly thrilled about it.

Who would hate it here? If you require rooftop bars, 24/7 latte artisans, or a boutique that stocks artisanal anything, this will feel like a retreat to the dark ages. The misfits are the ones who can’t appreciate a community that celebrates sticker sheets and shared juice boxes.

Who would love it? Families who like predictable routines, neighbours who actually swap phone numbers, and anyone who finds solace in a Tuesday morning where the loudest thing is a chorus of small people learning how to share. We’re proud of that.
