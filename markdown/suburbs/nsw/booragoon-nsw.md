# Booragoon (NSW)

Slug: `booragoon-nsw`

Back in the day we’d slide out of the house, do our rounds and call it a Sunday in Booragoon — because all the reporting we’ve got on hand is from an earlier era, we’re doing this nostalgic tour like it’s a mixtape.

We start at Westfield Booragoon, the circuit everyone knows. As Urban List and Melville Mums kept reminding us, that shopping hub has been where Bang On Brows set up, and Adore Beauty’s Perth push “landed” there when they opened a bricks‑and‑mortar. Westfield isn’t glamorous, it’s functional: drop‑off point for the recycling drive and the place where retail announcements like By Charlotte’s WA arrival quietly make the rounds.

Morning laps were a ritual at LeisureFit Booragoon — the old write‑ups name the address (521 Marmion Street) and the pool setup: a 50m indoor pool, a heated leisure pool and a kids’ spray area. Parents in the Mum blogs swore by the creche there (8 weeks to pre‑primary; fee notes even popped up in listings), and the occasional child‑care centre at 525 Marmion Street got a shoutout for the hourly option. We remember the town’s tiny infrastructure like it’s our own: EK Dance Academy running mid‑week classes at the Booragoon Scout Hall on the corner of Karoonda Road and Sicklemore Street, plant people heading to Little Leaf Co at Shop 4, 496 Marmion Street, and even Mobile Skips Perth quietly claiming local ownership.

There isn’t much tabloid scandal in the files — mostly lifestyle blurbs and service listings — so the local drama is the gentle kind: will we make it to sauna and ice bath? Sitchu once called Sauna and Ice Bath Booragoon a best‑kept secret; Anna Stibbs was named as its owner, which sounds very civilised until you’re shivering.

Who would hate living here? If you crave neon nightlife or a constant stream of “what’s new” headlines, Booragoon’s suburban practicalities and family‑first listings will bore you to tears.

Who would love it? Young families, people who judge suburbs by the quality of their creche and pool, plant obsessives and bargain‑hunter mall veterans. We’ll keep the quiet hush of our little hub — and the receipts from LeisureFit — to ourselves, thank you very much.
