# Redwood Park (NSW)

Slug: `redwood-park-nsw`

We’re the lot who know where every gum‑leaf shadow lands on 2–10 Lokan St, so yes — we will judge your picnic basket. Mornings here are a gentle tumble of schoolbags, tiny handprints on car windows and the regular chorus from the Redwood Park Nature and Sensory Playgroup — they meet Wednesdays 9–11am during school terms at the preschool, and yes, we checked the update (05/04/2026). If you haven’t seen toddlers learn the art of mud appreciation, you haven’t lived.

Back in the listing days (older online entries), Redwood Park School was the anchor at 2–10 Lokan St — the name on the staff list was Mr Ian Dickie, and there’s the old school website redwoodparkps.sa.edu.au and phone 08 8263 8333 if you feel like time travelling through school administration. An older community entry also recorded Redwood Park OSHC at the primary school, with Before School Care 7:00–8:45am, After School Care 3:05–6:00pm and Vacation Care 7:00–6:00pm — and the OSHC email redwoodpark.oshc@schools.sa.edu.au if you prefer your nostalgia via inbox.

Urban List once lumped us in with the autumn‑leaf pilgrimage circuit — “Redwood Park, which is exactly what it sounds like,” they said, and told picnic people to pack baskets. We’ll own it: the trees do blush nicely in autumn, and there is a subtle pride in being the sort of place an out‑of‑towner thinks is a movie set for wholesome family montages.

Local drama? The archives are mercifully dull — our most ferocious debate is whether the playgroup should add more sensory bins. We argue about glitter and compost like it’s a referendum. It’s thrilling in the way only small‑scale, very utilitarian civic passion can be.

Who would hate it here? People who crave nonstop nightlife, anonymous high streets and keeping their shoes spotless. Who would love it? Parents who want before/after school cover, carers who adore nature‑based play, and anyone who thinks a Wednesday morning full of organised sensory chaos is peak community life.

We’re quietly smug about our little patch. Come for the leaves, stay for the playgroup chaos, leave with a jam‑smeared smile.
