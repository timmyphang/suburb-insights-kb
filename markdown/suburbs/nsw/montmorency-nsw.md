# Montmorency (NSW)

Slug: `montmorency-nsw`

Saturday morning in Montmorency is a battleground of guitar chords and lung capacity. We shepherd kids to Weekends Playgroup, where Marissa — MJ to anyone who’s been once — will pull out her guitar and somehow get a chorus of toddlers and exhausted parents to harmonise like we rehearsed the whole thing. She even ended up a Playgroup Champion runner‑up in 2024, which, yes, we remind each other of at every BBQ.

From there it's a short amble to the footy oval, because of course it is. Our Montmorency Football Netball Club has been here since 1924 and wears that history like a jumper: proud, slightly woolly and impossible to ignore. They play in the Northern Football Netball League, keep us updated via a Linktree, and in June 2026 celebrated player milestones and match rounds — which means there will be a cake and a speech, and you will pretend you heard both.

Once upon a time (or at least in the listings) there was Australian Gas & Industrial Supplies at 52 Sherbourne Road — a family‑owned place with more than 45 years of experience. We used to pop our heads in for things you don’t ask about politely at dinner parties, and there was comfort knowing someone in our postcode could source it. That piece of Montmorency industriousness is part of the local texture, even if directories are the only ones who can prove it.

Back in the day Broadsheet noted (via a PwC City Pulse study) that Montmorency — often paired with Briar Hill on top‑20 lists — once rose as high as number five for Melbourne suburbs. We like that headline because it gives us something smug to say when visitors ask where our nearest artisanal anything is.

Who would hate living here? People who prefer anonymity to community singalongs, anyone allergic to weekend football fixtures, or those whose ideal Saturday involves more espresso machines and fewer volunteer coaches. Who would love it? Families who want actual neighbours, people who value a club that still posts match round updates in June 2026, and anyone who enjoys a suburb that’ll happily make a song out of your chaos and sing along.
