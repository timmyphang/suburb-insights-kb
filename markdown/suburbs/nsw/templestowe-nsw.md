# Templestowe (NSW)

Slug: `templestowe-nsw`

Back in the day we’d wake up, squint at the hills and decide whether today called for art or dumplings. We’re the sort of place where a drive through the undulating northeastern hills could end at Down The Rabbit Hole — yes, the Alice-in-Wonderland café those guides loved — or at the Heide Museum of Modern Art on 7 Templestowe Road, Bulleen, depending on how ambitious we felt.

There’s a rhythm to our weekends. Mornings used to begin with a rabbit-themed brunch (as Urban List and Time Out both once reminded us), afternoons were for wondering at Nell’s idiosyncratic ghosts or getting properly lost in the John Perceval survey at Heide, and late arvo meant packing a rug down by Fitzsimons Lane because Sitchu said it’s one of the nicer picnic spots out this way. If you wanted noisy family energy, The Templestowe Hotel — which Urban List flagged for its indoor playground next to the bar — would happily supply it.

We should admit: our media file is a bit nostalgic. There aren’t any recent scoops in the stack, so consider this a fondly dusted tour of what the guides used to tell visitors. As Time Out put it, we’ve always been quieter than Eltham and Doncaster — which is both a compliment and a polite warning.

Who would hate living here? If you crave sliced-by-the-minute nightlife, cramped apartments and a constant parade of things-to-do within walking distance, we’re probably not for you. We’re more about space, gardens and weekend sport — Sitchu even named us among suburbs with “room to stretch.”

Who would love it here? Families (the Templestowe Hotel’s play HQ was a classic), anyone who worships dumplings at Golden Dragon Palace, people who like their museums to feel like a house with a sculpture garden, and those who value green river stretches that got a scientific nod for swimming talk in broader coverage of the Yarra.

We’re polite, a bit leafy, and prone to recommending a low-key cultural hit over a late-night bar crawl. Come for the art, stay for the dumplings, and if you see us on the weekend, don’t be surprised if we’re arguing over the best spot for a picnic rug.
