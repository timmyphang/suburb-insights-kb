# Bibra Lake (NSW)

Slug: `bibra-lake-nsw`

Back in the day our suburb sounded like a school excursion playlist on repeat: rollercoaster screams, the squeak of skate blades and the occasional roar from a kid who’s just discovered a waterslide named Sea Serpents. We know Bibra Lake by the timetables — Saturdays at 8am meant Bibra Lake parkrun, and the rest of the weekend got chewed up by family outings.

If you grew up here you memorised the addresses like a sacred map: Adventure World at 351 Progress Drive — Time Out kept reminding everyone it was our staple for “twistiest rollercoasters” and water slides; Cockburn Ice Arena at 401 Progress Drive, which reopened as a proper rink and came with onsite cafés like Frostbite Café and Cabin 401; and Flip Out at 7 Sobek Pass for the kids who needed to be exhausted by someone else’s trampoline. Urban List even listed PowerPlay and the Grand Prix karting track as the place to settle sibling rivalries.

We’d queue for bánh mì from Bibra Lake Lunch Bar and pretend it made the family picnic gourmet. Perth Mums Group wrote glowingly about Chipmunks Playland and Café while also documenting the era when Squids closed its doors — a reminder that our indoor play scene has seen more comings and goings than a school fete. The Bibra Lake Scout Group once landed a spot in Grill’d Cockburn’s Local Matters, which felt like a small civic win for badge sewing. And yes, the Bibra Lake Regional Playground became the place every pram seemed to find.

There’s no local scandal here worth a front page — our drama is much smaller and far more wholesome: will the kids get more wet on Sea Serpents or slip on the ice first? Will mum make it from the parkrun finish line to Frostbite Café for a coffee? (Karvan beans were even getting a name-check down the line in Cottesloe’s North Street Store.)

Who would hate living here? People who crave boutique nightlife or uninterrupted quiet on a Saturday morning. Who would love it? Families, scouts, runners, thrill-seekers and anyone who considers a theme park, an ice arena and a karting track a reasonable suburban amenity. As Time Out said, Adventure World kept delighting — and honestly, that’s pretty much the vibe of our lot.
