# Evanston Gardens (NSW)

Slug: `evanston-gardens-nsw`

Back in the day our Saturday crawl was a short one: a jaunty shuffle down Angle Vale Rd to where the Evanston Gardens Community Centre used to be at 65 Angle Vale Rd and the primary school sat across the road at 71. We remember the community map being suspiciously small and very efficient.

Mornings were library-mornings — the Evanston Gardens Library kept proper, modest hours (Mon 9–12; Tue & Wed 14–17; Thu 10–13; Fri 9–12), which meant you timed your borrowing like it was a limited-release sneaker drop. The toy library and the Friends of Gawler Library were also on the roster, so yes: tiny people’s entertainment was taken seriously.

The Progress Association used to do the heavy lifting for local advocacy — SAcommunity lists the Evanston Gardens Progress Association Inc., with Nathan Lee named as a contact — and the Community Centre was affiliated with it. Hall hire was a thing (egpainc@gmail.com got the emails; there were also phones on the listing), so if you wanted to run a sausage sizzle, someone at least knew who to ask.

Who would hate it here? If you need a 24/7 espresso crawl or late-night culture, this place will feel like a long nap. Who would love it? Families who like their school principal to be a name you can actually google (Nikki Priestman was listed), neighbours who prefer committee meetings to cocktails, and anyone who enjoys a suburb that does things deliberately, on local time.

As SAcommunity noted, Evanston Gardens wasn’t flashy — and we’re quietly proud of that.
