# Beveridge (NSW)

Slug: `beveridge-nsw`

We stand on what everyone with a map and a mortgage spreadsheet calls “Beveridge” and shrug like locals who’ve been asked exactly this question since Time Out wrote about our future. Time Out reported (yes, reported) that Beveridge North West was slated to become a whole new suburb — 15,000 homes, four town centres, eight schools and enough parks to make a dog walker weep. For years the idea of 47,000 Victorians and 3,000 jobs turning up here felt like a council press release we read while making toast. Still, we like the ambition.

Back in the day, a shopping guide — Sitchu — even listed 445 Old Sydney Road, Beveridge as part of the Coburg Christmas Trees roundup. So when visitors ask where to find our choicest pine, we point vaguely down Old Sydney Road and say, “It was on a list once,” which is a very Beveridge answer to directions.

We also enjoy sharing a name with a confusingly large tribe of Beveridges on the internet. Concrete Playground name‑checked Naomi Beveridge in a festival lineup; Pedestrian.TV has a whole tag page for Michael Beveridge and even recounted his worst date; Judith Beveridge crops up in poetry-night lineups; Darrell Beveridge gets a shoutout in jerky coverage. It’s comforting to know if you can’t find a local headline here, someone with our name probably will do it for you somewhere else.

Who would hate living here? If your personal identity requires latte art, a weekly farmers’ market and a tram every four minutes, Beveridge will feel like a performance review you weren’t meant to pass. If you think “suburb” should always mean “already finished,” this place will be a perpetual, dusty construction site in your nightmares.

Who would love it here? People who like potential, elbow room and the idea that a suburb can be built with actual schools and parks pencilled in from day one. Families, planners, anyone who enjoys watching a map slowly become a town — that’s our crowd. We’re not posh. We’re not done. We’re Beveridge, and we’ll meet you at the future town centre (or at least where Time Out told us it’ll be).
