# Whalan (NSW)

Slug: `whalan-nsw`

Saturday mornings in Whalan: someone’s putting out folding chairs, the kettle’s doing its best, and we’re pretending “deep and meaningful” isn’t code for swapping recipies and childcare hacks. Back in the day, that ritual had a blueprint — MamaCollective’s Discussion kit — and we leaned into it like only locals do.

As MamaCollective puts it, a Discussion is two hours of peer‑facilitated chinwags that actually go somewhere. Their Resources page even had a Facilitator Guide and communication templates, and the Connect page introduced founder Tiere Hessert and Bridget Mariner (Head of Operations) — who, yes, we grudgingly credit for making organised small talk feel classy. There was a sign‑up form if you wanted to run one yourself; we once nearly convinced the whole street to try it. Nearly.

We’re not drama people, but we will roast you for skipping the kettle. If you flinch at the idea of purposeful, in‑person gatherings or the phrase “monthly topic guides,” you’ll hate living here — you’ll miss the honest chaos that comes with neighbours who actually show up. Conversely, if you’re a parent who wants support without the judgement, you’ll love Whalan: we’ve got the habit of connection, and a pile of downloadable tools to prove we’re not winging it.

So yes, Whalan wears its community like an old jumper — a bit stretched, a bit stubborn, and surprisingly warm. If MamaCollective helped shape that, well — we’ll keep the thermos heated.
