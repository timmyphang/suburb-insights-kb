# Rowville (NSW)

Slug: `rowville-nsw`

Back in the day our local press file was basically one thing: Stamford Park Homestead — the 19th‑century place that Broadsheet and Concrete Playground fussed over when it reopened after 30‑odd years of neglect. We used to joke that you could eat in someone’s refurbished colonial living room while the homestead chooks peered through the window. It was very much "dinner at great‑aunt’s, but make it heritage-listed." Broadsheet even put Rowville – Central on a PwC list of Melbourne suburbs once, which made us nod and keep mowing the lawn.

A typical weekend for us is gloriously low‑key. Drop the kids at Taylors Lane Playgroup, then drag them to whatever’s on at the Rowville Community Centre on Fulham Rd — the same place that hosts the Stringybark Festival, Knox’s sustainability and family‑friendly jiggle of live music, participatory art and awkward public dancing. If they still have energy, Rush HQ (formerly Jump Factory) on South Corporate Avenue is where we’ve watched small humans discover foam pits and wall runways with alarming enthusiasm.

We’ve also got practical things covered: there was a Darrell Lea Factory Outlet for bargain chook‑lovers, Qualitas Medical Practice offering fully bulk‑billed care, and listings for Rowville Health on Stud Road and Rowville Secondary College — yes, it’s a town that teaches your teenagers maths and the art of competitive lunchtime loafing.

There’s no petty council scandal in this file — the data reads like a community noticeboard, not a soap opera. Which, honestly, suits us. If you crave CBD sparkle 24/7 (we’re 28 kilometres out, in case you forgot), you’ll hate living here. If your idea of fun is rooftop bars and being seen, Rowville will politely not entertain you.

Who would love it? Families, bargain hunters, anyone who wants a community centre that actually runs a festival, and aerialists — because 5D Squad does pole, aerial hoops and silks, and you’ll spot them mid‑training like flannelette‑wrapped superheroes.

We don’t pretend to be the city. We’re the homestead with chooks, the trampoline empire, the playgroup on Taylors Lane — and we’re quietly proud of it.
