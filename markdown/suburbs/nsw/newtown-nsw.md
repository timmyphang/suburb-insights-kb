# Newtown (NSW)

Slug: `newtown-nsw`

Back in the day — and by that we mean the stack of older guides we were sent — Newtown felt like a tangle of King Street neon, unapologetic murals and more food options than sense. We used to start weekends bargaining for a lunchtime table by recalling Broadsheet’s old favourites: Joe’s Tavern, Piqu, Olotl, Kolkata Social and Fabbrica all getting a nod in the directories we clutch like a rosary. Time Out called us Sydney’s vegan capital; Concrete Playground celebrated our grunge-with-a-good-menu vibe. We wore both proudly.

A proper Newtown morning used to mean wandering King Street, coffee in hand (Soulmate Coffee would’ve been on the list), Cairo Takeaway on speed dial and Mapo for when we’d decided to be ambitious about lunch. Australia Street had its own little orbit — Osteria Mucca was praised as a charming trattoria by Time Out, and Urban List flagged Arre and Flora among the newer, greener openings that kept the neighbourhood interesting.

Yes, we have our soap-opera moments. Junkee mourned the loss of Newtown Social Club; Kuletos went up for sale after 42 years; PedestrianTV reported Buddy’s Bar clapping back at an IG call-out. There was even that Enmore Theatre wobble when part of the floor collapsed during a Genesis Owusu gig. And who could forget the Comedor fallout — a sudden closure after a mass staff walkout with serious allegations about pay and the owner’s behaviour. These are the authentic scars that make our King Street postcodes worth gossiping about.

Who would hate living here? Someone who wants a sterile cul-de-sac and an early night every night — you’ll be driven mad by live music notifications, bar reopenings and the aroma of something delicious wafting down the street.

Who would love it? Vinyl diggers still brag about a record shop that’s been in the same brick-and-mortar for 24 years, families find community through groups like Newtown Moms, and anyone who enjoys eclectic eateries, a stubbornly bohemian spirit and pubs that have stories to tell — even if some of those stories involve murals and police reports. We wouldn’t have it any other way.
