# Islington (NSW)

Slug: `islington-nsw`

We know Beaumont Street like we know the wrong end of a local argument — intimately, loudly and with a soft spot. Back in the day (and by that we mean all our sources are a bit vintage), guides used to point visitors to 3 Beaumont Street where places like Convoy Commune and Arno Deli were doing the heavy lifting for brunch rumours. Newcastle Food Month even slapped “Plate Date” deals down the road at 10 Beaumont Street, part of an 80-venue tango that made us look like a city that eats for sport.

Morning here used to mean slow coffee (if you could still get one) and scanning the strip to see which of the newer hospitality investments had survived the latest cycle of hype; an $8‑million spruce-up of the Beaumont Street strip was supposed to mean shiny plates and better footpaths. Instead it meant we developed a Pavlovian twitch every time an old favorite reappeared as an “exciting new concept” or – retrospectively – as “Closed”, like the small bar/diner actually called Closed at 17 Beaumont Street. You could write a whole thesis on the emotional labour of restaurant rollovers. We considered doing it, then decided to eat instead.

If you hate this place, it’s probably because you crave predictability. We’re not the suburb that stays the same long enough for you to memorise the coffee order. The short-stay listings used to promise “a flat in the heart of Islington” or a “3 bedroom designer home” with terraces and Wi‑Fi — perfect if your idea of permanence is two nights and a review. If you despise subtlety, artisan everything, and menus that change with the PNG of the season, well, you’ll loathe us.

But if you love food scenes that evolve faster than council paperwork, where Beaumont Street has been a proper stage for local dining (and where Newcastle Food Month once made 10 Beaumont Street a postcode to watch), you’ll feel gloriously at home. We’ll keep calling it our strip even if the names on the doors keep changing — because you can’t put a price on that particular kind of chaos.
