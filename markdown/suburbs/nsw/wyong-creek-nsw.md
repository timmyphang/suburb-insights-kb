# Wyong Creek (NSW)

Slug: `wyong-creek-nsw`

We know Wyong Creek the way you know the back seat of your ute — intimately and slightly cheesy.

Back in the day the calendar looked very different: Concrete Playground had us down for a 7am meetup at Yarramalong Turf Supplies for a communal sunflower harvest (they opened the gates in April 2019 and sold bundles for a dollar a stem), and their reviewers were making weekend pilgrimages to Little Creek Cheese — “a Central Coast gourmet cheese factory” in Wyong that amassed awards and once offered tastings and hands-on tours (reviewed in 2020, updated 2021). Sitchu even listed 1 Red Hill Road, Wyong Creek in a Central Coast round-up alongside Aquafun and stand-up paddle boarding on Avoca Lake, which is the sort of endorsement our grandmothers would wallpaper the house with.

A typical weekend vignette (when the listings were busy): we’d stagger out early, queue for sunflowers like it was a seasonal ritual, stagger to a cheese tasting pretending we understood washed-rind vocabulary, then nod seriously at the suggestion to try paddle boarding on Avoca Lake because why not.

Who would hate it here? If your hobby is rooftop cocktail bars, curated espresso rituals, or turning every walk into a TikTok, this place will calmly bewilder you. Also, anyone who needs everything to be “current” — the press loves to catalogue our highlights in neat little time capsules.

Who would love it? Sunflower hoarders, cheese pilgrims, weekend paddlers and anyone who likes their weekend plans to smell faintly of hay and brie. We’ll keep tending the turf, milking the cheese lore, and pretending the lake is our private playground.
