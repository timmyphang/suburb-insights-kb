# Newton (NSW)

Slug: `newton-nsw`

We live in Newton, NSW — the kind of place where your neighbour is either running the Book Group or quietly organising the next Salon and you only find out which when the RSVP comes through with a very sincere “2-bottles-of-wine” option. Back in the day the Newton Women's Forum (established 1998) made this official: Speaker Evenings, a monthly Book Group, Salon discussions, cooking reviews and seasonal social gatherings — all the things that make small-town civic life feel very grown-up.

Our weekend ritual used to be: someone posts the Seasonal Cooking Review: Feel Good Foodie (that one was on January 28, 2025), half of us promise to bring a recipe and the other half promise to bring the emergency wine. The Forum even ran salons with titles you can’t help but gossip about — “Women Didn't Look Like This Before — New Beauty Standards” — and later, “Hello Stranger! Why Greeting a Stranger Can Be a Boon” on June 11, 2026. We once argued for an entire meeting about whether Tom Lake by Ann Patchett deserved a second read (NWF Book Group, June 17, 2026). All the drama you need is contained in a Tea Towels vs. Thermomix sidebar.

There’s no juicy council scandal in our feed — just an efficient, polite, well-documented sense of community. The Forum’s website is powered by Wild Apricot membership software, which is about as thrilling as it sounds, but it does mean we get our calendars in order and our guilt in neat columns.

Who would hate living here? If your idea of paradise is absolute anonymity, zero small-talk, and never having to justify bringing two bottles to a salad swap, Newton will feel like a one-woman show where you missed your cue.

Who would love it? People who want neighbours who actually show up, who like salons more than silence, who enjoy a Book Group that will roast your reading list gently and a cooking review that will absolutely judge your mise en place. We roast ourselves because we live here, and frankly — someone has to keep bringing the wine.
