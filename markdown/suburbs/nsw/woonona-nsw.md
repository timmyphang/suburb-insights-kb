# Woonona (NSW)

Slug: `woonona-nsw`

Back in the day we’d brag that Woonona had the perfect weekend rhythm: toddler chaos followed by kebab crumbs and a nap on the couch while someone else cooked the schnitzel at the club. Playgroup NSW used to list Woonona East meeting on Tuesdays at 9:30am at Woonona Baptist Church (477 Princes Hwy) — so yes, our social calendar was reliably scheduled around craft glue and gold-coin donations. If you ever wondered how far we are from Stanwell Park, the notes even put Woonona East 16.45 km away, which is very specific and very on-brand.

For years Parents Guide pointed to the usual civic comforts: FMP Woonona on Princes Highway for that personalised GP visit, Woonona Early Childhood Centre for check-ups and new-parents groups, and the Woonona Bulli RSL Club — proudly sitting between the escarpment and the ocean about 15 minutes north of Wollongong — for family dinners and a playground that saves grandparents from hero duty. And then, shining like greasy neon at 2am, Woonona Kebab House: an Illawarra icon serving Turkish street food for more than 35 years. That’s the kind of longevity that earns you a place in our group chat history.

If you’re scanning this for local gossip, sorry — the archive prefers function over feuds. The story the records tell is unglamorous and lovely: playgroups, child health clinics, a reliable GP, a club with a playground, and a kebab shop that outlasted trends. We revel in being the suburb that’s more practical than picturesque.

Who would hate it here? If you need boutique cocktail bars and metropolitan mystique on demand, Woonona will feel like a long weekend in a place that refuses to pretend it's something else. Who would love it? Young families, new parents, anyone who values a community that organises its social life around toddler timetables and a kebab after midnight. We’re modest, a little salty, and distinctly Woonona — and yes, we miss those old Tuesday mornings.
