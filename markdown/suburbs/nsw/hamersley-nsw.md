# Hamersley (NSW)

Slug: `hamersley-nsw`

Back in the day we thought our biggest flex was knowing the quickest way down Glendale Avenue at school-run hour. Turns out the internet had bigger plans: while we were corralling lunches and lost socks, the same noble name “Hamersley” was getting gushed over for azure pools and orange rock in Karijini. As Urban List and Time Out noted, Hamersley Gorge and Circular Pool are the stuff of postcard daydreams — which is a lovely identity crisis for a suburb whose proudest street sign reads Glendale.

Our mornings are less cinematic cliff-jump and more childcare choreography. For years the local kid‑logistics read like a small-business directory: Keiki Early Learning Hamersley and Keiki Glendale Bush Preschool, Care for Kids OSHC — yes, the one on 21 Glendale Avenue — and Treasured Tots Early Education (led by Simone O’Brien) have been the unsung heroes of our PTA dramas. MumsPages even dutifully catalogued our therapists, gyms and kids’ health services, because nothing says “suburban glamour” like a neatly categorised list of appointments.

There’s no petty council war in the snippets we found — only the odd identity mix-up. Time Out and Pedestrian TV have been waxing lyrical about the karst wonders up in Karijini, and Time Out also uses the Hamersley name in Broome — Matso’s at 60 Hamersley St, the Broome Courthouse Markets at 8 Hamersley St, Aarli Bar at 16 Hamersley St — so when relatives Google us they either expect a national park or a market stall. We enjoy confusing visiting in-laws.

Who would hate living here? If you demand artisanal wilderness in your backyard, you’ll be disappointed — our grand canyon is the culvert behind the local oval. But who would love it? Families who need reliable childcare options, people who appreciate directories that actually list psychologists and gyms, and anyone who likes the idea that our name connects us, absurdly, to rock pools and mango beer in far-flung towns.

So yes, we’re Hamersley: not quite Karijini, occasionally mistaken for Broome, but very good at carpool.
