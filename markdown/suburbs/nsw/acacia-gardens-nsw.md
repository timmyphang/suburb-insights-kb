# Acacia Gardens (NSW)

Slug: `acacia-gardens-nsw`

We wake up on Saturday and, like all sensible locals, check Broadsheet’s Hot List to make sure someone famous hasn’t moved in overnight. As Broadsheet itself reminds us (their Hot List and that April 2025 roundup full of “thousand-layer” Korean tissue bread and a contender for Sydney’s best ramen), Sydney’s culinary press is busy inventing reasons to queue — and Acacia Gardens is not one of them. Fine by us.

Our weekend rhythm is simple: we potter in the backyard, swap gardening tips that would make Broadsheet’s “best nurseries” list shrug if it ever read suburbs, and wonder whether Urban List is still jetting off to list LA hotels and New York steakhouses while ignoring our cul-de-sacs. Junkee can keep reminiscing about Kings Cross; we’ll keep reminiscing about when the postie knew our name.

There’s no proper local scandal in the clippings we’ve seen — the closest thing was a far-flung Broadsheet story about a place called Acacia in Henley Beach, which apparently turned into a wine bar. Cute name coincidence, Adelaide. Not our problem. Our drama is smaller: which neighbour will borrow our ladder and not return it, and whether the footy on Sunday will be on the right channel.

Who would hate living here? If you need to be on a gastro map that updates monthly, if your ideal Saturday pivots around “new ramen” or “ice-cream for dinner” (both headline-friendly in Sydney’s feeds), you’ll find us a little sleepier than you like. If Urban List can’t turn your postcode into a city break, you’ll feel mildly offended.

Who would love it here? People who prefer being under the radar, who enjoy yards that actually fit a trampoline and neighbours who wave instead of post Instagram content about “discovering” us. We’re quietly decent, pleasantly unnoticed by Broadsheet, Urban List and Junkee — and honestly, we like it that way.
