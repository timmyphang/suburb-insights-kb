# Warnervale (NSW)

Slug: `warnervale-nsw`

We know the soundtrack here: squeaky pram wheels, someone arguing about whether snacks are “healthy”, and the church hall clock juddering through another fortnightly playgroup. Back in the day our social life — if you can call it that — was faithfully captured in parenting listings rather than stirring council dramas.

As Playgroup NSW noted, Warnervale Mackillop Lighthouse used to meet at St Mary of the Cross, Mackillop Catholic Parish Church — 91 Sparks Road, Woongarrah, NSW 2259 — on Thursdays at 9:30am, fortnightly. Little Locals Central Coast even ran a page for the group (they published an entry in August 2025 and, for reasons of regional charm, once spelled us “Warnerval”). Norah Head’s playgroup listing helpfully marked us as a neighbouring option — 4.34 km away, if you’re measuring love in decimal points.

If you scroll the archives you’ll find the usual parenting playbook: Social Mums Club as the friendly directory for meet-ups, Keeping Mum promising facilitated sessions and, yes, a “barista brewed coffee and morning tea” as part of the program. All comforting proof that our civic peak these years was organisation by email chain and a rota for snack duty.

There’s no juicy scandal in the files — no headline-grabbing spat, no plot twist about a shopping centre or freeway. The most dramatic thing is whether playgroup is fortnightly or weekly, and the occasional typo of our suburb’s name. If you want noisy nightlife, late-night ramen or someone to judge your weekend plans, you’ll hate it here. We’re not a suburb that promises that kind of adrenaline.

But if you’re the sort of person who judges suburbs by how many people will show up to hold a crying baby while you pee, then we are your people. Families, new parents, and organisers who love a good roster will warm to the steady, slightly chaotic, very neighbourly rhythm referenced across Playgroup NSW, Little Locals and the local mum networks. We’ll see you at St Mary’s on Thursday — bring snacks, bring patience, and bring the roster.
