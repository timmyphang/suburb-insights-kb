# Heckenberg (NSW)

Slug: `heckenberg-nsw`

We like to think we know Heckenberg like the back of our hand — which, frankly, is mostly fabric glue and yoga mats. Back in the day our community heartbeat was the Heckenberg Public School hub at 1 Jindabyne Street. The hub was always billed as “welcoming and positive,” and you could believe it: sewing groups, yoga, playgroups, English classes, study programs, volunteering and service connections all lived under the same roof.

For years the Liverpool listings kept us modestly famous — Heckenberg turned up on the Liverpool hub page alongside Austral and Hoxton Park — and the Hub Toolbox feed mirrored our little digital gossip, the place where local updates and comments would land. The contact details even included (02) 9607 8672, in case you wanted to ring up and check if the sewing circle had room for one more pair of scissors.

We won’t pretend we’re the nightlife or the restaurant row — what we are is stubbornly useful. If you hate communal craft projects, free English classes, study support or volunteering opportunities, this probably isn’t your scene. If your idea of fun is scrolling past local notices and pretending you don’t know anyone, you’ll find Heckenberg a touch intimate.

But if you love a place that actually runs programs that matter — playgroups for the littlies, English classes for new arrivals, study help for teens — then Heckenberg quietly does the heavy lifting. We’re not flashy. We’re the hub that turns up. And yes, we’ll bring the biscuits.
