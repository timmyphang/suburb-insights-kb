# Kallista (NSW)

Slug: `kallista-nsw`

We still talk about the morning the internet decided Kallista was a thing: an undated Playgroup Victoria snippet marking the launch of the Kallista Kindergarten Nature Playgroup on 12 February — "bring your little ones (ages 0-5) to explore, connect, and discover the wonders of nature together." We were there, in spirit (and in gumboots), waving tiny hands at whatever constituted a "wonders of nature" that week.

Our weekend ritual used to be the short, proud pilgrimage to that announcement — not because of clout, but because that's basically our village green publicity. It's the kind of place where the official digital footprint is a single, wholesome invite and we lean into it like it's a town crest.

Who would hate living here? Anyone allergic to children, mud, or the idea that a playgroup can double as a social policy statement. Who would love it? Families who want the tall, small, and loud to be the local soundtrack; people who like their suburbs low-key enough to fit entirely on one archived page.

Back in the day, that Playgroup announcement was our brochure. We wear it like a badge.
