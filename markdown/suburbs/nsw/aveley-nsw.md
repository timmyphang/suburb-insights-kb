# Aveley (NSW)

Slug: `aveley-nsw`

Aveley, NSW — we start our weekends the way the internet seems to expect us to: quietly baffled that every media hit tied to our postcode is actually about Perth, Paris or a Netflix binge list. The clippings we were handed are all older Sitchu and Urban List pieces — no fresh local features, no hot new cafe buzz, just "75 Of The Best Crime Shows On Netflix", The Reveley (which, funnily, is in Perth), and a Sitchu obsession with acai bowls and Sunday roasts that name-checks Johnny Fox’s. Back in the day feels like forever ago when the only thing anyone could file under Aveley was a browser tab.

Our morning routine is embarrassingly analogue: school run, a walk around the block, and speculation about why Google assumes we commute to Perth for brunch. The online dossier suggests the cultural reference points for our suburb are rooftop bars over the Swan River and “best restaurants in Paris” lists — which is to say, the internet thinks we either live beside Elizabeth Quay or we prefer croissants to cul-de-sacs.

There’s zero local petty drama in these clippings — which is probably for the best, because the only controversies on record involve whether Perth has better acai and where to find the best roast. So the biggest scandal we can deliver is that media outlets keep forgetting we’re in NSW.

Who would hate living here? If you need to be in every city guide, photographed next to exposed brick and a signature brunch bowl, Aveley will feel like social-media purgatory. Who would love it? If you prefer your suburb to be a place rather than a content opportunity — quiet streets, room to breathe, and the smug satisfaction of knowing the only press we get ties us to places like The Reveley and Johnny Fox’s instead of turning us into a weekend itinerary — then we’ll take our anonymity with pride.

We’re not trying to be famous. We’re fine being the suburb the internet keeps misplacing on its way to Perth.
