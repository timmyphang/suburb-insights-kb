# Rockingham (NSW)

Slug: `rockingham-nsw`

Back in the day we used to think Rockingham was just the place you stopped at on the way to Rottnest. For years the local guides have had the last laugh — as Sitchu reminds us, our foreshore got a glow-up and Rockingham Beach still serves up those picture‑perfect turquoise swims. We know every shell on that sand and which mornings the Shoalwater Islands Marine Park is glassy enough for the kind of snorkelling Urban List raves about.

Our weekend looks like this: a slow stroll along the newly restored foreshore, a squint out at Penguin Island and its cheeky little penguins (yes, Sitchu called them the world’s tiniest), and if the breeze stays merciful we’ll bob over to the Shoalwater islands for some reef gawking before the wind arrives. The dogs get the best bit — Rockingham Dog Beach is the calm, off‑leash miracle the city dogs only dream of, as Urban List once pointed out.

Petty drama? We remember when the political headlines came through — Mark McGowan’s retirement as member for Rockingham felt like someone taking the last chip at a family barbecue. And if you’re bored by nostalgia there was that brief, headliney phase where United Cinemas in Rockingham got name‑dropped in the Wahlburgers rollout — a reminder that we flirt with celebrity franchises and oversized cinnamon scrolls (yes, a Rockingham bakery once made headlines for buns stuffed with Nutella and candy; Urban List did not shame us).

Who would hate living here: people who need a 24/7 CBD soundtrack and a roster of late‑night options. If you’re allergic to kids, community centres and weekend markets, you’ll mutter about “too quiet” while Rockingham Mums and the Westerly Family Centre run Little Penguin’s Playgroup and low‑cost room hires that keep the place humming.

Who’d love it: families who actually like beaches, the folks who want affordable living (Time Out named Rockingham one of Australia’s most affordable cities), snorkellers, penguin spotters, and anyone who’d rather swap endless espresso splashes for actual sea lions and dolphins. We’ll keep the beaches, the community groups and the oversized cinnamon scrolls — and we’ll still wave at you from the foreshore, sand in our shoes and all.
