# Glenbrook (NSW)

Slug: `glenbrook-nsw`

We’re the ones who roll our windows down on the Great Western and sigh like we’ve just remembered where we parked our best life. Glenbrook isn’t flashy — it’s the sort of place that trades a boutique laneway for a gorge you can actually drown your phone in.

Weekends start with a shove out the door to Glenbrook Gorge Track. As Broadsheet noted, the descent is steep (three kilometres return if you’re being honest) and it kicks you out into views of the Nepean that make your city problems look very small. If you’re hot and smug, follow the Jellybean track (Time Out reckons it’s about 1km) to Jellybean Pool — named for its shape, not for the number of toddlers who’ve left their socks there. For a quieter dip, Blue Pool sits down a set of steep stairs and tends to be less visited, unless someone’s remembering they left the esky in the car.

We used to brag about local cafés in roundups — Kickaboom got a nod for being cheery and serving Mörk hot chocolate (Concrete Playground), and Vincent Diner was the family-run spot people mentioned for a reliable brunch and, yes, poached eggs (Urban List). Those write-ups are from the guide books of yore, so treat them like a good memory rather than today’s itinerary.

Parents will tell you Glenbrook Park got the glow-up in 2018: inclusive play equipment, a 5.4-metre climbing rope tower with a tunnel slide and a 20-metre double flying fox (Blue Mountains Mums). We push prams around the Glenbrook Lagoon Loop — a gentle 45-minute wetlands stroll — and pretend the fairy doors are a council-funded initiative.

We’ll walk our kids across Lennox Bridge and boast: “Oldest stone bridge on the mainland,” and mean it.

Who would hate living here? If you loathe stairs, creeks, or having to walk two kilometres from the train station before you earn your view (Broadsheet), this isn’t your vibe. If you want a shopping mall with mood lighting, move on.

Who would love it? People who’d rather climb down a gorge than queue for an elevator, families who appreciate a proper flying fox, and anyone who thinks a good day ends with river-sodden thongs and a sunburn across the shoulders. Glenbrook: small-town cardio, big-hearted scenery.
