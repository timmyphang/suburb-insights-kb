# Parmelia (NSW)

Slug: `parmelia-nsw`

We’ve got a confession: if you Google “Parmelia” hoping for our sleepy little NSW postcode, you’ll probably get whisked straight to a five‑star hotel in Perth. We know — we live here and we’ve long since accepted our digital doppelgänger.

Morning vibes in Parmelia, NSW? In our heads we picture a quiet walk; in Google’s head we’re elbowing into the lobby of the Parmelia Hilton on Mill Street to get to Community Coffee Co. Back in the day most of the coverage about “Parmelia” was about that hotel: Premier Suite views, a pool, gym, Executive Lounge access, and a lobby entrance to Community Coffee Co with a La Marzocco and those irresistible Greek baked goods. Urban List and Sitchu were very proud of a $45 million facelift in 2021 that reportedly refreshed the rooms, executive lounge and birthed the brasserie Samuels on Mill — complete with Chef Chad Miskiewicz name‑checked and a nod to Samuel Kingsford’s Mill Street history.

Petty local drama? Only what the internet has foisted on us: Broadsheet running a giveaway for a two‑night stay at the Parmelia Hilton (yes, that one) with Samuels on Mill vouchers tucked into the terms and conditions. The prize sheets and T&Cs made us feel like contestants in someone else’s holiday romance — and Sitchu’s dining roundups insist the hotel still counts as Perth’s first‑five star and a contender for steak and date‑night tiramisu.

Who would hate living here: people who like their suburb to have a single identity. If you’re the sort who gets twitchy when your postcode is repeatedly mistaken for a hotel lobby, you’ll be miserable. Likewise, anyone allergic to hotel brasseries, corporate giveaways or being romantically associated with a Perth Mill Street address — look elsewhere.

Who’d love it: families who secretly adore perks — the Hilton was once hyped for kids’ upgrades, a Kids Concierge and kids‑only dessert menus — and anyone who appreciates a good café tucked into unexpected places (Community Coffee Co’s Greek pastries and proper espresso kit get a nod). Also, parents who like resources: Playgroup WA’s Finder and PlayConnect+ show there are ways to find community, even if the internet keeps shipping us west.

We’re Parmelia, NSW — proud, a little bemused, and very good at being mistaken for somewhere with better views.
