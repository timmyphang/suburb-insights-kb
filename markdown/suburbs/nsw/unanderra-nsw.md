# Unanderra (NSW)

Slug: `unanderra-nsw`

We know Unanderra by the squeak of the roundabout as much as by name — we used to swing past the Unanderra Youth Centre on the corner of Factory Road and Princes Highway and think, "Yep, that’s where the next lot of legends will learn to play pool and make art." For families, our ritual used to be a morning at Unanderra Recreation Park on Albert Rd — the sort of place where kids can burn off the sugar before someone remembers there’s a basket of washing at home.

For years the local childcare drama was real: Your Kids Our Kids Early Learning Centre (Wollongong & Unanderra) was the holy grail, with enrolments open in Wollongong and, as Parents Guide noted, a waitlist for Unanderra. We muttered into our coffee cups (metaphorical — we don’t all drink one brand) and plotted alternate nap schedules.

If you wanted to feel proper athletic in Unanderra, McKeon's Swim School at the corner of Marley Place and Five Islands Rd was where you did it — indoor heated pool, lessons from bub to adult, aqua aerobics and the kind of pool parties that would haunt school photos. It all sounds quaint because these listings are from the old guide: we’re being nostalgic, not handing out current opening hours like gospel.

Who would hate living here? City slickers who need a different artisanal anything every weekend. Who would love it? Families who value a youth centre, a proper park on Albert Rd, and a swim school that used to run squad training six days a week. We’ll keep the roundabout — you keep the waitlist.
