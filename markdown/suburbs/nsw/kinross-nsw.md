# Kinross (NSW)

Slug: `kinross-nsw`

Back in the day a Sunday in Kinross, NSW felt suspiciously uncomplicated: someone would suggest the pub and nobody argued. We mean the Kinross Woolshed — yes, the one listed by Urban List at 5 Kinross Ct, Thurgoona — the sort of place you pointed yourself towards when you wanted a slab of local gossip with your dinner.

Our weekend routine used to look like this: roll out of the week, detour past farmland, and somehow end up chatting about the roast. Sitchu even name‑checked our produce when Chef Tom Cleland put “Kinross Station lamb” on a Sunday roast menu — proof that our lamb can leave the paddock and score a table in city menus. We like to think that makes us quietly smug, but only just.

We’ve had our moments. Pedestrian reported on the bird‑flu scare that touched the region — they named Kinross Farms and quoted Philip Szepe — and suddenly egg prices and anxious farmers were the talk of town. It was news, not a punchline, and we kept that in perspective: farms are business, animals are livelihood, and sometimes the countryside makes headlines for reasons we’d rather not.

Who would hate living here? If you need a new boutique every other weekend, or brunch that arrives with a grant application attached, you’ll probably sulk. If your idea of small‑town excitement is valet parking, Kinross will disappoint you with its charming lack of pretense.

Who would love it? People who like straightforward pubs, lamb that gets mentioned on recipe pages, and the kind of community that turns a farming scare into a collective problem to solve. We’re the sort of place that shows up in Urban List and Sitchu and even gets name‑checked in national news when things matter — not glamorous, but stubbornly useful.

So yes, we’re Kinross, NSW: a bit woolly, oddly proud, and perfectly happy to have our lamb do the talking while we get on with living here.
