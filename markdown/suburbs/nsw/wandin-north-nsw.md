# Wandin North (NSW)

Slug: `wandin-north-nsw`

We roll out of bed on a Wandin North weekend like it’s a ritual: slippers, sunhat, and a roughly calibrated tolerance for fruit‑stained hands. First stop, the farm gate — the Yarra Valley Farm Gate at 125 Quayle Road opens on the second Saturday of every month, and we like to think of it as our unofficial town square (but with more jam samples and fewer council meetings).

From there it’s a short wander to 47 Hunter Road, Wandin Valley Farms — the Sebire family’s cherry empire. As Broadsheet once cheekily noted, “The Cherry Boys in Wandin North” put us on the berry‑picking map, and we’ll happily queue for whatever’s in season. Nearby, if your house runs on bottled gas, Yarra Valley Gas at 51 Hunter Road will very calmly sort your delivery while you argue over which cherries to take home.

Back in the day Warratina Lavender — built in 1991 at 105 Quayle Road — used to be the place people posed for Instagram photos and bought sachets of lavender-scented optimism from the tearoom. We tell guests about it like it’s a local legend; you can almost hear the sickle‑harvest hymn in the hills.

We’re not immune to scenes: once the cops in Wandin North nabbed an 18‑year‑old for doing almost twice the speed limit, which gave us all a brief, unplanned reminder that this is rural charm with enforceable speed limits. And yes, the Wandin Rotary used to fill Wandin North Hall at 430 Clegg Rd with crafty chaos every last Sunday — mosaics, macramé, and a 50‑metre crochet eel named ‘Zoe’ according to old listings. We’ll forgive the eel for being more local legend than mayor.

Who would hate it here? If you despise long, sunlit Saturdays, seasonal produce, and a footy ground that doubles as community theatre, this isn’t your postcode. Who would love it? Families and folk who actually enjoy playgroups (MumsPages knows the scene), community sport at the Wandin Football and Netball Club on Clegg Road, and anyone who thinks monthly farm gates beat boutique malls.

We own the cherry‑stained reputation, and frankly, we like it. Bring cash, a hat, and zero city impatience.
