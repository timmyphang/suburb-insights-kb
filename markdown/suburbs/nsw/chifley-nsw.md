# Chifley (NSW)

Slug: `chifley-nsw`

Back in the day we used to joke Chifley was where suits go to nap between meetings. Walk down to the plaza and you’d feel it: the hum of briefcases, the scent of something expertly grilled and slightly smug. We remember District Brasserie — Broadsheet called it a 200-seat all-day venue in Chifley Plaza with its own bakery, cafe, bar and charcoal grill — the kind of place that serves a breakfast that could double as an annual report.

A weekend for us was a very particular sort of reconnaissance: a quick circuit of 1 Chifley Square to see if the heritage Qantas House had a new shine (hello Grill Americano, Time Out telling us it opened on November 19), a detour past Sushi Tei at 1 Chifley Square if someone was hankering for a reliable sushi train, and the odd poke bowl from Poku by Flying Fish down in the plaza — all of it vaguely corporate but inexplicably comforting. Concrete Playground liked the District Brasserie reviews; Time Out cheerfully labelled the precinct “the suit‑wearing end of town.” Fair.

There was activation energy too — Pedestrian.TV once wrote about a Chifley Plaza store change room being turned into a sweaty gimmick (they "pumped up the heat to disgusting temperatures" — yes, we felt that one), and even the retail rollercoaster made cameo appearances with The Daily Edited’s liquidation handled through Chifley Advisory. Nothing says prestige like drama in the shopping centre.

Who would hate it here? If you despise heritage-steakhouse glamour tucked into former Qantas House, can’t bear the sight of a bespoke Suit Shop showroom at 2 Chifley Square, or recoil at the idea of a boutique wellness club like Soma Collection with infra‑red saunas in Chifley Tower — you’ll find Chifley unbearably tasteful.

Who would love it? If you thrive on business lunches, appreciate a rack of charcoal-grilled things, want a tiramisu recommendation from the 1 Chifley Square crowd, or enjoy the novelty of a Wholly Schnit truck rolling by (18 Warburton Street on certain days) — Chifley has your number. We may be the CBD’s polished little corner, but we wear it like a well-cut suit: a bit stiff, very proud, and surprisingly warm once you sit down.
