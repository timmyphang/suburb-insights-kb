# Telarah (NSW)

Slug: `telarah-nsw`

We know Telarah like the back pocket of a school jumper — mostly because that’s where we stash spare socks for the Wednesday playgroup. Back in the day the Hunter Family zone had us pencilled in for a "Wednesday 09:30am" slot and MumsPages used to be the place you'd scroll through for karate classes for the kids and other essentials for mums in 2320.

Mornings here are a ritual: we herd small humans toward whatever park the car will fit us into. If you remember Maitland Park, you’ll grin — the Hunter Family zone wrote up its "fully fenced play area, bike track, balance mushrooms and a variety wheelchair swing" like it was a Disneyland for sensible parents. We nod, because yes, sensible is our vibe. Sensible and well‑alert when someone mentions community listings.

We don’t have much scandal — the only local drama is whether the MumsPages entry or the Hunter Family zone page is the most up‑to‑date source of parenting wisdom. Spoiler: neither will ever agree with your Google search.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who needs boutique nightlife or an app that recommends cafés we can’t name (we’d rather not guess). And anyone allergic to small talk about nap schedules.

Who’d love it? Families who like fenced parks, a reliable Wednesday routine, and directories that once tried very hard to keep everything organised. We’re low key, kid‑friendly and quietly proud of it — Telarah, 2320: where the playgroup start times are sacred, even if the website dates aren’t.
