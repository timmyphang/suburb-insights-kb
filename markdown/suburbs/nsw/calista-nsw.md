# Calista (NSW)

Slug: `calista-nsw`

We’ve got a confession: Calista’s greatest hits almost always start at the Child and Parent Centre on Harlow Road. Back in the day the Centre — run by Ngala Community Services in partnership with Calista Primary School — used to be our unofficial town hall, parenting bootcamp and excuse to get out of the house before nap time. It hung its hat at 22–50 Harlow Road and listed hours as Monday–Friday 8:30–4:00, phone 9367 0960 and email calista@ngala.com.au, and yes, Kate Ashchepkova was noted as the Acting Centre Coordinator.

Our Saturday morning vignette (in memory, because the records are from a few years back) is simple: we’d trade childcare bingo stories, pick up whichever session was on the Term 2 Program Planner 2026, and swap tips from the Paint Kwinana REaD crew and the Education, Care and Family Network Kwinana. It wasn’t flashy. It was practical, evidence‑informed and exactly what families wanted.

Who would hate living here? If you need round‑the‑clock boutique options or crave constant novelty, Calista’s weekday‑rhythm will bore you. The Centre’s listed hours make that obvious — we were never the 24/7 suburb.

Who would love it? Families who want supportive workshops, early‑literacy initiatives and a place that actually listens. If you like community that shows up between 8:30 and 4:00 on weekdays, and you appreciate a sensible Term planner rather than hype, welcome home — Calista used to be quietly excellent at that.
