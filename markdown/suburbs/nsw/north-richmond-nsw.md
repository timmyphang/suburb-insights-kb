# North Richmond (NSW)

Slug: `north-richmond-nsw`

We’re the lot who know every bend of Bells Line of Road — the ones who queue at the pub when it’s parmy night and quietly scroll Sitchu listings when we can’t afford the view.

A weekday morning for us starts at North, the cafe in Shop 6, 15 Bells Line of Road — yes, the one that brags about a Spicy Prawn Pasta special and opens just in time for our over-ambitious early starts (Mon–Fri 7:30am–2pm, Sat 7:30am–1pm, Sun 7:30am–11:30am). If we’re feeling social we’ll duck into the North Richmond Hotel at 13 Bells Line of Road for a pint and whatever’s on their Thursday PARMY night promotion — because nothing says community like a pub special that doubles as a town meeting.

We like that contrast: on one hand the property mags showing off Redbank Estate’s bespoke living — 47 Irrigator Drive sat pretty in Sitchu’s 2025 property guide — and on the other hand the earnest locals comparing parmy strategies at the bar. It’s our suburb’s personality in one headline: prestige glossy vs. pub specials. We’re not ashamed.

For families, Playgroup NSW lists a North Richmond session at the Community Centre, 33 William St — it used to run on Wednesdays at 10:00am (email playgroupnorthrichmond@gmail.com if you want to check the file notes). And if anything goes sideways, Hawkesbury Family Practice at 86 Lennox Street is the local clinic popping up in listings with services for all ages — yes, there’s a proper GP on the map.

Who would hate living here? If you’re allergic to juxtapositions — the “Redbank Estate” brochure on the coffee table beside a pub flyer for parmy night — you’ll find us mildly chaotic. If your idea of community is a quiet cul-de-sac catalogued in a brochure, North Richmond’s more of a lived-in postcard.

Who would love it? People who appreciate that the postcode contains both a tasteful property guide spread and a pub that will feed you on a Thursday. We’re small enough to recognise most faces on Bells Line of Road and big enough to have a proper clinic and a cafe that serves something spicy when we need it. Come for the pasta special, stay for the parmy debate — we’ll explain where to queue.
