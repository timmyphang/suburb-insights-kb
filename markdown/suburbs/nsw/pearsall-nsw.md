# Pearsall (NSW)

Slug: `pearsall-nsw`

We live in Pearsall, NSW — or at least we tell people that on the forms, because when we go rooting through the internet it keeps sending back identity crises.

Mornings here are a blur of “are we coastal, suburban or ranchland?” because the most specific internet hits for “Pearsall” are doing the rounds elsewhere. There’s the legendary Christmas lights at 8 Darlington Loop — for over 20 years that Perth display used to drop a Lemax collectable village in the garage and silhouette the roofline like a festive skyline. There used to be Pearsall Primary School listed at 95 Willespie Drive (that was a Perth listing taking Kindy enrolments for 2027). There’s even a community playgroup called Happy on the Hill at 125 Willespie Drive in Pearsall Hocking. Then, if you scroll hard enough, you’re suddenly in Texas: Pearsall GED at 523 E. Florida Street, Kidville at 805 S Willow St and Sunshine Day Care Center on E San Antonio — because apparently we also farm cattle and have childcare directories in the US.

Weekend routine? We like to imagine driving past 8 Darlington Loop to admire the Lemax village (again: that’s Perth’s thing), then dropping paperwork off at Pearsall Primary (Perth) while the kids run to a playgroup called Happy on the Hill (Perth). But reality is quieter: our actual local narrative for Pearsall, NSW is mostly a long game of mistaken identity. The press has given us postcards from other postcodes, which is charming in its own confusing way.

Petty drama section? The archives are disappointingly well-behaved. No council kerfuffles, no barista wars, just the internet politely misfiling our suburb under “Also try: Perth” and “Also try: Texas.”

Who would hate living here: anyone who wants to be on the front page of local listings and can’t stand being geographically ambiguous. Who would love it: people who enjoy a low-key postcode where, when you Google yourself, you get a festive Lemax village, a bustling primary school, and a Texan GED class — all without leaving town. For now, we’ll keep the address as Pearsall, NSW and relish the confusion. It’s cheaper than therapy.
