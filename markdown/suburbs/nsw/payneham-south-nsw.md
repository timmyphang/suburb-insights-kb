# Payneham South (NSW)

Slug: `payneham-south-nsw`

We know this postcode like the back of our hand — the school run, the short-cuts, the bloke who backs his ute into the same driveway every week (we won’t name names because the data never did).

Mornings start with a drop at St Joseph’s Catholic School on Portrush Road — yes, that’s 78 Portrush Rd, and the SAcommunity listing was even updated in 2026, so you can still logically blame the kids’ homework on the school. From there we either head past the neat rows where Wikipedia says the suburb was once market gardens, or we take the scenic detour by the Payneham Cemetery on Marian Road if we’re feeling reflective and slightly late.

We’ve got history and community in the oddest places. Broadsheet once wrote up Five Little Figs at 52 Devitt Avenue — a cafe with chickens and a veggie patch — so for a glorious, short-lived moment we were almost artisanal. That was then. The property-lust brigade also glanced at listings like 68 Hereford Avenue, a “luxury entertainer” on Sitchu, reminding us that new builds are creeping in on old blocks (Wikipedia even notes a building boom).

Our social calendar reads like an intergenerational variety show. SAcommunity lists the Payneham Senior Citizens Over 50’s Club at 54 Coorara Ave — the same address where the Payneham City Concert Band rehearses on Tuesday nights (they’re a 40-strong amateur outfit, in case you fancied community brass and a cup of tea). Eldercare Trowbridge House on Luhrs Rd is also on the directory, so if you like orderly gardens and reliable rosters, this is your jam. The mums’ directories and MumsPages listings remind us there are sensible services for families and pest control if the backyard throws a tantrum.

Who would hate it here? If you live for flashing inner-city nightlife or want every coffee spot to look like it was carved by an Instagram filter, you might sigh a lot. Who’ll love it? Families who want a proper primary school on Portrush Rd, gardeners nostalgic for market-garden mornings, and anyone who values a community band over a boutique pop-up.

We’re not trying to be trendy. We’re just Payneham South: a suburb that keeps its choir practises, its cemeteries, and its sense of humour — sometimes all on the same street.
