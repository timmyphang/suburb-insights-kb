# Forrestdale (NSW)

Slug: `forrestdale-nsw`

We pull up to 11 Acre Farm like it’s our own patch of semi-rural glory — back in the day The Good Paddock used to make the whole outing feel choreographed. Urban List and Sitchu had us believing in alfresco long lunches, kids’ Cubby Lane, and an orchard you could almost Instagram without shame. The Good Paddock at 448 Nicholson Road was the place for loaded breakfast bowls, burrata, beef tataki and even a fisherman’s broth if you were feeling very grown-up about weekend brunch.

Weekends were a gentle loop: we’d wander past landscaped gardens, pretend the alpacas were impressed by our outfits, queue at the Cubby Kiosk for something warm and sweet (Concrete Playground even name-checked the spot for premium hot chocolates), and then stretch out for a long, slow lunch that knew how to make farm-to-fork feel effortless. It all read like the kind of place that convinces you to buy a picnic blanket and three succulents on the same day.

There isn’t any juicy local council scandal in the clippings we found — these were glossy lifestyle write-ups, not front-page feuds — so the “petty local drama” is basically: who does brunch better in Perth? The papers decided The Good Paddock was worth the drive. That’s practically a rivalry.

Who would hate living here? If you loathe children’s laughter, small farm animals, and honest-to-goodness seasonal menus, this isn’t for you. If your heart belongs to neon-lit nightlife and anonymous takeaway, the orchard won’t soothe that ache.

Who would love it here? Families who want a day out without the fuss, anyone who believes a fisherman’s broth can be elevated to a ceremony, people who need alpacas in their weekend narrative — you’ll fit right in. Just remember: most of what we’re recalling comes from older pieces in Urban List, Sitchu and friends. The glory days of 11 Acre Farm and The Good Paddock were well reported — whether they’re still serving the same burrata, well, go check the Cubby Kiosk and tell us if the hot chocolate still earns the hype.
