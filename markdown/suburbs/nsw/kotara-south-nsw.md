# Kotara South (NSW)

Slug: `kotara-south-nsw`

We remember Kotara South as the suburb that ran on nappies, playground mulch and the comforting chug of passing trains. Friday mornings used to mean St James Playgroup at St James Primary School Hall on 30 Vista Parade — meet at 9:00am, the old listing even had the contact StJamesPlaygroupKotaraSouth@hotmail.com. Volunteers like Karina kept the show on the road for eight years; 12 grown-ups, 28 kids, organised chaos was our civic ritual.

Weekends were a two-act play: a stint at Kotara Park on Park Avenue where the under‑6s ruled and the trains provided percussion, then an indoor rescue at EMF jnr in Westfield Kotara when rain or tantrums intervened — two levels, about 1,200 sqm of soft play and toddler zones, plus a cafe for the exhausted adults.

We’re not here for a council scandal (there isn’t one in the file), so the real story is domestic and wholesome. If you crave absolute silence, or you measure suburban status by boutique bars, you’d probably hate it here — the soundtrack is playground shrieks and carriage brakes. But if you like predictable chaos, reliable playgroups and a park where kids can be small and loud, Kotara South was doing the job.

Back in the day, families loved that we had places that actually catered to little humans. We may not have big-city sparkle in the clippings, but we had a community that turned Friday mornings into an institution — and honestly, that’s enough bragging rights for a postcode.
