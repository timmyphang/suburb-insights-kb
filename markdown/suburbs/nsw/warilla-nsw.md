# Warilla (NSW)

Slug: `warilla-nsw`

Back in the day we’d blink awake on a Saturday and know exactly where the day would end — somewhere between a bowls scoreboard and a kids’ playground. We’re Warilla, and yes, our weekend cardio is walking from the carpark to the Warilla Bowls and Recreation Club for a woodfire pizza and a gelato that still tastes of community. As Parents Guide once noted, the Bowls had three well‑presented greens and a Lagoon Street space serving pizzas, burgers, cocktails, gelato and, crucially, a kids’ playground. Priorities, people.

Our local social calendar reads like a family-friendly bingo card. The Warilla Sports Club — “the Sporties” in the old listings — was described as our friendly local with value bistro meals, weekly live entertainment and trivia nights that get very serious about general knowledge and very casual about dress codes. The Warilla Hotel used to be the safe bet for a Sunday roast and a fortified fort of cabanas plus another playground for the small humans. Parents Guide even pointed out it was open seven days, which, in lieu of a supermarket late‑night, felt like commitment.

We’re also suspiciously organised: Warilla Child Care Centre at 8 Harvey St offered long day care, nutritionally balanced cooked meals and a transition‑to‑school program. The Smith Family reminds us some proper work happens here too — they flag Warilla North Public School and Assistant Principal Rachel Armer in stories about teachers transforming lives. And yes, our community groups throw craft sales at Warilla Grove Shopping Centre and meet up as the Shellharbour VIEW Club on Jason Street — which sounds quaint until you realise they sponsor learning programs.

Who would hate living here? Nightclub hunters and anyone who needs dramatic rooftop bars every weekend — we’re more “bring the kids, bring a folding chair” than “flashing lights and impossibly expensive cocktails.” If you loathe community bingo, playground politics, and the comforting thud of a meat pie, maybe don’t unpack the boxes.

Who would love it? Families who rate a playground higher than a nightclub, volunteers who enjoy craft sales and learning clubs, and anyone who finds joy in a bowls scoreboard and a mid‑week trivia night. We may not be flashy, but we’ve got heart, greens, and a surprisingly decent gelato.
