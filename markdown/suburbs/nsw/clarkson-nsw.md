# Clarkson (NSW)

Slug: `clarkson-nsw`

Every morning we try to look up Clarkson, NSW and the internet politely throws a shrug and says, “Which one?” — then feeds us Jeremy Clarkson punching a producer, Kelly Clarkson denying tabloid beef, and a US college hockey schedule. Back in the day we thought being anonymously brilliant was our thing; turns out it’s just our Google bio.

We live in the sort of suburb that, if you’re lucky, gets name‑checked indirectly. Urban List, for instance, has stronger feelings about a Dave & Buster’s opening — in Clarkson, Perth — and even listed The Nail Crib at 235 Ocean Keys Boulevard, Clarkson WA 6030. Concrete Playground’s streaming roundup mentions Clarkson’s Farm: Season Five. Time Out is busy selling tickets for Clarkson Golden Knights hockey matches across the globe. Pedestrian TV remembers Jeremy Clarkson’s more theatrical moments. Junkee and Sitchu have Kelly Clarkson in various cultural tangents. Basically, our online résumé reads like a confused pop‑culture mash note to anyone called “Clarkson.”

Our weekend routine is mostly us arguing over which Clarkson we’re allowed to borrow cultural capital from. Do we parade around quoting Jeremy? Absolutely not. Do we belt a Kelly Clarkson chorus in the car with the windows down? Sometimes, aggressively and without permission. Do we pretend the Dave & Buster’s reviews were written for us? Only at family dinners.

Petty local drama? There’s a small, sustained outrage that people on the internet think our postcode is a midwestern ice rink or an American TV host’s surname. That’s it. We complain, we meme, we move on. No council fights about neon arcades or celebrity farms — just gentle, sustained identity theft by search engines.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who needs to be the subject of every news cycle, or needs their suburb to come with its own branded chain restaurant. Who would love it? People who relish anonymity, cosy mischief and being able to say, with smug accuracy, “No, not that Clarkson — ours is quieter, and infinitely more ours.”
