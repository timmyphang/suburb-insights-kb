# Kellyville Ridge (NSW)

Slug: `kellyville-ridge-nsw`

We know the traffic on Filante Street like it’s our second postcode. Back in the day Sitchu even tipped Filante Street as one of Sydney’s trick-or-treat hotspots — and yes, the crowds and decorations are properly theatrical. If you want to feel like you’re queuing for Coachella but it’s just Halloween, this is your main stage.

Weekends for us are less “hipster brunch” and more “family logistics.” Concrete Playground once name-checked the Ettamogah in Kellyville Ridge as the family-friendly hotel with multiple dining areas, an undercover courtyard and a kids’ play centre — the kind of place where watching State of Origin felt like a family picnic with a scoreboard. For years the local directories have read like a mum’s survival kit: Sitchu listings promising homes near Kellyville shops, Kellyville Station, local schools and parks; MumsPages and Hills District Mums cataloguing mixed sports, performing-arts classes and even carpets and flooring for when someone finally learns to walk.

We’re small-business proud, too. Back in the day Hills District Mums listed Quick-flo Plumbing Solutions — Darin’s been a local name since 2001 — and DBSDesigns used to operate out of 1 Duchess Street making everything from stickers to wine-glass baubles for our school fetes. If you’ve ever bought a fundraiser badge or needed a blocked pipe fixed at 7pm, you’ve probably bumped into the life-blood of our suburb.

Local drama? Not the reality-TV sort — more the annual passive-aggressive lights competition. The Hills blog mapped out Bruton Ave, 37 Merrillville Road, Chino Place, 7 Vinegar Hill Road, 55 Singleton Avenue and 19 Annfield Street as must-see displays. We take our carols and LEDs very seriously here.

Who would hate it? If you loathe kids’ soccer timetables, Halloween queues and tasteful-but-competitive Christmas light choreography, Kellyville Ridge will test your patience.

Who would love it? Families, bargain-house hunters and anyone who likes their suburb practical, map-able and reliably full of community resources. For years the blogs and listings have read like a love letter from a parent who still likes a good light display. We’ll take that.
