# Toronto (NSW)

Slug: `toronto-nsw`

Back in the day we learned a useful internet truth: type “Toronto” into Google and you will not, in fact, find us. You’ll get Frank Gehry, the Distillery District and Time Out’s pilgrimage list before you ever see a single local post about our little patch of NSW — as Time Out cheerfully notes, Toronto (the other one) has restored Victorian streets and museum lists longer than a NEOPETS password.

Our morning routine now involves explaining this to bewildered friends. We wake up, make tea (no venue shout-outs here — the data’s thin on our cafés), and someone inevitably says, “Oh, Toronto — like the one with the film festival?” Cue us, sighing, pointing at Urban List’s nod to festival buzz and Concrete Playground’s mentions of world-class bartenders and Regent Park. Meanwhile Broadsheet will remind you about I‑Mei Milk Puffs being carted back from trips to Toronto, because apparently even snack nostalgia prefers our namesake.

The petty local drama? Not ours — it’s Google’s identity crisis. Junkee’s archives still trot out Rob Ford-era chaos and Pedestrian TV had headlines about a plane crash at Toronto Pearson. So if you’re trying to prove you live somewhere calming, prepare to be upstaged by mayoral scandals and aviation subheads when you’re just trying to post a photo of Sunday chores.

Who would hate living here: the kind of person who wanted their postcode to be immediately recognisable on international travel blogs, or anyone who thinks ‘Toronto’ should auto-complete to Frank Gehry selfies and Distillery District itineraries. You’ll be frustrated by every search, every out-of-towner who assumes you mean CN Tower, not central NSW.

Who would love it: people who enjoy being the quietly unexported version of a famous name. We’re the “other” Toronto — the one that doesn’t make Time Out’s museum roundups or Concrete Playground listicles, and frankly we’re happy about that. If you like a life that doesn’t come with Frank Gehry foot traffic, welcome — we’ll explain the postcode mix-up over a cup of tea, and maybe, if you want, we’ll even share an I‑Mei Milk Puff recipe we found buried in someone else’s travel story.
