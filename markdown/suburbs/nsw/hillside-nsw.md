# Hillside (NSW)

Slug: `hillside-nsw`

Hillside, NSW — we’ll admit it straight off: the internet likes our name more than it likes us. Back in the day the press kept sending people to other Hillsides — Broadsheet waxed lyrical about Basalt’s luxe studios “set on a hillside overlooking 12,000 cherry trees” near Orange, Concrete Playground was proudly listing venues from Hills Distilling Co to the Hillside Hotel in the Hills District, and Sitchu kept pointing travellers to a family-run Hillside Harvest orchard. Meanwhile, our own postcode gets the polite nod and a shrug.

Our morning routine? We make one up for the ‘gram and then go for a walk. There’s nothing about ritual espresso or rooftop whiskey here in the snippets we found — but the word “Hillside” turns up everywhere else: boutique stays, cabins, a hotel rooftop called Fire & Brimstone, even a Hillside community centre listed on Playgroup Victoria. So we like to think of ourselves as the original Hillside: low-key, unbothered and moderately smug that we don’t need a distillery or a designer bathtub to be content.

Petty local drama? The data is so thin we can’t responsibly invent one. Instead we’ll confess: we’re slightly jealous. As Concrete Playground noted, the folks behind Hills Distilling Co expanded fast — opening a Hillside Hotel and friends — and Broadsheet keeps celebrating hillside getaways with deep tubs and polished concrete floors. We watch from our own slope and roll our eyes lovingly.

Who would hate living here: people who need constant novelty, artisanal anything on-hand, or a steady stream of influencer content. They’ll be off to the Basalts and hillside retreats mentioned by the mags before breakfast.

Who would love it here: anyone who likes a low-profile suburb that refuses to play branding sudoku. If you want a place that shares its name with a lot of famous stays but still lets you enjoy a quiet walk without having to book a three-night minimum — that’s us. We’re Hillside in name, but also a little bit of every other Hillside the internet keeps stealing.
