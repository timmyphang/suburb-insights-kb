# Dover Heights (NSW)

Slug: `dover-heights-nsw`

We stand at Dudley Page Reserve with the harbour yawning beneath us and try very hard not to look smug. The view is the thing here — we’ve got the clifftop drama, the kind of ocean panoramas that make visiting relatives hand over property brochures mid-scone. Time Out even put our 2030 postcode in the “rich suburbs” bracket, which feels about right when the weekend walkers outnumber the pigeons.

Our weekend ritual: lace up, head off along the Federation Cliff Walk toward Watsons Bay, clock the sandstone cliffs and Macquarie Lighthouse, and traipse into Robertson Park like we earned it. Sitchu ran a few drool-worthy home tours on our streets — “Mirror Image”, “Budge Over Dover” — so yes, we’ll admit we enjoy pretending our houses were on a design blog. Waverley Council has been busy too: Concrete Playground noted they started impounding stray dockless e-bikes, and EasternSuburbsMums has been tracking the Clifftop Walkway and Diamond Bay boardwalk upgrades — the notices said sections were re-opened while other bits were still under work.

Petty local drama? Oh, we have that in spades. Pedestrian TV flagged the woman who was caught keying a car parked outside a Dover Heights home — proof that even amidst our manicured hedges, someone’s having a very bad day. Elsewhere, Broadsheet remembered when Fei Jai realised during COVID it had a Dover Heights delivery constituency — nostalgia for dumplings in a time of takeaway chaos.

Who would hate living here? If you despise scenic walks, are allergic to being overtaken by the City2Surf crowd along Old South Head Road, or can’t tolerate Saturday morning kids’ soccer at Dudley Page Reserve (Galaxy Sports Coaching, yes, that listing exists), then you’ll find us trying your patience.

Who would love it? Hikers, harbour romantics, families who want Dudley Page Reserve NYE vistas, and anyone who likes living in a postcode that gets featured in Time Out and Sitchu. We’re clifftop calm with a side of gossip — pretentious, picturesque and entirely ours.
