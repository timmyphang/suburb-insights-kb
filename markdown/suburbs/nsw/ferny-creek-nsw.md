# Ferny Creek (NSW)

Slug: `ferny-creek-nsw`

We wake up in postcode 3786, make a beeline for the patch of sunlight that hits Ferny Creek Reserve (Cnr Clarkmont & Hilton Rds), and silently judge anyone who thinks “the city” is easier. Weekends here have a rhythm: one minute you’re queuing for a plant tag at the Ferny Creek Horticultural Society stall, the next you’re apologising to a stranger for knocking over a fern because you simply had to have it.

We love that our horticultural society makes life interesting. They ran a mini Plant Expo on 14 March 2024 when the main show was cancelled for extreme heat and fire danger — and then shrugged and returned with the 20th Plant Collectors’ Expo on 8–9 March 2025. As Ranges Trader reported, we can’t resist a succulent or a rare speaker, and neither can the vendors. You’ll spot mums juggling between stalls because, yes, MumsPages lists every childcare and kids’ activity you could dream of here.

We gather in slightly scientific ways too — remember the “Reconnecting to Our Forest” walks from the Ferny Creek Scout Hall at 21 Clarkmont Road, Sherbrooke (beside the CFA)? Mums of the Hills organised mindful stations and we all pretended we weren’t secretly checking our shoes for leeches. And if you need a scan after wresting a tree into the ute, Mums of the Hills even points out Direct Radiology serves our patch.

Petty local drama? We have our classics. The new raised pedestrian island at the corner of Mt Dandenong Tourist Road and Sherbrooke Road lasted less than a week before a car introduced itself to it — as Ranges Trader noted with the exact tone of someone who’s seen too many island-encounters. Safety concerns were raised; we rolled our eyes and planted another azalea.

Who would hate it here? People allergic to community fetes, plant nerds, and anyone who thinks a traffic island is a national emergency. Who’d love it? Plant collectors, kids who need outdoor chaos, anyone who likes fundraisers like Halloween on the Green at Ferny Creek Reserve (the 2024 event did it proper), and folks who prefer community notices over anonymous city feeds.

We’ll keep tending the soil, tending the gossip, and pretending the traffic island was always part of the landscaping.
