# Madeley (NSW)

Slug: `madeley-nsw`

We walk like we own the place — partly because we once monopolised the picnic shelters at Spectator Dr and Sporting Drive and partly because the bronze raptors at Kingsway Dinosaur Park still give us that prehistoric side-eye. Back in the day Time Out made a fuss about those two raptors and the playground’s barbie facilities; we treated that like an official stamp of our weekend dominance.

Our morning ritual (circa memory lane) was predictable: a sausage roll for the small people, a lukewarm coffee from wherever the nearest takeaway was, then a trundle to the dinosaur park where the kids launch off the swings like they’re auditioning for Jurassic Park. Playgroup WA turned up on our radar too — you couldn’t swing a pram without hitting a meet-up — and BabyCenter threads like “New Mums Madeley” and “Mothers Group” were our unofficial noticeboard for who had spare nappies or a sympathetic ear.

There’s a weird little pedigree to the name. Concrete Playground once wrote about The Albion in Braidwood and Executive Chef Keira Madeley — so yes, our suburb’s name moonlights as a chef’s surname elsewhere. Netmums also had a few stories about a Chloe Madeley doing her celebrity thing. We like to tell newcomers this as a humble-brag: either we birth talent or we lend our name to it. Either way, it sounds posh when we say it at school pickup.

Who would hate living here? If your idea of leisure is rooftop bars and a late-night sushi crawl, you’ll find our suburbspeak of barbies, playground timetables and forum threads painfully quaint. If you hate the sound of small people being joyously relentless, this place will test you.

Who would love it? Families who value a good park, a strong local playgroup, and neighbours who'll pass you a packet of spare nappies without blinking. For years the local chatter lived in Parentopia listings and BabyCenter threads — and honestly, that’s been our quiet claim to fame. We wouldn’t swap it for the world.
