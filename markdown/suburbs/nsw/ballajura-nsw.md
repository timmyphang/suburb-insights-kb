# Ballajura (NSW)

Slug: `ballajura-nsw`

We know Ballajura, NSW by the unspoken rituals: morning grocery run, the vague hope of running into something sweet, and the communal shrug that says, “It’ll do.” Back in the day Urban List wrote about our humble spots — and honestly, the praise sticks like chocolate on a palm.

Weekend ritual: we used to pop into Nabil’s Acai Station on N Illawarra Cres in Ballajura City Shopping Centre. Urban List called it lowkey, and they weren’t wrong — towering acai cups layered with soft-serve, fruit and granola, drenched in pistachio, biscoff and chocolate. For those who asked, yes, this is our version of something unbearably hip elsewhere. It tastes of summer and very specific dietary choices.

Petty local drama: none of the articles bothered to start one, which is both a relief and an indictment. The real controversy here was whether Nabil’s was a Lebanese sweet shop or the “Perth’s home of Dubai chocolate goodness” (their words, not ours). Either way, we enjoyed the homemade bars and Dubai-chocolate strawberry cups while pretending we knew what that meant.

Who would hate living here? People who need a neighbourhood CV full of Michelin-starred things, glossy laneways and the kind of nightlife that requires a velvet rope. Also lovers of secrecy — we’ll happily tell you where the good acai is.

Who’d love it? Fans of Nabil’s former glory, the folks who rent gowns from Poison Ivy (a home-based dress-hire operation that used to save our formal lives), and anyone who likes their local scene quietly brilliant rather than loudly trying. We’ll keep our little treasures — and the rest can keep their ropes.
