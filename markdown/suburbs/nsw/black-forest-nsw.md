# Black Forest (NSW)

Slug: `black-forest-nsw`

Back in the day we’d tell newcomers the best way to get to know Black Forest was the school-run circuit — it’s practically our civic ritual. For years the choreography has been the same: Nido Early School Black Forest at 709–711 South Rd for the tiny tyrants, then the long march to Black Forest Primary School at 679 South Rd, and the post-drop detour past the Black Forest Licensed Post Office in the Black Forest Shopping Centre (Shop 6/715 South Rd) because someone always needs a parcel signed for.

We like to think we’re more than a series of drop-offs. The Village Baker — run by Ben and Tracy Sachse — used to have one of those bakery-cafe chips on its shoulder in Glenside and Black Forest, serving pies, finger buns and sourdough the way your nana would approve. As Broadsheet noted, we wore that cake-mote with pride, even if the internet prefers to graft our name onto patisserie: Urban List, Concrete Playground and others can’t stop inventing Black Forest gelato cakes, milk punches and croissants that sound like they belong in a dessert cult, not a suburb on South Rd.

There’s not much petty council drama in the files (which, frankly, makes us suspicious), so instead the running joke is that our brand identity has been hijacked by confectioners worldwide. As Urban List once pointed out, “Black Forest” doubles as a very buttery pastry and a smoked-pork pitch in the same breath. We take it as a compliment.

Who would hate living here? If you despise small routines, community noticeboards and the comforting hum of Saturday morning playgroups, you’ll be bored. If you’re allergic to being mistaken for a cake, you’ll be furious.

Who’d love it? Families, tired teachers, small-biz bakers with a soft spot for kirsch-flavoured nostalgia, and anyone who wants practical things: long‑day care and kinder services listed at Nido, a proper primary school on South Rd, and a Clarence Park Community Centre Playgroup (72–74 East Ave) that’s been running twice weekly (Mon 10am–1pm & Fri 2pm–5pm) — the sort of suburban infrastructure people actually mean when they say “community.”

We’re Black Forest: a place where your postcode doubles as a dessert and we’re fine with both.
