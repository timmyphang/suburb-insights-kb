# St Helena (NSW)

Slug: `st-helena-nsw`

We get it — people type "St Helena" and expect something glamorous, spooky or internationally famous. We, the actual St Helena, NSW locals, have perfected the art of shrugging and sending them on a wild goose chase.

Back in the day the travel pages loved our name more than they loved accuracy. Urban List once put “St Helena Island” on a Brisbane ghost-tour roundup — complete with the delicious line that on St Helena Island “dinner comes with a side of adrenaline.” Concrete Playground tagged St Helena Island as a Moreton Bay day‑trip highlight. Time Out has been even fussier: one guide celebrated Saint Helena Island’s diving and 699‑step Jacob’s Ladder, another breathlessly reported the airport regaining Category 6 status and flights resuming. None of this, it should be noted, is about our little patch of NSW — but people still knock on our digital door.

Meanwhile, Byron Bay got to cash in on our name. Urban List wrote up a Saint Helena in Byron Bay — a December 2025 opening that served “bougie coffee and European desserts” — and Cape Byron Distillery even sits on a road called St Helena Road in McLeods Shoot. And if you want true name‑brand confusion, Time Out in London noted a £32 cup of coffee made from beans grown on St Helena (the island), because apparently scarcity makes caffeine glamorous.

So our weekend routine? We don’t have a Jacob’s Ladder view, a Category 6 airport, or that £32 coffee to sip while we watch humpbacks. What we do have is the joy of being mistaken for every remotely titled St Helena on the planet. The petty drama is mostly us correcting booking sites and explaining that no, the ghost tours and the Mayfair brew are not in our postcode.

Who would hate living here: people who need to be found on the first Google hit, or anyone allergic to gentle identity theft by travel writers. Who would love it: the misfits who enjoy a low‑profile postcode that comes with free international glamour by name alone — and the kind of people who find humour in being everyone’s second favourite St Helena. We’ll keep the name. You keep the myths.
