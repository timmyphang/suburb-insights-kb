# Manor Lakes (NSW)

Slug: `manor-lakes-nsw`

Back in the day we’d pile the stroller into the car and make a morning pilgrimage to the Manor Lakes Community Centre — or to the Manor Lakes Community Learning Centre if we were feeling fancy. Playgroup Victoria used to list both venues, so if you wanted to find us you’d open their "Find a Playgroup" map and follow the tiny cartoon footsteps straight to us.

Weekends looked the same: a cluster of sun-creased parents corralling sticky fingers in a venue that promised "indoor outdoor areas" and, crucially, a "full facility with sink, toilet, change room and store room." Practicality won more hearts here than design trends ever could. Ambade Ratikanya even wrote up Manorlakes community Center for Playgroup Victoria on Jul 21, 2023 — proof we weren’t a secret, just gloriously suburban.

There’s no scandal in the archive — no council brawls, no café wars, just reliable, unflashy community infrastructure. Which is fine. If you want clifftop views, craft beer lists or a laneway mural to Instagram, you’ll hate it here. We make no promises about nightlife or late-night small plates.

Who would love it? Families who appreciate a place with actual nappies-to-hand logistics, and anyone who measures a suburb by whether the playgroup venue has a change room that doesn’t double as storage. Manor Lakes isn’t trying to be edgy; it’s trying to be useful. And honestly, that’s the roast and the compliment all in one.
