# Tarneit (NSW)

Slug: `tarneit-nsw`

We start our Saturday with a sentimental loop: drop the buggy at the Tarneit Community Learning Centre, where — back in the day — Playgroup Victoria listed the venue (posted Oct 7, 2019) as having an indoor–outdoor area and a fully equipped room with sink, toilet, change room and storage. We nod at the fluorescent lights like they’re family heirlooms.

If we’re feeling brave we’ll stroll to Tarneit Central Shopping Centre — again, historically the scene of a Tarneit National Playgroup Week celebration — where Wyndham City Council ran shopping-centre playgroups on a Monday morning (10:00–11:30am). It’s the sort of suburban choreography that makes our week predictable and, frankly, efficient: small humans toast to the plastic slide, parents compare childcare notes, we swap placentas of advice.

There’s no major local scandal in the files — just steady community logistics. The 2017 Playgroup Annual Report even noted Tarneit alongside Avondale Heights and Hillside as part of a multi-site project, which is basically the suburban equivalent of making the sports team.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who insists on relentless nightlife, artisanal everything, or arguing about which lane is the fastest during school pickup. This place is not for the perpetually spontaneous.

Who would love it? Families, neighbours who rate a good change room above a rooftop bar, and anyone who believes that a reliable playgroup schedule is the backbone of civilisation. Back in the day, our community folded itself neatly around the kids’ timetable — and honestly, we liked it that way.
