# Kearsley (NSW)

Slug: `kearsley-nsw`

We live in Kearsley, NSW — or at least we do, even if the internet can't decide which one. Some mornings we half-expect Google to tell us to take the kids to Birch Road for the Kearsley Children's Centre in the Kearsley ward of Bolton. Netmums certainly thinks Kearsley is a one-stop family resource: "Things to do with kids in Kearsley", toddler groups, party-planning tips — all the comforting, organised chaos of parenting, but archived in amber.

Back in the day (and, truthfully, still on the web), Netmums ran a neat shotgun-tile of family-friendly pages: a full guide to days out, a list of events for kids, and a specific note about that purpose-built children's centre. So when we try to organise a playdate, half our research is useful local intel and half an accidental detour to the UK.

If you dig a little deeper, findglocal throws in even more identity confusion — listings for a Young Explorers Forest School on Moss Lane, a Holts Riverside Fish and Chip Shop at 71 Market Street, and even a Kearsley High School at 4302 Underhill Drive in Flint, Michigan. The Flint Institute of Arts on E Kearsley Street turns up, too. It’s like our postcode is a tub of mixed jelly: wholesome, slightly soggy, and full of international chunks.

Petty local drama? The data is too polite to spill those beans. Instead, the real comedy is the world-wide case of mistaken Kearsley identity: our suburb gets lumped in with Bolton, Moss Lane, Market Street and American high schools. We wear that mix-up like a badge — it’s proof our little place has global cachet, whether we asked for it or not.

Who would hate it here: people who need up-to-the-minute, geo-fenced Yelp results and nothing else. They’ll be baffled by the historical breadcrumbs.

Who would love it: families who appreciate a good kids program (Netmums did the homework for us), anyone comfortable with a little ambiguity, and the kind of locals who can laugh when their suburb becomes a transcontinental mash-up on the internet. We’ll keep doing school runs regardless — even if half the web insists we’re in Bolton.
