# Russell Lea (NSW)

Slug: `russell-lea-nsw`

Back in the day our claim to fame was a quiet strip of Iron Cove and the Bay Run — and, to be honest, we’re still very proud of that. We start the weekend with shoes on: the Bay Run, the 7km circuit that humbly saunters through Drummoyne, Russell Lea, Rodd Point, Five Dock, Haberfield, Lilyfield and Rozelle, following the Iron Cove shoreline like a very polite dog that knows not to jump on anyone. The water views are, as Concrete Playground put it, refreshingly tranquil.

Our Saturday loop usually includes a detour past 27 Undine Street — you can’t miss the listing talk about DA-approved plans for a luxury home on a generous 445sqm block with city and water views. It’s where locals imagine a future we probably can’t afford but enjoy picturing over a cuppa. A few blocks along Lyons Rd there were Time Out write-ups — La Casa Ristorante at 271 Lyons Rd and the Birkenhead Hotel at 206 Lyons Rd — both noted in older guides, the latter for its cheap meal deals (yes, $10 steaks on Mondays and Tuesdays and $10 burgers on Thursdays were listed). Don’t lean on those as gospel for tonight’s plans; the mentions are from old listings, not a concierge.

Our community mood is decidedly small-town: for years the local playgroup met Fridays at 9:15am at Russell Lea Public School on Lithgow Street, and the Russell Lea Public School fete on Sun 09 Nov keeps everyone pretending they’ll volunteer more than they actually do. If you’re the sort who likes noisy, hardcore nightlife, Time Out warned you upfront — Russell Lea “may not boast a world of hardcore nightlife.” We’ll take that as a compliment.

Who would hate it here? Nightlife hunters and anyone who needs a different gastro-pub on every corner. Who would love it? Runners who cherish the Bay Run, families who actually show up to the school fete, and renovators who drool over DA-approved plans down Undine Street.

And if you hear whispers about a shiny new thing, remember Concrete Playground’s note: plans were reported for a $3.8 million Callan Park Tidal Baths near the Bay Run, expected to open by summer 2026 — so keep your laces tied and your hopes measured. We’re small, slightly smug, and perfectly seaside-adjacent.
