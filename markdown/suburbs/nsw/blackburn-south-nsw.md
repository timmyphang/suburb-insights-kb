# Blackburn South (NSW)

Slug: `blackburn-south-nsw`

We’re the people who can tell you where the best loaf used to be, the playgroup routine, and exactly which street the kids will ride their balance bikes on until someone calls them in for dinner.

Back in the day you’d find us queueing outside The Honey Thief Bakery at 140 Canterbury Road — Urban List reckoned they made some of the best bread in Melbourne, and we treated that like sacred text. Sitchu calls Blackburn South a “diverse and family‑oriented” pocket — they even tuck the 2021 census figure in there: 10,939 of us, spread across tree‑lined streets and the kind of parks that make you feel like you’ve escaped the city without actually leaving your postcode.

Weekends here are playgroup playlists: toddlers at St Edward Anglican Church’s hall, toddlers at The Avenue Uniting Church, mums and dads doing the delicate choreography of tea, wipes and nappy changes in halls that double as community HQs. MumsPages keeps a directory of everything mums need — from physiotherapists to holiday sports — because yes, we have Melbourne Football Academy kids zigzagging across local ovals.

Petty local drama? We’ve had our moments. As Broadsheet reported after an electrical storm, Yarra Valley Water once issued a precautionary boil‑water warning covering 88 suburbs and our name was on that list. Nothing bonds a street like collectively pretending bottled water is a lifestyle choice.

Who would hate living here: anyone who thinks “nightlife” requires actual neon and more than three options for dinner after 9pm. If you want to be within stumbling distance of an after‑work DJ set, we’ll cheerfully wave you goodbye.

Who would love it: families who measure success in school enrolments and playground layouts, people who like their bakery memories steeped in ritual (and sourdough), and anyone who values community halls that do more than host a yoga class. Sitchu’s right — we’re a quietly leafy, practical kind of suburb. We’ll mock ourselves, we’ll remind you of the water warning for laughs, and we’ll still save you a piece of that Honey Thief loaf — or at least the memory of it.
