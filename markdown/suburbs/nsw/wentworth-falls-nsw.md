# Wentworth Falls (NSW)

Slug: `wentworth-falls-nsw`

We get to the cliff edge before the tourists Instagram our waterfall into another dimension. Dawn at Jamison Lookout feels like someone left the world on mute: the Jamison Valley fog, the first gasp of Wentworth Falls tumbling off the rim, and us pretending we didn’t queue for the exact same selfie spot. As Time Out noted, this is basically Australia’s most Instagrammed waterfall — and yes, the Giant Staircase will remind you why gym memberships exist.

Back in the day Broadsheet and Urban List flagged us for starry nights and day walks — Blue Mountains Stargazing used to run sessions from the Wentworth Falls picnic area at Jamison Lookout, and Urban List recommended those astronomer-led tours on Fridays and Saturdays. If you believe the guides (and we do), the night sky here will make your city angst look very small indeed.

Our morning routine is equal parts gentle and savage: a family-friendly wander along Darwins Walk from Wilson Park (the playground’s Pirate Ship is a real icon for small people), then onto the Valley of the Waters for Empress Falls and the thigh-burn of Wentworth Pass and the National Pass. Concrete Playground kept telling everyone to chase waterfalls here — seven of them on the loop — and we obeyed.

Local drama? We’ve had our share of extremes: Pedestrian TV reported bushfires in the Blue Mountains — one in Wentworth Falls was treated as “suspicious” — and, bizarrely, snow a few weeks later. We don’t gossip about it so much as add another log to the fireplace and carry on.

Who would hate living here? People who loathe stairs, strangers who crave anonymity (there’s no hiding when you’re next to the most photographed waterfall), and anyone who thinks a weekend without a crowd is a town planning failure.

Who would love it here? Families who appreciate Wilson Park and the lake, hikers chasing the Grand Cliff Top Walk or Wentworth Pass, photographers who live for the valley vistas, and anyone who once read Urban List rave about Mountain High Pies in Wentworth Falls and thought, “We’ll try that someday.” For years the guides have been right: we’re dramatic, damp, and endlessly worth the drive.
