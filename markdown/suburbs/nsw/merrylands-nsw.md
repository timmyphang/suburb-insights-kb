# Merrylands (NSW)

Slug: `merrylands-nsw`

We wake up and the first thing we argue about is whether to walk the dog through Central Gardens Reserve or brave the siren call of pastry at the Mason & Main precinct. Back in the day the guidebooks loved to call Merrylands "a museum of traditional foods" — and they weren't wrong. We used to queue at Kabul House for charcoaled meats and just-fired bread, and Broadsheet once wrote lovingly about the family recipes that kept hundreds coming through the doors every day. Iftar on Main Lane turned heads too, with its awarma, egg manoush and the very online-friendly lamb shawarma tacos and wagyu kofta dumplings — Time Out and Sitchu both gave it a long, wistful look.

Weekend routine: playgrounds first. Central Gardens' native animal enclosure (yes, wallabies and emus make appearances), shaded play areas and the upgraded inclusive playground are our standard-issue flex. Holroyd Gardens still owns the "learn-to-ride" vibe with that purpose-built bike track and roundabout for trainees who think roundabouts are a personality test. Playgroups run out of Merrylands Oval Hall on Burnett Street and the church playtime keeps the littlies occupied while we pretend to socialise.

Petty local drama (because of course we have some): remember the so-called "Acai wars" that turned into a whole TikTok saga? It put our humble smoothie rivals on the map for a week. And we bore our share of headlines — Junkee once quoted a local on how Merrylands felt the sting of double standards during COVID — so yes, we carry our own headlines like badges.

Who would hate it here? People who want every latte to come with an ambience score of 11/10, or those who can't handle a 3,000-square-metre, two-level gym rolling into town (One Playground made waves for being exactly that).

Who would love it? Food obsessives, families who live for good parks and solid playgroups, and anyone who enjoys a precinct like Mason & Main that mixes pop-ups like Knafeh Bakery with the kind of local life you couldn't fake if you tried. We know every lane, every bakery crumb and every gossip — and honestly, we wouldn't swap our messy, flavour-packed Merrylands for anywhere posher.
