# Enmore (NSW)

Slug: `enmore-nsw`

We’re the ones who’ll tell you Enmore isn’t pretending — it’s perfected the art of looking like it fell out of a 1970s Art Deco photograph, then stayed for brunch. On a Saturday we used to stroll down Enmore Road, past the Enmore Theatre’s old marquee (you know, the 1912 one with the 1,600-seat auditorium), nip in for gelato at Cow & the Moon, duck into Hop and Grain Brew Store for something hipper than our Sunday plans, and wonder how so many different bars managed to squeeze into one block without a planning meeting.

Back in the day Broadsheet archived our strip as a curated hub — Deadwax was the vinyl lounge that reportedly moved into the old Enmore Country Club site with Wagyu pastrami sangas and a sesame oil martini, while Urban List and Time Out kept telling everyone that Enmore was somehow “one of the coolest neighbourhoods” (Time Out even put us at No.17 worldwide — yes, #17, in case you’d forgotten). The Duke of Enmore, Enmore Hotel and the Enmore Country Club all got showroom makeovers; Jacoby’s Tiki Bar and Bar Planet were name‑checked alongside the usual suspects.

Our petty local drama reads like a gig poster: the Enmore Theatre was reportedly set to be protected from noise complaints under a special entertainment precinct plan (as Pedestrian noted) — because apparently we make enough racket to warrant official immunity. We’ve also had the more dramatic police interest in offensive graffiti on posters and that unforgettable Genesis Owusu night when the floor partially collapsed mid‑set (Junkee and Pedestrian did not sugarcoat that one).

Who would hate living here? If you crave suburban quiet, manicured lawns and silence after 9pm, Enmore will chew you up. If you hate choice, character and the possibility your Thursday plans could be interrupted by a sold‑out gig or a themed tiki revival, this isn’t your postcode.

Who’ll love it? Lovers of live music, vintage lanes, strong gelato opinions, mezze and kebabs (Colombo Social earned a namecheck for hoppers and roti), and families who remember Enmore Park and the splashy Annette Kellerman pool — we’re charming, a little loud, and unapologetically uneven. We wouldn’t change a streetlight on Enmore Road for the world.
