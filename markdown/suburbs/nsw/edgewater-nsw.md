# Edgewater (NSW)

Slug: `edgewater-nsw`

We live in Edgewater, NSW — which, according to the internet, is either a Gold Coast deck, a New Jersey spa or a Maribyrnong street address. Our morning routine is: make tea, open the papers, sigh as the algorithm insists that “Edgewater” means somewhere with million‑dollar canal views or skyline pools, never our quiet cul‑de‑sac.

As Urban List once noted, Edgewater Dining was lavished with praise for its sprawling deck on the Isle of Capri, “up close and personal” with the Q1 and the Surfers Paradise skyline. Concrete Playground even mentioned Isla Cantina being “nestled next to Edgewater Dining & Lounge Bar.” Meanwhile Broadsheet took us to 36 Edgewater Boulevard in Maribyrnong to ogle Gorilla Grill’s Asian‑inspired tacos and ribs — which, to be fair, sounds like a better weekend than our local footpath. Over in the US, NJMom gushed about SOJO Spa Club in Edgewater, NJ, and name‑checked Archetypus Café and Tina Pearson Salon; findglocal listed Zen with Nina and the South County Senior Activity Center in another Edgewater entirely.

Petty local scandal? There isn’t one to slap on front pages, so the internet fills the void with other postcodes. We like that. It means council meetings stay for council people, not influencers. It means our busiest weekend event is the neighbour’s mildly competitive hedge trimming.

Who would hate living here? People who crave skyline selfies and waterfront spritzes; Instagrammers who need a decked, million‑dollar backdrop at 7pm sharp. They’ll be disappointed that our “views” are mostly jacarandas and the unglamorous geometry of powerlines.

Who’d love it? Anyone who enjoys being the real Edgewater — low on hype, high on elbow room, and weirdly proud that when you Google us you have to scroll past three other luxury versions before you find anything that might remotely be our postcode. We’ll keep our quiet corners. The rest of the world can keep its Q1 sunsets and spa tents.
