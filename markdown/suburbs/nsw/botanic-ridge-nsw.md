# Botanic Ridge (NSW)

Slug: `botanic-ridge-nsw`

Back in the day we used to brag that our idea of excitement was a new sticker on the Botanic Ridge Community Centre noticeboard. These days we still queue like it's a festival — just for playgroup sign-ups and kinder enrolments.

A typical morning for us used to be a convoy to the Botanic Ridge Family & Community Centre, dropping the brood off at Botanic Ridge Kindergarten (yeah, the one inside the community centre at 10 Echidna Parade). If you wanted to know where everyone was, you called the kinder line — (03) 9373 5100 — or checked who'd remembered to subscribe to the playgroup mailing list. We were the people who judged each other by who could actually read the PlayConnect+ PDF without squinting.

There’s no juicy council saga in our archive — mostly gentle logistics and good intentions. Playgroup Victoria used to list our community centre and encourage us to “join the fun” (and yes, become a member today), and Kids First was the non-sensational headline in 2024 when they were selected to deliver kindergarten services at Botanic Ridge Primary School in 2025. It reads like suburban gold to those of us who measure time in kinder terms.

Who would hate living here? If your hobbies are late-night crowds, flash restaurants or anything that requires a postcode with a tram line, this isn't your vibe. Also, if you can’t handle hearing “what’s for morning tea?” at 7am, we’ll probably find you exhausting.

Who would love it? Families who prize a proper community rhythm — playgroups, kinder pickup routines and a community centre that actually gets used — will find it comforting. For years the local organisations kept things ticking: Playgroup Victoria, Kids First, the Botanic Ridge Community Centre — the sort of places that make a suburb feel stitched together rather than just plotted on a map.

We’re not pretending we invented culture here, but we do know where the stickers go on the noticeboard. Come for the kinder, stay for the memos.
