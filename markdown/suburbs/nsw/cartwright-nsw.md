# Cartwright (NSW)

Slug: `cartwright-nsw`

You know we’re from Cartwright, NSW because we’ve learnt to be proud of being the place no-one quite means when they Google “Cartwright”.

Our morning routine here is half a shrug and half a scavenger hunt: we open Broadsheet to check Tina Cartwright’s author page — yes, there’s a Tina — and then we scroll through a dozen stories that think “Cartwright” is either a lighthouse on the Sunshine Coast or a reality-TV punchline. Urban List, for instance, has lovely things to say about Point Cartwright Drive and even once recommended Pt. Cartwright Seafood Market for live South Australian lobsters — very exotic for us, and very far away.

The petty internet drama that features our name is always a delight of misdirection. Pedestrian TV once ran the saga of Kyla and Jackson Cartwright, the vanlifers hit with a huge damages bill; Nancy Cartwright has her own Simpsons-sized column inches; Junkee remembered Bec Hewitt (née Cartwright) for being a Home And Away alum. We get celebrity by association, like a cameo in someone else’s biopic.

Who would hate living here? People who want their suburb to be on every travel list and latte map. If you’re the sort who needs a lighthouse in your backyard or whale-watching tips from Urban List, you’ll probably prefer Point Cartwright on the Sunshine Coast — it gets all the postcards and the sunrise hot takes. If you want your name to trend, try being a Cartwright who makes a scandal; the internet will do the rest for you.

Who loves it here? We do. We like the anonymity. We like that when the paper writes “Cartwright did this,” you actually have to ask which Cartwright. We like being the quiet suburb that lets other Cartwrights do the heavy lifting on headlines so we can get on with living our small, perfectly ordinary lives. Also, it’s lovely to tell visitors that yes, we’re Cartwright — and no, we are not that Cartwright.
