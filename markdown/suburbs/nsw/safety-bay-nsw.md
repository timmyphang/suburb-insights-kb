# Safety Bay (NSW)

Slug: `safety-bay-nsw`

We know Safety Bay like the back of our sunscreen-smeared hands. Saturday used to start with a pilgrimage to Bayside Barista — yes, as Urban List noted, that cute little cafe in Safety Bay was 100 per cent gluten‑free and coeliac certified, serving things like spinach and parmesan arancini with melted camembert and house‑made slow‑braised beef cheek pies. Back in the day it felt like the town's unofficial welcome committee: bring a toddler, bring a toddler’s friend, bring a coeliac.

There isn’t a heap of recent press about us — most of the coverage lives in directory vibes — so instead of local scandals we’ve ended up famous for being reliably family-shaped. MumsPages used to list our businesses and service providers, plus pages for classes and activities for kids, sports for kids and fitness in Safety Bay. Translation: this place was built around prams, footy shorts and after‑school drop‑offs, not rooftop cocktail culture.

No dramatic council battles to gossip about here (the articles are mostly directories), which is either a blessing or a lullaby, depending on your temperament. Who would hate living here? People who need a 24/7 nightclub, a thousand different late‑night eateries, or who hate the smell of eucalyptus and organised sport. Who would love it? Coeliacs who miss proper certified options, mums who want easy info on local classes, and families who like their weekends scheduled around kids’ fixtures and the promise of a gluten‑free pie.

We’re proud, low‑key and serviceable — not flashy, but very good at being our own sort of cosy.
