# Modbury (NSW)

Slug: `modbury-nsw`

We start our weekends like everyone else in Modbury: pretending Civic Park is a good place to have a quiet coffee until memory kicks in and we remember the time that park was taken over for A Day in the Gully — Chet Faker and Ball Park Music on the bill, local food and wine vendors doing their best to keep punters fed. Good times, loud speakers, and a smell of toasted something in the breeze.

Back in the day the family outing looked suspiciously similar: a pilgrimage to Inflatable World — the enormous inflatable playground that used to dominate our weekend itineraries — followed by the chain-pizza stop at One Sneaky Cheetah’s Modbury outlet when someone inevitably declared they were “still hungry.” For late-night consolation there was KOSSO Korean BBQ on North East Road (976) where Sitchu swore by the cold Korean noodles and the spicy octopus mandu dumplings. We used to say it was essential.

Yes, we’re the suburb the weather guides point at when they talk about inland heat — Christmas Day 2025 had Modbury pencilled in for mid-30s territory — so if you melt at 30°C you’ll grumble all summer.

Who would hate living here? People who like silent parks, predictable weather, and restaurants that never whisper “spicy octopus” into your ear. Who would love it? Families who want an inflatable monument to adolescence, festival-goers who remember the Civic Park takeover, and anyone chasing genuinely cheerful Korean BBQ and chain pizza with no pretense.

We’re loud, a touch bouncy, and unapologetically ours. Come for the slides, stay for the dumplings.
