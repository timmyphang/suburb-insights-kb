# East Geelong (NSW)

Slug: `east-geelong-nsw`

We know this stretch — the weatherboard fences, the pocket parks, the people who put a folding chair out for any council event. Our briefing had no recent, dedicated coverage of East Geelong, so we’re leaning on the older write‑ups that remember the place fondly.

Morning here used to mean a shoreline saunter: Eastern Beach Reserve and the Baywalk Bollards getting the early light while someone readies a thermos down by Eastern Park. For years the local roundups — Sitchu’s suburb profile and travel guides — pointed to those exact bits of coastline and green space as why folk choose this side of town. Broadsheet even kept a listing for Goose at 91 Garden St, the multi‑section shop that used to feel like the retail spine for anyone who wanted homewares and kid stuff without driving in.

There are echoes of a city finding itself. Broadsheet’s guide talked about Geelong’s reinvention since the Ford factory closure, and Urban List has nodded at a shiny new waterfront plan (they’ve been keen on Geelong’s dining revival and Little Malop and Pakington Street’s sparkle). Broadsheet also ran profiles for Local Geelong and Bakery By Local Geelong — remember when the bakery’s sourdough got a mention in the same breath as the neighbourhood wine bar nights? And Songbird made an entrance in the old Igni digs, with Chiang Mai‑inspired street food cooked over red gum wood and charcoal — all lovely things that got a write‑up.

Petty local drama? Our sources were surprisingly polite. No council spat made the cut, just tasteful mentions of heritage homes and a Sitchu home tour about a 1950s weatherboard revival by Kimmy Hogan — so the most scandalous thing here is tasteful wallpaper.

Who would hate it: anyone who needs a nightly cocktail of neon, turnstiles and 24/7 delivery options. If you loathe trees, quiet foreshore walks or people who actually know their neighbours, this won’t be your scene.

Who would love it: families, older couples, anyone who wants bayside calm with parkland on the doorstep. If the idea of Eastern Beach Reserve, Eastern Park and a dependable local shop on Garden St appeals, congratulations — you’ll fit right in.
