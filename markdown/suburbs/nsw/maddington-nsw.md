# Maddington (NSW)

Slug: `maddington-nsw`

We live in Maddington — the one the listings politely described as about 15–16 kilometres south‑east of the Perth CBD — so yes, we’re not pretending to be inner‑city. Morning here means a slow pilgrimage to the big shopping centre: as the Maddington Central listing used to note, the centre was home to Coles, Woolworths and Kmart alongside 88 specialty stores and—if you’ve ever measured suburban love in asphalt—over 2,100 car parking bays (32 of them ACROD, because inclusion has its own parking quota).

Weekend ritual: someone drops the kids at Buttercups or Buggles, while the early‑learning veterans at Moombaki (134 Westfield St) claim the “no vacancies” bragging rights. If you’re parenting, East Maddington’s Child and Parent Centre — on the grounds of East Maddington Primary School at 79 Pitchford Avenue, facing Westfield Street — was the sort of place you’d call for practical help; the centre listing even names Kathryn McIntyre as the coordinator. For everything that needs fixing (cars, fences, patios), there were local stalwarts: Maddington Smash Repairs (family run since 1985 at 6 Malcolm Road) and Mackson’s workshop proudly making fencing and security products in Maddington.

We’ll be honest: the listings are refreshingly unsexy. They tell you what we are — useful, serviceable and oddly historical: one page even walks through the suburb’s early settler story. There’s no implied boutique bar crawl here; the public record is far more interested in childcare vacancies, bulk‑billing GPs and workshops than curated Instagram angles. Maddington Village General Practice was listed as independently owned and fully bulk‑billing, open Monday to Saturday — practical for when life happens.

Who would hate it here? If you live for artisanal everything or fetishise two‑block‑walkable nightlife, you’ll find our data a bit dull. Who would love it? Families, people who value straightforward services, folks who like knowing their smash repairer has been turning up since 1985, and anyone who appreciates a suburb that wears its utility with pride.

We’re not flashy. We’re the sort of place with childcare, bulk‑billing GPs and a very serious commitment to parking. And honestly? That’s our vibe — quietly, usefully Maddington.
