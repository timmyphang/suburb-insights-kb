# Aubin Grove (NSW)

Slug: `aubin-grove-nsw`

Back in the day, we’d line up our kids’ paint-sticky shirts for the Aubin Grove Playgroup Dyeing Easter Eggs and pretend we weren’t secretly timing who could get to the biscuit table first. Playgroup WA even had us in their pages — the very activity is listed there — and if you ever needed proof we exist in the internet archive, a giveaway post names “Tracy H. – Aubin Grove” as a winner (winners drawn 15 September 2021). That’s our claim to fame: grassroots glitter and a movie-pass win.

We’re the kind of suburb that turned community-sourced craft sessions into local lore. Playgroup WA’s annual report also shows “Regents Garden: Aubin Grove” in partner listings, so yes, we’ve got multi-generational appeal: toddlers mastering glue sticks, and the nonna brigade rolling their eyes with approval. These mentions are older — there aren’t any recent press pieces in the pack we found — so consider this more of a fond, slightly fuzzy scrapbook than a what’s-on guide.

There isn’t much petty council drama in the files we read (thank goodness). Our biggest controversy, historically, involved whether to put an extra bin at playgroup — thrilling stuff. The data’s thin, so we won’t invent a turf war about Christmas lights or farmers markets, even though Perth lists and lifestyle roundups loom in the background of our internet footprint.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who needs high‑end nightlife, a constant rotation of artisan everything, or someone who treats “quiet” like an insult. Bring a desperate need for buzzy brunches and you’ll be checking the train timetable within a week.

Who’d love it here? Families who want a proper playgroup with egg-dye scars as badges of honour, and people who value a suburb that’s quietly stitched into community networks — the kind that shows up in Playgroup WA notes and annual reports. We’re not flashy. We’re glue, glitter, and the person who remembered to bring spare scissors. If that’s your vibe, welcome home.
