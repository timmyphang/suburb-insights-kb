# Hazelbrook (NSW)

Slug: `hazelbrook-nsw`

Back in the day we’d pretend we were rugged explorers and end up ankle‑deep in mud — because that was the whole point. As Blue Mountains Mums noted, Horseshoe Falls in Hazelbrook was a picturesque, easy-to-medium 2km return walk with up to five waterfalls (yes, five) and even a glowworm corner they called Glow Worm Nook Falls. The listing also warned it gets very muddy after rain and recommended hiking shoes and extra care with kids. Sound advice. We ignored about half of it.

Morning routine: we’d convoy the crew, argue over gumboots, then bail for the quieter option when someone’s ankles gave out — Golden Horizons Park. Blue Mountains Mums called it delightful, spacious and peaceful; it really is the place to pretend you’re having a picnic while actually supervising rogue shoe removal.

Local drama? The data didn’t bother with feuds, so our biggest civic dispute remains whether to brave the waterfalls or take the swing set. Hard hitting stuff. For years the local listings page aggregated childcare, bush walks and community activities, which is council‑approved code for “bring the kids, there’s stuff to do.”

Who would hate it here: anyone allergic to mud, small waterfalls, or the responsible parenting required near slippery tracks. Also people who think family holidays should come with hotel room service.

Who would love it: families hunting for a school‑holiday adventure, glow‑worm romantics, and anyone who enjoys a compact, tree‑lined pocket where the loudest beef is wet socks. We’ll meet you at Horseshoe Falls — bring proper shoes and a towel.
