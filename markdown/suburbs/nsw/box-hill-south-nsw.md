# Box Hill South (NSW)

Slug: `box-hill-south-nsw`

We wake up in Box Hill South and the first thing we do is squint at the horizon to see if the K Home sign is still bigger than our aspirations. Back in the day Time Out told us Kmart’s K Home would land here on June 18 — and yes, for a while it felt like we’d officially beaten Ikea at its own game. 

Our weekend routine used to be gloriously pedestrian: we’d pop into Wattle Park Green Grocer at 194 Elgar Road for something sensible, then let the kids (or our inner children) loose at Box Hill Skatepark on Canterbury Rd & Middleborough Rd. For the thrill-seekers among us, Sitchu reminded everyone that Action Indoor Sports Centre is the local place to prove you still remember how to jump.

For years the local dining map was the thing people actually argued about. Broadsheet and Urban List made much of Box Hill’s pan‑Asian scene — and yes, even Box Hill South has its claims, like Simon’s Peiking Duck at 197B Middleborough Road, which everyone used to name-drop at dinner parties. Box Hill Central’s food precinct was the place to get deliciously indecisive.

We’re not immune to change. As Broadsheet noted, the suburb was part of Melbourne’s old “dry zone” until December 2021 — so when Bar Nearby turned up at the end of 2024 it felt like someone finally gave the neighbourhood permission to be slightly less church-hall. Cue the gentle civic arguments that lasted until happy hour.

Who would hate living here? If you need non-stop nightlife, artisanal everything, or a cafe on every corner chanting “new season,” this will feel cosy and mildly infuriating. If you flinch at a suburban centre that likes its Peking duck and its giant homewares store in equal measure, you’ll complain loudly and be quietly correct.

Who would love it? Families who want playgroup options like Asterakia or the Box Hill Salvation Army rooms, people who actually use skateparks and indoor sports centres, and anyone who appreciates a neighbourhood that used to be modest and is quietly, deliciously ambitious. We’re Box Hill South — modest, opinionated, and secretly very well fed.
