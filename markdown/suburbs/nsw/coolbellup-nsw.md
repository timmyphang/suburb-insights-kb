# Coolbellup (NSW)

Slug: `coolbellup-nsw`

Coolbellup, NSW — we’re the suburb that turns up to December with fairy lights and a pragmatic shrug.

Picture this: it’s a December evening and Hargreaves Road used to be the place you’d point the kids at and say, “Look — Santa’s throne!” Urban List once name-checked the display for its walk-through setup, photo booth, wooden cut-outs and interactive bits, open from 7:30pm to 11pm throughout December. It was the sort of suburban pageant that made the rest of Perth quietly reassess their tinsel budgets. We watched tiny humans queue for photos and parents practice their ‘festive but not desperate’ faces.

Mornings here used to begin with the soft hum of community life rather than the siren of a boutique espresso machine. Wednesdays were for Creative Kids at Coolbellup Library — 90 Cordelia Avenue — where board games, LEGO and the much-loved Wildcard Wednesday made an appearance on the program, according to Perth Mums Group. Storytime was the quieter, craftier sibling of school drop-off; Len Packham Hall and Reserve at 96 Cordelia Avenue even hosted “Fun With Fathers” mornings that felt like organised chaos in the best possible way.

We’re not going to pretend we’re a Michelin-starred postcode. If your idea of suburb bliss involves rooftop cocktail bars, boutique shopping and late-night DJ sets, you’ll hate us here — we have more glue sticks than gin mixers and we’re fine with that. Likewise, if you need every local listing to be on a glossy “world’s coolest neighbourhood” map, Time Out’s criteria are lovely, but they weren’t written for backyard disco nights at the reserve.

Who will love Coolbellup? Families who value a library that used to run proper kids’ programs, neighbours who turn a street into a seasonal spectacle on Hargreaves Road, and anyone who prefers a community calendar over curated coolness. Back in the day, we put on shows, told stories, and made room for kids to be kids — and honestly, that’s enough swagger for us.
