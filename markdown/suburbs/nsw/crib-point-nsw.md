# Crib Point (NSW)

Slug: `crib-point-nsw`

Back in the day we’d roll out to Martin Street like it was our local festival — tiny backpacks, mismatched socks and a suspiciously high number of sticky hands. St Josephs Crib Point used to host the playgroup on the school grounds, where the play equipment doubled as an obstacle course and a social media backdrop for anyone brave enough to climb a slide with a baby on their hip. If the weather wasn’t playing ball, we decamped to the school library and pretended storytime was the only reason we’d brought snacks.

Playgroup Victoria put us on the map — you could find St Josephs listed in the PlayConnect+ PDFs (their October 2025 and the March 2026 lists among them), which is bureaucratic proof we existed as more than a rumour. Those timetables meant we weren’t a pop-up; we were scheduled, sanctioned, and possibly over-caffeinated.

There’s no petty council scandal here to gossip about in the archives, which is both frustrating and wholesome. We’ll admit: this place isn’t for people who want nightlife at their doorstep or who can’t handle craft glue on their carpets. If you hate small shoes in the hallway, best move along.

But for anyone who likes an honest morning routine — kids running wild on school equipment, neighbours who’ll lend you a spare nappy, and the comfort of seeing your suburb in a Playgroup Victoria timetable — Crib Point used to be quietly perfect. We miss it, and we remember it fondly.
