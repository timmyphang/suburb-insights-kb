# Patterson Lakes (NSW)

Slug: `patterson-lakes-nsw`

We should probably start with a confession: we couldn't find any recent write-ups about Patterson Lakes, so for now we’re leaning on the archival hits — think nostalgic local, not breaking-news suburban scandal. Back in the day (and in the directories), this is the version of us people wrote about.

Morning here involves a practical kind of calm. Once upon a time Mr Patterson at 21 Thompsons Road had the kind of homey warmth that made you stay: ricotta‑stuffed brioche French toast, Spanish sardines on toasted rye and, yes, Industry Beans coffee. We tell newcomers it’s the sort of menu that smells like someone’s thoughtful brunch fantasy — which is to say we’re proud it existed.

By arvo we’re toggling between the “is that a boat?” and “is that a burger?” energy. The Famished Wolf — listed for Braeside and Patterson Lakes — used to be the name bandied about when you wanted a loaded dim sim crowned with kewpie mayonnaise, sweet soy, sambal oelek, crispy shallots and spring onions. It’s proof our culinary claim to fame is enthusiasm for toppings that could double as confetti.

Real estate listings will tell you the sunny sales version: Sitchu flagged properties on the border with Seaford (56 Eel Race Road gets a name‑check), promising easy access to the Freeway and Seaford beaches. Belle Property did the paperwork; the brochures did the dreaming. We live where the pitch meets the water.

Community life, when it made the newsletters, looked like the Patterson Lakes Community Centre at 54–70 Thompson Rd hosting a National Playgroup Week party on Thursday 21 March, 9:30–11:30 — complete with the city council contact pattersonlakes.cc@kingston.vic.gov.au and (03) 9581 3040 if you wanted to RSVP a few years ago. That’s us: practical, local, organised enough to email about beach‑themed parties.

Who would hate living here? People who need constant hype, rooftop bars that spin, or newspapers that write daily thinkpieces about their suburb. Who would love it? Families who like community centres, anyone who appreciates a good dim sim with more condiments than guilt, and folk who want seaside access without the CBD price tag. We’re not flashy. We are reliably Patterson Lakes.
