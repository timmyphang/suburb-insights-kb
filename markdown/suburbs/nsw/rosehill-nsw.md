# Rosehill (NSW)

Slug: `rosehill-nsw`

Saturdays in Rosehill feel like someone fused a Victorian picnic with a corporate expense account and forgot to tell the horses. We roll out of bed, stagger past the gum trees and end up at Rosehill Gardens where the fashion is half fascinators, half practical rain jacket — because this is Western Sydney, not a perfume ad. As Broadsheet noted, the Sydney Autumn Racing Carnival used to land here, complete with trackside pop-ups and a brand-new Hard Rated Home Straight bar that promised to be “a fresh destination” for anyone who thinks a bar needs a straight line of sight to the finish.

If we’re feeling fancy we’d remind each other about The Riders Club, which was popping up trackside with roaming canapés, live cooking stations and, yes, a six-hour beverage package — which sounds like a medical condition but was very Instagrammable. For something less thundery and more tipsy, remember the Sydney Wine Festival at Rosehill Racecourse? Concrete Playground had the numbers: $25 online, $30 at the gate, ten wine-tasting tickets and a food spread from crepes and oysters to burgers and German sausages. We ate and we judged — mostly the sausages.

Back in the day we’d also drag out the history lesson. Time Out still praises Elizabeth Farm — birthplace of the Australian wool industry — so we’re old-money historically, if not in the ledger. Time Out even included Elizabeth Farm in its list of things to do for 2024, which is our polite way of saying: bring your Nan, she’ll like the quilts.

The local soap-opera subplot? Planning discussions about Sydney Metro West once poked at relocating Rosehill Racecourse and the possibility of up to 25,000 new homes on the site, as Time Out reported. We don’t have a pitchfork yet, but we do have opinions.

Who would hate it here: anyone allergic to crowds, horses, or a good sausage sizzle. Who would love it: people who like a soundtrack of thundering hooves, a decent wine pour, and a museum where you can pretend history is curated just for you. We’re Rosehill — loud, historical, slightly greasy, and unapologetically fun.
