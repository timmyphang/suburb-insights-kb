# Chirnside Park (NSW)

Slug: `chirnside-park-nsw`

We’ll be honest: our idea of grand civic theatre is lining up for gluten-free doughnuts at Just Yummy on 13 Meadowgate Drive and pretending it’s a Saturday market. Back in the day the guidebooks loved to remind outsiders we were both pastoral and oddly specific — Urban List even name‑checked Just Yummy as a celiac’s dream. We wore that badge with pride.

Mornings here were bedside with a map of weekend options: tee off or pretend to on the greens, book a room at Yarra Valley Lodge and eye the restaurant Bella like it’s a destination, or spend an hour perfecting your putting at Maroondah Adventure Golf Park on 363 Maroondah Hwy. Time Out used to wink at the lodge as the gateway to the valley; Urban List put our mini‑golf on a “must-play” list. We’ll take those bruised egos and keep swinging.

There was that one minor, very local drama where the water people made everyone boil up — yep, Broadsheet ran the list and our postcode was on it. As reality TV, it ranked somewhere between “spot the missing milk” and “mystery of the tepid shower.” No permanent scars, just a shared pot of boiled pasta and a story for visitors.

If you loathe small, charmingly specific pleasures, you’d hate it here. If your idea of a Friday night is a neon-club crawl, Chirnside Park will feel like a quiet documentary about tasteful cheesecake pairings — remember BASQUE, billed as our chic little burnt‑cheesecake-and‑champers spot? It’s not your scene. Also, if you require round‑the‑clock arts festivals, we did host a weekend takeover once (Concrete Playground wrote about the 24‑hour festival that blanketed Chirnside Park with live music and installations), but we’re not exactly a permanent rave.

Who’ll love it? Families — Oxley Kids on Old Melbourne Road was the community hub for little humans and Jellybeans Playgroup. Folks who like staying within arm’s reach of wineries and a decent lodge. People who take their mini‑golf, cheesecake and gluten‑free baking very seriously.

Heads up — these are the old press hits; we’re being nostalgic, not offering a live directory. Still, we know what we like, and we like it quiet, quirky and just a little bit proud.
