# Villawood (NSW)

Slug: `villawood-nsw`

Back in the day our claim to fame in the papers was an odd mix of screaming kids, screaming engines and earnest art workshops — and yes, all the write-ups you find about Villawood are from older pieces, so consider this a nostalgic love letter, not a tourist brochure.

Saturday mornings used to mean shuffling a stroller up to Wiggles World: Wiggly Play Centre at Unit 1, 850 Woodville Rd, where the ball pits and themed party rooms kept the tiny dictators entertained while adults pretended the cafe existed for them. If you wanted to prove you were not a softie, you could have taken the ankle-biters to Zone Bowling Villawood after its flashy 2019 refurb — arcade, laser tag, the whole carnival of consumer joy.

We’ve always been the kind of place that does adrenaline without apology: Extreme Indoor Go Karting’s three tracks — including that terrifying 500m “Extreme Track” with an overpass — and the smaller sibling to the big city gym, Elevate Climbing / Sydney Indoor Climbing Gym in Villawood, gave us bragging rights without the foot traffic of St Peters. For calm, there was the Woodville Alliance Supported playgroup at Unit G08, 1 Villawood Place, where grown-ups made polite small talk while paint-smeared children negotiated the economy.

The proper story, though, is more complicated. Our patch has been the backdrop for national conversations — Broadsheet covered the Refugee Art Project’s fortnightly workshops in the Villawood Immigration Detention Centre (Safdar Ahmed’s project has been running since 2010), and Junkee, Pedestrian and others have traced the centre’s long, difficult history. We wear that history: it’s part of who we are, and nobody’s getting a punchline about it here.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who wants sleek laneway brunches and zero noise — between birthday parties, kart engines and climbing chalk hands we are gloriously loud. Who would love it? Families who used to flock to Wiggles World and Woodville Alliance, climbers and kart nutters, and anyone who slid into Maria’s DMs for a cookie or a Master Baker Villawood Burnt Basque cheesecake pick-up from Maria’s Villawood HQ.

We know what the outside world writes about us — and they often miss the bit where this place is messy, surprising and properly lived-in.
