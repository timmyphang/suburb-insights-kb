# Bellbird (NSW)

Slug: `bellbird-nsw`

Back in the day the internet liked to tidy us into a few neat lines — and that’s mostly what we’ve got to work with. There aren’t any recent write-ups, so for this week’s roast we’re leaning on old listings and the bits the directories remembered: Sitchu’s property pages, Local’s community listings and the family tale spun on Bellbirdkidz’s About Us.

We know the scene here: we’ll admit we’re the kind of place where listings brag you’re “5 mins to Cessnock CBD, 15 mins to Wine Country, 1hr to Newcastle, 2hrs to Sydney.” We roll our eyes at the marketing-speak, then head out anyway. Mornings for us mean a wander past Carmichael Oval, waving at whoever’s dropping off at Honey Bee Preschool, and nodding to the playgroup crowd (Bellbird Playgroup has been doing its thing for local kids for years — Local has the details). The village vibe is real; Sitchu’s property blurbs loved calling it “quaint” and “community-focused,” and yes, we begrudgingly agree.

Our resume of local landmarks is short and honest: the iconic Bellbird Hotel turns up on every real-estate description, the workers club gets name-checked, and there’s a Bellbird Park Bowling Club if you enjoy lawn bowls or passive gossip. Need tyres? Kumho Tyre’s pages point to Bellbird Tyres as a Kumho Platinum dealer. Want to teach toddlers to glue things to cardboard? Bellbirdkidz’ story—started by “one man in a van,” then the Dean family—shows we punch above our weight in early-learning supply humbug.

Petty drama? The papers didn’t leave us any juicy council squabbles, so we’ll skip the made-up feuds. If anything’s controversial here it’s stylistic: some of us like the Wine Country proximity; others resent being reduced to a tagline in a Sitchu ad.

Who would hate it: people who need daily city theatre and a thousand cafes on tap — sorry, not our scene. Who would love it: families who value preschool playgroups, people chasing affordable land near Mount View and the Hunter Valley vineyards, and anyone who appreciates a town that turns up in property listings with a wink rather than a billboard. We’re small, stubborn and embarrassingly proud of it.
