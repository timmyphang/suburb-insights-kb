# Oakleigh South (NSW)

Slug: `oakleigh-south-nsw`

We know the walk from Eaton Mall to the skate rink like it’s a pilgrimage route: a loop that usually starts with doughnuts and ends with someone asking, “Who brought skates?” We’ll admit it — we’ll queue for The Oakleigh Doughnut Co (Nic and Yani Kabylakis opened the place in 2017, Eaton Mall, Greek‑inspired doughnuts, with Five Senses on the side) like it’s church. Then, if we’re feeling nostalgic or painfully competent, we’ll detour past the old spots Bar Olympia and the Greek bakeries — Nikos Quality Cakes, Vanilla Cakes and Lounge — and wonder whether we ever actually left our postcode.

If it’s a weird Tuesday we tell people about the time Pebble began in the car park of the La Roca bouldering gym back in November 2025 — a roving kitchen that started beside the climbing wall. We like telling that story because it makes our car parks sound like incubators for culinary fame. And when we want to really embarrass our out‑of‑towners, we mention the Olympic Ice Skating Centre on 1080 Centre Road — yes, suburban ice skating. We used to skate, you used to laugh, now you’re secretly jealous.

Local life is quietly busy: Oakleigh South Primary School has over 1,000 kids (we’re raising generations who can outrun you and then make a souvlaki), and the Oakleigh South Child & Family Centre runs the playgroups in a proper community hub with kindergarten and maternal child health services. That’s why weekends sometimes look like a choreography of prams, reusable bags and people holding leftover doughnuts like trophies.

Petty drama? We don’t have time for soap operas — we’re too busy planning our shopping runs to Fenton & Fenton’s warehouse sale (open Fri March 20–Sun March 22, 2026 at 6–8 Eskay Road, Oakleigh South). It’s the suburb’s version of gladiatorial clearance hunting and we’ll be there, cardigans ready.

Who would hate living here: anyone allergic to Greek hospitality, second‑helpings, or communal ice rinks. Who would love it: families who want a real hub, food obsessives who worship Eaton Mall, and anyone who enjoys a proud postcode that quietly punches above its weight. We’ll keep the doughnuts. You can keep your cynicism.
