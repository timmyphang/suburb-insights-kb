# Waratah (NSW)

Slug: `waratah-nsw`

Back in the day we thought being called Waratah meant somebody would at least decorate the main street with giant native flowers. Instead we learned the internet prefers two things: dusty real-estate photos and a very proud directory entry on MumsPages.

Our weekday morning looks like this: we drop the kids at the local primary school, steal five minutes on the playground with a thermos, then wander over to Waratah shopping village for whatever the day requires — milk, a chat with whoever’s working behind whatever counter is open, and the small civic pleasure of running into the same neighbour we’ve been bumping into since before the renovations started. If we’re house‑sniffing we’ll scroll old Sitchu listings to laugh at staged photos — remember 10 Vera Street? Renovated and ready for investors, apparently. Or the neat townhouse at 15/62 Allowah Street that bragged about being “moments to the uni, Mater Hospital and Waratah shopping village.”

Full disclosure: our media file is basically vintage. For years the local headlines were either property ads on Sitchu or directory pages on MumsPages. If you Google “Waratah” you’ll also get a lot of love for a two‑storey bar called The Waratah in Darlinghurst — lovely, if you live in Darlinghurst. That’s not us. We’re the folks who get the Mater shift workers on their way home and the kids learning to ride without training wheels in the parkland.

Who would hate living here? People who need nightlife that changes every month, or anyone convinced their suburb should trend on Urban List. Who would love it? Families, hospital staff, students who prefer cheap(ish) rentals to the inner‑city chaos, and anyone who enjoys knowing where the school bell is before the council website does.

We’re not flashy. We don’t need to be. We’ve got a shopping village that knows our name, a primary school within earshot of the park, and enough property listings to keep the real‑estate agents busy. That, frankly, suits us just fine.
