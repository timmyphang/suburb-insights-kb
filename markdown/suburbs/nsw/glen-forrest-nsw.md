# Glen Forrest (NSW)

Slug: `glen-forrest-nsw`

We know the exact moment Glen Forrest wakes up: when the kookaburras start their daily chorus over Hardey Road and someone pretends the lizards aren’t sunning on the footpath. The Hardey Road Bushwalking Circuit winds through Beelu National Park so quietly you’ll likely have it to yourself — and, as Sitchu cheekily reminds us, with a haunted reputation that gives every rustle a backstory.

Back in the day — Broadsheet ran the story in 2016 — a pair of Korean owners, Park and Ilmi Kim, opened Beagle Bagel at Shop 6, 3–5 Railway Parade. It felt very Glen Forrest: dog-friendly, bakery-fresh, and proudly community-focused. Broadsheet’s listings even gave us the comforting tiny-details: Sundays 9am–3pm, and they were on the WA Day round-up in 2017 with hours noted (8am–2pm). They made their bagels from scratch, which is basically artisanal witchcraft in the hills.

Local drama? If you count phantom footsteps on the Hardey Road Circuit as drama, we’ve got enough to keep a true-crime podcast entertained for a week. Otherwise, our scandals are smaller: someone parked poorly at a community event, someone else brought a sandwich that wasn’t worth talking about. Small-town pettiness, but with better birdlife.

Who would hate it here: people who require 24/7 nightlife, Michelin-starred dining options every two blocks, or those allergic to the idea of sharing a trail with a roo. If you need constant caffeine variety, we’ll remind you gently that famous inner-city brunch trappings don’t dominate our railway parade.

Who would love it: walkers, dog owners, anyone who thinks getting lost in a bush circuit is a weekend plan and not a crisis. Lovers of low-key community vibes — and nostalgics who remember the Beagle Bagel days — will feel right at home. We’re the kind of suburb that whispers rather than shouts, and honestly, we wouldn’t have it any other way. As Broadsheet and Sitchu observed: quiet, a bit haunted, and full of character.
