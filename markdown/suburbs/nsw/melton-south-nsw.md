# Melton South (NSW)

Slug: `melton-south-nsw`

Back in the day we measured weekends in fluorescent hall time, and if you told us “community centre” we’d know exactly which sticky corner you meant. Playgroup Victoria’s 2018/19 annual report even named Tiny Tempers Melton South, so yes — our identity crisis has a timetable and a tea towel.

We used to pile into Botanica Springs Children’s & Community Centre for whatever felt like a rite of passage: tiny chairs, louder toddlers, and whoever remembered to bring the biscuits. The paperwork says Melton was on the PlayConnect+ list (as of January 2026) with sessions at the Melton West Children and Community Centre — which is to say, we’ve been organised enough to make schedules and brave enough to actually turn up.

There’s no council melodrama in these snippets, no boutique cafés to gatekeep or late-night bars to complain about — just mums, dads and whoever’s on nappy duty, making this suburb’s culture out of craft glue, song time and staggered pickups. If you’re looking for biting nightlife gossip, the archive’s not giving it.

Who would hate it here? People who think small talk ends at “Is that marmalade?” and who miss having the quiet to themselves. Who would love it? Families who want a village vibe without the poshness, playgroup regulars proud enough to point at an annual report and say, “That’s ours.”

We wear our Tiny Tempers badge like a souvenir: unpolished, a little sticky, and genuinely ours.
