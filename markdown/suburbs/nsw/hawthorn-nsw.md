# Hawthorn (NSW)

Slug: `hawthorn-nsw`

Back in the day we’d stroll down Glenferrie Road like it owed us money, coffee to-go (or whatever the scene called for then) and a paperback from Readings Hawthorn tucked under our arm. Readings — remember the Federation-era shopfront that used to be an old bank? We used to pretend the history nook was where we became better people. As Broadsheet once listed, it felt suitably sincere.

Our weekend ritual used to be a tragic, delicious loop: a detour to Porgie + Mr Jones on Auburn Road for those beloved scrambled eggs and courtyard vibes, a gelato pit-stop at Piccolina’s new Hawthorn flagship (they unveiled that swanky fit-out in 2022), and then a record-sized palate cleanse at Sabi Sounds or Bar Selecta on Glenferrie — because nothing says ‘we live here’ like vintage JBL speakers and saké served in listening booths. The Lido Cinema’s rooftop was our excuse to pretend we were cultured. Concrete Playground and Urban List loved to remind us we were doing suburbality properly.

Petty local drama? Oh yes. For years the footy pages sniffed and snorted — Pedestrian gleefully roasted every alternative Hawks jumper design ever released, while also applauding the club for making history off-field. We cheered and rolled our eyes in equal measure; it’s Hawthorn, we do contradictions well.

Who would hate living here? People allergic to bookshops, listening bars and decent pastries. If you can’t stand a perfumery that looks like a European atelier (Peony, we’re looking at you, Time Out), or you find joyless a suburb with Piccolina, KumoKumo Cheesecake and a Yo-Chi for emergency lychee pearls, move along.

Who would love it here? Families who liked Glenferrie Festival’s market chaos (it was set to return on 29 March 2026 with over 100 stalls), cinephiles who worship rooftop screenings at the Lido, and anyone who wants leafy Anderson Park and a neighborhood that can be both properly posh and delightfully odd. We’ll keep the vinyl on, the pages turning, and the debate about the worst jumper design alive — because that’s Hawthorn, quietly smug and very, very ours.
