# Thornton (NSW)

Slug: `thornton-nsw`

We live in Thornton — yes, that Thornton — the one every search engine keeps trying to turn into a film festival. We’ll stand on Cambridge Street (the one that tags along on Sitchu as “1-2-13 Cambridge Street, Thornton NSW 2322”) and watch our postcode get poached by other Thorntons with better press agents.

Mornings here are scrolling through names that aren’t ours: Broadsheet championing Billy Bob Thornton’s performance in Landman, Pedestrian uploading chill stills of the man at a bar, and Concrete Playground running Warwick Thornton interviews about The New Boy. Time Out keeps sending us back to Samson & Delilah and Sweet Country like they’re hometown pride — which, technically, they’re not, but we’ll happily borrow the vibe. Urban List even slipped Sigrid Thornton into a Sydney-to-Hobart documentary sentence and we didn’t even get a heads-up.

Our local drama? Search-engine identity theft. One minute we’re a humble suburb, the next we’re swamped by listings for a yurt at “The Grove Farm Stay, Thornton” over in New Zealand, a tiny-cabin mention that thinks our name sounds exotic, and a Mulgowie Road detour in Queensland that clearly missed the memo. For years the internet’s loved to conflate every Thornton on the map, and honestly — we’re flattered and mildly annoyed.

Who would hate living here? People who want a unique Google footprint. If your thing is being un-findable, this is not the postcode for you. If you crave no confusion between your street and a filmography, prepare for disappointment.

Who’d love it here? Film nerds with a sense of humour — the ones who can enjoy that our name pops up next to Warwick Thornton’s AACTA‑winning chatter, Sigrid Thornton’s narration credits and the American gravitas of Billy Bob Thornton in Landman. We’re a whisper of celebrity by association, a suburb happy to be mistaken for drama as long as the kettle’s on and someone’s queued Samson & Delilah on the telly.

Back in the day we used to worry about council meetings. Nowadays our biggest flex is that three different Thorntons keep turning up in good copy. We’ll take it.
