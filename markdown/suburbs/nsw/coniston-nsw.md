# Coniston (NSW)

Slug: `coniston-nsw`

We live in Coniston, NSW — and every time we tell someone where we’re from they do a little mental flipchart: is that the Coniston Lane with the gigs, the Lake District Coniston with the Old Man hike, or the Coniston Community Centre that runs a two‑course lunch in Patchway? We get it. The internet’s file on “Coniston” is a confused buffet.

Morning here is mostly watching tourists on Google Maps. They click “Coniston” and end up planning their weekend around Concrete Playground’s Lost Movements line‑ups at Coniston Lane — you know, the listings that mentioned Mercury Sun, Moski Jo and those bi‑monthly showcases — before blinking and realising they’ve booked the wrong state. Time Out is over in England telling people about Coniston Launch and the Old Man of Coniston like it’s a pilgrimage. Meanwhile, Patchway’s Coniston Community Centre has more online intimacy than we do: Coniston Connect, a community café, room hire and the whole civic vibe is all over its site. Even Broadsheet seems to prefer writing about bakmi and brassicas than our postcode.

Petty local drama? We don’t have enough press to make anything worth a feud, so the drama is outsourced: arguments about which Coniston gets the better playlist, or whether Coniston Lane’s Audio Collective pool parties were wilder than ours might hypothetically be. We’ve never met those DJs, but we’ll take the clout, thanks.

Who would hate living here: anyone who wants their suburb to be famous, Insta‑tagged and on a booking confirmation by Friday. If you need a constant stream of features, festival‑level foot traffic and a local café that Broadsheet can’t ignore, move on — you’ll be scanning event calendars and sighing.

Who’d love it: people who like being the other Coniston. The ones who enjoy a quiet postcode, the occasional baffled tourist, and the smug pleasure of correcting someone with a straight face. For now we’ll keep letting Time Out, Concrete Playground and Patchway have the headlines. We’ll keep the postcode cosy — and the confusion entertaining.
