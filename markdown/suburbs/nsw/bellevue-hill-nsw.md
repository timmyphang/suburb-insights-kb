# Bellevue Hill (NSW)

Slug: `bellevue-hill-nsw`

We know Bellevue Hill by the weight of the magnolia petals on the footpath — the same magnolia Alesandro Ljubicic once photographed for his paintings — and by the way the traffic eases when the private-school drop-offs finish. We drive down Bellevue Road remembering the tiny Tucker pop-up Broadsheet wrote about, all “gourmet sangas, family-sized pies and general-store vibes,” and the way S’WICH began here with a short lease on a deserted deli back in May 2019 before becoming a Bondi story.

Weekends used to look like this: a detour to Patricks on Bellevue for bread and a fresh juice (yes, Joseph and his uncles Sam, Frank and Tony used to run the place like a family sitcom), a stop at The Grumpy Baker’s Bellevue Hill outpost for something dog-friendly, then a walk through Cooper Park — the hidden bushland everyone brags about. Even Cooper Park Community Hall got its moment: a $796,000 refit opened in April 2023 with a new kitchen, decking, meeting room, bike racks and yes, EV charging. It was very Bellevue Hill of us to upgrade the place and then Instagram it.

We also collect tabloid moments like postcards. Time Out had us third on the wealth leaderboard with an average taxable income of $250,899, so yes, we hear the jokes. Then there are the eyebrow-raisers: Scots College getting permission to skip lockdown for a six-month “manhood” camp, and those nights when headlines mentioned arrests after a botched assassination attempt tied to John Ibrahim — flashy stuff for a suburb that likes its privacy.

Who would hate living here? People who crave anonymity, bargain-bin cafes, or Uber fares that don’t feel like a small mortgage — Time Out and Pedestrian both wrote about eastern-suburb fare hikes, and we felt that sting. Who would love it? Families who want Cooper Park’s tracks and playground, parents who value proximity to Scots, Cranbrook and Bellevue Public School, and anyone who misses the boutique bodega vibe of Crave Wares and the local grocer charm that used to define our shopping run.

We’ll keep polishing the windows and pretending the next headline won’t be about us. It’s Bellevue Hill — beautiful view, loud backstory.
