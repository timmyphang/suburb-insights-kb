# Killara (NSW)

Slug: `killara-nsw`

Back in the day — and yes, everything we’ve got on Killara here is pre-2024 — we used to laugh at Google when it tried to tell visitors that our leafy north‑shore postcode was really a whisky distillery in Richmond, a Sunshine Coast farm stay and a Yarra Valley winery. Killara, it seems, is a name that refuses to be pinned down, and that’s half the fun.

We start the weekend like proper locals: drop the kids at Killara Connect 4 Kids (Fridays, 9:30am at Killara Public School, 1 Ridgeland Avenue — the playgroup listings say so) or sneak off to Choir Mamas if we fancy a daytime singalong while someone else supervises the toddlers. Then it’s a quick circuit of Selkirk Park — the sneaky backstreet playground with big trees, easy parking on Marian Street and the Marian Street Cafe just a stroll away, as the north‑shore mums noted.

Our social calendar still includes rites of passage. The Greengate Hotel on the Pacific Highway has been described as an institution: beer garden, rooftop atrium and that indispensable Sunday roast. Sitchu even reminds people to bring their coins for two‑up at The Greengate on Anzac Day — tradition, nostalgia, and a long bar to pretend we’re all sophisticated.

We’re quietly smug about the Seidler house on our hills — Harry and Penelope’s modernist masterpiece that pops up in weekend wander lists and opens to the public now and then. And for those who like their suburbs with secrets, there’s that three‑hectare English‑style garden Arthur Newling Swain tucked behind suburban houses — proper hideaway vibes.

Who would hate living here? If you loathe trees, weekday school‑run choreography, and pubs that double as local theatres of polite gossip, this isn’t your scene. Also, if you went expecting a Tasmanian whisky tour, the Killara Distillery in Richmond will disappoint you — it’s the wrong Killara.

Who’ll love it here? Families who value good schools (hello, Killara High School and Killara Public), people who want bushland on their doorstep (Lane Cove and Garigal National Parks nearby), and anyone who enjoys the calm counterpoint of a suburban Sunday roast, a hidden garden and a playgroup that actually knows your kid’s name. We’ll keep tending the map while the internet mixes up our whisky with our playgrounds — and we’ll laugh about it over a roast at The Greengate.
