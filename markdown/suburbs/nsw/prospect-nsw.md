# Prospect (NSW)

Slug: `prospect-nsw`

Back in the day the internet kept mixing us up with another Prospect and honestly, we let it — because our backyard is a lot quieter and wetter. There aren’t any recent pieces specifically about Prospect, NSW in the dataset we were given; most of the coverage points to Prospect in Adelaide. So consider this a modest, local love letter (with a roast) based on what actually is about us.

We start our weekends at the water. We’re the people who know the best line around Prospect Reservoir — the one Concrete Playground cheekily put on its list of “the ten best bike rides in and around Sydney.” Dawn there looks like someone paused the city and spilled a sky across the dam. We cycle the Orphan School Creek route when we want to convince ourselves we’re training for something, then admit we’re mostly training for second breakfast.

The internet’s idea of our social life is weirdly aspirational: Broadsheet and Sitchu were busily writing up Pantry on Prospect, Picoso Mexican Bar y Cocina, Anchovy Bandit and new pubs — but that’s the other Prospect. As Concrete Playground also reminded us, the festival screenings at Palace Nova Prospect Cinemas belong to Adelaide, not our patch. We have no beef — we’re flattered to be fashionable by proxy — but also slightly betrayed.

There’s not much local petty drama in the snippets we found (no council scandals, no soap-opera bakeries), which suits us. If anything, the main quarrel is pedestrian: who gets the good picnic table by the reservoir on Sunday.

Who would hate living here? If you need 24/7 nightlife, a glut of new wine bars, or a precinct that mistakes itself for a boulevard, you’ll be bored. We’re not flashy; we’re water, trees and routes.

Who would love it here? Cyclists, dog-walkers, families who like a half-hour commute to nowhere but great views, and anyone who’s quietly proud that our best PR is a dam and a creek. We’ll keep the reservoir, you keep the headline grabs — and when the rest of the internet finally wakes up to Prospect, NSW, we’ll be on our bikes watching it roll past.
