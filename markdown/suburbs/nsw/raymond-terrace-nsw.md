# Raymond Terrace (NSW)

Slug: `raymond-terrace-nsw`

We wobble out of the car at Grahamstown Dam like it's our town square — except quieter, flatter and suspiciously good for selfie-free cycling. Back in the day (and yes, most of our sources are older), Concrete Playground remembered us as the inland cousins of Port Stephens: away from the beaches, closer to Newcastle Airport, and boasting that very 11‑kilometre off‑road cycleway along the dam’s southern shoreline. We know every bit of that gravel like the back of a flaky hand.

Our weekend routine used to run like this: morning laps or a lazy ride at Grahamstown Dam, then a soft detour to Boomerang Park where Illuminate Raymond Terrace — which was scheduled for 14 June 2025 in the listings we’ve got — promised to turn the place into a glowing wonderland. If it sounded twee on paper, it sounded family-friendly in practice. For the smaller humans, Lakeside Leisure Centre was the reliably warm option, with a large indoor pool around 30°C and a very practical 50‑metre outdoor pool, plus water features that made toddlers into submerged philosophers.

Community here has always been more calendar than cocktail list. Thou‑Walla Family Centre used to run supported playgroups and parenting programs on the grounds of Irrawang Public School (21 Geer St), and MyTime Raymond Terrace met Wednesdays at 12:45pm at Irrawang’s SACCs on 11 Roslyn Street — so if your social life peaked at sandwich-making and a gentle cuppa at playgroup, you were in luck.

Petty drama? The press didn’t give us any juicy scandals — which is a scandal in itself. Instead, the narrative is stubbornly wholesome: parks (Boomerang, Bettles, Finnan, Lakeside Park Playground), family events, pools and playgroups. We’ll own that: it’s unsexy and dependable.

Who would hate living here: people who need a beach to Instagram five times a day, or anyone who finds cycleways insufficiently flashy. Who would love it: families, grandparents with clipboards, and anyone who values a sensible heated pool and a playgroup that actually exists on a Wednesday.

We’re not flashy. We’re practical, a little damp around the edges and embarrassingly proud of our parks. Come for the cycleway, stay for the warm indoor pool, and don’t expect a skyline.
