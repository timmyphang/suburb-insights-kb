# Hampton Park (NSW)

Slug: `hampton-park-nsw`

We start Saturdays like everyone else here: one parent slides into the car after school drop-offs at Hampton Park Primary and River Gum Primary, the other wrestles a reluctant teen toward Hampton Park Secondary College, and by 9am we’re circling for a park near Hampton Park Shopping Centre. Back in the day we turned to the Sitchu property pages for pep talks — they called the place “a grand family residence of timeless appeal,” which is either aspirational marketing or our official suburb anthem. We’ll take it.

If you liked the idea of our postcode having a personality, Broadsheet once shone a spotlight on Kalye Marinas — a Filipino seafood boil that set up shop at 502 Hampton Street. That felt like someone finally acknowledged we can do spicy, saucy and communal when we want to. Time Out also tied a ribbon to Robyn Court when it made their 2025 round-up of Australia’s best Christmas lights; yes, our cul-de-sacs know how to go hard for festive LEDs.

We’ve had our tabloid moments too. Pedestrian TV reported on counter-terrorism raids years ago that touched Hampton Park among other suburbs; an 18‑year‑old from Hampton Park remained in custody. Not our finest hour, and no one here enjoys being the centre of grim headlines — but we’re resilient. We talk about it, move on, and keep hosting barbecues.

Who would hate living here? If you crave boutique anonymity and artisan everything, Hampton Park will make you long for a laneway with more espresso snobbery than playground drop-off banter. If you can’t cope with suburban earnestness — the mums, the dads, the kids in school polos — this isn’t for you.

Who would love it here? Families, first-home buyers looking for something practical, anyone who prefers a neighbourhood where the shopping centre, Hallam Train Station and the schools are all within reach. For years the local listings and guides have been telling the same story: low-key, family-first, and surprisingly capable of a good seafood boil or an over-the-top Christmas street. We’ll keep our lights on for you.
