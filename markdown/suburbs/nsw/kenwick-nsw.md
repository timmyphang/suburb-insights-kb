# Kenwick (NSW)

Slug: `kenwick-nsw`

We know Kenwick by the smell that used to drift through Kenwick Village shopping centre — slow-roasted pork, curry leaves and something that makes you forget what suburb you’re in for a minute. Back in the day Broadsheet ran a portrait of Nasi Bali (Max Veenhuyzen, 17 Nov 2020, updated 22 Dec 2020) and, yes, as the piece said, most punters at that 20‑person joint were there for the babi guling — roast suckling pig with rice. They also tried the ayam betutu and bebek betutu, and honestly, who are we to argue with history?

Our weekend routine was simple: pretend we had plans elsewhere, then get lured to that cosy spot in the village. There’s a comforting humility to a place that doesn’t need neon signage or an influencer launch — it just does its Balinese thing and feeds you properly.

Petty drama? The record’s thin. Broadsheet’s search pages and Perth guide mention Kenwick, and that’s about the size of the archive. So instead of scandal, we’ll file this under: quiet pride and a single great meal everyone remembers.

Who would hate it here: people who need twenty different artisanal menus and clout-chasing coffee carts. Who would love it: anyone who knows that sometimes the best local credential is a proper babi guling and a place that fits twenty people and still feels like home.

For years the local food story was that one diner in the village — and that’s honestly our flex.
