# Bassendean (NSW)

Slug: `bassendean-nsw`

We start our Saturdays on Old Perth Road like it’s a ritual — not the latte kind, the stroll-past-heritage-buildings kind. Back in the day Bertie opened up at 77 Old Perth Rd (James Connolly’s little wine-bar experiment, if you remember) and for a minute Bassendean felt like the sort of place that served panko-armoured Scotch eggs and took wine-by-the-glass seriously. Time Out even pointed out Bertie was a mere eight-minute amble from Bassendean train station — yes, we’ll walk that far for a decent list.

Our town centre still smells faintly of baking thanks to Last Crumb Cake Co (they were unassumingly opposite a primary school in a riverside pocket), and if you pressed us for a pizza opinion we’d point you toward Old Lira on Old Perth Rd — thin, ultra-crisp Roman-style bases, or so Time Out rhapsodised. Holly Raye’s used to catch our eye with that Anya Brock mural; you couldn’t miss it if you tried.

We’ve had our municipal moments: councillors voted to remove Rolf Harris’s artworks from display — a council decision that made national headlines and reminded us we’re not just a pretty riverside postcard. And yes, for years the Bassendean Hotel got a glow-up (Urban List noted a $7-million reno) and became the sort of place families could actually take the kids to without fear of a solo beer toppling the vibe.

Who would hate living here? If you need inner‑city everything-by-midnight and can’t abide a ten-kilometre sort-of-out-of-town vibe (Sitchu gently reminds us we’re about 10km from Perth), you’ll complain about the train, the walk and the fact we still call the town a village when it suits us.

Who loves it here? People who want riverside green space, dog-friendly patios (3C James Street got a nod for that), dependable community staples — Casa Mia Montessori folk who started playgroups at the RSL Hall, Ability Dance nights at the Community Centre — and those who think a renovated pub and a good cake shop are markers of civilisation. We’ll happily admit: we’re small, a bit stubborn, and proud as anything.
