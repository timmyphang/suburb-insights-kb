# Manifold Heights (NSW)

Slug: `manifold-heights-nsw`

We’ve got a confession: on lazy Saturdays we still picture Shannon Avenue as our own very small runway — mostly because 139–141 Shannon Avenue once held Frankie Say Relax, and nothing says “local glam” like a lifestyle store that tried to make coastal chic happen inland. We used to wander in, pretend we understood which imported vase matched our hypothetical lounge room, then leave with nothing and a better sense of taste.

Our weekend ritual used to be simple: nip out for something cosy. Mano Deli was the retro gem people mentioned when they wanted to sound like they’d discovered a secret — Sitchu called it that, and Sitchu knows how to make a sourdough toast and a crumpet sound like culture. The menu used to be all about sourdough toast, crumpets and hearty toasties; we’d queue, eat, and then argue about whether it was vintage or just vintage-adjacent.

For the mums among us, the MumsPages directory once had our number — listing local services, mechanics and those endless “where to take small people” options. We were even on the Soccajoeys Geelong coverage list as Manifold Heights 3218, which felt official enough to pin to the fridge beside the takeaway menus and school notices.

There isn’t much catfighting in the paper here; the data reads like a suburb that would rather host a good coffee-and-toast combo than a council brawl. Which is probably why people who crave constant excitement, rooftop bars on every corner or nightly live music from the tram stop would hate living here. If you measure life in cocktail lists, craft cred and non-stop neon, this place will bore you gently and persistently.

Who would love it? People who like a small, dependable patch of the map — the kind who appreciate a niche lifestyle store, remember a deli that did proper toasties, and value directories that actually tell you where the mechanics are. We’re low-key, a little proud of it, and perfectly happy being the suburb you name when you want somewhere that feels like home rather than an occasion.
