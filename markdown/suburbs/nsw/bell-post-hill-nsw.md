# Bell Post Hill (NSW)

Slug: `bell-post-hill-nsw`

We wake up on Wolseley Grove and, no, we don’t hear espresso machines — we hear the sound of small people getting ready for Rollins Primary School, the one that used to list around 300 students and even a second campus in Batesford. Back in the day our local internet footprint read like a parent handbook: school excursions, weekly notices, playgroup registrations and the bowls roster. That is literally the vibe.

If you trawl the mum-blog archives you’ll find Bell Post Hill Grocery & Liquor Co. popping up with bargain specials — they even posted “Come in and grab yourself a bargain! Late week specials available Friday 29th May until Sunday 31st May 2026 only.” We like to imagine these were the suburb’s version of fireworks night: two nights of meat tray glory and a long-suffering bottle shop lino.

Our local social calendar wasn’t exactly Coachella. The high drama was the Bell Post Hill Bowls Club’s promise of “Social Bowls every Wednesday & Friday from 12.15pm.” Riveting. Heart-stopping. We live for a 12.15pm bowl. And for anyone with tiny humans, Kardinia Playgroups had the kind of practical posts mums actually read — “Registrations are open for Semester 1” — and a gentle parade of play-based learning announcements.

Look, the internet paints us as very respectable: postcode 3215, area about 6.08 km², under the Greater Geelong Council, population roughly 5,007 according to the community directory. We’re not trying to be flashy. Our press kit is basically a PTA meeting.

Who would hate living here? People who need neon-lit laneways, boutique everything or scandalous headlines. We don’t have that — our headlines are school notices and midweek bowls times. Who would love it here? Families who want Rollins-style mornings, caregivers who like reliable playgroup enrolments, bargain hunters who keep an eye on the Grocery & Liquor Co.’s specials, and anyone who considers “social bowls at 12.15pm” a lifestyle choice.

Yes, we know the internet’s CV for Bell Post Hill is mostly directories and mum-blogs. We’re proud of it. We’ll be at the bowls club at 12.15 — bring sensible shoes.
