# Kalkallo (NSW)

Slug: `kalkallo-nsw`

Kalkallo, NSW — Back in the day we’d schlepp down Toyon Rd like it was a pilgrimage, because our social life basically hinged on two things: the community centres and whatever class list MumsPages had bookmarked. Most of what we dug up is a bit old — the Playgroup Victoria and MumsPages listings pre-date 2024 — so consider this a fond roast from the archives.

We used to meet at the Kalkallo Community Centre (33 Toyon Rd) for mornings that were 80% sticky hands and 20% desperate coffee-that-we-had-to-warm-in-the-microwave. Playgroup Victoria had the place listed — and there was even a Kalkallo North Community Centre entry — which meant you could park a stroller and forget about civilisation for an hour. PlayBuddies was a proper nod to inclusivity: their Kalkallo listing ran on Thursdays, 10am–11.30am, though the note said at the time the group only had vacancies for children with additional needs and that you’d be joining a waiting list. So yes, we made friends in lobbies, not wine bars.

If you liked to kick things (metaphorically? literally?), GKR Karate Kalkallo was on the directory circuit. MumsPages billed it as for “everyone” and hammered home the selling points: self‑defence, confidence, positive attitude, determination, focus and fitness. Translation: bring the kids, they’ll learn to bow politely before becoming miniature ninjas.

The petty local drama? The archives don’t give us a juicy council saga, so instead we’ll roast what’s obvious: Kalkallo used to (and probably still does) run on community noticeboards, playgroup sign-up forms and the occasional karate kata. No late-night supper club gossip here — unless you count the heated debate over who’s bringing sausages to the next sausage-sizzle.

Who would hate it here: anyone who needs a rotating roster of trendy venues or a nightlife that stretches past toddler bedtime. Who would love it: families hunting for community, parents who treat Playgroup Victoria listings like gospel, and anyone who thinks a Thursday 10am playdate is peak civilisation.

We’re proud of that. We’re the sort of suburb where the community centre noticeboard is the town square — and yes, we’ll see you at the next waiting list.
