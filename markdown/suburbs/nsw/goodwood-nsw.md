# Goodwood (NSW)

Slug: `goodwood-nsw`

We stand on the corner of… actually, that’s the problem. When we look up “Goodwood” online, the internet insists we’re the one with a heritage Capri Theatre, Boulangerie 113’s buttery croissants and a wine bar called Good Gilbert doing all the heavy cultural lifting — and that’s Adelaide’s Goodwood, not ours. Back in the day there were a few mentions, but there are no recent local stories about Goodwood, NSW in the batch we found, so we’ve been reduced to borrowing other Goodwoods’ charm like a suburb on layby.

Mornings here are imagined rather than hashtagged. The Google map keeps promising Tonino’s home‑baked focaccia and Trouble and Strife’s seasonal menu — both Broadsheet entries for Adelaide — so we’ve turned them into a mental breakfast ritual. We picture a Boulangerie‑style pastry and, if we squint, a retro cinema organ courtesy of the Capri Theatre listing. As Broadsheet and Time Out documented, those places exist somewhere; here they’re mostly vibes.

There isn’t much petty local drama to roast — the data is mostly a tidy list of community services and directories. Instead, the fun contradiction is that every time someone tries to write a profile of “our” Goodwood they switch cities: Adelaide’s Goodwood gets the cafes and wine bars, Cape Town has its Goodwood Mall, and the online directories dutifully file us under every postcode imaginable. The most scandalous thing in the snippets is administrative efficiency.

Who would hate living here? People who need their suburb to have a curated brunch crawl, a “best of” list and a wine bar selfie ready at hand. The internet will keep directing them to other Goodwoods and they’ll storm off in a mildly offended, spreadsheet‑fuelled huff.

Who would love it here? Anyone who likes being unbothered by trends, who enjoys a place low on hype and high on local history — or who simply prefers the mystery of a suburb that the web can’t quite pin down. If you enjoy stealing other suburbs’ lunch (metaphorically), you’ll fit right in. For the rest of us, we’ll keep being quietly Goodwood: under‑written, over‑charming and perfectly content to let Adelaide and Cape Town do the Instagram heavy lifting.
