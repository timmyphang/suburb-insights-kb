# Berkeley Vale (NSW)

Slug: `berkeley-vale-nsw`

Back in the day the local paper couldn’t stop talking about us, and honestly, we kind of liked the attention. We know Wyong Road better than our own driveways, the kids’ kit is always in the boot for drop-off at Berkeley Vale Public School, and if you blink you’ll miss half the community notices on the Tuggerah Lakes Secondary College Berkeley Vale Campus newsletter. As Sydney Mums on the Central Coast reminded everyone, we’re a commuting‑distance little patch of the Central Coast that somehow keeps its beachside manners.

Weekends here are practical. For a while the internet directories had the usual suspects listed — Devo Designs and Australian Steel Framing made the local business rounds on MumsPages — so when the mums were measuring out gardens or quoting a renovation, we all knew where to look. Property chat has been its own soap opera: Central Coast News ran pieces saying our median was flirting with the million‑dollar mark, then celebrating us as one of the most affordable coastal towns under $1M. Take your pick: posh panic or bargain brag. We’ll be here, watching price graphs like they’re Netflix.

The petty local drama is mostly paperwork and the kind of council debates that make for excellent tea-room sport. The archives even remind us there’ve been police reports and the odd arson investigation — proof we’re exciting enough to get mentioned in the police blotter but not so exciting we need a follow-up docuseries.

Who wouldn’t love Berkeley Vale? If you want surf‑adjacent suburbs without Sydney prices (at least, as the reports used to say), good public schools, and the comforting knowledge that our patch was chosen to host a new HAZMAT vessel for Fire and Rescue NSW, you’ll fit right in. Who would hate it? If you crave 24/7 nightlife, micro‑brew theatrics or an Instagram feed that looks like a catalogue, you’ll be disappointed — we’re busy living life off‑screen.

So yes, we know what they say about us. They miss the bit where Wyong Road is still the spine of the place, where schools and small businesses keep the day ticking, and where being “affordable coastal” is something to own — preferably with a cuppa and a good laugh.
