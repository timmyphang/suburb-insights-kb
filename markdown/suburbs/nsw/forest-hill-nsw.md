# Forest Hill (NSW)

Slug: `forest-hill-nsw`

We’ll be honest: living in Forest Hill, NSW feels a bit like being the least convincing twin in a family photo. We know every flat little corner of our postcode, but every time we Google ourselves the internet replies with a slideshow of Horniman gardens, a shopping centre mini‑golf course and a café in Auckland and asks, “Which one did you mean?”

Back in the day (and by that we mean: all the articles about us are pre‑2024), the web had big ideas about “Forest Hill” everywhere. Time Out once wrote up Birdies Mini Golf at Forest Hill Chase and even noted the Robo Shop/Pop Mart presence at Forest Hill Chase, Sitchu listed Mazaj Lebanese Restaurant & Lounge and Zikrayat at 329/270 Canterbury Road, Concrete Playground applauded William Souter Espresso over in Forrest Hill, Auckland, and Urban List cheekily lumped a Forest Hill winery in with honeymoon itineraries. Broadsheet, for its part, quietly included Forest Hill on a Melbourne boil‑water warning list. It’s a flattering identity crisis.

Our morning routine doesn’t involve mini‑golf or boutique vinyl shops (unless you count searching for “which Forest Hill is this?”), but it does mean we inherit other suburbs’ good press. Mumsnet threads fawn over Forest Hill as a family area and debate the merits of local schools — even raising concerns about a reported school financial deficit in some threads — while The Motherhood remembered Gao opposite Forest Hill Station and recommended the Horniman Museum and The Signal as family outings. All of which is to say: strangers keep borrowing other Forest Hills’ charm and leaving us with the awkward job of smiling and nodding.

Who would hate living here? People who need a single, unambiguous Google result. Instagram influencers who require a bespoke café with matching cushions in the exact postcode would get frustrated. And cartographers who like tidy borders should probably live somewhere with fewer namesakes.

Who would love it? Families who want a quiet postcode away from hype, people who enjoy being happily anonymous, and anyone who finds the internet’s identity crisis endlessly entertaining. We’re Forest Hill, NSW — modest, serviceable, and secretly delighted that other Forest Hills are out there doing the flashy things. If the world is confused about who we are, we’ll take it as a compliment.
