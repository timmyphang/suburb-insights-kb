# Elanora Heights (NSW)

Slug: `elanora-heights-nsw`

We wake up to gumtrees, a view that hints at ocean and a village that takes its own sweet time getting dressed — welcome to Elanora Heights, where the roundabout is our civic centre and everyone knows which trailhead leads to the best sunset.

Back in the day our local profile reads like a catalogue for enviable domesticity. As Sitchu noted, we’re set high on the hill with world-class beaches, a lagoon on our doorstep and Garigal and Ku-ring-gai Chase National Parks hemming the streets. Weekend routine: a loop of hiking or mountain‑biking trails, a quick reconnaissance of the lagoon, then the kids off to whatever MumsPages listed for school‑holiday fun — Manly Warringah Basketball Assoc or Little Big Warrior Yoga get a tick on the directory, if you’re booking and not just winging it.

We used to boast a bit of culinary glamour: Time Out catalogued Frenchies Brasserie at Shop 3, 54–56 Kalang Rd — a Parisian sort of place whose head chef trained with Joël Robuchon. Urban List even pointed out that Frenchies’ success here spawned a Noosaville outpost. That chapter, though, is in the past — Time Out lists Frenchies as closed, so don’t go expecting a table by the window.

If you’re after petty local drama, well, the press hasn’t obliged — our file is more property listings and mum‑service directories than scandal. Sitchu’s property pages and the magazine pieces talk up multi‑generational homes and ocean outlooks more than council kerfuffles, and MumsPages keeps the focus on classes, architects and after‑school logistics.

Who would hate living here? People who think “village” means being five minutes from a nightclub and 24/7 commerce. If you crave neon and non‑stop options, this leafy postcode will look like a sleepy postcard.

Who would love it? Families, people who plan their weekends around trails and tide, and anyone who likes their neighbours to resemble a barbecue invite list more than a faceless city block. We’re earnest, a little smug about our outlooks, and perfectly content to be where the maps put green and promise.
