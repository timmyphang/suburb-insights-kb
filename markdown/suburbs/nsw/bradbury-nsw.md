# Bradbury (NSW)

Slug: `bradbury-nsw`

On a Saturday in Bradbury, NSW we don’t queue for brunch or pretend to love craft beer — we herd small people. Back in the day (and, honestly, for years after), Netmums used to be our bible: “Things to do with kids in Bradbury” was basically shorthand for survival. We’d eyeball the baby & toddler groups, pack snacks for “days out” and half‑plan birthdays off a Netmums party‑planning checklist.

Our weekend routine reads like a directory entry. MumsPages used to keep us honest about the extra‑curricular frenzy — sports for kids in 2560? Sorted. Dance, drama and music classes? Check. Bowling, laser tag and paintballing? Also listed. STEM clubs and arts & craft groups? Naturally. If it exists for a bored seven‑year‑old, someone in Bradbury’s already put it on a searchable list.

If you want a national magazine to do a deep dive on our street politics, tough luck — the press seems to have pigeonholed us into “family stuff” and directories. Time Out once gave a shoutout to Bradbury Park Playground (that one popped up on a Brisbane playground roundup), and otherwise the local story in the wild mostly lives in parenting sites and service listings. That’s not a bug; it’s our charm.

Who’d hate living here? If you’re allergic to kids’ sports, weekly sign‑ups or unsolicited advice about which dance studio is “the one”, you’ll find Bradbury exhausting. If your perfect Saturday is boutique shopping and small‑batch everything, this postcode will test your patience.

Who’d love it? If you’re raising humans, rehabbing a schedule, or need a list of electricians, plumbers, cleaners, mechanics, window cleaners, food banks or takeaways without digging too hard — Netmums and MumsPages used to (and still do, in spirit) make life easy. We’re the suburb that doesn’t pretend to be anything else: practical, loud, reliably useful and full of kitsch family energy. Come for the organised chaos, stay for the people who’ve already got a spreadsheet for school carnivals.
