# Lidcombe (NSW)

Slug: `lidcombe-nsw`

Back in the day we’d start the weekend with a quick detour through Lidcombe Centre — drop the littlies at ChouChou Bebe, wrestle a stroller past Kmart and Spotlight, then queue for kimbap from Irea if we were feeling fancy. The Remembrance Park playground might’ve needed a facelift, but it was still where the kids burned off sugar before the car nap.

We know the rhythms: communal tables that refuse cutlery (looking at you, Sizzling Fillo) and big bubbling jeongol at O Se Yo that make you bond with strangers over spicy broth. For a while Lidcombe bragged a little — Broadsheet wrote up Kmall 09 opening in Lidcombe Shopping Centre as a two‑storey, 2,000‑square‑metre slice of Seoul, and Yeodongsik’s original queues were the stuff of local legend before it decamped to Eastwood. If you were hungry, Lidcombe had stories to tell.

Yes, we had our dramas. There was that whole era of mayoral cavalcades and leaflets — you remember the fuss about streets being closed and explanations that didn’t sit well with locals — and the legal mess with caveats on properties in John Street where Sky Point Towers was rising. We watched the spectacle like everyone else: popcorn in hand, a little proud and a little exasperated.

Who would hate it here? People who can’t handle communal dining — the “no cutlery, no plates, one long table” ethos at Sizzling Fillo will horrify your need for personal space. If you crave suburbs with nothing but heritage houses and zero plans for apartments, the sight of developments like Vivacity or Lidcombe Sky will make you twitch.

Who’d love it? Families and anyone who loves the practical bits: playgroups like the Lidcombe Lorikeets at the Community Centre on 3 Bridge Street, Sportability sessions on Church Street, or a weekend fed by Filipino crispy pata or reliable dakgalbi at the old PR Korean. We were, and still are in memory, a patchwork suburb — a bit loud, a bit useful, full of meals that fix everything and developments that give the letterbox something to gossip about.
