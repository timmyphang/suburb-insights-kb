# Connolly (NSW)

Slug: `connolly-nsw`

Connolly, NSW — we wake up, make a cuppa, and immediately check whether our surname has done something famous again. Back in the day the papers loved a Connolly, and for a quiet little postcode we’ve become the collective noun for pub refurbs, kitchen swagger and one very memorable yolk.

Mornings here are low-key because there’s nothing here to actually queue for — but we still get our thrills vicariously. Sean Connolly once opened Sean’s Kitchen inside a casino and popped up on Urban List and Time Out profiles; Scott Connolly signed the lease on The George and oversaw a retro refit that Broadsheet and Concrete Playground wrote about; Robert Connolly directs thrillers like The Dry follow-ups (Concrete Playground even covered delays after the actors’ strike). For years the bylines have been stacked with Connollys and we tell visitors, with entirely unjustified pride, “that one’s ours.”

Petty local drama? Oh sure — we’ve got an egg. Will “Egg Boy” Connolly’s moment throwing an egg at Fraser Anning wasn’t exactly a Connolly family dinner topic, but Junkee, Pedestrian and Broadsheet all kept the story simmering — even a mural turned up in Chippendale, because of course it did. Meanwhile, a Connolly has rebooted pubs, another has been reviving hotel dining, and yet another is credited with classy cocktail programs. It’s like a surname-shaped arts grant.

Who would hate living here: people allergic to name-based humblebrags, or anyone who can’t cope with strangers assuming we’re related to a chef, a director, or an infamous viral egg-thrower. Also, rivals — we’re very soft when it comes to claiming celebrity-by-association.

Who’d love it: anyone who enjoys being part of a long, eclectic roll call — from The George refurbs (as Broadsheet noted) to film sets and restaurant shout-outs. If you like the idea of your postcode doubling as a footnote in articles about pubs, cinema and culinary clout, Connolly is our little private joke — and we wear it like a badge, yolk and all.
