# Woodbridge (NSW)

Slug: `woodbridge-nsw`

We live in Woodbridge, NSW, but let us tell you the truth: the internet has commitment issues. We wake up, make tea, and Google insists we’re actually a pub in Guildford, a Georgian mansion in Tasmania, or a riverside park in Perth. Back in the day the only mentions that came up were older pieces — Urban List praising The Woodbridge Hotel (aka “The Woody”) in Guildford for its parmis, Sitchu writing about “The Woodbridge” at 3435 Channel Highway in Tasmania as a restored 1825 mansion, and Sitchu again pointing out Woodbridge Riverside Park in the Swan Valley. Time Out even had the cheek to cheer on the Unruly Pig in Woodbridge, Suffolk. None of which are us, mind you — but pull up any map and the confusion gets Olympic-level.

Our morning routine is therefore one part local pride, two parts fact-checking. We’re proud residents; we also spend an embarrassing amount of time explaining that St Mary’s Playgroup on Burkitt Road and Sandlings Playgroup at Easton Road (both noted in community listings) are not phone-a-friend answers to the Great Woodbridge Mix-up. As the community site points out, St Mary’s has been running for 40 years — which is about how long it takes Google to stop thinking we’re someone else.

Petty local drama? Sure: the ongoing argument at brunch about whether to list ourselves under “Woodbridge, NSW” or “Woodbridge (anywhere but here).” It’s a vicious cycle of mistaken identity. If Urban List and Time Out are still out there celebrating other Woodbridges, we’ll happily let them — we like being underrated.

Who would hate living here: people who need constant validation from travel roundups and want their suburb trending by Tuesday. Who would love it: families who value long-running playgroups, anyone who likes the idea of a place so quietly content that even national guides mix it up with a 1825 Georgian mansion and a riverside park.

So yes, we know what they say about us. They just haven’t actually found our postcode yet — and frankly, we’re enjoying the anonymity.
