# Yennora (NSW)

Slug: `yennora-nsw`

We know the walk from 31 Orchardleigh Street to Yennora station so well we could do it in our sleep — kids in one hand, a tote of forms in the other. Back in the day the Community Kids Yennora centre (they had the number 1800 411 604 and an email Yennora@communitykids.com.au listed) was the kind of place that made the morning shuffle predictable: kiss, nappies, run for the train. Yennora Public School and Yennora Park were the landmarks we timed everything to — the sort of tiny orbit that keeps a suburb doing what it does.

If you like directories, you’ll adore Yennora. As CareforKids cheerfully counted, there were 38 childcare services in our postcode and 11 preschools — 32 of those 38 reportedly had vacancies. Which is practical. Also practical: someone, somewhere, thought to list Inspired Designer Kitchens at 12 Antill Street, so if you ever wanted a shiny benchtop in 2161, at least your kitchen could be aspirational.

Now for the petty local saga the internet accidentally created: different listings can’t agree on much. CareforKids quoted an average of $136.30/day for care around here, while a nearby council family‑care listing referenced Community Kids Yennora with an average of $157.10/day and a 4.9 rating. Translation for locals: bring your calculator, your patience and an opinion about price indexing.

Who would hate living here? People who crave constant, glossy updates. If you need up‑to‑the‑minute café culture or a feed of “what’s open right now” on loop, Yennora’s archive of listings reads like a sleepy librarian shrugging — useful, slightly inconsistent, and unapologetically local.

Who would love it here? Families who appreciate being within a short stroll of a train station, a public school and a park; folks who like the comfort of a neighbourhood with choices (38 childcare options is a flex); and anyone who enjoys the small victories of suburban life — finding a vacancy, getting the forms in on time, maybe even finally booking that kitchen quote at Antill Street.

As MumsPages put it — it’s where family support, childcare options and a surprisingly detailed directory meet. We’re not flashy. We’re functional, full of history and quietly indispensable. And yes, we’ll still know your alarm time.
