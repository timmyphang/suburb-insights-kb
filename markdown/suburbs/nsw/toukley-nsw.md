# Toukley (NSW)

Slug: `toukley-nsw`

Back in the day we’d wake up pretending we’d been whisked off to the Hamptons — The Beachcomber Hotel & Resort used to do that for us with its breezy linen, blonde timber panelling and those sweeping Tuggerah Lake views. From the balcony you could point and feel cultured about Norah Head Lighthouse being only a short hop away, then saunter back to town pretending we were on a mid‑week mini break.

Our weekend routine used to look suspiciously wholesome: a wander past Rise Mofo to check the sunny yellow mandala mural (because yes, we judge cafés by their murals), a detour to the original Noraville Green store for a plant that would definitely survive one week, then a lazy afternoon where Toukley Village Green would occasionally host live music as part of the June long weekend lineup. For families we weren’t exactly short on options — the library ran Toddlertime and Babytime sessions, and the playgroup at Toukley Neighbourhood Centre (7–9 Summerside St) kept the chaos coordinated.

Petty local drama? Oh, we’ve had our share. The Toukley Community Facilities Masterplan was paraded around like a winning float — adopted by Central Coast councillors — and then someone quietly remembered the one detail that matters: there’s no money attached. Then there was the whole McDonald’s saga: Maccas took the council to the Land and Environment Court over a site on Main Rd near the school (370–372 Main Rd, Noraville), which gave residents just enough theatre to keep their group chat lively for a month.

Who would hate living here? If you need every decision to come with instant funding, pristine certainty and zero community submissions, you’ll find us infuriating. If your interior design moodboard only features international flagship stores, we’ll wave you on to the motorway.

Who would love it? People who like water views that feel earned, who’ll spend a Saturday at a Taste of Wyong and Toukley tasting box, who want a town with live music in the park, a Lifeline op shop doing proper local work, and a nursery that began right here. We’re not trying to be Sydney. We’re Toukley: a little rough, quietly stylish in places, and stubbornly community‑minded.
