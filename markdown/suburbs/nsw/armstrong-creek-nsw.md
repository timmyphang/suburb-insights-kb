# Armstrong Creek (NSW)

Slug: `armstrong-creek-nsw`

We know the exact rhythm of a Saturday here: prams in a tentative line, shoes off on lino and someone announcing story time like it’s a town meeting. Back in the day Playgroup Victoria listed the Armstrong Creek Community Hub and Armstrong Creek East Hub as the places to do that — an indoor/outdoor community room where tiny dictators ruled the toy box.

We used to meet in the multi-purpose hall at Geelong Lutheran College on their Armstrong Creek campus — yes, the one with kitchen facilities and access to a playground — which made snack time a logistical marvel and every parents’ group a de facto casserole exchange. The listings on Playgroup Victoria were our yellow pages for baby wrangling.

There’s not much scandal in the archives: no council coups, no cafe closures to cry over, just a lot of gentle community administration and a surprisingly fierce debate about who forgot the tea bags. If you’re allergic to singalongs, sticky floors or other humans’ baby photos, you’ll hate living here. If you require nightlife, boutique this-or-that or the thrill of a new hipster opening every Tuesday, this isn’t your postcode.

But if you love knowing the person who’ll lend you a spare nappy, if the word “community” still makes you feel warm instead of suspicious, and if a multi-purpose hall with a decent kitchen and a playground sounds like a civic triumph, you’ll feel very much at home. We’ll be the ones keeping the Playgroup Victoria listings warm — and the biscuit tin fuller than strictly necessary.
