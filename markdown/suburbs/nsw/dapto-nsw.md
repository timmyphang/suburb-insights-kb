# Dapto (NSW)

Slug: `dapto-nsw`

You know you’re in Dapto when the Princes Highway hums like an honest alarm clock and someone’s telling the story of our greyhound track over a long coffee — or at least over a long Facebook thread.

We grew up with the track as a kind of local chapel: family rituals, arguments about form, and Mary Rachel Brown’s play The Dapto Chaser — born from research at that very track — put our private rituals onstage. Back in the day the play closed out a free theatre festival online (Time Out listed the April streams) and, predictably, the town had Opinions. Seeing “us” on stage made some of us swell with pride and others fidget in the aisle; either way, we were finally part of someone else’s program notes.

For years the local morning routine involved dropping kids at playgroups like NurtureGroup Wollongong at 93–109 Princes Highway before pretending the rest of the day would be restful. Those playgroups and nearby sessions kept the tiny tyrants entertained while we tried to remember where we left our sunglasses.

Then there’s the part of our biography most of Sydney forgot to include on the tourist map: chef Mark Olive, who grew up here, took native Australian produce and gave it a platform at Midden in the Sydney Opera House. We like to tell visiting relatives we trained him ourselves (we didn’t — but we did feed him).

Who would hate Dapto? If you think a town is only worth loving when it’s been polished into an Instagram set, you’ll despair at our practical charm. If you can’t handle a community that argues loudly about its own image — onstage, online, or at the pub — this isn’t your postcode.

Who’d love it here? Families who value real neighbours over curated feeds, anyone who enjoys a nightlife that sometimes smells faintly of the track, and people who take pride in our stories being told — even when they’re messy. We’re not a postcard; we’re a good yarn, a loud debate, and, yes, a surprising name-drop in a Sydney restaurant review. Come for the characters, stay for the theatre gossip.
