# Palmyra (NSW)

Slug: `palmyra-nsw`

We live in Palmyra, NSW — or at least we tell people we do before Google politely laughs and redirects them to another Palmyra. Our morning vibe is half proud small-town grit, half identity theft: try to find our local cafe online and you'll be offered acai porn from Oushk at 61A Carrington Street (Palmyra, Perth), a gluten‑free sourdough shrine called Panna Artisan at 67A McKimmie Rd, or a brewery note that Yeah Buoy is brewed in Palmyra (thanks, Gage Roads). None of these are ours, but they gatecrash our postcode like tourists at a prom.

Weekends are the same pantomime. We know what a farmers market looks like — Urban List has one listed as the Palmyra Western Farmers Markets (Sundays, 8am–12pm) — which is lovely, and also not ours. Meanwhile the internet will happily sign you up for Nido Early School at 56–58 Carrington Street or Carrington Street Childcare at 70 Carrington St if you just type “Palmyra childcare” and a lot of people are none the wiser until drop‑off.

There’s a petty, ongoing drama and it’s called “Which Palmyra?” We quote Junkee’s cheeky tweet — “weve come to palmyra for a holiday (polite laughter)” — because honestly, that’s the vibe. We share a name with a Perth acai bowl icon, a takeaway favourite Rym Tarng praised by Broadsheet, and even a community centre across the Pacific that has been running galas and gymnasiums since the 1940s (they’d pencilled a 2026 gala at the Woodcliff Hotel and Spa). We’re inadvertently cosmopolitan that way.

Who would hate living here? People who want a postcode that doesn’t require a footnote. If you crave being the only Palmyra in town, move to a place with a less well‑travelled toponym.

Who would love it here? Anyone who appreciates a village that quietly laughs as the rest of the internet borrows its name. We’ve got the local schools, choirs and community spirit — even if the web keeps trying to book you brunch in Perth. Come for the mystery; stay because at least our identity crisis is charming.
