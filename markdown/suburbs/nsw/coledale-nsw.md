# Coledale (NSW)

Slug: `coledale-nsw`

We wake up to the sort of morning that only a tiny strip of sand between two rocks can deliver — wind mostly blocked, waves doing their therapy thing, and the occasional dolphin politely reminding us we’re not that important. Back in the day the guidebooks loved this: Coledale Beach was a top beachside camping spot, sandwiched between rocks so you can avoid Wollongong winds, and Concrete Playground used to note our small grassy campsite had a kitchen, laundry and hot showers. Yes, really — camping with creature comforts. We’re that fancy.

A proper weekend for us looks like: stumble out of the tent, hop across to the deeply carved Coledale Rock Pool for a dip (Concrete Playground even mentioned train stations at Coalcliff and Coledale for Sydney day-trippers), then queue for fish ’n’ chips on Lawrence Hargrave Drive. Time Out once said Rosie’s Fish & Chips — run by Ben Sinfield and Tania Ho and named after their daughter Rosie — was among the best in the state. The line out the door was basically our version of haute cuisine.

We haven’t got the scandal pages to fill with petty council wars — our local drama is more “who’s bringing the sponge cake” than headline material. The Coal Coast Mums Club used to run lunches at Coledale Community Hall (Bridget Kirby founded the group), and CareforKids listings show Planet Long Day Care Centre at 707 Lawrence Hargrave Drive with set hours and fees, while Wild Care operated out of Coledale Public School. Quiet, domestic, and very organised.

If you hate sand in odd places, tiny communities that turn into film sets, or people who know the exact tide time, you’ll loathe living here. Also, nightlife seekers and anyone who measures a town by how many bars spill onto the footpath should steer well clear.

But if you love coastal camping that feels like secret luxury, honest fish and chips, rock pools sharp enough to cut your cynicism and a local surf club that’s been trading for years, we’re your kind of crowd. For years the travel pieces and guides kept coming back to us — Sea Cliff Bridge, Return to Paradise shoots, road-trip itineraries that stop here — so come for the view, stay for the community hall sponge cake (if you’re lucky).
