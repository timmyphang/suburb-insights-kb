# Yagoona (NSW)

Slug: `yagoona-nsw`

We love that Yagoona is the kind of place where local legend is either a charity shelter on Rookwood Road or a two‑hour burger flash sale. We remember 201 Rookwood Road — Time Out lists the RSPCA Sydney Shelter there — and Concrete Playground even ran an RSPCA free-adoption weekend back in March 2015 when the shelter had an extremely high population of rescue cats and kittens. That’s our sort of history: earnest, slightly fleecy, and fur‑covered.

Back in the day Pedestrian TV celebrated Yagoona’s Macca’s — yes, the Yagoona Macca’s that was reported as the first McDonald’s in Australia — doing 20‑cent hamburgers for two hours. We like to tell that story like it’s a town anthem: affordable, a little chaotic, and impossible to explain to anyone from a glossy inner‑city guide.

There’s not much recent hoo‑ha in the files — most of what turns up is nostalgia and shelter updates — so the local drama is low on scandal and high on good intentions. If you need a never‑ending list of artisan everything, you’d hate living here. If you’re allergic to cats, admit it now and leave.

Who would love it? Animal lovers, bargain hunters with a fondness for fast‑food folklore, and anyone who appreciates a suburb that wears its past proudly and quietly. We’ll take the RSPCA stories and the Macca’s tales over contrived coolness any day.
