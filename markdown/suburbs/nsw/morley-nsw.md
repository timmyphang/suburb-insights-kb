# Morley (NSW)

Slug: `morley-nsw`

We wake up in Morley, NSW, make a cup of tea and then spend ten minutes explaining to friends that yes, we are Morley — just not the one that keeps hogging the headlines. Back in the day the only mentions that turned up were… elsewhere. Broadsheet and Time Out were writing love letters to a Panini Bar and Charlie’s Fresh Food Market in Morley (Perth). Concrete Playground and Pedestrian.TV were grieving over a Blockbuster in Morley (Perth) that closed its doors. Urban List was excited about a $240-million revamp of Morley Galleria — also Perth. So that’s the vibe: the internet has a Morley identity crisis and we’re the politely amused cousin.

Our weekend routine is mostly low-key because, as far as the media’s concerned, the interesting food precincts, hot-pot thrills and bakery conti-roll cred happen in another postcode. Broadsheet even sent readers wandering to Morley (Perth) for multicultural groceries and Panini Bar paninis; Time Out name‑checked the same Panini Bar in its konti‑roll piece. Meanwhile a cafe called Mentmore and Morley crops up in Rosebery — which is at least NSW-adjacent, technically — set on the corner of Mentmore and Morley avenues. We like the symmetry.

There’s no juicy local council feud in our files, no scandalous cafe closure to dramatised; the petty drama is existential: we keep getting mistaken for someone more famous. Our cultural artefacts are apparently relocating — Time Out wrote that a Morley’s chicken-shop sign is bound for a London museum — and honestly, who are we to stand in the way of art?

Who would hate living here? People who need their suburb to be constantly validated by national thinkpieces, influencer brunch maps or a flashy mall redevelopment press release. Who would love it? Anyone who enjoys being under the radar, having streets that only mean something to the people who live on them, and the private joke of being the “other” Morley when your relatives try to tag you in a Perth food review.

So if you like a suburb that quietly does its thing while the rest of the internet fights over our name, welcome home — we’ll keep the sarcasm, you bring the patience.
