# Mosman (NSW)

Slug: `mosman-nsw`

Back in the day we’d roll out of bed, stride down to Balmoral Beach and pretend the Bathers’ Pavilion was our seaside drawing room. We’d eye the Middle Harbour like it’s our backyard and, if we were feeling humble, remember that Taronga Zoo — yes, Taronga — is basically our local wildlife neighbour. In January 2024 we even had a pygmy hippo calf, Lololi, making the headlines; you don’t get that in every postcode.

Our Saturday routine used to involve a Military Road mosey past boutiques and Pond (Home), a detour through Mosman Village, and the eternal question: Berkelo’s kale-and-feta roll or the Bottom of the Harbour fish and chips on the Esplanade? For special occasions we’d pretend we were on a Roman holiday and book Ormeggio at The Spit or let Ripples Chowder Bay charm us with its waterfront deck. Fourth Village Providore would supply the fancy crackers while Warringah Bowls Mosman promised Swiss-inspired snacks and skyline views for anyone brave enough to try barefoot bowls.

The petty local drama? Oh, we’ve got a cupboard full. We’ve been called one of Sydney’s “boujiest” suburbs — a title we wear like a linen blazer — and we’ve famously flapped at the idea of a KFC and even a 24‑hour Guzman Y Gomez. Mosman residents really know how to mobilise when a late-night burrito or bucket threatens the ambience. There was also that time Taronga’s lions strutted into internet fame via CCTV; apparently we moonlight as a wildlife documentary.

Who would hate living here? If you crave anonymity, round‑the‑clock fast food or Uber fares that don’t make your wallet wince, this isn’t the postcode for you. We also concede: surfers chasing endless beach carnage might prefer somewhere less polite.

Who would love it? Families who want cobblers‑beach snorkelling at Cobblers Beach, boat people who remember GoBoat’s picnic cruisers from The Spit, food lovers who once queued for The Boathouse Balmoral or the Penny Royal, and anyone who thinks a zoo with a wildlife retreat is an amenity, not a novelty. We’re waterfront, a touch precious, and unapologetically Mosman — and honestly, we like it that way.
