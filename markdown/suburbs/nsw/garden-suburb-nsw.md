# Garden Suburb (NSW)

Slug: `garden-suburb-nsw`

Back in the day our weekday mornings in Garden Suburb started on Prospect Road and involved more sandwich bags than dignity.

We used to drop the littlies off at Imagine Early Learning & Childcare at 58 Prospect Rd — the place that reads like it was designed for tiny Instagram influencers: “award‑winning architects”, purpose‑designed rooms, natural light and wooden furnishings. It even had three wings (Babies, Toddler and Pre‑School) and used to open 7am–6pm on weekdays; you could ring (02) 4920 9178 if you wanted to sound organised. The gallery photos were all soft light and tiny chairs, which made us feel both very modern and inexplicably guilty about our own kitchen chairs.

By mid‑mornings the community hall at 2A Prospect Rd filled up for Garden Suburb Jumping Jellybeans — Playgroup NSW’s cheerful listing says sessions run 9am–11:30am, in a hall with a large indoor play area, a kitchen and a small grassed patch for outdoor chaos. They even asked you to email jumpingjellybeansplaygroup@gmail.com before attending, which was our cue to pretend we’d remembered to RSVP to parenthood.

If you want the map‑maker’s version, Glendale Garden Elves’ page helpfully listed Jumping Jellybeans as 3.64 km away, which is code for “bring a snack and a spare shirt”.

There’s no juicy council scandal in these snippets — just a neighbourhood entirely devoted to tiny humans and the logistics thereof. The local story, judging by the listings, is that Garden Suburb is where childcare infrastructure goes to look classy: purpose‑built centres by architects, well‑organised playgroups and a community hall that knows its role.

Who would hate living here? People who require silence between 9am and 3pm, lovers of minimalist calm, or anyone who expects their weekend to be Lego‑free (metaphorically speaking). Who would adore it? Parents who want sensible hours, a proper local centre and a playgroup that asks you to email first — and anyone who appreciates natural light and wooden furniture even when it’s covered in glitter.

We’re proud of our Prospect Rd morning routine. It’s not glamorous — it’s very, very useful.
