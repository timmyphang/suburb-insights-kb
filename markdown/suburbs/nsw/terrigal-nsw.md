# Terrigal (NSW)

Slug: `terrigal-nsw`

We wake before the tourists do, not because we love dawn, but because that's the only time you can still hear the ocean over the sound of weekend-ers finding a park on The Esplanade. We shuffle down to Terrigal Beach, puff up the Skillion if we're feeling virtuous, then pretend we didn't see the beachfront Instagram shoots setting up where we usually stand.

For years this strip has been our weekender flex — Time Out and Concrete Playground made a fuss about the Crowne Plaza Terrigal Pacific and the oceanfront restaurants, and Urban List kept reminding Sydneysiders there's always a house or two with views stretching all the way to Terrigal. The Pav (Terrigal Pavilion) landed with promises of sun-drenched decks and woodfired pizzas, and downstairs The Tropic drew the glass-house crowd; Meribella arrived with Michelin-chat about its chef and selfie-approved sea views; Arc. Est kept doing what waterfront lunch spots do best: oysters and vibes. We mention them like older exes — fond, a little smug, and definitely part of our identity.

Our calendar is split between wholesome and loud: there’s the Terrigal Ocean Swim every Easter Saturday — a twenty‑year‑plus ritual where we pretend the ocean is territory and not a photo backdrop — and the beach markets that somehow fit locally made trinkets between surf schools and seafood. Rotary Park’s pirate ship and flying fox keep the pint-sized locals entertained while boutique Zarlak Kids proves we can do ethical kidswear and still be judgey about other suburbs' baby strollers.

Who would hate living here? If you loathe queueing for a view, despise coastal breezes that mess up your perfectly curated hair, or believe a weekend is sacredly for suburb‑hopping to somewhere quieter (Avoca’s a couple of beaches over, if you prefer the sleepy vibe), Terrigal is not your vibe.

Who loves it? We do — the weekend escape artists, the families who queue for the Ocean Swim, the walkers who clap at the Skillion sunrise and the food people who recall Meribella, Arc. Est and The Pav when bragging at dinner. Come for the beach; stay because you can't quite bear to leave the sound of the waves.
