# Glenmore Park (NSW)

Slug: `glenmore-park-nsw`

We know Glenmore Park the way you know the best shortcut through school drop-off chaos — intimately and with mild resentment. On any given Tuesday morning you’ll find us at Floribunda Community Centre (3–4 Floribunda Drive), clutching a paper coffee and a thermos of hope, because for years the Glenmore Park Little Wonders playgroup has run at 10:30am and frankly that routine is our suburb’s unofficial religion.

Weekends look like a Linear Park pilgrimage: pack the sunscreen, the scooters and the tiny plastic buckets and head to Linear Park, Mulgoa Rise (6 Frontier St). The kids are obsessed with the water play pumps and splash bits — Blue Mountains Mums devoted an entire roundup to it — while we patrol the barbecues and pretend the grass stains add character.

Our local infrastructure reads like a parenting spreadsheet. There’s Floribunda OSHC (3 Floribunda Avenue), Nangamay OSHC (1–23 Forestwood Drive) and the Glenmore Park Child & Family Centre on 31 Blue Hills Drive — all the places that rescue us during work weeks and school holidays. The listings for Grazn Gourmet Penrith at 93 Kiber Dr and a humble cleaner at 21 Coolabah Cres (prosofaclean) show that if you need catering for the kids’ soccer-mum snackathon or someone to pretend the toy room is tidy, we’ve got options listed in the usual directories.

Petty local drama? The articles we found are mostly community notices and playgroup schedules — gloriously low on scandal. There was, however, a serious news report connected to Glenmore Park that we won’t fan into gossip; some stories are heavy and deserve more than suburban snark.

Who would hate it here: anyone who measures suburb quality by rooftop bars and late-night neon. We’re not the Rocks — that’s a different Glenmore — and if your cultural compass points only to post-work cocktails and harbour views, this place will feel like a long commute from your vibe.

Who would love it: families, organisers, people who judge a neighbourhood by whether toddlers can run free and whether the Tuesday playgroup has space. For years the data points to one thing: Glenmore Park does community, quietly and reliably. We’ll roast it gently — but only because we’re saving the best parking spot in the carpool line.
