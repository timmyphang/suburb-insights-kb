# Bonnyrigg (NSW)

Slug: `bonnyrigg-nsw`

Back in the day we’d queue like it was a festival — not for champagne or curated brunch plates, but for a proper doner. As Broadsheet once pointed out, Keb Up was that family-run Berlin-style food truck parked in Bonnyrigg, run by the Hanna family, dishing out what they called a “magnificent” chicken veggie kebab. We miss that gloriously greasy geometry of meat and salad folded into bread the way other suburbs miss their local footy team.

For anyone who thinks Bonnyrigg is beige: it isn’t. Sitchu once sent us packing to the suburb for Sriracha Street Food, raving about their Special Sriracha Sauce, cashew nut noodles and the deep-fried pad cha quail. Translation: we like things loud, a bit smoky, and liable to leave you re-evaluating your life choices at 11pm. We’re proud of that. We’ll own our spice stains.

Full disclosure — the stories about our culinary glories are older pieces, so yes, this is us being nostalgic as much as boastful. The reviews in our scrapbook are pre-2024 reads, which means we’re fond of reminiscing and also suspiciously optimistic about what’s still parked where it used to be. Consider this a love letter written from a car park we recognize by aroma.

Who would hate living here? If you prefer lives that come with delicate napkins and a strict humidity-control policy, Bonnyrigg will make you twitch. If you can’t handle a family-run kebab crew being more trustworthy than some chain’s customer service line, you’ll be uncomfortable. And if "subtle" is your default setting, move along.

Who would love it here? Anyone who measures distance in how long it takes to get a kebab after midnight. Fans of bold sauces, bargain eats and the comforting chaos of a food truck doing a family trade would feel at home. We celebrate what we’ve been given — the Hanna family doner legend and the Sriracha street eats that put Bonnyrigg on a few people’s maps. Come for the food, stay for the unapologetic flavour, and don’t be surprised if we recognise you by the way you eat.
