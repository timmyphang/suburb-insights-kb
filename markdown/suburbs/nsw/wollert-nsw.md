# Wollert (NSW)

Slug: `wollert-nsw`

Back in the day we’d brace ourselves for the fortnightly Monday pilgrimage — 10.30am sharp — to the ONE Wollert playgroup. As Playgroup Victoria used to list it, that hour was where nappies met neighbourhood gossip and toddlers staged tiny, adorable coups. 

Weekend routine: we’d schlepp to Creeds Farm Living & Learning Centre for a community hit — not because it’s glam, but because Creeds Farm was literally billed as serving Epping and Wollert, and where else are you going to find that level of neighbourly persistence? If the weather turned on us, we might’ve been redirected to Gamboo community hall, or, in classic local indecision, the mysteriously interchangeable “Epping or Tuttle Reserve Hall Wollert” option. It’s our version of a choose-your-own-adventure.

Local drama? The only thrilling feud here is whether the playgroup should be in the hall or the hall-in-name-only. We’re sophisticated that way. No council scandals, no celebrity cafés — just real people, tea stains and a roster that runs on goodwill and the occasional muffled toddler scream.

Who would hate it here: anyone who needs a Michelin-starred latte on demand, or enjoys a postcode that changes its mind about meeting venues mid-week. 

Who’d love it: young families, new parents, carers and anyone after a warm, slightly chaotic community where Playgroup Victoria once promised a “welcoming space for families with children aged 0–5.” We’ll always defend that tiny kingdom of shared biscuits and collective exhaustion.
