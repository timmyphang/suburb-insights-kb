# Lake Coogee (NSW)

Slug: `lake-coogee-nsw`

Back in the day Sitchu wrote up Coogee as if it were a personality type — “white sandy beaches”, “Coastal Walk”, “sea pools” — and we’re here waving a towel and a smirk because, yes, we know all of that. We’re Lake Coogee locals, which means we’ve spent more time arguing about the best turn for the Coastal Walk than anyone sensible should. As Sitchu noted, this place has a proper café scene (they made a whole list once), and the strip of local cafés and shops that people trot off to between paddles and walks is basically our communal pride and mild anxiety.

Mornings here are a soft repeat: someone runs past muttering about sea pools, someone else is still reading the real estate blurb from years ago that promised instant glamour. Remember those Sitchu listings? There was a time 15 Hill Street was being sold as “a short 2-minute stroll to Coogee Beach” and 247 Carrington Road pitched itself as the private seaside entertainer’s dream — contemporary design, natural light, the lot. We like to joke that if you’ve ever owned a property here you’ve spent half your life reading copies of those listings at midnight.

There’s no big scandal in our feed — no council melee, no nightly outrage — which is refreshing. Petty drama? Our main quarrels are earnest: which stretch of sand is best for a walk, whether Gordons Bay is secretly the nicest spot, whether to detour for yet another coffee. Sitchu’s guide made it sound vibrant and lived-in, and honestly, it is.

Who would hate living here? People who think coastline means constant celebrity sightings and curated chaos. If you need a buzzy nightlife hub, move on — we’re more sea pools and Sunday stroll than late-night glitter.

Who’d love it? Walkers who want the Coastal Walk on speed-dial, families who value a patch of sand and a reliable sea pool, and anyone who secretly reads property listings for the plots. We’re low-key, sandy, and stubbornly proud of it — and yes, we know what they say. They just missed the part about the best coffee being a short detour away (Sitchu told you so).
