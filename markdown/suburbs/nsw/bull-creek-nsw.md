# Bull Creek (NSW)

Slug: `bull-creek-nsw`

We start our Saturday like proper locals: bleary-eyed, slightly defensive about our postal code, and pretending we meant to take the kids to a “cultural experience.” Back in the day our file on Bull Creek mostly contains other people’s love letters — Broadsheet raving about Bull Creek Hawker (Shop 16/118 Parry Ave) and Urban List telling us Kai Ramen at 4/110 Parry Avenue “might be tiny, but it’s serving up seriously good ramen.” So yes, our culinary prestige is archived in other people’s hot-takes. We’ll take it.

Morning routine: we’d drop by Stockland Bull Creek school-holiday events — remember the Peter Rabbit 2 Craft Zone? — then negotiate afternoon naps with the unshakeable confidence of parents who have read MumsPages’ directory and know exactly which psychologists and family-support services to bookmark. The Aviation Heritage Museum (Air Force Memorial Estate, 2 Bull Creek Drive) has been our go-to “educational outing that counts as fresh air.” PerthMumsGroup even ran a family fun day there; you can almost hear the kids saying “history!” between glue-stick emergencies.

Petty local drama? Our archive is mercifully dull. No council wrath, no viral petitions — just directories, good ramen mentions, a hawker stall earning a Broadsheet nod, and Dolce And Salato quietly starring in Urban List’s pastry round-ups. If anything, the scandal is that our bragging rights are outsourced to food writers.

Who would hate living here: people allergic to family-friendly programming, modestly priced noodles, and vanilla slices that Urban List refuses to stop recommending. Also anyone who demands their suburb come with a weekly controversy, because we’re more “neat little events calendar” than “tabloid material.”

Who would love it here: families who actually use the playground and the Stockland events, food people who will make a pilgrimage for Kai’s miso or the three-meat rice Broadsheet praised at Bull Creek Hawker, and anyone who thinks an Aviation Heritage Museum is peak weekend entertainment. We know our postcode isn’t flashy. We’re quietly useful, fond of good food, decent cake, and community days where the worst thing that happens is someone spilling glue on a Peter Rabbit tote. And honestly? That suits us just fine.
