# Baulkham Hills (NSW)

Slug: `baulkham-hills-nsw`

We roll out of bed on a Saturday like we own the cul‑de‑sac — because chances are we do. First stop: Crestwood Reserve, where the learn‑to‑ride track and playground keep half the street occupied until lunch. Then it’s over to Stockland Baulkham Hills for a quick lap of the fenced, shaded play area (you know, the one beside Little Rock and Charlie Lovett) while someone negotiates the grocery run.

Back in the day Time Out even put “Baulkham Hills & Hawkesbury” at fourth most liveable — apparently our elbow room and greenery count for something. We like to remind visiting friends that “Baulko” has parks: Balcombe Heights Estate’s Masonic Oval, the refreshed skate park, Turon Avenue’s upgraded playground and BMX track — basically the suburban Olympics, minus the medals or ego, depending on school pick‑ups.

Food scene? We’ve always been quietly smug. Old Northern Road used to hint at a Little Iran with Kian Bread and Saffran Patisserie rubbing shoulders with a local Persian restaurant — proper regional flavours, not just a gimmick. On the other end of the scale there was Burger Room’s Downunder — a burger that proudly wore beetroot and pineapple like it had somewhere to be. Mullane’s Hotel raised an eyebrow with Sarino’s upstairs; the place that brought in a bit of Italian glamour (and an in‑house distillery plan) so our dinner bookings could feel almost cosmopolitan. Level Two at Mullane’s featured on bar guides too, for when we wanted to pretend we’d gone out.

Local culture comes in pleasant packages: Hills Mums grew big enough to be a force (26,000 strong, they’ll tell you), there are playgroups at Village Green Community Centre and Gooden Drive, and the Waves Fitness and Aquatic Centre keeps us pretending we’re fit.

Who would hate living here? People who crave rooftop jazz every Friday night — Ciel was promised for Norwest and that might have helped, but mostly we’re suburban chill, not 24/7 nightlife. Who’d love it? Families, people who want big backyards, parks within a short stroll, and anyone who prefers a decent bakery and a reliable playgroup to a late‑night CBD sprint.

We know what the critics say — we also know where the best pastry is. Come for the space, stay for the playgroup gossip.
