# Belgrave Heights (NSW)

Slug: `belgrave-heights-nsw`

We know the hills like the back of our hands — the morning when the R.U.T.H. Run used to kick off at Birdsland Reserve (271 Mt Morton Rd) you could tell who’d been training and who’d just driven up for scenery and a selfie. We’d shuffle past the mums-and-buggies at the Belgrave Heights & South playgroup and pretend the 5km was a warm-up for a day of actually doing nothing.

Back in the day Concrete Playground wrote up Earthly Pleasures Cafe, tucked in that old stone building with gardens — we used to swear it made everything from scratch, which made us feel both virtuous and secretly smug. Broadsheet once pointed out the Belgrave Emporium as the seven-days-a-week local market where about 90 makers got to sell their stuff; we’d wander through, buy one artisan thing we didn’t need, and call it supporting community.

Petty local drama? Oh yes: the century-old Micawber Tavern was acquired by Julien Moussi’s Only Hospitality Group — Concrete Playground had the scoop — so if you like your watering holes with a side of hospo mogul energy, keep an eye on flashy refurbishments. Meanwhile, the Belgrave South Fire Brigade at 90 Colby Drive keeps the real adults calm while the rest of us argue about the best walking track.

Who would hate living here: people who can’t cope with dogs getting more pampered than they are. Wagtime’s pamper-and-groom options put some of us to shame, and anyone who needs a quiet, commerical-strip CBD life will be exasperated by our ferny feels and weekend dessert hunts up the ranges.

Who would love it here: families who value community — Belgrave Heights Christian School keeps the kids busy from Kinder to Year 12 — and bulk shoppers who remember Unwrapped Pantry on 164 Colby Drive. If you crave woodfires, fern gullies and a weekend guide to the Dandenong Ranges (Concrete Playground did us proud), you belong here. We’re small, a touch precious, and stubbornly proud of our little corner of the hills — come for the views, stay because you can’t find proper Wi‑Fi and secretly enjoy it.
