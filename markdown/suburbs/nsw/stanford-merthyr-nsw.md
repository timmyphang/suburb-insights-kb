# Stanford Merthyr (NSW)

Slug: `stanford-merthyr-nsw`

You know you’re in Stanford Merthyr when Friday night turns Lang St into a cross between Grease and a car show. We’ve learned to time our weekend like it’s a school bell: Friday afternoon, park near the main stage; Saturday, wander the Show and Shine; Sunday, nurse last night’s rockabilly in a folding chair while someone else wins the talent quest.

The Kurri Kurri Nostalgia Festival (Lang St, Fri 22–Sun 24 Mar 2024) does the heavy lifting for our social calendar — over 150 retro market stalls, hot rods gleaming like they’re trying to impress our nan, food vans doing their best battler-bistro impressions, nightly rock ’n’ roll and rockabilly dances, plus fashion parades and a main stage that refuses to let you stand still. It’s loud, it’s sparkly, and we wouldn’t have it any other way.

For context, the town has its quieter chapters too. Stanford Merthyr Infants School used to be listed on kid.com.au at Maitland St (enrolments 54; ICSEA 922 per the ACARA School Profile 2023). Small enough that the kids’ lost-property bin doubles as the civic archive. We like that — community where the teacher remembers your surname and your dog’s name, even if the internet listing feels like the only place we’ve formally introduced ourselves.

Who’d hate living here? If you can’t stand people who clap at a decent trumpet solo or you’re allergic to the smell of polishing wax on an early morning, this isn’t for you. If you demand three separate gourmet espresso options within a five-minute walk, you might find our weekend offerings a little… historically curated.

Who’d love it? Classic car obsessives, anyone who still owns a collar and a dance move, families who value a tiny school and a big festival, and anyone who appreciates a town that turns its main street into a time machine once a year. Come for the hot rods, stay because we’ll talk to you like you’re a returning local — even if you’re just here for the market stall mille-feuille.
