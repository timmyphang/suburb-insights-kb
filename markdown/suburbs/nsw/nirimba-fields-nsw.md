# Nirimba Fields (NSW)

Slug: `nirimba-fields-nsw`

We like to think of Nirimba Fields as that quietly confident suburb that doesn’t need to shout — mostly because the internet barely whispers about us. Back in the day, the one proper shout was from Mummy’s Cafe in Bells Creek, which used to bill itself as “Caloundra’s Best Coffee” and insist its “beautifully landscaped location is conveniently located at the entry point to Nirimba.” The website even listed 1 Tucker Street, Bells Creek and weekday hours (Mon–Fri 6:00am–2:30pm, Sat–Sun 7:00am–2:30pm), like proof we existed on someone’s map.

Morning routine, as far as we can reconstruct: roll up to a tidy alfresco spot, pretend we always knew where the entry to Nirimba was, and nod appreciatively at the event notices — because Mummy’s Cafe also pitched itself as a community event space. That’s our cultural life, apparently: landscaped patios and meeting rooms where you can book a thing.

Petty local drama? The archives are mercifully small. The biggest local dispute involves whether being “conveniently located at the entry point to Nirimba” is marketing bravado or a cartographic fact. Take your stance accordingly.

Who would hate living here: people who crave late-night neon and an embarrassment of choices. Who’d love it: folks who want tidy green spots, predictable opening hours, and somewhere that used to host the occasional community gathering. We’ll keep tending our low-key garden, even if the internet prefers to remember us via a Bells Creek café that once claimed the doorway to our world.
