# Eden Hills (NSW)

Slug: `eden-hills-nsw`

Back in the day we’d queue on Shepherds Hill Road like it was a ritual — and yes, we’re still nostalgic about it. As Broadsheet noted, Gang Gang moved into that tiny spot next to Little Caesars Pizza (the pizza place that’s been part of our strip since 1993) and squeezed nine seats into a takeaway-focused outpost. It felt very local: burgers, brekkie bagels and the kind of awkward elbowing you only get in a nine-seater.

A typical weekend for us used to look like this: drop the kids at Eden Hills Kindergarten on Willunga St, pick up the youngest from Camp Australia at 78 Wilpena St (they ran Before School Care 7:00–8:30 and After School Care 3:10–6:15), then park ourselves at Karinya Reserve where the youth/junior/senior playground, skate park, electric barbecue and toilets meant someone could be entertained for hours. If the crafty ones among us were showing off, they’d talk about Creative Mosaics on Wilpena Street or the Skein Machine on Shepherds Hill Rd and sign up for a CRAFTERNOON.

We don’t have a village green full of boutique everything — and that’s the point. Eden Hills is the place where Little Caesars used to be the late-night saviour and where a Disney-themed roller disco once took over 1 Northcote Road (June 2022 — yes, we still have the photos). The Scout Group in Karinya Reserve’s community hall kept the kids outdoors; Eden Hills Primary School covered Reception to Year 6 like a classic small-school neighbourhood hub.

Who would hate living here? If you need a constantly changing café scene or crave being spoon-fed city trends, the nine-seat Gang Gang next to a long-running pizza joint might be a daily affront. If you despise playgrounds, playgroups and community hall events, this place will test your patience.

Who would love it? Families, crafty types, anyone who likes community-run skate parks and a predictable, low-key patch of the Adelaide Hills. We’re not flashy. We’re not loud. We’re the suburb that still remembers which streets lead to which playgrounds — and we like it that way.
