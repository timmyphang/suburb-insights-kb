# Heathfield (NSW)

Slug: `heathfield-nsw`

We live in Heathfield, NSW, which is a bit like being the person at the party everyone assumes is on a different guest list. Our local file? Thin. Like, archaeologist-with-a-flashlight thin. Back in the day the only mentions that turned up weren’t even for us — they were for the Heathfields that actually get press: Mt Lofty District Community Sports Club at the corner of Heathfield & Longwood Rd (that one’s in SA), the Heathfield Resource Recovery Centre at 32 Scott Creek Rd, and the Mount Lofty Sports Centre on 99 Longwood Rd — all catalogued on SAcommunity. There’s also a Heathfield Youth Hub on High Street in East Sussex, TN21 0UP, and an Opportunity Playgroup meeting at St Andrew’s Centre, School Hill, Histon CB24 9JE. All older entries. All useful for crossword clues, not for local intel.

Morning in our Heathfield, NSW: we like to imagine a routine, but the internet insists our suburb is shy. If you want specifics, the record prefers other Heathfields — which is fair, we’re humble. SAcommunity once listed the Heathfield Netball Club on Longwood Rd (last updated 10/09/2024 in their notes), but that’s not our netball club. Opportunity Playgroup’s leaflet mentions meetings on Mondays between 1 and 3pm at St Andrew’s in Histon — quaint, but again, not ours.

There’s no petty council drama in our file because, apparently, our drama is offline and stubbornly analogue. No scandal, no viral café fight, just us and a very polite silence from search engines. We’ll take that as character.

Who would hate living here? The person who needs constant validation from local press — we’re not on the bandwagon, we’re the band’s quiet cousin tuning the guitar. Who would love it? People who appreciate a place that doesn’t overshare, where community is built in person (you know, the old-fashioned way) rather than in a press release. If you’re someone who likes your suburb understated and content to be confused with three other towns on a web search, come find us. We’ll be the Heathfield that asks for directions and then quietly nods and keeps walking.
