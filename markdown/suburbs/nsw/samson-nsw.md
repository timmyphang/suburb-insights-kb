# Samson (NSW)

Slug: `samson-nsw`

We live in Samson, NSW — the suburb the internet keeps mistaking for literally anywhere else. Our local bragging rights have been outsourced to Perth (hello, Samson’s Paddock in Mosman Park), Brisbane (Samson and Sophie, Newstead — as Concrete Playground adored), and even the Cannes circuit (Samson & Delilah keeps turning up in Time Out and Pedestrian.TV like it’s our school play).

Back in the day national sites loved a good Samson so much they sprinkled the name across the country: Broadsheet and Urban List kept raving about a whisky-and-steak bar called Samson’s Paddock in Mosman Park; Time Out was waxing lyrical about Warwick Thornton’s Samson & Delilah; Sitchu found a Samson Tall Wines in McLaren Vale; and Urban List remembered Samson Court in Duncraig for an utterly unreasonable 45,000-LED Christmas display. As Broadsheet noted, “Samson” is apparently a brand now — just not ours.

Our weekend routine, if you must know, involves less whisky-pairing and more quiet suspicion that another suburb has nicked our name. We don’t queue for brioche burgers in South Yarra or brunch by the Brisbane river — Concrete Playground already claimed that territory. We do, however, have a peculiar civic thrill when a tourism list forgets we exist and we all get to be smug together.

There’s no petty feud to gossip about here (national media did that for other Samsons), so the drama is existential: who gets to be Samson this week? If your life requires being on every “best of” list, you will loathe living here — we’re the place magazines skip while they hunt for better lighting and a larger whisky list.

But for those who like being quietly misattributed, who enjoy watching outraged comments sections bloom when someone confuses Mosman Park with us, and who prefer a suburb that keeps its secrets rather than its Instagrammable corners, we’re perfect. Love living in a place that’s so charmingly unclaimed that Broadsheet can’t quite place us? Join us. We’ll make you feel like a local — even if a national website calls you by the wrong postcode.
