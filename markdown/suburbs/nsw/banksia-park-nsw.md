# Banksia Park (NSW)

Slug: `banksia-park-nsw`

We live in Banksia Park, NSW — except when the internet doesn’t know which Banksia we are, so we spend our days answering for half the eastern seaboard.

Mornings here are low-key: we shuffle out expecting a local cafe and instead get a quiz. Broadsheet remembers a Banksia Bakehouse in Grosvenor Place with lemon‑meringue cruffins; Concrete Playground keeps talking about a Banksia Hotel on the Princes Highway and even a Demochi stall at 286 Princes Highway next to it; Time Out gushes about a Banksia Park Cherry Grove over in Bulleen. Back in the day the press loved using the name and then walking away — informative, if geographically chaotic.

We’ll admit it: it’s slightly humiliating being the suburb that’s basically a naming convention. As Broadsheet once put it, there’s a Banksia Tree Cafe serving XO crab and participating in compost networks — charming copy, questionable proximity. Sitchu even lists “Banksia Park, 7 Templestowe Road, Bulleen” as one of Melbourne’s hideaways, which is flattering until you realise Google Maps has given up and is phoning a friend.

Petty local drama? Our drama is existential. Are we the place with a gastro pub reviewed by Concrete Playground, the cherry-blossom grove Time Out recommends, or the kindy with precise session times the SA community pages so lovingly catalogued? For years the local internet identity has been playing musical postcodes — and we’ve lost count of which press release is ours this month.

Who would hate living here: people who want a suburb with a single, unambiguous personality. If you crave being the one called in every lifestyle roundup, freelancing a name across three states will make you twitchy.

Who would love it here: people who enjoy a quiet life with the minor thrill of baffling journalists. If you like parks that could be famous, schools that definitely exist somewhere under the name “Banksia Park,” and a community that rolls with the confusion, you’ll feel at home. We’ll keep tending our actual trees while the internet continues to rebrand us, one glowing review at a time.
