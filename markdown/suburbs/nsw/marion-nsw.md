# Marion (NSW)

Slug: `marion-nsw`

Back in the day we used to explain Marion, NSW by playing name-spotting: who’s the Marion you actually mean this week? We’re the Marion that quietly exists on maps, while the Internet keeps getting distracted by other Marions with better PR.

Mornings here are a humble ritual of Google clarifications — did Broadsheet mean our Marion or Andrew McConnell’s Marion at 53 Gertrude Street in Fitzroy? As Broadsheet and Concrete Playground once noted, that Marion was the wine bar that roped in critics and countdowns. Meanwhile Pedestrian TV reminds us Marion Grasby made her name on MasterChef, Sitchu takes us to Marion Outdoor Pool at 120 Hendrie Street, Park Holme (hello, Adelaide), and Time Out waves at Dom Panino on the corner of Marion Street in Sydney. It’s like being in a family where three cousins are on TV and we’re the one who knows how to fix the wi‑fi.

We don’t have glossy write-ups telling you to book a table or linger over anchovy gildas, so our weekend vignette is low-key: we’re the people who shrug when someone brags about eating at “Marion” and then has to specify which state. We’ve learned patience, and the ability to pronounce “Gertrude Street” with unbothered resignation.

The petty local drama? There isn’t one in the papers — just a slow-burning identity crisis. If our suburb had a PR team they’d be lovely and very apologetic. Instead we make do with being the reliable background Marion: not always Instagrammable, but also not trying too hard.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who needs their postcode to trend on Urban List. They’ll be furious every time a search autofills to Fitzroy or Park Holme and not to our front gate.

Who would love it here? People who enjoy anonymity, the cosy satisfaction of being “the other Marion” and the private smugness of having a suburb name so versatile it carries a wine bar, a chef, a pool and a paninoteca across the country. We’ll keep being quietly us — and if someone wants to borrow our name for a cookbook, we’ll probably say yes.
