# Elizabeth Grove (NSW)

Slug: `elizabeth-grove-nsw`

Back in the day we thought suburban glamour meant a tidy row of letterboxes. In Elizabeth Grove it meant the morning relay at 20 Haynes St — the old hub of our tiny empire. We used to shuffle kids into the Elizabeth Grove Children's Centre for preschool and playgroups, or drop off at Elizabeth Grove Primary School (EGPS) when Reception–Year 6 was the day’s headline. The listings used to brag about parenting programs and early intervention supports; we liked that it sounded important even when half of us were still untangling lunchboxes.

Our weekday choreography was gloriously practical: the OSHC at the primary school was listed as the place that did Before School Care from 6:30–8:30am and After School Care 3:05–6:00pm for five- to twelve-year-olds — perfect for anyone whose CEO job started before their small person realised shoes come in pairs. We didn’t have artisanal anything; we had craft, sport and cooking sessions that doubled as evidence we were raising functioning humans.

Weekends were either daisy-chain playgroups — Playgroup SA’s supported sessions and the Every Body Play program getting everyone doing Something Educational — or we’d be down at Dauntsey Reserve for a footy Saturday under the Elizabeth Grove Soccer Club banner. Their listing even had a website: www.egsc.com.au — which, back when we were young and had fewer notifications, felt like real civic pride.

There’s no hot-take drama to unpick here because the data’s mostly the kind of sensible listings you put on a fridge: comforting, slightly bureaucratic, and very child-centric. If you’re after nightlife with neon and cocktails, you’ll hate the honest practicality of our schedules. If calendars make you swoon, you’ll find our OSHC hours more romantic than a romcom.

Who would love it here? Families who measure success in nap routines and tidy backpacks. Misfits who want a quiet base with community bits already in place. We’ll happily wear the “not flashy” badge — because while we don’t trend, we do show up for craft mornings, school concerts and Saturday soccer, and that, frankly, is glorious.
