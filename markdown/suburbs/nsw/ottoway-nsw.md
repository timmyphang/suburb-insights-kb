# Ottoway (NSW)

Slug: `ottoway-nsw`

You know you’re in Ottoway when a Wednesday morning looks like a community services brochure come to life. We used to drop the littlies off at the Happy Space Intergenerational Playgroup — it ran Wednesdays 10am–12pm during school terms at Junction Community Centre, 2A May Tce, Ottoway 5013 — and swap survival tips with the other parents while someone else hid the foam blocks.

Back in the day our name mostly turned up in listings: MumsPages helping you find cleaning companies, karate classes for the kids, and other practical bits-and-bobs. If you wanted glossy magazine hype, nope — Ottoway wasn’t on the Broadsheet 20 best-everythings list. Instead we had the stuff that actually keeps a suburb moving: Adelaide Training & Employment Centre (ATEC) offering electrical, forklift, metal fabrication and welding courses out in Ottoway and Lonsdale — because when your weekend plans might include learning to weld a bit of life back together, you’re living here.

On weekends a fair few of us would be at Copernicus Hall over in Athol Park, where the Ottoway Theatrical Ensemble Inc. staged community performances and made the local stage our unofficial village square. No celebrity chefs, no limited‑edition menus — unless your limited edition is a one-night-only pantomime.

There’s no juicy council scandal in our file drawer, no barista wars, no late-night rooftop epiphanies in the press clippings. Our archival evidence is mostly useful: playgroups, training centres, community theatre, and pages on where mums can find services. So we’re leaning into it: Ottoway is quietly useful.

Who would hate it here? If you need a weekly roundup of hype, pop‑up ramen or a festival of espresso snobbery, you’ll feel starved. If you like practical courses, intergenerational playgroups, local theatre rehearsals and a suburb that helps you fix things — and people who will tell you where to get a reliable cleaner or sign your kid up for karate — you’ll love it.

Full disclosure: our sources here are older community listings, so consider this a fond, slightly dusty love letter rather than a what’s-open-this-weekend guide.
