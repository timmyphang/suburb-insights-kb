# Leura (NSW)

Slug: `leura-nsw`

We know the moment: the train sighs into Leura and we spill onto the platform, luggage and selfie sticks in tow, and within two minutes the Edwardian shopfronts of Leura Mall are doing their best impression of an English village that got lost on the way to Sydney. For years the old travel guides and features have been telling Sydneysiders the same thing — it’s about 90 minutes west and utterly charming — and, well, who are we to quarrel with a good entrance?

Our weekend ritual used to involve a slow wander down Megalong Street (Little Sista at 4/130–138 Megalong Street made a fuss about focaccia and vinyl records), a detour to Red Door Cafe for its long-serving reputation and the bakery smells from Lily’s Pad (19 Grose Street), and a mandatory, slightly smug window-stare at Josophan’s Fine Chocolates — the workshop that operated on the main street from 2005 before the founder took the sweets into the city. If you wanted boutique stays, articles used to name Cloud Parade and a clutch of cosy options, and guides would point you to the Fairmont Resort and Spa Blue Mountains for the full indulgent retreat.

Petty local drama? Oh yes — nothing brings out village fencing like Leura Gardens Festival season. Time Out said the festival would be “buzzing” for its 55th season, and we can confirm the town is briefly overrun by people who came for the cherry blossoms and stayed for the photo ops. The Leura Community Alliance and the Leura Cascades Bushcare crew quietly get on with keeping the place looking presentable while the rest of us argue about where to park.

Who would hate it here: anyone allergic to gardens, boutique shops, or the sound of vinyl drifting from a focaccia-first café. Who’d love it: families who actually use the Leura Cascades Round Walk, lovers of sweeping Jamison Valley views and the Everglades House and Gardens, people who can’t resist a lesser-known viewpoint like Elysian Rock Lookout, and anyone who thinks bees deserve their own little shop — remember BUZZBUZZ by beekeeper Joanne Day from Pollinator Week?

For years travel pages and local listings have done the heavy lifting on what makes Leura tick. We live here, we roll our eyes, and then we help someone with directions to the lookouts anyway.
