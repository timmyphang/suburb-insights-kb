# Milperra (NSW)

Slug: `milperra-nsw`

Back in the day we’d argue whether the real soundtrack of Milperra was the thunk of a pickleball paddle or the roar of a go‑kart — and still never reach a verdict.

Our Saturday routine used to look like this: stumble out of bed, begrudgingly queue at Four Little Foxes for brekkie (Concrete Playground used to recommend it), then either head to Pickle Point at 101 Raleigh Road to pretend we were Olympic athletes on eight world‑class courts, or drive to 20/361 Milperra Road — the Entertainment Park at Bankstown Aerodrome — to hurl ourselves around indoor go‑kart tracks and VR arenas until someone remembered they had a mortgage. The Urban List even listed Pickle Point for its Funday Pickleball sessions and regular social games on Monday, Wednesday and Sunday nights — $15 a pop, if memory serves.

If you wanted full‑on adrenaline, we used to send the kids (and the neighbours who couldn’t hear us over the noise) to Aussie Paintball at 2 Maxwell Ave — a sprawling 15‑acre complex with over ten arenas, according to Time Out. And when the sun went down, The Mill Hotel on 189 Beaconsfield St would kick off Mill Movies, trivia and PLAYTIME nightclub nights, because Milperra does both family‑friendly and mad‑cap in one breath.

There’s also the quieter, community side we’re proud of: Milperra Progress used to meet at Milperra Community Hall, 128 Ashford Avenue, every Monday at 10am — playgroup organisers even listed a contact email (milperraprogressplaygroup@gmail.com) on Playgroup NSW. So yes, we have prams, paddles, paintball masks and pub quiz glory all under one postcode.

Who would hate living here? People who like everything very solemn and silent. If you can’t tolerate the thwack of a pickleball match, the squeal of a kart, or the distant hum of nightclub bass, you’ll find us unbearable.

Who would love it? Families who want school‑holiday salvation, social‑sport obsessives who worship at Pickle Point, and anyone who believes a good pub trivia night is civic duty. Milperra: we’re noisy, ridiculous and somehow exactly what we say on the tin.
