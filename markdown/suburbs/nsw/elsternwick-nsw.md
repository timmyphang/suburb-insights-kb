# Elsternwick (NSW)

Slug: `elsternwick-nsw`

We roll out of bed, walk past Rippon Lea Estate’s Victorian hedges and into the small war of choices that is Glen Huntly Road. As Broadsheet put it, Elsternwick is one of those suburbs that treats brunch like civic duty — and yes, we count the likes of Next of Kin, Carter Lovett, Cafe Loco, Goat House Cafe and Lumos Bakery in the tally (Broadsheet’s 2024 guide didn’t miss a beat).

If we’re feeling fancy and historically sentimental, we linger at Elster — the Broadsheet feature remembers it opening in a 1930s service station (the old Glovers Station) and somehow turning petrol-pump nostalgia into cacio e pepe and steak frites. If it’s pizza night, we argue all the way to Gordon Street: Time Out’s 2024 love letter to 48h Pizza e Gnocchi (15 Gordon Street) is still our go-to excuse to queue like we’re at a gallery opening.

The evening options read like a good roll call: Shoten — the Hinoki Pantry team’s outpost, opened in January 2026 — is where we pretend we understand chirashi and buy ceramics on impulse. Classic Cinemas keeps things reliably nostalgic (and hosts festivals, if we want to feel cultured). And Sitchu once swore Riverland Bar at 218 Glen Huntly Road does mulled wine properly; we’ll leave them that.

There’s no real petty drama in the reporting — the local spats are less council coups and more existential cafe debates. As Concrete Playground and Urban List have shown over the years, Elsternwick’s identity is food-first, heritage-second, and slightly smug-about-it-third. If you’ve ever wanted to argue about which bakery makes the best sourdough while standing outside a 1930s service-station conversion, this is your ring.

Who would hate living here? People allergic to choice, gardens, or cinemas that still show foreign films. Who’ll love it? Food obsessives, weekend gardeners (hello, Rippon Lea), film buffs, and anyone who thinks “village” is a selling point worth defending.

We’d say Elsternwick is quietly competitive about being charming — and we’re more than happy to win that contest.
