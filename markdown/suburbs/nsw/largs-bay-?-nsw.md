# Largs Bay ? (NSW)

Slug: `largs-bay-?-nsw`

Back in the day we’d start the weekend with the sea wind doing our hair on Jetty Road and a pilgrimage to Queenies Store — that dog‑friendly beachside cafe that got its start in a historic building at 9 Jetty Road. Broadsheet used to rave about its simple, well‑executed dishes and locally sourced ingredients, and honestly, who are we to argue with a source that knows its coastline?

We’d wander the Esplanade and point at a house built in 1919 — Urban List loved reminding visitors that some of these places still have exposed brick fireplaces and proper sea views — and then make a half‑serious plan to buy one and become full‑time retirees by 45. Sitchu’s spreadsheets, however, had other ideas: Largs Bay popped up on their “suburbs to invest in” list after returning almost 18% capital gains in a year, with rental yields just under 4% and a median around $653,000. Translation: we’re quaint until someone with a calculator shows up.

Winter’s our mood, too. Sitchu suggested the Largs Pier Hotel as the spot to sink into oversized armchairs by a roaring fire after a beach walk — very photogenic, very local — and the Malt Shovel Taphouse on 198 Esplanade used to run happy hour Monday to Saturday, 4–6pm, for anyone who preferred sipping to sprinting.

For the fitness crowd, Momence listed the Largs Bay Health Hub with offers like a two‑week unlimited trial pass for A$49 and a 10‑class pass for A$185 — so if you ever wanted to pretend you were into HIIT before returning to the Esplanade for chips, there was a plan for that.

Who would hate living here? If you can’t stand calm beaches, cosy pubs, or the idea that your nearest café might be in a historic building rather than a glass box, you’ll probably last about a week. Who’d love it? Dog owners, people who like fireplaces, investors who enjoy charts, and anyone who thinks a 1919 house with beach views is a personality trait.

For years the local guides and listings told the story; we’re just the ones who’ve watched the tide come in, laughed at the price tags, and stayed for the view.
