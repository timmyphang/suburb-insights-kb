# Woodville Park (NSW)

Slug: `woodville-park-nsw`

Back in the day we used to swagger down Beaufort St like we owned the place — which, according to the SAcommunity listing, was where both the Woodville Park Childcare Centre (50 Beaufort St) and Mater Dei Catholic Church (55 Beaufort St) kept their doors open for the tiny humans and the very devout. As locals, we like to remind newcomers that our postcode (5011, if you were checking MumsPages) comes with its own set of conveniences: childcare, churches, and directories full of services that make suburban life quietly functional.

Morning routine: we’d drop the kids at the Woodville Park Childcare Centre, wave at the preschool crowd, and mutter thanks that Precious Cargo used to take the early shift over on Port Rd (703 Port Rd, as SAcommunity once listed). If there was a Serbian choir day, you could tell — St Sava Serbian Orthodox Church on Port Rd used to make its presence known; the SAcommunity page still remembers them at 675 Port Rd.

If you’re the sort who likes lists and comparisons, MumsPages used to be our go-to for health and wellbeing directories, NDIS providers and even legal services in the 5011 patch — thrilling reading for the kind of person who bookmarks telephone numbers and a spare receipt for later.

We won’t pretend there’s petty council drama unfolding on page one of the paper every week — the data doesn’t give us that gossip, so we’ll spare you the made-up feuds. What we do have is a quietly civic town stitched together by childcare, playgroups and parish notices. Playgroup NSW used to show the Woodville Alliance Supported playgroups (even listing a Carnes Hill address at 600 Kurrajong Road), so if community singalongs and paint-splattered mornings are your jam, we were once very much on the map.

Who would hate it here? People who need late-night rooftop bars and a constant stream of artisanal everything. Who would love it? Families, new parents and anyone who values a predictable kind of suburban kindness — the sort that turns up at Mass, the playgroup or the local childcare door when you need it. We’re not flashy. We’re dependable, a little mossy around the edges, and honestly quite proud of it.
