# Berkeley (NSW)

Slug: `berkeley-nsw`

We start most mornings standing on 180 Berkeley Road, pretending it's a town square and not just the place Google pins when people ask where we live. Back in the day the only write-ups that even bothered with our postcode were breezy Wollongong travel guides that lumped us in with Wollongong Beach and the nearby national parks — which, honestly, is fair. We’re the kind of place where a surf at Wollongong Beach and a bush walk in the same afternoon is Tuesday.

Our weekend routine is refreshingly simple: dodge the tourist traffic on the way to the beach, watch the sea do its reliable thing, then disappear into the escarpment for people-sized hiking instead of influencer-sized queues. The Sitchu travel notes that you can stitch together Wollongong attractions from here — and yeah, we stitch them well. There’s no artisan-hype parade at our bus stop; there’s just the occasional dog, the inevitable dad with a weekend hammering project, and us commenting on the weather like it’s performance art.

Petty local drama? There isn’t the mess of council scandals or coffee feuds in the snippets we found — mostly because the internet prefers other Berkeleys. The articles available are about Berkeley River Lodge up in the Kimberley, a Berkeley Street in Carlton that apparently hosts a famous roastery and cafes, and then the whole University of California, Berkeley diaspora. So our biggest gripe is being misfiled on other people’s itineraries. We get mistaken for a luxury lodge or an American campus — flattering, but we can’t exactly promise seaplanes or ivy-covered lecture halls.

Who would hate living here: lovers of non-stop nightlife, people who measure the quality of a suburb by how many espresso machines are in a 500-metre radius (yes, there’s a Berkeley Street in Carlton with that sort of thing), and anyone who needs their postcode to trend.

Who would love it: anyone after honest coastline, access to real national parks, and the sort of low-key community that doesn’t mind sharing its street number as shorthand for home. We’re not flashy. We’re Berkeley, NSW — the quiet cousin that somehow gets left off every other Berkeley’s group chat, and we’re perfectly okay with that.
