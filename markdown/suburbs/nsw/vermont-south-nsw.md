# Vermont South (NSW)

Slug: `vermont-south-nsw`

We start our mornings like any sensible Vermonter South: one eye on the kettle, the other on our inbox to see whether Yarra Valley Water has decided to make the suburb part of an impromptu nationwide hydration lesson. Back in the day Broadsheet Melbourne ran the paperwork — remember that 2020 freak-out? — listing Vermont and Vermont South among 88 Melbourne suburbs told to boil water as a precaution after a “potential water-quality issue”.

We wear that like a badge. It’s the sort of small-town civic drama that makes our neighbourhood feel properly lived-in: council alerts, cautious grandparents, and the communal thrumming of kettles. Sure, it’s not glamorous, but nor is it trying to be. People who hate a good municipal noticeboard, public service efficiency, or the comforting predictability of a boil-water advisory would not last a week.

If you like neighbours who actually read Broadsheet, and you appreciate a suburb that can turn a water warning into an unofficial community ritual, Vermont South is your kind of place. For everyone else — bring bottled water and a sense of humour.
