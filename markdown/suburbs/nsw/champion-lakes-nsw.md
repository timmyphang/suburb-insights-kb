# Champion Lakes (NSW)

Slug: `champion-lakes-nsw`

We’ll admit it straight up: we’ve rolled up stubbies-first to Joe Saunders Park on Barrington Avenue more times than we can count — back in the day it was our go-to for corral-ling tiny humans. As Perth Mums Group noted, the park sits on the banks of the gorgeous Champion Lakes Regatta Centre and used to be a delightfully low-drama, nature-based playground: stone steps, logs, a play kitchen, swings and sand play. Perfect if your weekend plan is “keep the kids occupied and survive lunch.”

Reality check: parking’s there, the picnic shelters are handy, but — and this is the bit that keeps our muttered parenting oath alive — there are no toilets. Yes, the official review actually says “no toilets.” So if you enjoy mid-playground bladder negotiations, you’ll fit right in.

For years the local events calendar even listed the Chung Wah Dragon Boat Festival at Champion Lakes, so there was a time when paddles, firecracker atmosphere and community bustle made this place feel like an event capital for a morning. Those were good mornings.

Who would hate living here? If you crave late-night action, restaurants on every corner or public conveniences within sprinting distance, this isn’t your suburb. Who would love it? Families after a gentle nature play vibe, people who appreciate a regatta-centre view, and anyone who thinks a play kitchen and carved logs are high culture.

As locals, we’ll keep bringing snacks, dodging the desperate loo dash and pretending the absence of toilets is a character trait. It’s Champion Lakes — charming, practical and just a tiny bit inconvenient in the best suburban way.
