# Oakville (NSW)

Slug: `oakville-nsw`

Back in the day we’d launch the weekend like any proper clan: a convoy of prams, snacks that are definitely not for the adults, and a very firm group chat where OakvilleMoms would post the weekly roundup. For years the local scene lived online — OakvilleMoms, momsinoakville.ca and Common Moms kept our social calendar full and our anxiety properly scheduled.

We used to swear by Memorial Park — Active Parents even called it a “hidden gem” in North East Oakville — where the new playground convinced half the street we were raising future Olympians and the other half that sand is, in fact, a life decision. If we weren’t there, we were at one of the Common Moms’ drop-ins: Sensory Baby, My 1st Art and Music Class, Little Crawlers, Little Learners — or the seasonal Messy Art in the Park. There was also a Father’s Day Celebration at Coronation Park once, which proved dads can be corralled into craft glue if sufficiently promised a sausage.

Our local amateur drama mostly lived in threads. The OakvilleMoms forum delivered everything a community needs: misplaced bake-sale tabulations, fervent stroller comparisons, and the occasional heated debate about which playground reigns supreme (Bronte Heritage Waterfront Park made the highlight reels). Nobody got famous for it, but we did get organised — “build your squad,” as Common Moms liked to say.

Who would hate living here? People who need a 24/7 party strip, or anyone whose idea of a weekend is not a curated calendar of toddler classes and park meet-ups. If you crave anonymity, beware: around here your kid will know three other kids’ middle names by Tuesday.

Who would love it? New parents, part-time Pinterest project managers, and anyone who likes neighbours who appear at every community event because, frankly, the online groups made sure they would. For years Mommy Connections, Active Parents and the rest kept our little town humming with practical chaos — playgroups, schedules, and more reasons to bring snacks than we knew what to do with. We’ll take that over pretence any day.
