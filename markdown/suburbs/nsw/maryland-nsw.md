# Maryland (NSW)

Slug: `maryland-nsw`

Back in the day, when the internet had more recipes than local council minutes, our name kept turning up in places that weren’t actually here. We’ll admit we get a kick out of it: Broadsheet once casually threw “4 large chicken Marylands” into Aunty Beryl Van‑Oploo’s outback‑spiced roast, and Meatsmith gave us a whole roast-chook moment. Even Stephanie Alexander recommended the “Maryland cut” for her Basque‑style chicken. If you’re ordering food online, expect to be confused with a poultry joint or a spicy dinner special more often than a postcode.

A typical weekend for us is low‑key: we take the short drive past Cameron Park and Fletcher, remind ourselves the Sitchu listing promised we’re “3.5km to Maryland Shopping Centre and Tavern, 5.3km to the buzz of Wallsend” and that the Newcastle CBD and beaches are “a 25 minute drive” away. Yes, a property ad once did our geography for us — thanks, internet real estate gods. We like that kind of modest brag.

There’s no local tabloid scandal in the files — mostly recipes, property blips and the odd global Mary‑land (the American kind) turning up in cultural pieces. So instead of petty soap opera, our local drama is existential: are we a shopping hub, a handy satellite of Newcastle, or the cut of chicken your aunt swears by? The media can’t decide, and frankly, neither can we. We own it.

Who would hate living here? If you crave 24/7 city theatre, rooftop espresso theatrics, or to be referred to exclusively by haute‑dining menu descriptions, you’ll find us a little provincial — and yes, you’ll be correcting strangers who assume you’re a dinner order.

Who would love it here? Families who like short drives to Wallsend and the Newcastle CBD, people who appreciate being in a practical pocket between Cameron Park and Fletcher, and anyone who enjoys the comforting possibility that your suburb might lend its name to someone’s Sunday roast recipe. We’ll keep the tavern, the shopping centre and our slightly confused fame — you bring the roast.
