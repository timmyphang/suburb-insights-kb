# St Helens Park (NSW)

Slug: `st-helens-park-nsw`

We’ll start with the farmhouse: we still like to pretend the Park Farm ACYP Community Centre is our rustic time capsule, because the website says “Park Farm is the original name for the old farmhouse which forms the main part of the centre.” In our head that’s code for “lovely hall where you can book a table for some mysterious class and pretend you’re getting cultured.”

Full disclosure: the only digital evidence we have is older, undated stuff from the St Helens Community Centres pages — a Toolkit, a Courses & Activities list and the Park Farm profile. So yes, this is nostalgia territory: these pages used to list hundreds of courses and invite people “interested in enrolling… thinking of volunteering?” For years the online pages presented St Helens as The Place For Extra-Curricular Redemption.

Our weekend routine (in fantasy): we wander in, pick a course from the “Courses & Activities” list, smile at the toolkit that promises organisation, and then find ourselves baffled but delighted by how many people are quietly keeping the place ticking.

Who would hate it here? Anyone allergic to community noticeboards, uncomfortable with fundraising sausage sizzles, or convinced that every meeting must be a slick corporate event. This place runs on committees, curtains and an old farmhouse personality — it’s cosy, a bit earnest, and not trying to be cool.

Who would love it here? Volunteers, people who want to learn something useless on a Tuesday night, and anyone who enjoys the idea of an original farmhouse being repurposed into a hub of local life. If that’s you, the (older) community pages were definitely waving a welcome sign.
