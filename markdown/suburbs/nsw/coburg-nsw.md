# Coburg (NSW)

Slug: `coburg-nsw`

Back in the day we used to brag that Coburg was the sort of place where you could spend a Saturday and still have stories by Sunday — largely because we’d drift from the Coburg Farmers’ Market at Coburg Primary School to Coburg Lake Reserve and somehow end up at Bridges Reserve for the Night Market, smelling far too many things we couldn’t pronounce. There’s always been a comfort in that loop: a bakery detour at Trivelli, a sticky pastry from Achillion, and somebody inevitably pointing at Bin Chicken Island like it was a local monument.

Our weekend ritual used to be embarrassingly precise. We’d buy veg at the farmers’ market, sidle past the playground equipment at the lake, and then take an obligatory look through the Pentridge precinct — where the old prison was reinvented into boutique experiences: The Interlude, the North & Common dining room, even The Glass Den with its slightly theatrical breakfast dishes. BrewDog’s 500-seat thing at Pentridge made for good gossip: craft-beer meets former cellblock, cue many photos.

There’s been local theatre too — not the stage kind — from plans for Koi Toy to the quiet thrills of Tochi Deli reopening with its tidy nigiri and chirashi, and new pastry-focused spots like Beit Siti rolling out jam-and-labneh danishes and musakhan-inspired treats. We’ve watched markets and makers’ fairs pop up at Coburg Town Hall and bridges over the Merri Creek, and we’ve been fond of the little community corners: East Coburg Neighbourhood House on Nicholson Street, the playgroups at Uniting Coburg and the Merri Creek Reserve meet-ups near CERES Joes Market Garden.

Who would hate it here? If you can’t bear a glorified ibis colony, or the juxtaposition of boutique hotels and prison tours, or three nights of markets where the DJ thinks they’re at a festival — you’d probably be miserable. Same goes if you like absolute silence and zero pedestrian traffic.

Who would love it? Families who prize green space, makers and food obsessives, anyone who thinks a drive-in movie is romantic, and people who enjoy a suburb that can be cosy and mildly chaotic all in one afternoon. We’ll always defend that. Even if the papers went quiet lately, we know where to find the best cake.
