# Ringwood North (NSW)

Slug: `ringwood-north-nsw`

Back in the day we’d wander the leafy streets of Ringwood North like we owned the map — past Ringwood Lake (Time Out once wrote it up), through whisper‑quiet courts and over to the odd salon on Warrandyte Road. Sitchu even singled out 9 Karima Court as “tightly held” and “whisper quiet”, with Athelstane Reserve and Ringwood North Primary School on the doorstep, which is our subtle way of saying: yes, it’s boring in the best possible way.

Mornings used to look like this: a school run that barely qualifies as a mission, a detour past Eastland if you need options (Time Out), and the comforting knowledge that at 176 Warrandyte Road there was a day‑spa the guides liked — Urban List noted Natskin at that address among Melbourne’s best massages, and Sitchu previously listed Botanica Day Spa there too. We’ve also had nights of paint‑and‑wine theatre: Frida’s Sip n’ Paint had a red‑carpet opening in October 2022, BYO tins and a paintbrush and you’re in.

Petty local drama? Our claim to infamy was practical rather than spicy: as Broadsheet recorded, Ringwood North made the Yarra Valley Water boil‑water list back in 2020. We still joke that the only thing more refreshing than our parks is the certified way we boiled our water.

Who would hate living here: anyone who needs neon lights, never‑ending events, or a postcode that doubles as a nightlife brand. If you think “tightly held court” is code for “no parking for your scooter,” this is not your jam.

Who would love it: families, people who prefer bike trails and leafy streets (Sitchu’s notes on the area made that crystal), spa seekers who liked what Urban List highlighted, and parents who once relied on North Ringwood Playgroup at 33a Wonga Road — they ran sessions, enrolments and term dates were a real thing, back when we kept everything quaintly scheduled.

Ringwood North: we’ll happily be mocked for being sleepy — and quietly proud, because someone has to keep the comfy corners of Melbourne intact.
