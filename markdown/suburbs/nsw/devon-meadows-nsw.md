# Devon Meadows (NSW)

Slug: `devon-meadows-nsw`

We start our mornings like everyone else around here—running the kids to school and pretending we enjoy it. Back in the day (and according to the Kids First pages we dug up), Devon Meadows’ little slice of order was the Devon Meadows Primary School Kindergarten at 19 Cross Road. It was listed as a Kids First early years service, open 8:30–4:30, with enrolments handled through the City of Casey — the same lovely lot that makes sure you bring the right form, in triplicate. Phone on the listing? 03 9373 5110. If you really want head office vibes, Kids First’s main number is 03 9450 0900.

As Kids First announced on 18/09/2024, they were selected to deliver four new Victorian kinders and Devon Meadows Primary School was one of the sites named. So yes, at least on paper we’re part of a strategic plan — sounds fancier than the way we describe the morning drop-off queue.

Petty local drama? If you count council-managed enrolments and the thrilling saga of paperwork as drama, then we’ve got an Emmy-worthy subplot. Otherwise we’re refreshingly low-key: no boutique chaos, just a postcode that knows its bus times and its forms.

Who would hate living here? Folks who need 24-hour latte theatrics or constant event listings. Who would love it? Parents after predictable kinder hours, people who want a sleepy base with actual school logistics sorted (or at least listed online). We’ll keep bringing the juice boxes; you bring your stapler.
