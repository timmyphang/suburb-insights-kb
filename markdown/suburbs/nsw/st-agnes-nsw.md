# St Agnes (NSW)

Slug: `st-agnes-nsw`

We start our weekends like any sensible St Agnes locals: one of us drops the dog at Embark Adelaide and the other queues — symbolically, not with rage — for the Espresso Bar. Embark (3A/108 Tolley Road, if you’re the sort who loses everything) used to bill itself as an outdoor, farm-based doggy daycare: think enrichment, exercise and full-time supervision on a fit-for-purpose farm property. As Sitchu put it, we’ve got a proper paddock for the hounds — and yes, we brag about it.

By mid-morning we gravitate towards the Espresso Bar, because Sitchu once gave our caffeine corner a tick of approval and we still like to pretend that counts as validation. Back in the day — and by “back in the day” we mean the articles that actually mention us — our little hamlet’s claim to culinary fame wasn’t a new coffee joint or a rooftop bar; it was our booze. Broadsheet has name-checked "St Agnes brandy" in dessert whispers and courtships with craft beer: Mark Best’s deserts were said to include canelés sweetened with South Australian St Agnes brandy and Blue Mountains honey, and a Collingwood brewer once designed a sorcery of a beer to be paired with St Agnes brandy as a boilermaker. We’re fancied up in places that are not ours — and we love the elevation.

Petty local drama? The archive is mercifully spare. No council scandals, no soap-opera bakery closures local to our postcode — mostly a sense that the outside world borrows our name when it wants a classy spirit and a scenic dog paddock. We’ll take it.

Who would hate living here: anyone who needs constant nightlife, 24/7 trend-chasing or a news feed stuffed with council rancour. Who would love it: dog owners who want a farm-style drop-off, coffee drinkers who appreciate being quietly recommended in a guide, and anyone pleased to see St Agnes brandy show up on menus two cities away. We’re humble, slightly posh by association, and audacious enough to let our brandy do the talking.
