# Tarrawanna (NSW)

Slug: `tarrawanna-nsw`

We’re standing on the footpath outside 3 Brissendon Cl pretending we don’t notice the baby bags and espresso thermoses, because once upon a time CareforKids listed Tarrawanna as a suburb that takes its nappies seriously. Back in the day the site counted 13 childcare services in 2518 — and only 3 of them had vacancies — so yes, pack your patience.

Tattle Tales Preschool was the local brag: a 5.0 (7) rating, hours 7:30am–5:30pm, and listed with No Vacancies. It felt like the place parents whispered about on playgroup circuit. The broader listing for Wollongong/Illawarra tied us into a neat chain of suburbs — Thirroul, Balgownie — as if we’re all in one very busy, very organised parenting relay.

If you like polite waiting lists and the minor thrill of winning a spot at preschool, you’ll find Tarrawanna quietly satisfying. If you hate queues, forms and the fact the average cost was pegged at $129.31/day, you’ll probably glare at our pram-laden streets and drive straight past.

We don’t pretend this is cutting-edge culture — our claim to fame is being the sensible answer on childcare search pages. So who should love it here? Young families who like predictability, neighbours who will swap a spare nappy at 3pm, and anyone who appreciates a suburb that used to be reliably listed on CareforKids. Who would hate it? People who wanted instant enrolment and a discount. We’ll keep your spot warm — if it ever opens.
