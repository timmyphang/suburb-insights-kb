# Rosebery (NSW)

Slug: `rosebery-nsw`

We know the exact Saturday morning the Engine Yards made national news — the weekend Lune Croissanterie opened its Sydney flagship at Rosebery Engine Yards on 7 December 2024 and we all queued like it was the Queen’s birthday. That’s Rosebery for you: industrial shells, copper lights and people pretending they’re allergic to suburbia until the bakers start pulling viennoiseries.

Our weekend routine used to be embarrassingly specific: stroll past Mentmore & Morley, duck into the corner café, pick up something from Frenchies Bakery & Pâtisserie at 4/61–71 Mentmore Ave, then wander The Cannery at 61 Mentmore Avenue where Archie Rose Distilling Co and the providores used to make the whole place feel like a grown-up school fete. The Cannery Markets — the ones that ran on the first weekend of every month, 9am–3pm — were our excuse to buy one more artisanal something for the bench seat we never actually use.

Local drama? Oh, we’ve had a few. The Cannery Markets got a relaunch under Cambridge Markets and suddenly everyone had an opinion about curation and circular fashion. There was that Woolworths Metro trial that went cashless in Rosebery — and yes, we read the Junkee piece and argued about wallets in front of the trolley bay like it was a community forum.

Who would hate living here? If the words “industrial‑chic” make you reach for a pitchfork, this isn’t for you. If you can’t stand queues at Lune, artisanal bottle shops like Drink Hive, Koskela’s design-led vibes, or Three Blue Ducks’ long-standing farm-to-table energy — well, you’ll be lonely on your pristine lawn.

Who would love it? Food obsessives, market people, ceramics hobbyists who like The Clayground ten-minute stroll from Green Square, and anyone who enjoys a converted-warehouse aesthetic (we’re looking at you, anyone who’s ever booked Rosebery Hall at 151 Dunning Avenue for a “heritage-style” knees-up). Rosebery used to be industrial; we turned it into a neighbourhood that smells faintly of butter, whisky and ambition. We’ll keep the queues — you bring the reusable bag.
