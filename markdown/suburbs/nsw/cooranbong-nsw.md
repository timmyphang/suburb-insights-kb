# Cooranbong (NSW)

Slug: `cooranbong-nsw`

Back in the day we made our weekend plans with the same level of ceremony most people reserve for brunch bookings. First stop: the wobble. As Newy with Kids and Lake Mac Family Life have pointed out, the Cooranbong swing bridge — the wooden one tucked behind the old Sanitarium (Weet‑Bix) factory — was built in 1934 so factory hands didn’t have to row across Dora Creek. Kids still treat that bit of history like an endurance test. In 2023 a new flood‑proof bridge was opened next to it as part of a 13km walking and cycling track, which, frankly, is the grown‑up version of admitting the original bridge was getting too theatrical.

Our ritual usually creaked on. We’d park off Freemans Drive, wander the Sandy Creek Walk along Dora Creek, then collapse at Cooranbong Reserve — which Newy with Kids and Lake Mac Family Life have both listed as a big, shaded playground with a play tower, accessible roundabout, water play, scooter track, BBQs, bubblers, toilets and a giant enclosed slide. Three sides are fenced, so you can pretend you’re only checking your phone for two minutes and actually mean it.

For the faintly outdoorsy among us, Time Out once recommended AAA Horse Riding & More at 252 Mount Nellinda Rd, Cooranbong 2265 (02 4977 2245) — they ran rides through the Watagan foothills with daily hours and a price spread Time Out listed as $25–$290. Not exactly the Harbour City, but if your idea of romance is a farm dog with a better sense of direction than your GPS, we got you.

Petty local drama? Mostly architectural: we argue in a kindly way about which bridge is more photogenic. Also, Playgroup NSW keeps reminding us there are playgroups for every tiny human phase — so there’s never a shortage of people to judge your pram choice.

Who would hate it here: anyone who needs a 24/7 espresso crawl or can’t tolerate wooden bridges that have opinions. Who would love it: families who like a proper playground, people who own sensible shoes for walks along Dora Creek, and anyone nostalgic for a town that still lets a swing bridge wobble on the agenda.
