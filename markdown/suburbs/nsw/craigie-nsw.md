# Craigie (NSW)

Slug: `craigie-nsw`

We’ll be honest: Craigie doesn’t slap you in the face with glamour. Back in the day most of the press we get are nostalgic footnotes — there aren’t any fresh, 2024‑plus features to parade around — so let us enjoy the archive like a well‑worn esky.

Morning here is a slow slide into the old favourites the internet keeps reminding us about. Broadsheet once noted Bollywood Spices as a Craigie favourite after it moved from North Perth, and the man they quoted, Glenn McCue, said he’d been a customer for more than ten years — so yes, the memory of our curry game is apparently still worth a mention. Urban List used to list Craigie Leisure Centre (751 Whitfords Avenue) for its 50‑metre outdoor pool, heated indoor pools, spa, water playground and even saunas — the kind of place parents brag about while holding a lukewarm coffee outside the doors. And for bakery purists, Craigie Hot Bread Shop at Craigie Plaza, 15 Perilya Rd, used to be a perennial contender in Urban List’s vanilla‑slice rounds; apparently the place has been at it for almost 40 years.

If you’re the sort who enjoys confusing Google, we’re fond of the fact that “Craigie” also turns up as everything from RoyAl’s‑owner Alasdair Craigie in Broadsheet pieces about Wembley and Vic Park to Time Out’s Boston love letter to Craigie on Main and its famous Craigie Burger. There’s even a Tasmanian vineyard called Craigie Knowe in the Sitchu files — honestly, we’ll take the name recognition where we can.

Petty local drama? The archives are quiet; no council soap operas to litigate, so we’ll just admit we like being underrated. Sitchu once pointed out our property jumped more than 13% to a median of $540,000 and that Westfield Whitford City is close by — so you can laugh at us now, or buy here later.

Who would hate it here: anyone needing 24/7 glamour, buzzy inner‑city night markets or constant new openings. Who would love it: families who grew up on the Craigie Leisure Centre splash zones, people who appreciate a bakery that’s been around since before influencer marketing, and anyone content with a suburb that quietly keeps doing its thing. We’ll be here, paddling in the lap pool, still proud of our oddly famous vanilla slice.
