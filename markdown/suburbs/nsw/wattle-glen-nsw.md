# Wattle Glen (NSW)

Slug: `wattle-glen-nsw`

Back in the day we’d all meet at Gate 1 on Sunnyside Crescent like it was a pilgrimage — not to a cafe, but to the playgroup that used to run at Wattle Glen Primary School. As Playgroup Victoria noted, we had our own designated classroom and, yes, both the indoor and outdoor facilities. It felt small-town official: sign in, corrugated iron, and a Kindergarten teacher who somehow kept 20 tiny conspirators in sync.

We don’t have a booming nightlife to brag about — our brag is a noticeboard. Find It Locally treated Wattle Glen as a proper township hub, the sort of place whose community pages link you to neighbouring seats of civilisation: Bend of Islands, Flowerdale, Yarrambat, even Plenty and Hurstbridge. If you scroll those local listings you can taste the civic-mindedness: sausage sizzles, lost chook notices, the quiet economy of flyers stapled to a pole.

Petty local drama? The paper trail is suspiciously wholesome. Instead, we’ll claim the big fight was always whether to hang another notice under “community groups” or “local produce.” Riveting stuff.

Who would hate living here? If you need 24/7 everything and a postcode that isn’t mentioned next to Epping or Mill Park, you’ll get bored. Who would love it? Families who once lined up at Gate 1, people who like their social life served via noticeboard, and anyone who prefers small-town rhythms over city neon. We’ll keep the gate warm.
