# Redfern (NSW)

Slug: `redfern-nsw`

We are standing under the Wunderlich Lane light boxes, coffee in one hand and the kind of cheap smugness you only get from living where Surry Hills met the train line and decided to stay. Broadsheet reckons we’re a dining destination now (they updated that guide in June 2024) and honestly, you can see why: Wunderlich Lane’s new precinct stitched Surry Hills to Redfern in late 2024 and suddenly your walk to the station is also a food tour.

Our weekend routine is eclectic and exhausting. If we’re celebrating something obscure, we book the theatrical 10-course R by Raita Noda that Broadsheet previewed in 2025 and feel very cultured. If it’s a Tuesday, we pop into Olympus or Mezepotamia — Sitchu’s 2026 round-up gives them a wink and we obey. The lane, the restaurants, the artist-run energy (107 Projects has been doing the heavy lifting for years) make our postcode feel like an always-on festival of good taste and earnest creative hustle.

We’re not pretending there isn’t history. For years the Redfern Aboriginal Tent Embassy and The Block activism have reminded everyone this place isn’t just a brunch map — it’s a site of protest and community struggle. The Redfern Legal Centre’s campaigning shows up in the record too. We wear that complexity like an old jumper: it’s patched, it’s real, and it doesn’t always match the new wallpaper.

Who would hate living here? If you crave suburban predictability, cul‑de‑sacs and zero murals, you’ll want to move to a different postcode. If the idea of your street having more restaurants than silence fills you with dread, Redfern will make you twitch.

Who would love it? Artists, food obsessives, anyone who likes a station upgrade story and a late‑night ramen tucked between restored pubs and ambitious new precincts. We’ve been many things — a hub of Indigenous culture, a gentrifying dining hotspot, the place where activists and restaurateurs orbit each other — and somehow all of it fits in a single walkable block. Come for the food, stay for the contradiction. We wouldn’t have it any other way.
