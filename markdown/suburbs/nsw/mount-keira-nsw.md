# Mount Keira (NSW)

Slug: `mount-keira-nsw`

We start the weekend the only way Mount Keira allows: by pretending we didn’t drive up for the view but for the climb. The Mount Keira Lookout still gives us that smug coastline reveal — the kind Broadsheet immortalised in its gallery (they even updated it on 15 December 2022, so yes, we’re still photogenic). Concrete Playground reminds anyone who’ll listen that the summit track is 460 metres and that rainforest can make you feel spiritual, or at least slightly damp.

Our morning routine is as reliable as the birds: park, lace up, negotiate the Dave Walsh Track if we’re feeling righteous, and detour for a contemplative nod at the Six Daughters of the West Wind sculpture. The walk alternates between “we are tiny under the canopy” and “we are very close to beating that cyclist on the Wollongong to Thirroul Bike Track” — Sitchu has long listed our trails for both walkers and riders, so we claim that double-threat status with pride.

There isn’t much juicy local tabloid fodder — no council ruckuses or café feuds in these clippings. The press coverage mostly wants us to be enjoyed: family-friendly, bushy, and quietly superior. The Parents Guide even named Keiraville and Mount Keira among top spots for families, and Kooee Kids ran Bush Kindy and nature play programs at 333 Mount Keira Road and Darkes Forest for the small people who prefer creek mud to iPads. We’ll wear that like a badge.

Who would hate it here? People who need a latte on every corner or crave chain-store ambience; for those folk, the Blue Mile and North Wollongong’s café strip remain the closest compromise. Also, anyone allergic to uphill apologies.

Who would love it? Families hunting leafy afternoon plans, kids enrolled in bush kindy, hikers after rainforest and escarpment drama, and anyone who takes skyline selfies featuring Brokers Nose, Mount Kembla and Mount Ousley in the same breath. We’ll keep the lookout warm and the tracks honest — and if you bring a camera, we’ll pretend we didn’t rehearse the pose.
