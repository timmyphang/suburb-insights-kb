# Clyde (NSW)

Slug: `clyde-nsw`

We like to think we start the day like locals, even if “local” for some of us just means we’ve learned the back way to the river. Weekends usually kick off at the Clyde Township Family & Community Centre — Playgroup Victoria actually lists us (yes, we made the 2026 roster) — where the kids run the show and we perfect the art of making small talk about tides.

Once we’ve handed over the squeaky toys, we gravitate toward the thing that everyone keeps writing about: the Clyde River. As Time Out and Sitchu have been reminding journos for years, our river is the main character — good for kayaking, houseboats, and staring meaningfully at mangroves while pretending we’re on a nature documentary. Time Out even sent people out to paddle past oyster racks, then shoo them to the Oyster Shed on Wray Street or Pearly Oyster Bar and Farm for shellfish sampling. We don’t have to convince you — travel guides have already done the hard sell.

Tourists get the ritual: book a Boatshed cruise (Time Out said the 11.30am run comes with famous fish and chips) and call it a spiritual experience. We call it “the one time we let someone else do the driving while we judge their anchoring.” For proper perspective hikes, Sitchu points to Clyde River National Park and Holmes Lookout — yes, the views are worth the sunscreen.

There’s not a whole lot of petty council drama in the files we’ve got — mostly guides and happy-people-to-do lists — so instead of gossip, here’s the vibe: we’re gloriously outdoorsy, a touch oyster-obsessed, and suspiciously charitable toward anyone who can actually thread a kayak through mangrove pylons without swearing.

Who would hate living here? People allergic to salt air, boats that rock when you’re trying to sleep, or having their holiday photos outclassed by an estuary at golden hour. Who would love it? Families who need a decent playgroup, kayakers, anyone who treats oysters like a hobby, and visitors who enjoy being gently shushed by a town that prefers tide charts over nightlife itineraries.
