# Fitzroy (NSW)

Slug: `fitzroy-nsw`

We’re standing on Brunswick Street, coffee in hand (as one does), watching tattooed twenty‑somethings, downsizing Baby Boomers and newly arrived migrants shuffle past the same graffiti‑tooled laneways that made this place famous. Walk Smith, Gertrude, Brunswick or Johnston and you’ll see the same parade Broadsheet used to applaud — except now we’ve memorised which bakery queue is shortest and who keeps leaving their bike where it won’t fit.

Weekends used to look like a checklist: Rose Street Market for whatever artisanal thing we definitely don’t need, a detour to Faraday’s Cage for a croissant, and maybe a Sunday lunch at Cutler & Co on Gertrude Street when we were feeling very grown‑up. We watched residencies land at 274 Brunswick Street (Little Rose had its short run), thumbed through vintage racks informed by Concrete Playground and Urban List, and raised a cautious glass to Hands Down on Greeves Street when it showed up with its French‑and‑Spanish spirit. Even Moondrop Bar made an entrance in December 2025 and gave us another reason to pretend we knew cocktail provenance.

Petty local drama? Oh, we had the lot. The Fitzroy Garage Sesh blew up on TikTok, went global, had its award moment and — yes — some idiots scrawled hateful stuff afterwards. Brian Taylor admitting the garage party was his? Classic. It was messy, loud and somehow very on brand.

Who would hate living here? People who like their streets quiet, their shops predictable and their weekends free from the romantic chaos of a bar crawl Time Out once mapped out. If you can’t cope with murals, long queues for interesting food, or hearing live music at 2am down a laneway, move on — we’ll take your terrace.

Who would love it? Creatives, bargain‑hunters, families who find solace in Fitzroy Gardens or the playgroups at Bargoonga Nganjin, and anyone who thinks the best part of a city is the strange little things that refuse to be polished away. Time Out once crowned us the coolest neighbourhood; we’ll concede the crown if you promise not to Instagram it every five minutes.
