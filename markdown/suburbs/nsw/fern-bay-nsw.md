# Fern Bay (NSW)

Slug: `fern-bay-nsw`

We like to think of Fern Bay as the place you end up when you prioritise fresh air over an espresso and sand in the kids’ sneakers over valet parking. Mornings here usually start with boots on the Worimi Forest Walk — the 1.4‑kilometre stroll that begins at the car park on the Fern Bay Access Trail and, as Concrete Playground cheerfully pointed out, eventually pops out at the southern end of Stockton Beach where the land remembers its Aboriginal history. It’s the sort of walk that makes you feel properly coastal without having to sit in traffic for an hour.

Back in the day our weekend choreography was embarrassingly wholesome: drop the tiny humans at Tillys Play & Development Centre (they opened in 2019 at 3–5 Vardon Rd and were even rated “Exceeding” under the National Quality Standard), swing by the fenced Fern Bay Reserve Playground opposite the public school car park, and then argue about whose turn it is to clean the sand out of the boot. The playground, as Newy with Kids noted, is the fully‑fenced, shade‑sailed kind with swings, climbing gear, a half‑size basketball court, a water fountain and those large sandstone blocks that double as seating and pretend‑rockforts. The Community Hall watches approvingly from across the way.

There’s no spicy local scandal to gossip about in our files — we’re a place that prefers a good sea breeze to drama — so we won’t pretend there’s one. If you need street art murals at every roundabout or a craft beer barista lineup, you’ll hate us. If your definition of excitement is a fenced playground that actually stops toddlers getting lost, or a walk that finishes on a beach with real history, you’ll love us.

We’re not trying to be flashy. We’re the suburb that knows the best spots for a morning stomp, a reliable childcare that once made the inspector smile, and a community hall that quietly hosts the things that matter. Come for the Worimi forest, stay because you forgot your keys in the car and ended up watching the sunset on Stockton Beach.
