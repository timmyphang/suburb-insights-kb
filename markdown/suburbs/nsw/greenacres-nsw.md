# Greenacres (NSW)

Slug: `greenacres-nsw`

Greenacres, NSW — we live here, we sweep the veranda, we know every corner, and apparently the internet thinks we’re imaginary.

Morning: we stretch, look out at our quiet streets and think, if anyone’s writing about us it’s probably because they misread “green acres.” Back in the day the only thing even faintly on the record is an Urban List snippet that kept talking about “covering several green acres” and a place called Creeksyde being crowned the world’s first environmentally friendly holiday park — which, to be clear, was about somewhere else. Urban List loves Queenstown and Adelaide; Greenacres just gets the scenic metaphor.

Weekend routine: we walk the local parks (unnamed in the press), greet the neighbours (unnamed in the press), and refresh the news feed hoping for a mention. No luck. That’s fine. We prefer being the suburb the guidebooks forget — less queueing, fewer influencers pretending to discover us.

Petty drama: non-existent in the archives. If you want council spats or cafe feuds, you’ll have to travel to an article that actually cares. We’re happily boring like that.

Who would hate it here? People who need constant content and curated brunch shots will file a complaint. Who would love it here? Anyone who likes roomy lawns, quiet nights and the rare thrill of being overlooked by Urban List. We’ll keep the green acres to ourselves, thanks.
