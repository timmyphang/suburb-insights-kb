# Kearns (NSW)

Slug: `kearns-nsw`

You know you live in Kearns when your best weekend planning involves a laptop, a cardigan and three separate searches for “Kearns” that return nothing about our cul‑de‑sacs and everything about other peoples’ Kearnses.

We start our Saturday by scrolling MumsPages — because for years the local online footprint has been a directory: “Businesses and Service Providers for Mums — Kearns (2558)”, “Child Care Centres in Kearns (2558)”, “Pregnancy and Birth Support Services — Kearns (2558)”. That’s our version of a tourist guide. It tells us where the babies are catered for, who to call when the toddler needs craft classes, and where the nearest supportive group is. Practical, comforting, and very much us.

If you type our postcode into a search bar and expect a bustling precinct tour, prepare for a detour. Broadsheet will cheerfully offer you Alex Kearns’ Paddington and Glebe adventures instead; Urban List will try to sell you Kearns Crescent in Applecross and Kona Café on the corner of Riseley Street and Kearns Crescent. There’s also a parenting duo at DadMumLife — Brad and Sarah Kearns — and Becky Kearns’ DefiningMum and Paths to Parenthub podcast waving like a friendly neighbour from the internet. We love that our name is getting around, even if it’s often the wrong suburb turning up for tea.

There’s no local celebrity spat to narrate, no boutique café with a pricy brunch board to sneer at — and frankly, that’s the point. We don’t need that noise. What we do have is the quiet satisfaction of a place where directories and parenting blogs take centre stage. It suits the people who live here: practical folk who care about child care lists, pregnancy and birth support options, and a reliable local network more than the latest city trend.

Who would hate living here? Folks who want their suburb on the culture pages every weekend and their name popping up in restaurant roundups. Who would love it? Families, new parents and anyone who’d rather know where the nearest supportive service is than which bar won some metropolitan award. We’ll keep the directory bookmarked — it’s ours, properly useful and utterly Kearns.
