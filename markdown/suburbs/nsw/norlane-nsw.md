# Norlane (NSW)

Slug: `norlane-nsw`

We live in Norlane and yes, we know where every noisy short-cut and sensible little business is — at least according to the listings we can find from back in the day.

Morning here, if you were the sort who still reads local directories, meant checking a couple of stalwarts in the old pages: Uptown Body Works — listed as an auto repair in Norlane with a solid 4.8/5 from 20 reviews — and Around Geelong Dental Care, which showed up as the local dentist. The Norlane Child and Family Centre also turned up in the archives with a 4.7/5 from 35 reviews, which is probably why everyone who’s stayed stuck in town for a few years mentions it with something like fond bewilderment. All of these entries are older listings, mind you — so we’re doing a bit of nostalgia rather than a “what’s open now” guide.

There isn’t any spicy council saga in the clippings we have, no viral petition drama, no café kerfuffle to roast — just the practical, steady stuff: your mechanics, your dentist, your community centre. Even our cultural confusion is bureaucratic: Junkee spent time talking to the band Northlane about their EP, so every now and then someone thinks we’ve got a touring metal act named after our postcode. We don’t — but we’ll happily take the association.

Who would hate living here? People who need a constant parade of boutique openings and curated skyline views. If your weekend priority is infinite brunch options and mood-lit cocktail bars, you’ll get restless. We’re not a backdrop for influencer content — we’re the sort of place where the list of useful phone numbers matters.

Who would love it here? Families who appreciate a proper child-and-family centre, anyone who values a reliable mechanic with decent reviews, folks who prefer substance over style. We’re practical, low-key, and oddly loyal. We don’t show up in every travel piece, and that’s fine — we like being the suburb that quietly gets on with things while the rest of the internet argues about latte art.
