# Mt Druitt South (NSW)

Slug: `mt-druitt-south-nsw`

We roll our windows down and know exactly when the morning has properly started in Mt Druitt South: when the car park outside a Mount Druitt primary school looks like a small, very dedicated nursery rhub. Back in the day Familylinks ran those free playgroups — “inclusive, evidence-based playgroups and parenting support,” as Children First puts it — and for years that’s been the soundtrack to our suburb: glue sticks, singalongs, and the logistics of preschool drop-offs.

We’re proud of the way this place shows up for families. Children First (yep, the local not-for-profit) has long listed preschools, long day care, occasional care and OOSH across the Blacktown area, and for Mount Druitt that hasn’t just been childcare — it’s been community scaffolding. The play-based activities, led by early childhood trained educators, feel less like a program and more like the suburb’s unofficial social calendar.

There’s no scandal here, only pragmatic heroics: parents swapping morning routines, grandies on standby, educators doing the real work. If you need drama, look elsewhere — our version comes in the form of a mysteriously missing snack container and one highly competitive story time.

Who would hate it: anyone allergic to craft, noise or being useful to their neighbours. Who’d love it: small people, folks who actually read the Children First centres page, early childhood educators, and anyone who prefers community to curated loneliness. We’ll keep the playgroups, the parenting workshops and the rumours of yet another lost lunchbox. Mount Druitt South: loud, loving and territorially committed to the craft table.
