# Orelia (NSW)

Slug: `orelia-nsw`

We like to think Orelia has mood: half practical, half unexpectedly regency. Picture us on a weekend: the kids at the playgrounds, the dog doing whatever dogs do in open green spaces, while someone scrolls property listings with a suspiciously optimistic grin.

Back in the day the suburb got two very different shout-outs. Sitchu called us "sought‑out by savvy investors and first‑home buyers," noting a median house price of $320,000 and rental yields around 6% — and praised our access to independent and government schools, public-transport links and "fantastic shopping at Kwinana Marketplace" (and yes, an easy 30‑minute drive into central Perth). As Sitchu reported, we are practical pickings for people who want a foot on the ladder.

Meanwhile Urban List blessed the internet with the Doen Orelia Tiered Embroidered Tulle Maxi Dress — because nothing says suburbia like being immortalised as a peach tulle frock. Urban List listed that dress at $916 (on sale to $458), which is peak internet glamour meeting suburban reality.

Who would hate living here? If your vibe is late-night rooftop artisanal everything and zero backyard room for a BBQ, Orelia will feel like a compromise. Who would love it? First‑home buyers, investors and anyone who wants playgrounds, green space and a short-ish commute — Sitchu basically wrote the love letter for you.

We’re unpretentious, a bit thrift-shop meets tulle, and very grounded — and honestly, we’ll take that.
