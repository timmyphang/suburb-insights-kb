# Fassifern (NSW)

Slug: `fassifern-nsw`

We know Fassifern like the back of our ute keys — a Sunday here still feels like a deliberate detour from civilisation. Back in the day, if we wanted water and drama we followed The Urban List’s directions: Boonah–Fassifern Road, a left onto Lake Moogerah Road, a right into Charlwood and then Gorge Road. You cross private land, cough up a few dollars at the gorge gate, and pretend you’re the first to discover The Gorge. It’s our premium “wild” where the only Instagram filter you need is a bit of grit.

For the lavender romantics among us, Time Out used to whisper about Kooroomba Lavender Farm — a six‑hectare vineyard in the Fassifern Valley at Mount Alford with an award‑winning cellar door and restaurant on the farm. We remember the haze of purple and the idea that somewhere nearby they were definitely serving something artisanal.

Petty local gripe: yes, we take two turns and a sliding gate to get our weekend therapy. If you hate paying a few dollars for park access, or you think “farm cellar door” sounds suspiciously like “bring a rug,” you’ll dislike our pace.

Who would hate living here: anyone who demands a coffee shop on every corner and a direct freeway on‑ramp. Who would love it: people who prefer a lavender-scented drive, secret gorges, and directions that feel like a treasure map. We’ll keep the gate money and the purple fields — you bring sensible shoes.
