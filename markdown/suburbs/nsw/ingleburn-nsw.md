# Ingleburn (NSW)

Slug: `ingleburn-nsw`

Back in the day we’d wander down Macquarie Road and argue about whether a burger could actually be a cultural experience. Suzie Dukes — the little burger joint at Shop 12, 100 Macquarie Road — made that argument for us with hand‑crafted burgers, house‑made milk buns and the ever-returning Slide Indomie special that made Time Out and Urban List give us nods like we’d suddenly become a food precinct.

Weekends used to look like this: kids off to Play Strong at Hallinan Park (61 Cumberland Rd) on Friday mornings, parents swapping tips while the PlayVan did its thing, while over on Oxford Road the town would turn into Ingleburn Alive — a street festival where the rides, stalls and live music made the whole main drag feel like a community fairground for the grown-ups who still like kettle corn.

We’ve always been that oddball suburb that can host a family play centre and a proper pub within walking distance. The Ingleburn RSL’s Tabatinga (upstairs, they’ll tell you) was the safe, air‑conditioned fortress of chaos for toddlers and mums; meanwhile the Hotel Ingleburn at 14 Ingleburn Rd has been reliably doing pub things — pool table, pokies, TAB and the attached Chinese diner — for the people who like their local to have a bit of rough and ready character.

On more industrial notes, we’re not just festivals and family mornings: the Volgren facility in Ingleburn did the fit‑out work for those new electric buses heading to the Western Sydney Airport routes, which is our way of saying we make stuff happen here too.

Who would hate it: anyone who needs a permanent string quartet, Michelin‑star interiors or a 24/7 boutique scene. We don’t pretend to be anywhere else. Who would love it: families who value playgroups and practical play centres, festival lovers who rocked up for Ingleburn Alive, riders and country escapees who use the Sydney Trail Riding Centre as their gateway, and anyone who secretly enjoys a pub with a diner attached. We’re small, we’re sincere, and we’re quietly proud — even if our best moments are the ones the city press filed under “quirky local.”
