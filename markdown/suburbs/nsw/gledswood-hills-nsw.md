# Gledswood Hills (NSW)

Slug: `gledswood-hills-nsw`

We know the trick: if the traffic on Gregory Hills Drive is crawling, that means it's Saturday and someone's gone looking for cannoli. Back in the day Urban List pointed out Mezzapica Cakes opened a second spot in Gledswood Hills — and yes, we have opinions about dessert priorities. We know where folks used to try to out-eat each other on pastry runs and which car parks filled up first.

Our weekend routine used to look simple: a slow drive along Gregory Hills Drive, a debate about whether to swing past Shop 12/1 Gregory Hills Drive (where Urban List once listed Mira La Vida Mexicana), and then an escape to the sprawling paddocks just beyond the suburb. Time Out even reminds tourists that Gledswood Homestead and Winery sits just outside the official boundary line of Campbelltown City Council — the kind of sobering reminder that some of our best views are politely outside our postcode.

Sitchu has a habit of filing us under the suburb profiles and lifestyle round-ups, which is flattering and vaguely formal — like being invited to a wedding we didn’t RSVP to but will gladly crash if there's cake.

Who would hate living here? If you need constant late-night neon, rooftop cocktail bars on every corner, or a CBD tramline to your doorstep, Gledswood Hills will test your patience. And if you're allergic to buffered boundaries — the "just-outside" winery, the second shops that opened and quietly settled in — you’ll mutter about inconvenience while we enjoy the quieter chaos.

Who would love it here? People who like their suburbs with a wink — the ones who remember Mezzapica’s Leichhardt roots and appreciated that second Gledswood Hills location, the ones who find comfort in a nearby winery being a short, not-immediately-urban drive away, and anyone who treats Sitchu’s suburb profiles like travel inspiration rather than a manifesto.

We’re not pretending to be a polished magazine spread. We’re the postcode that quietly shows up on lists, keeps its bakery receipts, and knows where the best excuses for a long drive begin.
