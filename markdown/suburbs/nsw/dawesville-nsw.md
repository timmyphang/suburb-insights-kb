# Dawesville (NSW)

Slug: `dawesville-nsw`

We know Dawesville like the back of our sunburnt hands — that long glide along the Dawesville Channel, the estuary breathing in and out (that’s the Peel-Harvey Estuarine System, for anyone who thinks water is just water). We wave at passing boats like they're old mates because, honestly, they've probably borrowed our jetty at some stage.

Morning routine: we’ll stroll the foreshore where Play by the Bay planted a shiny new waterside playground and a basketball court (that opening on 26 July 2025 was basically our town’s version of New Year’s Eve). If it's not a kid’s birthday party soundtrack ruining our zen, it’s the thud of a rogue three-pointer. Later, we might point out the channel to visitors who checked into Marina View Chalets in Wannanup and are very excited to discover we were steps away all along — “one minute’s drive to the Peel‑Harvey,” the listing promises, like it’s the final piece in their holiday puzzle.

We’re not drama-free, but the local soap is more “community calendar” than any proper feud. The Dawesville Community Centre opening on 31 August 2025 gave us music, multicultural dances, workshops, a gaming station and a playgroup — in other words, enough content for six Facebook arguments and one sincere thank-you card. The centre keeps ticking, with Wiggle It Fitness running an eight‑week class for those who prefer cardio to gossip. There’s even a “Cuppa with a Cop” in November if you want to discuss crime prevention over a polite brew — council outreach, but make it community theatre.

Who would hate it here? People who like permanent city noise, anonymous high-rises and restaurants you can never afford. Also anyone who thinks sand is for beaches and not for getting in their shoes at all hours.

Who would love it here? Families hunting for a waterside weekend that’s still yours after lunchtime. People who crave a bit of small-town choreography — playground ribbon cutting on Saturday, multicultural dance on Sunday, a fitness class on Monday. And those guests who booked Marina View Chalets thinking they’d found a secret; we’ll nod and say, “Welcome — we’ve known about the channel all along.” Time Out even put us in the top ten hidden gems for 2026, so go on, come see what the fuss is about. We’ll be the ones in boardies with a basketball under one arm.
