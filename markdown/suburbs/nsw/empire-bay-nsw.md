# Empire Bay (NSW)

Slug: `empire-bay-nsw`

Back in the day we liked to tell visitors Empire Bay was the kind of place where the pelicans get stage directions. As Time Out politely observed, “the light‑filled dining room looks out onto the calm waters of Empire Bay, and has floor‑to‑ceiling windows so you can see pelicans zoom in and out.” We can confirm: the birds are dramatic, punctual and utterly unimpressed by your camera.

Our morning looks the same no matter the season. We wander past the Progress Hall on 1–3 Gordon Road (you can’t miss the noticeboard) and nip into Shop 2, 2B Kendall Road where Salty Art by Mia used to hang watercolours and sign up for the Central Coast ‘Creative Journey’ artists trail. Then there’s the school run — for years the Empire Bay Public School page on FindGlocal kept everyone in the loop (Term 2, Week 9 — 12/06/2026, if you’re checking receipts), and the P&C even had an AGM listed for 25th November 2025. We clap politely at multicultural public speaking winners and go home feeling civic.

Local politics? Oh please — our real drama is childcare. CareforKids once listed 18 childcare services in postcode 2257 (the market’s competitive: 10 of 18 had vacancies, average cost $134.69/day). Community Kids at 433 Wards Hill Rd showed a gleaming 5.0 rating and fees that made our wallets sigh ($158.56/day). Meanwhile Bay Care at the school stubbornly read ‘No Vacancies’ like a Victorian memorial plaque. One minute you live here for the peace, the next you’re refreshing vacancy pages like it’s Melbourne Cup betting.

Who would hate living here: people allergic to pelicans, P&C newsletters and local progress associations. If you need a soundtrack of anonymous late‑night city hustle, this isn’t your postcode.

Who’d love it: families who actually read school notices, creatives who sign up for artists’ trails, and anyone who likes their waterfront views with a side of community. We roast because we love — and if you’re brave enough to join a P&C meeting, there’s always a pelican in the front row.
