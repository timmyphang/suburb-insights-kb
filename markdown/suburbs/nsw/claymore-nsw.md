# Claymore (NSW)

Slug: `claymore-nsw`

We stand on the borderline of Claymore, NSW, and grin whenever someone Googles us and finds half of Singapore and the Clare Valley pretending to be us. Back in the day the internet liked our name so much it stuck it on champagne bars, serviced residences and a winery with a leather jacket.

Morning here is short and unapologetic — we don’t pretend to be Orchard Road. Still, it’s funny to think “Claymore” also shows up at 1 Claymore Drive (Bhoomi) and 10 Claymore Road (Mosella) in Singapore, where menus promise fine dining and Champagne Buffet Brunches at places like Florette and Mosella. Those were listed in older write-ups; they used to be the glossy, photographed version of our name while we did the heavy lifting of postcode reality.

Time Out once had a go at a different Claymore altogether — Claymore Wines in the Clare Valley — and bless them, they leaned into it: a rock ’n’ roll tasting room with shiraz called Dark Side of the Moon, Voodoo Child chardonnay and Whole Lotta Love rosé. We like that image. If our suburb had a wine called Whole Lotta Love, maybe the council meetings would be shorter.

The web also used our name for a serviced-apartment listing — 8 on Claymore — promising pools, bikes and fitness centres. That felt aspirational in a charmingly antiseptic way, like someone put our postcode in a brochure and hoped for the best.

Who would hate living here? People who move in expecting boutique champagnes on tap, duck-fat pavé and rooftop terraces. They’ll be disappointed when the most glamorous thing is a nostalgic shout-out from a travel site or a cheeky wine label named after a Scottish sword.

Who would love it? People who enjoy being the underrated punchline: the suburb whose name gets invited to fancy parties elsewhere while we keep things honest. We’re the Claymore you can rely on — unflashy, world-famous in name only, and quietly proud of it.
