# Lalor (NSW)

Slug: `lalor-nsw`

Back in the day we’d start the weekend with a very Lalor ritual: shepherding the kids through the locked gate at the back of Lalor Neighbourhood House on 47A French Street (Buongiorno Bimbi—yes, the Playgroup listing tells you the code), then strolling past May Road to the Lalor Library, which Time Out used to note was our little civic pride in the City of Whittlesea — 37 public access computers, storytimes, teen nights, a seniors group and multiple-language collections. It felt like a small-town library that’d read your overdue fines out loud, with love.

All the coverage we could find about us is from the archives, so consider this more fond roast than a current events briefing. Time Out even put Lalor in the top ten most affordable and liveable suburbs in 2024, which is the nicest thing anyone’s said about our postcode since the Sitchu listing crowed about 3-48 Richards Street being “in the heart of Lalor” (two bedrooms, built-ins, central bathroom — very tidy on the brochure).

If you’re the sort of person who needs to pronounce every suburb like it’s a French vineyard, fair warning: Time Out once published a beginners’ guide to saying Melbourne place names and Lalor made the cut. We like to think we add a certain consonant-heavy gravitas to conversations.

Local drama? We don’t have a celebrity feud on loop — our biggest squabble is whether the playgroup should be indoors more often, which the Lalor Library Conference Room listing explicitly said would be welcome in winter. Riveting. Petty, but heartfelt.

Who would hate living here: anyone who despises locked gates, community noticeboards and the mild defeat of never being the trendiest dot on the map. Who would love it: families who actually use neighbourhood houses, people who need affordability without losing a library that runs teen nights, and anyone who knows the pleasure of a postcode that remembers you.

We’re not flashy. We’re not loud. We’re Lalor: practical, slightly eccentric, and surprisingly good at organising playgroup codes.
