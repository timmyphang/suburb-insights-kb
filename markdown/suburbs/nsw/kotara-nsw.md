# Kotara (NSW)

Slug: `kotara-nsw`

Back in the day — and by that we mean everything the internet still remembers about us — Kotara was the place you went when you wanted a weekend that oscillated between “family-friendly” and “let’s pretend we’re on a mall-holiday.”

We know the ritual: drop the kids at St James playgroup (Friday, 9am, St James Primary School Hall, 30 Vista Parade, Kotara South), or roll up to Kotara Park on Park Avenue (opened June 2022) and let them conquer the fort, double slide and rope wall while we pretend the picnic rug is a latte break. If the playground’s too small a drama for you, EMF jnr used to be the indoor play alternative — Shop 1067/8 in Westfield Kotara, corner of Park Avenue and Northcott Drive — where grip socks were currency and parents practiced the ancient art of scrolling.

Our high-stakes nightlife once included a Rockpool Dining Group experiment: Beerhaüs at Westfield Kotara — yes, the old Rockpool Beerhaüs rollout that Broadsheet wrote about — so if you’ve ever toasted a boilermaker in a mall food precinct, we invented that vibe for a minute. We even attracted pop-ups: Brooki Bakehouse set up shop on level two of Westfield Kotara opposite Country Road near Mecca for a long weekend. Big-city glamour, local carpark.

Petty local drama? We have it in wholesome form: Kotara High School made national noise trying to get KISS to visit — “goofy, dumb and incredibly sincere,” as Junkee put it. It’s our kind of flex.

Who would hate living here? If you loathe shopping centres, fenced playgrounds between Domayne and The Golden Goat at Home Co. Kotara, or the gentle hum of “is there rooftop dining tonight?” you’ll be miserable. If stadium proximity (Newcastle Entertainment Centre, McDonald Jones Stadium) feels too suburban for you, move to the city.

Who’d love it? Young families, playgroup volunteers (Playgroup NSW’s June 2025 newsletter even spotlighted our community model), bargain hunters and anyone who enjoys reliable retail, a decent playground and the pride of a viral high-school plea. Kotara: not trying to be edgy, just quietly very good at being useful.
