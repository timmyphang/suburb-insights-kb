# Reynella (NSW)

Slug: `reynella-nsw`

We wake up on Old South Rd like it’s a rerun — the same stretch that used to shepherd volunteers to the Reynella Horse Changing Station c.1854 and Alexander Cottage, where the Op Shop did its bit to keep the bricks from sulking. Back in the day our little main drag was less about trend and more about trades: heritage, hall hire and committee meetings.

If you get nostalgic for community paperwork, we used to pop into the Reynella Neighbourhood Centre at 164–170 Old South Rd. It ran programs and let you hire the Busybees room Monday–Thursday 9–4 — precisely the sort of thrilling schedule that builds character. For the tiny tyrants among you, Reynella Community Children's Centre at 65 Concord Dr catered to 6‑week‑olds through pre‑school hours (6:30am–6:30pm back then) and accepted Child Care Benefit; revolutionary, if you’re into tiny humans being logistically sorted.

Sporting types were, and always will be, catered for by the Reynella Sports and Social Club on Oval Rd — football, cricket, bowls, tennis and a function room for all the committee ends-of-year that felt like small reunions with better sandwiches.

Who would hate it here? People who want a neon nightlife, artisanal everything or a postcode that screams “influencer content.” Who would love it? Volunteers who like heritage projects, parents who prize proper childcare hours, and anyone who values a sports club that still smells faintly of victory and club meat pies.

Full disclosure: our sources are older listings — so think of this as a fond program from the archive rather than a current events guide.
