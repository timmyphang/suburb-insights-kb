# Cordeaux Heights (NSW)

Slug: `cordeaux-heights-nsw`

Back in the day — and by “the day” we mean whatever era those directory pages were last refreshed — we used to do our local research the old-fashioned way: refreshing MumsPages until our thumbs cramped. As residents of Cordeaux Heights, we know the suburb by its classifieds: MumsPages had whole sections for “Businesses and Service Providers for Mums”, “Storage & Removals” and “Health and Wellbeing,” which is bureaucratic shorthand for “everything you need when the bub has the flu and you need a van.”

If you ever wondered who shifts our flatpacks and moral stamina, 777 World Removals used to brag about over 10 years of furniture-moving experience across Wollongong — the sort of name you called when the pram finally outgrew the house and you needed someone to lift more than just your mood.

The childcare scene used to be the local sport. CareforKids listed 18 childcare services in Cordeaux Heights, and the stats were the weekly soap: 10 of the 18 had vacancies, which meant some relief for panicked parents, while the suburb average fee sat at $137.69/day — a number we’d mutter at playgroup while pretending it wasn’t our entire entertainment budget. There were four preschools/ kindergartens recorded; three of the four had vacancies and the NQS spread was “Meeting” overall, with 31% Exceeding and 69% Meeting. Practically academic.

And then there was Kids & Hugs Family Day Care — the listing showed no vacancies, a cosy 4.8 rating from one review, fees of $100/day and an NQS rating of Meeting. Which is to say: if you ever scored a spot there, you were basically the suburb’s VIP.

Who’d hate living here? People who like spontaneous weekend plans that don’t involve negotiating drop-off swaps or decoding childcare fee schedules. Who’d love it? Anyone who values a neighbourhood that reads like a directory of practical lifesavers: movers, day care options and a whole suite of “we’ve got you” listings.

We’ll admit: our local identity might be built on directories rather than boutiques, but at least we know where to find the help when the cot collapses and the van is already booked.
