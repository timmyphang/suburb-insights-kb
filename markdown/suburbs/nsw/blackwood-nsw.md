# Blackwood (NSW)

Slug: `blackwood-nsw`

Back in the day we’d weave our way down Surf Lane and stand in a line that somehow felt like a badge of honour — Blackwood Pantry was the sort of laneway gem that made the Shire feel tiny and very, very culinary. The place — dreamed up by Rob Lechowicz, Daniel Sorridimi and Paul Pisani — had that Luchetti Krelle light-and-clean vibe, Turkish Poached Eggs rubbing shoulders with blueberry and ricotta hotcakes and truffle chilli scrambled eggs on a menu that made brunch feel like a minor religion.

Then the brand did what good local celebrities do: it got a Bondi ego. Blackwood Bondi opened in 2022 on the sun‑kissed corner of the Seven Ways hub on Glenayr Avenue, bringing Friday and Saturday night aperitivo and the Sunday live‑music program we watched from afar and pretended we’d always meant to try. Time Out and Urban List followed us around like proud parents — yes, we saw the write‑ups — and the truffle toasties even made the city guides (for a bit, the triple‑cheese-and-truffle toastie was apparently a seasonal calling card).

We’re not mad. Well, not very. We’re proud that the Cronulla original spawned siblings and side projects — Dan Sorridimi even teamed up with Joel Bennetts for Sana in Eveleigh — but don’t expect us to forget those Surf Lane mornings where the efficient team would get you seated after what felt like ten minutes of queueing and smugness.

Who would hate living in our orbit? People who can’t deal with queues, with weekend aperitivo culture, or with a Sunday that smells faintly of burnt Basque cheesecake and smug live acoustic sets. If you prefer your suburbs quiet and unbranded, this isn’t your postcode.

Who would love it? Folks who appreciate design that isn’t trying too hard (thanks, Luchetti Krelle), menus that flirt with truffle and ricotta in equal measure, and the comfy arrogance of a café that started in the Shire and decided the Eastern Suburbs could use its particular brand of confidence. We loved it then; for years the local guides loved it too.
