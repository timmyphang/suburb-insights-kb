# Hughesdale (NSW)

Slug: `hughesdale-nsw`

Back in the day, a weekend in Hughesdale felt like a slow-motion montage of pastry crumbs, brunch orders and real-estate clichés — and yes, our file folder proves it. We’d saunter off Dandenong Road (where the Sitchu listing boasted an “away from the hustle and bustle” balcony life at unit 206, 1344 Dandenong Road) and let the suburb do its low-key thing.

We used to queue for small pleasures. Temperance Society — remembered in the Urban List — served brûlée yoghurt, black‑tea marbled eggs and orange blossom hotcakes, and even the “smashed herbed peas” made us feel slightly virtuous. Daniel Son was a sure-old south-eastern brunch stop: run by Trish and Todd Pryse (yes, Trish once ran Brew in Hughesdale and Mr Burch in McKinnon), it gave us a Karate Kid flashback on the menu and a reliable excuse to be nostalgic for the ’80s.

Our sweeter sin was Knafeh Nabulseyeh: the family who built knafeh machines opened in Hughesdale in 2015 and stayed until they relocated to Coburg in 2023 — so we can say with pride we had the crisp-cheesy Palestinian pastry scene on our block for a while. Even the food round-ups remembered us: a dumpling place at 68 Poath Road made Sitchu’s steamy list, which, to our shock, is the sort of acclaim we’ll whisper about at dinner parties.

Petty local drama? The clippings are oddly civil — more brunch listings than council feuds — so instead of scandal we’ll roast the suburb’s modest ambitions. If you want a permanent feed of buzzy reopenings and Met Gala-level spectacle, Hughesdale will quietly disappoint you (the archives are mostly older pieces, so consider this a nostalgia roast rather than a neighbourhood guide).

Who would hate it: people allergic to comfortable modesty, or anyone hunting for neon nightlife and daily cultural detonations. Who would love it: people who prize a dependable hotcake, a legendary knafeh that once lived on our street, and apartments that promise “light & bright” away from the Dandenong Road rush. We’re not flashy. We’re quietly full of treats — and we’ll happily tell you so with a wink.
