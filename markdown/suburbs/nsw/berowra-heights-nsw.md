# Berowra Heights (NSW)

Slug: `berowra-heights-nsw`

Back in the day we’d say good morning to Berowra Heights like it was a slightly embarrassed aunt at a school concert — polite, well‑turned‑out and quietly proud. A Saturday used to kick off with the Berowra Community Markets at the Berowra Uniting Church on Alan Road: first Saturday of the month, 9–2, local stalls, a bit of trash-and-treasure and a lot of small‑town elbow rubbing. By the time Monday rolled round, Berowra Heights Tiny Tots were relaunching their playgroup at 4/6 Alan Road, meeting at 10am for whoever had babies and the courage to mingle.

We know every small-business footprint in town because the directories used to tell us. Sam Mooney had her hair and makeup set-up in Berowra Marketplace — Shop 12/19‑23 Turner Road, where the old IGA used to be — offering mobile wedding glam for those who preferred to get made up without leaving town. The Spa Rooms used to promise full‑on indulgence on Berowra Waters Road (yes, there was even a phone number to ring: (02) 9456 0801). If you wanted to round off your local CV with a new hobby, MumsPages listed everything from painters to GKR Karate on Wideview Road — they were keen on free first lessons and the old chestnut of half‑price joining fees plus a free uniform.

If anyone’s going to roast us, it’s because we’re small, sensible and a touch earnest. North Shore Mums even flagged our postcode, 2082, as having the fewest serious offences in 2022 — just 24. We’ll wear that stat like a cardigan: safe, reliable, slightly itchy.

Who would hate living here? People who measure life in skyline views, all‑night bars and the thrill of being slightly anonymous. If you crave anonymous chaos, live somewhere with a tram line.

Who would love it here? Families who like playgroups and monthly markets, people who want a local spa or a familiar hair and makeup hand for their wedding, and anyone who enjoys being part of a tidy, quietly proud community. We’re not flashy. We’re the suburb that remembers your kid’s name and the date of the next market — and frankly, we’re very comfortable with that.
