# Upper Swan (NSW)

Slug: `upper-swan-nsw`

We love a weekend where the plan is basically “watch a river and pretend we’re rugged.” As The Urban List noted, Bells Rapids River Walk (Orlov Trail) on Cathedral Ave, Upper Swan WA 6069 used to be the reliably scenic 2.6km option for winter and spring — and yes, it was the unofficial front-row seat for the Avon Descent. We’ll never apologise for knowing the best rock to sit on and the precise moment a kayak looks dangerously optimistic.

Back in the day, our mornings were a civilised shuffle: fuelling up at the Swan Valley breakfast spots The Urban List used to round up, then wobbling between cellar doors. The same roundup name‑checked Taylors In The Valley, Mrs Rabbit Tearooms and Lavender Bistro for breakfast, and later pointed toward Sandalford and Oakover Grounds when we needed an afternoon existential crisis over which wine pairs with post‑walk soreness. The Urban List even mentioned Upper Reach Winery Spa Cottage (Baskerville) in its Swan Valley stays list — so yes, we’ve historically treated self‑care like an organised sport.

For the parents among us, the online directories have long been a comfort blanket. MumsPages keeps tidy lists for health and wellbeing, swimming lessons and psychologists serving Upper Swan, and the Child and Parent Centre – Swan — managed by the Shire of Mundaring in partnership with Middle Swan Primary School — has been the sort of local project that quietly does the heavy lifting for families.

Who would hate living here? If you need non‑stop nightlife, a tram every five minutes or coffee culture that comes with a cultural identity exam, you’ll be bored stiff. If your idea of a good Saturday is a rooftop in the CBD, Upper Swan will feel like we’re asking you to sit in a field and listen to the river gossip.

Who would love it? Families who like practical services and community programs, hikers who treat Bell’s Rapids as a weekly ritual, and anyone who prefers wine country walks to Instagram queues. We know what our postcode is for: good wine, river views and people who remember how to slow down.
