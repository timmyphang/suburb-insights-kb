# Mundaring (NSW)

Slug: `mundaring-nsw`

We start our weekends the only sensible way: by chucking a rug on the grass at Mundaring Weir and pretending we discovered this engineering flex ourselves. Back in the day the guidebooks told you to do exactly that — Sitchu still talks up the weir and its picnic-ready parkland — and who are we to argue with a good rug moment?

If we’re feeling virtuous (or annoyingly energetic) we’ll hop on the Munda Biddi. As Time Out reminded us, that 1,067-kilometre off‑road trail literally kicks off from our leafy village, which means we get the smug bragging rights without having to apply for leave. Prefer two feet to two wheels? The Kep Track follows old rail formations from Mundaring to Northam — Urban List even points out you can horse-ride bits of it or do the full 75km if you’re trying to win a midlife crisis.

We’re also quietly proud of our culinary lineage, even if the headlines are from “back in the day”. The Loose Box — Alain Fabregues’s legendary Mundaring restaurant — turns up in every chef CV worth reading: Paul Bentley did his apprenticeship there; Peter Manifis sharpened his seafood chops there; Gavin Chater learned pastry tricks there. Broadsheet’s obituary for Emmanuel Mollois even notes he was head pastry chef at The Loose Box for two years. Fancy? Maybe. Endearing? Absolutely.

Local nightlife is refreshingly uncomplicated. The Mundaring Weir Hotel, built in 1898, still has that old-hat pub charm and one of the largest beer gardens in Perth, though Time Out warns it doesn’t open for dinner most nights (only Saturdays). So if you need a late-night bistro needle-drop and tapas at 10pm, Mundaring will disappoint you — and that’s part of the charm.

Who’d hate it here? People who want their pubs open for dinner every night, or anyone allergic to trees, long bike trails or pubs with historic plumbing. Who’d love it? Hikers, cyclists, history buffs, pastry obsessives who nerd out on The Loose Box lineage, and families who fancy the Mundaring Weir to South Ledge stroll for an easy Sunday win. We’ll keep the rug warm.
