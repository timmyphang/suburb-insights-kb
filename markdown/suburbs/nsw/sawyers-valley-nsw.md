# Sawyers Valley (NSW)

Slug: `sawyers-valley-nsw`

We wake up in postcode 6074 like it’s a state of mind — a 170 km² shrug of Perth Hills that still answers to Sawyers Valley. Back in the day our best publicity lived in directories: MumsPages listing local services for parents, and the Mundaring Community Directory dutifully cataloguing doctors, hospitals and dentists for a place with a population of 954. That’s us: gloriously small, annoyingly specific.

Our morning routine is humble. We used to check the Mundaring Community Directory for who’s running the sports and recreation groups, then see if MumsPages had any new reviews for kids’ activities. And if someone needed to get to the airport or the hospital on short notice, Perth Hills Taxi WA — which used to promise 24/7 pickups and airport transfers across Mundaring, Mount Helena, Chidlow and Sawyers Valley — was the telegram of reassurance.

There’s no local scandal to gossip over in these snippets — only paperwork and practicalities — so the “petty drama” is more about logistics than betrayal. We’re a spread-out clutch of residents who read community directories like others read novels.

Who would hate living here? Anyone allergic to small-population life: the kind who wants a mall on every corner will find 954 people and 170 km² a bit too intimate. Who would love it? Mums and families who value local listings, anyone who likes room to breathe in the Perth Hills, and those who appreciated that we once had neat, dependable services catalogued for the region. Sawyers Valley: polite, practical, and catalogued.
