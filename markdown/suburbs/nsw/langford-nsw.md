# Langford (NSW)

Slug: `langford-nsw`

We’ll be honest — living in Langford sometimes feels like being the understudy for a much better-known name. We’re the Langford people who get pinged by Google for everything from an Indonesian kopitiam to a pocket island, and we’ve learned to lean into the confusion.

Morning routine: we roll out of bed, make a cuppa and watch the search results roll in. As Broadsheet noted, Ah Beng Kopitiam first opened in Langford in 2017 before relocating to Westfield Carousel — so somewhere out there a very good Indonesian diner once called Langford home. Meanwhile, online lists keep reminding us that “Langford” crops up all over the place: Morris House sits at 124 Langford Street in North Melbourne (yes, really), and Concrete Playground will happily tell you there’s a pocket-sized Langford Island near Hayman Island if you’re into underwater gardens and long sandy spits.

Petty local drama: there isn’t much — because most of our drama happens off-page and gets drowned out by celebrity searches. Katherine Langford keeps turning up in TV roundups and entertainment write-ups, Josephine Langford gets castings tracked, and a Lloyd Langford will pop up on comedy festival line-ups. We like to imagine they all owe us a coffee for sharing the surname.

Who would hate living here: anyone allergic to being the lesser-known namesake. Hate having your suburb mistaken for a North Melbourne address, annoyed by island envy, or tired of explaining you’re not the Langford on a celebrity wiki? This isn’t your postcode.

Who would love it here: if you’re the sort who enjoys a low-key life threaded through a web of charmingly random internet breadcrumbs — parent groups, toddler meet-ups (Netmums and a Churchill & Langford Mother & Toddler Group do tend to pop up in listings), festival line-ups and a surprisingly international name pedigree — you’ll fit right in. We’re small, unpretentious and oddly worldly thanks to all our lookalikes.

So yes, we know what they say about Langford. They also missed the bit where being the name everyone borrows comes with its own kind of local fame. We’ll keep the bench warm.
