# Oakleigh East (NSW)

Slug: `oakleigh-east-nsw`

We can still smell the jam doughnuts from Eaton Mall even though most of our press clippings are from “back in the day.” Broadsheet used to have a neat little directory page that name‑checked The Oakleigh Doughnut Co, Mythos GR, Orexi Souvlaki Bar, Kalimera Souvlaki Art, Vanilla Cakes and Lounge and Nikos Quality Cakes — which is to say: if it involved syrup, pastry or an argument about which roll was best, Oakleigh East had us covered.

Back when Time Out and Broadsheet wrote about it, Olympia was the fancy rooftop option above Oakleigh Market — whitewashed walls, olive trees on the carpark, the sort of place where we pretended to be sophisticated for an hour before real life called us back to Dandenong Road.

We don’t have any properly recent scoops — our file is mostly directories and childcare listings — so the real headline around here is that we’re very good at minding other people’s kids. MumsPages and CareforKids used to list Evoke Early Learning at 1692 Dandenong Road, Oakleigh Early Learning Journey at 1653–1655 Dandenong Road (they even had hours and a daily fee on record), and Guardian Childcare & Education Oakleigh East — which means there’s a professional-level ecosystem for stationary toddlers and their logistics.

Urban List’s old guide liked to remind people about Eaton Mall and the dining strip; it also once noted IGA Xpress Oakleigh East at 2 Macrina Street as a place stocking that viral Beyoglu Dubaco Dubai Chocolate bar — yes, even our local corner store tried to be content‑worthy.

Who would hate living here? People after non‑stop, buzzy nightlife or the newest boutique bar every other week. We’re not the inner‑city hype machine; we’re the comfortable precinct you discover after you decide your knees are done with stairs.

Who would love it? Families, finders of excellent cakes, anyone who values a reliable childcare waitlist and a Saturday morning doughnut pilgrimage. We have the shops and the old press to prove it — maybe not the freshest headlines, but all the essentials: good cake, a rooftop that once got a write‑up, and someone who knows where your kids are meant to be between 7:00 and 6:30. Oakleigh East — delightfully practical, quietly delicious, and content to let the rest of Melbourne have the trendiness.
