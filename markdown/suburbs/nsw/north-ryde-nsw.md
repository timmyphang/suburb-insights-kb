# North Ryde (NSW)

Slug: `north-ryde-nsw`

Back in the day our weekend started at the Macquarie Centre and felt like a minor pilgrimage. We’d nudge through the crowds, past the H&M and David Jones that cropped up in the $440‑million relaunch, queue for NeNe Chicken in the food court and whisper about who’d nabbed the last piece of Maggio’s pastries (the Macquarie outpost used to be a local flex at 8/144 Coxs Road). If we were feeling continental we’d reminisce about Choco Cannelle over on Ryde’s side — Jean‑François’ pastries felt like a cheeky passport.

Mornings could also mean a trot to North Ryde Park on 145 Cressy Road while the kids tested every swing and the barbecue survived another family sausage. For the sporty among us there was always North Ryde Soccer Club at North Ryde Oval (the corner of Magdala and Pittwater Roads) — where future semi‑pros and dads with very serious shin pads practiced under the same sky.

The big local drama we loved to clutch our pearls over? The Macquarie Ice Rink saga. AMP Capital’s plans to tear it down provoked a proper online petition and an interim heritage order — the rink, an Olympic‑sized relic and a training ground since 1980, survived the push to become yet another retail annex. We celebrated the hold‑up like it was a local grand final.

Who’d hate living here? If you worship inner‑city after‑dark chaos, or you loathe a suburb that knows how to do both a mall and a park, you’ll find us maddening. If your aesthetic is “noisy shopping precincts” then yes, the Macquarie Centre might give you the cold shoulder. And if you’re allergic to community groups, note the playgroups at Cutler Parade and the church‑run events down at North Ryde Anglican Church on 152 Coxs Road — there’s a lot of organised cheer.

Who’d love it here? Families, uni folk and anyone who values a saved ice rink, decent pastry, a proper shopping precinct and a local soccer club. We’re leafy, practical and a bit mall‑obsessed — and we wouldn’t have it any other way.
