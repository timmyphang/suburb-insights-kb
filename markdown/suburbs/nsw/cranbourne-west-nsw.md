# Cranbourne West (NSW)

Slug: `cranbourne-west-nsw`

Back in the day we used to complain Cranbourne West was all future subdivisions and a lot of road signs. Then Upparel turned up and gave us something gloriously industrial to brag about in the school pickup line.

We know the way the light hits that 10,500-square-metre shed — not exactly heritage, but impressively unpretentious. Broadsheet wrote that Upparel’s onshore textile recycling facility in Cranbourne West can process more than 10,000 tonnes a year, with a fully automated recycling line and a new obsession with traceability and transparency. Suddenly our suburb went from “where’s the nearest anything?” to “oh yeah, we recycle fashion here.”

We don’t have any recent local scoops to crow about — most of the coverage we’ve got is from before now — so for years the local playgroups were the real civic centre. Playgroup Victoria used to list sessions at Merinda Park Learning and Community Centre (it even had its own car park next to St. Therese’s Cranbourne Primary) and the Community Hub at Cranbourne Primary School made getting toddlers out the door feel like a public service.

Who would hate living here? If you’re allergic to factories, logistics, or the faint whirr of automation during school pickup, Cranbourne West will test your zen.

Who’d love it? Families who appreciated local playgroups and anyone quietly thrilled by circular-economy flexes — there’s a certain suburban pride in knowing our most photogenic building is a recycling plant. We’ll take it.
