# Oak Flats (NSW)

Slug: `oak-flats-nsw`

Back in the day we’d pick the kids up from school and head straight to Oak Flats Library for Crafternoon — yes, that delightful mess of glue and glitter. As sydneymumsgroup noted, it was a free after‑school ritual to “craft, color, paste, bedazzle, invent, and create.” We loved it because no one judged your glue gun technique and the borrowing fines felt like a distant threat.

There aren’t any recent write‑ups about Oak Flats in our pile, so we’ll stay nostalgic: imagine a place where sticker sheets are currency and the best local gossip happens between the nonfiction and picture-book shelves. No local council kerfuffle here, just scissors and sequins.

Who would hate it? People who can’t handle glitter, or anyone who expects silence in a library — and that’s a vote of no confidence in small scissors. Who’d love it? Families, crafty types, and anyone who considers bedazzling a life skill.

We’ll keep watching for fresh stories, but until then we’ll remember Oak Flats Library’s Crafternoon as the suburb’s quiet little coronation of glue sticks.
