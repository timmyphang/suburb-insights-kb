# Blue Haven (NSW)

Slug: `blue-haven-nsw`

We’re the Blue Haven that nobody’s quite sure exists — or at least that’s what the internet wants us to be. Back in the day, the travel lists were busy either sending you to Lucky Bay and Hellfire Bay on the Esperance coast (yes, Urban List lumped Blue Haven in with Twilight Bay’s turquoise flex) or recommending a pet-friendly stop called Blue Haven on a Perth–Esperance road trip. Concrete Playground even tucked a Blue Haven into a roundup of dog-friendly stays, next to Ocean Blue Coastal Retreats in McCrae. So congratulations: our name is famous, just not necessarily our postcode.

Our weekend routine, as far as the web is concerned, involves either posing for roo photos in Esperance or providing fenced outdoor areas for puppy play in McCrae. Meanwhile, down here in NSW, we’re the quiet namesake—the one that gets mistaken for Eleven Mile Beach, Salmon Beach or Ten Mile Lagoon whenever someone drafts a “best beaches” list.

Petty local drama? The only scandal is being misbooked on a road trip itinerary. We don’t have the luxury of curated coastal hype; we have identity theft by travel editors. Who would hate living here: name-droppers and influencers who need a caption that screams “Hellfire Bay” but settle for “Blue Haven.” Who would love it: dog owners and people who prefer their coastal life without being photobombed by a tourism article.

So yes, we know what they say about us. They just forgot to check which state we’re in.
