# Innaloo (NSW)

Slug: `innaloo-nsw`

Back in the day we’d time our Saturday so the smalls could burn off sugar at Millet Park — everyone calls it ‘Spiderman Park’ for that ridiculous climbing web — then limp over to Westfield Innaloo for Santa photos and whatever impulse purchase a mall will convince you your child needs. We know where the best playgrounds are, which early‑learning centres have vacancies (Little Light Preschool, Barnes Street, Climb Early Learning all show up on the lists) and where to stash a birthday cake while you sprint to the car.

Weekends mean curry options that make ordering online feel like a betrayal. Punjab, Dhaba and Anghiti all got shoutouts for doing proper Indian food in town — Punjab even sat opposite Event Cinemas on Scarborough Beach Road, a fact we repeat like a proud secret. Cinephiles among us still reminisce about first‑date stories at the Greater Union, and yes, we’ve never let Event Cinemas live down that Anzac Day snafu when a Hereditary trailer played before Peter Rabbit — Junkee had a field day and so did the parents.

Petty drama? Our highlight reel includes the slow-motion disappointment of national chains folding into history: Sizzler’s final Aussie closures listed Innaloo in 2020, which felt both like an end of an era and an excuse for one last salad bar pilgrimage. And for a beat there were breathless announcements — Vitaland teased a new Innaloo kids‑cafe with VR play — which read like a town‑meeting manifesto for parents seeking novel ways to lose their voices.

Who would hate living here? If you loathe the sound of children learning to conquer a climbing web, the smell of curry drifting down Scarborough Beach Road, or the comforting inevitability of markets at the Innaloo Sportsmen’s Club, this place will grate. If you want an isolated, child‑free echo chamber, move along.

Who would love it? Families, cinema romantics, curry devotees and anyone who judges a suburb by how many decent playgrounds, childcare options (36 listed and counting) and reliably good local pubs you can find. We’ll keep the climbing ropes tied, the parking timers set, and the curry warm.
