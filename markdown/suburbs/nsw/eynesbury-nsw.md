# Eynesbury (NSW)

Slug: `eynesbury-nsw`

We know the short drive to the Homestead so well we could do it backwards with a blanket and a thermos. Weekends here start with kids in park gear and parents who’ve memorised the Eynesbury Recreation Reserve Playground pick-up routine — Playgroup Victoria even has the reserve and the Eynesbury Discovery Centre listed as go-to venues (Apr 29, 2026), so no, you’re not imagining the baby brigade.

By mid-morning the place smells faintly of festival food because, well, we throw festivals. As Time Out recorded, we’ve hosted the Eynesbury Summer Festival and even a four-day Kingdom Festival at Eynesbury Homestead for the littlies, plus the Eynesbury Winter Festival made a return for the 2024 school holidays. So if your idea of a quiet Sunday is absolute silence, try not moving here.

Local brag: one of our own kitchen stories made national headlines. Time Out noted a Victorian chef with Eynesbury Homestead on his CV — Gagan Bhatnagar — took out Australia’s best pizza title in 2022 and even cracked the global top 15. We don’t erect statues (yet), but we do nod appreciatively when someone orders pizza.

Pet owners get their shout-out courtesy of a 2020 Concrete Playground item that named Eynesbury among suburbs earmarked for new dog parks — funding was boosted then, so yes, we were on the list for extra pup playtime. So if you’ve got a dog that judges your life choices, there’ll likely be somewhere for it to do so publicly.

No local soap-opera feuds here to air — the public record is mostly wholesome: playgroups, homestead pageantry and a chef with a pizza trophy. That’s our drama. If that bores you, you’ll hate living here: people who despise children’s rides, community ice-skating and the occasional churro stand will find Eynesbury unbearably lively.

But if you’re the sort who wants a place that treats school holidays like a civic event, loves a playground that actually exists on Playgroup Victoria’s map, cheers a hometown chef’s pizza win and is happy to leash up for a future dog park — welcome home. We’ll save you a spot on the Homestead lawn.
