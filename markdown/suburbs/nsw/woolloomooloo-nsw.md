# Woolloomooloo (NSW)

Slug: `woolloomooloo-nsw`

We wake up with the harbour and a conscience — Woolloomooloo’s Finger Wharf at dawn is where we rehearse being cultured while scrolling for a table. The wharf has long been the sort of place Broadsheet and Time Out parade as harbourfront glamour — think Kingsleys, China Doll and OTTO — and yes, we know how that sounds when you live two streets back and still fight for a parking spot.

Our weekend ritual is embarrassingly simple: a sea breeze loop that takes in the wharf, a passing nod to the Ovolo (Urban List once named it a top city stay), and a solemn appreciation of the Old Fitz — Time Out called it “one of Sydney’s greatest pubs”, with that cosy theatre vibe that makes us feel alternately cultured and mildly hungover. The Woolly Bay Hotel’s 2022 $14m revamp still gets name-checked in local guides for its Med-inspired rooftop — it remade the old pub story into something sunnier.

Locals like to grumble about the gentrification Time Out flagged; we complain about heritage timber being rebranded as “luxe” while secretly booking a table at the newest thing. Which, this June, meant Akti — a … very pretty-in-pink Greek restaurant that opened on Finger Wharf in June 2025, bringing Aegean sunset energy to our harbour. Urban List and Time Out both made a polite fuss, and so did we, because we love a new menu to judge.

Who would hate living here? If you loathe harbour views, converted wharves, boutique hotels, theatre nights at the Old Fitz or the idea of a Greek place stealing your sunset, this isn’t your vibe. If you want anonymity and cheaper rents, you’ll find us mildly smug from our waterfront vantage.

Who’ll love it? Anyone who prefers ducking into a harbourfront restaurant, strolling to the Royal Botanic Gardens, or blinking at a Russell Crowe UFO tweet while sipping something expensive — Pedestrian TV ran that one, and we still chuckle. Woolloomooloo wears its history and its glow-ups the same way: a bit theatrical, a bit proud, and always worth the walk.
