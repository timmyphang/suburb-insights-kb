# Mount Martha (NSW)

Slug: `mount-martha-nsw`

We start our weekends the only way Mount Martha knows how — squinting at the two-kilometre sweep of sand, counting the rainbow-coloured bathing boxes and deciding whether today’s mood is “cliff-fringe promenade” or “attempt the Pillars and look heroic on Instagram.” Back in the day the guides were unanimous: our beach made Tourism Australia’s top-ten, the bathing boxes are iconic and the water is famously calm. We’re not arguing.

Morning ritual used to be simple: shuffle down to Milkbar & Co. opposite Mount Martha Beach for footwear-optional brunches, or pop into Cibo for chef-quality take-home lasagnes, Red Hill Bakery sourdough and Sicilian-made coffee before pretending we didn’t buy two loaves. If the sweet tooth wins, Mount Martha Fine Foods has offered the thick vanilla slices people will schlepp for. For years the travel pieces also pointed out our fancy holiday homes — Martha Beach House, Blanc de Noir, Deja View — so if you like heated pools and 270-degree views, we get it.

Yes, we had that awkward local chapter: Mount Martha Primary once made headlines for banning hugging and high-fives. It was a weird moment of paperwork meeting playground, and we said what everyone says in these parts — raise the eyebrows, then organise another bake sale.

Who would hate living here? If you require 24/7 city buzz, rooftop bars on every corner or zero chance of ever meeting another stroller, you’ll wither on the Esplanade. If scaling a few rocks to get to a secret swim spot is a dealbreaker, The Pillars’ “bring care, not cocktails” warning will ruin your day.

Who would love it here? Families who’ve made playgroup pilgrimages to Mount Martha House Community Centre and the Mt Martha Uniting Church green; dog-walkers, cliff-walkers and anyone who appreciates a village that doubles as a summer postcard. We’ll gladly be the place that tourists rave about and locals quietly defend — especially while we’re still hoarding the best vanilla slice.
