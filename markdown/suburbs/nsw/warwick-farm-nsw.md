# Warwick Farm (NSW)

Slug: `warwick-farm-nsw`

We like to start our Saturday the way Warwick Farm intended: half a mind on horses, half a mind on doughnuts. Back in the day the William Inglis Hotel put a rooftop pool and panoramic racecourse views on our skyline — 144 rooms, the Newmarket Room, the 1867 Lounge — and suddenly we had boutique five‑star nonsense with a side of hay. It’s gloriously extra for a suburb that can also do slow‑braised beef cheeks and charcoal grills.

Our morning loop used to be painfully simple: grab something from The Doughnut Mum on 1 Sappho Road (they opened here in October 2019 and the family‑run story made sense to us), then drive past the racecourse and imagine we’re improbably dressed for the paddock‑to‑plate life. If you’re nostalgic for a second‑city taco moment, remember when El Topo Cantina opened next to the El Toro Motor Inn at 6 Homepride Avenue — street tacos, roughly forty tequilas and the kind of themed cocktails that make a Saturday feel celebratory.

We don’t pretend everything is gloss. As Broadsheet reported, visa changes — the old 457 saga — were a proper headache for local kitchens; Madera Kafe’s owner Petar Tasic was quoted about the trouble securing specialist chef visas, which reminded us the grill smoke here has real people behind it. Madera Kafe itself has long been our go‑to for karadjordjeva and the large mixed charcoal grill that feeds more opinions than numbers on a race day.

Who would hate living here? If you loathe the smell of cooking meat, rooftops that show off horses, or any hint that a five‑star hotel might look down on your Saturday arvo kebab, you’ll be miserable. Also, if you demand a CBD menu for breakfast, we’re probably not your vibe.

Who would love it? Horse people, tequila dabblers, families who use the Warwick Farm playgroup at Warwick Farm Public School on Lawrence Hargrave Rd, and anyone who appreciates a Serbian mixed grill followed by a doughnut. We’re proud of our odd mix of racetrack luxury and proper local grub — and we’ll happily wave at you from the rooftop pool while you queue for a chorizo croquette.
