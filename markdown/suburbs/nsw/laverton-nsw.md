# Laverton (NSW)

Slug: `laverton-nsw`

We are the kind of locals who can tell you where to park for McCormack Park without using Google — and no, we won’t move the stroller from the wrong side of the path for you.

Back in the day the weekend went like this: drop the kids at St Martin de Porres or Laverton P-12 College, lob them at the Laverton Swim Centre for three frantic laps, then pretend we’re cultured while peeking into the Wood Street Arts Space — the converted post office on 44 Wood St that Time Out said had already played host to international artists. For years the area’s mix of schools, parks and community runabouts made it more useful than glamorous, and that’s fine by us.

Our history is a bit of a badge: Time Out reminds folks Laverton was once home to an RAAF base (now decommissioned), and if you like quirky local history the Altona Homestead — home of the Altona Laverton Historical Society — used to run tours and even Lantern Ghost Tours. Mountain Goat’s core range was produced at a much larger site in Laverton too, so yes, we’ve had more than one warehouse with a personality.

Petty drama? Oh we have our headline moments. Pedestrian.TV recalled that infamous clip — seats set alight on a train at Laverton station — and yes, the police had a job to do. We’ll argue about whose kids kicked whose goal, but we leave the national news stunts to strangers.

Who would hate living here: people who need a daily parade of boutique-this-or-that, or who can’t handle a town that’s equal parts practical and history-heavy. If you require your suburb to be a permanent brunch runway, Laverton will test your patience.

Who would love it: families who lean on playgroups and community care (shoutout to the PlayConnect listings and the Laverton Children’s Centre at 15 Crown St), artists who don’t mind a converted post office gallery, and anyone who appreciates a suburb that’s honest about its past and useful in the present. We’re not trying to be the city — we’re the place you actually live in, and we’re pretty proud of that.
