# Spring Farm (NSW)

Slug: `spring-farm-nsw`

Back in the day — and by that we mean the only stories we could dig up are older listings — Spring Farm felt like a sleepy little secret with a very specific calendar. We know the weekend routine because someone actually wrote it down: we’d pile the kids into the ute (metaphorically) and head to Spring Farm Shopping Centre when Farm Days rolled through — those petting zoo weekends listed for Fri 11 Oct–Sat 12 Oct were basically our annual civic festival. The photos were all goats and sticky fingers; the memory is pure suburbia.

Our weekday rhythm used to revolve around Community Links Wellbeing’s playgroups. The Spring Farm listings (including a Transitional Playgroup and a Transitional Supported Playgroup — there’s even a 28 Apr flyer) put families in touch with sessions run out of 6 Harper Close, Tahmoor NSW 2573 (phone 02 4683 2776). So yes, our claim to fame is organised baby wrangling and supervised craft glue.

If you like numbers, the local directory gives us a tidy identity: postcode 2570, area 6.36 km², LGA Camden Council and a population headline that reads 1,189. Tiny enough to still recognise someone by their dog, big enough to have its own shopping centre and occasional petting zoo. Mount Annan’s community page even lists Spring Farm among neighbours like Blairmount, Camden Park, Campbelltown, Currans Hill, Glen Alpine, Menangle Park, Narellan Vale and Smeaton Grange — proof we’re officially on the map, albeit the quieter corner.

Who would hate living here? People who need 24/7 metropolitan drama, rooftop bars on every corner or a constantly updating events feed — our sources are all older, so we’re more “family noticeboard” than “city nightlife.” We’re not relentless; we’re quietly domestic.

Who would love it? Families who appreciate organised playgroups, people who rate a petting zoo weekend as cultural enrichment, and anyone who likes a small postcode that still knows its neighbours. We’re Spring Farm: modest, well-documented by mum blogs, and proud that our biggest flex is goats and glue sticks.
