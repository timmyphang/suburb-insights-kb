# Lara (NSW)

Slug: `lara-nsw`

We live in Lara, NSW — the kind of place where the internet thinks you’re a person, a celeb, or a feature story and not actually a postcode. Morning ritual: make a cuppa, check the news, and wonder which Lara will be trending today.

Back in the day the mags weren’t writing travel pieces about our cul‑de‑sacs so much as profiling people called Lara. As Broadsheet once sat down with Lara Vrkic about The Ladies Network and work uniform energy; Pedestrian keeps revisiting Lara Worthington’s reality‑TV moments and baby name reveals; Time Out has pieces on a variety of Laras (from chefs to whale‑swim partners); and Urban List even mentioned a new airport bus link between Lara Station and Avalon Airport — Route 18 — when Avalon was relaunching international flights. So our claim to fame is mostly nominative: we share a name with a national roster of Laras.

The petty local drama? Honestly, it’s mostly identity theft by search engine. People Google “Lara” and expect couture or a cookbook or an igloo dining roundup; they get us instead. We wave them through. If you were hoping for a constant stream of influencer photo drops, celebrity collabs and curated capsule wardrobes (as seen in those Dotshop and fashion features), prepare to be underwhelmed. This is not a runway; it’s a place that once featured in an Urban List transit note and otherwise prefers quiet over headlines.

Who would hate living here: anyone who needs their suburb to be a permanent content backdrop, with daily restaurant reveals and curated nightlife. You’ll be annoyed by our lack of glossy spin.

Who would love it: people who enjoy a town with the humble thrill of name‑share celebrity confusion, where your Google results read like a scattershot who’s‑who of Laras — chefs, writers, stylists and festival stand‑ups — and where the occasional transport note (Route 18 to Avalon, anyone?) is as much excitement as we ask for. We’re not famous. We’re not frantic. We’re Lara, NSW — quietly holding our nameplate while the internet does the rest.
