# Southern River (NSW)

Slug: `southern-river-nsw`

We roll our eyes and wave at the school-run like everyone else — we double-kiss at the Southern River College gate and pretend we’re not timing the pickup to the minute. Back in the day you could find the mums and dads plotting the week over playgroup listings on Playgroup WA; the old LINK Families Fiesta brochure even pointed people toward Riverton Library and nearby venues. If that sounds quaint, it is. We like it that way.

Our morning is simple: a Playgroup WA search, a hurried hello to whoever remembers the snack roster, then a slow retreat to the part of suburb life that doesn’t pretend it’s inner‑city. As Urban List once noted, Chimek was such a hit at the Freo Markets they ended up with outposts — including a Southern River branch at one stage — so yes, we ceremonially remembered the days when Korean fried chicken was our weekend flex. (Urban List said it; we just cheered.)

Petty drama? Our dossier is suspiciously wholesome. The best we can muster from the archives is that we’ve produced a band with a name so local it hurt — Southern River Band — who even cropped up on Time Out and as a listed support act in broader tour round-ups. As Time Out and Urban List noted, they were tied to Perth listings, which is as close to celebrity as our postcode gets.

Who would hate living here: people who want non-stop neon nightlife, boutique everything or a list of cafés pre-approved by influencers. If you measure life in espresso shots and gallery openings, this place will feel like a slow-loading website.

Who would love it: families who still use Playgroup WA, parents who value a proper school gate gossip at Southern River College, and anyone who enjoys a suburban tune that occasionally makes it into a Time Out roundup. We’ll keep our small claims of fame — Chimek memories, band posters and library storytime programs — and quietly pretend that’s exactly the sort of fame worth having.
