# Meadow Heights (NSW)

Slug: `meadow-heights-nsw`

We like to think Meadow Heights announces its arrival the same way we do: quietly, by committee, and usually with a spare stroller in tow. Back in the day most of the press we get was the dignified kind — a Broadsheet City File once listed us among 88 suburbs slapped with a precautionary Yarra Valley Water warning after storm-related electrical damage. We still tell that story at BBQs like it’s a badge of suburban honour.

Our weekday morning runs used to revolve around the same few things the listings reminded everyone about: Community Kids Meadow Heights at 29 Hudson Circuit — which used to advertise long day care and a government-approved kindergarten and those very civilised hours (Mon–Fri 6:30 AM–6:30 PM) — and the local playgroups that Playgroup Victoria held in the Meadow Heights Mosque classrooms. Yes, our mosque literally doubles as a preschool annex on playgroup days, and we’d argue that’s peak Meadow Heights pragmatism: sacred space, snack time, and a singalong about sharing the toys.

If you’re hunting for petty local drama, we’ll be honest: the archive wasn’t exactly overflowing. Our most glamorous media mentions are institutional and practical — service listings, childcare centres, a mosque-turned-playroom, and that water warning — which leaves us with the comfortable feeling that Meadow Heights is more about getting stuff done than getting itself into headlines.

Who would hate living here? If you need rooftop bars, artisanal everything, or a street that doubles as a fashion runway, you’ll spend your weekends commuting to be disappointed elsewhere. We’re not glossy and we don’t pretend to be.

Who would love it? Families who want dependable childcare options and a community that folds together when it needs to — the kind of place where playgroups meet in mosque classrooms and people actually know the name of your kid’s kinder teacher. There aren’t many recent stories about us — maybe that’s the point. We’re quietly useful, slightly under-celebrated, and very much ours.
