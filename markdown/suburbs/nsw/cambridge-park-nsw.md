# Cambridge Park (NSW)

Slug: `cambridge-park-nsw`

Back in the day our Saturday mornings looked like a very polite invasion: we’d swarm the Cambridge Park Community Playgroup, clutching thermoses and permission slips, and pretend we’d thought of the craft activity ourselves. We write this as locals, so yes — we know where the circle rug lives. Nepean Community & Neighbourhood Services used to run the lot: Breakfast Club, Cranebrook Kids & Kingswood Park Kids groups, Cambridge Park Playgroup & School — you get the idea. They even celebrated Reconciliation Week with the kids, because if you’re going to fingerpaint, you might as well do it with conscience.

If you’re keeping score, the NCNS brag sheet once said 370 participants a year in playgroups, sensory plays and parenting groups. That’s not a statistic, that’s a suburb-wide cardio class: tiny chairs, tiny spoons, and the occasional glorious meltdown over glitter.

MumsPages used to be our directory of choice — the kind of place that lists Psychologists, Counsellors and Life Coaches in 2747 alongside Party and Event Venues and NDIS Providers. Translation: someone is always hosting a kids’ birthday, someone else is doing the logistics, and there’s a professional on hand if you need to talk about it afterwards. Practical, honest, a bit exhaustingly organised. We wear 2747 like a badge and a to-do list.

Who’d hate living here? If you can’t handle parade-like school pickup logistics, community noticeboards and the constant hum of organised toddler chaos, Cambridge Park will feel like an obstacle course. If your idea of a relaxing weekend is silence, you’ll be moving out on a Monday.

Who’d love it? Families who like stuff that actually works — playgroups, school outreach, a "what’s on" page that means something — will feel at home. Folks who appreciate a place that’s more roots than runway will thrive here.

Look, we don’t have a flashy feed, and for years the local stories were mostly about community programs and directories rather than glossy cafes. But if you want grit, glue sticks and a surprisingly efficient support network, Cambridge Park will greet you with open arms and a laminated permission form.
