# Taperoo (NSW)

Slug: `taperoo-nsw`

We remember Taperoo as the sort of place where the school bell on Gedville Rd set the tempo for the day — Ocean View P–12 College at 47 Gedville Rd and its little sibling, the Ocean View College Children's Centre at 23 Gedville Rd, used to herd the smalls through playground politics while playgroups ran Mon–Fri 8:45am–3:45pm. As SAcommunity noted, that was the rhythm.

On a slow Saturday we’d stroll past 14–20 Yongala St and the UnitingSA Taperoo Community Centre, which used to offer everything from computer and internet classes to digital photography, art and an over‑50s social group — low‑cost workshops and accredited training for the neighbourhood that liked to keep busy. Our Lady of the Visitation Preschool was the quiet hero for the littlies, known for its transition‑to‑school program and playgroup (again, SAcommunity had the details).

There’s no scandal in our files — no boutique battles, no dramatic council coups — just a suburb that prefers workshops over window‑shopping. If you’re the sort who needs 24/7 nightlife, designer coffee or constant novelty, you’ll hate us: we’re more about literacy classes than late‑night razzmatazz.

But if you like knowing your neighbour’s name, value a proper preschool-to-12 setup on the same strip, and appreciate a community centre that used to run practical courses and socials, Taperoo scratches a particular itch. We’ll take a paint night and a volunteer program over pretence any day.
