# Huntfield Heights (NSW)

Slug: `huntfield-heights-nsw`

Back in the day — or at least in the snippets we’ve got — Huntfield Heights felt like a suburb designed by a PTA with an eye for timetables. We know Melsetter Rd, because pretty much everyone else here seems to know Melsetter Rd: Huntfield Heights Primary School sat at 78 Melsetter Rd and the preschool ran sessions you could set your watch by (Tues/Thurs 8.45–3pm, Wed 8.45–11.15am, and playgroup Mon 9–11am). For years the local rhythm was school drop-off, playgroup, rinse, repeat.

If you wandered a little off the main drag you’d find Eden Academy at 19 Lindisfarne Rd — one of those multi-branch nursery/kindergarten things — and on Piece 12 Brodie Rd the Stables Christian Centre ran the sort of community programming that made the suburb feel like a village hall with better biscuits: women’s and men’s bible study groups, seniors’ computer lessons and other local activities. We aren’t making this up; it’s literally listed.

We also learned that Huntfield Heights is the kind of place mums and dads consult when they need things for kids: Karate clubs made the MumsPages directory, and “Music for Wellbeing, Music for Learning” turned up at Beauvale Place. Translation: if your child can’t be calmed by a tiny keyboard or a kata, they’re probably beyond even our maternal resourcefulness.

Who would hate living here? If your idea of suburbia involves artisanal everything, foggy brunch reviews or an endless stream of boutique openings, Huntfield Heights will make you itch. The archival record reads like a parenting manual, not an influencer’s feed.

Who would love it here? Families, carers, and anyone who measures life in preschool sessions and community classes. For years the place looked after the practical stuff — schools, preschools, playgroups, karate and music lessons — and that quietly matters. We’ll roast our postcode, sure, but we’ll also be the first to queue up for the next playgroup on Melsetter Rd.
