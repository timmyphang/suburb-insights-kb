# Ingle Farm (NSW)

Slug: `ingle-farm-nsw`

We know exactly when to take the kids out of the house: Friday night, Roopena St, head to the Ingle Farm Recreation Centre for that gloriously retro roller‑skating session. For years the listing said it was a proper tween-and-teen mood‑lift — skate hire included, $8.50 a person or a family of four for $30 — and yes, we still fondly remember wobbling around to whatever the PA played.

Back in the day the local rhythm was simple: mornings at the Ingle Farm Children’s Centre on 2 Belalie Rd (they ran baby playgroup Mondays 9:30–11:00 during school terms — contact 0408 222 577 if you liked routine), afternoons at the Sporting Club on Belalie Rd pretending we were athletic, and weekends that sometimes ended at a Free Family Fun Day at Ingle Farm Primary School (that one on April 19, 2024 lives in our photo albums).

We also used to praise Cafe Salvo for being delightfully earnest — outdoor playground, indoor play area, $2 coffee and $2 cake in the write‑ups, and linked to the Salvation Army’s local work plus the Suspended Coffee movement. It felt like the kind of place where crumbs and community causes were served with the same smile.

If you need late‑night neon and rooftop cocktails, you’ll probably sigh here. If the idea of being within walking distance of a regional recreation centre, children’s services, and a sporting club gives you existential dread, Ingle Farm will test your patience. Likewise, anyone allergic to organised playgroups or retro skates might want a different postcode.

But who actually loves it? Families who like cheap, sensible options; people who value actual community infrastructure over curated instagrammable streetscapes; and parents who appreciate that you can once find a $2 cake and a play area in the same breath. We’ll own the small‑town, public‑service charm — because around here, the roller rink, the playgroups and the Sporting Club are the sort of things that keep us coming back, even if we do so with scuffed knees and a suspiciously enthusiastic playlist.
