# Figtree (NSW)

Slug: `figtree-nsw`

We start the day at Figtree Bakery — yes, the one at Shop 1b, 34 Princes Highway — where “where we rise and grind” apparently applies to both sourdough and our collective patience. They’ve been posting gluten‑free and vegan specials and, if that’s not a cry for staff, there was even a listing looking for an experienced baker/pastry chef in 2026. We like to think that’s code for “more croissants, less existential dread.”

If the knees complain after carrying a Kmart haul, BaiMed Physiotherapy on 1/147 The Avenue will sort you out — they’ve been decent enough to put clinic timetables and community class news on the record in 2026, so you can rehab like a local, not like someone who discovered gyms during lockdown.

For the practicalities of life, Figtree Grove Shopping Centre has long been the regional answer to “where do we buy everything at once?” The mall has proudly served Wollongong since 1965, with over 90 stores including Kmart, Coles and Woolworths — and back when someone wrote about it they even flagged concierge service, complimentary mobility hire, EV chargers and free parking. In other words: the sort of suburban romance that ends with a trolley full of nappies and a bottle of wine.

We’ll confess: we also still cheer for Figtree Gourmet Kitchen — once described as the Illawarra’s longest‑established wood‑fired pizza place — because nothing says local pride like passing down a favourite schnitzel order to your children.

Not everything here is cheery. The Illawarra escarpment around Figtree was the focus of police activity after searches in March and May 2023 and again in October 2024, so we don’t trade in cheap headlines — that’s serious local news, and it sits with us.

Who would hate Figtree? If you need late‑night cocktail bars and neon pretence, you’ll be restless. But who loves it here? Families who appreciate an early‑parenting centre at Figtree Private Hospital, parents using the Figtree Early Childhood Centre for newborn checks, churchgoers who make 8am or 10am at Figtree Anglican Church (yes, they list service times) — and anyone who values a proper bakery, a reliable physio and a pizza that remembers your name. We’ll keep the fig in our name and the rest as is.
