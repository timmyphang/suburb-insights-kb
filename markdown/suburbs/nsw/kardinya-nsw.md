# Kardinya (NSW)

Slug: `kardinya-nsw`

Kardinya — we’re the sleepy pocket down in Melville (WA) that everyone forgets until someone mentions mushrooms or a toy library.

Sunday mornings used to look like a low-key ritual: we’d amble past Frederick Baldwin Park at 150 Le Souef Drive — yes, the one with the man-made lake, fountains and a quota of ducks that own the place — and head toward the undercover carpark at Kardinya Park Shopping Centre. Kardinya Markets used to set up there on Sundays from 6–11am (corner of South Street and North Lake Road), a proper community swapmeet where you could buy a bargain, swap a story and still be home for lunch. Back in the day the shopping centre at 1–15 South Street anchored the town: TerryWhite Chemmart was the place to nab your flu jab and the Kardinya Tavern at 17 South Street did the local pub duties (and once ran ‘Nanna Bingo’ according to the notices).

We’re fond of our odd bits of local industry too. As Broadsheet noted, The Mushroom Guys operated out of the industrial estate, growing gourmet mushrooms that chefs across the state sourced — yes, actual fungi fame, from our very own suburb. It’s the kind of humble brag we enjoy.

If you like community infrastructure, we have a cupboard full: Kulungah‑Myah Community Centre at 136 Le Souef Drive has been doing the rounds for years with the Kardinya Toy Library and First Steps Pre‑Kindy, and Prime Movers held fitness sessions on Williamson Road. The property listings used to point out how close 6/14 Philmore Crescent was to Murdoch University, North Lake Senior Campus and Frank Cann Park — so yes, families and students found it handy.

Who’d hate living here? If you demand inner‑city buzz, rooftop cocktails or a revolving door of new “concept” cafes, Kardinya will feel like a slumbering aunt who refuses to be rushed. Archibald Street cafes get a mention in lifestyle lists, but we’re no metropolis.

Who’d love it? Families, students, anyone after a bit of practical community — playgrounds, toy libraries, markets, a tavern and even a world‑class mushroom operation in the industrial estate. We’re small (about 4.4 km²), surrounded by the usual cast — Coolbellup, Murdoch, Winthrop — and quietly proud of it.
