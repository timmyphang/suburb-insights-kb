# Cobbitty (NSW)

Slug: `cobbitty-nsw`

We know Cobbitty — we queue for school pick‑ups like it’s a spectator sport and we’ve memorised the quickest route to anywhere that serves kiddie chaos. Back in the day the Playgroup NSW site was our oracle: we’d punch in the postcode, hit the little tool and hope it spat out something that involved other adults and fewer toys in our cars.

If you were scrolling their listings you might’ve spotted Harrington Park Pixies & Munchkins — listed as a contact on Playgroup NSW’s pages — and breathed a sigh of relief that someone else was organising snack time. We don’t know if they’re still running the same singalong repertoire now, so let’s be honest: that bit of intel is pre‑2024 nostalgia, not a live recommendation.

There isn’t a lot of flashy media drama here; our local personality is low‑key, family‑oriented and suspicious of sudden change. That suits us. What the Playgroup NSW snippets shout is that Cobbitty is the kind of place where you want someone else to herd the kids for ninety minutes while you recalibrate.

Who would hate living here? People who prefer neon nightlife and surprise pop‑ups. Who would love it? Anyone who’s happy setting their parenting GPS to “find a playgroup” and letting a decent website point the way.

So we’ll keep leaning on Playgroup NSW’s old listings, and on each other. If you’re after chaos with community, Cobbitty’s quietly doing its thing — probably with a song and a snack in hand.
