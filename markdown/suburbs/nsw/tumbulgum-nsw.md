# Tumbulgum (NSW)

Slug: `tumbulgum-nsw`

Back in the day — and by that I mean all the stories we could find in the dusty corners of the internet — Tumbulgum was the sort of tiny riverside village people bragged about to city mates. We wake to the Tweed River doing its calm thing, look out across sugarcane and cows, and decide whether life is best measured in hay bales or rum bottles.

As Urban List and Concrete Playground fondly noted, Husk (Husk Farm/Husk Rum & Gin Distillery) used to be our calling card: a 60‑acre paddock‑to‑bottle operation on the Tweed, growing sugarcane on rich volcanic soil at 1152 Dulguigan Road, North Tumbulgum. They made agricole‑style rum, an indigo Ink Gin and, if you were lucky, ran distillery tours Wednesday to Sunday with tastings. It was very "country date idea" — cows in the background, a cocktail in the foreground, and someone explaining terroir like it wasn’t just swampy magic.

We rallied when we needed to. Time Out even listed a Tumbulgum Rock Bushfire Appeal once — proof we can put on our wellies and actually help when the chips are down.

If you care about small‑town infrastructure, you’ll appreciate that our village shows up on the essentials list: Tumbulgum Public School on 11 Fawcett Street and the Playgroup NSW listing remind you that families were part of the plan. We’ve got a riverside pub (Tumbulgum Tavern, yes — 124/136 Riverside Drive — was holding court), a very practical Tumby Park Firewood on Terranora Road, and tour operators like FAR OUT TOURS pointing out Cheers Farm on the Tweed when the road trip crowd rolls through.

Who would hate living here? People who can’t stomach cows, hay bales, or a skyline that’s mostly trees and river. If you need 24/7 neon and five different cuisines at 2am, Tumbulgum will sigh politely and hand you a pamphlet.

Who would love it? Distillery pilgrims, parents chasing a proper playgroup, art tourists heading toward the Tweed Regional Gallery & Margaret Olley Art Centre, and anyone who thinks a weekend measured in rum tastings and riverside sunsets is a very good idea.
