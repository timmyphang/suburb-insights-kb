# Ringwood (NSW)

Slug: `ringwood-nsw`

Back in the day we’d meet under the glass at Eastland’s Town Square and pretend we were in the city, then spend an hour deciding between Jimmy Grants, Huxtaburger or Paco’s Tacos and call that culture. As Broadsheet noted, Eastland’s redevelopment put Ringwood on the map — for better or for more parking — and even had the shopping centre manager lukewarmly excited about drones. Yes, Wing and DoorDash were apparently going to drop our takeaway from the sky; Greg Balmforth called it “another avenue of business.” We called it Tuesday.

Mornings used to be simple: a quick detour to Saazaa Coffee, where David Juma roasted beans from his family farm in Kenya and the cold Nairobi Tonic was a seasonal flex. Or we’d sip something less dramatic while pretending the floor‑to‑ceiling views at Mister Fox over the golf greens made us refined. Burnham Bakery’s Eastland site had the kind of pastries that made you forgive the escalator queues.

We’re not into forced drama — there’s been more paperwork than scandal in our archives — but you can feel the tug between leafy suburban calm and mall ambition. Time Out gave us a local guide that still reads like a love letter: Ringwood Lake, three train stations, parks and places to stretch out. Sitchu liked our “growing urban edge” and even hyped Suburban Rooftop as the place locals climbed three levels for sunset photos.

Who would hate living here? People allergic to choice. If you loathe multi-level shopping precincts, rooftop bars, Holey Moley x Strike at Eastland, or the idea that 400 Gradi was coming in to upstage your neighbourhood pizza, then yes, Ringwood is probably not for you. If the thought of proposed higher‑rise zoning (20 storeys, anyone?) makes you twitch, we get it — change gives some of us palpitations.

Who would love it? Families who want parks and playgroups (we used to point to North Ringwood Playgroup on Wonga Road for the toddlers), weekend brunchers who don’t mind a mall detour, and anyone who appreciates that Ringwood can be both leafy and loudly convenient. We’ll take being reliably unglamorous and oddly complete — and we’ll wave at you from the Town Square while a drone drops your dinner.
