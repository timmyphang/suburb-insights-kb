# Gorokan (NSW)

Slug: `gorokan-nsw`

Back in the day we planned our weekends around Lake Tuggerah like it was a public holiday. We’d pretend to be outdoorsy after breakfast and float our expectations on the water instead of actually doing anything strenuous.

As Urban List once pointed out, Gorokan had that postcard-perfect Airbnb with direct access to Lake Tuggerah — you know, the one that promised double kayaks, ping pong trophies and an air-hockey glory room for people who measure success in arcade points. Those were the kind of weekend ambitions we had: kayak at dawn, console at dusk.

For years the local kids’ calendar was refreshingly small and very specific: Lakes Playtime at Lakes Baptist Church, 45 Marks Rd, every Monday during term (it even cost $3), and the Mini kids playgroup at Tuggerah Lakes Salvation Army Hall, 90 Minnamurra Rd, Monday mornings at 10am. If your idea of community is a room full of sticky fingers, singalongs and someone’s aunt with a suspiciously good lamington recipe, this was home.

We couldn’t find any big scandal in the clippings — no council melodrama, no celebrity house swaps — just lakeside pragmatism and playgroup timetables. So who’d hate living here? Folks after 24/7 nightlife, endless boutique everything, or unpredictable urban chaos. Who’d love it? Families who appreciate honest lake access, organised Monday mornings and an Airbnb that once promised more ping pong than pretension. Gorokan: modest, lake-splashed and quietly excellent at being unflashy.
