# Macclesfield (NSW)

Slug: `macclesfield-nsw`

Macclesfield, NSW — we spend a lot of time explaining who we are because Google loves to mix us up with everyone else called Macclesfield.

Morning routine: we scroll our feeds and, as The Urban List noted, discover a "historic township of Macclesfield" with Longview Vineyard described as a luxury escape with 12 king suites, a restored homestead and a day spa — which is lovely, if only it were ours. That piece even sat the place in the Adelaide Hills and acknowledged the Peramangk and Kaurna peoples, so yes, very important story — just not our postcode.

By lunchtime the internet has us doing double duty: Time Out names Macclesfield as a UK location in a Claire’s-closures roundup and an FA Cup guide, and Pedestrian.tv reminds the world that Ian Curtis’s hometown has a new walking tour. East Cheshire Mumbler is busy courting parents with Hare Hill’s spring snowdrops and rhododendrons, while MyBump2Baby and regional directories list Rhythm Time Cheshire, Tumble Tots and Sweaty Mama among local toddler options. All very wholesome — again, all very much the other Macclesfields.

Petty local drama? We don’t have much recorded — mostly the ongoing identity crisis of being mistaken for a vineyard, a football club or a Joy Division pilgrimage. We’ll admit it’s funny watching strangers book vineyard stays in South Australia and parents in Cheshire RSVP to events that aren’t here.

Who would hate living here: anyone who needs their suburb to come with guaranteed rhododendrons, celebrity walking tours or an on-site cellar door. If your ideal weekend requires a curated spa-and-wine package or a weekly baby yoga class called out on MyBump2Baby, you’ll be disappointed — unless you’re happy being in the Macclesfield that isn’t the one the articles were writing about.

Who would love it: people who enjoy a bit of mystery and the odd case of mistaken identity. Back in the day our mentions mostly pointed to other Macclesfields — which, frankly, makes us smug. We’ll keep our quiet postcode and let the internet do the sightseeing.
