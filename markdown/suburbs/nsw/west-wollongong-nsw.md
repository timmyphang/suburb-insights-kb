# West Wollongong (NSW)

Slug: `west-wollongong-nsw`

We start the day like proper locals used to: ferrying the small people to Stuart Park or McCabe Park, arguing over whose turn it is to push the swing while the ocean does its background hum. Back in the day, South Coast Mums was our gospel — a one-stop for everything mums, bubs and kids — and they kept an eye on the best playgrounds across Wollongong and the wider Illawarra.

When the heavens opened (and they do), our pivot was predictable and practical — Beaton Park Leisure Centre was the rainy-day salvation listed in those same guides, or we trotted the kids off to the Wollongong Art Gallery for a bit of culture that doesn’t require sunscreen. For years the write-ups suggested museums, libraries and indoor pools as the cure for drippy weekends, and honestly, they weren’t wrong.

There’s nothing scandalous to gossip about here — no nightclubs to shame, no boutique coffee wars to script — which makes our petty drama wonderfully domestic: whose picnic rug blew away, whose toddler christened the fountain. If you hate the sound of small humans happily exhausted and the local internet cheerleading family-friendly things, you would absolutely loathe West Wollongong.

But if you’re someone who likes beaches within reach, parks named in parent blogs, and reliable rainy-day plans that include a pool or a gallery, you’ll fit right in. We may not be flashy, but we’re the kind of place South Coast Mums quietly pointed to when you wanted somewhere that actually works when the forecast is 80% chance of parenting.
