# Hunters Hill (NSW)

Slug: `hunters-hill-nsw`

Back in the day we’d stroll from the Hunters Hill Club, tip our heads up at the jacarandas and drift down toward the water under the Gladesville Bridge like we owned the postcard. That’s our version of a morning — floppy hat, suspiciously sensible shoes, and someone making a fuss about which footpath has the best leaf litter.

Weekends used to start at Stoneground Bakery — the family-run place that famously sells out if you don’t get there early — then a confusingly civilised breakfast that might involve Stain’s “breakfast in a teapot” and Toby’s Estate coffee (as Broadsheet once noted, the provenance nerds would approve). If we felt fancy we’d justify a rotisserie pilgrimage to Boronia Kitchen for the free-range chicken Simon Sandall brined for a day, or pop into Dachshund Coffee when we wanted something that sounded like it could double as a small dog.

Our local watering hole, affectionately HHH, has been through the rites of renovation: the Hunters Hill Hotel has been lovingly restored from its Fig Tree Hotel days into the bright, airy joint Concrete Playground admired. Urban List and Time Out used to point out the beer garden, trivia nights and that improbable 160” screen — we’ll never forget watching something enormous on it and pretending we were cultured.

Petty drama? Oh yes. We had a bakery eyebrow-raiser: Stoneground Bakery apologised after sharing an AI-generated Instagram post that featured Jeffrey Epstein. Pedestrian TV covered the fallout. We were mortified, we were embarrassed, and yes, we talked about it over coffee.

Who would hate living here: anyone who needs neon-lit nightlife, late-night kebabs or the unvarnished chaos of the inner city will find Hunters Hill politely judgmental. And if you like your suburbs anonymous, our jacaranda-lined streets and heritage homes are kind of show-offy.

Who would love it: families enjoying the $4.75m Figtree Park upgrade at 40A Gladesville Road, mums who meet on the Mum Walks, wine obsessives who once flocked to Hunters Hill Wine Room (Adrian Filuta’s boutique collection), and people who prefer their weekends leafy, locally-sourced and just a touch smug. We’ll keep tending the lawns and the gossip — someone’s got to.
