# Lake Illawarra (NSW)

Slug: `lake-illawarra-nsw`

We start our mornings like everyone else here: with one eye on the horizon and the other on whatever’s floating past in the lagoon. As Urban List charmingly put it, Lake Illawarra is “as much of a lake as she is a glorified gigantic ocean lagoon,” which is a polite way of saying we get the best of both — prawning, fishing and a riot of watersports when the mood takes us.

Back in the day the foreshore was mostly sandwiches and sunburn, but plans for the Warrawong Parklands Master Plan promised to change that: nearly 10km of pedestrian and cycle paths, a 320‑metre wetland boardwalk with panoramic views, an Olympic‑standard skatepark and better water access for paddling and fishing. We’ve been waiting for the boardwalk selfies and the skate tricks, and we’ll take the promise of improved water access like a promise of free parking — cautiously optimistic.

Petty local drama? Oh yes. Warilla gave us headline material when an $8,000 Pokémon card was reportedly nicked and detectives in the Lake Illawarra Police District even put together Strike Force Calmar to chase it down. Only here could collectible card crime be the thing that unites council meetings and corner‑store gossip.

If you can’t handle surprise weather — the Bureau of Meteorology was even reportedly investigating whether a tornado or waterspout ripped through parts of the Illawarra — then Lake Illawarra will give you reasons to panic-buy umbrellas and binoculars. Likewise, if your idea of a perfect weekend is a quiet city mall, you’ll hate us: we’re the people who drive out the Princes Highway toward Dharawal National Park, jam the car with kids and head to Jamberoo Action Park or Illawarra Treetop Adventures for the day.

Who’d love living here? Families who treat the South Coast like their backyard amusement plan, anglers who know the best prawning spots, paddlers itching for better launch points, and anyone who appreciates a place that can host both serene lake mornings and headline‑grabbing oddities. We’re proud, a little salty, and always ready to tell you where to stand for the best sunset over the lagoon.
