# Joondalup (NSW)

Slug: `joondalup-nsw`

We live in Joondalup — yes, the one with Arena Joondalup on the map and a history of bringing the kind of spectacles other suburbs RSVP to. Picture us on a weekend: the idea is a slow scroll through Lakeside Joondalup (Urban List was right — it “seriously has it all”), maybe a detour past Unit 3/7 Winton Rd where Time Out once announced Chuck E. Cheese would open its first Aussie outpost — doors, mascots and family-sized pizza hours included in the copy. If that doesn’t scream suburb priorities, we don’t know what does.

Mornings used to mean cake runs when Sweet’n Perth opened its first shop in Joondalup in 2025 (yes, cakes arrived and were celebrated). Other afternoons are for arguing about which nursery sends the best succulents — Broadsheet pointed out Dawson’s Garden World Forrestfield supplies a lot of plants to Joondalup stockists, so our gardens are basically outsourced perfection. For the active among us, Pedestrian wrote about Urbi launching in Joondalup, which sounds like getting around on two wheels with slightly more dignity.

Festival season is where we shed our sensible shoes: Joondalup Festival once floated a colossal whale and set alight glowing crystal figures, and major touring events like Wildlands and Laneway were pencilled into Arena Joondalup’s calendar. Archery Attack even made the HBF Arena a place to flail gloriously at foam bows — Time Out had that on the list of things to do.

We’re also the kind of place with very organised mums: Joondalup Mums keeps the events page humming, and Joondalup Family Centre at 25 Jolstra Crescent quietly runs pre-kindy, Grow Free Carts and FriYAY youth programs. It’s the civic glue you don’t brag about but quietly rely on.

Who would hate living here? If you crave anonymous inner-city edge or want a postcode that never hosts a floating whale, this will feel a bit theatrical. Who would love it? Families, festival-goers, plant collectors and anyone who enjoys a mall that doubles as a small social universe.

We know our corner of Joondalup — loud when it wants to be, community-minded most days, and always ready to host a parade, a pizza mouse, or a three-week art whale.
