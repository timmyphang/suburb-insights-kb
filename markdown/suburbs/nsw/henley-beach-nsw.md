# Henley Beach (NSW)

Slug: `henley-beach-nsw`

We know Henley Beach like the back of our sunburnt hands: the square, the jetty, the people who think fairy lights count as an interior design philosophy. Back in the day the papers loved to tell the story — Broadsheet called Seaview Road “one of Adelaide’s most exciting dining precincts,” Time Out waxed lyrical about “white and lemon‑yellow striped umbrellas” at Ocean Bar and Kitchen, and Sitchu reminded everyone the 500‑metre jetty was the place to pretend you’re contemplative.

Our weekend ritual used to be painfully simple: grab takeaway coffee (Broadsheet’s guide made it sound aspirational), drop a towel on the grass, and watch a SUP sesh drift past while someone attempts sunrise yoga on the sand (Sitchu’s suggestion, not ours — though to be fair, G&T at sunset has its merits). The upgraded Joe’s Kiosk was given a big refurb in 2020 and was reborn as a slick seaside destination — yes, we noticed the soft‑shell crab sliders and the kingfish sashimi move in (Broadsheet again). Henley House even got the three‑level, family‑friendly write‑up in Urban List — the sort of place where grandparents and toddlers can coexist with minimal eye contact.

Petty local drama? It’s subtle. Once, internet fame came our way when Junkee used Henley as the location for viral underwater footage of a crab fight — trust us, nothing brings the community together like a crustacean brawl hitting 17,000 views. Concrete Playground would also remind you there’s a Ramsgate Hotel with a view for cheering on the Socceroos, and Parwana Afghan Kitchen hangs on Henley Beach Road if you fancy something more earnest than beachfront tapas.

Who would hate it here? If you despise pastel sunsets, rooftop bars with a view, a square that glows like someone’s idea of holiday romance, or the whole “hip and healthy” vibe Sitchu insists we have, Henley will make you twitchy. If you loathe walkers, cyclists, watersport users and families mingling like it’s some coastal commune, move inland.

Who would love it? Families, runners, people who clap when the sun drops, and anyone who thinks a seaside meal is a legitimate life choice. We’ll keep the umbrella. You keep the drama. We’ve got the jetty.
