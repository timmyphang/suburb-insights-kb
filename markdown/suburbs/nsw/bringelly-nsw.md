# Bringelly (NSW)

Slug: `bringelly-nsw`

We roll the windows down and smell the future — or at least what someone on a press release calls it. Back in the day Concrete Playground ran the story that the NSW government planned a new western city called Bradfield just north of Bringelly, saying it would take up more than 100 hectares and sit beside the new western Sydney airport with a connecting metro line. We like to pretend the metro will arrive before our next barbie.

Weekends here are practical. While other suburbs brunch, we refresh MumsPages: it’s where you compare gyms, NDIS providers, in‑home care and other services for mums and kids — the little directory that quietly runs our logistics. If you want drama, scroll the reviews; if you want peace, book a nap.

There isn’t much scandal in the clippings — mostly big-picture plans and service listings — so we won’t manufacture feuds. The real vibe is low-key: paddocks, plans for Bradfield, and a handy online list of who’ll keep the kids occupied while we daydream about a nearby city centre.

Who would hate living here? People who need midnight cocktails, flash boutiques or a tram every five minutes. We can be gloriously quiet and a bit unpolished; if you worship constant buzz, go closer to the lights.

Who would love it? Families, carers and anyone who values space, a community that uses MumsPages to get things done, and optimists who enjoy picturing Bradfield on the horizon. We’re Bringelly — not quite the city yet, but we'll be ready for the name tags.
