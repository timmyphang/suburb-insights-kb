# Mundijong (NSW)

Slug: `mundijong-nsw`

We roll out of bed like everyone else — not because we’re organised, but because Lot 121 on Evelyn Street has already started setting up. As FindGlocal reported, Mundijong Markets used to open stalls from 8am (with auctions kicking off around 10:30am) on the 1st and 3rd Wednesday and the 4th Sunday each month, and that’s our unofficial alarm clock. There’s even a 18/06/2026 note about speculation regarding the market’s future, which means we now have The Great Mundijong Market Whisper to discuss over lukewarm tea.

Our weekend detour used to be a perambulation down Paterson Street: the listings spoke about Mundijong Mitre 10 at 42 Paterson Street — a funny little combo of hardware, stockfeed and rural supplies (the entry even said it’s closed on public holidays). Nearby sat the Mundijong Tavern & Drive-Thru at 32 Paterson Street, described as a local beer garden in the historic town — a place we’d totter past pretending we were only “looking” at the menu.

We’ve always leaned on markets to define ourselves. Perth Mums Group’s pages remember the Splendiferous Jolly Christmas Festival at the Mundijong Football Oval (6 Cockram Street) — over 100 stalls, amusements and live entertainment from Slim Jim and the Phatts — and there’s been a LiveLighter Community Fair on the listings too. Directories even used to list Mundijong IGA, SJ Farmers Market, Blue Rock Farm and Mundella Foods for when you needed emergency supplies after too much festival pie.

Petty local drama? It’s mercifully small: the “will the market stay?” conversation repeats like a favourite song. We don’t need council conspiracies or soap-opera-level feuds — just the everyday panic of whether Saturday’s auction will be the last.

Who would hate living here? People who need neon-lit, 24/7 anonymity, or anyone convinced success equals a skyscraper visible from the backyard. This town is stubbornly human-scale.

Who would love it? Market regulars, bargain hunters, families who like a yarn by the football oval, and anyone whose idea of excitement involves auctions at 10:30am and a beer garden on Paterson Street. We know every stall, every singalong, and we’re not giving them up without a proper argument — and a pasty.
