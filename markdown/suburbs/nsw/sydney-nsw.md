# Sydney (NSW)

Slug: `sydney-nsw`

Back in the day, when Broadsheet's 24‑hour cheat sheet said we could start with Manly ferry breakfasts and finish on a rooftop, we liked to pretend Sydney was just a very expensive postcard with good coffee and an attitude problem. As locals, we know the real loop: the ferry's churn, a half‑hearted attempt at the BridgeClimb, and then making a show of ‘discovering’ The Rocks' laneways — which, yes, BridgeMuseum and the guidebooks have already written about for us.

Weekends look like a tourism manifesto: Bondi to Coogee walks that double as cardio for people who want to be seen, a dip at Wylie's Baths if we're feeling artisanal, and the inevitable photo stop at the Opera House because we can't live here and not use it as a backdrop. Time Out gave us a checklist of 52 essential things to do and Urban List kept reminding us which CBD restaurants (they name‑checked Cafe Sydney and the likes of Bar Allora) were worth the panoramic harbour views — or at least the Instagram caption.

We have our festivals, too. Vivid Sydney lit up the harbour in 2026 (it ran from Friday 23 May until Saturday 14 June, if you were keeping score), and Pedestrian TV once ran a bit about renaming a whole new suburb — proof we’ll entertain anything that sounds like a plot twist. Playgroup NSW announced thousands celebrating the International Day of Play at Luna Park, which is exactly the sort of wholesome chaos we adore: kids, carnival lights, and parents comparing maternity hospital lists on the sly.

Who would hate living here? Anyone allergic to crowds, bucket lists or people who think coastal walks count as culture. If you want quiet streets, low‑key anonymity and zero ferry queues, move inland and please don’t tell your friends.

Who would love it here? Families who rely on the mum networks and community lists (hello, Eastern Suburbs Mums and Social Mums Club), runners who join Coogee Run Club for scenic guilt‑free laps, and anyone who measures time in sunsets over Darling Harbour and last‑minute tickets to whatever Time Out recommended this week. We know our city’s flaws — the koala warnings Junkee flagged in the Basin are a wake‑up call — but we also know how to sell the sunset like it's a lifestyle. We wouldn't live anywhere else.
