# Revesby Heights (NSW)

Slug: `revesby-heights-nsw`

We live here, so we know the weekend choreography: the small-person shuttle, the costume glitter, and that odd, comforting hum of someone in the neighbourhood doing an entirely different kind of training.

Back in the day MumsPages used to list all our parenting necessities — "Baby Support and Services in Revesby Heights (2212)" and "Musical Theatre Classes for Kids" — which meant our Saturdays looked like a split-screen. One half was tiny jazz hands at JAKSA Performing Arts Studio (that listing definitely existed), the other half was a bloke quietly signing up at Highway Truck Driving School for HR/HC licences because apparently someone needs to move the set pieces.

We’ll own it: our postcode doesn’t pretend to be inner-city chic. There’s a homespun logic to a suburb where drama class and heavy-vehicle training sit side-by-side in the listings. It’s part charming, part “only in 2212” — and we wouldn’t have it any other way.

Who would hate living here? If you crave neon laneway bars, endless boutique everything, or a postcode that doubles as an influencer backdrop, you’ll be restless. Who would love it? Families after easily found classes and baby support (MumsPages had those), and anyone who likes community that’s practical and a touch theatrical.

We’re low-key, a bit theatrical, and surprisingly well-prepared for the logistics of life. If that sounds like your kind of suburb, welcome — just don’t be surprised when your neighbour can sing and also tow a trailer.
