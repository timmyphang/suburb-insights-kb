# Panton Hill (NSW)

Slug: `panton-hill-nsw`

We roll out of the bitumen, breathe the kind of air that smells suspiciously like someone else’s weekend away, and immediately remember why we told the city kids Panton Hill was “rugged.” Back in the day, Broadsheet called us a “bucolic sandstone gem” and then, to our delight, spent most of the piece lingering over Teunis and Dorothy Kwak and their Panton Hill Winery — the place that made us feel like the Yarra Valley’s answer to a European postcard.

Our weekend routine used to be depressingly civilised: wander the gravel at the winery, admire the Southern Europe–inspired architecture Sitchu liked so much, and stand politely at the cellar door while somebody explained the fortified style like it was an heirloom. It’s the kind of place that makes you sip slowly and judge your life choices with a vineyard as a backdrop.

There isn’t much tabloid fodder here — the local news diet, according to the files, prefers tasting notes and community listings. Playgroup Victoria keeps showing up in our paper trail: Panton Hill Primary School appears as a playgroup venue (there’s even a music room, if you’re nostalgic for recorder practice), and the after school care building has its own listing. The PlayConnect+ PDF from March 2026 also reminded us we’re on someone’s roster of community sites, which is about as exciting as our council meetings get.

Who would hate living here? If you need round‑the‑clock restaurants, 10 boutique gyms and a tram that accepts your emotional baggage, you’ll be bored stiff. If you resent sandstone views and people who can identify a decent shiraz, this is not your terrain.

Who would love it? Families who like a school with a music room and a sensible after‑school care building, people who don’t mind driving a little further for the city’s conveniences, and anyone who enjoys a cellar door that treats wine like an occasion rather than a caffeine fix. We’re quietly proud, a little smug about our scenery, and eternally grateful that Broadsheet and Sitchu noticed us before the rest of Melbourne did.
