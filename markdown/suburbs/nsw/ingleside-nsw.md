# Ingleside (NSW)

Slug: `ingleside-nsw`

We are Ingleside, NSW — the suburb so cosmopolitan the internet once put us in San Francisco. Mornings here start with the usual: we brew a coffee, check the council notices, then, for a laugh, refresh a mum blog from the other side of the Pacific. For years the only local shout-out in the archive was a listing on San Francisco Parent Groups — yes, the "San Francisco Southern Neighborhood Mamas" and that tidy sitemap that lumps us in with Excelsior, Crocker Amazon, Outer Mission, Portola, Mission Terrace, Visitacion Valley and Sunnyside.

Back in the day (or at least in the undated snippets the web kept), our proudest press was being named among Bay Area neighbourhoods on a parent group sitemap. As the sitemap notes, we’re on the list — which is either global recognition or a clerical quirk. Either way, it’s flattering and mildly confusing.

There’s no juicy local council scandal in these pages, no block-party blow-up — the data gives us dignity, not drama. The only civic dispute seems to be continental: are we Sydney-adjacent or suddenly part of San Francisco's mum network?

Who would hate living here? People who can’t handle being perpetually misfiled on the internet or who demand up-to-the-minute online fame. Who would love it? Parents who secretly enjoy being in a global neighbourhood roll-call, and anyone who likes a suburb that can be quietly content with being low-key enough to be mistaken for someplace glamorous.

We’re Ingleside: small enough to be mysterious, big enough to be on a foreign sitemap.
