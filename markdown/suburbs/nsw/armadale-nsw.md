# Armadale (NSW)

Slug: `armadale-nsw`

We live in Armadale, NSW, but when we went looking for local lore the internet handed us a scrapbook full of other Armadales — mostly High Street glamour, a few playgroup PDFs and one very excited perfume ribbon‑cutting. The clippings we found are all older, so consider this less a current guide and more a cheeky postcard from someone who’s been scrolling too hard.

Back in the day (or at least according to Broadsheet’s Armadale directory) High Street was where the grown‑ups queued for Henne’s ’90s‑fitout, picked up bagels from Hank’s Bagelry, and debated whether Lune Croissanterie had finally perfected pastry. Broadsheet also name‑checked Nine Yards, Ruben’s Deli and a Friends With Frank opening — the kind of curated shopping strip Sitchu would call “world‑class.” Urban List chipped in with Moby 3143 and Bouzy, while Concrete Playground gave us Bansho — opened September 2023 at 960 High Street — as evidence that someone in town knew how to do French‑Japanese fine dining.

Time Out played the glam card too: Victor Churchill, Harvie rooftop bar, By The Glass in Kings Arcade (976 High Street), Aya Teppanyaki and Mammoth all got a mention, and Concrete Playground bookmarked Willow at 1205 High Street and Libertine Parfumerie at 1023 High Street for anyone keeping score. Even The Skin Bar (1240 High Street) made the cut. Meanwhile Playgroup WA and Community Playgroups reminded us there are other Armadales — Perth’s LGA and a Scottish one — quietly minding their playdates.

Who would hate living here? If you loathe beautiful shopfronts, art‑deco rooftops and the idea of a neighbourhood that treats a perfumery like communal theatre, then yes, you’d bristle. If you love it, you’d adore the pedestrian‑friendly strip, the boutiques (DISSH, MCM House, Jo Malone in Sitchu’s list) and enough specialty spots to make you invent an excuse to stroll High Street twice a day.

So, from our slightly confused corner of Armadale, NSW: we may not have High Street on our postcode, but we appreciate the aspirational vibes. Consider this a fond roast of a suburb that, according to the archives, once perfected the art of looking effortlessly expensive.
