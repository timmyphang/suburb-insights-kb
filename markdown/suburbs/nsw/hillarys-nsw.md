# Hillarys (NSW)

Slug: `hillarys-nsw`

We start our weekends like every proper Hillarys local: pretending we’re late for the 9am ferry but really here for the promenade views. Back in the day the kids used to beg for the Boardwalk Betty train on Sundays — Perth Mums Group even lists the Sorrento Quay Boardwalk and those free train rides as a local ritual. AQWA used to be our flex: Time Out called it home to Australia’s largest single aquarium and that gloriously long underwater tunnel, which we parade visiting relatives through like proud smug parents.

Our marina has always been a bit show-offy. Broadsheet remembered the Joondalup Festival’s water and pyrotechnics parade landing at Hillarys Boat Harbour — the kind of loud, sparkly thing that makes us forgive the traffic. Rottnest Fast Ferries runs from the harbour too, so we can be island-casual in 45 minutes if we feel like it.

Food and leisure used to roll through here in waves. Urban List flagged spots like The Breakwater and Eccolo on the marina strip, and Broadsheet wrote about the Hillarys Beach Club team opening Bar Ole over in Iluka. Whitfords Brewing Company was even pegged as a sister brewery for the Whitford City precinct, which felt very grown-up for a seaside suburb. Sitchu still praises Breakwater Bowl’s boutique bowling vibes — waterfront lanes and cocktails, because of course we bowl with an ocean view.

Petty drama? We’ve had our share. Pedestrian TV reported an AMSA probe into a diving school incident near the boat harbour — not our finest chapter, and something we remember with the solemnity of a wet towel on the line.

Who would hate living here? People allergic to prams, sand between towels, or weekend markets — Perth Mums Group lists Sunday Treasures Market and Sorrento Night Market and even Markets by the Sea 2024, so there’s always someone selling something handmade where you didn’t ask for it.

Who would love it? Families (Whitford Family Centre and the Giggling Gecko OSC have been quiet little anchors on Endeavour Road), ferry-hoppers, and anyone who thinks seaside pubs and small festivals are the point of life. We’re a bit salty, a bit sparkly and unapologetically marvellous. Come for the aquarium tunnel, stay for the Boardwalk Betty.
