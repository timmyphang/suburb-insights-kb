# Burnside Heights (NSW)

Slug: `burnside-heights-nsw`

We know Burnside Heights, NSW — the place you brag about in a polite, suburban whisper. Back in the day Broadsheet even gave us a mention: Enelssie’s Burnside Heights location used to sell pizzas topped with Filipino ingredients and, when it opened as a takeaway, offered lunchtime “Fill-Oh” rice bowls. We still remind each other of that like it was a trophy from a foodie spelling bee.

A typical Saturday for us was low-key: a stroll through the neighbourhood, arguing about which corner is invisibly rentier, and maybe swinging past the takeaway window that used to do those Fill‑Oh bowls. It felt like multicultural ambition with drive-thru convenience — a proud little fusion experiment that said, “We’re trying things, quietly.”

There’s no local soap opera in the files — no council kerfuffle or underground speakeasy scandal to pitch to the weekend crowd — which suits us fine. If you need drama, head elsewhere. If you hate the idea of a pizza with pancit vibes, you’ll angrily scroll past our postcode and call it “too adventurous.” That’s fine — we like our neighbours who aren’t afraid of a pineapple-level culinary curveball.

Who would love it here? People who appreciate the weirdly specific joys: a takeaway that once dared to mix cuisines, a suburb that quietly exists on the edge of being noteworthy, and anyone who enjoys saying, with a hint of smugness, “We had that here first — or at least we tried it.”
