# Kilaben Bay (NSW)

Slug: `kilaben-bay-nsw`

We get up early like we own a tiny kingdom — and honestly, with only 1.6 km² to patrol, we practically do. Morning for us is a lakeside perimeter check: the water of Lake Macquarie, a nod to Carey Bay and Coal Point across the bend, and a quick scroll through the old local directories because that’s how we know everything worth knowing. As MyCommunityDirectory noted, Kilaben Bay sits cheek-by-jowl with Toronto and is inside the Lake Macquarie council area — which is a fancy way of saying our postcode has very tidy borders and loudly opinionated noticeboards.

Back in the day the internet presence that represented us was very maternal: MumsPages used to be the parenting bible for the suburb, listing “Businesses and Service Providers for Mums”, kids’ sports, martial-arts classes and even specific karate coach listings. We still fondly imagine a montage of tiny gis and tiny trophies parading across whoever ran those pages. The FindItLocally community notice board group — the one that pairs our postcode with Wangi Wangi, Rathmines, Arcadia Vale and a half-dozen other shoreline hamlets — used to be where you learned who had a garage sale, who needed a babysitter, and who swore they’d seen a pelican stealing a sandwich.

There’s no scandal here, no gentrification soap opera, just dependable local infrastructure: directories, community clubs and people who like lists. If you need drama, you’ll have to look beyond the noticeboard — our biggest quarrel tends to be over whether the village has too many or too few kids’ sports options. MumsPages would have the receipts, anyway.

Who would hate it here? If you crave anonymous high-rise chaos, you’ll feel claustrophobic in our 1.6 km² of neighbourly surveillance. Who would love it? Families hunting kids’ sports, karate lessons and community clubs; anyone who likes knowing everyone’s name, or at least which local group is running the sausage sizzle next weekend.

We’re Kilaben Bay: small, lake-adjacent, listed in a lot of helpful directories and quietly smug about it. Come for the community notices, stay for the sense that someone — probably on FindItLocally — has already figured out the next fundraiser.
