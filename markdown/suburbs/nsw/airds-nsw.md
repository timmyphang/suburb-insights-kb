# Airds (NSW)

Slug: `airds-nsw`

We roll out of bed like it's game day and head down to Riverside Drive — not for artisanal anything, just to the big church on the corner: St Andrews Anglican Church, 1 Riverside Drive. Back in the day (and as the listings still tell it), Tuesdays in term time at 11:00 AM is Airds Mums & Bubs, and Wednesdays at 10:00 AM is Playtown Supported Playgroup Airds. Georgina from Anglicare is the human beacon you can email (Georgina.lane@anglicare.org.au) or ring on 0409 979 875 if you like schedules with a side of organisation.

We know this suburb by its practical loves: mums’ networks, kid-friendly timetables and the kind of local directories that list mechanics, admin services, sports for kids and even science lessons — MumsPages has us catalogued under 2560 like it's a useful little catalogue of "How to survive parenthood here." Western Sydney Mums waves a friendly flag at our events pages, and for the curious kids among us there's even the Western Sydney International Experience Centre on the list of things to put on the outing rota.

Time Out once did the numbers and, in a headline that made our accountants sit up, noted you could snap up a home in Airds for around $768,000 — outer-southwest arithmetic that made bargain hunters nod approvingly. We mention it as local lore rather than gospel; most of our sources are older, so think of this as the "remember when" edition of suburb research.

Who would hate living here? If you need 24/7 boutique city energy, or you only measure streets by their nearest rooftop champagne brunch, you might blink twice at our version of convenience. But if you like people who know the schedule for every playgroup, who can name the local support services without Googling, and who appreciate a postcode that isn’t trying too hard to be anywhere else — we are your crowd.

So come for the practical networks, settle in for the sensible amenities, and join us at St Andrews on a Tuesday or Wednesday. We’ll be the ones holding the toddler, the timetable and, somehow, the collective sense of humour.
