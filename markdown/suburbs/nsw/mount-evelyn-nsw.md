# Mount Evelyn (NSW)

Slug: `mount-evelyn-nsw`

We wake up like proper locals: boots on, backpacks half-packed, muttering about the bus timetable we barely need because the hill does the work for us. Concrete Playground once pointed out the six-kilometre stretch between Mount Evelyn and Wandin — reachable from Lilydale Station or a roughly 20‑minute bus — and honestly, the downhill walk flanked by ferns, forestry, native birdlife and wildflowers is our subtle flex.

Back in the day Broadsheet ran a few less flattering headlines about us — they mentioned buying "coffee beans from a Mount Evelyn roastery" after last week’s rains and floods, and noted that The Storehouse only regained power and was open for takeaway, with beans available online. They also helpfully listed Mount Evelyn in a water‑quality warning, where authorities advised residents to boil their water as a precaution. Drama? We call that Tuesday.

Who hates living here: people allergic to mud, vagueness and birds with opinionated morning routines. If the idea of a council notice about water quality sends you into a spin, you’ll be filing for a postcode upgrade before morning tea.

Who loves it: walkers who like a proper descent with wildflowers for moral support, folk who secretly enjoy a small town that can lose power and still sell you beans, and anyone who regards a Broadsheet write‑up as free marketing. We’ll meet you at the trailhead, damp socks and all — and yes, we’ll bring the coffee (ordered, murmured, and very, very local).
