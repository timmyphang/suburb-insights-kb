# Chelsea Heights (NSW)

Slug: `chelsea-heights-nsw`

We know Chelsea Heights like the back of our sunburnt hand — even if the internet’s mostly holding onto older snapshots of us. Back in the day (and according to the archives), our local rituals looked like this: kids bolting for a jungle gym called The Stadium at the Chelsea Heights Hotel, while we pretended not to watch them disappear into the climbing nets. As The Urban List once cheerfully pointed out, that place on the corner of Springvale Road and Wells Road came with a dedicated kids’ menu so parents could relax... or at least swap life-hack notes over a pint.

We’re small, but we keep ourselves busy. The Chelsea Heights Community Centre — yes, down at Beazley Reserve, 160 Thames Promenade — has been the true civic engine: hall hire, the community pantry, playgroups and a roster of classes that the centre’s site and Playgroup Victoria have long catalogued. For years the centre has been where we learn, meet, and pretend our craft projects will change the world.

Petty local drama? We’re not immune. The Chelsea Heights Hotel even turned up in Concrete Playground’s roundup when it joined a 12-week pilot anti-sexual-harassment program in 2018. Which is to say: our pub has doubled as a family hub, a nightclub for the “big kids” (Time Out’s description, not ours), and occasionally a venue for doing the right thing — multifaceted, like a slightly overstuffed esky.

Who would hate living here: anyone seeking non-stop inner-city glamour or a Michelin-starred hustle. If you need your weekends curated by influencers or your nightlife to run until dawn, you’ll find our version of a nightclub — polite, local, and likely to close before you’ve made a second espresso — tragically quaint.

Who would love it: families who like a pub with a playground, parents who rely on the community centre and Playgroup Victoria listings, and people who appreciate wetlands and neighbourly goodwill (the centre sits near the Seaford–Edithvale Wetlands, if you’re into morning walks that feel slightly damp and very real).

We’re not flashy. We’re resolutely useful, a little loud on weekends, and proud of it. Come for the jungle gym memories, stay for the community pantry, and leave your expectations of city chic at Springvale Road.
