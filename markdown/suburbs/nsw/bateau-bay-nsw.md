# Bateau Bay (NSW)

Slug: `bateau-bay-nsw`

We know Bateau Bay by the image of a Lululemon-clad mum in a LandCruiser, kids in school bags, two Karens arguing over the disabled bay at Bateau Bay Square at 7:45am while a bloke in boardshorts sips an oat flat white. We wear activewear as a uniform and call it “the lifestyle.” We surf at dawn or at least take a sunrise photo that proves we woke up. We head to the local cafe (yes, the one by the square with the barista who knows your order) for a double oat flat white before the school run. We roll a Subaru Outback or tow with a Toyota Hilux, and everyone pretends the cooler in the back is purely for spontaneous beach snacks. We have an F45 membership. We use it twice a month and pronounce that as commitment.

We are experts in tiny civic wars. We fight over two-hour parking in The Entrance Road stretch. We stage passive-aggressive signage about the new council bin-collection schedule on every letterbox after the change sparks a week of grumpy replies and one post that nets 400 comments. We watched a three-day viral rant about dogs on the beach unfold and then disappear when the publican at Bateau Bay Hotel put on a pie special. We tag friends in every “who remembers” post and then complain when someone ruins the memory with factual corrections.

Our Saturday is procedural. Sunrise surf or a sunrise snap. Coffee at the cafe. Lunch at Bateau Bay Hotel if it’s not too crowded. Sunday follows like a comforting routine: a Cavoodle on the headland walk, a hardware detour to Bunnings for a part we didn’t know we needed, then snags on the barbie while we scroll the local Facebook group and chuckle at the latest strata saga. Our kids do soccer down at the oval. Our neighbours borrow power tools and then leave them in the boot for a week.

We adore this place for its small-town shenanigans and genuine mornings. But we’ll be honest about who would hate it. The inner-city nightclub aficionado with a Brompton who rates bars by DJ creds will loathe us. They’ll despise the triumph of the local pub over any cocktail bar, the two-hour parking limits, and the fact an evening’s entertainment is cheering when a toddler nails the slide at the splash park.

We welcome the weekend warrior who wears leggings like armour. We love the person who thinks an almond flat white is a lifestyle choice, who keeps a cooler ready for 5pm escapes, who values footy at the oval and a friendly complaint about the bins. We’ll save you a park — if you don’t mind us tutting at your parking.
