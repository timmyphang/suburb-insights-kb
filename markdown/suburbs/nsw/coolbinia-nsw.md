# Coolbinia (NSW)

Slug: `coolbinia-nsw`

We’re the sort of suburb that wears its low profile like a comfy cardigan — a bit frayed, very practical, and not remotely interested in being front page fodder.

Morning here looks like this: we shuffle past Walcott Street and remember the one proper shout-out we can find. Back in the day Urban List listed Peachi Nails at 1/340 Walcott Street, Coolbinia WA 6050 — and yes, their Instagram apparently explains “why there’s a massive waitlist just to become clients for this extremely talented duo.” That’s our claim to fame: very polite fingers, very dedicated followers, and a postcode that confuses national editors.

If you’re scrolling Broadsheet hoping for a guide to our best new restaurants, you’ll find plenty about Sydney openings and Melbourne bagels instead. Which is fine — we like being overlooked. It keeps the queues short, the footpaths calm and the neighbours guessing whether that was a new mural or just someone’s laundry.

Who would hate living here? People who need constant hype, awards, and weekend brunch columns. If your idea of a good Saturday is discovering a city-wide “must-visit” that everyone has already Instagrammed, you’ll be impatient.

Who will love it? Folks who prefer a quiet postcode with a surprisingly good nail appointment in its back catalogue, and residents who enjoy being the place the magazines never quite get around to. Come for the understated vibes, stay because no one’s bothering to write about you — and honestly, that’s the point.
