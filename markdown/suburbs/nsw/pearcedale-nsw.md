# Pearcedale (NSW)

Slug: `pearcedale-nsw`

Back in the day we’d kick off the weekend like it’s a wildlife documentary — a slow pilgrimage to Moonlit Sanctuary Wildlife Conservation Park at 550 Tyabb‑Tooradin Road, where the roster reads like an Aussie greatest‑hits: dingoes, kangaroos, wombats and the occasional koala who clearly earns more naps than we do. It made us feel cultured and slightly judged by marsupials.

If we weren’t sneaking a look at the wallabies, we were queueing the pram outside Pearcedale Public Hall — Playgroup Victoria had that listed as one of the village spots (May 28, 2023, if you like receipts). For years the local mums and dads used to live by the Playgroup Victoria map and the PLAYCONNECT+ PDF; we leaned on those listings like a church social for people who’d rather mix baby wipes than wine.

Our commercial scene, such as it is, reads like a very small business directory: MumsPages kept the Rolodex of gyms, sports for kids, home services and the rest of whatever modern rural suburbs need. It’s less “boutique” and more “where do we find a footy class and a plumber?”—and MumsPages had our backs, bless them.

Petty local drama? The data’s polite: no council rumbles, no soap‑opera fireworks. Which suits us. We prefer the kind of friction that involves who gets the prime koala‑watching spot, not who covered the town hall in grafitti.

Who would hate Pearcedale? Nightclub fiends and anyone who needs a 24/7 avocado emporium. If your weekend metric is rooftop bars per square kilometre, we’re not it.

Who would love Pearcedale? Parents, small‑town folks who like a list of actual services on MumsPages, and anyone who thinks a Sunday among kangaroos beats another shopping precinct. We’re a slow‑paced postcard with a great wildlife cast and a public hall that knows your kid’s name. Come for the marsupials, stay because you accidentally joined playgroup and now you’re on the committee.
