# Felixstow (NSW)

Slug: `felixstow-nsw`

Back in the day in Felixstow we measured a big night out by how long the toddler lasted at Felixstowe Leisure Centre (Funtasia) before demanding a snack in the café area. We’re that sort of place: our version of chaos is a soggy cup of tea and a sugar-fuelled miniature emerging triumphant from the ball pit.

We spent our weekdays parsing the Netmums threads — the local “Mums in Felixstowe” chat that pointed us to toddler groups, preschool classes and the town noticeboard — because nothing says community like coordinating who brings the fruit platter. The Felixstowe Library was our quieter ritual, with its children’s corner, weekday Baby Bounce and Storytime sessions; yes, there was even a nearby café and a pay-and-display town car park to argue about in the comments.

We weren’t hardcore. The Felixstowe Opportunity Group used to be the gentle sort of lifeline that made the place: a safe, supportive play space for kids with additional learning needs. That’s our local flex — practical, low-key, and slightly soggy from juice boxes.

Petty drama? The data doesn’t give us a scandal, so our biggest feud was whether Storytime started at 10am or 10:15am. Wild stuff.

Who would hate it here? Anyone allergic to pushchairs, hummed lullabies or coordinated snack rotas. Who’d love it? Families, carers and anyone who values a library that remembers your kid’s favourite book. We wear our small-town, toddler-tolerant badge with pride — and a bib.
