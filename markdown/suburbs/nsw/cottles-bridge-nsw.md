# Cottles Bridge (NSW)

Slug: `cottles-bridge-nsw`

We start the weekend by refreshing the Cottles Bridge Community Notice Board Hub like it’s our weather app — because, honestly, it’s where the suburb’s gossip goes to get a digital haircut. Back in the day the Find It Locally page for 3099 used to list local events, workshops, things to do, businesses, support services, community groups, local produce, products and projects — the sort of thing that makes you proud of where we live and mildly worried about who’s selling what at the next market.

There aren’t any recent listings on file, so we keep treating that hub like a cherished ritual: check it, chuckle, post a question about a workshop that may never happen. No petty council saga for us to rehearse — the data’s quiet, and that suits us.

Who would hate it here? People who need a glossy, always-open entertainment strip and seven-night dining options. Who would love it? Folks after genuine community engagement, local produce and the kind of projects that actually get pinned to a notice board. Find It Locally reminded us of that, even if it’s a little nostalgic now.
