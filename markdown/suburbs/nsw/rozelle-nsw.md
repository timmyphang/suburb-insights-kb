# Rozelle (NSW)

Slug: `rozelle-nsw`

We like to tell people Rozelle is small but deceptive — like a cardigan with sequins stitched in. Back in the day our Saturdays started with the same ritual: queueing (politely, because this is Darling Street) for Eat at Robs’ Oklahoma-style smash burgers out the front of Darling Street Meats, then pretending we were “just popping in” to pick up a baguette from Lode before the tiramisu buns sold out. As Broadsheet and Time Out once pointed out, that was our version of culture.

For years the local guides have been obsessed with our contradictions: the white‑collar charm of Totti’s tucked inside the Three Weeds pub, The Sackville getting a glow‑up and still being the best place for people‑watching over a spritzer, and Fabbrica Bread Shop sneaking into Maloneys Grocer like it owned the block. Concrete Playground even wrote love letters to the Old Rosebud and Lode’s viennoiserie — we did used to queue, and we liked it.

There was talk — if you read the papers — about grand plans for the disused rail yards: the NSW Government unveiled designs for 10 hectares of Rozelle Parklands, more than 3,000 trees, wetlands and playing fields. It was meant to open at the end of 2023, which made for excellent excuse‑making about “I’ll pop down when it opens” for several successive weekends. Meanwhile the White Bay Power Station loomed in the skyline like a dramatic afterthought in every Time Out guide.

If you hate a village that thinks artisan bread is a personality trait, or you can’t cope with the idea of a market at Rozelle Primary School where you’ll find something for your house you didn’t know you needed, this won’t be your cup of tea. If you prefer anonymous high-rises and no fondness for a proper pub corner, you’ll find us insufferable.

But if you love weekend markets, inclusive playgrounds for the kids, a Bay Run amble, or the idea that a tiny suburb can host both gelato sundaes and serious sourdough, then welcome home. We’ll be the ones queueing politely and calling it local charm.
