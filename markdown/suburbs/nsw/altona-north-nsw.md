# Altona North (NSW)

Slug: `altona-north-nsw`

We wake up to the sound of someone else’s HIIT class finishing at Westgate Health Club, then roll past the Westgate Entertainment Centre on the corner of Dohertys Rd & Grieve Pde like it’s our own little town square. We’ve been to Cliffhanger enough times to know which route avoids the kids’ bouldering chaos — that 27m wall really used to make us feel simultaneously brave and small. Time Out and Urban List liked telling strangers about that tower; we used to tell mates it’s the suburb’s version of a skyline.

Weekends were a relay: a lap at BayFit on Mason Street, a quick spar at Weight of the World Fitness, then a caffeine run to Café Cubin — Broadsheet liked Cubin, calling it a low-key neighbourhood spot with a kid-friendly courtyard. As Broadsheet also noted, local makers even caused a fuss in foodie circles: there was a dark cherry liqueur made expressly for a venue by Altona North’s White Possum Distillery, which felt oddly regal for a suburb famous for indoor trampolines and tennis centres.

We’re not saying we’re posh. We’re saying we can queue for a cookie at The Confectionist, then judge each other’s recovery routines in the Westgate Health Club sauna. Sitchu showed us there’s also a warehouse-y side to town — Higher Dress Rental and A Closet for You have given our wardrobe drama a place to live. For parents, MumsPages had the map: Davies Music School, Ippon Karate Academy, Soccajoeys, Tumbles Playhouse and party venues mean birthday season is a local industry.

Local drama? The papers mostly preferred to write lists — best desserts, places to eat, gyms and climbing walls — rather than start feuds. So we’ll own that: if you hate indoor family entertainment, high walls and a suburb that once got flagged as “up-and-coming” thanks to the then-planned West Gate Tunnel, you’ll hate it here.

But who loves Altona North? Us. Fitness nerds, climbing converts, people who like cookies that get press, parents who want activities within shouting distance and anyone who secretly enjoys a suburban centre that does big stuff on a small stage. We know every corner — we’ve clocked the best route between the gym, the climbing wall and the cookie place. We belong here.
