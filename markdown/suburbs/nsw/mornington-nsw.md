# Mornington (NSW)

Slug: `mornington-nsw`

Back in the day, our perfect Mornington weekend looked suspiciously like a travel-magazine moodboard and we liked it that way. We’d stagger into the industrial end where Commonfolk used to roast and brew what Broadsheet cheekily called "arguably the best coffee on the peninsula," then pretend the rest of the day would be earned by trudging a coastal walk.

We weren’t wrong – Time Out and Sitchu kept reminding us there were clifftop strolls (Two Bays Trail, Bushrangers Bay) and Mills Beach for the dramatic shoreline selfie. If we were feeling indulgent Concrete Playground had already boasted about Johnny di Francesco’s two-level 400 Gradi precinct with its Zero Gradi gelato and Gradi Mercato deli — a useful alibi for eating pizza at 11am. And when we wanted to feel properly bougie, Urban List made sure we remembered Jackalope and its moonlight cinema and 30-metre infinity pool (because nothing says "we're on holiday" like an outdoor screening above a vineyard).

Petty local drama? Oh yes. The Arthurs Seat Eagle makeover turned into classic peninsula theatre — Urban List said the Allan government’s upgrades (roller-coaster luge, anyone?) stirred community groups into outrage. We love a shiny new attraction as much as the next person, but also enjoy the prophetic fury of locals who will complain loudest while booking their luxury stay.

Who would hate living here? People who despise brunch-adjacent luxury, hidden whisky bars and restored pubs — Concrete Playground even noted The Bon Vivant's Companion at 150 Main Street with its Japanese-inspired speakeasy behind a false wall — and anyone allergic to coastal wineries, Peninsula Hot Springs, or the very idea of a leisurely weekend that includes both a spa and a pizza.

Who would love it? Families who have always relied on places like Tanti Community House, Natured Kids and South Mornington Preschool for real-world anchors; hikers who live for the walking guides Sitchu and Time Out keep evangelising; and anyone who, like us, enjoys a town that can be equally serious about hot springs, heritage pubs and artisanal gelato. We’ll keep the towel by the pool and the second slice of pizza warm.
