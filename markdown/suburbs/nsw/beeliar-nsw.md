# Beeliar (NSW)

Slug: `beeliar-nsw`

We start our weekend with muddy sneakers and a sense of superiority: a 5km lap of the Spectacles Aboriginal Heritage Trail in Beeliar Regional Park. Back in the day Urban List called it "a lesson in history as much as it is exercise" and they weren’t wrong — paperbark trees, a proper bird hide and information boards mean we can pretend our dog is learning about culture while we scroll Instagram.

By mid-morning we’re congratulating ourselves for not living on a postage-stamp block. Old Sitchu listings remind us why: 11 Coppito Circle once dazzled the internet as a "beautiful family home with pool" on a generous 697sqm corner block (four beds, two baths, yes please). Nearby, 9 Possum Glade used to be the flex — a double‑storey, architecturally designed place with a downstairs room and its own powder room, reverse‑cycle air conditioning and a gas fireplace. The humble brag is real here: lawns, pools and room for a broom cupboard that isn’t also a pantry.

Petty local drama? There’s not much scandal in the snippets we’ve seen — just the eternal suburb rivalry over who’s got the bigger block and the better birdwatching spot. If you hate information boards, bird hides and people who own above-ground pools, you’ll loathe it.

If you love slow Saturday walks under paperbarks, a bird hide to eavesdrop on rosellas, and real backyard room for a trampoline (or a proper pool), this is our kind of modest paradise. We’ll be the ones waving from the Spectacles loop, towel in hand, smug as anything.
