# Frankston South (NSW)

Slug: `frankston-south-nsw`

Back in the day our Saturday started with the same choreography: we’d wobble down Nepean Highway and queue for Commonfolk — the Frankston outpost at 465 Nepean Highway that opened in 2021 and used to be our reliable brunch excuse. Then someone would suggest “one more bite” at Southside Social (remember the Fatty 5-0?) on 433 Nepean Highway and the day would officially derail.

We’re proud locals who know the weird little balance here: the waterfront, the pier and Oliver’s Hill give you the coastal postcard, while Bayside Shopping Centre at 2 Olsen St with its Myer, Target, Coles, Kmart, Aldi and Hoyts used to be where you proved you were practical. The Lona Group’s Hotel Lona used to hide in centre Frankston for when a big group wanted woodfired pizza and steaks without getting moral about it. For families, the Frankston South Community and Recreation Centre was the sort of place you could actually find a playgroup on a Wednesday — and yes, MamaTribe and Playgroup Victoria had listings to prove it.

We’ve hosted culture too: the South Side Festival and the Australian Sand Sculpting Championships used to turn the foreshore and Frankston Arts Centre into proof we could do arty and seaside at once. And if you ever fancied pretending you’d moved to the peninsula proper, there were Sitchu listings for grand homes like 191 Humphries Road and caravan spots on 2 Robinsons Road to keep things theatrical.

Who would hate living here? People who need to be whispered about, who demand every latte be caption-worthy, or who think a day is wasted if it doesn’t involve a tiny sourdough crumb photographed from above. Also, if you loathe a place that’s equal parts beach day, shopping run and community centre bake sale, this isn’t your postcode.

Who would love it? Families who actually use the community centres and playgroups, festival-goers who want neon installations and sand sculptures on their weekend itinerary, and anyone who appreciates that our Nepean strip used to balance burger indulgence with beach sunsets. We’ll keep the pier, you keep the sunscreen — and the rest is Frankston South, in all its comfortably messy glory.
