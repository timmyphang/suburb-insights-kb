# Dundas (NSW)

Slug: `dundas-nsw`

We start our Saturdays like proper Dundas locals: coffee in one hand, a pram in the other, and the kids primed for aquatic chaos at the Philip Ruddock Water Playground in Dundas Park. For years that splash zone — the one ParraParents and Ryde District Mums keep insisting is the best free fun on a hot day — has been our unofficial town square, right by the sports fields at Curtis Oval. Yes, council did a bit of asbestos remediation at the park once, and they were very clear there was no health risk, so you can judge us for the sunscreen choices instead.

Our weekend loop is spectacularly unglamorous: fence in at Anna Maria King Park for the tiny ones, a quick wander through Williams Reserve afterwards (apparently it’s the pleasant spot to go after brunch at nearby cafes, if you remember there used to be one called Big Tree House near the station), and then a detour to Dundas Library where the under‑fives used to get their weekly reading fix. We’ve even got the Little Vikings play area at Vikings Sports Club for when you want the kids to wear themselves out while you inspect the menu with the steely eyes of a seasoned parent.

There’s no high‑stakes council scandal in our files, no late‑night rave culture — mostly because the articles about us are nostalgic and practical. The data reads like a lovingly dog‑eared parenting blog and a property listing: Sitchu telling us about light‑filled duplexes near Yates Avenue Public School, and mums arguing about which water park has the best shade.

Who would hate living here? If you need a rooftop cocktail bar every other block, or crave wandering into an all‑hours cultural precinct, we’re probably not for you. If you can’t handle gated playgrounds, fenced reserves and the gentle tyranny of school pickup times, move along.

Who would love it here? Families who actually appreciate free water play, fenced parks, a small library with baby‑change facilities, and a neighbourhood that runs on playdates and weekend footy. We may not be flashy, but we’re mum‑blog approved, park‑map certified and quietly brilliant at raising the next generation of sandpit CEOs.
