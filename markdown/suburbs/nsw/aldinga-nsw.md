# Aldinga (NSW)

Slug: `aldinga-nsw`

We know the angle: wind, sand in your shoes and someone’s car where the tide eats it. We’re the lot that can smell salt and sourdough at the same time — and yes, that’s because Home Grain Bakery used to occupy a heritage‑listed former general store on Old Coach Road, opened by Cara and Toff West and praised for its bread and steak pies. Back in the day Broadsheet even wrote us up for it, like we were a particularly tasteful postcard.

Our morning looks like this: a slow shuffle to the beach, a muttered countdown until the ocean fog clears, then a trip past the refurbished Aldinga Beach Surf Life Saving Club — later reborn as the 120‑seat Silver Sands Beach Club with a wine list curated by Nick Stock and pizzas and seafood‑leaning dishes. It was dramatic: the rebuild after storm damage gave the dining room floor‑to‑ceiling ocean views, and someone from Broadsheet couldn’t stop smiling about it.

Petty local drama? We’ve had that too — Pedestrian TV once ran a clip of someone leaving their car below the high tide mark at Aldinga Beach. It’s the kind of headline that makes the town laugh and quietly check whether their own driveway is actually above the high‑water line. There was also a moment when plans for The Break Surf & Stay — a $100‑million surf‑park and 35‑villa “surf and stay” with an 11,700‑sqm lagoon — got planning consent from the City of Onkaparinga. Big ideas land here; sometimes they’re still in the paperwork.

Who would hate living here? People who can’t abide sand in everything, anyone allergic to small coastal-town quirks, and those who prefer their nightlife without wine bars that used to be 1850s buildings — we’re thinking of Fall From Grace, once reimagined by Jimmy Toone and Jo Leal into a 25‑seat European wine bar. Also, if you can’t handle bushland, our scrub country Airbnbs (Kestrels Nest, Coach Light Tiny House) with their showstopping outdoor baths will confuse you.

Who would love it? Wine people (Under the Sun’s converted barn hosted Brash Higgins, Golden Child and Poppelvej), adventurers who book flights from Aldinga Airfield on Colville Road, families who use the Aldinga Community Centre on Stewart Ave, and anyone who thinks a regional Fringe night — like South Coast Circus at Lot 5, 13 Rainbird Court — beats the city’s frantic hum. We’re sand in the sheets, but we’re also exactly the kind of place you didn’t know you needed.
