# Prestons (NSW)

Slug: `prestons-nsw`

We wake up in Prestons and, no, we don’t rush for latte art or sunset rooftops — we head for treasure. Back in the day Savers launched Sydney’s first mega-thrift in Hoxton Park (Hoxton Park Central, T4, 2 Lyn Parade, Prestons, NSW 2170) and suddenly our weekend ritual was a competitive rummage through 1,590 square metres of pre-loved gems. The warehouse was stocked with something like 100,000 items, many under $10, and yes, it doubled as a donation centre supporting not-for-profits — which, frankly, makes us feel better about the mountain of things we then cart home.

By mid-morning we might detour past 424/458 Hoxton Park Road where LCC Ice Rink was listed as the place you could do everything from ice discos to inclusive skate sessions, ice hockey and theatre on ice. Imagine telling people we have disco nights on actual ice — it sounds posh until someone faceplants in sequins. Time Out even noted the place sits about 40 minutes west of the CBD, which is our polite way of saying we’re close enough to the city to be relevant and far enough away to not give a stuff.

If you’re after local scandal, the papers mostly flirted with “huge thrift store opens” energy and “ice rink does cool things” headlines. There’s less high-society kerfuffle and more earnest joy in bagging a ridiculous find or booking an inclusive skate session. That’s our version of nightlife.

Who would hate living here? People who need their suburb to be a constant stream of boutique coffee drops and curated laneway murals — they’ll look at our superstore and rink and declare it “too raw.” Who would love it? Bargain hunters, skating nerds, families who need something to do on a Saturday that isn’t another brunch queue. We’re the postcode for practical joy: give us a warehouse of treasures and an ice disco and we’ll call it culture.
