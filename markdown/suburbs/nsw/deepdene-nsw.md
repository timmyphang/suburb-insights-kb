# Deepdene (NSW)

Slug: `deepdene-nsw`

We wake up in Deepdene, NSW, and the local buzz is so refined it fits neatly into a Melbourne Mums forum thread. Back in the day our online presence amounted to a listing on Melbourne Mums — that official support group and curated directory where mothers swap everything from house-cleaning tips to yoga recs. Grand metropolitan exposure, honestly.

Our weekend routine is low-key: instead of a cluttered Instagram map, we refresh the Forums Archive and the Topics Archive, marvel at how the forums still let you post as a guest, and note that the whole lot contains — drumroll — three topics. Glamorous, huh? The most cultured recommendation to pass through our digital veins once was a thread about good cafes in the inner west, which name-checked Wee Jeanie in Yarraville and Alfa Bakehouse. Note: those were mentioned in the thread about other suburbs, not ours — so don’t go thinking Deepdene is secretly an inner-west cafe hub.

No real local petty drama to gossip about (the archives are blissfully prosaic), which suits us fine. Who would hate living here? People who need a constant stream of brunch recommendations and podcast controversies — our internet footprint is resolutely maternal, practical and quietly curated.

Who would love it here? Families who value a supportive network over trend-chasing. If your idea of community is well-organised forum threads, a curated directory, and the comforting knowledge you can post anonymously — Deepdene’s for you. We’ll meet you on Melbourne Mums.
