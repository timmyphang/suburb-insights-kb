# Jerrabomberra (NSW)

Slug: `jerrabomberra-nsw`

We know how this goes: we lace up for the Jerrabomberra Wetlands Walk, plan a slow 1.5-hour loop, and get politely shushed by a Latham’s Snipe doing whatever majestic snipe things they do. As Concrete Playground notes, the wetlands have the bird hides, waterways and the whole “we’re hiding from the city” vibe — and yes, they started forming back in 1964, which makes them older than half our roundabout system.

By mid-morning we’re split into two camps: the folks who walk up to the reserve at the foot of Mount Jerrabomberra and the ones pencilling in Tuesday playgroup at the Jerrabomberra Community Centre (10:00–12:00 — contact Jerrabomberraplaygroup@gmail.com if you’re feeling brave). The kids learn craft and singing; the adults compare who can sew up a school uniform fastest — or who knows a tailor. Speaking of tailors, Jerrabomberra Clothing Alterations turned up in the listings in 2025, which is handy for anyone who treated formal wear like a wearable tent.

Our social season reads like a small-town rom-com: the Jerrabomberra High School P&C asking for baked goods for Carols @ Jerra (Saturday 6 December) while quietly scheduling an AGM for 6 November 2025. The Rotary Club of Jerrabomberra fills in the rest of the calendar with Jerra Community Day (14 February 2026) and an Australia Day 2026 breakfast, because nothing says local pride like being roped into a sausage sizzle with a side of committee minutes.

Who would hate it here? People who despise communal responsibility, or anyone who hears “call for baked goods” and thinks it’s a metaphor. Also, if you’re allergic to wetlands, reserve views and being gently guilted into volunteering, this isn’t your postcode.

Who would love it? Families buying into the “Jerra” lifestyle — think Numeralia Drive’s family-friendly pocket next to reserve at Mount Jerrabomberra — birdwatchers with binoculars, and people who want a place where the clinic is called The Surgery At Jerra (1/37 Jerrabomberra Parkway) and somebody always knows who’s running karate this term. We’re small, organised and a little bit smug about our wetlands. Come for the birds; stay for the bake stall.
