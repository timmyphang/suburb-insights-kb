# Gladstone Park (NSW)

Slug: `gladstone-park-nsw`

We know Gladstone Park, Balmain — the one on Darvall St, Sydney 2041 — by the sticky handprints on the playground equipment and the way the picnic rugs arrange themselves like small, competitive colonies. Back in the day Concrete Playground put us on the harbour-side playlist as the local green space to bring kids (and the suspiciously eager picnic brigade). We wear that badge with pride.

Weekends used to look like this in our heads: kids at the playground, someone folding up a rug, someone else squinting toward the bowls green where Time Out once described the Gladstone Park Bowling Club as cheap drinks and an old‑school vibe. That sentence alone is a mood board. The bowling club on Darvall St was always the kind of place that made suburbia feel slightly cinematic — like lawn bowls had a soundtrack.

We also hosted art: TURF rolled through, an installation of work by public‑housing residents that graced Gladstone Park (and later the Surry Hills Library Forecourt), complete with a launch party. For a minute we were a gallery with picnic crumbs.

Who would hate it here? If you loathe small-scale community theatre, children with sticky fingers, or the faint whiff of nostalgia, you’ll find it trying. Who would love it? Families who like playground logistics and people who appreciate a lawn that remembers its locals.

As locals, we’ll keep tending the green, swapping stories about past events, and quietly enjoying our perfectly ordinary, very own slice of Balmain.
