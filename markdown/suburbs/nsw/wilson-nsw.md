# Wilson (NSW)

Slug: `wilson-nsw`

We’re standing on Wilson Road, where a Sitchu wild‑swimming guide used to tell outsiders to hop the train to Heathcote Station and walk two minutes to the trailhead if they wanted Kingfisher Pool. Back in the day that felt like a secret; now it’s just our Monday‑morning routine in memory — a brisk walk, a dip, and the smug shake of soggy swimmers who still know the path.

Full disclosure: there aren’t any fresh bits of press about Wilson, NSW. Everything people write about us these days is retrograde — Sitchu’s lists and travel roundups — so consider this more of a fondly grainy postcard than a lifestyle edit. Sitchu even put “Wilson’s Rest” on its tiny‑cabin roundup: cosy loft beds, a firepit and valley views for couples wanting to disappear for a weekend. We’ve packed little suitcases there and judged each other’s campfire hot‑dog skills with undue seriousness.

If you’re into local history, Time Out once reminded the internet that a place called Wilson’s Lebanese Restaurant in Redfern has been doing hummus and shish kebabs since the 1950s — a reminder that “Wilson” turns up in more than one postcode and sometimes carries proper old‑school charm.

Who would hate living here? People who need perpetual hype. If you require a new café’s launch post every other week, or can’t abide an Instagram feed that’s mostly trees and mud tracks, you’ll be bored. Ditto anyone allergic to quiet — the closest thing we have to nightlife is arguing over which tiny cabin is the best for stargazing.

Who loves it here? Wild swimmers and people who measure weekends in trails, pools and firepits. Lovers of off‑grid tiny‑cabin romance will nod at “Wilson’s Rest” and smile; anyone who thinks a two‑minute walk from Heathcote Station to a trailhead is a perfectly civilised commute will fit right in.

So yes, we’re quiet. We share our name with beaches Time Out once crowned and cabins Sitchu once coveted. We’ll take the lack of press over the crowds any day — more room on Wilson Road for us, the smug swimmers.
