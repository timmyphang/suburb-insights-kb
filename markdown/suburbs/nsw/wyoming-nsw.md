# Wyoming (NSW)

Slug: `wyoming-nsw`

Back in the day we’d pretend Wyoming was the sleepy little secret between Gosford and “oh, that bit with the shops”, and honestly the internet mostly played along. There aren’t any fresh local scoops lately — most of what turns up is a bit older — so consider this our affectionate, slightly smug look back.

A typical Saturday used to feel like this: we’d mosey toward the Wyoming Shopping Centre, nip down to Gosford CBD if we needed something a bit more dramatic, then remind ourselves commuting wasn’t tragic — Sitchu even pointed out how handy the Narara and Gosford train stations are for hopping toward Sydney. That was our humble flex: close enough to the action, far enough to avoid traffic tantrums.

Our small-town soap opera? It never got boring. Pedestrian TV once ran a bonkers yarn about Natty Hagood — “a Wyoming ski instructor” — who literally crowdfunded surgery after a branch incident. We still tell that one at barbies when we want to see people blink. And for the mini-me locals, WyomingKidsGuide.com used to be our bible: the KidsScoop newsletter, calendars of family events and the Ford Wyoming Center trailed as the venue that could scramble any weekend into a circus, concert or kids’ show. Nostalgia, coupons and glitter glue: we had it all.

Who would hate living here? If you’re the sort who wants celebrity ranch drama — the kind Junkee wrote about when Kanye flew people to a mountain resort or Jeffree Star started selling yak meat from a Wyoming ranch — this is probably not your vibe. We’re more “community calendar and school-sports” than private-jet listening party.

Who would love it here? Families who want a steady stream of kid-friendly stuff, people who prize proximity to Gosford CBD without living in full-on metropolitan hustle, and those who enjoy the small-town characters that make every local café queue worth the wait (remember, we’re looking at older listings here, so that queue might be different now).

We’re proud of our low-key rep. We don’t pretend to be Wyoming, USA; we’re the NSW one with train stations, a shopping centre and a surprisingly solid calendar of family fun. Come for the convenience, stay for the stories.
