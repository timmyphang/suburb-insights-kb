# Woongarrah (NSW)

Slug: `woongarrah-nsw`

We know the way the sun hits Sparks Road at 9:15 on a Thursday — because for years that’s when we line up with prams and reusable nappies. Back in the day the Little Lighthouse Playgroup (listed as Warnervale Mackillop Lighthouse) used to meet at St Mary of the Cross, Mackillop Catholic Parish Church on 91 Sparks Road, Woongarrah — parking on Mataram Road, gate access if you knew the secret handshake. Fortnightly sessions, Thursdays at 9:30 AM: that was our unofficial civic calendar.

If you scroll the old playgroup listings you’ll see we were even showing up on Norfolk Island pages as a nearby option. That’s right — we made it onto someone else’s “other playgroups nearby” list. Humble brag.

We’re not big on nightlife gossip or weekend markets in the archives; the local press of record here is baby wipes and laminated sign‑in sheets. Mum Walk’s listings made it clear too: this postcode is proud of its pram‑walking movement. Free weekly walks, stay‑and‑plays, and a platform to start a local group — we’ve always been the kind of place where making mum mates is basically infrastructure.

Petty drama? The only scandal you’ll find in these snippets is whether the gate from Mataram Road was unlocked on time. No council petitions, no celebrity coffee wars — just the gentle politics of who brought the best snack for the kids. If that sounds dull, congratulations, you would have been the drama we never needed.

Who would hate living here: anyone allergic to scheduled toddler socialising, fortnightly Thursday rigmarole, or hearing the phrase “stay-and-play” more than once a week. If you’re here for avant‑garde nightlife or an en vogue café crawl, you’ll be disappointed — our archival moment is in bubbles of baby talk.

Who would love it: families with children 0–6, mums looking for a pram crew, anyone who values a community that shows up at 9:30 on a Thursday and stays for conversation and a biscuit. We’ll keep the gate unlocked on Mataram Road — someone always brings a spare cup of tea.
