# Glenwood (NSW)

Slug: `glenwood-nsw`

Back in the day, Friday mornings in Glenwood looked less like a suburb and more like an orchestra rehearsal for small people. We used to pack the Glenwood Community Hub at 72 Glenwood Park Drive and turn up — clocks set, nappies at the ready — for the Glenwood Gumnuts session that Playgroup NSW listed as Friday weekly at 10:00 AM.

We weren’t anonymous; the PDF case study even gave us a roll call. Nicole ran the show, attendance averaged about 18 adults and 24 children, and the whole thing had been operating for about 20 years. Playgroup NSW treated us like a success story, featuring Glenwood Gumnuts in their Community Changemakers collection — which is code for “we’ve been doing this for a long time and we’re quietly proud of it.”

If you want proof we take kiddie logistics seriously, look no further than the directories. MumsPages runs at least three Glenwood pages — a general businesses-and-services hub, a Sports for Kids list, and a Long Day Care Centres page — so you can cross-reference who does soccer for toddlers and who will accept your overfilled lunchbox.

We’re the sort of place where neighbouring playgroups keep a polite GPS distance: Marayong’s Hi Ma Wa Ri page notes Glenwood Gumnuts as roughly 5.33 km away. Yes, we measure suburb rivalry in kilometres between story time sessions now.

Who would hate living here? People who crave spontaneous late-night bars, or those who prefer their weekend plans to involve anything other than session times and snack rotations. If you recoil at the sight of another person with a stroller and a thermos, you’ll find Glenwood a bit claustrophobic — in the most organised way possible.

Who’d love it? Parents who want actual community, volunteers who like running things that matter (we see you, Nicole), and anyone who believes suburbs should come with schedules and a PDF to prove their credentials. For years the local playgroup scene was our defining feature — and honestly, we wouldn’t have it any other way.
