# Dean Park (NSW)

Slug: `dean-park-nsw`

We can still hear the echo of tiny shoes in the hallways — or at least we like to pretend we can, because that’s how we remember Dean Park. Back in the day Compass Playgroup used to be the sort of place that set the tempo: their site said they relocated to the Dean Park Pre‑School Centre and shared space with Dean Park Nursery, complete with a dedicated room, cloakroom and outdoor play area. We can picture the snack table like it’s a local landmark.

Mornings here used to smell like someone had already packed tomorrow’s lunch — the kind of suburban rhythm that made school runs a community production. Compass’s “A Day at Compass Playgroup” page described free play, active play and group time under the Curriculum for Excellence Early Level, which is either reassuring or intimidating depending on how many pushies you’ve mastered.

There’s not much petty council drama in our folder of memories — mostly directories. For years MumsPages listed everything a parent might need in postcode 2761: party and event providers, swimming lessons for kids, pest control services and pregnancy and birth support. If you were planning a birthday, learning to swim or trying to convince a bug it had picked the wrong house, the internet used to have a tidy list for that. Not exactly spicy, but useful in a “we all survive community life together” way.

Who would hate living here? People who crave late-night glamour or an endless stream of boutique worries. If your ideal Saturday is an experimental cocktail bar crawl, this suburb’s parenting directories and preschool logistics will read like a sleep aid.

Who’d love it here? If you’re the sort of person who appreciates a proper local pre‑school memory, a bulletin board of swimming lessons, and the comforting certainty that somewhere there’s a list of reliable pest control numbers — this is your postcode. We’ll keep the story simple: for families and anyone who measures life in snack times and swim classes, Dean Park has been quietly doing its thing.
