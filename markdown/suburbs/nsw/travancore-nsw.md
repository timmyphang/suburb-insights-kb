# Travancore (NSW)

Slug: `travancore-nsw`

We’ll be honest: we’re the suburb everyone forgets until someone mentions Flemington and then goes, “Oh right — the tiny Travancore bit.” Back in the day our press clippings were all nostalgia — Broadsheet remembered So & So as the local living-room cafe, Urban List wrote up Phat Milk at 208 Mt Alexander Road for its shish barak and maple bacon and strawberry “smuffin”, and property lists pointed out that for the price of a tiny CBD shoebox you could get a unit or townhouse among our leafy streets and art-deco apartments.

A typical morning used to look like this in the guidebooks: push past the repurposed furniture at So & So (Broadsheet’s description), wave at the dog-friendly garden, then stroll along Mt Alexander Road where you’d find Phat Milk’s Arabian-leaning sweets and savouries. If you were more domestic-life than brunch-life, the notes also flagged Fit Mums Physio operating out of the Moonee Valley Health and Fitness Studio at 376/378 Mt Alexander Rd — pregnancy Pilates, mums & bubs fitness and all the pelvic-strength talk that keeps our prams in formation.

There’s not much petty local drama in the clippings — they’re more keen to remind you of “racecourse fame” and affordability than any council theatrics — which suits us. We like being quietly smug about good city access without the queue for the nearest artisanal anything.

Who’d hate living here? If you need 24/7 bright lights, a perpetual buzz and an insta-perfect lane every five steps, Travancore will make you stab your map. If you measure suburbs by buzzy weekend markets and rooftop bars, we’re the apologetic cousin who gives you a polite wave and directions.

Who’d love it? People who want to borrow city convenience without mortgaging a kidney. Fans of art-deco charm, leafy streets, clever value (units and townhouses were name-checked for a reason), families who appreciate local physiotherapy options, and anyone who thinks a small suburb with racecourse cred and a couple of memorable hole-in-the-guidebook spots is exactly the sort of place worth keeping a little secret.

Note: our clips here are older pieces — so think “remember when” rather than “head to” when you’re reading the menu.
