# Stanmore (NSW)

Slug: `stanmore-nsw`

Back in the day — and by that we mean every profile our archive could rustle up — Stanmore felt like the Inner West’s polite, well-fed aunt: leafy streets, a clutch of grand Victorians and the odd Federation cottage where we keep our best plants.

Our morning used to start with the bakery holy trinity. Broadsheet and Concrete Playground loved gawking at Pantry Story on 336 Parramatta Road — lines for pandan buns and the fabled “brochi” were practically a local landmark. Nearby, Ard Bakery (Christiana Daaboul) at 2A Stafford Street got the Hot List nod from Broadsheet for doing creative, plant-leaning Lebanese bakes. We’d hear about Hearthe — Christopher Thé’s cake shop on 16 Douglas Street — and nod solemnly like we understood why paperbark cake sounds like haute bushwalking.

We’re also the kind of suburb that quietly puffs up when someone mentions Sixpenny or Meaty’s turning up at 98 Percival Rd after “nearly three decades without a local butcher,” as Broadsheet phrased it. Tenth Muse, Brighter Coffee at 102 Northumberland Avenue, Mas Tinto, Paper Cup and the mysterious Sakeshop all got their mentions. Even the humble “Stanno Maccas” has its place in Junkee’s heart — because where else do you recover from degustation?

Petty local drama? Our snippets don’t give us a juicy council kerfuffle, so we’ll admit defeat there. The data’s basically a tasteful menu of past and present places — no shouted letters to the editor about bike lanes, sorry.

Who would hate living here: anyone who needs a nightclub on their doorstep, or insists suburbs must shout to be alive. We’re not loud; we’re quietly smug about our kimchi toasties and native cakes.

Who would love it: families and couple-types who value trees, a short commute (less than 8 kilometres to the city, according to Inner West Mums) and food obsessives who memorise addresses — 336 Parramatta Road, 2A Stafford Street, 98 Percival Rd. As Broadsheet, Time Out and the guidebooks keep telling us: we’re small, leafy and full of very good things to eat. We’ll take that, thanks.
