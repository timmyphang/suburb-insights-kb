# Hamlyn Terrace (NSW)

Slug: `hamlyn-terrace-nsw`

Hamlyn Terrace, NSW — back when our nappies were still a weekly threat to the washing machine, we lived by one sacred bookmark: Playgroup NSW. Back in the day we’d type our postcode into their “find a playgroup” tool, flick through the filters and commit to whichever session promised the least crying and the most biscuits.

Our Wednesday mornings looked suspiciously similar no matter which hall we were in: a cluster of nervous grown-ups holding paper cups, kids already negotiating toy politics, and us quietly thanking the Playgroup NSW site for making it all less chaotic. The site even explained “playgroups for families with children birth to school age” — yes, that’s literally us, and yes, we leaned into it. When the weird seasonal panic hit, PlayConnect+ was the name we saw for extra support; when we needed reassurance, we clicked the Contact page for a bit of human help.

Local drama? Honestly, the biggest feud was which playgroup had the best biscuit roster. The rest of Hamlyn Terrace’s scandal sheet was largely mum-and-bub-level: swapped recipes, coordinated naptimes, the occasional push to try a new session.

Who would hate it here: anyone allergic to sticky hands, communal singing, and the idea that your social life runs on a roster. Who’d love it: the exact crowd Playgroup NSW wrote about — families with kids from birth to school age who want practical help, a bit of company, and a website that does half the organising for them. We’ll keep the tea warm.
