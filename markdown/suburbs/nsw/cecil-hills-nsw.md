# Cecil Hills (NSW)

Slug: `cecil-hills-nsw`

Back in the day we measured our weekends in playgroup timetables and the distance to the nearest patch of actual bush. If you turn up here at 9:30 on a Tuesday you’ll know exactly what we mean — Cecil Hills Community Centre, 5 Sandringham Drive, is where our small-town choreography happens. If you haven’t RSVP’d by emailing playgroup.cecilhills@gmail.com, prepare to be asked politely but firmly where you’ve been.

We like to think we’ve got the best of both worlds: organised morning routines and excuses to get out of the house. Playgroup NSW lists our Tuesday weekly session like it’s a civic duty, and even the Greenway Park Japanese KIZUNA page trots out our details as a nearby neighbour. CAPS Ashcroft Primary’s playgroup page reminds us we sit in the same local orbit (3.62 km away) — yes, we’re that close-knit your kid’s kinder-friend-of-a-friend is probably on the sign-in sheet.

If you’re the sort who needs fellow conspirators to start a mother’s group, we salute Jemma — the BabyCenter thread shows she was trying to kick one off around here, which, frankly, is peak Cecil Hills energy. There’s a comforting predictability to it: someone posts, someone emails, someone brings the biscuits.

When we need to leave the organised chaos behind, Concrete Playground points out the Spotted Gums Loop down in the southern end of the Western Sydney Parklands — it “heads deep into the bush near Cecil Hills.” Perfect for pretending you’re off-grid for an hour between nap time and pick-up.

Who would loathe living here? If weekly routines make you twitch and the idea of a Tuesday 9:30 meet-up fills you with dread, then, yes, Cecil Hills is not your kind of suburb. We run on school terms, playgroup fees ($40 a term or $5 a week for a casual visit, as the listing says), and the quiet competence of coordinated coffee-less chaos.

Who’ll love it? Parents who appreciate a good schedule, families who like to swap tips, and anyone who wants a quick bush fix without a long drive. We’re a tidy little social calendar wrapped around a loop of spotted gums — predictable, helpful, and quietly proud.
