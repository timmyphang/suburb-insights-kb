# Skye (NSW)

Slug: `skye-nsw`

We live in Skye, NSW — the postcode that’s less a sleepy suburb and more a name that keeps turning up in headlines and hotel brochures. Back in the day, our town didn’t need a marketing team; the internet did our branding for us.

Our morning routine used to be a simple flex: tell people we’re from Skye and watch their eyes do a double-take. Were we talking about the Skye Suites where the MAFS cast bunked in 2024 at Green Square? The SKYE Suites Parramatta reviewers praised for feeling like “new apartments” with an ice cave–themed pool? Or maybe the Sky Bar opulence people gush about on Broadsheet and Urban List? Take your pick — all perfectly valid answers.

Petty local drama, you ask? We don’t start it; the internet does. Our name gets tangled in influencer sagas — Pedestrian.TV followed Skye Wheatley’s cosmetic surgery story and the subsequent legal fight with trolls — so sometimes you can’t tell whether you’re a suburb or a trending hashtag. Meanwhile Urban List reminds us there’s a Skye & Callum podcast out there promising unfiltered chaos, because obviously our name needs its own content vertical.

Who would hate living here? People who crave anonymity. If you like being plain-old anonymous, move to a postcode without a rooftop bar named Sky and a hotel chain called Skye. You’ll miss celebrity cameos, MAFS check-ins, and the occasional cocktail called the Blood and Skye (shout-out to Antico’s theatrical drink, as Broadsheet noted).

Who would love it here? Folks who enjoy a little meta-irony: hotel lovers who’ve read Concrete Playground’s take on SKYE Suites Parramatta, pop-culture addicts who follow Pedestrian.TV and Urban List, and anyone who likes living somewhere that sounds like both a postcard and a podcast title.

We’re proud of our name, even if the rest of the country keeps confusing us for a rooftop, a suite, or a reality-TV cast home. Come for the ambiguity, stay because you’ve already told everyone you live in Skye and now you’ve got to see it through.
