# Orange Grove (NSW)

Slug: `orange-grove-nsw`

Back in the day — and by “back in the day” we mean everything our old clippings could find — we used to think the height of Lilyfield glamour was queuing for organic kale on a Saturday. We still remember the Orange Grove Market filling the Orange Grove Public School playground like it owned the place: stalls, truss tomatoes, technicolour capsicums and Bowen’s bacon and egg roll turning early birds into devotees.

Our Saturday routine, when the archives say we had one, went like this: stumble out, dodge the pram posse, wander past the corner of Perry Street and Balmain Road to the market that Time Out and Sitchu both treated like a local institution (Sat mornings, 8am–1pm). Concrete Playground used to boast it’s a short hop from the Bay Run — so you could do a jog, buy berries and then eat bacon and egg roll number two in public, which is peak Inner West multitasking.

We’ll admit we miss the suburban theatre of it all: Orange Grove Public School hosting movie nights during the holidays, and the Orange Grove Hotel at 311 Balmain Road doing what suburban pubs do best — meal deals, seniors specials, meat raffles and trivia nights that packed the room on game days. It all reads like a local romcom script, except the extras kept stealing the snacks.

There’s no proper scandal here in the files — just a steady, smug parade of farmers’ stalls and community nights. Elle Halliwell, bless her, was quoted strolling the markets as if she’d discovered a private garden; the press kept telling us this was one of Sydney’s best weekend rituals. It’s charming, predictable and a little smug — like a cardigan with elbow patches.

Who would hate it? If your idea of recreation is a rooftop infinity pool with a DJ and an espresso machine that gives you a diploma, Orange Grove will feel like a slow-moving postcard. Who’d love it? Families, early-rising market hoarders, Bay Run runners who need snack procurement, and anyone who thinks a meat raffle is peak entertainment.

We’ll keep the market memories warm — the articles may be older, but someone’s still got Bowen’s roll wrapped in paper somewhere, and that’s a heritage listing in our book.
