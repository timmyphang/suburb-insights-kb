# Tacoma (NSW)

Slug: `tacoma-nsw`

We live in Tacoma, NSW — or at least that’s what we tell people before Google redirects them to the other Tacoma and the internet goes full identity crisis on us. Back in the day the only local stories that showed up were about Point Defiance Park, the Museum of Glass and a very committed under-the-sea bowling experience at UNCLE BUCK'S FISH BOWL AND GRILL (yes, with novelty crocodile and octopus ball returns). Concrete Playground thought it was one of the ten most unique alleys in the world. Lucky us: famous by association.

Our weekend vibe, according to the hits, is equal parts family hikes and arty detours. ParentMap’s itineraries shove you towards kid-friendly trails (Prairie Line Trail, Puget Creek Trail, Wilson Way Bridge) and the kind of museum district where Dale Chihuly’s blown glass gets an affectionate standing ovation. Time Out also has a whole guide for “where to go, eat & sleep” — it’s thorough, it’s busy, and it’s definitely about the other Tacoma’s nightlife and Spanish Ballroom listings, not our quiet cul-de-sac.

Local services that made the cut were the wholesome kind: Our Pod Family’s trifecta — The Co-Lab, Tacoma Children's School and The Pod Works — complete with addresses (The Co-Lab — 3808 N 27th St; Tacoma Children's School — 3917 N 26th St; The Pod Works at 7024 27th St W). They even ran summer camps for 2026. So if you’re into co-working-with-childcare logistics, the internet approves.

Petty drama? None on file — which is probably for the best. The narrative the web feeds us is gently parental, slightly artsy and oddly boisterous about novelty bowling.

Who would hate living here? People who demand a pulsating late-night scene — Time Out’s club listings are elsewhere, mate. Who would love it? Families who want easy kid stuff, pod-work parents who need childcare solutions, and anyone charmed by the idea that our name evokes games like Tacoma (the video game) and queer-friendly narratives Junkee writes about.

We’re proud of our unintentional twin city fame. Tell your friends we’re the quiet Tacoma — a little anonymous, mildly internet-famous, and perfectly happy that UNCLE BUCK’S gets the tourist selfies.
