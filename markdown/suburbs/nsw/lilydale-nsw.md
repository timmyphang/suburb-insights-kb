# Lilydale (NSW)

Slug: `lilydale-nsw`

We like to tell newcomers our Saturday always started the same way: a slow roll down Main Street and a pilgrimage to Locavore — the place that used to be the crown jewel of Lilydale, sitting at 148-184 Main Street, where Locavore Studio and the Locavore Providore lived in glorious, pantry-filled harmony. Back in the day we’d stock up on cheeses, then pretend the rest of the Yarra Valley was just our morning commute.

Weekends meant a choice between wholesome and chaotic. Wholesome: the Lilydale to Warburton Rail Trail, which actually begins at Lilydale Station and ambled through vineyards and the Yarra Ranges — perfect for pretending we’re fit. Chaotic: Lillydale Lake, when Pokémon Go turned it into “Dratini Lake” and Broadsheet reported thousands turning up and council officers doling out parking fines. We still can’t decide which was more intense — the rare Dragonite or the traffic wardens.

We used to catch a coffee at The Lilydale General, which Concrete Playground called an outer‑eastern gem — bright interior, Coffee Supreme beans and dishes like corn and leek fritters; it was where the gentle locals and the slightly lost met. And for anyone who ever wanted to feel posh mid‑suburbia, Helen’s Hill Estate at 16 Ingram Road was your gateway to the wine‑filled Yarra Valley. Four Pillars even set up shop along Lilydale Road nearby, as if to reassure us our gin habits were culturally valid.

Petty local drama? Oh, we had it. A TikTok revealed a hidden pub on the platform at Lilydale Station — VRI Lilydale — which felt like someone found the last secret chook in the henhouse. And yes, the Showgrounds have always been the place for big feelings: the Lilydale and Yarra Valley Show and even Yarra Valley Oktoberfest turned us into festival veterans, damp socks and all.

Who would hate it here: anyone allergic to slow Saturdays, outdoor festivals, or the faint whiff of artisanal butter — Madeleine Butter was even made here, Broadsheet said, from grass‑fed Jersey milk. Who would love it: families who need playgroups (we’ve got the Lilydale Lake community room, SDA Lilydale and Wonderific on Hutchinson St), food obsessives who know Locavore’s history, and anyone who thinks a hidden pub on a platform is peak suburban magic. We know every corner — and no, we’re not leaving.
