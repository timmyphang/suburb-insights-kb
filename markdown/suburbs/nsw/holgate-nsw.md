# Holgate (NSW)

Slug: `holgate-nsw`

Back in the day, when journalists chased alpacas instead of Instagrammable bowls, Holgate’s weekend flex was delightfully low-key. We’re the place you tell people about like it’s a secret — then someone from the city Googles us and asks if we brew Temptress Choc Porter. (Spoiler: that’s Holgate Brewhouse over in Woodend, Victoria — Paul and Natasha Holgate’s show since 1999, and yes, Broadsheet and Time Out gushed about it.)

Our actual local claims to fame were a bit more pastoral. Sitchu once listed Iris Lodge Alpacas at 192 Wattle Tree Road, Holgate, as the spot to “Have Breakfast with Alpacas” — weekends and daily during school holidays, apparently. We used to wink at tourists who turned up expecting a microbrewery and instead met an alpaca who judged their shoelaces. Concrete Playground also tipped The Stables, Holgate as one of those secluded NSW stays — a modern cabin “minutes from the beach,” sleeps two, from about $250 a night. Romantic? Yes. Loud? No. Perfect for couples who like romance with a side of sea breeze and zero nightlife.

We don’t have a dramatic council saga to flog in the local rag — our press clippings are humble: alpacas, a cosy cabin, and the occasional mention in scenic-drive round-ups that drag us into people’s weekend plans. If that sounds boring, good. It means we keep the weird stuff warm for ourselves.

Who would hate living here? People who need their suburb to have seven craft coffee options by noon and a buzzy laneway mural on every corner. Also anyone who finds alpaca breakfast vibes unsettling — you do you.

Who would love it here? Couples wanting a quiet cabin near the beach, families who book school-holiday animal encounters, and road-trippers who enjoy a detour recommended on Concrete Playground or Urban List. We don’t pretend to be a metropolis. We’re the cosy, slightly eccentric pause button between one beach and the next — and yes, if you wander off to Woodend for a beer, we’ll raise an eyebrow and call you cultured.
