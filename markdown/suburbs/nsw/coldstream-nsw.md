# Coldstream (NSW)

Slug: `coldstream-nsw`

We’ll be honest: Sunday mornings here always start with fog rolling off the vines and someone in a ute pretending they didn’t just stop for the cellar door. Back in the day the guidebooks loved to remind visitors that Oakridge Wines had that north‑facing tasting room and outdoor deck overlooking 10 undulating hectares — the restaurant might’ve closed, but the view and cellar door did the apologising for it. We know where every bench faces.

Our loop is embarrassingly predictable. A detour past 878–880 Maroondah Highway reminds us that 400 Gradi once brought Johnny Di Francesco’s Neapolitan pizza pedigree to town (the papers tracked that expansion). Rochford Wines — same patch of highway — has also been the place for a blowout: Heavy Congress sold out in 2022 and was listed again for a November date, and Rochford’s harvest festivals have been racketed about in event listings. CherryHill Orchards used to be the festive excuse for everyone to rebrand themselves as a “cherry picker” at the Christmas market. You could almost write a travel piece where every paragraph ends in “and then we ate grapes.”

For the record, this is not sleepy in the wrong way. Time Out and Concrete Playground have both treated Coldstream like a dependable Yarra Valley pivot: pinot noir and chardonnay get name‑checked, Chandon ran Garden Spritz picnics on the lawns, and the Shortest Lunch festival once stamped Coldstream on the winter‑wine map. Sitchu even reminds us Coombe Yarra Valley is set on seven acres here — we’re roughly pastoral, not pastoral‑fantasy.

Who would hate living here? Someone allergic to tasteful event listings, or who expects a late‑night kebab after the harvest festival finishes. Also, anyone who hates picking their own cherries — Big Fella Cherries has ruined many a city eater’s dignity.

Who would love it? People who like their weekend plans decided by a brochure: cellar doors, seasonal markets, a pizza pedigree rolled out from the winery carparks, and picnics that look uncanny on Instagram. We’ll keep the deck chairs warm; the views do enough of the talking.
