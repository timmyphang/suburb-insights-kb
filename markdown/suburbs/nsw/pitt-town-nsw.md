# Pitt Town (NSW)

Slug: `pitt-town-nsw`

Back in the day, we’d tell visitors Pitt Town was where the festive spirit got a little competitive. Urban List had a soft spot for 4 Farmhouse Avenue — a “magical” lights setup with over 50,000 LEDs, interactive snow play and enough colour to make the CBD jealous. We used to queue to gawp like it was a weird suburban Disneyland.

Our weekend routine used to look a bit like this: someone would mention Percy’s Place Caravan Park — Sitchu called it riverside, 110 acres and “first-class” for camping — and suddenly we’d be roping in the whole extended family. Brunch conversation often veered to the odd culinary boast Concrete Playground once lifted into the ether: the famed Pitt Town beetroot, orange and endive salad with walnut vinaigrette, which we nod about like it’s our town’s unofficial anthem.

There’s local grit, too. The Pitt Town Football Club listing (8 Oakville Road, Oakville) reminded us who keeps the place honest: established in 1990, home grounds at Oakville Oval and Pitt Town Sports Club, and — believe it — it grew into one of the largest clubs in the Hawkesbury, registering 52 teams with the Nepean Football Association in 2019. If you’re into community commitment, that’s our flex.

Petty local drama? It wasn’t glamorous, but it was ours. Pitt Town Pizza! had posts thanking Hawkesbury SES for moving stock between shops and praising staff who “kept the shop open” — very small-town heroics, very real. The local FindGlocal pages even captured the everyday: Temptations at 254 Pitt Town Road was advertising a drive-thru with early hours (Mon–Fri 5am–1pm, Sat 6am–1pm) in its posts, and the Pitt Town PS page mirrored school-to-community chatter with a “Rules of Engagement” note about what comments represent.

Who would hate it here? If you despise oversized Christmas lights, riverside caravan parks, and football clubs that actually do the work, you’ll probably loathe Pitt Town. Who would love it? If you like a town that wears its charms loudly — from 4 Farmhouse Avenue’s LEDs to the volunteer grit behind Pitt Town Pizza! and a football club that registers 52 teams — you’d do worse.

We’re fond of our small spectacles. Back in the day, that was more than enough.
