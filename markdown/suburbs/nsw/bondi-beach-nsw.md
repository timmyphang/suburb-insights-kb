# Bondi Beach (NSW)

Slug: `bondi-beach-nsw`

We wake to the sort of sunrise that makes you briefly believe in Instagram again — Bondi’s east-facing shore still gets a tick on Sitchu’s sunrise list, and we’ve learnt to time our coffee runs around it. Back in the day we’d stroll the Bondi to Coogee coastal walk before the crowds; now we just claim we “beat the rush” while elbowing past other early risers.

By late morning the place is its predictable self: a million people and their cameras. Time Out once pointed out Bondi pulls up to three million visitors a year and even called it Australia’s most Instagrammed spot — which explains why we can’t get a park but can get a perfect sunset selfie. Time Out also cheekily ranked our beachgoers as the worst behaved in Australia, and honestly, we wear that like a badge and a pair of sunnies.

Food and drink have always been Bondi’s other religion. Broadsheet and Urban List have long sung about institutions like Icebergs, Bills, Sean’s Panorama and Promenade — and more recent-feeling additions like Alzado got a look-in too — so we’re spoiled for bragging rights when relatives come to town. Concrete Playground reminds us there’s a cafe on every corner (87–89 Glenayr Avenue gets a namecheck), and the Bucket List even used to promise jugs of cocktails and a million‑dollar view.

We also have a softer side. Families and kids find us handy: Bondi Park with its playground, skate park and barbecue stations is a proper lifeline, and local playgroups meet at Wairoa Avenue, Francis Street and Kimberley Reserve for the real community stuff. Eastern Suburbs Mums keeps us honest about family-friendly events like Bondi Firelight and the free FIFA big‑screen days at North Bondi Park.

Who would hate it here? If you need silence, private parking and zero queues, Bondi is a non-starter. Who would love it? If you worship sunrises, coastal walks, noisy community events, and a dining scene that Broadsheet and Urban List can’t stop praising, we’ll welcome you with a towel, a view and maybe — if we’re feeling generous — directions to where the playgroup meets.
