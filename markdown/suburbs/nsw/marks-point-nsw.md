# Marks Point (NSW)

Slug: `marks-point-nsw`

Back in the day we thought a park was something with swings and a sausage sizzle. At Marks Point, our picnic bench is a vista and our entertainment is the water. As one blogger put it, “We took it as far as it went and ended up at a very small park called Marks Point. We parked the car and walked towards the water. There wasn’t much to see so after 10 minutes we got back into the car…” — thanks, Mama Cormier, for keeping it honest.

Our weekend routine is short and efficient. We park, we walk to the foreshore at Marks Point Park, we look at the lake, and then we admire the sheer scarcity of distractions. If you’re scrolling Urban List for the nearest Michelin-adjacent experience, you’re in the wrong postcode; downtown restaurant lists are great reading but they belong to another life.

We’re not blind to the practical bits. The Pacific Highway slices through our suburb and, painfully, it’s been the scene of a fatal crash near Reid Street that made headlines — NBN News and the Newcastle Herald covered it in 2016. It’s a sober reminder why Lake Macquarie Council’s Principal Pedestrian Network map exists: someone’s planning footpaths and crossings, and we’re very much in favour of them getting on with it.

Who hates living here? People who need constant stimulation, curated cocktails, or a “discover this hidden gem” story to post every weekend. If your idea of a good time is ten artisan courses, Marks Point will feel like a prolonged intermission.

Who loves it here? Families and folk who like their outings short, their drives scenic, and their directories practical — MumsPages lists local businesses and service providers for mums in 2280 for a reason. If you want a quiet water view, an honest five-minute park, and a place that doesn’t pretend to be anything it isn’t, this is your kind of low-key heaven.

We’ll keep showing up, parking, peering at the lake and calling it a day — and honestly, that suits us just fine.
