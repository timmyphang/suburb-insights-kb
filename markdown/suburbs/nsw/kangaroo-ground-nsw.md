# Kangaroo Ground (NSW)

Slug: `kangaroo-ground-nsw`

We roll up to the corner of St Andrews and Heidelberg‑Kinglake Roads like it’s the town square — because, as Time Out used to tell us, that’s precisely where the St Andrews Community Market sets up every Saturday. Back in the day Time Out listed it as running 8am–2pm (other guides put the start at 9am), and yes, the market is the thing we point to when explaining to city friends what we actually do on weekends.

We’re not pretending this is a buzzing metropolis. Our online life has more MumsPages listings than nightlife reviews — accounting and financial services, eyebrow experts, SoccaJoeys coverage and even laser-treatment directories have all been trotted out for Kangaroo Ground in those same local directories. It’s practically a badge of honour: if you can find a reliable bookkeeper and an eyebrow tech within our postcode, you’ve won adulthood.

Petty local drama? The archives don’t give us scandal; they give us the sort of municipal soap-opera where the plot is “will the market start at 8 or 9?” and the climax is a precinct notice. The only genuine emergency we’ve been publicly named in was a 2020 Yarra Valley Water precautionary boil‑water warning — Broadsheet had us on the list of 88 suburbs to be wary of after storm damage. Romantic, no.

Who would hate living here: folks who need a postcode curated by glossy restaurant lists and late‑night bars, or anyone allergic to Saturday mornings where the main cultural offering is fresh produce, chin‑wagging and small-business directories. If your idea of excitement is a changing café menu, look elsewhere — our press clips are more market stall than food critic.

Who’d love it: parents who use MumsPages, market devotees who actually like knowing when community stalls open, and anyone who appreciates a place that’s quietly useful rather than loudly curated. We’ll grin about being name‑checked in directories and still be first in line at the market — because Kangaroo Ground’s charm is precisely that comfortable, slightly bureaucratic, very local rhythm.
