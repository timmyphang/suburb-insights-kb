# Parkdale (NSW)

Slug: `parkdale-nsw`

We get up early and shuffle down to the foreshore like everyone else — sandy shoes, suspiciously damp beach towel and the small-town certainty that someone left a picnic plate on the BBQ. Back in the day we’d wander the foreshore playground, fire up the path along the Bay Trail and debate whether to keep walking toward Mordialloc Beach’s cafe/kiosk or turn back to Parkdale Village for something less salty and more scandalous.

For years the local food map read like a suitcase of travel memories. We used to queue for Mi Peru D’Carmen’s aji de gallina and ceviche (South American expat favourite, as Urban List put it). There was Montezuma’s doing Tex‑Mex the proper melty-cheese way. Meet Patty — Andy Gale’s burger entry at Lower Dandenong Rd & Nepean Highway — showed up on the scene, and Burger Lab was listed at 194 Como Parade W like a burrito-shaped mystery from a neighbouring suburb. The Buono Parkdale team (Chef Morris Danzen’s Filipino/Italian fusion) even gave us Agnolotti del Plin and Spaghettini alle Vongole for when we felt fancy. G3 Artspace kept the gallery lights on for the futurists among us.

The housing spiel always reads the same in listings: Nepean Highway addresses that are “a short stroll to the beach,” Ilma Court serenity, Victoria Street development potential, and Second Street’s Art Deco family home — all promising that comforting mix of village convenience and sand-between-your-toes living. Sitchu loved that line about Parkdale Primary School and Parkdale Plaza being “nearby”; we loved it because it meant schools, corner shops and excuses for morning walks.

Who would hate living here? If you loathe playgrounds, Bay Trail bike rides, or the idea that your local consists of a village strip and a beach you can actually reach on foot, you’ll find Parkdale unbearably wholesome.

Who would love it? Families chasing catchment lines, beach people who want a cafe and a plaza within reach, and anyone who remembers Mi Peru D’Carmen, Buono Parkdale or a cheeky gallery opening at G3 Artspace and smiles. We’ll keep the sand in our shoes and the gossip on the promenade.
