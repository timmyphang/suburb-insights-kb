# Delahey (NSW)

Slug: `delahey-nsw`

We like to start the day by doing what any small suburb with a personality crisis does: refresh a MumsPages search and see who’s listed us. Back in the day the clearest thing anyone wrote about Delahey was a neat little entry titled “NDIS Providers in Delahey (3037)” on MumsPages — yes, the online marketplace for Everything a Mum needs. That, and a handful of neighbouring directory pages for The Parks (3037), Avondale Heights (3034) and even Eynesbury (3338).

So here’s our morning: we open MumsPages, compare the listings, feel briefly very organised, and then realise the most exciting local drama is which service provider has the better review blurb. Riveting stuff. We’ll admit it — it’s not glamorous. Our local brag is that the internet knows where to find family support and health services for mums and kids. That’s not nothing. It’s practical. It’s reliable. It is also the sort of thing that’ll make a property investor nod politely and then fall asleep.

Who would hate living here? If you need rooftop cocktails, artisanal everything or a nightlife guide, you’ll be underwhelmed — our media footprint is directory-style and low-key. Who’d love it? Families who want neighbourhood services to be, you know, actually findable. Parents who appreciate a tidy list of NDIS providers and local businesses will look at our MumsPages entries and think: finally, someone did the hard work for us.

Full disclosure: our public record is mostly older directory snippets. We wear that like a cardigan — comfortable, sensible, and secretly very useful.
