# Eastern Creek (NSW)

Slug: `eastern-creek-nsw`

Back in the day — and by that we mean most of the writing about us is a few years old — Eastern Creek used to read like a theme park for people who love engines, outlets and Filipino feasts. We know the headlines: Sir Manong opened in 2020 and quickly became a Western Sydney go‑to for kare‑kare, adobo and sisig (and yes, the team later spread to Chatswood). Kariton Sorbetes turned up in the Eastern Creek Quarter with its Melbourne gelato flavours. ECQ Outlet was billed as the new shopping colossus — more than 100 brands and, allegedly, Australia’s largest ASICS outlet — set to open on a Wednesday 25 March that we all circled on our phones.

Our weekend routine is unique: drag a pram (or a partner) out to Sydney Zoo at 700 Great Western Highway, Bungarribee and watch the kids point excitedly at the quokkas or queue for GLOW, Bricks & Beasts and whatever seasonal spectacle was running when the blogs got excited. Then detour past Sydney Motorsport Park, the Sydney Dragway and Sydney Premier Karting Park because one of us will need to demonstrate that horsepower is a personality trait. Concrete Playground and Broadsheet kept reminding us the World Time Attack Challenge and the Ferrari Festival live here — loud, proud and very punctual.

Local petty drama? It’s very simple: we complain about the noise, then we buy tickets to the End of Year Meet and stand in the paddock like proud ring-ins. The Need For Speed IRL activation and the weekly meets at the dragway make us hypocrites with great hearing.

Who would hate living here? If your zen requires absolute silence, Wallgrove Road bushwalks and the roar from Ferrers Road will ruin your meditative playlist. Also, if you came for boutique lane‑way coffee culture, our file is more “family day out” than “your influencer shot.”

Who would love it? Families, thrill‑seekers and anyone who thinks a good day is a Ferrari paddock display followed by gelato from Kariton and a share‑style kare‑kare at Sir Manong. We might be loud, slightly chaotic and blogged about in older pieces, but that’s our charm — an outlet mall, a zoo, and a racetrack walk into one gloriously honest postcode.
