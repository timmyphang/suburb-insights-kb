# Merriwa (NSW)

Slug: `merriwa-nsw`

Back in the day the internet couldn’t decide which Merriwa it loved, so we’ve learned to split our personality between a quiet library playgroup and a very confident Perth café scene. We’re the Merriwa that shows up at the library.

We start our Thursday fortnights at 9:30am because, as Playgroup NSW sensibly notes, Merriwa Create & Grow runs Thursday fortnightly at Merriwa Library — 34–40 Vennacher Street, MERRIWA, NSW 2329. There’s nothing so bonding as a circle of sticky-handed toddlers, a frazzled parent trying to remember where they parked, and the librarian pretending not to judge the glitter situation. It’s our weekly ritual (give it a year and someone will invent a matching T‑shirt).

The internet’s other take on us is fun: Urban List once fawned over 115 Collective at 869 Connolly Drive, Merriwa — which, hilariously, is in Perth. So yes, sometimes the national listicles are basically like your aunty’s group chat: lots of enthusiasm, questionable geography. Parenting blogs do the same; Parenting Connection WA runs Play and Chat and Puggles listings for a Merriwa that answers to “Perth.” We enjoy the confusion — free publicity, even if it’s about the other state’s playgrounds.

Local drama? We don’t have a soap opera on the main street, but we do have the kind of municipal suspense that keeps you awake: will the playgroup craft glue be glitter or no-glitter? Riveting stuff. If you hate being recognised by half the town while you’re still in your “school-run uniform,” or you’re allergic to waving at people who know your dog’s name, you’ll hate living here.

Who will love Merriwa? Families who appreciate a reliable fortnightly playgroup, anyone who thinks a library can be a proper social hub, and people who enjoy being part of a place that doesn’t pretend to be anything other than local. We’re not trendy. We’re practical, slightly glittery, and perfectly glad of it.
