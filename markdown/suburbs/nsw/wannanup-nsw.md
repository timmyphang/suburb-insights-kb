# Wannanup (NSW)

Slug: `wannanup-nsw`

We know the exact moment Wannanup wakes up — it’s the whiff of pine when Tom’s Pines rolls around. For years Tom’s Pines has grown Christmas trees for over 30 years and, back in the day, you could pick one up in Wannanup (sales starting in early December). Nothing says local like lugging a tree past the community notice board on a Saturday and pretending it wasn’t a two-person job.

Our weekend routine is low-key and delightfully regional. We check the Wannanup Community Notice Board Hub on Find It Locally when someone needs a hand, spot the same list of neighbours — Dawesville, Bouvard, Hamel, Herron, Lake Clifton, Preston Beach, Waroona, Clifton, Nanga Brook, Wagerup and Yalup Brook — and then argue about whether the best feed was the one True Local once rounded up in its “8 BEST local Restaurants in Wannanup” list. For a place that barely gets column inches, we’ve still somehow amassed a directory.

There’s no celebrity scandal here (Pedestrian TV is busy with jungle clues and Netflix reboots elsewhere), which is both a blessing and a curse. The petty drama in our feeds is mostly about which contractor got the quote — Habitat Additions did appear on the local listings once — or whether someone posted the wrong bake sale date on Find It Locally. Not exactly front-page stuff, but it keeps the notice boards alive.

Who would hate living here? If you need a constant stream of glossy city content, 24-hour late-night menus or a lifestyle writer inventing cafés on every corner, this isn’t your vibe. We’re not anonymous suburbs on trend pieces; we’re the place where community posts matter more than influencer posts.

Who would love it here? People who appreciate practical things — a reliable Christmas tree pickup, a community notice board that actually works, and a pocket of towns that all know each other’s dog names (or at least their contractors’ numbers). For years the local listings have done the talking, and honestly, we like it that way.
