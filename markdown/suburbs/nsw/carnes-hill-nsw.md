# Carnes Hill (NSW)

Slug: `carnes-hill-nsw`

We queue (politely, because we are from Carnes Hill) at the corner of Cumberland Drive and Kurrajong Road and watch the Yummy Yako food truck do its thing — yes, the one Urban List once wrote up for serving vanilla ice cream kataifi‑wrapped and deep‑fried. Back in the day it felt like our small, crispy, syrupy claim to fame.

A proper weekend here used to look like this: one of us drops the kids at the Carnes Hill Library on 600 Kurrajong Rd for Carnes Hill 1 Playgroup (9:30–11am) while the other sneaks off to the Michael Clarke Recreation Centre on Margaret Dawson Drive to browse the My Kids Market stalls. Then we gather for Carnes Hill 2 Playgroup (11:15–12:45pm) and, if luck’s on our side, linger for the half‑hour parent session at the library’s “Building Bridges to STEM” event alongside the Carnes Hill Science Fair. It isn’t glamorous. It is, however, very efficient.

There’s no local council scandal in these clippings, no turf wars over footpaths — the most dramatic thing on the record is whether someone’s pastry stayed golden enough to qualify as breakfast. That’s kind of our vibe: mild, practical, and determined to keep the kids amused.

Who would hate living here? If you’re allergic to kids’ markets, organised playtimes, or the idea that your weekend will involve negotiating preloved baby gear, steer clear. If deep‑fried ice cream off a truck makes you suspicious rather than delighted, you’ll feel judged by our snack choices.

Who would love it here? Young families who prize a library that hosts supported playgroups (shoutout to Woodville Alliance Supported – Carnes Hill) and a rec centre that becomes a marketplace for the next stage of parenthood. We’re not pretending to be Bondi; we’re proudly suburban, with community timetables tattooed on our phones and someone always with a spare pram hook.

So yes, Carnes Hill: not perfect, occasionally crumb‑strewn, and utterly reliable for playgroups, markets and the memory of that fried‑ice‑cream truck. We wouldn’t have it any other way.
