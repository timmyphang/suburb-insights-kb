# Frankston (NSW)

Slug: `frankston-nsw`

We start our Saturdays like proper locals: coffee cup in one hand, wind in our face on the Frankston Pier, watching people attempt to out‑pose the murals on the boardwalk. The pier — yes, the same “what’s left of the Frankston Pier” a reporter once spectacularly failed to leap onto (Pedestrian.TV — you should’ve seen it) — is still our eastern compass. Up the hill, Oliver’s Hill hands us the kind of bay view that makes the rest of Melbourne jealous for about five minutes.

By midday the Street Art Walking Tour has us pointing at murals like they’re family heirlooms. Concrete Playground’s 48‑hour guide wasn’t lying: the street‑art scene and waterfront actually give the place some colour (literally). Our festival calendar fills quicker than your weekend plans — South Side Festival, the Frankston Street Art Festival and, yes, the Australian Sand Sculpting Championships (hello April 2026) turn the waterfront into peak destination mode.

If you want nightlife that made the papers, Hotel Paradiso did that whole Retro Miami thing — mirrorballs, disco‑house DJs and pizza — and proudly turned Frankston into a venue you can both brunch at and dance off your brunch. Moon Dog’s beach club plans (490 Nepean Highway) promise a big, colourful, cabana‑heavy vibe on the highway strip. And if you remember the old listings, there was chatter about The Hop Shop and Bang Bang bringing big beer and hawker energy to the Peninsula — Frankston’s no longer hiding its party shoes.

Petty local drama? Sure — Pedestrian.TV reminded us the Bayside Shopping Centre was once the centre of a police op after an alleged machete incident. We don’t do gruesome here for jokes, but we do keep a dry sense of humour about the headlines that drift in.

Who would hate it here? People who require their suburbs to be solemn, quiet and mural‑free. Also anyone who detests sand competitions and disco pizzas (missed opportunities, honestly). Who would love it? Beach obsessives, street‑art pilgrims, festival folk and anyone who likes a weekend that can swing from sunset cocktails to a sculpted sand cathedral by Sunday arvo. We’ll keep the pier standing and the DJs loud — you bring the sunscreen.
