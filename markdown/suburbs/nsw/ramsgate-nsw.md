# Ramsgate (NSW)

Slug: `ramsgate-nsw`

Back in the day we’d drag ourselves out before the sun and pretend we were doing it for fitness, when really it was for the view. The Ramsgate Bath — that perfectly square 50‑metre pool at the southern end of Lady Robinson Beach, as Time Out put it — used to be our cold‑water church. We’d line up for laps like it was a ritual and then stagger to the markets.

Speaking of markets: the Ramsgate Foodies & Farmers Market at Ramsgate Public School used to be our Saturday alibi. Concrete Playground had the listing — open from 8am, pooch‑friendly, full of organic groceries, pre‑loved records and the kind of handmade jewellery that convinces you you’re supporting local artisans even though you paid way too much for a necklace. It was the place to bump into half the street and pretend you only bought flowers.

We’ve always been quietly jealous that Bondi nicked our address—Ramsgate Avenue crops up in every guide. Porch & Parlour at 17–18/110 Ramsgate Avenue, North Bondi, North Bondi Fish at 120 Ramsgate Avenue and Speedos at 126 Ramsgate Avenue were the Bondi names people would mouth like it proved we were cultured. They were the ones the roundups liked; we watched from our poolside with a cuppa.

Our dining scene had its dignitaries: Time Out’s old listings remember Chez Pascal at 250 Rocky Point Road as that unpretentious French bistro, and The Intersection Tavern on 221 Rocky Point Road with its sprung dancefloor was the place you tried to dance off a week’s worth of takeaway.

If you’re the type who needs a 24/7 buzzy cafe crawl and an influencer‑grade mural on every corner, Ramsgate will bore you senseless. We’re more about market mornings, saltwater hair, and an honest dinner out that doesn’t need a press release.

If you love a proper community rhythm — a Saturday market, a real swimming enclosure to call yours, an old‑school bistro and a tavern that’ll let you dance badly — you’d have loved our Ramsgate. As we used to say, it’s small, it’s salty and it doesn’t try too hard.
