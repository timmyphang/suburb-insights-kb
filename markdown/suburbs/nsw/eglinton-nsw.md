# Eglinton (NSW)

Slug: `eglinton-nsw`

We love waking up here, even if "waking up" means slowly prying ourselves from a freestanding stone bathtub while the Wingecarribee River fog does its soft-focus thing. Back in the day the travel guides noticed — Sitchu put BoxGrove (119 Duramana Road) in their round-up of tiny cabins and outdoor-bath escapes, and UrbanList once slipped an "Eglinton | Sleeps 2" into a Bathurst slow-escape list — so yes, people come for the tubs. We don't blame them.

Our weekend routine is very specific: someone rents one of the Rest at BoxGrove eco-cabins, marvels at the solar‑passive design and natural ventilation, then spends suspiciously long periods pretending the outdoor soak is a philosophical practice. The cabins are tiny, designer-ish and quietly smug about sustainability. We nod approvingly and water our own hibiscus.

There’s no town-centre drama to report — that’s the point. The local claim to fame has been curated tranquillity: tiny homes on riverbanks, indoor stone bathtubs, and the kind of "romantic getaway" write-ups that make you want to whisper when you return to your phone signal. If you’re after neon, six bars of mobile reception or a reason to queue for brunch, well, Eglinton’s PR team will take that as a compliment and hand you a tea towel.

Who would hate it here? People who think "outdoor soaking opportunities" are a mortal inconvenience, anyone allergic to pine, and lovers of big-city urgency. Also anyone who believes size equals importance — the cabins are proof we prefer good design over loudness.

Who would love it here? Eco-curious couples, friends needing a quiet restart, and anyone who enjoys a deliberately curated silence with a view of river light. As Sitchu and UrbanList once pointed out, we are small, slow and spectacularly unhurried — and honestly, we'd like to keep it that way.
