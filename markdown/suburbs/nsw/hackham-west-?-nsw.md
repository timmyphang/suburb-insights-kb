# Hackham West ? (NSW)

Slug: `hackham-west-?-nsw`

We start our week at 44 Glynville Dr like it's a ritual — not a hip ritual, more of a communal caffeine trudge. The Hackham West Community Centre has the coffee cart and the Everyday Café for those of us who judge mornings by pastry availability, and the Food Pantry for when the supermarket sweep is a mood. The centre also runs Hearts Crèche & Occasional Care, playgroups and venue hire — yes, it’s the hub we all pretend we knew about before we actually needed it (as their listing notes).

On Mondays we’re the slightly frantic crew at Move ’N’ Play (10–11:30am, school terms). Tuesdays we’re the explorers at Little Explorers (10–11:30am). These playgroups are current — the Playgroup listing was updated in March 2026 — so bring socks and a sense of humour, not an ego.

The “serious” locals drift into People Matters — Hackham West, which operates from the community centre and links up organisations with the City of Onkaparinga’s Community Connections Officer, Leeza Peters. It’s where good intentions and action meet over a meeting-room table.

For nostalgia fans, the Hackham West School used to be listed at 50 Glynville Dr — it showed Principal Shannon Little and Deputy Principal Jason Masters on the old entry, along with OSHC and a Children’s Centre. That’s the kind of detail you mention when you’re on deck to mind someone else’s kids.

Who’d hate it here? Nightlife seekers and boutique-chasing influencers. Who’d love it? Parents, playgroup regulars and anyone who rates a real community centre above a façade. We’re small, loud, and useful — and we wouldn’t have it any other way.
