# Campbellfield (NSW)

Slug: `campbellfield-nsw`

Back in the day we woke up to the same reliable symphony: the hiss of charcoal, the chime of a market stall folding up on Cooper St, and the occasional plume of something dramatic billowing from the industrial strip. If you’ve spent a Saturday here, you’ve probably guessed which chorus line we’re talking about.

Our morning routine used to be gloriously simple. We’d wander past the Campbellfield Market at 27–35 Cooper St — Time Out even noted it “reopened on the 6th of June following a nifty facelift” — and try to decide whether to let the kids run wild at Kids Haven (1663–1665 Sydney Rd, with its Koalas Room and Emus Room for the tiny scholars-in-training) or detour for a piece of El Jannah. Broadsheet reported El Jannah rolled a breakfast menu out to its Campbellfield location, and UrbanList once reminded us you could pick up a bird at the Campbellfield drive-through. Pedestrian TV would smirk about how close El Jannah is to the Campbellfield Red Rooster — neighbourhood food rivalry, old-school style.

We’re not above admitting the suburb has its theatrics. Concrete Playground covered that enormous industrial fire at 16 Thornycroft Street — over 100 firefighters, black smoke, the EPA watching air quality and locals told to stay indoors and seek medical advice if affected. Very on-brand for a place where freight yards and party-hire warehouses stand shoulder-to-shoulder.

Who would hate living here? If you’re allergic to practicality, fresh air, or anything that smells faintly of perfectly-charred chicken, you’ll be uncomfortable. If your Instagram aesthetic demands eucalyptus-filtered light and a matcha bowl in every frame, Campbellfield will ruin your grid.

Who would love it here? Logistics operators won’t blink — True Local has pages of logistics listings for Campbellfield — and if you’re in event hire, MUX Events used to list Unit 5/55-57 Randor Street as home base. Vintage World Australia even had a factory down on Merri Concourse, for the nostalgics and prop collectors among us. We’re industrious, unapologetic, and excellent at feeding you at odd hours.

We don’t pretend to be anywhere else. We’re Campbellfield: a bit rough, always useful, and frankly better for it.
