# Bonnet Bay (NSW)

Slug: `bonnet-bay-nsw`

Back in the day we’d time our mornings by the squeal of the Parrot room and the Kookaburra room — Bonnet Bay’s two tiny empires inside the Bonnet Bay Child Care Centre, tucked into the grounds of Bonnet Bay Public School on Tudar Road. We know which demountable has the sunniest paint crack and which grassed patch the kids claim as sacred; Shire ChildCare Centres used to list the centre as operating 7am–6pm, 50 weeks a year, and yes, it wore its ACECQA accreditation like a badge of honour.

Our weekend looks more like a logistics meeting than leisure: one of us checks the Vacation Care enrolment form (the Shire site — sometimes cheekily titled “Bonney Bay VC” — had the details and the 34R Tudar Road address), the other remembers the OSHC hours — 7am–9am and 3pm–6pm on school days — because someone forgot about before-school care again. The Out of School Hours Care was described as operating from a demountable classroom with expansive outdoor grassed play areas; in other words, the kids get to run rings around any adult who thought “quiet suburb” meant “quiet kids.”

There’s no council scandal to gossip about in our snippets — just pragmatic listings on MumsPages, where the suburb’s businesses and party providers, health and wellbeing services and photographers were once corralled for the benevolent chaos that is parenting. So our petty drama is small: who can make the lunch box the quickest without accidentally packing the missing shoe?

Who would hate living here? If the sound of imaginary parrots and kookaburras at 7am sends you into existential despair, or you think “outdoor grassed play area” is a euphemism for “we’ll find another sport for those kids,” then yes, maybe move to somewhere flashier that never needs a timetable.

Who would love it here? Families who delight in practical things — a child care centre right on school grounds, morning and afternoon OSHC slots, a vacation care service listed with an actual address — this is the kind of suburb that makes parent life function. We wouldn’t have it any other way.
