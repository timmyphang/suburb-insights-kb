# Landsdale (NSW)

Slug: `landsdale-nsw`

We know the exact rhythm of a Landsdale morning: toddler in one hand, list of enrolment forms in the other, and the Warradale Community Centre on Southmead Drive glowing like a beacon of laminate and coffee grounds. Back in the day — and yes, all the sources we dug up are older/undated — Landsdale Gardens Playgroup used to run in that big air‑conditioned hall next to the Child Health Nurse, with sessions capped (20 kids or 15 families, take your pick). We’ve all done the free trial, nervously emailed landsdaleplaygroup@outlook.com and waited for the enrolment officer’s thumbs‑up.

We’ll admit it: our local claim to culture is earnest and practical. Findglocal once listed Landsdale Primary School as part of the Ashdale cluster, and even the VIP Veterinary Centre turned up at 127 The Broadview — proof that at least someone in town prioritises both literacy and vet bills. There’s also an Ecocycle at 8/89 Christable Way and a Perth Training Institute on Kalinga Way if your idea of a Friday night is a fire safety course. Not glamorous, just useful. Perfect for people who like their community with a side of form filling.

Petty local drama? There isn’t any juicy stuff in the clips we found, so instead let’s roast the small triumphs of local bureaucracy: yes, the playgroup policies insisted every parent sign in and out of the attendance register for insurance purposes. Riveting. Mandatory record-keeping has never looked so wholesome.

Who would hate living here? If you’re allergic to playdough, communal craft, and the phrase “please remember to sign the visitor register,” move along. If suburbia without a pretentious artisan lane is your nightmare, Landsdale will feel like an extended PTA meeting.

Who would love it? Families who want a proper neighbourhood — school farm outings, playgroup singalongs, a vet that actually knows your dog’s existential crisis — will feel right at home. As Urban List once cheekily observed about Perth locals, consider us lucky; we’ve got the practical stuff sorted and the rest we’ll invent for weekend bragging rights.
