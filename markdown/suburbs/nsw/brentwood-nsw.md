# Brentwood (NSW)

Slug: `brentwood-nsw`

We love Brentwood, NSW — honest — even if the internet keeps confusing us with a string of other Brentwoods and their nicer zip codes. Back in the day the press we could dig up was mostly memory‑lane: Urban List once wrote that “Sundays in Brentwood basically revolve around” the Brentwood Farmers Market just off San Vicente, which is comforting because it suggests our Sundays should involve organic produce and the occasional A‑lister doing their weekly shop. As if.

Mornings here, per the archive, feel like a mash‑up of recommendations: Time Out called Brentwood “sandwiched between the Santa Monica mountains, the 405 and Santa Monica proper,” and listed the barn‑themed Brentwood Country Mart with shops like Jenni Kayne, James Perse, Sugar Paper, Caffe Luxxe and dining at Farmshop — plus picnic benches and pony rides on Sundays. Concrete Playground remembered Brentwood Manor in Upper Hutt as a tree‑lined day‑spa with wine, chocolates and a spa pool, which is the sort of fantasy weekend we occasionally RSVP to in our heads.

Petty local drama? The file drawer is full of celebrity real estate gossip: pedestrian pieces on LeBron James’ schmick Brentwood mansion and the Miranda Kerr/Evan Spiegel house purchase (once Harrison Ford’s) are the sort of headlines that make our property values feel aspirational from afar. There’s also a note that Flour Pizzeria & Cafe reopened in Brentwood after disaster elsewhere, and even mentions of national restaurant rows — Great White has had a Brentwood outpost, apparently — which means our culinary reputation is at least interestingly complicated.

Who would hate living here: people allergic to pony rides, boutique shopping villages, and the idea that Sunday is for shopping local while not being papped. If you loathe quiet, curated streets and the gentle hum of other people’s well‑weighted handbags, this isn’t your scene.

Who would love it: mums and anyone in search of a village. Mothers of Brentwood ran a proper membership club (dues listed at $75, prorated to $55) with Spring Fling, Trunk or Treat and Winterfest; FIT4MOM Brentwood advertised classes and meetups at Creekside Park, Veteran’s Park, Blue Goose Park, East County Performing Arts Center and Brentwood City Park. We’re noisy about our playgroups and polite about our piazzas — and if that sounds like home, drop in.
