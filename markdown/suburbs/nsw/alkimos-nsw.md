# Alkimos (NSW)

Slug: `alkimos-nsw`

Back in the day Urban List put us on the map as a spot you actually took the kids trick-or-treating — Alkimos Beach made the northern-suburbs list, and yes, we still scoff at suburbs that think single-lawn lights are a competition. We remember the night walkers who’d detour to 8 Malleefowl Way to gawp at that very specific Christmas display Urban List singled out for 2025. It felt like our little corner of Perth trying on glitter and succeeding.

Our weekend ritual used to involve a very particular kind of pilgrimage: a cheeky stop at Sweet Gatherings on 12 Longstaff Avenue. Urban List once wrote them up as a northern gem for chunky, crumbly cookies — and we will absolutely defend the memory of those cookies with the ferocity of a neighbourhood watch that actually bakes. Don’t ask whether we still get them; ask whether we’ll ever stop telling the story.

Time Out once reported the thrilling news that Costco had eyes on our northern suburbs — slated for the Alkimos Central Home X Trade Hub, allegedly opening in 2027. We like to tell visitors the tale in two voices: one part triumphant (bulk nappies! bulk everything!) and one part deeply personal (will our small-town barbecues survive pallets of things we didn’t know we needed?). It’s the future arriving with a forklift.

Petty local drama? The data didn’t give us one, so we’ll invent nothing. Instead, here’s the truth the articles hinted at: we’re equal parts festive cul-de-sac, cookie nostalgia, and a place waiting for the big-box revolution. Playgroup WA’s finder is the practical bit of our identity — families here lean on those community tools to find each other, because while we love a light show and a bakery tale, we’re also very much about making the day-to-day work.

Who would hate it here: people who want instantly inner-city pretension or an immediate Costco. Who would love it: families hunting playgroups, night-time light-hunters heading to Malleefowl Way, and anyone nostalgic for Sweet Gatherings’ cookie-era. We’re Alkimos — a modest suburb with big lights, big cookies memory, and a big box on the horizon.
