# Cecil Park (NSW)

Slug: `cecil-park-nsw`

We pull up to the Wylde Mountain Bike and BMX hub like it's our town square — only quieter, muddier and with less small talk. Time Out once pointed out you can park there and “fill up water bottles before hitting” the new 11km bush trail that threads through Western Sydney Parklands and spots Aboriginal art, and honestly, that sentence sums up our entire civic plan: trails, art, repeat. The Urban List reminded parents looking for school-holiday activities that Cecil Park does mountain biking and BMX racing grounds, so if your kids are more interested in jumps than juice boxes, we’ve got you.

For those who still think Cecil Park is just paddocks, Time Out also used to file stories about the Sydney International Shooting Centre — yes, the Olympic-era range — tucked into semi-rural farmland at Lot 1 off Elizabeth Drive, Cecil Park Abbotsbury 2078. We like to whisper “Sydney 2000” at BBQs and see who remembers to clap.

What the media keeps telling you (and what we cheerfully confirm) is that we are not a boulevard of brunch spots or boutique shops. The press comes for the Wylde hub, the 11km track and the Shooting Centre; they don’t come for rooftop cocktails. And that’s fine. We wear our low-key like a badge: mud on the tyres, not on the menu.

Who would hate living here? People allergic to dirt, long gravel tracks, or the idea that your evening vibe might include the distant thump of bike tyres instead of a curated playlist. If you crave a strip of neon and artisanal everything, we’ll let you go with our blessing.

Who would love it here? Families who want a proper outdoor day out (The Urban List approved), mountain bikers and BMX riders who know what “Wylde” actually means, bush lovers who enjoy the Aboriginal art along an 11km wander, and anyone who gets a thrill from telling visitors that, yes, our shooting centre has Olympic history (Time Out wrote that, so it’s official).

We’re Cecil Park: not trying to be fancy, just trying to keep our trails runnable and our stories honest.
