# Enfield (NSW)

Slug: `enfield-nsw`

We live in Enfield, NSW — or at least we live in the version of Enfield the internet remembers. Back in the day, the guidebooks loved to drop our name into odd corners: Time Out once wrote up Violet Chocolates (yes, the place that made a stiletto heel out of chocolate — because of course it did), and Concrete Playground’s trips pages listed Enfield House and Cottages as somewhere to take in a garden and free Wi‑Fi. Urban List even waved the Enfield flag out in Mudgee, noting an Enfield House king suite with a wrap‑around balcony, firepit and views over a 22‑acre farm. Fancy a tiny bit of rural glamour? Drive a few hours, apparently.

Our weekend routine, according to archived clicks, was charmingly uncomplicated: we used to wander past the Flower Power at Enfield — the one with a pet store and an on‑site bird aviary, according to Time Out — and then sigh at Sitchu listings for sleek, north‑facing apartments on Byer Street and Carlyle Street that promised “timeless minimalist design.” In other words: lots of plants, birds and the kind of apartments that whisper “I own three cushions” while also being marketed to people who love natural light and spreadsheets.

There isn’t much in the way of modern scandal in the files — no high‑octane feuds, no council kerfuffles — which is honestly part of our charm. The internet’s takeaway on Enfield seems to be an odd combo of novelty chocolate, a very sociable garden B&B an hour away, a pet‑friendly nursery, and tasteful apartment listings. We’ll own it: we’re quietly proud of our niche.

Who would hate living here? People allergic to chocolate heels, avian soundtracks or anything labelled “minimalist.” Who would love it? Anyone who wants a suburb that reads like a middle‑aged travel editor’s moodboard — a bit leafy, a bit twee, and strangely earnest about its chocolate. As the old listings remind us: we’re small, slightly peculiar, and entirely our own.
