# West Lindfield (NSW)

Slug: `west-lindfield-nsw`

We’re the lot who measure distance in pram-push minutes and judge neighbours by their spare nappies. Back in the day, Playgroup NSW listed Lindfield Nurturing Beginnings Montessori — that’s how half of us found our people and the other half found someone to swap childcare hacks with.

Saturday mornings here are basically a rotating cast of tiny socks and louder-than-you-remembered singalongs. As Playgroup NSW used to show, the suburb’s got that reassuring, soft-focus family vibe: organised, slightly competitive, and very comfortable with glitter in places it should never be. We know the routes, the drop-off awkwardness, and who will volunteer to lead the craft that inevitably becomes a glitter crime scene.

There’s no local scandal to fling because the main civic debate is whether the playgroup should meet on Tuesday or Wednesday — thrilling stuff, really. We don’t need late-night bars or hipster brunches; we’ve got shared snacks and someone who brings too many stickers.

Who would hate living here? If you loathe paste-sticky hands, elevator small talk about nap schedules, or the inexorable rise of children’s artwork on every available surface, move along. Who loves it? Families with kids from birth to school age, the kind Playgroup NSW exists to connect. If you want community that judges you gently and borrows your stapler without asking, welcome home — West Lindfield will make you an honorary kindergarten conspirator before you can say “snack time.”
