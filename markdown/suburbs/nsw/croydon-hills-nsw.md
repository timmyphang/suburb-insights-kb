# Croydon Hills (NSW)

Slug: `croydon-hills-nsw`

We know the precise rhythm of a Croydon Hills weekend: school drop-offs, a quick wander past the church, then whatever the rest of the day allows (because we’re not pretending there’s a nightclub on every corner).

Back in the day Playgroup Victoria listed Croydon Hills Baptist Church as “a lovely indoor and outdoor space” — and yes, we will judge your picnic rug choice as we shepherd the kids between swings and snacks. MumsPages used to be our directory-of-everything-mum, where you could compare school holiday camps and even find local legal services, massage and facial-treatment options. Those listings made us feel organised even when the real triumph was finding matching socks.

For a proper local drama, we have one classic: as Broadsheet noted, Croydon Hills was on Yarra Valley Water’s 2020 precautionary boil-water warning list. We boiled water like it was a ceremonial offering and learned to love kettle culture very fast. No scandal, just practical togetherness and slightly overcooked pasta.

Who would hate living here? Nightlife hunters, people who measure a suburb’s worth in rooftop bars and artisanal anything, and anyone who needs a late-night open-for-everything precinct. Pass.

Who would love it? Parents who need decent play spaces, folk who appreciate a small-suburb pace, and anyone who likes their community listed on MumsPages and Playgroup Victoria because it means someone’s thinking about camps, classes and saying hello. We’ll keep the playgrounds tidy, the kettle on standby, and the gossip affectionate — that’s Croydon Hills for you.
