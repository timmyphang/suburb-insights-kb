# Mount Helena (NSW)

Slug: `mount-helena-nsw`

Back in the day we used to think of Mount Helena as the place you escaped to when the city had had enough of you — and, full disclosure, our notes are from older Perth Hills guides, so this is a nostalgia roast.

We remember checking the lists: Urban List lumped us into its Perth Hills Airbnbs roundups, name-checking a Japanese Farmhouse Mount Helena — a private retreat supposedly a stone’s throw from the Mount Helena Tavern, sitting on five acres of jarrah forest and granite outcrops. Sitchu put us on the map too, with a guide that even listed a property at 1200 Alison Street and crowned the Mount Helena Tavern as the place to elevate your Sunday with roast potatoes and Yorkshire pudding. And there was Amaroo Retreat & Spa — tucked into the Darling Scarp foothills on 55 acres, with “luxury chalets, outdoor spa baths and an on-site spa,” if you were feeling very fancy for a hill town.

Petty local drama? Our sources were polite; the papers preferred cosy cabins to council rows. So our scandal is simply being too idyllic for our own good.

Who would hate living here: people who need 24/7 neon-lit footpath service, boutique shopping strips and three different brunch menus within walking distance. Who would love it: folks after jarrah forest views, spa baths with actual silence and a tavern that remembers how to roast a Sunday.

We’ll keep being small, green and a little bit smug — and yes, the brochures from back in the day were not lying.
