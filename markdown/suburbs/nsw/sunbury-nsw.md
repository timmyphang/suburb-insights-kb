# Sunbury (NSW)

Slug: `sunbury-nsw`

Back in the day we were the place you sneaked out to for a weekend that smelled faintly of fermenting shiraz and someone else’s wedding playlist. Broadsheet cheekily called us Melbourne’s closest wine region — our vines have been in on the gossip since the 1860s — and we used to point visitors up Sunbury Road to Goona Warra Vineyard (790 Sunbury Road) and Craiglee (785 Sunbury Road) like we were doing them a favour. Craiglee even opened its cellar door for tastings on the first Sunday of the month, except January, because apparently even wineries need a holiday.

Our Saturday circuit used to be a very particular kind of indulgence: Augustus Gelatery at 96A O'Shanassy Street, where the cabinet read like a festival line-up — chunky Tim Tam, Aperol spritz sorbet — and you left sticky and smug. If you wanted a pub with pedigree, Ball Court Hotel had reinvented itself with a multi-million-dollar glow-up and a 600sqm beer garden that meant footy days were less a game and more a communal buffet.

We’ve also been the secret salon stop for curly-haired Melburnians: folks used to trek to Curl Next Door at 45 Oshanassy Street for Bec’s dry-cut magic. And if you’d ever returned from a festival caked in mud, Junkee’s memory bank recalled exactly that — “coming back from Sunbury covered in mud” — which, honestly, is basically a badge of honour.

Yes, the trains have been through their dramas — the Metro Tunnel chatter and the Sunbury line reshuffle made commuting feel like a plot twist — but that’s part of the folklore now.

Who would hate it here? People who need 24/7 inner‑city excess, boutique everything and zero country air. Who would love it? Families who used to (and still might) find PlayConnect sessions at Dulap Wilim Hub, 13 Leichardt Street, or PlayBuddies at Goonawarra Neighbourhood House handy; couples hunting for a wedding with a view; wine nerds who like provenance more than pretence; and curly-haired travellers seeking Bec.

For years the local press loved telling our story — sometimes a little romantically, always a little proudly. We’re a small wine region with big hair, muddy memories and an excellent gelato cabinet. What’s not to love?
