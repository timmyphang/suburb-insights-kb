# Niagara Park (NSW)

Slug: `niagara-park-nsw`

We’re the lot who once navigated school-runs with the precision of a small navy. Back in the day CareforKids listed 67 childcare services in Niagara Park — yes, sixty-seven — and, believe it or not, 52 of them had vacancies. The headline? An average fee of $137.26 a day that made us gasp, then quietly rearrange our budgets.

Our morning ritual used to be a convoy to Little Miracles PreSchool & Long Day Care — Shop 21a, Valley Plaza, 16 Washington Ave — where the drop-off window read 6.30am to 6.30pm and we thanked the urban gods for extended hours. Across town, Coastal Kids Education and Care at 209 Narara Valley Drvie was the undercutter with a $108/day rate and a shiny 5.0 from ten reviewers. As CareforKids noted, there were 16 preschools and eight of them had vacancies — a weird kind of competitive generosity that made us feel both lucky and slightly suspicious.

Petty drama? The data’s too polite for rabbit holes, so we’ll admit the real squabble here is choosing which vacancy to claim before your neighbour does. It’s the suburban equivalent of a Black Friday trolley dash, only with more nappies and less dignity.

Who would hate living here: anyone whose life goal is an uninterrupted Friday night or who thinks playground chatter isn’t a community soundtrack. Who would love it: families who need reliable hours, bargain-seeking parents eyeing that $108/day gem, and anyone who enjoys the comforting hum of small people organising the future. We wouldn’t have it any other way.
