# Greenwood (NSW)

Slug: `greenwood-nsw`

We know the Greenwood like the back of our hand — partly because it used to be a sandstone school (Time Out says 1844 — yes, we’re old money and very literal), and partly because we once watched 50 tonnes of snow get dumped in our beer garden and thought, “Well, that’ll do.”

Mornings used to start with a detour past Greenwood Plaza: Blessed Fruit Pies had that two‑entrance setup (one opening onto the Plaza’s thoroughfare), and the food court even scored a poke kiosk — Nudefish — for when we needed lunch that said “healthy” but tasted like a dare. On rooftop duty up above the Plaza was Hunter Gatherer; it was the sort of place you’d pop to for a date that wanted to seem casual but was definitely trying. These are old notes — so yes, we’re talking in the past tense like an ex who still has the good plates.

Weekends, we’d gather at the Greenwood Hotel on 36 Blue Street. Broadsheet and Time Out loved to remind people that Nick Wills poured the first pint in 1992 and later helped restore the place — which explains why we treat the timber beams and sandstone walls like treasured houseplants. The Greenwood wasn’t always a Saturday-night destination, Time Out said; it thrived on after‑work drinks and mid‑week parties. We’re proud of that. We also went a bit wild when the Snow Festival rolled through: ten days, a 20‑metre toboggan slide and real snow — Concrete Playground and Urban List were here for the chaos, and so were we.

Petty drama? Oh, we had our moments. There was that time Monkey Shoulder moved in for a whisky residency and some very dedicated singles attempted to mix cocktails with matchmaking. Sitchu still remembers our Anzac Day two‑up — 10:30 AM at the Greenwood, with $1 from every Carlton Draught pledged to the Legacy Foundation. We do solemn and silly in the same breath.

Who would hate it here? If you loathe timber beams, faux alpine summers and a rooftop that’s trying very hard to be both casual and curated, you’ll be miserable. Who would love it? Anyone who likes a bit of history, a bit of theatre, and a pub that has the bones to pull off snowstorms and whisky dating nights — that’s us, through and through.
