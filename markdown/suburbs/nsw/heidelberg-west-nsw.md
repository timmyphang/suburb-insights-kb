# Heidelberg West (NSW)

Slug: `heidelberg-west-nsw`

We wake up in Heidelberg West the way everyone else wakes up: slightly surprised we’re still here, proud we stayed. Back in the day the maps used to tuck us in as the less glamorous siblings of Heidelberg and Heidelberg Heights, and Time Out even noted that — yes, we were the undercooked cousin — but also that we were tipped for a surge in buyer interest. We like that bit. It makes our pavements feel like potential.

Weekends used to mean a run past 69 Sheehan Road, where Cannoleria / That’s Amore opened a massive cannoli factory and, reportedly, plans for an on‑site eatery. Concrete Playground wrote about the move to the 500 sqm digs — so big you could lose your mate inside a ricotta swirl. Lunch used to be a pilgrimage to The Bearded Pig at 39 Mologa Road, which Urban List crowned a Bangin’ Sangas pick for a brisket sanga. We pass the old Austrian Club windows and remember the beef goulash and knödel mention in Time Out — a club founded in 1944 that kept things deliciously mid‑suburban.

For the kids (and the adults who secretly prefer foam pits to small talk), there was Bounce Heidelberg at 590 Waterdale Road — over an acre of interconnected trampolines and a giant airbag. Time Out had our back on that one. And for the mums, MumsPages used to keep a tidy list of in‑home care, baby gyms and even jazz dancing classes serving our postcode; practical, full of listings, and quietly proud of it.

Who wouldn’t love living here? People who like space, value for money, and a history that includes a cannoli factory and a club serving continental roast dinners. Who would hate it? Folks who want champagne nights and pretentious street names — we’ll give you neither, but we’ll give you a banging brisket sanga and enough trampoline stories to ruin your Instagram aesthetic.

We’re not the flashiest postcode, and that’s fine. As Time Out said, we were always the underrated sibling — and for years local guides and mags kept reminding everyone that we’re quietly getting better. Come for the sandwiches, stay because you can actually afford a backyard.
