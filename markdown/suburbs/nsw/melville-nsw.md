# Melville (NSW)

Slug: `melville-nsw`

Back in the day the internet kept getting us confused and, honestly, we learned to laugh about it. We’re Melville, NSW, but everywhere you click some journalist is either raving about Captain Melville in the city, praising Miinot Gelato on Melville Road, or planning pram‑friendly walks around the City of Melville. So yes, we’ve had our identity crisis, and we own it.

Our morning looks like this: we lace up and pretend we’re doing the Point Walter Reserve to Attadale Reserve promenade the way Melville Mums once recommended, then double back to Kadidjiny Park — the one everyone calls the “Dr Seuss” playground — to watch the small ones tackle the tripod swing. If the kids aren’t at LeisureFit Melville for Gymbakids (Bandicoot, Koala, Platypus — all very official‑sounding), we at least bump into someone from Melville Mums swapping tips about the best pram routes.

The internet’s petty drama? We’ve been inadvertently dragged into food fights we didn’t start. Broadsheet once wrote dreamy pieces about Captain Melville and Loch and Key, Urban List couldn’t stop nominating Captain Melville for summer venue glory, and Concrete Playground gave SHOP225 a national pat on the back — all fine establishments, just not our postcode. We’ve learned to enjoy being that convenient mystery on other people’s food tours.

Who would hate living here? If you need your suburb to be consistently name‑checked in weekend roundups and don’t enjoy being politely mistaken for Melville Road or the City of Melville, you’ll be grumpy. If you can’t handle a playground nicknamed after a children’s book character, move along.

Who would love it? Families who treasure local clubs and neighbourly institutions — the Melville Junior Football Club, Melville Amateur Football Club, Melville Friendship Club and services like Melville Cares — will feel at home. We’re quietly practical, community‑minded and fine with our mixed‑up rep; it gives us stories to tell while we walk the river, drop the kids at Gymbakids, and sip whatever gelato‑adjacent treat someone else keeps writing about.
