# Golden Grove (NSW)

Slug: `golden-grove-nsw`

We meet at 6pm sharp at The Village Tavern Hotel — not because we were invited, but because the Golden Grove Evening VIEW Club minutes say so. Back in the day (and yes, the paperwork still exists), we’d show up for a 6.00pm for 6.30pm start, hear a guest speaker — Wayne McAinsh on AI one month, Genevieve Harris from OzHarvest the next — then politely pretend the raffle was the real reason we came.

Our Saturday ritual is gloriously low‑key: a stroll through the pretty parks Sitchu praised (leafy, roomy, the kind of place where front lawns still outnumber espresso machines), a swing past the local shopping village, and if we’ve got littlies, the Croydon & Golden Grove indoor play centres get their turn. If we’re feeling civic, the Golden Grove Recreation & Arts Centre is where the action used to be — three courts, a timber floor that echoes community sports, and an Arts Centre that quietly programs things the rest of Adelaide later brags about.

We don’t have a scandal to gossip about because our “petty local drama” is minute‑level stuff: who forgot to bring a raffle prize, who left an apology in the newsletter, and whether the next VIEW Club outing will include another talk about AI or a dish of donated food from OzHarvest. Riveting stuff, honestly. The March 2026 newsletter was practically a thriller.

Who would hate living here? If you crave a 24/7 nightlife, rooftop bars on every corner, or an identity crisis that ends in a boutique, you’ll be bored. If you think “leafy” is code for “dull”, this place will test your patience.

Who loves it here? Retirees — we’ve got Golden Grove Lifestyle Village (Captain Robertson Ave), Helping Hand’s care home on The Golden Way, and Estia Health on Captain Robertson Ave — facilities and social calendars that keep the place humming in the best way. Families and hobbyists love the Rec & Arts Centre and the gentle, small‑town rhythm. And for the pedants among us who enjoy confusing tourists, remind them that “Golden Grove” also crops up on wine lists — Broadsheet once followed Golden Grove Estate’s Ray Costanzo all the way to the Young Gun of Wine — so when someone asks where our nearest cellar door is, we shrug and let them google their way to the Granite Belt.
