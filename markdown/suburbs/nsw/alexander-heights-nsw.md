# Alexander Heights (NSW)

Slug: `alexander-heights-nsw`

We know the lane: La Salle Road, Alexander Heights. Come December we’re the ones who used to turn our street into a seasonal pop-up theme park — 8 La Salle Road, complete with music, bubbles and a photo‑worthy sleigh — a display Urban List and Time Out once pinned on their best-Christmas-lights maps for 2025. The lights were listed as on roughly 7:30pm–11pm throughout December, so yes, we’ve practised crowd control with more enthusiasm than the council.

Weekends here are low-key: we’re the neighbour of Ballajura, Koondoola, Malaga, Noranda and Whiteman — Ballajura itself is about 14km from the Perth CBD, if you like to brag about being “close-ish” to the city (PerthMums pointed that out). That means you get the suburban perks without pretending to be inner‑city chic.

No local soap-opera worth printing in the paper — our claim to fame is festive excess and a decent sprinkling of goodwill. Who would hate living here? People who loathe bubble machines, singalongs, or being photobombed by families determined to get the perfect sleigh shot. Also anyone allergic to community spirit and inflatable reindeer.

Who’d love it? Families and anyone who enjoys a street that treats December like a civic hobby. For years we’ve been the kind of place where neighbours swap extension cords as readily as Christmas pudding recipes — and where, for a few weeks each year, La Salle Road gets the glow-up that out‑glitters the rest of us.
