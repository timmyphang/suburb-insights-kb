# Herne Hill (NSW)

Slug: `herne-hill-nsw`

We live in Herne Hill, NSW, and our entire media strategy is to be so quietly sensible that Google keeps sending writers to the other Herne Hills. Back in the day the only clippings we could find were older pieces about places that share our postcode in name only: Broadsheet and Urban List wrote at length about the Swan Valley’s turn to cellar doors — Sittella at 100 Barrett St and Limeburners & Giniversity at 45 Hyem Rd (the old Carilley Estate) — and even Little Lamonts Express at 660 Great Northern Highway cropped up. Time Out, meanwhile, was pining for Brockwell Park and the Herne Hill Market over in London. So yes, we’ve been outsourced in the press.

Our weekend routine is correspondingly modest. While Urban List rhapsodised about Sittella’s vineyard views and “harvest” lunch experiences elsewhere, we spend Saturdays perfecting the art of not being a headline. We don’t have a 200-seat restaurant on Hyem Rd (that one was retrofitted from Carilley Estate, as Broadsheet explained) and we’re fine with that — someone has to keep the quiet corners tidy.

There’s no petty local feud to report — the data didn’t find one, so neither will we invent it. The real story here is our invisibility: the online guides and parenting threads that praise Herne Hill as a family-friendly pocket (Mumsnet loved the parks and schools over in the London threads) kept referring to other Herne Hills, and honestly it’s flattering. We’d rather be low-key than a trade-show for influencers.

Who would hate living here? If you need weekend brunches that double as a content shoot for “cellar door living,” you’ll be cross — those 100% gluten-free menus and garden-facing dining areas featured in Urban List are over in the Swan Valley. Who would love it? Anyone who likes a suburb that keeps its dignity: quiet, family-minded, and suspiciously absent from articles that like lists and big venues.

So come for the anonymity. Stay because you secretly enjoy having a suburb that makes the media work for its clicks. We’ll wave to Sittella and Limeburners from afar — politely, from our own, considerably calmer Herne Hill.
