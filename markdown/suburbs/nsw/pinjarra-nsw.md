# Pinjarra (NSW)

Slug: `pinjarra-nsw`

We’re the sort of town that measures success in pastry medals and market days, and if that sounds faintly smug — well, guilty as charged.

Morning in Pinjarra: we used to queue at Pinjarra Bakery (Shop 6, 2 Peel Street) like it was the town meeting. The bakery collected medals for creative pies (Time Out name‑checked our Best Meat Pie wins in 2019 and 2021) and outlets from Broadsheet, Urban List and Sitchu all sent people our way for sausage rolls and slabby goodness. We’ll admit it — the red wine & garlic beef and creamy chicken & veg pies were worth the detour, or so the guides told everyone.

Weekends leaned into the Edenvale rhythm: the Pinjarra Markets at Edenvale turned Sundays into a hunt for local honey, bric‑a‑brac and something you didn’t know you needed until you saw it on a makeshift stall. And yes, the Pinjarra Festival — the two‑day shindig held 1–2 June 2024 — was Murray’s largest free community event, complete with live music, market stalls and vintage machinery that made dads suspiciously nostalgic.

Our civic life reads like a well‑labelled form: the Shire of Murray keeps its PO Box in town, the Murray Library (at 1906 Pinjarra Road) still hosted Rhyme Time and Story Time, and Pinjarra Doctors were listed on Murray Street (27 Murray Street) to remind us that pies have consequences. The Murray Districts Playgroup ran out of 16 Forrest St and even had a 2026 launch meeting — yes, we have meetings about launching.

Petty local drama? We don’t manufacture it; our personality is its own drama. If you hate being recognised by a bakery’s trophy cabinet, or you can’t handle weekends filled with stalls and antique fairs, you’ll hate living here. The three‑day Antique, Vintage & Collectables Fair will personally offend your minimalist aesthetic.

Who would love Pinjarra? People who enjoy a town with a clear calling — pie pilgrimage, markets at Edenvale, festivals that fill the main drag, and a playgroup that pulls in neighbours from Ravenswood to Mandurah. We’re compact, proud, and honest about what we do well: feeding you, entertaining you and making sure you leave with something in a paper bag.
