# Eden Hill (NSW)

Slug: `eden-hill-nsw`

We like to think Eden Hill is the sort of place you only need one hand to count its secrets — mostly because, as far as the inbox goes, there aren’t many. Back in the day, Broadsheet ran a piece about a tiny takeaway called Gang Gang — partners Nina Hadinata and Morgen Wyn‑Hadinata, a nine‑seat fit‑out next to Little Caesars Pizza — but that was Eden Hills, not us, and it’s an older story. So yes, when we refresh news feeds we’re often more curious than smug.

Our weekend routine is therefore delightfully low‑maintenance: we check what the rest of the internet is celebrating and nod politely. Urban List has a Garden of Eden Nursery listing at 365 Ferrars Street in Albert Park — lovely, but also on the other side of the city. These are older, pre‑2024 snippets; they’re nice to admire like holiday snaps of other suburbs.

There’s no local petty drama here in the clippings you can point to — just a pleasant lack of scandal. Which means our gossip is homegrown: who forgot to mow the verge, who actually owns the rusted ute on the corner — the usual.

Who’d hate it: anyone who needs a listicle of boutique openings and constant foot traffic to feel alive. Who’d love it: people who prefer being the kind of place the internet forgets for a few months, because when it remembers a suburb called Eden it tends to be someone else’s Eden. We’ll keep our calm and our gardens.
