# Gymea Bay (NSW)

Slug: `gymea-bay-nsw`

We’re the lot who still know where the historic clock tower on Kingsway hides its best gossip. Back in the day the talk was about who’d get the last table at Fior when it opened in that tower on May 2, 2024 — as Broadsheet and Time Out cheekily reminded us, the Arthur and Jane crew brought handmade pasta and a roaming gelato cart to Gymea’s main drag. We’ll never forget the way the 120-seat room tried to look casual while everyone pretended not to be impressed.

Mornings here are very down-to-earth: we do the Coonong Creek Walk with the dog (Urban List flagged it as kid- and dog-friendly), then rinse off at Gymea Baths at the end of Ellesmere Road because the netted pools used to be a selling point for swimmers and snorkellers, according to Concrete Playground. If the bike needs a tune afterwards, Endeavour Cycles on Gymea Bay Road used to be where the locals sent their frames for a proper service.

We’re not above delight — Essenze Chocolate on Gymea Bay Road had been a small-time obsession for years, making Belgian couverture treats that let us pretend we weren’t living on takeaway pasta and gelato. And if you wanted community energy, there was the Gymea Hotel, which Time Out noted as home to trivia, bingo and the occasional UFC crowd — always a good barometer for how rowdy the weekend will be.

There’s nothing scandalous in our files — no council coups, no reality TV melodrama — just a comfortably suburban rollcall of bowls (Gymea Bowls on Kingsway was once praised for barefoot sessions), artsy lunches at Hazel Kitchen & Bar in the Hazelhurst Arts Centre, and a neighbourhood that attracts people who prefer a netted pool to high-rise drama.

Who would hate it here? If you crave constant novelty, night markets every night or a skyline to Instagram, you’ll be bored. Who would love it? Families, swimmers, weekend walkers, and anyone who thinks a clock tower pasta dinner and a riverside snorkel is living the dream — even if, these days, we’re mostly reminiscing about when those bookings were the big news.
