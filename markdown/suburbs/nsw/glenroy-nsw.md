# Glenroy (NSW)

Slug: `glenroy-nsw`

Glenroy, NSW — we love you, even if the internet keeps confusing you with everyone else called Glenroy.

We know the routine: we’d drop the kids at the Glenroy Community Hub for playgroup and a quick Maternal and Child Health check (Playgroup Victoria used to list Meeting Room 1 as prime territory for sticky hands), then wobble down to Wheatsheaf Rd for a remedial rub at RedRock Natural Therapies when backs started to protest. At least, that’s what our dusty clippings say — everything in the file is dated “back in the day,” so consider this nostalgia, not a current guide.

Our press box is a mess. Time Out once plucked a Glenroy into its list of Melbourne’s most affordable and liveable suburbs for 2024 — flattering, even if the geography department was probably asleep. Urban List, meanwhile, is in love with a very different Old Glenroy Dairy: a restored 1920s homestay in the Sunshine Coast hinterland near Maleny. Lovely place, two hours from Brisbane, not exactly commute-friendly for local school runs.

We also have a grim clipping from Pedestrian.TV about a serious crime in Glenroy, Melbourne — a reminder that newspapers mix up postcodes and headlines. We’re not making light of it; just noting the glorious chaos of being one name on multiple maps.

Who would hate living here? People who crave boutique nothingness and cafés with impossible accents — the internet’s not very helpful about current cafes here, and the only hospitality stars in our file are a hinterland dairy and older listings. City romantics who need a constant supply of curated outings might find our file a little… understocked.

Who’d love it? Families who appreciate community rooms and reliable maternal-child services, bargain-hunters who like the idea of affordable suburbs (Time Out flirted with the idea), and anyone who enjoys being part of a place that doesn’t scream for attention. We’re folded into other Glenroys on paper, sure, but that just leaves more room for us to be quietly brilliant.
