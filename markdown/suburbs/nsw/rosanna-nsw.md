# Rosanna (NSW)

Slug: `rosanna-nsw`

We know the exact moment a Saturday feels like ours: the sun hits the parklands, someone complains about Rosanna Road being traffic-jammed (yes, Time Out said it first), and we haggle over whether Sideshow Burgers at 61 Beetham Parade was the herald of our "growth suburb" era or just a very committed sourdough experiment (UrbanList name‑checked the place and the Brasserie Bakery buns).

Back in the day Broadsheet ran the numbers — PwC’s City Pulse had us vaulting from 47th to second in 2021, listed under “Heidelberg – Rosanna.” We still use that like a humblebrag at school fetes. Nearby, Mister Nguyen at 133 Lower Plenty Road was the go‑to Vietnamese spot Broadsheet logged for us; Davies St. Food Co. at 29 Davies Street got its gluten‑free halo in UrbanList’s roundups. Funkie Brewster was the friendly neighbourhood cafe Time Out remembered fondly. All of these are snapshots from the archives — historical receipts, really — so we say “was” and “used to” like careful witnesses.

Our petty local drama? It’s a low‑key thing: are we a theatre town, a burger boomtown, or a quiet patch of parkland with a playgroup on the side? Heidelberg Theatre Company at 36 Turnham Ave shows we like our amateur theatrics; Playgroup Victoria’s pages remind anyone paying attention that Rosanna Fire Station Community House ran courses and our Rosanna Golf Links Primary School included a Deaf Education Hub and the Hand in Hand Playgroup. So yes, we put on plays, we feed kids gluten‑free pizza, and we book community classes between trips down the traffic corridor.

Who would hate living here: anyone allergic to the idea of a suburb with opinions — about parking, about which bakery supplies the right bun, about whether “growth” is a badge or a headache. Also, if you can’t bear the idea of an amateur stage and a playgroup sharing the same postcode, move along.

Who would love it: families who want community on tap, people who remember when Broadsheet made us cool, theater-goers, and anyone who appreciates a suburb that can be both leafy and quietly ambitious. We’re not flashy, we’re not finished — and honestly, we kind of like it that way.
