# Turramurra (NSW)

Slug: `turramurra-nsw`

We still talk about that Sunday-morning queue like it’s a local rite of passage — back in the day Flour Shop on Princes Street had people lined up for sourdough, croissants (yes, the caramel‑crunch ones Broadsheet raved about) and Single O coffee. That homey exposed‑kitchen thing felt like our civic centre for pastry worship, until urbanists decided to add “best bakery” stickers to everything.

Our weekend routine used to look suspiciously wholesome: grab whatever Flour Shop was selling that week, stroll past Honeysuckle Garden’s Turramurra outpost and pretend our balconies could grow into a suburban rainforest, then wind up at Tom’s Cellars or Exclusive Meats North Turramurra for something perfectly specific — handpicked wine or Nuremberg bratwurst at 288 Bobbin Head Road. If we were feeling decadent, Gillian Adams Salon & Spa (1356 Pacific Hwy) served as our excuse for an afternoon of bubble baths and “research” — Time Out has called it a dreamy bath house for years.

Kipling’s Garage Bar used to be the place you mentioned if you wanted to sound like you’d discovered something effortlessly local: Concrete Playground dubbed it an industrial‑chic wine bar less than a 60‑second walk from Turramurra station. Which, sure, is very on-brand for a suburb that’s simultaneously train‑friendly and suspiciously leafy.

There’s not a lot of local tabloid fury here — no weekly council punch‑ups in our data — mostly property listings on Kissing Point Road and Denman Street promising development potential and the inevitable people who salivate at “walk‑to‑rail” opportunities. Oh, and Pedestrian reminded the rest of Sydney that we sneaked into the double‑million median house‑price club — quaint, smug and entirely unsurprising.

Who would hate it here: anyone allergic to gumtrees, plant nurseries, or the idea of living somewhere people actually queue politely for croissants. Who would love it: gardeners stocking up at Honeysuckle, spa devotees still mentioning Gillian Adams like it’s a family heirloom, wine folks who know Tom’s Cellars, and anyone who likes getting to Ku‑Ring‑Gai Chase or Lane Cove National Park without a lot of faff.

For the record: most of what we’ve just reminisced about comes from older guides and reviews — so consider this a fond look back at the Turramurra we know, not an up‑to‑the‑minute directory.
