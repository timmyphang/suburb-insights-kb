# Aldgate (NSW)

Slug: `aldgate-nsw`

Back in the day we’d tell city friends to leave the skyline at home and come see what happens when a town decides its soundtrack is ‘70s vinyl and the occasional kookaburra. We start the weekend with the Aldgate Valley Nature Walk — Time Out even timed it for us: a roughly 30-minute drive from the CBD and a proper 14km return if you fancy pretending you’re training for something. The bandicoots, koalas and echidnas don’t care about our Instagram angles and that’s exactly the mood.

After we’ve earned our second breakfast, we’d mosey into the village. Broadsheet used to point people to FRED Eatery — the place that replaced Aldgate Providore in late 2015 — and to a retro newcomer called Dirty Doris on Strathalbyn Road, where former Low & Slow chef Denny Bradden served Reubens, ramen for breakfast and boozy milkshakes in full diner tribute. If you liked the idea of a converted 1800s chapel becoming a beauty salon, Concrete Playground had us covered with The Chapel Aldgate at 156 Mount Barker Road — very minimalism-meets-stained-glass.

We aren’t a ghost town: there’s a kindergarten on Fairview Rd, a Sports & Recreation Club and an Oval & Hall that still book out for community shenanigans, and the local RSL at 1 Kemp Rd if you need proper earnestness with your footy talk. Sitchu kept reminding newcomers that the Adelaide Hills backdrop is the whole mood, and Time Out even crowned this place a hidden-gem type once — not that we’d let that change the prices overnight.

Who would hate living here? If you need late-night rooftops, trams every five minutes or a shopping mall that’s open at 1am, you’ll sulk within 48 hours. Also, if bandicoots make you nervous, maybe park your anxiety elsewhere.

Who would love it? People who want weekend walks that end in a proper diner debate, families who value a village kindergarten and a local cricket oval, and anyone who’d rather drink coffee with gumtrees than watch a sunset behind a freeway. We know every corner here — and we’ll show you, provided you don’t expect a skyline selfie.
