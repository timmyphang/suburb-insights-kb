# Blakehurst (NSW)

Slug: `blakehurst-nsw`

Back in the day we’d call it a proper Saturday: a slow roll down King Georges Road to where Imperial Peking Restaurant — 979 King Georges Road, Blakehurst — stood like it had the lease on our nostalgia. It was an institution, was it not? The Peking duck was the thing people whispered about in the school pick-up line, and you could tell who’d been because they ate like they’d just done a tight budget for a whole month.

We’re a quietly practical bunch here. If you wanted excitement it usually involved who could get the better birthday-party package — MumsPages used to be our unofficial community noticeboard, listing everything from “Attractions and Places for Kids - Blakehurst (2221)” to local services like Photography by Paulina, ACTiv Elite Performers and Thriveable Fitness. Honestly, if your idea of a wild Friday is a toddler’s dance recital followed by a family photo that looks vaguely professional, you fit right in.

There’s not much tabloid-level drama in the archive — which, fair: our greatest scandals tend to be which bakery sells out of the good rolls first. The writing we found is mostly service listings and dining nostalgia, so we’ll keep the petty drama to the minimum: we’re too busy scheduling swim lessons and outranking each other on school-run logistics to stage anything scandalous.

Who’d hate living here? Nightlife hunters and people who measure suburbs by how many neon signs you can see late at night. If you’re after 24-hour bars and rooftop DJ sets, Blakehurst will bore you quietly and then ask you to take your shoes off before you leave.

Who would love it? Families who appreciate a reliable restaurant that’s been around long enough to have its own mythology, parents who use MumsPages like scripture, and anyone who wants a suburb that’s low-key, community-minded and unapologetically domestic. We’re not flashy. We’re the people who bring lamingtons to the barbecue and call it culture.
