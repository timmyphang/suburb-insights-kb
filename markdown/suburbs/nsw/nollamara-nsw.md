# Nollamara (NSW)

Slug: `nollamara-nsw`

We start our Sunday like locals used to: dragging the kids past 90 Hillsborough Drive, where Kakulas Sister Nollamara once filled our bags with dried chickpeas, Mediterranean deli tins and the kind of international confectionery that made school lunches mysterious. As Broadsheet noted, the Kakulas family — the same crew behind Kakulas Bros since 1929 — expanded into the suburbs and opened the Nollamara outpost in 2004, and for a while that was our small, smell‑good beating heart.

Full disclosure: the coverage we’ve got on file is all older stuff, so this is nostalgia, not a “where to brunch now” list. Back in the day Urban List even lumped Kakulas Sister in with Perth’s best delis, and there was a directory entry that mentioned Social House & Little Nolla Cafe (they sold eGift Cards, if you’re the sort who plans gift-giving years ahead).

If you’re looking for hot-take council feuds or late-night opening drama, the paperwork here is disappointingly calm. Parentopia used to list our mum groups and parenting resources, which is to say: more playdates than protests. Time Out once helpfully labelled Nollamara “affordable(ish)” with a house price snapshot of $627,000 — not flashy, but practical. We’ll take practical.

Who would hate living here? People who want their suburb to be a permanent launch party for new restaurants and Instagrammable concrete. If you need a curated nightlife crawl or a relentless rotation of buzzy openings, this quiet pantry-loving, family-friendly patch will bore you senseless.

Who loves it? Pantry purists who remember Kakulas Sister’s spice aisles, parents who relied on local mums’ groups, bargain-hunting first-home buyers who liked that Time Out number, and anyone who prefers a neighbourhood with proper staples over headline-seeking hype. We’ll keep our tins, our playgroup contacts and our modest house prices — and if you want adventure, well, Fremantle’s not far.
