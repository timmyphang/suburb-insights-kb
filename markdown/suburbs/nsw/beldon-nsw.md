# Beldon (NSW)

Slug: `beldon-nsw`

Back in the day — or, more honestly, in the slightly dusty corners of pre-2024 local listings — we were the suburb that quietly specialised in everything small humans and the people who love them need. Morning in Beldon, 6027: we shuffle about the house while remembering the time A Joey Named Jack Babywearing ran Kangatraining classes and even had a home showroom at 53 Lateral Loop, run by Em Hoskins. That memory is basically our town anthem.

Saturday routine: someone checks Playgroup WA’s finder, someone else scrolls MumsPages for sports for kids or party and event venues, and we all agree that the best civic planning is an afternoon that involves other people’s toddlers. MumsPages has been cataloguing our lives — businesses and service providers for mums, sports for kids, party venues — so for years the internet has known us as “where the mums are.”

We’ll be honest: the archive is older, so we’re doing a lot of remembering rather than headlining the latest openings. A Joey Named Jack used to consult from a home showroom at that Lateral Loop address — that’s a fact, not a recommendation for today. Parentopia’s broader listings once nudged families toward swim schools and pop-up party options; useful context, even if it feels a touch like reading last season’s stroller catalogue.

Who would hate living here? If you need neon nightlife, artisanal everything, or a postcode that shows off on Instagram, Beldon’s gentle, domestic hum won’t spark joy. If crowd-free weekends are your vibe, you’ll be bored by the abundance of Playgroup WA-supported meet-ups and MumsPages listings.

Who loves it? We do. Young families, babywearing converts, anyone who judges a suburb by the warmth of its playgroups rather than its bars — that’s our crowd. We’re the place that measures success in nap lengths and shared carpool turns. We might not be on the travel guides, but give us a push-pram, a playgroup finder and a friendly listing on MumsPages, and we’re content.
