# Kewdale (NSW)

Slug: `kewdale-nsw`

We’re the lot who know every corner of Kewdale — where the morning chorus is half kids at Kewdale Primary School on Kew Street and half the gentle thrum of things-that-move (we’re sandwiched by Perth Airport, remember?). Back in the day the community directory put our postcode at 6105, said we covered roughly 8 km² and cheerfully split us between the City of Belmont and bits of Kalamunda — electorates Belmont and Forrestfield, if you’re filling in forms.

A typical weekend starts with the parents who used to drop into Lathlain Playgroup — the one that catered to families from Carlisle, Cloverdale, Carlisle and us — and stroll past the site where the Town of Victoria Park was busy building a new multipurpose community centre at Lathlain Place. We like that: kids go to playgroup, go to Kewdale Primary at 264 Kew Street, and someone remembers to organise the sausage sizzle.

We’ve also had our more headline-y moments. Pedestrian TV once reported a really serious incident in a Kewdale home — grim and upsetting for everyone — and we don’t laugh about that. News like that reminds us why community stuff matters.

For the curious: once upon a time Urban List name‑checked Wright Street Collective at 10 Wright St as a top Perth tattoo spot, and Concrete Playground noted that Sixt used to offer pet‑friendly car rentals from Kewdale. Those were the days (or at least the articles said they were).

Who would hate living here? If you’re after leafy bayside promenades, heritage terraces or an uninterrupted soundtrack of boutique cafés, Kewdale will politely break your heart. We’re practical, a little patchwork, surrounded by Rivervale, Cloverdale, Welshpool and Wattle Grove — and we like it that way.

Who would love it? Families who want local services, a proper primary school, hands-on community groups and people who appreciate a suburb that does community infrastructure quietly but properly. We’re not trying to be anything else. Come for the convenience, stay because your kid made a friend at playgroup, or because that tattoo studio on Wright Street once caught an editor’s eye. We’ll keep the community centre warm for you.
