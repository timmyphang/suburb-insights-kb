# Marmion (NSW)

Slug: `marmion-nsw`

We start the weekend like proper locals: throw on something you don’t mind getting sea salt on, saddle up and ride the old Marmion to Burns Beach route until the sun tells us to slow down. Back in the day — and yes, our brief archive confirms there’s no recent coverage to brag about — that was the kind of morning that made us grin.

If we were staying for breakfast, Urban List used to point us toward nearby Cottesloe’s Daisies at 305 Marmion St (they made the 50 breakfasts list and even cropped up in the dog-friendly round‑ups). For sugar therapy, Urban List also flagged a Marmion address — 21 Sheppard Way, Marmion WA 6020 — when waxing lyrical about cinnamon scrolls. So picture us, sea breeze, sticky fingers, and the eternal debate of whether to bike another kilometre or nap on the sand.

Our community résumé is gloriously domestic. The Marmion Warwick Playgroup — volunteer-run, running weekday sessions at Duncraig Recreation Centre, with session times like 9–11am and enrolment details on their site — is the sort of thing that keeps us honest. They’ve been around since 1991; as their join page notes, membership was $35 per term plus a $35 Playgroup WA fee. We take our push‑and‑pull of picnic rugs and playlist requests very seriously.

And for the tabloids? As Broadsheet once reminded us, the most headline‑grabbing thing in circulation was a raffle prize: a $3.1 million fully furnished home in Marmion, Perth — plus half a million in cash. If that doesn’t sum up our hazards of being quietly desirable, nothing will.

Who would hate it here? Nightlife hunters and anyone chasing permanent novelty — the paperwork for our playgroup AGM is more thrilling than half the venues city types queue for. Who’d love it? Families who value a good morning routine, cyclists who worship a sunset ride, bakers and cinnamon‑scroll loyalists, and anyone keen on a seaside postcode that prefers kettles and community noticeboards to celebrity DJs.

We’re not loud about it. We just keep the sand in the car and come back for another playgroup session.
