# The Patch (NSW)

Slug: `the-patch-nsw`

We live in The Patch, NSW — the place you try to explain while someone on the phone Googles “patch” and comes up with half a country’s worth of salads, theatres and pop‑ups. Mornings here are a shrug and a smile: we make our own quiet rituals because, frankly, no one in the media has actually settled on which “patch” they mean. 

Back in the day Broadsheet was waxing lyrical about a Summertown restaurant that used “organic veggies grown at The Patch on co‑owner Anton van Klopper’s property,” and we still get a smug little buzz when anyone mentions organic produce and the name. Over in Stirling, Broadsheet once profiled Patch Kitchen & Garden — “like your grandpa’s garden shed, done schmick” — which, yes, sounds picturesque, and no, we are not claiming their courtyard as ours. Broadsheet Melbourne even fretted that The Little Veggie Patch Co.’s Pop Up Patch at Federation Square might be uprooted; we felt personally affronted on behalf of every plot of soil with a marketing budget.

If you grew up thinking “the patch” meant getting muddy and sticky at Beerenberg Farm strawberry picking, Sitchu reminded city folks to “head out to the patch” for the juiciest berries — which is to say: the word works hard. Patch shows up in Richmond as a paleo café, pops up as theatre lore in Edward & Ida’s history, and turns up on roundups of farm stays and long‑weekend escapes. It’s like everyone’s nicknaming their business after our surname.

Who would hate living here? People who want a unique postcode brag. If you’re into exclusivity, bespoke branding, or correcting strangers every time they mention a different patch, you’ll find us maddeningly communal. 

Who would love it? Anyone who likes being part of a cheerful ambiguity: the kind of people who don’t mind sharing their name with an artisanal garden shed, a strawberry field and a pop‑up in the city. We’re proud of the mundane: a patch can be an orchard, a theatre, a café, or just the place you call home. We’ll keep the name. You can keep Googling which one we are.
