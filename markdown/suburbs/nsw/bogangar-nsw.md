# Bogangar (NSW)

Slug: `bogangar-nsw`

Back in the day — and by that we mean the clippings we've got are more nostalgia than news — we’d wobble out of Yo Caba Caba’s pastel palace and pretend we were still on holiday. Time Out crowned that seven-bed, Palm‑Springs‑meets-beachhouse on our strip the Best Family Friendly Stay in Australia, and honestly the whole town hasn’t stopped posing for the brochure since.

Our weekend routine used to be gloriously simple: shuffle down Tweed Coast Road, try not to stare too hard at the neighbours’ pool, and pop into Fuel Bakehouse at 45 Tweed Coast Rd (they were in The Urban List’s pies and bakeries roundups). You could almost feel the tourist dollars exhale. If you needed to be soothed after hosting a week of airbnb guests, Kalm Springs (Shop 2, 27 Tweed Coast Road) was the spa that got mentioned in the lists — the kind of place you booked like you’d booked a therapist, but cheaper and with more eucalyptus.

We’ve even had culinary clout — No. 35 Kitchen & Bar gets name‑dropped in the same breath as proper restaurants; it used to be linked to Icebergs’ lineage via Daniel Medcalf, which is the kind of sentence that makes people take our dining scene seriously at parties.

Who would hate living here? If you loathe pastels, pools that are purposefully photogenic, and the faint hum of guests arguing about “is it walkable?” you’ll be miserable. If you despise being within easy reach of a holiday house that Time Out said was “family friendly,” then, sure: Bogangar might bruise your urban sensibilities.

Who would love it here? We know the types: people who want a beach town that clocks in as both low‑key and inexplicably Instagrammable, those who think a spa booking is a life choice, and anyone who sees “Fuel Bakehouse” on a hit list and feels comforted rather than confused. We’re small, a little smug about our award‑winning holiday house, and perfectly content to wave at the tourists as they discover our strip — again.
