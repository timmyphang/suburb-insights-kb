# Lakelands (NSW)

Slug: `lakelands-nsw`

We start our week like proper locals: back in the day Tuesdays meant Lakelands Puddleducks at Lakelands Community Hall — 10:00 AM, 135 Ambleside Circuit — where we’d pretend we weren’t just there for the tea and the gossip. As Playgroup NSW recorded, that was our little weekday ritual, the kind of thing that makes a place feel like a place rather than a postcode.

On weekends we were quieter than the city, and that suited us. UrbanList used to namecheck the lakelands for the turtles and that delightfully snaking boardwalk, the native bushfood garden, gumtree corridor and even an off‑leash dog area with a swimming lagoon — all the outdoorsy bits you brag about on a picnic blanket. Another UrbanList note even linked the name to the Coombabah Lakelands Conservation Area and the Koala and Melaleuca Track Loop, so if we sounded like we belonged in a nature calendar, that wasn’t entirely our fault.

If you’re assembling a list of suburban creature comforts, Findglocal had us down on paper: Lakelands Shopping Centre at 49 Banksiadale Gate with Coles, Aldi and “38+ stores” — the kind of line on a website that makes you think “ooh, local economy!” even as you queue at the register.

There isn’t much spicy local drama in these pages — mostly community listings and lifestyle writeups — which is fine. We’ll own the calm. What we don’t have (according to our admittedly older scrapbook of sources) is a frantic late‑night scene. If you need clubs, 24/7 neon or a parade of newfangled inner‑city hotspots, you’ll hate us: we’re too fond of boardwalks and playgroups for that.

Who loves it here? Families, walkers, dog people and anyone who secretly enjoys a quiet Saturday among turtles and gumtrees. We’re the kind of suburb that shows up in mums’ directories, community listings and the occasional nature roundup — modest, practical and low‑key. Full disclosure: these mentions are from older sources, so think of this as nostalgia rather than a current events notice. Still, if you like suburbs that feel like they have a heartbeat (and a puddleduck or two), we’re quietly proud to be it.
