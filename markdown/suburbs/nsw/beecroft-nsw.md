# Beecroft (NSW)

Slug: `beecroft-nsw`

Back in the day we’d prop the pram by the rotunda and pretend we were doing something cultural — that was Beecroft Village Green, cherished since 1886 and, as Hornsby Shire Mums noted, recently renewed after more than 135 locals chipped in feedback in 2022. We were proud, mildly smug and very particular about playground mulch.

There used to be a neat little option for sticky-handed commuters: the Beecroft Railway Station Gardens Playground sat right next to Beecroft train station and the bus stop, a convenient stop-off for tired kids (Hornsby Shire Mums ran that one, too). If we wanted proper tree ambience, Fearnley Park’s playground — tucked inside the Blue Gum High Forest with swings, a slide, monkey bars and picnic seating — was the place to pretend we loved the outdoors. Two entrances, remember? Hannah Street and Chapman Avenue, very helpful for the map-obsessed grandparents.

We even had a local childcare listed — Beecroft Buddies Childcare Centre, a 50-place spot that used to be near the station at 45 Wongala Crescent (they were listed with phone 02 9484 4740). That’s the sort of micro-detail we clutch like a heritage listing.

Who would hate it here? Anyone who needs 24-hour nightlife, skateboard parks bigger than a phone screen, or thinks a picnic table is a nightclub. Who’d love it? Families, people who rate school-run efficiency, and anyone who thinks “village green” is an actual lifestyle choice and not just a roundabout with good vibes.
