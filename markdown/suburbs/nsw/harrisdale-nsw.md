# Harrisdale (NSW)

Slug: `harrisdale-nsw`

Back in the day we’d roll out of bed and pretend we were five minutes from the city while still getting our photoshoot done: RainDance Photography used to promise a boutique newborn shoot “in our home studio nestled in Perth’s southern suburb of Harrisdale, just 25km from the CBD”. We’d smile for the camera, wrestle a toddler into polyester, and call it lifestyle content.

Weekend routine (circa when things were fresher): we’d duck into Timber Cafe — Urban List’s write-up loved its seasonal menu and that full-on scandi timber vibe — or wander the suburban marketplace for a pistachio‑topped cannoli because, yes, Urban List once name‑checked our spot on the best cannoli tour. There was even a deli with an “extensive olive selection” and a dedicated cheese room, if you liked your Sunday cheese to be judged by a blog.

Petty local drama? Oh, absolutely. Junkee immortalised the night a masked man tried to escape Harrisdale Bunnings by rappelling from the ceiling with a garden hose. We still tell the story with applause for the employees who intervened and for the fact the haul was only about $200 worth of power tools — peak suburbs.

We’re a bit of a mum hub, too. MumsPages lists our school holiday camps and family support services, and BabyCenter threads prove there’s a small army of local mums swapping recommendations, fitness classes, and playground times. If you like coordinating snack rotations and carpool spreadsheets, this is your spiritual home.

Who would hate living here? People who think suburbs should be anonymous squares on a map. Folks who demand 24/7 nightlife and zero prams will find Harrisdale maddeningly domestic. If you loathe cannoli, cheese rooms, and the gentle hum of photo flashes at 8am, move along.

Who would love it? Families, newborn photographers, deli devotees and anyone who enjoys a community where every park doubles as an audition for RainDance Photography and every local scandal involves a garden hose, a ceiling, and a couple of power tools. We’ll keep the biscotti warm.
