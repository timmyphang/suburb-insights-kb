# Tecoma (NSW)

Slug: `tecoma-nsw`

Tecoma — we’re the place your Melbourne Food & Wine Festival used to quietly flirt with before the big city remembered it had to be seen. Back in the day the festival’s World’s Longest Brunch made its first pit stop at Tecoma Lawn, where brunch-goers sampled Julia’s cinnamon buns, lemon mascarpone tart and chocolate and almond pasticcini, then wandered off to Taxodium Lawn and The Alto like nothing had changed. That’s basically our claim to fame: a tasteful cameo in a national parade of pastries.

A typical weekend for us, historically speaking, is less about nightlife and more about practical pleasures. We used to point new parents and anxious molar-touchers to Smile Place — Tecoma Dental (listed at 1586 Burwood Highway), and there’s the rehab-ish-sounding Endless Motion at Unit 6, 1579 Burwood Highway in the listings if you’ve overdone the gardening. Our online footprint is a proud directory mosaic: 23 hair salons once peppered the pages (M.Y.Salon, M J’s Hair Studio, Clip & Colour), bakeries asking to be featured, and a decent roster of takeaways — Tecoma Fish & Chips & Pizza, Tecoma Charcoal Chicken and Chicken A.go.go made the cut in one of those business lists.

If you want scandal, our archives are disappointingly wholesome. No council coups, no dramatic pub fights — mostly business listings and a festival stop. That tells you everything: we’re more organised-Suburb-Directory than tabloid fodder.

Who would hate it here? If your life requires a nightly rooftop DJ set, endless breaking news, or a revolving door of celebrity pop-ups, you’ll be bored. This wasn’t written for you.

Who would love it? People who like being near festival green space (Tecoma Lawn gets name-checked for a reason), who appreciate knowing where the nearest clinic or salon was listed, and who enjoy a suburb that turns up on festival programs rather than the police blotter. We’re quietly useful, fond of pastries in theory and annoyingly charming in practice.
