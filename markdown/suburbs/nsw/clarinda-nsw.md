# Clarinda (NSW)

Slug: `clarinda-nsw`

We love saying “Clarinda” like it’s an invitation, mainly because once a year someone at 59 Viney Street treats the whole suburb like their living room. Back in the day The Urban List even slipped us into a Melbourne round‑up: 59 Viney Street, a two‑storey Christmas lights extravaganza, tens of thousands of lights layering the front drive right up to the fence — impossible to miss. We still queue on the footpath, lips blue from winter and phones out, pretending we came for the community spirit and not the glow‑up content.

If you can’t stand seasonal excess, this is not your jam. Parking patience is a virtue and subtlety is a dirty word here. Conversely, if you think “more is more” and you measure holidays in bulbs per square metre, you’ll feel very seen.

We don’t have a scandal to bore you with — just one very committed street and the annual glow that makes the rest of us look like we tried. Come for the lights, stay for the neighbour who actually rewired a hedge. We’re Clarinda; we’ll outshine your suburb and apologise politely afterwards.
