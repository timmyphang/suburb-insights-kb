# Harrington Park (NSW)

Slug: `harrington-park-nsw`

Back in the day we could time our Fridays by the squeal quotient — the Harrington Park Pixies & Munchkins playgroup used to meet during the school term at the Harrington Park Community Centre, 33a Fairwater Dr, usually between 10:15am and noon. Playgroup NSW and Camden Kids both had the details, and yes, the contact was listed as pixiesandmunchkins@gmail.com — in case you miss nostalgia as much as we do.

Weekend routine: we’d saunter down to Harrington Park Lake on Royal George Drive, lug a pack of sausages to the BBQ, claim a covered picnic table, argue about who actually brought the esky, then trot off along the cycle path while someone pretended the fitness station was for exercise and not for checking phone reception. If sport’s your thing, Sir Warwick Fairfax Oval on Sir Warwick Fairfax Drive had the canteen, playground and the field to make you feel mildly competent on a Saturday morning.

There’s no local fury dossier in the files here — no council scandals or celebrity cake-bakes — just community listings and happy chaos. That means the “petty local drama” section is basically: who stole the last snag? (We don’t know. It was probably us.)

Who would hate it here? People who crave anonymous city grit, Michelin-starred chaos or nightlife that stays up past bedtime. Who would love it? Families, playgroup refugees, anyone who rates a good playground, a BBQ that won’t judge you, and a lake path that doubles as a promenade and escape plan. For years the data showed exactly that — soft-centred suburban pride, wrapped in picnic paper.
