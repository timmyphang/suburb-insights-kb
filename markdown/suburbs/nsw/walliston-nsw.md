# Walliston (NSW)

Slug: `walliston-nsw`

Back in the day — and by that we mean the only coverage we can find — Walliston’s claim to fame was petals and produce, not nightspots. We know this place by the taste of a warm peach and the sightlines down Halleendale Road where S&R Orchard stages its Blossom Festival (2–25 September, 8am–5pm). We’ve queued for drive‑thru produce, pre‑ordered boxes, and argued quietly about whether blossom season beats summer fruit picking (December–March). 

Our weekend ritual is embarrassingly wholesome: we roll out of bed, drive the short way to S&R, pretend we’re just “looking” and leave with four kilos of something sticky. Urban List used to send people our way with a grin — The Fruit Corner, Leotta’s Orchard and Tony’s Summer Fruits were name‑checked for orchard‑hopping — and we keep those routes like a local map tattoo.

There’s no juicy council brawl in the papers, no scandal — the archives are mostly guides and MumsPages listings. If you want drama, you’ll be disappointed; the internet’s version of Walliston is a directory for mums: party and event providers, kids’ sports, NDIS providers. That’s the vibe.

Who would hate it here? People who crave late‑night bars and boutique chaos; those allergic to seasonal fruit or being labelled “the place with the blossom trees.” Who would love it? Families, picnic obsessives, anyone who thinks a drive‑thru full of peaches is a Sunday one‑stop. We’ll keep tending our orchards and pretending the rest of Perth doesn’t secretly envy our annual pink carpet.
