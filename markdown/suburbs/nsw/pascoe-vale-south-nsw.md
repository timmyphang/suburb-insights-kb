# Pascoe Vale South (NSW)

Slug: `pascoe-vale-south-nsw`

We like to pretend Pascoe Vale South is our little secret, until a tourist asks for directions and we remember Melville Road is basically our main drag. Back in the day we'd wander down past 71 Melville Road for a quiet scoop at Miinot Gelato — Emma and Gary’s gelato was worth the detour — then keep strolling to 225 Melville Road where Shop225 used to be the pizzeria everyone talked about.

Weekend routine used to look like this: a quick nod to the old milk‑bar turned Emil’s Café (the El‑Khoury brothers John and George refurbished their parents’ shop into an 80‑seat, licensed all‑day spot with a fireplace and a modern Australian–Arabic menu that served things like Turkish eggs with sujuk and hummus shakshuka), a detour to Parkstone at 9A Parkstone Avenue for that plant‑filled, inner‑city‑cool vibe, and maybe a class at Sunroom Studio on 142 Melville Road — the pottery place that opened in 2023 with wheel‑throwing and kids sessions.

We’re not shy about our boasts: Shop225 was Coeliac Australia–accredited, pulling off gluten‑free pizzas from a wood‑fired oven with a 72‑hour rested dough. Time Out even named it among the Asia‑Pacific top pizza places once — seventh, if you were counting — which made our local strip feel like it had accidentally entered an eating contest with the rest of Melbourne.

Petty local drama? The press did most of it for us: reviews, rankings and roundups turned our sleepy strip into a food column storyline. We’ll admit it made for great bragging material at BBQs.

Who would hate living here? If you can’t stand the idea of a suburb that quietly doubles as a pizza pilgrimage, or you scoff at plant‑filled cafes bringing “inner‑city cafe cool” to the north, you’ll probably pack up and head somewhere with neon and a rooftop bar.

Who’d love it here? People who want awarded pizza, Coeliac‑friendly options, excellent gelato from Emma and Gary, a lovingly converted milk‑bar with a fireplace, and even the odd pottery class. We’ll keep the Melville Road loop warm for you — just don’t tell too many people.
