# Tootgarook (NSW)

Slug: `tootgarook-nsw`

We’re standing at Romney Park with sand in our sandals and a toddler’s lost sock in our hand, because this is Tootgarook — the kind of place where your weekend agenda is beach, playgroup, nap, repeat.

Back in the day the mentions we get are all from older guides and listings — Urban List once flagged The Keith Motel at 1929 Point Nepean Road as “quaint yet updated,” Time Out included our motel revival vibes in a roundup, and Concrete Playground remembered Shoreline Hideaway on our strip as part of Ocean Blue Coastal Retreats’ pet-friendly roster. So yes, we’ve made travel lists — mostly as a cosy coastal sidebar rather than a neon-lit destination.

Our mornings look like this: kids to Lyndel Childcare Centre (they’ve been here since 1973, bless them), playgroup at Romney Park, and then a walk past the house on 39 Ronald Street that the listing boasted was “750m to the beach” — which in Tootgarook terms means we can absolutely be in the water before we’ve fully processed sunscreen regret. The Tootgarook primary school hall still hosts the community things Mum & Dad love, and the peninsula’s sleepy charm is very much our vibe.

There’s no scandal in the feed — no council brouhaha in the files, just holiday cottages and retro motels getting a revival shout-out. If you want late-night CBD cocktails, five-star dining theatrics or a thumb-war with traffic every afternoon, you’ll hate it here. We don’t have that energy. We have sand in the car, fenced yards for dogs (Ocean Blue knows what’s up), and motels that feel like a warm nod to road trips of yore.

Who’d love Tootgarook? Dog people, small families, road-trippers nostalgic for a “seaside cottage dream” and anyone who thinks being within a brisk 750 metres of the beach is a legitimate life goal. Come for the quiet, stay for Lyndel Childcare’s reliability since 1973, and leave your rush at Point Nepean Road. We’ll keep a towel on the line.
