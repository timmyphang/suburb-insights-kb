# North Rocks (NSW)

Slug: `north-rocks-nsw`

We start the week like locals do: if it’s Thursday the North Rocks Koorana playgroup is already claiming Don Moore Reserve by 9:30am — Playgroup NSW lists them meeting there — so you’ll see prams, puppets and the soft thud of school‑run logistics. Don Moore Reserve itself sits tucked behind North Rocks Public School and, as ParraParents reminded us, has that very reassuring fenced playground with synthetic grass, toilets and on‑site parking. Practicality is our aesthetic.

On weekends we split time between the fully fenced North Rocks Park — yes, the one with the spider‑rope pyramid that makes primary kids feel like they’ve conquered Everest — and short bushwalks down the Murri‑Yanna Track to North Rocks Dam in Bidjigal Reserve. ParraParents has the route; we have the picnic rug and the inexplicable urge to compare climbing speeds. Hazel Ryan Oval is our stealth option for a quieter loop and a small playground when we want less spectacle and more snacks.

Back in the day Riverside Brewing Company got a lot of column inches — Broadsheet lists it at 2 North Rocks Road, North Parramatta, and Time Out once documented the brewery’s lineup (33 golden ale, 44 American amber, 55 pale ale, 69 summer ale… the sort of numerical bravado we respect). That’s history now, one of the local footnotes we trot out when we want to sound cultured.

Real estate listings on Sitchu love reminding everyone that we’re “blue‑ribbon” — handy if you enjoy school catchment bragging rights: The Kings School, Tara Anglican, Murray Farm Public School and the North Rocks Tennis Centre all get name‑checked in nearby listings. Angsana Education even used to offer Mandarin enrichment out of Shop T74 at North Rocks Shopping Centre, according to MumsPages — because yes, we like our EFL and extracurricula almost as much as our parks.

Who’d hate it here? If your life requires a thumping night scene or boutique laneway drama, North Rocks will quietly bore you — we prefer fenced playgrounds to rooftop clubs. Who’d love it? Families, bushwalkers, and anyone who values practical amenities, decent parking and a community that cheers when the local playground gets a new rope. We’re grounded, green and mildly competitive about our climbing pyramids — and proud of it.
