# Queanbeyan (NSW)

Slug: `queanbeyan-nsw`

Back in the day we used to brag that a weekend in Queanbeyan was the perfect hybrid: country pub, river scrub, and the kind of food scene that makes Canberra pretend it invented regional charm. We know where to start: the Royal Hotel Queanbeyan’s rib‑eye, fed by Bungendore Country Butchery and dressed in the sacred Kiss — keep it simple, stupid — used to be the kind of meal that shut up any visiting foodie from the capital for at least one course.

Mornings were for muddy boots and the Molonglo Gorge / Blue Tiles Trail through Kowen Forest — a three‑kilometre route with rock pools that feel like you’ve walked into a postcard. If you were feeling vertical, White Rocks above the Queanbeyan River and K7 Adventures used to be the spot for beginners to learn not to panic at limestone faces. Afternoons drifted to Cartel Taqueria (yes, the taco place that isn’t in Canberra at all), or a corner table at Campbell & George — the Sunday roast place “just over the NSW border in Canberra’s twin city Queanbeyan” at Campbell and George Streets that everyone referenced on their way to somewhere else.

We’ve hosted meat-pie devotionals, too — there was an exhibition in town honouring the humble pie that made us all unashamedly proud of our pastry game. And let’s not forget homegrown talent: Omar Musa, our poet/rapper/author, who came out of Queanbeyan with books and performances that made the rest of the country sit up and notice.

For years the region also got mentions in wider travel pieces — the Queanbeyan Heritage Walk shows up on road‑trip guides, and there were plans and chatter about rail upgrades and even emergency‑alert trials that included our postcode. So yes, we’re not a secret. We’re the well‑worn twin to a capital city, with enough trails, pubs and taco integrity to make a weekend worthwhile.

Who would hate living here? People who need a 24/7 skyline and don’t like a decent roast or a gorge to walk off it. Who would love it? Hikers, climbers, meat‑pie devotees, taco pilgrims and anyone who likes their city companion with a side of bush and a decent steak. We’ll keep the chips. You bring the map.
