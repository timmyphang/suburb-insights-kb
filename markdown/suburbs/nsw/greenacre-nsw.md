# Greenacre (NSW)

Slug: `greenacre-nsw`

We wake up in Greenacre to the domestic symphony: someone opening a shop roller door on Waterloo Road, a kid being dragged to St John Vianney, and the faint, lingering promise of pistachio. Back in the day we’d stroll down to 331 Waterloo Road — Café Levant — for ka’ak and those absurd Dubai chocolate pancakes that Urban List and Sitchu fell in love with. It felt like the whole street ran on pastry glue and strong tea.

Weekends used to mean options you don’t get in the brochure suburbs: a quick loop past 183 Waterloo Road where Al Aseel would be doing what Al Aseel does best — packed tables, hummus, baba ghanoush and falafels flying out to big family groups even on weeknights (Time Out made sure everyone knew). If we wanted something chilled and sweet, we’d nip to Dairee at 2/171A Waterloo Road for the ever-changing fro‑yo flavours and the toppings-for-$1 hustle that Urban List praised.

Our local drama? It’s less soap opera, more head-scratcher. There was that peculiar Pedestrian.TV story about a Greenacre rental listed for $0 per week — rent-free in theory, but you had to renovate it yourself. Classic: free rent, full renovation — almost sounds like an extreme home-reno reality show pilot episode nobody asked for.

If you hate noise, kids, and endless plates slid across big communal tables, this won’t be your jam. If the idea of councils fussing over flight paths — yes, Time Out flagged our suburb on a list of places likely to get more overhead traffic — makes you twitch, you’ll prefer somewhere where the only planes are the paper ones you toss in the park.

But here’s our boast: we’re messy in the best way. We’ve got proper local staples (the ones the guides used to write about), a Wednesday morning playgroup crowd at St John Vianney on Tempe Street, and a street that rewards curiosity — pistachio pancakes, fro‑yo experiments, and humus that’s worth elbowing for. If you love food that actually fills you up and streets that sound lived-in, Greenacre has been quietly thriving exactly like that.
