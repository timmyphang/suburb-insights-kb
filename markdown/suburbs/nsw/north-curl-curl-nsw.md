# North Curl Curl (NSW)

Slug: `north-curl-curl-nsw`

We wake to the sound of southerlies and the distant slap of surf against the rock shelves — or, if it’s summer, the tinny hum of a mint‑green kombi arriving with Pepitas’ vegan ice‑cream. Back in the day the kombi was our optimistic little French pastry rebellion; Broadsheet even wrote about Mélanie Chaix driving it around. We’d wander down to the Curl Curl Rock Pool on Huston Parade for a cautious lap — the pool’s been holding us since the mid‑1930s — then stroll the lagoon where the dog beach does its own version of community policing.

For years the local scene felt like a secret: Broadsheet called it “the perfect Sydney beach” — warm lagoon, sheltered corner, reliable surf and, famously, no tourists. Then there were the small victories of civilisation — Staple’s fifth outpost was rumoured to be coming to North Curl Curl (yes, the sourdough obsessives; yes, the 48‑hour lamination fantasies), and Cooh Café & Roaster used to promise organic everything from Rachel Trevarton and Scarlett Forward. Muchacha at 3/142 Pitt Road earned a nod from Urban List for “Cali‑style” Mexican that made crossing the bridge worth it.

Petty local drama? If you want soap opera we’ve got literal bricks washing up — Time Out reported huge packages of cocaine turning up on our stretch (Freshwater and North Curl Curl among the unlucky recipients). It’s the sort of headline that makes you lock the ute and check the tide chart twice.

Who would hate living here: people who loathe sand in shoes, dog hair on the sofa, or beaches that occasionally demand respect (Concrete Playground wasn’t shy about calling our breaks “exciting but dangerous” with southerly swells and hazardous rips). If you want a manicured nightlife or queues of tourists, this isn’t your Insta backdrop.

Who would love it: families who value the playgroup at the Curl Curl Youth and Community Centre (242 Abbott Road), volunteers who turn up for the first‑Sunday beach clean by the lagoon, walkers on the two‑kilometre Curl Curl to Freshwater coastal loop via McKillop Park lookout, and anyone who prefers a surf that’s earned. We’re small, salty and stubbornly local — and yes, we’ll keep sharing our ice‑cream if you bring the beach blanket.
