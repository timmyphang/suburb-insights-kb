# Hove (NSW)

Slug: `hove-nsw`

Hove, NSW — we’ll admit it: our favourite weekend move is turning up late to the seafront and pretending the ribbon-cutting was for us. Back in 2025 they shuffled the whole stretch from the King Alfred Leisure Centre to Hove Lagoon into something the council cheerfully called Hove Beach Park — a £13.7 million seaside facelift that arrived with a padel exhibition and a weekend of free activities. We clapped politely and then queued for the pedalo.

Mornings here are predictable in the best way. We stroll past Hove Lagoon Fun Play (yes, the pedalos, mini golf, trampolines and bouncy castle are all exactly where you think they are), listen for the whirr of the miniature trains at Hove Park Miniature Railway on The Droveway, and salute anyone who still remembers Hove Museum as a proper rainy-day time capsule. If you want to be smug about your parenting, there’s evidence we’ve long supported groups like Mothers Uncovered and nature-focused meetups such as Wyld Mothers Fire — the directories on MyBump2Baby have made it all embarrassingly easy.

Our local drama, if you like your drama with council paperwork, was all very polite. Brighton & Hove City Council — yes, the one that treats consultations like public theatre — sought feedback on plans to consolidate Hove Park School onto the Nevill Campus from September 2028 after a reduction in the school’s Published Admission Number. We attended the Q&A and practiced our outrage between sips of tea.

Who would hate living here? People who loathe family-friendly noise, padel demonstrations and mini-golf queues. Also anyone allergic to nostalgia: if you can’t handle a seaside town still flipping fondly through its past (and its museum exhibits), you’ll be frustrated.

Who would love it here? Young families, nostalgic wanderers, and anyone who thinks a seaside padel exhibition counts as culture. We’re low-key, a little sentimental, and perfectly happy to be the town that makes other places feel rushed. Come for the lagoon, stay for the miniature railway, and don’t forget to RSVP to the next consultation.
