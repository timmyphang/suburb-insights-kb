# Menangle Park (NSW)

Slug: `menangle-park-nsw`

We’re the ones who still know which fence line marks the border of Menangle Park and who can tell you the postcode off the top of our heads — 2563, thank you very much. Back in the day Urban List reported South West Sydney was set to roll out better bus services through Menangle and Menangle Park; Route 889 was slated to become a seven‑day service with an extra 83 weekly trips. We’ve been practising our surprised faces ever since.

A typical morning here is waving at the same neighbour at the stop and arguing about whether the 889 will actually turn up on time. For years the local internet lifelines were directory pages — MumsPages ran whole sections for Businesses and Service Providers for Mums, Sports for Kids, Health and Wellbeing, and even NDIS providers. So yes, we’ve got the organisational infrastructure; what we’ve been missing is the unanimous approval of every weekend timetable.

Petty local drama? The only scandal we can confidently reheat is transit timetables and the eternal debate about which side of Menangle vs Menangle Park gets the nicer view. That’s as spicy as it gets.

Who would hate living here: people who need a cosmopolitan parade every block or nightly rooftop DJs. Who would love it: parents who appreciate a local directory that actually lists kids’ sports and support services, anyone who enjoys knowing their bus route by name (we’re looking at you, Route 889), and people who like a postcode that quietly gets on with things while the rest of Sydney panics.
