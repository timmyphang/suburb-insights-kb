# Hocking (NSW)

Slug: `hocking-nsw`

We wake up in Hocking, NSW, make a coffee, and five minutes later Google apologises and sends our identity to Ohio. Welcome to postcode anonymity: we’re the place everyone confuses with a gorge.

Back in the day the internet did what it pleases — search Hocking and you get Ash Cave Gorge, Hocking Hills State Park and Time Out breathlessly describing 80‑foot waterfalls (great venue, wrong hemisphere). Meanwhile Broadsheet keeps bumping into our name in Melbourne: “Bounty of the Sun” (Ryo Doyama, Eben Hocking, Bill Stafilis), The Diplomat (partners Eben Hocking, Bill Stafilis and Russell Kingston) and even the Rare Hare weekend crowd sipping Willow Creek pinot noir at the Jackalope Hotel. As Broadsheet noted, “Hocking” is everywhere — just not necessarily here.

We like to imagine our Saturday ritual: we queue at a non‑existent café, argue about which Hocking is more photogenic (Dan Hocking’s hot‑air balloon snaps have standards we can only aspire to), then flick through Junkee pieces by Courteney Hocking like bittersweet local fan mail. It’s oddly comforting that the name Hocking carries weight — in vineyards, restaurants and bylines — even if none of those addresses are on our street directory.

The petty drama? There isn’t one we can claim — only a slow, ongoing turf war with search engines and Time Out editors who will forever romanticise someone else’s caves. Pedestrian once used the phrase “Bieber‑hocking” and honestly that sums up the confusion: we’re an identity meme, not a municipal scandal.

Who would hate living here: anyone who needs their suburb to come up first on a map, or folks who consider clarity a basic human right. Also city snobs who expect a Broadsheet review on Friday nights (we can’t help it that the nearest hip izakaya is an interstate problem).

Who would love it here: people who enjoy being off the tourist grid, who don’t mind sharing a surname with a photographer, a writer or a vineyard, and who find comfort in a place that quietly refuses to be famous on cue. We’re small, we're undercaffeinated, and we’re perfectly fine with being our own obscure little Hocking.
