# Tullimbar (NSW)

Slug: `tullimbar-nsw`

Saturday mornings in Tullimbar feel like a low-key episode of playgroup TV: we tiptoe through Lego minefields and follow the sad, soothing rhythm of a facilitated discussion. Back in the day Keeping Mum ran the sort of weekly program that made our suburb feel like a specialised little ecosystem — music/sensory class one week, messy play the next, a special guest or a themed dress-up with canapes and drinks when we weren’t too sleep-deprived to celebrate. As Keeping Mum put it, it’s where you “meet the same mums, bubs and toddlers each week” and find your coterie. We know those faces. We’ve swapped parenting hacks, swapped childcare horror stories, and swapped recipes we’ll never make.

MumsPages even listed Tullimbar (2527) as a place to compare home products and services — proof that we’re small but organised. It’s the kind of suburb where the directory matters more than a glossy cafe listing.

Who would hate living here? If you loathe playdough, facilitated chats about nap routines, or the idea of your social calendar revolving around sensory bins, you’ll feel misplaced. If you require nightlife, artisanal this-or-that, or absolute silence between 9pm and 6am, maybe keep driving.

Who would love it? Young families, stick-with-us mums looking for routine, people who don’t mind a suburb that’s quietly all about kids and community. We’re not flashy. We are practical, a little sticky, and impeccably organised on Google and MumsPages. And frankly, we like it that way.
