# Woodvale (NSW)

Slug: `woodvale-nsw`

We live in Woodvale, NSW — and if you’re expecting a hot take, so were we. Problem is: the internet hasn’t been to our street. The only press we could find about “Woodvale” is busy being very extra somewhere else, which is honestly peak us.

Morning routine: we make the coffee at home because there’s no local write-up telling us where to queue. Instead we read about Perth’s 18 Maitland Rise — Urban List called its Christmas lights a stand‑out, complete with a Santa sleigh, snow machine, bubble machine and cameo characters, and raising money for the Perth Children’s Hospital Foundation. Urban List also remembers a Woodvale Tavern at 143 Trappers Dr doing a mean Sunday carvery and staging Saint Patrick’s Day shenanigans, and a place billed simply as “The Beauty Gallery” described as “beauty heaven.” Junkee even drops our name into a pop‑culture list alongside “Karma” and “Global Boiling.” Helpful, except all of those Woodvales are not our Woodvale.

We checked community coverage too and found a very different vibe across the pond: CommunityNI’s listings for Woodvale show a proper community centre at 74a Disraeli Street (Parent & Toddler groups, Kids’ Disco, evening bingo, circuit training and counselling) plus an After School Club and Streetbeat Youth Project at 16 Woodvale Road. Heartening stuff — again, not here, but it gives us something to aspire to.

Petty local drama? None recorded. That’s either enviable peace or a scandal of neglect; we choose peace. There’s no trending feud for the suburb to gossip about, no council kerfuffle splashed across feeds. For our purposes, that’s fuel for darkly grateful pride.

Who would hate living here: people who need their postcode to come with a press release and a dedicated Instagram handle. Who would love it: anybody who likes anonymity, quiet streets and a postcode that refuses to be turned into clickbait. Until someone writes about us, we’ll keep the lights on ourselves and pretend we’re the real 18 Maitland Rise.
