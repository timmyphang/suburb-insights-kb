# Jewells (NSW)

Slug: `jewells-nsw`

We live in Jewells, NSW — a place so delightfully unbothered that national writers keep confusing us for other suburbs with shinier names. Back in the day the only headlines that dropped a variant of our name were about Adelaide’s Jewels of Thought (a record-shop-cafe-wine-bar, not us) and Concrete Playground’s cheerleading for Beautiful Jim Key in Jewell Station Village — which, amusingly, is also Not Jewells. It’s a bit like being famous for someone else’s outfit at the footy.

Our weekend routine is gloriously low-key. The most useful online lifeline has been Playgroup NSW — and the Newy with Kids guide to playgroups in Newcastle, Lake Macquarie & the Hunter — because for families that’s the local social calendar we actually use. We’ll admit it: if you want to know where toddlers are running the joint, the Playgroup NSW “Find a Playgroup” tool is our version of a nightlife listing. It’s practical, plain-speaking and disappointingly toothless as clickbait — but it’s ours.

Petty local drama? There isn’t much to report, which is the point. The closest thing we get to celebrity gossip is watching which well-meaning parent forgets the sippy cup. National outlets wrote long features about records, bead necklaces and breakfast spots a few suburbs over; for us, the scuffle is over pram space in the hall, and we mean that with pride.

Who would hate living here: anyone who needs a constant feed of Broadsheet-approved hype or who insists every suburb must have a boutique by lunchtime. If you measure a place by how many features it gets in Concrete Playground, you’ll be twitchy here.

Who’d love it: families who actually use the Playgroup NSW listings, people who enjoy quiet streets over curated venues, and anyone who likes being under the radar while still being part of a real community. We don’t have a profile piece in 2026, but we’ve got morning routines, trusted playgroups and a stubborn local heart. That’ll do.
