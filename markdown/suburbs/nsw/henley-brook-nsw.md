# Henley Brook (NSW)

Slug: `henley-brook-nsw`

We roll out of bed, drive past the bottlebrush and the occasional vineyard sign and into West Swan Road like we own the place — which, depending on how many school sausage sizzles you’ve volunteered at, we probably do. On a weekend you’ll find us waving at the Henley Brook pub on 9810 West Swan Road (yes, that one that turned up on FindGlocal in 2026) while the kids drag us towards the school run at Henley Brook Primary on Park Street — they were even doing a 15km Trail Hike Challenge in 2026, so don’t ask us about the laces.

We’re proud of our civic spread. AIC Henley Brook at 230 Henley Street is part of the local map now (FindGlocal, 2025), the primary school keeps the community ticking, and groups like “Mums with Toddlers” used to pop up on Active Locals when we needed a park date or moral support after a bake-sale disaster.

Back in the (Broadsheet/Concrete Playground) days we were flirting with food press glamour — Old Young’s Distillery (listed at 10581 West Swan Road) and its Old Young’s Kitchen got the feature treatment; Funk 2.0 at 38 Swan St and tearooms like Mrs Rabbit on West Swan Road also made the guides. Those write-ups are in the scrapbook, not necessarily this week’s itinerary — we like to treat them like our slightly embarrassing prom photos: fond, slightly over-posed, and essential for bragging rights.

Petty local drama? Our data doesn’t hand us any juicy council feuds, so we’ll roast ourselves instead: we’re the place you go when you want vineyards and family-friendly pubs more than late-night neon and fancy footpath cocktails. If you hate grapes, slow weekends and people who actually RSVP to school events, you’d probably sulk here.

Who’ll love Henley Brook? Families who measure weekends by sausage-sizzle attendance, visitors booking Swan Valley stays (Urban List, 2026) for vino adventures, and anyone who prefers a pub on West Swan Road over a poker machine in the city. We’re low-key, a bit vine-strewn, and utterly committed to our local sausage sizzle economy — and we wouldn’t have it any other way.
