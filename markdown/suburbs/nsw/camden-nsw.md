# Camden (NSW)

Slug: `camden-nsw`

Back in the day — and, full disclosure, our cuttings are from the archives — we’re Camden locals who still bristle with pride when the town does its earnest, slightly theatrical thing.

On a proper spring morning you’d see jacarandas purpleing the streets in late October–November (Broadsheet put us on the Purple Rain map), while at sunrise Balloon Aloft used to lift one‑hour flights over the valley, the kind of slow, woolly spectacle that makes you forgive everything. Our foodie lore is stubbornly provincial: Broadsheet once wrote up Burger Frank on 180–186 Argyle Street — house‑made milk buns, meat from surrounding farms — and Redoak’s beers were being brewed in a dedicated Camden brewery. Pasticceria Caruso even claims Camden as part of its cannoli footprint. It’s all a little earnest, and we like it that way.

Petty local drama? Oh yes. The Secret Garden festival — remember that two‑day gig that drew about 4,000 people — had punters and musicians complaining about an ‘intimidating’ NSW Police presence (Junkee covered it). And there was the theatre of Whiteman’s Arcade burning in 2020 and coming back as Argyle House, the multi‑million‑dollar rebirth with Jason McQueen in the kitchen — a proper love letter to our heritage‑reborn vibe (Time Out, Urban List). If you’re into soap operas, we’ve got art cred too: Camden artist Mitch Cairns won the Archibald, which is nice to brag about at the pub.

Who would hate it here? If you require a 24/7 inner‑city soundtrack, instant trains and zero chance of waking to the thump of a hot‑air burner, you’ll wince — Time Out did say we’re about an hour’s drive from the CBD. If you find daily, theatrical gestures like the Camden Valley Inn’s faux snowfalls for Christmas intolerable, maybe look elsewhere.

Who’d love it? Families and community folk — the Camden Mums Hub and local playgroups (Harrington Park Community Centre was listed at 33a Fairwater Dr) keep the place lively. Balloon nerds, cheesemakers, burger obsessives and people who enjoy heritage with a rooftop dome (hello Argyle House) will feel at home. And for a weird local flex: we once produced a bloke who built a working Batmobile. Try topping that, Sydney.
