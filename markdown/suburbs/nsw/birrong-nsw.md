# Birrong (NSW)

We’re not shy about it.

Who would hate living here? If you need a rotating menu of buzzy flagship venues, endless curated experiences, or a postcode that exists mainly for weekend photo ops, Birrong will feel like a long commute to nowhere. We’re not trying to be Everywhere City.

Who would love it? Families, carers, the people who actually need lists of services — MumsPages and FindGlocal make Birrong readable and useful. If you want a suburb that keeps its head down, updates its school newsletters, runs netball seasons, sorts out preschool pickups and even rallies after a workshop blaze, you’ll slot right in. We’ll keep the scoreboards, the newsletters and the modest local triumphs coming — and yes, we’ll accept that imaginary medal from Broadsheet with pride.
