# Illawong (NSW)

Slug: `illawong-nsw`

We start our Saturdays like every proper Illawong household used to: heading down — or at least remembering the walk down — to Cheryl’s Patisserie & Bakery Illawong for whatever consolation a finger bun can legally be called. Urban List once put Cheryl’s on a Sydney list for those sticky, glorious things; whether it’s still the same bun is up to history and Instagram, but the memory is delicious.

Back in the day the internet’s attention to Illawong read like a mother’s handbag: practical, specific and full of helpful lists. MumsPages has long been the MVP for our postcode — Illawong, 2234 — cataloguing everything from baby support to psychologists and life coaches. If you needed a local activity, a class for the little ones or a therapist who’d seen it all, the directory was where the locals quietly did their planning while everyone else bickered about city brunches.

You won’t find much glitz in our press clippings. The bigger lifestyle sites exist — Sitchu and their Roam guides, for instance, are built to seek out charming suburbs — but they’re the sort of people who prefer picturesque village vibes and hardback guides. We’re fine letting them have the glossy pages; we like our quiet corners and our dependable directories.

There’s no spicy local feud in the files, no scandalous council saga, just the steady hum of real suburban life: bakeries noted for finger buns, mums sorting services, directories doing their quiet work. That’s the point. We’re the place people go when they want care, community and a bun that remembers you.

Who would hate it here? If you need a 24-hour buzzy scene and a parade of trendified cafés served by influencers, this might not be your forever. Who’d love it? Families, people who like reliable local resources (thank you, MumsPages), and anyone who treasures the small ritual of a pastry that’s earned a mention in a Sydney list. We’ll keep the bun warm.
