# Frankston East (NSW)

Slug: `frankston-east-nsw`

Frankston East — we like to think of ourselves as the suburb that quietly delegates its baby-wrangling to the internet. Back in the day our best bit of local PR was the Playgroup Victoria listings, and for years that was basically the tourist brochure.

A typical weekend memory: we’d send a bleary-eyed new parent over to the Playgroup Victoria entry for Frankston North Community Centre — which, in its heyday on the page, bragged about a purpose-built playgroup room with an enclosed playground and even a venue with a cafe, library and programs. If that sounded like civilisation at 10am, then great. For slightly further afield we used to point people to the Frankston South Community and Recreation Centre listing. The Find a Playgroup Advanced map and the Venues Archive were our version of a welcome sign: earnest, practical, and formatted like it was built by someone’s sensible aunt.

There’s no juicy council feud in these files, no boutique reopening to mock — just a steady parade of playgroup listings and venue pages. If that bores you, you’ll hate living here: we don’t pretend to be flashy. We’re the place where the highlight is an accurate Playgroup Victoria map pin and knowing which community centre has an enclosed playground.

Who would love it here? New parents, caregivers, anyone who prefers sensible venue info over pretence. We’re small, utilitarian and quietly proud that our online presence is basically a helpful map and a list of community centres that, back in the day, did their job very well.
