# East Preston (NSW)

Slug: `east-preston-nsw`

We wake up in East Preston, NSW and immediately explain—again—that no, we don’t run Contraband Coffee Traders out of our garage. The internet has very strong opinions about “Preston”, and most of them point to Melbourne: Broadsheet’s roundups of cafes (Moon Rabbit, Boundary Espresso, Jackson Dodds), its long love affair with Preston Market, Time Out’s deliciously rude description of Preston Market as “cheap and loud. Rough and ready,” and even pieces about The Raccoon Club and The Keys. Lovely reads, just the wrong postcode.

Back in the day the only local name that kept turning up for our little East Preston was the earnest Happy Days Pre‑School Playgroup—its site says it’s been running for over 20 years, doing the steady, unflashy work of keeping small humans alive. Netmums even ran a heartwarming piece ages ago about a kid from East Preston meeting the Duchess, which is the sort of unlikely claim to fame we quietly enjoy admitting to at parties.

We’ll confess: the press record here reads like a case of mistaken identity. As Broadsheet and Time Out documented the northern-Melbourne scene and Sitchu praised Preston in other liveability roundups, we mostly got to enjoy being the calm cousin who doesn’t make lists. There’s a strange comfort in that anonymity—no constant column calling our High Street the coolest in the world, no 600‑person retro bowling parties crashing our Saturday morning.

Who would hate living here? People who need to be on a neighbourhood list every weekend. If you thrive on being recommended, photographed and turned into a “scene,” you’ll find our relative obscurity frustrating. And yes, influencers—this is not your most efficient content farm.

Who would love it here? Folks who like a sensible postcode, a place where the preschool has history and the loudest local drama is internet confusion over names. We’ll happily take quiet pride over viral fame. If you want a humble suburb that lets Melbourne have its latte belt while we get on with real life—welcome. We’ll show you the local way to enjoy anonymity.
