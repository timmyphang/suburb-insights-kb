# Bonbeach (NSW)

Slug: `bonbeach-nsw`

We start the weekend like we mean it: sandy towel down, dog reluctantly convinced to paddle, kids on that quiet stretch where the waves politely let you dip a toe. As Sitchu’s Jan 2025 readers’ poll put it, Bonbeach “may be one of Melbourne’s best-kept secrets” — and we’ve been hoarding that secret like it’s limited-edition sunscreen.

Our morning ritual is embarrassingly humble. We walk the foreshore towards the bit where Chelsea Beach waves at us from next door, feel superior because they have a bit more foot traffic, and spread a picnic — yes, the kind Sitchu’s picnic roundups make you pretend are effortless. No late-night bars, no buzzy laneway cafes, just golden sand and the occasional angler giving us the look that says, “This is my bench.”

Back in the day Broadsheet wrote about The Little French Deli on the Nepean Highway, a tiny French outpost that was the place to stop for croissants on a day trip. That was then; it’s the sort of memory we brunch-mourn about while actually eating something from the esky.

We’re not drama central. There's a Bonbeach Community Notice Board page on FindItLocally where opportunities and notices hang like vintage postcards — civic, quiet and oddly comforting. If you want scandal, go elsewhere; our biggest town argument tends to be whether the picnic rug should face east or north.

Who would hate living here? Nightclub-seekers, perpetual buzz-chasers and anyone who rates a suburb by the density of artisanal everything. They will look at our sleepy beach and sigh in various currencies.

Who will love it? Families who prefer a sandbox to a dance floor, walkers who call this coastline their morning cathedral, and anyone who appreciates being near Chelsea Beach without the souvenir shops. We keep the kettle on, the sand mostly clean, and the secret well—secret. Come for the quiet, stay for the shoreline, and don’t tell too many people.
