# Roxburgh Park (NSW)

Slug: `roxburgh-park-nsw`

We wake up on Somerton Road like it’s our civic ritual — kettle on, kids in the back, and someone always saying “Is it Rocks‑brah or Rox‑burgh?” (Time Out tried to settle it with “Rocks‑brah Park” and honestly, we clap for the effort). Before you ask: yes, we know the internet’s best summaries of us are a little vintage. All the local intel we’ve got is from older write‑ups, so consider this a fondly grainy mixtape of Roxburgh Park.

Weekends used to look like this: someone drops the little ones at the Roxburgh Rise Community Hub — they run free cooking classes, playgroups and meetups on Bluebird Way (Thursdays 10:00–12:00 was always a thing) — while others cart the kids to the Roxburgh Park Youth and Recreation Centre for chaos in the activities room. Time Out once listed Airborn Indoor Trampoline Park at Roxburgh Plaza (2/195 Somerton Road) with Roxburgh Park station as the nearest stop, and yes, we remember bouncing like the mortgage depended on it.

Petty local drama? Oh, we’ve had our headlines. Broadsheet once included us in a water‑quality warning (boil the kettle, they said — thanks for the adrenaline). Time Out flagged a 3.1% rise in vacant homes around here between 2019–2023, so the debate about “empty houses vs. space for new dreams” has been the understated soap opera of our streets. And for the foodies who enjoy lists, Broadsheet’s old guides cheekily placed us in Hume City cafe roundups and even noted a doughnut chain with a Roxburgh Park location — proof we have both good carbs and good character.

Who would hate living here? If you crave constant trend journalism, artisanal minimalism or a suburb that never ever had any council‑issued water warnings — you’ll be bored. Who’d love it? Families, mums finding each other on playgroup.org.au and Mama Tribe meetups, anyone who wants actual community hubs where people teach each other to cook and show up on Thursdays. We’re the place people keep coming back to, even if the articles about us are from the “remember when” era. Come for the bounce, stay for the Bluebird Way crew.
