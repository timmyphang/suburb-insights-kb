# Byford (NSW)

Slug: `byford-nsw`

Look, full disclosure: the clippings we dug up about Byford are all from before 2024 and mostly point to the Byford down near the Perth Hills — so we’ll roast what the files actually show. Still, we know our patch.

On a weekend we’d find ourselves at Found Lab, at 1 Olsen Gardens in The Glades — or, as the old write-ups once put it, “the lakeside gem.” Back in the day Found Lab filled a roomy 200-seat space with a bar, kitchen and a bespoke 600‑litre two-vessel brewing tank, all set in a greenhouse-y room of hanging plants and floor‑to‑ceiling windows so you could admire the lake and pretend you’re not only here for the beer. The outdoor area was, helpfully, dog friendly — which made sense, because someone always brought a bigger personality than the rest of us.

For a suburb that’s roughly 22 km² and sits inside the Shire of Serpentine‑Jarrahdale (state seat Darling Range, federal seat Canning), the local directory entries read like a comforting recipe: a family GP who encouraged continuity of care (Byford Family Practice), a chiropractic shop on 807 South Western Highway that promised results, and community sport — the Byford Bushrangers — listed as a tee‑ball, baseball and softball club whose brief was literally “fun, safe” with disabled access. That’s not drama; that’s civic virtue in sneakers.

There aren’t any juicy scandals or council cliffhangers in the files — no viral petitions we could weaponise, no trolley‑dash controversies. Which is either because nobody bothered writing about us, or because our biggest civic spat was which bench by the lake gets the afternoon sun.

Who would hate living here? People who need 24/7 neon nightlife or an endless scroll of artisanal novelty — we’re more lakeside brews and club days than boutique chaos. Who’d love it? Families who want kids in tee‑ball, dog‑owners who appreciate a beer garden that tolerates fur, and anyone who likes the idea of a local GP and chiro nearby when life insists on being mildly inconvenient.

So yes, we’ll take our mellow lake views, our past‑glory brewery aesthetic and our small‑town municipal pride. We’ll keep the roast gentle — and save a spot for your dog at the outdoor table.
