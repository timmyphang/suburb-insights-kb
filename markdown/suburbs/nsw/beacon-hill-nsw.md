# Beacon Hill (NSW)

Slug: `beacon-hill-nsw`

We’re the kind of lot who still judge a suburb by how many playgroups you can walk to with a pram that’s older than your mortgage. Back in the day the internet told us Beacon Hill was basically a boutique for small humans — Netmums ran not one but a whole saga: The Best Childcare And Education Options In Beacon Hill, Things to do with kids in Beacon Hill, The Best Family Days Out In Beacon Hill and The Best Activities For Kids In Beacon Hill. If there’s a toddler, there’s probably a newsletter about it.

Weekends here used to run to a rhythm: drop-offs, free drop-in New Moms Group at the North End Library, then a slow trawl of the “places to visit” lists while someone else mans the pushchair. The New Moms Group North End even linked us to local lifelines — Nazarro Center, NE Health Center, NE Library and Columbus Park Hill House on Beacon Hill — which, frankly, sounds like a fantasy estate for baby-weaning classes.

Petty local drama? We don’t need it — our civic sport is comparing which playgroup had the best snack policy that year. Also, if you don’t have kids and you crave late-night street food or a decent excuse to leave by 9pm, Beacon Hill will gently nudge you towards the next postcode.

Who would hate it: party people who measure nightlife in neon rather than nappy bins. Who would love it: parents, new carers, and anyone who secretly enjoys a suburban calendar that reads like festival programming. We know every pram route — and we’ll happily judge you for your choice of stroller.
