# Loftus (NSW)

Slug: `loftus-nsw`

Back in the day we had a neat little Friday clock-in: 10am at Loftus Community Hall, 129R Loftus Ave, where the under‑3s run the place and the grown-ups pretend they’re still awake. As Playgroup NSW cheerfully lists, our session ran 10–11.30am — babies bawling, coffee traded like contraband, new families waved to at loftusplaygroupfriday@gmail.com. That’s our version of high society.

By evening we’d haul picnic rugs to Loftus Reserve (119/129 Loftus Ave) for St Johns Open Air Carols — the sort of thing mums and dads plan for months because someone once forgot the thermos. Mums of the Shire even had the date down for 21 December 2025, 5–7.30pm, which tells you we’re serious about festive planning and getting home before it actually gets cold.

If you’re wondering where the glamour is, we’ll be frank: it’s not on Loftus Ave. The glamour migrated to the city and kept our name for itself. Over in the CBD there’s a whole Loftus Lane scene — Bouillon L’Entrecôte at 6 Loftus (steak frites and ultra‑cheesy soufflé, Time Out said), Bar Mammoni tucked in Quay Quarter Lanes (Shop 2/3 Loftus Lane, allegedly an all‑day Italian‑style spot), Neptune’s Grotto with its secreted entrance on Loftus Lane, and Deux Frères hiding at 6 Loftus Lane. Concrete Playground and Urban List loved it. We salute them. They took our name, put a zebra carpet on it and called it invigorating.

We also punch above our weight piewise: Pedestrian TV once quoted John Tsoutsas of Loftus Pies as the brand’s “chief pie officer” when IKEA dropped a meatball party pie. That’s right — we’ve influenced international frozen pastry decisions. Humble brag.

Who would hate living here? If your idea of community is a silent street and no playgroup schedule, you’ll find us alarmingly organised. Who would love it here? Families who like their suburbs with predictable rituals — Friday playgroup, community carols, and the comforting knowledge that, if nothing else, we make excellent pies and the city uses our name when it wants to look fancy.
