# Kanahooka (NSW)

Slug: `kanahooka-nsw`

Saturday morning in Kanahooka is basically a community sport: we assemble at whatever hall or backyard someone’s found, clutching thermoses and a suspiciously large pile of cardboard craft. For years the local kids and their carers have relied on Playgroup NSW to tell us where to be and when — you know, that Find a Playgroup tool we all pretend we understand while juggling a tutu and a sausage roll we didn’t mean to buy.

As Playgroup NSW explains, there are play-based programs, parent resources and even a page on Baby Development Milestones we’ve all bookmarked and then texted to our sisters at 2am. For anyone keeping score, Playgroup NSW also lists local Parent Support Groups — which is code for “people who will listen to your toddler’s latest dietary rebellion.”

The petty drama here is low-budget and heartwarming: who snuck the glitter into arts and crafts? Who forgot to load the nappies? These are the grand scandals. We don’t have big council battles in the headlines — we have heated debates over sharing the painting smock.

Who would hate living here? If you crave anonymity, silence after 8pm, or a postcode without inevitable tiny handprints on your welcome mat, this isn’t your jam. Who will love it? New parents, grandparents on standby duty, anyone wanting a community that actually shows up with biscuits and a lending ear.

Back in the day we used Playgroup NSW to find each other. Still do, in slightly stickier clothes and immeasurably more patient hearts.
