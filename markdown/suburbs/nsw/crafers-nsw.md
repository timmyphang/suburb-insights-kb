# Crafers (NSW)

Slug: `crafers-nsw`

We wake up on a Saturday and the first thing we do is squint down the hill to see which cars are crawling up the South Eastern Expressway — because if you’re from here, that’s how you measure the weekend. Back in the day the guides loved to tell visitors about our hilltop habits: the genteel lunchers at Mount Lofty House, the locals who once leaned on the bar at The Crafers Hotel, and the people who travelled here for a very serious Kitchener bun at Crafers Bakehouse. Yes, we know how that sounds. We also know where to find Piccadilly Road if you want to actually get to the summit.

Our morning ritual used to involve a quick pop past the bakehouse (the Kitchener bun was a thing), then a stretch class or birth-workshop-shaped excuse at Energy & Motion — Nurtured Mumma ran sessions from 5/7 Main Street, so don’t act surprised if your Pilates instructor knows the best view of the hills. Families will nod: Crafers Primary scored a $150,000 Fund My Neighbourhood grant in 2017 for the nature playground at Piccadilly Road, and Protea Reserve Playground on Protea Cres is the kind of tidy upgrade that makes grandparents overly proud.

If you’re a restaurant name-dropper, our old press kit is charmingly consistent: Crafers Pizza Bar had Myles Cook leaning on a Herringbone collaboration in 2021, Hardy’s Verandah at Mount Lofty House opened in 2017 under David Horbelt with Jin Choi in the kitchen, and The Crafers Hotel shed its gritty past after a 2014 renovation and now boasts a very serious dedicated wine room. Sequoia Lodge (1 Mawson Drive) gets the hilltop-romance tick from everyone compiling “best stays” lists.

Who would hate living here? People who crave never-ending takeaway options, or anyone allergic to verandahs, heritage pubs and panoramic restraint. Who would love it? Folks who want a weekend escape that still remembers its local bakery, families who appreciate decent playgrounds and before/after-school care stats (yes, the listings show nine childcare services), and anyone with a soft spot for a rooftop-ish lodge or a proper wine room.

We’re small, leafy and aware of our own press clippings — and honestly, we kind of like it that way.
