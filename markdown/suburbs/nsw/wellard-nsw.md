# Wellard (NSW)

Slug: `wellard-nsw`

We like to start our Saturdays like proper locals: pretending we’re commuting to the city but actually parking the car near Wellard Train Station and calling it ‘authentic suburban living’. Back in the day the listings used to brag (yes, Sitchu bragged) that Wellard was “built around Wellard Train Station” and an easy 35‑minute train ride into the CBD — which makes us feel metropolitan, even if the nearest late-night kebab is still a philosophical debate.

Our weekend ritual is embarrassingly wholesome. We used to scroll MumsPages for anything that stopped the kids from inventing new ways to glue their toys together: The King's College popped up for learning support, makeART Classes & Workshops for when culture was cheaper than therapy, and RainDance Photography for capturing the exact moment your toddler discovers paint. Cockburn Concrete was there too — because nothing says ‘we’re settling down’ like concrete options for the backyard you’ll never finish.

There isn’t much recent gossip to fling — the files we’ve got are all older listings and round-ups — so forgive us if this reads like a fondly annotated directory rather than a scandal sheet. We’ll own the calm: Alcoa Wellard Wetlands and the Baldivis Nature Reserve Trail get name‑checked for birdwatching and native flora (again, Sitchu supplied the itinerary), which means if you’re into ducks and the occasional interpretive walk, you’re living large.

Who would hate living here? If your weekly personality requires three rooftop bars and a different boutique espresso every morning, Wellard will test you. If your idea of exercise is queuing for a pilates class in the city, you’ll resent the 35‑minute commute like a lover scorned.

Who would love it? Families who like local directories full of actual services, anyone who treasures wetlands over nightlife, and people who value community notices more than trending hashtags. We’re not flashy. We are concrete choices, art classes and train timetables. And frankly, that’s most of the charm.
