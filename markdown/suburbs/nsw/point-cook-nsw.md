# Point Cook (NSW)

Slug: `point-cook-nsw`

Back in the day we used to joke that Point Cook was all playgrounds and sunsets — and the papers kind of agreed. We wake up in the Boardwalk Estate, tie the kids into their school shoes (Carranballac P–9, Emmanuel College Notre Dame campus, Point Cook Senior Secondary College all in striking distance if you’re into logistics), and wander down the new bit of Bay Trail boardwalk through the Cheetham Wetlands toward Point Cook Coastal Park. The gulls judge our life choices; the sky gives us the Aurora Australis on the rare, smug nights it shows up.

Weekends here are a very specific recipe: a stroller convoy to Jamieson Way Community Centre or the Point Cook Community Learning Centre playgroup, a library stop (yes, the Point Cook library doubles as a playgroup venue), then a coastal walk. If the weather remembers it’s summer, we recall that the Bureau of Meteorology clocked a scorch at Point Cook RAAF Base — 42.3°C on one memorable January day — so we hydrate like it’s an Olympic sport.

We used to rely on low-key local heroes: Hopheads at 13 Adelphi Boulevard quietly did beer pilgrimages (eight taps, packaged options), and Phoenix Kitchen quietly opened a roast‑meat takeaway stall in Point Cook back in 2021 for the times when you wanted Peking duck without the pretense. The entertainment precinct also changed the tempo when Holey-Moley and Strike Bowling set up a 4,000sqm complex with lanes, laser tag, arcade and mini golf — yes, the suburban amusement park your inner teenager still thanks you for.

Who would hate it here? If you despise playgrounds, community-run mums’ pages, coastal breezes that ruin your coiffure, or historic houses with ghost tours (Point Cook Homestead has the full spooky package), this suburb will test your patience. If your ideal weekend is a silent, anonymous tower block, Point Cook will give you a front-row seat to family life.

Who would love it? Families who like actual community, walkers who want uninterrupted sky and boardwalks, beer sleuths who appreciate a good bottle shop, roast‑duck lovers who know where to order takeaway, and anyone who thinks mini golf and laser tag are a perfectly respectable Saturday. We know every corner here — and we’ll happily show you the best spot to watch the southern lights while the kids argue about who won at mini golf.
