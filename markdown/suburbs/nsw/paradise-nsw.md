# Paradise (NSW)

Slug: `paradise-nsw`

Paradise, NSW — yes, the place with the name that ruins every holiday caption. We’re the lot who still answer to it at the school gates, at the library and, occasionally, in very confused Google searches.

Morning in Paradise used to mean a convoy of prams to the Paradise Library playgroup on the 1st and 3rd Tuesday — ten-minute book reading, sticky fingers, someone forgetting the spare nappies. Fridays once had us meeting at the Paradise Community Hall for the playgroup that required pre‑registration because apparently even toddlers need a RSVP. There was even a Paradise Stronger free playgroup offering an indoor reprieve in colder months, which is peak parental civilisation. If you came by for kid chaos, we had it scheduled and minute‑marked.

We should point out we share our name with several things that make us look posher than we are. Broadsheet has a Potts Point wine bar called Paradise — run by the Fratelli Paradiso team — that used to be the slick cousin who writes menus on chalkboards and, for a while, opened early on Saturdays to flog sfogliatelle and Roman pan pizza. Meanwhile, Glenworth Valley hosted Lost Paradise — a full‑on festival that, in 2025, booked Underworld, Ben Böhmer, KETTAMA, I Hate Models and Maribou State, and even had that delightful ticketing kerfuffle when Lyte went missing. So yes: our little postcode shares branding with artisan pastries and trance stages. Life is weird.

Who would hate living here? People who want their suburb to be a single, coherent brand. If you need your postcode to only stand for beachfront margaritas or 24/7 DJs, you’ll be unsettled by our mash‑up of community playgroups and name‑share imposters.

Who would love it here? Families who value routine, people who enjoy small, persistent community things — library storytime, hall coffee, a free playgroup that remembers your kid’s allergies — and anyone happy to let Potts Point and Glenworth Valley have the glamour. We’ll keep the name. You keep the itinerary.
