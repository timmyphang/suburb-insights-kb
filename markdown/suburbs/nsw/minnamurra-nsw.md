# Minnamurra (NSW)

Slug: `minnamurra-nsw`

Back in the day we’d roll out of bed, hop off the train and be at the elevated whale‑watching platform before the tourists had finished tying their hiking boots. Minnamurra has always been that smug little waystation at the top of the Kiama Coast Walk — the guidebooks love telling you it starts “conveniently at the train station” and then splits into three sections down to Gerringong. We’ll take the bragging rights for the headland: Minnamurra Lookout gives us Stack Island (Rangoon Island), the river, the ocean and a perfect spot to pretend we were born here.

Our weekend routine? A slow amble onto the Kiama Coast Walk — call it roughly 20–22 kilometres if you’re counting — then a detour into the Budderoo National Park for the rainforest circuit. The elevated boardwalks through the Minnamurra Rainforest and the route to Minnamurra Falls get name‑checked everywhere: some articles call the Falls loop about 4.2 km return, others point to a 1.3 km “The Falls” walk from the Minnamurra Rainforest Centre west of Jamberoo. Either way, there’s enough ferns and fig trees to make you feel sanctified without needing a shrine.

We’re small, a touch smug, and surprisingly accommodating — the lookout gets a tick for wheelchair access, so you’ll see everyone from grandparents with binoculars to day‑trippers armed with confidence and sunscreen. For years the Minnamurra Playgroup met on Thursday mornings at the Minnamurra Progress Hall on Charles Avenue (session times 9:30–11:30; term fees used to be $25 or $3 weekly), which explains why we have more pram parking than a boutique café could ever dream of.

Who would hate living here? If you’re allergic to dramatic coastline, humpback spotting, or the idea that a good weekend includes both a headland picnic and a rainforest loop, then yes — you’d be miserable. But who would love it? Families, bushwalkers, whale watchers and anyone who appreciates that a tiny place can fold rainforest, river mouths and a coastline into one smug, photogenic postcode. We’ll keep the lookout warm for you.
