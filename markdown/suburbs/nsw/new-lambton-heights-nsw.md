# New Lambton Heights (NSW)

Slug: `new-lambton-heights-nsw`

We know the bit — wake up, squint at jacaranda confetti on the windscreen and pretend we're in a rom-com. Back in the day Newy with Kids pointed out the best purple parade: the jacarandas opposite John Hunter Hospital (New Lambton Heights). We still measure April by how many petals make it into the mailbox.

Our Wednesday routine used to be a small, very Australian pilgrimage: the Playgroup NSW listing for New Lambton said the local mob met at the Uniting Church — 48 Regent Street, New Lambton — every school-term Wednesday at 9:30am. So yes, we’ve perfected the art of juggling cold tea, a sippy cup and an opinion about council bins while pretending to listen to other parents.

There isn’t much juicy council scandal in the clippings — this place has always preferred polite support groups and birthing info to backyard drama. Newy with Kids also listed John Hunter Hospital (New Lambton Heights) under Hospitals & Birthing, so if you like the idea of knowing where the Birth Suite and Birth Centre were on a map, we were quietly smug about that.

Who would hate it here? If you crave 24/7 nightlife or a cafe culture that requires a review in a glossy mag, you’ll find our petal-strewn serenity maddening. And who’d love it? Young families, jacaranda romantics and anyone who likes their community listed on Playgroup NSW and their hospital on the birthing guides — modest, reliable and quietly purple in April.

We’ll keep sweeping petals into neat little piles. It’s not flashy, but it’s ours.
