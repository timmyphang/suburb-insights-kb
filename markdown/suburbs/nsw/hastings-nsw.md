# Hastings (NSW)

Slug: `hastings-nsw`

Back in the day we used to roll our windows down and tell visiting mates: “No, not that Hastings — ours is the one with the river, not the half‑kilometre dining strip.” We know every bend of the Hastings River where we walk the dog, launch the tinny or pretend we’re going fishing and actually end up at the pub. Concrete Playground even recommended Port Macquarie as a remote‑working spot because of its views over the Hastings River, so yes: the water’s part of our morning ritual.

Mornings here are quiet in the best way — not a lineup for a buzzy Hastings Street espresso, but the kind of quiet where you can hear the council setting up the market and the occasional kookaburra doing its best traffic‑cop impression. Speaking of council, we lived through the festive fair that went viral for all the wrong reasons — Pedestrian TV cheekily said the Port Macquarie‑Hastings Council’s Christmas tree “was so bad, it might make Santa Claus retire.” It got replaced with a fake one afterwards, as Pedestrian TV later reported. We still tell that story like it’s a badge of local humility.

We’ve also got our celebrities: not the Instagram kind, but the furry, leaf‑munching ones. As Pedestrian TV covered, the Port Macquarie region has a nationally significant population of roughly 2,000 koalas, and there were efforts to release 26 koalas back into the area — so if you hate cuddly wildlife, you will hate living here.

Who would loathe Hastings, NSW? Anyone who arrives expecting Noosa’s Hastings Street — the one Broadsheet and Time Out have gushed about with places like Locale, Bang Bang and Cibaria — will be sorely disappointed. If your ideal afternoon is sunset cocktails three metres from Main Beach, you’ve come to the wrong postcode.

Who will love it? People who want the river to be a feature, not a backdrop; the kind who prefer a proper local story over curated beachfront everythings. We’re proud of our slightly awkward, slightly viral, very real little corner of the coast. Come for the river, stay for the koalas, and don’t ask us to point you towards Hastings Street unless you mean the other one.
