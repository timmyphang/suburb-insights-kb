# Padbury (NSW)

Slug: `padbury-nsw`

We love Padbury — partly because we’ve got nowhere else to be, and partly because the evidence trail reads like someone archived our suburb’s personality in a biscuit tin and forgot to throw it out.

Back in the day Alex Junior Espresso even brought Dunsborough coffee to town, tucked into an abandoned shopping-centre bakery, and Broadsheet watched in delight as 300 people a day queued for it. As Urban List once reminded us, Padbury’s (the one pegged at 114 Terrace Rd, Guildford WA 6055) served the Meringue Munro pancakes so Instagrammable they could double as small, tasteful wedding cakes — lemon curd, honeycomb shards and all. Those were the glory days of our feed, even if the write-ups feel like postcards from another postcode.

We know the practical stuff, too: for the tiny humans Padbury Playgroup runs free sessions for 0–5s — you park at 2 Caley Road, drop a name into padburyplaygroup@gmail.com, and someone on the volunteer committee will tame the chaos. Our pre-school? It’s run from a purpose-designed facility on the Winslow‑Buckingham road (A413), which Netmums praises for the friendly, active community vibe. Yes, we’re the suburb that schedules its heartbeats around naptime and snackboxes.

Local aesthetic upgrades included Luxe Ink Padbury — where French-born Aurelien specialised in ultra-natural brow tattooing, if you wanted your face to be both subtle and permanently on fleek. Urban List gave that a thumbs-up too, which tells us we can be both wholesome and very particular about our arches.

Who would hate living here? People who hate sticky hands on car doors, who find meringue offensive, or who cannot abide a roundabout conversation about which playground has the best sandpit. Also anyone allergic to community noticeboards, life in elastic-waist pants, or long, earnest discussions about kinder enrolment.

Who would love it? Families, people who secretly enjoy small-town notoriety, and anyone chasing those semi-legendary pancakes or a reliable playgroup meetup. We’re modest, slightly flour-covered, and very pleased with our lot. Come for the nostalgia, stay for the sunscreen-streaked hugs.
