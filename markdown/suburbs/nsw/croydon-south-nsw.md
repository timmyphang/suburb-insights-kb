# Croydon South (NSW)

Slug: `croydon-south-nsw`

We’re the lot who still judge your weekend by what pastry you bring to the park. Back in the day we’d stroll down to 15 The Mall for a Goldeluck’s Bakeshop dossant and feel like we’d won the suburb. They even used to cart those croissant-doughnut hybrids out to Donut Fest — yes, we were famous for our buns before the internet noticed us.

Mornings here used to smell like sugar and chocolate: Hahndorf’s Fine Chocolates (482 Dorset Rd) served what Time Out called “a bowl of hot chocolate the size of your head” and we accepted that challenge, because humility is for other suburbs. Broadsheet once put us on a list of 88 places to watch when Yarra Valley Water told half the city to boil their water — so of course we spent the afternoon perfecting kettle technique and discussing which tap tastes best.

For families we had the usual humble brag: Little Learners Day Nursery – South Croydon was a thing parents mentioned with relief; Local Mums Online pointed to new cheerleading classes at Coombe Wood (Future Generation Cheer), Sparks filmmaking for the 7–14s and a dozen playgroups and rhymetimes to keep toddlers off screens and into papier-mâché. Mumsnet threads used to weigh up whether South Croydon was the smart choice for a 10‑year plan — you can still read the spirited debates about schools like Purley Oaks and the accessibility quirks of the station, if you enjoy amateur urban sociology.

Petty local drama? Our biggest quarrel used to be which bakery got the best free-donut promo at Eastland. Riveting stuff. We don’t do skyline drama — we do pies, PTA motions and whether the rec playground needs another swing.

Who would hate it here? If you need rooftop bars, boutique fitness and a tram that drops you at the opera, you’ll flinch. Who’d love it? Parents who like choice, snack connoisseurs, and anyone who enjoys a suburb that wears its small-time pleasures proudly. We’re not flashy. We’re comfortable. And yes, we’ll happily fight you for the last dossant.
