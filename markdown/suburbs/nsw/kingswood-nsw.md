# Kingswood (NSW)

Slug: `kingswood-nsw`

Back in the day we’d squeeze ourselves into a Sydney Place alley and treat the whole thing like an urban pilgrimage. We weren’t going to a shrine — we were going to Kingswood Coffee’s little World Square window. You could watch the coffee theatrics, see a Steadfast blend from Sensory Lab get finessed into a milk-based cup, or stare longingly at a stack of Belgian waffles while the cronut crowd queued for the next sweet brioche hit. It felt like being part of a secret society that smelled slightly of espresso and melted chocolate.

Our morning routine was brief and performative: join the line, nod at the barista (we knew the backstory — Mikey Jordan, ex-Barefoot Coffee Traders, had brought the vibe), clutch a takeaway because Time Out warned about limited seating, and parade our cold-drip like it was a trophy. Kingswood rotated guest roasters like a DJ swaps records; it made us feel cultured without having to actually learn anything about roast profiles.

There wasn’t much proper drama to gossip about — unless you count watching a humble birria taco evolve into a phenomenon. Smoking Gringos started as an American barbecue joint in Kingswood, produced a Kingswood special that birthed their popular birria taco, then went on a little odyssey through Newtown’s Websters pop-up scene and eventually Blacktown. Our culinary claims to fame were modest but stubbornly proud.

Who would hate living here? People who need a comfy armchair and an endless brunch menu — Kingswood was never about sprawling breakfasts. If you couldn’t handle a queue, limited seating or the entire aesthetic being slightly more “grab-and-go theatre” than leisurely café, you’d be miserable.

Who would love it? Coffee obsessives who like their beans with a side of backstory, snack devotees who mourn cronuts with genuine feeling, and anyone who likes the idea that our little corner once produced a World Cup Tasters Champion (remember Kingswood’s Yama Jinwoo Kim in 2018?) and a taco that went travelling. We’re small, we’re proudly caffeinated, and yes — we once made something worth talking about.
