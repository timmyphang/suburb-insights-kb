# Lilli Pilli (NSW)

Slug: `lilli-pilli-nsw`

We like to think of Lilli Pilli as the suburb that makes you earn your beach days. Time Out once called our sandbar a "secret beach" that appears twice daily from the Hacking River — so yes, we set alarms like sailors and sprint into waist-deep water when the island deigns to exist. We time the tide like it’s a local opera.

Mornings here are modest miracles. Time Out pointed out the tidal pool at the end of Lilli Pilli Point Road, and we come armed with goggles because the water’s full of whiting, yellowfin bream and flathead (fancy that — real fish, not just Instagram silhouettes). Concrete Playground put Lilli Pilli Baths on a list of the best rivers to swim near Sydney, which is our version of a Michelin star for a netted pool east of Grays Point.

For families, Mums of the Shire did the comunity a solid by noting the Turriell Bay Road Reserve upgrade on the corner of Turriell Bay Road and Lilli Pilli Point Road, right next to Lilli Pilli Public School. The school’s P&C even had a "Berry Starry Night" Christmas Carols listing — acoustic carols on the oval, picnics and all — and our oval is, officially, at 597 Port Hacking Road according to the local listings. We bring rugs, we bring wine, we pretend we can sing harmonies.

We’ll admit the press about us skews sentimental and a touch niche — Time Out, Concrete Playground, Mums of the Shire — so if you’re after neon-lit nightlife or a restaurant crawl, the headlines didn’t bother to write about it. That’s not a gripe; it’s a filter.

Who would hate it here? If you can’t be bothered with tidal schedules, netted pools, or a community that treats a Christmas carol like a civic ceremony, Lilli Pilli will feel inconveniently charming. Who’d love it? Families who like upgraded playgrounds, swimmers who respect a good tide, and anyone who thinks a sandbar that vanishes twice a day is peak local entertainment.

Back in the day the mags kissed our little bits of shore with praise — we’ve kept the towel warm ever since.
