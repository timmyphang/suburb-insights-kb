# Doreen (NSW)

Slug: `doreen-nsw`

We start our Saturdays like any proper outer-suburb ritual: someone announces a babies-and-biscuits meet-up in a BabyCenter thread, another replies asking if there’s still room in the mothers’ group, and somewhere a photographer promises free newborn headshots. Back in the day, BabyCenter was where Doreen mums traded survival tips, and Claire Elizabeth Photography even ran a newborn shoot right here in Doreen, Melbourne — so yes, we do our own tiny-human culture pretty well.

If you’re wondering whether we stretch or sweat, Sitchu once remembered us on their roundup — Doreen got a shout as a spot for yoga and Pilates alongside Ringwood North and Mill Park. That’ll give you an idea: we’re the kind of place where you’ll find more baby carriers than craft beer guides, and where your morning commute is often to a park playdate rather than a coworking space.

Our name turns up in stranger places, too. Concrete Playground noted a Doreen Chapman among artists commissioned for the 2024 Biennale of Sydney — which is fun, if only because it confuses out-of-towners who Google us and expect a suburb map, not contemporary art credits. And then there are the cultural Doreens elsewhere — writers, musicians, festival organisers — which just proves the name gets around more than the 274 bus.

Who would hate living here? If your idea of fun is midnight cocktails and weekend laneway crawls, Doreen will politely ruin your social life. If you want a late-night kebab and a tram home, we’ll stare at you from the cul-de-sac and wish you well. We have pram-parks, mothers’ threads, and yoga mats — not neon signs.

Who’d love it? New parents, people who want a quieter postcode near Mernda and South Morang, and anyone who values community threads over clickbait headlines. We’re low-key, organised, and oddly well-documented in niche corners of the internet. So come for the baby groups, stay because you forgot why you left, and tell the city you’re “out in Doreen” like it’s a choice.
