# Helena Valley (NSW)

Slug: `helena-valley-nsw`

We wake to the kind of quiet only Helena Valley can muster — the kind Urban List once cheerfully called a 23‑kilometre hill that “isn’t for the faint‑hearted.” We lace up because someone needs to prove the valley is punishment disguised as cardio, then drive past the sign that points towards Amaroo Retreat & Spa, which was fondly listed for its rain‑water outdoor spas set to a toasty 32°C. Yes, this place is equal parts medieval ascent and post‑run indulgence.

Back in the day, MumsPages kept the local parents busy: listings for martial‑arts classes and karate coaches, fitness options and party and event venues. Translation: we have more kids in gis than cafés with plant pots, and suitable locations for turning eight‑year‑old birthdays into miniature ninja boot camps.

There’s an odd, lovely contradiction here. One minute you’re huffing up a 23‑k’s worth of hill that feels like suburbia’s answer to an obstacle course, the next you could be soaking in a warm outdoor spa “within a stone’s throw from Perth’s CBD,” as that Amaroo blurb coyly promised. It’s like wearing hiking boots to a day spa and not being judged.

Who would hate it here? If you despise hills, bushland proximity, or being in a place that expects you to either sweat or soak — you’ll be miserable. Who would love it? Serious runners, spa devotees, parents who need a thousand‑options directory for keeping kids busy, and anyone who likes their weekend to involve both suffering and serenity.
