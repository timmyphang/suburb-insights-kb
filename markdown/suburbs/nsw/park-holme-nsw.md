# Park Holme (NSW)

Slug: `park-holme-nsw`

Back in the day we’d start the weekend by sneaking down to Marion Outdoor Pool — the good old 50‑metre heated pool on Hendrie Street — and pretend we were training for something grand while really chasing the slides at a Movie, Swim and Slide night. Playgroup SA used to fill Hendrie Reserve (120B Hendrie St) with sticky craft and a coffee van, and yes, we all pretended the face paint made us look like film stars. As Playgroup SA put it, the “Playgroup on the Green” had everything: messy play, storytime and enough sensory bins to teach a census bureau about small plastic animals.

If you trawl the listings you’ll find our local library on Duncan Avenue once hosted Book Busters — an after‑school book club for eight‑year‑olds who fancied themselves future novelists. We used to chuckle at the “Movie, Swim and Slide (Moana)” event listing and then turn up in swimmers like it was our civic duty. Waves of Sound 2022? Yep — that was our attempt at a music festival withoutpostcode pretensions: free pool access, a bit of music, and the kind of community BBQ that doubles as a lost‑and‑found for thongs.

Our property pitch used to sound thrilling on Sitchu: a four‑bed place at 71A Weroona Avenue in “up‑and‑coming Park Holme” — which is code for: tidy lawns, sensible fences and neighbours who know exactly when you watered your garden.

Look, we don’t have late‑night rooftops or artisanal everything. Our clippings are mostly community notices and event posts, not glossy lifestyle spreads — as the archive shows. So if you’re after pulsing nightlife, you’ll hate that our idea of excitement is a well‑timed cannonball and a library craft session.

But for families who value proper 50‑metre lanes, kid‑centric events that actually mean something, and a suburban calendar made up of playgroups, pool nights and book clubs — we’re paradise. Come for the Marion Outdoor Pool, stay because you’ve been roped into running the next wreath‑making workshop at Park Holme Library. We’ll make you a cuppa and a paper crown.
