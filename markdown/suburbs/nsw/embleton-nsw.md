# Embleton (NSW)

Slug: `embleton-nsw`

We live in Embleton, NSW — and honestly, the internet treats us like an identity crisis. Back in the day the only things anyone could find about “Embleton” were a property listing for 38 Priestley Street (yes, “elevated high on the street” and opposite Embleton Primary School and leafy parklands) and a rave-up about Bayswater Waves at the corner of Broun Avenue and Priestley Street. Urban List once wrote up that indoor pool — the heated toddler pool with beach-style entry, the boat-shaped water slide, the palm-tree themed pool, family change rooms and an on-site café — as if that summed up our local culture.

Our morning looks nothing like a tourism brochure: we stroll past the school, wave to the park squirrels, and remember that half the internet thinks our suburb is in Perth or even Cockermouth (yes, Caterite Foodservice lists an Embleton in Cumbria — the world is very small and very confused). There’s no scandal here, no council meltdown to gossip about; mostly it's earnest 1960s homes that got stylish extensions and people who quietly love their patch.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who needs a constant stream of nightlife, boutique shops or trending destination cafés — you’ll get a pool write-up and a solid primary school instead. Who would love it? Families after a practical house opposite parklands, people who enjoy local normalcy, and anyone who appreciates a good boat slide memory when the internet insists that’s our entire personality. We’ll wear that boat-slide badge with pride.
