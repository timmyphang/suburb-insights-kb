# Middle Park (NSW)

Slug: `middle-park-nsw`

We’re standing on Armstrong Street with a tram clanking past the old stationmaster’s building — you know, the one Mart130 used to call home — and deciding whether to admire the Victorian and Federation houses or quietly resent them for their mortgage-flexing powers. Back in the day Ned’s baked loaves and pivoted into late-night bistro modes; you could smell pastries and ambition. Those were the rituals.

These days the big news is more elegant than scandalous: Concrete Playground noted Middle Park European opened at 20 Armstrong Street in June 2025, and yes, we’ve already booked imaginary tables. It’s running Tuesday to Sunday for lunch and dinner, bringing Italian‑French flair to the village we keep pretending is a sleepy bayside secret.

Our local legend, the Middle Park Hotel, has been written about enough times to make it an institution: Broadsheet and Time Out described the heritage facade, horseshoe bar and an upstairs hotel, a gargantuan bar with something like 45 taps (70 metres, Time Out claimed) and menus that once married kingfish ceviche and Wagyu tartare to pub classics. Time Out even called it the unofficial Grand Prix pit stop when Albert Park revs up — we like that image of cheeky race fans stopping by for schnitzel-adjacent comfort.

We’re also proud of the little things that gossip columns don’t love: the community centre that hosts playgroups, the village fish shop that’s been recommended for fresh seafood, and the fact a second Gnoccheria tucked away here once offered rotating gnocchi varieties for those with carb loyalty. Sitchu reminds us we’re a bayside suburb five-ish kilometres from the city, prized for walkability and a relaxed lifestyle — and yes, expensive. Time Out even ranked us second in popularity for 2025, which is the polite way of saying “bring a good solicitor.”

Who would hate living here? If you loathe trees, heritage facades and paying for a view of the bay, you’ll be very unhappy. Who would love it? Families, food obsessives and anyone who likes their wine bars stylish but walkable — and the kind of person who thinks a tram ride and a boutique gnocchi joint make a perfect afternoon. We get it. We do, too.
