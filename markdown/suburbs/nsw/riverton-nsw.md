# Riverton (NSW)

Slug: `riverton-nsw`

We are the proud locals of Riverton, NSW — or at least that’s what our mail thinks until it ends up on Torrens Rd. Picture us at dawn, standing in our (entirely hypothetical) main street, coffee-less and smug, explaining for the millionth time that no, we’re not the Riverton with a Beachhouse Restaurant on 126 Rocks Highway (that’s Riverton/Aparima — Urban List mentioned it as a prime spot overlooking Mitchell’s Bay). Urban List also once said Little Bird Boutique in Riverton was “packed to the rafters” with curios. Charming — just not ours.

Our morning routine now involves three key tasks: check the postcode, remind relatives we’re not the one Brendan Pang remembered his Grandmère cooking in (that memory mentions a house in Riverton), and laugh as Google tries to send our invites to a Riverton with a licensed post office at 28 Torrens Rd. Fun fact: the Riverton Licensed Post Office listing was last updated 04/02/2026. Meanwhile, the Riverton School Community Library (listed at Riverton High School, Oxford Tce) was updated 11/08/2024, and the Riverton Community Management Committee notes meetings on the first Tuesday of the month — last updated 23/09/2025. None of which are in our NSW backyard, but bless them for being so organised.

Petty local drama? Only that we spend an afternoon a week untangling which Riverton is being raved about. Are we a coastal seafood destination with ocean views? Urban List said that about a different Riverton. Do we have a community management committee with tidy minutes? Possibly — but the one online lists Torrens Rd. Do we have a library at 67 Riley Rd? PlaygroupWA has a Riverton Library there. In short: everyone else has the amenities, and we have the name.

Who would hate living here: people who crave clarity, delivery drivers who don’t enjoy a scavenger hunt, and anyone who needs to be on the same continent as their booked accommodation.

Who would love it here: anyone who enjoys being ambiguous, likes correcting strangers at parties, and appreciates a local identity that’s mysterious enough to make other Rivertons do all the heavy lifting. We’ll keep being Riverton, NSW — the one everyone accidentally loves.
