# Double Bay (NSW)

Slug: `double-bay-nsw`

We stand on the lawn outside Woollahra Council Chambers like proper locals, squinting at the harbour and pretending we don’t know which lane hides the best new thing we read about last week. Back in the day the Double Bay Tree Trail meet-up actually began here; we’d wander the 2km loop counting exotic trees from the Himalayas and then reward ourselves with something indulgent from Bills or a cheeky stop at Cali Press on New South Head Road.  

For years Double Bay has been a cocktail of harbourfront calm and small-scale culinary one-upmanship: Neil Perry’s Margaret and the later Gran Torino reworked the neighbourhood into a place where a casual breakfast felt suspiciously well-specified, and talk of converting a bar into Pizzeria Sotto made us all suddenly care about Roman-style thin crusts. Cà Phê Mai showed up as a back-alley Vietnamese-Australian mash-up doing salted cream coffee and bacon-and-egg banh mis by day and pho by night — because why not have your pho and patrimony in the same lane?

The petty local drama is deliciously specific. NSW Health once muddled Michael’s Charcuterie for “Michel’s Patisserie” and we collectively tutted at the official typo. Sophie Monk once stole a shoe in a Double Bay shop for a radio prank and mortified a shop assistant; the suburb’s reality-TV adjacent energy has never really left (we see you, Real Housewives mentions and Miss Double Bay meme-queen antics).

Who would hate it here? If you detest boutique shopping, perfect-for-Instagram restaurants, or the sound of a halotherapy cave at Reset Studio being described as “refined,” this isn’t your scene. If the idea of an airy corner café being a local landmark makes you sigh, move along.

Who would love it? Families who still go to Story Time and the Double Bay Christmas Festival, people who appreciated the old-school village feel before it got polished, and anyone who enjoys a harbour stroll that ends with dinner at one of those restaurants we name-drop like proud owners of local gossip. We’ll be here on the lawn, thank you — pretending we invented this place’s understated glamour.
