# Secret Harbour (NSW)

Slug: `secret-harbour-nsw`

Back in the day we used to sneak down to the bit of coastline people only bothered to mention in round-ups. Urban List once dropped our name into its "12 of Sydney’s Best Secret Beaches" guide — which made us feel like smug custodians of a postcard they forgot to publish. Concrete Playground liked to lump us in with those harbour-side strolls “through WWII batteries or secret harbourside beaches,” which sounded very cinematic until you realise the only battle is between seagulls and takeaway wrappers.

We remember when listings felt like validation: Time Out ran pages for a Secret Harbour Local & General Market and even a Secret Harbour Christmas Market — the type of listings that turn a place into something you tell about at dinner parties. Time Out Australia also name‑checked Secret Harbour Golf Links in its official list of the 30 Great Golf Courses of Australia, which is our polite way of saying some people think the greens are worth the detour.

Weekend routine, as per the archives: we’d lace up for a walk the Concrete Playground writers would’ve approved of, tiptoe past the quieter coves that Urban List admired, then pretend we were off the tourist map while still checking the phone for new market dates on Time Out. It’s a nice illusion — being “secret” while also being in three different lifestyle round-ups.

Who would hate living here? The people who need neon-lit bars on every corner, curated brunch queues, and an Instagram feed that only features espresso art. Secret Harbour has never promised that level of polish; we’re better at being a soft-focus footnote in travel articles than a headline act.

Who would love it? Anyone who likes a walk that ends with a view, local markets (remember those Time Out listings?), and the smug satisfaction of telling friends we were on “that secret beach” before it got a feature. We’re quietly proud of our cameo status in Urban List and Concrete Playground — and if that makes us a secret, well, we’ll keep it between us and everyone who reads those round-ups.
