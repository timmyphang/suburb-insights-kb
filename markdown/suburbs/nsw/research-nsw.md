# Research (NSW)

Slug: `research-nsw`

We wake up in Research, NSW — yes, the suburb whose name promises lab coats and grant money but mostly delivers quiet streets and the vague hope that someone, somewhere, will study us. We brew whatever we brew at home and scroll: Broadsheet bringing Monash University’s XYX Lab and the YourGround project to NSW to map public‑safety feelings; Concrete Playground reminding us that “Research” is handy advice when booking hotels; Time Out ranking universities; Pedestrian TV doing sunscreen sting‑tests; Junkee still running youth surveys. The irony writes itself.

Back in the day (and by that we mean all the pieces we can find — none of them recent profiles of our cul‑de‑sacs), the only time “Research” featured in headlines it was the concept, not our postcode. As Broadsheet noted, there’s a whole Safer Cities push and a crowd‑mapping project — wonderful, except it feels like they meant public spaces in NSW in general rather than our bit of shrub and silence. Concrete Playground, bless it, even lumped “Research” in with “Reviews” in its hotel‑hacking tips. We’re famous as a word, not a place.

Petty local drama? There isn’t the sort you can point to in an Urban List case study or a Time Out thinkpiece. If you crave the kind of suburb that has opinion pieces, listicles and exposés written about its coffee scene or weekly bar fight, you’ll be disappointed. The only controversies we get are national: sunscreen lab failures on Pedestrian TV, Gen Z quitting booze, or Foodprint Melbourne’s numbers about food waste — all important, all not about our letterboxed mail.

Who would hate living here: people who need to be seen by media or graded on Instagrammable credentials. If your life needs Urban List’s blessing, Research will make you twitchy.

Who would love it here: anyone who likes being un-Googled, who enjoys a place that shares a name with academic prestige but gives you the privacy to actually do the thinking. We’re the suburb that’s too modest to be researched — and honestly, we’re fine with that.
