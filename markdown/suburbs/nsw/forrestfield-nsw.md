# Forrestfield (NSW)

Slug: `forrestfield-nsw`

We start our Saturdays like everyone else in this postcode — by pretending we only need one plant and coming home with a ute-load. Dawson’s Garden World at 170 Hale Road is our cathedral: as Broadsheet noted, the chain’s Forrestfield HQ has history back to 1903 and the site was purchased in 1914. These days the place still runs the show — succulents, roses, fruit trees, and that inexplicable passion for potting mix.

If we want to feel like we did some exercise before lunch, we’ll nip out to Whistlepipe Gully from the northern end of Lewis Road. Urban List flagged it as one of Perth’s best short hikes — old house ruins, granite outcrops, creeks and all the scenic bits to pretend you “disconnected” for an hour. Concrete Playground even had a tip-off for Falls Road (160/186 Falls Road) if you want more splash for your gram.

Local press has tried to make us fancy — Urban List once reported a Ribs Lane smokehouse and microbrewery opening here and even mentioned Chef Joe Anastasio opening a second restaurant in Forrestfield in November 2024. We’ll raise a glass to ambition, then head back to the shopping centre for something reliably ordinary.

Speaking of ordinary, Hawaiian’s Forrestfield at 20 Strelitzia Avenue is comfortingly predictable: major supermarkets, variety stores, and the sort of promotions that make Tuesdays feel like an event. If you’ve got kids, the Forrestfield Football Club on Morrison Road is where “local hero” status is earned — Auskick, junior teams, and a Junior Photo Day scheduled for 26 & 28 June 2026. And for the practical among us, Mead Medical on 11 Salix Way has saved more than one weekend.

Who would hate living here? If you crave late-night cocktail bars on every corner, Michelin-starred pretension at breakfast, or a skyline view that matches your ego, this isn’t your suburb. You’ll grumble about the quiet and the fact our biggest scandal is a new brewery.

Who would love it? Plant nerds, families, weekend hikers, and anyone who likes a proper local supermarket, reliable medical care, and a footy club that remembers kids still need a game. We’ll take the nurseries, the gully walks and the football oval over downtown drama any day — and yes, we’ll help you pick the perfect grevillea.
