# Casula (NSW)

Slug: `casula-nsw`

We’re standing outside the old electricity plant, coffee forgotten, watching kids climb the sky-high thing we call a playground and thinking: we own weird. The Casula Powerhouse Arts Centre used to be a 1951 power station and, as Broadsheet reminded us, opened as an arts space in 1994 — and for that alone we will never stop bragging. Back in the day it turned 25 and hosted five new exhibits; there’s always something that convinces us we live in a functioning cultural hub and not just a postcode with good take-away.

A typical weekend for us? Start with the kids at the $4 million Casula Parklands Playground — eight-metre-high Sky Walk, flume-like slides and a Ninja Warrior–ish zone, as Time Out gushed — while someone else corrals toddlers into the smaller accessible area. Then it’s over to the Powerhouse where, historically, exhibitions after dark and festivals like Way Out West have kept us busy with family workshops, silent discos and free activities for kids up to 15. If you remember Soft Centre booting off at the Powerhouse in 2017 and coming back with its immersive, slightly unnerving projection-and-noise parties, you know our suburb can be both pram-friendly and weirdly avant‑garde.

We don’t have petty council feuds to gossip about in the clippings — just evidence that Casula oscillates between community playgroups and international art parties. The Play Strong Casula Supported playgroup lives at Casula Community Centre on 39 Ingham Drive (email: ps.casula@playgroupnsw.org.au) and Casula Holy Spirit runs fortnightly at Holy Spirit Aged Care, 76 Marsh Parade. That combo — intergenerational play and experimental festivals — tells you everything: we’re practical, then we’re very extra.

Who would hate it here? If your entire personality is “hopelessly trendy CBD cocktail bar,” you’ll find Casula a little stubborn and quietly committed to slides over skyline views. Who would love it? Families, festival nerds, kids who think an art centre is a playground for the imagination, and anyone who appreciates a Powerhouse that can host a Blake Prize exhibit one week and a bleepy underground dance under the same roof the next.

We’re proud of our slightly chaotic mix. Come for the Parklands, stay for the Powerhouse, and don’t be surprised if you leave with glitter in your hair and a playgroup flyer in your bag.
