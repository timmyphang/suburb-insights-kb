# Box Hill (NSW)

Slug: `box-hill-nsw`

We spill out of Box Hill Station like someone’s late for lunch and never apologises. One side is the hum of Box Hill Central on Market Street, the other is a ribbon of signs promising dumplings, hot pot and bubble tea until someone invents a new way to carbo-load us all. As Broadsheet put it, we’ve been living the “second Chinatown” flex for years — and happily so.

Mornings here are less about yoga and more about strategy: which lane to take to avoid the market crowd, which hot pot place will seat a group without booking, whether we’ve got room in the pantry for more noodles. Urban List once teased a “20-second walk” through the restaurants; we treat that as a challenge and a lifestyle.

Local petty drama used to be a debate about whether Box Hill even deserved a bar. We were a long-running dry zone — yes, really — until Broadsheet’s 2025 first-look noted Bar Nearby moving in. So now our midweek options are: dumplings, bubble tea or the revolutionary concept of having a neighbourhood bar. Progress, folks.

We’ve also had our weird flex moments. Time Out’s report found Box Hill listed among Melbourne hotspots for vacant homes — which feels like being blessed with choice that nobody uses. High-rises and empty apartments: glamorous in pamphlets, awkward in reality.

Who would hate living here? Someone who wants their suburb to smell faintly of nothing. If you crave a weekend with one café that serves one thing calmly and without queues, you’ll pack up quicker than a 20-minute train to the CBD. If you don’t like a commercial district that hums at 6am and still hums at 10pm, we’re not for you.

Who would love it? People who plan their week around food, students at Box Hill Institute, anyone who rates great public transport and festivals (yes, Chinese New Year and the community arts stuff have kept us busy) — and those who see a neighbourhood bar opening as a civic victory. We’re loud, delicious and unapologetically busy. Come for the dumplings, stay because you couldn’t possibly decide what to eat next.
