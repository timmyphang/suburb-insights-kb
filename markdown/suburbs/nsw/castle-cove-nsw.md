# Castle Cove (NSW)

Slug: `castle-cove-nsw`

We know the sound of Castle Cove on a Sunday: gumleaves tapping the footpath, a pram rattling past Denawen Ave and the faint memory of a bakery queue where Deepwater Road meets our suburb’s small-town ambition.

Back in the day we used to pop in to The Grumpy Baker at 16 Deepwater Road for rye loaves and pastries — the sort of place that made you forgive a neighbour for stealing your parking spot. Blessed Fruit Pies even began life in our tiny corner before packing up and moving to 103 Miller Street, North Sydney; yes, our pastry pedigree got poached by the big smoke. Bodhi Thai (16a Deepwater Rd) was another local name that cropped up when we wanted dinner without the effort of leaving the suburb.

But let’s be honest: we’re not all brunch-and-babka all the time. Time Out pointed out the other side of our leafy postcard — 265 trees were illegally killed in a year here, including native banksias and a 100‑year‑old angophora. Million‑dollar houses backing onto bushland meet suspiciously aggressive pruning, and for a while the local soap opera was council notices and angry letters. We roll our eyes, then go water the plants the loppers missed.

Who would hate living here? If you crave public transport at midnight, a booming nightlife, or zero leaf litter, Castle Cove will test your patience. If you think suburbs should be anonymous and interchangeable, you’ll find our puppet theatre, our small bakeries and our tree debates oddly specific.

Who should move here? Families. For years the kids’ circuit has included Puppeteria at 12 Denawen Ave (the Jeral Puppets, with stacks of handcrafted puppets and classic shows), Humpty Sports running sessions at Willis Park, Itty Bitty Dance classes in the area and local swim options like Carlile on the North Shore listings. Landscapers and gardeners — Northside Tree and Garden Services, Kangaroo Paw Gardening & Landscaping and Ace Landscapes & Turf Supplies — keep our gardens looking like someone’s Pinterest fever dream.

We’ll keep our leafy streets, our old bakery anecdotes and our slightly neurotic community meetings. Come for the puppet shows, stay because you’ve accidentally signed up to water a neighbour’s lemon tree.
