# Wanneroo (NSW)

Slug: `wanneroo-nsw`

We know the scene: Saturday at the Wanneroo Showgrounds, 10am, a line of sticky-handed kids queueing for the animal farm while we try to negotiate face‑painting, a gym obstacle course and a coffee from whatever truck’s still standing — Playgroup WA’s Annual Fun Day, 21 Ariti Avenue, is our annual audition for chaos and it never disappoints.

Our slow‑moving weekend then looks like this: a trawl through Wanneroo Central (listed at 950 Wanneroo Road with Coles, Kmart and Aldi and apparently endless free parking), a detour to the Swapmeet at Wanneroo Rd & Noonan Drive if we need vintage stuff or a mystery sausage, and maybe a dip at Wanneroo Aquamotion when swim‑school holiday spots finally open up. The guide used to put Panini Cnr and Cafe Elixir on the map for the local feed fix, and the mini‑golf at Wanneroo Botanic Golf (25 Drovers Pl) still gets name‑checked for its native‑plant landscaping and little sculptures — perfect for pretending you’re sophisticated while losing to your cousin.

We’ve also had our local headlines: as Pedestrian and Junkee gleefully reminded everyone, there was the unforgettable morning police call when a bloke yelled at a spider, and that bloke who told cops he ran a red light because he was rushing to Nando’s. We’re not proud, but we are consistent.

Who would hate living here? If you require artisanal, permanent bottomless brunch energy every weekend (Sitchu only lists us as "an area to check"), or you need a skyline of 24/7 cocktail bars, sorry — we’re more go‑kart roar and mini‑golf serenity than rooftop glamour.

Who loves it? Families who adore the Wanneroo Show — rides, animals, competitions and fireworks — the people who pick strawberries at Wanneroo Strawberry Picking Farm on a weekend trip out, the petrol‑head kids who grew up hearing Tiger Kart Club stories about Daniel Ricciardo and Garth Tander, and anyone who regards a swapmeet, a pet hotel like Hanrob on Adams Road, and a community fun‑day as proof that civilisation is thriving.

Yes, we know what outsiders say about us. They missed the part where we’ve got a showground, swim school, go‑karts and a lot of very stubborn local pride. We’ll be here — face paint intact, go‑kart helmet in hand.
