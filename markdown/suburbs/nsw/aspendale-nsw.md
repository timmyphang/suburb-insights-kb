# Aspendale (NSW)

Slug: `aspendale-nsw`

We wake up to the sound of the bay (yes, the bay — not the ocean pretending to be Bondi) and shuffle our way to Glen Street Reserve for the kind of stroll that makes city folk sigh and immediately regret their life choices. Back in the day, Sitchu called Aspendale a suburb with a "year‑round holiday vibe" and a "small‑town feel" — and honestly, they weren't wrong. The foreshore is a 10‑minute amble from plenty of homes, the station and bus stops are within reach, and Aspendale Village still sits there like a polite neighbour who brings baking.

If you want a postcard, Urban List once pointed out that Edithvale’s beach "stretches for 1.5km between Chelsea and Aspendale," praising the white sand. We like to pretend that's about us by association. For a touch of local lore, Time Out gave us the best cameo a suburb could ask for — Avalon Westcott and her hens (Poppy, Nonna, Nacho, Wednesday, Velma and Daphne) were a real Aspendale headline in 2016. Those chickens had more Instagram presence than we did.

There isn't a juicy council scandal in the data we looked at, so here's the petty reality: our drama is mild and domestic. Listings on Sitchu routinely bragged about being "seconds to the Glen Street Reserve" or "moments to Aspendale Primary and St Louis De Montfort's" — the sort of brag that gets the PTA email chain humming. One listing even noted being "securely let until Jan 2026," which is the most thrilling thing our suburb’s property gossip offered.

Who would hate living here? If your idea of paradise involves late‑night bars, underground raves or the relentless thrill of being lost in a never‑ending café crawl, Aspendale will gently bore you. We have a soft shore, sensible schools, tidy parks and a village centre that prefers fundraising sausage sizzles to neon.

Who would love it? Families who want Aspendale Primary and St Louis De Montfort’s on the walk‑to list, people who value a proper foreshore and a train that actually gets you to town, and anyone who appreciates a suburb that behaves like a holiday you can afford to stay in. We’ll keep the hens if you bring the lamingtons.
