# Hectorville (NSW)

Slug: `hectorville-nsw`

You know you’re in Hectorville when our social calendar is less brunch hype and more hall bookings and meeting minutes. For years the Neighbourhood Watch Hectorville — Area 84 used to meet at the Hectorville Community Centre on 47 Reid Ave (4th Tues, 7.30pm Feb–Nov, if you kept the calendar handy). John Nemcic was the name on the roster; yes, we all know that name.

Our weekend routine? Someone hires the large hall (160), someone else the small hall (60) — both with AV, kitchens and accessible facilities — and between the church tuckshop run and the tennis courts there’s always something on. The Catholic Church — Hectorville at 20 Montacute Rd ran Sunday School and parish programs that quietly kept the town ticking. The Hectorville Sports & Community Club (listed at 31 Fisher St Magill, with branch activity at Daly Oval Tennis Courts) used to be where our social lives doubled as fundraising efforts — contact 0410 328 547 if you wanted to be officially useful.

Petty local drama? The records don’t give us spicy gossip, just comforting bureaucracy: bookings, committee nights, and an inbox full of community emails. That’s our scandal — who gets the hall on a Saturday.

Who would hate living here? People who require constant novelty and rooftop bars. Who’ll love it? Families, organisers, and anyone who believes a humble community centre and a reliable sports club are the civic equivalent of a good cuppa. We’ll see you at Reid Ave — bring a clipboard.
