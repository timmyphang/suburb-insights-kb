# Blackett (NSW)

Slug: `blackett-nsw`

We wake up in Blackett, NSW and, honestly, our big claim to fame online is a mum app and a literary names thread. Back in the day — or rather, in the older corners of the internet — the suburb shows up in MamaMeet listings: “Find Mom Groups Near You,” “Meet Other Moms Near You.” As MamaMeet promises, it helps mums find local groups and connect — which, if you ask us, is the nicest sort of neighbourhood hustle you can have. No pretence, just people swapping tips and a lift to the park.

If you scroll a bit further into the archive crates, Mumsnet pops up, not with council dramas but with a character: Nancy Blackett from Swallows and Amazons. Someone calls her “bossy and go-getting but still quite likeable.” We like to think that’s us — a suburb that gets on with it, maybe a bit assertive about where the best footpath is, but largely amiable.

There aren’t any recent write-ups in the 2024–2026 window in this dataset, so we’ll own the low-profile life. For years the local internet presence has been quietly maternal and charmingly literary. No flashy thinkpieces, no viral rebuild stories, no restaurant reviews to make you jealous — just the practical business of people wanting friends who are awake at nap time.

Who would hate living here? If you demand round-the-clock media coverage, curated Instagram moments, or a postcode that trends every fortnight, you’ll be bored. We don’t exist for clout; we exist for playdates, borrowed coffee thermoses, and the thrill of finding someone who remembers the same children’s book.

Who would love it here? New parents looking for community via MamaMeet. Readers who grin at a Mumsnet nod to Nancy Blackett and enjoy living somewhere with a name that could be in a novel. We’re not loud or glossy — we’re useful, steady and a little bit bookish. Pop in, say hi to the mums on the app, and don’t expect a nightclub. Expect a friend who’ll bring spare nappies.
