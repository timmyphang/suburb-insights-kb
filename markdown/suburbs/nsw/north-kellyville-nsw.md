# North Kellyville (NSW)

Slug: `north-kellyville-nsw`

We know North Kellyville by its cul-de-sacs, its glossy listings and the one café people used to brag about on Instagram. Back in the day Urban List pointed to Leaf Café & Co in that brand‑new complex — they were fawning over the big breakfast and the smoked salmon bruschetta. We remember.

Our weekend looks the way the property ads promise: a quick spin past Thorogood Boulevard to check on the rooftop garden terrace and on‑site gym the apartment listings keep showing off, a detour to North Kellyville Square for supplies, then a school run to North Kellyville Public School. The Sitchu pages treat Winning Street, Nightcap Street and even G‑form units like a lifestyle choice — light‑filled, immaculate, conveniently central. Meanwhile, if you peek deep enough you’ll find the Stringer Road pocket’s five‑bedroom showstopper on Bouddi Circuit, which is basically the suburb’s idea of flex.

There’s no scandal here — mostly polished listings and practical suburban pride — so if you live for inner‑city chaos or 24/7 nightlife, this place will not RSVP to your vibe. Likewise, anyone allergic to neat floorplans and proximity to Kellyville Station will roll their eyes.

Who’ll love it? Families who want a tidy school commute, buyers who want modern apartments on Thorogood Boulevard or Winning Street, and anyone who thinks a rooftop garden terrace counts as a weekend plan. We’ll keep doing our rounds, waving from the cul‑de‑sac, and pretending the bougainvillea wasn’t planted just for the listing photos.
