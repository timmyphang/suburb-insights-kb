# Maribyrnong (NSW)

Slug: `maribyrnong-nsw`

Back in the day we’d start the weekend with the same river routine: shuffle down to the Maribyrnong River, pretend we’re training for a fun run on the Maribyrnong River Trail, then wander over to The Boathouse for riverside vertigo and the sort of outdoor deck views that make tourists Instagram quietly. We’d tell newcomers about the time a seal was spotted frolicking in the river — yes, a proper seal — because nothing says “we live here” like debating if it stole someone’s lunch.

If you wanted retail therapy, Highpoint Shopping Centre was our place for runways, pop‑ups and the comforting hum of a very large mall doing its thing. For weirdly specific cravings, we used to point people toward St. Burgs on Edgewater Boulevard or Gorilla Grill when someone demanded kimcheese fries with a side of existential Westside charm. And for history nerds we’d trot them to Jack’s Magazine and whisper about bluestone tunnels like it was our secret museum.

Local politics? Oh, we had drama. Back then a council meeting about parking meters allegedly ended in a literal brawl — councillors reportedly king‑hit and body‑slammed — which, for a suburb, ranks as peak civic engagement. Flood chat was also part of the small‑talk circuit, with residents saying the Flemington Racecourse wall made things worse when the weather turned nasty. We like to think that’s the version of adrenaline our council provides.

Who would hate living here: people who loathe weekend walkers, family picnics or the occasional mall activation. If you can’t cope with riverside joggers, bustling Highpoint events, or a community that will loudly debate parking, this isn’t the postcode for you.

Who’d love it: families — Anglers Tavern got the family‑friendly tick, playgroups flocked to the Maribyrnong Community Centre and Pipemakers Park, and curious humans loved the mix of riverside nature, hidden food spots and oddball local lore. We’re not posh, we’re not trying too hard, and we’ll happily tell you where that seal was last seen.
