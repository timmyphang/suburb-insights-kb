# Kirrawee (NSW)

Slug: `kirrawee-nsw`

We wake to the smell of dough from Thoroughbread Bakery and pretend we’re not waiting in line for a Bourke Street pastry at 3/455 The Boulevarde. Back in the day the weekend unfolded like this: a quick detour past Baked Patisserie on Marshall Road, a nod to whoever’s playing at Sunday Road Brewing, and then — if we’re feeling virtuous — a lap at Climb Fit to feel like Spiderman before lunch. 

For years the local guide pages liked to remind people Kirrawee isn’t just a roundabout and a highway slip‑lane. Smalls Cellar opened in late 2023 (Brooke Small and Sam Kane’s idea) and made a case for preservative-free choices here; Great White Tattoo — yes, the studio Urban List praised — put Sam and Brooke on the map for ink, piercings and removals. We’ve got family infrastructure you won’t brag about at a dinner party: Kirrawee Library+ with its recording studio and media lab, Mikarie Playgroup at 2 Mikarie Place for the toddlers, and Kirrawee Family Medical Practice on 455 President Avenue for when that toddler learns how to climb things other than the climbing gym. 

We’re fond of saying our centre’s got something for everyone. The Prince Hotel used to be the family‑friendly pub pick (ankle‑biters menu and all), South Village hosted Santa photos on the Princes Highway, and Orchard Early Learning on Oak Road runs a paddock‑to‑plate pitch so your kids can be smug about their carrots.

Who hates living here? If you loathe community studios, bakeries that make you cancel weekend plans, or the idea of a brewery being handsome enough to photograph, this place will drive you up the Princes Highway. Also, if you prefer not seeing the same friendly faces at coffee lines and kids’ programs, move on.

Who’d love it here? Families, climbers, people who like their beer live‑music‑adjacent, and anyone who appreciates a library that doubles as a creative hub. We’re small, we’re stubbornly useful, and we’ll always pretend not to notice you bought your birthday cake from Bourke Street.
