# Mentone (NSW)

Slug: `mentone-nsw`

We’ll admit it up front: there aren’t any recent headlines in this pile, so we’re doing our roast from memory and the odd PDF. Back in the day (and according to the archives), Mentone was the kind of bayside suburb that politely refused to be anything other than charming.

Morning routine: we’d roll out onto the Bay Trail, past the surf club and playground, and watch the parade at the Mentone Dog Beach — the west side, between Charman and Plummer Roads, where dogs run off‑leash all year, and everyone pretends the seagulls aren’t stealing snacks. After that, we used to duck into local bites: Broadsheet once chronicled Applehead Deli and Huff Bagelry’s Mentone outpost, and Gâteaux by Marc Frissard even relocated here (Broadsheet noted the move). Sunnyside Sliced was the place people name‑checked when they wanted an 18‑inch pie and a courtyard with a container cocktail bar, and Urban List had us on the map for Busy Boy and the gluten‑free hero Simply Gluten Free. We’ll say “used to” because, well, these pieces are from the archives — not tonight’s itinerary.

Our civic life is earnest: Mentone Public Library has a whole PDF about being “a renewed community meeting point,” and the Mentone Activity Hub at 29 Venice Street hosts everything from the Country Women’s Association to Kingston U3A ukulele sessions, according to community listings. Playgroup Victoria even lists Mentone Girls’ Grammar Early Learning Centre and Mentone Park Primary School as local playgroup venues — so yes, there are a lot of singalong sessions, snack negotiations and mysterious glitter on every surface.

Petty local drama? The papers didn’t give us a juicy feud, just the kind of bureaucracy you can’t roast: strategic plans for the Mentone Lifesaving Club (they’ve been around since 1920) and a steady stream of community notices. How scandalous.

Who would hate it here: anyone who thinks coastal living means non‑stop nightlife and zero responsibility. Who would love it: families hunting school‑belt cred (Mentone Grammar, Mentone Girls’ Grammar, Kilbreda, St Bede’s get a mention), dog owners, and people who like a main strip that quietly does its thing.

We’re Bayside, bureaucratic, and borderline smug about it — and if you’re into dog beaches, decent sandwiches and a library with ambitions, you'll fit right in.
