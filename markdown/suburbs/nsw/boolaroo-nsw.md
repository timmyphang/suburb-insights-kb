# Boolaroo (NSW)

Slug: `boolaroo-nsw`

We’ll be honest — there aren’t any proper recent gossip columns about Boolaroo in our packet, so we’re leaning into the comforting mundanity. Back in the day (and according to the pages we do have), our weekend peaks were less champagne rooftop and more community chest: the Boolaroo Sports Club on Eighth Street — once the Boolaroo Bowling Club — hosted Friday Night raffles, happy hour and Scroungers Bowls. It sounded exactly like the good kind of local predictability.

Our morning routine used to be almost cinematic: drop the kids at school, duck into the Lake Cinema on Main Road (opened 29 March 1974) for that lonely single-screen charm, then pop by SPAR at 26 Main Road to grab whatever the weekly special was. If anyone was feeling unwell, Boolaroo Medical Practice at 33 Main Road — which listed Dr Chikore and was taking new patients — handled the practical bits with clinic notices and flu vaccine posts.

There’s no juicy council feud here in the files — just useful services. TASK’s family-support page for Boolaroo pointed people to Relationships NSW, The Smith Family, Resourcing Parents, Koorana Services, Thrive, Gateway Family Services and Community Child & Family Health Services. Yawn? Hardly. That’s the backbone.

Who would hate it here: anyone after boutique nightlife, artisanal everything or a postcode that doubles as a status symbol. Who would love it: families who want steady community, retirees who appreciate a single-screen cinema’s stubborn romance, and anyone who enjoys knowing the local club still runs a raffle on a Friday. We’ll take the raffle.
