# Truganina (NSW)

Slug: `truganina-nsw`

Back in the day we’d drop the kids at the Truganina Community Centre on Everton Road and pretend the rest of our weekend was a curated Instagram reel. The playgroup sheets said “Creative Expression Playgroup” and the gate instructions for the school hub told you to use Gate 2 on Federation Drive — bureaucratic charm, really. If you wanted multicultural morning tea there was a Multicultural playgroup and the MNCW Wellbeing Series used to roll through with everything from cyber‑safety to traffic tips. Kids flipped at Flip Gymnastics during Wyndham Active Holidays while we practised smiling at other parents.

Landmarks? Oh, we’ve got the one that makes other suburbs jealous and slightly suspicious: the gigantic Cadbury distribution centre — the $130 million, twice‑the‑MCG behemoth that Time Out loved to remind everyone about. When the chocolate plant was the thing to name‑drop, we’d smirk and say, “Nah, our backyard ships half the nation’s chocolate.”

Retail theatre arrived in the form of warehouse sales. Five Star Factory Direct clearing Baccarat gear out of Truganina (4 Magnesium Place) meant you could pretend you were a bargain hunter with taste. Meanwhile, Concrete Playground’s Truganina events page used to list exactly zero results — which, honestly, made our Saturdays refreshingly uncomplicated.

Who’d hate living here? If you need a permanent line of buzzy pop‑up markets and curated laneway coffee stalls, you’d be exasperated. If your idea of nightlife is velvet ropes and DJs, Truganina’s quieter, family‑centric rhythms will make you check urban event calendars and then close the tab.

Who’d love it here? Families who appreciate community centres that actually run things, organisers of multicultural workshops, and anyone who enjoys having a gigantic chocolate landmark to use as a directional reference. And for those entrepreneurial types — Time Out once waved a flag at 3029 for its hustle — there’s a certain If‑you‑can‑build‑it energy we quietly own.

We’re not flashy. We’re practical, a little warehouse‑sensible and proud of it. Come for the playgroups, stay because your GPS already knows where 1 Everton Road is.
