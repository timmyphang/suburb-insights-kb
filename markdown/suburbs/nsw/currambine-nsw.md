# Currambine (NSW)

Slug: `currambine-nsw`

Back in the day we'd tell city friends Currambine was the place you could actually organise your life: drop the kids at Creative Kids Art Club in the Currambine Community Centre (64 Delamere Ave), wobble next door to the Dome Café for something warm, and know the afternoon would involve pizza, cookies or a spin class — or all three if you were hungry and indecisive.

Our scrapbooks are mostly older clippings, so this is a fond look back rather than a current neighbourhood guide. Urban List once listed a Currambine outpost of Get Chunky (yes, cookies), and both Urban List and Time Out mentioned Il Locale having a Currambine location — the kind of pizza rep that made us proud to eat in sweatpants. Fitness options used to get name-drops too: SpeedFit and CycleBar were the spin-and-sweat spots people cited when they wanted to feel virtuous before dinner.

If you were after quieter indulgence, Urban List even noted a Float Lab presence alongside other suburbs — soundproof pods and relax rooms, the sort of luxe silence that felt delicious after soccer practice. Speaking of kids, CareforKids recorded 17 childcare services in Currambine and flagged YMCA Currambine OSHC for before- and after-school care; the site also listed an average childcare cost of $165.53/day. There was also a family day-care option advertising fees of $90/day and an NQS rating of “Meeting” — practical things mums and dads actually read about.

There’s no juicy local scandal in our files — no council stoushes or late-night revelations — just the steady, domestic hum: art classes, soccer programs like Grasshopper Soccer, counselling listings and the endless logistics of family life. That’s the point.

Who would hate it here? People chasing perpetual nightlife or the latest buzzy laneway café — our clippings suggest Currambine has always been more cookie-and-community than cocktail-and-concept. Who’d love it? Families who value local classes, anyone who once queued for Get Chunky or liked the idea of Il Locale nearby, and those who preferred a Float Lab chill-out to a city bar crawl.

Consider this a nostalgia roast: affectionate, a little smug, and legally obliged to remind you these mentions come from older listings. We still love the place — even if we now Google to check what’s actually open.
