# Parkwood (NSW)

Slug: `parkwood-nsw`

We’re Parkwood people — proud, slightly bemused, and very good at pretending our suburb has been steadily upgrading since at least the pre‑2024 press cycle. Trouble is, most of the ink about “Parkwood” back then was actually about Parkwood Village on the Gold Coast, so our local bragging rights are mainly aspirational and come with a Napper Road postcode: 76–122 Napper Road, if you want to be precise.

Mornings here usually involve the same ritual: we make a cuppa and scroll articles that promised a whole new leisure district. Concrete Playground wrote about a $200‑million plan for The Club at Parkwood Village — a 200‑plus‑bed hotel, residential apartments and, yes, an Endless Surf pool. Urban List kept reminding us that Parkwood Village was “coming soon”, an integrated golf and surf village with an upgraded 18‑hole golf course, retail, medical and dining. The opening date they gave? Late 2026. Back in the day it felt like watching a trailer for a movie we’d already memorised.

If you want petty local drama, the main soap opera here is developer optimism vs. calendar time. No named feuds in the file, just a lot of plans that loved the future more than the present. Broadsheet’s roundups of Gold Coast secrets were happy to tuck Parkwood into itineraries that promised going beyond Surfer’s Paradise — which is flattering until you remember most of those pieces were about “the area” rather than our street.

Who would hate living here? People who loathe novelty leisure complexes and fully licensed mini‑golf with sneaky water traps. Urban List once listed Boulders And Badlands at Parkwood Village as an 18‑hole experience that’s open day and night — so if you object to swing poles, rocky terrain and nightlife putt‑putt, this might not be your scene. Nail salon haters, turn away now: Tips Nail Art Studio got a shoutout for personalised nail art and friendly service.

Who would love it? If the plans actually roll through, lovers of manufactured waves, night‑time mini golf, hotel stays that double as fantasy weekends and anyone who bookmarks “open late” will be quietly thrilled. For now, we’re the suburb that keeps its fingers crossed, reads the press releases, and tells visitors the future is literally being paved — on Napper Road.
