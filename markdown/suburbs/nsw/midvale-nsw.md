# Midvale (NSW)

Slug: `midvale-nsw`

Back in the day our biggest claim to fame was a single line in an Urban List round-up: "Costumes In The Valley Midvale". We like to imagine the whole town briefly went full masquerade after that — sequins on the footpath, corsets in the post office queue, wigs in the supermarket express lane. As Urban List noted, that place stocked masquerade and Halloween masks, colourful face paints, wigs, corsets and hosiery — practically a civic uniform.

A usual weekend for us used to be a rummage through those racks (memory lane, not a current recommendation — the mention is from older press), then a long debate about whether tights count as formalwear. The rest of our media footprint? Mostly Sitchu pages about Mona Vale and the Northern Beaches, which is to say our press kit is thin and a bit jealous of other suburbs' beaches.

We don’t have a local soap-opera on file, so the petty drama is more of an imagination exercise: who stole who’s feather boa, who wore the wrong mask to the wrong party. Not much to report — and we’re fine with that.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who needs a glossy lifestyle feature to justify their existence; seekers of endless brunch reviews will be disappointed (our clippings point elsewhere). Who would love it? Lovers of dressing up, bargain hunters, community theatre types and anyone who sees potential in a good wig and a ridiculous hat. Midvale: we may not make many headlines, but when we do, it’s in full costume.
