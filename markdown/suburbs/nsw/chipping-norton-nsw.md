# Chipping Norton (NSW)

Slug: `chipping-norton-nsw`

We know this patch of Chipping Norton like the soles of our wellies — or at least like the bits of mud our kids bring home on a Sunday. Back in the day (by which we mean the OxfordshireMummies posts from 2022–23), our weekend was a tour of playgrounds that promised adventure and delivered grass stains.

Morning ritual: shepherd the smalls toward Enstone playground — yes, the one tucked behind the primary school down Cox’s Lane. As the 13 April 2022 write-up noted, getting there feels like a treasure hunt because of the awkward access, but the new wooden play equipment and obstacle course make the detour worth it for that Instagram shot your cousin will pretend not to comment on.

If you want full-on vertical parenting, Middle Barton was, in the post from 24 January 2023, literally called a “climber’s paradise”: multiple climbing frames, slides, a cargo net and toddler gear. Surfacing and layout got an honest appraisal — great for energetic knees, slightly less great for those who favour spotless shoes.

Chadlington play park turned up in the 3 January 2023 notes as “decent” — functional but showing a bit of wear. We nod, we fix a squeaky swing with a stern look, we move on.

Who would hate living here? People who adore pristine playgrounds and short walks from car to cafe. Who’d love it? Families who measure happiness in obstacle courses, muddy knees and knowing which lane Cox’s Lane will send you out on.

As OxfordshireMummies showed us, this Chipping Norton is quietly brilliant — imperfect, wooden, and impossible to keep a straight face about.
