# Bundoora (NSW)

Slug: `bundoora-nsw`

Bundoora, NSW — Back in the day the papers liked to remind us we were mostly trees, a campus and the kind of suburban ambition that files its plans under “eventual.” We know the mood: we roll out of bed, take the kids (or the thesis) to La Trobe University, and spend the afternoon pretending the Homestead’s café always smelled like scones and culture. Time Out used to trumpet Bundoora Park as our sprawling green excuse to picnic, visit the children’s farm and pretend we’re outdoorsy.

Our Saturday checklist used to read like a brochure: a slow lap of Bundoora Park, maybe a detour to the Bundoora Homestead Arts Centre to nod at a Kenneth Jack landscape, then a coffee stop at the Homestead café — which, as Time Out noted, served housemade pies and seasonal fare. If we weren’t being quaint we were being useful: Uni Hill Town Centre on Janefield Drive used to be our go-to for shopping runs, and the FindGlocal posts hinted the centre was still posting news deep into 2026.

Petty local saga? Oh, we’ve got the long game. La Trobe’s $5 billion, 30-year masterplan was the sort of council romanticism that has Bundoora promising to become a city-for-now in theory. The Suburban Rail Loop also liked to flirt with us — proposed stops include Bundoora, due in the vague future of 2050 — so our commuter fantasies are either visionary or really patient.

If you’d hate living here: please, if you can’t stand students, heritage homesteads that double as art galleries, or the idea of a park that insists on being used for picnics and golf, walk on. If you need nightlife that never sleeps, Bundoora’s gentle civic hum will politely lull you to boredom.

Who we adore here: families who swear by Concord Playgroup at Cameron Parade, students who picked La Trobe as a long-term plan, pickleball converts who bookmarked Swing & Serve on Plenty Road, and anyone who secretly likes the idea of a campus that might someday become a tiny city. We’re quietly proud, slightly patient, and always ready to host The Hospitality Cup at Sports Drive — whenever the calendar decides to arrive.
