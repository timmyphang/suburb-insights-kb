# Caversham (NSW)

Slug: `caversham-nsw`

We’re Caversham, NSW — or at least that’s what our mail says. The internet, bless it, once decided we were the Swan Valley’s very own French pastry dream and a wedding-venue fantasy. Back in the day Broadsheet and Urban List kept singing about Maison Saint-Honoré baking 8,000–9,000 macarons a week and a chef from Le Jules Verne doing things to croissants that sound like culinary witchcraft. Meanwhile Urban List bookmarked Mandoon Estate (10 Harris Rd) and Sandalford (3210 W Swan Rd) as the places you take people when you’re auditioning them for future in-laws.

Morning here is less ‘cruffger and gravlax’ and more, well, whatever’s actually in our fridge. Still, we enjoy the delusion: Concrete Playground once filed Funk Cider at 55 Benara Road as our local cider house, and you can almost hear the eco-cider proudness in the copy — organic, cold-pressed, unpasteurised, bao and brisket rolls on the side. Time Out remembered the kid-friendly side of things too, pointing out Caversham Wildlife Park and the Children’s Forest as a family coupon-clipper. So we’ve been immortalised as either a patisserie, a winery, a cider hub or a playground — take your pick.

There isn’t any juicy local feud in the archives, unless you count whether a croissant-burger hybrid is sacrilege. The most dramatic entries are menus, addresses and review blurbs. The Mumsnet threads are more relatable: some parts objectively posh, others not — aspirational hills versus the everyday high street. Honest.

Who would hate living here? People who can’t cope with being a postal identity crisis — waking up in NSW and being mistaken for a Swan Valley wedding backdrop. Also anyone allergic to long lunches that require moral excuses.

Who would love it? Families (Time Out’s list didn’t lie), pastry obsessives who like to pretend Maison Saint-Honoré still runs a 9,000-macaron assembly line, and anyone who enjoys pretending their weekend has vineyards and balustrades, even if the real local view is a touch more domestic. We’re happy with that.
