# Woodlands (NSW)

Slug: `woodlands-nsw`

We wake up in Woodlands, NSW, brew whatever tea we have left and check the internet — only to be told, again, that we’re not the Woodlands you meant. Apparently we’re competing with a deli at 84 Rosewood Avenue, a pub on Sydney Road in Coburg and a waterfront that launches drone shows in Singapore. The world is very confused, and frankly, so are we.

Back in the day the only press that mentioned our name did so by accident. Broadsheet once ran a directory for Oh So Deli (that one lives in a Perth Woodlands Shopping Centre and sells katsu chicken sandos and pastrami), and the same venerable site celebrated The Woodlands Hotel in Coburg — 84–88 Sydney Road — as a microbrew wonderland and even a 2026 World Cup watch spot. Urban List is busy telling readers about a Singapore waterfront drone show and a Perth fish-and-chips that apparently wears our postcode like a name-tag. Meanwhile Concrete Playground reminds anyone looking for a walk in NSW that there are trails which wander “through woodlands and past the park’s picturesque pond” — useful line, but not exactly a local business listing.

The petty local drama? It’s being invisible and mistaken at the same time. We’re the suburb that gets identity theft by good copy. People tag our postcode under photos of coffee sangas and pub tap lists that aren’t ours. Our only crime is having a name that sounds like every other decent patch of trees on the map.

Who would hate living here? Folks who need their suburb to be on every lifestyle list, with a named café to brag about and a pub everyone can point to on Instagram. You’d be fed up correcting people at parties.

Who would love it here? People who like actual woodlands rather than curated, clickable versions of them — the sort of people who’d take Concrete Playground’s advice and wander “through woodlands and past the park’s picturesque pond,” even if the byline isn’t ours. We’ll keep being quietly lovely, even if Google keeps sending tourists to the wrong sandwich shop.
