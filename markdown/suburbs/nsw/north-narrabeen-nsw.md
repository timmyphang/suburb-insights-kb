# North Narrabeen (NSW)

Slug: `north-narrabeen-nsw`

Back in the day we’d finish a dawn surf, shuffle past the 50‑metre timber boardwalk at the North Narrabeen Rockpool and pretend we weren’t a bit smug about living here. We still are. We know the lagoon like the back of our boardies — kayak, sail or paddleboard it if you must — and then nip over to the 8.4‑kilometre boardwalk for the part where we pretend the exercise was for fitness, not brunch.

For years the local listings gossiped about places we love (or used to). Oceans Narrabeen was the beachfront haunt that served Allpress Espresso and açai bowls; Mexicano once promised modern street‑style tacos with a beachside buzz; Brot and Wurst used to keep the sausage roll contingent happy with kransky and weisswurst. Fat Pomelo and the Cabana Beach Kiosk made the area feel like a proper seaside village, and the Beaches Farmers Market (1472 Pittwater Road) gave us reason to queue on the weekend. The North Narrabeen Reserve at the same Pittwater Road address staged community life, and our community centre on Woorarra Ave was where we argued over bake sale tables and lost keys.

We’ll also own the nice flex: the place was set to play host to the GWM Sydney Surf Pro Presented by Bonsoy in May 2024 — yes, international surfers, yes, sand in unfortunate places, yes, we watched.

If you hate organised outdoor things, you’ll hate it here. If you loathe rock platform tours pointing out sea stars and limpets, loathe 50‑metre lap pools tucked into cliffs or can’t stomach a farmers market queue, this won’t be your cup of tea. If the idea of a playgroup at Narrabeen Baptist Church (13 Grenfell Ave) and community mums getting their act together while the surf calms down sounds like eternal small‑townness, move on.

But if you love surf breaks that have hosted the world’s best, a proper rockpool with a timber boardwalk, salty mornings followed by community markets and family‑run bakeries — well, we’d be delighted to have you. We’ll show you where the best view of the break is, just don’t tell the tourists.
