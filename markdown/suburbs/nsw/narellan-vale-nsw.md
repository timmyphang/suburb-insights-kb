# Narellan Vale (NSW)

Slug: `narellan-vale-nsw`

Back in the day we’d pile the car with nappies and a suspicious amount of snacks and ferry the small people to whichever childcare had free spots that week. As Camden Kids listed, Goodstart Early Learning on 1 Irwin Court was our reliable 7:00am–6:00pm option, Little Zak’s Academy sat on 42 Waterworth Drive, Jenny’s Kindergarten was over at 3 Liquidamber Dr, and St Mary Multicultural Early Learning Centre lived at 2 Yate Place — the sort of local rota that makes you memorise phone numbers in your sleep.

Our weekend opener? A courier run of lost lunchboxes between Liquidamber and Waterworth Drive while we debate, for the hundredth time, who taught a toddler to say “no” so precisely. There’s no high-stakes council scandal here in the archive — just the thrilling municipal drama of which kinder has space and whether we remembered to pack a spare hat.

Who would hate living here? People who need a nightlife that lasts past sunset or those allergic to the soothing hum of carpool logistics. Who’d love it? Anyone who measures community by playgroup coordinates and appreciates a suburb where you can swap kinder tips with the same three neighbours for years.

Sure, the online mentions we have are older — Camden Kids did the listing work — but that’s kind of our vibe: steady, suburban, a little behind the trendy guidebooks, and utterly committed to getting the kids to the right address on time.
