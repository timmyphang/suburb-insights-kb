# Mulgoa (NSW)

Slug: `mulgoa-nsw`

We’re the Mulgoa that gets mentioned in a lot of other people’s stories — Penrith’s quieter cousin at the foot of the Blue Mountains, the place with Mulgoa Road running through it and the Nepean River doing the heavy lifting for views. Back in the day (and, full disclosure, in the only articles we could find), most of the ink was spent on plans and names rather than our barista’s banter — so consider this a fondly begrudging local PSA.

Morning here is a slow roll: we used to point out the odd listing on Mulgoa Road — Sitchu even drops addresses like 83 and 123 Mulgoa Road into its travel roundups — and joke that one of them must contain all the suburb’s secrets. For years the local business chatter lived in directories: MumsPages keeps a tidy catalogue (including Mulgoa Quarries Pty Ltd, which has been doing the earth-moving since 1964), and Parentopia links mums to groups the way we link to the nearest stretch of river.

Our “fame”, such as it is, comes by way of names that sound too similar to be coincidental. There’s Mulga the artist — Joel Moore — who pops up in profiles and collaborations (Broadsheet wrote about his colourful work and a tie-in with The Alternative Dairy Co.), and even a Mulga Bill’s cafe turned up in Concrete Playground… in Brisbane. We’re flattered, if slightly confused.

Local drama? The papers were more into masterplans than bar fights: Time Out flagged Mirvac’s Everdene masterplan promising 1,200 homes in Mulgoa. Translation: more letterboxes, more joggers, and more people asking “Is this the one near the river?” for years to come. No juicy council tiffs in the clips we found — just steady suburban arranging.

Who would hate living here? If you need nightly rooftop DJ drops, waterside brunch openings every weekend or a constant stream of new cafes to review, Mulgoa will make you restless. Who would love it? People who like space, the shadow of the Blue Mountains, a long-running quarry legacy, and the idea that our biggest headlines are about masterplans, directories and an artist who shares our name. We’ll take that.
