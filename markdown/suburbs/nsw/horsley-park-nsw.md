# Horsley Park (NSW)

Slug: `horsley-park-nsw`

Back in the day we’d roll out of bed, stretch, and head for the Western Sydney Parklands — the big, breathing bit of space Time Out once praised for picnics, barbecues and paths where you can actually hear yourself think. It’s the sort of weekend where kids kick a ball, someone fires up a snag, and the sky feels unfairly large.

If we were feeling less domesticated, there was the Horsley Park Tavern down on Horsley Rd — Time Out called it a "pub in the scrub" where VB rules. It used to be the place to limber up for a Saturday arvo that did not involve the city’s neon buzz.

We’re also quietly proud that our corner of Fairfield City gets name-checked in the cultural bits: the 'Mình' exhibition at Fairfield City Museum & Gallery showcased the work of 17 artists and reminded people that Fairfield City stretches from Fairfield and Cabramatta right out to Horsley Park. That’s our subtle flex — not flashy, but full of stories if you bother to look.

Who would hate living here? If you need espresso-lined laneways, rooftop lounges and someone to tell you what cocktail is trending, you’ll be bored inside a day. Who would love it? Folks after wide skies, real parkland, and that comfortably blunt suburban honesty — plus anyone who appreciates a local museum quietly doing the heavy lifting of community memory.

We may not be headline-hunting, but we’re living exactly where the map says we are — and, frankly, we like it that way.
