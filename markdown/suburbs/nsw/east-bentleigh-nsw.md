# East Bentleigh (NSW)

Slug: `east-bentleigh-nsw`

We know the ritual here: a Saturday that starts with a stale tram of parents dropping kids at parkland and ends with a pastry-fuelled truce on Centre Road. Back in the day Broadsheet said The Merchants Guild brought some culinary purpose to 680 Centre Rd — and we still brag that Mario Minichilli and Vincent Conti once made our long communal table feel like a small, well-behaved revolution. Siciliadolce at 688 Centre Road used to sell giant cannoli that made weekend queues part of the experience. 

Our morning circuit had options: a Mediterranean-leaning all‑day diner called Time & Place (Broadsheet loved the falafel waffles), a Middle Eastern kiss from Boosa, or a guilt-free deli raid at Fine Gourmet Deli when someone wanted something Properly Eastern European. Urban List once pointed out North East Precinct (892 North Road) bringing New York–style food and Naked Espresso coffee to the strip — we’ll fondly remember that clatter.

Petty local drama? We had weather. Pedestrian TV caught that time our usually obedient streets went full river and East Bentleigh got a cameo in a flash‑flood montage. The gossip afterwards was all about whose driveway became a dock — exactly the kind of overblown conversation we do well.

Who would hate living here: anyone desperate for 24/7 city glamour or a postcode that never settles down. If you need non‑stop nightlife, artisan markets every single weekend and a skyline you can see from the kitchen sink, you’ll find our parks and Centre Road charm maddeningly provincial. 

Who would love it: families who bought here for schools, parkland and a sensible shopping strip (Sitchu noted the post‑lockdown growth and demand for those very things). Folks who appreciate a bakery that remembers how to make real cannoli, a charcoal chicken that takes its smokiness seriously, or a local Ritchies IGA that still feels like the kind of shoppeople you actually know. For years the local scene was quietly good — not needy, not flashy, just reliably delicious. We’ll take that.
