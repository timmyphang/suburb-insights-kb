# Toolern Vale (NSW)

Slug: `toolern-vale-nsw`

We start our mornings to the sound only a suburb full of daycare drop-offs could produce — engines, backpacks and the faint rattle of permission slips. Back in the day (our notes are older), the listings showed 58 childcare services in Toolern Vale, VIC 3337 — 47 of them had vacancies, with an average cost of $149.37/day. If that doesn’t tell you who runs this place, nothing will.

The records even name-check Camp Australia — Melton Christian College — Toolern Vale Campus OSHC, listed at 674–740 Coburns Road, and Toolern Vale OSHClub over on 361 Creamery Road, the latter noted as having vacancies and NQS ratings “Exceeding in several areas.” There were also 24 pre-schools and kindergartens listed, 18 with vacancies. Nostalgia, or practical advantage — take your pick.

Petty local drama? The archives don’t bother with gossip. They’re far more interested in ratios and vacancies. That’s our vibe: efficient, slightly bureaucratic, and very good at finding a spot for your kid.

Who would hate living here: anyone who wants a quiet, child-free cul-de-sac or a suburb defined by Instagrammable cafés (the files don’t mention any). Who would love it: parents hunting for after‑school care, people who like options, and anyone who enjoys knowing there’s probably a vacancy when everyone else says “good luck.”

We’re Toolern Vale — yes, our paperwork calls us VIC 3337 — proof that suburban romance sometimes comes down to enrolment forms.
