# Cardinia (NSW)

Slug: `cardinia-nsw`

We wake up convinced we live halfway to everywhere and fully in on the secret: Cardinia is where the city borrows our vegetables and leaves the noise behind. Back in the day, Broadsheet wrote about O.My’s two‑acre farm in Cardinia supplying the restaurant’s daily‑changing degustation menu — 350 growing beds, an orchard of 50 fruit trees — so yes, our lettuces have a résumé. Time Out used to point people to Cardinia Reservoir Park for picnics, walks across the dam wall and panoramic lookouts where you’ll see eastern grey kangaroos pretending to be scenic extras.

Our weekend plays out like a politely paced documentary. We stretch our legs around the reservoir, agree on which lookout has the best light, and nod with the smugness of locals when someone mentions the farm that used to feed O.My. Casey Cardinia Kids has long been our cheer squad — they’ve rounded up school holiday fun across the shire, from the Cardinia Cultural Centre’s shows to a fishing clinic at Emerald Lake Park and the Koo Wee Rup Mini Festival. And if you like small, reassuring certainties, Cardinia Primary School deserves a shout: a little country school near Officer, Clyde and Pakenham with about 180 students and an Eco Zone that makes paw‑print stickers seem suitably earnest.

Petty drama? There isn’t much to gossip about beyond which lookout gets the best sunrise. We’re mostly known for clean air, farms with ambition, and being the LGA Time Out politely included when explaining what counts as metropolitan Melbourne — which still throws new residents off when the postcode looks rural but the council looks metropolitan.

Who would hate living here: people who want their espresso within stumbling distance of every errand and who think a “local view” must include a skyline. Also anyone allergic to kangaroos photobombing family selfies.

Who’d love it: families who follow Casey Cardinia Kids for the holiday programs, diners who like to brag about where their dinner’s produce came from, walkers who actually enjoy silence, and anyone who appreciates being technically part of metropolitan Melbourne while still being able to find a quiet patch of dirt that feels like a proper escape. We’re not flashy. We’re just ours.
