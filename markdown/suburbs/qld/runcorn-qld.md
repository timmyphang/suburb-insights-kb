# Runcorn (QLD)

Slug: `runcorn-qld`

Back in the day we’d start a Saturday like it was a ritual: queue up for Mountain River Patisserie (84 Mango Street) because even the chefs can’t shut up about it — as Broadsheet put it, “everything is so good.” There was that tiny zen rock garden while someone ordered matcha (UrbanList swore by the matcha), and we’d pretend the cubed croissants at The Wheat House justified our life choices.

Mid-morning we’d wander past Sol Beauty Studio on Warrigal Road (2/124) pretending we weren’t eyeing nail art, then talk seriously about where to get ramen later. Ramen Tensho at 25/258 Warrigal Road cropped up on every “best ramen” list, so naturally we took that as gospel. Dessert plans read like a Brisbane Asian sweet tour: Sweet Treats Dessert Cafe popped up in round-ups for green tea crème brûlée and an Iced Royal Thai Tea Latte with in-house soy milk and wobbly soy custard — the kind of stuff that makes you forget you have responsibilities.

We aren’t flashy. Cheap pleasures defined us: Runcorn Swimming Pool was even named on the list of council pools in a $2-entry summer initiative, and UrbanList’s description — 25-metre outdoor laps, an indoor heated pool, and a lagoon-style play area with a kiosk for snacks — made afternoons feel like an affordable holiday.

If you’re after drama, our archive is disappointingly wholesome. Concrete Playground ran event listings; Walking Mums organised meetups at World of Wonder and pram-friendly walks. There aren’t many council scandals in the clippings — which is the point. We’re low-key, suburban snugness, not prime-time outrage.

Who would hate living here? People who need city-size nightlife, can’t stand bakeries, matcha, or ramen, or think $2 pool days are beneath them. Who would love it? Families and anyone who enjoys a pastry pilgrimage, low-key community events, and a play centre for littlies — Walking Mums literally ran World of Wonder meetups for 0–7s. Chefs used to name our patisserie as a favourite; we wear that like a badge.

We’re not trying to be glamorous. We’re the place you go when you want your pastry fixed, your kid entertained, and your week repaired by a cheap swim.
