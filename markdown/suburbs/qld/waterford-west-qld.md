# Waterford West (QLD)

Slug: `waterford-west-qld`

Back in the day we would’ve sworn Waterford West was designed around a single weekend ritual: park the minivan, march the small people to the playground, then retreat to whatever was left of civilisation with a beer and a screen.

Fitzy’s used to reopen after a full-scale makeover and, honestly, it read like someone finally listened to our collective priorities: an eight-metre mega screen for watching something important, an all‑weather terrace that looked out over the kids’ playground (so you could pretend to relax while secretly organising the next snack run), and a refreshed bistro menu promising pub classics and weekly specials. We remember thinking: this is suburban renaissance, or at least suburban rehab.

If we’re being practical, our feeds always point back to MumsPages — because mums and local Googling go together like sunscreen and school holiday chaos. That site used to list the usual local lifelines: a directory for businesses and services for mums, roof restoration providers when the weather decided to test our tin roofs, and a whole category for psychologists, counsellors and life coaches because, frankly, who among us hasn’t needed one by 3pm on a Tuesday. Even Superior Cleaning and Pest was on the books, listed at 18 Loganlea Road, for the times when “it’s fine” was not actually fine.

Petty local drama? The data doesn’t give us a juicy feud, so we’ll save the witch-hunt. Instead, picture this: the sort of place where your weekend plan reads like a checklist — playground, terrace, mega screen, then a search for someone to fix the roof before the next storm. Not glamorous, but efficient.

Who would hate living here? People who despise practicality, predictable weekly specials, and the comforting glow of a giant screen. Who would love it? Families who want a place that remembers you need services — from roof restorers to counsellors — and a pub that had the decency to put the kids’ playground where you can see it.

Waterford West: we know your terraces, your directories and your 18 Loganlea Road. Charming in a useful sort of way.
