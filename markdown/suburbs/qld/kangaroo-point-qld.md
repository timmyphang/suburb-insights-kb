# Kangaroo Point (QLD)

Slug: `kangaroo-point-qld`

Back in the day we’d roll out of bed, grab a croissant from Christian Jacques Bakery and pretend the climb up the KP stairs was a coffee-fuelled epiphany rather than punishment. We watched the sun melt into the river from the Kangaroo Point Cliffs, counted the climbers on the rocks, and felt smug about our lunchtime views while the city scrambled across the river.

We used to boast about a weird little ecosystem of places: Medley’s verandas, Sea Legs Brewing Co. in its old boxing-gym-turned-brewery glory, The Prawnster on a refurbished trawler at Dockside Marina, and Banter Bar & Seafood with its breezy deck — all the sorts of spots Broadsheet and Urban List once tattled on with envy. Urban List even called us “loved by cardio junkies, real estate moguls and rock climbers alike,” which we framed as a compliment and as evidence that we don’t have bad taste, just selective endurance.

There was also the much-hyped bridge talk. Designers revealed plans for a single-mast, cable-stayed green bridge with a viewing platform, and Concrete Playground reported the new Kangaroo Point Bridge opening to pedestrians and cyclists on December 15, 2024 — pause points, seating, solar cover, and even promises of overwater dining. Remember the headlines about Stilts and the Tassis Group’s Bombora and Mulga Bill’s taking up residence on bridge-level hospitality? We do. It felt like we’d finally got a proper excuse to wear linen and look contemplative.

Petty drama? We had art-world mischief — Street artist Lister getting nabbed after some graffiti business at Kangaroo Point — proof we can be cultured and chaotic in the same tide.

Who would hate it here? If you loathe stairs, sunsets, rooftop bars, or the idea that a brewery used to be a boxing gym, Kangaroo Point will give you a bad week. Who’d love it? If you’re into riverside walks, climbing, a weekend that involves bread, beer and a view, or you think an overwater restaurant is peak civilisation — that’s us. We’re the suburb that wears sunburn like a badge and calls the Story Bridge “our backdrop.”
