# Algester (QLD)

Slug: `algester-qld`

Back in the day we’d joke that Algester was the suburb you accidentally ended up in after missing a turn off the motorway — and the Algester Community Directory used to lean into that for us. The directory (yes, that very page) listed our postcode 4115 and that neat little 3.6 km² of homes tucked in the ward of Calamvale under Brisbane City Council, in the federal electorate of Rankin. We know our borders: Acacia Ridge, Calamvale, Larapinta, Pallara, Parkinson, Sunnybank Hills and Willawong — pick your escape route.

Weekends used to mean one of two things: pick up the kids from Algester State School (it was listed in the directory, with the usual contact details) or wander down to 41 Endiandra St, where Algester Sports Inc was described as a community club that offered two outdoor greens, free coaching for new bowlers and barefoot bowls by arrangement. Yes, barefoot bowls — the suburb’s unofficial uniform of socks-and-pride.

There’s no scandal here in the files we have; the local pages are refreshingly practical. If you despise low-key communal life and the idea of a suburb mapped in a directory rather than an influencer grid, you’d hate it here. If you’re after somewhere unapologetically suburban — a compact patch with a school on the books, a community club that once promised coaching, and neighbours who know where you live — you’ll feel very much at home. For years the directory kept the list; for years we kept the pace.
