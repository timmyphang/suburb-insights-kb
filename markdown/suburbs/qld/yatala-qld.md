# Yatala (QLD)

Slug: `yatala-qld`

Back in the day we’d time our weekend like it was a sacred ritual: breakfast at Yatala Pies, then a pilgrimage to the Drive‑In, and an argument in the car about whether Fast & Furious counts as cinema. All the write‑ups we can find are from earlier coverage, so this is a nostalgia roast, not a hard‑sell.

We’ll admit it — we live for the theatre of it. Yatala Pies (48 Old Pacific Highway) was the sort of place that earned its own fanfare: around 130 years old, reportedly shifting about 3,500 pies a day, and famous enough to be called a Q150 icon. Time Out and Urban List loved reminding visitors that steak, bacon & cheese pies, jam doughnuts and caramel tarts were not merely food but pilgrimage items. We’d queue like it was a religious order.

And then there’s the Yatala Drive‑In, our retro altar. Concrete Playground, Broadsheet and Junkee all gave it the star treatment — Valentine’s nostalgia nights screening 10 Things I Hate About You, Clueless/Mean Girls double features, and yes, a Fast & Furious marathon where the soundtrack was more revs than romance. The drive‑in even used to host the Boot Bargains Market on the third Sunday of the month at 100 Stapylton Jacobs Well Rd, Stapylton — because nothing says Sunday like cheap bric‑a‑brac and neon movie posters.

Local infrastructure gives good gossip too: Pedestrian TV once noted the “jumble of lights” on the M1 near the big BP in Yatala, and there was that time Debbie turned the pie shop into an underwater exhibit. We’ve got quite the backstory for a suburb that’s technically “not Brisbane” but still eats like one.

Who’d hate it here? People who can’t handle queues, retro kitsch or impromptu markets. Also anyone who prefers their motorway signage logical.

Who’d love it? Pie pilgrims, nostalgia romantics and bargain hunters who think a diner milkshake is a philosophical stance. We’re proud of our oversized pie, our outdoor cinema nights and the many articles that once celebrated them — even if, for now, we’re mostly a collection of fond headlines and great stories from back in the day.
