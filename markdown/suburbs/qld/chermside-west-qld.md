# Chermside West (QLD)

Slug: `chermside-west-qld`

Back in the day we’d start the weekend like proper locals: sneakers on, kids in tow, and a route that reads like a nature lover’s scavenger hunt. We’d wander through Chermside Hills Reserve at 412 Trouts Rd or duck into Raven Street Reserve (the main entrance on 815 Rode Rd) for that “you’re-almost-in-the-bush” feeling without leaving the postcode. Time Out used to gush about those gravelly paths and boardwalks — and honestly, who are we to argue?

After the walk there was the eternal local debate: Huxtable Park’s rainforest walk or John Goss Reserve for the picnic gear? The mums we know (Social Mums Club has had a go at organising the lot of us) could plan a week’s worth of meetups before our coffees even cooled. Speaking of coffee, Silky Oak Espresso used to be our tiny beacon at 114 Stafford Road, doing Toby’s Estate for the people who liked their caffeine with a side of suburbia.

When we needed to prove we could be civilised, Westfield Chermside was our cultural high ground — Kingpin on Level 3 for strikes and arcade glow, Sushi Jiro and El Camino Cantina in the food precinct if we wanted to pretend we were out for something exotic. Urban List even put El Camino and a Twelve Boar address on Rode Rd on roundups once, so yes, we’ve had ribs and margaritas in the same week. Those listings felt like national validation for our corner of north-Brisbane.

Petty drama? Our biggest quarrel is which street to recommend from the driving map — Dalkeith, Graymond or Board Street — and whether the pre-prep at West Chermside C&K still does that legendary play-based thing it’s been doing for decades. Sitchu calls us “peaceful” and “leafy”; we’ll accept that medal, but only if you promise not to call us boring.

Who would hate it here: anyone who needs a rooftop bar on every corner or believes “walkable” means CBD-adjacent. Who would love it: families, walkers, people who like their playgrounds plentiful and their shopping centres indecently convenient. We’re the suburbs that do simple very, very well — and we know exactly which reserve to meet you at.
