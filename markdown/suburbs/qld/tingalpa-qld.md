# Tingalpa (QLD)

Slug: `tingalpa-qld`

We’re the sort of people who judge a Saturday by how many plastic swords the kids can wrestle from The Jungle Adventure Play before coffee. Back in the day, our weekend rhythm used to look like this: drop-off at the Warehouse of Whimsy (The Jungle Adventure Play — yes, the place with the 16-hole jungle mini-golf and a cocktail bar at 31 Proprietary Street), then a caffeinated regroup at Mickle Pickle Espresso on Wynnum Road, followed by a tactical detour to Pawpaw Asian Kitchen at 1534 Wynnum Road when the hangry threat level hit red. Broadsheet even remembers Pawpaw’s humble market-stall origins — we do too, because we’ve been hauling home their curry paste since someone thought suburban spice was a good idea.

We’re proud of our slightly chaotic attractions. Concrete Playground once wrote about The Hawk — a 140-metre, eight-metres-up electric flying fox — and yes, we still tell visitors it’s “like the Myer Centre’s dragon coaster got a job in logistics.” BOUNCE’s Tingalpa trampoline park and the Jungle’s grown-up mini-golf nights are where locals go to remind themselves they can still compete for a plastic trophy after 30.

There’s not much local tabloid drama to harvest here (and we’re fine with that), so the petty bits are mostly arguments over who took the last halloumi chips at Lemoni Greek Cuzina on Wynnum Road. For the record, people have been praising Lemoni’s brisket pastitsio and honeyed halloumi; Sitchu and Urban List have the receipts.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who needs a sparkling high-street with twenty-four-hour neon and artisan everything. People who can’t handle warehouse-turned-funplexes, who scoff at flying foxes, or who think a 16-hole jungle putt is peak embarrassment will find Tingalpa a study in mild chaos.

Who’ll love it? Families who like options (karate classes, pottery workshops, trampolines, and a licensed mezzanine restaurant at the Jungle), people who enjoy Wynnum Road’s eclectic strip, and anyone nostalgic for a place that’s content to be a little bit rough around the edges and a lot of fun. And if you ever see us queuing for Pawpaw paste — don’t be shy. We’ll save you a slice of halloumi.
