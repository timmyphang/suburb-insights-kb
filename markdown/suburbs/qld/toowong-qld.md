# Toowong (QLD)

Slug: `toowong-qld`

We’re midway through a Saturday on Sherwood Road — coffee in one hand, eye on the horizon where Toowong Central is trying very hard to be the new main event. As Urban List put it, that 1.4-hectare project along Sherwood, High and Jephson is meant to give us a fresh food market, specialty shops and generous public space. We’ll be watching that heritage two-storey on High Street like it’s the finale of a reality show.

Our weekend loop is a peculiar mixtape: kids off to Perrin Park (56 Josling Street) for the pedal-powered cars and mini bike track, a detour to the Mt Coot-tha Botanic Gardens at 152 Mount Coot-tha Road when the weather’s polite, then a quick stop at French Patisserie on Morley Street — Urban List crowned it one of Brisbane’s best cake shops in 2026, and we’ll defend their éclairs like it’s civic duty.

We’re sentimental for the older spots too. Broadsheet used to write about Riser Bread’s rise into a Toowong Queenslander and Pitch and Fork was once the minimalist sunny cafe at Wests Bulldogs. Josie North and the Corner Store Cafe appeared in plenty of reviews; they’re part of the lore even if those write-ups belong to an earlier era. Nowhere Espresso was noted as a dog-friendly nook tucked away from the hubbub — the sort of place you’d take your mutt to judge other locals.

Petty local drama? We’ve had our fair share — Pedestrian TV reported that Mariosarti’s owner, Daniel Milos, was connected to a significant police investigation. It’s the sort of chapter that reminds us Toowong isn’t just river sunsets and cake queues.

Who would hate living here? If you crave full anonymity, zero festival chatter and pubs without stories, you’ll be frustrated. The Regatta Hotel is a riverfront institution — Sitchu assures us everyone’s got a tale from a Regatta afternoon — and now there’s Death & Co at 543A Coronation Drive, adding a new late-night note under the hotel.

Who would love it here? Families who live for Perrin Park and Anzac Park, plant people who worship Mt Coot-tha, cake devotees heading to French Patisserie, and anyone ready for the Toowong Central makeover. We’ll keep our tea warm and our opinions sharp while the suburb keeps getting itself together — beautifully messy, reliably local, and unapologetically Toowong.
