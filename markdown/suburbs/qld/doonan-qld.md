# Doonan (QLD)

Slug: `doonan-qld`

Back in the day — or at least in every article we could dig up (spoiler: there aren’t any recent ones) — Doonan presented like Noosa’s quietly smug cousin who wears a linen shirt to the farmers’ market. We live where the hinterland leans in and says, “Calm down, coast,” and mornings used to look like this: a slow drive past Nyell Road to drop the kids at Noosa Pengari Steiner School (86 Nyell Road), then a detour to whatever roastery had glass windows and very serious beans — Flying West Coffee Roasters was one of those hidden gems on the edge of town.

Weekends felt like a brochure. The Doonan — that sprawling garden-lined place at 6 Beddington Road with multiple beer gardens, a pizzeria, an ice creamery and a cellar door — was the sort of venue people wrote feature pieces about. Sitchu even noted a Lime & Yuzu Spritz on the menu, which we all pretended not to Instagram. There were also stay options that made us smug: Airbnbs such as ‘Doonan | Sleeps 8’ and the Eclectic Oasis, a two‑bedroom spot rammed with hammocks and board games, perfect for pretending we’d “escaped”.

We should admit: our media footprint is charmingly old-school. Time Out once called the Noosa hinterland — including Doonan — “a peaceful, green escape.” Which is fair. We hold that peace dear. It also means our biggest civic controversy is whether to plant six thousand native trees at the Doonan Planting Festival (yes, that festival was promoted with BUPA and Landcare Australia in the mix), not who got trolled on Facebook.

Who would hate living here? If you despise quiet roads, community planting, and venues that encourage lingering over a spritz, you’ll feel trapped. If you need neon and nightlife, Doonan will sigh politely and recommend you head to the coast.

Who would love it? Families, weekend escape artists, people who rate a proper roastery and a garden‑filled beer garden above a shopping mall. We’re low-key, green, and full of venues and stays that the internet once loved — and maybe still does, if you know where to look.
