# Redbank Plains (QLD)

Slug: `redbank-plains-qld`

We know the sound of a jacaranda dropping its purple confetti here — it’s our unofficial weather alarm. Mornings in Redbank Plains used to look like this: we’d shuffle out with leashes in hand (Time Out once named us one of Queensland’s most pet-packed suburbs), count the fur, and try to remember which of us is supposed to feed whom. If you’d asked Urban List back then they’d tell you Charles Roman at BFT Redbank Plains, 321 Redbank Plains Road, was our local fitness hype man — DJ playlist included — and yes, we pretended not to notice he made burpees sound fun.

For summers we leaned on the Brisbane City Council’s big cheap-dip idea — when the council discounted entry to its 22 public pools it felt like the whole place had a collective inflatable ring. Concrete Playground even noted attendance spikes at a few council pools; we were quietly proud to be part of that splash pack, even if we never made it to the cool-toned Instagram corners of Brisbane.

There isn’t much petty local drama in the clips we’ve been quoted in — which is probably a mercy. Instead, our reputation is quietly domestic: lots of paws, a gym with personality, and a suburban calendar that looks after its own. It’s not flashy; it’s the sort of place where community beats curated content.

Who would hate living here? If you crave artisanal everything, boutique pretence or a postcode that doubles as a lifestyle brand, you’ll be underwhelmed. If you need constant bright-city theatre, this isn’t your stage.

Who would love it? Pet people, families, anyone who appreciates a gym that doubles as morale support and a summer where the council’s cheap pool entry once made you feel like you’d won a civic lotto. We’re the suburb that doesn’t try too hard — except when jacarandas drop, the dogs go feral, and we all, inexplicably, decide burpees are a group hobby.
