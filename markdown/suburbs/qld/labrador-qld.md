# Labrador (QLD)

Slug: `labrador-qld`

We start our weekends like everyone else in Labrador: pretending we’re on holiday while the ferry-less Broadwater laps at our sandals. Back in the day, the guides loved to drop by — Broadsheet called the Gold Coast “nostalgic” and “laid-back” — and honestly, that fits us: small, salty, and suspiciously proud of our foreshore.

By mid-morning we’re on Marine Parade. Urban List once sang praises for that extra-wide foreshore path — gym equipment, room for the pram, room for the dog that thinks it’s a sea lion — and Charis Seafoods on Marine Parade used to be the place everyone queued at for fish and chips with Broadwater views. If we were feeling fancy (and by fancy we mean reliable and unpretentious), Casa Mexicana on Frank Street was the old-school spot you’d point to when someone asked for “authentic” without the attitude. And yes, Daark Espresso at 41 Musgrave Ave got the tick for dog-friendliness — oversized puppuccinos and a wrap-around deck that basically put Labradors on the VIP list.

Petty local drama? The data we have doesn’t offer any juicy council rumbles or barista wars, so we’ll keep it real: our scandals are mostly about who parks where on a Sunday and whether the jogger taking the whole path is actually training for something or just very committed to being inconvenient.

Who would hate living here: people who loathe dogs (this town is dog-centric enough that a puppuccino scandal could start a coup), anyone allergic to slow mornings, and those who insist every suburb must have a skyline selfie opportunity. Who would love it here: dog owners, people who like walking routes that don’t end in traffic, and anyone who enjoys a solid, no-frills seafood counter or a cosy, low-key Mexican feed.

Heads up — these shout-outs come from older guides and lists, so consider this more nostalgic local love than a current menu. Still, Labrador’s mood hasn’t changed much: we’re breezy, slightly bemused, and quietly proud of our little slice of Broadwater.
