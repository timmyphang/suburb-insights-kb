# North Lakes (QLD)

Slug: `north-lakes-qld`

We know this place like the back seat of a Prius — sticky cup-holder, kids’ sticker on the window and all. On a Saturday morning in North Lakes we used to orbit Westfield North Lakes (Centre Court, corner Anzac Avenue and North Lakes Drive), juggling Santa bookings and negotiating who gets the gummy snakes. Back in the day Urban List even said the suburb had “exploded onto the foodie scene” — and honestly, we’ve got the receipts: KatsuCo, Blaktan, Kinn Thai and Bulgogi all made the rounds in local round-ups, and The Yiros Shop went so far as to open a drive-thru so you didn’t even have to undo the baby carrier to get feta fries.

We watched grand plans arrive on glossy renderings — Urban List wrote about a $180 million town centre slated to bring a 130-room five‑star resort, a luxury lagoon pool and boutique retail. It sounded like the sort of thing you’d put on a vision board and then immediately lose behind the shopping list. IKEA was even scheduled to trial a Sustainable Living Shop here in April 2023, which felt very North Lakes: practical aspiration with bonus flat-pack optimism.

Our leisure credentials are minorly ridiculous — Concrete Playground noted Hey Caddy opened in North Lakes in 2022 (yes, mini‑golf inspired by the suburb itself), and writers have since included it among Brisbane’s themed-putt venues. For the thrift hunters among us, Sitchu flagged Lifeline Shop North Lakes at 1B/109 Flinders Parade as an op-shop worth the treasure hunt. And if you’re a new mum trying to keep someone sane between naps, Raising Mamas, Mama Tribe and Keeping Mum listed North Lakes groups and sessions around places like The Jungle Play Centre at 2 McLennan Ct — the kind of local village that actually saves you.

Who would hate it here? People who loathe kids’ birthday chaos, themed mini‑golf and the sound of prams on a Sunday morning. Who would love it? Young families, bargain stylists trawling Lifeline’s fairy-tale shelves, and anyone who enjoys a suburb that promises five-star dreams while still selling yiros through a car window.

Yes, we know what North Lakes was meant to become. We also know where to find the best second‑hand armchair while we wait.
