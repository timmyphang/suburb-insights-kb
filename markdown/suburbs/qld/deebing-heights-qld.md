# Deebing Heights (QLD)

Slug: `deebing-heights-qld`

For years we’ve watched the Brisbane Kids printable Ipswich driving maps turn our streets into a seasonal pilgrimage — the kind of fame you only get from being plotted on a PDF. Back in the day those maps pointed visitors to 3 Lomandra Street, Piccadilly Court and Pintail Circuit like holy sites, and yes, we learned to queue politely for other people's festive ambition.

We know who decorates: the snippet that won't shut up is 6–8 Newsky Court — allegedly over 30,000 LEDs synchronised to music, with themed songs from Bluey to Frozen. It’s the sort of suburban choreography that makes you either well up or roll your eyes, depending on how many mince pies you had for dinner. We drive past, all of us pretending we're not timing the light changes on our phones.

If you’re the type who hates a mapped-out neighbourhood attraction, who would loathe being on a printable Christmas-light route PDF, then Deebing Heights will try your patience. We are compact, earnest and very good at turning our cul-de-sacs into Saturday-night spectacles.

But if you love community theatre played out in tinsel and LEDs, if you delight in a GPS that says “turn left” and discover another house going full Las Vegas, you’ll adore it here. We’re the postcode that made the mum-blog map and kept a sense of humour about it. Come for the lights, stay for the polite applause as you drive by — and yes, bring snacks for the queue.
