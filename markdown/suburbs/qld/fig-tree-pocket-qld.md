# Fig Tree Pocket (QLD)

Slug: `fig-tree-pocket-qld`

Back in the day we’d tell visitors to bring river shoes and patience — Fig Tree Pocket is the kind of place where the wildlife gets centre stage and the late‑night scene gets a pass. Full disclosure: our reading pile for this roast is all pre-2024 stuff, so consider this more “remember when” than a live events guide.

Mornings used to look like this: the kids would be dragged to the boat‑themed playground at Fig Tree Pocket Riverside Reserve for uninterrupted river views while someone tried to get a picnic blanket past a flock of lorikeets. If we were feeling fancy we’d slip over to Biami Yumba Park (30 Kenny Street) for the lagoon and fort playground that Brisbane kids blogs couldn’t stop raving about. And of course the place that really puts us on the map — Lone Pine Koala Sanctuary at 708 Jesmond Road — loomed large in every itinerary, with Time Out and others noting “way more than just koalas” (over 130 of them, apparently) plus kangaroo feedings and platypus keeper talks. Concrete Playground flagged a change in the routine too — koala holds ended and a new close‑up experience was introduced from September 1, 2024, so the cuddling era is officially in our rear‑view.

Petty local drama? We’ve had our headlines. Pedestrian TV reported a home here was raided in a $750k cocaine and ice bust — yes, that one — which made the neighbourhood group chat briefly very awake.

If you’re a uni student chasing karaoke dens and cheap late‑night eats, you’ll probably sneer and move to St Lucia or Indooroopilly, which Urban List describes as the playgrounds for UQ kids and their appetite for ramen and arcade antics. Sitchu also noted Fig Tree Pocket in property chatter — rents around here saw big bumps (up to about 20%), so you’ll pay for the leafy peace.

Who would hate it here: lovers of buzz and neon — you’ll feel like you’re living in a calming screensaver. Who would love it: families, bird nerds, anyone with a soft spot for gum trees and koalas, and people who think river views beat nightclub queues every time. We’ll keep the barbies off the footpath and the lorikeets on the powerlines — you’re welcome.
