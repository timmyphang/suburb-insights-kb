# Hendra (QLD)

Slug: `hendra-qld`

We start our Saturdays at T.C. Beirne Park — the locals’ “old faithful” — where the jacarandas do their drama and the playground genuinely ticks all the boxes (brisbanemumsgroup will tell you so). One of us chases toddlers, the other scouts a shady bench and pretends we’re outdoorsy.

If you want to trace Hendra’s personality, follow the coffee smell back to Gerler Road. As Broadsheet noted, Wolff Coffee Roasters made a lot of noise here — on‑site roasting at 140 Gerler Road, nitro taps, training at the Wolff College of Coffee — and Concrete Playground even staged a Specialty Coffee Festival at the Wolff warehouse once upon a time. Then there’s Dandelion & Driftwood: Broadsheet and Concrete Playground remind us it helped put specialty coffee on the map in 2010, then returned to the strip in 2021 in a renovated Queenslander with high teas and house blends. Those were the days we bragged about to interstate visitors.

For kids who need to be tired out before 9am, Urban Xtreme used to be the go‑to — trampolines, parkour, ninja courses — all the things you’ve seen in Urban List copy. And if you once needed wings and beer, Concrete Playground pointed you to Chook Wings and Beer on 49 Gerler Road when it opened.

We don’t do drama here — what passes for scandal is whether Saabi on Manson was a “little gem” (brisbanemumsgroup liked it) or whether the florist has fresher roses today than yesterday. Poco Posy, listed by Sitchu, still runs a same‑day delivery and you can collect from their Hendra unit at 2/26 Navigator Place — yes, we will send one of us on that errand and make the other feel guilty.

Who would hate Hendra? Nightlife hunters and anyone who needs a 24/7 city buzz will find our pace quaintly stubborn. Who would love it? Families who value shade and sane parks, coffee obsessives who remember (and miss) the Wolff festivals, and anyone who appreciates a suburb that quietly keeps its own calendar of good cafés, florists like Alba Roses, and parks — no neon required. We’re Hendra: quietly particular, mildly caffeinated, and suspiciously fond of our jacarandas.
