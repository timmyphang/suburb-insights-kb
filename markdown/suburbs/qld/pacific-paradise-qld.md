# Pacific Paradise (QLD)

Slug: `pacific-paradise-qld`

We live in Pacific Paradise, QLD — yes, that’s the name on the mailbox and no, we didn’t get to pick it, but we do enjoy the irony. Mornings here are us, the surf, and the ritual of checking the MumsPages listings to see which local activity tonight’s logistics will allow. For years the MumsPages directory has been where parents actually find things — the internet’s quieter cousin that quietly knows where the kinder is.

Back in the day Urban List would turn up with glossy lists about Queensland beachside resorts and the Gold Coast’s best restaurants, name-dropping places like Green Island Resort and the theatrical teppanyaki joints over the border. We read those articles like postcards from someone who went on holiday and forgot to send us the bill. If Urban List was curating your next island fantasy, Time Out was supplying the mood board — their pieces on South Pacific islands and “Pacific paradise” cinema made us feel simultaneously smug and undercaffeinated (the film reviews were a whole other vibe).

Who’d hate living here? If you need your weekend to look like an illuminated brochure — the kind Urban List serves up for Byron Bay dinners at Raes — you’ll find us delightfully unprogrammed. If theatrical chefs, reservation lists and the kind of dining that doubles as entertainment are non-negotiables, try the Gold Coast; they’ve got the teppanyaki energy we politely admire from afar.

Who would love it here? People who like their paradise with a side of reality: the families who actually use MumsPages, the folks who appreciate proximity to coastal life without being a permanent entry in a travel roundup, and anyone who enjoys being within shouting distance of island guides and restaurant lists without being in the spotlight.

We’re not hiding; we’re just not auditioning. Come for the name, stay because you realised paradise doesn’t always need to perform — sometimes it just quietly answers that MumsPages ad and sorts out the school pick-up.
