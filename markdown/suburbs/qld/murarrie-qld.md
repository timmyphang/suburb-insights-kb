# Murarrie (QLD)

Slug: `murarrie-qld`

Back in the day we’d know Murarrie had arrived not by a shiny café window but by the refrigeration hum — as Broadsheet noted, BrewDog’s coldroom was less a storeroom and more a giant refrigerated warehouse. We used to park ourselves where the river stares back at the Gateway Bridge, gawking at the 420-seat DogTap, the keg feature wall and the view that made industrial suburbs look like fancy postcards.

Our weekend ritual used to be embarrassingly simple: push the kids towards the Colmslie Beach Reserve playground at 152 Colmslie Rd (the giant concrete fish and mock shipwreck were always a hit), then shuffle over to 77 Metroplex Avenue for a riverside beer at BrewDog DogTap — 28 taps, trivia on a Tuesday and, in peak weirdness, a contactless beer drive-through during the coronavirus era. Concrete Playground even remembered the time they did a barkuterie board for dogs. Yes, our dogs were bougie before we were.

We used to boast about the brewing corridor: BrewDog in town, Revel Rivermakers “just up the road” and suggestions for an eastside brewery crawl. Benz On Miller popped up in lists too, because nothing says suburban renaissance like a burger that got noticed beyond its food truck days. And for those chasing zen, Sitchu listed sound healing at 16 Queensport Road South — Murarrie had its industrial-hipster double act sorted.

There’s no petty council saga here in our files — mostly press pieces and family guides — so the local drama is low-key: closing the playground for upgrades (they mentioned work through June 2023), and, if you squint, an underdog suburb slowly being photographed from the river.

Who would hate living here? People after laneway glamour and boutique laneway lights might sigh at our scale — giant coldrooms, big taprooms and barbecue-friendly parking. Who would love it? Beer pilgrims, dog owners who appreciate permitted indoor access, families who still think climbing on a giant fish is cultural enrichment, and anyone who enjoys an industrial riverside that accidentally became cool.

Most of our clippings are from “back in the day,” so if you’re looking for right-now recs, consider this a nostalgia tour. We wear it proudly.
