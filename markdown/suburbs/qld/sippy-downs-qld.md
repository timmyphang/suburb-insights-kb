# Sippy Downs (QLD)

Slug: `sippy-downs-qld`

Back in the day — or at least in the pre-2024 notes the internet left lying around — we were the sort of suburb that proudly advertised a three-level tree canopy tower and a mega slide like they were Michelin stars.

We’re the ones who know Sippy Downs Forest Park by the squeal radius: shaded meadows, raised boardwalks, daybed-style seating, three playgrounds and barbecues. For years mums and dads wrote it up as the local picnic shrine, and yes, the park’s got lighting, public amenities and a drinking fountain if you’re pretending this is a camping trip. The Sunshine Coast Mums were very pleased.

Weekends used to look like this: we’d herd the ankle-biters to Kids World Adventure Indoor Playland at 8 Fairfax St for multi-level mayhem and a ball pit, then attempt to restore our spines at Live Pilates on 123 Sippy Downs Dr — reformers and premium fit-out, apparently — before collapsing into the warm, corporate embrace of Sippy Social, which Urban List said had quite the loyal following. Occasionally adult life intervened and we’d remember Chancellors Tavern at 20 Chancellor Village Blvd was open on Christmas Day 2023, because nothing says festive like a pub with a postcode.

We even had cultural moments: remember when Girls Kicking Goals screened at the venue on 90 Sippy Downs Drive? That ticked our #family box and made us feel like a town that does community cinema, not just stacked playgrounds.

There’s also the ubiquitous community notice board — the Find It Locally pages — where every lost pet, yoga class and local tradie once lived in glorious bulletin-board eternity. If you love reading listicles about indoor playgrounds and new eateries (Junk Sippy Downs opened a second Sunshine Coast venue, per Urban List), this is your parish.

Who would hate living here? People who crave 24/7 nightlife, artisanal melancholy and streets that never see a toddler with face paint. Who would love it? Families, picnic obsessives, Pilates people who like reformers, and anyone who wants a suburb that’s quietly proud of its playground towers.

We may read like an older guidebook, but that’s fine — we’re happy being the suburb you visit when you want clean bathrooms, a mega slide and a sensible community notice board.
