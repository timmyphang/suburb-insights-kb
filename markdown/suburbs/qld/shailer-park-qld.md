# Shailer Park (QLD)

Slug: `shailer-park-qld`

Back in the day we used to joke that Shailer Park’s entire nightlife was a well-lit playground and a thoughtfully listed realtor page. For years the local mum blogs raved about our green bits — Shailer Pioneer Park with its skate park, basketball and tennis courts, playground and flying fox; Homestead Park tucked next to a creek and trees; and Roselea Park with slides and a pirate-ship that keeps the small humans occupied for literal hours. We know them all by the squeak of the swings.

Weekends here are a choreography of drop-offs and detours: the kids to the flying fox, someone to the picnic table with the toilet conveniently nearby, someone else pretending to exercise on the park equipment. If you squint you can almost convince yourself you're at a destination suburb — Sitchu once even had a listing for 5/398 Chatswood Road, so at least the internet thinks we’re a place you can pin to a map.

Back when Urban List did round-ups, they sneaked us into their climbing guide — Core Climbing Cornubia at 146 Bryants Rd gets a shout for being “technically outside the Gold Coast but close enough to mention.” That’s our long-distance relationship with clout: we’re always invited to the party but told to stand by the punch bowl.

There’s no scandal to report (the articles didn’t give us one), so instead we’ll admit our charm is low-key and unapologetic. Who would hate it here? Anyone who demands artisanal everything, or likes their weekend to involve more queues and less elbow room. If you loathe playgrounds, creeks and reliable, slightly suburban predictability, you will not be pleased.

Who would love it here? Families who want parks that actually work, mums and dads who read Parentopia and want local groups, and anyone who values a skate ramp and a pirate ship over pretence. We’re quietly practical, fond of trees, and perfectly happy being the suburb that turned up to the internet’s party wearing thongs and a thermos.
