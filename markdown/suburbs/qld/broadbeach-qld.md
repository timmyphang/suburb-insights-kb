# Broadbeach (QLD)

Slug: `broadbeach-qld`

Back in the day we used to brag that Broadbeach was the grown‑up bit of the Coast — not quite Surfers Paradise’s bling, more “the casino” with manners. We still say it with a shrug and a grin as we walk The Esplanade, past the Broadbeach Cultural Precinct where Bleach Festival shows used to spill into Ed Hardy Park and the foreshore like cultured tidewater.

Our weekend routine looked like this: queue for takeaway pastries from the Brooki Bakehouse flagship on Surf Parade when it opened, mosey into Pacific Fair to catch the new IMAX at Event Cinemas (yes, they did launch it on December 19, 2024 — we saw Mufasa and Moana 2 in jaw‑dropping laser), then meet at 17 Albert Ave where Meltemi Mediterrania once convinced us the Aegean was only a short drive away. On a good week we’d spy the Isoletto Pool Club up at The Star Gold Coast, pretending we belonged on level six.

We’ve watched the precinct shuffle — Miss Moneypenny’s/Fat Freddy’s got a makeover and Cora and The Beach Hotel Broadbeach moved in under one roof, promising long summer lunches and fewer legacy neon signs. Time Out even noted Ciccio’s Pizza and Gelato — the place that had its chef win Best Classic Pizza — as part of our quietly fierce dining scene. Blues on Broadbeach and the marathon routing through our streets reminded us we’re both festival and finish‑line.

Petty grievance? We do mourn the little independent corners that get elbowed aside when a new hotel blows a wind of linen curtains through the suburb. And if you hate people who treat a Sunday like a soft‑shell, gastro‑event, you’ll hate living here.

But who would love it? Food lovers, festival folks, mums looking for community (shout‑out to the Social Mums Club and Broadbeach Mummas), and anyone who appreciates that we play second fiddle to the glitz on purpose — we’re the Coast that reads programmes, books an IMAX ticket and still finds time for an espresso and a sea breeze. We’ll keep the table by the window.
