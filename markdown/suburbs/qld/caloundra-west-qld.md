# Caloundra West (QLD)

Slug: `caloundra-west-qld`

Morning in Caloundra West and we’re the kind of locals who can point out 9 Caloundra Road with our eyes closed — Rollerdrome, which Urban List once noted flips from rollerskating arena into an icy rink during summer. It’s the kind of flexible suburb feature only we appreciate: skating one minute, skating again in a different temperature the next.

Back in the day the travel guides loved to parcel us up with the rest of Caloundra — Urban List name‑checked the Caloundra Street Fair, the Caloundra Mural/Art Trail (Lamkin Lane and Dingle Ave) and the Caloundra Regional Gallery, and Sitchu dutifully scribbled down tidy addresses like 51 Caloundra Road and Beachtree Distilling Co at 14 Industrial Avenue, Caloundra West. It’s a strange, charming combo: arty laneways and an Industrial Avenue with its own spirit (and apparently a distillery).

We brag quietly about community things. The Caloundra Community Centre Inc. runs two neighbourhood centres and, for years, the centre’s been the quiet backbone — playgroups, the grandparents as parents (GAP) program, the Diverse Queensland Workforce and migrant employment support live in their listings. Baringa Community Centre opened mid‑2019 and has been doing that slow, earnest community‑building work while the rest of the region shows up for murals and weekend markets.

Local drama? The files don’t have any juicy feuds — just guides and community programs. So our drama is more subtle: who forgot to wear skates to the Rollerdrome switchover? Who thought Industrial Avenue was just for trucks? Who keeps pretending the mural is a directional sign?

Who would hate living here: people who need rooftop cocktail bars every 100 metres and a tram to the CBD. Who would love it: mural hunters, families who value a community centre that actually runs playgroups, anyone nostalgic for a roller rink that becomes an ice rink (as Urban List noted), and the people who think "51 Caloundra Road" sounds like an address with an interesting story.

We’re not flashy. We’re a postcode that folds industrial, art, community and the occasional seasonal ice rink into one neat, quietly proud mess — and we wouldn’t have it any other way.
