# Griffin (QLD)

Slug: `griffin-qld`

We live in Griffin, QLD — and yes, we have to explain we’re not Lake Burley Griffin, nor the Griffin Theatre Company in Darlinghurst. Back in the day the only Griffins the journals cared about were Canberra’s handsome lake and Sydney’s cantankerous little theatre, not our tidy little postcode. Which, frankly, suits us just fine.

Weekend ritual: we scroll Broadsheet and Concrete Playground for a hit of culture — they’ve been on about the Griffin Theatre Company seasons, Batch Festival at the Stables Theatre and photographic credits to Griffin Theatre Company / Brett Boardman — and we raise a chilled can in solidarity. Time Out keeps wetting itself over Lake Burley Griffin’s GoBoat options and the floating sauna; Urban List trots out the Arboretum and lakeside cafés like Cafe Sosta. We read it all, we chuckle, and then we go back to our own quiet streets where nobody’s reviewed our sunsets (which, between us, are perfectly fine).

Petty local drama? The only scandal we have is being chronically unsexy in the press. As Concrete Playground and Broadsheet kept celebrating Griffin Theatre Company’s seasons in 2025 and 2026, we were left on the cutting-room floor. No complaints — it means our parks stay calm, our footpaths aren’t overrun by influencers, and our streets don’t need queue management.

Who would hate living here: people who require a constant feed of curated cultural clout. If your soul demands nightly write-ups in Time Out or a feature list on Urban List, you’ll wilt. We don’t manufacture festivals for clicks; the nearest headlines about Griffins are about other Griffins and that will drive you mad.

Who’d love it: anyone who likes being off-radar. We get the quiet, the space to invent our own fun, and the smugness of sharing a name with a famous theatre company and a very photogenic lake — while keeping our own life delightfully unreported. If you want applause, go to the Stables Theatre. If you want peace, come to Griffin, QLD — where the only drama is our collective ability to watch other people’s headlines and laugh about it.
