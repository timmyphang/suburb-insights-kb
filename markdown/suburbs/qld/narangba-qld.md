# Narangba (QLD)

Slug: `narangba-qld`

We know the ritual: kids in fluorescent socks at Stone Ridge Central Park until someone collapses on the flying fox, a circuit of Amity Park to test the blue playground’s slide-splash resilience, then the older kids disappear to the Narangba Skate Precinct while we pretend to supervise. For years the local press has been mostly retrospective — there aren’t many fresh takes in the pile we were given, so: back in the day (and in the archives), here’s our love-note-slash-roast.

Our bragging rights are refreshingly specific. The Hideout at 22 Main Street used to be celebrated for "pumping out the feasts of your dreams" — yes, including those weekend banger specials (a Yorkshire roll with sausage and mash rolled in handmade Yorkshire pudding). We’re still digesting the concept. The fitness nuts among us remember PEPT Narangba at 33 Ferrier Road — the mirrorless gym that allegedly has "energy off the charts" and helps you smash goals without checking your hair. Also, fun flex: somebody thought 1 Carmen Court merited a Sitchu listing, which is as aspirational as our cul-de-sacs get.

If you want seasonal spectacle, Time Out once pinned Bendee Court on its 2025 Christmas lights map — our street cred comes with a blinking soundtrack and a navigable driving trail. The suburbs that mock us for being low-key missed the bit where we go hard on choreographed tinsel.

Who would hate living here? If you need rooftop bars, late-night burritos and an inner-city postcode with a name you can shout across a tram, Narangba will irritate you. If your idea of fun is boutique chaos, we’ll seem like a series of well-meaning cul-de-sacs with playgrounds and good intentions.

Who would love it here? Families who treat Walmsley Park’s fenced toilets and slides like national treasures, parents who traipse the mega-park at Stone Ridge, and anyone who measures quality of life by how often the skatepark fills on a weekend. We’ve got parks, community, and one hell of a Yorkshire roll story — and honestly, what more do you need?
