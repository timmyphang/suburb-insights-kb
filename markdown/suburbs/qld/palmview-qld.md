# Palmview (QLD)

Slug: `palmview-qld`

We know Palmview by the sticky little evidence on our shirts and the distant whir of a family ride — back when Urban List was pointing things out. Back in the day they called Strawberry Fields Palmview "iconic" for pick‑your‑own strawberries (an hour’s drive north of Brisbane, if you like telling people how far you had to go for fruit). They also listed Aussie World at 73 Frizzo Rd, Palmview, as the place kids could run wild on mini golf, nature playgrounds and water play while adults practised the fine art of supervising from a bench.

So our Saturday routine, as per the old guides, was simple: brave a fruit patch, pretend the kids are exhausting themselves on the rides at Aussie World, and maybe tack on a scenic loop to the Glass House Mountains because Urban List said it made for a tidy day trip. It’s wholesome, a bit sunburnt, and pleasingly unpretentious.

There’s no salacious council drama in these snippets — which suits us. We don’t need it. If you hate sticky fingers, outdoor birthday parties and drives that end with you explaining how the Glass House Mountains are not actually glass, Palmview will test your patience. If you love a weekend that smells faintly of jam and cheap chips, and you enjoy the reliable chaos of family attractions, you’ll feel right at home.

All of this comes via older Urban List pieces, so we’re being nostalgic more than definitive — a fond roast of the Palmview we know from guidebook days, not a live events bulletin.
