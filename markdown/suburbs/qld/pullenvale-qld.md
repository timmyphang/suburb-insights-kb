# Pullenvale (QLD)

Slug: `pullenvale-qld`

We live in Pullenvale, the sort of place major city glossies glance at while flicking to the next feature. Mornings here are quietly smug — not because we’re featured in any weekend guides, but because the guides tend to be about somewhere else. As Urban List’s “Things To Do In Brisbane” reminds everyone where the festivals, markets and curated events are, we nod, sip our (unlisted) morning beverage and keep our map tucked away.

Back in the day — and still now, if you look — the travel pages prefer the flashy precincts. Urban List runs longreads about Surry Hills and the Gold Coast, Time Out compiles “The 37 Best Places To Visit in Australia” (updated 2026) and even crowning pies in regional Victoria get their own press cycle. Meanwhile Pullenvale gets the respectful, background hum of being quietly liveable rather than headline-worthy. That’s not a complaint; it’s a lifestyle choice.

There’s no petty council saga for us to roast here, because the article pile is more about big-city buzzy picks than our backyard. The data we’ve got talks festivals, top steak lists and best pizza in New York; it does not include a Pullenvale café with a line down the street, or a market stall that got its own feature. So our local drama is low-key and mostly involves not being photographed for a 2026 roundup.

Who hates living here? If you need to be on a curated “best of” list every other Saturday, if your social currency is being seen in a 2026 city-feature, then Pullenvale will feel like patiently waiting offstage. If you crave being the subject of a Time Out explainer or an Urban List roundup, you’ll sulkate (we mean sulk) and move on.

Who loves it here? People who like being politely overlooked by trend pieces. People who enjoy the kind of place that lets national guides keep their spotlights for the obvious shiny bits while we quietly get on with things. If being the suburb the magazines forget sounds like bliss, welcome home — we’ve been quietly perfecting it.
