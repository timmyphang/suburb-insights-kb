# Peregian Springs (QLD)

Slug: `peregian-springs-qld`

We wake up, roll down our street — yes, one of those leafy crescents Sitchu used to gush about — and decide between walking the dog to the beach or pretending we're playing 18 holes on the local championship course. For years the locals have loved that particular indecision; Peregian Springs was literally built for indecision, a master‑planned community where you can choose between clubhouse cocktails and sand between your toes (or at least the idea of both).

Back in the day we’d drift down to Hand of Fatima Peregian Springs for what Urban List called "some of the best coffee on the Sunshine Coast," grab a muffin, and watch every dog in the postcode parade past like royalty. If we were feeling slightly more cosmopolitan, Broadsheet reminds us Mariella Mexicantina sat near Peregian Beach promising margarita beachside vibes, Fairtrade tequila and mezcal, and share‑friendly Mexican street fare — the kind of place we liked to name‑drop when convincing city friends we’d "discovered" the Sunshine Coast.

Time Out used to call nearby Peregian Beach "a friendly, sleepy seaside village," which is generous and accurate — sleepy as in quiet neighbours and the occasional surfboard parked like a lawn ornament. Wikipedia told us that at the 2021 census we were a cosy 9,532 souls, tidily sandwiched between beach and hinterland, which suits us fine.

We don’t pretend everything’s all surf and scones. Pedestrian TV reported bushfires that once razed homes near Peregian Beach and forced evacuations; we remember that seriously, and remember how the community pulled together rather than the gossip doing the rounds. Tripadvisor has kept a steady log of visitors — about 246 reviews — so yes, people do notice us.

Who would hate living here? Nightlife‑seekers and anyone needing a 24/7 snack run; if you crave neon and nonstop action, the sleepy charm will feel like a slow Wi‑Fi signal. Who would love it? Families who relish parks and schools, dog owners who want a proper parade, golfers with a devotion to manicured greens, and anyone who prefers a hammock to a nightclub.

We know our quirks. We own them. And we’ll still pretend our coffee stop was where you were meant to be.
