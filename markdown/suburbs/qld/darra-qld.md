# Darra (QLD)

Slug: `darra-qld`

We roll out of the train at Darra like we own the platform — which, technically, we’ve been doing since the days when Scotts Rd Deli’s banh mi were the secret weekend smuggle into the office. Back in the day the internet wrote love letters about our takeaways: Scotts Road Deli and The Roll Shop used to get name-checked in the same breath as city sandwiches, and Yum Yum Peking Duck (146 Scotts Road) was the sort of place food writers pretended not to adore.

Our Saturday routine used to be gloriously simple: pick up something from Sol Breads’ Darra outpost (they began as a garage dream in West End) and then park ourselves at Ducie Street Park — yes, the one at 58 Ducie Street with the forts and slides that Brisbane Kids called “action-packed.” If the kids weren’t running wild we’d be watching the lights at the Darra BMX Park as riders looped the 640m asphalt track well into the night.

We’re not dramatic people, mostly because our drama is practical: where to get your roast duck or a five-course Peking feast without pretending to be on a “food crawl.” Urban List and Concrete Playground used to put Kim Khanh and Yum Yum on those lists, and Michael Nguyen’s memories of his grandfather’s house in Darra were the sort of small-people history that made Thai Hoa Grocer’s produce racks read like a map of family Sundays.

Who would hate living here? If you need artisanal pretension on every street corner, you’ll be frustrated — we don’t pretend to be everywhere’s fetishised version of “cool.” Who would love it? Families who like proper parks, riders who want a legit BMX track, and anyone who remembers that good food is often found on Scotts Road rather than in a philosophy essay about gentrification.

For years the local listings even mentioned a Darra Community Festival (they had a 2026 listing) and organisations like Darra Community Group Inc on Railway Parade and Northcott Disability Services on Station Avenue were quietly doing the actual community work. We’re not polished. We’re practical. We’re Darra — and we like it.
