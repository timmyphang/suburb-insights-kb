# Bellmere (QLD)

Slug: `bellmere-qld`

Back in the day we used to brag that Bellmere could feed, sweeten and light up a Christmas tour all in one postcode. We know where the good snags came from — The Bellmere Butcher at 65 Bellmere Road even made The Urban List for its specialty sausages, grass‑fed beef, pre‑made meals and a hot box for lunch — which is the kind of humble flex we love. 

On weekends you could pretend you were doing something cultured and nip to the Rivermakers markets in Morningside, where Bellmere Honey used to sell jars alongside Lunar Farms and mushroom stalls  — markets that ran 7am–1pm on a Sunday, if you liked getting up early enough to be smug about it.

We also own Christmas: our festive houses made the Moreton Bay North printable driving maps more than once. If you’ve ever driven past 5 Daintree St, 15 Belbrook Avenue or 5 Parkwood Place on a lights crawl, you know the drill — excessive tinsel, earnest blow‑ups, and neighbours trying to out‑cheer each other.

Who would hate it here? If your weekend centrepiece is a boutique laneway and three cafés arguing about beans, you’ll be restless. We don’t pretend to be inner‑city drama; we’re quieter, handier and full of pockets of weird pride.

Who would love it? Families who appreciate a proper butcher, people who buy local honey, and anyone who likes their suburb to be the sort that ends up on a printable map for Christmas lights. We’re Bellmere: not posh, reliably tasty, and embarrassingly festive.
