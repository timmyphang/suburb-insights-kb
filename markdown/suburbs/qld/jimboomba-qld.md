# Jimboomba (QLD)

Slug: `jimboomba-qld`

We’re the lot who know the back roads by heart and the best excuse to be late is a last‑minute detour to the country. Back in the day Urban List once named Cafe On Cusack Jimboomba in a round‑up of the best country cafes near Brisbane, so yes — we’ve long been bragging rights holders for farm‑fresh breakfasts and baked goods, whether you believe the list or not.

Weekends here are a choose‑your‑own‑adventure: the same guides that tipped us for country pubs also put our patch on itineraries for natural swimming holes around Brisbane, so you’ll find us alternating between pretending we’re roughing it and polishing our Sunday best for the pub. When the weather gets dramatic, we’ve seen Logan City Council open relief centres in Crestmead, Bethania, Jimboomba and Logan Village — so community spirit is practically compulsory.

Who would hate it here? If you need a suburb that only fits inside a glossy pamphlet, or you despise the sound of lawn mowers and honest conversation about the rain, Jimboomba will feel like someone else’s weekend. If you can’t handle mutual aid turning up faster than private contractors, maybe stick to the spreadsheets.

Who’ll love it? People who appreciate country cafes (yes, we were on those lists), lazy river swims, and pubs that feel like you’ve brought the correct amount of enthusiasm. We’re polite, a bit proud, and quietly smug that Urban List once noticed — even if the internet moved on, we didn’t.
