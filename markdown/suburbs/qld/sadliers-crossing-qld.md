# Sadliers Crossing (QLD)

Slug: `sadliers-crossing-qld`

Back in the day we’d time our evenings by the glow — the kind of suburban ritual that earns you a mention on a Brisbane Kids printable driving map. If you live on 24 Thomas Street or 1 Macfarlane St, congratulations: you’re officially part of Ipswich’s Christmas lights estate planning (2022, 2023 and even that 2025 PDF had our names on it). We wear that like a sash.

Our weekend routine used to be simple: pile into the car, grab the Brisbane Kids Ipswich lights PDF and follow the trail of tinsel like it’s sacred GPS. Neighbours who go big on tinsel become minor celebrities; those who prefer tasteful bulbs are thanked for their restraint on community forums that don’t exist in our data but definitely would. The map lists addresses; we supplied the suburb.

There’s no juicy council scandal here — the papers don’t give us one — just the quiet pride of a place that turns up on a printable driving map and embraces it. If you hate being photographed through windshields in a caravan of dads comparing battery-operated reindeers, you’ll hate living here. If you resent being a waypoint for families hunting the next inflatable Santa, move along.

Who will love Sadliers Crossing? Families who find joy in mapped-out traditions, people who consider a PDF-guided cruise part of December, and anyone who likes the idea of a postcode that quietly made the Ipswich Christmas-lights roster. We’re small, a bit luminous and surprisingly committed to being pointed out on Brisbane Kids’ list — and honestly, we wouldn’t have it any other way.
