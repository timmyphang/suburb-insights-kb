# Bethania (QLD)

Slug: `bethania-qld`

Back in the day we’d get off at Bethania train station and clock the Community Centre at about 300 metres – not a skyline, but a good walk if you’re lugging a hall-hire table. We know every step between the platform and the Have A Chat Café, which used to open Monday–Friday and pour out yarns, craft glue and the kind of community energy that doesn’t care about your postcode bragging rights.

Weekends here are low-key in the best way: one of us might be helping set up the large hall for a dance class, another sorting $10 food hampers or dropping off donations. For years the Bethania Community Centre was the thing that kept things ticking — free food during weekdays, a few showers for people getting back on their feet, furniture assistance for those leaving awful situations, and volunteer rosters that read like a small-town Hall of Fame. We say “was” because, full disclosure, our sources are older — so this is more nostalgia than a current events bulletin. Urban List even mentioned Logan City Council opening evacuation centres in Bethania back in flood season, so we know how quickly the centre pivots when the weather gets stroppy.

You might remember the time the Bunnings at Bethania rolled out pop-up vaccination clinics — jabs and snags, Concrete Playground reported — which felt fittingly Aussie and a touch chaotic. And if you like your suburb-name to mean more than a roundabout, our name travels: Bethania Kids does work overseas in Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh and Odisha and even has a North American arm raising funds. Fancy, right?

Who would hate living here? People who want buzz and boutique shops on every corner, or those who judge a place by how many cocktail bars it can name. Also, anyone allergic to committees, community noticeboards and the moral messiness of real neighbourly care.

Who would love it here? Folks who value being known, getting a hand when floods come through, or turning up for a Have A Chat and walking away with a hot cuppa and a plan. We’re not glossy; we’re useful. And honestly, that suits us fine.
