# Kuluin (QLD)

Slug: `kuluin-qld`

Back in the day our Saturday began like an exercise in small‑town joy: we’d amble down Fishermans Rd like it was a parade route and pretend the most exciting thing was a new sourdough drop. As Urban List noted, The Wonky Loaf was the standout new opening in 2023 — slow‑fermented sourdough, a cosy shopfront with a retail section, dog‑friendly courtyard and the kind of takeaway that made you feel slightly smug about your weekend. Yes, we queued. Yes, we judged the loaf.

For years the local listings read like a practical ode: MumsPages kept a tidy catalogue of party and kids’ services so our playdates didn’t devolve into interpretive chaos, while Localsearch mapped out the essentials — Kuluin Automotive (Unit 3, 14 Fishermans Rd) with its sterling five‑star reviews, Neil Mansell Landscape holding the horticultural fort at 39 Fishermans Rd, and Kuluin Clinic (Unit 6, 1 Indiana Pl) quietly promising bandaids and sensible advice. It’s the sort of place where you can track down a tyre, a sand delivery, and a sourdough in under an hour. Efficient, unglamorous, comforting.

If you were hoping for a local scandal, there’s not much to work with in the files — largely directories, bakery praise and mums doing the work of community organisation. Which is to say: our drama is practical. Someone’s ute needed a service, someone else wanted mulch, and a dog stole a crumb of bread. Riveting.

Who would hate it here? If you demand a three‑am nightclub crawl, endless boutiques, or a skyline of glass towers, Kuluin will bore the pants off you. If you need your suburb to announce itself with neon, you’ll be disappointed.

Who would love it? Families who appreciate a decent bakery, dog owners who miss being welcomed, anyone who likes knowing where to get their car fixed or their garden sorted — and mums who rely on tidy directories that actually list party entertainers without judgement. We’re quietly useful, mildly smug about our sourdough, and we know every local business listing like it’s a family member.
