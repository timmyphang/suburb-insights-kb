# Mount Warren Park (QLD)

Slug: `mount-warren-park-qld`

Mount Warren Park, QLD — back in the day our Saturdays sounded less like suburbia and more like a small conference for rattly toys. We are the ones who’d pore over the Playgroup NSW website like it contained the weekend weather forecast, hunting down the nearest playgroup or parent support session so the kids could burn off energy and we could borrow someone else’s parenting plan for an hour.

We know the listings — Playgroup NSW had the lot: age-specific groups, carer-focused sessions, even supports for folks who needed a bit more help. And on the quieter side of the internet, The Mom (yep, MomCo) used to host the Eastern Heights CC meet-up listings that made it easier to pretend we’d planned anything in advance. MOPS International Blog cropped up in our feeds when we needed a pep talk about getting out of the house; it’s the kind of blog that reassures you everyone else packs an extra change of clothes too.

There’s no council scandal here, no nightlife we can roast — our civic life is mostly snack swaps and whether someone remembered sunscreen. The data we’ve got is basically parenting infrastructure, and honestly, that’s our humble flex. We’re not trying to be the next inner-city hotspot; we’re the postcode that organised its communal chaos.

Who would hate living here? Anyone who likes silence between 9am and 3pm on weekends, or who thinks "structured play" should involve four walls and a yoga mat. If you can’t handle negotiated toy ownership, repeated renditions of the same nursery rhyme, or the thrilling administrative burden of booking a session via a postcode search, this will not be your jam.

Who would love it? Families with little ones, carers looking for community, and anyone who values practical, on-the-ground support — because for years the local way to find that was Playgroup NSW, and places like Eastern Heights CC showed up on The Mom as a place to actually meet other humans. We’ll keep bringing the snacks; you bring the patience.
