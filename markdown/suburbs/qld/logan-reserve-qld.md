# Logan Reserve (QLD)

Slug: `logan-reserve-qld`

Back in the day we’d meet at Wicks Reserve with a thermos, a pram that doubles as a storage unit, and the sort of optimism that only parents on a playground have. Playgroup Victoria used to list Logan Reserve Altona and the Montessori Mornings Nature Playgroup — sometimes Picnic Point Reserve got named in the same breath — so weekends felt like a rotating tour of outdoor nooks where the kids investigated mud and we pretended we were outdoorsy.

We know our streets by which directory lists them. MyCommunityDirectory once reminded us we occupy about 15 km² of postcode 4133, wedged comfortably between Buccan, Chambers Flat, Crestmead, Park Ridge, Waterford and Waterford West — which makes for excellent children’s-party logistics and a lifetime supply of detours. The same directory used to point to Logan Reserve SS’s Special Education Program at 369–379 School Road, and even had a listing for the Seventh-day Adventist Church at 558 Logan Reserve Road. Useful if you wanted to know where the morning calm happens or where to send Grandma on a Saturday.

If your roof was playing hard to get, MumsPages had you covered — literally — with a stack of roofing, roof restoration and roofing contractors listed under Logan Reserve. The upside: when it rains we can count contractors instead of pigeons.

We’re not here to promise nightlife or boutique everything. If you need inner‑city convenience, cocktail bars and a yoga studio every block, this isn’t your jam — you’ll be bored and we’ll still be enjoying the quiet. We do, however, relish the kind of place where playgroups and community directories matter, where School Road is a landmark and where neighbours swap roofing recommendations like trading cards.

Who would love it here? Families who like space, people who value local listings over glossy reviews, and anyone who enjoys a community that shows up to a reserve on a Saturday. We know every venue that ever made a page, and that’s our sort of pride. We also know how to find a good patch of grass — and a decent roofer — when the heavens decide to contribute to the gardening.
