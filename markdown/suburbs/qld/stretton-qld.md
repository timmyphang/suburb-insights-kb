# Stretton (QLD)

Slug: `stretton-qld`

We know the quickest way to start a Saturday in Stretton: herd the small people to Calamvale District Park and dare each other up the Sky Walk. We’re talking a 7.5‑metre-high steel‑and‑rope thing with enclosed ‘cabins’ linked by net bridges, followed by the obligatory scream down the 11‑metre slide. As Time Out described it, it’s the closest thing around to a backyard Beyond Thunderdome — and we love it for exactly that reason.

Back in the day — and yes, most of the copy about us is older stuff — the other big moment that made the feeds was a Stretton State College photo on International Women’s Day. Pedestrian.TV reported that the school posted students holding a nappy changing station with dolls, and, predictably, that image drew criticism. We’ll defend the kids’ acting chops but also admit: if you live here long enough you’ll watch a school photo become a national talking point and wonder which one of us is organising the PR blitz.

Who would hate living here? People who don’t like height, rope, or spontaneous parenting-themed controversy. If the idea of climbing into an enclosed cabin 7.5 metres up or explaining an IWD photo to strangers on the internet gives you hives, Stretton is probably not your gig.

Who would love it? Families who want a playground that doubles as a training ground for future adventurers and retirees who enjoy the steady background hum of kids inventing new ways to hurtle down slides. We’re not flashy; we don’t court trendy write-ups every week. Mostly we have a giant playground, a local state college that keeps the culture wars mildly entertained, and a community that treats both with affectionate exasperation.

As locals, we’ll take our Sky Walk and our occasional headline-making school photo over soulless character any day. Come for the slide, stay long enough and you’ll learn how to roast a controversy with a good-natured grin.
