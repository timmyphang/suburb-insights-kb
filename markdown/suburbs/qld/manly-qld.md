# Manly (QLD)

Slug: `manly-qld`

We live where the yachts quietly elbow for space and the horizon is mostly Moreton Bay, not existential dread. Back in the day travel pages called us a weekend escape — Sitchu even reminded everyone we’re “around 30 minutes from Brisbane CBD” — which is handy when you want seaside vibes without committing to a full coastal pilgrimage.

Our morning is slow and marina-minded: we stroll the waterfront, admire vessels of all shapes and sizes (yes, the marina really is centre stage) and, if we’re feeling gourmet, we used to duck into the Manly Boathouse or queue for a slice at Munja Pizza — both of which Urban List once flagged in its Wynnum and Manly roundup. The gossip in those pieces wasn’t about council wars or shocking scandals; it was whether your lunch comes with bay views or extra napkins.

There isn’t much petty local drama in the Brisbane coverage — the fight, if you can call it that, is purely culinary. No rumours here about seafaring celebrities or ferry soap operas: our headlines are restaurant roundups and “worth the drive” lists. Frankly, that suits us fine. We prefer arguments about whose pizza is more honest than arguments about whose parking space is sacred.

Who would hate living here? If you crave a non-stop nightlife, a Corso-style tourist treadmill or a surf break that demands a wetsuit and a prayer, this Moreton Bay version of Manly will feel like a sleepy cousin you keep apologising for. Likewise, anyone who needs constant city grub or 24/7 neon will mutter into their phone about “lack of options” while ordering takeaway.

Who would love it? Boaties, brunchers who like a view with their carbs, people who want seaside weekends without a ferry queue panic, and anyone who enjoys a tightly curated list of places worth visiting — because that’s our headline: low-key, scenic and delicious when the food guides write about us. As Urban List and Sitchu noted, we’re the kind of place you drive to, park by the marina and pretend you’ve escaped the city for an afternoon. We’ll keep the bay; you keep your agenda.
