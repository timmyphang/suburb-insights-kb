# Ashmore (QLD)

Slug: `ashmore-qld`

We like to think of Ashmore as the suburb that can’t decide if it’s a quiet family pocket or a late‑night entertainment zone — and honestly, we love that identity crisis.

Back in the day we’d start a weekend by swinging past Egg Wash Bake Shop for one of those rustic pies Urban List fussed over (yes, we queued like it was a bakery pilgrimage). Later we’d meander to 25 Dominions Rd where Do You Banh Mi was doing heroic things with beef brisket, crunchy crackling and the kind of chilli that makes you remember you’re alive. If we were feeling retro‑countryside, Ashmore on Bridge Street came across like a country cafe with exposed brick and a roaring fireplace — very “we’ll knit you a jumper and feed you stew.”

For the unimpressed uncle, Ashmore also had its modern side. Urban List wrote about Go Bowling at 137 Currumburra Rd — lanes, video entertainment and an 18‑hole mini golf challenge next door — and even a big, glossy indoor golf precinct (X‑Golf Ashmore) that was slated to open on Tuesday 26 November 2024, billed as the 30th X‑Golf in Australia. Somewhere between the pie stops and the simulators, boutique gyms like ACTV Strength Co cropped up, complete with niche touches like curated music and phone‑charger lockers. Sums up Ashmore: we can be wholesome, then very extra.

Our mums’ network has always been loud and proud — Social Mums Club ran a local Arundel/Ashmore community for meet‑ups, and Netmums kept a reliable list of kids’ activities. Musicland, tucked in the Ashmore Park shopping precinct since 1993, reminded us some of us are still in piano lessons and probably always will be.

Who would hate it here? If you crave an all‑night laneway of neon and boutique trousers, you’ll be mildly baffled. Who would love it? Families, pie devotees, the person who wants bowling then banh mi, and anyone who appreciates a place that wears its contradictions like a badge.

We’re Ashmore — a little bit country, a little bit simulated golf, and wholly ours.
