# Oxenford (QLD)

Slug: `oxenford-qld`

Back in the day we’d start a weekend by fighting the inevitable: do we detour past Production Drive for Movie World or squeeze in a splash at Wet’n’Wild on Entertainment Road? Our guidebooks — yes, the old ones — used to list Movie World at Production Drive and Time Out even gave Wet’n’Wild the 10am–5pm stamp. Those were the choices: roller-coasters or slides, sunscreen verdicts settled over burnt ears.

We remember when the news cycle taught us patience. As Broadsheet noted, beer bottles once strewn across the M1 backed traffic up about 20 kilometres from Yatala to Oxenford — a masterclass in communal sulking while we inched home. The other staple: events that turned Oxenford into a seasonal playground — Topgolf on Entertainment Drive used to promise climate-controlled bays and microchipped golf balls for the novelty curious, and Concrete Playground ran Easter and New Year’s Eve listings there. Movie World’s Fright Nights even shipped in Darkfield’s Seance 2022 and Flight 2022 for people who like their screams curated.

We weren’t all adrenaline or theatrics. Urban List used to point shoppers to Oxenford Square on Old Pacific Hwy, and the cheap-and-cheerful Viet Au Kitchen in town made a bánh mì for $6.99 feel like haute cuisine on a budget. Pilates Social popped up in round-ups too (2/141 Maudsland Rd) — proof some of us stretched more than our patience.

Petty local drama? That M1 mess was our most cinematic. Also, for a while the area was popping up in lists and event pages so we got a bit touristy — one minute you’re local, the next you’re a line in a “best of” slideshow.

Who would hate it here: anyone allergic to queues, roller-coasters, or the idea of sharing a weekend with themed attractions and novelty golf balls. Who would love it: families that live for a theme-park season pass, bargain hunters who miss Viet Au’s $6.99 bánh mì, and anyone who enjoys the delightful chaos of a place that’s always been more about big days out than quiet cul-de-sacs.

Full disclosure: our sources here are older — we’re reminiscing rather than compiling a live visitor guide. Still, Oxenford’s spirit? Timeless.
