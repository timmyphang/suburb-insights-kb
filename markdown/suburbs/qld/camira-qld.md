# Camira (QLD)

Slug: `camira-qld`

We are standing at the unofficial centre of Camira, pretending our Saturday plans are a curated lifestyle piece — and then remember: MumsPages has neatly indexed party venues for Morningside (4170), kids’ sports in West Pymble (2073), even mixed sports in Gruyere (3770) and providers in Richmond North (3121). Camira? Crickets. Lovely.

Morning routine: we shuffle the kids to a non-listed playground, flick through MumsPages like it’s a neighbourhood roll call, and shrug when our postcode doesn’t get a shout-out. For years the local parenting oracle has been busy cataloguing other suburbs’ party rooms and sports classes, leaving us gloriously uncurated. It’s either neglect or a badge of honour — we choose the latter.

Petty local drama: there isn’t one worth feeding to a directory. The biggest scandal is deciding which street’s cul-de-sac gets the weekend basketball hoop. No council exposés, no influencer coffee wars — just community-level logistics and a collective tolerance for chaos.

Who would hate living here? People who demand a suburb have its own online category on the first page of search results. If your idea of comfort is being catalogued and rated by every parenting marketplace, Camira will feel insultingly analogue.

Who would love it here? Families who prefer elbow room over exposure, folks who relish being the place MumsPages skipped when it was assigning suburbs like Morningside, West Pymble, Richmond North and Gruyere. We’re not invisible. We’re just off the curated map — and frankly, we like it that way.
