# Leichhardt (QLD)

Slug: `leichhardt-qld`

We should start by admitting we did a thing: we Googled "Leichhardt" and watched the internet angrily decide we live on Norton Street. As Leichhardt, QLD, we are equal parts amused and slightly offended — mostly amused.

Back in the day (and, by the look of the clippings, still) most write-ups are about the Sydney version: Broadsheet and Time Out have endless nostalgia for Bar Italia, Grappa and the Norton Street scene; Concrete Playground fell in love with Spicetown in the old Leichhardt Hotel; Urban List keeps trotting out La Bottega and Kollector Cars and Coffee. The web, bless it, is convinced we’re a piazza with panino and pasta on every corner. We’re not — but hey, we enjoy the identity theft.

For our side of town the only thing the files actually had was the political stuff. Junkee flagged the federal seat of Leichhardt (that one in far north Queensland) and reported a high proportion of Indigenous voters and worries about understaffed polling stations — a blunt reminder that democracy looks different depending on the postcode. As Junkee put it, people here were concerned their voices weren’t getting the airtime they deserved. Not glamorous. Extremely important.

Who would hate living here? People who enjoy anonymity and metropolitan metaphors. Also anyone scrolling for a Norton Street weekend guide and landing in federal-electorate reality — you’ll be disappointed if you came for spag bol and vintage cars.

Who would love it here? Folks who care about community, who care about fair access at elections, and who prefer a place where the conversation is about real civic things rather than whether a pasta place opened a CBD outpost. If you want drama, we’ve got it — it’s just the meaningful kind.

So yes: the internet keeps trying to graft a Little Italy onto our identity. We’ll wear that compliment with a shrug — and while the blogs argue about gelati and piazzas, we’ll keep insisting Leichhardt, QLD has its own beats and its own priorities.
