# Ipswich (QLD)

Slug: `ipswich-qld`

Back in the day we’d tell visitors Ipswich is where the river does a bit of its own skyline flex — and, truth be told, we still say it with a grin. We remember when Riverlink’s East Mall got its glow-up, the article raving about LED see‑saws, illuminated ping‑pong and a family play zone looking out over the Bremer. It felt like someone finally decided playgrounds should be theatrical.

Our weekend routine used to involve a very specific circuit: the Nicholas Street precinct for a family day (miniBOUNCE got a shout), That Dumpling Place hiding in Nicholas Street’s lineup, and the Pumpyard Bar and Brewery — Four Hearts’ place on Limestone Street that opened in 2015 and proudly announced Ipswich’s first new brewery in a century. For the artsy among us, the Ipswich Art Gallery hosted big shows — remember Leonard Brown’s Painting the Celestial (March 28–June 14) and the Bundanon ceramics tour Fantastic Forms (June 14–Sept 7, 2025)? We liked being the sort of town that took its pottery and its abstractions seriously.

Petty local drama? Oh, we’ve had our headlines. Queensland Rail’s reduced services once named Booval in a list of affected stations, which made train‑time a competitive sport. And yes, Pedestrian TV ran that surreal clip from the Racehorse Hotel — a man in a clown mask and a very shy robbery — which made for awkward pub chat for weeks. Time Out even labelled Ipswich one of the country’s “sweatiest” cities to avoid in summer; we wear that like a badge in January.

Who would hate living here? If you despise heat, festivals or the possibility of missing a train, Ipswich will test you. If giant cowboy boots and country music aren’t your thing, CMC Rocks at Willowbank Raceway will be a loud reminder of that.

Who would love it? Families who enjoyed the Riverlink revamp and the Nicholas Street family hub, festivalgoers who knew Willowbank, beer fans who celebrated the Pumpyard, and anyone who appreciated a proper art retrospective in our gallery. We’re proud, a little dusty in summer, and full of surprises — sometimes literal ones in clown masks.
