# Redbank (QLD)

Slug: `redbank-qld`

We wake up to the smell of something fried and very earnest — not our fault, Haeduri Chicken Redbank once made it impossible to be subtle about lunch. Urban List used to gush about their UFO Fondue Chicken, and honestly, who are we to argue with molten cheese and wings? Back in the day, that was our sort of civic pride.

Time Out once tried to civilise us, too — there’s an old “essential guide to Red Bank” and a list of “13 Restaurants in Red Bank” floating around, like someone being polite to a place that prefers not to dress up. Meanwhile Sitchu reminds us the name “Redbank” pops up in other, spookier calendars: the Redbank Range Railway Tunnel (the “Mushroom Tunnel”) near Picton made the haunted rounds with the legend of Emily Bollard. We like that our name gets around — respectable, haunted, delicious.

If you dig a proper local brag, Urban List also archived our fitness flex: Charles Roman at BFT Redbank Plains (321 Redbank Plains Road) was on a Brisbane personal trainers list. So somewhere between UFO chicken and motivational gym talk we hit our peak civic identity.

We’ll be honest: there isn’t a stack of glossy, recent headlines about us — most of the love lives in older guides and listicles — so our resume reads like the highlights reel of someone who stopped emailing press releases in 2019. That’s fine. We’re not trying to trend; we’re trying to keep the wings warm and the Haunted Tunnel stories convincing.

Who would hate living here? People who need a freshly curated Instagram moment every 48 hours. If your idea of fun is a constant stream of brand-new openings and influencer backdrops, you’ll find our charmingly unchanged corners dreadfully inconvenient.

Who would love it here? Families who want a sound of normal, night-shift workers who appreciate cheap, honest food options (UFO Fondue, we salute you in memos), and anyone who enjoys a suburb with a pastiche of guides — Time Out, Urban List, Sitchu — all saying, in their own ways, “there’s something here.” We’ll keep it that way.
