# Newmarket (QLD)

Slug: `newmarket-qld`

Back in the day we learned to time our weekends by the council’s summer dip bargain: Newmarket Olympic Swimming Pool was even on the $2 entry list, and yes, we treated that eight‑lane pool, the kids’ slide, splash pad, wading pool and public barbecues like a civic backyard. We’re the people who arrive with a towel, a packet of sausages and the exact spot by the shade tree memorised.

Our Saturday loop is embarrassingly domestic and proudly local. Train into the station, five kilometres and a short ride from the CBD — we’ve always liked that we can be in the city and back for afternoon soccer training — then a quick detour to Newmarket Village at 132 / 400 Newmarket Road for gelato because, as Sitchu cheekily reminds us, this isn’t just a shopping strip: it’s a neighbourhood that keeps you fed. And yes, the Mexican that first opened in Milton now has a home here, so expect table‑made guac and slightly dramatic Saturday evenings.

We’ve watched the commercial hub do its thing up on the main drag while the back streets switch gears into leafy family lawns and a different tempo. As Sitchu put it, we’re “well‑positioned” — busy where you need it, quiet where you don’t. That makes for proud practicality, plus the occasional suburban flex: “we’ve got a cinema nearby” and “no, the CBD is only five clicks away” said in the exact tone of someone who owns a garden hose and an echidna of authority.

Petty drama? Our biggest collective grumble was weather, not neighbours. Back in March 2025 sections of Newmarket Road flooded when ex‑Tropical Cyclone Alfred came through — a soggy reminder that our main road and the river of traffic sometimes share the same mood.

Who would hate living here: anyone who needs neon nightlife and never wants to hear a child’s laugh at 8am. If your weekend mission is bar-hopping until dawn, you’ll call us quaint and get on the next tram.

Who’d love it: families, swimmers, gelato devotees and people who want the convenience of a short CBD commute without giving up a verandah and a decent park. We’ll keep the barbecues warm and the pool lanes open — that’s Newmarket, unpretentious and exactly our speed.
