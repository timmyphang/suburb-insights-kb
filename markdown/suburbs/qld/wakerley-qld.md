# Wakerley (QLD)

Slug: `wakerley-qld`

We roll out of bed, squint at the papered-over creek views and watch the kids on their bikes — yes, the ones Sitchu swears are everywhere — zipping past our hedge like they're auditioning for a BMX ad. Back in the day Urban List even pointed out Sheriff Park here in Wakerley as one of the spots to play pickleball (and it paired that list with Greenways Esplanade Park in Parkinson, if you're keeping score).

For weekend plans we used to have options other than wandering the estate: Concrete Playground noted Makeit Carbatec woodworking workshops at Carbatec Brisbane (128 Ingleston Road) — perfect if you fancied a shelf that didn’t collapse after a week. There was even a Keeping Mum listing for a mothers’ group at a cheekily named “Super Secret Location” on Matthews Way, which tells you everything about our community calendar: low-key and slightly mysterious.

There’s no scandal or council kerfuffle in the snippets we’ve found — just tidy family suburbia, Tilleydale Estate’s show-home vibes (see 47 Talltrees Circuit) and neighbours who actually bring salads to barbecues. If you despise kids on bikes, community barbecues or the idea of someone honing their woodworking skills down the road, this isn’t your cup of tea.

But if you love wide streets, quality homes and the kind of neighbourly pride Sitchu champions, Wakerley is exactly the sort of place we brag about at school pickup — with smugness, and maybe some timber offcuts from Carbatec on the boot.
