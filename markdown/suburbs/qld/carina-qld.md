# Carina (QLD)

Slug: `carina-qld`

Back in the day we’d stretch the picnic rug across Minnippi Parklands and pretend we invented “urban nature” before it was trendy. Porter's Paddock—with its smug little lagoon—was our go-to for a weekend escape, and for reasons nobody really explains we still tell outsiders it’s only “a short drive” to Westfield Carindale as if that’s some kind of cultural credential.

We know our streets: Meadowlands Road and Rhuddlan Street turned up on property listings more often than any of us would like to admit, and yes, the local school catchments — Carina State School and Whites Hill State College — get whispered about at the school gate like they’re part of the family trust. Carina Heights gets praised in those glossy neighbourhood roundups for being leafy, walkable and annoyingly sensible. We’ll allow it.

There was a time the suburb was quietly proud of its small victories — Crusty Devil's doughnuts were put on lists, Meadowlands Hair Salon earned a polite nod, and Bare Knuckles BBQ made an appearance on a roundup with an address on Creek Road. And then there was Getta Burger: born in Carina, feted for its OTT burgers, grown up and gone — Concrete Playground noted its Brisbane outlets eventually closed. We like to tell new residents that kind of rise-and-fizzle is practically a local rite of passage.

Who would hate living here? If you crave constant novelty, rooftop cocktail bars and streets named after people who were vaguely tragic poets, Carina will bore you to tears. If you loathe having a mall nearby, dislike trees, or actively want to avoid a neighbourhood that prides itself on being annoyingly practical, step off.

Who loves it here? Families, obviously — the listings and neighbourhood guides have been saying it for years — and anyone who wants parks, parks and more parks, plus the convenience of Westfield Carindale when the kids demand something shiny. Oh, and every December we all secretly enjoy watching Stanley Road light up like a suburban Bond movie; it’s our own small-town sparkle, and we defend it like it’s ours.
