# Browns Plains (QLD)

Slug: `browns-plains-qld`

Back in the day we’d kick off a Saturday at the place that made sugar our civic anthem — yes, Doughnut Time branched into Browns Plains — and somehow it felt like a civic duty to queue for a doughnut before taking the kids to the flying fox at Berrinba Wetlands. Berrinba was our bit of wildernessy pride: roughly 80 hectares, more than 8km of pram‑friendly tracks, a huge flying fox over sand with an elevated start so the little ones can pretend they’re stunt pilots, and apparently over 100 nesting boxes for possums, gliders and birds. We weren’t exactly suburbia’s answer to Bondi, but we had trees that outperformed most shopping centres.

You could then meander to Forestglen Park — delightfully set back from the main road — for some sand play and shade structures while someone argued about which playground slide is the most Instagrammable. If we were feeling very civic-minded we’d talk fitness: EMF Performance & Recovery Centre used to note a Browns Plains location on its map, so we could hobble in complaining and leave pretending we’d fixed our posture.

Petty local drama? Oh yes: once upon a news cycle Chatime Grand Plaza was name‑checked as a past hotspot and we spent a week conducting temperature checks on our social lives. Pedestrian.TV even called one of our retailers “disturbingly clean” — yes, EB Games in Browns Plains made the list — which we took as both a compliment and a challenge to the rest of us to up our tidiness game.

Who would hate living here? People who think a glorious weekend involves only espresso shots and rooftop bars — we don’t do pretense well. If your vibe is “I only visit places with valet parking and menus in Helvetica,” you’ll get cranky fast.

Who would love it? Families, nature nerds, and anyone who likes fireworks and giant Easter egg hunts: Waller Park hosted the Queensland Easter Fair and later the Waller Park Family Fun Fair with rides, free entry and fireworks, and the Logan Eco Forum once threw a Family Adventure Day here. We’re low‑fuss, full‑heart, and fond of our flying foxes. Come for the doughnuts, stay for the wetlands, and leave your airs at the freeway.
