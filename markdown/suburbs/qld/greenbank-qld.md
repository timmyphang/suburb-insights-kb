# Greenbank (QLD)

Slug: `greenbank-qld`

We live in Greenbank — the place you move to when you decide you want a bigger backyard and a mortgage that doubles as a personality. Back in the day (and by "back" we mean the only stories we could rustle up), Time Out flagged Greenbank–North Maclean as one of those outer‑rung hotspots where buyers chase “brand new homes, larger blocks and family‑friendly areas.” So yes, our cul‑de‑sacs come with elbow room and the faint smell of freshly poured concrete. We wear it like couture.

A typical Saturday for us? We roll out of a glasshouse of a new build and admire the block size we paid extra for. There isn’t much in the way of glossy city listings to flick through — most recent coverage we found is from earlier years — so our small‑town rhythm is still very much: build, move in, repeat. For the historical record: Time Out did the heavy lifting on the growth narrative.

Petty local drama? We had our moment. Concrete Playground once put Greenbank on a Queensland Health watchlist during that 2020 cluster tied to the Brisbane Youth Detention Centre, so yes, we remember the nervous group chat and the extra hand‑sanitiser on the kitchen bench. Not funny, but true — and now a weird badge of suburban survival.

If you’d hate living here: someone who needs a tram to get moral validation, or who thinks “weekend vibes” require three Michelin stars and an underground bar. We’re not a glossy travel feature (and frankly, Time Out tends to save its Instagrammable spots for other postcodes). Also, pro tip: Netmums once wrote about a Greenbank Hotel in Falmouth — dramatic seaside views, kids’ menus and all that — which is delightful but not our Greenbank. Don’t pack your swimmers expecting Queensland sunshine on arrival.

If you’d love living here: families who want space, people who want new builds and quieter streets, and anyone who thinks bigger blocks are underrated luxury. For years the local childcare scene even got a nod in Netmums with a listing for Greenbank Preparatory School and Day Nursery — proof that somewhere between the new builds and the watchlists, we actually keep the little ones fed and mildly entertained.

We’re not fancy. We’re roomy, practical, and stubbornly suburban — and honestly, we prefer it that way.
