# Runaway Bay (QLD)

Slug: `runaway-bay-qld`

Back in the day, our Saturday started at the courts and drifted towards something that might double as brunch or a low-key aperitivo — because, yes, we are Runaway Bay and we multitask.

We’d warm up at the Runaway Bay Pickleball Centre — Urban List once pointed out the Gold Coast Pickleball Community Centre has four courts in Runaway Bay available for hire — swap paddles for plates, and mosey down Lae Drive. Espresso & Wine at 12/128 Lae Dr was even featured for its mont blanc and cold foam creations, and Urban List liked that Runaway Bay spot that “effortlessly marries relaxed cafe vibes with the sleek elegance of a wine bar.” For something more Vietnamese, Can Tho Kitchen and Bar on Oxley Drive used to get a nod as a word‑of‑mouth favourite for properly traditional flavours. Steak nights? The guide remembered Cavill’s Steakhouse at 6 Bayview Street — yes, the one with the 300g tenderloin/eye fillet on the menu.

It’s worth saying: we couldn’t find much recent local coverage in these snippets — most of this is the sort of “remember when” press that ages like sunscreen in the glovebox. So this is affection for our old favourites, not a live events calendar.

If you’re after petty local drama, our file is disappointingly wholesome — no council wars or billboard sagas here, just community groups. Social Mums Club has long listed Runaway Bay among its Gold Coast pockets, linking mums across Biggera Waters, Coombabah, Paradise Point and beyond. Concrete Playground even noted a Pizza Hut Runaway Bay turning up in a one‑day pizza box exchange — because nothing says community like recycling for a free pepperoni.

Who would hate it here? Nightlife chasers and anyone expecting a constant cavalcade of trend-chasing hotspots — we’re quieter than that, and yes, sometimes gloriously beige.

Who would love it? People who actually like playing sport, swapping recipes at a local mums’ meet-up, or savouring a mont blanc with a glass that could tip into wines-by‑night territory. We’re the suburb that quietly does its thing — reliable, slightly smug, and still happy to hire you a pickleball court if you ask nicely.
