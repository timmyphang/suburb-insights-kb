# Augustine Heights (QLD)

Slug: `augustine-heights-qld`

We start our Saturday at 2/24 Technology Drive, where we used to tee off under fluorescent lights because, yes, JC Golf Studio was the closest thing Augustine Heights had to a proper sporting debut. As locals, we’ll tell you: nothing says suburb-pride like arguing over your TrackMan numbers while the kids move from origami to finger-painting down the road. As MumsPages used to list — and still lists in its undated pages — arts-and-craft classes from Sumner Park to Springfield Lakes “cover” Augustine Heights, which means our children’s extracurriculars arrive like a helpful caravan from the neighbouring postcodes.

Back in the day the only press our postcode scored were Brisbane-wide round-ups — Urban List’s delightful love notes about mini golf, yum cha and Christmas-light routes — which is to say: we’re on the edge of the city’s fun, not the headline. Urban List keeps reminding us Brisbane is a treasure trove; we’re the sensible glove compartment where you keep the map.

There’s no juicy council scandal or artisanal coffee feud in the snippets we found — the data is older and politely domestic — so the “petty local drama” section is thin. That’s fine. We’ll roast our own reputation: yes, we’re the kind of place where directories and class listings do the PR for us, and where the most exciting headline might be a new schedule for kids’ drawing and origami.

Who would hate living here? If you crave neon nightlife and being name-checked in every foodie listicle, Augustine Heights will feel like a cul-de-sac for FOMO. Our media footprint reads like a “quiet suburb” playlist: practical, booked out with kids’ classes, and occasionally swinging a golf club indoors.

Who would love it? Families who want JC Golf Studio-style coaching options close by, parents who appreciate a MumsPages-style roundup of activities, and anyone who likes being five minutes from Springfield Lakes, Sumner Park and the rest of the sensible outskirts. We’re small, steady and unassuming — and we’ll keep your TrackMan score a secret, between us.
