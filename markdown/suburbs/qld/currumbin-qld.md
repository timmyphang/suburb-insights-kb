# Currumbin (QLD)

Slug: `currumbin-qld`

Back in the day we’d swear Currumbin woke up with the lorikeets — and, if it was Swell week, a line-up of sculptures pretending to be dunes. We know the stretch: Currumbin Beach, the wildlife sanctuary with its famously busy hospital (Time Out said it cared for more than 12,000 animals a year), and the odd shipping-container-turned-sea-adventure when the surf got cheeky (yes, Pedestrian filmed boxes of beer bobbing off shore).

Our usual Saturday used to look like this: a walk on the sand, a wander through the SWELL Sculpture Festival when it rolled through in 2024 and again in 2025, and then down to the Currumbin Community Markets at 164 Currumbin Creek Road — Thursdays, 7–11am — where organic veg, baked goods and a bloke with live music would try to out-perform the sea breeze. Broadsheet once told visitors to “grab brunch at one of Currumbin’s laid-back cafes,” and Urban List used to champion The Salt Mill and Barefoot Barista on every coastal roundup. Tarte Bakery’s dog-friendly outpost was also the kind of place you’d spot a pram and a wet paw sharing a chair.

We’ve got pedigree: Balter Brewing’s original brewery used to sit here in Currumbin (it employed 65 people), and local roaster history includes Bancroft Roasters Currumbin — Tom Wilcock moved to Brisbane, but the coffee legacy was a Currumbin thing for years. Even The Warehouses grew a bit more serious with Burly Bar and chef Harry Robbins running a small-plates program that made us feel slightly bougie for a week.

Who would hate living here? People who can’t stand a beach that doubles as an art gallery, or who panic when a koala gets more Instagram than they do. Also, if you need your city skyline visible from every window, we’re probably not for you.

Who’d love us? Families and nature nerds — Currumbin Wildlife Sanctuary, the Currumbin Rock Pools and Cougal Cascades inland, and Cooee Kids’ nature play programs meant the littlies turned mud into a curriculum. We’re a blend of surf, scrub and community markets: a place that’s been doing its own thing long enough to be unbothered by trend pieces. We’ll keep the lorikeets; you keep your skyline.
