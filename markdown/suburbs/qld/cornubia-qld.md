# Cornubia (QLD)

Slug: `cornubia-qld`

Saturday morning in Cornubia looks like us pretending we’re on a nature documentary: we park the car (because yes, we still drive) and head for the rainforest rather than a rooftop bar. Back in the day Urban List called Cornubia Forest Park a “green wonderland spanning 196 hectares,” and honestly, that line still reads like our suburb’s Tinder bio — big on trees, low on city attitude.

If you’re wondering where we go when we want to climb something that isn’t a gumtree, Urban List once shoe-horned Core Climbing Cornubia into a Gold Coast round-up — listing it at 146 Bryants Rd, Shailer Park QLD 4128 and helpfully adding that it “isn’t technically on the Gold Coast, but close enough.” We like that ambiguity. It’s the Cornubia vibe: close to everything, refusing to commit.

Our social calendar? It’s not neon; it’s a Facebook group. Social Mums Club has an SMC for Cornubia, Loganholme & Surrounds that promises to “provide a sense of belonging.” Translation: we swap class recommendations on a Tuesday and troop en masse to whatever school-holiday camp MumsPages has listed for the area. MumsPages itself is the local yellow pages for tiny humans — school holiday camps, classes, providers — all the thrilling admin that makes suburban life a contact sport.

Petty drama? The data’s thin on scandal, so we’ll stick with the truth: we’re quietly smug about our walking tracks and community groups, and we’re fine with that. People who would hate it here are the late-night cocktail hunters and anyone who measures suburb quality in artisanal bunting and neon signage. You won’t find that; you’ll find fig trees, a lot of green, and a heap of practical parenting advice.

Who’d love it here? Families who want a real patch of bush, folks who like their weekend plans to involve gumtrees, and anyone who prefers community threads to cocktail lists. We’ll be the ones in the carpark, boots muddy, phone full of MumsClub messages — and quietly pleased that Cornubia doesn’t try too hard.
