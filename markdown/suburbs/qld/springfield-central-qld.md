# Springfield Central (QLD)

Slug: `springfield-central-qld`

Back in the day — or at least in the bits of reporting we could rustle up — Springfield Central felt like a very organised plan that still remembered to have fun. We’ll admit it: we’re the sort of place that schedules a dog fashion show and sets up artificial snow for kids, then calls it a weekend.

Mornings here used to mean a stroll past Orion Springfield Central where the occasional community event was on — Concrete Playground even bookmarked Bark Fest Orion Springfield Central, with pooch yoga and a dog fashion show. If you had small people, Snow4Kids at Orion was the kind of novelty that made winter worth the drive: wander through the snow, build a snowman, chuck a snowball at a target and then warm up thinking about brunch.

For brunch, Urban List once described Lotus Cafe as “nestled in the heart of Springfield Central” — perfect for eggs bennie and, yes, smashed avo on sourdough if that’s your weekend religion. Later you could squabble over whether Ballistic Beer Co was a glorious rebirth or sacrilege — Broadsheet noted Ballistic Beer Co would occupy the former site of the Springfield Central Tavern as a 350-seat brewpub with 18 taps and an 800-litre R&D brewery. Smells like progress. Or at least like hops.

We don’t have any recent scandals to fling — the local press has been quiet — but the archives tell a charmingly suburban story: community meetups (hi Mamatribe), kids’ OSHC listings on What's On 4 Kids, and the slightly embarrassing admission on that same site that “No listings were found matching your selection. Something missing? Why not add a listing?” That one feels very Springfieldean: organised enough to have a page, forgetful enough to leave it blank.

Who would hate living here? If you require underground cocktail dens on every corner and never want to see a child in a dinosaur onesie, probably not your vibe. Who would love it? Families, dog owners, anyone into interactive Korean BBQ (Dadam was billed as Springfield’s first Korean BBQ house with built-in BBQ tables) and folk who like their local future-proofed — Time Out even had Springfield Central Stadium pencilled in as a proposed modern pentathlon venue for Brisbane 2032.

We’re tidy, a bit earnest, and occasionally throws a dog fashion show. Come for the events, stay for the brewpub debate.
