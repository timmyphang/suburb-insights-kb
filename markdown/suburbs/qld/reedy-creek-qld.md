# Reedy Creek (QLD)

Slug: `reedy-creek-qld`

We live in the postcode that quietly became the Gold Coast’s answer to “have you tried vegan ramen?” Back in the day the internet kept telling people to drive out to Shop 7/50 Woodland Drive — because Izakaya Midori, touted by Urban List as the Gold Coast’s first fully plant‑based Japanese restaurant, made Reedy Creek sound like a pilgrimage site for rich vegan ramen and wild‑mushroom nigiri. We still chuckle at that: our suburb’s serious claim to fame was food that convinced people to leave their suburb and then tell strangers about it online.

A typical weekend, historically speaking: we’d say “go to Woodland Drive” and someone would quote Urban List — “well worth the road trip” — as if that settled every argument about whether Reedy Creek had a personality beyond a postcode. Sitchu also remembered us kindly, listing the same Shop 7/50 Woodland Drive and even nodding to Govindas in the area, while another Sitchu travel piece suggested spending a night in “glamping luxury at Reedy Creek Retreat.” So yes, if you like your local rep a curious mash of vegan katsu don and boutique tents, we’ve got you.

There’s not much in the way of local tabloid drama in the files (which is a blessing — our gossip column remains in hibernation), so we’ll be honest: our narrative is basically curated lists. That’s fine. We lean into it. We know who we are.

Who would hate living here? People who want their suburb announced on radio every hour. Also anyone allergic to tofu, mushrooms or being told to “try the vegan ramen” by three separate listicles.

Who would love it? Vegans and gluten‑free huntresses on a culinary quest, fans of small‑town bragging rights about being named on “best of” lists, and anyone who thinks a weekend that includes izakaya and a glamping night sounds like a thriller. Our claim to fame might be quietly internet‑curated, but we’ll take “worth the road trip” as a compliment and keep inviting you out to Woodland Drive — at least in the memories of the review pages.
