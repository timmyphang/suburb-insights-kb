# Everton Park (QLD)

Slug: `everton-park-qld`

Back in the day we’d start Saturday like proper locals: drop the kids at Fallon Park and the Everton Park Library for a bit of quiet (ha), swing past Veganyumm on South Pine Road for whatever vegan pastry trend we were pretending not to love, then wander the newly spruced-up Everton Plaza to see who’d turned Park Lane into the suburb’s answer to a dining precinct.

We’ve had our share of glamorous delusions — Broadsheet once called Tetto Rooftop Bar “The Amalfi Coast, but in Everton Park,” and yes, we can still picture the white stucco and arches at 807 Stafford Road, DJ nights and spritz promotions that made a Saturday feel continental without the airfare. Nodo Donuts, Neon Ramen, Bird’s Nest and Mai Gai all popped up in the chatter, and Comuna Cantina’s bottomless margaritas even staged a takeover of the weekend at one point. There was always talk of Alfi Eatery & Bar arriving in Park Lane — Ghanem Group’s 350-seat plan promised a big, alfresco family scene (expected mid-2026, if you remember the hype).

Petty local drama? Classic. The Everton Park Hotel has been through a facelift so many times we’ve had to rebook Keeping Mum mothers groups, relocate meet-ups to the Crown Hotel in Lutwyche, and relearn which playground corner the carousel now lives in. One week the hotel’s “extensive revamp” made it the northside’s family-friendly comeback kid; the next week mums were on a waitlist trying to snag spots for a 10-week term. Welcome to Everton Park: a place where renovations are a local sport.

Who would hate living here? If you despise kids’ carousels, community Easter egg hunts at Teralba Park, or the idea of bumping into someone you taught at playgroup while lining up for donuts, this isn’t your postcode. If you need total anonymity, the suburb’s glow-up and persistent small-town chatter will make you twitch.

Who loves it? Families for the playgrounds and kid-friendly pubs, foodies who remember when Park Lane felt like discovery, and anyone who likes their Mediterranean fantasies served with a side of suburbia. We’ll take the carousel, the rooftop dreams and the noisy mornings — Everton Park wears its glow-up with a grin.
