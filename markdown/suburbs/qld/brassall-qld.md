# Brassall (QLD)

Slug: `brassall-qld`

We start our Saturday by scrolling the Brassall Community Notice Board on Find It Locally like it’s a local paper — slippers, lukewarm tea, and all. There’s a comforting sameness to it: school notices, a lost pet post (someone’s bound to recognise it), and the odd "garage sale" that promises treasures and delivers a stack of old magazines. As Find It Locally notes, our little hub is listed as "Brassall Community Notice Board - 4305" and, apparently, we’re part of a sprawling family: Brassall, Blacksoil, Haigslea, Ironbark, Marburg, Mount Marrow, Muirlea, Pine Mountain, Tallegalla & Walloon Community Group (882). Yes, 882 — we’re practically an online shire.

For years the notice board has been our unofficial council chamber, noticeboard and classified all rolled into one. There’s no drama in the headlines — that’s the point — but if you want a weekly digest of who’s selling what and when the next sausage sizzle might be, this is your goldmine.

Who would hate living here? City folks who need neon-lit options every night and an app for every minor decision. Who would love it? Anyone who likes to know their neighbours’ favourite lemon slice recipe, who appreciates a tight-knit online community and delights in the small, reliably local rhythms of life.

We don’t have to pretend we’re the next inner-city hotspot. We’re the notice-board suburb: modest, organised, and quietly excellent at keeping everyone informed.
