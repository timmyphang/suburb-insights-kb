# Mount Gravatt (QLD)

Slug: `mount-gravatt-qld`

Back in the day we’d start Saturday by pretending we’re summit athletes and stroll the Mount Gravatt Summit track — you know, the 2km there-and-back from Gertrude Petty Place across Mount Gravatt Outlook Drive. Time Out used to remind folks about the gum trees, sheltered gullies and the odd echidna or koala we like to pretend are personal mascots.

Afterwards we'd reward ourselves with the very suburban ritual of sandwiches and coffee. Broadsheet used to rave about Deli Dakota — the “Ham from the Heavens” and “Italian Stallion” were basically local folklore — and Concrete Playground once wrote up Little Black Pug for being the kind of dog‑friendly café that made you feel mildly superior about your well‑behaved fur child. Slack’s Track Cafe, Hallowed Grounds Espresso and The Gift Shop all featured in the old listings, and Sitchu even pointed out a dog‑friendly spot at 6/1297 Logan Road and vegan option You Came Again at 2/1420 Logan Road. Small world, big sandwiches.

Retail theatre hit Westfield Mt Gravatt for a bit of spectacle too — Concrete Playground previewed Holey Moley and Hijinx Hotel joining the centre, promising OTT mini‑golf, karaoke and a 350‑person precinct. Westfield Garden City also got name‑checked in property listings for being “minutes away” and handy for Sunday markets, if we were feeling domesticated.

We’re ashamed to admit the official paper trail here is more venue previews than vicious local gossip — so the only petty drama we can serve is that our nightlife aspirations are mostly fulfilled by themed putt‑putt and baked goods. Time Out even had Lindwall Street, Upper Mount Gravatt on its 2025 Christmas lights map, which is the sort of suburban glory we’ll cling to.

Who would hate it here: people who require a rooftop cocktail bar on every corner, or anyone allergic to sensible walking tracks and reliable sandwich artistry. Who’d love it: families (Newnham Hotel was even listed as kid‑friendly with a playground), dog owners, hikers, and anyone who thinks a frozen margarita and a smoked‑brisket taco once upon a time at Tex‑Mex Cantina South Austin sounds like progress.

We know what they say about us. They also missed the part where our summit views pair perfectly with a very good sanga.
