# East Ipswich (QLD)

Slug: `east-ipswich-qld`

Back in the day, when Urban List was telling Brisbane to hop in the car and “wear stretchy pants” for Ipswich, we were here, already two coffees deep and pretending not to know the tourist route. We’re the ones who can point you to the Bremer River and say, with total local smugness, “that’s where The Link at Riverlink’s East Mall was meant to be a big new riverside rendezvous” — yes, that revitalised precinct the Urban List wrote about, overlooking the water, for the people who like their promenades with a side of planning docs.

Weekend routine for us? Drop the pram by 191A Brisbane Street where Everyday Mums used to run its meetups, wrangle the tiny ones into something approximating public attire, then detour past the family listings MyBump2Baby put up — Masterson Grove and Clip ’n Climb Ipswich get a knowing nod from anyone with sticky-handed offspring. If you’re here for food, remember those old Urban List rounds of “best restaurants” and “best cafes” that put Ipswich on the map for city folk who don’t mind the 40-minute pilgrimage.

We’re not pretending every headline is pretty. Pedestrian TV once gave us a very Ipswich week: from Ipswich Grammar boys accused of a baffling theft overseas, to CCTV at the Racehorse Hotel of a shy clown-masked robber who made off with the cash, to an alleged stabbing reported at the Coles in Yamanto Central. There was also that dramatic self-scan Mi Goreng saga. It’s a lot of plot for a suburb that otherwise just wants to be left alone to grow its brunch reputation.

Who would hate living here? People who need everything sanitised into an Instagram filter — the news cycle here has texture, and not all of it matches your curated grid. Who would love it? Young families who need real community programs (yes, the mums’ groups were doing the heavy lifting), food hunters happy to drive out for a good feed, and anyone who likes a riverside precinct that’s trying to turn into something a bit more fancy without losing its character.

We know what folks say about us. We also know where the best crumbs fall. Come for the food list, stay for the community, and bring sensible shoes.
