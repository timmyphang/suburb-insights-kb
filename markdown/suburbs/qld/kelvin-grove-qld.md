# Kelvin Grove (QLD)

Slug: `kelvin-grove-qld`

We’re standing on Blamey Street with a paper bag of pastries and the kind of smugness only a Saturday-morning Kelvin Grove local can carry. Back in the day (and, let’s be honest, in most of the write-ups we’ve got), the weekend ritual was simple: Kelvin Grove Village Markets on Blamey Street and Musk Avenue, Banneton Bakery making cameo appearances, and the bloke at the stall who looks like he’s always been there since the markets “blocked traffic to an entire street every weekend,” as Concrete Playground put it.

We’ll admit it — we used to worship at Lola’s Coffee Bar for its matcha wizardry. As Broadsheet noted, the crew once churned out roughly 2,000 matcha-based drinks a week, and yes, there was that glorious panic when the matcha ran low. We remember Marcus Allison’s humble market origins too — Bunker Coffee’s story literally started at the Kelvin Grove markets, according to Broadsheet, which makes our local caffeine folklore refreshingly literal.

Evenings were a different species: The Menagerie on Carraway Street (near QUT) was that dimly lit, mismatched-furniture hideout that doubled as cafe-by-day, bar-and-live-music-by-night — the kind of place Concrete Playground and Urban List both enjoyed trotting out. Room 60 — listed at 22 Carraway Street — gets the cosy-bar write-up, and Yozo sounded like the froyo dreamscape Urban List described. Donana Mini Hotpot’s conveyor-belt-and-robot-train thing? Urban List had that interactive dinner covered — because we are nothing if not mildly futuristic at 7pm on a Tuesday.

Petty drama? We had matcha shortages and a closed Thai Ayuthaya tucked into an old tavern, per Time Out. Kevin Rudd’s handball cameo at Kelvin Grove State College — yes, Pedestrian.TV remembered that spectacle — which is the sort of local fame we treasure and never let go of.

Who would hate living here? People allergic to markets, vintage furniture, and anything that might block a street on a Saturday. Who would love it? Market devotees, mini-golf romantics at Victoria Park Putt Putt, theatre heads who remember La Boite’s Roundhouse, and anyone mourning for a perfect matcha.

We’ll point out one thing: most of our sources are archival — these are the stories we’ve been telling ourselves. For the freshest gossip, you’ll have to wander Blamey and Musk and see what’s new.
