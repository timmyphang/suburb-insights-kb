# Palm Beach (QLD)

Slug: `palm-beach-qld`

We like to think Palm Beach, QLD greets you with a sand-scrubbed handshake and a wink. On a Saturday morning we used to shuffle along to the Palm Beach Currumbin Farmers Market at Currumbin State High School — the one Urban List praised — where up to 80 stallholders set up between 6:00am and 11:30am and Mio’s Buckwheat Crepes and The Brownie Hut kept morale high for the people who take their weekend breakfast very seriously.

Back in the day the guides were excited about our food scene: Urban List even put together a 2026 roundup of the best restaurants (Costa Taco made the list), and their cafes feature included Mylk Well’s rosemary hash benedict as evidence we can do brunch with some ambition. Cadillac Supper Club shuffled into town in August 2024 and reinvented itself as an Italo‑American pizzeria and bar, and Madame V arrived on the scene with its moody, Asian‑inspired approach. It’s the kind of churn that makes locals pretend we’re blasé while quietly Googling opening hours.

We’re proud of our bit of coastline — Sitchu called Palm Beach a narrow stretch of beachside suburbia with wide white sand, patrolled zones and dog‑friendly areas, and even noted Tallebudgera Creek for quieter water shenanigans. For years we’ve been politely smug about getting named one of Australia’s cleanest beaches; yes, we know, we’re insufferable about it, but someone has to keep the sand pristine.

If you hate mornings that smell faintly of crepes, think a nine‑storey Pescado development on Jefferson Lane is the peak of civilisation, or prefer inland anonymity, you’ll probably bristle here. If you’re allergic to paddleboards, farmers markets, or a seaside town that’s quietly trying to be both chilled and a little bit tastier than it used to be, move along.

But if you love wide sand, patrolled swimming, lazy creek afternoons at Tallebudgera, and a local food scene that local roundups couldn’t stop talking about — well, we’ve got a sunburnt spot saved for you.
