# Pelican Waters (QLD)

Slug: `pelican-waters-qld`

We know Pelican Waters by the sound of golf clubs at dawn and the polite shuffle at the shopping village carpark. Back in the day the Urban List sent families packing their clubs to Pelican Waters Resort at 38 Mahogany Dr — two- and three-bedroom apartments, balconies for the in-law sprawl, and a short drive to Caloundra Beach. We could almost hear the tiny cheers when someone sank a putt.

Our weekend routine used to look like this: drop the kids at Studio Pilates (listed on the local site), swing past Woolworths Pelican Waters for a decidedly unglamorous top-up, then sit in the shade pretending we don’t live for the waterfront views. The Pelican Waters Shopping Village at 44 Pelican Waters Blvd even had a number you could ring (07 5447 3788) when you wanted a proper human confirmation that the pharmacy or dental surgery still existed — because the community site kept everything in one neat directory.

There’s no big scandal here — the pelicanwaterscommunity.com.au reads like a very efficient PTA: shopping maps, business listings, and community notices. Charming, bureaucratic and slightly smug about its own organisation.

Who would hate it here? Nightlife seekers and anyone allergic to responsible children’s birthday parties. Who would love it? Golf-loving parents, families after a roomy apartment near Caloundra Beach, and people who measure weekends by how many errands you can complete before noon. We’ll keep the esky cold and the barbeque ready.
