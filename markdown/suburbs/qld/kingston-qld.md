# Kingston (QLD)

Slug: `kingston-qld`

Kingston, QLD — Saturdays here smell faintly of rubber and victory. We know because we’ve spent more weekends than we’re proud of leaning on the fence at 20 Mudgee Street, watching karts judder into the first corner and pretending we don’t care who’s overtaken whom.

Back in the day Urban List wrote about Kingston Park as a longstanding go-karting venue with multiple outdoor tracks, events and weekly competitions — and that’s the headline we lean into. Our weekend routine is simple: coffee (we bring it), sunscreen (not negotiable) and a lawn chair on the viewing mound while the kids and the serious types do the laps. Sometimes it’s a family picnic, sometimes it’s a local comp where mum inexplicably remembers she was a race engineer in a past life.

There aren’t many glossy travel pieces about our bit of Brisbane — which suits us. We don’t have an arts precinct in every laneway or a bar with more wrinkles than the mayor. What we do have is a place where the sound of engines reads like a church bell on race day. If you’re after boutique restaurants, we can point you to delicious options in other Kingstons mentioned in the papers, but for us the action was always on Mudgee Street.

Who would hate living here? If you detest outdoor noise, speed, kids with helmets and adults who temporarily remember their single-seat dreams, you’ll have a bad time. If you need your suburb to feel like a perfectly curated Instagram grid rather than a slightly greasy grandstand, move on.

Who’ll love it? Petrolheads, kids with big plans, grandparents who secretly enjoy a good overtake, and anyone who likes a community that cheers when someone finds their racing line. As Urban List put it, Kingston Park was the sort of place people returned to — and that’s the real draw. We’re proud locals, we know the best vantage spots, and if you see us cheering louder than is polite, now you know why.
