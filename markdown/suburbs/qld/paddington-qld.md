# Paddington (QLD)

Slug: `paddington-qld`

We wake up here with the sort of resigned pride that comes from living on a hill you swear you never noticed until you try to park on it. Paddington, QLD: where our Queenslanders sit like tiny castles on their perches and antique shops judge you for daring to walk in sneakers.

Our weekend looks the same every time — we thread the steep streets to the gourmet markets everyone writes about (Urban List flagged them as a highlight in 2025), because apparently browsing artisanal condiments while panting uphill is the new cardio. If we’re feeling decadent and not still jigging from the climb, we’ll queue at Lulu Ramen — Urban List named it in its 2025 roundup of the suburb’s best. It’s the kind of spot that makes you forgive the hill, for a slurp.

Back in the day, Sitchu used to list Padre, Juniper Cafe and Bake Bar among the places that kept us well-caffeinated; that was when brunch felt like a neighbourhood ritual. Treat those mentions as fond history rather than a timetable — their shout-out came from older listings, so consider them memories we like to tell visitors when they ask where to go.

There’s not much local soap-opera in the data for our side of town, which suits us. We’re smugly content to be known more for our antiques, galleries and sensible hills than for scandal. If permanent drama is your vibe, you might be disappointed — we prefer measured taste over tabloid energy.

Who would hate living here? If you loathe steps, adore chain stores, or believe “antique” means “dusty and useless,” you will not be happy. Paddington’s got personality and posture — and it will not apologise for either.

Who would love it? Art lovers, food people with a soft spot for ramen, collectors who dream of finding that one perfect lamp, and anyone who likes their suburb with a little altitude and a lot of character. We don’t need a flat block or a shopping mall to tell us who we are — just point us up the hill and we’ll show you.
