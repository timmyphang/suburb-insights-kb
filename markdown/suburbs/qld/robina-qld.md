# Robina (QLD)

Slug: `robina-qld`

Back in the day — or at least in the stories we have lying around — Robina felt like someone tried to plan suburbia and nailed the moodboard. We know every roundabout and lake: West Lake, Robina Waters and Lake Orr, the master-planned lawns that make neighbours compare sprinkler systems like they’re wine lists. As Sitchu put it, state-of-the-art living — and we’ll take that as a compliment.

Our Saturday starts with a saunter past Robina Town Centre, where Broadsheet once reported the actual orange Friends couch made a pitstop (yes, that couch). For years the Town Centre has been our grand mall theatre: The Kitchens was the jaw-dropping foodie precinct, Adore Beauty opened a bricks‑and‑mortar store there, and Event Cinemas Robina even introduced the ScreenX format in 2023, because when you’re a planned suburb you demand surround-screen drama for your popcorn.

We used to queue at the Robina Farmers Market at Robina State High School on Sundays — fresh produce, street food and crafts — and on other days you could find a lagoon-side deck at Robina Pavilion doing the casual-bistro thing, while Double Zero supplied wood-fired pizza for the evenings when we pretended to be Italian. Concrete Playground reminded us that sports night is never far: Cbus Super Stadium hosted a Matildas friendly on December 1, 2024, and the stadium keeps us cheering on game day.

There’s not much petty scandal in the files — no juicy feuds — so our drama is small and sincere: a rivalry over whose townhouse is the most Instagrammable, and whether the Detour Street Art Festival should stay on the concrete or go full mural. The Gold Coast Mums Group keeps us organised, too — they ran a Robina Alliance Breakfast on February 21, 2025, with John Warn from Experience Gold Coast, and ArtVo’s kids club used to terrify and delight in equal measure.

Who would hate living here? Folks who need the beach on demand — the Sitchu listing reminds us it’s about 7km to the surf, and that distance is a personality test. Who would love it? Bond University students, shoppers who adore choice, sports fans, families chasing weekend markets and anyone who thinks a planned suburb is a perfectly reasonable life choice.

We’ll keep watering the lawns and pretending lake views were always this effortless.
