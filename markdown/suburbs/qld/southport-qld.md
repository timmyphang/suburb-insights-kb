# Southport (QLD)

Slug: `southport-qld`

We wake up to the Broadwater doing what Broadwaters do — pretending it's still a secret — and we, the locals, know every smug selfie angle.

Our weekend looks like this: a brisk stroll along the new Seaway promenade at The Spit (that 700‑metre ribbon of smug exercise, Concrete Playground told you about), a furtive coffee at one of the many cafés Time Out keeps recommending, and a late lunch riverside at River House on 2 Barney Street when we feel like wearing linen and pretending our mortgage is a lifestyle choice. If it's raining, we polish off the pretence at Dendy Southport — the 11‑screen cinema that popped up in Queen Street Village and gave us somewhere to glare at subtitles indoors.

Back in the day the Miele Experience Centre in Southport was listed as hosting winter masterclasses, and for a while we fancied ourselves a bit more Shannon Bennett than we were. Developers fancied themselves even more: One Park Lane was approved as those futuristic twin towers — a 101‑storey residential monolith and a 60‑storey commercial sibling — promising to redraw our skyline and our ability to recognise the sunrise from our own living room.

Our bragging rights are earned. Urban List has been calling us a dining contender; Concrete Playground lit us up for Big City Lights; Time Out tells visitors to consider us the base for the Gold Coast Marathon, with Meriton Suites and TRYP by Wyndham ready to tuck you in (TRYP even opened with that rooftop pool and talk of a luxe day spa). Laneway Festival packing up Brisbane and pitching its tent at Southport Sharks was the sort of neighbourhood flex that made us high‑five like it was a school sports day.

Who would hate living here? If you want permanent sand between your toes, only ever surf, and believe the skyline should never challenge your eyes, you'll complain about our cinemas, festivals and new towers. If you think a riverside restaurant is a crime against authenticity, move along.

Who would love it? Food‑obsessed weekenders, festival people who like their indie bands with a hint of marina, cinephiles, marathon runners looking for a convenient base, and anyone who enjoys watching a town patiently reinvent its skyline while pretending it's always been this cosmopolitan. We call that character.
