# Clontarf (QLD)

Slug: `clontarf-qld`

Back in the day, we used to show off Clontarf like it was a sneaky little Bayside secret — the kind of place Sitchu mooned over as part of the Redcliffe Peninsula, sitting proudly next to Scarborough, Redcliffe and Woody Point. We’ve got that family-friendly beachside lifestyle, café culture and local amenities they write about when they want to sound sentimental on a Tuesday.

Our morning routine is simple: we don’t queue for Insta-famous toast, we queue for real estate snippets. You’ll find us stalking the Sitchu listing for 103 Thomas Street, Clontarf QLD 4019 like it’s the plot twist in a soap opera — because nothing says community like refreshing a property page three times before breakfast.

For years the local brag was less about craft beer launches and more about the Christmas ham aisle. As Broadsheet Brisbane noted, Meat at Billy’s used to source free-range hams from Gilly’s Smallgoods in Clontarf — yes, that Gilly’s. We will defend our pedigree of pork to anyone who questions our culinary seriousness.

Petty local drama? There isn’t much in the archive — which, let’s be honest, is the point. We’re the place you move to when you want to trade late-night neon for early-morning low-tide walks and neighbours who know your name because they borrowed your ladder. If you’re allergic to calm and crave perpetual inner-city chaos, you’ll hate everything about us. Also, if you find joy in anonymous living, Clontarf’s small-town courtesy might feel smothering.

Who would love it here? Parents, paddlers, ham enthusiasts and anyone who thinks “café culture” means actually being able to get a park outside the bakery. We’re for people who want seaside mornings without the tourist trawl, and for those who think refreshing a property listing counts as cardio.

So yes, we’re a little quiet, a little proud, and very easily confused with other Clontarfs on the internet. We wouldn’t have it any other way.
