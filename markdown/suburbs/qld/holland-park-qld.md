# Holland Park (QLD)

Slug: `holland-park-qld`

Back in the day we’d roll down Logan Road wondering if the suburb’s personality lived in a park, a sandwich shop or the latest place to rename 109 Holland Road. For years the local listings read like a rotating cast: Concrete Playground remembered Stomp Espresso at 109 Holland Road, Broadsheet later wrote up Clover Neighbourhood Bar in that same postcode, and Dee Dot (Deedot Coffee House) lingered in older cafe directories with its white picket-fence vibe.

A typical weekend used to look like this: we’d frogmarch the dog toward Toohey Forest Park — yes, the one Urban List said covers about 250 hectares and can be accessed from our side of town — then limp back for a sandwich from Wilbur’s General Store or the old-school deli Dakota over on Wilbur Street (Sitchu had a soft spot for their BBQ brisket). If the weather turned, there was always a chance we’d get locked into Puzzled Room Escape on Logan Road and be forced to use our brains for once.

We’re fond of the well-meaning, slightly earnest things that make us Holland Park: Stretch Yoga put on puppy and kitten yoga adoption days (Concrete Playground — adorable and effective fundraising for DARE and Kitt Kat Care), and there was even a Ukrainian Club that took its time to open — reportedly delayed for 14 months while the team helped displaced Ukrainians before their 12 May launch, according to Urban List. Borgo Salumi in our suburb even made it into Antica’s supplier notes, which is the sort of culinary name-drop we’ll happily brag about at the dog park.

Who would hate living here? If you need to be in the CBD in five minutes and think “parks and sandwich joints” sound tragically quaint, this isn’t your postcode — Sitchu did call us “about 15 minutes’ drive south” of the city centre. We are, however, exactly for people who like schools in easy reach (Holland Park State School and Cavendish Road State High School catchments get a nod), leafy local parks like Glindemann Park, a medley of cafes along Logan Road, and small suburban scenes that quietly keep reinventing 109 Holland Road.

We’re proud of our modest ambitions. We don’t pretend to be inner-city glamour; we’re the suburb that quietly feeds you, walks your dog, and occasionally locks you in an escape room for fun.
