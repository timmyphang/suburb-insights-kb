# Slacks Creek (QLD)

Slug: `slacks-creek-qld`

We know the ritual: Saturday mornings used to start with a detour to Extraction Artisan Coffee at 7/3375 Pacific Hwy — not because we were on some Instagram pilgrimage, but because Urban List once raved about their house-made pastries, sausage rolls and that legendary ‘breakie bun’ (yes, with Beenleigh Butchery bacon). Back in the day, that was our version of haute cuisine.

If you wanted to lean into the local sweet tooth, Sooo Sweet at 6/7 254 Kingston Rd was the spot Urban List pointed to for Lebanese and Mediterranean desserts — we’d queue like it was a festival and come out with sugar on our sleeves. And for the no-nonsense burger faithful, 2 Bros — the Underwood and Slacks Creek outpost — definitely told us “We don't cluck around,” which, frankly, is a promise we’ve all appreciated on hangovers.

You might have noticed Broad-stroked headlines: Time Out once had Slacks Creek on its 2025 radar in the units ranking, proving what we’ve always suspected — folks are quietly snapping up places here. Meanwhile, Concrete Playground cheekily reported Slim's Quality Burger was planning a Slacks Creek drive-thru, complete with nose-in parking etiquette, because some of us like our retro diners with minimal gym effort. And if you need to release a little aggression after brunch, Rage Cage was the oddly specific suggestion: hit stuff with a baseball bat, manage your anger and call it an unconventional date. Only in Slacks Creek, right?

Who would hate living here? People who think every weekend should arrive with an artisanal ceremony and a queue for communal table reservations — Slacks Creek never apologised for having drive-thrus, dessert shops and a Rage Cage on the eclectic roster. If you hate the idea of convenience sliding into charm, this suburb will make you twitch.

Who would love it? Anyone who likes their breakfasts built by places that bake on-site, enjoys a slogan that promises commitment, or wants a suburb quietly doing its own thing while the rest of Brisbane fusses about “scenes.” We’ll keep your parking spot warm.
