# Springfield (QLD)

Slug: `springfield-qld`

Back in the day — and yes, most of our intel here comes from older listings and local guides — we’d tell newcomers Springfield was the place that tried very hard to be everything at once.

Mornings used to look like this in our heads: we’d have been excited about Ballistic Beer Co Springfield being listed at 37 Sinnathamby Boulevard as a brewpub/taproom opening soon, then debate whether to celebrate with a family-friendly arvo at the Springlake Hotel (1 Springfield Lakes Boulevard), which Urban List politely noted was surrounded by bushland and looking out over the lake. If we were feeling fancy, there was even the soft-launch chatter about Dadam — Springfield’s first Korean charcoal BBQ house — sliding into feeds and promising DIY grilling.

There was always a weekend contingency plan: Orion Springfield Central used to host Bark Fest, so the dog got cultured while we pretended the Cross River Rail restructuring (remember when Concrete Playground mapped how services from Rosewood, Ipswich and Springfield would run through Central?) would make our commute glamorous. It also made us collectively remember the time trains didn’t run between Darra and Springfield Central for a three-day block — buses replaced the services and everyone discovered whether they actually liked standing in a bus queue.

Local drama? It was less soap opera, more timetable anxiety. Queensland Rail reductions and the promise of trains every 15 minutes on the Ipswich and Springfield lines made for long, opinionated group chats. Also, Time Out once listed Springfield Central Stadium as a proposed modern pentathlon venue for Brisbane 2032 — which gave us Olympic-sized daydreams and equally big “don’t wreck our lake” takes.

Who would hate living here: people who need perfect, uninterrupted train timetables and zero festival noise. Also anyone allergic to chain wellness centres — EMF Performance & Recovery and Total Fusion were both noted as having had locations in Springfield, so if you loathe rooftop wellness and contrast therapy, maybe look elsewhere.

Who would love it: families who appreciate a pub with a playground, dog owners who remember Bark Fest at Orion Springfield Central, wellness addicts who liked EMF/Total Fusion, and anyone charmed by the idea of a local brewpub at 37 Sinnathamby Boulevard. We’re proudly suburban, slightly aspirational, and always ready for whatever’s opening (or almost opening) next.
