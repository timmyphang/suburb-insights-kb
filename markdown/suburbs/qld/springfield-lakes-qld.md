# Springfield Lakes (QLD)

Slug: `springfield-lakes-qld`

We wake up to a lake that’s trying very hard to be a postcard and a suburb that’s trying equally hard to be sensible. Our Sunday ritual is embarrassingly domestic: a walk around the water, a salute to the pelicans, and a detour to the Springlake Hotel at 1 Springfield Lakes Boulevard because, as Urban List noted, it’s got a playground and actually overlooks the neighbouring lake — the kind of family-friendly brag no one else gets away with.

If you want proof this place was built for practicality rather than posing, look no further than the school run. YMCA Springfield Lakes OSHC used to be situated at Springfield Lakes State School — the room was in the school hall and best accessed via the main school carpark (63 Springfield Lakes Blvd). Their before-school, after-school and vacation hours were the sort that make modern parenting run like a spreadsheet: Before School Care 6:15–9:00am, After School Care 2:30–6:00pm, Vacation Care 6:15–6:00pm. Not glamorous. Very necessary.

We also notice the small comforts: Epic Hair Designs had a Springfield Lakes spot in Springlakes Village, Shop 13, 31 Springfield Lakes Boulevard (ph: 07 3288 3430) — a reminder that even our hair appointments can be planned and colour-coded. It’s the kind of place where your salon is on the same strip as the kinder notices and the real estate agent with a clipboard.

Who would hate living here? People who crave grit, unpredictability, or a laneway you can’t park in. If your idea of fun is accidental late-night chaos, you’ll find Springfield Lakes a little too neatly scheduled. If you can’t handle playgrounds that double as social infrastructure, this suburb will test your patience.

Who would love it? Families who appreciate being able to actually get through a weekday without improvising childcare, people who like lakes that behave like lakes, and anyone who prefers their pubs with swings and sensible opening hours. We don’t pretend it’s edgy. We are the suburbs that turned sensible into an art form — and honestly, we wear it well.
