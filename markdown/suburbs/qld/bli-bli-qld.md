# Bli Bli (QLD)

Slug: `bli-bli-qld`

We know Bli Bli like the back of our sunburnt hands — the slow parade along David Low Way, the castle that refuses to be sensible, and the occasional new roof that thinks it’s Mediterranean. There aren’t any recent pieces in this batch of local yarns, so we’re doing a bit of nostalgia-meets-tour-guide from what’s been written about us.

Morning in our part of the coast used to look like this: kids sprinting at McMartin Park for Bli Bli Little Athletics (44 Lefoes Road), while somewhere between the turrets of Sunshine Castle (292 David Low Way — yes, an honest-to-God fairytale castle) and the multi-level hum of Bli Bli Village (304–312 David Low Way) someone argued about whether the rooftop at the new three-level pub actually has a retractable roof or just impressive optimism. Concrete Playground wrote about that Bli Bli Hotel — opened late October 2023 — and its wood-roasted prawns and rooftop margarita ambitions. We remember the ribbon-cutting; the seagulls do too.

Our local calendar reads like a wholesome indie festival: bird walks at Maroochy Wetlands Sanctuary (end of Sports Road), a fungi talk that was scheduled for 16 March 2026, kids learning to hurdle and adults pretending burpees are a good idea. There’s even a Castle that once hosted Opera in the Castle (we’ll take classy, thank you very much). For the wellness set, Tank Bathhouse on 457 Bli Bli Rd was written up as a boutique escape on rolling farmlands — magnesium pools, saunas and the kind of exclusivity that comes with acreage.

Who would hate living here? People who need a tram every five minutes, or anyone allergic to small-town quirks: medieval dioramas, a village precinct that’s still finding its centre, and the comforting reality that your Saturday might involve refereeing a soccer match for Bli Bli United on Yandina Road.

Who would love it here? Families who breathe athletics and community footy, bird nerds who’ll happily trudge the wetlands, folks who once booked a spa day at Tank, and anyone charmed by the idea that Sopra Cucina & Bar from Bli Bli made Time Out’s 2025 list — proof our little hub can surprise a city critic or two. We’re unapologetically modest, a touch theatrical, and stubbornly proud of our turrets.
