# Keperra (QLD)

Slug: `keperra-qld`

Back in the day we’d start Sunday by shuffling the kids into the car and pretending we were on a mini-escape. We used to drop by The Crampton Social — Urban List reckoned it was our cosy little secret — where the vegetable and halloumi stack and house-made corn fritters got a nod and the cheesecakes got fussed over. Somewhere between a playground run and a picnic rug at Enoggera Reservoir we’d remember Keperra is mostly about space: Sitchu called it a hidden gem for families who like a lush yard and parks galore.

For the wellness types among us, Urban List also noted City Cave had a Keperra location, so there was always that fantasy of a steam, a soak and a quick escape from sticky birthday-party cleanups. Speaking of birthdays, Brisbane Kids reviewed Arana Leagues Club at 247 Dawson Parade — yes, the one with the supervised kids’ club — so we could adult for a hot second while someone else herded the sugar.

We even had practical wins: Keperra Bulk Billing Medical Centre at 14 Dallas Parade used to bulk-bill standard in-clinic appointments on weekdays, which made sense when the kids managed to discover a new way to bruise a knee. Children First set up a purpose-built centre at 15 Ridgeline Way that was designed for 120 little people, complete with big outdoor playscapes and vegetable gardens — and they even offered a Defence Force family discount in the promo materials, which felt very on-brand for a suburb that quietly looks after its neighbours.

Our outdoor cinema nights in Heiner Park used to make us feel slightly cosmopolitan — Brisbane Kids listed screenings like How to Train Your Dragon on 22 May — so we’d bring blankets, a suspiciously large thermos and pretend the mosquitoes were part of the ambience.

Who’d hate Keperra? If you live for late-night trams, cocktail-list bragging rights or tiny courtyards with no room for a trampoline, you’ll be bored stiff. Who’d love it? Families who want space, dog walkers who like trails, parents who value a childcare centre with veggie patches and anyone who appreciates a practical local life that trades gloss for breathing room.

Yes, we’re a bit suburban. We’ll take it — green, loud and quietly useful — and we’ll still argue about where the best cheesecake once was.
