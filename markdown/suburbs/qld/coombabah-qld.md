# Coombabah (QLD)

Slug: `coombabah-qld`

Back in the day we’d wake up to the kind of alarm only Coombabah can offer: the distant scream of an osprey over Coombabah Creek and the smug rustle of someone else’s recycling being collected. We’d lace up and disappear into the Koala and Melaleuca Track Loop at Coombabah Lakelands Conservation Area — Urban List used to bang on about those wetlands, eucalypt forests and creeks like they were our secret spa. Spoiler: they aren’t, but we like pretending.

Our Saturday is a simple recipe: a slow paddle where eastern curlews, brahminy kites and osprey keep an eye on us (the kayakers, not the smug joggers), then a detour past Ross Evans Garden Centre on Oxley Drive — the six‑acre place Urban List called a Gold Coast institution. For years that nursery was our excuse to buy yet another pot and tell ourselves we’re “building a habitat”.

We even get press appearances. As Pedestrian TV/ABC reported, Coombabah had a cameo in a statewide crime story when a 21‑year‑old from here was named in a GBH charge — not our proudest marketing moment, but it did remind us we’re on the map.

If you’re allergic to quiet, wildlife and the idea that you might actually have to drive for a decent latte, you’ll hate living here. If you need rooftop bars, a tram every five minutes, or boutique sandwich shops on every corner, move on — Broadsheet’s big-city lists are charming, but they don’t come with wetlands.

Who loves it? Mums who want community — Social Mums Club has had Coombabah in its roundups for years — gardeners who worship Ross Evans, bargain hunters trawling the op‑shops that Urban List once included us in, and anyone who likes the mix of suburban tidy and wild nature on their doorstep. We’re not flashy. We don’t pretend to be. But we’ve got creeks that host migrating birds, tracks that make you feel virtuous, and a nursery that will sell you a small forest and a smug sense of civic duty. Come for the birds, stay because you can’t be bothered moving.
