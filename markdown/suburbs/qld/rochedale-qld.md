# Rochedale (QLD)

Slug: `rochedale-qld`

Back in the day we’d roll out of bed, do a quick cruise past 329 Gardner Road and pretend we were on a proper weekend — Cache Cache used to serve a cornflake-crusted chicken stack with eggs and avo and a bacon-and-egg breakfast burger that made you rethink your life choices. If you needed ceremony afterwards, Icing Hill at the same 329 Gardner Road was the whimsical, hassle-free cake fix for every last-minute birthday panic.

By lunchtime the neighbourhood could get weird in the best way — Urban List once pointed us to HongDae GopChang at 9/11 Lorisch Way, which “isn’t your regular Korean BBQ — here, the focus is all on intestines.” We’ll say no more, except: adventurous appetites, you were warned and rewarded.

If you open real estate tabs on a boring Tuesday you’ll see what Sitchu loved about us: split-level homes on Circle Court, restful views from Fulica Street, dual-living dreams on Minerva Street and cosy lowset charms on Clonara Street. The listings kept trotting out the same sales pitch — elevated oasis, leafy surrounds, minutes to Rochedale Shopping Village and Underwood Park — and honestly, it’s not wrong. For years the local narrative has been very suburban: family renovations, green bushland outlooks and properties promising room for grandparents, trampoline and smug hedges.

There’s no scandal in our file — no council kerfuffle, no nightlife feuds — just cafes, cake shops, Korean BBQ and earnest real-estate photography. That’s the story the articles told, and when the only community group you can dig up is bubs + buds — which ran a 0–4 months mothers group in Term 2, 2026 with spaces limited to 15 per group — you get the vibe: this place is quietly organised.

Who would hate it here? If your idea of fun is late-night raves, city skyline views and never having to explain what a kids’ birthday cake looks like, Rochedale will make you twitch. Who would love it? Families, new parents, anyone into tidy renovations, Underwood Park playdates and the small-town luxury of a bakery and a bold Korean BBQ within suburb limits. We’ll keep the bbq tongs warm.
