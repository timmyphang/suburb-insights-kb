# Burbank (QLD)

Slug: `burbank-qld`

Back in the day our internet presence read like a confused travel agent: a patchwork of Time Out pieces about Burbank, California, an Urban List nod about "Luther’s Scoops" and a stack of mum-blog pages called Burbank Mom and MOMS Club Burbank West. There aren't any recent pieces in the pile — most of what the archive knows about us is decidedly pre-2024 — so consider this a nostalgia-soaked roast from locals who remember bookmarking things we weren’t always sure were about the same place.

Our morning looks like this in theory: we’d stroll through what the Burbank Mom homepage once billed as "Downtown Burbank" — yes, the same copy that name-checked movie theatres, fine dining and shopping — and wonder whether the Urban List review of Luther’s Scoops (which leaned into Luther Burbank’s horticultural legacy) was meant to be a compliment or a cry for help. Luther’s Scoops was the kind of mention that made us feel fancy for approximately 20 minutes.

We haven’t got a council scandal to flog, and thank heavens for that. Instead our local drama is the internet itself insisting we’re either Hollywood’s backyard or a suburban parenting utopia. The MOMS Club Burbank West pages make the point plain: volunteer-run playgroups, service projects and an email contact (burbankwestmoms@gmail.com) for anyone who wants in. Charming, organised and terrifyingly wholesome.

Who would hate living here? If you need your suburb to be constantly trending on lists, festivals and glossy city guides that publish every fortnight, you’ll be frustrated — our online footprint prefers being a cameo in other people’s articles. Who would love it here? Parents who want community, folks who like a quiet downtown that can still flex “movie theatre” when guests arrive, and anyone who appreciates a place that doesn’t try too hard to be someone else.

So yes, we know what commentators and old listicles say. They missed the best bit: we’re contentedly between two different Googles, and honestly, we wouldn’t have it any other way.
