# Maroochydore (QLD)

Slug: `maroochydore-qld`

Back in the day, we’d stumble out of an apartment on Alexandra Parade, blink at Maroochydore Beach and decide whether today called for a slow riverside walk or a stubbornly short surf. As The Urban List used to tell newcomers, the Maroochy to Mooloolaba trail was our default excuse for exercise, and Maroochydore Beach was the place to pretend we were doing it for the views.

Weekends used to look like this: a stroll past Sunshine Plaza, a detour to Cotton Tree Craft Markets if we were feeling artisanal, then a weak attempt at doing brunch right before a visit to the Homemaker Centre. The Urban List and Concrete Playground spent a lot of time writing about our cafés, the seven‑storey Rooftop Bar & Garden near Ocean Street, and family-friendly Northern Grounds on the Maroochy River, so you could humbly brag about having options without actually having to choose.

Local drama? We had it in bite-sized, very Queensland fashion. Pedestrian TV once captured our collective meltdown over a heatwave that saw every supermarket’s stock of Zooper Doopers vanish — we’ll never forget the cardboard graveyards where boxes used to sit. And for a while the Big Top Shopping Centre’s new Funlab precinct — Holey Moley, Archie Brothers and Strike Bowling shoved into one building — gave parents a single place to relinquish their children and their dignity all at once.

Who would hate living here? If your personality requires a 24/7 CBD with neon and non‑stop bar options, Maroochydore will feel like a sleepy extra in someone else’s drama. If you have an allergy to family events, Mocka’s Sunshine Coast launch and the Homemaker Centre’s Family Fun Days (Kenilworth Donuts included, historically speaking) will test you.

Who would love it? Families, laid‑back weekenders and anyone who knows the difference between a waterfront view and a lifestyle. As Sitchu put it, we balance work and play — and organisations like Raising Mamas and headspace in Maroochydore mean real community stuff happens here. We’re not flashy; we’re honest beachside charm with a rooftop cocktail and a khao soi nod from Samila Gaeng in our past‑tense foodie biography.
