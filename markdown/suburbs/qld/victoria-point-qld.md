# Victoria Point (QLD)

Slug: `victoria-point-qld`

We know the exact spot to watch the sun shrug off another polite Stradbroke Island sunrise — we launch our kayaks from Victoria Point like we own the bay, because according to Urban List that’s precisely what you do here. Mornings see us wobbling down to the water or corralling small people to WH Yeo Park, which Brisbane Kids called “a huge space” — yes, it’s fenced, shaded and built for chaos that looks cute from a distance.

Weekends are a rostered affair. Some of us used to book massages at the Body & Soul Beauty Clinics (the Urban List said the Victoria Point location was their flagship) and then limp over to the waterfront for a regrettable number of craft beers and cocktails at Lakehouse — which even appears in local listings and, notably, is the current meeting place for Keeping Mum’s mothers group (each Monday from 20 April 2026, 10–11:15am, if you want to judge our baby-wrangling technique).

We’re quietly proud of being on foodie lists in the past: Bluegrass BBQ at 2/34 Bunker Road got a shout in Urban List’s BBQ roundup, and Thai Tamarind has popped up in the Thai restaurants list — so no, we don’t only do sausage sizzles and park benches. And if you have a kid with boundless, inexplicable energy, Brisbane Kids points to Inflatable World here as one of the largest indoor inflatable playgrounds in Australia — paradise for tots, armageddon for parents’ feet.

We don’t have much time for suburb snobbery. If you’re the sort who needs boutique everything and a new mural on every roundabout, this won’t be your scene — you’ll hate our sensible playgrounds, our mums’ groups and the way we actually talk to our neighbours. If you’re allergic to sunscreen and community noticeboards, move along.

But if you love a lazy paddle, shamefully good BBQ lists, a playground that survives real kids and a local social scene that runs from Social Mums Club meet-ups (shoutout to the old-school organisers) to Keeping Mum Monday mornings at Lakehouse — well, you’ll fit right in. We’re Victoria Point: modest, waterfront-adjacent, and delightfully unbothered.
