# Waterford (QLD)

Slug: `waterford-qld`

We know the ritual: Saturday arvo, we corral the kids, find a table on the all‑weather terrace and pretend we’re only here for the view. Fitzy’s Waterford used to make that illusion work for us — Urban List still remembers its big comeback after a full‑scale makeover. They gave us an eight‑metre mega screen, a refreshed bistro menu of pub classics and weekly specials, and a terrace that looks straight onto the playground. Glamour, meet practicality.

Back in the day the write‑ups loved the makeover angle — shiny new sports‑bar energy, a mega screen you could land a plane on, and a dining room that said “we’ve got the party” without actually having to host it. We loved that Fitzy’s was watercooler central: kids play, adults pretend they’re watching the sport, someone orders something from the “bistro” which is a polite way of saying pub food that somehow tastes better when you’re on a weeknight.

There’s no local scandal in our brief — the papers weren’t interested in undercover turf wars or petty council feuds, just one reliable headline: Fitzy’s reopened and looked the part. So if you were after drama, you were misreading our feed; if you wanted a place that doubles as a playground lookout and weekend scoreboard, that was our scene.

Who would hate living here? If you’re the sort who needs a rotating roster of insta‑fancy degustations and candlelit small plates three nights a week, Waterford will test your patience. We’re not a Michelin map, we’re a terrace with a view and a bistro that does weekly specials like it’s an act of civic duty.

Who would love it here? Families who appreciate a pub that thinks about prams as much as pints, locals who like their sport loud and their kids within arm’s reach, and anyone who considers a mega screen a public service. Urban List called Fitzy’s our favourite watering hole after the makeover — and honestly, that about sums up what we are: a practical sort of pride, dressed up for a night out and still home by bedtime.
