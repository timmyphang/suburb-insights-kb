# Hamilton (QLD)

Slug: `hamilton-qld`

Back in the day we’d saunter down to the river at Portside Wharf like we owned the foreshore — linen blazers optional, sensible shoes inevitable. Sitchu used to call Hamilton “leafy” and “cosmopolitan”, and it’s true: galleries, cafés and parklands give it that polished-while-paddling vibe. If you squinted you could pretend you were in a seaside town, not five minutes from the CBD.

Weekend routine (the one we nostalgically brag about): a stroll along Racecourse Road to sample the parade of international restaurants the area was known for, then a slow drift over to Portside Wharf where Broadsheet once wrote about Fosh — a Hamptons-esque, riverfront seafood spot that served oysters, king prawns and Moreton Bay bugs alongside a very respectable wine list. We’ll always remember the light on the water and the way people used to talk about “oyster season” like it was a local religion.

There’s no scandal in our file — the local papers weren’t handing out feuds — so instead we’ll admit our civic vanity: for years the suburb’s biggest flex was being both leafy and annoyingly convenient. Urban List even flagged a grand plan called Hamilton Grove, a luxury precinct touted to kick off in early 2026 and reshape the skyline. Whether that becomes the thing that finally makes us queue for an espresso or just another construction hoarding, well, that’s the plot twist we’re happy to sit through with a take-away prawn roll.

Who would hate living here? People who loathe park benches, hate being near the water, or can’t stomach having an international restaurant three doors down. If you’re the type who wants anonymous suburbia with fewer brunch photos and more mystery, Hamilton’s politely glossy charm will feel like too much.

Who would love it? Anyone who likes riverfront dining, a leafy walk before work, period properties and schools that make you smug at end-of-year concerts. As Sitchu put it: cosmopolitan, close to the CBD and bitingly charming — and honestly, we wouldn’t have it any other way.
