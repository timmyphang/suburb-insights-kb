# Forest Lake (QLD)

Slug: `forest-lake-qld`

Back in the day — and by that we mean the Urban List era of yore — our best mornings began at Forest Lake Parklands under the blue shade sails. We’d throw down our exercise mats for the 5.30–6.30pm Monday wellbeing sessions (they ran weekly until school holidays, as Urban List politely recorded), feel virtuous, then pretend that counts as our cardio for the week. That’s the kind of community energy we’re quietly proud of.

We’ve also been the kind of suburb that features in slightly surprising places: Concrete Playground once listed Pizza Hut Forest Lake as part of a one-day pizza-box exchange promotion, which is to say, yes, we have our own slice of national marketing drama. And if you like your suburb to double as a property portfolio, Sitchu has crawled through our houses — there was a listing at 20 Richmond Place and another at 5 Canterbury Close that read like a suburban romcom: open-plan living, large bedrooms, built-ins. Domestic bliss, staged for internet tastemakers.

Urban List even drove our streets in search of Brisbane’s best Christmas lights for 2025, which explains why half the cul-de-sacs look like someone lost a battle with a string of LEDs. We embrace it. We roll down our windows and gawp with the rest of them.

Who would hate it here? People who want a round-the-clock nightclub scene, or those who require artisanal everything and an endless parade of buzzy cafés on every corner. Also anyone allergic to lawns, playgrounds and the faint hum of suburban neighbourliness probably won’t make it past the roundabout.

Who would love it here? Families who rate parks and community classes over city cocktails, bargain hunters who enjoy a Pizza Hut campaign cameo, first-home buyers eyeing those Sitchu-style specimens with sensible floorplans, and anyone who likes their Christmas lights with a side of friendly gawking. We’re not pretending to be the centre of cultural gravity — but we’ve got green space, predictable good neighbours, and enough local character to keep us all entertained. Come for the lake, stay for the blue shade sails, and don’t be surprised if you leave with a slightly sunburnt ego and a newfound respect for suburban sincerity.
