# Ferny Grove (QLD)

Slug: `ferny-grove-qld`

We breeze out of Lanita Road on a Saturday like we own the cul-de-sacs — from 57 Lanita Road to 229 Lanita Road, the property listings will tell you we’re either an “urban escape” or an acreage wannabe, depending on how many gumtrees you’ve paid for. Back in the day the papers loved reminding people we’re a trained commute away: Sitchu noted it’s a 40-minute train ride into the CBD from Ferny Grove station, and some listings brag a 10-minute walk to that very platform.

For years the Ferny Grove line has been our pride and our mild inconvenience. Concrete Playground flagged the smart‑ticketing trial that began on June 6, 2022, when we were among the first to tap on with a credit card, smartphone or smartwatch between Ferny Grove and Central — futuristic, until they put buses on for track works and someone reminded us suburbia still runs on weekend maintenance. The Cross River Rail plans even pencilled our name into a 2025 network running from Ferny Grove through Central and South Bank to Cleveland, which made us feel momentarily very important on the map.

Food scene flex? Broadsheet ran with Little Nyonya’s rise from a market stall to two spots, one at The Grove Sports Club in Ferny Grove, serving nasi lemak, laksa and wok‑fired bits — a reminder that our bowlo does better curry than some inner-city gastropubs ever will.

If you loathe a proper commute, weekend engineering buses and the idea of being judged by your council’s train timetable, you’ll hate it here. If your whole romantic ideal is late-night cocktail bars and 24/7 neon, Ferny Grove will feel like a polite yawn.

Who’ll love it? Families. Sitchu and the real estate listings sell the school catchments — Patrick’s Road State School and Ferny Grove State High School — and MumsPages keeps a trove of school holiday camps, ballet and acrobatics classes for the tiny locals. We’re the suburb for people who want front‑yard space, a sensible train into town (on most days) and bowls-club laksa that reminds you Brisbane wasn’t invented overnight. We’re modest, a little proud, and we will quietly win your heart — or at least feed it nasi lemak.
