# Durack (QLD)

Slug: `durack-qld`

We like to start the weekend with a slow roll down Bowhill Road, pointing out the parcel of land everyone pretends is still boring industrial. Back in the day Concrete Playground told us the spot off Bowhill Road used to be a sewage treatment plant, and that the Archerfield Wetlands District Park was planned to sprawl across four hectares as part of a 150‑hectare Archerfield Wetlands transformation. Fancy, isn’t it — from pipes to paperbark in the space of a press clipping.

Our Saturday morning routine is mostly aspirational: we stand where the old plant stood and practice our birdwatching faces, imagining boardwalks, swampy glories and the kind of nature signage that makes people forget where they parked. Because the reporting on this was older, we say it like a memory — “was going to be” — rather than like a hip new thing to tag on the ‘gram.

There isn’t exactly a local tabloid feeding frenzy here. The most dramatic plot twist Durack seems to have offered newspapers was ‘sewage plant to wetlands’, which, frankly, is the kind of glow‑up story we will forgive the suburb for repeating. For clarity: we are not the place Time Out and Concrete Playground were rhapsodising about when they praised the Fanny Durack Aquatic Centre and the Splash kiosk with its chip‑butties in Petersham — that’s a different Durack reference entirely, and yes, that Splash has fans who love a poolside chippy butty.

We also share our name with a faraway Durack in WA, where El Questro Station and homestead make for Kimberley dreams. So if you turned up expecting thermal springs and a million‑acre cattle station, we’ll politely point you at the map and invite you to stay for the wetlands plans instead.

Who would hate it here? If you want instant boutique cafes and endless nightlife, you’ll be uncomfortable with our patient, slightly swampy ambition. Who would love it? People who enjoy reclamation projects, long horizons and the idea that a sewage plant can, in theory, become a park you can actually be proud of. We do.
