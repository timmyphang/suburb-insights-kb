# Virginia (QLD)

Slug: `virginia-qld`

We wake up on Sandgate Road, blink at 1904 Sandgate Rd and ask, “Which spa are we today?” — because the local listings used to be having a personality crisis. Urban List and Time Out wrote about Merse Wellness landing on Sandgate Road (they promised you could soak, steam, float and cold plunge), while Sitchu had a Heavenly Spa by Westin listed at the same address. Only Virginia could be famous for wellness and spreadsheet confusion.

By mid-morning we’d amble past where Vend Cafe and Marketplace Virginia used to be the reliable combo of breakfast plus shopping — the kind of place Urban List singled out as kid-friendly and Sitchu even booked for November pop‑ups during the Christmas market season. Parents loved the convenience; we loved the people-watching while pretending our cappuccino was tax-deductible.

If we needed to knock a few balls out of our frustration we could have popped into the indoor golf simulators the guides praised — Urban List called Virginia “a breath of fresh air” for indoor golf. And if retail therapy is more your speed, Sitchu once trumpeted the suburb’s seven‑day indoor market with over 100 stalls like it was Brisbane’s answer to a weekend away you didn’t have to book.

Local drama? The only turf war we’ve seen is between spa listings and market stallholders fighting over who owns 1904 Sandgate Rd in Google’s eyes. We’ll let the search engine sort that one out — we’ve got steam rooms to argue about.

Who’d hate living here: people who can’t stand being five minutes from Brisbane Airport, the DFO or the Gateway Arterial, or who refuse to be within cooee of Banyo Village amenities. If you’re allergic to convenience, quiet markets and the occasional wellness fad, move on.

Who’d love it here: families after a kid-friendly cafe and pop-up market, bargain hunters who worship a seven-day market, golfers who prefer simulators to windmills, and anyone who thinks a spa‑and‑shop morning is a perfectly acceptable religion. We’re not flashy, we’re useful — and a little bit smug about it. Welcome to Virginia, QLD: come for the cold plunge confusion, stay for the markets.
