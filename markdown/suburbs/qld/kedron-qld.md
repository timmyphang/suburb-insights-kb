# Kedron (QLD)

Slug: `kedron-qld`

We know the exact spot the Sunday queue used to split into two camps: the keen brunchers and the people who just wanted to sit in a country‑barn chair and pretend they live on acreage. Back in the day we’d line up outside The Farm House at 9 Somerset Road — Urban List even put it in a 2026 roundup — and pretend the pancakes were worth the ritual.

Our morning ritual was part pilgrimage, part commute: a spin along the Kedron Brook Bikeway (yes, the family‑friendly green ribbon Concrete Playground praised from Mitchelton to Nundah) and then a detour through the Kedron Brook Rd/High‑street blur where Wilston’s venues once elbowed into our postcode. Antica at 70 Kedron Brook Road, the Alcove at 92B, Half Moon Wine Store’s northside expansion, and that Fritzenberger move into 4/99 Kedron Brook Road — they all read like our weekend guest list, even if some of those headlines were technically Wilston’s.

We’re the suburb with useful mundanities: Kedron State High School (listed at Park Road, Wooloowin), Queensland Ambulance Service tucked on 125 Kedron Park Rd, and Kedron Family Dental on 138 Gympie Road — the places you name when you’re updating next‑of‑kin details and pretending you’ll finally floss.

Local colour? We had Nana & Da’s at 12 Homebush Road, Istanbul 1923 on 385 Gympie Road (the iskender people still sigh about), and The Costume Company at 19 Kitchener Road — yes, the one Urban List mentioned where you could buy vampire teeth and fake blood for that one themed party nobody will admit to organising.

Civic flex: Concrete Playground flagged Kedron Brook and Oxley Creek for a 2032 Olympics‑era revamp, which is council‑speak for “we’ll keep what we like and landscape the rest.” Upper Kedron’s growth made it into property listings, so half our relatives started sending us renovation inspo links and the other half sent slightly smug “think of the resale!” messages.

Who would hate it here? People who crave edgy nightlife or to be first in line for every new thing. Who would love it? Families, cyclists, brunch devotees and anyone who likes their weekend to come with reliable amenities and a smell of wood‑fired pizza drifting over from the high street of our slightly antic, quietly loved corner of Brisbane.
