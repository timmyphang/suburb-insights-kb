# Edens Landing (QLD)

Slug: `edens-landing-qld`

We live on the street that turns into a suburban theme park every December — 11 Lucy Drive — and yes, Urban List put us on the Brisbane Christmas-lights pilgrimage (they even paired us with 7 Benarkin Close, 40 Gem Circuit and 73 Timor Avenue). If you want spectacle, we’ll give you spectacle: the gumtrees wear LEDs and the letterbox competition is real.

Don’t expect a glossy Broadsheet food crawl through our streets. For what passes as lifestyle press love around here, the nearest thing was Broadsheet writing about Eden Gardens in Carseldine — Dragonfly café, ducks on Gympie Road — which is lovely, just not in our postcode. Back in the day the mags treated Edens Landing like the “after” shot for festive ornamentation, not a nightlife hotspot.

Our morning consists of walking past suburban lawns still drying from the neighbour’s latest light rehearsal, and nodding at the person who remembers when 11 Lucy Drive was just a house, not a spectacle. There isn’t an only-in-our-suburb scandal to report — unless you count the Great Tinsel Tug-of-War between houses 9 and 13.

Who would hate living here? People after a permanent Broadsheet Hot List lifestyle — if you require front-page hipster validation, prepare to commute. Who would love it? Families who treat Christmas as a civic duty, people who enjoy slow streets and louder decorations, and anyone who’s ever thought, “Why have one animated reindeer when you can have twelve?”

We’re proud. We’re a little glittery. And when December rolls around, we’re the reason the rest of Brisbane takes the slow drive out of curiosity.
