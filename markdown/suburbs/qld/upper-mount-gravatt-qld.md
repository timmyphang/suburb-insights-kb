# Upper Mount Gravatt (QLD)

Slug: `upper-mount-gravatt-qld`

Back in the day we used to joke that Upper Mount Gravatt was where the city came to learn how to party quietly. We still know every loophole in that plan.

Our Saturday ritual used to be simple: pick up the fluffiest Dough Ma doughnut from 1932 Logan Road (as Urban List remembered), wrestle the kids into sunscreen and head to Mount Gravatt Park off Logan Road or Abbeville Street Park — the council was, in fact, planning upgrades there in December 2025 (Brisbane Kids noted). If the kids were hyper, there was always the Hibiscus Sports Complex Pool at 90 Klumpp Road with its bright yellow twisty water slide — on a strict timetable of course. For evenings when we wanted to feel like tourists in our own postcode, Westfield used to trot out the big attractions: Hijinx Hotel and Holey Moley at Westfield Mt Gravatt (Kessels Road) turned the mall into a carnival of mini‑golf, karaoke and claw‑machine bravado (Concrete Playground and Urban List gave the full preview).

We watched South Austin — the Tex‑Mex cantina from the Barbecue Mafia crew — open in town, promising hand‑made tortillas, smoked meats and frozen margaritas (Broadsheet hyped the arrival). The Whisk, our homegrown patisserie, even earned a mention when Coffee Anthology expanded into the CBD — proof we’re small‑town in postcode only (Broadsheet again).

If you can’t stand photobooth backdrops or the smell of chipotle, you’ll hate us. In The Box Photo & Karaoke at 2049 Kessels Road gives you more props than a soap opera, and Rebel Garden City at Westfield makes sure we can test sneakers and play FIFA between runs to the supermarket (Urban List, yes).

Who would love it here? Families, obviously — Newnham Hotel’s playground and weekend kids’ stuff made parenting marginally easier (Sitchu/Brisbane Kids), and the Mount Gravatt Mini Golf & Foot Golf next to the Showgrounds is forever cheerful and unpretentious (Time Out). We’ve got Sunday markets, bus links to the CBD and even an I‑MED Radiology Network if you overdo the margaritas (Sitchu).

So yes, we’re a little suburban, a touch chaotic and inexplicably proud of our yellow slide. Come for the doughnuts and mini‑golf; stay because you can’t beat a Lindwall Street Christmas light show when it goes off (Time Out).
