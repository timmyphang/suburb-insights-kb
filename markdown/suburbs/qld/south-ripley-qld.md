# South Ripley (QLD)

Slug: `south-ripley-qld`

We know South Ripley like the back of our hand — which is mostly useful for folding prams into cars after a morning class. Back in the day the internet’s idea of us was either a list of nearby swimming spots or mums comparing nappies: Urban List once compiled "12 Of The Best Waterfalls Near Brisbane" (yes, we all go on those day trips), and Everyday Mums ran the Mother and Baby Group listings for Ipswich, Springfield and Forest Lake — you could even "Book a Baby Class" or lose an afternoon on their Childcare Blog.

Our Saturday ritual is hardly glamorous: ferrying small humans to a class someone found via Everyday Mums, then convincing each other that a hike to a waterfall makes us outdoorsy parents rather than people who forgot to sort the washing. We don’t have the glossy profiles — we’re proudly suburban and slightly damp from the first rainy walk to the car.

Who would hate living here? If your ideal weekend is rooftop bars and 24/7 artisanal anything, you’ll file us under "quaint emergency." We thrive on car seats, playgroups and the kind of local knowledge that would bore a city influencer to tears.

Who would love it? Young families who want friendly, practical suburbs linked into Ipswich and Springfield resources, weekend waterfall seekers who read Urban List, and anyone who values a community that actually shows up for classes and coffee runs (the latter organised via the mums’ WhatsApp we will not name). South Ripley: unglamorous, useful, and very much ours.
