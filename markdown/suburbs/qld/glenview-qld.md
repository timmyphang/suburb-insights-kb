# Glenview (QLD)

Slug: `glenview-qld`

Back in the day we’d tell people Glenview was the kind of place where your weekend plan is decided by whether the dam is calm enough to look at and the BMX track has less mud than last time. As The Urban List once noted, The Hut Glenview was the poster child for that vibe — an eco container‑style Hinterland stay, two bedrooms, a fenced outdoor area (yes, pet‑friendly), and about a $150 a night flex for anyone who likes roughing it with a mattress and a view of the sports fields. We still whisper about it like it’s a local legend.

Our mornings are modest: the sun climbs over Ewan Maddock Dam and we pretend the water is our private fjord while the town warms up. The kids head for the BMX track, the footy mob hit the sports fields, and the rest of us argue about whether ‘eco container’ is chic or just an expensive shed.

Full disclosure: there’s also a Glenview that keeps showing up in internet searches — Glenview Retreat, Emerald — which Concrete Playground once lauded for “secluded country elegance” and a private garden where the pup can roam. Nice, but that’s a different postcode and we reserve the right to be jealous.

There’s no big scandal to fling about here, no council wars in the papers — which is either a blessing or precisely why our gossip contains so many very specific complaints about potholes and whose dog keeps using the oval as a personal bathroom. We don’t need front‑page drama; we’re an area that gets by on dam sunsets, community sport and a weird pride in being quietly off the beaten track.

Who would hate living here? People who need nightly rooftop bars, artisan everything and a tram every six minutes. Who would love it? Families after affordable weekend escapes, dog owners who appreciate a fenced yard, BMX kids, and anyone who measures a good day by how calm Ewan Maddock Dam looked at dawn.

We’re Glenview: not broke, not bougie — just gloriously, stubbornly hinterland.
