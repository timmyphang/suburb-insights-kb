# Grange (QLD)

Slug: `grange-qld`

Back in the day we’d roll out of bed, sigh at the lawn, and wander down to Kedron Brook & Bikeway like it’s our personal coastal walk — only with fewer seagulls and more lycra. The suburbs here are quiet for a reason: Lanham Park and Grange Forest Park give you plenty of room to pretend you’re outdoorsy without actually sweating in public.

We used to brag that our weekend circuit had something for every hobby: The Plant Bunker at 126 Days Road was that gloriously leafy trap where you go in for a fern and leave with a New York‑style cookie and a hand­trowel. As The Urban List put it, Bella & Tortie had graduated from online to bricks‑and‑mortar, selling canelés and sausage rolls that made us suspiciously proud of our pastry standards. Huskk Cafe at 2/8 Days Road got a shout for its cosy minimalist vibe — which is to say, we’d queue politely and pretend to be bemused by the menu.

If you bend quietly towards zen, SoHo Yoga at 16 Blandford Street was noted for its “stunning Grange digs” and something like 35 weekly classes — great if you like your spirituality served with good lighting. Sitchu’s old listings even name‑checked our local institutions: Grange Thistle Football Club, Grange Social Bowls Club and the much‑walked Kedron Brook & Bikeway. And yes, someone once put 13 Prince Street on a real estate listing with the same tired optimism we reserve for renovation TV.

The only real debate we can sustain is artisanal and edible: which sausage roll reigns supreme. It’s a low bar, but give us a hot pie and we’ll run a ballot.

Who hates living here? People who crave neon and all‑night kebabs — we’re cul‑de‑sac central with minimal traffic and a suspiciously calm soundtrack. Who loves it? Plant collectors, yoga converts, parents who relish park options, bowlers, local‑club loyalists and anyone who’d rather queue for a flaky pastry than jostle at a nightclub. For years the local write‑ups have said it best: small, leafy, and quietly smug — and honestly, we wouldn’t have it any other way.
