# Taigum (QLD)

Slug: `taigum-qld`

We know Taigum like the back of our sunburnt hands, and yes, that includes every squeaky swing at the playground we used to drag the kids to before brunch.

Mornings used to start with a pilgrimage to the Hidden World Playground on Roghan Road — Time Out once wrote it up — complete with wooden pavilions, boardwalks over a sandpit and that unforgettable 360-degree tyre swing. We’d stand there, coffee-less and heroic, wondering how many more Velcro shoes we’d have to retrieve from the sand before lunch.

The suburb’s practical side shows up in listings and community staples. There was a very on-point Sitchu listing for a light-filled three-storey townhouse on Handford Road; perfect for those who like vertical living and not having to mow a front lawn. And for parents who like their childcare with a mission statement, Taigum Child Care Centre opened on 12 January 2015 as a not-for-profit, church-based service at 241 Church Rd — the site lists hours of 6:30am–6:30pm Mon–Fri, and staff names like Katie (Director) and Penny (Admin Manager) if you enjoy knowing who sorts the snack roster.

We’ll own the awkward bits. Broadsheet flagged a measles alert in the old days after a child who’d returned from overseas visited Just Pharmacy at Murrumba Downs Shopping Centre and Taigum Square on Beams Road — between 10.15am and 11.15am on a July Friday, if you want to be precise. Yes, we laughed nervously, checked our immunisation history and then laughed some more.

Who would hate living here? If you can’t handle communal sandpits, 360-degree tyre swings, or the knowledge that local headlines can be oddly specific about pharmacy visits, this isn’t your lane. Also, anyone who needs a boutique latte-and-laptop scene on every corner will probably file Taigum under “nice, quiet, not-very-Instagrammable”.

Who would love it? Families who value pragmatic childcare, buyers after a light-filled townhouse on Handford Road, and people who prefer playgrounds that actually make kids play instead of just posing for selfies. We’re not flashy. We’re dependable, a bit sandy, and we know our Roghan Road swings by name.
