# Coomera (QLD)

Slug: `coomera-qld`

We know the sound of our suburb before our own alarm goes off: the distant whoop from Dreamworld, like a civic rooster. We’ve learned to tell time by whether the rollercoaster is doing laps at sunrise or practising for evening fireworks — Dreamworld on Dreamworld Pkwy has been our skyline since forever, and Time Out still reminds people it’s the Gold Coast’s big, loud brag.

On a typical weekend we’d stroll past where Pause Button Cafe was billed as Coomera’s cheeky new Asian-leaning brunch spot on Dreamworld Pkwy, wave at the Westfield Coomera carpark (103 Foxwell Road — yes, the one with the indoor playgrounds listed for kids who never get tired), then swerve toward whatever cheap-night-market temptation Dreamworld was hosting that week. Urban List used to point out the Night Markets at Dreamworld like a local secret that somehow still had cheap entry and questionable souvenirs.

For years the local conversation has been the kind property developers dream about. Ardent Leisure once announced plans for a 250-room Dreamworld Resort — hotel, bungalows, the whole tourist-park fantasy — and Urban List ran headlines about a $2.5–$3 million Merse Wellness Spa coming to town, billed as our very own standalone bathhouse. We’ve been promised fancy recovery experiences and enormous resorts, which is half the joy: the waiting room of potential.

We’re also a place pets clearly rule. Time Out named Coomera on the Gold Coast pet-packed leaderboard, which explains why you can’t walk two streets without encountering someone trying to explain their dog’s social calendar. The Social Mums Club kept us grounded too, with free local meet-ups that made parenting feel less like a spectator sport and more like a communal BYO-nap plan.

Who would hate living here? People who crave silence, solitude and the smug belief that suburbia is a quiet suburbia — also anyone allergic to small, yappy citizens (the pets, obviously). Who would love it? Families who treat theme-park drama as background music, pet people who revel in being top of the leaderboard, mums who like organised chaos with a side of community, and anyone who finds the idea of a bathhouse spa-and-resort future wildly comforting. We’re loud, a little busy, and strangely proud of it.
