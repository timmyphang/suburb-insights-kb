# Currimundi (QLD)

Slug: `currimundi-qld`

Back in the day we’d tell anyone who’d listen that Currimundi is the kind of place where your morning commute might be paddling past someone’s toddler and a very committed Labrador. We’re an hour north of Brisbane, and our skyline is mostly paperbark and the occasional sunscreen bottle left behind at Currimundi Lake.

Weekends used to look like this: we’d drag the esky down to Currimundi Lake Conservation Park, find shade under the trees for a picnic, and watch the calm, shallow water do what it does best — make small humans feel like Olympians. You could hire kayaks and stand-up paddle boards from the nearby surf shop and pretend we weren’t minutes from the highway. By sunset, the Currimundi Lake Twilight Market (the third Friday of every month) used to spill out with food trucks, pop-up bars and live music — Urban List called it a prime sunset-watching spot, and we won’t argue.

We’ve always had our indulgences. Sunshine Sunshine Espresso used to be the place of pastry-based dreams — donuts with M&M’s and Nutella injectables were apparently a thing people queued for. The thrift-hunters among us loved Beautiful You Funky Op Shop; it was the local staple if you wanted pre-loved threads, books or shoes without leaving town.

If you hate dogs, you’ll hate Currimundi Beach — it’s listed as a huge stretch of unleashed doggy heaven (beach access 255 to 262), where dogs, kids and owners all do their best impression of a sand rugby scrum. Time Out once reminded folks that the lake opens onto the ocean but keeps a gentle-moving current, which is precisely why families drifted here like migrating seagulls.

We’re not pretending we’re a cultural capital. For years the local vibe has been low-key: markets, kayaks, op shops, and mutts who run faster than your fitness tracker can handle. Who would love it here? Families, dog owners, sunset chasers, and anyone whose idea of a night out is a twilight market with a Nutella-injected donut in hand. Who would hate it? People who need flashing lights and late-night cocktails — we’ll keep your quiet corner, thanks very much.
