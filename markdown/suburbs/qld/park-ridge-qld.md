# Park Ridge (QLD)

Slug: `park-ridge-qld`

We know who we are: the Park Ridge that answers to Logan, not the bougie “Parkridge” by Noosa with resort vibes. We’re the postcode that gets mistaken in emails, not the one with FISH Parkridge on the waterfront. Urban List used to list that Noosa Parkridge — FISH Parkridge and The Grounds Providore were their shiny examples — but that was over on the Sunshine Coast, not here. Calm down, influencers.

Our mornings are less smashed-little-cupcake-and-toast and more checking whether the bus actually turned up. Because yes, remember March 2025? Tropical Cyclone Alfred didn’t just make the weather dramatic — Concrete Playground pointed out that all public transport across southeast Queensland was cancelled, and our own Park Ridge Transit (along with Clarks Logan City) was on the list of operators hit. We folded our laundry into an emergency kit and learned every Uber surge price in Logan by heart.

Petty local drama? It’s mostly argument by text chain about whether being confused for Parkridge Noosa is an upgrade. Spoiler: it isn’t, unless you like paying for coastal brunches and pretending you meant to be “resort-style.”

Who would hate living here: anyone who demands reliable buses on cyclone notice, or thinks a providore is just another word for a corner shop.

Who would love it: people who actually know the difference between Park Ridge and Parkridge, who appreciate honest suburbs, and who can laugh when Google maps whispers “did you mean Noosa?” We’ll keep our local pride and let the tourists have the prawns.
