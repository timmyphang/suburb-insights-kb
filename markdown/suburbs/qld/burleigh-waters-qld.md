# Burleigh Waters (QLD)

Slug: `burleigh-waters-qld`

You know you’re in Burleigh Waters when our weekend planning meeting looks like a food critic’s mood board and a parent-teacher email thread had a baby.

Back in the day we’d start the morning pilgrimage to Profile Coffee Co‑Lab on West Burleigh Road — the kind of place where we pretended the co-roasting space was an artisanal excuse to be late for everything. If you were feeling virtuous (or trying to out-vegan your neighbour), Doko Demo V on Sunlight Drive was the plant-based flex we name-dropped at barbecues. And yes, there was always the sensible option: Yum Cha Gallery on 7 Classic Way, because nothing says community like sharing dumplings and pretending the kids won’t steal your last prawn.

Our very refined local identity, as chronicled in the papers and lifestyle lists, oscillated between “very multicultural vegan discovery” and “delightfully bougie deli.” Ambarella Fine Foods at Santa Maria Court used to be the place we blamed for our suddenly sophisticated sandwich orders — one minute you were buying mince, the next you were discussing charcuterie like it’s a personality trait. And for those who wanted fizz with attitude, Hard Fizz HQ Taproom on Hibiscus Haven was the immersive hard seltzer spot the magazines politely called “a scene.”

If we’re sentimental, we’ll also remind you that practical moments made headlines too — remember when vaccination clinics were set up at the Bunnings site here? Thrilling stuff. As for schools, Marymount College gets the nod for keeping things steady when the rest of us were deciding whether to brunch or buy bulk gelato.

Who’d hate living here? People who crave anonymous suburbs or those allergic to having too many genuinely good food options within shouting distance. Who’d love it? Food obsessives, coffee nerds, parents who need a reliable school and a deli that doubles as a lifesaver, and anyone who enjoys a place that can be both earnest vegan haven and yum cha capital in the same breath.

We’re proud, a little smug, and yes — we’ll argue about the best dumpling until the deli runs out of gelato.
