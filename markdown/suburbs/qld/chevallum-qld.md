# Chevallum (QLD)

Slug: `chevallum-qld`

We live in Chevallum, QLD — the kind of place that makes you lower your voice and say “hinterland” like you’ve just ordered something artisanal. Back in the day the internet’s evidence of our existence was delightfully specific: a cat cafe, a school, a ski-lake event and one famously twinkling house.

Mornings for us are nowhere near frantic. If you wanted a novelty at 129 Dales Road there used to be The Cat Retreat Cafe — Urban List said they had up to eight beautiful cats to cuddle. If that sounds like civilisation, you’re not wrong; if you’re picturing a bar crawl, you’re in the wrong postcode. Our community directory quietly noted Chevallum covers about 9 km² and sits in postcode 4555, and that cheery little bureaucratic paragraph is basically the local tourism brochure.

Our social life, such as it is, revolved around the one thing the directory called “the only community building”: Chevallum State School at 460 Chevallum Road. School fetes, notices on the door, the odd sausage sizzle — that’s our council chamber. Once upon a time an events roundup even pointed visitors to Marshall Ski Lakes at 1 Leafy Lane for regional goings-on, and someone’s festive spirit earned a mention on the Sunshine Coast Christmas lights list for 347 Chevallum Road. That’s about as much local drama as Google will hand you.

If you hate quiet, you’d loathe us. If your Instagram requires three artisan latte shots before breakfast, you’ll get restless. If you need a shopping strip that never sleeps, we’ll disappoint — our online footprint is fondly modest, not trending.

But here’s the thing: we like being small. We’re the hinterland spot for people who prefer community listed in a directory to a buzzing nightlife postcode. We’re for families who appreciate a local school that doubles as a town hall, for cat people who once had a cafe with actual cats, and for anyone who thinks the best kind of lights are the ones on a suburban house that made a regional roundup. Come for the quiet, stay because you own the corner of Chevallum nobody else did.
