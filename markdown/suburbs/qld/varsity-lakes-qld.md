# Varsity Lakes (QLD)

Slug: `varsity-lakes-qld`

You know the bit where everyone pretends seaside suburbs are all palms and pavements? We live in the bit with lakes, gin and a community noticeboard that knows your name. On a Sunday we used to wander past the always‑humming Blackboard — Urban List called it a love‑at‑first‑visit cafe — and pretend we were only popping in for eggs benedict. Then we'd detour to Side Piece at 2 Capital Court (yes, the one Urban List praised for its loaded "sandos") and debate whether toasties qualify as ambition.

We remember when train chaos felt like a rite of passage. Broadsheet warned about that morning when an overhead fault brought trains to a standstill and buses were replacing trains between Kuraby and Varsity Lakes — the less romantic version of a lakeside commute. And there was chatter about Cross River Rail plans that were set to slot Varsity Lakes into a new 2025 sector; promises of better connections always make for nice dinner party speculation.

Our nightlife, such as it is, once came with a botanical twist. Wildflower Gin Distilling (486 Scottsdale Drive) opened a cellar‑door‑turned‑bar and was serving cocktails like a lamington espresso martini, running gin‑making classes and even welcoming dogs — Concrete Playground ran it like it was the coolest way to learn about juniper and regret. The Lash Spa was an option for anyone wanting to emerge from a community event looking less like they'd been reffing at a local clean‑up and more like they'd been sunning on Instagram — Urban List loved the plush fit‑out.

Who would hate it here? People who insist true coastal living must include constant surf and zero community meetings. If you can't forgive the occasional bus replacement, or you recoil at the idea of a Robina Lions Club breakfast after a Clean Up Australia Day at Jim Harris Park, Varsity Lakes will test your patience.

Who would love it? Families who sign up for playgroups at the Varsity Lakes Community Resource Centre on Lyn Muller Lane, volunteers who fill Jim Harris Park on clean‑up day, gin tasters and dog‑walkers who once found a dog‑friendly gin bar. We’ll take our lakes, our cafes (as the listings once promised) and our slightly chaotic commute — because where else can you get that much local colour and a lamington cocktail in the same postcode?
