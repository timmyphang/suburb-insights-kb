# Golden Beach (QLD)

Slug: `golden-beach-qld`

We start our mornings at 1 Esplanade, Golden Beach — not because we're instagramming (we're not), but because the Caloundra Coastal Walk is 25 kilometres of absolute show-off coastline and it’s rude not to salute it. We know every headland between here and Mooloolaba, how the tide writes itself across the sand and which bench gives the best excuse to pretend we’re doing "research".

Weekends here are slow in the way only a proper beach village can be: families dragging buckets and memories towards the patch of coastline that travel lists keep stealing from us. Broadsheet has, at various times, kindly reminded city folk to "go beyond Surfer’s Paradise" and yes, they do keep finding our bit of coast when they want something less loud. Concrete Playground and Sitchu have also name-checked Golden Beach in their beach round-ups — which is flattering, even if none of them actually live here to help with the bins. (Speedy Bins used to list Golden Beach in its Sunshine Coast delivery area — we notice these things.)

We’ll admit a soft spot for the food lore: Urban List wrote about Pizzeria Sisily and the venerable Cafe Sisily at 40 Landsborough Parade — noting the cannoli that used to make people clap politely with their mouths full. Pizzeria Sisily was billed as the cheeky younger sibling to the cafe; that feels very Golden Beach — small, slightly theatrical, and keen to be liked.

Who would hate living here? If you need neon nightclubs, 24-hour latte theatrics or the kind of permanent festival energy that eats traffic for breakfast, this stretch will feel like a village pretending to be a holiday. We don’t hum like the Gold Coast towers; we hum like sea-glass.

Who will love it? Walkers, stroller parents, sun-hour hoarders and anyone who enjoys the sting-free pleasure of a coast that quietly keeps getting named in other people’s lists. Even Time Out has a Golden Beach in Hong Kong (yes, really), so if you like sharing a famous name without the queues, this is your postcode. We love it here — imperfect, sandy, and annoyingly photogenic.
