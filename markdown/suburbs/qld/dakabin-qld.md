# Dakabin (QLD)

Slug: `dakabin-qld`

We roll out of bed, shrug into something sensible and head up to Dakabin lookout — because where else are you going to claim panoramic ocean views within chatting distance of your own postcode? Back in the day Urban List even sent us a nod for that hike, which feels almost quaint now that the hospitality big-players have clocked our skyline.

Speaking of glow-ups, we watched the Comiskey Group tiptoe into our lives. Urban List kept teasing a sneak peek of the Dakabin Hotel — “handiwork” of the team behind Eatons Hill Hotel, Sandstone Point Hotel and The Doonan, they splashed words like “signature restaurant” and a two-kitchen concept around like confetti. For a suburb that used to compete mainly in quiet charm, suddenly we were being compared to Sandstone Point’s $250 million expansion and the North Lakes precinct. Big adjectives. Bigger pillows.

We’re not pretending everyone loved the idea (some of us cherish our low-key hikes), but we also couldn’t help admiring the theatre of it all: locals who still remember the simple weekend trail and newcomers doing their best “we always liked Dakabin” poses.

Who would hate it here? If you require eternal anonymity and a zero-percent chance of boutique hotel energy creeping into your suburb, you won’t last a weekend. Who would love it? People who want a lookout with actual sea-sky views and front-row seats to how a small place gets a hospitality makeover — even if it’s only in Urban List headlines for now.
