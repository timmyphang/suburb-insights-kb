# Rochedale South (QLD)

Slug: `rochedale-south-qld`

We’re the lot who’ve driven every cul‑de‑sac in Rochedale South until the satnav begged for mercy. Mornings here used to start with the gentle promise of bushland views — you only had to squint at the listing photos for 10 Fulica Street to feel zen — and end with someone refreshing the Sitchu pages to admire the dual‑living at 25 Minerva Street like it’s the Mona Lisa.

Weekend routine: a slow walk past Underwood Park because, yes, the Sitchu copywriters weren’t lying about the greenery. We’d watch kids practise footy — Soccajoeys was the name that cropped up for classes in nearby Underwood — and then pop our phones back open to check whether 9 Circle Court’s tasteful renovations had sprouted another “before” photo. If you liked house porn, Rochedale South was a buffet: split‑level elegance at 9 Circle Court, lowset brick with cathedral ceilings at 14 Clonara Street, and the perpetually renovated haven on 43 Parfrey Road that name‑checked Rochedale Shopping Village like a badge of honour.

If there’s one local drama worth mentioning, it’s the thrilling world of home improvement directories. MumsPages had us covered for roofing and gutters, plus school holiday camps and activities — which, for a suburb that quietly doubles as logistics for exhausted parents, felt like gospel. The MomCo’s South group and Keeping‑Mum listings were the sort of community scaffolding that meant playgroups outnumbered public scandals by about ten to one.

Who would hate living here? If you need 24/7 neon, rooftop bars, or a constant stream of curated brunch spots, you’ll find Rochedale South achingly peaceful and suspiciously suburban. Urban List once rounded up the best breakfasts on Brisbane’s south side, but if you’re hunting Insta‑famous cafés inside our postcode, you’ll be disappointed.

Who’d love it? Families who want space, renovators who like to play ‘before and after’ on Sitchu, mums hunting a local tribe via MomCo or Keeping‑Mum, and anyone who thinks a proper yard and Underwood Park are underrated luxuries. We’ll keep tending our roofs, swapping school‑holiday tips and admiring the next tasteful renovation — quietly smug, happily suburban.
