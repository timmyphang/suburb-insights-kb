# Walloon (QLD)

Slug: `walloon-qld`

We know the bend in the road before 7001 Parkland Drive like it's our own mailbox — you can smell the smoke from the weekend even when the place is quiet. Back in the day, that address was home to Phat Boyz Smokehouse & Kitchen, the massive warehouse eatery Urban List drove 43 minutes from the city to praise. As they put it, their barbecued meats were "some of the best you’ll find on this side of the Pacific." We still tell that story like it’s a local legend.

There aren't any recent write-ups about Walloon, so for years the local brag has been this proud, slightly ridiculous claim: we’re a minor pilgrimage for anyone chasing proper American-style barbecue without having to brave inner-city traffic. If you blink, you’ll miss a glossy feature — which suits us. We like being the town that gets name-checked for one excellent thing and otherwise gets on with it.

Petty local drama? The archive’s short on gossip, so our squabbles tend to be about practical matters: who gets the prime spot in the lay-by on Saturdays, and whether the bins at the big eatery should be rearranged (we’re very passionate about bin placement). No tabloid feuds, no celebrity chefs; just a big shed, good smoke and people who drive a long way because someone on the internet said it was worth it.

Who would hate living here: the kind of person who needs five artisan outlets within walking distance and an events calendar that updates hourly. If your hobby is boutique everything, you’ll be disappointed — there’s not much in the way of buzz, and the only thing making national rounds was a barbecue joint from a few seasons ago.

Who would love it: people who like space, low-key bragging rights and a weekend drive that ends with a plate of meat so good someone on Urban List declared it worth the trip. We’ll keep our quiet corners and our smoky memories — and if you want directions to 7001 Parkland Drive, well, we already know the way.
