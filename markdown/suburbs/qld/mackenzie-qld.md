# Mackenzie (QLD)

Slug: `mackenzie-qld`

Mackenzie, QLD — we’re the suburb people tag when they mean someone (or something) else.

Back in the day the internet did more name-stealing than reporting on us. We wake up to notifications: Time Out gushing about Bell Shakespeare’s Mackenzie at The Neilson Nutshell (yes, that glamorous Macbeth-adjacent thing by Yve Blake and Virginia Gay), Concrete Playground posting about Matildas chatter and Mackenzie Arnold, and Broadsheet’s Grace Mackenzie writing about Sydney eateries as if we’d dispensed espresso tips from here. Meanwhile Urban List is off selling Skylark Cabin in the Mackenzie Region of New Zealand and Sitchu has us vaguely waterfall-adjacent with MacKenzie Falls in Victoria. Identity crisis? Us? Never.

Our weekend routine is mostly checking who’s borrowed our name this week. One minute we’re a five-star theatre review in Sydney, the next we’re an image credit at Spilt Milk or a caption on someone else’s scenic cabin. Locals have learned to roll with it: we don’t gatekeep the moniker. If the world wants to turn our name into a goalkeeper, an indie play, or an alpine hideaway, who are we to argue?

The petty local drama, such as it is, is the baffling stream of misdirected praise. As Time Out noted, Mackenzie (the show) is “flamboyantly fresh” — we’re flattered, and also slightly resentful the applause didn’t make its way back here. Concrete Playground’s Matildas pieces gave Mackenzie Arnold a moment in the sun; we accepted the glory tax and kept gardening.

Who would hate living here? If you need your postcode plastered across theatre programs, festival lineups, and glossy travel lists, you’ll be grumpy. If you insist your suburb come with a curated cabin on Urban List or a celebrity goalkeeper on speed dial, move to wherever those things actually are.

Who would love it here? People who enjoy living under the radar, who find charm in being the name everyone borrows but rarely visits. We’re content: modest, slightly bemused, and always ready to cheer when another Mackenzie knocks it out of the park — even if we’ll never quite be sure which one they meant.
