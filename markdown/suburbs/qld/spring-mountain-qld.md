# Spring Mountain (QLD)

Slug: `spring-mountain-qld`

Look, we don’t pretend Spring Mountain is the centre of everything — we’re the postcode that quietly waits for someone else to notice. Back in the day (and by that we mean: the only proper shout‑out we’ve got in the clippings), Urban List included 4 Larkhall Street, Spring Mountain in its roundup of Brisbane’s best Christmas lights for 2025. That’s our high‑gloss moment: one house, a string of bulbs, and suddenly we’re on a list. We wear it like a badge. Well, one of us wears it; the rest of us just nod and pretend we always knew.

Mornings here are predictably low‑rent glamorous: no artisanal brunch precincts to dodge, no boutique shopping strip to patronise — mostly the quiet hum of a suburb that lets its Christmas decorations do the entertaining. We can point to Larkhall Street and say, with total sincerity, “That’s the one.” That’s the morning routine — glance out the window, check if the lights are still on, adjust your expectations for the year.

If you’re after theatre backed by dramatic canyon walls, burros braying, tiki bars with fruity rum cocktails or celebrated robatayaki spots — relax. That’s a different Spring Mountain entirely. Time Out’s pages remind us there’s a Spring Mountain Ranch State Park and a Super Summer Theatre (not here), and that Las Vegas’ Spring Mountain Road hosts places like The Golden Tiki and Raku. Cute, isn’t it? Same name, wildly different agendas.

Who would hate living here? If you need late‑night neon, a constant stream of food reviews, or more than one famous address to brag about at dinner parties, you’ll find Spring Mountain excruciatingly uneventful. If your vibe is “I want my suburb to trend,” this isn’t your feed.

Who loves it? People who enjoy a bit of suburban calm, who think a single expertly decorated house can make December magical, and who prefer being pleasantly obscure until someone publishes a list. We’re not flashy. We’re content. We’re the place you tell your friends about when you want them to think you’re humble and selective. And honestly? That’s exactly enough.
