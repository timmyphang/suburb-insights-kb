# Logan Central (QLD)

Slug: `logan-central-qld`

Back in the day — and yes, our filing cabinet is mostly older cuttings — Logan Central felt like a compact little city with its own rhythms. We’d start the weekend by pretending 2.9 km² was vast: someone would say “let’s go out” and we’d end up by Wembley Road, where Kickin Inn at 38/74 used to do those terrifyingly generous Kajun seafood boils, and Mista Boo at 114 used to run a drive‑through sushi set-up that made us all feel slightly clever for ordering nigiri from our cars.

If you loved the idea of a proper community hub, you could file Logan Central under “practical and unflashy”: the Logan Central Community Centre on 9 Jacaranda Avenue used to be the place for local groups and Meals on Wheels, and the library — yes, the Logan Central Library — quietly did the heavy lifting on services and accessible home loans of reading material. We also had a go-to rainy-day pilgrimage: Logan City Tenpin, the bowling spot with roughly twenty lanes, where bad shoes and good attitudes were the dress code.

We’ll be nostalgic because our sources are older — Sitchu reminds us Global Food Markets Logan Central were set up in 2005 by the Ethnic Communities Council of Logan as a way to connect migrant farmers with the neighbourhood, and honestly that sums up our local vibe: practical, feed‑the‑people energy. The community mental health centre on 51 Wembley Road is a reminder that this place looks after its own, not that we ever make it the subject of snark.

Who would hate it here? If you crave a glossy boutique strip and think “community centre” sounds like a lifestyle downgrade, you’d probably scoff. If you need endless new-age brunch experiments, you might be disappointed — our glory is less about trend-chasing and more about useful places that actually work.

Who’d love it? Families, people who prize community services, anyone who fondly remembers lining up for seafood by the kilo, or who appreciates a library that comes to your home. We’re small, bordered by Berrinba, Karawatha, Kingston, Slacks Creek and Woodridge, and tucked into Logan City Council in the electorates of Woodridge (state) and Rankin (federal). Not flashy. Reliable. Ours.
