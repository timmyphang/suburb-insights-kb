# Burpengary (QLD)

Slug: `burpengary-qld`

Back in the day Urban List pointed a polite finger north and said, “If you’re cruising around Caboolture, Burpengary and Bribie Island, you’re in luck” — and honestly, that’s still our favourite compliment. We know the spots Urban List used to write up: Aldo’s Patisserie (yes, the one with zesty lemon baked cheesecakes, pulled beef brisket pie and chocolate ganache tarts) and Project Salubrious at 143 Rowley Road — the paleo-ish, completely gluten- and dairy-free place that allegedly looks out over a nature reserve.

Our weekend ritual used to involve a very civilised argument: inhale Aldo’s Friday-night dessert bar vibes (they even had live music, per the listing) or do the wholesome detour to Project Salubrious for something that tastes virtuous and expensive. We’ll admit we missed the point of some menu descriptions, but we admire the commitment. Urban List even recommended a road trip for clean-eating pilgrims — we’ll take that as high praise for a postcode many people still pronounce wrong.

If you ever try to stalk the market online, you’ll find the occasional Sitchu listing for places like 21 Biske Court, which proves Burpengary has its share of very normal suburban dreams and actual addresses. And yes, the parenting support networks are loud and proud in the background: Mama Tribe lists meetups across the region, and national guides like Mums & Bubs have the usual pages about baby gear — so this is where the sensible families land.

Who would hate living here? People who need a skyline and continental coffee pretence at 7am. Who would love it? Folks chasing sensible pies and decadent desserts in equal measure, parents who like nearby meetups and a nature reserve for the kids to run off the sugar, and anyone who appreciates a town that can be wholesome and indulgent without apologising.

We wear our regional badge with pride: not quite Bribie glamour, not Caboolture chaos — just Burpengary, where the cakes are earnest, the paleo is scenic, and the neighbours know your business (and probably your favourite tart).
