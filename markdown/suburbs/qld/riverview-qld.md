# Riverview (QLD)

Slug: `riverview-qld`

We’re Riverview, QLD: the suburb so effortlessly nondescript we get borrowed by every other Riverview in the country. Back in the day (and by that we mean all the articles we’ve got are older than our last council email), the internet kept sending people to other Riverviews — Morrisons Riverview Winery on the Murray, the Riverview Hotel with its glossy red doors in Balmain, a Riverview A‑Frame perched above the Numeralla, even a Riverview Homestead with a private pool over in Auckland. We wave politely.

Our morning routine is mostly making peace with identity theft. Somewhere between Broadsheet calling Morrisons Riverview “a stunner of a spot for a leisurely paced lunch” and Time Out rhapsodising about the Riverview Hotel’s firelit pies in Balmain, a Gold Coast ramen guide quietly dropped Hanakyo at 2 Riverview Rd, Nerang QLD 4211 into the map — listed as a “recent addition to the local ramen scene.” So if your idea of breakfast is being slightly offended by misdirected foodie pilgrims, you’ll fit right in.

Petty local drama? We don’t have much — Concrete Playground’s Riverview events page literally shows 0 results, which is either zen minimalism or passive-aggressive invisibility. Junkee once used the Riverview name when talking about St Ignatius’ College Riverview and its six rugby fields, which only fuels the conspiracy that “Riverview” is code for wealthy boarding-school real estate. Spoiler: we’re not that dramatic.

Who would hate living here: people who demand a curated inner‑west aesthetic — the ones in search of charcoal paint jobs, glossy red doors and house pies by a roaring fire can book a train to Balmain. Also anyone allergic to ambiguity (and to being mistaken for multiple riverfronts across three states and a different country).

Who would love it here: folks who like being quietly unremarkable, who enjoy collecting stories about Riverviews they’ll never actually visit, and anyone happy to let the internet do the heavy lifting of identity for a while. We’re happy to be the Riverview you didn’t know you needed — or the one you keep confusing with somewhere much fancier.
