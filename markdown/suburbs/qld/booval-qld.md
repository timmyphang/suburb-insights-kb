# Booval (QLD)

Slug: `booval-qld`

Back in the day we’d stroll down Brisbane Road and argue over which was our proper local flex: Char’d at 170 Brisbane Road or the Prince Alfred Hotel’s award-winning paddock-to-plate thing. As The Urban List once pointed out, Booval could do fancy dinners if it wanted to be fancy, even if we mostly used those later-night strolls to decide whether pie or chips was the superior carb.

Mornings here had a certain rhythm: some of us used to pop into community stuff for the kids — there were school holiday camps in North Booval, and Everyday Mums ran their Ipswich hub out of 191A Brisbane Street for anyone with a pram and questions. If your version of self-care was punching the air, Scott itf Taekwondo at 11a Green St put on classes for the “feel good” crowd. For the crooners among us, the Blackstone‑Ipswich Cambrian Choir rehearsed at 10 South Station Road — we’re still not over the soprano who hit the high note in 2019 and made the entire shopping strip stop.

Petty local drama? Oh yes: the trains. As Concrete Playground reported, when Queensland Rail’s pay dispute kicked in, trains were put on Saturday schedules during weekday peaks and Booval station was named on the list of stations to be unstaffed on weekends. Nothing brings out our collective civic poetry like queuing for a timetable update and pretending we don’t care.

Who would hate living here? If you need a concierge, staffed stations every hour, and cuisine that only uses metaphors you learned in a wine brochure, Booval will make you twitchy. We tolerate the train timetable like a long-running soap: dramatic, occasionally unreasonable, and best observed with snacks.

Who would love it? Families who appreciate local programs, anyone who thinks a restored 1830s Queenslander called Fairlight tucked down a quiet street is peak charm, and people who like a town that quietly offers choir practice, martial arts, and a proper restaurant on the same postcode. We’ll keep roasting ourselves — but only because no one else does it as lovingly.
