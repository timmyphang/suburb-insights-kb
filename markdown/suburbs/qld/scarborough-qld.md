# Scarborough (QLD)

Slug: `scarborough-qld`

Back in the day, when the internet still liked picturesque seaside towns, the write‑ups called us simple things: coastal beauty, parks and a relaxed seaside lifestyle. As Sitchu noted, Scarborough has a Boat Harbour and a village‑cafe vibe — and honestly, that’s the headline we wear with pride.

We like to start our mornings down at the Boat Harbour, pretending we’re doing something nautical and important while really just watching the tide and other people’s holiday plans. The village cafes — the ones the guides used to talk about — provided the unofficial soundtrack: low‑key, slightly earnest and very good at making being on holiday feel compulsory. These mentions are older features, mind you, so consider this a nostalgic wink rather than a current business directory.

There isn’t much here for people who need constant stimulation. If you require neon signs, endless late‑night venues or a never‑ending feed of big city trends, you’ll find our calm almost aggressively boring. We’re not mad — we just know we’re not for everyone. That’s the fun of it. The people who pack up and leave usually do so clutching a guide that wasn’t written for them.

We’ll own our reputation: we’re the suburb that would rather show you a perfect park and a stretch of sand than a flashy event listing. We’re proud of the kind of low‑pressure charm that makes people come back, or move in and quietly become the sort who waters other people’s plants while they’re away.

Who will love it here? Families who want parks and predictable horizons, boat lovers and anyone who measures weekends in tides rather than tabs. And if you can appreciate a village cafe vibe — again, as Sitchu put it — Scarborough will wrap you up in its unpretentious, slightly weathered shawl and refuse to apologise for being exactly what it is. We’ll see you at the harbour, pretending we’re busy.
