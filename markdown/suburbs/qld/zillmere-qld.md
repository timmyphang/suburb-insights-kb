# Zillmere (QLD)

Slug: `zillmere-qld`

We’ll admit it: we used to start our Saturdays arguing over whether the flaky meat pies from Ca Phe In (formerly Zillmere Bakery) or the pork from Banh Mi Factory Zillmere deserved the post-footy pilgrimage. Time Out once called Ca Phe In a “community icon” at 3/40 Handford Rd, and Urban List remembered the Banh Mi Factory’s family-recipe rolls — so yes, our food fights have receipts. Back in the day, that was breakfast culture.

Weekends looked like this: toddlers off to Keeping Mum sessions at the PCYC on 340 Zillmere Road (they ran regular Tuesday and Wednesday groups), older kids at Northside Wizards Basketball down the road on 20 Cambewarra Street, and charities like Be Uplifted Inc manning the op-shop at B1/194 Zillmere Road, where you could find someone’s vintage triumph or your neighbour’s misspent fashion choices. The Northside Wizards’ online presence bragged about being North Brisbane’s basketball home — WizKids to NBL1 and 5,000+ members — so if you enjoy organised chaos and whistle drills, that was our stadium of dreams.

We’re not big on glossy trend pieces — for years the local guides liked calling out the “zeitgeist of Zillmere” more in passing than in breathless lifestyle spreads — which suits us fine. Our community centre on 54 Handford Road used to run Open Days and even partnered with Brisbane Festival, which proves we can host a proper event without a pop-up latte bar or an influencer in a floppy hat.

Who would hate living here? If your life goal is artisan everything, boutique everything else and a curated street mural on every corner, you’ll feel out of place. If you can’t handle the idea of community programs, charity op shops and a basketball club that actually serves real kids (and real ambition), then move along — we won’t miss you.

Who would love it here? Families who need wrap-around support (Jabiru Communities and the community centre were built for that), bargain hunters, parents who appreciated Keeping Mum mornings, and anyone who likes their suburb with a functioning heart and actual people in it. We’ll keep the pies, the banh mi memories and the local teams — you bring the sense of humour.
