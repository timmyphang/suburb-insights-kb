# Crestmead (QLD)

Slug: `crestmead-qld`

We wake up to the sound of scooters being loudly educated at Crestmead Park — the one on the corner of Gimlet Street and Royena Court that everyone pretends is just “a park” but secretly doubles as our mini-traffic training ground and skatebowl. If you know how to parallel-park a scooter between the faux zebra crossing and the bench, you’ve officially earned your local stripes.

Back in the day the Crestmead Community Centre — PCYC Crestmead at 10 Gimlet Street — used to be the place you’d accidentally stay for three hours because there was a bingo game, a choir rehearsal, and someone always knew where the biscuits were. The Crestmead 40 Plus Club held cards, indoor bowls and monthly concerts there; it’s the kind of organised chaos we wear like a badge. Our medical emergencies were politely handled down the road at Crestmead Medical Surgery on 55 Waratah Drive — modest, reliable and exactly where you go when your kid learns a new scooter trick.

We’re not precious about our postcode. Logan City Council has had to open evacuation centres here during floods in the past, and yes, that’s not glamorous — but it did reveal what we always knew: when things get soggy, we look out for each other. Also, our patch isn’t a secret island — we’re tucked between Berrinba, Heritage Park, Logan Reserve, Marsden, Park Ridge and Waterford West, which makes for excellent “which suburb is that again?” conversations at school pick-up.

Who would hate living here? If you crave boutique cafés, lane-way murals and a cafe menu that quotes Nietzsche, Crestmead will politely refuse you. If you like your weekends curated by algorithms and Instagrammable avocado arrangements, you’ll find our skatepark and community centre programming shockingly unfiltered.

Who loves it here? Families who want real green space for kids to spend the day at the playground and scooter track. Folks who appreciate a community centre that actually runs things — locals, older residents who adore the 40 Plus Club, and anyone who’s ever queued at the Scholastic Children’s Book Super Sale when it rolled through in July 2026. We’re a tidy 7 km² of suburbs-with-attitude inside Logan council, and we’ll keep doing our thing — noisy scooters, bingo wins and all.
