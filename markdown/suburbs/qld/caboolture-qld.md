# Caboolture (QLD)

Slug: `caboolture-qld`

We know Caboolture the way our mums know the school pick‑up line: a bit chaotic, oddly comforting, and full of historical oddities you won't find on the tourist brochure. On a weekend morning we used to say the best start was the river — The Urban List even pointed the sup crowd to the Caboolture River, launching from Rafting Ground Road or Saint Smith Road — because nothing says "we're rural chic" like gliding past mangroves while someone else complains about tidal currents near Deception Bay.

For years the local oddities kept people coming: the Abbey Museum Caboolture was often flagged by guides for its medieval and Renaissance curios, and the Caboolture Historical Village — sprawling over about 12 acres — used to be the place you took visiting aunty to show off our 70‑odd buildings and 110,000 museum pieces. Back in the day we even had a Sizzler in town; it lived the buffet dream until it closed with the rest of the national chain.

Yes, the trains deserve a mention. Plans around Cross River Rail meant the Caboolture line was talked about like a celebrity — being reshuffled into new sectors — and state service changes have left us fond of checking timetables. Concrete Playground noted the joys of fewer staffed stations and timetable tweaks, so if punctuality is your religion, maybe bring a stopwatch.

We won't pretend bad headlines haven't hit home. National outlets covered tragic incidents here; people outside sometimes reduce us to those lines in the paper. We know the stories are real and painful, and we also know our suburb is more than the worst paragraph.

Who would hate living here? Folks who need every cafe to have a velvet queue, commuters who demand someone always on the platform after 1pm, and anyone allergic to a town that owns its history rather than neat façades. Who would love it? People who like space for curios, cheap day‑trip access to the Scenic Rim, paddling on a calm river, and the comfort of a place that still remembers how to be itself.

We're Caboolture: not polished, occasionally loudly proud, and stubbornly worth a weekend detour.
