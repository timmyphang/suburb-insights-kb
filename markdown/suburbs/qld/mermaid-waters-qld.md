# Mermaid Waters (QLD)

Slug: `mermaid-waters-qld`

We know the canals here like we know our own letterbox: a bit weathered, very photogenic, and always pretending to be Venice when the tide’s in. Back in the day Mermaid Waters was the kind of place where Saturday morning meant a riverside walk, a polite debate about whether your neighbour’s boat was actually a yacht, and a detour for something sweet.

For years the local press liked to call it quiet — Sitchu described our “quieter suburban feel” and those “beautiful homes along the stunning canal” — and you can see why. The bikeways, parks and nature reserves make it feel like someone banned stress on the zoning map. Broadsheet even noted Bleach Festival events including Playbook and Wayfinders happening in Mermaid Waters, which is our elegant way of saying we host culture without having to shout about it.

The food press loved us too — Urban List ran pieces on Mercado (which opened on the former Espresso Moto site), Double Barrel Kitchen as a local gem for breakfast, Nude Sisters Whole Food Cafe, The Pastry Emporium, and Lucky Bao. Concrete Playground name-dropped La Macelleria’s outpost and pegged the Mermaid Waters Hotel as a relaxed spot to watch the Socceroos. Worth noting: those write-ups are from previous years, so think of them as fond postcards rather than a live menu.

We’re also the kind of suburb that invites renovation. Mumspages ran listings for renovation builders servicing family homes in Mermaid Waters — which explains why there’s always one more site visit and a polite conversation about whether that outdoor room is finally happening.

Who would hate it here? If you crave tramlines, 3am kebabs and a skyline you can’t ignore, Mermaid Waters will feel like a sleepy postcard you can’t reschedule. If canal upkeep gives you hives, maybe don’t.

Who would love it? Families, people who want calm without disappearing off the map, and anyone who enjoys festival fringe culture — as Broadsheet showed, we get our moments. We like our canals, our cakes (ask The Pastry Emporium), and our low-key pride. We’re Mermaid Waters: quietly confident, modestly stylish, and always ready to wave at your neighbour’s boat.
