# Merrimac (QLD)

Slug: `merrimac-qld`

We’re the lot who’ll brave the blinking Santas to get the best photo op. Remember when Urban List flagged 16 Petherbridge Avenue as a must-see for its giant Santa and a North Pole postbox? That’s our street: traffic-stopping tinsel, neighbours turning their front yards into a seasonal theme park and us pretending we only came for the lights.

Saturdays used to look like this: we’d mosey down to the Organic Gold Coast Farmers Market held at Merrimac State High School (Dunlop Ct, Mermaid Waters), snag whatever counts as seasonal, then argue about whether the market stall with the oddest preserves is actually a culinary genius or a dare. Urban List once put that market on a winter-best list, so yes, we smugly tell out-of-towners it’s a local institution — on the quiet.

There was also local news that actually made us mutter into our cuppas: the Australian German Club, after more than 50 years in Merrimac, was slated to relocate as part of a new commercial development. We’re not saying people gathered at meetings wearing sensible shoes, but the town noticed a little bit of its history shift gears.

If you like your suburbs loud, chaotic and eternally under renovation, you’ll fit right in. If you can’t cope with a steak being described as “family atmosphere” serious — well, Urban List did name Mike’s Kitchen at 2 Gooding Drive as a top casual steak spot in one roundup — this is not the place to complain about tradition. And if you require an endless parade of boutique bars and flashing nightlife, Merrimac will bore you into a polite nap.

Who does love Merrimac? Families who appreciate a good school nearby (Merrimac State High School gets a name-check as a local option), people who treat a market morning like a sport, and anyone who thinks suburban Christmas competitiveness is a civic duty. There’s a cheap-stays entry in a past guide that promised homely rooms and free parking close to a local tavern — so if your idea of romance is a budget night and an epic lights display, we’ll see you at Petherbridge Avenue. We’ll be the ones holding the map and pretending we’re not actually lost.
