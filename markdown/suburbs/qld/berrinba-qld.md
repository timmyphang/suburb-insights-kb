# Berrinba (QLD)

Slug: `berrinba-qld`

Back in the day we’d set our alarms for 7am and shuffle down to Wayne Goss Drive because the community directory said parkrun — Berrinba was a thing. It was the kind of Saturday that starts with a volunteer-organised trot and ends with someone announcing they “only had one yiros” like it’s a moral choice.

We know every corner of our roughly 5 km² patch — the directory tells you that — and we can point out the borders without looking: Browns Plains, Crestmead, Drewvale. If you need proof we’re a proper suburb, we’re in Logan council and even sit in the electorates of Woodridge and Rankin. The local school was no latecomer either: Berrinba East State School was established in 1977, so we’ve been reheating sausage rolls at school fetes longer than some inner-city cafes have been open.

On weekend patrol we’ll admit our secret glitter: the Berrinba Wetlands. Urban List once put them in the pram-friendly column — about 8 kilometres of walking tracks, picnic spots, roughly 100 nesting boxes for birds/possums/gliders and a playground. Translation: bring a stroller, bring binoculars, bring someone who’ll carry the picnic rug when you’ve already walked three kilometres looking for a good photo angle.

Our most aspirational moment — at least according to older listings — was when The Yiros Shop opened a drive-thru here. There was an opening celebration (Wednesday 11 January) and free yiros from 4pm. We still tell that story like it’s a civic ceremony; there’s more ceremony in a midweek kebab giveaway than in some council meetings.

There isn’t a lot of scandal recorded about us — mostly community listings, wetlands and a love of early-morning volunteer runs — which suits us. If you’re after rooftop bars and late-night theatre previews, you’ll hate living here. If you’re allergic to nesting boxes, pram-friendly paths or yiros celebrations, this isn’t your postcode.

But if you like local routines, a school with history, weekend parkruns on Wayne Goss Drive and a wetlands that actually feels like a small wild thing tucked into Logan — you’ll love Berrinba. We do.
