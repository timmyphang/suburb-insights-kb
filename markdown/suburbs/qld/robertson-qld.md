# Robertson (QLD)

Slug: `robertson-qld`

We live in Robertson, QLD, and every morning we check the internet like it's a neighbour with a dodgy memory — it greets us with pictures of someone else’s backyard. Google thinks "Robertson" means a ten-metre concrete vegetable (the Big Potato), truffle dogs, or a foreshore picnic at Robertson Park in Watsons Bay. Back in the day the press liked those other Robertsons — Junkee even christened the Big Potato "Australia’s Shittiest Big Thing" and Time Out sent people truffle-hunting at Robertson Truffles in the Southern Highlands. So if you arrive here expecting black gold, giant tubers or riverside terraces you might be disappointed… or very, very confused.

Our weekend routine: we roll out of bed, make a plan, and then cancel it while scrolling through three separate Robertsons — the Pie Shop at 4400 Illawarra Highway (if you like pies, Time Out and Sitchu have opinions), the Robertson Potato Festival at the Showground (yes, the festival exists in someone’s Robertson), and assorted destination restaurants in Robertson Quay and beyond. It’s like a game of suburban spot-the-difference, except all the landmarks are in different states and countries.

Local petty drama? The internet’s done that for us. The Big Potato being named the nation’s worst tourist attraction is basically our civic anthem now. We don’t need to invent scandals — the name confusion and national mockery do the work. Also, shoutout to the Robertson Pub and the quaint property listings that insist our main ambition is being "village convenient" and walkable to cafés we can’t actually verify without consulting a map of NSW.

Who would hate living here: people who require a single, clear Google result for their postcode, or anyone allergic to ambiguous identity. Who would love it: people who enjoy being overlooked by travel roundups, or who like the idea of a town whose reputation is a national in-joke. We’ll take it — while others argue over which Robertson owns the truffle dogs, we’ll be here, quietly enjoying being our own Robertson, even if the internet keeps sending tourists to the wrong place.
