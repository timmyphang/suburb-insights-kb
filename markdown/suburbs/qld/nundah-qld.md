# Nundah (QLD)

Slug: `nundah-qld`

We’ve got a confession: we still miss the Nundah that used to surprise outsiders — the one where a heritage pub with more stories than sense stood next to a retro rooftop that looked like Granny raided an op shop and then opened a bar.

Back in the day we’d wander from the train at Nundah Railway Station, pop into Nundah Village and try to decide whether to start with bubbles at Blockhouse Eat/Drink (they used to run an AM menu until 2:30pm and once served maple bacon on fried ramen — yes, really), or head for Fitz + Potts with its 1960s wallpaper, vintage furniture and rooftop AstroTurf. The Plant Lounge on Station Street used to be our excuse for bringing home yet another pot plant after one of their plant-care classes; it even shared space with Vessel Nundah in that eco-friendly way that made us feel better about breathing.

We watched the Royal Hotel get a glow-up — a $1.1 million makeover that reopened the 135-year-old place in July 2023 with a refreshed bistro and a 200-person upstairs music room. Nundah by Night once rolled through with 120-ish stalls, a pop-up bar and a $3 entry fee, reminding us that the village could do busy, buzzy and slightly chaotic very well.

If you hate live jazz in a brick-walled room (The Village Social at 1188 Sandgate Road used to be full of it), curated share plates, or people who talk earnestly about succulents, you’ll loathe Nundah. If the word “heritage” makes you twitch and you prefer suburbs that stay politely anonymous, fold up your weekend plans and keep going north.

But if you’re into neighbourhoods that quietly reinvent themselves — where a renovated Queenslander sits within shouting distance of community festivals like the Village Street Festival and the Nundah Farmers Markets, where parents used to gather at Tiddlywinks Dance & Play Cafe for Keeping Mum sessions, and where you can still picture a pasta bar tucked away in the village — then Nundah used to be our little mischief and might still be yours to discover. We’ll keep the plant cuttings warm.
