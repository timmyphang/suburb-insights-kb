# Clear Island Waters (QLD)

Slug: `clear-island-waters-qld`

We wake up to the gentle clunk of a kayak paddle against our artificial canal and think: yes, this is living. Clear Island Waters was built around concrete canals for people who like water views without the sand in their shoes — Wikipedia even calls it a residential pocket built around those waterways. As Queensland Places politely reminds visitors, we’re the upmarket, canal‑estate suburb tucked west of Mermaid Waters.

Our weekend routine used to be embarrassingly simple: a stroll past the Surfers Paradise Golf Club (it’s one of those local features people point out), a quick scroll through realestate.com.au listings for fun, and maybe reading Tripadvisor’s take on what visitors actually think of our quiet little enclave. Back in the day Urban List described Matteo’s Woodfired Pizza as a “hidden gem” in town, and Goji Granola Bar & Cafe was the place that served a vegan “Coco Fluff” soft‑serve if anyone felt like being virtuous after a round at the club. Sapori at 60 Santa Cruz Blvd even made Urban List’s pizza and tiramisu roundups — yes, 60 Santa Cruz Blvd, don’t get it wrong.

We should probably own the vibe: GoldCoastInfo once summed us up as a quiet, established canal community suited to boating households and retirees. And that’s fine. We like our low‑key flex — manicured lawns, a boat moored out the front, and neighbours who quietly know the value of a good back fence chat.

Who would hate living here? If you crave a flashing nightlife, a footpath café scene on every corner, or a postcode that screams “party,” we’ll wave you off to the beaches. We’re not trying to be Broadbeach.

Who would love it? Boating households, people after upmarket canal living, retirees who enjoy the golf club shuffle and anyone who appreciates a neat suburb where the main controversy is which potted palm is Instagrammable. Population: 4,395 at the 2021 census, postcode 4226 — small, tidy, and cheerfully unpretentious.
