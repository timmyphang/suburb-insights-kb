# Kallangur (QLD)

Slug: `kallangur-qld`

Back in the day — and in the files we’ve been handed — Kallangur felt less like a postcode and more like a well-stocked community noticeboard. We know this place: 1347 Anzac Avenue used to hum with the Norths Leagues Club crowd (Concrete Playground reminded us it was once Pine Rivers RSL), where the late-night menu featured Turkish lamb wraps, teriyaki pork rice bowls and honey chicken. You could practically time your night by the chips.

Our Saturday ritual — as the archives tell it — involved Encircle Kallangur on 51 Marsden Road. The place ran everything from a fortnightly Craft Club to a weekly playgroup for the tiny humans, a “You Me She” women’s morning, and even a wood-burning workshop. If you wanted community spirit and a class to fix your social calendar, Encircle had it; the listing even included their contact, 07 3465 3200. For those who liked to be coached on what to eat and how to move, Lifestar Nutrition & Exercise Physiology was listed as having a Kallangur location in the Urban List directory.

We’ll admit our glamour credentials are humble, but Urban List once named Spray Tan HQ among Brisbane’s best — so someone here is getting golden. That’s the level of contradiction we love about Kallangur: leagues club late-night chips and polish-your-look spray-tans in the one postcode.

There isn’t tabloid-level drama in the documents — no council coups or viral scandals — just the pleasant, persistent hum of local life. If you want fight-club energy or boutique-espresso-to-go theatre, you’ll be disappointed. This isn’t the place for late-night neon or being seen; the scene here is knitting needles, playgroup stickers, and a teriyaki pork rice bowl that will not pretend to be Michelin.

Who would hate it: people after constant shiny novelty, the kind who need a new mural every week. Who would love it: parents who actually want a friendly playgroup, craft nerds who’ll show up for a wood-burning class, and anyone nostalgic for the Norths Leagues Club menu mentioned in Concrete Playground. We’ll keep the wrap warm.
