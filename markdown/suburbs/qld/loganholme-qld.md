# Loganholme (QLD)

Slug: `loganholme-qld`

Back in the day we’d roll out of bed, hair still protesting, and trundle down to Bryants Road like it was main street in a much better rom-com. The Foxy Coffee (37–59 Bryants Road) used to be our greenery-adorned refuge when we needed something to go with a heated debate about whose suburb had the best pie. Syd’s Pies — yes, next to the British Food Shop — was the one we’d defend like it was our own family recipe. Urban List even said Syd’s had the awards to prove it, so shove your opinions and give us a pie.

Weekends could look like this: a sleepy stroll past the shopping precinct, coffee in hand, then a detour to Fitzy’s on Bryants Road and Pacific Highway when someone declared it was suddenly “Christmas in July.” We’d pretend the pub’s traditional spread was a cultural experience. If we were feeling adventurous, we’d whisper about Sugar Creek Smokehouse — Urban List tipped that a Texas-style, shipping-container place was coming up in the Distillery Road Market at Eagleby — so we could pretend we’d driven for miles for authentic meat sweats.

Our local drama? Oh, we’ve had our hits. Once, a woman named Rose tried to lease that dodgy two-storey place and turn it into a haunted house; Logan City Council reportedly said no and our spooky dreams were dashed. And let’s not gatecrash history — Sizzler’s Loganholme was on the list when the chain wound up in Australia; Concrete Playground had the receipts that it closed with the pack in 2020.

Who would hate living here? If you can’t handle community groups, you might struggle. We’ve got Social Mums Club hosting Cornubia, Loganholme & surrounds meet-ups, and Keeping Mum running local mothers’ groups (they even used Keng Tudor Park on Clarks Road in their listings). If structured weekly catch-ups and swim schools like Swimtastic make you roll your eyes, best steer clear.

Who would love it? Families, mums, and anyone who likes a neighbourhood that still smells faintly of takeaway pies and pub chips. We’re low-key, useful and a bit stubborn — LSKD was even headquartered down the highway in Loganholme once, so we’ve got industrial cred. Come for the pies, stay for the local groups, and don’t expect a haunted house unless you bring your own fog machine.
