# Buddina (QLD)

Slug: `buddina-qld`

Back in the day we’d strut down to 5/5 Bermagui Cr like we owned the block — mostly to snoop at the timber-toned interior and mid-century vibes of Hey Day Buddina. Ashleigh and James, whoever they were in our coffee-adjacent folklore, had the playlist and the rotating filter brews that made us feel cultured on a Tuesday.

On a Saturday we used to swing by Pantree Wholefoods — that small, family-owned organic wholefood grocer the write-ups said was all about sustainable, waste-free living and stocking local Sunshine Coast products. It was the sort of place where you left with a reusable jar and a smug sense of doing right by the planet.

When someone wanted something faster and less precious, Hikaru Sushi Train at 138 Point Cartwright Drive, Buddina, 4575 QLD was the functional alternative — sushi on a conveyor, no mid-century philosophy required.

Look, the archive we found is a nostalgia playlist rather than a live stream — these mentions are from older write-ups — so this is more wistful roast than a current guide. There’s no juicy council scandal in the files, just tasteful timber, wholefoods ethics and a steady sushi ride.

Who’d hate it here? People after booming nightlife or endless novelty bars — Buddina’s vibe was never about that. Who’d love it? Folks who appreciate good design, rotating filter brews, sustainable groceries and a sushi train you can point at without ceremony. We’ll take the mellow, thanks — even if it’s all in the memory now.
