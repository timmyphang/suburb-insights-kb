# Old Reynella (SA)

Slug: `old-reynella-sa`

Back in the day we’d start the morning by pretending we were proper historians: wandering the walking-trail brochure the Reynella History Group put together, stopping by the Reynella Horse Changing Station c.1854 and Alexander Cottage to admire things older than our worst family gossip. There’s an op-shop that used to raise funds for the preservation — yes, even our nostalgia pays rent.

For weekday practicalities we relied on the listings: the Reynella Neighbourhood Centre (164–170 Old South Rd) used to run programs, hire out halls and rooms and keep sensible hours (Mon–Thurs 9am–4pm). Further along Old South Road, Community Bridging Services (CBS) at 211 Old South Road was the go-to for employment help and support for people with disability or disadvantage — they were on the clock Mon–Fri 9am–5pm, doing the steady community work that never gets a filter.

When we wanted our civic sport and social life, there was the Reynella Sports and Social Club at 1 Oval Rd — the kind of place the listing promised had bar hours, function hire and affiliates for football, cricket, bowling and tennis. It’s the sort of parish that measures status in match-day memberships and careful scallop-nosed chairs.

Full disclosure: our sources are older, so we’re reminiscing rather than recommending. If you hate low-key, communal suburbia and prefer a postcode that hums 24/7, this won’t be your scene. But if you love patched-up history, practical neighbourhood centres, and a sports club that still remembers scoreboards and sausage sizzles — welcome home. We do it quietly, reliably and with more op-shop elegance than we probably deserve.
