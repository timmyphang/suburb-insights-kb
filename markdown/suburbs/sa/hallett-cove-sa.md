# Hallett Cove (SA)

Slug: `hallett-cove-sa`

We get up early, lace the shoes we brought for actual walking, and head for the Hallett Cove Boardwalk where the sea does the yelling and the cliffs do the sculpting. You can stand on the loop and stare at the Sugarloaf — Time Out’s “famous” white sand-and-clay bit of geology — and pretend you’re on a very local Planet Earth episode. As Broadsheet politely suggested, it’s one of the cheap thrills: gaze at the coast from the boardwalk, breathe sea air, pretend we haven’t driven here.

Weekends here are a two-act play: Act One is the Marion Coast walking trail — moderate, five kilometres one-way if we’re doing it properly, with steps to climb and gullies to descend (Sitchu and Time Out made us promise). Act Two is the Pavilion on 246 Lonsdale Road, where we regroup among Woolworths, Drakes, BIG W, Aldi and BWS like responsible adults who also value impulse-buy clearance racks. The kids are at Hallett Cove School (yes, R‑12 — we see you at Sports Day), the dog is checked in at Hallett Cove Veterinary Surgery (they’ve been here since 1986, so they know our nonsense), and the footy is at The Cove Sports & Community Club when sport is the weekend religion.

We’re not above a little civic pride either: the Friends of the Hallett Cove Conservation Park quietly pull weeds, plant natives and run guided walks — real people doing real revegetation work. Nectar even used our coastline on their coffee packaging back when they introduced themselves, which is basically celebrity endorsement for rocks.

Who would hate it here? If you need a nightlife that runs past midnight, or you think “steps” and “gullies” are unacceptable lifestyle choices, you’ll be scrolling listings before you finish the boardwalk. If subtle geological wonders and a family-friendly pitch don’t light you up, this won’t either — and that’s fine.

Who’ll love it? Families, hikers, volunteers, people who like a shopping precinct that stocks what you actually need, and anyone who enjoys living beside cliffs that keep you humble. Time Out once called us underrated — we’ll keep that secret between the rocks and us.
